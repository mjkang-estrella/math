## Coordinate-dependent scalar compression: unsupported, parked

/root/h007_additive_relations supplied one executable card and rejected padding
with old bucket, energy and expensive orbit-canonicalization mechanisms.
For R=(x,y), set m(R)=centered_p(-y/x) and F(R)=[m(R)]R. Handle infinity, x=0
and m=0 mod q explicitly without compression. Draw independent R=aG+bQ,
hash F(R) with labels (ma,mb) modulo q, recover k from a collision with unequal
second labels, and verify kG=Q. All generation, inversions, scalar multiplications,
hashing, stored points/labels and failed recoveries are paid.

The exact symmetry m(-R)=-m(R) folds negation, already allowed to the baseline.
The suggested motivation t=-x/y, [m](t)=mt+... and mt=1 does not prove concentration:
a formal expansion is not a small-error approximation at arbitrary finite-field
points. Coordinate-field and group-order arithmetic are different. Prior context:
[PARI formal elliptic parameter documentation](https://megrez.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/html/Elliptic_curves.html).
No novelty established for this particular map.

Let pi be its output distribution and kappa=sum pi(S)^2. Conditional on useful
collision labels, independent sampling needs the birthday scale kappa^(-1/2),
with that many records. An O(log q)-group-operation sample would need roughly
q*kappa much larger than log²q just to repay this overhead against optimized rho;
kappa>=q^(-1+delta), fixed positive delta, would be a sufficient exponent margin
up to paid logarithmic work. Only the negation-scale concentration is established.
The unsupported assumption is much larger useful fibers on ordinary curves.

A finite synthetic fiber census could test a frozen finite prediction, but cannot
establish asymptotics. Proposer and root do not recommend a pilot merely because
this arbitrary map is easy to implement. Park as unsupported, not disproved for
all sizes. Verified improvement NONE.
