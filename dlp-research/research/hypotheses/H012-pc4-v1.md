# H012 PC4-v1: bounded-degree certificates with exhaustive witness fallback

Status 2026-10-08: concrete speculative operation, correctness preflight passed
on paper; NO cost/yield evidence or executable prototype. Engine deferred after
review; see findings/H012-pc4-disposition.md and retained single-node protocol.
Native Astra proposer

/root/h007_additive_relations; root integration. This is standard polynomial
calculus applied to a root-domain decomposition search, not a newly discovered
proof system. The hypothesis is inexpensive useful pruning, not correctness alone.

## Family and collector gate

p>3, E:y^2=x^3+Ax+C, FULL #E(Fp)=q odd prime, labelled target R=aG+bQ.
Five coordinate domains x_i=u_i+z_i, z_i in mu_(B_i), B_i=ell_i^k_i,
ell=(2,3,5,7,11), B_i|p-1, B_i~B~q^(1/5). Supply certified generators beta_i.
Parameter generation, order tests, generators, factor-base rational lifting,
signs, target seeds and all failed setup attempts are PAID. This restrictive
family is not established uniformly, nor transferred to secp256k1/arbitrary p.

The proposed one-row-per-query collector needs setup+O(B*K/sigma+B^2) even under
favorable rank accounting. To improve its exponent, K/sigma must beat B^(3/2)
by a fixed power margin. Actual rank/yield, memory and complete bit costs remain
unproved. A successful pruning predicate alone cannot satisfy this condition.

## Systems without expanded S6

Process all31 nonempty subsets of domains in increasing arity then lexicographic
index order. A singleton uses direct signed-point membership. For m>=2 use m-2
intermediate abscissae and m-1 successive S3 equations, ending in r=x(R):

    S3(X,Y,Z)=(X-Y)^2 Z^2
      -2*((X+Y)*(XY+A)+2C)*Z+(XY-A)^2-4C(X+Y).

m2 has just S3(x1,x2,r). Intermediate variables are over the algebraic closure:
DO NOT add their Fp field equations. Across all charts there are49 S3 equations,
23 intermediate variables and5 singleton checks, plus domain-membership copies.
Charts can run sequentially; each chain is copied into15 nonsingleton charts.

Domain chain z_(i,j+1)=z_(i,j)^ell_i, z_(i,k_i)=1 is broken into quadratic gates
by exponent schedules (2), (2,3), (2,4,5), (2,3,6,7), (2,4,5,10,11).
Gate counts per level g_i=(1,2,3,4,5). With t_i low exponent digits fixed to e_i,
the boundary is z_(i,k_i-t_i)=beta_i^(e_i*ell_i^(k_i-t_i)); substitute it and
remove constant higher gates. Let Q_g=sum g_i*(k_i-t_i). Before other
simplification a chart has n=Q_g+m-2 variables and Q_g+m-1 equations.

## Sound degree4 closure and branching

Use exact Fp sparse polynomials, fixed graded lex order and stable normalized
row-echelon basis in the vector space of monomials of total degree<=4.
FIFO starts with gate equations then S3 equations, in fixed index order.
Reduce each request against the basis. Discard zero; an actual nonzero constant
is a contradiction certificate. Otherwise insert its independent normalized row.
If its ACTUAL degree<=3, enqueue its product with EVERY active variable.
Lazy queue records may avoid eager copies, but all realized work is charged.
Low-degree remainders produced by cancellations must also be multiplied.
Degree-compatible echelon pivots make the low-degree part of the span generated
by its low-degree basis rows; this is not just one batch of input multiples.

Every row has an ideal-membership derivation by scalar linear combinations and
variable multiplication. Store a charged derivation DAG and independently replay
a claimed constant certificate before pruning. Queue exhaustion without constant
means UNRESOLVED, never nonempty or empty. Any work cap also means UNRESOLVED.

For unresolved nodes, round-robin branch across unfinished selected coordinates,
skipping completed ones: t_i'=t_i+1 and e_i'=e_i+c*ell_i^t_i for c=0,...,ell_i-1.
Depth-first increasing digits. No root/DLP oracle: digits index powers of known
beta_i. At leaves use explicit original coordinates, paid factor-base point
lookups and at most3^5 sign/omission choices; verify the actual group sum equals
R. Do not solve intermediate variables. Coalesce overlapping/repeated points and
cancel coefficients before declaring a useful row. At most one verified row per
query. Identity targets have separate explicitly charged handling.

Any genuine decomposition of nonzero R has a minimal nonempty signed subset
with no zero proper prefix, yielding finite intermediate abscissae in one chart.
Conversely, raw algebraic chain solutions need not be rational useful witnesses;
verification controls soundness. Original x in Fp lifts over Fp2. The sum of
irrational anti-Frobenius inputs is rational if their total plus rational inputs
is +/-R, hence 2-torsion. Full odd #E(Fp) forces this sum to O. A nonempty
rational signed subset remains for R!=O. Merely having an odd-order subgroup
would NOT suffice for this extraction argument. Exhaustive leaf checking is the
correct fallback even with extraneous chain solutions.

## Costs, falsifier and implementation gate

M=binom(n+4,4) bounds basis dimension: <=M insertions and
<=Q_g+m-1+n*M queue requests. Conservative dense elimination upper bound
O((Q_g+m+n*M)*M^2) field operations is NOT a lower bound or predicted runtime.
Basis storage O(M^2), with additional paid derivation history, queue, branch state
and certificate replay. Retain actual sparse-fill/structural counts in any run;
no claim that sparse work follows matrix dimensions alone. All charts/nodes,
branch powers, term operations, inversions, copies, references, leaves, setup,
target seeds, rank processing and failed attempts count.

Unsupported assumption: enough empty cosets have inexpensive degree4 constant
certificates to make whole-query K/sigma pass the collector gate. No evidence.
A paper falsifier could exhibit, for many empty nodes, a proper subspace
containing inputs, excluding1, closed under all allowed degree-limited variable
multiplications. Static input-multiple pseudoexpectations alone are insufficient.

At k_i=1 all five domains have15 gates,n18 and M7315. M is capacity, not a
minimum cost. Initial quadratic-multiple count15*binom(20,2)=2850 also does not
price closure/elimination. Independent preflight found a single-node diagnostic
potentially meaningful; no fit or speedup claimed. Before any run freeze exact
synthetic setup, target distribution, node, simplifications, queue/ledger and
certificate checking under one total1M event/60second cap. A cap hit rejects only
that practical schedule; one-node success establishes neither full-query yield
nor asymptotic improvement. No broad algebra engine is authorized by this card.

## Other compressed cards retained

The companion-norm one-slice reference in H012-compressed-witness-gate.md is
correct, but leaves B^4 outer work. Carrying a further coordinate in a quotient
costs dimensionB before the remaining B^3 scan; no cheaper selector was supplied.

Trace sketch: in the grid algebra of dimension N=product B_i | p-1, let A_s
be multiplication by s=S6(u_i+z_i,r). Since it splits into N Fp evaluations,
N-Tr(A_s^(p-1)) modulo p is the exact integer zero count (0<=count<=N<p).
Computing that high-index trace and recovering a witness are missing operations.
For 16k<min B_i, degree bounds and root-of-unity orthogonality give
Tr(A_s^k)=N*s(0,...,0)^k: all nonconstant exponents are too small to wrap.
Thus the short degree1 moment recurrence does not reveal roots or an annihilator.
This scoped observation is not a lower bound on every trace-based algorithm.
