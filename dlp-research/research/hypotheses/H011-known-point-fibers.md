## Concrete first subcase

On E/F_p with p>3 and full odd prime rational group order q, consider
f_T(P)=x(P-T) for rational centers T. These are degree-two maps. Seek a common
nonconstant h=H_i(f_Ti), allowing rational outer maps, then use shared rational
fibers and known numerical scalar anchors to build useful dependencies. All
function construction, enumeration, point identities, rank/extraction and
collisions must be paid. No free list of a fiber's unknown discrete logs.

Root proposed the following cheap screen. Each f_T is invariant under reflection
R_T(P)=2T-P. A common h for distinct T1,T2 is invariant under translation
2(T1-T2), which has order q. Translation acts freely on geometric points; an
invariant pole divisor consists of q-point orbits with constant multiplicity.
Thus q divides its positive degree. Also deg(h)=2deg(H_i), so 2q divides deg(h).
This would exclude a common composite of degree below q in this subcase only.
Native proposer confirmed the orbit argument; independent Sol review is live.

## Essential high-degree caveat and test

The proposer identified an explicit compact candidate:

    h(P)=x((pi-[1])P),  pi=Frobenius.

The proposed check is that pi-[1] is separable with rational-point kernel of
size q, hence h has degree2q and is invariant under every rational reflection.
It factors through each degree-two translated-x quotient as a rational function.
On E(F_p), however, pi-[1] vanishes, so the common fiber is the whole rational
group and the h-value test supplies no target selection. High degree does not
prove costly evaluation. Independent reviewer must check all these statements.

The actual algorithmic question is whether known numerical scalar anchors and
this shared fiber enable a paid operation better than ordinary reflection /
translation collision search. A compact expression for the whole group is not
a free enumeration or a discrete-log oracle. The proposer must specify a
changed operation or give a scoped rejection; general higher-degree functions
and other divisor arrangements remain outside the degree-two screen.
