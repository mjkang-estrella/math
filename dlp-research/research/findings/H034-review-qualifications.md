## Factory sparse-pair cost/prior-art report

Its bounded literature search found no exact random-label C4-free dictionary
construction. This is not a novelty proof. Shared-inverse paired x outputs,
negation's ideal birthday constant, and structured collision-avoidance methods
are prior art; the specific composition and proof need separate assessment.

The small ideal key-count margin against empirical grumpy, paid seed work,
and need for matched peak are accepted concerns. The stated numerical seed
crossover near 2^66 is an UNVERIFIED rough model, not a tested threshold or
a proof that all testable sizes lose. It assumes an unimplemented comb setup,

The headline that every ingredient is known does not prove that the composition
is known. The claim that H024's family floors are the 'only route' below the
random-model constant is not accepted as a universal exclusion.

Other unaccepted extensions:

- Using small b_i changes the independent uniform-label premise. Regenerating
  labels through a PRF does not instantiate the exact IID theorem for free.
- The query-only continuation coefficient after a retained archive requires a
  conditional collision/hazard argument. No such continuation is in the frozen
  contract, which instead uses a fresh independently blinded H019 fallback.
- C4-light graphs and the asserted K=6 loss/crossover need new combinatorial
  and probabilistic analysis. They are speculative alternatives, not an accepted
  optimization or a reason to abandon the current reviewed graph.
- Shared-vertex duplicate counts are already covered conservatively by the
  mathematical screen's O(m Delta) term. Specific finite percentages need the
  actual graph prefix and q, not just exponent substitution.
- A seed equal to O or an equal-x edge need not solve the DLP: the corresponding
  target coefficient can be zero. Handle nulls and verify candidates.
- The frozen archive stores exact x, not a truncated fingerprint; there is no
  free lookup-parity claim. Seed reads, head zeroing, hash arithmetic, record
  traffic and baseline's available spare memory remain leading-cost issues.
- The suggested graph traversal/locality differs from the frozen order and is
  not a measured cache result. It cannot silently replace the contract.
- Scalar-value simulations can reproduce geometric collision laws under the
  exact independent-label model. They cannot measure full EC solver cost.
- The sample-power estimate is a rough variance calculation, not a performed
  experiment or precise test-design theorem.

The raw provider usage footer is provider-reported metadata, not a root-verified
token/credit measurement. No experiment or repository modification by reviewer.

## Grok live-coverage report

Task ecdloop-h034-live-coverage-compositions-grok-20261008-r1 completed.
It offered serial doubling, Fibonacci and doubling-orbit mechanisms, retaining
known fixed-stride families as prior art rather than new discoveries. Source
constants were taken from project records, not re-read in this review. No run.

Root found a concrete omission in the serial doubling rejection. For

    P_n = 2P_(n-1) + G, P_0=Q,
    (a_n,b_n)=(2^n-1,2^n),

positive equality only yields k=-1 (outside coefficient degeneracies), as the
reviewer says. But NEGATIVE equality gives

    (2^n+2^m)(k+1)=2,
    k=-1+2/(2^n+2^m)

whenever that denominator is nonzero. For n=1,m=0 this is -1/3, and for
n=2,m=0 it is -3/5, distinct over primes other than 2,3,5. Thus the asserted
O(1) total image does not follow; the signed collision image was omitted.
This correction alone establishes neither an efficient generator nor any gain.

Likewise the counting floor for a set of at most t(t-1) candidate scalars needs
a label schedule independent of unknown k, or a separate adaptive decision-tree
argument. It is not accepted for arbitrary coordinate-adaptive rules. Claimed
universal exclusions from per-key arithmetic require validating the selected
coordinate formula and complete schedule. Root has not independently checked
every Fibonacci or x-doubling subtotal; preserve them as scoped reviewer
screens, not broad mathematical impossibility. Thresholds at t=32 and64 are
suggested triage criteria, not necessary conditions for all improvements.

Next work remains the candidate-specific matched-memory complete-cost gate.
These corrections prevent strong reviewer headlines from substituting for that
analysis. No additional generic infrastructure or numerical benchmark follows.
