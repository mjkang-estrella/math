Sol inspected Kohel–Shparlinski §3, Theorem1/Corollary1, PDFpp4–5:
https://www.i2m.univ-amu.fr/perso/david.kohel/pub/character.pdf
The paper's q denotes field size. With project notation the unnormalized bound
is 2d*sqrt(p), and group normalization divides by q=ord(G). Pole assignments
need separate corrections. The printed broad statement requires the usual
additive-character nondegeneracy qualification: psi(h^p−h) can be constant.
A rational function with a pole order prime to p gives straightforward
nondegeneracy. No unconditional bound for every rational expression is adopted.

In characteristic>3, x([2]P)+x([3]P) is itself rational with pole degree at most24
and genuine order2 poles. Thus this fixed mixture is not automatically outside
the applicable estimate. This says nothing universal about arbitrary public
computations, learned features or adaptive mixtures.

If a supplied Fp-homomorphism sends G to a point of order q, reindexing transports
the scalar character to that image subgroup; use the image observable's degree.
This does not give free map construction/evaluation. In particular [u]Q costs
O(log u) group operations by ordinary multiplication; endomorphism degree or
norm is not that cost. Withdraw the raw small-norm symmetry exclusion. Its PRF
card still needs a compatible observable action and a paid useful decoder.

Arbitrary rational-map fibres are not kernel cosets. For a fixed Fp-isogeny phi
and geometric preimage R, Frobenius on its fibre is affine:
T↦pi(T)+(pi(R)−R); the offset vanishes for rational R. A separate exact statement
holds for nonzero targets in a prime-order subgroup: all fibres above kG, k≠0,
are Frobenius-isomorphic for that fixed isogeny. Choose u≡k modq coprime to
q*deg(phi), possible by CRT. Every fibre point is killed by q*deg(phi), so [u]
is invertible on the relevant torsion, maps the G fibre onto the kG fibre and
commutes with Frobenius. The zero fibre may differ. This covers neither arbitrary
rational maps nor target-dependent covers; computing the bijection would use k.

For a fixed good p-adic curve lift, the prime-to-p torsion lift has zero extended
elliptic logarithm. Distinguish this from the formal logarithm's formal-subgroup
domain. It does not make every lift-independent invariant constant: reduction
of x of the lift is the varying x(Q). H003 already distinguishes arbitrary
sections/corrections from the torsion section. No useful concentration of a
computable correction is supplied here.

Retain as missing constructive operations: a useful PRF-action decoder,
target-dependent-fibre signal, or lift correction with a useful distribution
and paid evaluation. Do not turn these missing constructions into impossibility
theorems. Likewise H019's particular compact membership evaluator loses its
complete-cost comparison, not because all such representations are circular.
No universal two-type information classification is accepted. Verified gain NONE.
