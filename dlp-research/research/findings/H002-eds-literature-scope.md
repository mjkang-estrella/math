# Fixed EDS shifts: source scope correction

Root retrieved the full [Bhakta–Shparlinski preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.09271v1),
Theorem1.2 and Question1.1; native Astra independently obtained the same scope.
The first raw review's 'only large H' description overlooks bound1.3. For R≈p
and N≈R, its normalized bound is
`H^(-1/8)*p^o(1) + H^(1/2)*p^(-1/8)*log(p)`.
For polynomial H=p^alpha, balancing powers gives alpha=1/5 and saving1/40,
ignoring logarithmic/subpolynomial factors. Bound1.4 instead has normalized
size `H^(-1/4)*p^(5/24)` up to logarithms, hence saving1/24 at H≈p.

Both concern averages of absolute correlations over shift boxes, including
fixed higher correlation orders. Neither gives a useful bound for our fixed
small offset tuples, proves no exceptional tuple exists, or supplies a predictor.
Thus higher-order correlation study itself is prior art; only a specified cheap
exceptional-family mechanism might add something. No ECDLP gain follows from
the open fixed-shift question or these source bounds. This narrows novelty and
corrects scope without prematurely rejecting A1.
