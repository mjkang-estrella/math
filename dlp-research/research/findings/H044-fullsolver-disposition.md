## Side probe: an assumed collision law is not an optimistic bound

The nonexceptional affine field invoice 6M+2S+epsilon_K for a full sum and
opposite abscissa is retained from H043. It excludes detection, labels,
memory, exceptional branches, replay, verification and failed attempts.
epsilon_K=(I−3M)/K belongs to the actual finite batch, not a measured I/M ratio.

In the stipulated ideal model with t stored main keys and two independent
uniform probes into N~q/2 keys, eligible-pair hazard is approximately 2t/N.
The corresponding leading Rayleigh mean is sqrt(pi*q/8). This differs from
detecting every pair among two emitted samples per round, whose leading
round count is half the one-sample count. The reported arithmetic comparison
against the conditional x-only BSGS schedule may be calculated within these
models. Neither law has been established for the deterministic correlated
side probes of the proposed walk. Correlation need not only increase cost;
an IID estimate is not a universal lower or upper bound for adaptive walks.

Using the mean stopping count as a BSGS table size is also not a matched fixed
memory-cap experiment. Tail failures, bytes per record, label representation,
setup, batching and actual lookup/recovery work must be matched. The prior
0.19sqrt(q) crossing is a field-only conditional comparison, not a universal
memory threshold. No measured whole-solver superiority or inferiority follows.

## Sum-feature fiber: correct involution, narrower exclusion

Use the H043 feature, with anchor G=(a,y_G), f(t)=t³+a_E*t+b_E,
F=f(a)!=0 and C=f'(a). For u=t−a,

    phi(t)=2a+2C/u+4F/u².
    partner(u)=−2F*u/(C*u+2F).

Factor phi(a+u)−phi(a+v) to obtain the ordinary branch u=v and
C*u*v+2F*(u+v)=0. The displayed partner is a nontrivial projective Möbius
involution: its matrix has determinant −4F² and its square is scalar 4F².
It fixes u=0 and u=−4F/C, with the second fixed point at infinity if C=0.
Poles, infinity and addition exceptions need explicit dispatch. This computes
a fiber of abscissae, not a scalar decoder.

The raw translation-degree argument is false. Translation by T generally
does not descend to a rational function of x(P). Descent would require
x(P+T)=x(−P+T) as a function identity, forcing 2T=O. For nonzero two-torsion
T=(tau,0), the actual descended map is the Möbius function

    x(P+T)=tau+f'(tau)/(x(P)−tau).

There is nevertheless a narrow automorphism-lift exclusion for this partner
when the anchor G has odd prime order. Suppose an algebraic automorphism
A(P)=eta(P)+T descends through negation to the partner. Descent gives 2T=O.
Since the partner fixes x(G), eta(G)+T=±G. Thus T is both q-torsion and
two-torsion, so T=O and eta(G)=±G. The square of the descended involution is
identity, so eta² is either identity or negation (the two deck maps of x).
If eta²=1 then eta=±1, since nonzero elliptic endomorphisms have nonzero
composition; their descent would be identity. Hence eta²=−1, contradicting
eta²(G)=G for odd-order G. This excludes that algebraic automorphism lift,
not an arbitrary finite-set correspondence or other extraction algorithm.

The appropriate status is still: no scalar-recovery rule for nontrivial
feature hits was supplied. No fixture or broad impossibility claim follows.

## Progression blocks: signed events and the actual block invoice

Assume D=dG!=O is known-log and P_(i,j)=C_i+jD, j=0,...,ell−1, with
C_i=a_iG+b_iQ. Offset intervals below must not wrap modulo q.
Distinct within-block positive equalities are impossible under that assumption.
Within-block NEGATION hits instead satisfy

    2C_i+(j+h)D=O,

with potentially useful target coefficient 2b_i. For j<h, achievable integer
sums are 1,...,2ell−3 when ell>=2. Direct infinity checks give additional
cases. Therefore the raw claim that all within-block hits are null is false.

Cross-block positive equality satisfies C_i−C_m=(h−j)D; if the difference
is delta*D, then P_(i,j)=P_(m,j+delta), correcting the raw sign. Negation
satisfies C_i+C_m=−(j+h)D. Each sign has L=2ell−1 possible atoms.
When L<=q and centres are independent uniform in an odd-order group,
(C_i−C_m,C_i+C_m) is jointly uniform. The exact pair union probability is

    2L/q − L²/q²,

not 2L/q. Uniform difference alone does not suffice: C_m=−C_i with C_i
uniform gives uniform difference but a certain cross-negation hit at j=h=0.
Different block-pair events are still dependent. An asserted one-addition
"full mix" does not establish independent uniform centres. Useful label
coefficients, nulls and recovery require their own law.

The stated block contains one mix and ell−1 subsequent additions, so its
arithmetic price is C_f+(ell−1)C_x. The raw C_f+ell*C_x double-counts one
step. Setting expected collision count to one is not computing the expected
first-hit time. Dropping the finite −2 in 4ell−2 is especially unjustified
when optimizing to a constant small ell.

For illustration only, stipulate an additional cross-block Poisson/Rayleigh
approximation at fixed ell and large q, ignore internal hits and nulls, and
assume the proposed generation prices apply. Then

    E[blocks] ~ sqrt(pi*q/(8ell−4)),
    W ~ (C_f+(ell−1)C_x)*sqrt(pi*q/(8ell−4)).

Continuous minimization gives ell=C_f/C_x when admissible, and coefficient
sqrt((pi/2)*C_x*(C_f−C_x/2)). For C_f=6 and C_x=3.5, this is about4.83,
not the raw6.5, in units M*sqrt(q); integer ell and all omitted work remain.
It is a corrected conditional arithmetic calculation, not a validated cost
of this walk or a runtime lower bound. Even this illustration shows no gain
over the referenced3.5 field-only BSGS coefficient at S/M=1. Actual admission
still needs generation, useful-event law, detection and matched memory.

For the raw hypothetical C_f=C_x, the same corrected model instead minimizes
at ell=1 with coefficient sqrt(pi)/2*C_x, not sqrt(2)*C_x. The offset count
therefore does not even prove the raw negative assertion under all its own
ideal prices. This is not a realizable cheap refreshed-centre operation; it
is a countercheck on the claimed invoice, with no algorithmic gain inferred.

No generic distinguished-point slowdown exponent follows without a concrete
detector and coalescence analysis. Replacing all-history storage by a sparse
detector changes both the event set and recovery protocol; neither is free.
