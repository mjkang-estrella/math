## Accounting that was frozen

A retained record is an abscissa plus a public index. The index recovers \((a,b)\) for \(X=[a]G+[b]Q\). A useful check is \(X_1=\varepsilon X_2\) with \(b_1-\varepsilon b_2\not\equiv 0\pmod{q}\), so
\[
k\equiv(\varepsilon a_2-a_1)(b_1-\varepsilon b_2)^{-1}.
\]
One hash probe of a new key against \(M\) stored keys tests at most \(2M\) such equations. Generation, the probe, a scalar verification on a hit, and the stored field element are all charged. Field additions are omitted, as in the H019 ledger. Constants of the curve may be precomputed once.

These comparisons are not used, because H033 withdrew them as universal bounds: the two-phase identity \(f(\alpha)\ge 1\), the false probe minimum \(q/(2M)\), a constant hazard on a stopping-dependent retained set, and a full-storage birthday constant for Brent or Floyd.

## Why the equality floor is not a new schedule

For any rule that keeps every key and only tests the new key against earlier keys,
\[
|C_t|\le t(t-1),
\]
so a uniform full-range scalar satisfies \(E[T]\ge(2/3)\sqrt{q}\) up to lower-order endpoint terms. That counting floor is about \(0.667\sqrt{q}\) keys. It sits under \(0.943\sqrt{q}\) and under the empirical \(0.90\sqrt{q}\). Reaching it requires almost no repeated collision values.

Paying a full batched addition \(5M+S\) for each of those keys loses at every \(s=S/M\in[0,1]\). The field-work ratio against interleaved x-only keys is
\[
\frac{5+s}{\sqrt{2}\,(2.5+s)}\ge 1.21.
\]
Shared-stride side keys do not repair this. If every retained abscissa is \(x(C_n+[u]T)\) for one public \(T=[s]G\) and \(u\) in an interval of length \(L\), then each center pair and each sign contributes \(O(L)\) scalars, not \(L^2\). Long blocks therefore add keys faster than they add residue classes. Short blocks do not receive the \(2.5M+S\) amortization. Distinct strides restore an \(L^2\) image per family pair, and that is the H024 family model.

## Rejected mechanisms

**Serial doubling chain.** Freeze \(P_0=Q\) and \(P_n=[2]P_{n-1}+[\sigma]G\) with \(\sigma\in\{0,1\}\) public. Then \(b_n=2^n\). For \(\sigma=0\), every positive equation is \((2^n-2^m)k\equiv 0\), so only \(k\equiv 0\) is ever excluded. For \(\sigma=1\), \(a_n=2^n-1\) and every positive equation returns \(k\equiv -1\). Generation is one doubling per index. The image has size \(O(1)\), so a new point does not supply a growing set of useful checks. This is an identity, not a runtime estimate.

**Fibonacci frontier.** Freeze \(U_0=G\), \(U_1=Q\), \(U_n=U_{n-1}+U_{n-2}\), labels \((F_{n-1},F_n)\), cap \(M=t\) as the chain grows. Each step is one full addition, or one general differential addition if only abscissae are kept. The project ledger for that differential step is the EFD XZ cost \(7M+2S\), which is above \(5M+S\). Even the impossible perfect image \(E[T]=(2/3)\sqrt{q}\) then loses to interleaved x-only by the factor above. Catalan-type repetitions make the real image strictly smaller. Example: the gap \(d=2\) produces \(k=-F_m/F_{m+1}\), while other gaps repeat values such as \(-1/2\). No curve run is needed.

**x-only doubling orbit of a baby interval.** Freeze starts \(Q+[i]G\) and replace each retained abscissa by the double
\[
x(2P)=\frac{(x^2-A)^2-8Bx}{4(x^3+Ax+B)}.
\]
With additions omitted and batch inversion, a general \(A\) costs \(4M+2S\) per key. Against interleaved x-only keys the perfect-image field-work ratio is
\[
\frac{4+2s}{\sqrt{2}\,(2.5+s)}\ge 1.13
\]
on \(s\in[0,1]\), about \(1.20\) at \(s=0.8\). If \(A\) is a small integer, \(A x\) becomes additions and the cost drops to \(3M+2S\); at \(s=0.8\) that is still about \(3\%\) worse than empirical grumpy at a perfect image, and the image is not perfect. The labels are \((2^j i,\ 2^j)\): arithmetic progressions with strides \(2^j\) and coefficients \(2^j\). That is an H024 power-of-two family list, built more expensively than translating those same progressions at \(2.5M+S\).

Anchor slopes are the same boundary. A slope from a new full point to a fixed anchor completes the addition that produces the third abscissa. It is one batched addition, not an extra probe below addition cost.

Higher than pairwise equality was not carded. One new point against every stored pair can exclude \(O(M^2)\) scalars only if those triple tests are actually formed. A product-tree test of cost soft-\(O(M)\) per new point gives total work \(M+q(\log M)^2/M\), whose minimum is still \(\sqrt{q}\) times a logarithm. A genuine \(M^\alpha\) exact test with \(\alpha<1\) would change the exponent, and no such test is given here. That is the same missing primitive as the implicit pair-sum query, not a frontier schedule.

## What was not reopened

Fixed-stride families, signed and interleaved BSGS, grumpy shift tables, phase rebuilds, retired-endpoint promotion, and coalescent eviction are existing H024 and H033 material. No lookup-structure retuning is proposed. Prior-art constants above are the in-repo GWZ and H019 ledgers, not PDFs re-read in this pass. The \(0.90\sqrt{q}\) figure remains an experimental GWZ average, not a proved expectation.

A later card in this lens would need a frozen label recurrence whose generated keys cost at most \(2.5M+S\), are not a union of fixed public progressions, and whose exact collision image at \(t=32\) and \(t=64\) satisfies \(|C_t|/t^2\ge 0.8\) by integer arithmetic alone. Failing either test rejects it before any curve operation. No schedule meeting that bar was found.
