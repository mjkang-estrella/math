## Accepted two-phase leading model

Under the specified listed-search costs, first cover fraction alpha of a uniform
full-range target using beta sqrt(q) babies; on failure solve the complement
with the SAME stipulated optimized listing backend. Its leading lower bound is

    W/sqrt(q) >= beta + alpha(2−alpha)/(4beta) + (1−alpha)^(3/2).

Optimizing beta gives f(alpha)=sqrt(alpha(2−alpha))+(1−alpha)^(3/2)>=1.
For u=1−alpha, squaring nonnegative sides reduces this to sqrt(u)(1+u)<=2
when u>0; endpoints hold directly. Equality occurs only at alpha=0 or1, with
alpha=0 omitting the unused first table. This independently agrees with the
restricted switching screen in H033-stopping-policy-screen.md.

The comparison needs the sqrt(q)/2-record optimal table to FIT the memory cap.
A lower bound sqrt(q) alone does not defeat a more expensive constrained-memory
baseline. Rounding, setup and operation prices remain separate. This inequality
does not prove the raw claims about arbitrary sliding windows, overlap, repeated
phases or all lifecycle policies. No unrestricted backend lower bound is assumed.

## Corrected unstructured-probe calculation

The raw 'probe minimum' is wrong. q/(2M) strictly decreases with M; at
M=sqrt(q/2) it equals sqrt(q/2). Instead the elementary optimization is

    min_{M>0} [M+q/(2M)] = sqrt(2q).

This expression assumes ONE unit of construction per stored record and the
stipulated probe price. q/(2M) is a geometric mean only for independent uniform
full-group probes against M distinct nonzero negation classes, with identity
handled separately. Actual DP endpoints, stopping-dependent retained sets,
correlated walks and useful-denominator conditions do not inherit it for free.
No claim is made that promotion of arbitrary retired endpoints obeys this model.

## Recovery, memory and detection qualifications

- Same-b positive equality is null; same-b negative equality has coefficient2b
  and can be useful when b is nonzero. All signs must be retained explicitly.
- A stored key can recover coefficients through a paid public schedule/index,
  not only a seed. Reconstruction need not be another discrete logarithm.
- Changing a step function can destroy continuation coalescence but leaves
  exact useful collisions with stored labelled endpoints meaningful.
- Deletion only removes reports under a FIXED trajectory/query/cost contract.
  It does not settle policies that spend freed resources differently.
- Brent/Floyd must pay their actual function evaluations and recovery work;
  they do not receive full-storage birthday constants for free.
- Parallel/rebuilt walks need their own useful-collision and reporting law.
  Serial rho heuristics are not universal total-work lower bounds.

The report's numeric crossover is at most a leading field-work comparison of
its specified listing models. It is not matched DRAM, calibrated hardware or
all-budget dominance. Current best supported reference remains generic square-
root search with known algorithm-dependent constants. Improvement NONE.
