## Parallel chords

For a chord through labelled U,V, its third intersection is T=-(U+V). The
induced coefficient label for T is the negative sum of U,V labels, so adding
the three labels gives identically(0,0), not a new nonzero equation for k.
A third intersection needs an INDEPENDENT label or some other explicit extraction
mechanism to become useful. Equal slopes and unequal intercepts do not provide
that missing label. The raw unspecified degree4 residual has no derivation
showing bounded-degree scalar candidates. Its two known cubic roots already
determine the third by the ordinary chord law.

The projective explanation in the raw report is wrong: a nonvertical line
Y=lambda X+mu Z does NOT contain O=[0:1:0]. Its affine line function has pole
order3 at O, so its three intersections have divisor class3O. That explains
the group-law relation without asserting the line passes through O. No slope-
birthday experiment can substitute for the missing recovery equation. Do not
accept the proposed slope-count falsifier or constant improvement prediction.

## Adjacent-coordinate state

The state(x(P),x(P+G)) is generically injective on this odd prime subgroup, not
an automatic negation quotient. The only possible other point with x(P) is-P;
x(P+G)=x(-P+G) would imply2G=O or2P=O. Except identity/exceptions these are
excluded. State length and signs therefore need correct handling, not a free
factor2 or 'times two' convention.

Writing d_n=g-x(P_n), alpha=4h²,J=6g²+2A, the adjacent-coordinate map has the
rational inverse d_(n-1)=J/d_n-alpha/d_n²-d_(n+1) where d_n!=0. It is a
translation recurrence, not inversion of doubling by a degree4 root search.
Exceptions need full-point handling. Even unique subgroup halving would not
make extraction free merely by proving uniqueness. The raw proposed branch-count
test investigates the wrong map and is not authorized. A re-encoding of BSGS
alone supplies no shorter search or demonstrated lower complete arithmetic cost.
Independent P2 net-cost review treats the actual recurrence separately.

## Broader rejected assertions are not accepted as theorems

- General rational f can have several poles with overlapping translates; the
  claimed distinct poles at-iG needs a single-pole or another explicit argument.
- A relation on rational group points does not uniquely determine a global map's
  degree. For multiplication by k^(-1), the literal degree is that multiplier's
  square, not k²; alternate endomorphism representatives also matter.
- Ordinary prime-field/prime-order conditions alone do not prove that every
  embedding degree is infeasible or exclude all pairing-friendly special cases.
- Large sketch fibers do not universally imply that recovery is another DLP;
  an explicit recovery construction is missing, not proved impossible for all maps.
- Saying a characteristic-p formal logarithm is 'inseparable' conflates existence,
  denominators and formal-group homomorphism issues. The raw sentence is not a
  substitute for an actual obstruction with hypotheses.
- Listed MITM and factor-base cost balances constrain the named constructions;
  they do not close all batching/adaptive/coordinate algorithms. General word
  'closed' does not overwrite the qualified earlier findings.
