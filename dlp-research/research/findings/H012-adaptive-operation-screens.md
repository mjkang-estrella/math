## LD8-v1: endpoint concentration cannot pay for this descent

Freeze a full prime-order group E(Fp)=<G>, q>=19, Q=dG. Precompute and pay for
binary powers of G,Q and offsets {+-1,+-2,+-4,+-8}G. Each independent restart
samples uniform a,b modulo q using n-bit rejection, n=ceil(log2 q). Construct
aG+bQ using ONE accumulator of selected binary powers, in fixed order, skipping
only its first assignment. This explicitly resolves the reviewer's seed ambiguity.
Even exceptional group-addition calls count; no data-dependent algebraic shortcuts.

At every nonidentity current point evaluate ALL eight neighbors. Key O=(-1,0)
and affine points by canonical integer (x,y), lexicographically. Move to the
unique least key if it is smaller; update A by the offset and leave B unchanged.
Otherwise stop. O is absorbing. No path memoization or early path truncation.
Store exact endpoints with affine labels. Different-B collisions recover
d=-(A-A')/(B-B'); an O endpoint with B!=0 recovers -A/B. Verify [d]G=Q.
Equal-B collisions are paid failures. Every restart completes its descent.
Setup, point normalization, comparisons, RNG, labels, dictionary and verification
are charged in a full ledger; the lower bound below conservatively omits most.

The endpoint map F depends on G and point keys, not Q or label bookkeeping.
For each fixed d, (a,b)->(a+bd,b) is bijective. Thus starts are uniform and
independent of B=b. Write basin sizes s_H, pi_H=s_H/q, kappa=sum pi_H^2.

After l moves a start reaching H differs from H by [j]G with |j|<=8l.
There are at most 16l+1 such starts, also when the interval wraps modulo q.
Sort a basin's path lengths starting at index0: l_j>=j/16. Therefore

    E[l] >= (q*kappa-1)/32,
    E[descent additions] >= (q*kappa-1)/4.

Success in t trials requires an O endpoint or a pair of equal endpoints, so
P(T<=t)<=t*pi_O+binom(t,2)*kappa<=t*sqrt(kappa)+t^2*kappa/2.
Taking t=floor(1/(2 sqrt(kappa))) yields E[T]>=3/(16 sqrt(kappa)).
Overcounted unhelpful O/equal-B events only weaken this valid bound.

The lower half 0<=a<2^(n-1) has probability >1/2; conditional popcount mean is
(n-1)/2. Consequently E[popcount(a)+popcount(b)-1]>(n-3)/2.
The actual seed addition count is at least this quantity, even for a=b=0.
It is NOT at least1 on every trial. Its EXPECTATION is >=1 for q>=19.

Let C_i count only intrinsic seed+descent additions on restart i. These are iid,
although correlated with endpoint/success. {T>=i} depends only on earlier trials,
so Tonelli and independence give E[sum_(i<=T) C_i]=E[C_1] E[T], also in the
extended sense. Full table/verification costs need NOT be iid: total work X is
at least this intrinsic sum. This qualification corrects the raw review's broad
Wald wording. With L=(2n-7)/4>0 and s=sqrt(kappa),

    E[X] >= (3/16)*(L/s + q*s/4)
         >= (3/32)*sqrt(q*(2n-7)) = Omega(sqrt(q log q)).

Thus this exact operation has no asymptotic work gain over the square-root
reference. This is an EXPECTED group-addition lower bound for every fixed Q,
not a bit-runtime calibration, high-probability claim, or universal coordinate
lower bound. Cheap correlated starts, memoization, growing offsets, capped paths
or adaptive restart rules require different analysis. No numerical pilot needed.

## Filtered S6 lattice: the literal t=2 module cannot certify a root

Factor-base coordinate interval 0<=x<B, B=ceil(q^(1/5)). Pay for enumeration,
point lifting and signs. Draw labelled targets R=aG+bQ, b!=0, accepting only
finite r=x(R)<C. Acceptance density delta is measured/derived, not granted;
expected seed work per accepted target is c_seed/delta. Narrowing this public
coordinate does not narrow the secret. Rank yield eta and verified relation
yield sigma are also unproved. The proposed collector needs setup plus
B/(sigma*eta)*(c_seed/delta+K_lattice+K_extract+K_verify)+linear-algebra work.

Let F be the centered integer lift of S6(X1,...,X5,r) modulo p. Freeze exactly
the 28-row Z-module generated by p^2 X^alpha (|alpha|<=2), p F X^alpha
(|alpha|<=1), F^2. Column scaling is B^total-degree. A polynomial is accepted
only if its scaled coefficient L1 norm is STRICTLY below p^2, sufficient to
convert its congruence at a box root to equality over Z. All LLL and subsequent
root extraction costs would be paid, and independence of equations is not free.

The existing S6 leading-face identity and individual degrees<=16 give the unique
total-degree80 monomial (X1 X2 X3 X4 X5)^16 with coefficient1, for every r.
Any module element is g=cF^2+pFh1+p^2h2, with integer c, deg h1<=1, deg h2<=2.
If c!=0, its unique degree160 top coefficient cannot cancel and scaled norm is
>=B^160. Otherwise, if h1!=0, a nonzero top coefficient of pFh1 cannot cancel
against h2, giving norm>=p B^80. Otherwise nonzero g has norm>=p^2.
Whenever B^80>=p, NO nonzero g passes the strict threshold. This holds eventually
on q~p families; no target filter changes the leading coefficient.

This rejects only the frozen low-shift module and certification rule, not all
small-root algorithms, changes of variables, larger modules, or coordinate
filtering. Root checked the degree argument; no LLL software or scan needed.

## Adaptive interval insertion: birth rows pay new-column debt

For labelled R, scan old base K to select four distinct finite anchors with
smallest |x(P_i)-x(R)|, tie-breaking by coordinates. Form U=R-sum P_i, reject O,
and enumerate/lift the shifted interval x(U)+{0,...,B-1} modulo p. It contains U,
so the decomposition is guaranteed, at paid seed/scan/addition/enumeration cost.

But its birth equation is log U=a+bd-sum log P_i. If U is kept as that defining
expression, the row reduces to zero. If it is a fresh unknown, it supplies that
column's pivot. Chronologically distinct fresh residuals give a triangular new-
column block; their birth rows alone do not constrain d. Promoting an entire
interval creates yet more columns. Prior valid columns cannot simply be dropped.
Useful information needs independently represented/repeated residuals or extra
relations, with useful label differences. Adaptive anchors destroy the simple
uniform-residual argument; root does NOT assign L/q probability here.

This rejects guaranteed birth yield as an informative collector, not every
adaptive reuse selector. A paid selector with sufficiently frequent independent
reuse remains the missing operation. No engine or trial justified by these rows.
