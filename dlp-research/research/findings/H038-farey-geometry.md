# H038 height-factor-base geometry and assembly gates

Status: focused PAPER bridge independently checked by native Astra. A separate
Opus task is exploring constructive height/lattice operators; no complete-work
gain or universal small-root exclusion is claimed. No experiment or source edit.

For nonsingular E:y²=f(x)=x³+A*x+B in characteristic p>3 and Q=(u,v), v!=0,
the relation S3(x,z,u)=0 parametrizes (x(P),x(Q-P)). On the usual slope chart,

    z+u+x=(v+y)²/(u-x)²,
    y=[(z+u+x)*(u-x)²-f(u)-f(x)]/(2v).

The inverse has the positive sign shown. At finite x=u the relation has one
finite solution z=x(2Q), since its coefficient of z is-4v²; the inverse remains
defined and returns y=-v. P=O gives(infinity,u), P=Q gives(u,infinity), and
P=-Q gives(u,x(2Q)). The forward formula uses the appropriate exceptional chart.

Set x=N1/D1,z=N2/D2 and Segre coordinates
(U,V,W,Z)=(N1*N2,N1*D2,N2*D1,D1*D2). Then

    UZ=VW,
    (V-W)²*u²-2*((V+W)*(U+A*Z)+2B*Z²)*u
      +(U-A*Z)²-4B*(V+W)*Z=0.

The Segre quadric is P1xP1. The relation curve is birational to E, so its
normalization has genus one for v!=0. It is not a rational conic in disguise.
This excludes a genuine nonconstant genus-zero rational parametrization, not
finite-field interpolation, finite enumeration, correspondences or height and
lattice algorithms. It is no complexity lower bound.

For finite abscissae require D_i nonzero modulo p, and each numerator/denominator
pair nonzero. A modular rank-one Segre solution does NOT automatically have
bounded primitive integer factors; reconstruction and verification are paid.
If |N|<=H,1<=D<=H,gcd(N,D)=1 and2H²<p, the rational encodings are injective
modulo p: congruence implies an integer cross-product difference of magnitude
less than p is zero. The choice D>0 removes sign duplicates.

Counting allowed x-values differs from counting points: each x supplies
1+chi(f(x)) finite curve points. A claim that half the x-values qualify needs
its distribution/error hypothesis; counting both ordinates adds both points.
The field-height set is not automatically a known-log factor base or an
integer-rational lift of the curve. Subgroup membership/cofactor handling must
also be stated when the full curve order is not q.

## Completed standard two-point assembly screen

For a fixed factor base F of B group points in the order-q group, a uniform
R has exactly B²/q expected ordered decompositions P1+P2=R. Thus the probability
of any decomposition is at most B²/q. Root's proposed literal-policy gate is:
if full-rank assembly requires B relations, per-attempt cost is at least T,
and base construction costs at least B units, then charged work is at least
B+q*T/B, hence at least2sqrt(q*T). This would rule out an exponent improvement
from m=2 under those premises even with unit-cost extraction.

Native Sol independently accepted the gate with the following precise stopping
and multi-row qualifications. This does not cover every partial-base/cycle/adaptive policy,
free known-log advice, or many-target amortization. A different assembly scheme
must specify its recovery and complete costs rather than borrow this bound.

Primary check already retained in H036: Semaev2015 section2 supplies the S3
formula and degree rule (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.01175). Root's present
bounded web search found no directly read height-base improvement; that is an
access/search outcome, not novelty or impossibility evidence. Native Astra
checked the exact inverse, Segre equations, boundary points and genus scope.

Let X(R) count ordered decompositions in the fixed F. Conditional on the past,
each started target must be fresh uniform. If Y_i is the NEW rank generated by
returned decomposition rows, then Y_i<=X(R_i) pathwise; linear combinations of
these rows cannot add rank. Predictable inclusion and Tonelli give

    E[sum_{i<=N}Y_i] <= (B²/q)*E[N].

Almost-sure full B-rank termination implies E[N]>=q/B. A per-started-attempt
pathwise or conditional expected cost floor T>=1 and explicit materialization
cost>=B imply E[W]>=B+q*T/B>=2sqrt(q*T). No IID success indicators, exact success
probability or Wald shortcut are needed. With full-rank success probability pi,
the rank requirement is pi*B and the lower bound uses pi*q*T/B instead. A tiny
censored success probability cannot inherit the almost-sure conclusion.

For attempts returning at most one row, the sharper bound is
E[N]>=B/min(1,B²/q). Its E[N]>=B part must NOT be transferred to arbitrary
multi-row returns. Commutativity and negation conventions can alter constants;
the conservative B²/q ordered-decomposition bound counts the actual group set.

Disposition: reject only standard full-rank m=2 assembly as an exponent-gain
route, even with unit-cost extraction. This does not reject height-specific
constant improvements. For m>=3 the corresponding counting expression is
B+q*T/B^(m-1); extraction still must earn a gain. Small-component/cycle recovery,
on-demand target-dependent bases, implicit representations without B materialization,
or separately amortized known-base logs require their own complete accounts.
None is automatically an improvement. No experiment is admitted by this lemma.
