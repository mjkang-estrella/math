## Algebraic Opus report: retained, not adopted as lower bounds

Raw report: H002-algebraic-opus-raw.md. Its four mechanisms are useful speculation,
but several claimed rejections exceed the supplied argument:

1. Norm coefficient-box losses rely on distribution/counting and an unspecified
   lattice enumerator. They are not universal lower bounds on capturing splitting.
2. Any rational map admits an image correspondence by elimination; exact necessary
   pruning does NOT require an isogeny respecting the target addition law. The
   actual issue is image degree/size and pruning yield, not existence of an image.
3. For a parity predictor wrong at B points, multiplying adjacent predictions
   can introduce up to2B bad pairs, hence a sum error up to4B. The report's leap
   from near-perfect agreement to any advantage above one-half is unsupported.
   Its unverified Weil threshold is not adopted. Different mixed-character bounds
   may apply, but require their actual hypotheses.
4. A root-count statement for one-variable functions cannot simply be transferred
   to zero loci on E². Expanded product-tree BSGS still enumerates conventional
   points and has no shown exponent gain; that fact does not prove arbitrary
   multilevel constructions impossible.

## Filter review: conditional acceptance of cost algebra, not of its pilot

H002-filter-cost-review-raw.md agrees with the constant-C renewal gate and offers
the useful variable-cost form `(a+g+E[C*1_A])/P(A∩U)`. The baseline must reuse
training walks and receive the model's storage budget. Prefix costs and timeouts
must be explicit; generally success is P(A∩U∩finish), not t*P(finish|U) without
the required conditioning. The report's opening no-cost/outcome-dependence phrase
is inconsistent with its later correct renewal discussion; inside-attempt
correlation is allowed, independent fresh-attempt renewal is the key condition.

Its conditional small-coverage endpoint-filter regime may exceed a constant gain
if a very cheap accurate coordinate filter exists. Retain that as a hypothesis,
not the raw claim that endpoint filters can only improve constants. No such filter
is supplied. L4 needs an actual factor base/solver and changing relation-rank state.

The proposed pilot is NOT approved as written: event counts treat a whole model
inference as one event and leave training arithmetic outside that counter; setup
and deviation claims are estimates, not guarantees; merged walks can invalidate
iid confidence intervals; shuffled controls can win by chance without proving an
implementation invalid. Design must separate algorithmic counts from measured
time, account for training completely and use correct dependence/statistical tests.

## Lifting review: retain the core interpretation, qualify the obstruction

torsion section; complete defects telescope independently of the addition chain.
Small-support approximation is the substantive unproved hypothesis. Smart and

Do NOT adopt all reviewer assertions: exceptional j values do not justify the
blanket claim that all lifts are isomorphic; using j as a complete deformation
coordinate needs care at extra automorphisms. Equivariance-based counting needs

a shortest-vector lower bound; a finite curve-specific check is not an asymptotic
exponent proof. Success/nonzero-log probabilities were not established for the
specified finite section family. These qualifications preserve the speculative
generic-j question without funding an unspecified solver.
