## Algebra that can guide the next test

For equal centered prefix length L and strides s_i=sigma_i(M+i), a channel
numerator has differences generated by M+i and M+l. Fixed shifts and the
nonzero inverse of b_i-epsilon*b_l do not change within-channel equality.
For i<l, let g=gcd(M+i,M+l), which divides l-i. The primitive integer collision
vector has absolute components (M+l)/g and (M+i)/g. Thus an integer duplicate
exists in an L-by-L index square exactly when L-1>=(M+l)/g. This concerns one
channel, not the complete union or runtime. For pair0,3 with3 dividing M,
threshold t=4L has leading coefficient2sqrt(2)/3 times sqrt(q). For one of
pairs0,2 or1,3, g=2, with leading threshold sqrt(2)*sqrt(q). For g=1 the
threshold is later. Modular collisions may add others. A sufficient condition
excluding two distinct integer numerator values differing by q is
(L-1)*(2M+i+l)<q; this does not assert the shifted interval never crosses zero.

At L=lambda*sqrt(q), M~sqrt(q/2), the coordinate-error upper bound from the
accepted chart argument contains LM/q~lambda/sqrt(2). It does not tend tozero.
Consequently distinct limiting origins by themselves do not prove finite
critical-prefix channels disjoint. This is a gap in that particular separation
argument, not a proof that favorable correlation is impossible. Multiple M
residue classes matter if larger fixed tests are admitted.
