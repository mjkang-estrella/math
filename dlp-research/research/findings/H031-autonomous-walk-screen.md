## Card1: fixed state-hash portals

The operation uses the H024 reflection quotient R(P)=G−P, with invariant slope
μ and an actively maintained canonical root. Cheap steps cost the existing
leading4M+S; portal jumps cost10M+2S, including two dependent inversion batches.
The cheap-step recurrence is known, not a new formula. Portal membership and
jump choice depend on μ alone; this preserves a state-defined successor but
does not prove random-map behavior.

Even granting portal fraction ρ along the work, its leading arithmetic is
4+s+ρ(6+s), s=S/M. Beating5+s requiresρ<1/(6+s), NOT the raw s/(6+s).
At the proposed hypothetical s=.8,ρ=.8/6.8, subtotal5.6M versus5.8M leaves
only a29/28 permitted step-count factor before other work. These are symbolic
costs, not calibration. Furthermore root notes that a fixed hash's density over
states is not necessarily its frequency along stopped trajectories. With W
total steps and N_p portal steps, the relevant mean subtotal is

    (4+s) E[W] + (6+s) E[N_p],

under the stated limiting arithmetic model. Replacing E[N_p]/E[W] byρ needs
justification. Hashing, labels, setup, exceptional work, dependent batch overhead,
replay and in-flight work must also be charged.

Exact structural objection: the regular H023/H024 cheap reflection step has
null two-cycles. At reversal its canonical label update(a,b)→(−a,−b) is undone
by the return step. If neither state in such a cycle is portal-marked, the cycle
is permanently closed. Repeated evaluation of the same hash gives no fresh
escape chance. Without a DP on that cycle, reporting may never occur; with a DP,
replaying a null collision and then continuing stays in the cycle. This proves
closure CONDITIONED on an unmarked regular cycle; no trap-basin mass or existence
for a particular unspecified hash/fixture has been measured here.

The raw operation says discard a null collision and keep walking, but its cost
list mentions null-coefficient restarts. These are different algorithms. A
specified retirement/restart distribution, exceptional-state dispatch, fixed
hash construction and global stopping contract are missing. No experiment can
silently add them while claiming to test the original operation. The proposed
single-curve/seed falsifier and approximate rho constant do not supply a matched
complete solver comparison or a scaling result.

Disposition: no run. The closed-cycle implication is a cheap paper falsifier
of a fresh/geometric portal-escape assumption; it is not a prohibition on every
portal construction. A new exact paid escape contract and favorable full-cost
premise would be required before any replacement is considered.

## Card2: choose minimum of two translates

Each transition computes two full canonical additions and discards one image.
The leading10M+2S subtotal requires useful-collision steps below half of the
single-addition baseline, before all extra costs. A birthday-factor model would
need collision enhancement greater than4, not merely2. Under the previously
considered iid min-of-two pushforward enhancement4/3, the arithmetic ratio is
2sqrt(3/4)=sqrt(3)>1. That heuristic is NOT this EC map's stationary/stopping law;
no universal two-choice-walk lower bound is accepted.

Literal labels also need repair. If the selected canonical image is ε(W+J),
its labels are ε(a+a_J,b+b_J). Simply adding the chosen jump coefficients omits
the canonicalization sign. Explicit null-hit handling, exceptional dispatch,
finite retirement/restart and matched success accounting remain necessary.

Disposition: unsupported gain premise and incomplete protocol, no run. A proven
or credible candidate-specific useful-collision law sufficient to pay for both
branches would change the decision; mere image-size sampling would not.
