# H006 net schedules: qualified arithmetic review, no complete-cost gain

Native Astra /root/encoding_review independently checked Sol's proposal and
root's H005 hybrid disposition. Read-only paper work; no experiments, numerical
trials or Lean claims. Proposal: ../hypotheses/H006-net-schedules-proposal.md.

Accepted on regular segments: constants alpha,beta,J, geometric gauge, Somos and
quotient identities, raw continued/terminal ledgers, quotient continued/terminal
ledgers, paired-center regular ledger, and y-recovery sign. Raw terminal four-
output blocks require three births and inversions of the first two newborns;
omitting that whole final batch was an earlier withdrawn error.

For R paired centers and h offsets each, the unforeseen terminal-hit cost after
the combined inversion is (5Rh+3R-3)M+2RhS+I+6RhA_f. If the last block is known
in advance and center denominators were omitted, it is instead
(5Rh-3)M+2RhS+I+6RhA_f. These are the stated schedules, not universal minima.

Startup correction incorporated in the proposal: raw seed quartet emits
x1,x2,x3,x4, but x1 already came from the paid seed P+G. Only three new outputs
per stream. Starting at n=2 requires paid warmup/cache preparation. Known unit
terms can also save startup arithmetic; the regular ledger is not an optimized
finite startup ledger. Quotient first produces x2 without duplication.

The quotient's regular arithmetic excess against paired output is
5RhM-R(2h+1)S-3RA_f. For h=1 this is positive under S<=M and A_f<2M/3.
This is conditional regular-segment evidence, not a theorem for all stopping
times. Terminal savings, seed/stream counts, order, joins, label arithmetic,
exceptional recovery and reseeding remain charged in a whole-solver claim.
Working-word counts are plausible layouts, neither measurements nor lower bounds.

Independent reviewer found no material overstatement in H005-hybrid-disposition.md:
the chord tautology, nonvertical-line/O correction and rational adjacent-state
inverse are sound. Universal closures in the raw Grok report remain unaccepted.
