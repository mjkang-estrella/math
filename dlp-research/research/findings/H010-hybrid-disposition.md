# H010 Grok hybrid cards: qualified disposition

2026-10-08. Grok4.7 completed the retained H010 hybrid task; full report is in
H010-hybrid-grok-raw.md. Native Astra /root/h007_additive_relations independently
reviewed all three cards; root checked the counting arguments. No experiment,
new Lean claim or accepted speedup. All statements below are paper analysis.

1. Equal-tangent associates: for nonzero slope mu, the quartic
   (3x^2+A)^2-4mu^2(x^3+Ax+C)=0 and y=(3x^2+A)/(2mu) are valid, with rational
   root, multiplicity and exceptional-case handling. The function
   3x^2+A-2mu*y has pole degree4, giving a four-point sum relation. This does
   NOT supply known affine scalar labels for individual associates. Recovery
   requires an explicit branch phi(P)=[alpha]P+[beta]G with computable known
   alpha,beta and a stated domain. None was supplied. Degree4 is not a runtime
   bound on root extraction, and proposed useful-collision statistics are not
   established. Next meaningful step is a symbolic branch law and paid root
   schedule, not the proposed all-point BSGS sweep.

2. Prime-to-p torsion-lift selector: its correction digit is undefined until
   curve lift, initial section, local parameter, normalization, representatives
   and infinity conventions are frozen. H003 already records section dependence.
   For ANY generator-independent point bit f, agreement A_G with canonical
   scalar parity over 1<=n<q obeys A_G+A_(-G)=1, since q odd reverses parity.
   Hence positive advantage uniformly over ALL generators is impossible. This
   says nothing against a fixed orientation or generator-dependent predicate.
   An average bias also does not prove the needed trajectory/collision rate.
   With P_i=a_iG+b_iQ, correct recovery is
   d=(a_j-a_i)/(b_i-b_j); the raw card reverses coefficient roles. No engine
   justified until the selector and quantified bias/dynamics claim are supplied.

3. Fixed-m coarse bins plus translations |t|<=s: if bin sizes are n_j, iid
   uniform point pairs have >=q^2/m same-bin ordered pairs. At most q(2s+1)
   ordered pairs differ by a tested translation. Thus conditional useful
   fraction <=min(1,m(2s+1)/q). Fixed m and s=o(q) cannot give near-universal
   useful bin hits in this sampling model. Long progressions do not control
   cross-pairs, and arbitrary-step progressions need not have the contiguous
   differences tested. Charge all O(s) tests and reconstruction of stored points
   if only scalar labels/bin IDs were retained. Deliberately correlated walks
   are outside this counting screen and need their own explicit joint law.
   Nonconstant second difference does not imply maximal interpolation degree;
   that raw argument is unsupported. No progression sweep is needed here.

One finite-curve failure does not establish o(1), O(log^2 q), or asymptotic density
claims without a separately quantified finite prediction. Proposed q~2^16 sweeps
were not authorized or run. Known permutations/endomorphisms are not ruled out
by the raw blanket wording. None of these supplied cards currently has a complete
paid improvement mechanism. Revisions remain possible within the explicit limits.
