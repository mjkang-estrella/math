## Corrections to raw review

- Exceptions are not assumed rare on an actual deterministic trajectory. Global
  fibre bounds and generic heuristics do not give visited frequencies.
- 'h is non-rational' is not established and is misleading for a function on
  a finite subgroup. Its nonidentity values can be interpolated as y*g(x):
  h(P)/y(P) is constant on each negation pair. This does not give a cheap
  evaluator. Conversely the form y*g(x) alone is not an arithmetic lower bound.
- Uniqueness is valid in its proper domain: two normalized functions satisfying
  the same slope cocycle on the whole finite group, including compatible
  exceptional/odd/identity laws, differ by a homomorphism to additive Fp.
  Since q!=p that homomorphism is zero. This classifies that cocycle, not every
  imaginable cheap orientation function.
- Historical Cell canonicalization/multiplication instruction ratios are not
  current hardware measurements. Transferring another representative's empirical
  16-adding factor or a heuristic look-ahead cost into a mandatory kill inequality
  is not justified. Different layouts/reduction/cycle rules need their own counts.
- Asymptotically small setup relative to sqrt(q) does not make it free; the
  exact precomputation and opportunities for useful setup collisions remain paid.
