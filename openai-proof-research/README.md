# Consequences of the OpenAI mathematics corpus

Research started 2026-10-08. The user directs us to assume all upstream proofs are true. We seek short, substantive consequences newly accessible from those results, with exact statements and independently checked deductions. “Previously impossible” is interpreted as previously blocked by an unavailable theorem, not a claim of logical impossibility.

Upstream: https://github.com/openai/math; the exact revision is in [sources/upstream.json](sources/upstream.json). Source catalogue and overview are preserved under the upstream Apache-2.0 license in sources/LICENSE.

Work in progress: broad independent candidate generation, then adversarial proof and novelty review. Each accepted finding will receive a separate commit. No claim of literature priority is made without evidence.
