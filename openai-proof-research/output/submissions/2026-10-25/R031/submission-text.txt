A superquadratic sensitivity separation for transitive Boolean functions

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

For every C>0 we construct a nonconstant total Boolean function f, invariant under a group transitive on all input coordinates, with f(0)=0 and bs(f,0)>C s(f)^2, where s is ordinary sensitivity maximized over all inputs. We also obtain a fixed exponent alpha>2 and a sequence of such functions with bs(F_m,0)>=s(F_m)^alpha and diverging zero-input block sensitivity. The pinned OpenAI manuscript supplies the nested-predicate and sensitivity-recurrence framework. First-difference labels on a fixed-depth Paley tournament product simultaneously give a small label alphabet, a constant head-pattern exclusion bound and affine label covariance; these preserve transitivity on the full raw coordinate set while meeting the recurrence estimates. The manuscript includes all four profile bounds, zero-input blocks, balancing and the composition endpoint. Source architecture, earlier spectral/cyclotomic symmetry constructions and classical composition are credited. The accompanying Lean code verifies selected exclusion, affine-action and scalar-recurrence interfaces, not the full Boolean theorem. No optimal exponent or worldwide priority is claimed.
