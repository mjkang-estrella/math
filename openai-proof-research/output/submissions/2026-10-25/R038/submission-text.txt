Spectral chart selection and average trace-class distortion

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

We construct genuine finite negative-type metrics with n points whose first uniform-average distortion into the trace class S_1(H) is at least c sqrt(log n)/(log log n)^(9/2), uniformly over all complex Hilbert spaces H. The construction uses the multichart Hilbert kernel of the stipulated pinned uniform-sparsest-cut source. A dimension-independent matrix selection replaces scalar coordinate selection: scalar moment matching supplies spectral cutoffs, and a positive Gaussian dual pairing averages them without requiring commutation.
