Fourth-moment comparison of zero-field SK free-energy fluctuations

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

We compare zero-field Sherrington–Kirkpatrick log partition functions with independent, possibly nonidentically distributed, centered variance-one couplings under a uniform fourth-moment bound. The mean error is bounded independently of system size, the variance error is bounded by a constant times one plus the Gaussian standard deviation, and bounded smooth tests at scale a ≥ 1 have error O(1/a). Third moments need not vanish. Reflected-star replacement controls the cubic term, and a finite-hybrid argument supplies the variance bound without fifth moments. Assuming the explicitly identified Gaussian low-temperature theorem, the comparison transfers its variance prefactor and limiting law with each model’s own mean and variance. The Gaussian theorem is an input, not proved here.
