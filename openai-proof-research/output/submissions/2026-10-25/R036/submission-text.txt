One integral mapping-class orbit in every rank

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

For every closed oriented surface of genus g ≥ 2 and every rank r ≥ 2, we construct a representation into SL_r(O_L), for a number field L embedded in the complex numbers, whose character has Zariski-dense orbit in the entire complex SL_r character variety under the orientation-preserving mapping class group. The rank-two assertion is credited to Coccia–Litt and antecedents. In higher rank, two families of simple curves give a full cotangent certificate. Arithmetic parameters satisfy finitely many open conditions; countably many nonresonance conditions are imposed on a complex torus already contained in one arithmetic orbit closure. Classical twist Hamiltonians and stipulated pinned source inputs complete the deduction.
