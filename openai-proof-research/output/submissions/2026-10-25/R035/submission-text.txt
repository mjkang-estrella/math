A source-relative factor-two approximation for reverse-complement superstrings

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

Assuming the count, periodic-layer and connection lemmas of the pinned ordinary shortest-common-superstring construction of OpenAI, we give a deterministic polynomial-bit-time factor-two algorithm for superstrings with reverse complements. The explicitly encoded alphabet and involution may vary with the input; fixed letters, palindromes, empty requirements, duplicates and containment are included. The extension combines a phase-free 2k+1 count rule for self-reversed periodic texts, adjacent sorted profiles sharing both reflection axes, and an odd core of 2k−1 layers in a 2k-layer budget unit. Established connection and symmetric traversal operations complete the algorithm. This uses stronger structural source lemmas, not merely black-box existence of an ordinary factor-two algorithm.
