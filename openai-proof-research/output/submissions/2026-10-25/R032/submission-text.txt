Quantum verification of full-continuum Coulomb energy with unit nuclei

Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation)

We prove uniform QMA membership for the spectral-infimum decision problem for the full spinful antisymmetric Coulomb Hamiltonian with unit nuclei, distinct rational nuclear positions, unary electron number and inverse-polynomial promise gap. Nuclear repulsion is omitted. Positions and thresholds use binary rational encoding; the result assumes neither binding nor a spectral gap, geometric separation, bounded physical diameter, a selected finite basis, a fixed spin sector or an efficiently prepared low-energy state. The proof combines quantitative localization including escaped electrons, Hardy-controlled Coulomb caps, a finite kinetic cutoff, exact shifted sampling with L2 error control, and a polynomial-size verifier on arbitrary witness states. Together with the hardness theorem of the pinned source manuscript, this gives QMA completeness for the same problem. Classical sector localization, finite-basis electronic complexity, smooth-box discretization and earlier real-space simulation estimates are credited. The accompanying Lean support proves selected finite sampling, representation-average and scalar error interfaces; it does not formalize the full continuum or quantum-complexity theorem. No extension to exponentially large binary nuclear charges or worldwide priority claim is made.
