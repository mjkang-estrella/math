# MathDB submission and review invitation

Dimension-uniform coefficient extraction from nonlinear curl

Myeongjin Kang, Independent researcher

Let L(x) = sum_r x_r X_r, where the X_r are real symmetric m by m matrices and sum_r X_r^2 is bounded above by K times the identity for a fixed finite K. For the Gaussian inverse Mills ratio q, we prove that the mean squared row curl of q(L) controls both the lack of full symmetry of the coefficient tensor and the sum of all squared coefficient commutators. After division by m, the estimate has a modulus independent of the dimension, bounded by C_K/log(e/t) when the normalized curl energy t is small. The proof compares divided differences at x and -x and controls a cutoff on all tensor indices by Gaussian operator moments. We also construct a fully symmetric tensor with a controlled operator-energy bound and controlled algebraic residual.

Preprint and source: https://zenodo.org/records/23305996.

Independent feedback is welcome on the proof and its relation to existing work. In particular, I would appreciate checks of the dimension-independent spectral cutoff in Section 3, the passage from curl energy to the algebraic residual in Section 4, and the energy-controlled symmetric repair in Section 5. The operator-energy bound is a hypothesis of the theorem. This preprint has not undergone independent human peer review.
