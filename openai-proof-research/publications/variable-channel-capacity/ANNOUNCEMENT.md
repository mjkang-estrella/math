# MathDB announcement draft

**Weighted capacity for four-state reconstruction with variable edge channels**

Myeongjin Kang — Independent researcher

Assuming a specified moment-saving inequality for a preserved class of four-state posterior laws, I prove a weighted-capacity criterion for reconstruction on deterministic locally finite trees with variable symmetric ferromagnetic edge channels. The parameters may approach zero or one, and perfect transmission is allowed. Quantitative bounds have constants independent of the tree, its degrees, and the edge parameters.

The proof uses posterior estimates that remain valid without channel smoothing and controls accumulated information loss through branch splitting. The paper also treats zero channels by pruning and includes the exact ray calculation and a counterexample without local finiteness.

The upstream assumption is stated explicitly in the paper and refers to fixed versions of two manuscripts in the OpenAI mathematics repository. The result is a conditional deduction; this announcement does not claim an unconditional solution or established worldwide priority.
