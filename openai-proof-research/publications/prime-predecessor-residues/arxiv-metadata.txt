Abstract:
Let p range over the primes in a fixed reduced arithmetic progression. We give a proof, conditional on specified analytic results in the OpenAI mathematics repository, that Omega(p-1) modulo any fixed positive integer is asymptotically uniform and independent of the ranked logarithmic sizes of the prime factors of p-1. The limiting factor-size distribution is Poisson-Dirichlet with parameter 1. For parity, the joint limit also includes the squarefree indicator, with its explicit progression-dependent density. This implies the Moree-Hommersom parity conjecture under the same assumptions. The proof treats the Liouville sign by an uncentered marked correlation. For nonreal roots of unity, it constructs a common principal term from the full small-prime part of each integer. This term respects shared prime labels and can be summed through the allocation identities before its absolute value is estimated. The assumptions refer to an immutable source revision and are listed by statement in the paper.
