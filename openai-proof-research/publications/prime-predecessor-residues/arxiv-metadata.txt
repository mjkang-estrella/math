Title: Factor-count residues and Poisson-Dirichlet limits for prime predecessors

Abstract:
Assuming specified analytic estimates for shifted primes and Dirichlet L-functions, we prove that Omega(p-1) modulo any fixed positive integer is asymptotically uniform and independent of the ranked logarithmic prime factor sizes, for primes in each fixed reduced arithmetic progression. The factor-size limit is the Poisson-Dirichlet distribution with parameter one. For parity, we also obtain joint independence from the squarefree indicator, with its progression-dependent density. This gives the Moree-Hommersom parity conjecture under the same assumptions. The proof combines marked correlations with a principal-term model that is compatible with small-prime multiplication and the extraction of prime arguments.
