# MathDB submission and review invitation

A conditional power stability estimate for the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality

Myeongjin Kang, Independent researcher

Assuming the smooth variance and moment-coordinate identities specified in this paper, we prove a power stability estimate for the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality on origin-symmetric convex bodies. In each dimension at least three, a small deficit forces the two bodies close in Hausdorff distance to independent positive dilations of common complementary factors. The exponent depends only on dimension; the constant also depends on fixed inner and outer radii and on an interior range of interpolation parameters. No boundary regularity, common reflection group, facet bound, or lower bound on block angles is required. The proof retains nonzero derivative energy in a compact-target moment limit, converts a weak boundary identity to an absolute flux estimate, and constructs one product body whose two dilations approximate the original endpoints. The exponent is explicit but not sharp.

Preprint and source: https://zenodo.org/records/23306006.

Independent feedback is welcome on the conditional theorem and its relation to existing stability results. In particular, I would appreciate checks of the retained derivative energy in the compact-target limit (Sections 2–3), the boundary-flux estimate (Section 5), and the recovery of both endpoints from a common product body (Sections 6–7). The result depends on Assumption 2.1; it is not an unconditional proof of logarithmic Brunn–Minkowski. This preprint has not undergone independent human peer review.
