Let $K_{2n}$ be the complete graph on $2n$ vertices, and let $\mathcal{F}$ be a family of its perfect matchings. The family is **$t$-intersecting** if $|m\cap m'|>t$ for all $m,m'\in\mathcal{F}$. It is **trivially $t$-intersecting** when, for some collection $T$ of $t$ pairwise disjoint 2-sets of $[2n]$, 
$$
\mathcal{F}_T=\{m\in\mathcal{M}_{2n}:T\subseteq m\}.
$$
 **The full Erdős–Ko–Rado conjecture for t-intersecting perfect matchings.** If $\mathcal{F}$ is a $t$-intersecting family of perfect matchings of $K_{2n}$, then 
$$
|\mathcal{F}|\leq (2(n-t)-1)!!.
$$
 Moreover, equality holds if and only if $\mathcal{F}$ is trivially $t$-intersecting. This extends the known Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem for intersecting families of perfect matchings and remains an open generalization for $t$-intersecting families.
