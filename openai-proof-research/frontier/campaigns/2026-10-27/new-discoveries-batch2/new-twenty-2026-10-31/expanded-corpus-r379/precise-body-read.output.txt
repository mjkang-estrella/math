17 
18                                                         Given a graph G, let Gσ be an oriented graph of G with the orientation σ and
19                                                     skew-adjacency matrix S(Gσ ). Then the spectrum of S(Gσ ) is called the skew-
20                                                     spectrum of Gσ , denoted by SpS (Gσ ). It is known that a graph G is bipartite if
21                                                     and only if there is an orientation σ of G such that SpS (Gσ ) = iSp(G). In [D. Cui,
22                                                     Y. Hou, On the skew spectra of Cartesian products of graphs, Electron. J. Combin.
23                                                     20(2013), #P19], Cui and Hou conjectured that such orientation of a bipartite graph
24                                                     is unique under switching-equivalence. In this paper, we prove that the conjecture
25                                                     is true. Moreover, we give an orientation of the Cartesian product of a bipartite
26                                                     graph and a graph, and then determine the skew-spectrum of the resulting oriented
27                                                     product graph, which generalizes Cui and Hou’s result, and can be used to construct
28                                                     more oriented graphs with maximum skew energy.
29 
30                                                     Keywords: oriented graph, skew-spectrum, skew energy, bipartite graph, Carte-
31                                                     sian product.

49     Let Gσ be an oriented graph of G with the orientation σ, which assigns to each edge
50 of G a direction so that the induced graph Gσ becomes an oriented graph or a directed
51 graph. Then G is called the underlying graph of Gσ . The skew-adjacency matrix of Gσ

54 eigenvalues are purely imaginary numbers or 0, which form the spectrum of S(Gσ ) and
55 are said to be the skew-spectrum of Gσ . The eigenvalues of S(Gσ ) are called the skew
56 eigenvalues of Gσ .
57     The energy E(G) of an undirected graph is defined as the sum of the absolute values of
58 all the eigenvalues of G, which was introduced by Gutman in [5]. We refer the survey [6]
59 and the book [10] to the reader for details. The skew energy of an oriented graph, as one
60 of various generalizations of the graph energy, was first proposed by Adiga et al. [1]. It
61 is defined as the sum of the absolute values of all the eigenvalues of S(Gσ ), denoted by
62 ES (Gσ ). Most of the results on the skew energy are collected in our recent survey [9].
63    In [1], Adiga et al. obtained some properties of the skew energy and proposed some
64 open problems, such as the following two problems.
65

115    In this section, we consider bipartite graphs and their orientations. We prove that
116 Conjecture 1.3 is true and also obtain the characterizations of orientations of a bipartite
117 graph G with SpS (Gσ ) = iSp(G).
118     First, let’s recall some definitions. Let Gσ be an oriented graph and C2ℓ be an undi-
119 rected even cycle of G. Then C2ℓ is said to be evenly oriented relative to Gσ if it has an
120 even number of edges oriented in clockwise direction (and now it also has an even number
121 of edges oriented in anticlockwise direction, since C2ℓ is an even cycle); otherwise C2ℓ is
122 called oddly oriented. The even cycle C2ℓ is said to be oriented uniformly if C2ℓ is oddly
123 (resp., evenly) oriented relative to Gσ when ℓ is odd (resp., even). It should be noted that
124 if an even cycle C2ℓ is oriented uniformly in Gσ , then after switching operations on Gσ ,
125 C2ℓ is also oriented uniformly. The following lemma was obtained in [3].
126 
127 Lemma 2.1 [3] Let G be a bipartite graph and σ be an orientation of G. Then SpS (Gσ ) =
128 iSp(G) if and only if every even cycle is oriented uniformly in Gσ .
129
