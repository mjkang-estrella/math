For a proof beyond enumeration, let N(c) be the independent neighbours of a clique vertex. There is also a useful limitation. For ANY balanced split graph with side size m, r-connectivity and K₁,r+1-freeness imply all cross-degrees on both sides equal r: independent degrees are ≥ r, clique independent-neighbour counts are ≤ r, and edge totals force equality. Excluding K₁,r+1 + e then forces pairwise neighbourhood intersection ≥ 2. Counting over clique pairs gives m·C(r,2) ≥ 2·C(m,2), hence m ≤ r(r − 1)/2 + 1. Thus there is no balanced witness at r = 4 with order ≥ 15, and none at r = 5 with order > 22. These statements do not rule out unbalanced counterexamples, or other obstructions. The cyclic m = 16 failure at r = 6 does not exclude noncyclic designs.
