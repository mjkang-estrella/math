The search turned up the Kaul et al. paper on bounding the list color function threshold for K_{2,n}, but it doesn't match the query's framing in two ways.

- **Upper bound is linear, not square-root.** The Kaul et al. paper proves for any n ≥ 2, τ(K_{2,n}) ≤ ⌈(n+2.05)/1.24⌉. That is a linear bound in n. It is about 2.8 times better than the general bound of Wang, Qian, and Yan, τ(G) ≤ (|E(G)| − 1)/ln(1 + √2) + 1, which is the source of the ln(1+√2) constant, not 4 ln 2.
- **The square-root result is a lower bound on the gap.** A separate paper (Kumar et al., arXiv 2202.03431) is summarized on an aggregator site as showing that τ(K_{2,l}) − χ_ℓ(K_{2,l}) grows at least as fast as C√l for sufficiently large l. That result is about τ versus the list chromatic-type quantity, and it is what disproves a universal constant α. I only saw this through a secondary summary, so check the original before relying on it.

On the other parts of the query:

- **Enumerative chromatic-choosability:** The Kaul et al. paper shows τ(K_{2,3}) = χ(K_{2,3}) and τ(K_{2,4}) = τ(K_{2,5}) = 3. Its keywords include enumerative chromatic-choosability, and it notes that cycles and chordal graphs are already known to be enumeratively chromatic-choosable.
- **Resolved by enumeration:** The search did not show a specific resolution of K_{2,n} by enumeration for all n. The paper has a conjecture (Conjecture 8) that is only proved for small values of m, so it is not fully settled.
- **Asymptotic/4 ln 2:** Nothing in the results mentions 4 ln 2. If that constant appears in your source, it is not in these results.

The Kaul et al. paper was published in *Involve* 16 (2023), no. 5, pp. 849–882.
