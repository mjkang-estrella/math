The search didn't surface any source that names a "Kaul–Mudrock Conjecture" or states that τ(K_{2,l}) = Θ(√l). What it did show is a related lower-bound result.

- **Lower bound (proved):** Kumar, Kaul, Mudrock, To, Rewers, and Shin's paper "On the List Color Function Threshold" (arXiv 2202.03431) shows that τ(K_{2,l}) − χ_ℓ(K_{2,l}) is at least C√l for l ≥ 16, for some constant C. This answers Thomassen's 2009 question about whether a universal constant α bounds τ(G) − χ_ℓ(G), by showing no such constant exists. The paper proves there exists a constant C such that τ(K_{2,l}) − χ_ℓ(K_{2,l}) ≥ C√l for l ≥ 16. The authors' own summary is that the gap grows roughly like the square root of l. Their proof is inductive and gives τ(K_{2,l}) > ⌊l/4⌋/ln(16/7) in the square-root form. I did not find a matching √l upper bound in these results, so this supports only the Ω(√l) direction.

- **Upper bound (earlier, weaker):** Kaul et al., "Bounding the List Color Function Threshold from Above" (arXiv 2207.04831), gives a linear bound: τ(K_{2,n}) ≤ ⌈(n+2.05)/1.24⌉ for n ≥ 2. Their general bound for any graph is τ(G) ≤ (|E(G)|−1)/ln(1+√2) + 1. So the best upper bound in these results is linear in l, not √l.

- **Θ(√l):** Whether τ(K_{2,l}) is Θ(√l) is not settled by these results. The lower bound is Ω(√l) on the gap, and the known upper bound is linear. A tight Θ(√l) claim would need a further source.

- **Other:** A 2026 arXiv paper (2607.16810) on the unlabeled list color function is related work by Kaul, Mudrock, and Straub, but it does not address K_{2,l}.
