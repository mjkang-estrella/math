- **Threshold growth for $K_{2,l}$:** One summary says a paper disproves the existence of a universal constant $\alpha$ in Thomassen's 2009 question, showing that $\tau(K_{2,l}) - \chi_{\ell}(K_{2,l})$ grows at least like $C\sqrt{l}$ for large $l$ (It disproves the existence of a universal constant $\alpha$ by demonstrating that for complete bipartite graphs $K_{2,l}$, $\tau(K_{2,l}) - \chi_{\ell}(K_{2,l})$ grows at least as fast as $C\sqrt{l}$ for sufficiently large $l$.). That summary comes from a third-party aggregator, so I'd check the original paper (arXiv 2202.03431) before relying on it.
- **Upper bounds on $\tau(K_{2,n})$:** A 2022 paper by Kaul, Kumar, Liu, Mudrock, Rewers, Shin, Tanahara and To, published in 2023, gives an upper bound on the threshold of complete bipartite graphs. They develop tools for bounding the list color function threshold of complete bipartite graphs from above. The abstract's formula was garbled in the extraction, so I wouldn't quote its exact form.
- **Mudrock's 2024 question, answered:** A September 2025 paper by Kaul, Marciaga and Mudrock (arXiv 2509.16733, revised November 2025) answers a question Mudrock posed at the 2024 Sparse Graphs Coalition workshop. The question asked whether $\chi_{\ell}(K_n \square K_{a,b}) = n+a$ holds exactly when $b$ meets a factorial-type threshold. The paper shows the answer is yes, by studying $\chi_{\ell}(H \square K_{a,b})$ when $H$ is strongly chromatic-choosable, using the list color function and analytic inequalities such as that of Karamata.
- **Note on "conjecture settled":** This is about list chromatic number of Cartesian products, not directly the $\tau$ threshold. I didn't find a 2026 result or a source explicitly settling the $\sqrt{\ell}$ question for $\tau$.
