The total number of Dressian cells has a lower bound from sparse paving matroids and an upper bound of order $\binom{n}{r}\log(n)^2/n$ on the logarithmic scale, while the source notes a remaining gap even in fixed rank. The conjecture predicts that smooth cells contribute negligibly to the logarithmic count, so that nonsmooth valuations predominate for uniform matroids.
