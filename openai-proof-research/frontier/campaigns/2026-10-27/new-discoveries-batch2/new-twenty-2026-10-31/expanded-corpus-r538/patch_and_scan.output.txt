Corollary 19. We have τ (K2,3 ) = 2 and τ (K2,4 ) = τ (K2,5 ) = 3.
Proof. By Theorem 9, τ (K2,3 ) ≤ 4, implying Pℓ (K2,3 , m) = P (K2,3 , m) for all m ≥ 4. Theo-
rem 7 give that Pℓ (K2,3 , m) = P (K2,3 , m) for each m ∈ {2, 3}. Since χℓ (K2,3 ) = 2, it follows
that τ (K2,3 ) = 2.
    Also, Theorem 9 gives τ (K2,4 ) ≤ 5, implying Pℓ (K2,4 , m) = P (K2,4 , m) for all m ≥ 5.
Theorem 7 gives that Pℓ (K2,4 , m) = P (K2,4 , m) for each m ∈ {3, 4}. Since χℓ (K2,4 ) = 3, it
follows that τ (K2,4 ) = 3.
    Finally, Theorem 9 also gives τ (K2,5 ) ≤ 6, implying Pℓ (K2,5 , m) = P (K2,5 , m) for all
m ≥ 6. Theorem 7 gives that Pℓ (K2,5 , m) = P (K2,5 , m) for each m ∈ {3, 4, 5}. Since
χℓ (K2,5 ) = 3, it follows that τ (K2,5 ) = 3.
