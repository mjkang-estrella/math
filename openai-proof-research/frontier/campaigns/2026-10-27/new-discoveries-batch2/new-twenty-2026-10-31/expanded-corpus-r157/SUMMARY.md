## Strongest lead: 367834 — the conjecture is proved (candidate, unreviewed)

**Statement.** Dovgoshey–Cantor–Rovenska, arXiv:2504.02425, Conjecture 6.2. For an infinite ultrametric space X, these are equivalent:
- (i) X is the completion of a totally bounded proper subspace generated by a labeled ray;
- (ii) X is compact and generated by a labeled starlike rayless tree.

**Source check.** The published version (Online J. Anal. Comb. 20 (2025), DOI 10.61091/ojac20-01, open-access copy read) keeps the statement word for word.

**Proof.** [CASE-367834.md](CASE-367834.md) gives a complete, self-contained proof of both directions, about one page.
- (i) ⇒ (ii):
  - Total boundedness forces the ray labels to tend to 0.
  - The completion then adds exactly one point p, with d(p, x_n) = max of the labels from index n on.
  - Cut the ray after each right-to-left record and hang each finite segment from p, which gets label 0. This gives a starlike rayless tree generating X.
- (ii) ⇒ (i):
  - Compactness forces the centre's label to be 0, and only finitely many vertices lie at distance ≥ ε from the centre.
  - Split each leg into groups at its strict prefix records.
  - List the groups by decreasing height, writing each group with its record last. With the original labels, this ray generates X minus the centre, which is dense and totally bounded.

**Diagnostics.** `scripts/ultra62.py` checked both constructions on 300 random exact-rational truncations per direction, and all distances agreed. This is evidence only.

**Formal status.** `lean-draft/Conj62.lean` fixes the intended literal statement and a lemma plan. It is UNCOMPILED, never elaborated, and its proofs are `sorry` outlines. It is not a certificate. The case file includes a statement-correspondence table.

It does not use the source's Theorems 2.14 or 5.3–5.6. The companion papers (2402.15774, 2502.04228, 2510.01667, 2607.14098) were screened by statements and keywords, not by every proof body. None states or resolves Conjecture 6.2. A web search found no resolution.

## Other sustained routes (no counterexample found)

- **366608** (oriented discrepancy of Hamilton cycles, Ore-type s\*):
  - Two independent exact searches ran over every underlying graph K_n − H with s\* ≥ 2, up to isomorphism, and every orientation, using backtracking on Hamilton-cycle constraints. The cases s\* ≤ 1 are trivial, and graphs covered by Freschi–Lo were skipped.
  - n = 6, 7, 8: complete, no counterexample.
  - n = 9: 258 of 265 graphs decided, no counterexample. The other 7 (s\* = 4, T = 7) are undecided exactly. Held-Karp local search on them reached max σ = T but never went below it.
  - Random and construction-seeded joint searches at n = 9 and 10 found no counterexample.
  - An early version of `oridisc2.py` had a bookkeeping bug that produced false counterexamples. It was fixed, and the fixed version agrees with the independent `oridisc.py`. The buggy run's logs were overwritten; this is disclosed in results.json.
- **373723** (Aprile–Di Summa Conjecture 30, multicolored cycle matchings):
  - The conjecture reduces to finding a matching with p_M ≤ k and |M| ≥ ℓ − h + 2.
  - Exhaustive search: h = 4 for ℓ ≤ 6 (700,075 colourings at ℓ = 6) and h = 5 for ℓ ≤ 5. No violation.
  - Local search: up to ℓ = 16 at h = 4 and ℓ = 12 at h = 5–6. No violation.
  - A brute-force-verified tight instance at h = 4, ℓ = 10 shows the bound is attained.
  - A literal h = 2 reading is false, but that is a wording issue and earns nothing.
- **375230** (EF1 under heterogeneous matroid constraints):
