Let V=F2^p, W=F2^2, U=F2^2 with fixed standard bases. Write a tensor T uniquely as
T=sum_(j,k=1,2) a_jk ⊗ e_j ⊗ e_k.
Its flattening A has the four column vectors a_11,a_12,a_21,a_22 in V. Let O_p be the tensors whose four columns are independent.

1. O_p is a single orbit. Any two ordered independent four-frames extend to bases of V. The linear map sending the first completed basis to the second is invertible and sends each of the four columns to its counterpart. Consequently it sends the entire tensor to its counterpart, using the identity in the two GL2 factors. Conversely the rank of this flattening is invariant under the full product action: the GLp factor multiplies on the left and the other two factors induce an invertible linear map on W⊗U, hence on the four-column coordinate space. Any decomposition into r simple tensors has all four columns in the span of its r first factors. Since A has rank4, r≥4. This proves equality and supplies the source rank bridge, not just matrix rank.

3. In fact no tensor outside O_p has rank4. Identify W⊗U with M_2(F2), with simple tensors the nonzero rank-one matrices. Under invertible row/column operations B has rank-one normal form E11 or rank-two normal form I; applying the corresponding inverse transformations to the slices preserves rank-one matrices. Therefore H is equivalent either to {M:M11=0} or to {M:tr M=0}. The first hyperplane has rank-one basis E12,E21,E22. The second has rank-one basis E12,E21,J, where J is the all-ones matrix. In characteristic2, tr J=0, and these three matrices are independent (the diagonal entries force the J coefficient to vanish). Every slice of T is a linear combination of the chosen three rank-one matrices. Collecting each of their coefficients across the p slices writes T as a sum of at most three simple tensors. Zero first coefficient vectors are omitted. Thus tensor rank≤3. The relation between slice span and flattening rank is ordinary equality of row rank and column rank. This argument covers p<4 as well, so rank4 is impossible there; the source's asymptotic claim begins at p≥4.

|O_p|=(2^p−1)(2^p−2)(2^p−4)(2^p−8).
