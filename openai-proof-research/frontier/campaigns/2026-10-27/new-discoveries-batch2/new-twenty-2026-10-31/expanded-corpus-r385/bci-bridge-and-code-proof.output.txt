Lemma 3.3. Let Γ = BCay(G, S) be a 2-PCayley graph of a group G. If Γ is 2PCI, then the
following statements are equivalent.
(1) Γ is a K2PCI-graph.
(2) NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is transitive on V (Γ).
(3) There exist α ∈ Aut(G) and g ∈ G such that S α = S −1 g.
Proof. Assume that Γ = BCay(G, S) is 2PCI. We first prove the equivalence between (1) and
(2). Suppose that Γ is K2PCI. By Theorem 2.4, NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is transitive on the bipartition
{G1 , G2 } of V (Γ). Since R(G) acts regularly on each part Gi , it follows that NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is
transitive on V (Γ). Conversely, assume that NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is transitive on V (Γ). Since Γ is 2PCI,
by [38, Theorem 4.5], every semiregular subgroup of Aut(Γ) that is isomorphic to G and has the
orbit set {G1 , G2 } on V (Γ) is conjugate to R(G) within Aut(Γ). Since NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is transitive
on V (Γ), the sufficiency part of Theorem 2.4 yields the conclusion that Γ is K2PCI.
    Next we shall prove that (1) and (3) are equivalent. We first prove that (1) implies (3). By
[6, Proposition 2.1 (4)], we have Γ ∼  = BCay(G, S −1 ). Since Γ is K2PCI, by Corollary 3.2, there
exist α ∈ Aut(G) and g ∈ G such that S −1 = S α g −1 . This proves (3). Now we prove that (3)
implies (1). Since Γ = BCay(G, S) is 2PCI, for any 2-PCayley graph BCay(G, T ) isomorphic to Γ,
by Corollary 3.2 there exist β ∈ Aut(G) and h ∈ G such that either T = h−1 S β or T = (S −1 )β h.
When T = h−1 S β , Corollary 3.2 gives that Γ is K2PCI. Now, consider the case when T = (S −1 )β h.
By our assumption, S α = S −1 g for some α ∈ Aut(G) and g ∈ G. Then, T = S αβ g −β h. Take
γ = αβ and k = g −β h. Then T = S γ k for some γ ∈ Aut(G) and k ∈ G, and we obtain the desired
result from Corollary 3.2.
   Let G be an abelian group, and let Γ = BCay(G, S) be a 2-PCayley graph of G. It is easy
to see that α : g1 7→ g2−1 and g2 7→ g1−1 for any g ∈ G, is an automorphism of Γ. Since G is
abelian, g 7→ g −1 is an automorphism of G. By Proposition 2.1, α normalizes R(G), and hence
NAut(Γ) (R(G)) is transitive on V (Γ).
Corollary 3.4. Let G be abelian. Then a 2-PCayley graph BCay(G, S) is 2PCI if and only if it
is K2PCI.
