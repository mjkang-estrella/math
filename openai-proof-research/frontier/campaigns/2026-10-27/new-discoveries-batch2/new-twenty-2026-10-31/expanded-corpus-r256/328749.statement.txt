Let $\Gamma$ be the matching derangement graph, whose vertices are the perfect matchings under consideration, and let $\eta_{\min}(\Gamma)$ denote its least eigenvalue. **The least-eigenvalue conjecture for the matching derangement graph.** The least eigenvalue of the matching derangement graph is 
$$
\eta_{\min}(\Gamma)=-\frac{!!n}{2(n-1)}.
$$
 Computations verify the corresponding ratio equality for $n<8$, while the analogous conjecture for the permutation derangement graph was proved by Renteln; the matching-graph assertion is presented as an open question.
