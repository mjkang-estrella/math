Let $A\subset \mathbb{Z}$ be a finite set with $|A|=m$. A **Freiman isomorphism** is a map preserving the additive relations of $A$; write $[N]=\{1,\ldots,N\}$. **Konyagin–Lev conjecture.** There exists a Freiman isomorphism

$$
\phi:A\to\mathbb{Z}
$$

such that

$$
\phi(A)\subset [2^{m-2}+1].
$$

This conjecture asserts an exponential upper bound on the size of an interval containing a Freiman-isomorphic copy of any finite subset of the integers. The supplied text gives no evidence that the conjecture has been resolved.
