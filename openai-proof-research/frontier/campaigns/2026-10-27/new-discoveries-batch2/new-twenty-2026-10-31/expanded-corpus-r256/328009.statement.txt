Let $H$ be a trigraph, let $H_0$ be an induced cycle of $H$, and let $S$ be a set of disjoint paths on 3 vertices. Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a robust ear decomposition of $H$ that admits $H_0$ and $S$. **Cut avoidance conjecture.** There is a pair $H'$, $\mathcal{H}'$ in the closure of $H$, $\mathcal{H}$ such that $\mathcal{H}'$ is superb. This restricted avoidance of cut-obstacles is presented as sufficient for proving the directed cycle double cover conjecture; the supplied text gives no resolution of the claim.
