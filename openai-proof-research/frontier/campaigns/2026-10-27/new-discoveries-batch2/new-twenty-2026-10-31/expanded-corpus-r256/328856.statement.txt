Let $G$ be a $d$-regular simple graph. For integers $d$ and $t$, define

$$
\rho(d,t)=\begin{cases} \frac{d-4 + \sqrt{(d+4)^2-8t}}{2}, & \text{when } t \text{ is odd}, \\ \frac{d-3 + \sqrt{(d+3)^2-8t}}{2}, & \text{when } t \text{ is even}.\end{cases}
$$

**O's conjecture.** For $t\geq 3$, if

$$
\lambda_2(G)<\rho(d,t),
$$

then $\kappa'(G)\geq t+1$.
This conjecture extends known spectral sufficient conditions for higher edge-connectivity of regular simple graphs; the supplied text does not state whether it has been proved or disproved.
