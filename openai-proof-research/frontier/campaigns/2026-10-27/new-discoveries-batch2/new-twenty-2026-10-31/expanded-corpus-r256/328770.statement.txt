Let $S_n$ be the polytope of symmetric doubly-stochastic matrices, and consider the toric ideal $I_{S_n}$ using only the vertices of $S_n$. For $n
eq 2$, let an $n$-free monomial mean a monomial not divisible by $t_i^n$ for any variable $t_i$. **The $n$-free generators conjecture.** For $n
eq 2$, every reverse lexicographic initial ideal of $I_{S_n}$ is generated by monomials of degree $3(n-2)$, and its minimal generators are $n$-free. That is, no minimal generator is divisible by $t_i^n$ for any variable $t_i$. This is proposed for triangulations using only vertices rather than all lattice points; the source gives no proof or resolution.
