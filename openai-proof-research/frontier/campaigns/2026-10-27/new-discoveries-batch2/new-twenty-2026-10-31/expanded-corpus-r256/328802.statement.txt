Let $G$ be a planar graph. A **proper 3-coloring** of $G$ is a partition of $V(G)$ into three independent sets. Two triangles are **intersecting** if they are not disjoint.

**Borodin–Raspaud Bordeaux conjecture.** Every planar graph without intersecting triangles and without $5$-cycles is properly $3$-colorable.

This conjecture combines the ideas of Havel's triangle-separation proposal and Steinberg's conjecture. The supplied text does not state a resolution of the conjecture.
