Let $p$ be prime, let $U$ be a set of $p^2$ points in the affine space ${\rm AG}(3,p)$, and let $N$ be the set of directions not determined by pairs of points of $U$. **The cylinder conjecture.** If

$$
|N|\geq p,
$$

then $U$ is the union of $p$ parallel lines.
This conjecture asserts that the known cylindrical construction is the only such configuration with at least $p$ non-determined directions; the supplied text gives no resolution.
