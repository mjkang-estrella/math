UQI(n, k). Since the group fix(P ) is very large and difficult to work with, we give a

    For partitions P, Q ∈ V (QI(n, k)) define the meet table of P and Q to be the
k × k array with the i, j entry |Pi ∩ Qj |. Denote the meet table of P and Q by MP,Q .
Partitions P and Q are qualitatively independent if and only if the meet table of P
and Q has all entries non-zero.
    Two meet tables are isomorphic if some permutation of the rows and columns of
one array produces the other array. Different orderings on the classes in the partitions
P and Q could produce different meet tables, but the tables would be isomorphic. If
P = Q, then the meet table MP,Q is isomorphic to the array with diagonal entries
c = n/k and all other entries are zero.
