- **The 2023 paper:** "Bounding the List Color Function Threshold from Above" by Kaul, Kumar, Liu, Mudrock, Rewers, Shin, Tanahara, and To appeared in *Involve* vol. 16, no. 5 (2023), pp. 849–882. Its abstract says the authors build tools for bounding the threshold of complete bipartite graphs from above. For K_{2,n}, they give an upper bound of roughly (n + 2.05)^1.24. The abstract notes that the proof uses Rolle's theorem and Descartes' rule of signs. The garbled formula in the search snippet should be checked against the PDF. ([msp.org](https://msp.org/involve/2023/16-5/p07.xhtml))
- **The "On the List Color Function Threshold" paper:** This is arXiv 2202.03431 by Kaul et al. It conjectures that τ(K_{2,l}) = Θ(√l) as l → ∞, and the authors note their lower bound likely captures the behavior of τ(K_{2,l}). The search results did not confirm a Journal of Graph Theory publication for it, so treat that venue as unverified. ([arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03431](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03431))
- **Background:** The threshold τ(G) is the smallest k ≥ χ(G) such that the list color function equals the chromatic polynomial for all m ≥ k. The 2022 arXiv preprint of the bounding paper (arXiv 2207.04831) frames complete bipartite graphs as central to the extremal behavior of τ. ([arxiv.org/abs/2207.04831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04831))
