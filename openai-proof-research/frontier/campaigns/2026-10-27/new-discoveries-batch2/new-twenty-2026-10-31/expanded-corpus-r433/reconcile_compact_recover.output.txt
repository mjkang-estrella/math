the sizes of their largest weak (resp. strict) convex subposets are the same.
Proof. Observe that in (P, ω), a subposet S forms a weak convex subposet if and
only if its elements can all have the same image in some (P, ω)-partition. Therefore,
again considering the monomials in K(P,ω) , the size of the largest weak convex
subposet of (P, ω) is exactly the highest power of any variable xi that appears
in any one of the monomials. For the strict case, apply the same argument to
(P, ω).                                                                            
