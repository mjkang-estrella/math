Let $\mathcal{H}=(V,\mathcal{E})$ be an $r$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices. For a positive integer $k<r$, let $\nu_k(\mathcal{H})$ denote its $k$-matching number, and let $a\geq2$ with $n\geq r\cdot a$. Set

$$
n_0=ra-(a-1)(k-1)-1.
$$

For each integer $i$ satisfying

$$
0\leq i\leq\frac{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{a-1}\right\rfloor-k}{2},
$$

let $\mathcal{F}_i$ be a $(n,r,k,a,i)$-Frankl family. **Generalized Erdős' matching conjecture.** If $\nu_k(\mathcal{H})<a$, then

$$
|\mathcal{E}|\leq\max\left\{\binom{n_0}{r},|\mathcal{F}_i|;\ 0\leq i\leq\frac{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{a-1}\right\rfloor-k}{2}\right\}.
$$

The claim extends the classical matching problem to $k$-matchings and proposes that the extremal examples are the complete $r$-uniform hypergraph on $n_0$ vertices and the indicated Frankl families. The supplied text gives no resolution status beyond its formulation, so the conjecture is recorded as open.
