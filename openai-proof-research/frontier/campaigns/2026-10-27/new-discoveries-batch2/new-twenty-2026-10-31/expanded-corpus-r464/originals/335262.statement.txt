An **ultrafilter satisfying a partition relation** is an ultrafilter with some partition property, and a forcing is **$c3$-closed** when every descending sequence of conditions of length less than $c3$ has a lower bound. A **topological Ramsey space** is a space whose finite-approximation structure satisfies the Ramsey-space axioms.

**Topological Ramsey space forcing conjecture.** Every ultrafilter which satisfies some partition relation and is forced by some $c3$-closed forcing is actually forced by some topological Ramsey space.

The conjecture proposes a broad connection between ultrafilters with weak partition properties and topological Ramsey spaces, extending the examples in which dense subsets of the relevant forcings form topological Ramsey spaces. Its status is not resolved in the supplied source.
