Let $G$ be an infinite graph with vertex set $E$. For distinct vertices $x,y\in E$, write $G|E\setminus\{x\}$ for the subgraph induced by deleting $x$, and write $\sim$ for graph isomorphism. **Ulam reconstruction conjecture.** If

$$
\forall (x,y)\in E^2,\quad x\neq y;\quad G|E\setminus\{x\}\sim G|E\setminus\{y\},
$$

then $G$ is Ulam-reconstructible. The source presents this as a conjectural sufficient condition for infinite-graph reconstruction, with no supplied resolution.
