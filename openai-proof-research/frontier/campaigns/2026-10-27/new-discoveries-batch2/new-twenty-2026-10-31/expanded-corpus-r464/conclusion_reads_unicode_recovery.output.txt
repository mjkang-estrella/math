5     Concluding remarks
This paper establishes the necessary and sufficient conditions for the existence of a G-magic
rectangle set MRSG (a, b; c) for any finite abelian group G, thereby confirming Conjecture 5.1
presented by Cichacz and Hinc in [3].
    Magic rectangle sets can be seen as a weak version of 3-dimensional magic rectangles, which
was introduced by Hagedorn [8]. Let a1 , a2 , . . . , an be positive integers. An n-dimensional
magic rectangle n-MR(a1 , a2 , . . . , an ) is an a1 × a2 × · · · × an array with entries di1 ,i2 ,...,in which
are elements of {1, 2, . . . , a1 a2 . . . an }, each appearing exactly once, such that all sums in the
k-th direction, 1 ⩽ k ⩽ n, are equal to a constant σk . That is, for every k, 1 ⩽ k ⩽ n,


                                                      13
Pak
  j=1 db1 ,b2 ,...,bk−1 ,j,bk+1 ,...,bn   = σk for every selection of indices b1 , b2 , . . . , bk−1 , bk+1 , . . . , bn and
σk = ak (1 + a1 a2 · · · an )/2.
    Compared with a 3-dimensional magic rectangle, a magic rectangle set features each hori-
zontal layer (an a × b array) with equal row sums and equal column sums, while vertical column
sums are unrestricted. Although the necessary and sufficient conditions for the existence of a
3-dimensional magic rectangle have been established in [13, Theorem 6.1], it appears that no
literature focuses on the existence of 3-dimensional magic rectangles over finite groups. A fur-
ther research direction is to examine the existence of 3-dimensional magic rectangles over finite
groups.
