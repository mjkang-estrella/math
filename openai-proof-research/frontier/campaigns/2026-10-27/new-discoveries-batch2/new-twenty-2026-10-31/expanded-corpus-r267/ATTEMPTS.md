## 379909 — Collinear-triple conjecture for permutation graphs

Primary depth: Cooper Conjecture 2 and Proposition 4; v1/v2 conjecture statements; Li section 1 including the incidence/Kakeya reduction proof.

- For odd q, form K_sigma as the union of slope-i lines through (0,sigma(i)). Li gives |K|=q(q+1)/2+sum_e binom(|e|-1,2). The sharp Kakeya bound gives at least (q-1)/2 for this sum; binom(s,3)>=binom(s-1,2) for s>=3 finishes. This is already published, not new.

- For the unrestricted literal characteristic-two domain, sigma(x)=x^2 on F_(2^h) has no collinear triple: a nonvertical line meets y=x^2 in at most two points and a vertical line in one. Squaring is a permutation. Thus missing oddness produces an excluded domain correction, not a candidate.


Disposition: excluded_known_odd_field_result_and_domain_fix. Remaining gap/exclusion: Odd-field result is public (Li 2008). Even-field literal refutation is a domain repair. Related source-family r74/379908 already rejected the Kakeya resolution; no duplicate investment.
