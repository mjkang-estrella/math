## 366460: full graph-dependent literal route, excluded scope bridge

Read arXiv:2501.00157v1 and v2 target passages, latest Definitions 23–24, Conjectures 25 and 29, and the polynomial framework. The literal quantifier is “for each G there exists c.” The surrounding demand for a constant bound is naturally a uniform demand. We do not treat the weaker printed quantifier as a discovery.

Here is a complete elementary reduction for the finite simple-graph reading of the printed game. Put m=|E|, D=m and c=(m+1)(m+2)+1. Form the polynomial
P = product over uv in E of (sum over e incident to u of x_e − sum over e incident to v of x_e).
Each factor is nonzero over any field: x_uv cancels, and if no other variable remains then uv is an isolated edge, excluded by hypothesis. Thus P is nonzero in the polynomial ring over the field, of total degree at most D. An assignment makes P nonzero exactly when every adjacent pair has different weighted sums.

Maintain that P, after substitution of the already assigned variables, is a nonzero polynomial Q. When the adversary marks nonempty X and offers a, if some e in X admits Q|x_e=a nonzero, assign just that e the value a. Otherwise assign none. This is allowed since Definition 23 permits X' empty. In either case retain the invariant. The offered value is removed from A, so values are distinct, and removing finitely many values leaves an infinite set.

For any remaining variable e during a phase when no assignment occurs, Q is fixed. There are at most D values a for which Q|x_e=a is identically zero. To prove this, regard Q as a polynomial in e over the fraction field of the polynomial ring in the other variables and apply the univariate root bound. Hence in a phase e is marked and rejected at most D times. There are at most m+1 such phases, separated by at most m successful assignments. Before e is assigned it also loses a token on at most m successful rounds assigning another variable. Consequently e is marked without being assigned at most (m+1)D+m times, strictly less than c−1. Its budget remains positive, including at the terminal state required by Definition 23. There cannot be an infinite phase with nonempty markings: finitely many remaining variables each permit at most D rejections. Therefore all variables are assigned after finitely many rounds and Q is a nonzero constant. The recursive game predicate follows by well-founded induction on the remaining-variable count and the sum of budgets; the specified response reduces this measure and has the invariant and positive-budget properties just proved. Empty E is immediate.

This is a generic finite polynomial avoidance argument, not a graph-independent c. It resolves only the weak literal placement of c, by a classical root bound and a standard greedy game argument. It supplies no full route to the intended uniform online 1-2-3 relaxation. Exclude: quantifier/scope correction and routine classical bridge. Do not turn this into a CANDIDATE or formalization assignment for campaign credit. The same-source characteristic-two Alon–Tarsi refutation found in historical trureturing notes is a different statement and does not refute this game.

## 391316: Johnson graph / design mechanism, stopped at exact obstruction

For k-subsets S,T, sharing k−1 elements is equivalent to being distinct k-subsets of one (k+1)-set. Thus the requested coloring is exactly a proper (k+1)-coloring of J(2k,k). Conversely, properness makes the k+1 subsets of each (k+1)-set have distinct colors, so all colors occur. This is a full equivalence, not a witness.

For a fixed (k−1)-set R, its k+1 one-element extensions form a clique and use every color. Thus each color class is a Steiner system S(k−1,k,2k), and the classes partition all k-subsets. Fixing an r-subset gives the necessary integrality condition
C(2k−r,k−1−r)/(k−r) is an integer for 0≤r≤k−1.
For r=k−2 this forces k even. More generally set t=k−r. Then t divides C(k+t,t−1) for all 1≤t≤k. If a prime p divides the composite k+1 with p≤k, taking t=p and reducing C(k+p,p−1) modulo p gives 1, a contradiction. This excludes composite k+1.

Read the complete four-page Ma–Tang primary proof: Proposition 2.1 proves precisely these divisibility constraints, Theorem 2.2 proves precisely the composite obstruction, and Lemma 2.3 proves that every one of these constraints holds when k+1 is prime. Thus extending the same divisibility calculation cannot close the residual case. The first small cases 3..8 are already reported excluded in the live problem entry/current formal source. A cyclic coloring by sum of labels modulo k+1 also fails: 2k labels cannot all be distinct modulo k+1, and two equal-residue labels together with a common (k−1)-set create a forbidden monochromatic pair. No known small-instance exception is promoted to a full negative answer to the existential question. Remaining gap: construct a complete large set of these designs at some prime-minus-one k>2, or exclude every such k by a mechanism beyond divisibility. No finite witness or executable certificate is supplied. Source-subsumed partial argument is excluded; full question parked.

## 362181: finite-field matching extremal mechanism, no complete bound

Read all three versions' Section 4.2 target context, latest Theorem 4.7 including proof, Conjecture 4.8, and the matching definition. Disjoint means intersection {0}; it does not mean that the whole collection is a direct sum. The exact min-bound in MathDB was not located as a conjecture in those passages. The primary source proves the first bound only for sufficiently large n and states a different structural conjecture about largest s-EM families. This mismatch is an unresolved fidelity issue, not permission to substitute another target.

First construction test: all k-subspaces containing one fixed line have matching number 1 and size [n−1 choose k−1]_q, meeting the second bound at s=1. All k-subspaces inside a (2k−1)-space likewise have matching number 1. Their size [2k−1 choose k]_q equals [2k−1 choose k−1]_q, attaining the second bound at n=2k. These classical stars/top families are not counterexamples. A union of s stars has matching number at most s by pigeonhole and size at most s[n−1 choose k−1]_q, so it cannot refute the second bound either.

A seemingly broader set-system transfer—take every k-space meeting one fixed s-dimensional space—is unsound. A vector s-space has more than s lines; pairwise zero-intersecting intersections inside it do not have to be linearly independent. Thus dimension counting cannot certify matching number ≤s for that construction. Conversely, for a maximal matching U1,..,Ut (t≤s), every family member meets some Ui. Covering through each line in the Ui gives only the weak bound s[k choose 1]_q[n−1 choose k−1]_q, with an unpaid factor [k choose 1]_q. Removing that factor requires structure not furnished by maximality. The source asymptotic proof transfers a hereditary matching bound and takes a limit to obtain its first bound; this does not prove both inequalities for all n≥(s+1)k. No asymptotic or star case is a full answer. Remaining gap: full exact finite-range min inequality, or an explicitly specified family exceeding it with pairwise-intersection matching certificate. No candidate, no enumeration.

## 355709: rigidity plus canonicalization, printed obstruction

Source Conjecture 6.2 requires a polynomial decision algorithm for every d≥3, not merely an algorithm for one token count or for paths. Read latest Lemma 6.3 and its complete proof. It already computes rigid tokens in O(n²). The natural attempt was to delete rigid tokens, compare component token counts, then move the remaining tokens to canonical leaves. Figure 6 (page 12, visually inspected) blocks this approach even with no rigid tokens: a central vertex u with four arms of length d−1, with two tokens at endpoints of two arms. Moving a token through u would put it distance d−1 from the other endpoint token, which is forbidden. Tokens can move within their arms, hence none is rigid, but the occupied arms cannot change. The endpoints can be chosen black on the left two arms and gray on the right two, as drawn in the source. Thus the failure is already a printed recipe, not a new obstruction.

An enhanced state recording arm occupancy repairs this one spider but not arbitrary trees: successive branching vertices interact through available distances and tokens in the connecting paths. No proof that local reachable boundary profiles compose without losing correlations was found. Enumerating all configurations is exponential and is not a polynomial-time algorithm. A reduction to independent-set reconfiguration on the (d−1)-power preserves feasibility but not legal moves: adjacency in the power allows jumps that are not slides in the original tree. The TJ theorem in the source therefore does not pay the TS semantic bridge. Precise gap: polynomially many compositional boundary states with a sound and complete reachability/gluing proof for arbitrary branching trees. Park, no Pi task.

## 388032: already answered publicly, explicit scope bridge

Guan–Jin–Kálmán, arXiv:2309.13639v1 and v2, Question 1.1 and Theorem 3.5. Read latest definitions of slices, Theorems 3.4–3.5 and full proof of 3.5. For coordinate t let α_t=min a_t, β_t=max a_t over bases a. Fix each coordinate value j and delete that coordinate to obtain the slice polymatroid P-hat_j. If α_t<β_t, their formula is
T_P = x T_(P-hat_α) + y T_(P-hat_β) + sum_(α<j<β) T_(P-hat_j).
If α=β the factor is (x+y−1) times the one slice polynomial (Theorem 3.4). The extreme slices are deletion and contraction. Interior slices are lower-ground-set polymatroids; demanding only two extreme terms would be a stronger question than the literal existence of deletion-contraction formulas.

Their proof moves t last by Sn invariance. Activities for all other coordinates agree in a slice and the ambient polymatroid. Coordinate t is internally active only at the minimum, externally active only at the maximum, and inactive otherwise; retaining a fixed coordinate makes it active both ways. Summing the activity expansion by slices and removing the constant coordinate gives exactly the displayed recurrence. The source explicitly says this answers Question 1.1. Therefore the literal general formula request is already publicly answered; rederiving/formalizing it is excluded. The 2025 recursive-definition successor was downloaded and its role located, but its full proof was not reviewed; the separate IMRN final journal body was not retrieved. Those limitations cannot make this public prior answer novel.

## 351711: pinned upstream answer, explicit full exclusion
