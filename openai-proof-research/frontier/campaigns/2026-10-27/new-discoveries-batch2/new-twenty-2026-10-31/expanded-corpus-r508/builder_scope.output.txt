                                                 strategy that, with high probability, allows him to construct a graph of purchased edges
                                                 possessing a target graph property P, all within the limitations of observation time and
                                                 total budget. We show the following:

    We continue to Hamiltonicity. The classical hitting-time result of Bollobás [17] and indepen-
dently of Ajtai, Komlós, and Szemerédi [1] states that the random graph process, whp, becomes
Hamiltonian as soon as its minimum degree reaches 2. As we reminded earlier, τ2 = Θ(n log n),
while a Hamilton cycle has only n edges. The following result states that there exists an online
algorithm choosing only linearly many edges from the ﬁrst τ2 edges of the random graph process
that still obtains a Hamilton cycle whp. Moreover, as we show in the proof, this algorithm is
extremely simple, deterministic, and requires only local information. The theorem recovers – and
substantially strengthens – the aforementioned classical result.
