Abstract
Let M be an arbitrary matroid with circuits 𝒞⁡(M). We propose a definition of a derived matroid δ​M that has as its ground set 𝒞⁡(M). Unlike previous attempts of such a definition, our definition applies to arbitrary matroids, and is completely combinatorial. We prove that the rank of δ​M is n−r⁡(M), that it is connected if and only if M is connected, and compute a range of examples.
