In general, if M is representable, we conjecture that the combinatorial derived matroid is generic in two different ways; one categorical and one algebraic-geometric. In order to state Conjectures 8.5 and 8.6, we need some auxiliary standard definitions.
Definition 8.4.
Let E be a finite set. The weak order the class ℳ of matroids with ground set E is the partial order with M1≥M2 if every dependent set in M1 is also dependent in M2.
Conjecture 8.5.
Let M be a representable matroid on ground set E. Then δ⁡(M)≥δO​W​(R) for every representation R of M, where the order relation is the weak order on the set of matroids on E.
Note that Lemma 8.2 proves Conjecture 8.5 in the case that δ​M=(𝒞,𝒜0). It is conceivable that Conjecture 8.5 may be approached with techniques similar to those in [6]. There, the authors studied conditions under which the collection a matroids with some circuits prescribed have a maximal element in the weak order.
