323015 Let $r$ be a positive integer, let $P$ be an $r$-differential poset, let $P_n$ denote its $n$th rank, and write $p_n:=|P_n|$ for the rank cardinalities. **Stanley's conjecture.** An $r$-differential poset $P$ has

$$
p_1<p_2<p_3<\cdots.
$$

The conjecture asks whether the weak rank growth known for differential posets is always strict. The paper establishes this strict growth, so the conjecture is solved. 

323032 Let $(M_k\mid k\in K)$ be a family of matroids on the same ground set $E$. A **packing** consists of pairwise disjoint spanning sets, one for each $M_k$, and a **covering** consists of independent sets $I_k$ in $M_k$ whose union covers $E$. For $P\subseteq E$, write $M_k\restriction_P$ for restriction, and for $C\subseteq E$, write $M_k.C$ for contraction. **Packing/covering conjecture.** The ground set admits a partition

$$
E=P\mathbin{\dot\cup}C
$$

such that $(M_k\restriction_P\mid k\in K)$ has a packing and $(M_k.C\mid k\in K)$ has a covering. This conjecture is stated as equivalent to the matroid intersection conjecture and unifies the infinite base packing and base covering problems. 

323059 Let $G$ be a $d$-regular graph on $n$ vertices, where $d\equiv 2\pmod 3$ and $n$ is divisible by $2(d-2)$, and let ${\rm mis}(G)$ denote the number of maximal independent sets of $G$.

**Regular-graph maximal-independent-set conjecture.** Every $d$-regular $n$-vertex graph satisfies

$$
{\rm mis}(G)\leq 2^{n/(2(d-2))}3^{n/6}.
$$

The proposed extremal construction is a disjoint union of copies of a specific $d$-regular graph built from triangles, which attains the displayed quantity under the stated divisibility conditions. Whether this construction is always extremal is left as a conjectural question. 

323064 Let $O=(n_1n_2\dots n_t,p_1p_2\dots p_t)$ and $P=(m_1m_2\dots m_l,q_1q_2\dots q_l)$ be two finite octal games. Write $\mathcal{G}_s$ for the scoring-play Sprague–Grundy function, and let $+_{\ell}$ denote the disjunctive sum and $\triangledown$ the selective sum.

**Selective-sum period conjecture.** If $\mathcal{G}_s(n+_{\ell}m)$ eventually has period $p$, then $\mathcal{G}_s(n\triangledown m)$ also eventually has period $p$.

This is proposed as a stronger form of the preceding periodicity conjecture: changing from the disjunctive sum to the selective sum should not change the eventual period. The paper gives no proof or resolution. 

323065 Let $O=(n_1n_2\dots n_t,p_1p_2\dots p_t)$ and $P=(m_1m_2\dots m_l,q_1q_2\dots q_l)$ be two finite octal games. Write $\mathcal{G}_s$ for the scoring-play Sprague–Grundy function, and let $+_{\ell}$ denote the disjunctive sum and $\rhd$ the sequential join.

**Sequential-join period conjecture.** If $\mathcal{G}_s(n+_{\ell}m)$ eventually has period $p$, then $\mathcal{G}_s(n\rhd m)$ also eventually has period $p$.

The conjecture claims that the sequential join preserves the eventual period already present under the disjunctive sum. The paper does not establish it. 

323164 Let $\mathtt{Frk}_{i,n-i}$ be the fork with $n$ vertices and stem of size $i$, and let $\#\mathbb{F}(\mathtt{Frk}_{i,n-i},k,0)$ denote the number of closed flows of size $k$ on this fork. **Closed-flow enumeration conjecture for forks.**

$$
\#\mathbb{F}(\mathtt{Frk}_{i,n-i},k,0)=\binom{i}{k}\binom{n}{k}-\binom{i+1}{k+1}\binom{n-1}{k-1}.
$$

The formula proposes an explicit binomial enumeration of closed flows on forks. The source provides no resolution status. 

323165 Let $\mathscr{Y}=\mathscr{E}\circ\mathscr{D}^{-1}$ be the quotient series in the group $\mathsf{G}_{\operatorname{PreLie}}$, let $\mathscr{Y}_T$ be the coefficient of a rooted tree $T$ in $\mathscr{Y}$, let $\mathscr{E}_T^s$ be the generating series for saturated flows on $T$, and let $L(T)$ be the number of leaves of $T$. **Saturated-flow quotient formula.** The coefficient $\mathscr{Y}_T$ is the monomial

$$
\mathscr{Y}_T=(-1)^{L(T)-1}\mathscr{E}_T^s.
$$

This formula would give a simple description of the quotient series in terms of saturated flows and the leaf count. The source provides no resolution status. 

323325 Let $G$ be an isostatic graph. For a realisation $(G,p)$, let $c(G,p)$ be the number of equivalent realisations, and let $c(G)$ be the corresponding count for a generic realisation. Assume that $c(G,p)$ is finite.

**Specialisation conjecture.** One has

$$
c(G,p)\leq c(G).
$$

This asserts that specialising an isostatic framework cannot increase the finite number of equivalent realisations. The source gives no proof or resolution, so the claim remains open in the supplied material. 

323400 Let $G$ be a connected planar graph, let $b(G)$ be its bondage number, and let $g(G)$ denote its girth. **Planar girth-bounds conjecture.**

$$
b(G)\leqslant7.
$$

Furthermore,

$$
b(G)\leqslant\begin{cases}5,&g(G)\geqslant4,\\4,&g(G)\geqslant5.\end{cases}
$$

The source states that these bounds were conjectured after known results established weaker bounds and says that their remaining sharpness was open. 

323420 Let $M$ be a matroid, and let an **MLS of degree $n$** be a matroidal Latin square of degree $n$, whose rows and columns are bases of $M$. An **independent partial transversal** is an independent set of entries with no two entries in the same row or column. **Kotlar–Ziv conjecture.** Every MLS of degree $n$ has an independent partial transversal of size $n-1$. This is the proposed matroidal analogue of Brualdi's conjecture. The conjecture asks for a nearly full independent partial transversal in every matroidal Latin square; the paper's abstract establishes only a guaranteed size of $\ceiling 2n/3 \rceil$ and gives examples with maximal size at most $n-1$, so the conjecture remains open. 

323505 Let $N\subset M$ be a subfactor with index in the interval $(5,3+\sqrt{5})$. Its principal graph is the graph describing the associated standard invariant; write $A_\infty$ for the infinite $A$-type principal graph. The paper also specifies two further principal-graph pairs, denoted here by $\Gamma(\mathcal A)$ and $\Gamma(\mathcal B)$.

**Classification conjecture.** Any subfactor with index in $(5,3+\sqrt{5})$ has principal graph $A_\infty$, $\Gamma(\mathcal A)$, or $\Gamma(\mathcal B)$.

The theorem preceding this statement proves the assertion for 1-supertransitive subfactors, while the conjecture removes that hypothesis and is left open by the paper. 

323535 Let $G$ be a bipartite graph on $X\cup Y$ with $|X|=\ell$ and $|Y|=M\geq \ell$. For each $x\in X$, let $d_x$ be its degree, and define

$$
\psi(d,t)=t^{-1}\bigl[\log d!-\log \Gamma(d-t+1)\bigr],
$$

where $\Gamma$ is the usual gamma function. Let $\Phi_\ell(G)$ denote the number of matchings of size $\ell$ in $G$.

**Ilinca–Kahn conjectured matching bound.** If, for each $x\in X$, $t_x=\ell d_x/M$, then

$$
\log\Phi_\ell(G)\leq \sum_{x\in X}\psi(d_x,t_x).
$$

This proposed inequality is a precise version of an earlier entropy bound discussed in the paper. The source proposes it as a conjecture and does not provide a proof or resolution. 

323541 Let $a$ be an energy maximal exponent tuple, and let $4d:=\delta(a)4d$. Define $\Lambda^0(d)=d$ and, whenever defined, $\Lambda^i(d)=\Lambda(\Lambda^{i-1}(d))$. A divisor vector is framed when it satisfies the paper's framing condition; an iterated vector is **unframed** when it does not. 

**Framing conjecture.** Suppose that all $\Lambda^0(d),\ldots,\Lambda^j(d)$ exist and that $\Lambda^j(d)$ is unframed. Then $\Lambda^0(d),\ldots,\Lambda^{j-1}(d)$ are all framed.

This conjecture asserts that, along the balancing sequence of an energy-maximal exponent tuple, an unframed terminal vector can occur only after every preceding vector is framed. The supplied text gives examples motivating continued balancing and says that framing appears important, but provides no resolution. 

323542 Let $A,B,C\subset \mathbb{R}$ be large finite sets of numbers. Write $AA=\\{ab:a,b\in A\\}$ and $BC=\\{bc:b\in B,c\in C\\}$. 

**Additive-shift multiplicative-structure conjecture.** If

$$
|(AA+1)\cap BC|\approx |AA|,
$$

then

$$
\min\\{|B|,|C|\\}\lesssim 1.
$$

This conjecture asserts that an additive shift of a product set cannot have substantial overlap with a product set formed from two genuinely large finite sets. It strengthens the paper's theorem excluding containment in a generalized geometric progression, but no resolution is given here. 

323566 Let $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,n}$ be the moduli space of stable $n$-pointed genus-zero curves, and let $D_\Gamma$ be the divisor associated with an irreducible hypertree $\Gamma$ of order at most $n$. **Castravet–Tevelev's hypertree conjecture.** Every extremal ray of

$$
\operatorname{Eff}(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,n})
$$

is either a boundary divisor or the divisor $D_\Gamma$ of an irreducible hypertree $\Gamma$ of order at most $n$. This is an optimistic converse to the construction of hypertree divisors and predicts a complete description of the extremal rays of the effective cone. 

323667 Let $D53A_n$ be the set of permutations of $[n]$, let $\operatorname{inv}\sigma$ be the number of inversions of $\sigma$, and let $\operatorname{exc}\sigma$ be the number of excedances of $\sigma$. Define

$$
S_n(q,t/q):=\sum_{\sigma\in \mathfrak{S}_n}q^{\operatorname{inv}\sigma-\operatorname{exc}\sigma}t^{\operatorname{exc}\sigma}.
$$

**Blanco–Petersen's conjecture.** There exist polynomials $\gamma_{n,k}(q)$ with nonnegative integer coefficients such that

$$
S_n(q,t/q)=\sum_{0\leq k\leq (n-1)/2}\gamma_{n,k}(q)t^k(1+t)^{n-1-2k}.
$$

This conjecture proposes a $q$-analogue of the gamma expansion of the Eulerian polynomial, refining the joint distribution of inversions and excedances. The paper's abstract states that the conjecture is proved in this work. 

323692 A **cograph** is a graph obtained from a single vertex by repeatedly applying disjoint union and complementation. For a graph $G$, the **edge-clique cover problem** asks whether the edges of $G$ can be covered by at most $k$ cliques, for a given integer $k$. **Edge-clique cover conjecture.** The edge-clique cover problem is NP-complete for cographs. This would establish NP-completeness for a graph class considered in the paper, complementing the polynomial-time results for the independence number of edge-clique graphs of cographs and distance-hereditary graphs. The supplied text gives no resolution of the conjecture. 

323710 Let $G$ be a rooted graph on $n$ vertices, with root $r$, and let $cc_r(G)$ and $CT_r(G)$ denote its cover cost and cover time from $r$. The path on $n$ vertices rooted at an endpoint maximises the ratio

$$
\frac{cc_r(G)}{CT_r(G)}
$$

over all rooted graphs $G$ on $n$ vertices.

This conjecture concerns extremal comparisons between cover cost and cover time, which can have substantially different orders of magnitude depending on the graph. A proof would provide an extremal bound for using cover cost to estimate cover time; the source gives no resolution. 

323752 Let $\mathcal{H}^1$ and $\mathcal{H}^2$ be the word algebras used for multiple zeta values, with shuffle product denoted by $?$ and stuffle product by $*$. For an integer $n$, take words $w_1,w_0\in\mathcal{H}^1$ with $\operatorname{degree}(w_0)+\operatorname{degree}(w_1)=n$. **Kaneko–Noro–Tsurumaki conjecture.** To obtain all multiple zeta value relations of degree $n$, it is enough to take

w_1\in\mathcal{H}^2,\qquad w_0\in\{x_1,x_0x_1,x_0^2x_1,x_0x_1^2\},

with the stated degree condition. This conjecture gives a finite reduction of the regularization relations and is intended to control the number of equations needed to generate all relations in each degree. The supplied source gives no evidence of a resolution, so its status is left open.
