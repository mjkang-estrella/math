## Decisive published examples and exact bridge

Hyun Jin Kim, Whan-Hyuk Choi and Yoonjin Lee, *Construction of reversible self-dual codes*, **Finite Fields and Their Applications 67 (2020), 101714**, DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2020.101714. The published paper states an online publication date of **14 July 2020**, before the original conjecture's July 2022 submission.

The actual published PDF was downloaded from the authors' university, without using Elsevier/ScienceDirect: [Yonsei-hosted paper](https://yis.yonsei.ac.kr/liberaledu/activities/research.do?articleNo=143562&attachNo=123378&mode=download). Local source: `receipts/reversible2020.pdf`; extracted body: `bodies/reversible2020.txt`.

Section 2, printed p. 3, defines Aut(C) to be **all coordinate permutations preserving C**, exactly the target's PAut(C). It defines reversibility at length 2n by invariance under

\[
\tau=(1,2n)(2,2n-1)\cdots(n,n+1).
\]

**Theorem 5.1, printed p. 15**, asserts existence of at least nine pairwise inequivalent reversible binary self-dual codes with parameters [70,35,12], each having full automorphism group of size two. Its immediately following discussion repeats the full group order and lists their weight-enumerator parameters. Printed p. 16 gives four explicit matrices A for systematic generators [I_35 | A]. The remaining matrices are referenced to a supplement, which was not needed or fetched.

For any code in that theorem, reversibility puts the nonidentity permutation tau in PAut(C). At length 70, tau has exactly 35 disjoint transpositions and no fixed coordinate. As the full group has size two, PAut(C) = {1,tau}. Its subgroup generated by tau acts freely, so C is a quasi group code of length 70 = 2·35 > 2. This is precisely a counterexample to the universal 2022 arXiv Conjecture 4.9. There is no self-duality or small-distance restriction in that conjecture that could exclude it.

The 2020 theorem explicitly records the necessary properties; this investigation supplies only the elementary bridge to the later conjecture. It is not inference from an abstract-group realization, an assumed subgroup, or a title/snippet.

As a limited transcription check, `validate_matrices.py` extracts all four printed 35-by-35 matrices and checks AA^T = I and A = R A^T R over F2. All pass. Consequently [I | A] gives a dimension-35 self-dual binary code invariant under reversal. See `matrix-validation.json` and `bodies/published-A1.txt` through `published-A4.txt`. **The distance and full automorphism order were not independently recomputed here**; those are the read published theorem's assertions. No length-70 formal certificate is claimed.

A useful precise secondary account was found in Garcia-Claro–Tapia-Recillas, *Fq[G]-modules and G-invariant codes*, arXiv:1711.10671v3, introduction pp. 2–3. In normalized form, it reports the following Knapp–Schmid result: for a linear q-ary code C of length n > 6, if the permutation projection of its monomial automorphism group contains A_n, then C is equivalent to zero, the full space, the repetition code or its dual. The same passage says that the small-degree exceptions were classified and the Mathieu cases were investigated; it does not list their exact statements. This is a precise secondary theorem account, not a read original theorem.
