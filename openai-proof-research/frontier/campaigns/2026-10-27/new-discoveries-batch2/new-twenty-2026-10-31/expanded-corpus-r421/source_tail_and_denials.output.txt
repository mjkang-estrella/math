We have checked that Conjecture 1 is true up to n = 25. The algorithm
is easy to describe. For each subset A ⊆ Zn \ {0}, we choose a random
permutation of the elements of A. If that ordering does not yield distinct
partial sums, then we choose another random permutation. We repeat this
process until we find an ordering of A that gives distinct partial sums.
