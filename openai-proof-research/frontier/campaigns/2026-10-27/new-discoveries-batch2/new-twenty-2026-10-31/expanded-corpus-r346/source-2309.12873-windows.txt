This connection between Sidorenko’s conjecture and random Turán problems gives new lower bounds on $\mathrm{ex}(G_{n,p}^{r},F)$ whenever $s(F)>0$ , and further allows us to establish upper bounds for $s(F)$ whenever upper bounds for $\mathrm{ex}(G_{n,p}^{r},F)$ are known. Building on their work, we further establish a connection between Sidorenko’s conjecture and random Turán problems. Along the way, we obtain good estimations for how “far” certain hypergraphs $F$ are from being Sidorenko.
