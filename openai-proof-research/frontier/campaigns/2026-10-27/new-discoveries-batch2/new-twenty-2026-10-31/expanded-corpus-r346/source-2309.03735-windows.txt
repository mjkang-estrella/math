Looms appear naturally in the context of a conjecture of Gyárfás and Lehel on the covering number of cross-intersecting hypergraphs. The union of all edges in a maximal matching is a cover, and hence in an $r$ -uniform hypergraph  $H$ , $\tau(H)\leqslant r\nu(H)$ .
