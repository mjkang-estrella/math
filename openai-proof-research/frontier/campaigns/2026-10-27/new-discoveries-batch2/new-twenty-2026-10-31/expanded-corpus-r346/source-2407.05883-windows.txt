They proved that Maximum Independent Set can be solved in quasi-polynomial time on graphs having no induced packing of $k$ cycles.
Lima et al. [27] obtained an algorithmic metatheorem for the problem of finding a maximum-weight sparse induced subgraph satisfying a fixed property expressible in counting monadic second-order logic (CMSO2).
This metatheorem implies that the Minimum Weight Feedback Vertex Set and Minimum Weight Odd Cycle Transversal can be solved in polynomial time on every class of $K_{1,t}$ -free graphs containing no induced packing of $k$ cycles.
