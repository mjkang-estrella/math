# Proof architecture and normalization

This round preserves all 92 inherited Lean source modules byte for byte. The archive comparison is in inherited-source-comparison.json. Original literal definitions, controls, finite-system equivalence, truth-table CNF generator, and RUP soundness are unchanged. Acceptance and mathematical novelty are for root to adjudicate; this lane claims no campaign credit.

Collineation.lean defines an incidence-preserving pair of point and line permutations, with explicit inverse laws and a permutation proof for the complete point enumeration. Its action on an infection is pullback along the point permutation. Permutation invariance of filtered-list length proves invariance of line infection counts. Incidence preservation and surjectivity of the line map then prove step_transport5. Induction proves rounds_transport5 for every natural round. Surjectivity of the point map proves preservation of fullness, hence percolation and TimeIs. To preserve Minimal, pull any proposed proper percolating subset back using the inverse point map; its forward transport is the original subset. This contradicts literal minimality against all proper subsets and all natural percolation times.

Noncollinear.lean proves that a line is fixed under threshold 5: every different line intersects it in exactly one point, below threshold. Every line omits some point. The inherited separator theorem consequently shows that every percolating infection has a point outside each line. Choose an infected point, then an infected point outside a line through it, and then an infected point outside their unique joining line. The three points are noncollinear. Unique joins are deduced from the inherited plane axiom by expressing its filtered enumeration as a singleton. The initial large finite-check attempt hit the RSS watchdog and was replaced by this argument, without changing a resource limit.

The explicit family has 86 collineations. The 31 CollF modules each send point 0 to the indexed point. The 30 CollG modules fix point 0 and send point 25 to any other point. The 25 CollH modules fix 0 and 25 and send point 30 to any point outside their joining line. Each module independently proves both inverse laws, the point enumeration permutation, and all incidence equalities by kernel reduction. Tables were generated from invertible three-by-three matrices over residues modulo 5, but neither the generator nor matrix algebra is trusted: the finite permutation and incidence assertions themselves are Lean theorems. CollineationFamily.lean checks the three required mapping specifications.

Normalize.lean selects a noncollinear triple in a witness, pulls back by a member of family F, then G, then H, and obtains an infection containing exactly the required fixed triple (other initially infected points are permitted). Minimality and time 2 are preserved at each step. The inherited witness_satisfies_inputCNF5 applied to this transported witness gives satisfaction of the base formula. Its three infected points satisfy normalizedUnits5, literally [[(.a 0,false)],[(.a 25,false)],[(.a 30,false)]]. Here false is a forbidden value, so each literal is positive.

NormalizedExport.lean evaluates the actual Lean list normalizedCNF5 and its original variable numbering. An independent script compares its complete DIMACS output byte for byte with the copied normalized.cnf. The match is 14,695 base clauses followed by units 1, 26, 31, over 1,085 variables. generator-correspondence.json records this check. This data comparison is not a substitute for the logical normalization theorem; both are supplied.

The normalized replay reuses inherited kernel-proved RUP soundness. Every selected input theorem proves membership in the actual generator, or reuses the byte-identical inherited theorem for that same input. The three units are proved members of normalizedUnits5. Each derived clause names only its actual hints and applies CNF.rup_sound with a kernel-reduced RUP check. The certificate is split into chained 500-addition modules, with the final remainder smaller than 500. Each exports a sound chunk and a sound accumulated prefix. No solver result or runtime evaluation is used to prove a clause. The source packet includes the exact normalized trace so another round can continue from any verified frontier.

Final verification status, actual replayed additions, module count, endpoint, axiom reports, and times are recorded in SUMMARY.md and results.json. Generated source alone is never counted as replayed progress. If the replay stops, only a theorem with an explicit frontier refutation hypothesis is delivered as the endpoint; the exact next retained step and LRAT ID are recorded. No unconditional theorem is claimed unless its final module passes both compiler and checker.

The final packet also includes a resource-bounded factorization for the next 500 additions. NDefs024 defines their literal clauses. Five NCheck024 modules separately prove the RUP Boolean equations for 100 clauses each. NReplay024 imports those checked equations and proves the 500 clause-satisfaction theorems using the unchanged general RUP soundness theorem. Thus a failure of the original aggregate 500-addition compilation at 120 CPU seconds was resolved by moving finite checks into helper modules, with no resource limit increase. The delivered normalized prefix has 12,500 additions; generated later drafts are not counted.
