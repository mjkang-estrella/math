Introduction In 1985, Kasteleyn posed the bunkbed conjecture, which then circulated as an intriguing problem in percolation theory (see [ 7 , Remark 5] ). Interest in the problem has waxed and waned over time, but, especially in recent years, the conjecture has attracted significant attention. A feature of the problem which makes it particularly appealing is its apparent simplicity and how, as has been noted by several authors, it appears to be intuitively clear.
