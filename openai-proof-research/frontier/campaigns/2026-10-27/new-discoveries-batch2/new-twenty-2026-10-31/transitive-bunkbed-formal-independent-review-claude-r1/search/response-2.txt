- **Counterexample exists:** Przybyłowski's arXiv paper (2506.22284, June 2025) constructs a simple acyclic directed graph for which the bunkbed conjecture fails, resolving conjectures posed by Leander and by Hollom. The paper constructs a simple acyclic directed graph for which the Bunkbed Conjecture is false.
- **Earlier open status:** An Emergent Mind summary of Hollom's 2024 paper listed the acyclic case as unresolved. That was before Przybyłowski's result, so it is outdated.
- **Background:** Hollom's earlier directed counterexample used multigraphs and contained many directed cycles, which is why it did not settle the acyclic question. Gladkov, Pak and Zimin's counterexample for the general (undirected) conjecture was built on Hollom's graph.
- **Related positive result:** A November 2025 arXiv note (2511.13589, Donderwinkel, Jorritsma, Perarnau) proves the undirected bunkbed conjecture for forests. It does not cover directed graphs. The same note cites Przybyłowski's result for the directed acyclic failure.
- **Open variant:** Hollom conjectured that a simple digraph (no multi-edges) should also violate the directed inequality under a fully directed model, and that remains a stated conjecture in the sources I found.
