- **Origin in Hollom's 2024 paper.** Hollom's "not robust to generalisation" paper (arXiv 2406.01790) considers generalisations to site percolation, to hypergraphs, and to directed graphs. A summary of that paper suggests that acyclic directed graphs are a restricted case where the conjecture's truth might still hold. That points toward the tournament question, though the search didn't confirm it.
- **Main bunkbed result.** Gladkov, Pak, and Zimin's 2024 paper (arXiv 2410.02545) disproved the original bunkbed conjecture for general graphs. It builds on an example of Hollom refuting the 3-uniform hypergraph version, but Hollom's example alone cannot disprove the conjecture because it cannot simulate a single 3-hyperedge using bond percolation.
