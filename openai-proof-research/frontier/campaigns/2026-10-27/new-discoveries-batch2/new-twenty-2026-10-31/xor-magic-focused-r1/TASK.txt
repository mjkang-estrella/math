2. Twisted lift (graph bundle) n -> n+2. Let M be multiplication by a primitive cube root of unity on F_4 = (Z_2)^2, so I + M + M^{-1} = 0. Take an open (closed) d-regular XOR-magic G with labels l_G and with a 2-factor (cycles of length >= 3); orient each cycle.
