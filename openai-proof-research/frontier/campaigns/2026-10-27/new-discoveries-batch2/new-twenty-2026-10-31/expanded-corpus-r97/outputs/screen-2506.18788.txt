3808: Our data set of small graphs (table 6) contains 208 151 graphs that are planar and 3-connected. They all have  \mathsf{N}_{2}(G)=1 , supporting Conjecture 1.13. Further evidence comes from Conjecture 1.15 (about the 3-sum), because already its special case (1.7) (the star-triangle identity) would imply Conjecture 1.13. This can be seen as follows:
3809: 
3810: 
3811: 
3812: 
3813: The proof of Steinitz’s theorem given in [27, §13.1] shows that every 3-connected planar simple graph  G_{0}  can be reduced to  G_{n}=K_{4}  by a suitable sequence of moves  G_{0}\mapsto G_{1}\mapsto G_{2}\mapsto\ldots  where each move consists of either a star-triangle operation followed by simplification of any parallel edges thus created (if any), or of a triangle-star operation followed by series reduction of any 2-valent vertices thus created (if any). The construction ensures that each  G_{i}  along the sequence is itself a 3-connected, planar, simple graph.
