4445: In his Chapter [34, Section 9-7.4], Reading proposed a generalization of the shard intersection order for any congruence uniform lattice  \mathscr{L}=(L,\leq) . This is an alternate order structure on  L  which was coined the name core label order by Mühle in [24]. If  \mathscr{L}  is the poset of regions of the Coxeter hyperplane arrangement of  W  one recovers the shard intersection order of  W . If  \mathscr{L}  is the classical Tamari lattice  \Tam(n)  then its core label order is the classical lattice of noncrossing partition of  [n] .
4446: In this section we recall the definition of the core label order, and propose the core label order of a framing lattice as the natural analog of the poset of noncrossing partitions for framing lattices.

4451: Let  \mathscr{L}=(L,\leq)  be a finite congruence uniform lattice.
4452: For a cover relation  u\prec v  in  L  let  cg(u,v)  be the finest lattice congruence of  \mathscr{L}  in which  u  and  v  are equivalent. These are precisely the join irreducible lattice congruences of  \mathscr{L} . However, different covering pairs  u\prec v  may lead to the same lattice congruence. In fact, if  \mathscr{L}  is congruence uniform then the map  \mathfrak{j}\rightarrow cg(\mathfrak{j}_{*},\mathfrak{j})  is a bijection between join irreducible elements and join irreducible lattice congruences of  \mathscr{L} , see [34, Section 9-7.4]. In other words, for every covering pair  u\prec v  there is a unique join irreducible element  \mathfrak{j}  such that  cg(u,v)=cg(\mathfrak{j}_{*},\mathfrak{j}) . We denote this unique  \mathfrak{j}  as  \mathfrak{j}_{cg(u,v)} . We label the edges  u\prec v  of the Hasse diagram of  \mathscr{L}  by their corresponding join irreducible elements  \mathfrak{j}_{cg(u,v)} .
