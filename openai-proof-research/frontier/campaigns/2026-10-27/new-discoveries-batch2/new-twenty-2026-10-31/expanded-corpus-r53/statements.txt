### 359713 Liu–Morris–Prince logarithmic-factor conjecture for multicolour connectivity
Let $f(n,r,s)$ be the smallest integer such that every $r$-edge-colouring of the complete graph $K_n$ contains a set of at least $f(n,r,s)$ vertices whose induced subgraph is connected using at most $s$ colours. Here $r$ is the number of colours, $s$ is the allowed number of colours, and $n$ is sufficiently large.

**Logarithmic-factor conjecture.** For any

$$
1\leq s\leq \frac{\sqrt r}{\log r}
$$

and $n$ large enough,

$$
f(n,r,s)=\frac{s^2(\log r)^{1-o_r(1)}}{r}\cdot n.
$$

The conjecture asks whether the logarithmic factor in the known lower bound is necessary throughout this range of $s$. It is motivated by the case $s\approx \log\log r$, where the authors show that a $(\log r)^{1-o_r(1)}$ factor is necessary; the general range stated above remains open.

### 359719 The Hochbaum–Nishizeki–Shmoys conjecture on polynomial-time edge coloring
Let $G$ be a multigraph, let $\Delta(G)$ denote its maximum degree, and let

$$
\Gamma(G):=\max_{H\subseteq G}\left\lceil\frac{|E(H)|}{\left\lfloor\frac{1}{2}|V(H)|\right\rfloor}\right\rceil
$$

be its density. A proper edge-coloring assigns colors to the edges so that adjacent edges receive different colors.

**Hochbaum–Nishizeki–Shmoys conjecture.** There exists a polynomial-time algorithm that finds a proper edge-coloring of every multigraph $G$ using $\max\\{\Delta(G)+1,\Gamma(G)\\}$ colors.

The paper states that its main theorem confirms this conjecture by providing such an algorithm. Therefore the conjecture is solved.
