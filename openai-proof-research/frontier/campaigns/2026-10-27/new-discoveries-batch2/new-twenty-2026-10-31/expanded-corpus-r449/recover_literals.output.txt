338284 Let $G$ and $H$ be connected graphs, let $G\boxtimes H$ denote their strong product, and let $\pi(G)$ denote the pebbling number of a graph. The notation $|G|$ and $|H|$ denotes the numbers of vertices of $G$ and $H$, respectively.

**Strong-product pebbling conjecture.**
For any connected graphs $G$ and $H$,

$$
\pi(G\boxtimes H)\leq \max\left\{\frac{1}{2}\pi(G)\pi(H),\ |G||H|\right\}+2.
$$

This is proposed as a variation of Graham's conjecture for strong products. The paper gives motivating bounds and an example showing that a simpler factor-$\frac12$ bound does not hold, while this sharper variation remains unresolved in the source.
339649 Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices with no isolated edges, and let $\chi_{r\text{-vsdt}}(G)$ denote its $r$-strongly vertex-distinguishing total chromatic number.

**Vertex-distinguishing total coloring conjecture.**

$$
\chi_{r\text{-vsdt}}(G)\le n+\lceil\log_{2}n\rceil+1
$$
\nand equality holds if $n=2^{k}-2$.

This conjecture proposes a general upper bound based on the number of vertices; the equality condition is motivated by the known values for complete graphs. Its resolution is not supplied in the source.
341595 Let the edges of the complete graph on vertex set $[n]$ be colored with three colors I, II, and III. For a triangle with vertices $a<b<c$, consider the pattern in which $\{a,b\}$ has color I, $\{b,c\}$ has color II, and $\{a,c\}$ has color III. **Erdős–Hajnal's conjecture.** The number of triangles having this color pattern is at most $g_3(n)$. This conjecture is relevant because, for $k=3$, Erdős and Hajnal proved that it would imply their first growth-threshold conjecture. The source does not state a resolution of this three-color triangle bound.
343167 Let $[m]_q=(q^m-1)/(q-1)$ denote the $q$-analog of $m$. A graph is **Singer-graceful** when it satisfies the graceful-labeling condition associated with the Singer difference set. For integers $v$ and $i$ with $1\leq i\leq [v-2]_q$, let $\Gamma$ be a regular graph of order $[v-1]_q$ and degree $qi$. **Singer graceful-graph conjecture.** The graph $\Gamma$ is Singer-graceful, and consequently there exists a cyclic $2$-$(v,\Gamma,i)$ design over $\mathbb{F}_q$. This is presented as a specialization of the preceding difference-set conjecture; it would produce an infinite family of non-trivial graph decompositions over finite fields, and the source gives no resolution.
343251 A graph is **claw-free** if it has no induced subgraph isomorphic to $K_{1,3}$. A graph is **2-connected** if it is connected and remains connected after deleting any one vertex. A **Tutte cycle** is a cycle $C$ such that every component of $G-V(C)$ has at most three neighbours on $C$.

**Jackson's conjecture.** Every $2$-connected claw-free graph has a Tutte cycle.

This is presented as an equivalent formulation of Thomassen's open conjecture on Hamilton cycles in 4-connected line graphs. The equivalence is obtained using the closure technique, but the conjecture itself remains unresolved.
343642 Let $G$ be a graph, and let $\Lambda G$ be its tangle compactification. A **TST** of $\Lambda G$ is a spanning standard subspace of $\Lambda G$ that is also a TST-like subcontinuum. **Existence conjecture.** If $G$ is any graph, then $\Lambda G$ admits a TST.

This asks whether every graph's tangle compactification contains a spanning continuum with the prescribed separation-order structure. The supplied passage gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.
344196 For graphs $H$ and $H'$ obtained from $H$ by removing a single vertex, let $r(H)$ and $r(H')$ denote their two-color Ramsey numbers.

**Vertex-deletion Ramsey conjecture.** There is an absolute constant $c$ such that

$$
r(H)\leq c\cdot r(H').
$$

The conjecture is presented as a way to remove the logarithmic factor in the concentration result for random-graph Ramsey numbers; its status is not resolved in the supplied text.
344604 Let $M_1=(S,\mathcal{I}_1)$ and $M_2=(S,\mathcal{I}_2)$ be loopless matroids. Write $\rchi(M_i)$ for the coloring number of $M_i$, namely the least number of independent sets into which $S$ can be partitioned. **Aharoni–Berger conjecture.** The ground set $S$ can be partitioned into

$$
\max\{\rchi(M_1),\rchi(M_2)\}+1
$$

common independent sets. This would give the best possible upper bound, as described in the source, for covering the ground set by common independent sets; the supplied text does not state a resolution.
344760 Let $S\subset\mathbb{R}^2$ be a three-point set, let $s_0\in S$, and call a copy of $(S,s_0)$ **almost-monochromatic** if the points corresponding to $S\setminus\{s_0\}$ have one colour while the point corresponding to $s_0$ has a different colour.

**Erdős et al.'s conjecture.** There is a non-monochromatic colouring of $\mathbb{R}^2$ that contains no almost-monochromatic copy congruent to $(S,s_0)$ if and only if $S$ is collinear and $s_0$ is not an extreme point of $S$.

This conjecture characterizes the three-point configurations for which a non-monochromatic colouring can avoid almost-monochromatic congruent copies. The source attributes it to Erdős, Graham, Montgomery, Rothschild, Spencer and Straus; no resolution is given.
345344 Let $f\colon(0,1]\longrightarrow\mathds R$ be the Ramsey\e1Tur\e1n density function. For $k\ge2$, define

$$
f_k(\alpha)=k(k-1)-2k(3k-4)\alpha+(3k-4)(3k-1)\alpha^2.
$$

**Ramsey\e1Tur\e1n density formula.** The function $f$ is given by

$$
f(\alpha)=\begin{cases}
\alpha,&\alpha\le\frac13,\\
f_k(\alpha),&\frac{k}{3k-1}\le\alpha<\frac{k-1}{3k-4}\text{ for some }k\ge2,\\
\frac12,&\frac12\le\alpha\le1.
\end{cases}
$$

This is the precise prediction for the density function that would follow from the preceding extremal conjecture. Thus its status is tied to the still-open range $\alpha\in(\frac13,\frac38)$, while the portions corresponding to the established cases are known.
346209 Let $n$, $m$, and $b4$ be integers with $n > b4$. Let $\mathcal G(n,m,b4)$ denote the set of bipartite graphs with parts of sizes $n$ and $m$ in which every vertex in the part of size $n$ has degree at least $b4$. **Jackson's conjecture.** If a graph $G \in \mathcal G(n,m,b4)$ is $2$-connected and satisfies

$$
m\leq \left\lfloor \frac{2(n-\alpha)}{b4-1-\alpha} \right\rfloor (b4-2) + 1,
$$

where $\alpha = 1$ if $b4$ is even and $\alpha = 0$ if $b4$ is odd, then $G$ contains a cycle of length at least $2\min(n,b4)$. This conjecture remains open.
346439 Let $H$ be a finite group. A **gain-graphic matroid** is a frame matroid obtained from a graph with edge gains in $H$, and a class is **well-quasi-ordered** when it has neither an infinite strict descending sequence nor an infinite antichain under the relevant minor relation. **Finite-group gain-graphic well-quasi-order conjecture.** The class of $H$-gain graphic matroids is well-quasi-ordered. If true, every minor-closed class of $H$-gain-graphic matroids would have at most finitely many excluded minors that are themselves $H$-gain-graphic. The source says this is widely believed and expected to be approachable, at least for abelian $H$, while the corresponding assertion fails for infinite groups.
346690 Let $A_{s,k}=\{s,2s,\ldots,ks\}$ be a homogeneous arithmetic progression, and let an $h$-majority coloring be a red-blue coloring $c$ satisfying

$$
c(s)\sum_{j=1}^{k}c(js)\leqslant h.
$$

**Majority-coloring conjecture.** There exists a constant $h$ and a red-blue coloring of $\mathbb N$ which is $h$-majority on every homogeneous arithmetic progression $A_{s,k}$. This is a relaxed version of the Erdős Discrepancy Problem; the source does not give a resolution.
348303 Let $\Gamma$ be a zillion graph with $\omega$ odd cycles and $\mu$ even cycles, and let $|E(\Gamma)|$ denote its number of edges. **Edge-size conjecture.** There exist a quadratic polynomial $f\in\mathbb{Z}[x]$ and a linear polynomial $g\in\mathbb{Z}[x]$ such that $\Gamma$ is graceful whenever

$$
|E(\Gamma)|\geq f(\omega)+g(\mu).
$$

Kotzig's lower bound, as adapted in the source, shows that a zillion graph with $\omega$ odd cycles must satisfy a quadratic lower bound in its number of edges for gracefulness. The proposed conjecture gives a quadratic contribution from odd cycles and a linear contribution from even cycles, and remains open.
348319 Let $(G,-)$ be a signed graph whose underlying graph is $G$, with every edge negative, and let $(K_{k+1},-)$ denote the complete graph on $k+1$ vertices with every edge negative. A signed graph $(H,\pi)$ is a **minor** of $(G,\sigma)$ if it is obtained by deleting vertices or edges, contracting positive edges, and switching. **Odd-Hadwiger conjecture.** If $(G,-)$ has no $(K_{k+1},-)$-minor, then

$$
\chi_c(G,+)\leq k.
$$

This conjecture strengthens the Hadwiger conjecture and generalizes Catlin's result, whose case $k=3$ is stated in the source. Its status is not resolved in the supplied material.
348635 Let $G$ be a properly edge-colored multigraph with $2n-t_n$ colors and exactly $n$ edges of each color, where

$$
t_n=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }n\text{ is even},\\1&\text{if }n\text{ is odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

**Barát–Gyárfás–Sárközy conjecture.** Then $G$ has a rainbow matching using $n$ colors.

The conjecture concerns the number of colors needed when every color class has exactly $n$ edges. The source cites best-known partial results, but does not state a resolution of the conjecture.
351370 Let $k\geq2$ be a fixed positive integer and let $G$ be a graph. **$(k)$-total coloring conjecture.**

$$
\chi”(G)\leq\Delta(G)+k.
$$

This is presented as a weaker version of the total coloring conjecture obtained by relaxing the upper bound. The case $k=2$ is the original total coloring conjecture, while larger $k$ give weaker assertions; the supplied text does not indicate a resolution of this general formulation.
352639 Let $G$ be a graph without isolated edges. An edge-coloring is **neighbor-sum-distinguishing** if any two neighboring vertices have different sums of the colors on their incident edges. **1-2-3 Conjecture.** Every graph $G$ without isolated edges admits a neighbor-sum-distinguishing edge-coloring with the colors $\{1,2,3\}$. The conjecture asks whether three colors always suffice; the best known general upper bound is five colors, so the conjecture remains open.
368430 Let $(G,-)$ be the signed graph obtained from a graph $G$ by assigning a negative sign to every edge, and let $(K_t,-)$ be the all-negative signed complete graph. A signed graph minor is obtained by vertex deletion, edge deletion, contraction of positive edges, and switching at vertices. **Odd Hadwiger's conjecture.** If $(G,-)$ is $(K_t,-)$-minor-free, then

$$
\chi(G)\leq t-1.
$$

This is a strengthening of Hadwiger's conjecture introduced independently by Gerards and Seymour. The cases $t=3$ and $t=4$ are known, the case $t=5$ implies the four-color theorem, and all remaining cases are open according to the source.
376136 Let $G$ be a finite simple undirected graph with $n$ vertices, adjacency eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$, and positive and negative square energies

$$
s^+(G):=\sum_{\lambda_i>0}\lambda_i^2,\qquad s^-(G):=\sum_{\lambda_i<0}\lambda_i^2.
$$

**Elphick–Farber–Goldberg–Wocjan conjecture.** Every connected graph $G$ on $n$ vertices satisfies

$$
\min\{s^+(G),s^-(G)\}\ge n-1.
$$

The paper states that this conjecture is proved using a framework based on doubly nonnegative matrix relaxations of positive semidefinite matrix Hadamard squares.
