## 381028: two symbolic routes

N=prod_{j=1}^k(x_n-x_j) prod_{i=k+1}^{n-1}(x_i-x_1).
The shifted northeast pattern consists of pairs (i,j) with j>=i+2, NOT the ordinary Vandermonde on x2,...,xn. Thus f=chi_J=Delta*N/D as a polynomial. Degree f=degree Delta. Its alternation Alt(f)=sum_sigma sign(sigma)*sigma(f) is an alternating polynomial of the minimal alternating degree, hence a constant times Delta: each xi-xj divides it, and their product has the full degree. Since Delta=sum_sigma sign(sigma) prod_i x_sigma(i)^(n-i), comparison of the coefficient of prod_i xi^(n-i) shows that constant equals the source monomial-orthonormal inner product <f,Delta>. Therefore exactly
<f,Delta>=sum_sigma sigma(N/D).

## 316628: two construction routes

Actual final BHNP2009 pp439-443 proof of Theorem3.2, product Theorem3.5, pyramid Theorem3.8 and free-sum Theorem3.10 read; Conjecture5.6 p450 read. A centrally symmetric face F, translated by one of its vertices to include identity e, is the minimal face through e and its antipode g: a face containing both antipodes contains their midpoint, which is in relintF and therefore contains F. Theorem3.2 makes its vertices exactly the subelements of g belonging to G. Such subelements commute since they are products of disjoint cycles of g. Replace G by their generated subgroup H; restricting P(G) to P(H) preserves this face and its vertices. Thus arbitrary centrally symmetric faces admit a binary slice in a product of cyclic groups, V=H intersect {0,1}^r, with all-ones element g in H. This is a reduction, not a free-sum realization.

Route1: generalize the diagonal doubling construction of Theorem3.10. In the exponent3 setting, off-diagonal element (hg,h) can have both entries subelements of g only for h=e: any overlapping selected cycle squares to exponent2, excluded from binary exponents. This makes exactly two apices and the diagonal copy of the old face. For even order cycles this fails. If g has order2, every binary overlap still has exponent0 or1, so many off-diagonal elements survive. Source theorem starts with independent3-cycles and iterates only elementary abelian3-groups. It does NOT convert an arbitrary input face to that setting. UNPAID: arbitrary-face encoding with the required no-overlap rigidity, and a selector combining two arbitrary faces rather than adding a single interval.

Route2: replace a binary cyclic subgroup slice by its span over an odd prime field, aiming to use the preceding construction. This does not preserve the allowed binary vertices. Already the even-weight subgroup of C2^4 contains every vector e_i+e_j; the same vectors span all F3^4, since (e1+e2)+(e1+e3)-(e2+e3)=2e1 and2 is invertible. Thus the odd-characteristic slice becomes the entire four-cube whereas the original binary even-weight slice has only even-weight vertices. This is a symbolic obstruction to this conversion, not a refutation of Conjecture5.6. UNPAID: auxiliary coordinate construction that enforces every modular relation with unique lifts and preserves the exact face lattice; merely projecting a larger polytope is insufficient.

Cartesian products provide products, and pyramids provide joins with a point; neither creates the central free sum because the factor centers are not vertices of a permutation polytope. ## 316627: transpose route, hard exponent2 branch, then actual public resolution

If some g^2!=e, T is nonidentity and cannot equal any left translation (only identity translation fixes e). Therefore affine group has more than |G| for exponent>2.
