122: Definition 2.2. For an n-element poset P , a labeling is called tangled if it has sorting time n − 1. A
123: labeling is called quasi-tangled if it has sorting time n − 2. We also say a labeling L is k-promotion-
124: sortable (or just k-sortable) if ∂ k (L) ∈ L(P ). We call 1-sortable labelings sortable.
125: 
126:     We let the set of all tangled labelings of P be denoted by T (P ); we denote the set of all sortable
127: labelings by Σ(P ). If P is a poset, L a labeling of P , and γ ∈ N, we also will frequently use the
128: shorthand Lγ to denote ∂ γ (L). Note that L0 = L.

1207:           10. Partitions of a q-matroid into prime-free minors
1208:    The correct numbers of prime-free minors of each length and activities can be
1209: obtained by the conversion process we have been using to pass from the rank gen-
1210: erating function to the Tutte polynomial. It seemed for a while that any maximal
1211: such partition (maximal in the lattice of partitions into minors) in which all minors
1212: are prime-free, would be a partition leading to the Tutte polynomial. This is false,
1213: as the example below will indicate. It may still be true that any partition into
1214: a minimal number of prime-free minors will lead to the Tutte polynomial. (That
1215: remains to be proven.) There may, however, be maximal partitions (that is, in
1216: which no two partition parts can be merged) that do not have a minimal number
1217: of parts, as the following example, on the uniform q-matroid U2,1 , shows.
1218:    The bottom flat b will be clopen in a minor of length two, with internal activity
1219: 2. The top flat t will be clopen in a minor of length 1, with external activity 1.

1221: prime-free minors will form a maximal covering of the graph on the left of Figure 10
1222: by disjoint edges. There are two such maximal coverings, shown center and right.
1223: But the choice on the right has a smaller number of partition parts, so it is maximal
1224: in the strong sense, even though it cannot be formed by merging two vertices of
1225: the center subgraph into an edge.
