## 347515 — subcubic bound, integrality and strengthened induction
Actual source 2008.01863v3 assumes finite simple undirected graphs. gamma is minimum cardinality of a maximal matching. Theorem 3 already proves gamma<=(4n-m)/6 for every connected noncubic graph. Route A: for a cubic graph m=3n/2, n even, and Theorem 4 gives gamma<=5n/12+1/3. As gamma is integral, this implies gamma<=5n/12 whenever n is 0,6,8,10 mod12. For n=2 mod12 the fractional part of 5n/12 is 5/6, and for n=4 mod12 it is 2/3. The extra 1/3 permits the next integer in both cases; integrality cannot close them. K4 accounts for n=4 but does not remove n=16,28,..., and K3,3 is excluded separately. Exact unpaid classes: connected cubic graphs other than the two exceptions with n=2 or4 mod12. This complete bridge proves only the stated partial classes and receives zero credit.

Route B: remove adjacent u,v. Every maximal matching M' in G-{u,v} extends by uv, since u,v are matched and all other edges have both ends in the residual graph or an end u,v. Here delta n=2, delta m=5, so the uncorrected potential (4n-m)/6 gains only 1/2 against one added edge. If u,v share a neighbor, that neighbor becomes a leaf and the -n_1/6 correction gains at least 1/6, still leaving a 1/3 deficit. The source pays exactly this with its +I/3 correction. For triangle-free bridgeless G the source's Lemma8 supplies a replacement edge ab making residual components noncubic. Deleting u,v and adding ab yields delta n=2, delta m=4, hence uncorrected gain 2/3. If ab is absent in M', add uv. If present, replace ab by ua,vb. Both completions cost one edge, leaving precisely 1/3 unpaid. Therefore merely copying the source induction with I removed fails in the final cubic step.

Further exchange attempt: to save an edge in the replacement case, keep only ua after deleting ab. Then v and b may both be unmatched, and vb is an uncovered augmenting edge, so maximality fails. An alternating exchange would have to match v or b elsewhere without increasing cardinality and without exposing an edge between two other unmatched vertices. No such universal exchange lemma was proved. Exact unpaid lemma: a replacement-edge/residual-matching choice whose completion saves the deficit, or an independent structural reduction for the two outstanding order classes. Connected cubic graphs with bridges also require reduction; Lemma8 cannot silently cover them.

Strongest significance case: the additive 1/3 loss sits at the exact difficult cubic step, and closing it uniformly would settle the source conjecture.
