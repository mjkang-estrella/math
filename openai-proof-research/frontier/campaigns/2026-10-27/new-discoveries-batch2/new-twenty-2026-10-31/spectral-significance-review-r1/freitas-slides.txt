Cauchy-Davenport Theorem
Let p be a prime, S, T ⊆ Zp . Then

                        |S + T | ≥ min{p, |S| + |T | − 1}.

Using linear algebra (Dias da Silva and Hamidoune, 1990):
    Take DS , DT diagonal matrices with the elements of S and T on the
    diagonal.
    Notice that |S + T | is the size of the spectrum of
    M := (DS ⊗ I ) ⊕ (I ⊗ DT ).
    This is also the degree of the minimal polynomial of M, since M is
    diagonalizable.
    This in turn is given by max{dim <w , Mw , M 2 w , . . .> : w ∈ Cn }.
For S + T 6= Zp , one finds w for which this dim is at least |S| + |T | − 1.

Erdős-Heilbronn

Erdős-Heilbronn Conjecture, 1964
Let ∅ 6= A ⊆ Zp , for a prime p. Let 2ˆA := {a + b : a, b ∈ A, a 6= b}.Then

                            |2ˆA| ≥ min{p, 2|A| − 3}.

Proved in 1994 by Dias da Silva and Hamidoune: if A ⊆ F ,

                        |nˆA| ≥ min{p(F ), n|A| − n2 + 1}

where p(F ) = p if p is the characteristic of F , or p(F ) = ∞ if the
characteristic is zero.
Also used techniques of multilinear algebra.
Extensions: Noga Alon, M. Nathanson and I. Ruzsa in 1996, and others.

Erdős-Szemerédi



Erdős-Szemerédi Conjecture
No A ⊆ N will yield values of |A + A| and |A · A| that are both “small”.
Meaning: for A ⊂ N a finite set, we have that for all δ < 1,

                        max{|A + A|, |A · A|}  |A|1+δ .

Erdős-Szemerédi Conjecture
No A ⊆ N will yield values of |A + A| and |A · A| that are both “small”.
Meaning: for A ⊂ N a finite set, we have that for all δ < 1,

                        max{|A + A|, |A · A|}  |A|1+δ .

Best δ so far: Solymosi, 2009, δ = 1/3.
|A + A| is smallest for arithmetic progressions, |A.A| is smallest for
geometric progressions.

Proposition (B. Schwartz, 1964)
Let M = [mij ] ∈ (R+ )n×n and let v = (v1 , . . . , vn ) be its Perron vector.
Suppose that for some indices r , s, t, with s < t,

                          (mrs − mrt )(vs − vt ) < 0.

Then the matrix obtained from M exchanging entries mrs and mrt has a
greater spectral radius than M.
