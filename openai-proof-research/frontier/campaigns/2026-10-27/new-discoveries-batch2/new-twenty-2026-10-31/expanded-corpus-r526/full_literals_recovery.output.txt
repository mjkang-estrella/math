
ID 357983 The generic linear-form reduction conjecture for subspaces without powers 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02332 
 Let $A={\mathbb{C}}[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ be a polynomial ring, let $A_d$ denote its degree-$d$ component, and let $W\subseteq A_d$ be a $k$-dimensional subspace with $k\le d-1$ and $k\le n-1$, where $n\ge3$. Write $\overline W$ for the image of $W$ after the relevant generic linear-form reduction. **Generic linear-form reduction conjecture.** If $W$ contains no $d$-th power of a linear form, then for a generic linear form $l\in A_1$, either $\overline W$ contains no $d$-th power of a linear form, or $n=k+1$ and $W=L_1^{d-1}{\mathbb{C}}[L_2,\dots,L_{k+1}]_1$ for some basis $L_1,\dots,L_{k+1}$ of $A(k+1)_1$. This proposed reduction would sharpen the preceding reduction to $2k$ variables to $k+1$ variables; the surrounding text presents it as likely and notes that only one counterexample is known, with small monomial cases checked computationally.

ID 382154 Lascoux expansion conjecture for symplectic involution Grothendieck polynomials 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04226 
 Let $I^{\mathsf{fpf}}_\infty$ denote the fixed-point-free involutions, let $\mathfrak G^{\mathsf{Sp}}_z$ be the corresponding symplectic involution Grothendieck polynomial, and let $D^{\mathsf{Sp}}(\alpha)$ and $D^{\mathsf{Sp}}(z)$ denote the associated symplectic diagrams. **Lascoux expansion conjecture.** For every $z\in I^{\mathsf{fpf}}_\infty$, there is a set $\mathcal Z(z)$ of skew-symmetric weak compositions such that

$$
\mathfrak G^{\mathsf{Sp}}_z=\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal Z(z)}\beta^{|D^{\mathsf{Sp}}(\alpha)|-|D^{\mathsf{Sp}}(z)|}L^\mathsf{P}_\alpha,
$$

so the coefficients belong to $\{\beta^n:n\in\mathbb N\}$. This generalizes the P-key expansion conjecture and was checked computationally through size $n\leq8$; no general resolution is given.

ID 333275 Embedded components in powers of non-complete-intersection curves 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.00935 
 Let $X\subset\mathbb{P}^3$ be a reduced and unmixed subvariety of codimension $2$, and let $I_X$ be its ideal. Suppose there is a point $P\in X$ such that the localization of $I_X$ at $P$ is not a complete intersection. **Embedded-component conjecture.** For every integer $m\geq 2$, the saturation of $I_X^m$ has an embedded component at $P$. In particular,

$$
I_X^{(m)}\neq I_X^m\qquad\text{for all }m\geq 2.
$$

This conjecture concerns the converse to the implication that the local complete intersection property prevents powers from acquiring non-irrelevant embedded components. It is supported by examples and computer experiments, but the stated general assertion is not resolved in the supplied source.

ID 326290 The finite-field Furstenberg set lower-bound conjecture 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.2372 
 Assume $n\geq 2$, $0\leq\beta\leq 1$, and $p$ is prime. Let $S\subseteq\mathbb{F}_p^n$ be a subset such that for every direction there is a line $l$ in that direction satisfying

$$
|l\cap S|\geq p^{\beta}.
$$

**Finite-field Furstenberg set conjecture.** Then

$$
|S|\gtrsim q^{\frac{n-1}{2}+\frac{n+1}{2}\beta}.
$$

The preceding construction shows that this exponent is attained up to constants in the paper's examples, so the conjecture asserts sharpness for general prime fields. The statement contains the source's notation $q$ although the hypotheses specify the prime field $\mathbb{F}_p$; this should be checked against the original definition of $q$.

ID 361156 Conjecture on unipotent tensor squares for GL(n,q) 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09157 
 Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $\mu=(\mu_1,\mu_2,\dots)$ be a partition of $n$, and let $\tau$ be a partition of $n$. For triples of partitions define

$$
U_{(\mu,\mu,\tau)}(q):=\left\langle\mathcal{U}^{\mu}\otimes\mathcal{U}^{\mu}\otimes\mathcal{U}^{\tau},1\right\rangle_{{\rm GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)},
$$

where $\mathcal{U}^{\lambda}$ denotes the unipotent character of ${\rm GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ indexed by $\lambda$. **The unipotent tensor-square conjecture.** (i) $U_{(\mu,\mu,\tau)}(q)\neq 0$ for every partition $\tau$ of $n$ if and only if $\mu_1\leq\lceil n/2\rceil$. (ii) If $\mu_1>\lceil n/2\rceil$, then

$$
U_{(\mu,\mu,(1^n))}(q)=0.
$$

This gives a conjectural necessary and sufficient condition for a unipotent character's tensor square to contain every unipotent character. The claim is verified experimentally for $n\leq 8$; the paper proves the staircase case but leaves the general criterion open.

ID 358998 Nonexistence conjecture for prime $v$-palindromes 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00770 
 Let $v\colon\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{Z}$ be the additive function defined by $v(p)=p$ for primes $p$ and $v(p^\alpha)=p+\alpha$ for prime powers $p^\alpha$ with $\alpha\geq2$. For integers $n\geq1$ and $b\geq2$, call $n$ a **$v$-palindrome in base** $b$ if $b\nmid n$, $n\neq r_b(n)$, and $v(n)=v(r_b(n))$, where $r_b(n)$ is the integer obtained by reversing the base-$b$ digits of $n$; in base $10$, call it a $v$-palindrome. **Nonexistence conjecture for prime $v$-palindromes.** There are no prime $v$-palindromes. The claim is motivated by extensive computer calculations and complements known infinite families of composite $v$-palindromes; whether any prime examples exist remains open.

ID 366363 Transition-system conjecture for symmetric matrix Schubert varieties 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17320 
 Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $\boldsymbol{\rm Mat}^{\mathrm{sym}}_{n\times n}$ be the space of symmetric $n\times n$ matrices, and for $w\in\boldsymbol{\rm Mat}^{\mathrm{sym}}_{n\times n}$ let $\mathsf{MSV}^{\mathrm{sym}}_w$ be the corresponding symmetric matrix Schubert variety, with ideals $I(\mathsf{MSV}^{\mathrm{sym}}_w)$ and $J^{\mathrm{sym}}_w$. **Transition-system conjecture.** There exists a transition system containing

$$
\left\{\left(I(\mathsf{MSV}^{\mathrm{sym}}_w),J^{\mathrm{sym}}_w\right):w\in\boldsymbol{\rm Mat}^{\mathrm{sym}}_{n\times n}\right\}.
$$

The naive family is too small because it need not even be a partial transition system, and non-radical ideals are required. The conjecture is verified computationally for $n\leq4$, while its validity in general is open.

ID 333496 Conjecture on negabent monomials of Niho type 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08952 
 Let $n=2m$ and let $d=r(2^m-1)+1$, where $2\leq r\leq 2^{m-1}+1$. A function of the form ${{\rm Tr}}_1^n(\alpha x^d)$ over ${\mathbb F}_{2^n}$ is called **negabent** when it has the negabent property.

**Negabent Niho-exponent conjecture.** The function ${{\rm Tr}}_1^n(\alpha x^d)$ is negabent if and only if one of the following conditions holds:

1. $m$ is odd, $r=2^{m-2}+1\equiv \frac{3}{4}\pmod {2^m+1}$, and $\alpha\in {\mathbb F}_2$.
2. $r=2^{m-1}+1\equiv \frac{1}{2}\pmod {2^m+1}$, and $\alpha+\alpha^{2^m}\neq 1$.

The conjecture is based on computer experiments and proposes that, within the stated range of Niho exponents, these cubic and quadratic cases are exactly the cases yielding negabent monomials. The cited theorem for the second case is known, while the conjectured classification as a whole is not resolved in the supplied text.

ID 324262 The stronger parking-function bijection conjecture 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.2705 
 Let $\mathcal{A}_c$ denote the set of parking functions with composition $c$, let $\operatorname{dinv}(PF)$ and $\operatorname{ides}(PF)$ be the diagonal inversion number and inverse descent set, and let $\operatorname{diagword}(PF)$ be the diagonal word. **The stronger parking-function bijection conjecture.** For integers $b\leq a-1$, there exists a bijection

$$
f:\mathcal{A}_{[b,a]}\cup\mathcal{A}_{[a-1,b+1]}\longleftrightarrow\mathcal{A}_{[a,b]}\cup\mathcal{A}_{[b+1,a-1]}
$$

that increases $\operatorname{dinv}$ by exactly one, preserves $\operatorname{ides}$ and the diagonal word, and satisfies

$$
f(\mathcal{A}_{[a-1,b+1]})\subset\mathcal{A}_{[a,b]},\qquad f^{-1}(\mathcal{A}_{[b+1,a-1]})\subset\mathcal{A}_{[b,a]}.
$$

This strengthens the preceding conjecture by requiring preservation of the diagonal word and specifying the images of the two summands. It was motivated by computer data and theoretical considerations, and the source gives no resolution.

ID 364554 Nonnegativity conjecture for Schubert expansions of transparent diagrams 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16694 
 Let $D$ be a diagram, and write its flagged Schur character in the Schubert-polynomial basis as

$$
\mathfrak{S}_D=\sum_w c^w_D\,\mathfrak{S}_w,
$$

where the sum is over all permutations and $c^w_D$ are integers. A diagram is called **transparent** when it has the transparency property defined in the paper. **Nonnegativity conjecture.** When $D$ is transparent, the coefficients $c^w_D$ are nonnegative:

$$
c^w_D\geq 0
$$

for every permutation $w$. This conjecture predicts Schubert positivity for the characters of flagged Schur modules associated with transparent diagrams; the source motivates it through computer experimentation, while noting that related classes such as translucent and percentage-avoiding diagrams can have negative coefficients.

ID 341858 The factorization conjecture for the two-variable Bernoulli determinant 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05302 
 Let $r\geq 1$, and let $\bar F_{r,2}(x_1,x_2)$ be the determinant polynomial defined in the paper for $D=2$. **Factorization conjecture.** One has

$$
\bar F_{r,2}(x_1,x_2)=(-1)^{\binom{r}{2}} \frac{[1!2!\cdots(r-1)!]^3}{r!(r+1)!\cdots(2r-1)!}(x_2-x_1)^r \prod_{j=0}^{r-1}((x_2-x_1)^2-j^2)^{r-j}.
$$

The formula was supported by computer experiments and checked in the source for $r\leq 4$, but no proof for all $r$ is given.

ID 381480 MacDonald's conjecture on closest projections to nilpotent operators 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09635 
 Let $\mathcal{N}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ denote the set of nilpotent operators on $\mathbb{C}^n$, and let $\delta_n$ be the distance from the set of non-zero projections in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ to $\mathcal{N}(\mathbb{C}^n)$. Let $\nu_{1,n}$ denote the distance from the set of rank-one projections in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ to $\mathcal{N}(\mathbb{C}^n)$. MacDonald's conjecture states that the closest non-zero projections to $\mathcal{N}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ have rank one. **MacDonald's conjecture.**

$$
\delta_n=\nu_{1,n}=\frac{1}{2}\sec\left(\frac{\pi}{n+2}\right)\quad\text{for all}\quad n\in\mathbb{N}.
$$

The formula for $\nu_{1,n}$ was proved by MacDonald, and the conjectured equality is known for $n=3$ and has since been verified for $n=4$. It remains open for all $n\geq 5$.

ID 340019 Generalized powers-of-two positivity conjecture for conjugacy-action functions 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01416 
 Let $k,m\geq1$, and let $\psi_{2^r}$, $h_2$, and $p_1$ denote the symmetric functions used in the paper. **Generalized powers-of-two positivity conjecture.** The symmetric function

$$
\psi_{2^{k+m}}-h_2p_1^{2m-2}\psi_{2^k}
$$

is Schur-positive; consequently, for every integer $r$ with $1\leq r\leq m$, so is

$$
\psi_{2^{k+m}}-h_2^rp_1^{2m-2r}\psi_{2^k}.
$$

The assertion has been verified for $1\leq k,m\leq5$, while the general case remains open.

ID 340598 Polynomial Stillman bound conjecture for projective dimension 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00413 
 Let $R$ be a polynomial ring over a field, and let $I\subseteq R$ be an ideal generated by $n$ forms of degree at most $d$. Fix $d$, and let $C_d$ be a positive constant depending only on $d$.

**Polynomial Stillman-bound conjecture.** For all characteristics, the projective dimension of $R/I$ is at worst

$$
C_dn^d.
$$

This is presented as a conjectural polynomial Stillman bound. The paper notes that the analogous conjecture for ${}^\eta\!A$ is verified for $d\leq 3$, while the Stillman-bound conjecture is not known even for quadrics.

ID 337032 Full equivalence class criterion for ribbons 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.03011 
 Let $\alpha=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\ldots,\alpha_m)$ be a ribbon with $\alpha_1\geq\alpha_2\geq\cdots\geq\alpha_m$, where each $\alpha_i\geq2$ and $m\geq3$. Let $N_j$ denote the quantity defined in the necessary condition of the paper. **Full equivalence class conjecture.** The ribbon $\alpha$ has full equivalence class if and only if

$$
N_j<\sum_{i=j+1}^m\alpha_i-(m-j-2)
$$

for all $1\leq j\leq m-2$. This conjectures that the necessary condition established in the paper is also sufficient; it is known for ribbons with three or four rows and has been verified computationally in the stated cases for five, six, and seven rows.

ID 330998 The Cohen–Macaulayness conjecture for generalized power-sum varieties 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07485 
 Let $\boldsymbol{\tau}=(\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3,\ldots)$ be a partition, and let $X_\lambda$ denote the variety associated with the partition $\lambda$ in the algebra of generalized power sums. **Cohen–Macaulayness conjecture.** $X_\lambda$ is Cohen–Macaulay if and only if one of the following holds: (1) $\lambda=(m^{(r)},1^{(s)})$ with $r\geq 1$ and $m>s\geq 0$; (2) $\lambda=(2^{(r)},1^{(s)})$ with $r\geq 1$ and $s\geq 0$; (3) $\lambda=(2m,m^{(s)})$ with $m\geq 1$. ID 328016 The nonvanishing solution conjecture for invertible complex matrices 
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7126
