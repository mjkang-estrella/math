5     A support for Conjecture 1.12
In this section, we show that Conjecture 1.12 holds if we add the extra condition that
(G, σ) has no (K5 , −)-minor. A signed graph (H, π) is a minor of (G, σ) if it is ob-
tained from (G, σ) by a sequence of the following operations: Deleting vertices or edges,
contraction of positive edges, and switching.
Our claim concludes from some known results on the frustration index of (K5 , −)-minor-
free signed graphs.
