Theorem 9. If a graph G ∈ Gm,n is bipartite with parts V1 and V2 , |V1 | ≤ |V2 |,
then one of the following conditions holds:

1) |V1 | = |V2 | and n = m + 2 and m, n are even
2) |V1 | = m and |V2 | = n and n = m + 1
