                                                   Abstract. We formulate a categoriﬁcation of Robertson’s conjecture analo-
                                                   gous to the categorical graph minor conjecture of Miyata–Proudfoot–Ramos.
                                                   We show that these conjectures imply the existence of a ﬁnite list of atomic
                                                   graphs generating the homology of conﬁguration spaces of graphs—in ﬁxed
                                                   degree, with a ﬁxed number of particles, under topological embeddings. We
                                                   explain how the simplest case of our conjecture follows from work of Barter
                                                   and Proudfoot–Ramos, implying that the category of cographs is Noetherian,
                                                   a result of potential independent interest.

                                       7. Cographs
   As shown above in Theorem 4.5, the simplest case of the categorical Robertson
conjecture holds. In this section, we explore the consequences of this fact for a
certain class of graphs.
Definition 7.1. The class of cographs is the smallest class of simple graphs satis-
fying the following three properties:
    (1) the isolated vertex is a cograph;
    (2) a disjoint union of cographs is a cograph; and
    (3) if G is a cograph, then so is the graph with vertices VG and edges the
        non-edges of G.

                                                    We show that these conjectures imply the existence of a ﬁnite list of atomic
                                                    graphs generating the homology of conﬁguration spaces of graphs—in ﬁxed
                                                    degree, with a ﬁxed number of particles, under topological embeddings. We
                                                    explain how the simplest case of our conjecture follows from work of Barter
                                                    and Miyata–Proudfoot, implying that the category of cographs is Noetherian,
                                                    a result of potential independent interest.

                                       7. Cographs
   As shown above in Theorem 4.5, the simplest case of the categorical Robertson
conjecture holds. In this section, we explore the consequences of this fact for a
certain class of graphs.
Definition 7.1. The class of cographs is the smallest class of simple graphs satis-
fying the following three properties:
    (1) the isolated vertex is a cograph;
    (2) a disjoint union of cographs is a cograph; and
    (3) if G is a cograph, then so is the graph with vertices VG and edges the
        non-edges of G.
