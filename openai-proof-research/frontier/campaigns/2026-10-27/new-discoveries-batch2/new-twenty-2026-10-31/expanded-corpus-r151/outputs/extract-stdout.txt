323573 The characterization of positive graphs 
 A graph $G$ is called **positive** if its homomorphism number into every edge-weighted graph is nonnegative. It is called **symmetric** if its vertices can be partitioned into three sets $(S,A,B)$ such that $S$ is an independent set, there is no edge between $A$ and $B$, and an isomorphism between the subgraphs spanned by $S\cup A$ and $S\cup B$ fixes $S$.

**Positive-graph characterization conjecture.** A graph $G$ is positive if and only if it is symmetric.

This conjecture gives a structural characterization of graphs whose homomorphism numbers are nonnegative for every edge-weighted graph, including graphs with negative edgeweights. The paper proves the conjecture for various classes, including all trees, and establishes computationally that it holds for all graphs with at most $9$ vertices; the general case remains open.
