    The above work on list edge-coloring is motivated by similar work on list vertex-coloring. In the vertex
setting, Reed [39] conjectured that if a graph H and list assignment L for V (H) has maximum (vertex) color
degree ∆, and |L(v)| ≥ ∆ + 1 for every vertex v, then there exists a proper L-coloring. This was suggested
as a color degree generalization of the greedy upper bound on the list chromatic number, χℓ (H) ≤ ∆(H) + 1.
Haxell’s theorem [24] on independent transversals implies that a proper L-coloring exists if |L(v)| ≥ 2∆ for
every vertex v, and Reed and Sudakov [40] proved that a proper L-coloring exists if |L(v)| ≥ (1 + o(1))∆ for
