  First we present our counterexample to Conjecture 1.1. Let A := {A, Ā, B, B̄}, where
the pairs (A, Ā) and (B, B̄) represent the complementary letters. We claim that the
word P = B B̄ ĀAAĀĀAB B̄AĀAĀ has |V (P )| = 11. Indeed, there are two ways to
match up the Bs with the B̄s. If we match the adjacent B B̄ pairs we get 7 possible
matchings, if we match the further away B B̄ pairs we get 2 · 2 = 4 possible matchings.
