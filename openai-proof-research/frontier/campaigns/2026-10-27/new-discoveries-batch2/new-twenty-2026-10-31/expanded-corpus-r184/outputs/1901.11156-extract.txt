
                                                                                        Abstract
                                                     Recently, in the paper [4] we suggested the two conjectures about the di-
                                                 ameter of io-decomposable Riordan graphs of the Bell type. In this paper, we
                                                 give a counterexample for the first conjecture. Then we prove that the first
                                                 conjecture is true for the graphs of some particular size and propose a new
                                                 conjecture. Finally, we show that the second conjecture is true for some special
                                                 io-decomposable Riordan graphs.

                                           1 Introduction

    It is known [4] that the Pascal graph P Gn = Gn (1/(1 − z), z/(1 − z)) and the
Catalan graph CGn = Gn (C(z), zC(z)) are the io-decomposable Riordan graphs of
the Bell type. The following two conjectures introduced in [4] show significance of
the Pascal graph P Gn and the Catalan graph CGn .
Conjecture 1. [4] Let Gn be an io-decomposable Riordan graph of the Bell type.
Then

                     2 = diam(P Gn ) ≤ diam(Gn ) ≤ diam(CGn )

of the Bell type whose diameter is 2 for all n ≥ 4.
