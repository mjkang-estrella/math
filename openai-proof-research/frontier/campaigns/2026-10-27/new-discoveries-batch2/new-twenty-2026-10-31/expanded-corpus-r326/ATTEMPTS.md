## 369671: all-choice termination of Bui's triangle swaps

Primary arXiv:2508.04602v1, Section 8.2 and Conjecture 8.1 were read, including the finite permutation transition graph. The actual geometry is n hull vertices Q_i and n interior points B_j in general position. An edge of the state graph swaps two assigned interior points when their hull-edge triangles intersect in the intended interior sense. Adjacent triangles always share hull vertices, so closed-set nonempty intersection cannot be used as the predicate without changing the problem. The exact endpoint is acyclicity for every such order type and every legal choice sequence, not existence of a good matching, termination of one pivot rule, or the known n<=5 computations reported by the author.

First mechanism: transfer Euclidean uncrossing from red-blue segment matchings. A swap here is triggered by overlap of triangles, not intersection of two chosen matching segments. The source itself distinguishes the segment-to-object setting of Aloupis et al. Midpoint matching does not repair this. For a unit square, use bottom hull edge with apex a=(1/10,1/5) and left hull edge with apex b=(7/10,9/10). The triangles overlap: on any sufficiently short ray y=(3/2)x out of the bottom-left corner, the ray lies below the first triangle's apex slope 2 and above the second triangle's slope 9/7. However, the midpoint segment from a to (1/2,0) lies in y<=1/5, and the segment from b to (0,1/2) lies in y>=1/2; these segments do not cross. Thus the standard crossing-length inequality has no applicable hypothesis. This is a diagnostic for a proposed potential, not a cycle certificate.

Second mechanism: summed unsigned triangle areas. In the same configuration, before the swap the two areas sum to ((1/5)+(7/10))/2=9/20. Afterwards they sum to ((9/10)+(1/10))/2=1/2. A permitted overlap swap increases total area, refuting the proposed decreasing potential. This robust open configuration can be perturbed and supplied with two more generic interior points to realize an in-scope square/n=4 setting. No cycle or failure of termination follows: only the area proof fails.

Third mechanism: charge intersections with the other triangles or a lexicographic topological potential. Removing the local pair overlap need not control overlap with a third assigned triangle. A proof would need a global monotonicity inequality, or a directly defined acyclic orientation argument valid for all swaps. Finite state space and absence of a two-cycle do not exclude longer cycles. No such invariant or exact cycle was found. The source's proof of existence of compatible triangulations and finite tests do not supply this missing all-choice argument. Parked, credit 0; no solver plan.

## 369951: full ETF existence equivalence

Primary arXiv:2508.18685v1, Definition 1.1, Conjecture 1.1, Theorem 3.1 and its geometric implications and Corollary 3.1 with proof were read. The target is both directions for all integral d>3, not a numerical dimension or a statement restricted to designs of minimal type.

Forward mechanism: take the antipodal tight 5-design associated to a maximal ETF. If a nonzero vector alpha has all scalar products in {0,+1,-1}, the second and fourth design moments give ||alpha||^2=(d+2)/3 and precisely 2(d-1)(d+1)/3 points perpendicular to alpha. Choosing one from each antipodal pair yields an ETF in dimension d-1 with (d-1)(d+1)/3 vectors, by the source's angle-set and Welch equality computation. This closes only the source's conditional implication. The missing lemma is existence of such alpha for every maximal ETF (the nontrivial minimal-type property), not the elementary cardinality calculation. Failure of minimal type for a given design does not show no ETF with the requested lower-dimensional parameters exists, so it is not a refutation of the conjecture.

Reverse mechanism: start with the lower-dimensional ETF Y and try to adjoin the upper/lower latitude layers. Theorem 3.1(ii)->(i) explicitly requires THREE designs X1,X2,X3, with X1=-X3, specified sizes, and cross-angle sets, where X2 is the antipodalization of Y. Existence of X2 alone does not construct the other two layers or enforce their cross Gram entries. The missing lemma is a completion theorem producing these layers for every permitted Y. A Gram matrix positive-semidefinite identity or Naimark complement does not by itself give those cardinalities or cross angles: a Naimark complement has dimension N-(d-1), generally not d. No full reverse construction follows.

Small examples d=7 and d=23 agree with both sides and cannot settle all d. The source's nonexistence arithmetic for minimal-type designs is not nonexistence of arbitrary maximal ETFs. No ordinary endpoint, finite counterexample or formal execution plan survives. Parked, credit 0. Predecessor XXY21 Conjecture 4 identified (2021 journal / arXiv:2007.13999), but its full proof bodies and all citing developments were not read. ArXiv metadata exposes only v1, 2025-08-26; no exhaustive journal/correction clearance.

## Source exclusions, with explicit bridges

369676: Antolini–Dewar–Tanigawa, arXiv:2508.04798v1, Section 4.2 already gives exactly the needed counterexample. Let E be the six permutations (a,b,c) of (1,2,3), and L_i be the column space of the incidence matrix of partitioning E by coordinate i. Every row is nonzero, so each L_i satisfies the no-coordinate-hyperplane hypothesis. Hadamard coordinates are the monomials x_a y_b z_c. The product of the three even-permutation coordinates equals the product of the three odd-permutation coordinates, giving nontrivial algebraic dependence. In contrast E is independent for the predicted induced matroid: projection onto the first two coordinates gives the 6-cycle, whose proper edge subsets are forests; for every nonempty proper F, its graphic rank equals |F| and r_3(F)>=1, while on E the graphic rank is 5 and r_3(E)=3. The inequalities |F| <= (r_1+r_2-1)^MD(F)+r_3(F)-1 therefore hold for every nonempty F. As (r_1+r_2-1)^MD <= r_1+r_2-1, they also imply |F|<=r_1(F)+r_2(F)+r_3(F)-2, the literal conjectured independence criterion. Thus the predicted independence and actual dependence disagree. This is the author's published source example, not a new result; 0 credit. The stronger iterated-truncation failure was not used as a substitute: the inequality above explicitly bridges to the original weaker assertion. Source date 2025-08-06 precedes this lane.
