                                          Abstract: If distinct colours represent distinct technology types that are placed at the vertices of a simple graph in
                                          accordance to a minimum proper colouring, a disaster recovery strategy could rely on an answer to the question: “What is
                                          the maximum destruction, if any, the graph (a network) can undergo while ensuring that at least one of each technology
                                          type remain, in accordance to a minimum proper colouring of the remaining induced subgraph.” In this paper, we introduce
                                          the notion of a chromatic core subgraph H of a given simple graph G in answer to the stated problem. Since for any
                                          subgraph H of G it holds that χ(H) ≤ χ(G), the problem is well defined.
