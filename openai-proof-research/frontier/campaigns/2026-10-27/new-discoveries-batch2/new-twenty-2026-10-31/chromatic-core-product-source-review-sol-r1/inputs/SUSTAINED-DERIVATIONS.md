This follows because the Schur complement is B-(e^T A^-1 e)ff^T and the rank-one determinant identity follows by multilinearity of determinant in columns: terms using the rank-one perturbation twice have proportional columns and vanish. Thus the full conjecture would follow from proving that the two rooted inverse diagonal entries never multiply to1 in nonsingular block graphs. Nonsingularity alone does not imply this; it is the missing block-specific lemma.

Repeated updates can create zero pivots; simply claiming all inverse diagonals are negative has not been proved.
