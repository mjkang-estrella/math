Remark 9.8. For the rest of this subsection, we consider arbitrary bounded affine permu-
tations f ∈ Bound(k, N ), with no restrictions on fixed points. Consequently, we allow our
plabic graphs to have interior vertices of degree 1, 2, or 3. Vertices of degree 2 can be freely

may be contracted. Conversely, we can place a degree two vertex on any edge and attach a
leaf of the same color to it. These extra moves are included in the notion of restricted move
equivalence.
   We say that f ∈ Bound(k, N ) has a left descent at i ∈ Z if i + 1 ≤ f (i) < f (i + 1) ≤ i + n.
