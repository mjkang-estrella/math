                                                   Abstract. In this paper we study some determinants and permanents.
                                                   In particular, we investigate the new type determinants
                                                    det[(i2 + cij + dj 2 )p−2 ]1≤i,j≤p−1 and det[(i2 + cij + dj 2 )p−2 ]0≤i,j≤p−1
                                                   modulo an odd prime p, where c and d are integers. We also pose some
                                                   conjectures for further research.
