A Probabilistic Characterization of the Dominance Order
on Partitions

Abstract We present a probabilistic characterization of the dominance order on
partitions. Let ν be a partition and Yν its Young diagram, i.e. a stack of rows of
cells with row i containing νi cells. Let the cells of Yν be filled with i.i.d. draws
from the random variable X = Bin(r, p) with r ≥ 1 and p ∈ (0, 1). Given j, t ≥ 0,
let P (ν, j, t) be the probability that the sum of all the entries in Yν is j while the
sum of the entries in each row of Yν is no more than t. It is shown that if ν and
µ are two partitions of n, ν dominates µ if and only if P (ν, j, t) ≤ P (µ, j, t) for
all j, t ≥ 0. It is shown that the same result holds if X is any log-concave integer
valued random variable with {i : P (X = i) > 0} = {0, 1, . . . , r} for some r ≥ 1.
Keywords dominance order, majorization order, Riordan matrices, total
nonnegativity, Pólya frequency sequences
