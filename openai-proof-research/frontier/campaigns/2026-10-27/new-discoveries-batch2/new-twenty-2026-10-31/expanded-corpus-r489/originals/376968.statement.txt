Let $N$ be odd, and let

$$
d_{M'}=\sum\limits_{i+j=N,\ i\,\text{ is even}}d^{i,j}_N(k,k,k;q,z)
$$

be the contribution associated with the singular point on ${\cal M}_N$. **Polynomiality conjecture.** Then

$$
d_{M'}=\frac{P_N(q,z)}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^N(1-q^{-i}z^{-1})},
$$

where $P_N(q,z)$ is a polynomial. This statement is proposed to describe the contribution of the singular point on ${\cal M}_N$; the supplied text gives no evidence that the claim has been proved or disproved.
