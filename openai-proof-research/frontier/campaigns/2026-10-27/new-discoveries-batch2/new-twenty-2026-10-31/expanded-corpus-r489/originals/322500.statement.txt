A **rotor type** is a finite sequence of directions; a rotor type is **palindromic** if it reads the same forwards and backwards, and **block-repetitive** if it is a concatenation of uniform blocks of a common length. A rotor type is **boppy** if it is palindromic or block-repetitive, and **unboppy** otherwise. Let $\mathbf{ROT}$ be the class of all rotor types, $\mathbf{U}$ the class of universal rotors, and $\mathbf{BOP}$ the class of boppy rotors. Complements are taken in $\mathbf{ROT}$. **Universal rotor classification conjecture.** As classes of rotor types, $\mathbf{U}=\overline{\mathbf{BOP}}$. This would classify precisely the nonuniversal rotor types as the palindromic and block-repetitive ones; the statement is presented as a conjecture, and its general status is not resolved in the supplied text.
