Let $1\leq p<k<r$ be integers, and let $m,n$ be real numbers satisfying

$$
m>{r\choose k}n^{k/r}.
$$

Define $w(i)=k$ if $i\leq k$ and $w(i)=r$ if $i>k$. Consider positive real numbers $a_1^k,\dots,a_k^k,a_1^r,\dots,a_r^r$ satisfying

$$
n=a_1^r a_2^r\dots a_r^r,
$$

$$
m=\sum_{i_1<i_2<\dots<i_k}a_{i_1}^{w(i_k)}a_{i_2}^{w(i_k)}\dots a_{i_k}^{w(i_k)},
$$

with $a_i^k\geq a_j^k$ whenever $i<j$ and $a_j^i\geq a_j^k$ whenever $i<k$.

**Equal-block minimizer conjecture.** The unique choice of constants minimizing

$$
\sum_{i_1<i_2<\dots<i_p}a_{i_1}^{w(i_p)}a_{i_2}^{w(i_p)}\dots a_{i_p}^{w(i_p)}
$$

is also the unique choice satisfying

$$
a_1^k=\dots=a_k^k=a_1^r=\dots=a_k^r>a_{k+1}^r=\dots=a_r^r.
$$

This conjecture identifies the proposed extremal pattern for minimizing lower-dimensional faces after fixing the numbers of $k$- and $r$-faces. It is presented as an explanation for why equal parameters within the relevant blocks are a natural construction, but its general validity remains open.
