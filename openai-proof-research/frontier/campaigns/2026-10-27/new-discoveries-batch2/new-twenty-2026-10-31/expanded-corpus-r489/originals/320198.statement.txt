A $(\{1,2,3\},6)$-sphere is a sphere whose face sizes belong to $\{1,2,3\}$ and whose vertex degree is $6$; a $(\{2,3\},6)$-sphere is defined similarly with face sizes in $\{2,3\}$. A sphere is **$z$-knotted** or **$c$-knotted** when it has only one zigzag or only one central circuit, respectively. Its symmetry is denoted by the corresponding point-group symbol, and its $c$- and $z$-vectors record the lengths of its central circuits and zigzags.

(ii) A $(\{2,3\},6)$-sphere with only simple central circuits has symmetry $T_d$, $T_h$, $D_{6h}$, $D_{3d}$, $D_{2d}$, $D_{2h}$, $D_3$, $C_{2h}$ or $C_{3v}$.
