Let $q$ be an odd prime power, and let ${\mathbf B}$ and ${\mathbf B}_0$ be the incidence matrices of internal points versus secant lines and external points versus passant lines, respectively, in ${\mathrm{PG}}(2,q)$. Let $\mathcal{L}$ and $\mathcal{L}_0$ be their column $\mathbb F_2$-null spaces. **Droms–Mellinger–Meyer's conjecture.** The dimensions are

$$
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}(\mathcal{L})=\begin{cases}\frac{q^2-1}{4}-q,&q\equiv1\pmod4,\\frac{q^2-1}{4}-q+1,&q\equiv3\pmod4,
\end{cases}
$$

and

$$
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}(\mathcal{L}_0)=\begin{cases}\frac{q^2-1}{4},&q\equiv1\pmod4,\\frac{q^2-1}{4}+1,&q\equiv3\pmod4.
\end{cases}
$$

These formulas concern the dimensions of the four low-density parity-check codes constructed from submatrices of the point-line incidence matrix of ${\mathrm{PG}}(2,q)$. The article confirms this conjecture, so the claim is solved.
