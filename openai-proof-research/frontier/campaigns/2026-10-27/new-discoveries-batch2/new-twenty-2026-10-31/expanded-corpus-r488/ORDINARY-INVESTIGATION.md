## 378080 family-pair majorization
Actual Duval math0310327 selected section5.1–5.3 body is read. K is a k-family, K' a(k−1)-family, and only K'∩bdK matters. The boundary B retains rows in bdK minus K'. L=B*B, NOT the full up-plus-down Laplacian. d_lambda counts F∈K with lambda∈F and F\{lambda} NOT in K'. Required prefix sum is sum(top_j eig L)≤sum_lambda min(j,d_lambda). Every retained boundary incidence contributes1 to trace(L) and exactly1 to a degree, so the TOTAL prefix/trace identity is automatic. It cannot settle intermediate prefixes.

Route1: exploit the full simplex exterior boundary. With r active vertex labels, exterior contraction by the all-ones vector has squared norm r (decompose the exterior algebra along that vector and its orthogonal complement). Restricting columns to K and rows to retained faces is a compression, so largest eigenvalue≤r. Delete labels with d_lambda=0: their contraction entries all vanish on retained rows, so B is the sum only of the active contractions, giving r=#nonzero d_lambda=d^T_1. Thus the FIRST prefix holds. This does not combine with trace to prove all prefixes; majorization is stronger.

Route2: attempt a positive rank-projection decomposition. If L could be written as sum P_lambda, with 0≤P_lambda≤I and rank≤d_lambda, Ky Fan's variational principle would give exactly sum min(j,d_lambda). But B=sum D_lambda and L contains cross terms D_lambda*D_mu. Anticommutation cancels these for the whole exterior boundary in special settings, but arbitrary retained rows break the needed cancellation. Ignoring these terms changes L. No valid decomposition was found. The source shifted-pair recursion and Theorem5.7 provide equality for shifted pairs, not arbitrary pairs.

Route3: row deletion induction. Removing one boundary row subtracts an outer product vv*, whose support size is its number of incident facets. Ky Fan gives an upper bound on eigenvalue-prefix loss, but the desired comparison requires the RIGHT lower loss versus the simultaneous changes of several degrees. Interlacing alone has the wrong direction for that. No all-j monotonic defect or separating witness paid. Source notes Stephen graph-pair implication; graph pairs are only k=2. No transfer of modern graph Grone–Merris to all k. PARK at arbitrary-family intermediate-prefix lemma; no claimed counterexample.
