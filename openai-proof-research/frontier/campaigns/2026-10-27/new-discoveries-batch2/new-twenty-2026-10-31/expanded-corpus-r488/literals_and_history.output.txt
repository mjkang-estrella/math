346828 Let $G$ be a connected graph, let $T$ be a well-covered tree, and let $I(G,x)$ denote the independence polynomial of $G$. **Levit–Mandrescu conjecture.** If

$$
I(T,x)=I(G,x),
$$

then $G$ is a well-covered tree. The conjecture concerns whether independence-polynomial equivalence with a well-covered tree forces the other graph to share that structure; the paper later gives an infinite family of counterexamples, so the conjecture is refuted.

Assume that both types of colorings of the hexagonal prism described in the surrounding discussion occur. **Klein-bottle prism conjecture.** The prism is egc, that is, it admits an edge-girth coloring.

This conjecture gives a sufficient parity condition for edge-girth colorability of these Klein-bottle prisms and requires the presence of both local coloring types. The source reports computational evidence in support, but no proof or resolution.

a(G)\leq 2.
$$

The preceding discussion gives the weaker bound $a(G)\leq 3$; this conjecture proposes that the bound can be improved to two.
377351 Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $p$ be a prime with $p^2\geq n$, and let $k$ be any field of characteristic $p$. Let $\triangle_n$ be the Vandermonde determinant, let $\mathbb D_n^k$ be the algebra of divided differential operators in $n$ variables, and let

$$
\operatorname{Diff}_n(\triangle_n):=\mathbb C[\partial_{X_1},\dots,\partial_{X_n}]\cdot\triangle_n.
$$

**Divided-differential-operator dimension conjecture.** One has

$$
\dim_k\mathbb D_n^k(\triangle_n)=\dim_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{Diff}_n(\triangle_n).
$$

The paper motivates this as a characteristic-$p$ analogue of the classical Vandermonde dimension result and notes the restriction $p^2\geq n$. Its resolution is not supplied in the source.
377459 Let $G$ be a planar bipartite graph, and let $P(G,t)$ be its chromatic polynomial. Let $\tau=(1+\sqrt{5})/2$ be the golden ratio. **Salas–Sokal conjecture.**

$$
P(G,t)>0\quad\text{for }t\in[\tau^2,\infty).
$$

Equivalently, planar bipartite graphs should have no real chromatic roots at or above $\tau^2$. The source notes that Salas and Sokal constructed families with chromatic roots tending to $\tau^2$, giving evidence for sharpness.
377583 Let a weakly Eulerian graph or digraph be given, and for each integer $k$ let the number of its partitions into $k$ circuits be counted. **Circuit-partition unimodality conjecture.** For any weakly Eulerian graph or digraph, the number of partitions into $k$ circuits is unimodal as a function of $k$. The conjecture is motivated by the circuit-counting interpretation of $xq_G(1+x)$ for interlace graphs and would extend the corresponding unimodality assertion beyond the cases directly represented by interlace polynomials.
377653 Let $T$ be a well-covered tree and let $G$ be a graph. Suppose their independence polynomials agree:

$$
I(T;x)=I(G;x).
$$

A graph is **well-covered** if all its maximal stable sets have the same cardinality.

**Independence-polynomial characterization conjecture.** If $T$ is a well-covered tree and $I(T;x)=I(G;x)$, then $G$ is well-covered.

The paper motivates this conjecture by examples of claw-free graphs that are both well-covered and have the same independence polynomials as the well-covered trees under investigation. The source provides no resolution status.
377660 Let $\lambda$ be a partition with $\ell$ parts and fixed parity for each part. Let $G_\lambda(x/x)$ be the specialized symmetric function defined by substituting $2p_{2i-1}$ for $p_{2i-1}$ and $0$ for $p_{2i}$, and let $Q_\mu(x)$ denote Schur's shifted $Q$-function. **Symmetric-function generalization of the Eremenko–Gabrielov conjecture.** There are integers $c_1,\dots,c_k$ and integer vectors $\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_k\in\mathbb{Z}^\ell$ such that

$$
(-1)^{r(\lambda)}G_\lambda(x/x)=\sum_{i=1}^k c_i Q_{\frac12(\lambda+\gamma_i)}(x).
$$

This conjecture is intended as a symmetric-function refinement of the Eremenko–Gabrielov conjecture. The source presents the definition as possibly requiring modification and gives no resolution status.
377665 Let $G$ be the abelian group under discussion, and call a subset **saturated** if it is a maximal avoidable set. The sets described above are those obtained as solution sets of

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n a_i x_i\equiv 1\pmod{2},
$$

where each $a_i$ is $0$ or $1$, not all $0$, in the decomposition of $G$ given in the preceding context. **Characterization conjecture.** The sets described above are the only saturated sets containing no even numbers. This would classify all saturated sets avoiding even elements; the source does not provide evidence resolving the assertion.
377676 Let $[n]^{(r)}_k$ denote the collection of $k$-separated $r$-sets in $[n]^{(r)}$: sets $A=\{a_1,\dots,a_r\}$ with $a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_r$ satisfying $a_{i+1}-a_i>k$ for $i=1,\dots,r$, where $a_{r+1}=a_1+n$. Let $n$, $k$, and $r$ be positive integers satisfying

n\geq (k+1)r.

For an intersecting family $\mathcal{A}\subseteq[n]^{(r)}_k$, define the star at $1$ by

**Holroyd and Johnson's conjecture.** If $\mathcal{A}\subseteq[n]^{(r)}_k$ is intersecting, then

$$
|\mathcal{A}|\leq|\mathcal{A}_1^*|.
$$

The conjecture asserts an Erdős–Ko–Rado-type bound for intersecting families of $k$-separated sets. The supplied text does not state whether it has been resolved; the status is therefore recorded as open.
377690 Let $W_{\mathbb C^d}(\{0\}_{n\omega})$ be the indicated local Weyl module for $sl_2$, and let $W_{\mathbb C^d}(\{0\}_{n\omega})^{n-2i}$ denote its weight space of weight $n-2i$. **Higher Narayana number conjecture.** For $i=0,\dots,n$, one has

$$
\dim W_{\mathbb C^d}(\{0\}_{n\omega})^{n-2i}=\frac{(d+n-1)!\cdots(d+n-i)!}{(n-1)!\cdots(n-i)!}\cdot\frac{2!\cdots(i-1)!}{d!\cdots(d+i-1)!},
$$

and these dimensions equal the dimension of the irreducible representation of $sl_n$ with highest weight $d\omega_i$ for $i=1,\dots,n-1$, while they equal one for $i=0,n$. This proposes a higher-dimensional analogue of the Catalan and Narayana number formulas established earlier in the paper; the source gives no resolution of the proposed formula.
377727 Let $i$ be the number of faces of an irreducible $i$-hedrite, and let a **central circuit** be a central circuit of the hedrite. An irreducible $i$-hedrite is **maximal irreducible** when it cannot be extended while remaining irreducible. **Central-circuit characterization.** An irreducible $i$-hedrite is maximal irreducible if and only if it has $i-2$ central circuits.

The surrounding discussion gives examples of irreducible $i$-hedrites with $i-2$ central circuits for the relevant values of $i$, and notes that these examples are pure. The parser supplies no evidence resolving the stated characterization, so its status remains open.

**Curvature-graph conjecture.** The graph of curvatures of any tight graph of type $3_n$ is the graph in the first of those three cases.

This conjecture concerns the structure of tight polyhedral graphs with four triangular faces; the source gives the three possible curvature graphs but does not state a resolution of this tighter classification claim.
378021 Let $K_{m,n}$ be the complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes $m$ and $n$, and let $I_{K_{m,n}}$ be its graph ideal. Here $\min\{m,n\}$ denotes the smaller part size.

This extends the established result for $K_{2,n}$; the paper notes that the conjecture is supported by the preceding result and remains unproved there.

$$
\mathbb{E}\#\operatorname{LE}(R)\geq c\sqrt{|G|}.
$$

Here $c$ is an absolute positive constant. The vertex-transitivity assumption excludes highly non-transitive examples such as a tree, where the loop-erased walk follows the unique path and can have length of order $\log N$. The conjecture proposes a universal mean-field lower bound for loop-erased random walks on finite vertex-transitive graphs.
378070 A simplicial complex is **of type $\{3,4,5\}$** if the link of every $(n-2)$-face is one of the cycles $C_3$, $C_4$, or $C_5$. **Finiteness conjecture.** In every fixed dimension $n$, there are only finitely many simplicial complexes of type $\{3,4,5\}$. This asserts a finiteness phenomenon for simplicial complexes whose codimension-two links have bounded cycle lengths; the supplied text does not state whether the conjecture has been resolved.

378105 Let $[1\dots n]$ be labeled cyclically, and let a **non-crossing** family be a family of pairwise non-crossing $k$-subsets, where non-crossing means that no chord with endpoints in $I-J$ crosses a chord with endpoints in $J-I$ for two $k$-subsets $I$ and $J$.
