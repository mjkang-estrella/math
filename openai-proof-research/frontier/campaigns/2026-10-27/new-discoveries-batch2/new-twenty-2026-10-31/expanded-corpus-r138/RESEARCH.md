## 365964: forest cuts, strongest finite-certificate route

Read both arXiv versions 2411.17885v1 and v2. The target is v1 Conjecture 3 / v2 Conjecture 5. An external neighborhood means N(S) = union of the neighborhoods of vertices of S, minus S. Every S of size at most two must either dominate or have a cycle in the graph induced by N(S). Vertex connectivity, not edge connectivity, must be at least four. The source has examples below the ratio at orders 7 and 8 and an octahedral-block family attaining 7n/3. Its separate 1-cyclic counterexample is not a counterexample here. No author exhaustive verified range is reported.

`forest.c` tests edge-exchange local searches at orders 9 and 10. Initial 2-cyclic hits fail vertex connectivity. `forest-connect.c` penalizes three-vertex cuts near feasibility. Each complete run has 300 restarts of 20,000 edge exchanges, at m = ceil(7n/3)-1, for n = 9,10,11,12,14,16,18. `forest-seeded.c` additionally starts near the source's tight octahedral construction: octahedra with four distinct external ports, two edges between consecutive blocks, and one edge removed. Orders 12,18,24 were tested. These are heuristic searches, not exhaustive graph enumerations. The penalty has discontinuities, so its local minima are not structural theorems.

`forest_sat.py` gives a separate exact diagnostic for order 9. A counterexample has at most 20 edges. Adding edges preserves both 4-connectivity and 2-cyclicity, so a smaller counterexample can be augmented to 20 edges. At that size, minimum degree four implies the existence of a degree-four vertex, which may be labelled 0 with neighbors 1,2,3,4. The CNF imposes exactly 20 edges, minimum degree four, and connectivity after every deletion of three vertices. The latter plus minimum degree four also ensures connectivity after fewer deletions.

For each S and each non-dominating putative external neighborhood N, the CNF forbids the induced graph on N from being a subgraph of any labelled spanning tree of the complete graph on N. Every forest extends to such a tree. Neighborhood indicators are equivalent to the OR of the incident edges from S. Thus these clauses are equivalent to the source condition. If |N| >= 7, minimum degree four in an order-9 graph gives induced minimum degree at least two, and hence a cycle; those clauses can be omitted. Cardinality constraints use forward sequential threshold implications and prohibit threshold b+1. Their existential auxiliary variables give exactly the desired at-most constraint.

A solver UNSAT answer is a bounded diagnostic, not a kernel-certified theorem. No UNSAT proof replay or Lean proof is claimed. The general missing step is a structural degree-deficit inequality, or a finite graph satisfying every hypothesis with fewer than 7n/3 edges. A useful equivalent charging target is 2 n_4 <= sum_(d>=5) (3d-14)n_d. The source's octahedral blocks attain equality. The small-order exceptions explain why a naive local degree inequality without global exceptions fails.

## 339996: exact consumption

Read arXiv:1808.01074v1 Conjecture 7.14 and current v2 Conjecture 8.14, together with Definitions 5.1–5.3 and Theorem 5.4. A group H <= S_n consumes a partition if some set partition of that type has trivial stabilizer in H. Lambda >=_c mu means EVERY subgroup consuming lambda consumes mu. The desired consumed set is therefore the intersection of all consumed sets of subgroups consuming lambda. Existence of that intersection as a row is not a consequence of the definition.

`consumption.py` computes this directly through n=5 (all 156 subgroups). Its n=6 run reaches the CPU cap and is not counted. `consumption.c` independently completes n=6 (1,455 subgroups, 203 set partitions, 11 integer partitions). `consumption-conj.c` stores all conjugates but only processes one representative of each conjugacy class. This is complete: conjugate an arbitrary predecessor subgroup to a processed representative, then conjugate the new generator as well. Every subgroup is reached by successive generator adjunctions. The reduced implementation reproduces every n=6 witness count and completes n=7: 11,300 subgroups, 877 set partitions, 15 integer partitions. Every partition has a realizing row. The unoptimized n=7 run is CPU-capped and is not counted separately.

This is not a proof for arbitrary n. A finite counterexample would require a complete subgroup classification at its degree, creating substantial Lean semantic/classification cost even if found. No generator-product or subgroup-intersection argument was found that realizes the ideal uniformly. The source reports consumption/dominance order comparisons at 2,3,5,7; it does not supply a general exact-realization theorem.

## 333551: square ice

The corpus has a missing symbol, so definitions were recovered from Section 2 of arXiv:1607.01890 and the published Numdam paper. The baseline h0 is piecewise |x|+|y| for x<0, |x+y| for 0<=x<ell, and |y+ell-1|+|x-ell+1| otherwise. Heights differ by one across each lattice edge. Volume is sum (h-h0)/2. A legal increment adds 2 to a local minimum. Configurations are stored canonically as sorted lists of the cells incremented, including repetitions.

The finite enumeration is complete within its completed layers: for any target height above the current one, a deficit cell with a lower current neighbor has that neighbor also in deficit. Descending current height within the finite deficit set therefore reaches a local minimum at which a legal increment stays below the target. Repeating produces the target. A volume-t configuration has support within distance t of the central crease, so the fixed grid margin 16 safely covers t<=12. Hash equality compares every stored cell, not merely hashes.

`ice.c` independently reproduces the source's width-5 and width-6 tables. Complete layer counts for widths 5 through 20, with explicit six-million-state stops, are retained. `ice-larger.c` completes width 17 through volume 11 under a twelve-million-state cap. The authors' formula check was n<=9, ell<=20, although their separate exact tables go farther in n at widths<=6.

At n=10, interpolation on ell=5..15 gives binomial coefficients g_0..g_10:
1, 0, 20, -7, 161, -109, 738, -734, 2371, -2985, 6188.
The three additional completed widths 16,17,18 have zero residual. All printed general formulas g_0..g_6 match. At n=11, all twelve widths 6..17 are complete. Fitting g_7 as a cubic from n=7,8,9,10 predicts -1058 at n=11; the enumeration gives -1058. This is supporting evidence, not a uniform polynomiality proof. No source-table contradiction or counterexample was found. A full combinatorial cluster/transfer argument controlling all volumes and widths is missing; a numerical polynomial fit cannot be the Lean endpoint.

## 347136: spanning trees, not rooted out-branchings

Read the primary definitions, Theorem 23, Conjecture 26, Theorem 27, and the r135 proof of 347138 (read-only). The source proves the spanning-tree statement for n>=14, so only 9..13 remain. The general source hypothesis is lambda>=2 (the corpus says =2); tested examples have equality because the two-block construction has exactly two arcs in each direction across its block cut. Alpha<=2 follows from both blocks being semicomplete. The r135 W1 out-branching obstruction is not an obstruction to deleting an arbitrary orientation of an undirected spanning tree. Its source gadget/padding bridge is excluded and was not recycled.

`digraph.py` samples narrow-cut two-semicomplete-block digraphs. It verifies all nontrivial directed cuts have size >=2, then solves a finite MILP for n-1 tree arcs. Every undirected cut must meet the selected arcs; every directed cut must retain a residual arc. Feasible integer results are independently checked for tree connectivity and every residual directed cut. Eight eligible examples at each order 9..13 were completed; all 40 have explicit witnesses in outputs/digraph.json. This is sampling, not an exhaustive classification. General orientations without a Hamiltonian cycle and with 2-cycles remain a source gap. Neither the rooted obstruction nor the source's adjacent lemmas close it.

## Later strengthened checks and corrections

The order-9 CNF has 1,310 variables and 853,473 clauses; CaDiCaL returns UNSAT (normal solver exit20). The symmetry-reduced order-10 CNF has1,991 variables and 2,450,548 clauses. Its first unsplit solver run reached the CPU cap and has no answer. Splitting by the graph induced on the four neighbors of vertex0 is exhaustive: a cyclic graph on four vertices is, up to isomorphism, a triangle plus an isolated vertex, a paw, C4, a diamond, or K4. Each of these five branches returns UNSAT. Thus the diagnostic coverage is all eligible graphs at orders 9 and 10, subject to the documented CNF correctness argument and solver trust; no kernel theorem is claimed. The reduced encoding also returns SAT at orders 7 and 8. Those models were independently checked for every deletion of0..3 vertices and every required neighborhood; they reproduce the source-known exceptions and are not claimed discoveries.

A stronger reading of Theorem 23 reveals that ALL initial two-semicomplete-block digraph samples are already source-covered: at n>=9, one block has at least five vertices. These40 witnesses are only implementation checks and provide no new coverage of the unresolved regime. This correction supersedes any inference to the contrary from the initial sampling strategy.

The replacement `digraph_ramsey.py` uses induced subgraphs of the complement of the13-vertex circulant with differences ±1,±5. Triangle-freeness and independence number at most4 of the circulant are checked by all triples and all 5-sets. Hence the resulting underlying graphs have alpha<=2 and clique number<=4. Every sampled digraph contains digons, so neither Theorem 23's semicomplete5-clique hypothesis nor Theorem 27's no-digon hypothesis holds automatically. All directed cuts and all 5-vertex clique tests are performed literally. `digraph_ramsey2.py` further forces a vertex with exactly two outgoing arcs (both reciprocal), so each eligible sample has lambda exactly2. Outputs retain every arc, selected tree and exact minimum directed-cut count. These are bounded samples of the actual remaining regime, not an exhaustive orientation classification. Their final completed counts and statuses are in results.json and the logs.

The cyclicity diagnostics use nonempty S of size1 or2. The source's examples require this intended convention; including the empty set literally in the phrase “at most2” would make every nonempty graph fail the definition. No credit is sought for that vacuity or any source-wording correction.
