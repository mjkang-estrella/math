Let $A,Bsubset \mathbb Z$ be finite sets of equal size, and let $\Gamma\subset A\times B$ be a set of pairs defining the restricted sumset $A+_{\Gamma}B$. Suppose the hypotheses of Corollary hold: for every $\varepsilon>0$, $|A+_{\Gamma}B|\leq (3-\varepsilon)N-4$ and $|\Gamma|\geq (1-\delta)N^2$, where $|A|=|B|=N$. **Polynomial dependence conjecture.** One can take

$$
\delta=c\varepsilon^C
$$

for some absolute constants $c,C>0$. The conjecture would give polynomial quantitative dependence on $\varepsilon$ in the almost-all restricted-doubling structure theorem; the source states that this should be possible but does not prove it.
