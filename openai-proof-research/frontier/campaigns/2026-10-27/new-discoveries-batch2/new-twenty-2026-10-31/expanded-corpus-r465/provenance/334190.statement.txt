Let $T$ be a 2-increasing sequence in $[r]\times[s]\times[t]$.

**Classification conjecture for extremal 2-increasing sequences.**

$$
|T|\le (rst)^{1/2},
$$

and equality holds if and only if $T$ can be built as follows: choose a coordinate, partition the product of the other two coordinate sets into interval rectangles $R_i\times S_i$ ordered in a 1-increasing fashion, partition the chosen coordinate set into increasing disjoint intervals $T_i$ with $|R_i||S_i|$ proportional to $|T_i|$, place extremal examples in each $R_i\times S_i\times T_i$, and allow permutations of rows, columns, or labels whenever they preserve the 2-increasing property with a different order.

This is a proposed classification of all equality cases, strengthening the preceding decomposition conjecture. The paper says it has survived small-scale searches for counterexamples, but supplies no proof.
