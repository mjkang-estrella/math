Let $H$ be the non-backtracking matrix of the directed graph under consideration, let $\rho(H)$ denote its spectral radius, let $p$ be the bond-occupation probability, and let $n$ denote the graph size. **Non-uniqueness conjecture.** For $p\rho(H)<1$, if there is a percolating cluster, it is not unique with probability approaching one at large $n$. This conjecture asserts that any large clusters below the spectral threshold are unstable and fracture into two or more large clusters, so a percolating cluster cannot be unique with high probability.
