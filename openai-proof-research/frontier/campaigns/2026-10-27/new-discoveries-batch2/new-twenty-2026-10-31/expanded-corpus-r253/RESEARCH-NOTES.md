## 330695: distinct abelian-square factors

The literal quantifier is over all finite words on arbitrary alphabets, with a binary comparator of the same length. Count distinct strings, not occurrences and not Parikh-vector classes. Fici–Mignosi, arXiv:1506.03562v1, Conjecture 1, is literal. The later journal title is *Abelian-square-rich words*, TCS 2017, DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2017.02.012; the indexed journal excerpt labels it Conjecture 27. The complete journal body is not locally verified. The complete arXiv body contains examples and small tables, and investigates richness rather than a certified optimum for all binary words.

Read the entire two-page purported proof by Salah Triki, arXiv:1907.05369v1. Its Lemma 1 and conjecture proof induct by extending a word only by a copy of its last letter. This does not cover an arbitrary length n+1 word. Moreover, the asserted preservation of exactly k distinct squares under that extension is not automatic: appending a letter may create new distinct square factors. Thus this body does not supply a complete proof that can be relied on here. This is an identified proof gap, not a claim that the conjecture is false or a comprehensive literature verdict. The 2022 Fici–Puzynina survey was also downloaded and the square-count discussion read; no independent resolution was identified there.

First ordinary mechanism: merge letters into two colours, preserving the Parikh equalities of each old square. Every square maps to a binary square, but the map on distinct factors need not be injective. For example, w = aabbcc has distinct square factors aa, bb, cc. Every letter-to-bit map identifies two of these three length-two factors. This defeats a proof by an injective fixed letter map. It does NOT refute the conjecture, because the binary image can have new squares and the desired comparator need not be a morphic image.

Second mechanism: append a letter and track newly introduced distinct factors by their shortest prior occurrence. An inductive state consisting only of (length, count, last bit) loses the equality pattern of suffixes, so it cannot justify a matching extension. A complete extension lemma would have to preserve an ordered suffix-equivalence profile together with the set of previously seen square factors. There is no proved compression to binary profiles. This is the exact missing lemma; no claimed ordinary endpoint.

## 330509, 330510, 330512: entropy mechanisms stopped by sources

The survey's f^3 is c(v) times ECCENTRICITY, not total distance, orbit cardinality, sphere entropy, or degree. With equal positive weights, scaling cancels, so H(G) = log S - sum_v e(v) log e(v)/S, S = sum_v e(v). This supplies an exact log-to-integer certificate route when comparing two finite graphs: multiply by the two positive total eccentricities and exponentiate. Pi would prove distances, eccentricities, normalizations, log identities and one integer product inequality. Floating-point comparisons would not suffice.

For 330509 an initial path-versus-star idea was to compare the eccentricity lists (10,9,8,7,6,5,6,7,8,9,10) and (1,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2). Their sums are 85 and 21. A proposed strict inequality H(P11) < H(S11) reduces exactly to

For 330512, if u vertices are universal in a diameter-two graph, e = 1 on u vertices and 2 on the others. Hence H = log(2n-u) - 2(n-u)/(2n-u) in base two. Differentiating the real relaxation gives u/n = 2 - 2 ln 2, about 0.614. A tempting global proof uses radius r and e in [r,2r], with entropy minimized at endpoint eccentricities. A graph with r>1 attaining both r and 2r must have an intermediate eccentricity, preventing equality. This is already Cambie–Dong Theorem 17 and Remark 18; the latter also shows that 'few deleted edges' is misleading, since extremizers can have quadratically many nonedges. No new result.
