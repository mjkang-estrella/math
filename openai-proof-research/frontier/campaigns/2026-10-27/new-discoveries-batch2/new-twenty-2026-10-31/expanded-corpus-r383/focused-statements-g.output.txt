23:                                             Agrawal [1] considered a type of row-column design which now goes by the
24:                                           name of “triple array”. He observed that such an array implies the existence of
25:                                           a symmetric balanced incomplete block design, and asked whether triple arrays
26:                                           can be constructed from such designs. The statement that this can always be
27:                                           done, with exception for trivial and the smallest non-trivial design, is known as
28:                                           Agrawal’s conjecture.
29:                                           Definition 1.1. A triple array is an r × c array on v symbols arranged so that no
30:                                           symbol occurs more than once in any row or column, and satisfies the following
31:                                           four conditions:
32:                                             TA1. Each symbol occurs k times (equireplicate).
33:                                             TA2. Any two distinct rows contain λrr common symbols.
34:                                             TA3. Any two distinct columns contain λcc common symbols.
35:                                             TA4. Any row and column contain λrc common symbols.
36:                                             For a triple array we use the notation T A(v, k, λrr , λcc , λrc : r × c). An array as
37:                                           above that satisfies conditions TA1–TA3 is called a double array with notation
38:                                           DA(v, k, λrr , λcc : r × c), and a double array that does not satisfy TA4 is called
39:                                           a proper double array.
40:                                             If we interchange the roles of columns and symbols in a triple array, we get
41:                                           what is called the RL form of a triple array. It is an incomplete r × v array,
42:                                           with no repetition in any row or column, satisfying the following four conditions,
43:                                           corresponding to the above conditions TA1–TA4:
