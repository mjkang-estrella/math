1302:     We have seen in this paper how Kuo’s graphical condensation method can be
1303: used to prove the exact enumeration of tilings of two families of regions on the
1304: lattice obtained from the triangular lattice by drawing in the altitudes in all the unit
1305: triangles. We showed how this implies Blum’s conjecture on the number of tilings
1306: of the hexagonal dungeons, which was open since 1999. This illustrates the power
1307: of this method on a lattice different from the square and hexagonal lattices, the two
1308: lattices involved in the vast majority of the applications of graphical condensation
1309: in the literature.
1310:     We conclude by pointing out that the method used in the original proof of the
1311: Aztec dungeon theorem (i.e., using a certain local replacement rule to transform
1312: the problem into a weighted Aztec diamond enumeration problem, see [1]) can be
1313: considered for hexagonal dungeons as well, but despite initial promise it does not
1314: seem to lead to a solution. It does lead however to some new families of regions that
