322870 Bounds for covariance and correlation of incident arcs in uniformly random DAGs

These ranges summarize values observed in complete enumerations for DAGs with three through seven nodes; their validity beyond the enumerated cases is not formally established.

322880 The $K_4^-$-density conjecture for $(K_4^-,C_5)$-free $3$-graphs

The lower bound comes from an iterated balanced blow-up of a $3$-edge, while the source gives an upper bound below $0.251073$; the conjectured equality is unresolved.

325649 Conjecture on collecting all but one twelfth of a planar graph 
Let $G$ be a planar graph. To **delete** a vertex means to remove it and its incident edges. To **collect** a vertex means to remove it when its current degree is at most $4$; a set is collectable if its vertices can be collected in some sequence. **Collecting conjecture.** One can delete at most $1/12$ of the vertices of $G$ so that all remaining vertices can be collected. The collected vertices consequently induce a $4$-degenerate subgraph. The preceding results guarantee deletion of less than $1/9$ of the vertices, while the icosahedron shows that one deleted vertex may be necessary; the source presents the stronger $1/12$ bound as believed optimal and gives no resolution.

326872 Armstrong's average-size conjecture for simultaneous core partitions 
Let $s$ and $t$ be coprime positive integers. An $(s,t)$-core is a partition that is simultaneously an $s$-core and a $t$-core; its size is the sum of its parts. **Armstrong's conjecture.** The average size of an $(s,t)$-core is

$$
\frac{(s+t+1)(s-1)(t-1)}{24}.
$$

Equivalently, since there are $\frac{1}{s+t}\binom{s+t}{s}$ such cores, the sum of their sizes is

$$
\frac{(s+t+1)(s-1)(t-1)}{24(s+t)}\binom{s+t}{s}.
$$

The conjecture predicts a remarkably simple statistic for simultaneous core partitions and is the Catalan case of Armstrong's broader conjecture on simultaneous core partitions. The source presents it as a conjecture; no resolution is supplied here.

327263 Fractional covering conjecture for separated d-intervals 
Let $H$ be a hypergraph of separated $d$-intervals. Write $
u(H)$ for its matching number and $\tau^*(H)$ for its fractional covering number. **Fractional covering conjecture.** One should have

 This is known to fail for non-separated $2$-intervals, where an intersecting family can have $\tau^*\geq\tfrac{11}4$; whether every intersecting family of non-separated $2$-intervals satisfies $\tau^*<3$ remains open.

327381 Blum's conjecture on tilings of hexagonal dungeons 
Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers with $b\geq 2a$. The **hexagonal dungeon** $HD_{a,2a,b}$ is the lattice region whose hexagonal contour has side lengths $a,2a,b,a,2a,b$ in clockwise order. A tiling is a covering of this region by tiles, each formed by two fundamental regions sharing an edge.

**Blum's conjecture.** The number of tilings of $HD_{a,2a,b}$ is

$$
13^{2a^2}14^{\left\lfloor\frac{a^2}{2}\right\rfloor}.

327855 Armstrong–Hanusa–Jones average-size conjecture for core partitions 
A partition is an **$(s,t)$-core** if it is simultaneously an $s$-core and a $t$-core, and it is **self-conjugate** if its Ferrers diagram is invariant under conjugation. For coprime positive integers $s$ and $t$, consider the finite sets of $(s,t)$-core partitions and self-conjugate $(s,t)$-core partitions, with size given by the number of cells in the Ferrers diagram.

**Armstrong–Hanusa–Jones conjecture.** The average size in each of these two sets is

$$
\frac{(s+t+1)(s-1)(t-1)}{24}.

The conjecture concerns a common statistic on two related finite families of core partitions. The paper states that the self-conjugate case is proved using a bijection with lattice paths; the source therefore treats this conjecture as resolved.

328364 Armstrong's average-size conjecture for $(a,b)$-core partitions 
Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers, and let an **$(a,b)$-core partition** be a partition with no hook length divisible by either $a$ or $b$. For a partition $\lambda$, write $|\lambda|$ for its size. If $\gcd(a,b)=1$, then **Armstrong's conjecture.**

\frac{(a-1)(b-1)(a+b+1)}{24}.

331201 The Normal Graph Conjecture

370989 Maximality conjecture for abstract birigidity matroids 
Let $[m]\times[n]$ be a round set, and let an **$(m,n,a,b)$-abstract birigidity matroid** be a matroid on this set of rank $bm+an-ab$ in which every $A\times B$, with $A\subseteq[m]$ of size $a+1$ and $B\subseteq[n]$ of size $b+1$, is a circuit. Let $\mathsf{MR}(m,n,a,b)$ denote the correctability matroid of the tensor product of an $[m,m-a]$ MDS code and an $[n,n-b]$ MDS code.

**Maximality conjecture.**

$$
\mathsf{BR}(m,n,a,b)=\mathsf{MR}(m,n,a,b)
$$

is the maximal $(m,n,a,b)$-abstract birigidity matroid.

This conjecture is equivalent to an affirmative answer to an open question raised by Mason over 40 years ago concerning the most general possible tensor product of two uniform matroids.
