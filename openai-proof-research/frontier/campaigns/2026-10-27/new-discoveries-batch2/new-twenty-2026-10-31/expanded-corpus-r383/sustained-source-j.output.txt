780:    As one can see in this example, the nice exponents s 6≈ 1 are rare, and
781: all have differential multiplicity 2. Surprisingly, this seems to be a general
782: fact. Indeed, for the characteristics 3 to 31, all the observed nice exponents
783: s 6≈ 1 have 2 as a differential multiplicity.
784: Problem 6.7. All nice exponents s 6≈ 1 over the prime fields of character-
785: istic p < 5000 were found in the numerical experiment [15], and they all
786: correspond to the paradigmatic examples of Propositions 6.5 and 6.6. Is
787: this a general property?
788:      We propose some conjectures based on our numerical evidence.
789: Conjecture 6.8 (nice exponent). Let s 6≈ 1 be an exponent over a finite
790: field of odd characteristic. If s is nice, then 2 is a differential multiplicity.
791:      We can make an even stronger conjecture.
792: Conjecture 6.9 (optimist). Let s 6≈ 1 be an exponent over a finite field of
793: odd characteristic. If s is invertible, then 2 is a differential multiplicity.
794:      Then we claim that we have the following implications among conjectures
795:            optimist =⇒ nice exponent =⇒ Helleseth Three-Valued.
796: Remark 6.10. It is interesting to notice that Conjecture 6.8 implies the
797: Helleseth Three-Valued Conjecture. Indeed, let s be an exponent over a
798: field L of characteristic p > 3 with [L : Fp ] a power of two, and suppose that
799: s is three-valued with values 0, A, and B. Write A = αpa , B = βpb , and
800: A − B = γpc with p ∤ α, β, γ. Now assume Conjecture 6.8 holds, so that 2 is
801: a differential multiplicity of s. Since we must be in case (ii) of Theorem 5.2,
802: this shows that |αβ| = 2 and a = b = 12 [L : Fp ]. Since the nonzero spectral
803: values A and B are opposites in sign  p (as was seen in our proof of Theorem
804: 5.2), this means that |A − B| = 3 |L|, but since p > 3, this contradicts
805: the fact that |γ| = 1, which also must hold in case (ii) of Theorem 5.2.
806: Thus, if Conjecture 6.8 is true, then Helleseth’s Conjecture will be true in
807: all characteristics p > 3. Since Helleseth’s Conjecture is already proved in
808: characteristic 2 and 3 (see Corollary 5.4), it would then be fully established.
