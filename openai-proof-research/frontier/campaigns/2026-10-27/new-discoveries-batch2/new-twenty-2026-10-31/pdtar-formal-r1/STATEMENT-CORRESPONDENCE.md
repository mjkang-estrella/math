Encoding choices:

* Vertices are in a finite `Type` in universe 0; subsets are `Finset`. The endpoint uses mathlib's `completeBipartiteGraph (Fin s) (Fin t)` on the sum type, without redefining that graph.
* `closed G S` is precisely membership in S or adjacency to a member of S. `obs 0 = S`, `obs (n+1) = propagate G (closed G S) n`, and `propagate` iterates simultaneous `step`. This avoids a compiler recursion implementation and preserves the source's round indexing.
* `step` uses a unique-unobserved-neighbour predicate, permitting an already observed u in the existential branch because those vertices are retained anyway. `Fidelity.mem_step_literal` proves the exact equivalence to the source's singleton-difference equation. No fort definition is used to define power domination.
* `IsPD` means that some literal round equals `univ`. The source begins with a nonempty initial set. `Fidelity.empty_not_isPD` proves that the empty set cannot satisfy this predicate on any nonempty graph. The empty vertex type is allowed by the requested all-finite-types quantification; it has one PD set and a one-vertex TAR graph. It cannot be TAR-isomorphic to any positive K(s,t), whose full set and one-vertex-deleted set are distinct PD sets.
* `IsFort` says a fort is nonempty and every outside vertex adjacent to one fort vertex is adjacent to a different fort vertex. `Fidelity.isFort_card_literal` proves equivalence to the cardinality-not-one definition. `FortDuality.fort_duality` is a proved theorem derived from rounds and finite stabilization.
* `tar` has exactly the PD-set subtype as its vertex type; adjacency is cardinality 1 of the symmetric difference. The graph structure's symmetry and looplessness are proved.
* `TARUnique` quantifies over every `W : Type` with finite enumeration and decidable equality, and every graph H on W without isolated vertices. A TAR isomorphism implies a base-graph isomorphism. It adds no connectedness or equal-order assumption. The isomorphism direction is reversed from the source's displayed order using symmetry, which does not change the property.
* Every finite graph on any finite type has an enumeration equivalence to `Fin n` (n its cardinality). Transporting adjacency and finsets along that equivalence transports every round, PD sets, and TAR adjacency. Thus all finite types in universe 0 include representatives of all finite graph isomorphism classes, including finite types originally presented in other universes. We use the direct all-finite-types encoding, rather than an additional unproved Fin-only transport lemma.
* `CompleteBetween K A B` records a disjoint covering ordered partition and the exact cross-edge adjacency iff. It avoids the ill-typed draft's graph definition on possibly overlapping parts. Swapping A and B is proved in `completeBetween_swap`. All completed core statements use arbitrary ordered partitions.
* The core family relation `SamePD K H` is pointwise iff, with no graph-isomorphism assertion built into its definition. `ReductionRemaining` explicitly retains the unproved bridge from TAR isomorphism to a relabelled graph on the target type with the same PD sets. `CoreRemaining` retains the unproved general core equality. These must not be treated as completed mathematics in Lean.
