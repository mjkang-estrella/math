106:     It was conjectured by Jackson and Ordaz [24] that the (needed) slight strengthening κ(G) > α(G)
107: in fact ensures that G is pancyclic. Following a recent breakthrough by Draganić, Munhá Correia,
108: and Sudakov [13], who proved an asymptotically optimal version of this conjecture, it has been
109: completely resolved (for large graphs) by Letzter [26]. It is natural to ask whether the same (or,
110: possibly, slightly strengthened — in particular, n must be odd) condition also implies Cn (G) = C(G).
111: We prove that this is indeed the case up to a multiplicative constant factor, provided κ(G) is not
112: too small as a function of |V (G)|.

185: ity (namely, Theorems 1.1 and 1.9) are trivial for bipartite graphs. Indeed, if G is a bipartite graph

187: obviously Hamiltonian. In an attempt to devise an effective variant of the Chvátal-Erdős criterion
188: for bipartite graphs, the following parameter was defined by Ash [3].

190: Definition 1.11. Let G = (X ∪ Y, E) be a balanced bipartite graph, i.e. |X| = |Y |. An independent
191: set A ⊆ X ∪ Y is said to be balanced if |A ∩ X| = |A ∩ Y |. The balanced independence number of G,
192: denoted αBIP (G), is the largest size of a balanced independent set in G.
