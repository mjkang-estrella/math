Adversarial proof-body audit. Theorem 2.6's printed lower-bound proof describes a coloring and calls the lower bound obvious; a construction by itself does not establish necessity. Its reference to consecutive indices of K_n also should not be read as a hidden n>=3 hypothesis. Here is a full repair of the needed lower bound, avoiding trust in that exposition. In the attached-copy graph, write I_i for copy i minus its root r_i. No vertex is adjacent to both a vertex in I_i and one in I_j for i!=j. Therefore the palettes P_i used on I_i are pairwise disjoint in a dominated coloring. Each nonempty class restricted to I_i has a dominator in its own copy, since every neighbor of an interior vertex lies in that copy. Restrict these classes to I_i and add {r_i} as one fresh class, dominated by a neighbor inside H (H connected of order at least two). This is a dominated coloring of H with |P_i|+1 colors. Hence |P_i|>=chi_dom(H)-1. Summing disjoint palettes proves the printed lower bound for all connected H of order >=2, including the present clique specialization.
