345884 Let $a_0=1$, and for each positive integer $n$, let $a_n$ be the number of triangulations of a regular $n$-gon such that every internal vertex has degree at least $6$. Define the generating function

$$
A(x)=1+
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_nx^n.
$$

Let $b_n$ be the dimension of the invariant subspace in the $n$-th tensor power of the $7$-dimensional fundamental representation of $G_2$, and define

$$
B(x)=1+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_nx^n.
$$

Kuperberg's conjecture. The exponential growth rate of the triangulation numbers is

$$
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{a_n}=\frac{7}{B(1/7)}.
$$

Equivalently, the radius of convergence of $A(x)$ is exactly $B(1/7)/7$. Kuperberg's identity $B(x)=A(xB(x))$ gives the corresponding upper bound on the growth rate, and the conjecture asserts that this bound is sharp.

364589 Let $P$ be a set of $m$ points and let $\mathcal{L}$ be a set of $n$ lines in the plane. **Mirzaei--Suk strengthening.** For any set of points $P_0$ and any set of lines $\mathcal{L}_0$ in the plane, there is a constant $\varepsilon=\varepsilon(P_0,\mathcal{L}_0)>0$ such that the maximum number of incidences between $n$ points and $n$ lines in the plane containing no subconfiguration isomorphic to $(P_0,\mathcal{L}_0)$ is $O(n^{4/3-\varepsilon})$.

365513 Let $n$, $k$, and $s$ be positive integers with $k\geq 2$, and let $\mathcal{F}\subseteq \binom{[n]}{k}$ be an $s$-almost $1$-intersecting family such that

$$
\bigcap_{F\in\mathcal{F}}F=\emptyset.
$$

Assume that $n$ is sufficiently large. **Alishahi–Taherkhani's conjecture.** If $\mathcal{F}$ has maximum size, then

$$
\mathcal{F}\cong\left\\{F\in\binom{[n]}{k}:1\in F,\\ \left|F\cap[k+1]\right|\geq 2\right\\}\cup\mathcal{A}\cup\left\\{[k+1]\setminus\left\\{1\right\\}\right\\},
$$

where $\mathcal{A}$ is an $s$-subset of

$$
\left\\{F\in\binom{[n]}{k}:F\cap[k+1]=\left\\{1\right\\}\right\\}.
$$

This conjectures the extremal structure of maximum-sized almost intersecting families with empty total intersection, extending the asymptotically optimal upper bound established by Alishahi and Taherkhani for $t=1$.
