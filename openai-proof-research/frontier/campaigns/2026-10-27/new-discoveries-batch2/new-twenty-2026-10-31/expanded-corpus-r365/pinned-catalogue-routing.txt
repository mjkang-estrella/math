&emsp;[Hellinger contraction with arbitrary Boolean output bias](preprints/Hellinger-contraction-with-arbitrary-Boolean-output-bias-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

We prove the Hellinger conjecture for Boolean functions on the uniform discrete cube, with arbitrary output bias. For a Boolean function of mean <i>m</i> and every $`\rho\in[-1,1]`$, the loss $`\sqrt{1-m^2}-\mathbb E\sqrt{1-(T_\rho f)^2}`$ is at most $`1-\sqrt{1-\rho^2}`$, with equality for signed coordinates. The proof combines asymmetric dimension induction, a calibrated noise-semigroup energy estimate, and finite exact arithmetic certificates. The Hellinger inequality also yields the Courtade–Kumar information bound.

**176. The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture with an edge-count bound.** Proves the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture: for every finite simple graph <i>H</i> with <i>h</i> ≥ 1 edges and at most <i>n</i> vertices, its appearance threshold in $`G(n,p)`$ is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, with universal <i>C</i>. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of <i>H</i> has expected copy count at least 1/2. ([Lean](lean/docs/176.md))

&emsp;[The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture](preprints/The-second-Kahn-Kalai-conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture. For every finite simple graph <i>H</i> with <i>h</i> ≥ 1 edges and at most <i>n</i> vertices, the threshold for $`G(n,p)`$ to contain an ordinary copy of <i>H</i> is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, where <i>C</i> is universal. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of <i>H</i> has expected copy count at least one half.
