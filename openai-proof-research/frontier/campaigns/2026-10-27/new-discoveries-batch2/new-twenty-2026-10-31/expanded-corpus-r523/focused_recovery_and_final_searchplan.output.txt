                                                 Abstract



      Balogh, Barát, Gerbner, Gyárfás, and Sárközy made the following conjecture. Let G be a graph
      on n vertices with minimum degree at least 3n/4. Then for every 2-edge-colouring of G, the
      vertex set V (G) may be partitioned into two vertex-disjoint cycles, one of each colour.
