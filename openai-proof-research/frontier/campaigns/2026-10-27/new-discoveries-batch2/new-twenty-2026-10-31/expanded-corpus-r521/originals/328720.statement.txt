Let $f(n)$ denote the minimum possible length of the shortest cycle in a tournament on $n$ vertices. **Asymptotic shortest-cycle conjecture.** For all $n\ge 4$,

$$
f(n)=\left(\frac{1}{3}+o(1)\right)n^2.
$$

This is the asymptotic form of the claim that the paper's construction is best possible: the available bounds give the matching leading constant, while the exact value remains unresolved in general.
