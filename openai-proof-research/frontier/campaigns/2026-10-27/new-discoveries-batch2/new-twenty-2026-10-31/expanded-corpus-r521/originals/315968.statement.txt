For a compact set $D\subseteq\mathbb{R}^2$, an integer $n\ge
3$, and points $p_1,\dots,p_n\in D$, let

$$
m(p_1,\dots,p_n)=\min_{1\le i<j<k\le n}\operatorname{area}(p_ip_jp_k),
$$

where
$\operatorname{area}(p_ip_jp_k)$ denotes the area of the triangle with vertices $p_i,p_j,p_k$,
taken to be $0$ when the three points are collinear. Define

$$
\Delta_D(n)=\max_{p_1,\dots,p_n\in D} m(p_1,\dots,p_n),
$$

the maximum being attained because $D$ is compact and $m$ is continuous.

If $D$ and $D'$ are compact convex sets of nonzero area, then there are
constants $0<c\le C$ depending on $D$ and $D'$ but not on $n$ such that

$$
c\,\Delta_{D'}(n)\le\Delta_D(n)\le C\,\Delta_{D'}(n)\qquad\text{for all }n\ge 3,
$$

since some affine image of $D'$ is contained in $D$ and an affine map multiplies
all triangle areas by a fixed factor. Consequently the growth rate of
$\Delta_D(n)$, up to constant factors, does not depend on the choice of compact convex $D$
of nonzero area; write
$\Delta(n)$ for this common growth rate, and write $\Delta_\square(n)$ when
$D$ is the unit square $[0,1]^2$.

Determine the asymptotic behaviour of $\Delta(n)$ as $n\to\infty$: exhibit an
explicit function $f$ with $\Delta(n)=\Theta\bigl(f(n)\bigr)$. In particular,
determine whether the limit

$$
\alpha=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\bigl(1/\Delta_\square(n)\bigr)}{\log n}
$$

exists, and determine its value, i.e. determine the least exponent
$\alpha$ such that $\Delta(n)=n^{-\alpha+o(1)}$.
