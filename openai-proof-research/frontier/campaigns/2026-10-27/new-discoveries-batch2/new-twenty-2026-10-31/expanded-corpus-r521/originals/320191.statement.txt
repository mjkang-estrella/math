Let $r\ge 2$ be fixed, and let $\operatorname{cp}(n,r)$ denote the minimum number of cliques in a partition of the $r$-element subsets of an $n$-element set.

**Asymptotic clique partition conjecture.** For every fixed $r\ge2$,

$$
\operatorname{cp}(n,r)=(1+o(1))n^{r/2}\quad\text{as }n\rightarrow\infty.
$$

The preceding lower bound gives $\operatorname{cp}(n,r)\ge(1-o(1))n^{r/2}$ for each fixed $r\ge2$, while the paper constructs an almost-complete family of $r$-sets with a clique partition of size $(1+o(1))n^{r/2}$. The conjecture asserts that this lower bound is asymptotically sharp for full clique partitions; extending the construction to full partitions without adding many more subcliques remains open.
