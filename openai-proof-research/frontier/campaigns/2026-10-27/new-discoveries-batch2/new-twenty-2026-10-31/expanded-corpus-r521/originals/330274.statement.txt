For a fixed positive integer $k$, let $\zeta_k(n)$ denote the asymptotic growth rate associated with the maximum number of $k$-dominating independent sets in an $n$-vertex graph, and let $\zeta_k(G)$ denote the corresponding growth rate for a graph $G$.

**Attainment conjecture.** For every $k$, there exists a graph $G$ such that

$$
\zeta_k(G)=\lim \zeta_k(n).
$$

This asks whether the global asymptotic growth rate is realized by a single graph rather than only approached by a sequence of graphs. The source gives no resolution.
