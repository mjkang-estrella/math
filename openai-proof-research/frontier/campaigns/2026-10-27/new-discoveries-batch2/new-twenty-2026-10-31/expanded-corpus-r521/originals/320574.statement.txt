For fixed $d$, let $\beta(n)=o(n)$ be a function, and let $\mathcal G'_{d,\beta}$ be the class of graphs $G$ whose degeneracy is at most $d$ and whose bandwidth is at most $\beta(|G|)$. A graph class is **always-good** when all sufficiently large members are $H$-good for every graph $H$. **The degeneracy-bandwidth conjecture.** For each fixed $d$ and each function $\beta(n)=o(n)$, the class $\mathcal G'_{d,\beta}$ is always-good. This is proposed as a weakening of a bounded-maximum-degree hypothesis; the source places it among open problems.
