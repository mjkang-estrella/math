Let $\psi(m)$ denote the minimum possible size of $\mathcal{F}_{\Delta}$ over all $m$-colourings of $\mathbb{N}^{(2)}$. For a natural number $n$, consider integers $m$ in the interval between consecutive triangular-number thresholds, subject to

$$
{n\choose 2}+\ell\leq m\leq {n+1\choose 2}-\ell.
$$

**Narayanan's conjecture.** For every $C>0$, there exists a positive integer $\ell$ such that, for every $m\in\mathbb{N}$ satisfying the displayed inequalities,

$$
\psi(m)\geq C\sqrt{2m}.
$$

This predicts that $\psi$ becomes arbitrarily large relative to the baseline $\sqrt{2m}$ when $m$ stays away from the interval boundaries. It remains open in the source, alongside the broader question of the asymptotic growth of $\psi$.
