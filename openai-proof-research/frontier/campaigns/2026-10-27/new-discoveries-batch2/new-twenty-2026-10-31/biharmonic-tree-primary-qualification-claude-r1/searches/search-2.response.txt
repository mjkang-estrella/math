The search didn't surface a source that states the tree extremal result directly, so I can't confirm which trees maximize or minimize the sum from these results. The closest hit is a 2022 paper on the biharmonic index, which covers trees and gives the identity behind your query. Its abstract defines the index as BH(G) = (1/2) Σ_u Σ_v d_B²(u,v) = n Σ_{i=2}^{n} 1/λ_i²(G), where the sum runs over the nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues. It also says the paper derives the extremal value on the biharmonic index for all graphs with diameter two, trees and firefly graphs. That is the likely source for the path/star result, but I haven't checked its theorem statements.

The second-order coherence connection comes from Yi et al. We show that these performance measures are closely related to the concept of biharmonic distance; the square of the biharmonic distance plays a similar role in the system performance as resistance distance plays in the performance of first-order systems. The same authors' work also gives analytical results for the performance measures and concepts for complete graphs, star graphs, cycles, and paths. Those results cover paths and stars but not general trees.
