Full arithmetic is in `logs/independent-signs.json`; the main result is `logs/math.log`. Matching sizes are 4,5,5,5,5. Selected sums are 1 and all other residual sums are strictly negative.

Here is a self-contained lower bound, independent of the source's proof. A decomposition of an r-regular graph into at most r matchings needs exactly r nonempty matchings, and each must be perfect, since every vertex has r incident edges and each part meets it at most once. If the first perfect matching has positive sums, their total is W = sum of all vertex weights > 0. The remaining graph is (r−1)-regular, so the sum of its edge sums is (r−1)W > 0. Every such edge has nonpositive sum by positivity of the first matching, a contradiction for r ≥ 2. Hence pmd ≥ r+1. Our graph is 4-regular and the certificate gives an upper bound 5, so **pmd(C_3 □ C_4) = 5 exactly**. The formal refutation needs only the upper bound.
