329377 Let $\ell\geq 2$ be a fixed constant. A graph is **$\ell K_2$-free** if it has no induced subgraph consisting of $\ell$ pairwise disjoint edges, and a graph is **$(\ell-1)$-connected** if deleting fewer than $\ell-1$ vertices leaves it connected.

**Algorithmic edge-dominating-cycle conjecture.** For every fixed $\ell\geq 2$, there is a polynomial-time algorithm that finds an edge-dominating cycle in every $(\ell-1)$-connected, $\ell K_2$-free graph.

This would extend the paper’s algorithmic results beyond $2K_2$-free graphs. The provided text presents it as a proposed direction, and gives no resolution for the general fixed-$\ell$ case. 

331065 Let $G$ be a graph with a **Kempe-coloring** ${\frak C}$ of size $k$, and let a **transversal** $T$ of ${\frak C}$ contain one vertex from each color class. A **clique minor traversed by** $T$ is a clique minor whose branch sets are each traversed by $T$. **The Kempe-coloring clique-minor conjecture.** For every transversal $T$ of ${\frak C}$, there exists a clique minor traversed by $T$. The source introduces this as a common generalization of two open projects concerning Kempe-colorings and clique minors, and does not report a resolution. 

333068 Let $E$ be a finite set, let $\mu$ be a unitary connectivity function on $E$, and let $\mu\downarrow X$ denote the connectivity-function minor obtained by the operation defined in the source. **Finite-obstruction conjecture.** There is a finite set $S$ of connectivity functions such that, if $\mu$ is a unitary connectivity function on $E$, then $\mu$ is κ-matroidal if and only if, for every $X\subseteq E$, the connectivity function $\mu\downarrow X$ is not isomorphic to a member of $S$. This is presented as a weaker version of the preceding 4-cycle obstruction conjecture. The supplied text does not resolve whether such a finite obstruction set exists. 

333766 Let $\chi(G)$ denote the chromatic number of a graph $G$. **The weak Hedetniemi conjecture.** For every integer $n$, there exists an integer $f(n)$ such that whenever $G$ and $H$ are graphs satisfying

$$
\chi(G)\geq f(n),\qquad \chi(H)\geq f(n),
$$

one has

$$
\chi(G\times H)\geq n.
$$

This is a weaker assertion than Hedetniemi's conjecture and is introduced as relevant to the survey's discussion of adjoint functors. The supplied text gives no evidence that it has been resolved. 

334518 Let $\alpha_G(\lambda)$ denote the occupancy fraction of a graph $G$ in the hard-core model, and let $H_{3,8}$ be the $(3,8)$-cage, also known as the Levi graph or Tutte–Coxeter graph. **Tutte–Coxeter occupancy-fraction conjecture.** For every $3$-regular graph $G$ of girth at least $7$ and every $\lambda>0$,

$$
\alpha_G(\lambda)\leq\alpha_{H_{3,8}}(\lambda).
$$

The conjecture identifies the $(3,8)$-cage as the maximizer in this girth-constrained problem and is open. 

335867 A graph $G$ is **1-unique** if, for every vertex $v$ of $G$, there is a tree-depth labeling using labels from $\{1,\dots,\operatorname{td}(G)\}$ in which $v$ is the unique vertex receiving label $1$. A graph is **critical** when it is $k$-critical for its tree-depth $k$: every proper minor has smaller tree-depth.

**Every-critical-graph conjecture.** Every critical graph is 1-unique.

The conjecture is motivated by the fact that every critical graph with tree-depth at most $4$ is 1-unique, and that critical graphs produced by certain known constructions are also 1-unique. Its status is not resolved by the supplied text. 

336392 Let $G$ be a graph, let $\therefore$ denote the graph-minor relation, and let $b3(G)$ be its homological dimension. For a positive integer $d$, consider the complete graph $K_{d+2}$. **Homological dimension conjecture.** For every positive integer $d$ and graph $G$,

$$
K_{d+2}\prec G \iff \gamma(G)\geq d.
$$

The result is proved in the paper for $d=1,2,3$, detecting $K_3$, $K_4$, and $K_5$ minors; the conjecture extends this correspondence to all complete minors. 

336785 Let $F$ be a fixed strict digraph. An **$F$-subdivision** is a digraph obtained from $F$ by replacing each arc $ab$ with a directed $(a,b)$-path; the associated problem asks whether an input digraph $D$ contains an $F$-subdivision as a subdigraph. **Bang-Jensen et al.'s dichotomy conjecture.** For every digraph $F$, the $F$-subdivision problem is polynomial-time solvable or NP-complete. This conjecture concerns the algorithmic classification of fixed-digraph subdivision problems; the paper gives further support to it and, for digraphs of order four, classifies all but five exceptions. 

336931 Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices, and let $\mu(G)$ denote its spectral radius. A graph has **no $K_p$ minor** when no graph obtained from it by vertex deletions, edge deletions, and edge contractions is isomorphic to $K_p$. **McCarty's conjecture.** For every integer $p\ge3$, every graph $G$ on $n\ge p-1$ vertices with $\mu(G)\le p-2$ has at most $(p-2)n-{p-1\choose2}$ edges.

The conjecture is also recorded as an open problem and is implied by a result cited in the source. No resolution of the conjecture itself is stated. 

338040 Let $H$ and $H'$ be graphs with $m_2(H)\geq m_2(H')$. **Asymmetric Ramsey-density conjecture.** If neither $H$ nor $H'$ is a matching and at least one of $H$ or $H'$ contains a cycle, then

$$
m(F)>m_2(H,H')
$$

for every Ramsey graph $F\in R(H,H')$. This conjecture would, together with asymmetric random-Ramsey results, imply the expected Ramsey-infinite classification for unordered graph pairs containing cycles; the source gives no resolution. 

339024 A **planar graph** is a graph that can be drawn in the plane without crossings; two triangles are **adjacent** if they have at least one common edge. A graph is **4-choosable** if, for every assignment of a list of four colors to each vertex, it has a proper coloring choosing a color from each vertex's list.

**Wang–Lih conjecture.** Every planar graph without adjacent triangles is 4-choosable.

Wang and Lih proved the analogous statement for planar graphs without intersecting triangles, while the conjecture for graphs without adjacent triangles remains open. The paper studies the stronger correspondence-coloring setting, in which ordinary list coloring is generalized by allowing edge-specific matchings between color lists. 

339570 Let $G$ be a finite simple graph. A list assignment $L$ assigns a list of colors to each vertex; it is a $k$-assignment when $|L(v)|=k$ for every $v\in V(G)$. An equitable $L$-coloring is a proper coloring using colors from the lists such that each color appears on at most $\lceil |V(G)|/k\rceil$ vertices. The graph is equitably $k$-choosable when it has an equitable $L$-coloring for every $k$-assignment $L$.

**List Hajnál–Szemerédi conjecture.** Every graph $G$ is equitably $k$-choosable when $k\geq \Delta(G)+1$.

This is the list-coloring analogue of the Hajnál–Szemerédi theorem, which asserts equitable $k$-colorability for $k\geq\Delta(G)+1$. The source attributes the conjecture to Kostochka, West, and the third author; its resolution status is not specified in the supplied text. 

341117 Let $s$ be an integer. An edge $\lambda$-choosable graph is a graph whose line graph is colourable from every list assignment whose list-size pattern is the partition $\lambda$. **The bounded-part edge list-colouring conjecture.** For every integer $s$, there is an integer $k(s)$ such that, whenever $k\geq k(s)$, $G$ is edge $k$-colourable, and $\lambda$ is a partition of $k$ in which every part has size at most $s$, then $G$ is edge $\lambda$-choosable. This is proposed as a weaker version of the List Colouring Conjecture; the supplied text gives no resolution evidence. 

341719 Let $G$ be a critical multigraph, meaning that $\chi'(H)<\chi'(G)$ for every proper subgraph $H$ of $G$, and let $m$ be an odd integer with $m\geq 3$.

**Jakobsen's bounded-order conjecture.** If

$$
\chi'(G)>\frac{m\Delta(G)+(m-3)}{m-1},
$$

then $G$ has at most $m-2$ vertices.

The source attributes this conjecture to Jakobsen and states that Andersen proved it weaker than the Goldberg–Seymour conjecture. It is therefore solved by the result established in the paper. 

342104 Let $k$ and $r$ be positive integers. A **$k$-uniform hypergraph** is a hypergraph whose edges are $k$-element subsets of its vertex set, and a **tight cycle** is a cyclically ordered sequence of vertices in which every $k$ consecutive vertices form an edge; single vertices are also regarded as tight cycles. **Gyárfás's conjecture.** There is a constant $c=c(k,r)$ such that the vertices of every $r$-edge-coloured complete $k$-uniform hypergraph can be partitioned into at most $c$ monochromatic tight cycles. The paper confirms this conjecture, so the asserted bounded partition exists for all positive integers $k$ and $r$. 

342129 For a matroid $M$, let $\d(M)$ denote its density, and call $M$ **strictly $t$-density-critical** when $d(M)>t$ while every proper minor $N$ of $M$ satisfies $d(N)\leq t$. **Finiteness conjecture.** For every $t\geq 0$, there are finitely many strictly $t$-density-critical matroids. The case $t=2$ is known from the explicit classification of the ten strictly $2$-density-critical matroids; the assertion for all $t\geq 0$ remains open. 

343253 A graph is **claw-free** if it has no induced subgraph isomorphic to $K_{1,3}$. For vertices $a$ and $b$, an $(a,b)$-path is a path with endpoints $a$ and $b$. A **Tutte path** is an $(a,b)$-path whose off-path components have at most three neighbours on the path, and a path is **maximal** if it cannot be extended while preserving the relevant path conditions.

**Claw-free Tutte-path conjecture.** For every pair of vertices $a,b$ of a connected claw-free graph, there is a maximal $(a,b)$-path which is a Tutte path.

The paper presents this as a seemingly stronger reformulation of Jackson's conjecture and proves that it is equivalent to the same open problem. Thus the assertion remains open despite the equivalence with the other formulations. 

343634 Let $M$ be a connected $k$-polymatroid and let $N$ be a connected minor of $M$. A **connected minor-preserving deletion or contraction** is a deletion or contraction that remains connected and retains $N$ as a minor. **Connectivity conjecture.** When $N \neq M$, there is an element $e$ of $E(M)-E(N)$ such that $M\backslash e$ or $M/e$ is connected and has $N$ as a minor.

The paper proves this assertion for $2$-polymatroids and suggests that the same result should hold for all $k>2$; its status is therefore unresolved in the supplied source. 

347620 A connected graph $G$ is **double-critical** if $\chi(G)=t$ and

$$
\chi\bigl(G\setminus\{x,y\}\bigr)=t-2
$$

for every edge $xy\in E(G)$. **Double-Critical Graph Conjecture.** For every integer $t\geq3$, the only double-critical $t$-chromatic graph is the complete graph $K_t$. This is the $s=2$ case of the Erdős–Lovász Tihany Conjecture and is stated as an open problem; its classification remains unresolved in general. 

381347 For positive integers $s$ and $t$, let $K^*_{s,t}$ be the complete join of $K_s$ and $\overline{K_t}$. A graph is **$K^*_{s,t}$-minor-free** if it contains no minor isomorphic to $K^*_{s,t}$. For a graph $G$ and integer $r\geqslant1$, let $\operatorname{wcol}_r(G)$ denote its weak $r$-colouring number.

**Polynomial weak colouring-number conjecture.** There exists a function $f$ such that for every $K^*_{s,t}$-minor-free graph $G$ and every $r\geqslant1$,

$$
\operatorname{wcol}_r(G)\leqslant f(s,t)\,r^s.
$$

The conjecture would generalise the displayed bounds for $K^*_{2,t}$- and $K^*_{3,t}$-minor-free graphs and would give polynomial weak colouring-number bounds for these minor-closed graph classes. The source gives no resolution status.
