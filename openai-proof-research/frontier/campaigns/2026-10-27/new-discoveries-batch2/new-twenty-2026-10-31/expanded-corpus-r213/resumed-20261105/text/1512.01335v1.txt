 5      Convex Crossing Number in Lower Dimensional Space
In this Section, we show that c∗3 = 3. Recall that c∗3 denotes the 3-dimensional convex
crossing number of K63 . Since we obtain from Theorem 4 that cm   3 = 3, it proves that
Conjecture 1 is true for d = 3.

◮ Lemma 16. The number of pairwise crossing hyperedges of K63 is 3 when all the vertices
of K63 are in convex as well as general position in ℜ3 .

Proof. Let A be the set of vertices of K63 that are in convex as well as general position in
ℜ3 . Let D(A) denote the Gale Transformation of A. Since the points in A are in general
position, Lemma 6 shows that the 6 vectors in D(A) are in general position in ℜ2 . Since
the points in A are also in convex position, Lemma 8 implies that these vectors can be
partitioned by a line l passing through the origin in two possible ways, i.e., the number of
vectors in the opposite open half-spaces created by l can be either 4 and 2, or 3 and 3.
Note that the second case is also known as a proper linear separation that corresponds to
a crossing pair of 2-simplices spanned by the points in A. Without loss of generality, let us
assume that l partitions the vectors in D(A) in such a way that one of the open half-spaces
created by l contains 4 vectors and the other contains 2 vectors. We rotate l clockwise
using the origin as the axis of rotation until one vector changes its side. Since Lemma 8
Anurag Anshu, Rahul Gangopadhyay, Saswata Shannigrahi, and Satyanarayana Vusirikala


shows that l cannot partition the vectors such that there exists 1 vector on one of its side,
this new partition obtained by rotating l is a proper linear separation. We again rotate l
clockwise using the origin as the axis of rotation until one vector changes its side to obtain
a new partition having 4 vectors on one side and 2 on the other side. We continue rotating
l clockwise till we reach the first partition to obtain three proper linear separations of the
vectors in D(A).                                                                            ◭

We obtain from Theorem 4 that the number of pairwise crossing 3-simplices when all the
vertices of K84 are placed on the 4-dimensional moment curve is 13. An alternate way to
state Conjecture 1 for d = 4 is as follows: the number of proper linear separations of the
vectors in the Gale Transformation of 8 points in convex as well as general position in ℜ4 is
at least 13. The following observation obtained by Lemma 10 might be useful to prove this.

◮ Observation 17. Any hyperplane h of the form ax + by + cz = 0 that creates a proper
linear separation of the vectors in the Gale Transformation of 8 points on the 4-dimensional
moment curve satisfies that a, b, c 6= 0 and exactly one of the a, b and c is positive.
