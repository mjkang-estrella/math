## 339684: exact language counting

Marberg–Pawlowski arXiv:1806.11208v3, Section 6.3, Conjecture 6.2. Earlier v1/v2 PDFs were retained and inspected at the corresponding closing section; no claimed removal. The source reports checks through rank 6. The ordinary and twisted words are **sets after replacing t1' by t1**, so summing raw word counts is unsound. type_d.py instead determinizes a finite automaton whose states are sets of twisted involutions. Its transitions are the twisted weak-order actions: if theta(s)y=ys use ys, otherwise theta(s)ys; allow only length-increasing moves. Merge generators 0 and 1 as a single letter. Count distinct paths in the determinized acyclic automaton, and compare with the integer hook-length formula.

This reproduces 5775 at rank 5 and gives equality at ranks 7, 9 and 10 (both twists at 9 and 10). At rank 10 the ordinary count is 1942022240272816871468931531900, and the twisted count is 388404448054563374293786306380. These finite checks do NOT resolve the conjecture. Remaining task: a uniform enumeration/bijection for collapsed involution-word languages in all ranks. A Lean certificate for one finite rank would be a parameter check and earn zero credit.

## 331973: exact convex configurations

Anshu et al. arXiv:1512.01335v3, Conjecture 1, and Gangopadhyay–Khan arXiv:1908.04654v6. The latter proves d=4 (Theorem 3) and explicitly leaves d>4 open in Section 8. This is NOT a full resolution of the reserved statement. All v1/v2 original PDFs were fetched in addition to the current body.

geometric_search.py samples 2d distinct signed permutations of (1,...,d). All points have equal Euclidean norm, hence each is an exposed convex-hull vertex (inner product with that point uniquely maximizes there). Reject configurations with any vanishing (d+1)-point homogeneous determinant. The exact Bareiss determinants give signs of every affine circuit on d+2 vertices. A balanced partition is crossing if compatible signed circuits cover every vertex: summing such dependencies gives a strictly positive dependence of the required sign; conversely every positive dependence decomposes into conformal circuits. This is a mathematical justification of the diagnostic, not a formal certificate.

For d=5 the baseline is 45 crossing pairs; for d=6 it is 181. This is a seeded sample of convex order types, NOT exhaustive. General position and strict relative interiors matter; degenerate configurations are not counted. No failure found in completed runs. Remaining route: a convex realizable order type with larger crossing count, then explicit rational points and a sound exact Lean checker, or a full extremal proof. General continuum formalization makes a proof significantly more expensive than a finite exact refutation.
