Remark 23. The above implies that the Alice-In-Chains variant has a maximum attainable prob-
ability of n2 . It also proves an interesting result about the form of optimal strategies: every optimal
strategy in the needle in a haystack setting is such that every element c ∈ [n−1] has an image which
is present in all of the strategy’s classes.
