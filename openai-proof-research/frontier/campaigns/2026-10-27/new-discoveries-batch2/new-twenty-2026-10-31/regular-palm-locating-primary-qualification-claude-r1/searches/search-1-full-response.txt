The search found the paper this query most likely refers to, but not a resolution of the conjecture. "On the Locating Chromatic Number of Trees" (arXiv:1912.05775) lists Yusuf Hafidh as a submitter, and the results don't mention Baskoro as an author.

- **Asymptotic result:** The paper shows that for each fixed k, the locating chromatic number of a regular palm is Θ(n^{1/k}) (Theorem 5.1). As k grows with n fixed, χ_L(S_n(k)) tends to ⌈log_3(n/4)⌉ + 3.
- **Exact cases:** For k = 1 and k = 2, the paper gives exact values, and for k = 3 it gives an asymptotic form, (1 + o(1)) times the cube root of 4n.
- **The conjecture:** Conjecture 5.1 covers k ≥ 4 and states that the locating chromatic number is (1 + o(1)) times ((k−1)/2) times the k-th root of 4n (χ_L(S_n(k)) = (1 + o(1)) ((k−1)/2) ∜[k]{4n}, per the paper's notation).
