Let $a$ and $b$ be two co-prime integers. Let $\mathcal{L}_{a,b}$ be the poset of lattice paths in the rectangle $a\times b$, and let $\mathrm{Dyck}_{a,b}$ be the poset of $(a,b)$-rational Dyck paths. **Rational Dyck-path derived-equivalence conjecture.** The poset $A_{a+b}\times\mathrm{Dyck}_{a,b}$ is derived equivalent to the poset $\mathcal{L}_{a,b}$. This conjectural relation is motivated by the equality of cardinalities suggested by the rational Dyck-path enumeration formula, although the two posets are not isomorphic; the source gives no resolution status.
