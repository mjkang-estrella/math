Let $p\in\{2,3,\ldots,8\}$, and let $G$ be a bipartite graph with $V\geq 2p-5$ vertices. A graph has no $K_p$-minor when $K_p$ cannot be obtained from it by taking a subgraph and contracting edges.

**Bipartite excluded-minor extremal conjecture.** A bipartite graph on $V\geq 2p-5$ vertices with no $K_p$-minor has at most

$$
(p-2)V-(p-2)^2
$$

edges.

This conjecture extends the known extremal bounds for graphs excluding complete-graph minors to bipartite graphs through $p=8$. Its resolution is not supplied in the source.
