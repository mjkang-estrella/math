Let $G$ be a $t$-perfect graph, meaning that its stable set polytope is determined by non-negativity, edge, and odd-cycle inequalities. **Four-colour conjecture for $t$-perfect graphs.** Every $t$-perfect graph is $4$-colourable. The fractional chromatic number of every $t$-perfect graph is at most $3$, and examples are known that are not $3$-colourable; whether four colours always suffice remains open.
