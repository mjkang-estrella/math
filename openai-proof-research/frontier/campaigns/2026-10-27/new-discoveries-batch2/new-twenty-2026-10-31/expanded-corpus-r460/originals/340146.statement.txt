Let $D$ be an oriented simple graph. For a nonempty proper subset $S\subset V(D)$, let $N_k^+(S)$ be the set of vertices $u$ such that $\min_{s\in S}d(s,u)=k$, where $d(s,u)$ is directed distance, and define $d_k^+(S)=|N_k^+(S)|$. **Subset version of Seymour's Second Neighborhood Conjecture.** Every oriented simple graph $D$ contains a nonempty, proper subset $S$ of its vertices such that

$$
d_1^+(S)\leq d_2^+(S).
$$

This is a new conjecture related to Seymour's Second Neighborhood Conjecture. The paper introduces it as a subset perspective and investigates related bounds for directed graphs, while the conjecture itself remains open.
