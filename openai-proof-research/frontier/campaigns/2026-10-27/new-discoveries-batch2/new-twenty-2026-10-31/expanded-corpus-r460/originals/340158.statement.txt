Let $M$ be a set of $m$ segments drawn in the plane. An **EFL coloring** of $M$ with $k$ colors is a function

$$
f:P(M)\rightarrow\{1,\ldots,k\}
$$

such that if two intersection points $p_1,p_2$ belong to the same segment, then $f(p_1)\neq f(p_2)$. **Segment Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture.** The set $M$ has an EFL coloring with $m$ colors.

This is the segment specialization of the geometric Erdős–Faber–Lovász coloring conjecture. The source presents it as a conjecture; its status is not resolved in the supplied text.
