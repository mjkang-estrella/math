Let $G$ and $H$ be finite undirected simple **triangle-free** graphs, meaning that neither graph contains a copy of $K_3$. Let $G\times H$ denote their tensor product and let $\chi(G)$ denote chromatic number. **Triangle-free Hedetniemi conjecture.** 

$$
\chi(G\times H)=\min\{\chi(G),\chi(H)\}.
$$

This is presented as a weaker version of Hedetniemi's conjecture, motivated by the fact that the known counterexamples contain large cliques. The source does not provide a resolution of this triangle-free case.
