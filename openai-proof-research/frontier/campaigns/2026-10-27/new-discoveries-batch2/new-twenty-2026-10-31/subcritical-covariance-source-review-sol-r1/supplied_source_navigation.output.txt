We obtain precise asymptotics of its expectation and variance, and show it obeys a law of large numbers. We also prove that the scaled fluctuations of the susceptibility around its deterministic limit converge to a Gaussian law. We further extend our results to higher moments of the component size of a random vertex, and prove that they are jointly asymptotically normal. Introduction\n\nThe susceptibility χ(G) of a graph G(deterministic or random) is deﬁned\n\nas the mean size of the component containing a random vertex. (As is well\n\nknown, for random graphs of the random-cluster model, this, or rather its\n\nexpectation, corresponds to the magnetic susceptibility in Ising and Potts\n\nmodels.) If G has n vertices and components C 1,...,C K, where K is the\n\nnumber of components, then thus\n\nχ(G) =\n\nK\n\nX\n\ni=1\n\n|C i|\n\nn|C i|=1\n\nn\n\nK\n\nX\n\ni=1\n\n|C i|2.(1.1)\n\nWe deﬁne, for integers k≥1,\n\nS k(G) :=\n\nK\n\nX\n\ni=1\n\n|C i|k.(1.2)\n\nThus χ(G) = n−1 S 2(G), and similarly n−1 S m+1 is the m th moment of the\n\nsize of the component containing a random vertex. (Note that by choosing\n\na uniform random vertex, we bias the components by their sizes. The mean\n\nsize of a uniformly chosen random component is n/K, which is diﬀerent and\n\nwhich will not be treated here.)\n\nThe purpose of this paper is to study χ(G(n, p)), or equivalently S 2(G(n, p))\n\nfor the standard Erd˝os–R´enyi random graph G(n, p) with n vertices where\n\neach possible edge appears with probability p, independently of all other\n\nedges; we will also give extensions to S k(G(n, p)) for larger k.\n\nWe consider asymptotics as n→ ∞, with p=p(n) a function of n.
