2    Inductive colouring construction
For a property P (e.g. K4 -uniqueness) of edge-colourings of Kn , we call an edge-colouring of Kn
satisfying P a P-colouring of Kn .
Definition 2.1. For a property P of edge-colourings of Kn , let rP (n) be the least number of colours
needed for a P-colouring of Kn .
As examples, we have rH (n) = rH-odd (n) and uH (n) = rH-unique (n).
Given P, we shall construct a P-colouring of Kn by induction on n. More precisely, given a P-
colouring of Kn , we aim to produce a P-colouring of Knm , for an appropriate choice of m = m(n),
with the help of an auxiliary edge-colouring of Km satisfying an often “weaker” property Q.
To do so, we make use of the following method of amalgamating an edge-colouring c of Kn with an
auxiliary edge-colouring d of Km to produce an edge-colouring of Knm , which we denote by c ⊗ d.
Here we take the vertex sets of Kn , Km , Knm to be [n], [m] and the Cartesian product [n] × [m]
respectively. We shall visualise the vertex set [n] × [m] in a grid with horizontal axis [n] and vertical
axis [m].
