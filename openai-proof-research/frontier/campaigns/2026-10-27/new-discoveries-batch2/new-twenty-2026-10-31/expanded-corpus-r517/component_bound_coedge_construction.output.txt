    In 2021, Haemers wrote a note [7] clarifying the history of the Hoffman bound. We call a
(co-)clique that meets the Hoffman bound a Hoffman (co-)clique. Note that in the literature a
Hoffman clique is also called a Delsarte clique.

Definition 4.2. (i) A Hoffman coloring in a strongly regular graph G is a partition of the vertex
     set into Hoffman co-cliques;

 (ii) A Hoffman spread in a strongly regular graph G is a partition of the vertex set into Hoffman
      cliques.
