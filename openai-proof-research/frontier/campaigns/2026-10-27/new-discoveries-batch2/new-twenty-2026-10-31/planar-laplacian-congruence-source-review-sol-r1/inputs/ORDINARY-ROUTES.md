## 349249: exact autocorrelation versus palindrome-prefix sets

For sigma∈S_k and 1≤d<k, an overlap permutation has length k+d. Its first and last length-k factors occupy consecutive value intervals of length k, translated copies of sigma. Their union covers all values of the overlap permutation. Their common positions number k-d and all other positions are distinct. Thus their value intervals intersect in exactly k-d values, and the translation difference is +d or -d. Equality on their common positions is therefore exactly

    sigma(i+d)=sigma(i)+d for all 1≤i≤k-d,

or the corresponding minus relation. At a given d both signs cannot occur, since a nonempty overlap would force d=-d over Z. Conversely either relation does produce a unique overlap: in the plus case put sigma in the first k positions and append the translated suffix from sigma+d. Compatibility is the displayed relation; the shared values are exactly [d+1,k], because the common positions supply k-d distinct values in that interval. The new values are [k+1,k+d]. The minus case follows by value complementation. Thus Omega(sigma,sigma) is the sum of x^d over the exact admissible shifts d, with coefficient one. This proves an all-k reduction; it does not count the realizable shift SETS.

For a binary palindrome w of length k+1, a prefix of length l is itself a palindrome iff it is a border. Indeed the full palindrome identifies its suffix of length l with the reversal of the prefix. A border of length l is equivalent to a period d=k+1-l, for 0≤d≤k. Hence b_(k+1) counts distinct period sets of binary palindromes of length k+1. The endpoint d=0 and d=k (one-letter borders) are compulsory and should be removed before comparison with permutation overlap shifts 1..k-1. This pays the k versus k+1 offset exactly.

First attempted completion: use the difference word f_i=sigma(i)-i. The positive overlap relation says f_(i+d)=f_i, i.e. d is a period. However it is NOT enough to use arbitrary integer words with those periods: sigma must be a permutation of [k], imposing a global range and distinctness condition. Negative overlaps instead impose periodicity of sigma(i)+i. Mixing both polarities needs separate treatment. A generic word-period theorem alone does not count the target.

Second attempted completion: reconstruct sigma by residue chains modulo a least admissible d. In the positive case, each positional chain maps with step +d to a value chain, and the chain starting values must be exactly [d]. A value≤d cannot appear after the first position of a chain; therefore each appears at one of the first d positions. The chain lengths constrain which residues may be paired. This gives a parameterized family of permutations with the chosen period, but additional larger shifts may be induced. What remains unpaid is exact realization WITHOUT EXTRA shifts for every candidate period set, plus the reverse map to a binary palindrome. Counting permutations with a period is not counting distinct full shift sets.

Third attempted completion: closures under common periods and Fine-Wilf-type gcd constraints. Those are necessary local overlap constraints, not proved sufficient for simultaneously realizing all shifts with the required signs and the permutation range. Likewise palindrome symmetry imposes its own constraints beyond arbitrary binary word periods. No full set-preserving bijection or equally indexed recurrence was obtained. A program enumerating sets was neither run nor used. Full conjecture PARK at exact two-way realization/classification, not at small-k evidence. A feasible literal formal endpoint would require finite permutation overlap objects and binary palindrome period-set cardinalities for ALL k≥3, not a computed table or only one direction.

    d_w=sum_(S⊆[m]) (-1)^(m-|S|) F(std(w_S)).

Proof: substitute the occurrence recursion in the right side. An occurrence on positions T appears once for every S⊇T. Its coefficient is sum_(S⊇T)(-1)^(m-|S|), zero for T proper and one for T=[m], by pairing S with S toggled at any fixed position outside T. This is symbolic finite-set cancellation, not numerical enumeration or a solver. All repeated identical standardized patterns are retained by their position subsets, which pays the occurrence multiplicity.
