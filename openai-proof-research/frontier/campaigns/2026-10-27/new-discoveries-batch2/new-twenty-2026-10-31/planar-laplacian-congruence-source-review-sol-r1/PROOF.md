Orient the sphere and each transverse dual edge consistently. Each vertex row of B is, up to a single globally fixed sign convention, the boundary of its corresponding dual face. The connected sphere embedding is cellular: a thickening of a spanning tree is a disk; adding each remaining disjoint edge band splits a complementary disk. The induced dual embedding has the same edge set and disk faces indexed by primal vertices. Bridge sides appear twice with opposite signs; loops and parallel edges are retained.
An integer circulation on the dual is a sum of integral directed simple cycles: reverse edges with negative coefficient, follow positive edges using conservation until a vertex repeats, remove the resulting cycle times the minimum positive coefficient, and repeat. Each removal strictly decreases the sum of absolute coefficients. A loop is a one-edge cycle; a pair of parallel edges can form a two-edge cycle. Each such embedded cycle is a Jordan curve on the sphere. Sum oriented face boundaries on one side. Every interior edge side cancels, including doubled bridge sides, and exactly the cycle remains. Hence every integer circulation is an integer facial-boundary sum. Thus cut(G)=flow(G*) integrally, not only over R.
