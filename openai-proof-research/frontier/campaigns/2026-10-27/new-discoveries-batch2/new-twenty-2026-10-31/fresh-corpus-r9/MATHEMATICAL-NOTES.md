## 330096: complete but routine source refutation

Use the source definitions: an OSODS is out-dominating and each outside vertex v can replace some u in S with arc u→v while preserving out-domination; an ISODS uses v→u instead. These parameters are not open total-domination numbers. Source Propositions 2.9 and 5.9, including their proofs, give both values ceil(2n/3) on the consistently directed n-cycle.

For a disjoint union D=D1⊔D2 and either parameter, every qualifying S restricts to a qualifying set on each component. Domination cannot come from a different component. A defender of an outside vertex must share its component, and replacing it changes nothing on other components. Conversely, the union of qualifying component sets qualifies. Consequently each minimum is additive. For three directed C4 components, n=12, minimum semidegree=1, and both minima equal 9, whereas ceil(2n/3)=8. This refutes the literal unrestricted Conjecture6.4. It is immediately subsumed by the source's own cycle results plus elementary disjoint-union additivity and is EXCLUDED under the campaign's significance rule. No Lean package was built.

For completeness, the cycle lower bound at n=4 needs no induction: two selected vertices cannot work. Opposite selected vertices leave two outsiders, and moving the only defender of either outsider removes domination from the other. Adjacent selected vertices fail out-domination at the vertex preceding that pair. One selected vertex fails domination; three selected vertices work for both defender conventions. This also verifies the local ingredient independently of the source's general cycle induction.

## 329766 and 329765: the asymptotic shortcut does not remove torsion

Fix k and suppose an N(k) works for every cyclic modulus n≥N(k). Given any modulus m and a k-subset A⊆Z_m\{0}, choose t with tm≥N(k). The map x mod m ↦ tx mod tm is an injective additive map and preserves nonzero elements and cardinality. Any ordering of its image lifts uniquely to an ordering of A. Two lifted partial sums coincide iff their images do, by injectivity. Thus a sequencing of the image sequences A. Conversely the all-moduli statement trivially implies the large-moduli statement. The fixed-k question is therefore equivalent to sequencing all k-subsets in all cyclic groups, not merely large prime moduli. Any counterexample embeds at arbitrarily large moduli. It also embeds at arbitrarily small density k/(tm), so a positive uniform density theorem in 329765 would imply the unrestricted main sequencing conjecture. This last equivalence is a useful obstruction to the entire proposed low-density route, not a resolution.

## 329898: kernel formulation and failed alternatives

The intended i,j,k are NONNEGATIVE (Theorem1), even though Conjecture1 and the normalized record abbreviate them to integers. Exploiting arbitrary negative indices is an ineligible wording correction. For positive a,b,c, a nonzero integer relation ax+by+cz=0 cannot have all coordinates strictly of the same sign. After changing signs, one coordinate is nonpositive and the other two nonnegative (zero coordinates can be placed on either side). Moving the former to the other side gives one of the three forbidden equalities with index sum |x|+|y|+|z|. The converse is immediate. Hence the decisive lemma is a sharp universal l1-short-vector bound for the rank-two integer kernel when max(a,b,c)<c_n.

Read source Theorem1, constructive appendix proof, Lemma1, the weaker Siegel bound in Lemma2, and the 2017 Bos recipe and proof context. The latter constructs convenient coprime-product frequencies and explicitly does not claim optimality. The source's pigeonhole/Siegel estimate only has the right growth order, not the required sharp constant, so applying it is not a proof. A second route enumerated all strictly increasing positive triples with maximum<c_n for n=1,…,8 and tested every signed kernel vector with l1 norm≤2n. Repeated coordinates immediately give a length-two relation; permutations do not change existence. No counterexample appeared. Script and diagnostic retained, not treated as a mathematical certificate, proof, or evidence of novelty. Larger search alone does not close the unbounded n quantifier.

## 330103: why the WLP proof does not close SLP

Read Theorem4.10 proof and Conjecture4.11 in the arXiv body and October2015 author manuscript. For source degrees i≥2, multiplication is a direct sum of Boolean-lattice inclusion blocks, indexed by the line containing the monomial. Comparing only adjacent degrees recovers the weak Lefschetz criterion but is insufficient for strong Lefschetz. All powers must have maximal rank. Maps from degree1 have point-versus-collinear-subset incidence matrices coupling different lines. Their ranks can drop in positive characteristic even when every within-line block has maximal rank. A real positive Gram-matrix argument would not prove the arbitrary-field endpoint. The exact remaining task is uniform incidence-rank classification over all characteristics and all nonequipointed linear spaces, plus compatibility of injective/surjective directions across every pair of degrees. No residual rank assertion is assumed as a theorem.
