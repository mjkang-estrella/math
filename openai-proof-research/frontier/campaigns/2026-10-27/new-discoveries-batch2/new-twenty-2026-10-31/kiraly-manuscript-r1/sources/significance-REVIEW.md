## Endpoint and contribution

The intended conjecture asserts that a finite clutter C is nonideal exactly when functions p on C and q on its full blocker exist, choosing an element of each member, with equal choices permitted only when that edge–transversal intersection has size greater than one. The reviewed candidate negates the nonideal-implies-orientation direction on eleven vertices. Its edges, in frozen order, are

`{1,7,10}, {2,3,8}, {2,3,4}, {0,3,4}, {0,1,4}, {0,5,6}, {5,6,7}, {1,6,7}, {2,8,9}, {8,9,10}, {5,9,10}`.

The claimed full blocker consists of 11 four-sets and 25 five-sets. I read the explicit lists in `clutter-characterization-r1/runs/cert-n11-1.json`, the formal definitions, and the candidate summary. The JSON field `core` means the whole unsatisfiable constraint collection here, not the minimum-cardinality Lehman core; those notions must not be conflated. Nonideality follows from the fractional vertex (1/3,...,1/3). The substantive addition is absence of p,q for **all** choices, not the fractional vertex, known matrix, catalogue membership, mni diagnostic, or formalization.

For fixed p, q(Y) exists precisely when some v in Y is not selected by any edge meeting Y only at v. Thus the proposed certificate proves that every p has a completely blocked minimal transversal Y. This is a legitimate finite mathematical obstruction, not numerical evidence. The 6,517-leaf tree and its kernel soundness proof are relevant evidence of completeness, not independent evidence of significance or priority. I did not rerun or repair Lean. ## Significance decision and acceptance boundary

This would settle the full named characterization negatively, rather than add another positive parameter instance. It shows that nonintegrality need not be detectable by choosing one distinguished element of each clutter and blocker member in the proposed way. The known ideal-implies-no-orientation theorem survives; the converse characterization fails. It also demonstrates that the core perfect-matching construction cannot always be salvaged by choosing a different core assignment. This last consequence follows from the full obstruction, not merely from one failed extension.

The family of 39 old accepted outcomes and recorded rejection families in both ancestor ledgers/prior-campaign-context were screened; none credits this clutter orientation obstruction. The misere320321 exclusion is the relevant standard: a public theorem need not explicitly name the final conjecture if a short same-object bridge already proves the endpoint. A finite search followed by an exact certificate is not automatically routine in the campaign's substantive sense.
