# Theorem and data map

All names below are in namespace `KiralyClutter`, in the unchanged source.

- Definitions: `IsClutter`, `IsTransversal`, `blocker`, `coveringPolyhedron`,
  `IsIdeal`, `KiralyProperty`, `KiralyConjecture`.
- Literal data: `XL` (eleven triples), `YL` (36 certified blocker members),
  `DL` (choice domains), `KL` (singleton-intersection edge lists), `cert` (tree).
- `chk_sound`: generic induction proving checker soundness.
- `cert_ok`: kernel evaluation of `chk DL KL cert [] = true`.
- `transversal_Y`, `minimal_Y`, `Y_mem_blocker`: certified blocker membership.
- `fact_dom`, `fact_cover`, `fact_single`: finite-data bridges.
- `not_kiraly`: absence of the maps for C11, using the checked tree.
- `eq_xstar`, `xstar_mem`, `xstar_extreme`: real fractional vertex.
- `C11_not_ideal`: nonintegrality of the real covering polyhedron.
- `counterexample`: IsClutter C11 AND not IsIdeal C11 AND not KiralyProperty C11.
- `kiraly_conjecture_false`: negation of the universally quantified conjecture.

No full-blocker equality, minimal nonideality, canonical catalogue label,
minimal size or novelty theorem is present. The single-h source form is excluded
by restricting h to p and q; this ordinary argument appears in the manuscript.
The optional review supplement was not included in this proof packet.
