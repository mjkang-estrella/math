2. Cactus group and its action on standard Young tableaux





2091 


However, we believe that after a certain number of boxes, all “generic” partitions will have A_{N} as the image of C_{n}. We define generic as those partitions that fit into a 2\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{|\lambda|} by 2\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{|\lambda|} grid. This definition of generic accounts for all randomly chosen partitions of n in the limit according to the Plancherel measure described by Vershik and Kerov in [VK77]. We checked by computer how frequent the image of C_{n} is A_{N} or S_{N} for all partitions up to 52 boxes, and Figure 1 in the Appendix shows the distribution for all partitions and for generic partitions. The data suggest that the number of images in the alternating group will eventually dominate the number in the full group S_{N}, although both are seen to increase in these finite cases.




Our explanation of this phenomenon relies on the following conjecture which we expect to be true from the hook-length formula.



Conjecture 8.1.



2100 


Conjecture 8.1.



For any constant c, there is a number of boxes b such that for all n>b, every generic partition \lambda of n boxes that fits into c\sqrt{n} by c\sqrt{n} grid will have an even number of standard Young tableaux.





This implies the following


2114 


Conjecture 8.2.



There exists some m such that for all generic partitions with n>m boxes the image of C_{n} is contained in A_{N}.




Proof.



2127 


(modulo Conjecture 8.1)
The group C_{n} can be generated by the elements s_{[1i]} with i\leq\frac{n}{2} and t_{j} with j\geq\frac{n}{2}. Note that s_{[1i]} is conjugate to s_{[n-i+1,n]} in the cactus group, so, by Proposition 2.1 all these generators are at least conjugate to the ones preserving subtableau formed by the boxes that contain the numbers up to \frac{n}{2}-1. Next, for any standard Young tableau T of sufficiently large generic shape \lambda\vdash n, its subtableau T^{\prime} formed by the boxes that contain the numbers up to \frac{n}{2}-1 satisfies the condition of Conjecture 8.1. So, the number of independent transpositions in any of the above generators is a multiple of the number of such T^{\prime} – which is even! Thus all the generators of the image of C_{n} are even permutations of \text{SYT}(\lambda).
∎
