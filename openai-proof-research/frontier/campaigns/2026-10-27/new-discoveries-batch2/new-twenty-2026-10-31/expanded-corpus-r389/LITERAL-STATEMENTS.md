## 322966 — The even Goldbach conjecture

Let $x$ be an even natural number. **Even Goldbach conjecture.** Every even natural number $x$ can be expressed as the sum of at most two primes. This is one of the central open problems in additive number theory; numerical verification is known to very large bounds, but no proof is known in general.

## 323399 — Conjecture on bondage numbers of Cartesian products of paths

Let $P_n$ and $P_m$ be paths, and let $P_n\times P_m$ denote their Cartesian product. Write $b(\,\cdot\,)$ for the bondage number. **Path-product bondage conjecture.** For $m\geqslant5$,

$$
b(P_n\times P_m)\leqslant2.
$$

The source motivates this by the behavior of vertex removal in path products but gives no resolution.

## 323604 — Borsuk's conjecture on partitioning sets of bounded diameter

Let $S$ be a set of diameter $d>0$ in Euclidean $n$-space $\mathbb R^n$. **Borsuk's conjecture.** Every such set is the union of $n+1$ sets, each of diameter less than $d$. The conjecture is the classical Borsuk problem on reducing the diameter of a set by partitioning it; its resolution depends on the dimension, and the general statement is not valid in all dimensions.

## 324992 — One-versus-t hardness conjecture for acquaintance time

Let $t\in\mathbb{N}$ be a constant and let $G$ be a graph. Write $\mathcal{AC}(G)$ for the acquaintance time of $G$. **One-versus-$t$ hardness conjecture.** It is $\mathcal{NP}$-hard to decide whether

$$
\mathcal{AC}(G)=1
$$

or

$$
\mathcal{AC}(G)\geq t.
$$

This is proposed as a stronger hardness result than the paper's factor-$2$ gap, and remains conjectural in the source.

## 325643 — Partial rainbow matching conjecture for matchings of size k

Let $k$ matchings of size $k$ be given in a bipartite graph. A **partial rainbow matching** is a matching containing at most one edge from each given matching. **Partial rainbow matching conjecture.** These matchings possess a partial rainbow matching of size $k-1$. The paper states that this would imply the Brualdi–Stein conjecture for Latin squares.

## 325818 — The gap conjecture for vertices of non-degenerate 3-way transportation polytopes

Let $p \times q \times s$ be a non-degenerate $3$-way transportation polytope specified by $2$-margin matrices $U,V,W$, and let $f_0(M(U,V,W))$ denote its number of vertices. **The gap conjecture.** It is impossible to have

$$
(p-1)(q-1)(s-1)+1 < f_0(M(U,V,W)) < 2(p-1)(q-1)(s-1).
$$

The conjecture is known to hold when $p,q,s \leq 3$, but its general status is not established here.

## 325858 — Chromatic-polynomial coefficient unimodality conjecture

Let $P$ be the chromatic polynomial of a graph. **Unimodality conjecture.** The sequence of coefficients of $P$ is unimodal. The source reports this as a long-standing conjecture from graph theory and immediately says that it was very recently proved.

## 326155 — Reduction conjecture for interval colorings of complete tripartite graphs

Let $K_{k,m,n}$ be the complete tripartite graph with part sizes $k,m,n$, where $k\leq m\leq n$ and $n>k+m$. **Reduction conjecture.**

$$
K_{k,m,n}\text{ is interval colorable}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad K_{k,m,n-k-m}\text{ is interval colorable}.
$$

This is proposed as a generalization of the $K_{1,m,n}$ colorability problem; the source gives no resolution of the reduction in this range.

## 326241 — CoNP-completeness of balanced skew partition detection

Let $\operatorname{BSP}$ denote the decision problem whose instance is a graph $G$ and whose question is whether $G$ has a balanced skew partition. **The balanced skew partition conjecture.** The problem $\operatorname{BSP}$ is coNP-complete. The paper establishes NP-hardness; the conjectured coNP-completeness would require the corresponding membership in coNP.

## 326296 — The Eulerian conjecture for the augmented wiring-diagram poset

Let $W_n$ be the poset of equivalence classes of lensless wiring diagrams, ordered by breaking crossings, and let $\widehat{0}$ be a new minimal element adjoined to $W_n$. **Eulerian conjecture.** The poset $W_n\cup\{\widehat{0}\}$ is Eulerian. The source states that this conjecture implies the Eulerian conjecture for $EP_n$. Its resolution is not given in the supplied text.

## 326994 — Kinser hierarchy dual-containment conjecture

For each integer $n>4$, let $\mathcal{K}_n$ be the class of matroids satisfying the $n$-th Kinser inequality, and let $\mathcal{K}_n^*$ denote its dual class. **Kinser hierarchy dual-containment conjecture.**

$$
\mathcal{K}_{n+1}^*\subseteq\mathcal{K}_n.
$$

This conjecture describes how the dual of each Kinser class is expected to lie inside the preceding class. The source gives no resolution.

## 327022 — The planar subcubic spectral-radius ratio conjecture

Let $G$ be a planar subcubic graph, and let $R(G)$ denote the ratio defined earlier in the paper. **Planar subcubic conjecture.** If $G$ is a planar subcubic graph, then $R(G)\le 1$. No planar subcubic graph with $R(G)>1$ is known, and the conjecture remains open.

## 327966 — The circle extension distinguishing number conjecture

Let $\mathbb{S}^1$ be the circle with its natural group action, and let $\operatorname{ext}_D(\mathbb{S}^1)$ denote its extension distinguishing number.

**Circle extension conjecture.**

$$
\operatorname{ext}_D(\mathbb{S}^1)=6.
$$

The paper proves the lower bound $\operatorname{ext}_D(\mathbb{S}^1)\geq 6$ and conjectures that this lower bound is sharp; the matching upper bound is left open.

## 328397 — Edge self-amalgamation genus-distribution conjecture

Let $(H,e,f)$ be a **doubly edge-rooted graph** with 2-valent roots $e$ and $f$. Consider the two graphs that can be formed by amalgamating the edge-roots $e$ and $f$. **Edge self-amalgamation conjecture.** The genus distributions of both resulting graphs are log-concave. This is a restricted form of the general genus-distribution log-concavity conjecture; the source gives no resolution.

## 328789 — Points-Lines-Planes conjecture for Whitney numbers of rank-four matroids

Let $M$ be a rank-four matroid, and let $W_k$ denote the number of flats of $M$ of rank $k$. **Points-Lines-Planes conjecture.**

$$
W_2^2\geq\frac{3}{2}W_1W_3.
$$

This is a special case of conjectured log-concavity inequalities for the Whitney numbers of the second kind, with stronger inequalities attributed to Mason. The source gives no resolution of this rank-four case.

## 328880 — The strong cylinder conjecture in affine 3-space

Let $p$ be prime and let $U$ be a set of $p^2$ points in the affine space ${\rm AG}(3,p)$. **Strong cylinder conjecture.** If every plane meets $U$ in a number of points divisible by $p$, then $U$ is the union of $p$ parallel lines.
This strengthens the preceding cylinder conjecture by using the divisibility condition supplied by the direction theorem; the supplied text gives no resolution.

## 329222 — The (I3) conjecture for path-transversal systems

A **path-transversal system** is a system arising from path transversals in a dimaze. **The (I3) conjecture.** Any path-transversal system satisfies (I3). The preceding discussion notes that path-transversal systems need not satisfy (IM), and asks whether the weaker independence axiom (I3) always holds; its status is not resolved in the supplied text.

## 329223 — The duality conjecture for $C^A$-free gammoids

A **$C^A$-free gammoid** is a gammoid avoiding the excluded configuration $C^A$. **The duality conjecture.** The class of $C^A$-free gammoids is closed under duality. This is presented as an expected strengthening of the result that every $R^A$-free strict gammoid is dual to a gammoid; the supplied status evidence marks it as open.

## 331891 — Jaikin-Zapirain's profinite conciseness conjecture

Let $w$ be a group-word and $G$ a profinite group. Suppose that the set of $w$-values in $G$ is countable. **Profinite conciseness conjecture.** Then the verbal subgroup $w(G)$ is finite. The paper presents this as a conjecture in the context of relaxing the usual definition of conciseness for profinite groups; no resolution is given.

## 365494 — Second-range formula conjecture for the universal bound $g^*(r)$

Let $r\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}$, and suppose there exists $t\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ such that

$$
t(t+1)\leq r\leq (t+1)^2.
$$

**Second-range formula conjecture.** Then

$$
g^*(r)=\frac{2}{t+1}+\frac{1}{t}+\frac{r}{(t+1)^2}-\frac{r}{t(t+1)}.
$$

The formula is conjectured as part of the claimed optimality of the constructions in Theorems 7 and 8; no proof or resolution is supplied in the excerpt.
