Theorem 11.6 (Coprime weighted-square critical groups). Let G = F22 , with nonzero vectors ordered as
e1 , e2 , e1 + e2 . Give these generators multiplicities

                                  (a, b, 0),     a, b ∈ Z>0 ,         gcd(a, b) = 1.

Then the Cayley multigraph is connected and its reduced Laplacian has Smith normal form
                                                                         
                                      SNF(L#a,b ) = diag 1, 1, 2ab(a + b) .

Consequently
                                                                          
                                   K(Cay(F22 ; (a, b, 0))) ∼
                                                           = Z/ 2ab(a + b) Z.

Proof. With the vertex e1 + e2 deleted, a reduced Laplacian is
                                                                 
                                              a + b −b         −a
                                    L#a,b =  −b a + b          0 .
                                               −a      0     a+b

Its determinant is 2ab(a + b). The first determinantal divisor is the gcd of its entries, hence equals 1 because
gcd(a, b) = 1. Among its 2 × 2 minors are −ab and (a + b)2 , and
                                                            
                                            gcd ab, (a + b)2 = 1.

Thus the gcd of all 2 × 2 minors is 1, the first two Smith invariant factors are 1, 1, and the third is the
determinant. The graph is connected because e1 , e2 generate F22 .
    For (a, b) = (1, 5) and (2, 3) the final invariant factor is 60 in both cases. The multiplicity multisets
{0, 1, 5} and {0, 2, 3} differ, so the two triples are not related by GL2 (F2 ) and form a counterexample to
the literal Conjecture 5.3 of [20]. This particular collision is not new to the available structure: Section 7 of
the 2018 REU report determines the generic r = 2 Sylow-2 part, while its Proposition 2.6 determines every
odd-primary part from the eigenvalues [19]. Together those results already imply the two Z/60Z groups.
The new statement here is the closed Smith form for every coprime pair (a, b); Theorem 11.9 gives the
fixed-degree r = 3 counterexample used below as the principal disproof of Conjecture 5.3.


FULL GL COUNTERPART Theorem 11.9 (Degree-seven isospectral fixed-degree counterexample to Conjecture 5.3). In the same vector
order, let
                         α = (0, 0, 1, 0, 2, 2, 2),   β = (0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 3).
The corresponding connected Cayley multigraphs are both generic, have multiplicity gcd 1, and are 7-regular.
They have the same complete Laplacian spectrum

                                               {0, 4, 6, 6, 8, 10, 10, 12}

and the same reduced Smith form

                           SNF(L#α ) = SNF(L#β ) = diag(1, 1, 1, 1, 12, 60, 240).

Hence
                             K(Gα ) ∼
                                    = K(Gβ ) ∼
                                             = Z/12Z ⊕ Z/60Z ⊕ Z/240Z.
Their multiplicity multisets are

                                   {0, 0, 0, 1, 2, 2, 2}   and {0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 3},

so they are not GL3 (F2 )-equivalent. Thus Conjecture 5.3 of [20], and equivalently Conjecture 10.2 of the
2018 REU report [19], fails even after imposing both equal degree and equality of the complete Laplacian
spectrum.

Proof. The character formula used in Theorem 11.7 gives the common spectrum by direct substitution. Delet-

     In this generic r = 3 setting, Theorem 8.2 of the 2018 REU report determines the Sylow-2 structure from
the eigenvalue valuations, while Proposition 2.6 determines the odd-primary structure from the eigenvalues
[19]. Thus their formulas already determine the common critical group from the common spectrum; the
obstruction exposed here is that the same spectral and critical-group data do not determine the GL3 (F2 )-
orbit of the generator multiplicities.
