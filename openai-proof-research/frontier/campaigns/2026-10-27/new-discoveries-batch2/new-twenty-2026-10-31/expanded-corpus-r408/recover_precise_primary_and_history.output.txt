Category `research open` Description tretoef-estrella opened on Oct 11, 2026 Issue body actions What is the conjecture Let K(Q_n) be the sandpile (critical) group of the n -dimensional hypercube graph Q_n , and Syl_2 its Sylow 2-subgroup. For a finite abelian 2-group, let c_r be the exponent e of the r -th largest cyclic factor ℤ/2^e . J. Gao, J. Marx-Kuo, V. McDonald, C. H. Yuen, Sandpile groups of Cayley graphs of 𝔽_2^r , arXiv:1912.06919 (v3), state two conjectures: Conjecture 4.14. For n ≥ 3 , v_2(c_n(Q_n)) = max( max_{x<n−1} (v_2(x) + x), v_2(n−1) + n − 3 ) . Conjecture 5.4. For k ≥ 1 , Syl_2 K(Q_{2^k}) ≅ Syl_2 K(Q_{2^k−1})^2 × ℤ/2^{2^k+k−1} . (Their Theorems 4.1 and 4.2 give the largest factor and the next n − 2 .) Both are proved in the preprint The Dancing Sand Theorem — the 2-part of the sandpile group of the hypercube, for every n (R. Amichis Luengo, version 11, 14 October 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23356712 ; concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23188298 ; also on the Hexagon preprint server as hexagon:2610.00205 ), as Corollaries 1.6 and 1.7 of a rule for the whole 2-part of K(Q_n) . They are proved in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with no sorry and only the axioms propext , Classical.choice , Quot.sound : Conjecture 4.14: PodiumCor.G2 (for every n ≥ 3 ); Conjecture 5.4: Mirror.Y2 (for every e ≥ 1 ); Theorem 4.1 of the same paper, for every n ≥ 2 : SmallCases.G1_all . Attribution for Conjecture 4.14. It had been proved earlier, on paper, by D. N. Betzer ( Sandpile groups of Cayley graphs over 𝔽_2^r: Sylow-2 structure, isospectral counterexamples, and Boolean incidence algebra , preprint, Zenodo, August 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22065778 ), through Remark 4.13 of Gao, Marx-Kuo, McDonald and Yuen, which they state without proof. The proof in the paper and in Lean does not use that remark, and version 11 of the paper credits him. Conjecture 5.4 and the whole 2-part are not in his paper. The Lean project, a certificate that compares each Lean statement with the paper, and a clean-rebuild log are archived at doi:10.5281/zenodo.23298436 and in the lean/ folder of https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/dancing-sand-theorem , where a GitHub Actions workflow builds the project from source. The certificate and the paper (up to version 10) were read cold by separate readers before release; the attribution change of version 11 was checked against Betzer's paper and Gao et al.'s, and was not read cold. Those readers are AI systems, and the work has not been refereed by a human expert. This description was updated on 14 October 2026: version 11 of the paper, the Hexagon identifier, and the attribution above (see also the comment below). The proposal is to add the two statements with @[category research solved] and @[formal_proof using lean4 at "https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23298436"] ; the category is your decision. Prerequisites needed The sandpile group of a finite graph (cokernel of the reduced Laplacian, or the torsion of the cokernel of the Laplacian), and the hypercube graph, which this repository already defines ( SimpleGraph.hypercube in FormalConjecturesForMathlib/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Hypercube.lean ). In the Lean project above, K(Q_n) is defined as the torsion subgroup of the cokernel of the Laplacian f ↦ n·f(g) − Σ_i f(g + e_i) on the functions (ℤ/2)^n → ℤ ( CubeLaplacian.K ); the definitions are printed verbatim in §1.2 of the certificate. betzer-prior.html Sandpile Groups of Cayley Graphs over 𝔽₂ʳ: Sylow-2 Structure, Isospectral Counterexamples, and Boolean Incidence Algebra | Zenodo Skip to main Communities My dashboard Log in Sign up Published August 23, 2026 | Version v1 Preprint Open Sandpile Groups of Cayley Graphs over 𝔽₂ʳ: Sylow-2 Structure, Isospectral Counterexamples, and Boolean Incidence Algebra Authors/Creators Betzer, David Description This paper determines new 2-primary sandpile invariants for Cayley multigraphs of the elementary abelian 2-group 𝔽₂ʳ and develops the Boolean incidence-algebra machinery underlying their computation. For the n-dimensional hypercube Qₙ, the paper determines the first previously unknown Sylow-2 cyclic exponent and thereby closes Conjecture 4.14 of Gao–Marx-Kuo–McDonald–Yuen. The exponent is given explicitly by cₙ(Qₙ) = max{ max₁≤ₓ<ₙ₋₁ (x + ν₂(x)), n − 3 + ν₂(n − 1) }. For an arbitrary connected Cayley multigraph of 𝔽₂ʳ, an exact parity theorem is proved for the number d(M) of Sylow-2 cyclic factors: d(M) is even ⇔ every nonzero generator has odd multiplicity. In this unique parity class, d(M) = 2ʳ − 2; for every other multiplicity-parity pattern, d(M) is odd. This replaces the previously proposed eigenvalue-valuation criterion by a complete classification in terms of the generator multiplicities modulo 2. The paper then constructs explicit isospectral counterexamples. For every r ≥ 3 there are primitive equal-degree Cayley multigraph pairs with identical complete Laplacian eigenvalue multisets but nonisomorphic sandpile groups. Their Sylow-2 factor counts are respectively 3·2ʳ⁻² − 1 and 2ʳ − 2. These families disprove both the published conjecture that the sandpile group is determined by the Laplacian eigenvalue set and the stronger earlier formulation asserting dependence only on the unlabeled eigenvalue multiset. A separate fixed-degree construction in 𝔽₂³ gives two generic 7-regular isospectral Cayley multigraphs with the same sandpile group ℤ/12ℤ ⊕ ℤ/60ℤ ⊕ ℤ/240ℤ but lying in distinct GL₃(𝔽₂)-orbits. Exact orbit enumeration proves that degree 7 is minimal for a same-degree critical-group collision among connected Cayley multigraphs of 𝔽₂³. The paper also derives the complete Smith normal form for every coprime weighted square with generator multiplicities (a,b,0): SNF(L♯ₐ,ᵦ) = diag(1,1,2ab(a+b)), and hence K(Cay(𝔽₂²;(a,b,0))) ≅ ℤ/(2ab(a+b))ℤ. The proof calculus is developed through Boolean incidence algebra. Indicator vectors identify set operations with exact polynomial and matrix operations; the Boolean zeta and Möbius transforms receive recursive Kronecker factorizations; inclusion–exclusion and exact-membership inversion are expressed as matrix transforms; and staged Boolean-lattice transforms are given exact operation counts. The paper also proves a deterministic worst-case optimal Θ(nk) algorithm for computing the cardinality of the union of k explicitly represented subsets of an n-element universe. Further extensions treat bounded-multiset product posets, product-of-chains zeta and Möbius transforms, spectral formulas, sparse Möbius-transform questions, and quantum realizations of set operations.
