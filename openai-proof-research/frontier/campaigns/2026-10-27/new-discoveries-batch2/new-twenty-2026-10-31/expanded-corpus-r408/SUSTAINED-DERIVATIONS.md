344907: independently derived weighted-square route, now publicly resolved and zero novelty credit. On F_2^2 use positive multiplicities (a,b,0) on e1,e2,e1+e2. Connectivity follows from the two basis generators. Delete vertex e1+e2. Up to reordering the other vertices the reduced Laplacian is [[a+b,-a,-b],[-a,a+b,0],[-b,0,a+b]]. Expanding its determinant gives (a+b)((a+b)^2-a^2-b^2)=2ab(a+b). The gcd of entries is gcd(a,b); two 2x2 minors have absolute values ab and (a+b)^2. If gcd(a,b)=1, any prime dividing ab divides exactly one of a,b and cannot divide a+b, hence these minors have gcd one. The determinantal-divisor characterization of integral Smith form therefore gives diag(1,1,2ab(a+b)). At (1,5) and (2,3), both groups are cyclic of order60. Their multiplicity multisets {0,1,5} and {0,2,3} differ, while GL_2 permutes the three nonzero vectors. Thus they are not GL-equivalent. 344905: the first mechanism is modulo2 rank, not numerical eigenvalue scanning. Source Lemma2.3 proves d=2^r-1-rank_F2(L). In A=F2[u1,...,ur]/(ui^2), L is multiplication by p=sum_(v!=0) parity(mu(v))(1+x^v), with xi=1+ui. Let Omega=product ui and tau select its coefficient. The pairing tau(fg) is nondegenerate: a nonzero polynomial's inclusion-minimal monomial support S is isolated by multiplying by u_(S complement). On V=A/Ann(p), B(f,g)=tau(pfg) is nondegenerate. Its dimension is rank(L). Every f squares to its constant term. If tau(p)=0 then B is alternating and rank even. If tau(p)=1, choose w with pw=Omega, which exists by the same minimal-support argument. Unless p=Omega, w has zero constant term; otherwise w is a unit and p=Omega*w^-1=Omega. Thus B(w,w)=0, B(w,f)=const(f)=B(f,f). Choose z with B(w,z)=1. The w,z plane is nondegenerate, and its orthogonal complement is nondegenerate alternating, so rank is again even. If p=Omega rank=1. If p=0 rank=0. Finally {1+x^v:v!=0} is an augmentation-ideal basis and its sum is Omega, so p=Omega iff every nonzero multiplicity is odd. Hence d is even precisely in that parity class, where d=2^r-2; all other classes give odd d. This sustained proof resolves the rank-parity obstacle left by the elementary observation 'even degree gives alternating L'. It does not establish the conjectured eigenvalue criterion.
