                                          Abstract

                                          We introduce some special Hilbert spaces to present the class of infinitesimal operators with
                                          non-basis family of eigenvectors. The famous Hardy inequality plays a crucial role in this
                                          construction. Moreover, we introduce certain new Banach sequence spaces to present the class
                                          of infinitesimal operators with non-basis family of eigenvectors acting on them.
                                          Keywords: Hardy inequality, infinitesimal operator, C0 -group, Riesz basis, eigenvectors,
                                          symmetric basis, right shift operator associated to the basis, H∞ -calculus
                                          2010 MSC: 34L10, 47D06, 47B40, 46C05, 46B45, 46B15

      5. Concluding remarks

         The results of the present paper allow us to say the following. A Theorem 1 cannot be
      improved. Moreover, it is impossible to obtain any analog of Theorem 1 concerning non-basis
      family of eigenvectors by means of refusing or weakening of the condition (2). On the other
      hand, it is interesting to obtain some analogs of Theorem 1 in Banach spaces with certain
      classes of bases, e.g., symmetric bases, unconditional bases.
         Theorem 14 and Remark 2 allow us to say that symmetric bases in ℓp spaces behave like
      Riesz bases in H. Consequently, we arrive at the idea of possible generalization of the Theorem 1
      to the case of operators with symmetric basis family of eigenvectors, which generate C0 -groups
      on the spaces ℓp , p > 1, and propose the following.

      Conjecture 18. Let A be the generator of the C0 -group on the space ℓp , p > 1, with eigenvalues
      {λn }∞                                                                                     ∞
           n=1 (counting with multiplicity) and the corresponding (normalized) eigenvectors {en }n=1 .

         If Lin{en }∞                                    ∞                            ∞
                    n=1 = ℓp and the point spectrum {λn }n=1 satisfies (2), then {en }n=1 forms a

      symmetric basis of ℓp .

         Finally, the answer on the following important question does not yet exist. Is it possible to
      construct the generator of a C0 -group with non-Riesz basis family of eigenvectors? Note that the
      construction of the generator of the C0 -semigroup with non-Riesz basis family of eigenvectors
      is trivial, see, e.g., [12](Section 5.5).
