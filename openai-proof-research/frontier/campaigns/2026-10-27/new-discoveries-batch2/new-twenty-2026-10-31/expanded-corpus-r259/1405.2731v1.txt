                                                                                        Abstract
                                                      We introduce some special Hilbert spaces to present the class of infinitesimal
                                                  operators with non-Riesz basis family of eigenvectors. The famous Hardy inequality
                                                  plays a crucial role in this construction. Moreover, we introduce certain Banach
                                                  spaces to present the class of infinitesimal operators with conditional basis family of
                                                  eigenvectors.

This result also has an essential generalizations [20, 21]. We note that Theorem 1.1 follows
from the main result of [20], but the approach used by H. Zwart in [21] differs from one
proposed by G.Q. Xu and S.P. Yung in [20]. The proof of Theorem 1.1 in [21] is based,
on the one hand, on Carleson’s interpolation theorem [5], and, on the other hand, on the
fact that each generator of a C0 -group on H has a bounded H∞ -calculus on a strip [7, 8].
    The authors of [20] do not consider the question of essentiality of the condition (2) for
the Riesz basis property of eigenvectors of the generator A on H. In [21] only the case
when eigenvalues {λn }∞ n=1 of A do not satisfy (2), but can be decomposed into K(< ∞)
sets, with every set having a uniform gap, is considered. It is shown that in this case A
also generates a C0 -group, but Theorem 1.1 need not to hold.
    In the present work we give a positive answer to the following important question:
Is it possible to construct the generator of a C0 -group with non-Riesz basis family of
eigenvectors? Namely, we construct the generator of a C0 -group with eigenvectors forming
non-Riesz basis of H. More precisely, in Theorem 3.1 we consider the case when {λn }∞    n=1
do not satisfy (2) and, moreover, cannot be decomposed into K sets, with every set having
a uniform gap, and present the construction of infinitesimal operator with non-Riesz basis
family of eigenvectors. It must be emphasized that the famous Hardy inequality (1) (for
p = 2) plays a crucial role in this construction. As a consequence, we found that, if we
refuse the condition (2) in Theorem 1.1, then three mutually exclusive phenomena for the
family of eigenvectors are possible (they form Riesz basis, non-Riesz basis, or do not form
a basis), see Corollary 3.6 for details.

5    Concluding remarks
The results of the present paper allow us to say the following. It is impossible to obtain
any analog of Theorem 1.1 concerning non-Riesz basis family of eigenvectors, or non-basis
family of eigenvectors by means of refusing or weakening of the condition (2). On the
other hand, it is interesting to obtain some analogs of Theorem 1.1 in Banach spaces with
certain classes of bases, e.g., symmetric bases, unconditional bases.
