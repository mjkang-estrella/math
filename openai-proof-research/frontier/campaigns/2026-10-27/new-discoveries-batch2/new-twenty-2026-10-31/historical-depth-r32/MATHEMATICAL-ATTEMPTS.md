## 1. 388081: selecting an extendable weighted k-set

**Literal scope.** Arbitrary edge-weighted bipartite input, variable k, polynomial-time optimum among k-edge matchings contained in a perfect matching. Original Egres body remains unavailable; no requests to inherited denied Egres routes were made. In particular rational/integer encoding and deterministic/randomized conventions have not been restored. We may examine mechanisms but cannot silently supply those conventions.

An exact elementary reformulation is useful: the answer equals max over perfect matchings M of the sum of the k largest weights in M. Every feasible F is contained in an M, and replacing F by the k heaviest edges of that M cannot reduce its weight; conversely that selected subset is feasible. This applies to negative weights too, provided k is a feasible cardinality. It is a known bridge, not a discovery.

**First shortcut fails.** Maximizing total perfect-matching weight does not maximize the chosen subset. On C4, give the two perfect matchings weights (10,0) and (6,6). For k=1, total-weight optimization selects the latter (12>10), while the extendable one-edge optimum is the former's edge of weight10. All edges here are extendable, so this failure does not depend on an unextendable edge.

**Second, stronger route: joint assignment LP.** Introduce perfect-matching variables y and selected-edge variables x, with 0<=x<=y and sum x=k. It is tempting to claim assignment integrality makes this polytope integral. It does not. Use the bipartite six-cycle with diagonal edges (i,i) and cyclic edges (i,i+1 mod3), for i=0,1,2. Its only perfect matchings have weight vectors (10,0,0) and (6,6,6). For k=2, their top-two values are10 and12, so the integral optimum is12. Put y=1/2 on all six edges, x=1/2 on the weight10 edge and the three weight6 edges, and x=0 on the two remaining edges. Every assignment row/column sum of y is1; x<=y; sum x=2; the objective is14. Thus this relaxation has a strict gap. Enumeration in diagnostics.py verifies the two matching supports and exact rational point; the preceding argument is also an ordinary proof of this obstruction. It is a helper counterexample to our proposed method, not a refutation of the MathDB algorithm question.

The threshold identity top_k(M)=min_t(kt+sum_{e in M}max(w(e)-t,0)) does not fix this: interchanging max_M and min_t produces the relaxation. For the same C6 example and k=2, the competing threshold expressions on 0<=t<=6 are10+t and18-t. Their intersection is t=4 with value14, exceeding the true value12. Outside this interval the upper-envelope value cannot improve below14 (for t<0 it is >=18, for 6<=t<=10 it is10+t>=16, and t>=10 gives2t>=20). A parametric ordinary-assignment oracle therefore does not close the problem.

**Third route and primary comparison.** Read both pages of El Maalouly–Wulf, arXiv2209.09661v1, including all of Lemma1's proof. The source replaces each edge by a length-five path and adds k isolated forced edges. An original red edge contributes either one weight3 edge or two weight2 edges. With R original red edges and r of them selected, truncating to2R edges yields threshold4R+k exactly when r=k. The subdivision is odd-length, hence preserves bipartiteness; the isolated edges also preserve it. Thus a general deterministic polynomial algorithm for our bipartite top-k problem would solve exact bipartite matching via this published reduction. The reverse reduction's cited source was not body-audited here. The incidental CVIT2016 template heading/DOI in the PDF is not treated as a publication date: the body is explicitly arXiv v1,17September2022. A thesis fetch returned HTTP200 homepage HTML, not the thesis; no thesis proof clearance is claimed.

**Remaining lemma.** A deterministic exact bipartite-matching algorithm (including a complexity proof) or an alternative exact top-k augmenting structure avoiding this relaxation gap. No such lemma is proved. Merely invoking randomized algebraic detection changes a potentially material source convention. Estimated Lean work is a full graph/algorithm/complexity development; there is no small certificate for a universal algorithm endpoint. Outcome: PARK_EXACT_ALGORITHM_GAP plus inherited HOLD_SOURCE; zero credit.

## 2. 387172: character counts versus p-prime subgroup class counts

Read Kourovka21.92 in arXiv1401.0300v48 and Malle–Navarro–Robinson's author PDF, dated10March2021. Its introduction explicitly identifies l(G), the number of p-regular classes, with the number of irreducible p-Brauer characters; Conjecture4.7 is the exact group-theoretic question. This identifies the intended universal finite-group scope. It does not formally prove the modular-character bridge in Lean.

**Route A: small/simple counterexamples.** Theorem2 already establishes the bound for every quasi-simple finite group. Read its concluding proof, Propositions4.2/4.3 in full, and the definitions of pi-boundedness. Proposition4.3 uses restricted-weight counts and sufficiently large tori in defining characteristic; its representation-count/tori references are external dependencies, not re-proved here. Consequently alternating, sporadic, PSL2 and other quasi-simple sweeps would target a known family. No such sweep was run. This is a restricted-family exclusion, not a claim that the universal problem is solved.

**Route B: extend witnesses through composition factors.** Read complete Lemmas3.1/3.2 proofs. For pi=p-prime, normal p-subgroups can be factored out using Schur–Zassenhaus and p-regular conjugacy-class correspondence. If G/N is a p-group, every p-regular element lies in N, and class fusion gives l(G)<=l(N), so a witness inside N suffices. These are source-contained reductions. The apparent composition-series induction breaks at a general mixed quotient: a p-prime witness in the quotient need not lift to a p-prime subgroup through a kernel containing p-elements, and the class count needed is not simply multiplicative under a semidirect action. No witness-lifting theorem for that step was established.

**Route C: products and p-constrained groups.** For a direct product A x B, conjugacy classes are pairs, and p-regularity is coordinatewise. If H_A and H_B are witnesses, H_A x H_B is p-prime and has at least l(A)l(B)=l(A x B) classes. This standard closure cannot yield a counterexample by taking direct powers of a known good group. Read the full concluding proof of Theorem3: it assumes p-prime boundedness and derives the block bound via the known k(GV) theorem. Reversing this implication to establish p-prime boundedness would be circular. Theorem3 and Corollary4.6 do not settle arbitrary group extensions.

**Remaining lemma.** A genuinely general witness construction controlling p-regular class fusion in mixed extensions, or a source-valid counterexample outside the already treated families. Neither is available. Full Lean endpoint would additionally require actual irreducible modular representations and the p-regular-class counting bridge, not a replacement predicate called a character count. Outcome: PARK_EXTENSION_AND_SEMANTIC_BRIDGES; zero credit. No global novelty clearance is claimed.

## 3. 387463/387462: isolated real zeros

Read Shapiro's published HTML Section3, Problems2/3 and Conjecture4. The exact questions ask for extremal functions in all degree/variable counts. The source itself gives the planar SOS answer k^2 and the lower bound k^l; these are not new routes.

The tempting Bezout upper bound fails to handle positive-dimensional complex common-zero loci with isolated real points. A public primary proof now supplies an explicit obstruction to the conjectured formula: DannyExperiments/ottaviani-shapiro-sos-counterexample, immutable commit41e3d18a8c536e5a859201afd5faefe2b0ecd315, commit date10August2026. Read all of manuscript.tex, including both construction proofs and the algebraic-structure argument. For q0=x0^2+t(t-8) and qi=xi^2-(t-i+1)(t-i),1<=i<=8, simultaneous real solvability forces t in{0,...,8}. Exactly two radicands vanish in each fiber, leaving seven independent signs; hence nine fibers each have128 points. The nonnegative quartic sum of nine squares thus has1152 isolated real zeros in ten variables, exceeding1024. The complex coordinate algebra is free over C[t] on squarefree monomials in nine x variables, so the complex common-zero locus has positive dimension. This addresses the precise old Bezout objection rather than ignoring it. diagnostics.py reproduces only this public fiber arithmetic.

The source also gives a uniform even-degree selector construction; changing blocks, tensoring independent examples, or formalizing this public construction cannot count as a new family. Neither the public example nor the general lower bound computes the exact extremal function requested by either reserved ID.
