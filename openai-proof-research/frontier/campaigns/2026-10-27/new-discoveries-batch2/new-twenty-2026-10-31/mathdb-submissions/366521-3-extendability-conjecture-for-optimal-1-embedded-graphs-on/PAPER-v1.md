# A twelve-vertex counterexample to the 3-extendability conjecture for 8-regular optimal 1-embedded graphs on the Klein bottle, with a kernel-checked literal Lean endpoint

Target: MathDB 366521, "3-extendability conjecture for optimal 1-embedded graphs on the Klein bottle"; source: S. Koizumi and Y. Suzuki, *Connectivity and matching extendability of optimal 1-embedded graphs on the torus*, arXiv:2501.02726v2 (7 Jan 2025), Conjecture 25 (printed p. 13). Version PAPER-v1, 2026-11-15. Status: candidate only. No acceptance, novelty or credit is claimed; a fresh independent extraction/rebuild/semantic replay is still required.

## 1. The statement

Source definitions (pp. 1-2, read in the owned v2 text and in rendered pages 1 and 13). All graphs are finite, simple and connected. A graph is 1-embeddable on a closed surface F if it can be drawn on F so that every edge crosses another edge at most once; drawings are good: (i) vertices are distinct points, (ii) adjacent edges do not cross, (iii) no three edges cross at one point, (iv) nonadjacent edges do not touch tangentially. A 1-embedded graph on F with at least three vertices has |E| <= 4|V| - 4chi(F); it is optimal when equality holds. A matching M is extendable if some perfect matching contains M; a graph with at least 2m+2 vertices is m-extendable if every matching of size m is extendable.

Conjecture 25: every 8-regular optimal 1-embedded graph on the Klein bottle is 3-extendable. Since chi(Klein bottle) = 0, optimal means |E| = 4|V|.

Theorem. There is a finite simple connected 8-regular graph G on 12 vertices with 48 = 4*12 edges, a good drawing of G on the Klein bottle in which every edge has at most one crossing point, and a matching M of 3 edges contained in no perfect matching of G. Hence Conjecture 25 is false. The order 12 is even and at least 8, so this is not an odd-order or small-order artefact.

## 2. The surface

Let R = [0,4] x [0,3]. Define the boundary normal form nf: corners go to (0,0); (4,y) goes to (0,y); (x,3) goes to (4-x,0); every other point is fixed. Put p ~ q iff nf p = nf q, and K = R/~ with the quotient topology. The classes are: single interior points, pairs {(0,y),(4,y)} for 0<y<3, pairs {(x,0),(4-x,3)} for 0<x<4, and the four corners. Gluing theorem (Lean `mk_eq_iff_eqvGen`): ~ is exactly the equivalence relation generated by the two standard side pairings (0,y)~(4,y) and (x,0)~(4-x,3). Proof: each pairing preserves nf (case split on corners); conversely every point is joined to its normal form by at most two pairings, e.g. (0,3)~(4,3)~(0,0). So K is the standard reflected-rectangle Klein bottle, presented with no second model and no homeomorphism to another model.

K is compact (image of the compact R) and Hausdorff: the polynomial map F(x,y) = (x(4-x), y(3-y), x(4-x)(x-2)(3-2y), y(3-y)(2y-3), x(4-x)(x-2)y(3-y)) is constant on classes and separates normal forms (the positivity pattern of the first two coordinates gives the type of normal form; inside a type one cancels a positive factor from the third, fourth or fifth coordinate), so it induces a continuous injection K -> R^5.

Interior chart. With U = (0,4)x(0,3), the map iota: U -> K is an open embedding: nf is the identity on U and the only point of R with an interior normal form is that point, so iota is injective and the full preimage of iota(V) is V itself for V open in U.

Seam charts. On S_a = (0,4)x(-1,1), Phi_a(x,y) = [x,y] for y >= 0 and [4-x,3+y] for y < 0 is an open embedding: it is continuous because the two formulas agree on y = 0 ((x,0)~(4-x,3)); it is injective by comparing normal forms; and its images of open sets have explicit open saturated preimages {(a,b): b<1, (a,b) in O} union {(a,b): b>2, (4-a,b-3) in O}. Symmetrically Phi_b(x,y) = [x,y] for x >= 0 and [4+x,y] for x < 0 on S_b = (-1,1)x(0,3).

## 3. The graph

Vertices are 0,...,11, with v = 4y + x for the lattice point (x,y), 0<=x<=3, 0<=y<=2. The twelve unit squares of R give, after identification, the faces
[0,1,5,4] [1,2,6,5] [2,3,7,6] [3,0,4,7] [4,5,9,8] [5,6,10,9] [6,7,11,10] [7,4,8,11] [8,9,3,0] [9,10,2,3] [10,11,1,2] [11,8,0,1].
G joins two distinct vertices iff they lie in a common face (each face is a 4-clique). Each vertex has two horizontal neighbours in its row and three neighbours in each of the other two rows, eight in all; so G is 8-regular and |E| = 12*8/2 = 48 (Lean: degree count by kernel evaluation, edge count by the handshake lemma). Every vertex is within distance two of vertex 0, so G is connected.

## 4. The drawing

Vertex v is drawn at [x_v, y_v]. These points are pairwise distinct because nf fixes the lattice representatives. The 48 edges are drawn as the quotient images of the 48 affine unit segments of the squares [i,i+1]x[j,j+1] (i<4, j<3): horizontal sides (i+t, j), vertical sides (i, j+t), diagonals (i+t, j+t) and (i+1-t, j+t), t in [0,1]. Each lies in R, its endpoints are the lattice points whose labels give the edge (seam endpoints such as (4,j) or (i,3) are identified with their labelled vertex by nf), and every edge of G arises from exactly one segment.

Key observation: at every OPEN segment parameter 0<t<1, nf fixes the point (horizontal sides have 0<x<4 and y in {0,1,2}; vertical sides have x in {0,1,2,3} and 0<y<3; diagonals lie in U). Consequently every coincidence of open edge points in K is a coincidence in the plane. Then:
- Edge interiors avoid vertices: an open segment point has a non-integer coordinate.
- Each arc is injective: open points by planar injectivity, endpoints by distinct labels and vertex injectivity.
- Intersection classification (`seg_meet`, `arc_meet`): open points of two segments with indices k != l coincide only if k, l are the two diagonals of one square, and then only at the centre (t = 1/2 on both). The proof is a 16-case analysis of linear equations with integer offsets, using that no integer lies strictly between 0 and 1. `diagonals_cross` shows the centre really is a crossing.
- The two diagonals of a square have four distinct endpoints (finite check), so adjacent edges never cross; no point lies on three edge interiors; each edge has at most one crossing point (sides have none).
- Transversality in a genuine chart: near the centre c of square (i,j) the chart iota exhibits the diagonals as c + (s-1/2)(1,1) and c + (t-1/2)(-1,1), with determinant 2 != 0.
Thus the drawing is a good 1-embedding (Lean `kleinDrawing_good` proves the full predicate `IsGoodOneDrawing`).

## 5. Euler characteristic and optimality

K carries the canonical one-vertex cell structure of the rectangle model: 0-cell [0,0]; 1-cells a(s) = [4s,0] and b(s) = [0,3s]; 2-cell f(s,t) = [4s,3t]. Each characteristic map is continuous on the closed cube; its restriction to the open cube is an embedding (the 2-cell through the interior chart, the 1-cells through the seam charts Phi_a, Phi_b); cube boundaries map into lower cells; and the four open cells partition K (classification of normal forms). With K Hausdorff this is a finite CW complex, and chi = 1 - 2 + 1 = 0. Therefore 4|V| - 4chi = 48 = |E| and the 1-embedding is optimal. The Lean statement `Conjecture25 K C` takes chi from such a certified cell structure C; `conjecture25_false_of_euler_zero` refutes it for every cell structure with chi = 0. Disclosed limit: the classical topological invariance of the Euler characteristic (independence of the cell structure) is used to identify this number with the chi(F) of the source; that invariance theorem is not formalized.

## 6. The matching

M = {15, 39, 7 11}: each is a square side ({1,5} in [0,1,5,4], {3,9} in [8,9,3,0], {7,11} in [6,7,11,10]) and the six endpoints are the odd vertices. Removing them leaves {0,4,8} and {2,6,10}, each a triangle, with no edge between them. In a perfect matching P containing M every odd vertex is matched by its M-edge, so the partner of a vertex of {0,4,8} is even, hence in {0,4,8} (no even neighbour outside); a fixed-point-free partner involution on three vertices is impossible. So M is in no perfect matching and G is not 3-extendable, although |V| = 12 >= 8. (Lean: `matchM_not_extendable`, using Mathlib's `Subgraph.IsMatching` and `IsPerfectMatching`.)

/-- Interior parameters `(0,1)` of the unit interval. -/
def openUnit : Set unitInterval := {t | 0 < (t : ℝ) ∧ (t : ℝ) < 1}

/-- A drawing of a simple graph in a space: a point for every vertex and a continuous curve
for every edge, running from one endpoint to the other. -/
structure Drawing {V : Type} (G : SimpleGraph V) (X : Type) [TopologicalSpace X] where
  pt : V → X
  arc : G.edgeSet → C(unitInterval, X)
  ends : ∀ e : G.edgeSet, ∃ u v : V, (e : Sym2 V) = s(u, v) ∧ arc e 0 = pt u ∧ arc e 1 = pt v

variable {V : Type} {G : SimpleGraph V} {X : Type} [TopologicalSpace X]

/-- The interior points of a drawn edge. -/
def Drawing.inner (D : Drawing G X) (e : G.edgeSet) : Set X := D.arc e '' openUnit

/-- Transverse crossing of `γ` at `s₀` with `δ` at `t₀`: in a genuine local chart (an open
embedding `ψ` of an open planar set `U` into the surface) both curves are straight lines
through a common point with linearly independent directions near the parameters. -/
def TransverseAt (γ δ : C(unitInterval, X)) (s₀ t₀ : unitInterval) : Prop :=
  ∃ (U : Set (ℝ × ℝ)) (ψ : U → X), IsOpen U ∧ IsOpenEmbedding ψ ∧
    ∃ (c v w : ℝ × ℝ) (ε : ℝ), 0 < ε ∧ v.1 * w.2 - v.2 * w.1 ≠ 0 ∧
      (∀ s : unitInterval, |(s : ℝ) - (s₀ : ℝ)| < ε →
        ∃ h : c + ((s : ℝ) - (s₀ : ℝ)) • v ∈ U, γ s = ψ ⟨_, h⟩) ∧
      (∀ t : unitInterval, |(t : ℝ) - (t₀ : ℝ)| < ε →
        ∃ h : c + ((t : ℝ) - (t₀ : ℝ)) • w ∈ U, δ t = ψ ⟨_, h⟩)

/-- A good drawing in which every edge crosses other edges at most once (a 1-embedding). -/
structure IsGoodOneDrawing (D : Drawing G X) : Prop where
  pt_injective : Injective D.pt
  arc_injective : ∀ e, Injective (D.arc e)
  inner_avoids_vertices : ∀ e, ∀ t ∈ openUnit, ∀ w, D.arc e t ≠ D.pt w
  adjacent_inner_disjoint : ∀ e f : G.edgeSet, e ≠ f →
    (∃ w, w ∈ (e : Sym2 V) ∧ w ∈ (f : Sym2 V)) → Disjoint (D.inner e) (D.inner f)
  no_triple_crossing : ∀ e f g : G.edgeSet, e ≠ f → f ≠ g → e ≠ g →
    D.inner e ∩ D.inner f ∩ D.inner g = ∅
  crossings_transverse : ∀ e f : G.edgeSet, e ≠ f → ∀ s ∈ openUnit, ∀ t ∈ openUnit,
    D.arc e s = D.arc f t → TransverseAt (D.arc e) (D.arc f) s t
  at_most_one_crossing : ∀ e : G.edgeSet,
    {x | x ∈ D.inner e ∧ ∃ f : G.edgeSet, f ≠ e ∧ x ∈ D.inner f}.Subsingleton

/-- A finite cell structure of dimension at most two with cubical characteristic maps
(a finite CW structure): characteristic maps on `[0,1]` and `[0,1]²` are continuous and
embed the open cubes; boundaries go to lower cells; the open cells partition the space. -/
structure CellStructure2 (X : Type) [TopologicalSpace X] where
  n0 : ℕ
  n1 : ℕ
  n2 : ℕ
  c0 : Fin n0 → X
  c1 : Fin n1 → ℝ → X
  c2 : Fin n2 → ℝ × ℝ → X
  c1_cont : ∀ k, ContinuousOn (c1 k) (Icc 0 1)
  c2_cont : ∀ k, ContinuousOn (c2 k) (Icc (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Icc (0 : ℝ) 1)
  c1_emb : ∀ k, IsEmbedding ((Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1).domRestrict (c1 k))
  c2_emb : ∀ k, IsEmbedding ((Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1).domRestrict (c2 k))
  c1_bd : ∀ k, c1 k 0 ∈ range c0 ∧ c1 k 1 ∈ range c0
  c2_bd : ∀ k, ∀ p ∈ (Icc (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Icc (0 : ℝ) 1) \ (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1),
    c2 k p ∈ range c0 ∪ ⋃ j, c1 j '' Ioo 0 1
  partition : ∀ x : X, ∃! i : Fin n0 ⊕ (Fin n1 ⊕ Fin n2),
    Sum.elim (fun k => x = c0 k)
      (Sum.elim (fun k => x ∈ c1 k '' Ioo 0 1)

def CellStructure2.euler (C : CellStructure2 X) : ℤ := (C.n0 : ℤ) - C.n1 + C.n2

/-- `m`-extendability (source p. 2) with Mathlib's matching predicates. -/

    toK ((a : ℝ), (b : ℝ)) = pt (lab a b) := by

  rw [toK_eq_iff (lattice_mem ha hb) (pt_mem _)]
  interval_cases a <;> interval_cases b <;> norm_num [lab, labN, vx, vy, nf]

/-! ## Edges -/

inductive Kind
  | H
  | V
  | Dp
  | Dm
  deriving DecidableEq

instance : Fintype Kind where
  elems := {Kind.H, Kind.V, Kind.Dp, Kind.Dm}
  complete := fun x => by cases x <;> decide

/-- Edge index: square `(i,j)` and kind. -/
abbrev EIx := Fin 4 × Fin 3 × Kind

/-- Affine parametrisation of the representative segment. -/
noncomputable def segPt : EIx → ℝ → ℝ × ℝ
  | (i, j, .H), t => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ))
  | (i, j, .V), t => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ), ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)
  | (i, j, .Dp), t => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)
  | (i, j, .Dm), t => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 - t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)

def startN : EIx → ℕ × ℕ
  | (i, j, .H) => ((i : ℕ), (j : ℕ))
  | (i, j, .V) => ((i : ℕ), (j : ℕ))
  | (i, j, .Dp) => ((i : ℕ), (j : ℕ))
  | (i, j, .Dm) => ((i : ℕ) + 1, (j : ℕ))

def endN : EIx → ℕ × ℕ
  | (i, j, .H) => ((i : ℕ) + 1, (j : ℕ))
  | (i, j, .V) => ((i : ℕ), (j : ℕ) + 1)
  | (i, j, .Dp) => ((i : ℕ) + 1, (j : ℕ) + 1)
  | (i, j, .Dm) => ((i : ℕ), (j : ℕ) + 1)

def src (k : EIx) : Fin 12 := lab (startN k).1 (startN k).2
def dst (k : EIx) : Fin 12 := lab (endN k).1 (endN k).2

theorem bounds_aux : ∀ k : EIx, (startN k).1 ≤ 4 ∧ (startN k).2 ≤ 3 ∧
    (endN k).1 ≤ 4 ∧ (endN k).2 ≤ 3 := by decide

theorem src_ne_dst : ∀ k : EIx, src k ≠ dst k := by decide +kernel

theorem adj_src_dst : ∀ k : EIx, adjB (src k) (dst k) = true := by decide +kernel

theorem adj_has_ix : ∀ u v : Fin 12, adjB u v = true → ∃ i : Fin 4, ∃ j : Fin 3, ∃ κ : Kind,
    (src (i, j, κ) = u ∧ dst (i, j, κ) = v) ∨ (src (i, j, κ) = v ∧ dst (i, j, κ) = u) := by
  decide +kernel

theorem cross_disjoint : ∀ (i : Fin 4) (j : Fin 3) (w : Fin 12),
    (w = src (i, j, .Dp) ∨ w = dst (i, j, .Dp)) →
    (w = src (i, j, .Dm) ∨ w = dst (i, j, .Dm)) → False := by
  decide +kernel

theorem segPt_zero (k : EIx) : segPt k 0 = (((startN k).1 : ℝ), ((startN k).2 : ℝ)) := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  cases κ <;> simp [segPt, startN]

theorem segPt_one (k : EIx) : segPt k 1 = (((endN k).1 : ℝ), ((endN k).2 : ℝ)) := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  cases κ <;> simp [segPt, endN]

theorem continuous_segPt (k : EIx) : Continuous (segPt k) := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  cases κ
  · show Continuous fun t : ℝ => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ))
    fun_prop
  · show Continuous fun t : ℝ => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ), ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)
    fun_prop
  · show Continuous fun t : ℝ => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)
    fun_prop
  · show Continuous fun t : ℝ => (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 - t, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + t)
    fun_prop

/-- The edge arc of index `k`. -/
noncomputable def segArc (k : EIx) : C(unitInterval, KleinBottle) :=
  ⟨fun t => toK (segPt k t), continuous_toK.comp ((continuous_segPt k).comp continuous_subtype_val)⟩

theorem segArc_apply (k : EIx) (t : unitInterval) : segArc k t = toK (segPt k t) := rfl

theorem segArc_zero (k : EIx) : segArc k 0 = pt (src k) := by
  show toK (segPt k 0) = pt (src k)
  rw [segPt_zero]
  exact toK_lattice _ _ (bounds_aux k).1 (bounds_aux k).2.1

theorem segArc_one (k : EIx) : segArc k 1 = pt (dst k) := by
  show toK (segPt k 1) = pt (dst k)
  rw [segPt_one]
  exact toK_lattice _ _ (bounds_aux k).2.2.1 (bounds_aux k).2.2.2

/-! ## Planar facts about open segment points -/

theorem not_nat_frac {a b : ℕ} {t : ℝ} (ht0 : 0 < t) (ht1 : t < 1) (h : (a : ℝ) = b + t) :
    False := by
  rcases lt_trichotomy a b with hab | rfl | hab
  · have : (a : ℝ) + 1 ≤ b := by exact_mod_cast (show a + 1 ≤ b by omega)
    linarith
  · linarith
  · have : (b : ℝ) + 1 ≤ a := by exact_mod_cast (show b + 1 ≤ a by omega)
    linarith

theorem nat_eq_of_close {a b : ℕ} {s t : ℝ} (hs0 : 0 < s) (hs1 : s < 1) (ht0 : 0 < t)
    (ht1 : t < 1) (h : (a : ℝ) + s = b + t) : a = b := by
  rcases lt_trichotomy a b with hab | hab | hab
  · have : (a : ℝ) + 1 ≤ b := by exact_mod_cast (show a + 1 ≤ b by omega)
    linarith
  · exact hab
  · have : (b : ℝ) + 1 ≤ a := by exact_mod_cast (show b + 1 ≤ a by omega)
    linarith

theorem nat_eq_of_twice {a b : ℕ} {s : ℝ} (hs0 : 0 < s) (hs1 : s < 1)
    (h : (a : ℝ) + 2 * s = b + 1) : a = b := by
  rcases lt_trichotomy a b with hab | hab | hab
  · have : (a : ℝ) + 1 ≤ b := by exact_mod_cast (show a + 1 ≤ b by omega)
    linarith
  · exact hab
  · have : (b : ℝ) + 1 ≤ a := by exact_mod_cast (show b + 1 ≤ a by omega)
    linarith

theorem fin4_le (i : Fin 4) : ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) ≤ 3 := by
  exact_mod_cast (show (i : ℕ) ≤ 3 by have := i.isLt; omega)

theorem fin3_le (j : Fin 3) : ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) ≤ 2 := by
  exact_mod_cast (show (j : ℕ) ≤ 2 by have := j.isLt; omega)

/-- Open segment points lie in the rectangle and are their own normal form. -/
theorem segPt_open {k : EIx} {s : ℝ} (hs0 : 0 < s) (hs1 : s < 1) :
    segPt k s ∈ rect ∧ nf (segPt k s) = segPt k s := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  have hi := fin4_le i
  have hj := fin3_le j
  have hi0 : (0 : ℝ) ≤ ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) := Nat.cast_nonneg _
  have hj0 : (0 : ℝ) ≤ ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) := Nat.cast_nonneg _
  cases κ
  · show (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + s, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ)) ∈ rect ∧
      nf (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + s, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ)) = (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + s, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ))
    refine ⟨mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hj0, by linarith⟩⟩, ?_⟩
    exact nf_self_mk (fun hc => hc.1.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
      (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith)
  · show (((i : ℕ) : ℝ), ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + s) ∈ rect ∧
      nf (((i : ℕ) : ℝ), ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + s) = (((i : ℕ) : ℝ), ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + s)
    refine ⟨mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hi0, by linarith⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩, ?_⟩
    exact nf_self_mk (fun hc => hc.2.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
      (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith)
  · have hm : (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + s, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + s) ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩
    exact ⟨openRect_subset_rect hm, nf_of_openRect hm⟩
  · have hm : (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 - s, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + s) ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩
    exact ⟨openRect_subset_rect hm, nf_of_openRect hm⟩

/-- Open segment points are not lattice points. -/
theorem segPt_not_lattice {k : EIx} {s : ℝ} (hs0 : 0 < s) (hs1 : s < 1) (a b : ℕ) :
    segPt k s ≠ ((a : ℝ), (b : ℝ)) := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  intro h
  cases κ <;> simp only [segPt, Prod.mk.injEq] at h <;> obtain ⟨h1, h2⟩ := h
  · exact not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h1.symm
  · exact not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h2.symm
  · exact not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h1.symm
  · exact not_nat_frac (a := (i : ℕ) + 1) (b := a) hs0 hs1 (by push_cast; linarith)

/-- Two diagonals of the same square, in either order. -/
def IsCrossPair (k l : EIx) : Prop :=
  k.1 = l.1 ∧ k.2.1 = l.2.1 ∧
    ((k.2.2 = .Dp ∧ l.2.2 = .Dm) ∨ (k.2.2 = .Dm ∧ l.2.2 = .Dp))

/-- **Planar intersection classification** of open representative segments. -/
theorem seg_meet {k l : EIx} {s t : ℝ} (hs0 : 0 < s) (hs1 : s < 1) (ht0 : 0 < t) (ht1 : t < 1)
    (h : segPt k s = segPt l t) :
    (k = l ∧ s = t) ∨ (IsCrossPair k l ∧ s = 1 / 2 ∧ t = 1 / 2) := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  rcases l with ⟨i', j', κ'⟩
  cases κ <;> cases κ' <;> simp only [segPt, Prod.mk.injEq] at h <;> obtain ⟨h1, h2⟩ := h
  -- H H
  · have hi := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h1
    have hj : (j : ℕ) = j' := by exact_mod_cast h2
    have hi' : ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hi]
    exact Or.inl ⟨Prod.ext (Fin.ext hi) (Prod.ext (Fin.ext hj) rfl), by linarith⟩
  · exact (not_nat_frac ht0 ht1 h2).elim
  · exact (not_nat_frac ht0 ht1 h2).elim
  · exact (not_nat_frac ht0 ht1 h2).elim
  -- V _
  · exact (not_nat_frac ht0 ht1 h1).elim
  · have hj := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h2
    have hi : (i : ℕ) = i' := by exact_mod_cast h1
    have hj' : ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((j' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hj]
    exact Or.inl ⟨Prod.ext (Fin.ext hi) (Prod.ext (Fin.ext hj) rfl), by linarith⟩
  · exact (not_nat_frac ht0 ht1 h1).elim
  · exact (not_nat_frac (a := (i' : ℕ) + 1) (b := i) ht0 ht1 (by push_cast; linarith)).elim
  -- Dp _
  · exact (not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h2.symm).elim
  · exact (not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h1.symm).elim
  · have hi := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h1
    have hj := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h2
    have hi' : ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hi]
    exact Or.inl ⟨Prod.ext (Fin.ext hi) (Prod.ext (Fin.ext hj) rfl), by linarith⟩
  · have hj := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h2
    have hj' : ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((j' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hj]
    have hst : s = t := by linarith
    have hi := nat_eq_of_twice hs0 hs1 (show ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 2 * s = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 by linarith)
    have hi' : ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hi]
    refine Or.inr ⟨⟨Fin.ext hi, Fin.ext hj, Or.inl ⟨rfl, rfl⟩⟩, by linarith, by linarith⟩
  -- Dm _
  · exact (not_nat_frac hs0 hs1 h2.symm).elim
  · exact (not_nat_frac (a := (i : ℕ) + 1) (b := i') hs0 hs1 (by push_cast; linarith)).elim
  · have hj := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h2
    have hj' : ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((j' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hj]
    have hst : s = t := by linarith
    have hi := nat_eq_of_twice hs0 hs1 (show ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) + 2 * s = ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 by linarith)
    have hi' : ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hi]
    refine Or.inr ⟨⟨Fin.ext hi.symm, Fin.ext hj, Or.inr ⟨rfl, rfl⟩⟩, by linarith, by linarith⟩
  · have hj := nat_eq_of_close hs0 hs1 ht0 ht1 h2
    have hj' : ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((j' : ℕ) : ℝ) := by rw [hj]
    have hst : s = t := by linarith
    have hi : (i : ℕ) = i' := by exact_mod_cast (show ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) = ((i' : ℕ) : ℝ) by linarith)
    exact Or.inl ⟨Prod.ext (Fin.ext hi) (Prod.ext (Fin.ext hj) rfl), hst⟩


/-! ## Arcs in the quotient -/

theorem unit_cases (s : unitInterval) : (s : ℝ) = 0 ∨ (s : ℝ) = 1 ∨ s ∈ openUnit := by
  rcases s.2.1.eq_or_lt with h | h
  · exact Or.inl h.symm
  · rcases s.2.2.eq_or_lt with h' | h'
    · exact Or.inr (Or.inl h')
    · exact Or.inr (Or.inr ⟨h, h'⟩)

theorem segArc_at_zero (k : EIx) {t : unitInterval} (ht : (t : ℝ) = 0) :
    segArc k t = pt (src k) := by
  have : t = 0 := Subtype.ext ht
  rw [this]
  exact segArc_zero k

theorem segArc_at_one (k : EIx) {t : unitInterval} (ht : (t : ℝ) = 1) :
    segArc k t = pt (dst k) := by
  have : t = 1 := Subtype.ext ht
  rw [this]
  exact segArc_one k

theorem toK_open_eq {k l : EIx} {s t : unitInterval} (hs : s ∈ openUnit) (ht : t ∈ openUnit)
    (h : segArc k s = segArc l t) : segPt k s = segPt l t := by
  have h1 := segPt_open (k := k) hs.1 hs.2
  have h2 := segPt_open (k := l) ht.1 ht.2
  have h' := (toK_eq_iff h1.1 h2.1).mp h
  rwa [h1.2, h2.2] at h'

/-- Intersection classification in the Klein bottle. -/
theorem arc_meet {k l : EIx} {s t : unitInterval} (hs : s ∈ openUnit) (ht : t ∈ openUnit)
    (h : segArc k s = segArc l t) :
    (k = l ∧ (s : ℝ) = t) ∨ (IsCrossPair k l ∧ (s : ℝ) = 1 / 2 ∧ (t : ℝ) = 1 / 2) :=
  seg_meet hs.1 hs.2 ht.1 ht.2 (toK_open_eq hs ht h)

/-- Edge interiors avoid all vertices. -/
theorem arc_ne_pt {k : EIx} {s : unitInterval} (hs : s ∈ openUnit) (w : Fin 12) :
    segArc k s ≠ pt w := by
  intro h
  have h1 := segPt_open (k := k) hs.1 hs.2
  have h' := (toK_eq_iff h1.1 (pt_mem w)).mp h
  rw [h1.2, nf_pt] at h'
  exact segPt_not_lattice hs.1 hs.2 (vx w) (vy w) h'

theorem segArc_injective (k : EIx) : Injective (segArc k) := by
  intro s t h
  rcases unit_cases s with hs | hs | hs <;> rcases unit_cases t with ht | ht | ht
  · exact Subtype.ext (hs.trans ht.symm)
  · rw [segArc_at_zero k hs, segArc_at_one k ht] at h
    exact absurd (pt_injective h) (src_ne_dst k)
  · rw [segArc_at_zero k hs] at h
    exact absurd h.symm (arc_ne_pt ht _)
  · rw [segArc_at_one k hs, segArc_at_zero k ht] at h
    exact absurd (pt_injective h).symm (src_ne_dst k)
  · exact Subtype.ext (hs.trans ht.symm)
  · rw [segArc_at_one k hs] at h
    exact absurd h.symm (arc_ne_pt ht _)
  · rw [segArc_at_zero k ht] at h
    exact absurd h (arc_ne_pt hs _)
  · rw [segArc_at_one k ht] at h
    exact absurd h (arc_ne_pt hs _)
  · rcases arc_meet hs ht h with ⟨_, e⟩ | ⟨⟨_, _, hc⟩, _, _⟩
    · exact Subtype.ext e
    · rcases hc with ⟨h1, h2⟩ | ⟨h1, h2⟩ <;> rw [h1] at h2 <;> exact absurd h2 (by decide)

/-! ## Edge indices versus graph edges -/

abbrev toSym2 (k : EIx) : Sym2 (Fin 12) := s(src k, dst k)

theorem exists_ix : ∀ z : Sym2 (Fin 12), z ∈ kleinGraph.edgeSet → ∃ k : EIx, toSym2 k = z := by
  refine Sym2.ind (fun u v => ?_)
  intro h
  rw [SimpleGraph.mem_edgeSet] at h
  obtain ⟨i, j, κ, hk⟩ := adj_has_ix u v h
  refine ⟨(i, j, κ), ?_⟩
  rcases hk with ⟨h1, h2⟩ | ⟨h1, h2⟩
  · rw [toSym2, h1, h2]
  · rw [toSym2, h1, h2, Sym2.eq_swap]

/-- The edge index of a graph edge. -/
noncomputable def idx (e : kleinGraph.edgeSet) : EIx := Classical.choose (exists_ix e.1 e.2)

theorem idx_spec (e : kleinGraph.edgeSet) : toSym2 (idx e) = e.1 :=
  Classical.choose_spec (exists_ix e.1 e.2)

theorem idx_inj {e f : kleinGraph.edgeSet} (h : idx e = idx f) : e = f :=
  Subtype.ext (by rw [← idx_spec e, ← idx_spec f, h])

theorem mem_edge_iff (e : kleinGraph.edgeSet) (w : Fin 12) :
    w ∈ (e : Sym2 (Fin 12)) ↔ w = src (idx e) ∨ w = dst (idx e) := by
  rw [← idx_spec e]
  exact Sym2.mem_iff

/-- **The drawing of `G` on the Klein bottle.** -/
noncomputable def kleinDrawing : Drawing kleinGraph KleinBottle where
  pt := pt
  arc e := segArc (idx e)
  ends e := ⟨src (idx e), dst (idx e), (idx_spec e).symm, segArc_zero _, segArc_one _⟩

/-! ## Crossing pairs -/

theorem crossPair_no_common {k l : EIx} (hkl : IsCrossPair k l) (w : Fin 12)
    (hk : w = src k ∨ w = dst k) (hl : w = src l ∨ w = dst l) : False := by
  rcases k with ⟨i, j, κ⟩
  rcases l with ⟨i', j', κ'⟩
  obtain ⟨h1, h2, h3⟩ := hkl
  obtain rfl : i = i' := h1
  obtain rfl : j = j' := h2
  have h3' : (κ = Kind.Dp ∧ κ' = Kind.Dm) ∨ (κ = Kind.Dm ∧ κ' = Kind.Dp) := h3
  rcases h3' with ⟨rfl, rfl⟩ | ⟨rfl, rfl⟩
  · exact cross_disjoint i j w hk hl
  · exact cross_disjoint i j w hl hk

theorem crossPair_unique {k l m : EIx} (h1 : IsCrossPair k l) (h2 : IsCrossPair k m) :
    l = m := by
  obtain ⟨a1, b1, c1⟩ := h1
  obtain ⟨a2, b2, c2⟩ := h2
  have hκ : l.2.2 = m.2.2 := by
    rcases c1 with ⟨e1, f1⟩ | ⟨e1, f1⟩ <;> rcases c2 with ⟨e2, f2⟩ | ⟨e2, f2⟩
    · rw [f1, f2]
    · rw [e1] at e2; exact absurd e2 (by decide)
    · rw [e1] at e2; exact absurd e2 (by decide)
    · rw [f1, f2]
  exact Prod.ext (a1.symm.trans a2) (Prod.ext (b1.symm.trans b2) hκ)

theorem dp_affine (i : Fin 4) (j : Fin 3) : ∀ r : ℝ, 0 < r → r < 1 →
    segPt (i, j, .Dp) r =
      (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 / 2, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 / 2) + (r - 1 / 2) • ((1 : ℝ), (1 : ℝ)) ∧
    segPt (i, j, .Dp) r ∈ openRect := by
  intro r hr0 hr1
  have hi := fin4_le i
  have hj := fin3_le j
  have hi0 : (0 : ℝ) ≤ ((i : ℕ) : ℝ) := Nat.cast_nonneg _
  have hj0 : (0 : ℝ) ≤ ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) := Nat.cast_nonneg _
  refine ⟨?_, ?_⟩
  · show (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + r, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + r) = _
    simp only [Prod.mk_add_mk, Prod.smul_mk, smul_eq_mul]
    congr 1 <;> ring
  · exact mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩

theorem dm_affine (i : Fin 4) (j : Fin 3) : ∀ r : ℝ, 0 < r → r < 1 →
    segPt (i, j, .Dm) r =
      (((i : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 / 2, ((j : ℕ) : ℝ) + 1 / 2) + (r - 1 / 2) • ((-1 : ℝ), (1 : ℝ)) ∧
    segPt (i, j, .Dm) r ∈ openRect := by
  intro r hr0 hr1

  rw [isOpen_iff_mk, hpre]
  refine IsOpen.union ?_ ?_
  · exact ((isOpen_lt continuous_snd continuous_const).inter hW).preimage continuous_subtype_val
  · exact ((isOpen_lt continuous_const continuous_snd).inter
      (hW.preimage (by fun_prop))).preimage continuous_subtype_val

theorem isOpenEmbedding_seamA : IsOpenEmbedding seamA :=
  IsOpenEmbedding.of_continuous_injective_isOpenMap continuous_seamA seamA_injective
    isOpenMap_seamA

/-! ## Strip across the left/right seam -/

def stripB : Set (ℝ × ℝ) := Ioo (-1 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 3

theorem mem_stripB_mk {x y : ℝ} : (x, y) ∈ stripB ↔ (-1 < x ∧ x < 1) ∧ (0 < y ∧ y < 3) :=
  Iff.rfl

theorem isOpen_stripB : IsOpen stripB := isOpen_Ioo.prod isOpen_Ioo

/-- Chart across the left/right seam: left of the seam use the translated right strip. -/
noncomputable def seamB (p : stripB) : KleinBottle :=
  if (0 : ℝ) ≤ p.1.1 then toK p.1 else toK (4 + p.1.1, p.1.2)

theorem seamB_of_nonneg (p : stripB) (h : (0 : ℝ) ≤ p.1.1) : seamB p = toK p.1 :=
  ite_eq_left h

theorem seamB_of_neg (p : stripB) (h : ¬ (0 : ℝ) ≤ p.1.1) :
    seamB p = toK (4 + p.1.1, p.1.2) :=
  ite_eq_right h

theorem seamB_agree {y : ℝ} (hy0 : 0 < y) (hy3 : y < 3) : toK (0, y) = toK (4, y) := by
  rw [toK_eq_iff (mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨le_refl 0, by norm_num⟩, ⟨hy0.le, hy3.le⟩⟩)
    (mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by norm_num, le_refl 4⟩, ⟨hy0.le, hy3.le⟩⟩)]
  exact nf_left_right y

theorem continuous_seamB : Continuous seamB := by
  have hc1 : Continuous fun p : stripB => toK p.1 := continuous_toK.comp continuous_subtype_val
  have hc2 : Continuous fun p : stripB => toK (4 + p.1.1, p.1.2) :=
    continuous_toK.comp (by fun_prop)
  show Continuous fun p : stripB =>
    if (0 : ℝ) ≤ p.1.1 then toK p.1 else toK (4 + p.1.1, p.1.2)
  refine continuous_if_le (f := fun _ : stripB => (0 : ℝ)) (g := fun p : stripB => p.1.1)
    continuous_const (continuous_fst.comp continuous_subtype_val)
    hc1.continuousOn hc2.continuousOn ?_
  rintro ⟨⟨x, y⟩, hxy⟩ hp
  obtain ⟨-, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩ := mem_stripB_mk.mp hxy
  obtain rfl : x = 0 := hp.symm
  show toK (0, y) = toK (4 + 0, y)
  rw [add_zero]
  exact seamB_agree hy0 hy3

theorem seamB_injective : Injective seamB := by
  rintro ⟨⟨x, y⟩, hp⟩ ⟨⟨x', y'⟩, hq⟩ h
  obtain ⟨⟨hx0, hx1⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩ := mem_stripB_mk.mp hp
  obtain ⟨⟨hx0', hx1'⟩, ⟨hy0', hy3'⟩⟩ := mem_stripB_mk.mp hq
  apply Subtype.ext
  show (x, y) = (x', y')
  by_cases hx : (0 : ℝ) ≤ x <;> by_cases hx' : (0 : ℝ) ≤ x'
  · rw [seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx, seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(x', y'), hq⟩ hx'] at h
    have m1 : (x, y) ∈ rect := mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0.le, hy3.le⟩⟩
    have m2 : (x', y') ∈ rect := mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx', by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0'.le, hy3'.le⟩⟩
    have hn := (toK_eq_iff m1 m2).mp h
    rwa [nf_self_mk (x := x) (y := y)
        (fun hc => hc.2.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
        (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith),
      nf_self_mk (x := x') (y := y')
        (fun hc => hc.2.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
        (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith)] at hn
  · rw [seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx, seamB_of_neg ⟨(x', y'), hq⟩ hx'] at h
    have m1 : (x, y) ∈ rect := mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0.le, hy3.le⟩⟩
    have m2 : (4 + x', y') ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0', hy3'⟩⟩
    have e := eq_of_nf_eq_openRect m2 ((toK_eq_iff m1 (openRect_subset_rect m2)).mp h)
    obtain ⟨e1, -⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
    exfalso; linarith
  · rw [seamB_of_neg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx, seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(x', y'), hq⟩ hx'] at h
    have m1 : (4 + x, y) ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩
    have m2 : (x', y') ∈ rect := mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx', by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0'.le, hy3'.le⟩⟩
    have e := eq_of_nf_eq_openRect m1 ((toK_eq_iff m2 (openRect_subset_rect m1)).mp h.symm)
    obtain ⟨e1, -⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
    exfalso; linarith
  · rw [seamB_of_neg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx, seamB_of_neg ⟨(x', y'), hq⟩ hx'] at h
    have m1 : (4 + x, y) ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩
    have m2 : (4 + x', y') ∈ openRect :=
      mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0', hy3'⟩⟩
    have e := eq_of_nf_eq_openRect m2
      ((toK_eq_iff (openRect_subset_rect m1) (openRect_subset_rect m2)).mp h)
    obtain ⟨e1, e2⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
    rw [Prod.mk.injEq]
    constructor <;> linarith

theorem isOpenMap_seamB : IsOpenMap seamB := by
  intro V hV
  obtain ⟨O, hO, rfl⟩ := isOpen_induced_iff.mp hV
  have hW : IsOpen (stripB ∩ O) := isOpen_stripB.inter hO
  have hpre : (Quotient.mk kleinSetoid) ⁻¹' (seamB '' (Subtype.val ⁻¹' O)) =
      Subtype.val ⁻¹' ({p : ℝ × ℝ | p.1 < 1} ∩ (stripB ∩ O)) ∪
      Subtype.val ⁻¹' ({p : ℝ × ℝ | 3 < p.1} ∩
        (fun p : ℝ × ℝ => (p.1 - 4, p.2)) ⁻¹' (stripB ∩ O)) := by
    ext ⟨⟨a, b⟩, hr⟩
    obtain ⟨⟨ha0, ha4⟩, ⟨hb0, hb3⟩⟩ := mem_rect_mk.mp hr
    simp only [mem_preimage, mem_image, mem_union, mem_inter_iff, mem_ofPred_eq]
    constructor
    · rintro ⟨⟨⟨x, y⟩, hp⟩, hpO, hpr⟩
      obtain ⟨⟨hx0, hx1⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩ := mem_stripB_mk.mp hp
      rw [← toK_val] at hpr
      by_cases hx : (0 : ℝ) ≤ x
      · rw [seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx] at hpr
        have m1 : (x, y) ∈ rect := mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0.le, hy3.le⟩⟩
        have hn := (toK_eq_iff m1 hr).mp hpr
        rcases hx.eq_or_lt with hx' | hx'
        · subst hx'
          rw [nf_self_mk (x := 0) (y := y)
            (fun hc => hc.2.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
            (by norm_num) (fun e => by linarith)] at hn
          rcases nf_eq_left hy0 hy3 hn.symm with e | e
          · obtain ⟨rfl, rfl⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
            exact Or.inl ⟨by norm_num, hp, hpO⟩
          · obtain ⟨rfl, rfl⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
            refine Or.inr ⟨by norm_num, ?_⟩
            rw [sub_self]
            exact ⟨hp, hpO⟩
        · have mi : (x, y) ∈ openRect :=
            mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨hx', by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩
          have e := eq_of_nf_eq_openRect mi hn.symm
          obtain ⟨rfl, rfl⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
          exact Or.inl ⟨hx1, hp, hpO⟩
      · rw [seamB_of_neg ⟨(x, y), hp⟩ hx] at hpr
        have mi : (4 + x, y) ∈ openRect :=
          mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩
        have e := eq_of_nf_eq_openRect mi ((toK_eq_iff (openRect_subset_rect mi) hr).mp hpr).symm
        obtain ⟨rfl, rfl⟩ := Prod.mk.inj e
        refine Or.inr ⟨by linarith, ?_⟩
        rw [add_sub_cancel_left]
        exact ⟨hp, hpO⟩
    · rintro (⟨-, hmB, hmO⟩ | ⟨ha3, hmB, hmO⟩)
      · refine ⟨⟨(a, b), hmB⟩, hmO, ?_⟩
        rw [seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(a, b), hmB⟩ ha0]
        exact toK_val ⟨(a, b), hr⟩
      · refine ⟨⟨(a - 4, b), hmB⟩, hmO, ?_⟩
        obtain ⟨-, ⟨hy0, hy3⟩⟩ := mem_stripB_mk.mp hmB
        rw [← toK_val]
        by_cases ha : (0 : ℝ) ≤ a - 4
        · rw [seamB_of_nonneg ⟨(a - 4, b), hmB⟩ ha]
          have ha4' : a = 4 := by linarith
          subst ha4'
          show toK (4 - 4, b) = toK (4, b)
          rw [sub_self]
          exact seamB_agree hy0 hy3
        · rw [seamB_of_neg ⟨(a - 4, b), hmB⟩ ha]
          show toK (4 + (a - 4), b) = toK (a, b)
          congr 1
          rw [Prod.mk.injEq]
          constructor <;> ring
  rw [isOpen_iff_mk, hpre]
  refine IsOpen.union ?_ ?_
  · exact ((isOpen_lt continuous_fst continuous_const).inter hW).preimage continuous_subtype_val
  · exact ((isOpen_lt continuous_const continuous_fst).inter
      (hW.preimage (by fun_prop))).preimage continuous_subtype_val

theorem isOpenEmbedding_seamB : IsOpenEmbedding seamB :=
  IsOpenEmbedding.of_continuous_injective_isOpenMap continuous_seamB seamB_injective
    isOpenMap_seamB


/-! ## Characteristic maps of the canonical cell structure -/

noncomputable def cellA (s : ℝ) : KleinBottle := toK (4 * s, 0)
noncomputable def cellB (s : ℝ) : KleinBottle := toK (0, 3 * s)
noncomputable def cellF (p : ℝ × ℝ) : KleinBottle := toK (4 * p.1, 3 * p.2)

noncomputable def c1fun (k : Fin 2) : ℝ → KleinBottle := if k = 0 then cellA else cellB

theorem c1fun_zero : c1fun 0 = cellA := ite_eq_left rfl
theorem c1fun_one : c1fun 1 = cellB := ite_eq_right (by decide)

theorem fin2_cases : ∀ k : Fin 2, k = 0 ∨ k = 1 := by decide
theorem fin1_eq : ∀ k : Fin 1, k = 0 := by decide

theorem continuous_cellA : Continuous cellA := by
  unfold cellA; exact continuous_toK.comp (by fun_prop)
theorem continuous_cellB : Continuous cellB := by
  unfold cellB; exact continuous_toK.comp (by fun_prop)
theorem continuous_cellF : Continuous cellF := by
  unfold cellF; exact continuous_toK.comp (by fun_prop)

/-- Open 1-cell `a` sits in the seam chart `seamA`. -/
noncomputable def ιA (s : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1) : stripA :=
  ⟨(4 * s.1, 0), mem_stripA_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith [s.2.1], by linarith [s.2.2]⟩,
    ⟨by norm_num, by norm_num⟩⟩⟩

noncomputable def rA (p : stripA) : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 :=
  ⟨p.1.1 / 4, div_pos p.2.1.1 (by norm_num), (div_lt_one (by norm_num)).mpr p.2.1.2⟩

theorem isEmbedding_ιA : IsEmbedding ιA := by
  refine IsEmbedding.of_leftInverse (f := rA) (g := ιA) ?_ ?_ ?_
  · intro s
    apply Subtype.ext
    show 4 * s.1 / 4 = s.1
    ring
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun p : stripA => p.1.1 / 4
    fun_prop
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun s : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 => (4 * s.1, (0 : ℝ))
    fun_prop

noncomputable def ιB (s : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1) : stripB :=
  ⟨(0, 3 * s.1), mem_stripB_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by norm_num, by norm_num⟩,
    ⟨by linarith [s.2.1], by linarith [s.2.2]⟩⟩⟩

noncomputable def rB (p : stripB) : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 :=
  ⟨p.1.2 / 3, div_pos p.2.2.1 (by norm_num), (div_lt_one (by norm_num)).mpr p.2.2.2⟩

theorem isEmbedding_ιB : IsEmbedding ιB := by
  refine IsEmbedding.of_leftInverse (f := rB) (g := ιB) ?_ ?_ ?_
  · intro s
    apply Subtype.ext
    show 3 * s.1 / 3 = s.1
    ring
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun p : stripB => p.1.2 / 3
    fun_prop
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun s : Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 => ((0 : ℝ), 3 * s.1)
    fun_prop

noncomputable def ιF (p : (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 : Set (ℝ × ℝ))) : openRect :=
  ⟨(4 * p.1.1, 3 * p.1.2), mem_openRect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith [p.2.1.1], by linarith [p.2.1.2]⟩,

noncomputable def rF (q : openRect) : (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 : Set (ℝ × ℝ)) :=
  ⟨(q.1.1 / 4, q.1.2 / 3),
    ⟨⟨div_pos q.2.1.1 (by norm_num), (div_lt_one (by norm_num)).mpr q.2.1.2⟩,
     ⟨div_pos q.2.2.1 (by norm_num), (div_lt_one (by norm_num)).mpr q.2.2.2⟩⟩⟩

theorem isEmbedding_ιF : IsEmbedding ιF := by
  refine IsEmbedding.of_leftInverse (f := rF) (g := ιF) ?_ ?_ ?_
  · intro p
    apply Subtype.ext
    show (4 * p.1.1 / 4, 3 * p.1.2 / 3) = p.1
    rw [Prod.mk.injEq]
    constructor <;> ring
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun q : openRect => (q.1.1 / 4, q.1.2 / 3)
    fun_prop
  · apply continuous_induced_rng.mpr
    show Continuous fun p : (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 : Set (ℝ × ℝ)) =>
      (4 * p.1.1, 3 * p.1.2)
    fun_prop

theorem embF : IsEmbedding ((Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1).domRestrict cellF) := by

theorem bdA0 : cellA 0 = toK (0, 0) := by unfold cellA; rw [mul_zero]

theorem cellF_bd (p : ℝ × ℝ)

  · right

    rw [c1fun_one, e0, h]
    unfold cellB
    congr 1
    rw [Prod.mk.injEq]

    exact ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩

/-! ## Partition into open cells -/

noncomputable def shapeIdx (q : ℝ × ℝ) : Fin 1 ⊕ (Fin 2 ⊕ Fin 1) :=
  if q.1 = 0 ∧ q.2 = 0 then Sum.inl 0
  else if q.2 = 0 then Sum.inr (Sum.inl 0)
  else if q.1 = 0 then Sum.inr (Sum.inl 1)
  else Sum.inr (Sum.inr 0)

def cellMem (x : KleinBottle) : Fin 1 ⊕ (Fin 2 ⊕ Fin 1) → Prop :=
  Sum.elim (fun _ => x = toK (0, 0))
    (Sum.elim (fun k => x ∈ c1fun k '' Ioo 0 1)
      (fun _ => x ∈ cellF '' (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1)))

theorem cell_rep {x : KleinBottle} {i : Fin 1 ⊕ (Fin 2 ⊕ Fin 1)} (h : cellMem x i) :
    ∃ q : ℝ × ℝ, q ∈ rect ∧ x = toK q ∧ nf q = q ∧ shapeIdx q = i := by
  rcases i with k | k | k
  · have hx : x = toK (0, 0) := h
    refine ⟨(0, 0), mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨le_refl 0, by norm_num⟩, ⟨le_refl 0, by norm_num⟩⟩, hx,
      nf_corner_mk ⟨Or.inl rfl, Or.inl rfl⟩, ?_⟩
    rw [fin1_eq k]
    dsimp only [shapeIdx]
    exact ite_eq_left ⟨rfl, rfl⟩
  · have hx : x ∈ c1fun k '' Ioo 0 1 := h
    rcases fin2_cases k with rfl | rfl
    · rw [c1fun_zero] at hx
      obtain ⟨s, ⟨hs0, hs1⟩, rfl⟩ := hx
      refine ⟨(4 * s, 0), mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩, ⟨le_refl 0, by norm_num⟩⟩,
        rfl, nf_self_mk (fun hc => hc.1.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
          (fun e => by linarith) (by norm_num), ?_⟩
      dsimp only [shapeIdx]
      rw [ite_eq_right (fun hc => by linarith [hc.1]), ite_eq_left rfl]
    · rw [c1fun_one] at hx
      obtain ⟨s, ⟨hs0, hs1⟩, rfl⟩ := hx
      refine ⟨(0, 3 * s), mem_rect_mk.mpr ⟨⟨le_refl 0, by norm_num⟩, ⟨by linarith, by linarith⟩⟩,
        rfl, nf_self_mk (fun hc => hc.2.elim (fun e => by linarith) (fun e => by linarith))
          (by norm_num) (fun e => by linarith), ?_⟩
      dsimp only [shapeIdx]
      rw [ite_eq_right (fun hc => by linarith [hc.2]), ite_eq_right (fun hc => by linarith),
        ite_eq_left rfl]
  · have hx : x ∈ cellF '' (Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1 ×ˢ Ioo (0 : ℝ) 1) := h
    obtain ⟨p, ⟨⟨a0, a1⟩, ⟨b0, b1⟩⟩, rfl⟩ := hx
    have hi : (4 * p.1, 3 * p.2) ∈ openRect :=
