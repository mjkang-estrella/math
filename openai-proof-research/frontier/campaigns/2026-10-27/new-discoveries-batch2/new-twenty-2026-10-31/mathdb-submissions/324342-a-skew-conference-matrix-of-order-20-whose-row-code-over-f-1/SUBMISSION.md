## Summary (paste into the MathDB summary field)

Refuted: the Etzion-Vardy-Yaakobi conjecture that the code of length p+1 over Z_p generated by a conference matrix of order p+1 is MDS of dimension (p+1)/2 with minimum distance (p+3)/2. At p = 19, an explicit Goethals-Seidel skew conference matrix of order 20 has an F_19 row code of dimension 10 containing a nonzero codeword of weight 9, below the claimed 11. The proof is formally verified in Lean 4 with Mathlib and kernel-checked with only the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. It was produced with AI assistance and independent AI review, with a bounded literature search and no human peer review.

## Result

Etzion-Vardy-Yaakobi conjecture that the code of length p + 1 over Z_p generated by a conference matrix of order p + 1 is MDS of dimension (p+1)/2 and minimum distance (p+3)/2. False at p = 19: an explicit Goethals-Seidel skew conference matrix of order 20 has an F_19 row code of dimension 10 containing a nonzero codeword of weight 9 < 11.
