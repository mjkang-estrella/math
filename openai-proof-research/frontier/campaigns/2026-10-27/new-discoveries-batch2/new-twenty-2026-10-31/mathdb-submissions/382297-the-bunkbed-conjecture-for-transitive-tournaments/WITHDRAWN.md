# Withdrawn on 2026-11-23

This result is no longer counted as a new result of this campaign.

The bunkbed inequality for every transitive tournament in Hollom's independent-edge model
(Conjecture 6.4 of "The bunkbed conjecture is not robust to generalisation", arXiv:2406.01790;
Conjecture 6.6.4 of Hollom's thesis) was proved publicly on 2026-10-09 in the repository
github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog, file
`solutions/round-2026-10-09/probability/Q270-transitive-tournament-bunkbed.md`, in greater
generality (any retention probabilities), before this campaign accepted its own proof.
The earlier work was found on 2026-11-23 by a prior-art audit.

The proof and the Lean source in this folder are kept as an independent verification of the
statement; they should not be cited as the first proof.
