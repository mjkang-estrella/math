## Problem statement as recorded on MathDB

Let a proper spherical triangle on the unit sphere have side lengths and area that are rational multiples of $\pi$, and let its measurement be the tuple $(E,a,b,c)$, with $a\le b\le c$. Define $\Lambda_1$ and $\Lambda_2$ as the sets of measurements given in the source. **Rational spherical triangle measurement conjecture.** The tuples in $\Lambda_1\cup\Lambda_2$ are the only measurements of proper rational spherical triangles. In particular, there are only finitely many measurements of proper rational spherical triangles with mutually distinct side lengths, namely, the seven measurements found by Little and Coxeter. This conjecture would classify all proper rational spherical triangles and implies that the mutually distinct-side cases are exactly the seven known examples.

## Summary of the result

The conjecture holds: the rational solutions of L'Huilier's equation in the proper range are exactly the two-right-side family and the eight sporadic tuples of the source (one in Λ₁ and the seven of Λ₂), so by the source's Proposition 2.2 these are the only measurements of proper rational spherical triangles, and the seven with three distinct sides are the only such triangles. The proof is computer-assisted. In tangent coordinates the equation becomes a vanishing sum of 23 roots of unity. The new step is a line lemma: if the equation holds along a whole line of parameters through a proper point, the behaviour at the two ends of the segment forces the line into the two-right-side family. With the polygon inequality this gives a rank condition at every other solution, which shows that only the primes 2, 3, 5, 7 divide the common denominator and bounds their exponents, so the denominator divides 352,800 = 2⁵·3²·5²·7². An exact search over these denominators finishes the classification. Lean defines the solution set Ω₂ and the sets Λ₁, Λ₂ literally from the source and proves Ω₂ = Λ₁ ∪ Λ₂ with no hypotheses; the line lemma, the prime support, the exponent bounds and the whole finite search (replayed in the kernel in certified batches, with proofs that the batches cover every case) are inside Lean.
