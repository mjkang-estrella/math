In this section, we seek to extend Theorems 2.1 and 2.4 to the case when A ⊂ C. Konyagin
and Rudnev [6] generalised the result of Solymosi, from [8], in order to prove that

neighbouring lines without overcounting the quantity we were interested in. An ingenious
