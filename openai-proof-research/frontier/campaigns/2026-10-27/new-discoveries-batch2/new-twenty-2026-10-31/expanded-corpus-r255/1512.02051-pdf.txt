Definition 1.1. The graph G is called a (+Kt )-graph if G + e contains a new

3       Algorithms
In this section we present algorithms for finding all graphs in Hmax (a1 , ..., as ; q; n)
with the help of a computer. The remaining graphs in H(a1 , ..., as ; q; n) can
be obtained by removing edges from the maximal graphs. The algorithms
are modifications of the algorithm from [2]. However, we will present them
in detail since they are essential to this paper. The idea for these algorithms
comes from [27] (see Algorithm A1). Similar algorithms are used in [5], [33],
[13], [29], [1] and [3]. Also with the help of a computer, results for Folkman
numbers are obtained in [9], [31], [30] and [6]. Let us also note the important
role of the nauty programs [17] in this work. We use them for fast generation
of non-isomorphic graphs and graph isomorph rejection.
