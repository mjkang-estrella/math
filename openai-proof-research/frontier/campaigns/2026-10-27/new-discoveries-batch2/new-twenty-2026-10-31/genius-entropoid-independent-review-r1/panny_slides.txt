  Murdoch/Toyoda/Bruck (1939–1944):
   Theorem. For every entropic quasigroup (G, ∗), there exists an
   abelian group (G, ·), commuting automorphisms σ, τ of (G, ·),
   and an element c ∈ G, such that
                         x ∗ y = xσ · yτ · c .

Structure theorem


  Murdoch/Toyoda/Bruck (1939–1944):
   Theorem. For every entropic quasigroup (G, ∗), there exists an
   abelian group (G, ·), commuting automorphisms σ, τ of (G, ·),
   and an element c ∈ G, such that
                          x ∗ y = xσ · yτ · c .



  =⇒ All non-associative powers of an element x are of the form
                             xA = xξ · cγ
  with ξ, γ ∈ Z[σ, τ ].

Structure theorem


  Murdoch/Toyoda/Bruck (1939–1944):
   Theorem. For every entropic quasigroup (G, ∗), there exists an
   abelian group (G, ·), commuting automorphisms σ, τ of (G, ·),
   and an element c ∈ G, such that
                          x ∗ y = xσ · yτ · c .



  =⇒ All non-associative powers of an element x are of the form
                             xA = xξ · cγ
  with ξ, γ ∈ Z[σ, τ ]. Recovering ξ, γ reduces to DLP in (G, ·).

  Previous slide: Recover ξ, γ ∈ Z[σ, τ ] such that gA = gξ · cγ .
  Idea: (ξ, γ) is an equivalent key for A.


  However, (ξ, γ) are non-unique given just one pair (g, gA ),
  and different solutions yield different x 7→ xξ · cγ .


  Solution (me, 2021):
   Lemma. The correct solution (ξ, γ) satisfies ξ = 1+(σ+τ −1)·γ.
   Any such (ξ, γ) computes x 7→ xA correctly for all x in hgi∗ .

  =⇒ Have only one unknown γ instead of two (ξ, γ).
