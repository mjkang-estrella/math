positive probability if the limit limN →∞ p(CP, N ) is strictly positive.

     If z + [0, 1]n ⊂ [0, 2]n and z = (z1 , . . . , zn ) ∈ N1 Zn then zi ∈ {0, N1 , . . . , 1}. Take
a discrete cube packing CP = (z i + [0, 1]n )1≤i≤m of [0, 2]n . For a given coordinate
1 ≤ j ≤ n we set φ(zji ) = ti,j with ti,j a parameter if 0 < zji < 1 and φ(zji ) = zji if
zji = 0 or 1. If z i = (z1i , z2i , . . . , zni ) then we set φ(z i ) = (φ(z1i ), . . . , φ(zni )) and to CP
we associate the combinatorial cube packing φ(CP) = (φ(z i ) + [0, 1]n )1≤i≤m .
     Take a torus discrete cube packing CP = (z i + [0, 1]n )1≤i≤m with z i ∈ N1 Zn . For a
given coordinate 1 ≤ j ≤ n we set φ(zji ) = tk,j if zji ≡ Nk (mod 2) and φ(zji ) = tk,j +1 if
zji ≡ Nk +1 (mod 2) with tk,j a parameter. Similarly, we set φ(z i ) = (φ(z1i ), . . . , φ(zni ))
and we define φ(CP) = (φ(z i ) + [0, 1]n )1≤i≤m the associated torus combinatorial cube
packing.
