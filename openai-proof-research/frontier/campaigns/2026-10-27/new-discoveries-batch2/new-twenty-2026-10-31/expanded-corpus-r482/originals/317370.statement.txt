Let $n$ be even, and consider combinatorial torus cube packings, where a packing is **non-extensible** if no further cube can be added while preserving the packing conditions. The number of parameters of a packing is denoted by $N(\mathcal{CP})$.

**Even-dimensional parameter-count conjecture.** There exist non-extensible combinatorial torus cube packings with $n+2$ cubes and

$$
N(\mathcal{CP})=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
$$

The preceding proposition proves the analogous minimal-cube and parameter statement for odd $n$, while this even-dimensional existence assertion is posed as an unproved conjecture in the supplied text.
