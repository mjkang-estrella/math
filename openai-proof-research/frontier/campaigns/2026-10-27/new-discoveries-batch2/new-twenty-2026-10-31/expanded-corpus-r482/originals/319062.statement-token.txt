"Let $f:\\{0,1\\}^n\\to\\{0,1\\}$ be an $n$-variable monotone Boolean function, and let $L\\in GL_n(\\mathbb{F}_2)$ be an invertible linear transformation. Define\n\n$$\n(Lf)(x)=f(Lx).\n$$\n\n**Keller–Pilpel's conjecture.** The total influence of $f$ should satisfy\n\n$$\nI(f)\\leqslant I(Lf).\n$$\n\nThe conjecture asks whether applying any invertible linear transformation over $\\mathbb{F}_2$ can only increase the total influence of a monotone Boolean function. It was raised by Keller and Pilpel; the supplied source does not establish its resolution."
