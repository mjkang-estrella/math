"Let $K\\subset L$ be a commutative field extension, and let $A,B\\subset L$ be nonzero finite-dimensional $K$-subspaces. Let $\\langle AB\\rangle$ denote the $K$-linear span of the products $ab$ with $a\\in A$ and $b\\in B$, and let $H$ be its stabilizer. **Hou's conjecture.** One should have\n\n$$\n\\dim _{K}\\langle AB\\rangle \\geq \\dim _{K}A+\\dim _{K}B-\\dim _{K}H.\n$$\n\nThis is the version of the linear Kneser theorem expected to remain valid without assuming that all algebraic elements of $L$ over $K$ are separable. The source reports that it is known when $\\dim_K A\\leq 5$, while the general case remains open."
