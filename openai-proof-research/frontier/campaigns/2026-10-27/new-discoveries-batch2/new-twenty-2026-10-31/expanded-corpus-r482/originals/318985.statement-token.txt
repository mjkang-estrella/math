"Let $Z_p$ be the cyclic group of $p$ elements, let $S$ be a sequence of $p$ elements in $Z_p$, and let $h(S)$ denote the maximal repetition value of $S$. A subsequence has index $p$ when its index, defined by\n\n$$\n\\operatorname{Index}(T)=\\min_{(m,p)=1}\\{\\sigma(|mT|_p)\\},\n$$\n\nis equal to $p$. **Prime-order repetition conjecture.** The sequence $S$ contains a subsequence $T$ with index $p$ and with length satisfying\n\n$$\n|T|\\leq h(S).\n$$\n\nThis is proposed as a generalization of the $d=p$ case of the Lemke–Kleitman conjecture. The paper presents it as an open problem; it is consistent with the proven repetition-based results discussed there."
