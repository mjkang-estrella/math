Let $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ be an $n$-variable monotone Boolean function, and let $L\in GL_n(\mathbb{F}_2)$ be an invertible linear transformation. Define

$$
(Lf)(x)=f(Lx).
$$

**Keller–Pilpel's conjecture.** The total influence of $f$ should satisfy

$$
I(f)\leqslant I(Lf).
$$

The conjecture asks whether applying any invertible linear transformation over $\mathbb{F}_2$ can only increase the total influence of a monotone Boolean function. It was raised by Keller and Pilpel; the supplied source does not establish its resolution.
