Let $G$ be a $2$-connected non-planar graph contained in a closed surface $F$. For a pair $(F,G)$, define its representativity by

$$
r(F,G)=\min_{C\in \mathcal{C}_F}|C\cap G|,
$$

where $\mathcal{C}_F$ is the set of all essential loops embedded in $F$. Define

$$
r(G)=\max_{F\in\mathcal{F}}r(F,G),
$$

where $\mathcal{F}$ is the set of all closed surfaces containing $G$. **Strong embedding conjecture.** It holds that

$$
r(G)\ge 2.
$$

This is attributed in the source to Robertson and Vitray. The excerpt gives no evidence that the conjecture has been resolved.
