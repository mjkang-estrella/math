Let $d$ be a positive integer, and let a **$k$-chain** be a graph of the type defined in the paper, with vertices embedded in $\mathbb{R}^d$. A graph is **generically globally rigid** if a generic realization is uniquely determined up to Euclidean congruence by its edge lengths.

**Connectivity conjecture.** Any $(d+1)$-connected $k$-chain in $\mathbb{R}^d$ with more than $\binom{d+2}{2}$ vertices is generically globally rigid.

The paper presents this as an experimentally supported conjecture about the unexplored range above $\binom{d+2}{2}$ vertices; its resolution is not supplied here.
