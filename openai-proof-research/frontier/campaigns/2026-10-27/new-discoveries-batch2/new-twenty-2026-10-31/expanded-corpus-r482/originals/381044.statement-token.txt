"Let $f:\\mathbb{F}_2^n\\to\\mathbb{C}$ be a $K$-approximate quadratic, meaning that $f$ has large $U^3$ norm in the sense used in the paper. A quadratic phase is a function of the form $(-1)^\\psi$, where $\\psi$ is a quadratic polynomial. **Polynomial inverse conjecture for the $U^3$ norm over $\\mathbb{F}_2^n$.** The function $f$ $K^{-C}$-correlates with a quadratic phase $(-1)^\\psi$. This is one of the polynomial inverse conjectures for the $U^3$ Gowers norm; the source gives no resolution evidence."
