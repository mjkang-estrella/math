"Let $G$ be a (weighted) graph. Let $\\mathcal{F}$ be the set of all spanning forests of $G$, and let $\\mathcal{B}$ be the probability distribution on spanning forests in which each forest has probability proportional to its weight. For two distinct edges $e_1,e_2$ of $G$, **Grimmett–Winkler's spanning-forest correlation conjecture.**\n\n$$\n\\mathrm{Pr}_{F\\sim\\mathcal{B}}[e_1\\in F \\:|\\: e_2\\not \\in F] \\geq \\mathrm{Pr}_{F\\sim\\mathcal{B}}[e_1\\in F \\:|\\: e_2\\in F].\n$$\n\nThis is an analogue of the negative correlation inequality for spanning trees. It was stated by Grimmett and Winkler and is still open."
