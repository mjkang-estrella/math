323005 Let $\mathcal{C}_{2^k}$ be the cone constrained by the Laplacian minor of a leafed $n$-cycle, where $n=2^k$ for some integer $k\geq 2$. Let $f(q)$ be the numerator in the proposed expression

$$
\sigma_{\mathcal{C}_{2^k}}(q,1,1,\ldots,1)=\dfrac{f(q)}{\left(1-q^{2^k}\right)\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}\left(1-q^{2^{k-(i+1)}}\right)^{2^i}}.
$$

**Near-symmetry conjecture.** The generating function has this form, and if $(a_0,\ldots,a_j)$ is the coefficient list of $f(q)$, then appending $0$ to the end of this list and subtracting its reverse produces the coefficient list of

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n-2}\binom{n-2}{i}(-1)^i q^{ni}.
$$

This conjecture is based on experimental evidence for leafed cycles of length a power of two and describes the observed near-symmetry of the numerator despite the absence of a reflexive $n$-th slice in the non-prime case.
325240 Let $G$ be a simple hypergraph with edge ideal $I=I(G)$, and let $\operatorname{gr}_I=\bigoplus_{q\geq 0}I^q/I^{q+1}$ be the associated graded ring. A hypergraph is **König** when its matching number equals its vertex-cover number; the source identifies this with equality of the height and monomial grade of its edge ideal, and minors are obtained by vertex deletions and contractions. **Algebraic Conforti–Cornuéjols conjecture.** If $G$ has the packing property, then $\operatorname{gr}_I$ is reduced. Equivalently, if $G$ and all its minors are König, then $\operatorname{gr}_I$ is reduced. By the stated equivalences, reducedness of $\operatorname{gr}_I$ is equivalent to equality of symbolic and ordinary powers in every degree and to $I$ being normally torsion-free; the source presents this as a restatement of the unresolved Conforti–Cornuéjols conjecture.

This is presented as an interesting weakening of Hadwiger's conjecture, since $\operatorname{had}(G)\leq\operatorname{had}_f(G)$. Its general validity remains open.

This is presented alongside $\chi(G)\leq\operatorname{had}_f(G)$ as another weakening related to Hadwiger's conjecture. Its general validity remains open.
331056 Let $G$ be a graph with no isolated edges. For an edge colouring $c:E(G)\to\{1,2,\ldots,k\}$, define the weighted degree of a vertex $v$ by

$$
s_c(v):=\sum_{u\in N(v)}c(uv).
$$

The colouring is **neighbour sum distinguishing** if $s_c(u)\neq s_c(v)$ for every adjacent pair $u,v$.

**1-2-3 Conjecture.** Every graph $G$ containing no isolated edges admits a neighbour sum distinguishing $3$-edge colouring.

A neighbour sum distinguishing $5$-edge colouring is known for every graph without isolated edges, but whether three colours always suffice remains open. The conjecture is equivalent to seeking a locally irregular multigraph obtained by replacing each edge with a number of parallel edges from $1$ to $3$.

333629 Let $n,k,s$ be positive integers, and let $\mathcal F\subset {[n]\choose k}$ be a $k$-uniform family. Write $\nu(\mathcal F)$ for its matching number and $\tau(\mathcal F)$ for its covering number. For $i\in[k]$, let

$$
\mathcal A_i^{(k)}(n,s):=\Bigl\{A\in {[n]\choose k}:\bigl|A\cap[(s+1)i-1]\bigr|\ge i\Bigr\},
$$

and define

$$
\mathcal H^{(k)}(n,s):=\Bigl\{H\in {[n]\choose k}:H\cap[s]\ne\emptyset\Bigr\}\cup\bigl\{[s+1,s+k]\bigr\}-\Bigl\{H\in {[n]\choose k}:H\cap[s]=\{s\},\ H\cap[s+1,s+k]=\emptyset\Bigr\}.

**Hilton–Milner-type conjecture.** If $\nu(\mathcal F)=s$ and $\tau(\mathcal F)>s$, then

 This conjecture seeks the sharp bound for families with matching number exactly $s$ that are not covered by $s$ elements, generalizing the Hilton–Milner phenomenon; its resolution status is not specified in the source.
335074 Let $H_1,\ldots,H_r$ be graphs with $m_2(H_1) \geq \ldots \geq m_2(H_r)>1$, and let $m_2(H_1,H_2)$ denote their asymmetric density as defined by

$$
m_2(H_1,H_2):=\max \left\{\frac{e(H_1')}{v(H_1')-2+1/m_2(H_2)}: H_1' \subseteq H_1 \text{ and } e(H_1') \geq 1\right\}.
$$
For a graph $G$, being $(H_1,\ldots,H_r)$-Ramsey means that every $r$-colouring of $G$ contains a copy of $H_i$ in colour $i$ for some $i$. **Kohayakawa–Kreuter's conjecture.** There are positive constants $c,C>0$ such that

$$
\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \mathbb{P}\left[ G_{n,p} \text{ is } (H_1,\ldots,H_r)\text{-Ramsey} \right] =\begin{cases} 0 &\text{ if } p<cn^{-1/m_2(H_1,H_2)}; \\ 1 &\text{ if } p>Cn^{-1/m_2(H_1,H_2)}.\end{cases}
$$
This predicts the threshold for the asymmetric Ramsey property of a random graph; the source notes that the conjecture is actually stated for $r=2$ and describes the threshold as sharp. Its resolution status is not established by the supplied material.
338620 Let $S_1,\ldots,S_k\subset[n]$ be random sets, formed by including each $j\in S_i$ independently with probability $1/2$. A matrix $A$ satisfies the associated zero-pattern conditions when

$$
A_{i,j}=0\quad\text{whenever }j\in S_i,
$$

and every $k\times k$ minor not forced to be singular by these zero constraints is nonsingular.

**Exponential field-size conjecture.** Assume that such a $k\times n$ matrix exists over a field $\mathbb{F}$. Then, with high probability over the random set system, there is an absolute constant $c>0$ such that

$$
|\mathbb{F}|\ge c\binom{n}{k}^{c}.
$$

The conjecture asserts that most set systems require exponential field size, so the probabilistic construction with field size roughly $\binom{n}{k}$ cannot be significantly improved. The source presents it as an open problem; only specialized examples requiring exponential field size are known.
338745 Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, let $T(G)$ be its total graph, and let $\chi_l(T(G))$ denote the list chromatic number of $T(G)$. A graph is **equitably $k$-choosable** if every list assignment with lists of size $k$ admits a proper coloring in which each color is used on at most $\lceil |V(T(G))|/k\rceil$ vertices. **The List Equitable Total Coloring Conjecture.** For every graph $G$, the total graph $T(G)$ is equitably $k$-choosable for every

This is the natural combination of equitable total coloring and list total coloring, and the paper introduces it as an open extension of Fu's conjecture.

**Extension of the main Berge-cycle theorem.** If $\mathcal H$ has no Berge cycle of length $k$ or longer, then

$$
e(\mathcal H)\leq \frac{n-1}{k-2}\binom{k-1}{r}.

Moreover, equality should hold if and only if $\partial_2\mathcal H$ is connected and every block $D$ of $\partial_2\mathcal H$ satisfies

$$
D=K_{k-1}\quad\text{and}\quad \mathcal H[D]=K_{k-1}^{(r)}.

This is the boundary-case extension of the paper's proved theorem for $k\geq r+3$, including its equality characterization. The source does not report a resolution, while it distinguishes the shorter-cycle range $k\leq r+1$ as different.
343166 Let $D$ be a $(v,k,\mu)$ difference set in a group $G$, and define

is $D$-graceful; consequently, there exists a $(v,\Gamma,\lambda_i)$ difference graph with vertex set $D$.
