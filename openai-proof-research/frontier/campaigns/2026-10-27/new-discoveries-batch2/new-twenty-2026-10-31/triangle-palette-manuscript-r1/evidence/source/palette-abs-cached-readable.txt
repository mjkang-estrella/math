     Title: A note on edge colorings distinguishing all triangles in a graph 
     Authors: Monika Pilsniak ,  Mariusz Wozniak              View a PDF of the paper titled A note on edge colorings distinguishing all triangles in a graph, by Monika Pilsniak and 1 other authors 
     View PDF 
     HTML (experimental) 



     
             Abstract: We consider edge colorings of a graph in such a way that each two different triangles have distinct colorings. It is an extension of the well-known idea of distinguishing all maximal stars in a graph. It was introduced in literature in 1985 and studied by many authors in various variants, but always for stars. We estimate new invariants regarding triangles for proper and general colorings.
