For any n-edge tree T, there exists a labelling \phi\colon V(T)\to\mathbb{Z}_{n} so that for all xy\in E(T), \phi(x)+\phi(y) attains a distinct value, and furthermore, \phi uses exactly one label on two vertices. Latin squares are in one to one correspondence with 1-factorisations of complete digraphs (with loops allowed) [ 31 ] , and therefore upon the deletion of a colour class (corresponding to the self-loops), yield n-vertex, (n-1)-regular properly coloured digraphs.
