For a graph class \mathcal{C}, the sandwich problem for {\mathcal{C}} is the following computational problem, denoted by \SP({\mathcal{C}}): The input is a pair of graphs ((V,E_{1}),(V,E_{2})) where E_{1}\subseteq E_{2}, and the task is to decide whether there is an edge set E such that E_{1}\subseteq E\subseteq E_{2} and the graph (V,E) belongs to \mathcal{C}. In particular, the SP for \mathcal{C} is at least as hard as the recognition problem for \mathcal{C}: a graph (V,E) belongs to \mathcal{C} if and only if the input ((V,E),(V,E)) is a yes-instance of the SP problem for \mathcal{C}.
