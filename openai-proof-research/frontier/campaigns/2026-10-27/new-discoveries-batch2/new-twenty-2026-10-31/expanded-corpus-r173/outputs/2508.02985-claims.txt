We provide some support in favour of its statement in both directions. 4.1 A family of locally s-colourable graphs G with chromatic discrepancy at most \chi(G)-s+1 In this subsection we briefly justify that, if true, Conjecture 1 is almost best possible. Our construction is again based on Mycielski graphs. For integers k\geq s\geq 2, we recursively define \mathscr{M}_{k}^{s} as follows. If k=s then we let \mathscr{M}_{k}^{s} be the complete graph on s vertices. Otherwise, k>s and \mathscr{M}_{k}^{s} is obtained from \mathscr{M}_{k-1}^{s} by adding a twin of each vertex and a vertex adjacent to all the twins — that is, \mathscr{M}_{k}^{s} is the Mycielskian of \mathscr{M}_{k-1}^{s}.
