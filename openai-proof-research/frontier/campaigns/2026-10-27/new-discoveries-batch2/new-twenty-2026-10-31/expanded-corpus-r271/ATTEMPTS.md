## 379976 - Avoidability threshold for powers in odd-difference arithmetic progressions

Disposition: parked_uniform_word_avoidance.

Read: Kao-Rampersad-Shallit-Silva v1/v2 Conjectures 12-13, paperfolding construction, Theorem 14 proof.

A finite forbidden-prefix proof for any fixed r<2 would only prove that exponent, not the whole continuum. Compactness can turn a finite obstruction into nonavoidability once proved, but supplies no uniform bound as r tends to 2. The source paperfolding examples have critical exponent 2 and cannot refute the claim.

Remaining gap or exclusion: Need either a proved uniform structural classification of square-free odd-progression words or a new infinite construction with critical exponent strictly below 2. Neither survived ordinary analysis; no finite sweep run.

## 377899 - Monotonicity of relaxation time for ferromagnetic Ising Glauber dynamics

Disposition: parked_uniform_spectral_monotonicity.

Read: Nacu introduction heat-bath and coupling conventions; cycle monotonicity theorem and proof routing; later mean-field result identified by live primary search only.

For a reversible heat-bath matrix, derivative of a simple eigenvalue is <f,P_prime f>_pi after normalization. Ferromagnetic association does not directly give the sign of that expression because P_prime has signed entries and the eigenfunction changes with couplings. Cycle invariant subspace arguments do not cover arbitrary degree.

Remaining gap or exclusion: Need an all-graph sign argument or an exact algebraic crossing witness for the second eigenvalue, with heat-bath rates held fixed. No matrix search or numerical execution. Mean-field equal-coupling result is not coordinatewise all-graph resolution.
