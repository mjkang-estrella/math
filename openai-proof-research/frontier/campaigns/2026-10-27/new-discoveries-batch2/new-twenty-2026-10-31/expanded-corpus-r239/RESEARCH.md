**346057, Levin–Ramos–Young, Conjecture 4.9.** The random chain selects a tree edge uniformly and swaps its two occupation statuses; the stationary distribution on unordered pairs is uniform. Definition 4.1 also tosses a fair holding coin first; the script omits that coin, so its matrices are twice the source covariances. Equality tests are unaffected. This normalization is not a counterexample. For a rooted plane unitrivalent tree, use DFS vertex numbering. At each branch vertex v with ordered children a,b, its coordinate counts moves along (v,b) when the second particle is in a's subtree. This implements the 'lies on top' summation of Definition 2.9 and Theorem 2.10. If L is the unweighted two-particle configuration Laplacian, D the divergence vectors of those signed edge indicators, and c_i their positive oriented-edge counts, the diagnostic computes

    Sigma_ij = 2/(choose(N,2)*(N-1)) * (delta_ij*c_i - D_i^T L_grounded^{-1} D_j).

The grounded solve is performed with rational arithmetic, with zero-sum and matrix-symmetry checks. The source covariance is one half of this displayed nonlazy covariance. This is an ordinary exact diagnostic derived from Theorem 3.11, not a Lean semantics certificate. No collision between nonisomorphic abstract trees was found in the initial search. Equal matrices for different plane/root encodings of the SAME abstract tree are discarded: the source's abstract and Conjecture B use tree isomorphism, and a planar-order-only issue would not earn credit. The source's Figure 2 comparison and Proposition 4.16 already use leaf-swap symmetry; no source-symmetry bridge is claimed. Further work would require either a genuinely new abstract-tree collision, or a full reconstruction proof.
