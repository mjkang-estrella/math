Let $K_n$ be a complete graph whose edges are colored red and blue. A **same-color monochromatic path cover** is a collection of monochromatic paths, all in one common color, whose vertices cover $V(K_n)$.

**Erdős–Gyárfás same-color path cover conjecture.** The vertex set of every $2$-colored $K_n$ can be covered by at most

$$
\sqrt{n}
$$

monochromatic paths of the same color. This would be best possible.

A bound of $2\sqrt n$ is known. The sharper bound remains open.
