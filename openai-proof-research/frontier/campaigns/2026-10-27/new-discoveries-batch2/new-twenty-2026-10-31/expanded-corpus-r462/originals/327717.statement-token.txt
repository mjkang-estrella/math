"Let $H$ be a directed graph. The $H$-colouring problem asks whether an input directed graph admits a homomorphism to $H$.\n\n**Dichotomy conjecture.** The $H$-colouring problem is polynomial-time solvable or NP-complete.\n\nThis conjecture seeks a complete complexity dichotomy for homomorphism problems to fixed directed graphs. The source states that it remains open; no graph-theoretic classification is known."
