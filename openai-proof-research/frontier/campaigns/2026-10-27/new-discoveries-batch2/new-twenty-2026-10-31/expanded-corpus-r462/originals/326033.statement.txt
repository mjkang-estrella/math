Let $b$ and $k$ be positive integers, and consider kiss-free snakes inscribed in a rectangle of size $b\times k$. The length of an inscribed partially directed snake is $\left\lfloor \frac{(b+1)(k+1)}{2}\right\rfloor-1$. **Maximal-length conjecture.** The maximal length of a kiss-free snake inscribed in a $b\times k$ rectangle is

$$
\left\lfloor \frac{(b+1)(k+1)}{2}\right\rfloor-1.
$$

This conjecture has been generalized to tree polyominoes and proved by other authors, so the claim is solved.
