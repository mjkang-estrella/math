Let $T$ be a tournament on $n$ vertices, and let $F$ be a **forest** on at most $n$ vertices with $c$ non-trivial components. **Forest embedding conjecture.** If $F$ has at most

$$
\frac{n}{2}+c-1
$$

edges, then $T$ contains every orientation of $F$.

The source states that this follows from Havet and Thomassé's theorem together with Sumner's conjecture, via a proposition. Since Sumner's conjecture is not presented as fully resolved, this derived conjecture is also not presented as resolved.
