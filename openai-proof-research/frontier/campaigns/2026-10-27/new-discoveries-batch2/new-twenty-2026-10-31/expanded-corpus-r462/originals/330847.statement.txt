A graph is **$t$-perfect** if it satisfies the defining $t$-perfection property for graphs. **Shepherd–Sebő's four-colour conjecture.** Every $t$-perfect graph is $4$-colourable. This is a main open question about $t$-perfect graphs; four colours would be best possible, and the paper notes that $P_6$-free $t$-perfect graphs are $4$-colourable while the corresponding statement for $P_7$-free graphs is unknown.
