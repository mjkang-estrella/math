"Let $G$ be a group, let $R_k$ be a right zero band with $k$ elements, let $S=G\\times R_k$, and let $C$ be a generating set. Write $\\pi_G(C)$ for the projection of $C$ to $G$, and let $\\underline{\\operatorname{Cay}}(\\cdot,\\cdot)$ denote the underlying undirected Cayley graph. **Minor conjecture.** The graph\n\n$$\n\\underline{\\operatorname{Cay}}(G,\\pi_G(C))\n$$\n\nis a minor of\n\n$$\n\\underline{\\operatorname{Cay}}(S,C).\n$$\n\nThe conjecture was proposed as a stronger form of the preceding lemma, but it is false in general: the source explicitly notes that the analogous assertion for products $G\\times S$ with $S$ not a right zero band fails."
