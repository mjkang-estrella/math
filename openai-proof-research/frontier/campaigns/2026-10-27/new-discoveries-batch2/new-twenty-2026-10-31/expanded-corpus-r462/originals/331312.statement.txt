Let $H_1$ and $H_2$ be non-null graphs, and let $H$ be their disjoint union. The extremal function $c(H)$ is the supremum of $e(G)/v(G)$ over non-null graphs $G$ containing no $H$-minor.

**Qian's conjecture.** One should have

$$
c(H) \leq c(H_1)+c(H_2)+1.
$$

This would extend the proved bound for disjoint unions of $2$-connected graphs by removing the connectivity restriction on the components. Its resolution is not given in the supplied text.
