This note reports the formal refutation of MathDB item 352972, the balanced-graph conjecture for ﬂat graphs.
The production endpoint uses the generic discrepancy contrapositive. Additional generic identities prove the
empty-clique convention, ﬁnite support, cutoff invariance, convention decomposition and integer/real cast se-
mantics. Independent Sol r7 review repeated strict builds, kernel checks, the full 156-declaration audit and
source-only replay. Its checked audit supplement links the arbitrary-cutoff real formulation to the endpoint. The
permitted axiom closure is exactly {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}. The author controls contain 11 ex-
ample blocks under labels C1-C10.

2. Source-level statements
    The source layer (module FlatBalance, 502 lines) deﬁnes ﬁnite simple graphs as a structure with symmetry
and irreﬂexivity proofs, clique sets as edge-complete Finsets of vertices, the clique counts c(k), the parity
weighted sums oddCliqueSum and evenCliqueSum over range (card V + 1), the ﬂatness predicate Flat, bal-
ancedness Balanced as discrepancyNat at most one, and the conjecture FlatBalancedConjecture. The K(3,3)
witness on Fin 3 (+) Fin 3 is exhibited ﬂat with clique counts c(0) = 1, c(1) = 6, c(2) = 9, giving odd sum 6, in-
clusive even sum 10 and positive even sum 9: discrepancy 3 under the positive-index convention and 4 under
the c(0)-inclusive convention; either exceeds one. The literal endpoint not_ﬂat_balanced_conjecture is the
source refutation.

3. The generic bridge layer (R1)
    The new module FlatBalanceBridges proves everything below over an arbitrary ﬁnite simple graph G on
any vertex type V; the witness is used only at the ﬁnal instantiation.
3.1 The c(0) = 1 convention, empty graphs included
    Theorem cliqueFinset_zero: for every G, the 0-cliques are exactly the singleton of the empty vertex set.
Theorem cliqueCount_zero: c(G,0) = 1. Both are unconditional in G, so they apply to the empty graph and to
every discrete graph; the convention is a theorem, not an assumption.
3.2 Finite support and cutoff invariance
    Lemma cliqueFinset_empty_of_card_lt and cliqueCount_of_card_lt: no k-cliques exist once card V < k,
since every vertex Finset embeds in univ. The private range-extension lemma sum_range_cutoff states that a ﬁ-
nitely supported summand has cutoff-independent partial sums. Consequently, for any cutoff N with card V <
N, the odd selector sum over range N equals oddCliqueSum G (oddCliqueSum_cutoff), and likewise for the
even selector with c(0) included (evenCliqueSum_cutoff) and the positive-index even part (evenClique-
SumPos_cutoff). The parity sums therefore have honest ﬁnite support; no hidden divergence or convention-de-
pendent truncation exists.
3.3 Convention separation by an exact decomposition
    Theorem evenCliqueSum_decomp: evenCliqueSum G = c(G,0) + evenCliqueSumPos G, pointwise in G, via
the term split (if d = 0 then c else 0) plus (if d even and d > 0 then c else 0). The two source conventions remain
distinct objects; neither is substituted for the other anywhere in the certiﬁcate. Theorem oddAddEvenClique-
Sum recombines the parity selectors into the plain clique-count sum.
3.4 Cast bridges and bound equivalence
    natAbs_cast_abs: the integer cast of Int.natAbs z is the true |z| in Z; no truncated natural subtraction enters
any cast. natAbs_cast_real lifts this to R for natural inputs, and discrepancyNat_cast_real transports the discrep-
ancy exactly: the real cast of discrepancyNat G equals the absolute value of the difference of the real casts of
the odd and even clique sums. balanced_iff_absInt and balanced_iff_absReal: Balanced G agrees with the ab-
solute-value bound at most one, over Z and over R respectively. The endpoint hypothesis therefore transfers
through the cast layer without reproof.
3.5 The generic contrapositive and the endpoint
    not_balanced_of_one_lt_discrepancy: any ﬁnite simple graph whose clique count discrepancy exceeds one
is not balanced; the proof uses the discrepancy hypothesis and nothing else. ﬂat_not_balanced_ex-
ists_via_bridges instantiates the contrapositive at K(3,3) via the source lemma K33_discrepancy_gt_one, and
not_ﬂat_balanced_conjecture_via_bridges re-derives the literal negated universal. The bridge layer is thus for-
mally connected to the source-level conjecture negation through that contrapositive. The cutoff, cast, c(0), par-
ity and bound-equivalence lemmas certify the source semantics; they are not all dependencies of the production
endpoint. In r7, the separately checked audit/R1Link.lean proves source_bound_iff and source_universal_nega-
tion for the arbitrary-cutoff real formulation. This supplement has only the permitted axiom trio and leaves pro-
duction unchanged.
