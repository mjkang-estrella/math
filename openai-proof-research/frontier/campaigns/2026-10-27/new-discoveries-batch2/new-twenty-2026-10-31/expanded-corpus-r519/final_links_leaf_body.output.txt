Conjecture C ([18, Conjecture 6.4]). There exist a constant α > 0 and a function N = N (n)
tending to infinity as n → ∞ such that every 1–3 tree of order n contains at least αN distinct
leaf-to-leaf path lengths between 0 and N .

   Our next result disproves Conjecture C in the following strong form, namely, with a poly-
sublinear upper bound.
