A. Source fidelity

I read the supplied raw MathDB records, PROOF-372630.md, ATTEMPTS-372631.md, results entries, validation, source bodies and receipts, and relevant scripts/outputs. The raw statement of 372630 describes intersection vaguely as a common cycle; that wording is not a reliable definition. The cited paper defines intersection using σπ⁻¹. Definition 1 removes the identity from the Cayley connection set; independent sets impose a condition only on distinct members. Requiring diagonal pairs would make every nonempty family fail, so the submitted distinctness condition is faithful, not a weakening. An even cycle is an orbit with positive even exact length, not an even permutation or merely a return at an even exponent.

The paper defines a maximum singular family to have largest cardinality. Thus Maximum must compare with all intersecting families, rather than just inclusion-maximal families; the Lean definition does. The source discusses left/right translates, and the Lean definition describes exactly aPb, with arbitrary permutations a,b and a Sylow 2-subgroup P. Double-translates of subgroups are cosets of conjugate subgroups.

Immediately before Conjecture 16 the author writes that Sylow subgroups are “precisely the extremal families for all n” with n ≥ 2. This explicitly supports the biconditional classification. A reader seeing only an isolated assertion that Sylow subgroups are extremal might reasonably infer just attainment of a bound. The surrounding text rules against that narrower interpretation as the complete intended scope. Nevertheless, the refutation must always be labelled a uniqueness/classification refutation. It does not disprove the weaker maximum-cardinality claim.

B. Independent mathematics

scripts/independent.py is independent code using direct tuple composition, inverse lookup, cycle traversal and a complete bitset maximum-clique search. It does not call the candidate's search scripts. outputs/pairs.json records all 28 quotients and cycle decompositions. Each has an even cycle. The identity and a 3-cycle provide negative controls. The family is not closed: w1*w5 = (2,0,1,4,3), outside the eight listed elements.

For the double-translate argument, choose h0 in P with a h0 b = 1. Then b = h0⁻¹a⁻¹ and aPb = aPa⁻¹, a subgroup. Equivalently, products are a(h1 h0⁻¹ h2)b. Thus an identity-containing nonclosed family cannot be any subgroup double-translate. Since 120 = 2³·15, every Sylow 2-subgroup of S5 has order 8.

Assume the proposed universal classification. Its reverse implication makes a Sylow subgroup maximum of size 8. The submitted intersecting family also has size 8, so it is maximum. The forward implication then makes it a Sylow double-translate, contradicting nonclosure. This argument does not assume the independently computed optimum as a premise.

The exhaustive search enumerates all maximum cliques containing identity. Its greedy independent color classes bound clique size; pruning uses strict inequality, retaining ties. Every family can be translated to contain identity, and conjugation preserves cycle type. This justifies the normalization and the global counts obtained by counting incidences with vertices. In particular uniqueness holds at n = 4, the sharp power-of-two case. n = 5 is the first failure, but is not literally the first non-power of two: n = 3 also is not a power of two and uniqueness holds there.

There is also a product extension without doing an S6 search. For n = 5+m with no carries in binary addition, F × P_m has the size of a Sylow subgroup of S_n: v₂(n!) = v₂(5!)+v₂(m!). For differing first components an even cycle already occurs there; for equal first components a nonidentity quotient in the 2-group P_m has even order and hence an even cycle. F. Advisory on 372631

The ordinary argument is sound for each odd prime p. With U = I+N, N²=0 and N nonzero, U has order p and central -I has order 2. Therefore A=-U has order 2p. For 0 < |d| < p, order(A^d) = 2p/gcd(2p,d) is either p or 2p. The first p powers form a pairwise p-singular family of size p. Since |GL₂(F_p)| = p(p-1)²(p+1), its Sylow p-subgroups have size p. The family contains I and fails closure at A*A^(p-1)=A^p=-I, which is outside the listed exponents. The same classification contradiction applies. scripts/gl.py independently verifies ten odd primes through 31; this is a diagnostic for the symbolic argument, not a universal proof by enumeration.

The raw MathDB record incorrectly describes individual p-singularity. Identity in the source's allowed families and Definition 1 show that the intended condition is singularity of quotients of distinct members. The construction addresses that source condition, not individual element orders. It refutes uniqueness only. It has no submitted literal GL Lean endpoint and shares the elementary non-subgroup uniqueness obstruction; advise no separate significant-result credit. A different ambient group and a parametric central twist do not warrant a second count.
