64: 2.1    Traditional pebbling
65: A pebbling distribution D is a V (G) → N function. If D(v) ≥ 2 and u and v are adjacent vertices, then we can
66: apply a (v → u) pebbling move. It decreases D(v) by two and increases D(u) by one. A vertex v is reachable
67: under D if either D(v) ≥ 1 or we can apply a sequence of pebbling moves such that the last one is an (u → v)
68: move.
69:     A distribution is solvable if each vertex is reachable under it. We use |D| for the size of distribution D, which
70: is the total number of pebbles placed on the graph. A distribution is optimal on graph G if its size is minimal
71: among all solvable distributions of G. The optimal pebbling number is the size of an optimal distribution and it
72: is denoted by πopt (G).
73:     The coverage of distribution D is the set of reachable vertices. We denote the size of this set by Cov(D).
74:     The covering ratio of D is defined as Cov(D)

1848: Finally, an emerging topic of interest in the general study of permutation classes has been strong and
1849: broad rationality and algebraicity (see [4, 8]). While the presence of infinite antichains necessarily
1850: implies that a class has subclasses whose generating functions are not D-finite, we have shown that
1851: certain subclasses of the 321-avoiding permutations are nevertheless well-structured. To make this
1852: notion precise we say that a class is broadly rational if it and all of its finitely based subclasses have
1853: rational generating functions and/or strongly rational if this holds for all of its subclasses. Therefore
1854: Theorem 1.1 shows that all proper subclasses of the 321-avoiding permutations are broadly rational.
1855: The same counting argument as above shows that every strongly rational class must be well-quasi-
1856: ordered. Thus Theorem 1.1 also implies the following.
1857: Corollary 10.1. A subclass of Av(321) is strongly rational if and only if it is well-quasi-ordered.
1858: 
1859: This represents one more piece of evidence for the following conjecture (which is also supported by
1860: the results of [4]).
1861: Conjecture 10.2. A permutation class is strongly rational if and only if it is well-quasi-ordered
1862: and does not contain the class of 312-avoiding permutations or any symmetry of it.
