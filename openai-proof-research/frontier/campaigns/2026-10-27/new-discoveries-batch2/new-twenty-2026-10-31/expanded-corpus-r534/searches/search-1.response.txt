The Glass–Wagner paper is "Arithmetical Structures on Paths With a Doubled Edge" (arXiv 1903.01398, published in *Integers* 20 (2020), A68). The search didn't surface a full proof of the growth conjecture, so the evidence points to it remaining a conjecture.

- **The conjecture:** The authors propose that the number of smooth arithmetical structures on the doubled-edge path grows at the same rate as a binomial coefficient, the binomial coefficient (m+n-1 choose n), which is Conjecture 1.4 in the paper.
- **What the paper does prove:** It aims to count smooth structures on paths with a doubled edge for small m, specifically P_{1,n}, P_{2,n}, P_{3,n}. The paper also says it will prove a growth-related condition on the end labels directly in Theorem 2.9, but that is not the full conjecture.
- **Current status:** A 2020 follow-up by Kaplan and others describes Glass and Wagner as having formulated a conjecture for how the number of arithmetical structures grows in this case, and a 2019 survey lists the smooth-structure count on this family as a research problem. I found no source reporting a proof of the full conjecture.

I'd treat the conjecture as open unless a more recent source says otherwise. A search for a 2023–2026 follow-up paper would be the next step if you need the current status.
