4  Conjectures and future work
In this section, we discuss some conjectures and directions for future work.

4.1  Rowmotion orbits of interval-closed sets of [m] × [n]
Experimental evidence suggests that it will not be possible to fully catalogue rowmotion orbits in [m] × [n]
for m > 2 and arbitrary n. Nevertheless, some steps in this direction may be possible. It follows from
Theorem 1.2 that for large n, the vast majority of ICS of [2] × [n] belong to orbits of size n + 3. Experimental
evidence suggests that this extends to [m] × [n], in the following sense.
Conjecture 4.1. Fix any positive integer m. Let r(n) be the fraction of interval-closed sets of [m] × [n] that
belong to orbits of size m + n + 1. Then r(n) → 1 as n → ∞.
  One may view this conjecture as asserting that, although the overall behavior of rowmotion on ICS is
complicated and of large order, its behavior on typical ICS is very nice. It would be possible in principle
to prove Conjecture 4.1 by identifying a sufficiently large family of orbits of size m + n + 1, and comparing
with the enumeration of all ICS. We outline a strategy in this direction here.
