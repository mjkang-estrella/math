8.     Conclusion
    In summary, we introduced a new model inspired by problems in opinion dynamics. We described various
phenomena related to this dynamics and examined the system’s long-term behavior. We provided a systematic way
to calculate the frequencies of specific configurations, such as the densities of ultimate leader pairs of order zero.
This method also allows us to compute the probability of an agent leaving its party. Additionally, we numerically
analyzed the long-term behavior of parties and derived general results about the shapes of limiting follower parties.
We also show that follower parties are finite in size at steps 0 and 1. For future work, we plan to prove that follower
parties stay finite at all steps, supported by simulations. Furthermore, we aim to demonstrate that the limiting
objects can only be either ultimate leader pairs or ultimate followers. We also plan to explain the observed ratio
between the densities of the ultimate leader pair point process and the ultimate follower point process. Finally, we
hope to extend these results to higher dimensions.
