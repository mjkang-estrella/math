                                          1 Introduction
                                          In this paper, we only consider graphs or directed graphs (digraphs in short) with no loops, no parallel edges or
                                          arcs nor anti-parallel arcs (in particular our digraphs contain no cycle of length 2).
                                              Given an undirected graph G, we denote by ω(G) the size of a maximum clique of G and by χ(G) its chromatic
                                          number. Given a digraph G, we denote by −     →
                                                                                        χ (G) its dichromatic number, that is the minimum integer k such that
                                          the set of vertices of G can be partitioned into k acyclic subdigraphs.

4.3 Relation with the Erdős-Hajnal property and the BIG ⇒ BIG conjecture

T , T contains a transitive tournament on |T |c vertices. It was proven in [2] that the famous Erdős-Hajnal conjecture
on undirected graphs is equivalent to the conjecture saying that every tournament has the Erdős-Hajnal property.
