## Proof decomposition (matches PROOF-343536.md)

1. `grid_adj_iff`: coordinate adjacency.
2. `rowFlip`, `colFlip`, `transpose`, `A0`: explicit automorphisms.
3. Easy direction: orbit partitions are equitable. `x ↦ g x` restricts to a bijection
   `N(u) ∩ [w] → N(g u) ∩ [g w] = N(g u) ∩ [w]`; use `Finset.card_bij`.
4. `row_determinism` (L2) and its three mirrored versions. Induction on rows with
   multiset/`Finset.card` bookkeeping. It is convenient to reformulate equitability as
   neighbour-class multisets `Multiset.map (Quotient.mk r) (neighborFinset u).val`.
5. Layer functions: `ℓ` for m, n ≥ 3; `min(j, n+1-j)` for m ≤ 2; transposition for n ≤ 2.
   Cell invariance of the layer function is proved by induction on k using the
   neighbour-count invariance of unions of classes.
6. `propagation` (L3): a short induction on the layer value.
7. `border_transitive`: the main case work.
   * m, n ≥ 3: side walks (L4) as functions `Fin (d+1) → V`, corner timing, and the explicit
     choice of g ∈ A0 by cases on the target corner/side; then `row_determinism`.
   * m = 2: column determinism plus regularity of A0 on corners; 2×2 by `decide` on `Fin 2 × Fin 2`
     (15 setoids) or the cycle argument.
   * m = 1: path determinism.
8. Stabilizers: explicit computation in A0, then Corollary B. Use `main` on
   `orbitSetoid (stabilizer x)` and on `orbitSetoid ⊤`.
9. Corollary C: monotone `H ↦ orbitSetoid H`, order-reflecting when a free vertex exists. The
   2×2/3×3 non-isomorphism follows from `Fintype.card` comparison, since ⟨rotation⟩ ≠ A0 have
   the same orbits.

## Expected size and risks

Expect roughly 1500–3000 lines. The heaviest parts are the side-walk/corner-timing case
analysis and the explicit `A0` element choice.

Alternative for the case m, n ≥ 3: replace side walks by a uniform parametrisation of the
border cycle `ZMod (2(m+n)-4) → V` and prove the cycle determinism once.

No `decide` is needed beyond 2×2 and 3×3 sanity cases. Do not use native_decide.
