344161 The specialization conjecture for symmetry-breaking link homology 
Let $L$ be a link, let $E^{\ast,\ast}_2(L,\overline{\theta})$ denote the $E_2$-page associated to the specialized twist $\overline{\theta}$, and let $\wp(x)=x^n$ be the algebraic specialization. The groups $E^{\ast,\ast}_q(L,\overline{\theta})$ denote the pages of the resulting spectral sequence.

**Specialization conjecture.** Under the algebraic specialization $\wp(x)=x^n$, the groups

$$
E^{\ast,\ast}_2(L,\overline{\theta})
$$

are isomorphic to $sl(n)$-link homology. This specialization has a topological lift, meaning that it can be lifted to a map of filtered spectra. Consequently, there is a spectral sequence

$$
E^{\ast,\ast}_q(L,\overline{\theta}),\qquad q\geq 2.
$$

The conjecture is motivated by computations for the Hopf link and the $(2,3)$-torus knot. The proposed topological lift is intended to provide a route toward resolving the conjecture.

344785 The cube-root fluctuation conjecture for the LCS concatenation gain 
Let $V$ and $W$ be independent random words of length $2n$, written as concatenations $V=V_1V_2$ and $W=W_1W_2$, where each component has length $n$. Define

$$
\Delta(V,W)=\operatorname{LCS}(V,W)-\operatorname{LCS}(V_1,W_1)-\operatorname{LCS}(V_2,W_2),
$$

and write $\Delta(2n)=\Delta(V,W)$. **Cube-root fluctuation conjecture.** There are constants $c_1,c_2$ such that

$$
\operatorname{\mathbb{E}}\Delta(n)\sim c_1n^{1/3}
$$

and

$$
\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}\Delta(n)}\sim c_2n^{1/3}.
$$

The conjecture is suggested by the paper’s computational experiments on the excess LCS obtained by allowing matches to cross the two concatenated blocks. The cited prior estimate gives only the upper-order control $\operatorname{\mathbb{E}}\Delta(V,W)=O(\sqrt{n\log n})$, so the cube-root asymptotics remain open.

338731 The dense subgraph detection conjecture 
Let $\mathbb{G}_n$ be the set of graphs with $n$ vertices, and let $G(n,1/2)$ and $G(n,1/2,k,q)$ denote the null and alternative graph distributions, respectively. 341292 The final-matrix exponential-pattern conjecture 
Let $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)$ denote the final-matrix value and let $\mathrm{ST}_n$ be the recursively defined Sierpinski-triangle set. **Exponential-pattern conjecture $(SE_n)$.** Suppose that $i,j\in\{0,\ldots,2^n-1\}$. Then:

1. $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)>0$ if and only if $i$ is even, $j$ is odd, and $(i+1,j)\in\mathrm{ST}_n$.
2. If $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)>0$, then $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)=2^i$.
3. If $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)<0$, then $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)=-2^k$ for some $k$. Furthermore:
 - if $j$ is odd, then $0<k\leq i$, $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^{i'},2^j)=-2^k$ for $k\leq i'\leq i$, and $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^{k-1},2^j)=2^{k-1}$;
 - if $j$ is even and $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^{j+1})>0$, then $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)=-FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^{j+1})$;
 - if $j$ is even and $FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^{j+1})<0$, then $2\cdot FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^j)=FM_{2^n}^{-}(2^i,2^{j+1})$.

The conjecture was experimentally verified for $n\leq5$, but the excerpt also states that, assuming a rank-into-rank cardinal, some $n$ makes $(SE_n)$ false. Thus its universal form is refuted relative to that stated hypothesis.
