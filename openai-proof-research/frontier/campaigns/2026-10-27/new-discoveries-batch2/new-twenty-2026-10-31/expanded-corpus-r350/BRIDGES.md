# Complete correction and routine bridges, all zero credit

## 357407: exact stripped uniqueness witness

Let L be the square grid on Z^2 with edges at Euclidean distance one. It is the 1-skeleton of the usual square cellular decomposition of the plane; its dual is another square grid. It is simple and 3-connected: after removal of at most two vertices, every surviving vertex has at least one of its four disjoint coordinate rays avoiding the removed vertices. That ray reaches the exterior of a sufficiently large rectangle containing the removed vertices. The grid outside the rectangle is connected (walk around the boundary and along rows or columns). Hence every two surviving vertices connect through that exterior. The same applies to the dual.

Let B consist of radius-1/2 circles centred at (i,j), i,j integers. Two distinct circles are tangent iff the squared centre distance is one, equivalently they are grid neighbours, because positive integral squared distance has minimum one and equals one exactly for coordinate neighbours. Non-neighbouring interiors are disjoint. Let D be the radius-1/2 circles at (i+1/2,j+1/2). Its tangency graph is the dual grid. Let D'=2D, all centres and radii multiplied by two. It has the same tangency graph and cellular embedding type as D. Both (B,D) and (B,D') meet the record's separate tangency-graph conditions. Any common similarity mapping (B,D) to (B,D') must have scale one because B contains only radius-1/2 circles. It then cannot change the dual circle radii from 1/2 to one. So simultaneous uniqueness fails under the stripped definition.

This exploits exactly the dropped coupling conditions: the source Definition 2.1 requires orthogonal primal-dual intersections at corresponding face/vertex incidences, disjoint interiors otherwise, and at most one accumulation point, in addition to the separate packings. The pair above is not asserted to satisfy those source conditions. Thus this is a complete literal correction only, not a refutation of source Conjecture 3.3. Independently rescaling the two families is inadmissible for that source problem. No credit or Pi dispatch.

## 367204: complete source-essentiality reduction

Use the source construction C_H in which the H-free subclass has bounded treewidth, while C_H is hereditary weakly sparse and has unbounded treewidth. If Pi is a property prevented by containing H, every Pi subclass is H-free and therefore bounded-treewidth. This refutes the promised Pi subclass in exactly that scope. In the treewidth-hereditary version, choose the source t such that containing every graph of treewidth at most t prevents Pi. In C_t, an unbounded-treewidth subclass cannot omit any such H, since an H-free subclass is bounded-treewidth by Theorem 1.2. It contains all those H, so fails Pi. This is precisely the public source reduction. It does not refute every arbitrary property Pi; in particular it supplies no bounded-twin-width resolution.

## 371331: digon equivalence

Every signed simple edge is switching-equivalent to negative K2. Forbidding that induced object leaves only absent edges and digons. Every digon is a negative 2-cycle. Therefore a vertex set in tilde G is balanced iff it contains no adjacent pair of G: necessity follows from the negative digon, sufficiency from having no edges at all. Thus a balanced covering is exactly an ordinary proper coloring, with identical optimum. Induced tilde F and tilde K_t correspond exactly to induced F and K_t in G. Applying this in both directions identifies the signed conjecture with classical Gyarfas-Sumner. This full reduction is a restatement, not a proof of either universal conjecture, and is not credited.
