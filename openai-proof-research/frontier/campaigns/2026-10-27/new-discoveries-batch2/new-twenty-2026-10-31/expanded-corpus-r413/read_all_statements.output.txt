340707 Let $Q$ be a quiver and let $\alpha$ be a root for $Q$. Write $c_i$ for the coefficients in the polynomial $a_\alpha(q)=\sum_i c_iq^i$ counting absolutely indecomposable representations over finite fields. A cellular tree normal form means a cellular normal form for the indecomposable representations in which the representatives are tree modules.

**Cellular tree normal form conjecture.** The root $\alpha$ admits a cellular tree normal form, with $c_i$ cells of dimension $i$.

This conjecture refines Kac's cellular decomposition conjecture by proposing tree modules as a mechanism for constructing the cells. The paper presents it as likely difficult and does not state that it has been resolved. 

340739 Let $\mathcal L$ satisfy the partial symmetry hypothesis. The component sets of a maximizer are sets $E_i$ occurring in a maximizing tuple $\mathbf E$. **Global convexity conjecture under partial symmetry.** Under suitable strict admissibility and nondegeneracy hypotheses on $({\mathcal L},{\mathbf e})$, the component sets $E_i$ of any maximizer $\mathbf E$ are convex, without assuming that $\mathcal L$ is a small perturbation of a tuple of mappings possessing $\operatorname{Sl}(d)$ invariance. This would extend the paper's perturbative convexity theorem to the full partial-symmetry setting; it remains open. 

340761 Let $G$) be a connected, infinite, locally finite graph. For vertices $u,v,w$ forming a path $uwv$ with $uv\notin E(G)$, write $N(x)$ for the neighborhood of $x$ and $d_G(x)$ for its degree.

**Diestel's conjecture.** The graph $G$ has a Hamilton circle if

$$
d_G(u)+d_G(v)\geq |N(u)\cup N(v)\cup N(w)|
$$

for each such path $uwv$.

This conjecture extends the finite condition of Asratian and Khachatryan to infinite locally finite graphs, replacing Hamilton cycles by Hamilton circles. The source gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open. 

340771 Let $G$ be a spatial trivalent graph with a perfect matching $M$. Let $\langle G:M\rangle_2$ be its $2$-factor polynomial and let the cohomology theory constructed in the paper be associated to $(G,M)$. **Spatial invariance conjecture.** The $2$-factor polynomial and cohomology of $(G,M)$ are invariants of the spatial graph and perfect matching. The source presents this as a natural next step after establishing invariance for planar graphs, and gives no resolution. 

340773 Let $G$ be a planar trivalent graph with perfect matching $M$. Call $M$ **even** when every cycle of $G\setminus M$ has an even number of edges, and let $\langle G:M\rangle_2$ denote the $2$-factor polynomial. **Even perfect matching positivity conjecture.** If $M$ is even, then

$$
\langle G:M\rangle_2(1)>0.
$$

The source states that this follows from the now-proved $2$-factor polynomial counting conjecture, so it is solved. 

340869 Let $C^*(E_1)$ and $C^*(E_3)$ be unital graph $C^*$-algebras with finitely many ideals, and consider the unital extension

$$
0\longrightarrow C^*(E_1)\otimes \mathbb{K}\longrightarrow \mathfrak{X}\longrightarrow C^*(E_3)\longrightarrow 0.
$$

**Graph extension conjecture.** The following are equivalent:

1. $\mathfrak{X}$ is a graph $C^*$-algebra.
2. $\mathfrak{X}$ has real rank zero.
3. The map $\partial_0:K_0(C^*(E_3))\to K_1(C^*(E_1))$ vanishes.

This conjecture seeks a complete characterization of when such unital extensions belong to the class of graph $C^*$-algebras. The status evidence indicates that Bentmann provided a complete solution in the Cuntz–Krieger case, while this broader formulation is recorded as a conjecture. 

340969 Let $\lambda$ be a rectangular staircase of the form

$$
\lambda=\delta_d\circ b^a.
$$

Let $w\in\mathfrak{S}_n$ be a vexillary permutation of shape $\lambda$, meaning that its Rothe diagram can be transformed to $\lambda$ by a permutation of rows and columns. **Reiner–Tenner–Yong's CDE conjecture.** The initial weak order interval $[e,w]$ is CDE with edge density

$$
\frac{(d-1)ab}{a+b}.
$$

This conjecture generalizes the known result for dominant permutations, which are the essentially unique vexillary permutations whose Rothe diagrams equal the prescribed shape. It extends the family of known CDE posets beyond distributive lattices and the previously established dominant weak-order intervals. 

340993 Let $G$ be a directed graph. Theorem~ gives a realization result for graphs without $2$-cycles or $\Delta$-cliques.

**Generalized realization conjecture.** The conclusion of Theorem~ holds even for directed graphs $G$ that may contain $2$-cycles and $\Delta$-cliques.

The conjecture proposes that the restrictions excluding $2$-cycles and $\Delta$-cliques are unnecessary, extending the construction to the more general class of directed graphs. Other realization methods already give robust realizations under the weaker assumption that $G$ has no $1$-cycles, but this stronger extension is not established here. 

341108 Let $n,k$ be positive integers, let $0<\alpha<1$, and let $G\in\mathcal{G}_{n,k}$. Here, $\mu_\alpha(G)$ denotes the generalized distance spectral radius of $G$, and $K(n,k,1)$ is the specified extremal digraph in the paper. **Generalized distance spectral radius conjecture.** One has

$$
\mu_\alpha(G)\geq\mu_\alpha(K(n,k,1)),
$$

with equality if and only if $G\cong K(n,k,1)$. This conjecture proposes the extremal characterization for the general case, extending the cases established earlier in the paper; its resolution is not supplied in the source. 

341122 Let $G$ be a connected graph on $n$ vertices. Let $Sun$ denote the sun graph and let $Balanced\ double\ sun$ denote the balanced double sun. **Gutman–Furtula–Bozkurt conjecture.**

$$
RE(G) \leq \begin{cases} RE(Sun)&\text{if $n$ is odd,}\\ RE(Balanced\ double\ sun)&\text{if $n$ is even.} \end{cases}
$$

The conjecture concerns the connected graphs with maximal Randić energy. The paper proves it for several families, including starlike graphs of odd order, but its general validity is not resolved here. 

341146 Let $A$ be an extremal multidimensional matrix with deficiency $\delta$, and let $\Lambda=(\lambda_{i,j})$ be an optimal hyperplane cover of $A$.

**Divisibility conjecture.** Every entry $\lambda_{i,j}$ is an integer multiple of $\delta$.

The conjecture would impose a discrete structure on optimal hyperplane covers and, as the source observes, would imply uniqueness of the optimal cover. It is presented as an open conjecture, although the paper proves related consequences in special settings. 

341153 Let $w$ be a multilinear commutator word and let $G$ be a finite group in which $|ab|=|a||b|$ whenever the $w$-values $a,b$ have coprime orders. **Shumyatsky's conjecture.** Then $w(G)$ is nilpotent. This conjecture asks whether the order-multiplication condition on coprime-order $w$-values forces the verbal subgroup $w(G)$ to be nilpotent. It is known for certain families of commutator words, including the simple commutator words described in the preceding result, but remains open for general multilinear commutator words. 

341163 Let $\mathcal T$ be the tree of numerical semigroups, and let $S$ be a numerical semigroup such that the subtree $\mathcal T(S)$ rooted at $S$ is infinite. **Infinite-subtree growth conjecture.** The successive levels of $\mathcal T(S)$ have non-decreasing cardinalities. This is the second conjecture in the paper's final subsection and is presented as an open problem about growth in infinite subtrees. 

341293 Let $FM_n^{-}(x,2^y)$ denote the negative final-matrix value. **Final-matrix periodicity conjecture $(EP_n)$.** Suppose that $r$ is a natural number and $x<2^{2^r}-1$. Then

$$
FM_n^{-}(x,2^y)=FM_n^{-}(x,2^z)
$$

whenever $y=z\mod 2^r$ and $\max(y,z)<j\cdot2^r<n$ for some $j$.

The claim predicts periodicity in the exponent parameter with period $2^r$ under the stated bounds. The excerpt supplies no resolution status. 

341317 Let $P(\mathbf{m})$ be the natural unit interval order associated with a sequence $\mathbf{m}=(2,m_2,m_3,n,\ldots,n)$, and let $G$ be its incomparability graph. Let $\widetilde{X}_G(t)$ be the chromatic quasisymmetric function of $G$, and let $X_G=\widetilde{X}_G(1)$ be its chromatic symmetric function. **Three-parameter e-positivity conjecture.** The function $\widetilde{X}_G(t)$, and consequently $X_G$, is $e$-positive.

The claim concerns a specific family of natural unit interval incomparability graphs and is presented as a conjecture in the source. Its resolution status is not specified there. 

341318 Let $P(\mathbf{m})$ be the natural unit interval order associated with a sequence $\mathbf{m}=(m_1,m_2,m_3,n,\ldots,n)$, and let $G$ be its incomparability graph. Let $\widetilde{X}_G(t)$ denote the chromatic quasisymmetric function of $G$. **Generalized three-parameter e-positivity conjecture.** The function $\widetilde{X}_G(t)$ is $e$-positive.

This extends the preceding conjectural family by allowing the first three entries of $\mathbf{m}$ to vary. The source does not specify whether the conjecture has been resolved. 

341329 For finite two-dimensional sets $A,B
one$ in the plane that are not collinear, let $[A]$ and $[B]$ denote their convex hulls, and let ${\rm tr}(A)$ and ${\rm tr}(B)$ be the numbers of full-dimensional simplices in triangulations using the respective sets as vertex sets. **Planar discrete Brunn–Minkowski conjecture.**

$$
{\rm tr}(A+B)^{\frac12}\geq {\rm tr}(A)^{\frac12}+{\rm tr}(B)^{\frac12}.
$$

This replaces volume in the Brunn–Minkowski inequality by the number of triangles in a triangulation; in the plane this number is independent of the triangulation. The conjecture is presented as the basic planar case of the paper’s proposed discrete analogue. 

341402 Let $n$ and $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ be nonnegative integers, and let $q$ be an indeterminate. Consider the Laurent polynomial

$$
\prod_{1\leq i<j\leq n}(1-x_iq/x_j)(1-x_iq^2/x_j)\cdots(1-x_iq^{a_j}/x_j)(1-x_j/x_i)(1-x_jq/x_i)\cdots(1-x_jq^{a_i-1}/x_i).
$$

Define $[m]_q=1+q+\cdots+q^{m-1}$ and $[m]_q!=[m]_q[m-1]_q\cdots[1]_q$. **Andrews' q-Dyson conjecture.** The constant term of this Laurent polynomial is

$$
\frac{[a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n]_q!}{[a_1]_q![a_2]_q!\cdots[a_n]_q!}.
$$

The conjecture remained unsettled for a decade before being proved by Zeilberger and Bressoud. 

341413 Let $G$ be a graph of order $n$. A **Gallai $4$-extremal graph** is a graph maximizing the number of Gallai $4$-colorings among graphs of order $n$.

**Hoppen–Lefmann–Odermann's conjecture.** The only Gallai $4$-extremal graph of order $n$ is the complete bipartite graph

$$
K_{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor, \lceil n/2 \rceil}.
$$

This conjecture concerns the extremal structure of graphs supporting Gallai colorings. The cited context states that this bipartite graph is close to extremal and that the conjecture is believed to hold, but provides no proof of uniqueness. 

341420 Let $N$ and $N'$ be non-binary tree-child networks on the same leaf set. A **tree-child sequence** (TCS) of $N$ is a sequence of reductions associated with the tree-child network. **Universal TCS containment conjecture.** The network $N'$ is contained in $N$ if and only if every TCS of $N$ reduces $N'$; equivalently, 
$$
N'\text{ is contained in }N \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{all TCSs of }N\text{ reduce }N'.
$$
 The analogous statement with existence of a suitable TCS is known for the tree-child setting in the reconstructible classes, while the universal condition is proposed here for general non-binary tree-child networks.
