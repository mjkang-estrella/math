Let $A$ be the adjacency matrix of a graph $\Gamma$ on $n$ vertices with vertex set $V$. Let $C$ be the binary linear code generated by $[I_n|A]$, and let $d(C)$ denote its minimum distance. For a nonempty subset $S$ of $V$, let $\operatorname{von}(S)$ denote its von Neumann neighborhood. **The von Neumann neighborhood conjecture.** If

$$
d(C)=|S|+|\operatorname{von}(S)|,
$$

then either $S=\operatorname{von}(S)$ or $S\cap\operatorname{von}(S)=\varnothing$. The observation preceding this conjecture verifies the claim for complete graphs, while its validity for general graphs is left open.

343653 Chen--Lih--Wu's equitable Brooks conjecture

**Chen--Lih--Wu's conjecture.** The graph $G$ has an equitable $\Delta$-coloring unless one of the following holds: $\Delta=2$ and $G$ is an odd cycle; $G \cong K_{\Delta+1}$; or $\Delta$ is odd and $G \cong K_{\Delta,\Delta}$.

344110 Independent domination conjecture for Cartesian products
Let $G$ and $H$ be undirected graphs. Write $\gamma(G)$ for the domination number of a graph, and let $i(G)$ denote its **independent domination number**, the smallest size of an independent dominating set of $G$. The Cartesian product $G\square H$ has vertex set $V(G)\times V(H)$. The **independent domination conjecture.**

$$
i(G\square H)\geq \min\{i(G)\gamma(H),\gamma(G)i(H)\}.
$$

This is an analogue of Vizing's conjecture for independent domination and would imply Vizing's conjecture. The stronger product inequality $i(G\square H)\geq i(G)i(H)$ is known to fail for some graphs, so this formulation avoids that obstruction; its resolution is not supplied in the source.

344116 The super-approximation conjecture for finitely generated linear groups

344582 Bolza surface conjecture for extremal-length systoles in genus 2
Let $\mathcal{M}_2$ denote the moduli space of conformal classes of closed genus-$2$ surfaces. For a conformal class $X\in\mathcal{M}_2$, let $\operatorname{sys}_{\operatorname{EL}}(X)$ be the minimum extremal length of an essential simple closed curve on $X$. **Bolza surface conjecture.** The maximum of $\operatorname{sys}_{\operatorname{EL}}$ in genus $2$ is attained by the conformal class of the Bolza surface. The Bolza surface is known to realize the maximum of the hyperbolic systole in genus $2$ and also appears as a maximizer for other notions of systolic length; whether it maximizes the extremal-length systole remains open.
SOURCE Dídac Martínez-Granado and Franco Vargas Pallete, “Comparing hyperbolic and extremal lengths for shortest curves”, arXiv:1911.09078 (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09078

344612 The volume-product conjecture for finite metric spaces
Let $M$ be a metric space with $n+1$ points. Let $\mathcal F(M)$ be its Lipschitz-free space, let $B_{\mathcal F(M)}$ be the unit ball of this $n$-dimensional space, and define

$$
\mathcal P(M):=\mathcal P(B_{\mathcal F(M)}).
$$

**Volume-product conjecture for finite metric spaces.** One has

$$
\mathcal P(M)\geq \mathcal P(B_1^n).
$$

Here $B_1^n$ is the cross-polytope, whose volume product equals that of the cube and is the conjectured minimum among symmetric convex bodies. This is presented as the paper's main conjecture for the volume products of Lipschitz-free spaces; no resolution is given in the supplied text.
SOURCE Matthew Alexander, Matthieu Fradelizi, Luis C. García-Lirola and Artem Zvavitch, “Geometry and volume product of finite dimensional Lipschitz-free spaces”, arXiv:1911.10642 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10642

344659 Finiteness conjecture for eigenvalues of the Neumann–Poincaré operator on planar corners
Let $\Gamma$ be a piecewise $C^3$ Jordan curve with finitely many corners, whose angles lie strictly between $0$ and $\pi$. Let $K \colon H^{1/2}(\Gamma) \to H^{1/2}(\Gamma)$ be the Neumann–Poincaré operator. **Finiteness conjecture.** The operator $K$ has finitely many eigenvalues. This is an outstanding question for planar domains with corners; existing constructions exhibit eigenvalues embedded in the absolutely continuous essential spectrum, but do not suggest the existence of infinitely many eigenvalues.
SOURCE Karl-Mikael Perfekt, “Plasmonic eigenvalue problem for corners: limiting absorption principle and absolute continuity in the essential spectrum”, arXiv:1911.12294 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.12294

344728 Jones' Conjecture for planar graphs
Let $G$ be a planar graph. A **cycle packing** is a set of vertex-disjoint cycles in $G$; let $\operatorname{cp}(G)$ denote its maximum size. A **feedback vertex set** is a set $S$ of vertices such that $G-S$ is a forest; let $\operatorname{fvs}(G)$ denote the minimum size of a feedback vertex set.

**Jones' Conjecture.** Every planar graph $G$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{fvs}(G)\leqslant 2\cdot\operatorname{cp}(G).
$$

This conjecture concerns the relationship between cycle packing and feedback vertex sets, strengthening the general Erdős–Pósa-type bound for planar graphs. The source confirms the conjecture for subcubic planar graphs, but the general planar case remains open.
SOURCE Marthe Bonamy, François Dross, Tomáš Masařík, Wojciech Nadara, Marcin Pilipczuk and Michał Pilipczuk, “Jones' Conjecture in subcubic graphs”, arXiv:1912.01570 (2019). https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01570

344734 Gan–Loh–Sudakov triangle extremal conjecture
Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices with maximum degree $d$. Write

$$
n=q(d+1)+r,\qquad 0\le r\le d.
$$

Here $q$ and $r$ are the quotient and remainder in the division of $n$ by $d+1$. **Gan–Loh–Sudakov's conjecture.** The number of triangles in $G$ is at most

$$
q{d+1 \choose 3}+{r \choose 3}.
$$

The paper's abstract states that this conjecture is resolved by the paper itself, so the claimed bound is a theorem rather than an open conjecture.
SOURCE Zachary Chase, “The Maximum Number of Triangles in a Graph of Given Maximum Degree”, arXiv:1912.01600 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01600
