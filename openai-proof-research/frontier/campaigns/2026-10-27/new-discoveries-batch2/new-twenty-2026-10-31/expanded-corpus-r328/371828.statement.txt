Let $C_n^p$ be the $p$th power of the cycle graph on $n$ vertices, and let $\Delta_{2}^t(C_n^p)$ denote its total $2$-cut complex. Let $2p+3\leq n\leq3p$, and let $r$ be a positive integer satisfying

$$
2p+\frac{p}{r+1}<n\leq2p+\frac{p}{r}.
$$

**The intermediate-range homotopy conjecture.** If $n=2p+\frac{p}{r}$, then

$$
\Delta_{2}^t(C_n^p)\simeq\bigvee_{n-2p-1}\mathbb{S}^{n-2r-3}.
$$

If $2p+\frac{p}{r+1}<n<2p+\frac{p}{r}$, then

$$
\Delta_{2}^t(C_n^p)\simeq\mathbb{S}^{n-2r-4}.
$$

For each fixed $p$ and every $n$ in the stated range, the source notes that the positive integer $r$ is unique; the conjecture therefore covers the entire intermediate range, whose homotopy types are otherwise unknown.
