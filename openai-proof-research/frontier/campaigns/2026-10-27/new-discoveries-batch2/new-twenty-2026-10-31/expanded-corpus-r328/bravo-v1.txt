                                                                                          Abstract

                                                    We study the total cut complexes and their Alexander duals. The homotopy type of these
                                                complexes is calculated for de pth power of a cycle with at least 2rn vertices where p ď r,
                                                solving part of a conjecture of Bayer, Denker, Milutinović, Rowlands, Sundaram and Xue.
                                                The homotopy type of the 2-total cut complex for any rth power of a cycle with r ě 3 also
                                                is calculated, solving a conjecture of Chauhan, Shukla and Vinayak. We give some results
                                                about the connectivity. The homotopy type of the complexes for complete multipartite graph
                                                is determined. We also study the complexes of cartesian products of paths and of cartesian
                                                products of complete graphs for the total 2-cut complex.

                                           Keywords: graph complexes, homotopy type
                                           Mathematics Subject Classification: 05E45, 05C76, 55P10, 55P15

1     Introduction
We study the homotopy type of two filtrations of graphs complexes. These filtrations are related
through the Alexander duality. The complexes of the filtrtions are the ones define in [18]–which we
will call bounded independence complexes– and the total cut complexes defined in [2]. The first
family of complexes was studied for the problem of colorful independent transversals of graphs.
These complexes have been also called robust clique complexes [11] and in [14] were define similar
complexes called clique-free complexes –these complexes are the bounden independence complexes
for the complement graph. Inspired by a result of Fröberg [13] the total k-cut complex of a graph
was defined and lately these complexes have receive a lot of attention (see for example [6, 8, 23]) .
    Here we partially solve the following conjectures.

4     Connectivity results
In this section we give some results on the connectivity of the complexes of both filtrations. We
begin with some result on the connectivity for pairs of bounden independence complexes and a
more general result on the connectivity of the bounded independence complex. We show how the
girth and the order give us bounds for the connectivity of the total cut complex and for the 2-total
cut complex we will see that these two parameters can determine the homotopy type.

Proposition 18. For any graph G and d ě 2 the pair pBId`1 pGq, BId pGqq is pd ´ 2q-connected.

Proof. Notice that in BId`1 pGq ´ BId pGq the remaining simplices have dimension at least d ´ 1,
thus pBId`1 pGq, BId pGqq is pd ´ 2q-connected (see [16, Corollary 4.12]).

    For a graph G and r ě 1, we have that BId pGr q Ď BId pGr`1 q, so is natural to ask if we can
say something about the connectivity of the pair.
