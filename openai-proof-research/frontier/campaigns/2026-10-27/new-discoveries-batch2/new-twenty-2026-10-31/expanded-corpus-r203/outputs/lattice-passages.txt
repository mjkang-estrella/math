For any m ≥ 3 , at least one construction of maximum m -distance set can be realized by repeatedly removing points from the aforementioned hexagons. This can be confirmed with a maximum 4 -distance set construction, which can be found by removing 3 corners from an equiangular hexagon with side length alternating 1 and 2 , and also with a maximum 6 -distance set, which can be obtained by deleting 6 corners from a regular hexagon with side length 2 . In the end, we found that for m = 19 , 23 , 28 , 29 , 34 , the construction with equiangular hexagons has more points than the construction with the maximum clique method. The main reason is that some distances have low multiplicities if we use the m smallest distances in the maximum clique method. Substituting distances of small multiplicities out for a bigger integer yields a construction of larger size by the hexagon. This gives yet another conjecture about the multiplicities. Conjecture 5 .
