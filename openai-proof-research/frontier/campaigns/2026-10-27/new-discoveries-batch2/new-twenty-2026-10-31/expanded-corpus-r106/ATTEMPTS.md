## 367773: source-lemma combination gate

Read source 2504.00129 Conjecture 1.2, the cosine recurrence, Lemmas 4.1, 4.2 and 4.4, Theorem 5.4 and the diameter-three computation section. Its reported antipodal parameter sweep reaches valency 50. The source has a wording problem in the hypothesis of Theorem 5.4 (excluding antipodal graphs while its surrounding discussion uses odd-diameter antipodal graphs); the underlying Lemmas 4.1 and 4.2 themselves have the broader hypotheses. Do not silently cite that printed theorem at an excluded hypothesis.

Here is a short algebraic collapse of the source's necessary conditions, using the standard antipodal diameter-three parameters
`{k,(r-1)c,1; 1,c,k}`. Write the least eigenvalue as `-s`, with `s>0`. Then
`delta=k-1-rc`, `s²+delta*s-k=0`, and the cosines are
`(1,-s/k,s/(k(r-1)),-1/(r-1))`.
Let `alpha,beta,gamma` be the source partition counts at image diameter two. They satisfy `beta+gamma=1`, `alpha != gamma`, and
`gamma-alpha > -s+k-c-1`.
Lemma 4.1 gives
`-alpha*r*s + beta*(k-(r-1)*s) + gamma*(k+s)=0`.
Put `h=alpha+1-gamma`. This equation is `k=s*(r*h-1)`, so `h>=1`. Substitution into the eigenvalue equation gives `c=(s-1)*h`, hence
`a2=k-c-1=s*((r-1)*h-1)+h-1`.
The strict inequality becomes
`s*((r-1)*h-2)+2*h < 2`.
For `r>=3`, or for `r=2,h>=2`, its left side is at least two. Thus only `r=2,h=1` remains, which forces `alpha=gamma`, contradicting the source's necessary condition. This is a short combination of source ingredients with the classical antipodal parameterization, precisely the sort of source-only bridge excluded by the assignment. It is not nominated as a new theorem. The standard parameter/cosine bridge has not been independently formalized here.

The exact parameter diagnostic checked 3,281,346 arrays with `2<=k<=1000`, positive `c`, and nonnegative `a1,a2`. It found 35,974 arrays with integer least root and zero triples satisfying all source constraints. For a surviving triple the displayed equation would force a rational least root; the script's integer-root restriction follows from the monic integer eigenvalue polynomial. These are parameter arrays, not constructed graphs or an endomorphism enumeration.
