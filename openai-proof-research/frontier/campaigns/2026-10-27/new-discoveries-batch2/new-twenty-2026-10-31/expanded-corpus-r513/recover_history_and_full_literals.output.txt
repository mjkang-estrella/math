325478 For a graph family $\mathcal F$, let $\oplus_k\mathcal F$ denote its closure under $k$-clique sums, and let $c_1(\mathcal F)$ denote the supremum of the least distortion needed to embed members of $\mathcal F$ into $L_1$.

**$k$-sum embedding conjecture.** For any family of graphs $\mathcal F$, we have

$$
c_1(\mathcal F)=O(1) \quad\text{if and only if}\quad c_1(\oplus_k\mathcal F)=O(1) \text{ for every } k\in\mathbb N.
$$

Lee and Sidiropoulos showed that this conjecture together with the planar embedding conjecture is equivalent to the GNRS conjecture. The case $k=1$ is folklore, and the case $k=2$ has seen progress but remains open.

320901 Let $p_i$ be the $i$-th prime, and let $F_k$ be the set of integers divisible by at least one of $p_1,\ldots,p_k$. Let $k\ge3$ and let $l$ be a positive integer. For an integer $a$ with $-p_{k+1}+1\le a\le p_1p_2\cdots p_k-p_{k+1}$, let $B_{k,l}(a)$ be a subset of $[-p_{k+1}+1,a]$ such that the set

$$
\{p_1p_2\cdots p_kl+b:b\in B_{k,l}(a)\}
$$

contains no $k+1$ pairwise coprime integers.

**Shifted-block bound conjecture.** For every such $k,l,a$,

$$
|B_{k,l}(a)|\le |F_k\cap[-p_{k+1}+1,a]|.
$$

The conjecture would control all admissible shifted blocks by the natural extremal set of offsets divisible by one of the first $k$ primes; it is used in the paper as a mechanism for extending finite cases to all sufficiently large $n$.
380412 Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a hereditary property of ordered hypergraphs. For every constant $c>0$, suppose there exists an $N=N(c)\in\mathbb{N}$ such that $|\mathcal{P}_N|>c^N$. **The ordered-hypergraph speed conjecture.** Then, for every $n\in\mathbb{N}$,

$$
|\mathcal{P}_n|\geqslant\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}{n\choose 2k}k!=n^{n/2+o(n)}.

This conjecture would provide a common generalization of the stated results for ordered hypergraphs, partitions, ordered graphs, and ordered graphs excluding fixed complete and complete bipartite ordered graphs. Its status is not resolved in the source.

377639 A random K-SAT instance has $n$ Boolean variables and $m=cn$ clauses, where $c$ is the clause-to-variable ratio and $K$ is the number of literals per clause. **The linear phase transition conjecture.** For every $K\geq 2$ there exists a constant $c^*_K$ such that a random K-SAT formula is satisfiable when $c<c^*_K$ and is not satisfiable when $c>c^*_K$, with high probability as $n\to\infty$. Equivalently, satisfiability undergoes a linear sharp phase transition at $m=c^*_Kn$. The sharp phase transition is known in a more general setting, and the case $K=2$ is solved with $c^*_2=1$; the existence of the linear threshold for higher $K$ is the outstanding part of the conjecture.

368607 Let $\mathrm{AG}(n,2)=\mathbb{F}_2^n$, and let $S\subseteq\mathbb{F}_2^n$ be a point set of size $m$. A **$[k,t]$-flat** is a $k$-dimensional affine subspace containing exactly $t$ points of $S$. Write $[n,m]\to[k,t]$ when every $m$-element subset of $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ induces a $[k,t]$-flat, and define

$$
\operatorname{Sp}(n,k,t)=\{0\le m\le 2^n:[n,m]\to[k,t]\},\qquad
\rho(n;k,t)=\frac{|\operatorname{Sp}(n,k,t)|}{2^n}.
$$

**Kovács–Nagy density conjecture.** For every $k\ge 1$ and $0\le t\le 2^k$, we have

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\rho(n;k,t)=1.
$$

This conjecture asserts that, for each fixed pair $(k,t)$, almost every cardinality $m$ forces a $[k,t]$-flat in sufficiently high-dimensional binary affine space. It was posed as a binary affine-space analogue of an intersection problem and is presented in the source as an open conjecture.

where $p\in[2t:\lfloor q/2\rfloor]$. The code is required to function-correct for the Lee weight function, the Lee weight distribution function, and the modular sum function. **The optimal one-symbol redundancy conjecture.** For all such $t$ and $q$, there exists a $p\in[2t:\lfloor q/2\rfloor]$ for which this encoding yields an FCLC for each of the three functions and achieves optimal redundancy $1$. 362884 Let $H$ be a graph. Its homomorphism density in a bounded measurable symmetric kernel $W:[0,1]^2\to\mathbb{R}$ is

$$
t_H(W)=\int_{[0,1]^{V(H)}}\prod_{ij\in E(H)}W(x_i,x_j)\,d\mu^{V(H)},
$$

where $bc$ is Lebesgue measure. An automorphism $\phi$ of $H$ is a **stable involution** if it is an involution for which there is a partition $L\cup R\cup F$ of $V(H)$, with $F$ a vertex cut separating $L$ and $R$, such that $\phi$ exchanges $L$ and $R$, fixes $F$, and $F$ is an independent set. A graph is **positive** if $t_H(W)\geq 0$ for every such kernel $W$.

**The positive graph conjecture.** A graph $H$ is positive if and only if it has a stable involution.

The forward implication would characterize all graphs whose homomorphism densities are non-negative on arbitrary kernels; the reverse implication follows from the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality. The conjecture is also viewed as an analogue of Hilbert's seventeenth problem, with stable-involution graphs playing the role of squares. No resolution is supplied in the source.

**Spectral bowtie conjecture.** For $q\ge 3$, if $G$ is a graph of sufficiently large order $n$ satisfying

$$

360368 Let $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $\binom{[n]}{k}$ denote the family of $k$-subsets of $[n]$, and let $\mathcal{F}\subset\binom{[n]}{k}$ be a **$t$-intersecting family**, meaning that $|F\cap F'|\ge t$ for all $F,F'\in\mathcal{F}$. Write $\operatorname{co}_2(\mathcal{F})$ for its codegree squared sum, and let a $t$-star be the family $\{F\in\binom{[n]}{k}:[t]\subset F\}$.

**Exact codegree-squared-sum conjecture.** If $t\ge1$ and $n\ge(t+1)(k-t+1)$, then

$$
\operatorname{co}_2(\mathcal{F})\le\binom{n-t}{k-t}\bigl(1+(n-k+1)(k-t)\bigr).
$$

If $n>(t+1)(k-t+1)$, equality holds only when $\mathcal{F}$ is isomorphic to a $t$-star. This conjecture seeks the codegree-squared-sum analogue of the classical $t$-intersecting Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem; the corresponding bound is proved in the paper for sufficiently large $n$, while the stated optimal range remains open.
