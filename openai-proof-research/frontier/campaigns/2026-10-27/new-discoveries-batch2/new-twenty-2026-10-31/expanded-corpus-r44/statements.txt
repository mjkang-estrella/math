ID 351574 Conjecture that wreath products preserve intersection density

ID 351599 The Bernstein–Sato formula for the singularity level of local complete intersections
Let $Z$ be a reduced local complete intersection of codimension $r$. Let $p(Z)$ denote its singularity level, and let $\widetilde{\alpha}(Z)$ be the negative of the largest root of the reduced Bernstein–Sato polynomial $\widetilde{b}_Z(s)=b_Z(s)/(s+r)$. **Bernstein–Sato conjecture.**

$$
p(Z)=\max\{[\widetilde{\alpha}(Z)]-r,-1\}.

For hypersurfaces, the analogous formula is known in terms of the minimal exponent. The displayed formula is proposed as the corresponding description for reduced local complete intersections and remains open.

ID 351662 Thomassen's crumby coloring conjecture for 3-connected cubic graphs
A **crumby coloring** of a graph is a red-blue vertex coloring in which the blue subgraph has maximum degree at most $1$ and the red subgraph has minimum degree at least $1$ and contains no path with $3$ edges. **Thomassen's conjecture.** Every $3$-connected cubic graph has a crumby coloring. This conjecture was refuted by Bellitto, Klimošová, Merker, Witkowski and Yuditsky, who constructed an infinite family of $3$-connected cubic counterexamples; related open cases include outerplanar, $K_4$-minor-free and bipartite graph classes.
