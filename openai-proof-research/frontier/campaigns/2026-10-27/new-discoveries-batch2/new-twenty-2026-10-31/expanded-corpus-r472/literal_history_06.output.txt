317234 Let $k$ be a positive integer and color all primes with $k$ colors. For an integer $l\geq 3$, consider monochromatic primes $p_0,p_1,p_2,\ldots,p_l$.

**Schur-type conjecture.** For every $l\geq 3$, there exist monochromatic primes $p_0,p_1,p_2,\ldots,p_l$ such that $p_1,\ldots,p_l$ form an arithmetic progression with common difference $p_0-1$.

This conjecture extends the proved Schur-type theorem for primes, which gives monochromatic primes satisfying $p_1+p_2=p_3+1$, and is motivated by the Green–Tao theorem on arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions in the primes. Its resolution is not supplied here.
317296 Let $\mathbb{U}_1^{(2)}$ be the partial field

$$
\mathbb{U}_1^{(2)}:= \mathbb{P}(\operatorname{GF}(2)(\alpha),\{\alpha,1+\alpha\}).
$$

**Uniform partial-field characterization conjecture.** A matroid is representable over $\operatorname{GF}(2^k)$ for all $k>1$ if and only if it is representable over $\mathbb{U}_1^{(2)}$. The conjecture is described as lying just outside the scope of the Lift Theorem, and no resolution is given.
317338 Let $G$ be a knotted trivalent graph. Its colored Jones invariant is interpreted using the KTG version of the invariant, and let $\mathrm{Vol}(G)$ be the volume defined as the sum of the volumes of the hyperbolic pieces in the JSJ decomposition of the graph exterior.

**so(3) volume conjecture.** The so(3) volume conjecture holds for all knotted trivalent graphs.

The paper proposes this as an extension of the knot-and-link conjecture after defining the corresponding volume and colored Jones invariant for KTGs; the supplied source gives no evidence that the general statement is resolved.
317366 Let ${\rm Sch}_\infty$ be the infinite Schaeffer tree, and let $\Delta h$ be the antisymmetric function on its directed edges defined by the increments of the Busemann function. Consider the pair $({\rm Sch}_\infty,\Delta h)$.

**Distribution conjecture.** The pair $({\rm Sch}_\infty,\Delta h)$ is distributed as a Galton–Watson tree with geometric-$1/2$ offspring distribution, conditioned to be infinite, with increments distributed independently and uniformly among $\{-1,0,1\}$.

The conjecture seeks an explicit probabilistic description of the infinite Schaeffer tree and its edge increments. The supplied text gives no resolution, so it remains open.
317545 Let $X_\Gamma=\{\Psi_\Gamma=0\}$ be the graph hypersurface associated with a graph $\Gamma$, embedded in its ambient projective space, and let $H$ denote the hyperplane class. Write its Chern–Schwartz–MacPherson class as a polynomial in powers of $H$. **Positivity conjecture.** The coefficients of all powers $H^k$ in the Chern–Schwartz–MacPherson class of an arbitrary graph hypersurface $X_\Gamma$ are non-negative. The conjecture proposes a general positivity phenomenon for Chern–Schwartz–MacPherson classes of graph hypersurfaces, motivated by computations for banana graphs and other sample Feynman graphs. At the time of the source, no conceptual explanation or general proof was known.
317593 Let $r\geqslant 1$ be an integer, and let $X$ be a positive parameter. **Spacing of unit fractions.** The number of $x_1,\dots,x_{2r}\in[X,2X)$ satisfying

$$
\left|\frac{1}{x_1}+\dots+\frac{1}{x_r}-\frac{1}{x_{r+1}}-\dots-\frac{1}{x_{2r}}\right|\leqslant\frac{1}{X^{r+1}}
$$

is $\ll_{r,\varepsilon}X^{r+\varepsilon}$ for all $\varepsilon>0$. This is a further conjecture concerning the spacing and additive relations among reciprocals of integers; the source provides no resolution, so it remains open.
317608 Let $I$ be an interval and let $\varphi:I\to\mathbb{R}^2$ be a piecewise linear path. Two maps are **approximable by link maps** when they can be approximated by maps with disjoint images.

**Disjoint-arc criterion.** The path $\varphi$ is approximable by embeddings if and only if, for every pair of disjoint arcs $I_1,I_2\subset I$, the restrictions $\varphi:I_1\to\mathbb{R}^2$ and $\varphi:I_2\to\mathbb{R}^2$ are approximable by link maps.

This criterion relates approximability by embeddings to the corresponding disjoint-image problem for every pair of disjoint subarcs. The supplied text gives no resolution status.
317817 Let $G$ be the disjoint union of two connected graphs $H$ and $K$, and let $\Sigma$ be a surface. An **optimal drawing** of $G$ on $\Sigma$ is a drawing whose crossing number equals the crossing number of $G$ on $\Sigma$.

**DeVos–Mohar–Samal conjecture.** For every optimal drawing of $G$ on $\Sigma$, the restrictions to $H$ and $K$ do not intersect.

This conjecture concerns whether the connected components of a disconnected graph can always be drawn separately in an optimal drawing on any surface. Its status is not resolved by the supplied source context.
317923 Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite graph and let $\tilde G=G\times K_2$ be its bunkbed graph. Let $T\subseteq V(G)$ be the set of vertices whose vertical edges are present. Let $\boldsymbol p=(p_e)_{e\in E(G)}$ be a probability vector with $0\le p_e\le 1$. In the model $E_2^{\boldsymbol p,T}(\tilde G)$, each pair of horizontal edges corresponding to $e\in E(G)$ is independently present with probability $p_e$.

**Generalized Bunkbed conjecture.** For every $u,v\in V(G)$,

$$
P(u_0\longleftrightarrow v_0)\ge P(u_0\longleftrightarrow v_1).
$$

This extends the original conjecture by conditioning on an arbitrary set of present vertical edges and allowing edge-dependent horizontal probabilities. The paper proves the assertion when $|T|\leq 1$, while the general statement is left open.
318342 Let $G \simeq C_{n_1} \oplus \dots \oplus C_{n_r}$, with $1<n_1\mid\dots\mid n_r\in\mathbb{N}$, be a finite Abelian group, and let $S$ be a sequence over $G$ with $|S|=\mathsf{D}(G)+i-1$, where $i\in\llbracket 1,n_1\rrbracket$. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be any $(i-1)$-subset of $\llbracket 1,n_1-1\rrbracket$. **Girard's conjecture.** The sequence $S$ contains a non-empty zero-sum subsequence $S'$ such that 
$$
|S'|\not\equiv b\pmod {n_1}
$$
 for every $b\in\mathcal{A}$. This conjecture would yield consequences for the invariant $\mathsf{s}_{m\mathbb{N}}(G)$ and related inverse problems; its general status remains open.
318495 Let $P$ be a parallelogram-free pattern. For every real $\varepsilon>0$, there is $N(\varepsilon)>0$ such that for all $n\geq N(\varepsilon)$,

$$
S_{P}^{\nparallel}(n)\leq n^{1+\varepsilon}.
$$

**Near-linear upper-bound conjecture.** The function $S_{P}^{\nparallel}(n)$ should satisfy this bound for every parallelogram-free pattern $P$. The paper suggests that the construction giving the existing lower-bound behavior is close to optimal, but that a stronger upper-bound argument is needed; the conjecture remains open.
318535 Let $G$ and $H$ be graphs, let $s,t$ be positive integers, and let $x\in V(G)$ and $y\in V(H)$. Write $\pi_r(G,x)$ for the $r$-pebbling number targeting $x$, and similarly for $H$ and $G\times H$. **Parameterized vertex-target pebbling conjecture.** For all such graphs, integers, and vertices,

$$
\pi_{st}(G\times H,(x,y))\leq\pi_s(G,x)\pi_t(H,y).
$$

The conjecture is obtained by taking point-mass distributions $s\delta_x$ and $t\delta_y$ in the single-distribution conjecture; the source gives no resolution.
318536 Let $G$ and $H$ be graphs, let $s$ and $t$ be positive odd integers, and let $x\in V(G)$ and $y\in V(H)$. **Odd-parameter vertex-target pebbling conjecture.** For all such graphs, integers, and vertices,

$$
\pi_{st}(G\times H,(x,y))\leq\pi_s(G,x)\pi_t(H,y).
$$

This is an additional specialization of the parameterized vertex-target conjecture, motivated by the doubling theorem in the source; its status is not resolved there.
318635 Let $n,r\bin\mathbb N$ with $r\le n$. A **bit string with $r$ runs** has the form $1^{n_1}0^{m_1}\cdots 1^{n_r}0^{m_r}$ with all $n_j,m_j>0$ and total length $2n$. Write

$$
n=\ell r+a,\qquad 0\le a<r.
$$

**Balanced-run maximization conjecture.** For every such bit string $\mathbf{S}$,

$$
\varphi(\mathbf{S})\leq \varphi\bigl((1^{\ell+1}0^{\ell+1})^a(1^\ell0^\ell)^{r-a}\bigr).
$$

The paper explains that the corresponding assertion is proved when $r$ divides $n$, while this stronger formulation remains conjectural when $r$ does not divide $n$.
318862 Let $\mathbf{F}$ be a finite field, and let $\mathbf{F}[t]$ denote its polynomial ring. An affine space in $\mathbf{F}[t]$ is a set of the form

$$
\{f+Pg: \deg(P)<k\},
$$

where $f,g\in\mathbf{F}[t]$ and $g\ne 0$.

**Green–Tao conjecture for function fields.** For every finite field $\mathbf{F}$, the monic irreducible polynomials in $\mathbf{F}[t]$ contain affine spaces of arbitrarily high dimension.

This is the function-field analogue of the Green–Tao theorem that the primes contain arithmetic progressions of arbitrarily large length. In the formulation given here, the claim concerns configurations of monic irreducible polynomials parametrized by polynomials of increasing degree.
319110 A **$p$-uniform hypertree** is a uniform hypergraph in which every hyperedge has $p$ vertices and whose underlying incidence structure is a hypertree. A $k$-cordial labeling assigns labels from $\mathbb{Z}_k$ to the vertices, with the vertex and induced hyperedge labels distributed as evenly as possible.

**Generalized Hovey conjecture.** All $p$-uniform hypertrees are $k$-cordial for all $k$.

This is proposed as the main open problem generalizing Hovey's conjecture from trees to uniform hypertrees. The paper establishes the ordinary cordial case, but leaves the assertion for every $k$ open.
319246 Let $H$ be an $n$-balanced $r$-partite $r$-graph, and let $I$ be a subset of $[r]:=\{1,2,\ldots,r\}$. An **$I$-tuple** is an element of $\times_{i\in I}V_i$, and an $I^c$-tuple is defined using $I^c:=[r]\setminus I$. For tuples, let $d(\cdot)$ denote degree in $H$. **Equal complementary-tuple degrees conjecture.** If

$$
d(e)=d(f)
$$

for every two $I$-tuples $e,f$, and

$$
d(g)=d(z)
$$

for every two $I^c$-tuples $g,z$, then $H$ has a perfect matching unless $r$ is odd and $n$ is even. The conjecture proposes that uniform degrees on both complementary tuple classes force a perfect matching, apart from the stated parity exception. The source describes this as a bold conjectural weakening of the Brualdi-Ryser condition; no resolution is supplied.
320203 Let $G$ be a simple, finite, undirected graph with $N$ vertices that is $d$-regular, and let $i(G)$ denote its number of independent sets. The graph $\frac{N}{2d}K_{d,d}$ is the disjoint union of $\frac{N}{2d}$ copies of the complete bipartite graph $K_{d,d}$. **Alon–Kahn conjecture.** For every such graph $G$, 

$$
i(G) \leq \left(2^{d+1}-1\right)^\frac{N}{2d}.
$$

Kahn proved this bound for regular bipartite graphs, while the conjecture asserts it for all regular graphs; the general case remains open in the supplied text.
338770 Let a tessellation be a graph embedded in the plane so that every face is a polygon and the embedding satisfies the usual tessellation conditions. At each vertex $v$, let its combinatorial curvature be

$$
\kappa(v)=1-\frac{d_v}{2}+\sum_{f\sim v}\frac{1}{d_f},
$$

where $d_v$ is the degree of $v$ and $d_f$ is the degree of the face $f$. **Higuchi's conjecture.** If $\kappa(v)>0$ for every vertex $v$, then the tessellation is a finite graph. This is proposed as a discrete analogue of a weak form of the Bonnet–Myers theorem; the source gives no resolution of the conjecture.
368797 Let $n_{g,r}$ denote the number of numerical semigroups of genus $g$ with ordinarization number $r$, where the ordinarization number is the length of the path from the semigroup to the ordinary semigroup in the ordinarization tree. **Bras-Amorós's ordinarization-number conjecture.** For all $r,g \ge 1$ we have

$$
n_{g,r} \le n_{g+1,r}.
$$

This conjecture concerns the distribution of numerical semigroups by ordinarization number and is presented as a partial motivation for the paper. The source provides no evidence of a resolution.
