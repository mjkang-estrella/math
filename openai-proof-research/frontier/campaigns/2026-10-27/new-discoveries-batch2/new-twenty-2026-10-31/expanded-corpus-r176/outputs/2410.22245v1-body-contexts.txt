It is named after the Norwegian mathematician Thoralf Skolem [ 38 ] . A Skolem sequence of order n is a sequence of 2n elements that consists of the numbers 1,1,2,2,\ldots,n,n arranged in such a way that for each k (where 1\leq k\leq n), the two occurrences of k are exactly k positions apart. For example, the sequence 42324311 is a Skolem sequence.
