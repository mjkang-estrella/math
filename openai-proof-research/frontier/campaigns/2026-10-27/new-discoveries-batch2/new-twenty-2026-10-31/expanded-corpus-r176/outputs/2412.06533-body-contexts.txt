We present numerical minimizers for the first seven eigenvalues of the planar magnetic Dirichlet Laplacian with constant magnetic field in a wide range of field strengths. Adapting an approach by Antunes and Freitas, we use gradient descent for the minimization procedure together with the Method of Fundamental solutions for eigenvalue computation. Remarkably, we observe that when the magnetic flux exceeds the index of the target eigenvalue, the minimizer is always a disk. 1. Introduction Let \Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{2} be a bounded, open set. The last decades have seen a rise of interest in problem ( 1.7 ) and similar spectral shape optimization problems and there is now a wealth of literature on the topic. A surprisingly difficult task is already that of establishing the existence of minimizers. For problem ( 1.7 ), this has been achieved through a series of papers in the 1990’s-2010’s [ 43 , 27 , 26 , 56 , 44 ] by relaxing the class of open sets to so-called quasi-open sets. Parallel to these developments, several works have adressed questions of regularity, connectedness and convexity of minimizers, see e.g. [ 71 , 58 , 57 , 44 ] . Numerical investigations of problem ( 1.7 ) and its Neumann boundary condition counterpart have been carried out by Oudet [ 63 ] and Antunes and Freitas [ 8 ] , presenting numerical optimizers up to n=15. Analogous numerical studies for three-dimensional and four-dimensional geometries have been conducted in the following [ 13 , 12 ] . Robin instead of Dirichlet boundary conditions have been considered in [ 9 , 10 ] and diameter instead of measure constraints were discussed in [ 24 ] .
