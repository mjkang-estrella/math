Hu and Ye conjectured that for a k-th order and n-dimensional tensor \mathcal{A} with an eigenvalue \lambda and the corresponding eigenvariety \mathcal{V}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{am}(\lambda)\geq\sum_{i=1}^{\kappa}\mathrm{dim}(V_{i})(k-1)^{\mathrm{dim}(V_{i})-1}, where \mathrm{am}(\lambda) is the algebraic multiplicity of \lambda, and V_{1},\ldots,V_{\kappa} are all irreducible components of \mathcal{V}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{A}). In this paper, we prove that if \mathcal{A} is a nonnegative weakly irreducible tensor with spectral radius \rho, then \mathrm{am}(\lambda)\geq|\mathbb{V}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{A})| for all eigenvalues \lambda of \mathcal{A} with modulus \rho, where \mathbb{V}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{A}) is the projective eigenvariety of \mathcal{A} associated with \lambda. Consequently we confirm Hu-Ye’s conjecture for the above eigenvalues \lambda of \mathcal{A} and the least H-eigenvalue of a weakly irreducible Z-tensor. We prove the equality case in Hu-Ye’s conjecture for some eigenvalues of the adjacency tensor or Laplacian tensor of several classes of hypergraphs.
