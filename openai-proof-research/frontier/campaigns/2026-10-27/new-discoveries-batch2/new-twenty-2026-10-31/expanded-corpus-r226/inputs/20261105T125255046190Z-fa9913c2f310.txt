     Title: The spiders $S(4m+2,\,2m,\,1)$ are $e$-positive 
     Authors: Davion Q.B. Tang ,  David G.L. Wang ,  Monica M.Y. Wang              View a PDF of the paper titled The spiders $S(4m+2,\,2m,\,1)$ are $e$-positive, by Davion Q.B. Tang and 1 other authors 
     View PDF 
     HTML (experimental) 



     
             Abstract: Using the composition method, we establish the $e$-positivity of spiders of the form $S(4m+2,\, 2m,\, 1)$, as conjectured by Aliniaeifard, van Willigenburg and Wang. Following a divide-and-conquer strategy, we group one or two positive-coefficient $e_J$-terms with each negative-coefficient $e_I$-term, where each $J$ is a composition obtained by rearranging the parts of the composition $I$, and prove that the resulting total coefficient is nonnegative. Our main contribution is the explicit construction of the required injections.
