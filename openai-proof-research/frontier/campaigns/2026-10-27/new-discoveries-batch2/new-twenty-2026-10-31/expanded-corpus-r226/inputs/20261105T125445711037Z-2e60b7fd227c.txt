 Locally analytic vectors and decompletion in mixed characteristic 
 
 
 Gal Porat
 
 Email:  galporat1@gmail.com 
 

 Abstract 
    
 In  p p -adic Hodge theory and the  p p -adic Langlands program, Banach spaces with  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} -coefficients and  p p -adic Lie group actions are central. Studying the subrepresentation of  Γ \Gamma -locally analytic vectors,  W la W^{\mathrm{la}} , is useful because  W la W^{\mathrm{la}}  can be analyzed via the Lie algebra  Lie ⁡ ( Γ ) \mathrm{Lie}(\Gamma) , which simplifies the action of  Γ \Gamma . Additionally,  W la W^{\mathrm{la}}  often behaves as a decompletion of  W W , making it closer to an algebraic or geometric object. 
    
 This article introduces a notion of locally analytic vectors for  W W  in a mixed characteristic setting, specifically for  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p} -Tate algebras. This generalization encompasses the classical definition and also specializes to super-Hölder vectors in characteristic  p p . Using binomial expansions instead of Taylor series, this new definition bridges locally analytic vectors in characteristic  0 0  and  p p . 
    
 Our main theorem shows that under certain conditions, the map  W ↦ W la W\mapsto W^{\mathrm{la}}  acts as a descent, and the derived locally analytic vectors  R la i ​ ( W ) \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{la}}^{i}(W)  vanish for  i ≥ 1 i\geq 1 . This result extends Theorem C of  [ 21 ] , providing new tools for propagating information about locally analytic vectors from characteristic  0 0  to characteristic  p p . 
    
 We provide three applications: a new proof of Berger-Rozensztajn’s main result using characteristic  0 0  methods, the introduction of an integral multivariable ring  𝐀 ~ LT † , la \widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\mathrm{LT}}^{\dagger,\mathrm{la}}  in the Lubin-Tate setting, and a novel interpretation of the classical Cohen ring  𝐀 𝐐 p {\bf{A}}_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}  from the theory of  ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules in terms of locally analytic vectors.

 1  Introduction 

 
 In  p p -adic Hodge theory and in the  p p -adic Langlands program, one often encounters a Banach space  W W  with  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} -coefficients endowed with an action of a  p p -adic Lie group  Γ \Gamma . It has proven useful to study the subrepresentation of  Γ \Gamma -locally analytic vectors  W la ⊂ W . W^{\mathrm{la}}\subset W.  One reason for this is that  W la W^{\mathrm{la}}  has an action of the Lie algebra  Lie ⁡ ( Γ ) , \mathrm{Lie}(\Gamma),  which is often simpler to analyze than the action of  Γ \Gamma . Another reason for this usefulness is that  W la W^{\mathrm{la}}  sometimes behaves as a decompletion of  W W  which is then closer to being of an algebraic or geometric nature. 
 
 
 As a classical example we consider Sen theory. In this setting, we let  𝐐 p cyc \mathbf{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{cyc}}  be the cyclotomic extension obtained by adding all  p p -power roots of unity to  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} ,  K K  be a finite extension of  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} ,  K cyc = K ​ 𝐐 p cyc K^{\mathrm{cyc}}=K\mathbf{Q}_{p}^{\mathrm{cyc}} ,  H K = Gal ⁡ ( K ¯ / K cyc ) H_{K}=\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{K}/K^{\mathrm{cyc}})  and  Γ = Gal ⁡ ( K cyc / K ) \Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{cyc}}/K)  which is isomorphic to an open subgroup of  𝐙 p × \mathbf{Z}_{p}^{\times} . We also let  𝐂 p \mathbf{C}_{p}  be the completion of  𝐐 ¯ p \overline{\mathbf{Q}}_{p} . If  X X  is a smooth proper variety over  K K  let  V = H ét n ​ ( X K ¯ , 𝐐 p ) V=\mathrm{H}^{n}_{\text{\'{e}t}}(X_{\overline{K}},\mathbf{Q}_{p}) . Then  V V  is a  Gal ⁡ ( K ¯ / K ) \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{K}/K) -representation, and we set 
 

 
 
 W = ( 𝐂 p ⊗ 𝐐 p V ) H K . W=(\mathbf{C}_{p}\otimes_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}V)^{H_{K}}. 
 
 
 It can then be shown (see for example Théorème 3.4 of  [ 3 ] ) that  W la W^{\mathrm{la}}  is a vector space over  K cyc K^{\mathrm{cyc}}  to which  W W  descends. Furthermore, the action of  Lie ⁡ ( Γ ) ≅ 𝐙 p \mathrm{Lie}(\Gamma)\cong\mathbf{Z}_{p}  gives a linear operator, called the Sen operator, which acts on  W la W^{\mathrm{la}} . Its eigenvalues belong to  𝐙 \mathbf{Z}  and as a set are equal to the negatives of the Hodge numbers  { h i , j } i + j = n \{h^{i,j}\}_{i+j=n}  of  X X . 
 
 
 One issue with the classical definition of  Γ \Gamma -locally analytic vectors is that it is available only in the setting of  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} -coefficients, while one would want to consider spaces  W W  with  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p}  and  𝐅 p \mathbf{F}_{p} -coefficients as well. Nevertheless, it is clear that some phenomenon of this kind exists. For example, a Sen operator in a mixed characteristic was recently introduced in work of Bhatt-Lurie ( [ 6 ] ). It is unclear how to interpret it in terms of locally analytic vectors.
In another direction, Berger and Rozensztajn introduce in
( [ 8 ] , [ 9 ] ) the notion of super-Hölder vectors, which serve as an analogue of locally analytic vectors in characteristic  p p . 
 
 
 In this article, we introduce a notion of locally analytic vectors for  W W  with coefficients in a mixed characteristic setting, namely for  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p} -Tate algebras. This notion generalizes the classical notion and specializes to the super-Hölder vectors notion of Berger and Rozensztajn in the characteristic  p p  setting. The basic idea is to use binomial expansions rather than taylor series expansions, as already observed in  [ 15 ] ,  [ 16 ]  and  [ 8 ] . 
 
 
 One nice feature of this new definition is that it allows to link locally analytic vectors in characteristic  0 0  and characteristic  p p . A template for this situation is as follows: we are given a module  A A  with coefficients in a  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p} -Tate algebra so that  A ¯ = A / p \overline{A}=A/p  is also a  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p} -Tate algebra, together with an injection of  A ⁡ [ 1 / p ] A[1/p]  into a  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} -algebra  B B . If  A A  and  B B  are endowed with  Γ \Gamma -actions, we have maps  A la → B la A^{\mathrm{la}}\rightarrow B^{\mathrm{la}}  and  A la → A ¯ la A^{\mathrm{la}}\rightarrow\overline{A}^{\mathrm{la}} . Now  B la B^{\mathrm{la}}  is a  𝐐 p \mathbf{Q}_{p} -vector space, so can be studied via  p p -adic analysis and the action of  Lie ⁡ ( Γ ) . \mathrm{Lie}(\Gamma).  If the map  A la → A ¯ la A^{\mathrm{la}}\rightarrow\overline{A}^{\mathrm{la}}  is sufficiently well behaved, one can propagate information from  B la B^{\mathrm{la}}  to  A ¯ la \overline{A}^{\mathrm{la}}  and thus study the analytic vectors in a characteristic  p p  object using  p p -adic analysis in characteristic  0 0 ! 
 
 
 We then go further and analyze the extent to which  W ↦ W la W\mapsto W^{\mathrm{la}}  acts as a decompletion. We suppose  W W  a finite free module over a  𝐙 p \mathbf{Z}_{p} -Tate algebra  R R  endowed with a semilinear action of a  p p -adic Lie group  Γ \Gamma . The main result of this article is the following. 
 
 
 Theorem 1.1 . 
 
 Suppose that: 
 
 
 i. The ring  R R  satisfies the Tate-Sen axioms (TS1)-(TS4) (see  § ​ 3.2 \mathsection{3.2} ). 
 
 
 ii. The Lie algebra  Lie ⁡ ( Γ ) \mathrm{Lie}(\Gamma)  is abelian. 
 
 
 Then 
 
 
 1. The natural map 
 

 
 
 R ⊗ R la W la → W R\otimes_{R^{\mathrm{la}}}W^{\mathrm{la}}\rightarrow W 
 
 
 is an isomorphism. 
 
 
 2. The derived locally analytic vectors  R la i ​ ( W ) \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{la}}^{i}(W)  of  W W  are  0 0  for  i ≥ 1 i\geq 1 . 
 
 
 
 Note that the vanishing of  R la 1 ​ ( W ) \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{la}}^{1}(W)  is precisely the type of behaviour that will allow us the propagate information regarding locally analytic vectors along reductions. This theorem is a generalization of Theorem C of  [ 21 ] . The basic idea of the proof is in essence to take a ”fiber product” of the original method of  [ 21 ]  and the mixed characteristic results of  [ 20 ] . As Theorem C of  [ 21 ]  has been applied by several subsequent articles, we hope the current result will be useful in a similar way. 
 
 
 Finally, we give three applications of our results. Our first result is a new proof of the main result of  [ 8 ] . There, Berger and Rozensztajn show that for the action of  Γ = 𝐙 p × \Gamma=\mathbf{Z}_{p}^{\times}  on the  X X -adic completion of  ∪ n 𝐅 p ( ( X 1 / p n ) ) \cup_{n}\mathbf{F}_{p}((X^{1/p^{n}})) , taking  Γ \Gamma -locally analytic vectors undoes the  X X -adic completion. We show how to deduce this using characteristic 0 methods, as sketched above. Our second application is the introduction of an integral multivariable ring  𝐀 ~ LT † , la \widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\mathrm{LT}}^{\dagger,\mathrm{la}}  in the Lubin-Tate setting, together with an appropriate overconvergence result for  ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules. Previously, such a ring was only available rationally with pro-analytic vectors. Our third and final result is a surprising description of the classical Cohen ring  𝐀 𝐐 p {\bf{A}}_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}  appearing in the theory of  ( φ , Γ ) (\varphi,\Gamma) -modules in terms of locally analytic vectors. We show how to endow the ring  𝐀 ~ 𝐐 p \widetilde{\bf{A}}_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}  of Witt vectors of  𝐐 ^ p cyc \widehat{{\bf{\mathbf{Q}}}}_{p}^{\mathrm{cyc}}  with the structure of a mixed-characteristic Fréchet space, and its ring of locally analytic vectors is  φ − ∞ ​ ( 𝐀 𝐐 p ) . \varphi^{-\infty}({\bf{A}}_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}}).  This reveals an analogy between  φ − ∞ ​ ( 𝐀 𝐐 p ) \varphi^{-\infty}({\bf{A}}_{\mathbf{Q}_{p}})  and the ring  ∪ n 𝐐 p ( ζ p n ) [ [ t ] ] \cup_{n}\mathbf{Q}_{p}(\zeta_{p^{n}})[[t]]  of locally analytic elements in  𝐁 dR + \bf{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+} , and suggests a way to give a lift of the field of norms beyond the Lubin-Tate setting, a question which was studied by several authors and which has motivation in Iwasawa theory (see for instance  [ 4 ] ,  [ 22 ] ).
