359283 The two-dimensional integer covering asymptotic conjecture 
Let $f(n,2,k)$ be the minimum number of lines, counted with multiplicity, that cover every point of the triangular grid $T_2(n)$ at least $k$ times, and let $f^*(k,2)$ be the fractional covering density for multiplicity $k$ on $T_2(k)$. The notation $O_k(1)$ denotes a quantity bounded independently of $n$ for fixed $k$.

**Two-dimensional asymptotic conjecture.** For all positive integers $n$ and $k$,

$$
f(n,2,k)=f^*(k,2)n+O_k(1).
$$

Computational evidence for $k\leq 7$ motivates the conjecture, which would identify the fractional covering density as the asymptotic slope of the integer problem in dimension two.

359284 The three-dimensional covering conjecture 
Let $f(n,3,k)$ be the minimum number of hyperplanes, counted with multiplicity, needed to cover every point of the three-dimensional triangular grid $T_3(n)$ at least $k$ times. The notation $O_k(1)$ denotes a quantity bounded independently of $n$ for fixed $k$.

**Three-dimensional covering conjecture.** For all $n,k\geq 1$,

$$
f(n,3,k)=
\begin{cases}
\left(\frac{k+1}{2}\right)n+O_k(1)&\text{if }k\text{ is odd},\\
\left(\frac{k(k+2)}{2(k+1)}\right)n+O_k(1)&\text{if }k\text{ is even.}
\end{cases}
$$

The formula is supported by the proved cases $k\leq 5$, the additional asymptotic result for $k=7$, and computational evidence. It predicts linear growth in $n$ with the displayed parity-dependent slope.

351267 Alon–Krivelevich–Sudakov conjecture on coloring graphs with forbidden subgraphs 
Let $F$ be a graph. A graph $G$ is **$F$-free** if it contains no copy of $F$; write $chi(G)$ for its chromatic number and let $Delta$ be its maximum degree.

**Alon–Krivelevich–Sudakov conjecture.** For every graph $F$, there is a constant $c_F>0$ such that, whenever $G$ is an $F$-free graph of maximum degree $Delta\geqslant 2$, one has

$$
\chi(G)\leqslant c_FDelta/\log Delta.
$$

Alon, Krivelevich, and Sudakov verified the conjecture for a class of graphs including all bipartite graphs, and subsequent work gives sharper bounds for some complete bipartite forbidden graphs. The conjecture is therefore resolved for some $F$, but the general assertion remains open.

346730 The diameter-two conjecture for extremal connected induced subgraph counts 
Let $n\geq 5$ be an integer, and let $G$ be an $n$-vertex graph. Write $\overline{G}$ for its complement and let $\eta(G)$ denote the number of connected induced subgraphs of $G$. **Diameter-two conjecture.** If

$$
\eta(G)+\eta(\overline{G})\geq \eta(H)+\eta(\overline{H})
$$

for every graph $H$ of order $n$, then $G$ has diameter $2$. The conjecture concerns the maximisation counterpart to the paper's results on the minimum of this Nordhaus–Gaddum-type sum. Exhaustive searches for $n=5,6,7$ support it, but a proof appears difficult because adding an edge between vertices at distance $3$ does not always increase the sum.

345015 Asymptotic sharpness conjecture for broadcast domination in the infinite grid 
Let $t$ and $r$ be positive integers, and let $\delta_{t,r}(\mathbb{Z}^2)$ denote the minimum density of a $(t,r)$-broadcasting set in the infinite square grid. **Asymptotic sharpness conjecture.** For every $r\geq 2$, there exists an integer $t_0$ such that for every $t\geq t_0$,

$$
\delta_{t+1,r+2}(\mathbb{Z}^2)=\delta_{t,r}(\mathbb{Z}^2).
$$

This is proposed as a weakening of the earlier sharpness conjecture after computational evidence showed that the unrestricted assertion fails for some small parameters. The asymptotic range of the equality remains open in the supplied text.

358046 Closed-form conjecture for the near-extremal asymptotic coefficients 
Let $k\geqslant4$, and let $a_{k,j}$ be the coefficients in the asymptotic expansion, with parameters $\alpha$, $\varkappa$, $\varepsilon$, $b$, and $a_{1,\pm1}$ as defined in the paper. **Near-extremal coefficient conjecture.** For $k\geqslant4$,

$$
a_{k,\pm(k-2)}=\pm\frac{\mathrm{i}\sqrt{3}\,a_{1,\pm1}^{k-2}}{2^k3^k(\varepsilon b)^{1/3}}\left(3(k-2)^2(\alpha^2+8\varkappa\alpha+10\varkappa^2)-5(k-2)^2+24(k-2)-24\mp12(k-2)^2\alpha\mp6\big(5(k-2)^2+8(k-2)\big)\varkappa\right).
$$

The formula is extrapolated from explicit computations at levels through $k=10$; the supplied text does not establish it for all $k\geqslant4$.

343978 Gibbs's concentration conjecture for the Ulam sequence U(1,2) 
Let $U(1,2)$ be the Ulam sequence, and for a positive real number $\lambda$ interpret reduction modulo $\lambda$ by taking representatives in an interval of length $\lambda$. **Gibbs's concentration conjecture.** There exists a real number $\lambda_2\approx2.443442967784743433$ such that for all $\epsilon>0$, for $K$ sufficiently large,

$$
U(1,2)\cap[K,\infty)\mod\lambda_2\subset\left(\frac{\lambda_2}{3}-\epsilon,\frac{2\lambda_2}{3}+\epsilon\right).
$$

The conjecture concerns the apparent concentration of the residues of $U(1,2)$ in the middle third modulo a distinguished real period. It was confirmed computationally for the first trillion terms, but the asymptotic assertion remains open.

343980 The asymptotic period conjecture for coefficients of U(1,X) 
Let $U(1,X)$ denote the polynomial-ring analogue of the Ulam sequence, and let $a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i$ be its coefficients. For real $X$, reduction modulo $3X+\lambda'$ is taken using representatives in an interval of length $3X+\lambda'$. **Asymptotic period conjecture.** There exist real numbers $\lambda'\approx0.417031$, $\sigma_1\approx1.86$, and $\sigma_2\approx-1.3$ such that, for every $\epsilon>0$, if $i$ is sufficiently large, then

$$
a_iX+b_i,\ c_iX+d_i\mod(3X+\lambda')\in\left(X+\frac{\lambda'}{3}-\epsilon,X+\sigma_1+\epsilon\right)\cup\left(2X+\sigma_2-\epsilon,2X+\frac{2\lambda'}{3}+\epsilon\right).
$$

This conjecture gives a more precise form of the observed period phenomenon for the block endpoints of $U(1,n)$; the source reports numerical evidence but no proof.

338530 Periodicity conjecture for star-Kayles games 
Let $STARKAYLES_{k,n}$ be the graph game in which the underlying graph is obtained by starting with a star graph on $k$ vertices and extending one branch to a path of edge-length $n$. A move selects a vertex and removes a positive number of edges incident to that vertex; terminal positions are edgeless graphs. **Star-Kayles periodicity conjecture.** For every fixed $k$, the sequence $STARKAYLES_{k,n}$ is periodic in $n$, with period a multiple of $12$. The authors report computational verification for small values of $k$ and motivate the conjecture by the expectation that a fixed star does not affect the asymptotic behaviour.

376143 The exact-value conjecture for the two-color Hales–Jewett number 
Let $HJ(k,2)$ denote the least dimension such that every coloring of $[k]^{HJ(k,2)}$ with two colors contains a monochromatic combinatorial line. **Exact-value conjecture.** For all $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, we have

$$
HJ(k,2)=k.
$$

The paper notes that computations for $k \in \\{2,3,4,5,6,7\\}$ support the conjecture, while the values for $k \geq 8$ were not determined there. The asymptotic result $HJ(k,2)=k(1-o(1))$ gives a close lower bound, but the exact equality remains open, particularly for composite $k$.

367510 The SPR-neighbor OLA distance bound for phylogenetic trees 
Let $T\in\widehat{\mathcal{RBT}}_n$ be a fixed phylogenetic tree, and let $T'$ be a random tree differing from $T$ by a single subtree-prune-and-regraft (SPR) move, chosen uniformly. Write $\operatorname{ht}(T)$ for the number of edges from the root to the farthest leaf, and let $d_{\operatorname{OLA}}$ denote the ordered leaf attachment (OLA) distance. **SPR-neighbor OLA distance conjecture.** The expectation satisfies the asymptotic upper bound

$$
\mathbb E\bigl(d_{\operatorname{OLA}}(T,T')\bigr)=O\bigl(\operatorname{ht}(T)\bigr).
$$

The conjecture is supported by a computational experiment sampling SPR neighbors; the paper also describes a heuristic suggesting a bound by $8$ times the tree height. The source does not state that the conjecture has been resolved.

368883 Faudree–Lehel's irregularity-strength conjecture 
Let $G$ be a $d$-regular graph on $n$ vertices with $d>1$. An **irregular edge weighting** assigns real weights to the edges so that all vertices have distinct weighted degrees, and $s(G)$ is the least $k$ for which an irregular weighting using weights from $\{1,2,\ldots,k\}$ exists. **Faudree–Lehel's irregularity-strength conjecture.** There is a constant $C>0$ such that, for every such graph,

$$
s(G)\leq\frac{n}{d}+C.
$$

The source says that this conjecture remains open, although its asymptotic validity has been confirmed. It is a central conjecture concerning irregularity strength.

364104 Elphick–Linz–Wocjan square-sum generalization of the Bollobás–Nikiforov conjecture 
Let $G$ be a simple graph on $n$ vertices with adjacency matrix $A(G)$, eigenvalues $\lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_n$, clique number $\omega(G)$, and $n^+$ positive eigenvalues. Define

$$
s_k(G)=\sum_{i=1}^k\lambda_i^2,\qquad \Lambda_k(G)=\frac{s_k(G)}{m},
$$

where $m$ is the number of edges. Set

$$
\ell=\min\{n^+,\omega(G)\}.
$$

**Elphick–Linz–Wocjan conjecture.** For every graph $G$,

$$
\Lambda_\ell(G)\leq 2\left(1-\frac{1}{\omega(G)}\right).
$$

This generalizes the Bollobás–Nikiforov conjecture and was proposed after computational investigation. The paper verifies it for graphs with at most $O(m^{1.5-\varepsilon})$ triangles for some $\varepsilon>0$, including planar, book-free, and cycle-free graphs; the general statement remains open.

354645 Asymptotic NL-hardness conjecture for core trees 
For each positive integer $n$, consider the finite set of **core trees with $n$ vertices**, meaning oriented trees whose only endomorphisms are automorphisms. Let the fraction of these trees that are NL-hard be measured uniformly. **Asymptotic NL-hardness conjecture.**

$$
\text{The fraction of core trees with }n\text{ vertices that are NL-hard tends to }1\text{ as }n\to\infty.
$$

The conjecture is suggested by the computational data in the paper. No proof or disproof is supplied, so its asymptotic status remains open.

354927 Exponential decay conjecture for the Hamming-cube theta number 
Let $H(n,s)$ denote the Hamming hypergraph on $\mathbb{H}^n$ whose edges are $s$-triangles, and for fixed $c>1$ let $s(n,c)$ be the even integer closest to $n/c$. The quantity $mathvartheta(H(n,s(n,c))) / 2^n$ is the normalized theta number for this hypergraph.

**Hamming-cube decay conjecture.** For every fixed $c>2$, the quantity

$$
\vartheta(H(n,s(n,c))) / 2^n
$$

decays exponentially fast with $n$, whereas

$$
\vartheta(H(n,s(n,2))) / 2^n
$$

decays linearly fast with $n$.

This conjecture concerns the asymptotic density bound produced by the theta number for subsets of the Hamming cube that avoid $s$-triangles. The exponential-versus-linear transition is supported by numerical evidence, but no proof or resolution is supplied here.

346797 Eventual Turán-goodness conjecture for every graph 
Let $H$ be any graph. For a positive integer $k$, say that $H$ is **$k$-Turán-good** if the relevant Turán graph $T_{k-1}(n)$ is extremal for copies of $H$ in every sufficiently large graph subject to the corresponding $k$-chromatic forbidden-subgraph condition. **Eventual Turán-goodness conjecture.** For every graph $H$, there is an integer $k_0$ such that, whenever $k\geq k_0$, the graph $H$ is $k$-Turán-good. The conjecture is known for complete multipartite graphs, while the general case remains open; the paper also notes that the analogous assertion need not hold for small $k$.

347718 Spectral condition conjecture for attractive delta NLS solitary waves 
Fix $q<0$ and $p>4$. Let $\sigma=-1$ denote the focusing case and $\sigma=1$ the defocusing case, and let $\Omega=\Omega(q,p)$ be the minimal unstable frequency. The **spectral condition** is the condition given in Definition~. **Spectral condition conjecture.** In the focusing case, there exists $\omega_1=\omega_1(q,p)\in(\tfrac12q^2,\Omega)$ such that the spectral condition holds for $\omega\in(\tfrac12q^2,\omega_1)$. In the defocusing case, the spectral condition holds for all $\omega\in(0,\tfrac12q^2)$. This conjecture describes the frequency ranges in which the spectral hypothesis needed for the asymptotic stability theorem is expected to hold; the paper combines analytic and numerical evidence but does not establish the full claim.

344301 The asymptotic equidistribution conjecture for singleton double descent sets 
Let $dd(I;n)$ denote the number of permutations in the symmetric group on $n$ letters whose double descent set is $I$. For fixed $0<\alpha<\beta<1$, **the asymptotic equidistribution conjecture** asserts

$$
\sum_{\alpha n<i<\beta n}dd(\{i\};n)\sim(\beta-\alpha)\sum_{i=2}^{n-1}dd(\{i\};n).
$$

This predicts that singleton double descents become uniformly distributed across the interior positions as $n$ grows, based on computer-generated data; its asymptotic validity remains open.

344598 The power of one conjecture for majority dynamics on random graphs 
Let $\rho(k,n)$ be the probability that Red wins when Red's camp initially has size $n/2+k$, with edge probability $p=.5$. **The power of one conjecture.** There is a constant $b4>0$ such that

$$
\rho(1,n)\geq 1/2+b4
$$

for all sufficiently large $n$. This conjecture asserts that a single initial-vote advantage gives Red a nontrivial asymptotic advantage; the paper reports numerical evidence but does not establish the claim.

340510 Strichartz's almost-periodic spectral asymptotics conjecture for nonpositive curvature 
Let $S$ be a surface of constant zero or negative curvature, with finite area and piecewise smooth boundary, and let $A(t)$ denote the ordinary average error in the refined spectral counting asymptotic. A function is **uniformly almost periodic** if it is almost periodic uniformly on the real line.

**Strichartz's conjecture.** There exists a uniformly almost periodic function $g$ such that

$$
A(t)=g(t^{1/2})t^{-1/4}+O(t^{-1/2})
$$

as $t\to\infty$. If the curvature is zero, $g$ has mean value zero. The conjecture refines the expected bounded decay of the averaged error and is supported here by numerical evidence; its extension to positive curvature is stated separately, and the source notes that the proposed expansion could not be tested in this paper.
