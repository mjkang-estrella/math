## Theorem D (the complete reduction)

Fix m>=1. All but O_m(n^(2m-1)) interval-closed sets of [m]x[n] lie in rowmotion orbits of size exactly N=m+n+1.

Proof.
1. Let E be the nonfull ICS, |E|<=m(1+binom(n+1,2))^(m-1)=O(n^(2m-2)). Row is a bijection of IC(P), so A:={I: Row^t(I) full for 0<=t<=N+1} misses at most (N+2)|E|=O(n^(2m-1)) sets.
2. For I in A put I_t=Row^t I. Every I_t with t<=N lies in F:={full with full image}, so Theorem C gives psi(I_t)=Row_J^t psi(I_0).
3. J([m]x[n]x[2]) is J([2]x[m]x[n]) by permuting factors, and Row commutes with poset isomorphism. By E2, Row_J^N=id, so psi(I_N)=psi(I_0). psi is injective on F, so Row^N I=I.
4. Let the orbit size be d. Then d|N. Suppose d<N. As I_d=I, step 2 gives Row_J^d x=x for x=psi(I). By E3 with (a,b,c)=(2,m,n), q=N, the set S:=Con(Psi_3(d(x))) is a subset of Z_N with |S|<=2m, S nonempty, invariant under a nontrivial rotation. Such S number at most sum over e>=2, e|N, and s<=2m with e|s of binom(N/e,s/e)=O_m(N^m), and each S carries at most (2m)^(2m) tableaux in Inc^N(2xm). Since Psi_3 o d and the factor permutation are bijections and psi is injective on F, at most O_m(n^m)=O_m(n^(2m-1)) sets I in A have d<N.
With PROOF.md Section 2, r(n)=1-O_m(1/n). QED.
