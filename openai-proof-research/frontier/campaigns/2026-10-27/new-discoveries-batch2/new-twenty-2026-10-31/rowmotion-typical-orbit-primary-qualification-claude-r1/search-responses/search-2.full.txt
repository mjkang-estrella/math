**What the sources cover**

- The paper introduces resonance for discrete dynamical systems, formalizing that in various combinatorially-natural cyclic group actions, orbit cardinalities are all multiples of divisors of a fundamental frequency.
- Its main result is an equivariant bijection between plane partitions in a box (equivalently, order ideals in a product of three chains) under rowmotion and increasing tableaux under K-promotion, as stated in the abstract.
- A worked example: the K-promotion orbit of the depicted increasing tableaux in Inc^12(4 × 4) has cardinality 36. The binary content is, however, of order 12 under cyclically shifting.
- The paper also gives corollaries on the order of K-promotion, including new proofs of plane partition results of Brouwer–Schrijver '74 and Cameron–Fon-der-Flaass '95.
- A follow-up generalization by Dilks, Striker and Vorland extends the resonance result from increasing tableaux to increasing labelings of arbitrary finite posets, as Theorem 3.12 extends the resonance result on increasing tableaux to increasing labelings.
- Patrias and Pechenik later proved the Cameron–Fon-der-Flaass periodicity conjecture for rowmotion on the poset B_{a,b,c}, which concerns orbit sizes being multiples of p = a + b + c − 1 when p is prime, per the conjecture statement in their paper.
