
353638 L1 convergence for greedy lattice paths
Let $\{X_{v}:v\in\mathbb{Z}^d\}$ be i.i.d. random variables, and let $M_n$ denote the maximum weight of a lattice path of length $n$ from the origin. Assume that there exists $\alpha>0$ such that

$$
E(X_0^{+})^d(\log^{+}X_0^{+})^{d+\alpha})<+\infty
$$

and that

$$
\int_{0}^{+\infty}P(X_0< -t)^{2d}\,\mathrm{d}t<+\infty.
$$

**$L^1$ convergence conjecture.** There exists a constant $M\in(-\infty,+\infty)$ such that

$$
E|M_n/n-M|\overset{n\to\infty}{\to}0.
$$

The stated negative-tail integrability condition ensures that $EM_n>-infty$; the conjectured $L^1$ convergence for greedy lattice paths is presented as an open problem requiring new ideas beyond the lattice-animal argument.

353706 Cranston's maximum-average-degree conjecture for odd colorings
Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, let $\operatorname{mad}(G)$ be the maximum of $\frac{2|E(H)|}{|V(H)|}$ over all non-empty subgraphs $H$ of $G$, and let $\chi_o(G)$ be the least positive integer $c$ for which $G$ has a proper $c$-coloring in which every vertex of positive degree has some color appearing an odd number of times among its neighbors. **Cranston's conjecture.** For $c\geq 4$, if

$$
\operatorname{mad}(G)\leq\frac{4c-4}{c+1},
$$

then $\chi_o(G)\leq c$. Cranston proved this conjecture for $c\in\{5,6\}$, while the cases $c\geq 4$ in general remain open. It gives a sharp-looking sparsity threshold guaranteeing an odd coloring.

353749 Tensor-product Fourier dual-certificate conjecture for Hadamard matrices
Let $U(d)$ be the unitary group, let $\nu$ be normalized Haar measure on $U(d)$, let $e$ be its identity, and let $\mu_{[\bm H]}$ be the smoothed measure associated with the equivalence class of a Hadamard matrix $\bm H\in U(d)$. **Dual-certificate conjecture.** For every $d$, there exists a Hadamard matrix $\bm H\in U(d)$ such that

$$
\delta_e+d\mu_{[\bm H]}\succeq(d+1)\nu,
$$

or equivalently, $\widehat{\mu_{[\bm H]}}(\pi)\succeq-\frac1d\bm I$ for every irreducible representation $\pi$ of $U(d)$. If $d=p_1^{k_1}\cdots p_m^{k_m}$ is the prime factorization of $d$, one can take

$$
\bm H=\bigotimes_{k=1}^m\bm F_{p_k}^{\otimes k_k}.
$$

Such a certificate would imply the conjectured dimension-independent lower bound $B(d)=d+1$. The construction is motivated by observed tensor-product patterns in known mutually unbiased bases, but its validity remains to be verified.

354288 Merino–Welsh conjectures for matroids
Let $M$ be a matroid without loops and coloops, and let $T_M(x,y)$ denote its Tutte polynomial.

**Merino–Welsh conjectures.** The following three inequalities should hold:

$$
\max\left(T_M(2,0),T_M(0,2)\right)\geq T_M(1,1),
$$

$$
T_M(2,0)+T_M(0,2)\geq 2T_M(1,1),
$$

$$
T_M(2,0)T_M(0,2)\geq T_M(1,1)^2.
$$

These are respectively the maximum, additive, and multiplicative versions of the graph conjecture. The multiplicative inequality implies the additive one, which implies the maximum inequality.
