Let $\Phi(x,y)$ be a standard polynomial of partial degree $d$. Let $G(\Phi)$ be its associated polynomial digraph, and let $G(\Phi)^*$ denote the nonsingular part of this digraph. Suppose that $H$ is a $d$-regular digraph isomorphic to a component of $G(\Phi)^*$. **Component-isomorphism conjecture.** All components of $G(\Phi)^*$ are isomorphic to $H$. This conjecture is motivated by cases in which $\Phi$ is homogeneous, has partial degree one, or is symmetric of partial and total degree two, where the components have been shown to be mutually isomorphic. The general assertion for standard polynomials of partial degree $d$ is left open in the source.
