Let $G$ be a connected finite simple graph. A **finite planar cover** of $G$ is a finite graph that covers $G$ in the graph-theoretic sense, with the covering graph planar. **Negami's Planar Cover Conjecture.** The graph $G$ has a finite planar cover if and only if it can be embedded on the projective plane. The sufficiency is clear, while the necessity remains open; the rotation-compatible version has already been proved.
