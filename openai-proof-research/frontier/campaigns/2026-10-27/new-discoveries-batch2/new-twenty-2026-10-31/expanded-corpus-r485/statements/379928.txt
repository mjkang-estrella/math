For a finite set $E$ of squares in the short interval

$$
E\subset\{k^2:N\le k\le N+N/(\log N)^\delta\},
$$

let $r_{E+E}(m)$ count representations of $m$ as a sum of two elements of $E$. **The local energy conjecture.** There exists $\delta>0$ such that

$$
\sum_m r_{E+E}^2(m)\ll |E|^2.
$$

The source introduces this as the special square-polynomial case of the short interval conjectures and states that it remains open.
