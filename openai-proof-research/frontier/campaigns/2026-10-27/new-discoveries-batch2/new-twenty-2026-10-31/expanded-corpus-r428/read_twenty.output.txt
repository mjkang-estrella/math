324807 Let $P$ be a finite poset, let $Q_P$ be its order polytope, and let $L_P$ be the $q$-Ehrhart polynomial of $Q_P$. Write the Newton polytope of the numerator of $L_P$ for the convex hull of the exponent vectors of the monomials having nonzero coefficients in that numerator.

**Newton-polytope shape conjecture.** The Newton polytope of the numerator of the $q$-Ehrhart polynomial $L_P$ has an horizontal top edge, an horizontal bottom edge and a diagonal right edge. Every element $x$ of the poset $P$ gives rise to a segment on the left border with inverse slope given by the length of the maximal chain of elements larger than $x$.

This conjecture is based on experimental observations and is illustrated by the paper's figure; no resolution is supplied in the given text.
324820 Let $H$ be a fixed graph, and let an **$H$-free graph** be a graph with no induced subgraph isomorphic to $H$. A CS-separator is a family of cuts separating every disjoint clique from every stable set. **Clique–Stable Set separation conjecture for $H$-free graphs.** The Clique–Stable Set separation conjecture is true on $H$-free graphs.

The source presents this as a proposed restriction of the general separation conjecture, motivated by evidence for perfect graphs and other graph classes. No resolution is given in the supplied text.
324908 Let $G$ be a permutation group on a countable set. A graph is **$G$-invariant** if its edge relation is preserved by every element of $G$, and let a random $G$-invariant graph be chosen according to the random model under consideration. **The invariant-graph characterization.** The following are equivalent:

- Some $G$-invariant graph is isomorphic to the random graph $R$.
- A random $G$-invariant graph is isomorphic to $R$ with positive probability.

This gives an unknown general criterion for when a permutation group admits an invariant copy of the random graph and when such a copy occurs with positive probability.
324916 Let $\alpha$ be a weak composition, let $J_{\alpha}^{(\beta)}$ be the generating function for diagrams produced from $\operatorname{Skyline}(\alpha)$ by the $K$-Kohnert rule, with each diagram weighted by $\beta^{g(D)}x^D$, where $g(D)$ is the number of $g$'s in $D$ and $x^D$ is its column weight. Let $\Omega_{\alpha}$ denote the dual stable Grothendieck polynomial associated with $\alpha$. **The $K$-Kohnert conjecture.**

$$
J_{\alpha}^{(-1)}=\Omega_{\alpha}.
$$

This conjecture extends Kohnert's rule from key polynomials to the dual stable Grothendieck basis by incorporating marked, unmovable $g$'s. The source provides no resolution or evidence of a proof, so its status remains open.
324943 Let $G=A_n$ with $n$ odd, let $C$ be a conjugacy class of $n$-cycles, and let $H$ be the centralizer in $G$ of an element of $C$. Let $N_G(H)$ denote the normalizer of $H$ in $G$, and let $\phi$ be Euler's totient function. **Alternating-group intersection conjecture.** Then

$$
\max\{|gH\cap C|\mid g\in G\}=|H\cap C|=|N_G(H):H|\in\{\phi(n),\phi(n)/2\}.
$$

The claim would give a sharp bound for the quantity governing intersections of conjugacy classes with cosets of centralizers, in the alternating-group case. The source presents it as a conjecture and gives no resolution status.
324986 Let $X^n$ be i.i.d. Bernoulli$(1/2)$, let $Z^n$ be i.i.d. Bernoulli$(\alpha)$ independently of $X^n$, where $0\leq\alpha\leq 1/2$, and let $Y^n=X^n\oplus Z^n$. Write $\Omega=\{0,1\}$ and $\Omega_n=\{0,1\}^n$. A Boolean function $b:\Omega_n\to\Omega$ is **lex** when $b^{-1}(0)$ is the initial segment of the lexicographic ordering having cardinality $|b^{-1}(0)|$. For fixed $n$ and fixed $|b^{-1}(0)|$, **the lexicographic minimization conjecture.** The conditional entropy

$$
H(b(X^n)\mid Y^n)
$$

is minimized when $b$ is lex. This is proposed as a refinement of the mutual-information conjecture and concerns the extremal behavior of Boolean functions under binary symmetric noise; it remains open in the supplied text.
325064 Let $M$ be a matroid of rank $r(M)$ whose ground set is the union of two disjoint bases.

**Cyclic ordering conjecture.** It is possible to place the elements of the ground set of $M$ on a circle so that every $r(M)$ consecutive elements form a basis of $M$.

This conjecture connects matroid basis exchange properties with cyclic orderings. The source attributes its formulation to Kajitani, Ueno, and Miyano, following an earlier suspicion of Gabow; its general status is not resolved in the provided text.
325098 A **topological graph** is a graph drawn in the plane with vertices represented by points and edges by curves connecting their endpoints; two edges cross when their curves intersect away from common endpoints. **The crossing-pairs conjecture.** In every topological graph with $n$ vertices and $m\geq 4n$ edges, there are two disjoint sets of edges, each of cardinality

$$
\Omega\left(\frac{m^2}{n^2\log\frac{m}{n}}\right),
$$

such that every edge in one set crosses every edge in the other. The claimed bound would be tight and is presented as a consequence of the sharper extremal conjecture.
325118 Let $X$ be a normal surface singularity. It is **taut** if every other singularity with the same resolution graph is isomorphic to $X$, and **quasi-homogeneous** if it admits a compatible weighted grading. It is **simple** if only finitely many isomorphism classes occur in its versal deformation.

**Stevens's conjecture.** A normal surface singularity is simple if and only if it is taut and quasi-homogeneous.

This formulation is presented as equivalent to the graph-theoretic characterization in the paper, using Laufer's classification of taut and pseudotaut singularities. The source does not provide a resolution status for this formulation.
325145 Let $\mathbb P$ be a piece with set of basic moves $\mathbf M$, and consider nonattacking configurations of three pieces. **Configuration-type conjecture.** The number of nonattacking configuration types of $3$ pieces depends only on $|\mathbf M|$. This would provide a consistency check for formulas for $u_{\mathbb P}(q;n)$, extending the known facts that the numbers for one and two pieces are $1$ and $|\mathbf M|$, respectively; the source gives no resolution of the conjecture.
329414 Let $L$ be a code loop, and let $V_{1}$ and $V_{2}$ be representations of $L$. The **degree and type** of a representation are the corresponding representation invariants.

**Degree-and-type conjecture.** If $V_{1}$ and $V_{2}$ have the same degree and type, then they are isomorphic.

The claim is motivated by the fact that, for code loops of ranks $3$ and $4$, the type determines the code loop up to isomorphism. The supplied text gives no resolution beyond suggesting that the rank-$3$ and rank-$4$ observation may hold in general.
329427 Let $G$ be a graph of order $n$ with no isolated vertices. A graph is **twin-free** if no two distinct vertices have the same open or closed neighborhood, and let $\gamma_L(G)$ be its location-domination number. **Foucaud–Henning conjecture.** Every twin-free graph $G$ of order $n$ without isolated vertices satisfies

$$
\gamma_L(G)\le\frac{n}{2}.
$$

This is proposed as a strengthening of the Garijo–González–Márquez conjecture and as an analogue of Ore’s one-half bound for the domination number; its status is open.
329472 Let $g\ge 0$ and let $\mu$ be a partition with $|\mu|\ge 3$. Write $h_{g,\mu}$ for the corresponding simple Hurwitz number, $r(g,\mu)$ for its associated ramification number, and $\ell(\mu)$ for the length of $\mu$. **Parity condition.** If $h_{g,\mu}$ is odd, then $r(g,\mu)$ is even, every part of $\mu$ is odd, and

$$
\ell(\mu)\le 2.
$$

This condition records a parity restriction found in the study of simple Hurwitz numbers. The supplied text does not indicate whether the condition has been proved or remains open.
329523 Let $L$ be a finite, contractible 2-complex. A **3-deformation** is the operation, described in the source, of successively gluing 3-balls along open 2-cells and performing inverse operations. **Andrews–Curtis conjecture.** $L$ 3-deforms to a single vertex. The conjecture is a presentation-theoretic and topological strengthening relevant to finite contractible 2-complexes; the source states it as the assertion that every such complex can be reduced to a point by 3-deformations, without providing a resolution.
329538 Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the set of symmetric binary matrices with no row sum greater than one. For $s\in\mathcal{S}$, let $A(s)$ be the set of upper-triangular positions containing a one, define $F(s)$ as in the source, call $s$ admissible when no two links satisfy $i<i'<j<j'$, and let $\operatorname{Adm}(b)$ be the admissible matrices mapped to the binary string $b$. For a Type A diagram $D(b)$, **Type A component formula.** 
$$
\Psi_{D(b)}=\sum_{s\in\operatorname{Adm}(b)}F(s).
$$
 The formula is presented inside a conjecture environment and gives an explicit combinatorial expression for selected ground-state components; no proof or resolution is supplied.
332356 Let an $(s,t)$-core partition be a partition that is simultaneously $s$-core and $t$-core, and let $F_n$ denote the $n$th Fibonacci number. **Amdeberhan's conjecture.** The number of $(s,s+1)$-core partitions into distinct parts equals

$$
F_{s+1}.
$$

This refines Anderson's Catalan enumeration of all $(s,s+1)$-core partitions; the conjecture concerns the restricted class of partitions into distinct parts and motivates the results proved in the paper.
332842 Let $r$ be a positive odd integer, let $t$ satisfy $0\leq t\leq r$, and let an **$\{r-t,t\}$-factor** be a spanning subgraph in which every vertex has degree either $r-t$ or $t$.

**Akbari–Kano conjecture.** Every $r$-regular graph has an $\{r-t,t\}$-factor.

The conjecture was disproved by Axenovich and Rollin. The paper records partial positive and negative results identifying ranges of $r$ and $t$ for which such factors must exist or can fail to exist.
356257 Let $T\subseteq \mathbb{Z}_v\setminus\{0\}$ satisfy

$$
\sum_{t\in T}t\neq0.
$$
 An ordering of the elements of $T$ is **simple** when its partial sums are all distinct and non-zero. **Alspach's conjecture.** Every such $T$ admits a simple ordering. The conjecture would shorten some proofs concerning the existence of cycle decompositions. It is known when $|T|=v-1$, when $|T|=v-2$, and for $v\leq16$; the general case remains open.
366254 Let $\Sigma(r,n)$ be the hypersimplex, and consider a subdivision of $\Sigma(r,n)$ into matroid base polytopes. An interior face is a face of the subdivision not contained in the boundary of $\Sigma(r,n)$. **Speyer's tropical $f$-vector conjecture.** The number of $(n-i)$-dimensional interior faces in such a subdivision is at most

$$
\frac{(n-i-1)!}{(r-i)!\,(n-r-i)!\,(i-1)!}.
$$

This conjecture concerns the face numbers of subdivisions of hypersimplices into matroid base polytopes, which arise in the study of tropical linear spaces. It is proved in the paper via the Cohen–Macaulayness of the external activity complex and the resulting formulas for its $K$-polynomial and related matroid invariants.
373806 Let $G$ and $H$ be graphs. Suppose every precoloring of at most $k < \chi'(G)$ edges of $G$ extends to a proper edge-coloring with \chi'(G) colors, and every precoloring of at most $l < \chi'(H)$ edges of $H$ extends to a proper edge-coloring with \chi'(H) colors. Let $G \square H$ denote their Cartesian product.

**General Cartesian-product conjecture.** Every precoloring of at most $k+l+1$ edges of $G \square H$ is extendable to a proper $(\chi'(G)+\chi'(H))$-edge-coloring of $G \square H$.

This is proposed as a far-reaching generalization of the preceding conjecture and would resolve numerous open problems about edge-precoloring extension in Cartesian products. The paper establishes initial cases, while the general statement remains open.
