These are computational diagnostics, not kernel-certified universal theorems, and may be subsumed by existing source nonexistence criteria.

For the flow route, DFS used exact XOR conservation in Z_2^3, fixed an initial vertex's values to 1,2,3, propagated forced edges and checked distinct nonzero color pairs. A separate validator checks every saved flow, matching condition, prohibited adjacency and 3-edge connectivity by deleting all sets of at most two edges. This does not classify cubic graphs or all flower snarks.

For the multipartition route, exact F2 series arithmetic and Gaussian elimination tested membership in the prescribed eta-factor span with epsilon_(a,1,0)=1 forced.
