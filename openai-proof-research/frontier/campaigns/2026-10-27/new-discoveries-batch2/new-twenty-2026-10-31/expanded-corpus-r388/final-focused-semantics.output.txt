Sharing Nim is a game played with the following rules. The
game starts with 3 piles of objects that each contain 1 or more objects. 2 players
take turns either removing a certain number of objects from 1 pile or transferring
a certain number of objects from 1 pile to another. The only restriction is that
players may not transfer objects from a pile of greater size to a pile of smaller size.
The game ends when no more objects remain and the last player to remove an
object wins.
