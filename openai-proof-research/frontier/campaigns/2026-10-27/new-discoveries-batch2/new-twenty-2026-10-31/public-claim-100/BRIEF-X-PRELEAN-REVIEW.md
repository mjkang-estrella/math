## Why
The user wants 100 results of the first tier: a conjecture listed as open on MathDB, proved or refuted in a way a specialist would not call routine, nothing public, with a kernel-checked Lean proof whose statement is the conjecture as printed. GPT-6.1 Sol proof lanes claim complete proofs. So every claimed proof now comes to you first: you are both the adversarial reviewer and the cross-model check, and Lean (which is expensive) is paid for only if you clear a result. A wrong CONFIRMED costs far more than a wrong rejection. Of the last twelve claims reviewed this way, five were cleared.

## Per assigned id (about 40 minutes each), in this order, stopping at the first failure
Record: `python3 -I C/root-intake-r60/corpus_row.py <id>`, run from your own folder. The proof file and its scripts are named in your task.
1. **Public.** All arXiv versions and the journal version of the source; papers citing it; the obvious later literature; OEIS where relevant; `C/public-claim-100/principia-ids.txt` and the Principia clone (also sibling ids of the same source); the omega registry: `gh search issues --repo the-omega-institute/trureturing "<arXiv id>" --limit 10 --json number,title,createdAt`, the same with `gh search prs`, and both again with two or three distinctive words (three seconds between calls); a web search. This registry and Principia have resolved hundreds of MathDB problems; six results of an earlier campaign died on omega issues nobody had searched.
2. **Statement.** Read the source. Is what is proved the whole literal conjecture, in its full stated range, with the source's definitions? Not a special case, not a different reading.
3. **Routine.** Write down the shortest proof you can find from published theorems and from the source's own lemmas, formulas and tables. If it is a few lines, the verdict is NOT_COUNTED as routine: give the lines. Say what the source's authors proved themselves, what they tried, and why they left this as a conjecture. Give your probability that a specialist referee would call the result not routine.
4. **Correctness.** Check every step of the written proof; test each lemma by your own exact computation where cheap; check the small and boundary cases of the stated range.
5. **If it survives: the Lean endpoint.** The exact literal statement you require, every definition included, as Lean-like statements; the cheapest route (what can be a certified finite computation with generated data, which Mathlib theory exists in the pinned version); an honest estimate of hand-written lines; the riskiest step. Classify FORMALIZABLE (about 3000 hand-written lines or fewer) or NOT_FORMALIZABLE_CHEAPLY (say what blocks it, and whether an honest cheaper statement exists in which the only non-Lean link is a published theorem of the source).

Verdict per result: CONFIRMED_TIER1 (provisional, pending the Lean statement), NOT_COUNTED (public, routine, trivial or not the stated problem: give the citation or the short route), FAIL (first gap in the proof; say whether it looks repairable and how) or BLOCKED (name the paper and the exact question).
