# Addendum to the tier-1 proof pass (root, 2026-11-20, after the first 35 pools)

Read `BRIEF-T1-PROOF-PASS.md` first; this file adds what the first reviews taught. Of about 45 claims so far, about half survived the cross-model review.

1. **Depth.** Choose few targets and go deep. The brief asks for about three hours; a pass that stops after one hour with attempts still open has stopped too early.
2. **Routine.** A proof obtained by replaying the source's own proof of its main theorem with a small change is judged routine. So is a proof that follows in about ten lines once two or three published papers are put side by side: search the literature for the key identification before calling a step new. So is a method already published for a sibling problem, applied to the next family. Before investing, ask whether the source's authors could have written your proof in an afternoon with the tools in their own paper.
3. **Refutations.** A refutation whose counterexample a plain search over small instances finds in a minute is second tier however elegant your construction of it: run that plain search first. Check whether the source's own printed examples already sit in an infinite family (stretch a gap, increase a parameter): if so the refutation is a one-step change of a source example.
4. **Duplicates.** Several conjectures appear in MathDB twice (a thesis and a paper, or two papers). Before investing in a target, search its authors and key words in `C/public-claim-100/LANES.json` ("candidates") and `C/public-claim-100/claim-evidence.json`.
5. **Prior art.** Besides the checks of the brief run `gh search prs --repo the-omega-institute/trureturing "<arXiv id>" --limit 10 --json number,title,createdAt` (that registry also resolves problems in pull requests), three seconds apart from other searches.
6. **Lean cost.** Results whose honest Lean proof needs more than about 3,000 hand-written lines are being held unformalized: prefer targets below that. Statements in finite combinatorics, graph theory, elementary number theory and polynomial identities have gone through; q-supercongruences modulo cyclotomic squares and statements needing stochastic or representation-theoretic infrastructure have not.
7. **If you write Lean yourself.** State the source's quantities without caps or default values that could make part of the statement true by definition (a maximum defined by a bounded search, x/0 = 0), and prove existence or non-emptiness where a predicate could be unsatisfiable (a function defined by a functional equation, a strategy). Two lanes' own Lean endpoints were partly vacuous for these reasons.
8. **Interruptions.** The provider has dropped connections today. Write REPORT.md and result.json early and update them as you go, so that an interruption loses little. Set `sys.dont_write_bytecode = True` (or `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`) before importing libraries that live in another lane's folder, so that nothing is written outside your own folder.
