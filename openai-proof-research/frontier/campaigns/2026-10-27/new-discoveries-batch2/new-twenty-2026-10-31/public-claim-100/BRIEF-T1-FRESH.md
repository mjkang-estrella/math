# Fresh pools for the tier-1 proof pass (root Claude Fable 5.2, 2026-11-20)

Read `BRIEF-T1-PROOF-PASS.md` and `BRIEF-T1-ADDENDUM.md` first. Everything there applies, with these differences.

1. **Your pool is fresh.** Pools 049 onward hold 30 MathDB records that no lane has looked at. There are no earlier lane notes and nothing to reread: the `earlier` list of every entry is empty. Record text: `python3 -I C/root-intake-r60/corpus_row.py <id>`, run from your own folder. The corpus is a snapshot of August to October 2026, so a record marked open may have been settled since.
2. **Triage (about thirty minutes for the 30 records).** For each, one line in REPORT.md: probably true or false, a visible proof mechanism or none, the Lean weight, and whether the statement is elementary. Drop at once what needs heavy theory (see the list in the brief) and what is a famous or long-open problem.
3. **Before investing in a target, three quick checks (ten minutes each at most).** (a) Is it already settled: the later arXiv versions and the journal version of the source, the papers citing it, `C/public-claim-100/principia-ids.txt`, and the two omega registry searches (issues and pull requests). (b) Is it the same conjecture as one already handled here: authors and key words in `C/public-claim-100/LANES.json` ("candidates") and `C/public-claim-100/claim-evidence.json`. (c) Does a plain search over small cases refute it within a few minutes: if so it is second tier; record it in one line under `tier2_side_finds` with the witness and move on.
4. **Then go deep on the three or four best targets,** as the brief describes: at least forty minutes each, compute to find structure, and finish what opens up, with the full stated range and the small cases covered.
5. **What has gone through so far** (68 results): exact characterizations and identities in finite combinatorics and graph theory, congruences and divisibility for explicit sequences, unimodality and positivity of explicit polynomial families, statements about words and morphisms, small extremal problems with a rigid extremal structure, and refutations by a designed infinite family or a witness no enumeration reaches. Proofs that were one published theorem plus a few lines did not go through.

Deliverables, rules and the final message are those of the brief.
