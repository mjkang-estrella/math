FacePermutationCorrect uses an explicit inverse. The edge flip theta is a bounded involution. The vertex rotation rho is bounded and its cube is the identity. Therefore rho after theta has inverse theta after rho squared. The proofs include n = 0 by the empty Fin domain and require no alteration of raw endpoint arithmetic.

ChordModel2Subdiagram transports a pairing to the increasing enumeration of any closed finite position set. Given a proper inclusion A in D, it chooses an endpoint outside A; its partner is also outside A. Removing those two endpoints constructs B with exactly one fewer chord and factors A -> D through A -> B -> D. A side assignment on A extends through every inclusion by injectivity.

ChordModel2Heredity proves EulerRealization from Deletion by induction starting from the empty annulus. The two signs in the deletion law give genus g or g + 1 on insertion. No truncation or division is used. It also proves the genus under deletion is g or g - 1, then proves MaxTwo heredity by induction along the constructed deletion chain. Every small assignment is extended, its ambient genus exists by the preceding induction, and the deletion equation gives the required bound.
