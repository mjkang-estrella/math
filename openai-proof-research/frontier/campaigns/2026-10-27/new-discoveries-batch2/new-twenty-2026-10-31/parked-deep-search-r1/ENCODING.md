# Encoding and certificate scope

`encode.py` and `encode_complement.py` are new lane-local programs. They do not import or execute any inherited program. Every Boolean gate is encoded by its complete truth table. Full adders form exact binary population counts; comparison with a constant forbids the first differing bit from exceeding that constant. Source membership variables use their literal numbered indices.

For 345754, the tail length is k-1. With a positive root, its number of pluses is at most floor((k-1)/2). With a negative root, it is at least ceil((k-1)/2). All d,k with kd<=N are included. A global sign flip justifies x1=true. No multiplicativity hypothesis is imposed. The checker independently scans all cascades of the emitted finite sign string and checks all CNF clauses against the complete SAT model. An initial prototype omitted the first term of each binary prefix counter. Its outputs are retained under `.invalid` and are not evidence; the corrected code explicitly initializes the counter to x_(2d).

For 340618, the finite group is CYCLIC of order 32. For all 1024 ordered pairs (x,y), the support {x,y,x+y mod32} is formed. Equal supports share an AND variable, repeated according to its exact ordered multiplicity in the objective sum. This includes x=y and zero. The cardinality is exact M. All centered M-sets are enumerated independently of the solver to obtain the comparison threshold. The formula asks for a set with a strictly smaller ST value. It uses no symmetry assumptions.

The second encoding instead selects the complement B and imposes ST(B)>1024-96b+3b²-T, where b=32-M and T is the centered value. Indeed among ordered pairs x,y, each of x,y,z=x+y is in B in 32b cases, each pair of these conditions holds in b² cases, and all three hold in ST(B) cases. Inclusion-exclusion gives the formula. This identity is not a novel mathematical result.

`check.py lrat` ignores deletions, traces all positive LRAT dependencies backward from the final empty clause, and checks each retained addition by assigning the negation of that clause and verifying each indicated unit propagation until contradiction. It rejects negative/RAT hints. The checker has no imports from either encoder. Trimmed proofs retain the original DIMACS clause identifiers. Full CNF files, solver logs, raw proofs, trimmed proofs, byte sizes and hashes are preserved. This is an ordinary independently programmed checker, not a Lean kernel check. Only reports with RUP_verified=true certify a case here.

`check_fast.py` is an optional streaming positive-LRAT checker with bounded addition windows. An isolated successful window does not certify a whole proof. `exact_small_complements.py` is an independent optional exhaustive finite oracle. Unexecuted helpers establish no coverage.

Reproduction: from this directory use `./scripts/run.sh python3 -I scripts/encode.py "$PWD" cube 31`, then invoke the absolute CaDiCaL path under the same wrapper with `--lrat --no-binary`, followed by `./scripts/run.sh python3 -I scripts/check.py "$PWD" lrat cube-31`. Solver status 20 means UNSAT, status 10 means SAT. Do not use shell set -e without handling these expected nonzero exit statuses. All Python and solver invocations must use the wrapper.
