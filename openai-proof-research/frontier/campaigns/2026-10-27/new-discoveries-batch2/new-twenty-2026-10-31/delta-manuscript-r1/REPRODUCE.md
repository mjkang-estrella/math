2. Run `python3 -I scripts/cap.py python3 -I scripts/recompute.py --offline`. 3. The portable editable paper source is paper.md, generated by scripts/build.py from paper.template.md and the recomputation. paper.ps is the exact editable typesetting source used for the PDF. To recreate the PDF without dependencies beyond Ghostscript, run `python3 -I scripts/cap.py ps2pdf -dSAFER -dCompatibilityLevel=1.4 paper.ps paper.pdf`. Then render `python3 -I scripts/cap.py pdftoppm -r 100 -png paper.pdf qa/page`. Visually inspect every page again after any change. scripts/build.py is the original-environment assembly script: it recopies provenance from sibling lanes and is not required for the standalone PS-to-PDF route.

A stricter 6,000,000,000-byte virtual-memory bound applies per child. It records resources and reaps the target. Run one invocation at a time. No Lean process was run by this task. No process remains running at handback.
