          5. Comparison with known results and final remarks
  We conclude with some remarks and open questions.

5.1. Theorem 1 only gives a sufficient condition for a lattice Λ to be a stationary
point for the height. We do not know yet whether having a 2-design on every layer
is a necessary condition for a lattice to be critical. Moreover, as our first aim is to
study the minima of the height function, if the 2-design condition is not necessary,
we would like to know whether the minimum of h is to be found among the fully
critical lattices. This is true for the hexagonal lattice A2 in dimension 2, and for
the face-centred cubic lattice A∗3 in dimension 3; this is also true for D4 , which
achieves a strict local minimum of h in dimension 4.

5.2. Moreover, Theorem 1 does not tell whether a fully critical lattice is a local
maximum or minimum, or a saddle point for h.

5.3. We noticed a quite intriguing property of strongly eutactic lattices: up to
dimension 6, if a strong eutactic lattice Λ is not fully critical, then the sequence of
2-designs already stops at the second layer, i.e. M2 (Λ) is not a 2 design. As we
found no counterexample among the strongly eutactic lattices at our disposal in
dimension 7 and 8, we are lead to the following

Conjecture 21. If the first two layers M1 (Λ) and M2 (Λ) of a lattice Λ are spherical
2-designs, then all the other layers are 2-designs, in particular Λ is fully critical for
h.

   The conjecture is perhaps hazardous in high dimension, but thanks to the com-
plete classification of strongly eutactic lattices in small dimension, we can state it
as a theorem in dimension up to 6:

Theorem 22. Let Λ be a lattice of dimension 2 ≤ n ≤ 6. If its first two layers
M1 (Λ) and M2 (Λ) are spherical 2-designs, then all the other layers are 2-designs,
in particular Λ is fully critical for h.
