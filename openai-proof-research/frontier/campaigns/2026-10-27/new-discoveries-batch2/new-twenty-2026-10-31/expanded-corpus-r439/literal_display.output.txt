A $D$-regular directed graph is a digraph in which every vertex has in-degree and out-degree equal to $D$, and an **oriented graph** is a digraph without $2$-cycles.

**Conjectures on Hamilton cycles in regular directed and oriented graphs.**

(a) For each $D>2$, every $D$-regular oriented graph $G$ on $n$ vertices with

$$
D\geq \frac{n-1}{4}
$$

is Hamiltonian.

(b) Every strongly $2$-connected $D$-regular digraph on $n$ vertices with

$$
D\geq \frac{n}{3}
$$

is Hamiltonian.

(c) For each $D>2$, every $D$-regular strongly $2$-connected oriented graph $G$ on $n$ vertices with

$$
D\geq \frac{n}{6}
$$

is Hamiltonian.

These are directed analogues of Hamilton-cycle questions for connected regular graphs. The source attributes (a) to Jackson and says that (b) and (c) were raised in a survey; their general status is not resolved in the supplied text.
