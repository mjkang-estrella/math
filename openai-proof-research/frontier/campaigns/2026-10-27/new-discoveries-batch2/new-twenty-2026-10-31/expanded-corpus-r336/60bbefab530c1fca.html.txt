Abstract
    

Let  {\mathcal{G}}_{n} 𝒢n{\mathcal{G}}_{n} be the set of class of graphs of order  n nn. The first Zagreb index  M_{1}(G) M1​(G)M_{1}(G) is equal to the sum of squares of the
degrees of the vertices, and the second Zagreb index  M_{2}(G) M2​(G)M_{2}(G) is equal to the sum
of the products of the degrees of pairs of adjacent vertices of the underlying
molecular graph  G GG. The three set of graphs are as follows:
    







 \displaystyle A=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}>\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\},~B=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}=\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\} A={G∈𝒢n:M1​(G)n>M2​(G)m},B={G∈𝒢n:M1​(G)n=M2​(G)m}\displaystyle A=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}>\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\},~B=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}=\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\}




and








 \displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~C=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}<\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\}. C={G∈𝒢n:M1​(G)n<M2​(G)m}.\displaystyle~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~C=\left\{G\in{\mathcal{G}}_{n}:\,\frac{M_{1}(G)}{n}<\frac{M_{2}(G)}{m}\right\}.
