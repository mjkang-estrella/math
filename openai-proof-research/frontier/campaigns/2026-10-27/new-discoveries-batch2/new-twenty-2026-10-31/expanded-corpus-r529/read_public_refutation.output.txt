                                                                                         Abstract
                                                       For integers r ≥ 1 and n ≥ 2, let sr (n) be the least N such that every sequence
                                                   of N vectors in Zr , each nonzero modulo n, contains n terms whose sum is zero
                                                   modulo n but nonzero modulo n2 . Let wr (n) be the corresponding least N when
                                                   any nonempty subsequence is allowed. These constants were introduced as zero-sum
                                                   problems of new types by Z.-W. Sun. We prove sr (n) ≥ (2r+1 − 2)(n − 1) + 1 by
                                                   constructing an explicit sequence from a lattice polytope, disproving his conjecture
                                                   s2 (n) = 4n + 1 for n ≥ 4. We obtain wr (2) = 2r + 1 by classifying all sequences
                                                   avoiding such nonempty subsequences modulo 4. We also prove Sun’s conjecture
                                                   w2 (n) = 4n − 3 for n = 2k 3ℓ p, where p is a prime and k, ℓ ≥ 0 are integers, by
                                                   classifying the extremal sequences of length 4p − 4 modulo p2 when n = p. For every
                                                   fixed n ≥ 2, we prove wr (n)/s(Zrn ) = On (r2 /2r ) → 0 as r → ∞, where s(Zrn ) is
                                                   the classical Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv constant, with an explicit threshold on r for strict
                                                   inequality. Thus, for every fixed modulus n ≥ 2, Sun’s proposed equality fails for
                                                   sufficiently large r.

                                           Keywords: Zero-sum sequence of new type, sequence classification, extremal sequence,
                                           lattice polytope, Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv constant,

    Note that the condition in (1.1) depends only on residues modulo n and does not
determine whether the selected sum also vanishes modulo n2 . For prime moduli, Gao et
al. [7] studied exact divisibility conditions for zero-sum subsequences. For a prime p, the
p-adic valuation of an integer x is vp (x) = sup{k ∈ Z : k ≥ 0 and pk | x}. For r = 1
and n = p, their constant s(p, 1) coincides with the least length such that every integer
sequence of that length whose terms are not divisible by p contains a p-term subsequence
whose sum has p-adic valuation exactly 1. Equivalently, the selected sum must vanish
modulo p but not modulo p2 . Recently, it was shown in [8] that s(p, 1) ≤ 3p − 2 for
every prime p, improving the bound s(p, 1) ≤ s(Z2p ) = 4p − 3 obtained from Kemnitz’s
theorem [11, 18].

    By definition, we immediately have wr (n) ≤ sr (n) and s1 (p) = s(p, 1). For n > 2,

conjectured in the same paper that w2 (n) = 4n − 3 for every n > 1 in Conjecture 1.1(ii)
and s2 (n) = 4n + 1 for every n > 2 in Conjecture 1.2. In Remark 1.2, he also asked
whether wr (n) = s(Zrn ) for all integers n > 1 and r > 0. More related constants and
conjectures can also be found in [7, 20].
    This paper aims to study the zero-sum problems of new types and to answer Sun’s
conjectures and questions listed above. In the following, we call an admissible sequence
bad if no n-term subsequence satisfies (1.2), and w-bad if no nonempty subsequence does.
Such sequences give lower bounds for sr (n) and wr (n), respectively. We write |A| for the
length of A, σ(A) for the sum of its terms in the ambient abelian group, and v[m] for m
copies of v. In addition, sequence products mean concatenation and an empty product
denotes the empty sequence.
