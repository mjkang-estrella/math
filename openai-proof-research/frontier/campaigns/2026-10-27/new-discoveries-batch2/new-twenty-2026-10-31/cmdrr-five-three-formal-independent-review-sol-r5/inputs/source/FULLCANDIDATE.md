Suppose a schedule exists. Relabel spouses as (M_i,F_i), i=1,2,3. Players 4 and 5 are unpaired. For each sex, every pair opposes once, except pair {4,5}, which opposes twice. Every nonspouse mixed pair partners exactly once and opposes exactly once; spouses never share a game. Games have two distinct men and two distinct women, partitioned into mixed teams. There are 25-3=22 partnership cells, each occurring once, so eleven games.

For each male i define p_i on the females available to him: in the unique game with partner F_j, let p_i(j) be the opposing female. Each female opposes M_i once, so p_i is a permutation, without fixed points. A 2-cycle j<->l means two distinct games of M_i use female pair {j,l}. Only pair {4,5} can repeat globally. For i<=3 the available set is {j,k,4,5}, where {i,j,k}={1,2,3}. A fixed-point-free permutation on four symbols has either one 4-cycle or two 2-cycles. The latter would repeat two distinct female pairs, impossible because only {4,5} may repeat. Hence p_i is a 4-cycle. Viewed as undirected edges, a cycle on {j,k,4,5} contains edge 45 if and only if it contains edge jk (the two are opposite edges of the complete four-vertex graph). Both indicators are 0 or 1.

Let A count games whose men are both in {1,2,3} and whose women are {4,5}; let B count games whose men are {4,5} and whose women are both in {1,2,3}; let z count games with both men {4,5} and women {4,5}. These are nonnegative integers, counting games with multiplicity.

Sum the preceding opposite-edge indicator identity over male i=1,2,3. There are exactly two games with women {4,5}. Of these, A have two spouse men, z have zero spouse men, and 2-A-z have exactly one spouse man. Thus the left sum is 2A+(2-A-z)=2+A-z. For each female spouse pair {j,k}, the unique game using it cannot involve M_j or M_k. Its male pair is therefore {i,4}, {i,5}, or {4,5}, where i is the third spouse index. It contributes 1 to the right sum precisely in the first two cases. There are three female spouse pairs, and exactly B of their games use men {4,5}. The right sum is 3-B. Consequently

                     A+B=1+z.                         (1)

Claim: neither unpaired male permutation has a 2-cycle. Suppose p_m, m in {4,5}, has one. It must be (4 5). The other three female indices form a 3-cycle, since fixed points are forbidden. Thus both games with female pair {4,5} involve M_m, and the other three games of M_m have female pairs exactly {1,2},{1,3},{2,3}. In particular A=0. Let M_t be the other unpaired male. M_m opposes M_t in exactly two games. Exactly z of these use female pair {4,5}. All the other 2-z have female pairs in {1,2,3}. Every game counted by B involves both unpaired males, hence M_m; therefore B=2-z. Substituting into (1) gives 2-z=1+z, or 2z=1, impossible for an integer. The claim follows. A fixed-point-free permutation of five symbols without a 2-cycle must be a 5-cycle: every cycle length is at least three and two such lengths already exceed five.

Thus the three spouse male permutations are 4-cycles (sign -1), and the two unpaired male permutations are 5-cycles (sign +1). Their product as disjoint row permutations has sign -1. Interchanging sexes proves exactly the same fact for the corresponding column permutations; no new assumption is used, because Definition 1.2 is symmetric in sexes.

Here is the precise final sign bridge, including direction. Let X={(i,j):1<=i,j<=5} minus {(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)}. Define U:X->X by U(i,j)=(i,l) when the unique game with partnership (M_i,F_j) has opposing team (M_k,F_l). Define C:X->X by C(k,l)=(i,l) in that same game: for female l it maps her partner male k to her opponent male i. These are respectively the disjoint row and column permutations just analyzed; uniqueness of every mixed partner/opposition makes both bijections. Let T(i,j)=(k,l), the opposite partnership in that game. Then C(T(i,j))=U(i,j), so T=C^{-1}U. Therefore sign(T)=sign(C)^{-1}sign(U)=(-1)(-1)=+1. But T swaps the two partnerships of each game. There are eleven games, with disjoint cells and no fixed cell, so T is the product of eleven disjoint transpositions and has sign (-1)^11=-1. Contradiction.
