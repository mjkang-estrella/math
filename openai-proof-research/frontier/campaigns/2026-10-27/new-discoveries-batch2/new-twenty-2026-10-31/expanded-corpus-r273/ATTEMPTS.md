First mechanism: iterate cell transfer to produce an improving pair. This terminates at a fixed pair but does not show no OTHER product dominates that fixed pair. Second mechanism: use a competing interior offset after the smallest one is blocked. A pair fixed by this operation has no strictly interior containment at all (a nontrivial interior offset changes the larger size from A to two sizes strictly between B and A); hence another allowed offset cannot furnish a witness. Third mechanism: compare shuffle descent distributions between two distinct fixed pairs. If L_gamma L_delta - L_alpha L_beta is nonnegative, its specialization at M equal variables is nonnegative for every M. For alpha of size a and length u, L_alpha(1^M)=binomial(M+a-u,a), so any proposed domination must satisfy the resulting product-of-binomials inequalities. This supplies a necessary screen, not a coefficientwise proof; two descent distributions can pass every such specialization while having negative individual coefficients. Leading coefficient also requires 1/(c!d!) >= 1/(a!b!). No concrete pair beyond the source's n<=10 checked range has been exhibited. No small-parameter or helper credit.
