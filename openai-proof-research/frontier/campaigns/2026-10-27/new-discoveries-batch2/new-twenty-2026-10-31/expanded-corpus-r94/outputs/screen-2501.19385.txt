4629: Conjecture 5.62 is proven for comparability graphs (recall that a comparability graph is a graph with a transitive orientation), triangle-free sub-cubic graphs, regular graphs with degree at most five, and graphs with large size. The  \gp -spectrum of cycles and some families of trees are determined. For complete bipartite graphs, the balanced complete bipartite graph  K_{n,n}  is  \gp -full, whereas for  r\geq 4  the  \gp -spectrum is  K_{r,2}  is  [\left\lceil\frac{r}{2}\right\rceil,r+2] . It would be interesting to determine the  \gp -spectrum of the remaining complete bipartite graphs.
