198: Abstract

200: 
201: Generalizing Chvátal’s classic 1972 result, Hoàng proposed in 1995 the following conjecture, which strengthens Chvátal’s result in terms of toughness: Let  t\geq 1  be a positive integer and  G  be a  t -tough graph on  n\geq 3  vertices with degree sequence  d_{1},d_{2},\dots,d_{n}  in non-increasing order. Suppose for each  i\in[1,\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor] , if
202:  d_{i}\leq i\text{ and }d_{n-i+t}<n-i  implies
203:  d_{j}+d_{n-j+t}\geq n  for all  j\in[i+1,\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor] ,
204: then  G  is Hamiltonian. Hoàng verified the conjecture for  t=1 . In this paper,
205: we verfity the conjecture for all  t\geq 4 . Our proof relies on
206: a toughness closure lemma for  t\geq 4  that we previously established. Additionally, we show
207: that the toughness closure lemma does not hold when  t=1 .

266: Let  n\geq 3  and  t\geq 1  be integers, and  G  be  t -tough graph with degree sequence  d_{1},d_{2},\dots,d_{n} . If for all  i<\frac{n}{2}  it holds that  d_{i}\leq i  implies  d_{n-i+t}\geq n-i , then  G  is Hamiltonian.

342: When investigating Conjecture 2, Hoàng and Robin [7] in 2024 introduced
343: the analogous closure concept for  t -tough graphs  G , called the  t -closure, where  t\geq 1  is
344: an integer. The  t -closure of  G  is
345: the graph obtained from  G  by iteratively adding all edges
346: joining pairs of nonadjacent vertices whose degree-sum is at least  n-t .
347: Hoàng and Robin [7] further showed that for  t\geq 2 , a  \frac{3t-1}{2} -tough graph  G  is Hamiltonian if and only if its  t -closure is Hamiltonian.
