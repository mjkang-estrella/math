2300: In Theorem 1.3, we prove that the polynomial  P_{\lambda}(z;q,t)  factors nicely when  \lambda=(n^{n}) . Based on computations for small partitions,  P_{\lambda}(z;q,t)  appear to have many factors of the form  (z+q^{i}t^{j}) . We conjecture that these linear factors are determined by the rank of the partition  \lambda , that is, the size of the Durfee square contained in  \lambda  ([18, Page 289]). We have verified it for all partitions of size at most  9 .
