205: Let  \mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}=\{G_{1},G_{2},\ldots,G_{k}\}  be a multiset of graphs on vertex set  [n]  and let  F  be a fixed graph with edge set  F=\{e_{1},e_{2},\ldots,e_{m}\}  and  k\geq m . We say  {\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}}  is rainbow  F -free if there is no  \{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{m}\}\subseteq[k]  satisfying  e_{j}\in G_{i_{j}}  for every  j\in[m] .
206: Let  \mbox{ex}_{k}(n,F)  be the maximum  \sum_{i=1}^{k}|G_{i}|  among all the rainbow  F -free multisets  {\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}} .
207: Keevash, Saks, Sudakov, and Verstraëte (2004) determined the exact value of  \mbox{ex}_{k}(n,K_{r})  when  n  is sufficiently large and proposed the conjecture that the results remain true when  n\geq Cr^{2}  for some constant  C . Recently, Frankl (2022) confirmed the conjecture for  r=3  and all possible values of  n . In this paper, we determine the exact value of  \mbox{ex}_{k}(n,K_{r})  for  n\geq r-1  when  r=4  and  5 , i.e. the conjecture of Keevash, Saks, Sudakov, and Verstraëte is true for  r\in\{4,5\} .

397: Let  n\geq r\geq 3  and  k\geq\binom{r}{2} . Let  \mathcal{G}=(G_{0},f)  be a  k -weighted graph, where  G_{0}\cong K_{n}  on vertex set  [n] .
398: Suppose that  G_{0}  contains no complete subgraph  K_{r}  with weight sequence bound  (1,2,...,\binom{r}{2}) . Let  k_{2}=\left\lceil\frac{\left(\binom{r}{2}-1\right)\binom{n}{2}}{t_{r-1}(n)}\right\rceil  and  k_{1}=k_{2}-1 . Then the following statements hold.

413: Theorem 2.3.
414: 
415: 
416: 
417: Conjecture 2.2 implies Conjecture 1.3.

422: Proof.
423: 
424: 
425: 
426: Let  {\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}}=\{G_{1},G_{2},...,G_{k}\}  be a RB K_{r} -free system with maximum  |\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}| .
427: By Lemma 2.1, we may assume  G_{k}\subseteq G_{k-1}\subseteq\ldots\subseteq G_{1} . Let  G_{0}=K_{n}  and define a  k -weighting  f  on  G_{0}  by setting  f(e)=\max_{e\in G_{i}}i  for every edge  e\in G_{0} . Thus  f(e)  is the number of graphs containing  e  in  \mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}  because  G_{k}\subseteq\ldots\subseteq G_{1}\subseteq G_{0} . Therefore, we have  f(G_{0})=|{\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}}| . We may assume  k\geq k_{1} , otherwise, we can add  k_{1}-k  copies of the empty graphs  K_{n}^{c}  into  \mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}  with the property RB K_{r} -free and the  k -weighted graph  G_{0}  unchanged.
428: 
429: 
430: 
431: 
432: Now we show that there is no  K_{r}  in  G_{0}  with weight sequence bound  (1,2,...,\binom{r}{2}) . Otherwise, suppose there is a copy  H  of  K_{r}  in  G_{0}  with weight sequence  (a_{1},a_{2},...,a_{h})\geq(1,2,...,\binom{r}{2}) , where  h=|H|=\binom{r}{2}  and assume  H=\{e_{1},e_{2},\ldots,e_{h}\}  with  f(e_{i})=a_{i}  for any  i\in[h] . Since  (1,2,...,\binom{r}{2})\leq(a_{1},a_{2},...,a_{h}) , we have  f(e_{i})=a_{i}\geq i  for every  i\in[h] . Thus, by the definition of  f  and  G_{k}\subseteq\ldots\subseteq G_{1}\subseteq G_{0} , we have  e_{i}\in G_{i}  for every  i\in[\binom{r}{2}] . This leads to a rainbow  H\cong K_{r}  in  {\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}} , a contradiction.
433: 
434: 
435: 
436: 
437: Now we suppose Conjecture 2.2 is true. If  k=k_{1}  or  k_{2}(=k_{1}+1) , then  |{\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}}|=f(G_{0})\leq\min\left\{\left(\binom{r}{2}-1\right)\binom{n}{2},k\cdot t_{r-1}(n)\right\} . Thus Conjecture 1.3 holds when  k=k_{1}  or  k_{2} .
438: If  k>k_{2} , let  {\mathcal{G^{\prime}}_{n}^{k_{2}}}=\{G_{1},...,G_{k_{2}}\} . Obviously,  {\mathcal{G^{\prime}}_{n}^{k_{2}}}  is still RB K_{r} -free. By Conjecture 2.2,  |{\mathcal{G^{\prime}}_{n}^{k_{2}}}|\leq k_{2}\cdot t_{r-1}(n) . Since  G_{k}\subseteq\ldots\subseteq G_{k_{2}}\subseteq\ldots\subseteq G_{1} , we have  |G_{i}|\leq|G_{j}|  for every  k_{2}+1\leq i\leq k  and  1\leq j\leq k_{2} . Hence
439: 
440: 
441: 
442: 
443:  \sum_{i=k_{2}+1}^{k}|G_{i}|\leq\frac{k-k_{2}}{k_{2}}\sum_{j=1}^{k_{2}}|G_{j}|=\frac{k-k_{2}}{k_{2}}|{\mathcal{G^{\prime}}_{n}^{k_{2}}}|\leq(k-k_{2})t_{r-1}(n), 
444: 
445: 
446: 
447: which implies that
448: 
449: 
450: 
451: 
452:  |{\mathcal{G}_{n}^{k}}|=\sum_{i=k_{2}+1}^{k}|G_{i}|+\sum_{j=1}^{k_{2}}|G_{j}|\leq(k-k_{2})t_{r-1}(n)+k_{2}\cdot t_{r-1}(n)=k\cdot t_{r-1}(n).
