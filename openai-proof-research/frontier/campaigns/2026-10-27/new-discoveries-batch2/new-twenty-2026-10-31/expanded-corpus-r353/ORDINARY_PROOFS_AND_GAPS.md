## 354627: full exact ordinary proof, publicly subsumed

Let n>=1 and 1<=k<=n, let f:[n]->P([n]) have union [n], and partition [n] into k nonempty consecutive blocks. Interpret f(A) as union over a in A. Set F(A)=the integer interval hull of that union, with hull(empty)=empty. The witness below always has nonempty iterates, so the empty-set convention does not affect it. The required iterate is positive, 1<=l<=k; no vacuous l=0 is used.

First prove a cyclic-permutation lemma. For a cyclic permutation sigma of [m], m>=2, consider Q^m with basis e_1,...,e_m, the permutation operator P(e_i)=e_sigma(i), and its invariant codimension-one subspace W={sum x_i e_i: sum x_i=0}. A basis is b_i=e_(i+1)-e_i, 1<=i<m. Write B for P|W in this basis. Telescoping gives

P(b_i)=e_sigma(i+1)-e_sigma(i)
 = sum_(j=sigma(i))^(sigma(i+1)-1) b_j if sigma(i)<sigma(i+1),
 = -sum_(j=sigma(i+1))^(sigma(i)-1) b_j otherwise.

Thus B_(j,i) is nonzero precisely when [j,j+1] is contained in hull{sigma(i),sigma(i+1)}. Its sign is irrelevant to graph adjacency. Put an arc i->j at every such nonzero entry.

Because sigma is an m-cycle, det(I-tP)=1-t^m: in the ordered orbit basis P is the cyclic shift, or this follows directly by expanding that determinant. P induces the identity on Q^m/W, via the sum-of-coordinates quotient. In a basis adapted to W the matrix is block triangular, and consequently

(1-t)det(I-tB)=1-t^m,
 det(I-tB)=1+t+...+t^(m-1).

For any 1<=q<m the coefficient of t^q is nonzero. Expanding the determinant and first choosing the q positions where a B entry rather than an identity entry is used shows that this coefficient is (-1)^q times the sum of all q-by-q principal minors det B[S,S]. At least one such minor is nonzero. Its Leibniz expansion has at least one nonzero product. The bijection indexing that product decomposes S into disjoint directed cycles of the support digraph of B. (Rows and columns can reverse the written permutation, but the arcs are column->row and still form precisely these cycles.) Therefore at least q distinct vertices lie on cycles of lengths at most q.

For an arc i->j, hull(sigma({i,i+1})) contains {j,j+1}. Hull-image iteration is monotone. Iterating this inclusion around a directed cycle of length l proves {i,i+1} subset (hull sigma)^l({i,i+1}). Thus at least q adjacent pairs have return time <=q.

Return to general f. Make a directed graph on [n], putting an arc x->y when y belongs to f(x). Every vertex has an incoming arc. Following predecessors indefinitely in a finite graph produces a directed cycle of distinct vertices; let S be its vertex set, m=|S|, and sigma the cyclic successor on that cycle. Then sigma(x) belongs to f(x) for every x in S. Order S by its inherited integer order. Its intersections with the original consecutive blocks give k' nonempty consecutive blocks, where k'<=k and k'<=m.

If m<=k, choose any r=s in S and l=m. Successively taking hulls never loses the chosen cycle orbit point, so F^m({r}) contains r, as required. This also handles m=1. Otherwise m>k>=k'. Apply the cyclic lemma with q=k'. At most k'-1 adjacent pairs in the ordered S cross a block boundary. Among at least k' pairs returning within k', one pair {r,s} lies inside an original block. In the ordered S its restricted hull-iterate contains r,s after some l<=k'<=k.

For completeness, restricted-hull containment transfers to full [n]. If A subset S and A subset X subset [n], then sigma(A) subset union_(x in X) f(x). The full hull of the latter contains the restricted S-hull of sigma(A). Starting with X=A={r,s}, induction gives F^l({r,s}) containing every element of the restricted l-th iterate. In particular it contains r,s. This proves the entire literal conjecture, not just permutations or a continuous-map consequence.

## 350062: full exact bridge to a public proof

Let D be the literal generating series counting Dyck paths with no peak at a positive height congruent to 1,...,m-1 modulo m. A positive height has exactly one residue in {1,...,m}; hence its allowed peak heights are precisely the positive multiples of m. Xin–Zhang, arXiv:2112.05936v1, define F^(m,r)=D^(m,[m] minus {r}). Thus, for every m>=3 in the literal statement, D=F^(m,m), coefficient by coefficient including the empty path. Both sources define the unshifted Hankel determinant of order n as det(a_(i+j)) for 0<=i,j<n, and the displayed sequences start at n=1. There is no shift or reversal.

Xin–Zhang Theorem 1 with r=m, explicitly restated as Corollary 2, gives exactly (1,0^(m-2),1,1)* when m=1,2 mod4 and (1,0^(m-2),-1,-1,-1,0^(m-2),1,1)* when m=0,3 mod4. Substitution proves the full recorded endpoint. This is a complete reduction to a named public theorem, not a claim that its proof was independently formalized.

Sustained route: first-return decomposition gives D=1/(1+x-xD_shift) at each forbidden residue and 1/(1-xD_shift) at the allowed residue. Cycling these Möbius transforms gives the quadratic equation
 D=1/(1+sum_(i=1)^(m-1)x^i - (sum_(i=1)^m x^i)D).
Ordinary nonzero J-fraction determinant products fail because the required sequence has zero terms. A Dodgson condensation quotient likewise cannot divide through those zeros. The public proof uses Sulanke–Xin quadratic transformation instead. Sections 2.3–2.4 and 3.2 were read in extracted text: the F1->F2->F3->F1 cycle reduces the determinant index by m+1 with sign (-1)^(binom(r-1,2)+binom(m-r+1,2)), plus initial determinants. Setting r=m gives sign (-1)^binom(m-1,2) and the required alternating/nonalternating blocks. The coefficientwise identification and identical indexing above are the semantic bridges, so no new computation is warranted.

Both v1 and v2 of Chien–Eu–Fu retain Conjecture 7.1; the source was published in European J. Combin. 101 (2022), 103478. Xin–Zhang's public v1 was submitted 2021-12-11 and its journal DOI is 10.1016/j.disc.2022.113063. Independent GETs of the two journal pages returned 403 and were not retried; the journal proof bodies therefore remain unread. Search returned the journal's explicit Corollary 2 attribution, but that is supplementary to the actual arXiv proof body. No contradiction between the old open label and current public proof is assumed away. 0 credit, no candidate.

## 350503: sustained ordinary route, exact missing lemma

The literal question quantifies over every finite abelian Gamma of order >=2 and every finite field F. Both arXiv versions, ISAAC 2021 Conjecture 9, and the actual Combinatorica journal HTML Conjecture 1.9 state the same k<=ell characterization. The literal's description as gain graphs is imprecise: the actual construction uses vertex-labelled graphs, not an arbitrary edge-gain matroid. Feasible edge sets are acyclic and satisfy the source's component-wise gamma-nonzero condition, including the special all-zero component rule, and twisting is allowed in representation. This matters to every attempted witness.

The sufficiency is already Theorem 1.6. Here is its full determinant mechanism in the nonzero-label case. If k<=ell, inject Gamma=(F_p)^k additively into F=(F_p)^ell by choosing linearly independent alpha_i and phi(c)=sum c_i alpha_i. For a vertex-labelled graph with every gamma(v) nonzero, orient its edges, let I be the oriented incidence matrix, and let B be diagonal with entries 1/phi(gamma(v)). Then A=I^T B I is symmetric. A cycle makes the corresponding incidence columns dependent, so any edge set with a cycle has singular principal A submatrix. For a tree component H with edge set E_H and vertex set V_H, Cauchy–Binet and the fact that deleting any row of a tree incidence matrix has determinant +/-1 give
 det A[E_H,E_H] = (product_(v in V_H) 1/phi(gamma(v))) * sum_(v in V_H) phi(gamma(v)).
It is nonzero exactly when sum gamma(v) is nonzero. For an edge forest the matrix is block diagonal across edge-components. An isolated vertex has empty determinant 1 and nonzero label, so causes no exception. Thus nonsingular principal sets agree with the required feasible sets. Source Lemma 7.4 replaces a zero-labelled vertex v by adjacent vertices v,w with labels g,-g and contracts vw to recover v. Iterating removes zero labels; source minor closure and representation closure under minors extend the construction, including the all-zero component convention. Twisting transports the result to every Gamma-graphic delta-matroid. These are source lemmas, not unproved new discoveries. No credit for this half.

Necessity already forces Gamma elementary abelian of the same characteristic (Theorem 1.7), but does not force k<=ell. First failed extension: repeat the Ramsey-star proof of Theorem 1.7. Give the star centre label -g and ordinary leaves label g. Selecting p+1 ordinary leaves gives component label -g+(p+1)g=pg. If g has order different from p, the published homogeneous-matrix singularity contradicts feasibility. In the remaining elementary abelian case, pg=0, and the selected set is INFEASIBLE. Its singularity is then correct. Reusing the source obstruction cannot distinguish two extension degrees in the same characteristic.

Second route: use many distinct leaf labels to encode all nonzero vectors of Gamma and attempt to recover an additive injection into F from a hypothetical symmetric representation. The forward construction A=I^T B I suggests this, but representation is not required to be an incidence-Gram matrix. Equality of feasible principal-minor patterns alone does not prove such a factorization or its uniqueness. Ramsey homogenization can select only repeated labels and destroy the desired distinct-label information; bounding the number of colors does not recover the dimension. Needed precise lemma: an explicit finite vertex-labelled graph H(Gamma) whose Gamma-graphic delta-matroid, if represented by ANY symmetric or skew-symmetric matrix over F, forces an injective additive map Gamma->(F,+), after allowed twists. Prove that lemma and k<=ell follows from finite vector-space cardinality p^k<=p^ell. Neither such graph nor its normalization/rigidity proof was obtained. This is a mathematical gap, not a proposed solver architecture. No finite witness or full necessity proof is claimed.

The paper explicitly distinguishes its nonzero constraint from Gamma-graphic delta-matroids and leaves their relationship for future work; its representable-matroid algorithms cannot be substituted as the missing theorem. The actual 2023 Combinatorica body still poses Conjecture 1.9; that does not prove it remains open in 2026. Searches and current registry screens did not establish resolution or absence. Material classical matrix-representation normalization literature remains unread. Scope incomplete, source review targeted, prior/subsumption/novelty HOLD. No CANDIDATE.md and no Pi dispatch.
