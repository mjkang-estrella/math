## 371594 PSD propagation classification
Actual 2511.16335v1 pp1-5 and11-16 definitions, fort criterion, fast-join construction and converse-for-joins proofs read. The literal says maximum over minimum forcing sets; actual Definition1.2 uses MINIMAL forcing sets. Conjecture1.7 concerns that upper parameter. The change is substantive, not silently repaired into a new theorem.

Route1: a dominated adjacent pair N[u] proper subset N[v] gives a slow forcing set V minus {u,v}. No blue vertex can force u first; a vertex in N(v) minus N[u] forces v first; v forces u second. Thus any source-fast graph has no such pair. This reproduces actual Lemma5.1; it does not prove the converse. Cycles and many regular graphs evade the domination test, so stopping there would not resolve classification.

Route2: use an induced path a-b-c as the white set. If a blue vertex sees exactly endpoint a but no blue vertex sees exactly b within the triple, b is delayed, and after an endpoint is forced the remaining induced tree is forced. However outside vertices may see exactly b, forcing it immediately; an induced P3/P4 alone does not guarantee a slow set. The source's join proof suppresses such private middle neighbors with a common opposite-side vertex. No corresponding suppression mechanism in an arbitrary connected nonjoin graph was proved.

Route3: maximal induced trees. Let T be a maximal induced tree of size>=3 and l a leaf, p its parent. Every outside vertex adjacent to T has at least two neighbors in T, since otherwise it could be appended preserving an induced tree. Start with T minus l white and all other vertices blue. The blue l sees exactly p in that connected tree, so forces p; thereafter PSD forcing propagates through the tree, since deletion of a forced tree vertex separates its remaining branches. Thus this is a forcing set. Outside T, a first-round singleton neighbor v of T minus l can arise only from a vertex whose neighbors in T were exactly {l,v}. Therefore if every forcing set is fast, EVERY v in T minus {l,p} must have a witness outside T with N(x) intersect T={l,v}. This is a necessary structural constraint for every leaf of every maximal induced tree. It is stronger than the failed adjacent-pair shortcut but does not yet imply that the complement is disconnected (a join). Missing lemma: those simultaneous two-neighbor witnesses force a decomposition into the complements of disjoint complete bipartite components, or permit a larger/different induced tree yielding a slow forcing set. No proof of this lemma, no full classification or counterexample, no credit.
