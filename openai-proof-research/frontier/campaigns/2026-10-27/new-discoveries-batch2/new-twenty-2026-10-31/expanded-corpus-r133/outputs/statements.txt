352307 Central moment conjecture for continuously increasing subsequences 
Let $\mathfrak{S}_{m,n}$ be the set of multiset permutations under consideration, let $\pi$ be uniformly random in $\mathfrak{S}_{m,n}$, and let $L^1_{m,n}(\pi)$ be the continuously increasing subsequence statistic. Define

$$
\mu=\mathbb{E}[L^1_{m,n}(\pi)].
$$

For each $r\ge 1$, define

$$
c_r=\begin{cases}\dfrac{r!}{2^{r/2}(r/2)!}& r\text{ even},\\[4pt]\dfrac{r!}{3\cdot 2^{(r-1)/2}((r-3)/2)!}& r\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

**Central moment conjecture.** For all $r\ge 1$, if $n$ is sufficiently large in terms of $m$, then

$$
\mathbb{E}[(L^1_{m,n}(\pi)-\mu)^r]=c_r m^{\left\lfloor r/2\right\rfloor}+O\left(m^{\left\lfloor r/2\right\rfloor-1}\right).
$$

The conjecture is based on computational evidence for higher moments. Together with the known asymptotic standard deviation $\sigma\sim m^{1/2}$, it would imply convergence of the standardized moments to the Gaussian moments, and hence convergence in distribution to a standard normal law.

348108 The limiting energy conjecture for Barabási–Albert trees 
Let $\{X_n\}_{n>0}$ be a sequence of trees with parameter $\alpha$, and let $\mathcal{E}(X_n)$ denote the graph energy of $X_n$.

**Limiting energy conjecture.** For every $\alpha$, there exists $g(\alpha)$ such that, almost surely,

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\mathcal{E}(X_n)}{n}=g(\alpha).
$$

Moreover, $g(\alpha)>1$ for $\alpha<0.79$, $g(\alpha)<1$ for $\alpha>0.81$, $g(\alpha)$ is strictly decreasing and continuous in $\alpha$, $g(\alpha)\to0$ as $\alpha\to\infty$, and $g(\alpha)\to1.273$ as $\alpha\to\infty$.

These claims formalize simulation observations about the asymptotic energy density of the random trees. In particular, the claim at $\alpha=0$ would imply that random recursive random trees are not hypoenergetic. The final two limiting assertions are internally inconsistent as written, since both use $\alpha\to\infty$; the source should be checked for a likely typographical error.

345545 The growth constant for the stack-sorting map 
Let $S_n$ be the symmetric group, let $s:S_n\to S_n$ be the stack-sorting map, and let $\deg(s:S_n\to S_n)$ denote its maximum fiber size. The paper considers the exponential growth rate

$$
\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\deg(s:S_n\to S_n)^{1/n}.
$$

**Stack-sorting growth conjecture.** The value of this limit lies in the interval $(1.68,1.73)$.

The interval is suggested by computations on random permutations for $n=100$ and $n=300$. The problem of obtaining improved asymptotic estimates, or exact formulas, remains open.

358020 Asymptotic formula for linear-system chromatic number

341220 The asymptotic conjecture for minimum splitters of splittable set families 
Let a **splittable $n$-set family** be a family of $n$ sets admitting at least one splitter, and let $k$ denote the size of the underlying ground set. **The asymptotic splitter conjecture.** The minimum number of splitters of a splittable $n$-set family is asymptotic to

The cases $n=1$ and $n=2$ are known, and computational evidence supports the $n=3$ case; the general assertion remains open.

373488 Conjectural description of maximizers for the maximum product of distances 
Let $n\ge 3$. Let $P=\\{z_1,\dots,z_n\\}\subset\mathbb{C}$ be an $n$-gon with $\operatorname{diam}(P)\le 2$ that attains $\Delta(P)=\Delta_{\max}(n)$. 

**Maximizer structure conjecture.** The following properties hold:

1. If $n$ is odd, $P$ is a regular $n$-gon.
2. If $n$ is even, $P$ has an axis of symmetry. Moreover, if $6\mid n$, then $P$ is invariant under rotation by $2\pi/3$, and hence has dihedral symmetry compatible with this $120^\circ$ rotational symmetry.
3. If $n$ is even, the diameter graph of $P$ is obtained from a cycle $C_{n-3}$ by attaching three pendant edges to vertices of the cycle.

This conjecture refines the structural restrictions and numerical evidence developed in the paper, including the observed behavior for small $n$ and explicit asymptotic constructions. The proposed description remains conjectural because the extremal configurations for finite $n$ are difficult to determine; proving these properties would give a substantially sharper characterization of the maximizers.

358865 The density conjecture for distance-restricted firefighting on square grids 
Let $P_n$ be the path graph on $n$ vertices, let $P_n\mathbin{\square}P_n$ be the Cartesian-product square grid, and let $\operatorname{DRMVS}(G,s;d,b)$ denote the maximum number of vertices saved in the distance-restricted firefighting process on graph $G$, starting from source $s$, with distance restriction $d$ and $b$ firefighter available per time step. 382109 Asymptotic separation between systolic bounds for Ramanujan surfaces 
Let $g$ denote the genus of a hyperbolic surface, let $\sys(M)$ denote its systole, and let $\Nsmall$ and $\lambda_1(M)$ be the quantities used in the paper. 347590 Conjecture on the asymptotic threshold for asymmetric choosability 
For positive integers $k_A$ and $k_B$, let $\xi_m(k_A)$ denote the asymptotic threshold parameter for the choosability of complete bipartite graphs, and let $\xi_g(k_A)$ be a positive constant. A bipartite graph is $(k_A,k_B)$-choosable when every list assignment with list sizes $k_A$ and $k_B$ on the two parts admits a proper coloring.

**Asymptotic threshold conjecture.** There exist positive constants $\xi_g(k_A)$ such that every bipartite graph $G$ satisfying $\xi<\xi_g(k_A)$ is $(k_A,k_B)$-choosable. Moreover, if the $\xi_g(k_A)$ are chosen to be the largest constants for which this statement is true, then

\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{\log(\xi_g(k_A))}{k_A}

exists.

341979 Superlinear area conjecture for subtree-separated orthogonal drawings of complete ternary trees 
Let $T$ be an $n$-node complete ternary tree. 373769 Conway–Guttman asymptotics conjecture for occurrences of length-four patterns 
Let $S_{n,r}(q)$ denote the set of permutations of length $n$ with $r$ copies of the pattern $q$, and let $S_n(q)$ denote the set of permutations of length $n$ avoiding $q$. For a pattern $q$, write $|S_{n,r}(q)|$ and $|S_n(q)|$ for their cardinalities.

**Conway–Guttman conjecture.** For $q

|S_{n,r}(q)| \sim K|S_n(q)|.

For $q\in \{4123,3412,4231,4213,3142\}$, there is a constant $K$ such that

364593 The alternating-group derangement bound conjecture 
Let $G=A_n$ be a finite simple transitive permutation group with point stabiliser $H$, and let $\delta(G)$ denote the proportion of derangements in the action.

**Alternating-group derangement bound conjecture.** The following hold:

$$
\delta(G)\geqslant\frac{13}{45},
$$

with equality if and only if $G=A_8$ and $H={\rm AGL}_3(2)$; and, if $n\geqslant9$,

with equality if and only if $H=A_{n-1}$.

365487 Thouless-type spectral-measure inequality for the critical almost Mathieu operator 
Let $p$ and $q$ be coprime integers with $q$ odd, and let $\Sigma_{p/q}$ denote the periodic almost Mathieu spectrum. Let $C=0.915\ldots$ be Catalan's constant. **Thouless-type spectral-measure conjecture.** For $p\ne1,q-1$,

$$
\frac{32C}{\pi}>q\cdot|\Sigma_{p/q}|>q\cdot|\Sigma_{1/q}|>\sqrt{\frac{2}{q}}.

The inequalities refine the known asymptotic relation between the measure of rational-frequency spectra and rational approximation to an irrational frequency. They were verified computationally for $q\le95$, but are not established in general.

382036 Minimal-size conjecture for tight irreducible affine vector space partitions 
An **affine vector space partition** is a partition into affine subspaces. It is **tight** if the intersection of the linear parts of all its pieces contains only the zero vector, and **irreducible** if no proper subset of more than one part has a union that is a part. The size of a partition is its number of parts.

**Affine-vector-space minimal-size conjecture.** The minimal size of a tight irreducible affine vector space partition of length $n$ is

$$
\frac{3}{2}n-o(n).
$$

Computations in the paper give minimal sizes $4,6,7,8,10$ for lengths $3,4,5,6,7$, respectively. The stated asymptotic behavior is suggested by later constructions but remains open.
