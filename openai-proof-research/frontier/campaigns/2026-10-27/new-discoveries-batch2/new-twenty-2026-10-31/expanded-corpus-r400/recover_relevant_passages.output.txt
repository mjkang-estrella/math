                                                     In this paper, we introduce a two-player impartial game on graphs, called a

                                                 can be regarded as the undirected edge geography with an additional rule that
                                                 the first player who goes back to the starting vertex wins the game. We consider
                                                 the feedback game on Eulerian graphs since the game ends only by going back to
