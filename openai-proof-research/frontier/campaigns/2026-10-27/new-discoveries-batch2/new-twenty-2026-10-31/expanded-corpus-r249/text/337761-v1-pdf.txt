                               4. A partition result
   A non-trivial digraph G is said to be strongly <ℵ0 -connected if for every two
vertices v and w of G, there are infinitely many independent directed v − w paths,
and also infinitely many independent directed w − v paths (equivalently: after
deleting any finite number of vertices from G − {v, w}, the vertices v and w lie in
the same strongly connected component).
   In this section we gather two technical results. The first can be extracted from
the proof of [3, Theorem 1.3].
Lemma 7. Every countable strongly <ℵ0 -connected digraph can be covered by a
single one-way infinite directed path.                                    

give the complete proof.
