## Source scope objection O1: finiteness does not certify genericity

Page4 introduces Eq.(2.1) with u “sufficiently generic”. The subsequent ED-degree proofs actually use genericity, including excluding equal absolute coordinate values in the p5 argument. The witness u=(2,...,2) does not satisfy that auxiliary separation condition. This is not proof that it fails every possible precise ED-genericity condition: no canonical exceptional locus is specified or verified in the packet. It is proof that generic admissibility cannot be inferred merely from nonzero entries and finiteness.

There is substantial textual support for the broader finite-set reading: Section 2.3 explicitly changes to real data, discusses variation with location of u, and asks for a maximum finite real count; Theorem 2.13's proof explicitly takes u outside the cone with all entries nonzero, without restating genericity. Conjecture 2.14 follows that discussion and does not repeat a generic premise. The normalized record expressly adopts the finite-set reading. For acceptance as an unqualified resolution of the source conjecture, this remains **HOLD**. ## Independent mathematical audit

For each two-element subset S of eighteen indices, put x_i=-2 on S and 1 elsewhere. Then sum x_i^3=16-16=0, every coordinate satisfies x_i-2=-x_i^2, and hence every pairwise equation holds. The vector is nonzero and gradient entries are 3 or 12. Two different subsets differ in a coordinate and therefore give different actual critical points. There are binomial(18,2)=153, exceeding 2*17-1=33. These conclusions require no numerical root approximation.

For source-equation equivalence, let f=sum x_i^3 and D_u=sum(x_i-u_i)^2. At a nonzero real cone point, some x_j^2 is nonzero. The pairwise equations imply x_i-u_i=lambda*x_i^2 for all i by division using that j; conversely that proportionality implies every pairwise equation. Thus grad D_u=(2lambda/3)*grad f, exactly stationarity on the smooth hypersurface. At the displayed points lambda=-1. This is an ordinary differential-geometric justification; the Lean file proves the literal equations and does not formalize derivatives or a Lagrange-multiplier theorem. The normalized record defines its critical points by the equation, so no unproved differential bridge is needed for that equation endpoint. Smoothness is not nondegeneracy of a stationary point and implies no perturbation theorem.

Finiteness was checked for the *entire* real nonzero set. Set a=x_0. If a=0, the equation with index0 forces every coordinate zero, excluded. If a=2, it forces every coordinate2, contradicting the cubic sum144. Factoring the equation gives (x_i-a)*((a-2)*x_i-2a)=0, hence x_i is a or b=2a/(a-2). Let S={i:x_i=a}, k=|S|>=1. This also handles the potential coincident-branch case without assuming branches distinct. Multiplying the cone equation by (a-2)^3 and cancelling nonzero a^3 yields k*(a-2)^3+8*(18-k)=0. For fixed S, k>0 and real cubing is injective, so a is unique and every coordinate is unique. Thus the encoding into the finite powerset is injective. No assumption that the displayed 153 points exhaust the set is made, and no complex-set finiteness or exact cardinality is claimed.
