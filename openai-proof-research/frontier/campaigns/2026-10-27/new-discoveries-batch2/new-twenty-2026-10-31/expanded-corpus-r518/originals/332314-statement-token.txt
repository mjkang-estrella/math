"Let $G$ be a chordal graph with $n$ vertices. Call $G$ **4-chordal** if every edge of $G$ is contained in a clique with four vertices, and call a set of vertices $U$ a **clique transversal** if it meets every non-trivial maximal clique of $G$.\n\n**Optimal-size clique transversal conjecture.** Every $4$-chordal graph with $n$ vertices has a clique transversal of size at most $2n/7$.\n\nThe question generalizes known bounds for $2$- and $3$-chordal graphs. Andreae and Flotow constructed arbitrarily large examples requiring at least $2n/7-O(1)$ vertices, suggesting that the bound is asymptotically tight; the paper proves the stronger integral bound $2(n-1)/7$ for $n\\geq 5$."
