Let $G$ be a graph of order $n$, and let $W(G)$ denote the sum of distances between all unordered pairs of vertices. For positive integers $n$ and $r$ satisfying $n\ge 2r$ and $r\ge 3$, let $G_{n,r,s}$ be the graph defined in the paper by blowing up two consecutive vertices of the cycle $C_{2r}$ into cliques $K_s$ and $K_{n-2r+2-s}$. **Chen–Wu–An's conjecture.** For any graph $G$ of order $n$ with radius $r$, 
$$
W(G) \ge W(G_{n,r,1}).
$$
 Equality holds if and only if $G \cong G_{n,r,s}$ where 
$$
1 \le s \le \frac{n-2r+2}{2}.
$$
 This conjecture proposes the extremal graphs minimizing total distance for fixed order and radius; the paper studies and resolves the analogous question for sufficiently large $n$ relative to $r$.
