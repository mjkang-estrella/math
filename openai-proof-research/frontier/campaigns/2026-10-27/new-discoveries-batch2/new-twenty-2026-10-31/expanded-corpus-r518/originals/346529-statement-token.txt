"Fix an integer $d\\ge 2$. Let $\\alpha\\in\\mathbb R^d_{>0}$ be a Thoma sequence, let $\\lambda\\simeq \\alpha n$ be a TVK $\\alpha$-shape, and let $P_\\lambda$ be the associated Young-diagram poset. Let $\\delta(P_\\lambda)$ denote its sorting probability.\n\n**TVK-shape sorting-probability conjecture.** There is a universal constant $C>0$ such that\n\n$$\n\\delta(P_\\lambda)\\le \\frac{C}{n^{5/4}}.\n$$\n\nThe paper proves an $O(n^{-1/2})$ upper bound in this setting and suggests that it is not tight. The conjectured $n^{-5/4}$ rate remains open."
