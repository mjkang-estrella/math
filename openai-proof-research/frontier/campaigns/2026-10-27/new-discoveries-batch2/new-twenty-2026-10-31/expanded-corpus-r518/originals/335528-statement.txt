An oriented graph is **$r$-partite** if its vertex set can be partitioned into $r$ parts with no edges within a part; the same terminology applies to digraphs. **Typical structure conjecture for blow-up transitive tournaments.** The following hold:

(i) Almost all $T^t_{r+1}$-free oriented graphs are $r$-partite.

(ii) Almost all $T^t_{r+1}$-free digraphs are $r$-partite.

The conjecture proposes that the exact typical structures for $T^t_{r+1}$-free oriented graphs and digraphs agree with the corresponding structures for $T_{r+1}$-free graphs. The source states that these exact structures remain out of reach, so both assertions are open.
