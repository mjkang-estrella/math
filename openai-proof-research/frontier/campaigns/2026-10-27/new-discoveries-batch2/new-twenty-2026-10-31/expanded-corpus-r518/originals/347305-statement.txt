Let $m\ge4$ and let $n$ be sufficiently large. Suppose that $\mathcal{H}\subset\binom{[n]}{3}$ contains no non-trivial intersecting family of size $3m+1$. A **Steiner $(n,3,m-1)$-system** is a $3$-graph on $n$ vertices in which every pair of vertices is contained in exactly $m-1$ edges. **Mubayi–Verstraëte's conjecture.** Then

$$
|\mathcal{H}|\le\frac{m-1}{3}\binom{n}{2},
$$

with equality if and only if $\mathcal{H}$ is a Steiner $(n,3,m-1)$-system. The conjecture is refuted: the paper constructs, for the relevant parameters, a $3$-graph with more edges that still contains no non-trivial intersecting subgraph of size $3m+1$.
