"Let $\\alpha(G)$ denote the stability number of a graph $G$, let $\\omega(G)$ denote its clique number, and call $G$ **free of** a family of graphs if it contains none of them as an induced subgraph. Consider the family consisting of the six-vertex bipartite graphs with a perfect matching and their complements. **The bounded-deficiency conjecture.** There exists a constant $c$ such that every graph $G$ free of this family satisfies\n\n$$\n\\alpha(G)+\\omega(G)\\geq |V(G)|-c.\n$$\n\nThis conjecture asks whether excluding these natural obstructions forces the deficiency $|V(G)|-\\alpha(G)-\\omega(G)$ to be bounded independently of $G$; no resolution is given in the source."
