Here is an independent ordinary verification, using the source matching formula (Lemma 3.3). All seven vertex colors are a. Edge words are P=(u,v,v,u,u,v), Q=(u,u,v,u,v,v), with distinct formal colors u,v. Put U=u² and V=v². The determinant equals

  a⁷ − a⁵ M₁ + a³ M₂ − a M₃,

where M_j is the sum of products of squared edge labels over j pairwise nonincident edges. This follows by determinant permutation expansion: only fixed points and disjoint adjacent transpositions can occur in a path, and each transposition contributes the negative squared edge label.

For both words M₁=3U+3V. For P, the legal pairs of edge positions are (1,3),(1,4),(1,5),(1,6),(2,4),(2,5),(2,6),(3,5),(3,6),(4,6); their products are respectively UV,U²,U²,UV,UV,UV,V²,UV,V²,UV. For Q the products in the same position order are UV,U²,UV,UV,U²,UV,UV,V²,V²,UV. Thus both have M₂=2U²+6UV+2V². The only legal triples are (1,3,5),(1,3,6),(1,4,6),(2,4,6). Their products for P are U²V,UV²,U²V,UV² and for Q are UV²,UV²,U²V,U²V. Both have M₃=2U²V+2UV². These small symbolic checks are written ordinary calculations, not an executed enumeration.

Consequently the generic determinants are equal. The words differ at edge 2. The reflection of P is (v,u,u,v,v,u), differing from Q at edge 1. Since m=7 is odd, Theorem 3.6 cannot apply. Theorem 3.8 requires all odd edges in the first word to equal all even edges in the second, and conversely. In any reflection choice, the odd edge colors of the first word P or reverse(P) are mixed: (u,v,u) or (v,u,v). They therefore cannot equal every even edge of the other word. The exceptional alternative fails for all four independent-reflection choices, covering an interpretation wider than simultaneous reflection. This is a full source-faithful refutation, with no positivity restriction on generic matrices needed. If positive definiteness is desired, equalities are polynomial identities and diagonally dominant positive specializations form a nonempty allowed region; the refutation itself concerns generic color polynomials.

## 363751: six fundamental-module bases; spanning remains unpaid

Primary: Terwilliger, arXiv:2407.00551v1, Sections 9–10 and Conjecture 12.3. The sole listed arXiv version is June 30, 2024. Selected journal theorem/conjecture bodies were read; no claim of a complete line-by-line journal comparison. No independent correction identified in bounded searching.

Write V=C^X. Crucially, E_h^{*(1)} tests the distance between the SECOND and THIRD vertices: it is not an ordinary idempotent acting on tensor factor one. P_{h,i,j} is the indicator of ordered triples (x,y,z) with distances d(y,z)=h, d(z,x)=i, d(x,y)=j. E_h^{(1)} is the ordinary primitive idempotent on the first factor. Lambda is the unique irreducible module containing 1⊗1⊗1, equivalently T(1⊗1⊗1). Q_{h,i,j}=|X| sum_x E_h x⊗E_i x⊗E_j x. These distinctions must survive any future semantic endpoint; the corpus's informal description of both types as tensor-factor operators is insufficient.

Route A, orthogonality and irreducibility. Source Lemmas 9.10–9.12 show P_{0,i,i} lie in Lambda and are orthogonal. Their squared norms are |X| k_i>0, so all D+1 vectors are independent. Likewise Lemmas 9.15–9.17 give Q_{0,i,i} in Lambda with squared norms |X| m_i>0. Tensor permutation transfers independence to all six families. These facts prove containment and independence only. A spectral projector on an irreducible module may have higher rank; irreducibility alone does not show rank D+1. Neither the rank-one triple projection E_0^(1)E_0^(2)E_0^(3) nor cyclic generation controls a single projector's rank.

Route B, invariant coefficient functions. The P target space S consists of functions supported on y=z and constant on each d(x,y)=i. A complete proof would show E_0^{*(1)} Lambda is contained in S. Since Lambda=T1, a precise sufficient missing lemma is that for every word W in the six source generators, E_0^{*(1)} W1 has coefficients depending only on d(x,y) on triples (x,y,y). This is stronger than checking a few generators. Intermediate triples have three distinct vertices; adjacency excursions then involve triple-intersection data, not merely the ordinary intersection numbers. No proof that these excursions collapse uniformly was found. For the Q claim an additional independent compressed-algebra bound is needed; invoking a duality only available for translation schemes would change the universal scope.

Route C, automorphism-fixed tensors and the actual comparator. Source Proposition 10.4 gives Lambda contained in Fix(Aut Gamma). If automorphisms are transitive on ordered pairs at each distance, the diagonal restriction y=z has at most D+1 orbits, and Route A gives equality for that P space. Universal Q-polynomial distance-regular graphs need not have this symmetry, so this is only a helper. The source itself warns in Problem 12.5 against a universal subconstituent-algebra isomorphism, citing twisted Grassmann graphs. No exact counterexample to Conjecture 12.3 is supplied by that warning.

## 368826: internal diameter-two cover; two sustained repairs fail

Primary: Gyárfás–Sárközy, arXiv:2506.11696v1, Conjecture 2 and actual proof of Theorem 1/Lemma 5. The version page lists only June 13, 2025 v1. The journal body, Graphs and Combinatorics 42:43 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00373-026-03036-6, published March 25, 2026, was also read through the critical-pair proof. Its Lemma 5 writes X=N_2(x) minus {y}, Y=N_2(y) minus ({x,x'} union N_2(x)), clarifying the disjoint parts versus the v1 formula. This change does not make the whole theorem an induced-diameter result. The stored journal PDF was downloaded but not rendered or cognitively read; the actual journal HTML proof was read. Figure 1's partition is understood through the proof text; its image itself was not visually inspected.

Let v' be the unique nonneighbor of v. If a nonedge vv' is critical for red (no red length≤2 connection), blue stars centered at v,v' cover everything: every other vertex has a blue edge to at least one center. This closes that branch, with induced diameter≤2. If red- and blue-critical edges meet at x, the source proves the cover by two stars or by two monochromatic blown-up C5 subgraphs. The journal proof supplies all five nonempty parts and all consecutive complete bipartite edges. Such a subgraph has diameter 2, and adding same-color edges inside its vertex set cannot increase distances. Thus this branch also proves exactly the reserved internal-diameter conclusion, but it is a complete public lemma, not new credit.

The surviving case has disjoint endpoint supports P,Q of all red- and blue-critical pairs. The source sets A=V\P, B=V\Q. These cover V and are red/blue 2-reachable in the ambient graph. An ambient witness z for a pair in A may lie in P and hence outside A. Therefore this construction proves only the relaxed theorem.

Repair 1: absorb all omitted red witnesses into A and blue witnesses into B. For a new z∈P, being a witness for one pair a,a'∈A only gives two red incidences. It says nothing about its red distance to every other vertex of A. In fact z is by definition an endpoint of a red-critical pair. Absorption can introduce an explicitly forbidden pair if its partner is also absorbed, and no monotone closure argument preserves induced diameter. Allowing overlaps between A and B does not fix these within-set constraints. The unpaid lemma is a witness-selection rule whose absorbed set avoids every red-critical pair and still has internal witnesses for all its pairs, simultaneously in the other color.

Repair 2: choose two centers and cover by monochromatic stars. For any v, its red and blue closed stars cover all vertices except v'. Add v' to the red star if for EACH red leaf u there is a red length≤2 path v' to u inside that star enlarged by v'. The fact that v' has some red neighbor in that star guarantees the distance to v, but does not guarantee the distances to all leaves (via v would cost three). Swapping obstructing leaves to the blue star can create the analogous blue obstruction and has no established decreasing potential. The unpaid lemma is an exchange with preserved pairwise internal witnesses, rather than just center reachability.
