## 364596: Pell maximal divisibility indices

The source Conjecture 18 and preceding Pell recurrence, sum convention and following Theorem 19 proof were read. The source itself proves divisibility through S_(4k-1) = 2 P_(2k)^2 and S_(4k) = 2 P_(2k) P_(2k+1); divisibility alone does not prove maximality. An exact integer diagnostic tested every possible dividing positive index for sums n = 1 through 1000. Strict growth bounds the search, and no discrepancy appeared. The n = 0 zero-sum issue was deliberately not used as discovery credit.

Before further investment, the live registry path screen found `PellPartialSumMaxIndex.lean`. Its complete proof body and imported Pell definitions and coprimality proof were read. Its theorem `result` explicitly states all four `IsGreatest` clauses, with positive k for the two zero/negative-index boundary classes. The proof derives addition and norm identities, strong gcd identities, compresses a divisor of a product into gcd factors, bounds proper index divisors by half the index, and compares growth. Odd residue classes use coprimality of odd-index Pell numbers with every companion number. This is a direct full-scope prior bridge for the meaningful source conjecture. No new Pell candidate is offered. The registry source was not locally rebuilt; this lane does not certify its dependency environment or worldwide priority. Dated immutable URLs and exact downloaded hashes are retained.

## 365143: Laplacian sums and matching number

Conjecture 5.1, Theorem 1.5, its complete proof, and the following bipartite observation were read. Write epsilon_k = sum of the k largest Laplacian eigenvalues minus |E|. Theorem 1.5 proves epsilon_k <= k nu(G) + floor(k/2). Its odd-set cover partitions edges into a vertex-covered part of cover size r and odd components of maximum order at most 2 nu(G) - 2r + 1. The latter +1 produces the precise floor(k/2) loss. Merely imposing k <= n_nonisolated - 2 does not remove that +1 from the argument. A proof would need a sharper allocation of the spectral budget across the two edge parts or an additional structural inequality controlling the odd components.

The vertex-cover bound epsilon_k <= k tau(G), together with Konig's equality, handles bipartite graphs already. It is an explicit known special-case bridge, not a resolution of the nonbipartite claim. Complete odd cliques have gap [n(n-1) - k(n+1)]/2, which is at least 1 at k <= n-2, so the previously sharp k = n-1 obstruction does not give a counterexample within scope. Adding a disjoint edge to an odd clique increases the matching-number penalty and also does not transfer that obstruction. For K_(2a+1) plus K_(2b+1), using the top 2a eigenvalues of the larger clique gives excess a - b(2b+1) - 2ab, which is negative for b >= 1. These attempts motivate looking beyond unions of odd cliques.

A bounded random search on orders 4 through 12 evaluated 16,153 admissible samples, using exact maximum matching recursion and floating eigenvalues. The largest apparent excesses were rounding-scale values, not positive exact witnesses. These are diagnostic non-findings, not a theorem about all sampled or untested graphs. No full proof or exact counterexample survives. The remaining lemma is precisely the elimination of floor(k/2) within the literal range, or a certified nonbipartite spectral counterexample.
