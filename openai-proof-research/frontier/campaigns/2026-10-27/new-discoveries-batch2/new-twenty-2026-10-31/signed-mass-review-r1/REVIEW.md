## Source fidelity

I read the candidate's literal MathDB JSON, primary-source introduction and Section 10, and compared freshly fetched arXiv v1 and v2. The receipts retain both versions and extracted Section 10 text. The toy definitions, position-direction variations and conjecture agree substantively between versions. V1 has an extra word in the conjecture and a shorter electrostatics remark. V2 is dated 24 November 2025. The record's reference to no resolution as of April 2025 is not a current literature check.

In Section 10, masses are attached to particles, their squared absolute values sum to one, and the displayed variation (10.13) uses vectors in the product of position spaces. No mass tangent directions or minimization over the mass sphere are specified. This supports holding masses fixed during position variations. The notation m(x) alone does not establish joint minimization. The toy conjecture is associated with the eigenvalue problem, rather than asserted equivalent to it.

The introduction assumes 0 < s < 1 and uses R^n. Section 10 likewise uses R^n and d >= 2. I examined the introduction, preliminaries and complete Section 10, and searched the full text for dimension restrictions. I found no n >= 2 assumption governing the toy model. Geometric intuition about moving a middle component away in Remark 10.11 is not a written restriction and is precisely problematic on a line. The published 2026 version, whose body is also retained, continues the toy conjecture as Conjecture 10.9 with energy (10.7) and position variation (10.8). It does not supply a resolution in that section. This review does not refute the continuous eigenvalue conjecture.

Local minimum ordinarily means non-strict. Strict minima would already be ruled out by common translations, even without homogeneity. More decisively, the source's stationary-and-stable definition allows nonnegative, rather than positive, second variation. The candidate satisfies both an actual non-strict local minimum and those two variation conditions.

Equation (10.12) literally includes diagonal terms, which are undefined in the classical real-valued energy with nonzero masses. The formalization sets them to zero. This is an explicit interpretation, supported by the off-diagonal sums in (10.17) and the cross-interaction origin of the toy model. It is not a literal equality to an undefined classical diagonal expression. The counterexample survives omitting self-interactions and is not obtained by exploiting that defect. Injectivity in the formal conjecture similarly restricts attention to the natural collision-free domain. These repairs should be stated whenever describing the endpoint as a source refutation.

## Independent exact mathematics

Write r = sqrt(129). The masses (8,-1,8)/r are nonzero, their squares sum to one, and the positions (-1,0,1) are distinct. For u = x1-x0 > 0 and v = x2-x1 > 0, collecting the six ordered interactions yields

E = (32/129)(u^-2 + v^-2 - 8(u+v)^-2).

Clearing positive denominators gives exactly

u^-2 + v^-2 - 8(u+v)^-2 = (u-v)^2(u^2+4uv+v^2)/(u^2 v^2 (u+v)^2).

Thus E >= 0 throughout the open ordered chamber and E = 0 at u = v = 1. For example, coordinate perturbations smaller than 1/4 preserve order. This proves a local minimum in all three position coordinates, rather than merely along a selected family of motions. It is not a global minimum across all chambers: relabeling the spatial order with masses fixed can put the two positive particles adjacent and allow negative energy. No such relabeling is an arbitrarily small perturbation of this collision-free witness.

For a position velocity (a,b,c), put A = b-a and B = c-b. Along that line, E(t) = (16/43)(A-B)^2 t^2 + O(t^3). Consequently the first variation is zero and the symmetric second variation is (32/43)(2b-a-c)^2 >= 0. In position coordinates the Hessian is (32/43)ww^T, w = (-1,2,-1). Translation and dilation span its nullspace. The exact symbolic script independently checks the rational factorization and gap Hessian; the proof of positivity is the displayed positive-factor argument, not a numerical sample.

Root observation (i) is correct on the collision-free domain: for p = n+2s > 0, E(lambda x) = lambda^-p E(x) for lambda > 0. Differentiating at lambda = 1 shows any stationary point, hence any local minimum, has E = 0. This gives a necessary condition, not instability at zero energy.

Observation (ii) is correct, with an explicit descent path avoiding reliance on a Perron argument: keep positions fixed and replace masses by (8,-1+t,8)/sqrt(129-2t+t^2). Their energy is -64t/(129-2t+t^2), negative for every sufficiently small positive t. Hence the witness is not a joint local minimum on positions times the unit mass sphere. That is a different variational problem.

Observation (iii) needs an exponent qualification. If the exponent is artificially held at 2 while embedding in higher dimension, moving the middle point to (0,epsilon) gives (64/129)((1+epsilon^2)^-1 - 1) < 0. In the source, however, changing n while keeping s = 1/2 changes p to n+1. With the same masses the corresponding energy is (64/129)(1+epsilon^2)^(-p/2) - 256/(129*2^p). Its difference from epsilon = 0 is negative. For p > 2 even the initial energy is positive, so homogeneity also excludes stationarity. The proposed witness therefore does not extend to any n >= 2 in the source range. This does not invalidate a counterexample to the stated universal quantifier.

## Significance, objections and recommendation

The literal fixed-mass, dimension-unrestricted statement is refuted, subject to the stated diagonal convention. This is not merely a collision, zero-mass, normalization or strict/non-strict wording trick. Excluding dimension one would be a change to the written conjecture, rather than something demonstrated by the inspected source. A one-dimensional counterexample can be a genuine refutation while still falling below the campaign's significant-new-result standard.

Here there is a decisive reason for caution beyond proof length. For any p > 0, set q = 2^(p+1) and normalized masses proportional to (q,-1,q). In a one-dimensional ordered chamber their energy is

(4q/(2q^2+1))(u^-p + v^-p - 2^(p+1)(u+v)^-p).

The function z -> z^-p is strictly convex on the positive half-line: its second derivative is p(p+1)z^(-p-2) > 0. Jensen's two-point inequality makes the displayed expression nonnegative, with equality exactly when u=v. The chosen mass ratio is obtained directly by balancing the outer particle's two forces at equal gaps. The submitted p=2 witness is exactly this routine convexity construction. No new general convexity theorem, exceptional exponent mechanism or higher-dimensional stability phenomenon is proved. Under the explicit exclusion of routine corollaries/consequences, I recommend FAIL on significance, even though the refutation is mathematically correct. This is a reasoned campaign judgment for root, not a claim that an exact previously published duplicate was found.

Novelty verdict: HOLD, not cleared and not proved false. The Wu follow-up must be read in a lawfully accessible body before asserting a new-to-literature result. The advanced registry head also needs reconciliation beyond its current commit metadata. Overall recommendation: HOLD with zero credit; even if novelty were later cleared, the campaign significance objection remains.
