367091 Czabarka–Singgih–Székely diameter conjecture for graphs with bounded clique number 
 Let $k\geq3$, let $\delta\geq\left\lceil\frac{3k}{2}\right\rceil-1$, and let $G$ be a connected graph of order $n$ with minimum degree at least $\delta$. Write $\omega(G)$ for its clique number and $\chi(G)$ for its chromatic number.

**Czabarka–Singgih–Székely conjecture.** If $\omega(G)\leq k$, then

367107 VC-dimension and blowup threshold conjecture 
 Let $H$ be a graph. Let $\delta_{\textup{VC}}(H)$ be the infimum of those $\alpha$ such that every maximal $H$-free graph $G$ with minimum degree at least $\alpha n$ has bounded VC-dimension, and let $\delta_{\textup{B}}(H)$ denote the blowup threshold.

**VC-dimension–blowup threshold conjecture.** For any graph $H$,

$$
\delta_{\textup{VC}}(H)=\delta_{\textup{B}}(H).
$$

The paper notes that $\delta_{\textup{VC}}(H)\leq\delta_{\textup{B}}(H)$ always, while equality is left open; for odd cycles, it is specifically open whether $\delta_{\textup{VC}}(C_{2k-1})=\frac{1}{2k-1}$.
