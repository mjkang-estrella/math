Definition 2.1. A path graph Pn of order n and size n − 1 is a graph with vertex set
V (Pn ) = {v1 , v2 , . . . , vn } and edge set E(Pn ) = {v1 v2 , v2 v3 , . . . , vn−1 vn }. In addition, a cycle
graph Cn of order n and size n is obtained from path graph Pn with an additional edge v1 vn .
Definition 2.2. A star graph Sn of order n and size n − 1 is a graph with vertex set
V (Sn ) = {v1 , v2 , . . . , vn−1 } ∪ {x0 } and edge set E(Sn ) = {vi x0 : i = 1, 2, . . . , n − 1}, where
x0 is called a central vertex of Sn .
Definition 2.3. A wheel graph Wn of order n is obtained from a cycle graph Cn−1 such that
every vertex of Cn−1 is adjacent to a new vertex x0

                   E(G1 × G2 ) = {(v1 , u1 )(v2 , u2 ) : v1 v2 ∈ E(G1 ) and u1 u2 ∈ E(G2 )}.
