The search didn't turn up a source that applies the Weil bound to a sum of Legendre symbols of Fibonacci numbers over a Pisano period. The closest results are these:

- **Weil-type estimate for polynomial sums:** Grechnikov's paper treats sums of Legendre symbols of an odd-degree polynomial modulo a prime. It cites the Weil estimate, which bounds the absolute value of such a sum by (n−1)√p, and proves a sharper version. For the sum S of the Legendre symbols of a polynomial of odd degree n≥3 modulo primes p≥3, Weil's estimate |S|≤(n-1)√p and Korobov's estimate are well known. This concerns polynomial values, not Fibonacci terms, so it is only a general template.
- **Pisano periods and Legendre symbols:** An arXiv paper on Sophie Germain primes works with the Pisano period and the symbol (5/p). It notes that these numbers are sometimes called Fibonacci periods or Pisano periods, and it relates the Pisano period to the eigenvalue order when (5/q) = −1. That's background, not a character-sum bound.
- **Computed Legendre counts:** OEIS A395295 counts, for each odd prime p, how many Fibonacci residues over one period are residues versus non-residues versus zero, and reports the difference. This is a numerical sequence, with no bound attached.

For a real answer, the next step would be a search for Weil bounds applied to multiplicative character sums along linear recurrences, or for the Fibonacci Legendre-symbol sum over a full period, which may be exactly computable from the Binet form rather than needing Weil at all.
