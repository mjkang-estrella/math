Definition 2.6. [8] The (a, b)-Pisano period of a positive integer m, denoted by πm (a, b)
(where F0 = a and F1 = b are initial values), is the smallest positive integer k such that
Fn+k ≡ Fn (mod m) for all n.

Definition 2.7. Let p be an odd prime and let S = {0, 1, . . . , πp (a, b) − 1} where a and b
are the initial values. Suppose that Λpj (a, b) = {ξ ∈ S : (Fξ /p) = j} for j = −1, 0, 1. If
k = |Λp1 (a, b)| − |Λp−1 (a, b)| − |Λp0 (a, b)|, then p is called k-Pisano-Legendre prime relative to
(a, b) (abbreviated by k-PL prime relative to (a, b)). The Pisano set of (a, b), denoted by
χ(a, b) is the set of all integers k such that there exists an odd prime p for which p is a k-PL
prime relative to (a, b).
