The representative work uses the actual 6n half-edge face function. EndSame compares the two end-edge half-edges by fuel-bounded orbit reachability. EndInvariant says either b+2=n and the edge is single, or b=n. All 64 base assignments satisfy it by a kernel computation. appendSides and oldSides are proved inverse in the needed directions. EndEdgeSurgery records the exact missing split/merge identities: same side adds a face and preserves connectivity; opposite sides add a face if the old edge was single, otherwise remove a face, and the new edge is single. Assuming those identities, invariant_append and invariant_all prove the invariant for arbitrary n. Equal-side extensions preserve a witnessed genus. The base witnesses and induction then prove RepresentativeExistence and RepresentativeStep, explicitly conditional on EndEdgeSurgery. The surgery obligation is NOT proved. In particular this is not a closure of RepresentativeStep.

The subdiagram work constructs compress and compression for ANY pairing-closed finite endpoint set of cardinality 2k. The increasing order isomorphism from Fin (2k) transports the partner involution, and its strict monotonicity proves that Crosses is preserved and reflected. Compositions, identity subdiagrams and transport of triangles are also proved. A selection of k distinct lower chord endpoints defines an injective map Fin k × Bool to all 2k endpoints. The corresponding set is pairing-closed and has the exact cardinality. Thus any five selected chords compress to a literal Subdiagram without a reindexing hypothesis. FiveProperty forbids any selection of five chords in which every chord has a crossing neighbor. A five-vertex path is a special case; no assertion of inducedness is needed. This discharges the compression part of TriangleReduction, but not the existence of the odd-cycle window. OddCycleWindow is explicitly left as a Prop. reduction_of_window combines it with explicit BipartitePlanarity to prove TriangleReduction.

For planarity, innermost_monochromatic minimizes the span of a chord on a chosen side. An interior endpoint of that side either crosses the selected chord, or has its partner inside and produces a shorter monochromatic chord. Both alternatives contradict the hypotheses. removeChord and its Subdiagram are constructed by compression of the complement of the two endpoints. MonochromaticSplit states the missing refined +1 boundary-count formula for an innermost monochromatic chord. From it, boundary_of_bipartite proves b=n+2 by induction, transporting the assignment through the literal Subdiagram; planarity_of_split then supplies a genus-zero witness. The split formula is NOT proved and is not inferred from the weaker Deletion disjunction.

Together with the inherited n=3 case, the remaining arc argument can now be isolated at n≥6 as TriangleRigiditySix. No large-n pair, arc, adjacency or equivalence-transport proof is claimed.
