# Round 2B proof packet

This is a partial formalization, not acceptance. Root decides.

The first target is the exact frozen `Chord.FiveChordLemma`, with no model hypothesis. Its finite certificates operate directly on the defining 6n half-edge boundary count. `tableDiagram` builds each concrete involution with kernel proofs of involutivity and absence of fixed points. The matching enumeration pairs the least unpaired endpoint with each possible remaining endpoint, recursively. It has 9 × 7 × 5 × 3 × 1 = 945 leaves. Each `cases5_g` is a universal eliminator for its 15 diagrams: it proves an arbitrary predicate from proofs on those diagrams. It uses only partner equations, involutivity, absence of fixed points, finite endpoint elimination, and diagram extensionality. Thus completeness is a Lean theorem, independent of the Python enumeration generator.

Each aligned leaf certificate proves either `FiveProperty`, or an explicit side assignment of genus 3. In the latter case `MaxTwo` is contradicted. A positive certificate names an isolated chord and either rules out all triangles by kernel evaluation, or supplies a literal dihedral conjugation to `r3Block 5`. The finite diagram encoding is name free, so this enumerates labelled endpoint matchings without requiring a separate word renaming quotient. Certificates are split into 63 modules of 15 matchings. `fiveChordLemma` assembles the exact original Prop.

For four chords, the positive range certificates are the unchanged Round 1 theorems for `exception0` through `exception3`. `Orbit4` is a finite relation containing the 16 literal cyclic rotations/reflections. The proof `equivalent_orbit4` handles the reflexive, symmetric, transitive closure in `Equivalent`, rather than replacing that closure by a different equivalence. Finite closure and inverse tables for these 16 endpoint permutations are checked in the kernel. This permits a kernel proof of the four representatives' pairwise inequivalence. Completeness enumerates all 7 × 5 × 3 = 105 endpoint matchings. Each leaf either supplies a literal conjugation to one of the four representatives, or supplies a genus 0 assignment, contradicting `Range12`. Seven modules contain 15 leaves each.

The three-chord auxiliary result enumerates all 15 matchings: the only matching having three pairwise crossing chords is literally `r3Block 3`. It proves the n = 3 part of triangle rigidity. It does not establish the large-n arc argument.

Unfinished targets remain explicit Props. General representative existence still needs the end-edge transfer argument; `representativeExistence_of_step` supplies only the induction assembly from an explicit unproved `RepresentativeStep`. General bipartite planarity, shortest odd cycle reduction and n ≥ 5 triangle rigidity are not proved here. No theorem in the packet silently assumes a model Prop. `Chord.Comb2.conj34_intended` remains conditional on `RepresentativeExistence`, `Heredity`, `TriangleReduction` and `TriangleRigidity`. Its alternate version exposes `RepresentativeStep` and `TriangleRigidityLarge`. The four-chord endpoint is unconditional.
