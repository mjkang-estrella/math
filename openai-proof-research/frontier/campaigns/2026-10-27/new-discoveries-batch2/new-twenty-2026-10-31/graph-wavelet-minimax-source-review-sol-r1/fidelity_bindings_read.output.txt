324342 Let $p$ be a prime, and let ${\mathbb C}$ be a code of length $p+1$ constructed from a generator matrix that is a conference matrix. **Conference-matrix MDS conjecture.** The code ${\mathbb C}$ is an MDS code of dimension $\frac{p+1}{2}$ and minimum Hamming distance $\frac{p+3}{2}$. This would give an MDS code with parameters determined by the rank of a conference matrix over ${\mathbb Z}_p$; the source provides no resolution of the claim.
330264 Let $T$ and $T'$ be trees. Assume that they are simultaneously co-spectral, their complements are co-spectral, and their line graphs are co-spectral. **Geodesic-growth conjecture.** Then $T$ and $T'$ have the same geodesic growth, meaning that the associated right-angled Coxeter groups $G(T)$ and $G(T')$ have the same geodesic growth series. The conjecture asks whether these three spectral data determine geodesic growth for right-angled Coxeter groups based on trees; the paper establishes examples supporting the question but does not resolve the general implication.
331705 Let $G\subset\mathbb{R}^n$ be a domain that is starlike with respect to $x\in G$, and let $B_{c_G}(x,r)$ denote the Cassinian ball of radius $r$. **Starlikeness conjecture.** For every $r>0$, the ball $B_{c_G}(x,r)$ is starlike with respect to $x$. This is a proposed extension of convexity results to starlike domains, and the paper does not establish it.
332037 Let $A,a,B,b$ be integers with $A \geq B \geq 0$ and $a \geq b \geq 0$. For $m,n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\mathcal{U}^m_n$ denote the set of compositions of $n$ with $m$ non-negative parts. For compositions $\lambda$ and $\mu$, write $\lambda\mu$ for their concatenation, and write $\trianglerighteq$ for the dominance order induced by the non-increasing rearrangements. **Combinatorial injection conjecture.** There is an injection

$$
\gamma: \mathcal{U}^A_b \times \mathcal{U}^B_a \hookrightarrow \mathcal{U}^A_a \times \mathcal{U}^B_b
$$

such that, for every $(\lambda_1,\mu_1) \in \mathcal{U}^A_b \times \mathcal{U}^B_a$, if $(\lambda_2,\mu_2)=\gamma((\lambda_1,\mu_1))$, then

$$
\lambda_1\mu_1 \trianglerighteq \lambda_2\mu_2.
$$

This conjecture gives a combinatorial formulation implying the case $k=2$ of the paper's theorem relating total nonnegativity of the matrices $\mathcal{T}_p$ and $\mathcal{S}_p$.
332371 Let $k\geq 2$, and let $M$ be a matroid of rank $r$ on a ground set $E$ with $|E|=kr+1$. Suppose $x,y\in E$ are such that both $E\setminus\{x\}$ and $E\setminus\{y\}$ can be partitioned into $k$ pairwise disjoint bases.

**Common-basis conjecture.** There exist partitions of $E\setminus\{x\}$ and $E\setminus\{y\}$ into $k$ pairwise disjoint bases that share a common basis.

This is the second conjecture introduced as closely related to White's conjectures. It expresses a common-basis condition for two complementary decompositions and is not resolved in the supplied text.
333569 Let $(X,T)$ be the full two-shift, let $\alpha$ be the partition used to define information, and consider the space of one-step Markov measures on $X$. A measure is fully supported when every allowed cylinder has positive measure, and a local maximum is understood relative to this space. **Fully supported local-maximum conjecture.** There is a fully supported one-step Markov measure that is a local maximum of $\operatorname{Int}_\mu(X,T,\alpha)$ among all one-step Markov measures. The assertion is suggested by numerical computations in the source; no proof or resolution is supplied.
333709 Let $\Pi_q$ be a finite projective plane of order $q$, let $r$ be the infection rate, and let $t_r(A)$ denote the percolation time of a percolating set $A$. **Two-round minimal percolating set conjecture.** For each $r$ with

$$
4\leq r\leq q,
$$

there exists a minimal percolating set $A_r$ with

$$
t_r(A_r)=2.
$$

This asserts the existence of minimal percolating configurations that infect the entire plane in exactly two rounds throughout the stated range. The source gives a construction and proof around the conjecture, but the supplied parser status is unresolved.
334599 Let $\mathcal{L}_{\Rrightarrow^n}$ be the propositional language with the $n$-ary supervenience operator $\Rrightarrow^n$.

**Expressivity hierarchy conjecture.** For all $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $\mathcal{L}_{\Rrightarrow^{n+1}}$ is more expressive than $\mathcal{L}_{\Rrightarrow^n}$.

The paper establishes the corresponding result for $n=0$ and leaves the generalization to future work.
338258 Let $G_n(g,zg)$ be a singular io-decomposable Riordan graph, let ${\cal A}_n$ be its adjacency matrix, and let $X=[x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n]^T$ be an eigenvector with eigenvalue $0$. Define the odd-index and even-index subvectors by

$$
X_o=[x_1,x_3,\ldots,x_{2\lceil n/2\rceil-1}]^T,
$$

$$
X_e=[x_2,x_4,\ldots,x_{2\lfloor n/2\rfloor}]^T.
$$

**Odd-coordinate kernel conjecture.** Then

$$
X_o=O_{\lceil n/2\rceil}.
$$

This claim is motivated by Sage computations and, together with the preceding block-matrix lemma, would constrain the zero-eigenspace of every singular io-decomposable Riordan graph. No proof or resolution is supplied.
338887 Let $C_n$ be the bidirected cycle on $n$ vertices, and let $\mathcal{C}_{n,m,i}$ denote the unique recurrent component of level $m$ and weight $i\in\mathbb{Z}_n$. Write $r(\mathcal{C}_{n,m,i})$ for the number of recurrent configurations in this component.

**Recurrent-component cardinality conjecture.** For $n\geq 3$, $m\geq 1$, and $i,j\in\mathbb{Z}_n$, one has

$$
r(\mathcal{C}_{n,m,i})=r(\mathcal{C}_{n,m,j})
$$

whenever

$$
\gcd(n,m,i)=\gcd(n,m,j).
$$

The conjecture refines the observed near equality of recurrent-component sizes in sinkless rotor networks on bidirected cycles. It is supported by data for small values of $m$ and $n$, but the supplied source does not state a resolution.
339235 For $n\ge 2$, let $M_{2n}$ be the M{"o}bius ladder obtained from the cycle $C_{2n}=u_1u_2\cdots u_nv_1v_2\cdots v_nu_1$ by adding the edges $u_iv_i$ for $1\le i\le n$. Let $\chi_{la}$ denote the local antimagic chromatic number.

**M{"o}bius ladder conjecture.** For even $n\ge 4$,

$$
\chi_{la}(M_{2n})=4.
$$

The surrounding results establish the value for odd $n\ge 3$ and note that $M_4=K_4$ has local antimagic chromatic number $4$. The even-order case stated here is presented as a conjecture, with no resolution supplied in the text.
341850 Let $0\leqslant r\leqslant d$ be integers and let $n=\binom{d}{\leqslant r}$. Define

$$
\phi:\{0,1\}^d\to\{0,1\}^n,
\qquad \phi(x)=\left(\prod_{i\in S}x_i\right)_{S\subset[d]:|S|\leqslant r},
$$

and let ${\mathcal F}_{r,d}=\phi(\{0,1\}^d)$. A function $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\mathbb Z$ is **submodular** if $f(x)+f(y)\geqslant f(x\vee y)+f(x\wedge y)$ for all $x,y$.

**Submodular separation conjecture.** For every such $r,d$, there exists a submodular function $f$ such that $f(x)$ is computable in $\mathrm{poly}(n)$ time, $f(x)\leqslant-1$ for every $x\in\mathcal F_{r,d}$, and $f(x)\geqslant0$ for every $x\notin\mathcal F_{r,d}$. If true, this would yield a hardness result for submodular minimization with modular constraints, complementing the known prime-power algorithms; the source does not resolve the conjecture.
342364 Let $c_m(n)$ denote the $m$-colored binary partition function, and let $
u_2$ be the 2-adic valuation. For positive integers $k,n$ and nonnegative integers $k$ as specified below, consider the differences of the values of $c_m$. **2-adic valuation conjecture.** For $kin\mathbb{N}_{+}$ and each $n\in\mathbb{N}_{+}$, 
$$
\nu_{2}(c_{2k}(4n)-c_{2k}(n))=\nu_{2}(n)+2\nu_{2}(k)+3.
$$
 Moreover, for $k\in\mathbb{N}$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}_{+}$, 
$$
\nu_{2}(c_{4k+1}(4n)-c_{4k+1}(n))\geq\nu_{2}(n)+3,
$$
 
$$
\nu_{2}(c_{4k+3}(4n)-c_{4k+3}(n))\geq\nu_{2}(n)+6.
$$
 In each case, equality holds for infinitely many $n\in\mathbb{N}$. These predictions are motivated by known sharp congruences for the ordinary binary partition function and numerical computations for $m\leq10$ and $n\leq10^5$; their general validity remains open.
342414 Let $P$ be a $d$-regular edge-rooted pattern of depth $r$ and girth $2r+2$. For each edge-rooted pattern, let $\mathbf{e}(P)$ denote its constraint vector, and let $\mathcal{T}'_r(d)$ be the specified family of $d$-regular edge-rooted trees of depth $r$. The constraint $\mathbf{e}(P)$ is **weaker** than a convex combination of constraints from this family in the sense that there exist $T_1,\dots,T_m\in\mathcal{T}'_r(d)$ and $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_m\in[0,1]$ with

$$
\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i=1
$$

and, for every $\bm{\alpha}\in{\left(\mathbb{Q}^+\right)}^{r+1}$,

$$
\bm{\alpha}^{\!\top}\left(\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i\mathbf{e}(T_i)\right)\ge 1\quad\implies\quad\bm{\alpha}^{\!\top}\mathbf{e}(P)\ge 1.
$$

This would explain why the bounds obtained from edge-rooted trees remain valid at girth $2r+2$, and would generalize the cases checked for $r=2$ and $d\in\{3,4\}$.
343536 Let $P_m\oblong P_n$ be the rectangular grid graph, and let its automorphism group act naturally on its vertices. An equitable partition is a partition in which every vertex in a cell has the same number of neighbors in each cell.

**Grid-graph equitable-partition conjecture.** The equitable partitions of $P_m\oblong P_n$ are the orbit partitions of natural actions of the subgroups of the automorphism group of the graph. The lattice of equitable partitions is isomorphic to the lattice of subgroups exactly when there is a vertex with trivial stabilizer subgroup. This happens unless $2\le m=n\le3$.

The claim concerns the relationship between equitable partitions and symmetry-induced orbit partitions for rectangular grid graphs. The supplied text reports this as an apparent pattern based on computations, but gives no resolution or proof.
343790 Let $L$ be a code loop, and let $V_{1}$ and $V_{2}$ be representations of $L$. The **degree-and-type uniqueness conjecture.** If $V_{1}$ and $V_{2}$ have the same degree and type, then they are isomorphic. The claim is motivated by the fact that, for code loops of ranks 3 and 4, the type determines the loop up to isomorphism; whether the analogous uniqueness holds in general is left open.
345545 Let $S_n$ be the symmetric group, let $s:S_n\to S_n$ be the stack-sorting map, and let $\deg(s:S_n\to S_n)$ denote its maximum fiber size. The paper considers the exponential growth rate

$$
\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\deg(s:S_n\to S_n)^{1/n}.
$$

**Stack-sorting growth conjecture.** The value of this limit lies in the interval $(1.68,1.73)$.

The interval is suggested by computations on random permutations for $n=100$ and $n=300$. The problem of obtaining improved asymptotic estimates, or exact formulas, remains open.
346167 Let $G$ and $\overline{G}$ be nontrivial connected graphs of order $n\geq 8$. Write $rvd(G)$ for the rainbow vertex-disconnection number of $G$. **Rainbow vertex-disconnection Nordhaus–Gaddum conjecture.**

$$
rvd(G)+rvd(\overline{G})\geq n.
$$

This conjecture proposes an improvement of the established lower bound for the sum of the rainbow vertex-disconnection numbers of a graph and its complement. The paper notes that the bound holds when $rvd(G)=1$, but does not establish it for all nontrivial connected graphs of order at least $8$.
