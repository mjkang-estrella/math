The Thue list number of the infinite path is 3 3 . We provide a formulation of this conjecture in terms of combinatorics on words. By an alphabet , we mean any finite set. Given a sequence ( 𝒜 i ) i ≥ 1 (\mathcal{A}_{i})_{i\geq 1} of alphabets, we say that a word w = w 1 ​ … ​ w n w=w_{1}\ldots w_{n} respects ( 𝒜 i ) i ≥ 1 (\mathcal{A}_{i})_{i\geq 1} , if for all i i , w i ∈ 𝒜 i w_{i}\in\mathcal{A}_{i} (the definition naturally extends to infinite words).
