# Finite search encodings

All scripts are diagnostics; only the Lean packet proves a general statement.

Subtraction: state bit x−1 is the output at offset −x. The update is one iff some allowed predecessor is zero. Full states are followed to their first repetition, giving a certified eventual period; the output preperiod is pulled backward while equality at distance p holds. Extension b is checked by Proposition 2.8: no n≥0 may have w(n)=w(n−b)=0. Values before the seed are one. For fixed k and b=kp+x it suffices to check n<b+mu+p, since beyond that both arguments are in periodic tails. Once b−alpha≥mu, all comparisons involving negative seed indices occur against the periodic tail with a residue independent of k; the earlier n values see predecessor one. Hence the k loop through floor((mu+alpha)/p)+2 suffices, and our script uses that inclusive bound. This is a diagnostic justification, not a Lean-certified extension bridge. All seeds are enumerated, so bit order is immaterial to coverage.

Cochordality: complement graphs are tested by simplicial-vertex elimination. For each co-chordal G and r≥2, the complement clutter consists of disconnected (r+1)-subsets. A maximal subcircuit has r vertices, belongs to some circuit, and is simplicial iff every (r+1)-subset of its closed neighborhood is a current circuit. All possible deletions are explored recursively, with memoized failed circuit sets; this is not a greedy-only failure claim. r=1 reduces to the input chordality test; r≥n−1 is trivial and omitted. n=7 is a partial labeled edge-mask prefix, not an isomorphism-complete enumeration.

Domination: enumerate every labeled connected graph with at least n edges, every direction of every edge, retain only strictly positive indegrees. For each valid orientation enumerate subsets in increasing size and test incoming domination at every vertex. Sizes below three are impossible in loopless orientations without digons. Record its actual minimum; a missing integer between the spectrum extrema would be a counterexample.

Pebbling: root configurations with root count positive are already solvable. In the remaining configurations the pendant root can first be reached iff its cube neighbor can acquire two pebbles. The recursive solver enumerates all cube moves; every move reduces total by one, so recursion terminates. Weights use lcm(1,...,n) to avoid floating-point arithmetic; the bound is the exact all-ones weight. Only overweight configurations require a solvability check. The total caps do not establish validity for arbitrary configurations.

Upcycle: every four-letter cyclic window has exactly one wildcard. Wildcard positions must repeat every four positions; rotation puts them at 0,4 in a length-eight cycle. Enumerate all 2^6 binary assignments to the remaining positions; expand every window's wildcard both ways and check that all 16 binary four-words appear exactly once. This recovers known examples only.
