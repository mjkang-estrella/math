Theorem 3.6. Let 2 ≤ m ≤ n and let k be any integer between 4 and m + 2. Then
there exists an orienting mapping f of Km,n such that γt ((Km,n )f ) = k.

Proof. Let G = Km,n and write V (G) = X ∪ Y where X = {x1 , . . . , xm } and
Y = {y1 , . . . , yn } are the partite sets of G. If k ∈ {4, m + 2}, the result follows
from Theorem 3.4 or Theorem 3.5. Thus, let 5 ≤ k ≤ m + 1. Let Gf be the
orientation of G with arcs (yi , xi ) ∈ A(Gf ) for all i ∈ [k − 3], (yk−2 , xi ) ∈ A(Gf ) for
all i ∈ {k − 2, k − 1, . . . , m} and with all other edges directed from X to Y . Let A
be a γt -set of Gf . Since each xi has in-degree 1, {y1 , . . . , yk−2} ⊆ A. In addition, to
dominate Y we need at least two vertices from X since the out-degree of each xi is
n − 1. Thus, |A| ≥ k. Since {x1 , x2 , y1 , . . . , yk−2 } is a total dominating set of Gf ,
γt (Gf ) = k.
