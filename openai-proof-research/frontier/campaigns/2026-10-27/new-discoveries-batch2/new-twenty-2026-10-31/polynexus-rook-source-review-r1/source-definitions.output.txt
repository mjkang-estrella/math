12 Summary
In this paper, we defined a noncommutative, inverse monoid Mn ; showed that
the nonzero elements of Mn can be represented as hd, k, mi, where d, k, and m
are properly defined integers; and used this “triplet representation” to study
numerous properties of Mn . Both Mn and the set Sn = Mn \ {1} (where
1 is the monoid identity, corresponding to the n × n identity matrix) are
