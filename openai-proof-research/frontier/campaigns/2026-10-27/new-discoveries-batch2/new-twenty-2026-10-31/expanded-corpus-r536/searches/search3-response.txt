The search turned up the core picture: Anderson et al. set the bounds, conjectured 1/13 is the true asymptotic density, and Bozeman et al. pushed the lower bound closer to it.

- **Anderson et al. (upper bound and conjecture):** Their theorem gives an upper bound of 1/13 on the normalized porous exponential domination number of C_m × C_n as m and n grow, and they conjectured that 1/13 is also the asymptotic lower bound (proved that mn/15.875 ≤ γ*e(Cm × Cn) ≤ mn/13 and conjectured that mn/13 is also the asymptotic lower bound).

- **Bozeman et al. (arXiv:1803.01933):** They use linear programming to sharpen the lower bound to mn/(13.7619+ε(m,n)). This is a real improvement over Anderson et al.'s 1/15.875 bound, but it still does not reach the conjectured 1/13. Their keywords include linear programming and mixed integer programming.

- **Discrepancy:** The ar5iv HTML version of the same paper quotes an earlier general bound of 1/18 from Anderson et al., which conflicts with the 1/15.875 figure in the PDF abstract.
