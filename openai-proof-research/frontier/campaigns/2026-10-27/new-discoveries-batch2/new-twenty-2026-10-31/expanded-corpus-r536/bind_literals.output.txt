Let $F$ be a graph with $\operatorname{ex}(n,F)=\Theta(n^\alpha)$ for some $\alpha\in(1,2]$. Define the 2-density of a graph $F$ by

$$

$$
\operatorname{ex}(G_{n,p},F)=\begin{cases}\max\{\Theta(p^{\alpha-1}n^\alpha),n^{2-1/m_2(F)}(\log n)^{O(1)}\} & p\geq n^{-1/m_2(F)},\\ (1+o(1))p{n\choose 2} & n^{-1/m_2(F)}\gg p\gg n^{-2}.

$$

$$
\operatorname{ex}(G_{n,p},F)=\begin{cases}\Theta(p^{\alpha-1}n^\alpha) & p\geq n^{\frac{2-\alpha-1/m_2(F)}{\alpha-1}}(\log n)^{O(1)},\\ n^{2-1/m_2(F)}(\log n)^{O(1)} & n^{\frac{2-\alpha-1/m_2(F)}{\alpha-1}}(\log n)^{O(1)}\geq p\geq n^{-1/m_2(F)},\\ (1+o(1))p{n\choose 2} & n^{-1/m_2(F)}\gg p\gg n^{-2}.

This conjecture predicts two phase transitions for the random Turán problem for bipartite graphs: one near $p=n^{-1/m_2(F)}$ and another near $p=n^{(2-\alpha-1/m_2(F))/(\alpha-1)}$, up to polylogarithmic factors.
