2.4. A partitioned form of the Mecke formula
For the next step, we need a power of a Poisson integral multiplied by a function of the
same process. We record the following standard consequence of the multivariate Mecke
equation [22, Theorem 4.4], recalled in Lemma 2.6. We obtain it by grouping the factors
in the power according to set partitions. We include the complete reduction to specify the
treatment of repeated locations and the absence of any additional factorial.
