Three induced colours on the Möbius ladder M_12
DRAFT — AI-assisted research manuscript — 5 November 2026
This draft was prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance in source screening, computation, proof exposition and
document production. No human authorship or affiliation is asserted. It has not been accepted by the campaign root or
submitted for publication. No priority claim is made.

Abstract
An explicit local antimagic edge labelling of the Möbius ladder M_12 induces exactly three vertex colours. An odd
cycle supplies the matching lower bound, so χ_la(M_12) = 3. This refutes Conjecture 3.2 of Lau, Shiu and Ng in
arXiv:1805.04888, which asserts value four for every even parameter n ≥ 4 in M_2n. A separate labelling of M_8 also
contradicts Theorem 2.4 of Lau and Shiu's arXiv:2203.06594; its proof uses an incorrect sum of induced colours. We
distinguish the elementary proof of exact value three from the supplied Lean certificate, whose endpoint negates the
universal conjecture. A bounded prior-art screen found no earlier valid determination for an even parameter in the
conjecture's range, subject to stated access and scope limits.

1. Definitions and the source question
All graphs here are finite, simple and undirected. Write E(v) for the edges incident with v. The source introduction [1]
defines a local antimagic edge labelling as follows (quoted with mathematical typography normalized): “a bijection f :
E → {1, …, |E|} such that the induced vertex labeling f^+ : V → Z given by f^+(u) = ∑ f(e) (with e ranging over all
the edges incident to u) has the property that any two adjacent vertices have distinct induced vertex labels”. It calls
f^+(u) the “induced color of u under f” and defines the colour number c(f) as the number of distinct induced colours.
Thus
χ_la(G) = min{c(f) : f is a local antimagic labeling of G}.
Immediately before its odd-parameter theorem, [1] defines the ladder (same typography convention): “For n ≥ 2, let
M_2n be the Mobiüs ladder obtained from C_2n = u_1 u_2 ··· u_n v_1 v_2 ··· v_n u_1 by adding the edges u_i v_i, 1
≤ i ≤ n.” We use vertices 0, …, 2n−1 and edges (i, i+1 mod 2n), followed by (i, i+n) for 0 ≤ i < n. For n ≥ 2 this lists
each of its 3n edges once.
The two source statements, verbatim apart from typography, are:
“Theorem 3.11. For odd n ≥ 3, χ_la(M_2n) = 3.”
“Conjecture 3.2. For even n ≥ 4, χ_la(M_2n) = 4.”
These are the arXiv numbering and hypotheses. The journal is Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory 41 (2021),
133–152, DOI 10.7151/dmgt.2177. The review's public indexing evidence identifies the corresponding journal
conjecture as Conjecture 3.14 and the odd theorem as Theorem 3.12; a complete journal-version comparison was
unavailable. MathDB record 339235 reproduces the even-n conjecture. Its title's “even-order” does not enlarge the
mathematical scope: all these ladders have even order, whereas the conjecture restricts n itself to be even.

To check adjacency without relying on the tables' appearance, read the sums along the cycle:

Every consecutive pair is unequal, including the last-first pair (25,29). On the antipodal edges the pairs are (29,31),

proving χ_la(M_12) ≤ 3.
For the opposite bound, vertices 0,1,2,3,4,5,6 followed by the edge (6,0) form a cycle of length seven. A graph with an
odd cycle cannot be coloured with two colours: following the cycle forces alternating colours and makes the closing
edge monochromatic. Since any local antimagic labelling induces a proper vertex colouring, χ_la(M_12) ≥ χ(M_12) ≥
3. Combining the bounds proves the theorem. The parameter six is even and at least four, so its value three contradicts
the conjectured value four. □
As an independent aggregate check, the twelve sums total 342, which equals twice the sum 1+···+18. This identity
alone would not prove propriety or bijectivity; those were checked separately above. The same lower-bound argument
works for every even n ≥ 4: the path 0,1,…,n and the chord (n,0) form an odd cycle of length n+1. No general upper
bound of three for all such n is proved here.

The labels form a permutation of 1, …, 12. Consecutive cycle sums are unequal, including the closing pair (15,22).
The antipodal sum pairs are (22,20), (20,22), (15,20), (22,15). Its three colour classes have sums 15, 20 and 22, of
sizes two, three and three respectively. The cycle 0,1,2,3,4,0 has length five, so the lower bound is again three. Thus
χ_la(M_8) = 3.
The proof of [2, Theorem 2.4] prints the line
“2x + 3y + 3z = 2 × (sum of all labels) = 132”.
For a cubic graph on eight vertices there are twelve edges. Since each edge label is counted at both endpoints, the total
must instead be 2(1+···+12) = 156. Our colour classes give 2·15 + 3·20 + 3·22 = 156. The erroneous total enters the
case restrictions in that proof; the theorem itself is false, not merely its displayed calculation. This remark is separate
from the main M_12 result, which lies outside the order-eight theorem. It is not offered as an independent priority
claim.
Computations. All witness checks in this draft were freshly recomputed by scripts/verify.py using integer arithmetic.
The program constructs the edge sets independently of the Lean packet, checks their simplicity, checks the label
permutations, recomputes every vertex sum and tests every edge. Its JSON output also records all the incident labels
and the aggregate checks.
For context, we performed fresh bounded heuristic searches at n = 8 and n = 10. For each graph a deterministic greedy
proper three-colouring was fixed. A seeded label-swap heuristic minimized the sum of squared differences between
vertex sums within each fixed colour class. Each search used 20,000 proposals, and neither found a witness. This is
not an exhaustive search over labels or colourings; it says nothing about nonexistence. Fixing a colouring also limits
the search space independently of the proposal bound. The script and seeds are preserved, and no SAT or
integer-programming solver was used.
The earlier discovery and independent review likewise record bounded larger-parameter searches, preserved as
provenance rather than mathematical evidence. Neither those unsuccessful runs nor the fresh runs justify extending the
theorem to all even parameters. The proof here consists of the two explicit witnesses and the odd-cycle lower bound,
not of search statistics.

4. Formal correspondence and reproducibility
The supplied packet uses Lean with Init only, rather than a generic graph library. In namespace Mobius, edge n i lists
the 2n cycle edges first and then n antipodal edges; edgeIndices n is the list of indices below 3n. vertexSum n labels v
filters that list to edges incident with v and adds their labels. colorCount takes the length after duplicate sums are
removed from the list of sums at all vertices below 2n.
LocalAntimagic n labels requires both a permutation of the integers from 1 to 3n and unequal sums at the two
endpoints of every enumerated edge. The permutation clause enforces exact list length; default out-of-range list
indexing cannot contribute extra labels to a successful witness. The ordered edge representation serves only to
enumerate undirected edges.
LocalAntimagicChromaticNumberEq n k is an attained-minimum relation: a valid labelling with k colours exists, and
every valid labelling has at least k colours. This expresses equality of the minimum to k, rather than mere existence of
a k-colour labelling. For the particular graph used here, simple_edges_nodup and no_loops_and_in_range certify
simple, in-range edges, while adjacency_literal checks every pair against the literal cycle-plus-antipodes adjacency
condition. These bridge declarations concern the counterexample graph; no uniform general-family simplicity theorem
is claimed.
The declarations witness_valid, witness_colors and witness_sums certify the displayed M_12 assignment.
number_not_four applies the lower-bound clause of a hypothetical minimum-four assertion to that witness and derives
the impossible inequality 4 ≤ 3. The final endpoint is
Mobius.conjecture_false : ¬ (∀ n : Nat, 4 ≤ n → n % 2 = 0 → Mobius.LocalAntimagicChromaticNumberEq n 4).
It specializes the universal assertion to n = 6. The Lean packet therefore proves the literal negation needed for the
MathDB refutation. It does not prove the exact value three: the non-bipartiteness lower bound in Section 2 is an
ordinary mathematical proof, not a theorem of this packet. The M_8 remark is also checked by the fresh arithmetic
script and proved above, rather than certified by this endpoint.

5. Related work, limits and significance
Arumugam, Premalatha, Bača and Semaničová-Fenovčíková [3] introduced the local antimagic chromatic parameter
and studied basic graph families. Haslegrave [4] proved the existence of local antimagic labellings for connected
graphs other than the single edge. Existence alone does not bound the number of induced colours by three. Lau, Shiu
and Ng [1] settled the odd-parameter ladders at three and proposed the even-parameter assertion quoted above. Its
odd-parameter formula is not a labelling formula for even n: its half-integer expressions and parity ranges require the
stated odd hypothesis.
Later work must be distinguished by hypotheses and invariant. The circulant constructions in arXiv:2009.01996 use
unit generators strictly below half the order; the antipodal generator of the present cubic ladder is not covered. The
lexicographic constructions of Lau and Shiu, arXiv:2203.16359, impose even-degree hypotheses that fail here. The
later note arXiv:2208.14707 uses equal numbers of even and odd incident labels in its auxiliary graph, again forcing
even degree there. General constructions do not establish a three-colour labelling of this ladder merely by advertising
small colour numbers.
The completed bounded screen records all 31 OpenAlex citing records returned in the review's snapshot, with
body-based scope checks for the plausible remaining edge-labelling constructions and explicit exclusions for different
invariants. HTML arXiv author listings supplied further later work. Among the additional bodies examined were
arXiv:2311.15520 (even-degree LAU constructions), arXiv:2404.18049 (matrix constructions retaining degree-two or
higher-degree vertices), arXiv:2410.17674 (matching-null joins and splits), and arXiv:2610.14486 (middle graphs and
direct products). None was found to determine an even ladder in the conjecture's range or to print its three-colour
labelling. Details and receipts are in prior-art-completion.json; this conclusion is bounded source screening, not a
proof of novelty.
Gallian's survey [5], specifically its 28th edition, summarizes local antimagic results, including small regular graphs
and even-degree constructions. Its silence on this counterexample cannot exclude an unindexed or unnoticed result.
The mathematical body of the purported workshop slides from 2020 remained unread behind the available access
interface. The complete journal body and correction history of [1] also remained inaccessible through the verified
routes used. The journal indexing retains the conjecture, and the later paper [2] still asserts four at order eight; this is
retention evidence, not a substitute for those missing bodies. The root's preserved registry refresh is a clean bounded
path/index screen, not an exhaustive proof-content search.
The contribution is modest and concrete: one explicit finite counterexample refutes a universal assertion. It supplies
neither a classification of even ladders nor a new general construction. No claim of first discovery, publication priority
or campaign acceptance is made. Independent source comparison and root adjudication remain separate from
preparing this draft.
