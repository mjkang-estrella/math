3.4 The payoff. Conditionally on (a,b), we have U - V = (1-Z)(X-Y), hence
g(a,b) := E[U-V | a,b] = (1/2)(2ˆ(-b) - 2ˆ(-a)).
The function g is antisymmetric, g(b,a) = -g(a,b); it vanishes on the diagonal and is nonnegative above it, because a
> b gives 2ˆ(-a) < 2ˆ(-b). For any law µ with ∆µ(a,b) ≥ 0 for a > b,
E_{µ}[U-V] = Σ_s µ(s) g(s) = Σ_{a>b} ∆µ(a,b) g(a,b) ≥ 0,
by pairing s with ρ s and using g(ρ s) = -g(s) and g = 0 on the diagonal.
