What r119 established:
- Reduction. By Bong, Carlson, Curtis, Haas and Hogben (Graphs Combin. 2023), cited as Theorem t:main of the survey arXiv:2406.05509, a PD-TAR isomorphism forces the same power-dominating families after relabelling. Uniqueness of PD-TAR(K_{s,t}) is therefore equivalent to this statement: for s, t >= 3 and (s,t) != (3,3), K_{A,B} is the only graph on A ⊔ B without isolated vertices whose PD sets are exactly those of K_{A,B}.
- SAT. This core statement is UNSAT for every s + t <= 11 and for (3,8), (3,9) and (4,7). The controls (2,3) and (2,4) have other solutions, and (3,3) has only relabellings.
- Blocker step. The inclusion-minimal sets N[F], for F a fort, are exactly B ∪ {x,y} and A ∪ {u,v}.
- Partial proof. If every minimal fort Psi_xy with N[Psi_xy] = B ∪ {x,y} equals {x,y}, then H = K_{s,t}.
- Missing lemma. The mutual-poverty configuration is impossible: some q in B with N_A(q) ⊆ {x,y} inside a minimal Psi_xy, together with poor A-vertices for every Phi_qb'.
