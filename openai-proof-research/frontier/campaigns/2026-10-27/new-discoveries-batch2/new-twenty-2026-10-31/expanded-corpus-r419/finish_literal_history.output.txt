334115 Let $K_{(n)}$ be the complete $k$-partite graph with $N=n_1+dots+n_k$ vertices, whose $k$ color classes have sizes $n_1,dots,n_k$. For odd $r$, let $M_r(K_{(n)})$ denote its matching polynomial, and let $\Delta(r+1; a)$ denote the parameter list associated with the step $r+1$. **The complete multipartite matching-polynomial conjecture.** For odd $r$, the matching polynomials are given by

$$
M_r(K_{(n)}) = x^N \,{}_{r+1}F_{0} \left[\begin{matrix} \Delta(r+1; -n_1)\ldots \Delta(r+1; -n_k) \\ - \end{matrix}; \frac{-(r+1)^{r+1}}{(2x)^{r+1}}\right].
$$

This is suggested by the established formulas for complete graphs and complete bipartite graphs, but the source does not provide a proof or resolution for the complete $k$-partite case.
334187 Let $G\subset[n]\times[n]$ be labelled with points from $[n]$, and suppose the labelling satisfies Conditions 2 and 4, where Condition 4 is the upper/lower-completion disjointness condition described in the paper.

**Condition 4 power-bound conjecture.** There exists $\epsilon>0$ such that every such labelling has at most

$$
n^{2-\epsilon}
$$

labelled cells.

This is a weakening of the $[3,2]$ problem. The paper notes that the natural Behrend-based construction does not approach this setting, so a non-trivial power bound appears plausible, but no resolution is supplied.
334206 Let $q$ be a prime power, let $b\ge 2$, and let $n$ satisfy

$$
2b+1\le n\le \frac{q^{b+1}-1}{q-1}.
$$

A linear MDS $(n,2b+1)_q$ $b$-symbol code should exist.

This conjecture would provide a general inductive construction of linear MDS $b$-symbol codes by ordering points in $PG(b,q)$ so that no $b+1$ cyclically consecutive points lie in a projective $(b-1)$-space. The source presents the claim as an appealing consequence of the proposed ordering strategy and notes that further constructions support it, but gives no proof or resolution.
