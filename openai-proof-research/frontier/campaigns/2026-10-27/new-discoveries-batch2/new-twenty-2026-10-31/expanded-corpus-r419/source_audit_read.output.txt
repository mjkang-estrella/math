874:      We remark that Conjecture 1 claims that conditoning a graph will reduce the
875: average effective resistance between points; it is not true that the resistance between
876: all points will be reduced. If the weight on edge (i, j) is reduced, then its effective
877: resistance between points i and j is increased. Since we impose that the trace of the
878: Laplacians be preserved, if any edge weights are increased, then by conservation at
879: least one other edge’s weight must be decreased. The vertex pairs for those edges
880: will then have an increased effective resistance between them.
