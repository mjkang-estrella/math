## 353590 Krenn–Gu conjecture on the matching index
Let $G$ be a finite undirected graph with an even number of vertices. For an edge-colouring $c$, let $\mu(G,c)$ be the dimension achieved by a perfectly monochromatic edge-coloured graph, and let $\mu(G)$ be the maximum of $\mu(G,c)$ over all such colourings.

**Krenn–Gu conjecture.** If $G$ is non-isomorphic to $K_4$, then

$$
\mu(G)\leq 2
$$

and

$$
\mu(K_4)=3.
$$

The conjecture would show that $K_4$ is the unique graph, up to isomorphism, with matching index at least $3$, thereby ruling out higher-dimensional GHZ-state constructions from other finite graphs. The paper presents this as an open problem and later proves the corresponding characterization in the unweighted setting.

## 353619 Bounded Weil index of K-semistable Fano varieties
Let $n\in\mathbb{N}^*$. For an $n$-dimensional K-semistable Fano variety $X$, define its Weil index as the largest integer $q$ such that $-K_X\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}qA$ for some Weil divisor $A$.

**Weil-index boundedness conjecture.** There exists a constant $N>0$ depending only on $n$ such that the Weil index of every $n$-dimensional K-semistable Fano variety is at most $N$.

This would give a uniform dimensional bound on the Weil indices of K-semistable Fano varieties. The paper presents it as a stronger statement motivated by the boundedness conjecture for minimal log discrepancies of Kollár components; it remains open.
