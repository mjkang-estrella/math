For every fixed $`k\ge2`$, on a formula with $`n`$ variables and $`m`$ clauses, our algorithm uses space polynomial in $`n`$ and $`m`$ and runs in time $`(n+m)^{O_k(1)}2^{n/(\log n)^{1/2+o(1)}}`$. Our algorithm refutes the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), which postulates that deciding 3-SAT on $`n`$ variables requires $`2^{\Omega(n)}`$ time in the worst case.
