Proof. Let 2ℓ denote the smallest even number greater than or equal to k. In other words, 2ℓ =
k + (k mod 2). By Lemma 2.1, the complete graph K2ℓ is 1-factorable: it can be decomposed into
(2ℓ−1) perfect matchings, each containing ℓ edges. We assign a unique color to each perfect matching,
using at most k colors, and ensuring that no monochromatic P3 exists. Next, we incrementally add
vertices to form a larger complete graph. First, we add n1 − 1 vertices, denoted by X, and connect
each of these vertices using only edges of color 1. We then add n2 − 1 vertices, connecting them with
edges of color 2. This process continues until we add nk − 1 vertices, each connected by edges of
color k. At this point, the total number of vertices in the complete graph is
