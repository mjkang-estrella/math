Let $G$) be an infinite graph with vertex set $E$. For $x\in E$, write $G|E\setminus\{x\}$ for the subgraph induced by deleting $x$, and write $\sim$ for graph isomorphism. **Ulam reconstruction conjecture.** If

$$
\forall x \in E,\quad G|E\setminus\{x\}\nsim G|E,
$$

then $G$ is Ulam-reconstructible. This is one of several proposed sufficient conditions for reconstructibility of infinite graphs; the supplied text gives no evidence that it has been proved or disproved.
