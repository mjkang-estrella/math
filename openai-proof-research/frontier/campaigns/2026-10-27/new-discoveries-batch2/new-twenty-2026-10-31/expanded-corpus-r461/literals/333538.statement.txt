Let $bbda=(bbda_1,dots,bbda_m)$ be a partition, and let $C_{bbda}(x,q)$ be its orbit $q$-Eulerian polynomial. A factor is **unitary** when it is a factor of the form $1+xq^j$. **Rectangularity conjecture.** The polynomial $C_{bbda}(x,q)$ has a unitary factor if and only if $bbda=(r^m)$ is a rectangle with $r$ odd and $m$ even. In that case,

$$
C_{r,m}(x,q)=(1+xq^{\frac{rm}{2}})C'_{r,m}(x,q),
$$

where $C'_{r,m}(x,q)$ has no unitary factor. This generalizes the established one-column factorization and the cited equal-part odd case, but the full assertion remains open.
