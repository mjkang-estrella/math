Let a **TDAG** be a transitive directed acyclic graph, let d-minor operations be the directed-minor operations for TDAGs, and let a class be **d-minor-closed** when it is closed under those operations. For a class $\mathcal{G}$ of TDAGs, let $G_1,\ldots,G_r$ be TDAGs.

**A Wagner conjecture for TDAGs.** Every d-minor-closed class $\mathcal{G}$ of TDAGs has a finite set of excluded TDAGs $R=\{G_1,\ldots,G_r\}$ such that

$$
G\in\mathcal{G}\quad\text{if and only if}\quad\text{no }G_i\in R\text{ is a d-minor of }G.
$$

This is the directed analogue of Wagner's conjecture for undirected graph minors. The supplied text presents it as a major question and gives no resolution, so its status remains open.
