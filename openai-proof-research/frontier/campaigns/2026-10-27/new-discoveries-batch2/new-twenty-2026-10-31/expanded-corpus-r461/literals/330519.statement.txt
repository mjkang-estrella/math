Let a graph $G$ be a **$(\Delta+1)$-graph** if it admits an incidence coloring with $\Delta(G)+1$ colors, and let $\mathbin{\square}$ denote the Cartesian product of graphs. Let $H$ be a graph whose incidence chromatic number satisfies $\chi_i(H)\leq\Delta(H)+2$. **Incidence coloring conjecture for Cartesian products.** If $G$ is a $(\Delta+1)$-graph and $H$ satisfies $\chi_i(H)\leq\Delta(H)+2$, then 
$$
\chi_i(G\mathbin{\square}H)\leq\Delta(G\mathbin{\square}H)+2.
$$
 This would extend the classes of Cartesian products known to satisfy the Brualdi–Massey bound; the source proves the result under additional degree conditions, but leaves this general assertion as a belief.
