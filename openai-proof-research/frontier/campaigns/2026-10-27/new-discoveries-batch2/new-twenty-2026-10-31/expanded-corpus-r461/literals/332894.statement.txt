Let $s,k,n\in\mathbb{N}$ satisfy

$$
n\geq(s+1)k.
$$

Let $d\in\mathbb{N}$, and let $\mathcal{F}\subseteq[n]^{(k)}$ have matching number $m(\mathcal{F})=s$. If

$$
|\mathcal{F}|\geq\max\left\{\binom{k(s+1)-1}{k}+1,\binom{n}{k}-\binom{n-s}{k}-\binom{n-s-d}{k-1}+\binom{n-s-d}{k-d}\right\},
$$

**Sharp stability conjecture.** There exists $B\in[n]^{(s)}$ such that

$$
|\mathcal{F}\setminus\operatorname{OR}_B|\leq\binom{n-s-d}{k-d}.
$$

This conjecture strengthens the paper's stability theorem for the Erdős matching problem. The surrounding text identifies resolving the Erdős matching conjecture itself as the main open question and presents this as a further sharp stability problem.
