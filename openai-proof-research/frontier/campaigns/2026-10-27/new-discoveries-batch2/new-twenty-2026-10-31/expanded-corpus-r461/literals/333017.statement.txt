Let $H$ be a hypergraph on vertex set $[n]$, let $M$ be a positive integer, and let $f$ be an objective function used to assign each edge its weight. Write $Z(H,M,f)$ for the set of isolating weights, and let $Y(M,n)$ denote the universal lower bound on $|Z(H,M,f)|$. The singleton hypergraph $S_n$ has vertex set $[n]$ and edges $\\{1\\},\dots,\\{n\\}$.

**Singleton-hypergraph extremal conjecture.**

$$
Y(M,n) = n\sum_{i=0}^{M-1} i^{n-1}
$$

The conjecture asserts that the singleton hypergraph, and equivalently its complementary hypergraph in the stated extremal count, gives the tight universal bound for the number of isolating weight functions.
