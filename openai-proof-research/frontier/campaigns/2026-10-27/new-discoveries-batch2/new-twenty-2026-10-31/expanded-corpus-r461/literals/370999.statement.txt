Let $G$ be a connected cubic graph of order $n$, and let $i(G)$ denote its independent domination number. Reed's bound concerns domination in cubic graphs, and the exceptional graphs here are $K_{3,3}$ and the $5$-prism $C_5 \mathbin{\Box} K_2$.

**Goddard–Henning conjecture.** If

$$
G \notin \{K_{3,3}, C_5 \mathbin{\Box} K_2\},
$$

then

$$
i(G) \leq \frac{3}{8}n.
$$

The conjecture asks whether Reed's $\frac{3}{8}$ upper bound for the domination number extends to independent domination, apart from the two stated exceptions. The supplied source presents it as a conjecture; its resolution is not specified here.
