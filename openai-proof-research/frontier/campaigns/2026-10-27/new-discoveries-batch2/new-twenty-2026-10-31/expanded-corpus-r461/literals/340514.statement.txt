Let $G$ be a connected cubic graph, and let $V(G)$ denote its vertex set. A **dominating set** is a subset of vertices such that every vertex outside the subset has a neighbor in it. **Reed's conjecture.** Every connected cubic graph $G$ contains a dominating set of at most

$$
\left\lceil \frac{|V(G)|}{3} \right\rceil
$$

vertices. This conjecture gives a universal upper bound on the domination number of connected cubic graphs; the source presents it as a statement that has been suggested and widely discussed, without supplying evidence of a resolution.
