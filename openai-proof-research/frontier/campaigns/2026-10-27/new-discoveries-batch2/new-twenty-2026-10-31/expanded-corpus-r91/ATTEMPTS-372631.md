# Central twisting for the GL conjecture

Source: arXiv:2601.12613v1, Definition 1 and Section 6, Conjecture 17. Pairwise p-singularity refers to orders of quotients of distinct members, not orders of the individual members.

Let p be an odd prime, U=[[1,1],[0,1]] in GL2(Fp), and A=-U. Then A has order 2p. Put F={A^i : 0≤i<p}. Every nonzero difference between two such exponents has absolute value less than p. Its power of A therefore has order p or 2p, hence is p-singular. F has p members, the same cardinality as a Sylow p-subgroup of GL2(Fp), since |GL2(Fp)|=p(p-1)^2(p+1). It contains I but is not a subgroup: A*A^(p-1)=A^p=-I is outside F. It is not a subgroup double-translate, by the identity-containing double-translate argument in PROOF-372630.md.

Exactly as for S5, assuming the stated classification makes a Sylow subgroup maximum of size p, then makes F maximum, contradicting uniqueness. This refutes the full classification under the source's word 'the' interpreted as uniqueness. The finite exhaustive GL2(3) search independently found maximum size 3 and 60 non-subgroup identity-containing maxima. `scripts/gl_central.py` checks explicit central-twist examples for ten odd primes through 31. These are ordinary mathematical arguments and diagnostics, not a Lean endpoint.
