327456 Degree-two Cohen–Macaulay Hirsch conjecture 
Let $S$ be a polynomial ring and let $I\subset S$ be an ideal generated in degree $2$. Write $G(I)$ for the dual graph of the minimal primes of $I$, and call $I$ **Hirsch** when

$$
\operatorname{diam} G(I)\leq \operatorname{height} I.
$$

**Degree-two Cohen–Macaulay Hirsch conjecture.** If $S/I$ is Cohen–Macaulay, then $I$ is Hirsch.

The conjecture seeks a Hirsch-type diameter bound beyond squarefree monomial ideals, for which a related result is known. The source also records non-Hirsch squarefree monomial examples with Gorenstein quotient, but those examples are generated in high degree.

327502 Hyperplane classification conjecture for Veronese spaces over polar spaces 
Let ${\goth M}:=\VerSpace(k,\PolarSpace(\varpi))$, and let ${\mathscr H}$ be a hyperplane of $\VerSpace(k,\text{\boldmath$\goth P$})$ determined by a polarity $\varkappa$. The intersection

$$
{\mathscr H}\cap{\mathfrak y}_k(Q_0(\varpi))
$$

is a hyperplane in ${\goth M}$. **Hyperplane classification conjecture.** The hyperplanes of ${\goth M}$ are precisely those of the form

$$
{\mathscr H}\cap{\mathfrak y}_k(Q_0(\varpi)).
$$

The claim proposes a complete description of the hyperplanes in the Veronese space associated with a polar space; the source gives no proof, so its status remains open.

327602 The second-minimal total irregularity conjecture for connected graphs 
Let $G$ be a simple connected graph with $n$ vertices, and let $\operatorname{irr}_{t}(G)$ denote its total irregularity. A graph is **regular** if all its vertices have the same degree. **Second-minimal total irregularity conjecture.** If $G$ is not a regular graph, then

$$
\operatorname{irr}_{t}(G)\geq 2n-4.
$$

The paper identifies $0$ as the minimal total irregularity, attained by regular graphs, and proposes $2n-4$ as the second-minimal value for simple connected graphs on $n$ vertices. The claim is presented as an open problem for further research.

327613 Kinnersley–West–Zamani 3/5-conjecture for isolate-free forests 
Let $G$ be an isolate-free forest of order $n$. In the domination game, Dominator starts in $Dominator-start game$, whose game domination number is denoted by $\gamma_g(G)$, while $\gamma_g'(G)$ denotes the corresponding Staller-start game number.

**Kinnersley–West–Zamani 3/5-conjecture.**

$$
\gamma_g(G) \leq \frac{3n}{5}\quad\text{and}\quad \gamma_g'(G) \leq \frac{3n+2}{5}.
$$

This conjecture proposes the sharp upper bounds for the two domination-game variants on isolate-free forests; the paper presents it as an open problem.

327808 The first index-theoretic alternative form of the rational cuspidal curve conjecture 
Let $C\subset\mathbb{C}P^2$ be a rational cuspidal curve of degree $d$ with $\nu$ cusps and let $F$ be the function defined from the semigroup counting functions of its singularities as in the alternative combinatorial form. **First index-theoretic alternative conjecture.** Under these conditions,

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{d-3}F(jd)\leq\sum_{j=0}^{d-3}\frac{(j+1)(j+2)}{2}=\frac{d(d-1)(d-2)}{6}.
$$

This is obtained by summing the coefficientwise inequalities and is therefore weaker than the alternative combinatorial conjecture. It is part of the same open conjectural package, although the paper proves the stronger statement for two cusps.

328059 Walsh's conjecture on weakly regular bent functions and weighted partial difference sets 
Let $p>2$ and let $f:GF(p)^n\to GF(p)$ be a weakly regular bent function corresponding to a weighted strongly regular graph via the stated analogy. Let $\mu_{ii}$ denote the diagonal intersection number for the associated weighted partial difference set, for $1\leq i\leq s$. **Walsh's conjecture.** One has

$$
\mu_{ii}=0,\qquad 1\leq i\leq s.
$$

This predicts a structural restriction on the weighted partial difference set associated with a weakly regular bent function and its weighted strongly regular graph. The supplied text gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.

328164 Stable fluctuations for biased random walks on Galton–Watson trees 
Consider a $\beta$-biased random walk with exponent $\alpha\in(1,2)$, where $\alpha$ is defined at the paper's equation labelled $\alpha_{\mathrm{tree}}$. Let $\Delta_n$ denote the hitting-time quantity used in the paper, and let $v$ be the speed. **Stable-fluctuation conjecture.** There should be no recentering and rescaling of the walk that yields scaling limits. Nevertheless, after recentering $\Delta_n$ by the inverse of the speed and rescaling by $n^{1/\alpha}$, the resulting sequence should be tight and should converge along exponential subsequences to an infinitely divisible distribution. The statement describes the expected non-Gaussian fluctuation regime for the tree walk; its precise formulation depends on the paper's definitions of $\Delta_n$, the speed, and $\alpha$.

328182 Lexicographic shellability conjecture for the uncrossing matching poset 
Let $P_n$ be the uncrossing partial order on matchings, and let ${\hat P}_n$ denote its augmented poset. **Lexicographic shellability conjecture.** The poset ${\hat P}_n$ is lexicographically shellable. The poset is known to be thin, and lexicographic shellability would provide a strong topological property consistent with the expectation that the associated spaces are homeomorphic to balls. The conjecture is presented as widely expected, but no resolution is given here.

328306 The k-optimal set edge-colouring conjecture 
Let $G$ be a graph, let $k\in\mathbb{Z}^+$, and let $D$ be a **$k$-optimal set**, meaning a $k$-dependent set maximizing $\phi_k(D)=k|D|-|E(G[D])|$. A subgraph is **$k$-edge-chromatic** if its edges can be coloured with $k$ colours so that adjacent edges receive different colours. **The $k$-optimal set edge-colouring conjecture.** If $D$ is a $k$-optimal set in $G$, then $G$ has a $k$-edge-chromatic subgraph in which every vertex of $V(G)-D$ has degree $k$.

This conjecture is introduced as a natural strengthening of the results developed earlier and is stated to imply the special case of Tuza's conjecture for graphs of the form $I_k\vee H$. Its resolution is not supplied in the source.

328379 The maximal affine-set linearity conjecture 
Let $\mathcal{U}\subseteq\operatorname{AG}(2,q)$ be a maximal affine set, let $\mathcal{D}$ be the set of directions determined by $\mathcal{U}$, and let $s$ and $t$ be the associated parameters.

**Maximal affine-set linearity conjecture.** If $t=s>2$, then $\mathcal{U}$ is an affine $\operatorname{GF}(s)$-linear set.

The conjecture proposes linearity for maximal affine sets in the case $s=t>2$, following examples of maximal affine sets that are not linear when $s=1$. The supplied text gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.

328423 Elphick's graph energy lower-bound conjecture in terms of irregularity 
Let $G$ be a connected graph with $n$ vertices, $m$ edges, eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ of its adjacency matrix, graph energy $E=\sum_{i=1}^n|\lambda_i|$, and vertex degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_n$. Define

$$
\epsilon=\frac{n\sum_{i\sim j}\sqrt{d_i d_j}}{2m^2}.
$$

**Elphick's conjecture.** The graph energy satisfies

$$
E\geq\frac{n}{\epsilon}.
$$

This conjecture was communicated by Clive Elphick and would improve the known lower bound involving the spectral radius, since $\beta=\lambda_1n/(2m)$ and $\beta\geq\epsilon\geq1$. Its resolution is not specified in the source.

328435 General Star Decomposition Conjecture 
Let $H$ be a graph with edge densities $b3_e$. For each proper labeling $f$, consider the weighted monotone-path tree $T_f(H)$, with the edge weights interpreted as densities, and suppose these weights ensure the existence of $T_f(H)$. **General Star Decomposition Conjecture.** The given densities ensure the existence of the graph $H$. This conjecture asserts that the lower bound on the critical edge density obtained from the tree construction is sharp; its status is not resolved in the source.

328436 Uniform Star Decomposition Conjecture 
Let $H$ be a graph, let $S(H)$ be the set of proper labelings of $H$, and let $T_f(H)$ denote the weighted monotone-path tree associated with $f$. Write $d_{crit}$ for the critical density of $H$ and $bb(T_f(H))$ for the spectral radius of $T_f(H)$. **Uniform Star Decomposition Conjecture.** The critical density satisfies

$$
d_{crit}=\max_{f\in S(H)}\left\{1-\frac{1}{\lambda(T_f(H))^2}\right\}.
$$
 This is the asserted sharpness of the star-decomposition bound from the preceding corollary; the source supplies no evidence that it has been resolved.

328437 Critical density formula for complete bipartite graphs 
Let $K_{n,m}$ be the complete bipartite graph. Denote its critical edge density by $d_{crit}(K_{n,m})=d(n,m)$ and the best edge density obtained from star decomposition by $d_s(n,m)$. **Complete bipartite star-decomposition conjecture.**

$$
d_{crit}(K_{n,m})=d_s(n,m)=1-\frac{1}{n+m-1}.
$$
 The formula is stated in the complete bipartite graph case, but the source does not provide a resolution status for this conjectural claim.

328498 You and Yang's signless Laplacian power-sum conjecture for bipartite graphs 
Let $G$ be a bipartite graph with $n$ vertices. For a graph $G$, let $q_1(G),\ldots,q_r(G)$ be its nonzero signless Laplacian eigenvalues, and define

$$
S_\alpha(G):=q_1(G)^\alpha+\cdots+q_r(G)^\alpha.
$$

**You and Yang's conjecture.** If $\alpha>1$, then

$$
S_\alpha(G)\leq n^\alpha+\left(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\right)\lceil n/2\rceil^\alpha+(\lceil n/2\rceil-1)\lfloor n/2\rfloor^\alpha,
$$

with equality if and only if $G=K_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor,\lceil n/2\rceil}$.

This conjecture complements the known inequality for connected bipartite graphs when $0<\alpha\leq1$ and predicts that the balanced complete bipartite graph maximizes the signless Laplacian power sum for powers greater than one. Its validity is stated as open in the source.

328523 2-core conjecture for weakly cuspidal unipotent modules 
Let $X_\lambda$ be the unipotent $kG$-module labelled by a partition $\lambda\in\mathcal{P}_n$, and let $e$ be the parameter defining the relevant block-theoretic core. An **$e$-core** is the partition obtained from $\lambda$ by removing all possible $e$-hooks, and a **$2$-core** is defined analogously. **2-core conjecture.** If $X_\lambda$ is weakly cuspidal, then the $e$-core of $\lambda$ is a $2$-core.
This is a combinatorial restriction on labels of weakly cuspidal unipotent modules and is presented as a conjecture in the source. Its resolution is not specified.

328645 The cycle rank conjecture for metric dimension and zero forcing number 
Let $G$ be a graph. Write $\dim(G)$ for its metric dimension, $Z(G)$ for its zero forcing number, and $r(G)$ for its cycle rank. **Cycle Rank Conjecture.**

$$
\dim(G)\leq Z(G)+r(G).
$$

The conjecture proposes that the difference between metric dimension and zero forcing number is bounded by the cycle rank. The paper gives examples showing that the analogous bound using the even cycle rank is false, while this cycle-rank bound is presented as an open conjecture.

328722 Hereditary algebraicity of sum-closures of ordered binary structures 
Let $\mathfrak D$ be a hereditary class of indecomposable ordered binary structures. Suppose that $\mathfrak D$ is hereditary well-quasi-ordered and hereditary algebraic. **Sum-closure conjecture.** The sum-closure of $\mathfrak D$ is hereditary algebraic. This conjecture would imply that the sum-closure of the class of critical bichains is hereditary algebraic; the latter is known to be well-quasi-ordered and algebraic, but its hereditary algebraicity remains open.

328727 Davenport constant equality for multiplicative semigroups of polynomial quotient rings 
Let $p>2$ be a prime, let $f(x)\in \mathbb{F}_p[x]$ be a non-constant polynomial, and let $\mathcal{S}_{f(x)}^p$ denote the multiplicative semigroup of the quotient ring $\mathbb{F}_p[x]/\langle f(x)\rangle$. Let $U(\mathcal{S}_{f(x)}^p)$ denote its group of units. **Davenport constant equality conjecture.** One has

$$
D(\mathcal{S}_{f(x)}^p)=D\bigl(U(\mathcal{S}_{f(x)}^p)\bigr).
$$

The paper proves this equality when $f(x)$ factors into pairwise non-associated irreducible polynomials, and verifies the special case $f(x)=(x+1)^2$; the general case for non-constant $f(x)$ remains open.

328752 The componentwise inertia sum-of-squares conjecture 
Let $G$ be a simple undirected graph with $n$ vertices and $\kappa$ connected components. Let $\mu_1\geq\cdots\geq\mu_n$ be the eigenvalues of its adjacency matrix, and let $\pi$ and $\nu$ be the numbers of positive and negative eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity. Define

$$
s^+=\sum_{i=1}^{\pi}\mu_i^2,\qquad s^-=\sum_{i=n-\nu+1}^{n}\mu_i^2.
$$

**Componentwise inertia sum-of-squares conjecture.** One has

$$
\min\{s^-,s^+\}\geq n-\kappa.
$$

For a connected graph, this specializes to the conjectured lower bound $\min\{s^-,s^+\}\geq n-1$, and either one-sided inequality implies the corresponding Hong-type bound. The supplied source gives no evidence of a resolution for the general disconnected-graph formulation.
