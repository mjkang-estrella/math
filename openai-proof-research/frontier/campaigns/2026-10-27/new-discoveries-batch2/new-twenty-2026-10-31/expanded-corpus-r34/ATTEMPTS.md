## Route 1: 342350 / 342351 — multipartite irredundance

Read Alon–Defant arXiv:1904.02595, Introduction definitions and Conjectures 1.1/1.2, Section 2 including the full proof of Theorem 1.3, and Section 3 through its weighted-stability application (not all later technical stability proofs). The 37 exceptions are exceptions to their proof, not counterexamples. Theorem 1.2 does NOT prove the full balanced conjecture. General unbalanced factors are a genuinely broader question, not automatically cleared by the balanced result.

Exact definitions: the product is coordinatewise adjacency, not Cartesian adjacency. S is irredundant iff for every v in S, N[S minus {v}] differs from N[S]. Equivalently a selected isolated vertex witnesses itself; every selected nonisolated vertex needs an external private neighbor. Thus independent sets are irredundant, giving alpha <= IR.

First mechanism: the source multilinear-polynomial argument bounds the number of social vertices by 2^n. For each social y choose private p_y and form product_i (X_i - c_{p_y}(i)). Evaluation on the selected type vectors gives a diagonal nonsingular matrix. The polynomials lie in a 2^n-dimensional space. This proves only IR <= alpha + 2^n, not equality. The missing step cannot be erased by integer rounding: for the smallest unresolved balanced product K3^4 the bound leaves room for 16 extra vertices.

Second mechanism: quotient by false twins, retaining multiplicities. Each product of partite blocks is an independent false-twin class of weight equal to the product of block sizes. At most one SOCIAL selected vertex can lie in such a class: any external neighbor of either twin is also a neighbor of the other, so cannot be private. A class containing LONELY selected vertices can be saturated. Indeed it has no selected neighbors, and adding its remaining twins cannot destroy a private witness of another social vertex: such a witness was already forbidden from being adjacent to the existing lonely twin. A maximum independent set likewise saturates each selected class. Therefore the question admits a finite weighted type-graph reduction. But social private witnesses in a selected social type may require an unused clone in that type, so simply replacing weights by one loses witness availability. Uniform blow-up results do not imply nonuniform ones.

Third mechanism: attempt to transplant source Theorem 1.2. Its proof needs uniform independent-set stability on the type product, with the specific estimates epsilon_0 = 2^n*t_min/|V| and mu(Lon outside a coordinate slice) < 4 epsilon^eta. Under unbalanced factors the induced measure on types is nonuniform and neither slice cardinalities nor the source isoperimetric hypotheses retain this form. A sufficient remaining lemma is an independent-set construction of weight >= the total saturated lonely-class weights plus the number of selected social types for every feasible private-neighbor pattern, including clone availability. No proof of that lemma was found. It is essentially the substantive unresolved inequality, not a completed bridge.

Diagnostics: diagnostics.py exhaustively checks all subsets for all 28 unordered pairs of complete multipartite factors with factor orders 2..4 and at least two parts. Products have at most 16 vertices; alpha=IR in all. These small two-factor results are not the full claim, and the balanced two-factor cases are already known. They do not support a novelty assertion or establish the weighted lemma. Park both IDs, with 342351 ranked first for structural investigation but a substantial formalization gap. An endpoint would need literal graph products, neighborhoods, maximum-cardinality definitions, and the all-factor weighted inequality; no surrogate theorem was written.

## Route 2: 341944 — determinant interpolation and source ambiguity

Read Scheinerman arXiv:1902.09644v3 Definitions 1.1–1.8, Gram volume and row-partition inequality, the argument around Theorem 7.1, Conjecture 7.2 and its claimed consequence. T(n,ktilde) consists of ZERO-ONE square matrices with n*ktilde ones, not real-valued rows. R(m,n,k) is rectangular with every row of weight k; M_R is maximum square-root Gram determinant. Conjecture 7.2 defines m1=(1-gamma)n, m2=gamma*n but prints m_k and m_(k+1) on its RHS. This ambiguity is present in the actual primary PDF, not just MathDB.

Under the natural interpretation m_k=m1, m_(k+1)=m2, the block matrix diag(J3-I3,1) has n=4, ktilde=7/4, determinant 2 and seven ones. M_R(1,4,1)=1 and M_R(3,4,2)=2, so the proposed upper bound would be 2^(3/4)<2. The exact Gram bound is elementary: the diagonal entries are 2; off-diagonal intersections are integers 0,1,2. Identical rows give determinant zero. For distinct rows, intersections are 0 or 1, and no three weight-2 subsets of a four-element set are pairwise disjoint. With intersections a,b,c the determinant is 8-2(a^2+b^2+c^2)+2abc. One intersection gives 6 algebraically but cannot occur combinatorially: if one row is disjoint from both others, the others must be identical. Thus the realizable maxima are 4 (two or three intersections), attained by a star or triangle. diagnostics.py independently enumerates all 6^3 triples and confirms 4. Fourth powers compare 16>8.

Alternative mechanism: without the interpolation exponents, the Gram row-partition inequality gives volume <= product of the two block volumes whenever rows already have weights k,k+1. Our example then gives equality 2, not a counterexample. The paper's subsequent exponential consequence is also suggestive of an indexing/exponent presentation problem. This means the arithmetic alone does not determine the intended full-scope research question. No scope repair is authorized. HOLD_SOURCE_AMBIGUITY, not a qualifying discovery; no Lean investment. A published corrected statement or unambiguous author-written formulation would be needed before endpoint selection. No author contact occurred.

## Route 3: 342431 / 342432 — generalized stars

Read Cary arXiv:1904.06293v3 Definition 1, Theorem 1, Lemmas 1/2 and proofs, the generalized-star definition, Lemmas 5/6 and construction proof, and Conjectures 1/2. GS_m^k is m paths of k EDGES attached at a common end vertex. Definition 1 literally requires domination in the open out-neighborhood but does not exempt sinks. Its constructions and directed-path statement operationally exempt sinks. Under the literal definition, every orientation of a finite tree has a sink and no coloring exists. That definitional issue is NOT a discovery.

Even under the source's operational convention, routine known obstructions exist. With m=2, GS_2^k is P_(2k+1). At k=3 it is P7; source Theorem 1 already gives minimum 4, whereas Conjecture 1 prints 3. At k=4 it is P9; the same quoted theorem gives 4 whereas the conjecture prints 5. This is an explicit full refutation bridge from a known source result, not a new significant solution. If a convention requires m>=3, the bounded diagnostic at m=3,k=3 instead gives minimum 5 versus 3; the source's odd-k construction skips the terminal obligation. Such a correction is not promoted here.

For the maximum, a directed P_(2k+1) has 2k+1 colors by the source's own directed-path observation, greater than the claimed m(k-1)+2=2k at m=2. For m=3,k=2 one can also orient all arms toward a leaf: label edges a2->a1->o->b1->b2 and c2->c1->o. The five vertices a1,o,b1,b2,c1 are each the sole out-neighbor of another vertex and therefore must have distinct singleton colors. The two remaining source leaves share a sixth color, so chi_d=6>5. This is the standard anti-arborescence singleton argument already used in source Lemma 1; the failure is the leaf/root convention in its specialization. These routes are routine/definition corrections, excluded from credit.

To check that these were not only accidental arithmetic substitutions, dominator.py enumerates ALL proper set partitions and all orientations for (m,k)=(2,3),(3,2),(2,4),(3,3), under an explicitly diagnostic sink-exempt convention. Results are min/max 4/7,3/6,4/9,5/9 respectively. The diagnostics are not Lean certificates. The stronger computed m>=3 failures do not override the campaign's routine-consequence/correction exclusion. g++ is unavailable; the C++ attempt was not run, and Python produced the completed bounded logs.
