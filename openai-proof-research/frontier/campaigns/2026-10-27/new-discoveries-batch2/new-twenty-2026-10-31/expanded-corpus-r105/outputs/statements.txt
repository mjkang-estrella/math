(k-1)\left(2+\frac{1}{r}\right)+1\le n\le(k-1)\left(2+\frac{1}{r-1}\right)+1,

\operatorname{div}(\mathcal{F})\le\operatorname{div}(\mathcal{D}_r).
$$
