proofs for the different values of ℓ considerably increase in difficulty as ℓ increases, but they are
almost contained one in the next. Presenting these five proofs separately would be too repetitive
and thus we decided to present them together using the parameter ℓ. What this means is that early
in the proof we conclude with the cases ℓ = 0 and 1; a bit further with cases ℓ = 2 and 3; and the
rest of the proof is only for the case ℓ = 4.
