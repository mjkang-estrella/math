# Independent hand proof, before author proposal access
Take the complexified real A2 reflection representation: c acts on V* by diag(zeta,zeta^-1), s swaps x,y, zeta a primitive cube root. These generate the dihedral group of order six; two generating reflections s and cs have product c, hence rank 2 and Coxeter number 3. Set k=3, d=10. Define theta1=(x^10+3x^4*y^6)/4, theta2=(y^10+3x^6*y^4)/4.
Every monomial has degree 10. The c-weights are 10 and 4-6, both 1 mod3, and their negatives for theta2. Swapping x,y swaps theta1,theta2. Equivariance on generators extends to the generated group. Their distinct x^10 and y^10 coefficients imply linear independence. A common zero on an axis is the origin. Off axes the equations give x^6=-3y^6 and y^6=-3x^6, so 8x^6=8y^6=0, impossible. Thus their common zero set is {0}, giving an hsop under the source zero-locus definition (source binding still to inspect).
Let Fi=thetai-xi. Direct hand expansion gives yF1-xF2=xy(x^3-y^3)^3/4. Every fixed point is the origin, a nonzero axis point, or lies on x^3=y^3. The two nonzero axes each have nine solutions t^9=4, all distinct in C. Off axes write y=rho*x, rho^3=1. Both fixed equations are equivalent to x^9=1; there are nine distinct nonzero roots for each of the three distinct ratios. The cases are exhaustive and pairwise disjoint: 1+18+27=46, strictly less than (kh+1)^n=100.
At the origin DF=-I, so multiplicity one. At (a,0), a^9=4, DF has diagonal (9,-1) and off-diagonal zero; the other axis is symmetric. At any nonzero ratio-line point, x,y are units and the local ideal (F1,F2) equals (F1,(x^3-y^3)^3). The curve u=x^3-y^3=0 is smooth there; the derivative of F1 in its radial direction is 9, because theta1=x^10 on the line and x^9=1. Hence (F1,u) are local coordinates and the quotient has length 3. This is a local algebra argument, not merely a singular Jacobian inference. Multiplicities total 1+18+27*3=100. At (1,1), DF=[[9/2,9/2],[9/2,9/2]], determinant zero, rank one.
A single failed first conjunct refutes the universal conjunction; this proves neither failure of every hsop nor any failure of the existential or generic conjecture.
