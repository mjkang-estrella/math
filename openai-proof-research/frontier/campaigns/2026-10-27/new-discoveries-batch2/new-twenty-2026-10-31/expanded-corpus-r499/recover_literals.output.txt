316025 reconstruction conjecture 
 All graphs below are finite, simple and undirected.

For a graph $G=(V,E)$, a *vertex-deleted subgraph* of $G$ is a subgraph
$G-v$ obtained by deleting exactly one vertex $v \in
V$ together with all edges incident to it; it is an induced subgraph of $G$. The
*deck* of $G$ is the multiset

$$
D(G) = \{\!\{\, [G-v] \;:\; v \in V \,\}\!\},
$$

where $[H]$ denotes the isomorphism class of $H$; the members of
$D(G)$ are called *cards*. Two graphs $G$ and $H$ are *hypomorphic* if $D(G) =
D(H)$ as multisets.

An *edge-deleted subgraph* of $G$ is a subgraph $G-e$ obtained by deleting
exactly one edge $e \in E$ (keeping all vertices), and the *edge-deck* of $G$ is
the multiset

$$
ED(G) = \{\!\{\, [G-e] \;:\; e \in E \,\}\!\}.
$$

**(1) Reconstruction.** For every pair of graphs $G$ and $H$ with
$|V(G)| = |V(H)| \ge 3$, if $D(G) = D(H)$ then $G \cong H$.

**(2) Set reconstruction.** For every pair of graphs $G$ and $H$ with
$|V(G)| = |V(H)| \ge 4$, if the *sets* of isomorphism classes of vertex-deleted
subgraphs of $G$ and of $H$ coincide, that is, if $D(G)$ and $D(H)$ have the
same underlying set, then $G \cong H$.

**(3) Edge reconstruction.** For every pair of graphs $G$ and $H$ with at least
four edges, if $ED(G) = ED(H)$ then $G \cong H$.
