Personal structural reconstruction beyond the LYM shortcut: if r>=4 and n-3 levels occur, singleton multiplicity r prevents all larger sets from meeting those singleton points. It eliminates at least r large levels plus level zero; this is incompatible with only four missing levels. Complementation excludes level n-1. Thus the occurring levels must be exactly 2,...,n-2. Let E be the 2-level and M the complements of the (n-2)-level. Each E meets each M. If E contains disjoint pairs ab,cd, then M is contained in {ac,ad,bc,bd}. For r>4 this contradicts multiplicity; for r=4 equality forces E to be contained in {ab,cd}, again a contradiction. Hence E is intersecting, and, since it has at least four members, is a star with centre x. Every member of M contains x, so E={{x,p}:p in P} and M={{x,q}:q in Q}, with |P|,|Q|>=r. Any intermediate set B avoiding x must contain Q: otherwise choose q in Q\B and obtain B subset [n]\{x,q}. Any intermediate B containing x, above size two, avoids P. This is a complete necessary two-branch reduction, not a solution.

First constructive route: force each lower-half set to contain x, choose r petals P for level two, an extra marker u for level three, and an independent label antichain for the remaining levels. For each level t>=4 choose a label L_t on a disjoint marker set and r distinct filler subsets of size t-1-|L_t| from a reservoir. Distinct labels forbid containment across levels; equal size forbids containment within one level. Level-two petals and level-three marker distinguish those exceptional levels. Reflect by complementation. Cross inclusion from a lower set F into a complement is impossible because both original lower sets contain x. Reverse inclusion is impossible by cardinality, including the even-n middle level where equality would contradict x membership. This verifies the mechanism used by the public source, including its crucial even/odd middle case. It does not produce a new theorem.

Why constant overhead is unpaid: the label construction consumes about log2 r marker coordinates, and its palette of labels must grow with the number of levels. Replacing labels by nested fillers makes smaller lower-half sets lie in larger ones; sharing the petals breaks level-two avoidance. A putative n=2r+C construction therefore needs a new simultaneous incomparability mechanism, not just larger binomial capacities. At n<=2r+2, the source star reduction uses the reservoir U=[n]\({x} union P), |U|<=r+1: at |U|<=r, r-1 sets near the top of that branch force every next-lower set into an already selected superset; at |U|=r+1, the r triples through x force nearly all (r+1)-sets into the Q branch, and then leave at most two available extra points for r distinct pairs.
