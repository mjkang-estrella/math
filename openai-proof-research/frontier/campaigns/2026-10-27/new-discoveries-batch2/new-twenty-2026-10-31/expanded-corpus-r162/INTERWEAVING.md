# 344323: ordinary counterexample mechanism, not promoted

The current arXiv source and the Liverpool accepted manuscript both define an interweaving relation by stochastic links Λ, Λtilde intertwining in both directions and Λ Λtilde = E P_τ, for an arbitrary nonnegative warm-up time τ. No injectivity, reversibility or infinite divisibility is imposed in Conjecture 2. Definition 1 separately names the infinitely divisible restriction. The manuscript itself explains that constant stationary links give trivial intertwining, and interprets interweaving as weak invertibility. Its discussion after the two-state example invokes the existing isospectral intertwining theorem; that is not a proof of the full conjecture.

Here is an ordinary exact obstruction to the stated necessity direction. Let C be the 3 by 3 cyclic permutation matrix and L=C-I. Let Π be the all-1/3 matrix. Put B=C+(1/2)I-(3/2)Π. Then ΠB=BΠ=0 and B²=-(3/4)(I-Π). Thus

P_t = exp(tL)
    = Π + exp(-3t/2) [ cos(√3 t/2)(I-Π) + (2/√3)sin(√3 t/2)B ].

This identity follows by decomposing the constant line and its invariant complementary plane, or by differentiating and checking P_0=I. With T=2π/√3 and a=exp(-3T/2)>0, P_T=Π-a(I-Π). Give τ masses a/(1+a) and 1/(1+a) at 0 and T. Then E P_τ=Π. If strictly positive delays are desired, shift both support points by any s>0: P_s Π=Π, so the same identity remains true.

Take the other generator to be Ltilde=Π-I, on the SAME three-state space. Both generators are irreducible; both have stationary uniform law. The constant stationary links Λ=Λtilde=Π are stochastic, intertwine in both directions, and their product is Π=E P_τ. Hence there is an interweaving relation from L to Ltilde. But L has non-real eigenvalues -3/2 ± i√3/2 and Ltilde has only eigenvalues 0,-1,-1. Extended spectrum inclusion fails even before Jordan block sizes are considered.

This is not a numerical argument. It also avoids singleton-state or zero-delay endpoint dependence. Nevertheless it is a generic finite-time mixing/Laplace-zero obstruction and uses the source's own trivial stationary-link observation plus a classical directed 3-cycle calculation. Under the brief's generic-failure and source-ingredient precedents it has serious zero-credit risk. No significance clearance, literal Lean theorem, or claim of new literature priority is made. The 2025 Miclo conference report discusses faithfulness, nonnegative eigenvalues and spectral models in discrete time; its full relation to this continuous-time counterexample has not been cleared.
