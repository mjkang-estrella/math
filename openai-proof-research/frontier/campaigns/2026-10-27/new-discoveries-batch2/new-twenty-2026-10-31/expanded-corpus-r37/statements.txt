SOURCE Giulio Belletti, “An upper bound conjecture for the Yokota invariant”, arXiv:2002.01904 (2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.01904

345435 The mixed-volume Minkowski-sum volume conjecture
Let $d$ be a positive integer, let $m$ be a real number, and let $K_1,\ldots,K_d$ be convex bodies in $\mathds{R}^d$ satisfying

$$
\operatorname{Vol}(K_1) \ge 1,\ \ldots,\ \operatorname{Vol}(K_d) \ge 1,\quad\text{and }\ \operatorname{V}(K_1,\ldots,K_d)=m.
$$

For an integer $\ell$ with $1\leq\ell\leq d$, **the mixed-volume Minkowski-sum volume conjecture.** The maximum of $\operatorname{Vol}(K_1+\cdots+K_\ell)$ equals $(m+\ell-1)^d$ and is attained when $K_1=mK_2=\cdots=mK_d$ with $\operatorname{Vol}(K_d)=1$.
SOURCE Gennadiy Averkov, Christopher Borger and Ivan Soprunov, “Inequalities between mixed volumes of convex bodies: volume bounds for the Minkowski sum”, arXiv:2002.03065 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03065

345500 Lewis–Reiner's Hurwitz orbit conjecture for reflection factorizations
Let $W$ be a well-generated finite complex reflection group, and let $T$ and $T'$ be reflection factorizations of a Coxeter element of $W$. Two factorizations are said to have the same **multiset of conjugacy classes** when the multisets formed by the conjugacy classes of their reflection factors coincide. **Lewis–Reiner's conjecture.** $T$ and $T'$ lie in the same Hurwitz orbit if and only if they share the same multiset of conjugacy classes. The conjecture predicts that these conjugacy-class data completely determine Hurwitz orbits of reflection factorizations of Coxeter elements; the paper confirms it for the complex reflection group $G_6$.
SOURCE Gaurav Gawankar, Dounia Lazreq, Mehr Rai and Seth Sabar, “Hurwitz Actions on Reflection Factorizations in Complex Reflection Group G_6”, arXiv:2002.05102 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05102

345509 Tree-chromatic weakening of Hadwiger's conjecture
Let $G$ be a graph and let $t$ be an integer. The **tree-chromatic number** $\mathsf{tree}\textnormal{-}\chi(G)$ is the minimum, over tree-decompositions of $G$, of the maximum chromatic number of a bag. **Tree-chromatic Hadwiger conjecture.** If $G$ is a graph without a $K_{t+1}$-minor, then $\mathsf{tree}\textnormal{-}\chi(G) \leqslant t$. This is proposed as a weakening of Hadwiger's conjecture and is open; the paper proves it for $t=5$ without using the Four Colour Theorem.

345538 Samuels' conjecture on sums of independent non-negative random variables
Let $0\leq c_1\leq\cdots\leq c_l$ with $\sum_{i=1}^l c_i<1$. Consider $l$ independent non-negative random variables $X_1,\ldots,X_l$ satisfying $\mathbf E[X_i]=c_i$. Let $k$ be an integer, let $\beta_j$ be Bernoulli random variables for $j>k$, and set

$$
b=\sum_{i=1}^k c_i.
$$

**Samuels' conjecture.** The supremum of $\mathbf P(X_1+\cdots+X_l\geq1)$ over all such collections is a maximum attained by $X_j=c_j$ for $j\leq k$ and $X_j=(1-b)\beta_j$ for $j>k$.

The conjecture predicts that an optimizer accumulates the smaller expectations deterministically before switching to bold play. The source presents it as an open conjecture and notes that, when all expectations are equal, it predicts optimization by a binomial.
SOURCE Robbert Fokkink, Ludolf Meester and Christos Pelekis, “Optimizing stakes in simultaneous bets”, arXiv:2002.06804 (2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.06804

345543 The asymptotic degree of the bubble-sort map on partitions
Let $\operatorname{Part}(n)$ denote the set of partitions of $n$, and let $\mathscr B:\operatorname{Part}(n)\to\operatorname{Part}(n)$ be the bubble-sort map. Write $\deg(\mathscr B:\operatorname{Part}(n)\to\operatorname{Part}(n))$ for its maximum fiber size.

**Bubble-sort degree conjecture.**

$$
\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\deg(\mathscr B:\operatorname{Part}(n)\to\operatorname{Part}(n))=3.
$$

Computations for random partitions suggest that the degrees are close to $3$, but the paper states that no method is currently known to improve the preceding estimates.

345589 Kalai's tight-tree conjecture for tight paths
For $n \geq r \geq 2$ and $k \geq 1$, let $P_k^r$ be the tight $k$-path in an $r$-uniform hypergraph, and let ${\rm ex}(n,P_k^r)$ denote the maximum number of edges in an $n$-vertex $r$-uniform hypergraph containing no tight $k$-path. **Kalai's conjecture.**

$$
{\rm ex}(n,P_k^r) \leq \frac{k-1}{r}{n \choose r-1}.
$$

This is presented as a special case of Kalai's conjecture on tight trees, generalizing the Erdős–Sós conjecture. The claim concerns the extremal number of tight paths in uniform hypergraphs; the source does not state whether it has been resolved.

345616 Vizing-Goldberg type gap-one conjecture for equitable coloring of block graphs
Let $G$ be a block graph. Write $\omega(G)$ for its clique number, $\alpha_{\min}(G)$ for the minimum size of a maximal independent set, and $\chi_{=}(G)$ for its equitable chromatic number. **Gap-one conjecture.**

$$
\max \left\\{\omega(G),\left\lceil \frac{|V(G)|+1}{\alpha_{\min}(G)+1}\right\rceil\right\\} \leq \chi_{=}(G) \leq 1+\max \left\\{\omega(G),\left\lceil \frac{|V(G)|+1}{\alpha_{\min}(G)+1}\right\rceil\right\\}.
$$

The lower bound follows from general constraints on equitable colorings, while the conjecture asserts that the upper bound is at most one larger. The paper confirms the conjecture for well-covered block graphs, those satisfying $\alpha_{\min}(G)=\alpha(G)$; the general case is left open in the supplied text.

345652 Transience conjecture for the frog model on supercritical Galton–Watson trees
Let $\mathsf{GW}$ be a Galton–Watson measure defined by $(p_i)_{i\geq 0}$ with mean offspring

$$
\sum_i i p_i>1.
$$

For a tree $\mathsf{T}$ sampled from $\mathsf{GW}$, let $\mathrm{FM}(\mathsf{T},\eta,\mathsf{SRW})$ denote the frog model with sleeping-frog mean $\bar{\eta}$.

**Transience conjecture.** There exists a constant $c=c(\mathsf{GW})>0$ such that $\mathrm{FM}(\mathsf{T},\eta,\mathsf{SRW})$ is transient for $\mathsf{GW}$-almost all infinite realizations $\mathsf{T}$ whenever $\bar{\eta}<c$.

The conjecture would extend the paper’s transient-phase result from offspring distributions with bounded support and sufficiently large minimum positive offspring to every supercritical Galton–Watson measure. The source gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.

345677 Adjoint Reidemeister torsion vanishing conjecture
Let $M$ be an oriented compact $3$-manifold with torus boundary, and let $X^\mathrm{irr}(M)$ be the character variety of irreducible $\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{C})$-representations. Suppose that every component of $X^\mathrm{irr}(M)$ has dimension $1$ and that the interior of $M$ admits a hyperbolic structure. For a boundary curve $\gamma\subset\partial M$, let $\mathbb{T}_{\gamma}$ denote the adjoint Reidemeister torsion and let

$$
\mathrm{tr}_{\gamma}:X^\mathrm{irr}(M)\longrightarrow\mathbb{C}
$$

be the trace function of $\gamma$. **Adjoint Reidemeister torsion vanishing conjecture.** For any boundary curve $\gamma\subset\partial M$ and generic $C\in\mathbb{C}$,

$$
\sum_{\chi_\rho\in\mathrm{tr}_{\gamma}^{-1}(C)}\frac{1}{\mathbb{T}_{\gamma}(\chi_\rho)}=0.
$$

The paper's abstract states that this conjectural identity is proved for all hyperbolic twist-knot exteriors, while the general claim under the stated hypotheses is presented as conjectural.
SOURCE Seokbeom Yoon, “A vanishing identity on adjoint Reidemeister torsions of twist knots”, arXiv:2002.12576 (2021). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12576
