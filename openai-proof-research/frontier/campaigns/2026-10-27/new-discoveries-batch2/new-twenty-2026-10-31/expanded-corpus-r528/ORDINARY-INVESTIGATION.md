## 323448: exact ceiling recurrence, three routes

Full 1204.6714v1 text read in contiguous ranges [0,14000), [14000,28000), [28000,42000), [42000,53583). Source (1.1), Theorems 2.1–2.5, Conjecture 3.3, and the distinction between formal satisfaction and generation were read. The conjecture concerns reduced positive r/q and positive inner delays a_ij, positive arities p_i and k; s_i and nu are integers. A domain counterexample obtained by allowing nonpositive recursive arguments would be invalid. The rational representation must be reduced; unreduced r=2,q=4 is only a wording correction.

Write B(n)=ceil(rn/q), alpha=r/q, H_i(n)=n-s_i-sum_j B(n-a_ij), and P=sum_i p_i. Generated solutions have alpha=(k-1)/P and p_i alpha<=1 by the source's slope/argument discussion. If r>1 and gcd(r,q)=1, equality p_i alpha=1 is impossible. Therefore all d_i=q-rp_i are positive integers, all are congruent to q modulo r, and sum_i d_i=q. Also r divides k-1. Hence k>=r+1, and p_i<=floor(q/r). This leaves a finite arity budget for each r/q, but not a contradiction: r=2,q=5 allows k=3, (p_i)=(1,2,2), d=(3,1,1). That source-tested configuration illustrates precisely why a slope proof cannot close the conjecture. I did not run the finite algorithm.

Route 1: q-shift residue rigidity. H_i(n+qt)=H_i(n)+d_i t. Thus an exact full recurrence would give, for every n and every integer t in the eventual range,
 sum_i ceil((r H_i(n)+r d_i t)/q)=B(n)+rt-nu.
The bounded error is periodic in t, so eventual equality implies equality for all t in each residue class. This is an exact simultaneous covering problem, not an asymptotic approximation. Positivity of d_i and sum d_i=q do not forbid a sum of rational staircase functions being affine. The unpaid lemma is that the PARTICULAR phase functions H_i(n), for all n mod q, cannot give such a covering when r>1. Treating phases as independent constants loses the coupling that the nested recurrence imposes.

Route 2: average-error arithmetic. Suppose additionally gcd(p_i,q)=1 for every i (automatic for prime q). Then d_i is invertible modulo q. Write e(n)=B(n)-alpha*n. Its mean over q consecutive residues is mu=(q-1)/(2q). For fixed n mod q, as t runs over q residues, H_i(n)+td_i runs over every residue mod q, so the outer rounding errors also average to mu. Averaging the recurrence over q^2 consecutive n, and using alpha P=k-1, gives exactly
 nu=alpha*sum_i s_i-alpha^2*sum_ij a_ij,
 or q^2 nu=rq sum_i s_i-r^2 sum_ij a_ij.
Consequently q divides sum_ij a_ij, and r divides nu. The derivation includes the delay contribution alpha^2 sum a; omitting it would yield a false obstruction. This is compatible with the source equivalences, not a contradiction. For composite q, outer residues occupy proper classes and their means depend on n; simply assuming uniformity is invalid. Unpaid: an all-phase second-moment or Fourier obstruction that survives these correlations.

Route 3: primitive Fourier modes. In t, the i-th rounding error has period q/gcd(p_i,q), so primitive q-frequency components can only arise from i with gcd(p_i,q)=1. Cancellation must hold at every primitive mode and for all n mod q. A proof would need to exploit the phases rH_i(n), themselves sums of shifted ceiling functions, rather than just nonnegativity of d_i. The available expression permits complex phase cancellation; positivity alone gives no sign. No uniform noncancellation theorem has been proved here.

Generic/routine attempt: the source's finite residue reduction is fully applicable and already supplies decidability for fixed r/q. It does not settle all reduced r>1. The slope, averaging and Fourier routes above do not furnish a generic theorem implying Conjecture 3.3. No full candidate exists, so no significance or novelty PASS is claimed. Literal Lean endpoint would quantify r,q,k,p_i,s_i,a_ij,nu and a positive sequence on positive naturals; require eventual well-founded recursive arguments and exact generation, then prove r=1. Nat/Int, finite sums, rational ceilings and well-founded recursion are existing mathlib concepts, but this exact recurrence and the source-generation bridge require new definitions. A residue proof could avoid analytic limits entirely. No Lean was run.

## 373679: powers of two for Pinn's D, three routes

Read actual 2603.17509v1 introduction, (1), detrending (4), (6), Section 3.1, Conjectures 1 and 2, table context and bibliography; the continuous-model proof is not an integer-sequence proof. D(1)=D(2)=1 and D(n)=D(D(n-1))+D(n-1-D(n-2)); d(n)=2D(n)-n. Target is D(2^k)=2^(k-1) for k>=1 and D(2^k+1)=2^(k-1) for k>=2. The source really calls this a conjecture, despite the inconsistent corpus references field saying solved. Source [1] Pinn 2000 and [4] Kubo–Vakil 1996 are specific unread primary leads, not authenticated target subsumers. Conway's A recurrence differs in its second argument; A's proven facts cannot be transferred.

Route 1: exact endpoint induction with the missing data exposed. Put N=2^k, M=N/2, k>=3. Assume D(M)=D(M-1)=M/2, D(N-1)=M and D(N-2) in {M-1,M}. The recurrence gives D(N)=D(M)+D(M) or D(M)+D(M-1), hence D(N)=M. Then D(N+1)=D(M)+D(N-D(N-1))=2D(M)=M. Thus a preterminal-pair lemma suffices to propagate both required endpoint values. But the target endpoint identities at scale M do not give D(N-1) or D(N-2). Claiming induction without those values is circular. No lemma controlling the two preterminal values has been proved.

Route 2: terminal plateau trapping. If D(n-1)=D(n-2)=M then D(n)=D(M)+D(n-1-M). If a previous-generation terminal interval has value M/2, this propagates a terminal plateau near N after TWO consecutive seed values equal M. The mechanism controls propagation, but not entry into that plateau from the chaotic interior. A backward recurrence cannot supply the seed because the defining equation is forward and not invertible. The unpaid statement is a uniformly certified entrance pair in each dyadic generation, with all parent indices in the appropriate earlier plateau. Constant plateau length growth is not justified.

Route 3: parent-generation bounds. The parent's first index is D(n-1), and its second is n-1-D(n-2). Parent containment in the previous generation at n=N and n=N-1 would force M-1<=D(N-2)<=M, exactly one of the preterminal requirements above. But Conjecture 2 is itself unproved, and it does not immediately yield D(N-1)=M. Replacing the entire chaotic block by the continuous backbone (15) discards the one-step correction in (6), so endpoint continuity in that model cannot pay the discrete bridge. The unpaid invariant must control both recursive index validity and terminal entrance; a pair of endpoint values is not a sufficient state summary presently proved.

Generic/routine attempt: the usual slow-sequence argument for Conway A is inapplicable because D need not be monotone or slow; no generic theorem on these recursions has been authenticated. None of the three routes proves the target or a negation. Literal Lean endpoint can be an integer-valued sequence D:N->N satisfying positive-index bounds and the exact recurrence, with the two universal power-of-two identities and an explicit canonical generation bridge. Nat recursion/powers and Int arithmetic exist; the particular D definition and totality/positive-parent invariant do not come prepackaged. This is cheap infrastructure only IF the missing invariant is found; no formal result or dispatch.
