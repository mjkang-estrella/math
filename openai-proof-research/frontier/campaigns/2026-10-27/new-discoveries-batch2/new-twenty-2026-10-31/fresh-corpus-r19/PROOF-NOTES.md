## 338640: finite maximal intersecting family

The original is Bovdi–Leung, arXiv1803.03457v1, Section3 pp5–8, Conjecture2 p8. The actual definition of s-bicommutative is **maximal intersecting among all s-subsets**, not merely pairwise intersecting, maximal among a restricted universe, or minimum among a special construction class. Theorem1 and its full proof pp6–7 were read, along with the Fano-system definition and proof of Lemma3. Conjecture2 p8 was also visually inspected.

Take k=2, n=15, m=7. Translate the paper's ground set [15] to {0,...,14} by subtracting one. The distinguished seven-element core is C={8,...,14}; its Fano lines are {8,9,12},{8,10,13},{8,11,14},{9,10,14},{10,11,12},{12,13,14},{9,11,13}. The paper's A_7 consists exactly of seven-subsets S with |S∩C|≤2 or S∩C one of these lines. Its size is

    7 choose(8,4) + choose(7,2) choose(8,5)
      + 7 choose(8,6) + choose(8,7) = 1870.

The retained witness F is the 1505 integer masks in `bicommutative-search.json`, interpreted literally as subsets of {0,...,14}. No graph eigenvalue or surrogate extremal inequality is being substituted. `bicommutative-cover.json` lists, for **every** one of the 4930 seven-subsets S outside F, a specific T in F disjoint from S. The independent direct-set validator checks all 1,131,760 unordered distinct pairs in F and finds nonempty intersection in each; all members have size7 and are distinct. Every self-pair intersects because the sets have size7. It also constructs the literal source A_7 from its ten B_i families and obtains1870. Thus a finite exact certificate establishes the ordinary-mathematical refutation of minimum size, conditional only on replay of these transparent finite checks; it does not establish the exact minimum, smallest counterexample, or an asymptotic theorem.

Construction: seeded random-order greedy independent domination in the odd graph KG(15,7), 100 trials. Vertices are all6435 seven-subsets, and the eight neighbors of S are the seven-subsets of its eight-element complement. Greedy selection adds S only when no chosen neighbor exists and blocks S and its eight neighbors. At termination independence and domination follow inductively. Trial sizes range1505–1626; the selected witness is the smallest retained one. This algorithmic argument is an independent explanation of maximality, in addition to direct exhaustive validation of the final family.

Prior-art limits: public title/author/Conjecture2/bicommutative and independent-domination searches located no explicit matching resolution. This is not novelty clearance. Bounds for m(7), with variable ground-set size, do **not** themselves supply a family on15 points. The projective-plane constructions there do not immediately settle this fixed ground set. The 2024 random-intersecting-process paper's Theorem4 and introductory scope were inspected, not its full proof; the asymptotic big-O bound has no displayed explicit numerical specialization proving the present1505 bound. Underlying Dow/Furedi/Blokhuis/Boros proof bodies, the actual2018 journal version, citing literature, and present MathDB solutions remain unread/inaccessible. Small finite counterexample significance needs independent review; ease of greedy construction is not significance evidence.

## 339278: a nontrivial positive split fails

Original arXiv1805.07019v1, Definitions1.7–1.9 and2.1–2.3, Proposition7.1 with full proof, Conjecture7.2 and Remark7.3 were read. The published2025 chapter in Games of No Chance6, pp405–431, Definitions2.5–2.7, Proposition10.1 proof and Question10.2/Remark10.3 pp429–431 were read. Published Question10.2 retains the question. Winning takes priority: Windsor must return nonzero nim-sum to zero; Luca may make any legal move. V=N_L−N_W, with optimal opposing play.

Consider G=[12,20,24], a strictly increasing genuine P-position: 12 xor20 xor24=0. Its only **positive** no-carry split into more than one part is [4,8], up to permutation. To prove uniqueness, for a list of positive integers let c_i count the parts having binary bit i. Its sum minus its xor is sum_i 2^i(c_i−(c_i mod2)), a sum of nonnegative terms. Equality forces c_i≤1 for all i. Since12 has just bits2 and3, exactly two positive parts are possible and they must be4 and8. Thus it suffices to compare H=[4,8,20,24].

The exact recurrences give V(G)=38 and V(H)=36, equivalently N_W(G)=9 and N_W(H)=10. Two independent implementations agree: sorted-positive-pile recursion for Windsor's take and fixed labeled piles including zeros for the signed score. They also reproduce the source's four small Proposition7.1 examples, V(3,4,7)=6, V(1,2,4,7)=8, V(3,5,6)=6, V(1,2,5,6)=6. These are exploratory computational validations, not Lean proofs.

Here is a complete ordinary recursive specification of the finite verification. For a position g with nonzero xor x, set

    T(g)=min_i (−(g_i−(g_i xor x)) + T(g with i replaced by g_i xor x)),

where i ranges over g_i xor x<g_i. At a nonempty zero-xor position set

    T(g)=max_(i,b<g_i) (g_i−b + T(g with i replaced by b)).

Set T(0,...,0)=0. Each successor strictly decreases total candies, so strong induction proves T is the optimal Luca-minus-Windsor score. Windsor's legal responses have exactly the listed form by Bouton's theorem (source Theorem1.5 / published Theorem1.3), or by the elementary xor identity. The finite tables include **all** states in rectangles [0,12]×[0,20]×[0,24] and [0,4]×[0,8]×[0,20]×[0,24], 30450 states total, in lexicographic order, with no counterexample/state truncation. `validate_candidates.py` independently recomputes every entry. Entries outside zero-xor positions are continuation scores when it is Windsor's turn, not the source V for arbitrary starting turn. The claimed endpoint uses only zero-xor starting positions.

Scope issue: MathDB explicitly requires **positive** split parts. Original/published question omits positivity, and published Definition2.7 permits zero piles. If zero parts are admitted, [0,a] is a trivial unchanged game and there is no refutation of that reading. This candidate targets the positive-parts MathDB statement; it must not be sold as a refutation of the permissive-zero primary wording. The power-of-two obstruction is separately excluded as routine; the12 witness really permits a legal positive split and compares optimal strategies. Whether this still qualifies under the source-fidelity/significance gate is for independent review.

No kernel theorem for these semantic bridges or endpoint is supplied. Public exact-witness/splitting searches were misses, not novelty clearance. Albert2004 slides and author's downloadable program/other citing work were not audited. No worldwide-priority claim.

## Third route, 339052: frame balancing obstruction

Ivanov arXiv1804.10055v3 (26Sep2019), Conjecture5.1 and Section5 pp14–17 were inspected, including actual proofs of Lemmas5.2,5.4,5.5 and Theorem1.4. This is a fixed-rank orthogonal projection optimization, not a comparison across arbitrary dimensions. Represent the projection by a tight frame v_i with sum_i v_i v_i^T=I_k; F=sum_|J|=k |det(v_J)|. The equal-edge condition is ||v_i||^2=k/n.

Attempt1: rotate two columns, v_i(t)=cos(t)v_i−sin(t)v_j and v_j(t)=sin(t)v_i+cos(t)v_j, preserving the tight-frame equation exactly. Within a fixed determinant-sign chamber, F(t)=C+A cos(t)+B sin(t), with terms containing both columns fixed. At a local maximum F'(0)=B=0; this is exactly the source's stationarity condition, not equal norms. At the same time d/dt ||v_i(t)||^2 at0=−2<v_i,v_j>, which need not vanish. Thus stationarity alone cannot force equality of column norms.

Attempt2: change one column and re-tighten by A(t)^(-1/2). The volume ratio gets divided by sqrt(det A(t)); first order yields <sigma_i,d_j>=<v_i,v_j>, exactly Lemma5.5. Summing diagonal identities gives kF=kF (Euler homogeneity), not n independent equal-norm equations. Pair rotations by pi/4 produce only the already-known ratio bound of Theorem1.4. A third local perturbation or global comparison is needed. No finite numerical maximizer would settle the global equal-norm assertion. Precise residual: prove a global norm-balancing transformation which preserves tightness and does not decrease all absolute-minor sum, or bound the supremum over all equal-norm frames strictly below one exact unequal-norm frame. Neither was obtained. No candidate/Lean credit.
