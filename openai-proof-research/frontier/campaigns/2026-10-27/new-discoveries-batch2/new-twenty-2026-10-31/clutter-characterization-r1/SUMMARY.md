## 1. 388087 — exact statement (restored from primary sources)

- **Egres Open.** Source: archived page in `../lane-1/sources/egres/`, last modified 2 Jan 2015, still "open". The page asks: for a clutter C on V with blocker B, is C nonideal ⇔ ∃ p: C→V, q: B→V with p(X)∈X, *"p(Y)∈Y"* and [p(X)=q(Y) ⇒ |X∩Y|>1]?
- **Typo.** "p(Y)∈Y" should read q(Y)∈Y. Pap's EGRES TR-2010-09 (`sources/egres-tr-10-09.pdf`, Conjecture 1.2, a single h on C∪B) and Pap's PhD thesis (`sources/wb-pap-julia-2015.pdf`, Conjecture 3.4.5(ii) "q(B)∈B") both use q(Y)∈Y.
  - The reading with q unconstrained is degenerate. The ideal clutter {01,12} would then admit (p,q): p = (0,2), q({1}) = 1, q({0,2}) = 1. That contradicts the proved direction.
- **Proved direction.** Ideal ⇒ no (p,q): Király–Pap, polyhedral Sperner lemma (thesis Theorem 3.3.22).
- **Open direction.** Nonideal ⇒ (p,q) exists.
- **Known partial results:**
  - reduction to minimally nonideal (mni) clutters (Claim 3.4.6);
  - the restriction to the Lehman cores always holds (Theorem 3.4.7);
  - the case of a cyclic core holds (Theorem 3.4.9);
  - OK₅ was checked by hand.
- **Definitions used** (as in the sources):
  - A clutter is a family of pairwise incomparable sets.
  - The blocker is the family of inclusion-minimal transversals.
  - C is ideal iff P(C) = {x ≥ 0 : x(X) ≥ 1 for all X ∈ C} is integral, i.e. every extreme point is integral.

## 2. The counterexample C11

Ground set {0,…,10}; eleven edges:

```
{1,7,10} {2,3,8} {2,3,4} {0,3,4} {0,1,4} {0,5,6} {5,6,7} {1,6,7} {2,8,9} {8,9,10} {5,9,10}
```

- **Lehman structure.** The edge matrix A is a cubic (11,3,4) Lehman matrix with A·B̄ᵀ = J + I, where B̄ is the 11 four-element blocker members (`runs/lehman-structure-n11-1.txt`).
- **Construction.** It comes from the Möbius ladder C₅³ by two 3-rung ladder insertions (Mayhew–Pivotto–Royle, arXiv:1805.07576 §3.2).
- **Core is not cyclic.** The cyclic class C₁₁³ satisfies the conjecture (Pap). C11 is one of the other three isomorphism classes of (11,3,4) Lehman matrices, and A ≅ Aᵀ.
- **Blocker.** b(C11) has 36 members: the 11 core 4-sets and 25 five-element sets.

**Nonideal.** x* = (1/3,…,1/3) lies in P(C11) and is an extreme point. If x* = a·y + b·z with y, z ∈ P(C11) and a, b > 0, then every edge constraint is tight for y and z. The 11 tight equations have the unique solution x*, so y = z = x*.

**Minimally nonideal** (exact diagnostic, not part of the Lean proof):
- Exact vertex enumeration gives P(C11) 37 vertices: the 36 blocker members plus x*.
- All 22 single-element deletions and contractions are ideal (`runs/check-mni-n11-1.txt`, float bases with exact Fraction recomputation).
- This agrees with Mayhew–Pivotto–Royle Table 3: all four (11,3,4) cubic Lehman matrices are mni.

So C11 lies exactly in the class the conjecture had been reduced to, and its core is non-cyclic, outside Pap's proved case.

**No (p,q).** Once p is fixed, q decouples across blocker members. q(Y) is impossible exactly when every v ∈ Y is "killed", i.e. some edge X has X∩Y = {v} and p(X) = v.
- For each of the 3¹¹ choices of p, some blocker member is fully killed. All 36 members are needed: the minimal unsat core is the whole blocker.
- This is the obstruction Pap observed for the naive core matching on OK₅. Here it is unavoidable for every p.
- In particular, Pap's core assignment (Theorem 3.4.7) never extends.
- The proof is a finite case analysis. I found no short conceptual argument; the certificate is a 6,517-leaf branching tree.

Verification of "no (p,q)":
1. `scripts/kiraly.js`: an exhaustive CSP with forward checking.
2. `scripts/verify_independent.py`: independent code, including its own blocker computation by definition and plain enumeration of all 3¹¹ choices of p.
3. Sanity check of both solvers' logic on known cases:
   - F₇, C₅², C₇², C₉², C₅³, C₈³, C₁₁³, J₃ and OK₅ all admit (p,q), with verified witnesses.
   - The ideal cases (path, C₄, C₆, Q₆, a single edge) admit none, matching Sperner.
4. The Lean kernel, below.

## 5. 388146 and 388171 — no endpoint

**388146 (Cooper; Bóna).**
- The random model is uniform on Av_n(τ), and E_n(σ,τ) = (number of σ-occurrences summed over Av_n(τ)) / |Av_n(τ)|, for all σ, τ, n.
- **Barrier:** for |σ| = n, E_n(σ,τ) = [σ avoids τ]/|Av_n(τ)|. A complete answer would therefore include exact enumeration of Av_n(1324), which is an open problem.
- Fixed instances are finite rational computations, so they are tautological.
- Published partial answers: Bóna (arXiv:0910.1343) and Homberger (EJC 19(3) P43).
- No endpoint.

**388171 (Erdős; Cooper; erdosproblems #876, secondary question).**
- S has no element equal to the sum of a nonempty set of other distinct elements, and R = sup Σ_{s∈S} 1/s.
- Known: 2.0649 < R < 3.9998, and Levine–O'Sullivan conjecture R ≈ 3.01. Cooper's attribution of the two bounds is swapped relative to MathWorld.
- Determining R exactly is a long-standing open problem with no finite certificate route.
- erdosproblems shows two "proof claims" that I did not assess.
- No endpoint.
