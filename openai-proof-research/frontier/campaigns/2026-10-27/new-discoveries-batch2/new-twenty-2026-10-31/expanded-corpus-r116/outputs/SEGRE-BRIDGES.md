# Segre questions: short source-formula bridges, ineligible for discovery credit

Read source arXiv:2408.08421v4, Definition 4.11, Theorem 4.13 and its proof, Theorem 4.20 and its proof, Proposition 4.23 and Conjectures 4.24–4.26. No claim that these bridges are new to literature.

For 364372, Theorem 4.13(3) says that the product-group multiplicity at shapes (mu,nu) is sum_lambda c_lambda K_(mu,lambda) K_(nu,lambda). Take mu=(1^n). The Kostka number K_((1^n),lambda) is zero unless lambda=(1^n), when it is 1. The source's Definition 4.11 gives c_(1^n)=1. Thus the product-group homology at t=2 contains sign tensor V_nu with multiplicity K_(nu,1^n)=dim V_nu for EVERY nu. Restriction to the diagonal contains each irreducible V_(nu') (sign tensor V_nu), with positive multiplicity. Consequently the t=2 representation has full irreducible support, making the proposed support containment automatic for every t>=3. This is one paragraph from the source's own theorem and classical Kostka/sign facts. Exclude as routine, do not formalize for credit.

For 364371 use Theorem 4.20(3). Evaluate at an n-cycle. The permutation character M^lambda vanishes there unless lambda=(n): an ordered partition of [n] with block sizes lambda can be invariant under a transitive cycle only if it has a single block. At lambda=(n) the value is 1, and c_(n)=(-1)^(n-1). Therefore the homology character at an n-cycle is (-1)^(n-1), independent of t>=1. In the elementary basis e_lambda, the corresponding character at an n-cycle is zero except for lambda=(n), when it is the sign (-1)^(n-1). This follows either by induction from the Young subgroup sign representation, or by the power-sum coefficient: [p_n]e_n=(-1)^(n-1)/n, and no product of two positive-degree elementary functions has a p_n term. Thus the coefficient of e_n is 1. Again, routine source-formula bridge, excluded.
