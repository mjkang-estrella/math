     A set of vertices I ⊆ V (G) is independent if no two elements of I are adjacent.

Definition 1.3. A graph G is called fully regular if for an independent set
I ⊆ V (G), the number of vertices in V (G) \ I not adjacent to any element of I
is determined by the size of I.
