# MathDB 371639: refutation of Conjecture 7.5 of Clow–Kumar–Pragada

Status: candidate for root review. It is proved in Lean, but proposed credit is 0 until independent fidelity, novelty and significance review and root acceptance.

## Source and exact statement

Source: A. Clow, H. Kumar and S. Pragada, *Improved Bounds for the Ultimate Independence Ratio of Odd Wheels*, arXiv:2511.18747v1 (24 Nov 2025; only version on the abs page fetched 2026-11-01; receipt `sources/2511.18747.abs.html.receipt.json`). Section 7 (open problems) closes with:

> **Conjecture 7.5.** There exists a constant c > 0 such that if G is a graph with χ(G) > ω(G) and H is a largest induced subgraph of G with χ(H) ≤ ω(G), then χ_f(G) < c (ω(G) |V(G)| / |V(H)|).

The MathDB record 371639 (raw line in `raw/371639.jsonl`, provenance in `results.json`) states the same thing. Graphs are finite and simple. χ_f is the usual fractional chromatic number: the least total weight of a nonnegative weighting of independent sets that covers every vertex with weight at least 1. Write h(G) = |V(H)|; it depends only on G.

## Theorem

For every real c, there is a graph G with χ(G) > ω(G) and χ_f(G) ≥ c · ω(G) |V(G)| / h(G). So Conjecture 7.5 is false. The same holds within connected graphs.

## Construction

Let C₅ be the 5-cycle on Z/5. For k ≥ 1, let L_k be the k-th lexicographic power C₅[C₅[⋯]], with vertex set (Z/5)^k. Two distinct words are adjacent exactly when their first differing letters are adjacent in C₅. Let G_k be the disjoint union of L_k and a set R of 5^k isolated vertices.

**Lemma 1.** Every independent set and every clique of L_k has at most 2^k vertices.

*Proof.* Induction on k; for k = 0 there is one vertex. Let S be independent in L_{k+1}. Let T be the set of first letters used by S. If a ≠ b are in T, then a and b are not adjacent in C₅, because words beginning with a and b are adjacent whenever a ~ b. So T is independent in C₅ and |T| ≤ 2. For each a ∈ T, the words of S that begin with a are mutually non-adjacent. Their tails are distinct and form an independent set of L_k, because two words with the same first letter are adjacent exactly when their tails are. So each fibre has at most 2^k words and |S| ≤ 2 · 2^k. The clique case is the same, using ω(C₅) = 2 and the fact that two words with the same first letter are adjacent only through their tails. ∎

**Lemma 2.** ω(G_k) ≤ 2^k.

*Proof.* A clique that meets R is a single vertex. Any other clique is a clique of L_k. ∎

**Lemma 3.** χ_f(G_k) ≥ (5/2)^k.

*Proof.* Let y be any fractional colouring. Every independent set I of G_k meets V(L_k) in at most 2^k vertices (Lemma 1). Double counting gives

5^k ≤ Σ_{v∈V(L_k)} Σ_{I∋v} y(I) = Σ_I y(I) |I ∩ V(L_k)| ≤ 2^k Σ_I y(I). ∎

**Lemma 4.** χ(G_k) > 2^k ≥ ω(G_k).

*Proof.* In a proper m-colouring, each colour class meets V(L_k) in an independent set of at most 2^k vertices. So 5^k ≤ m 2^k. Taking m = 2^k would give 5^k ≤ 4^k, which is false for k ≥ 1. ∎

**Lemma 5.** h(G_k) ≥ 5^k.

*Proof.* R induces an edgeless graph, and χ(G[R]) = 1 ≤ ω(G_k). ∎

**Proof of the theorem.** Fix c > 0. Since |V(G_k)| = 2 · 5^k, Lemmas 2 and 5 give

c ω(G_k) |V(G_k)| / h(G_k) ≤ c · 2^k · 2 · 5^k / 5^k = 2c · 2^k.

Choose k ≥ 1 with (5/4)^k ≥ 2c. Then 2c · 2^k ≤ (5/4)^k 2^k = (5/2)^k ≤ χ_f(G_k) by Lemma 3. The hypothesis χ(G_k) > ω(G_k) holds by Lemma 4, so the strict inequality of the conjecture fails. ∎

**Connected version.** In the vertex set (Z/5)^k ⊔ (Z/5)^k, fix a hub vertex z in the second copy. Join z to every other vertex, and keep the lexicographic edges on the first copy. The graph is connected: every vertex is adjacent to z. Moreover:

- ω ≤ 2^k + 1. A clique containing a non-hub vertex of the second copy has at most 2 vertices; any other clique is z together with a clique of L_k.
- χ > 2^k + 1 for k ≥ 2, since 5^k > 4^k + 2^k.
- χ_f ≥ (5/2)^k, by the same double count.
- The 5^k − 1 non-hub vertices of the second copy are independent, so h ≥ 5^k − 1.

So the right-hand side is at most c (2^k + 1) · 2 · 5^k / (5^k − 1) ≤ 8c · 2^k. This is below (5/2)^k once (5/4)^k ≥ 8c.

## Remarks on scope and significance (for review, not self-assessment)

- The padding is essential. For vertex-transitive G we have χ_f(G) = |V|/α(G). Also h(G) ≤ ω(G) α(G), since H is a union of ω(G) independent sets. Hence c ω |V| / h ≥ c |V| / α = c χ_f, and the inequality holds for every c > 1. The conjecture fails because χ_f is a maximum-type invariant, while the right-hand side can be diluted by attaching an easily coloured part.
- The source's motivating graphs (Cartesian powers of odd wheels) are not covered by this refutation. No claim is made about any repaired version of the conjecture.
- Bounded prior-art search (2026-11-01; `outputs/search-notes.json`, `outputs/registry-screen.json`) found no earlier refutation. The current Omega D5/Problems path listings, the OpenAI preprints listing, and the arXiv abs page (v1 only) were checked. This is not an exhaustive literature search.
