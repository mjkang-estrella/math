## 345467: full literal counterexample, excluded as direct published-example endpoint adaptation

Read Li–Ning, arXiv:2002.04198v3, pp.1–4 and concluding remarks pp.14–15, especially Conjecture 4 and its immediately following example. Source conjecture and corpus both quantify 0<alpha<=1/2 without an integrality restriction. This is not an invention from a missing MathDB adjective.

Let a>=2 and k>=2a+1 be integers. Put b=k-1-a>=a. Take a clique A of size a and an independent set B of size b+2, with every A–B edge. Choose distinct x,y in B. Thus G=K_a join independent_(b+2), n=k+1. Removing any vertex leaves a nonempty clique complete to the remaining independent vertices, hence a connected graph. This proves 2-connectivity, including the explicit n>=6 case below.

Every vertex in A has degree (a-1)+(b+2)=k. Every vertex in B has degree a<k. Consequently exactly a vertices outside {x,y} have degree >=k, and n-2=k-1.

For any simple x–y path, let s be its number of clique vertices and r its number of independent vertices, including both endpoints. Consecutive independent vertices are impossible. Hence r<=s+1 and the path has s+r-1<=2s<=2a edges. There is a path of length exactly 2a: alternate a distinct clique vertices with a+1 independent vertices, starting at x and ending at y. There are enough independent vertices since b+2>=a+2. So longest x–y path length is exactly 2a; there is no enumeration hidden in this bound.

Choose any rational alpha satisfying a/k<alpha<a/(k-1). The interval is nonempty and its upper endpoint is <=1/2. Then a>alpha(k-1), so the strict conjectural premise holds, while 2alpha k>2a, so every x–y path fails its conclusion. This pays all quantifiers needed for a negation: one finite graph, one admissible positive k, one admissible alpha, two distinct endpoints, satisfied premise, and universal path upper bound.

Concrete witness: a=2,k=5,b=2,alpha=9/20,n=6. The two clique vertices have degree 5; all four independent vertices have degree 2. The number of high-degree nonendpoints is 2>9/5. The conclusion asks for length at least 9/2, whereas the maximum is 4.

Mechanisms investigated beyond this witness: (1) alpha=1/2 is not a counterexample: the high-degree proportion is exactly 1/2 and the premise is strict; the actual Theorem1.1 is untouched. (2) Degree-uniform clique lobes glued at x,y do not work: their high-degree vertices also allow paths as long as k. (3) The split graph separates degree k from attainable endpoint length, and rational perturbation rather than endpoint equality gives the contradiction above. Repeating source lobes t times preserves the local path bound but is unnecessary; t=1 already has exact fidelity.

Its t=1,alpha0=1/2,k=5 graph is exactly the graph above. Holding this graph and k fixed, replace alpha0 with 9/20. Degree count and longest path do not change. The two displayed rational inequalities immediately give the literal negation. Thus the entire mechanism is already in the primary paper and requires only varying a free real parameter inside the gap between k and k-1. ## 325281: exact all-k matching route remains unpaid

Read primary arXiv:1304.0871v1 Section2 (definitions, Theorem2.3, Conjecture2.5) and Section3's actual Berge-barrier/kernel proof. Also read the author manuscript of Gyárfás–Sárközy2019/Discrete Math2020, Theorems1.3–1.4, Lemma1.5, and Section2's atom proof. The later primary explicitly distinguishes a basic 2-matching (disjoint edges and odd cycles) from an ordinary matching.

Route A, six ordinary Tutte barriers: for each pair ij of the four colors let G_ij have all vertices and edges in those colors. If no pair contains a matching of size k, Berge's formula gives a set X_ij with oddcomp(G_ij-X_ij)-|X_ij|>=n-2k+2. The desired threshold is n>=floor((8k-2)/3), so these deficiencies are of order n/4. For a complementary pair ij,lm, edges between distinct components of G_ij-X_ij are all in lm. This is stronger than arbitrary unrelated matching problems and is the plausible source of a contradiction.

Unpaid step: a component is not an independent vertex. Inside an odd component of G_ij-X_ij there can be many ij edges. Two exceptional vertices falling in the same component do not force an lm edge. The 2013 missing-one-color kernel lemma uses one excluded color and an ordering of barriers; it does not supply consistent disjoint kernels for six overlapping allowed color pairs. Specifically a pair ij and a pair il share color i; a crossing edge constrained to complements {l,m} and {j,m} can retain color m, so the single-excluded-color contradiction does not carry over. A full six-barrier compatibility inequality forcing some deficiency<=n-2k is still unproved.

Route B, strongest published fractional/basic result: Theorem1.3 supplies a basic bicolored 2-matching covering >=floor(3n/4) vertices. Its actual proof partitions by isolated vertices of complementary two-color Tutte sets, then compares the three partitions and their AB/ABB' atoms. An isolated vertex forbids all relevant pair-color edges incident outside the barrier; a whole odd component does not. Thus simply replacing isolated-vertex deficiency with odd-component deficiency is invalid.

If a basic 2-matching covers v vertices and has c odd-cycle components, selecting maximum matchings within each component covers v-c vertices. It yields (v-c)/2 matching edges, not v/2. There is no paid bound making c vanish or bounding the losses by the exact floor slack. Completeness of the ambient graph allows edges between cycles but may introduce a third or fourth color. Two odd cycles can be joined to improve an ordinary matching if a suitable edge in the SAME allowed pair joins them: expose the chosen endpoint in each cycle and use the joining edge. The obstacle is that such joining edges can all use the complementary pair. No argument yet synchronizes a color-pair switch without losing the other components. This is the exact missing augmentation lemma, not a numerical gap.

Route C, asymptotic lifting: Theorem1.4 loses eta*n vertices, has n0(eta), and uses regularity and blow-up machinery. Setting eta=1/n is not allowed because the threshold itself depends on eta. It cannot give a uniform all-k exact statement. No field/range exception rescues that inference.

Extremal witness analysis by hand: the source partition vector [p,p,2p,4p+1] on 8p+1 vertices has no bicolored matching of size 3p+1. Edges of colors1,2 touch their respective first parts; choosing {1,2} covers at most 2p edges, and {1,3} or {2,3} at most 3p. With {1,4} or {2,4}, at most p edges touch the small part and at most 2p remain in the final part. With {3,4}, all usable edges lie within the union of the last two parts (size6p+1), hence at most3p edges. This confirms the source obstruction is just below the proposed threshold, since floor((8(3p+1)-2)/3)=8p+2. It does NOT refute the conjecture. All six cases are covered symbolically, no finite search was run.

Final gate: ordinary GAP; strongest applicable source bodies partially read, exact final2020 equivalence not authenticated; current bounded search found no paid exact proof; no worldwide novelty claim. Both finite counterexample and all-parameter proof remain absent. Lean route would require the missing structural theorem, not just a finite executable test.

To reach c>=2k one needs a new global bound relating the high-degree vertices ON C to the deficient components off C. No such bound was proved.
