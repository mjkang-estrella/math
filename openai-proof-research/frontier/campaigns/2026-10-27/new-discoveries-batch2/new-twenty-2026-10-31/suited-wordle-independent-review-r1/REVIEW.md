Thus the exponent sorting convention for B_g is the asserted one and max exponent is fdes, not fmaj.

For arbitrary exponents sorted by pi with increasing index ties, use colors equal to those sorted exponents modulo s. At a descent, sorted exponents differ by at least s+c_i-c_{i+1}; if colors agree a descent forces a strict exponent difference, hence at least s. At a non-descent the difference is at least c_i-c_{i+1}. The last quotient b_n is nonnegative. Therefore b_i=(a_pi_i-f_i)/s is a decreasing nonnegative integer tuple. This establishes division and fdes<=max a without an unstated assumption on residues.

## Count and actual attainment

For n>=1 its maximum is fdes(g)+s*b_1. Successive differences of b are n independent nonnegative integers; their sum is b_1. Counting all monomials by maximum exponent therefore gives

    F_n(t)=(1-t)(1-t^s)^n sum_{j>=0}(j+1)^n t^j.

This is a formal power-series argument; denominators have constant term 1. Differentiating the series, with H_0=1 as a separate empty-size convention, gives

    H_n=(1+t+...+t^(s-1)+s(n-1)t^s)H_(n-1)

At coefficient k, the t^s terms contribute s(n-1)-(k-s)=sn-k; the derivative at k contributes k; the constant H term contributes 1.
