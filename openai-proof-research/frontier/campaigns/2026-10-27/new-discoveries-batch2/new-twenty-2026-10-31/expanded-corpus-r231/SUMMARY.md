## Substantive investigations

**359843 — pinnable cross-intersecting hypergraphs.** Implemented exact bit-mask incidence and covering checks. A deterministic random pass checked 1,401 saturated pairs with equal full support at parameter combinations `(n,p,r,s)=(10,5,3,3),(12,6,3,3),(14,7,3,3),(12,6,3,4),(14,7,4,4)`, with 11,000 total seed draws. No failure occurred. A subsequent Galois NextClosure enumeration completed all 1,355,482 closed pairs for ten vertices, five pin vertices and two 3-uniform families, including 147,395 pairs with equal full support. Every such union had a four-vertex cover. The capped eleven-vertex extension is in `concepts11-search.json` and its full log; its explicit completion flag controls what may be claimed.

Saturating a cross-intersecting pair cannot decrease its covering number or lose existing support, so checking saturated pairs is appropriate. For triples, both the pin and its complement are covers. Hence orders at most nine are automatic; on ten vertices the only nonautomatic pin size is five. This is bounded diagnostic evidence, not a general proof and not a discovery-credit claim. A useful structural filter is that any cross pair sharing at least two vertices gives a cover of size at most `r+s-2` immediately. A genuine counterexample must have every cross-intersection of size exactly one. The source's singleton-intersection constructions and its composition mechanisms must be excluded before promoting any future example.

**360496 — simple orderability.** Enumerated every inverse-free subset of C29 (4,782,969 ternary choices; 164,928 zero-sum sets of size at least three) and C31 (14,348,907 choices; 462,866 admissible sets). All admitted simple orderings. An additional 14,000 seeded draws in seven odd-order abelian groups produced 7,117 admissible sets, all passing, including noncyclic C3^4 and C7^2 and sets of sizes through 22. Counts are draws, not distinct isomorphism classes. The DFS fixes a first element by zero-sum cyclic rotation symmetry and forbids repeated proper prefix sums including zero. No all-group construction or obstruction emerged.

**360000 — type-B transitive partitions.** Built a constraint solver on literal integer B_n roots, fixing distinct simple-root colours and imposing exact rank-two positive-cone constraints. It reproduces source counts through rank six and gives `1165, 4135, 14845, 53791, 196417` at ranks seven through eleven, agreeing with the conjectured formula. Full constrained assignment enumeration completed in every recorded rank. The source reports checking only through six. These are **diagnostic CSP counts**: the equivalence of rank-two closure with the full positive-cone definition has not been supplied as a proved semantic bridge here. There is no disagreement to promote and no uniform enumeration proof.

**360511 — hypercube geodesics.** Checked every unrestricted Q3 edge colouring (4,096), every antipodal Q4 colouring (65,536), 100,000 seeded unrestricted Q4 assignments and 20,000 antipodal Q5 assignments. All passed their respective one-change/monochromatic targets. Hollom's May 2026 bound is asymptotically weaker than the reserved claim, so it is not misclassified as a resolution.

**361217 — NV2 positivity.** Recovered the actual forward spectral domain S(N), its distinction from the extended domain, and the determinant definitions. Crucially, the source's vector pairing is the indefinite symmetric form `u1*w2+u2*w1`, not a positive dot product. A proposed naive Gram argument therefore does not establish positivity. Sampling arbitrary positive vectors in the extended space would not refute the reserved statement. No such invalid counterexample or routine small-N source calculation is presented as a candidate.
