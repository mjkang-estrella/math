A finite system of inequalities has a relatively open feasible ball in the intersection of two independent supporting hyperplanes if there is a point at which exactly those two inequalities vanish and all others are strict. Restrict a sufficiently small ball to the hyperplanes; finitely many positive slacks remain positive. Its face therefore has dimension D-2. Conversely three independent forced equalities preclude a ridge. This proves the dimension criterion used below, without a solver or a numerical rank test.

N_A,N_B: set the two selected coordinates to zero and all others to one. For m>=3 every simplex has at least m>=3 positive coordinates, so every L is strict.
