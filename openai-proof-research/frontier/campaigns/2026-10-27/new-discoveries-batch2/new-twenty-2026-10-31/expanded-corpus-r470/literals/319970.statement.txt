Let $\widetilde{{\mathfrak A}}_{n}\left(\substack{\displaystyle{\niv{\scriptstyle 1{\kern 1.75pt}2}{\kern 1.75pt}{\scriptstyle 3}} \\ \displaystyle{\vinb{\scriptstyle 2{\kern 1.75pt}1{\kern 1.75pt}3}}}\right)$ be the set of permutations in the relevant pattern-avoidance equivalence classes, and for $\delta$ in this set let the location of $1$ in $\delta$ denote its position. **Equivalent to RH.** The inequality

$$
\sum_{\delta \in \widetilde{{\mathfrak A}}_{n}\left(\substack{\displaystyle{\niv{\scriptstyle 1{\kern 1.75pt}2}{\kern 1.75pt}{\scriptstyle 3}} \\ \displaystyle{\vinb{\scriptstyle 2{\kern 1.75pt}1{\kern 1.75pt}3}}}\right)} (\textrm{location of $1$ in $\delta$}) < e^\gamma \log \log n
$$

holds for all $n \geq 5041$. By Robin's theorem, this is a reformulation of the Riemann Hypothesis through the sum-of-divisors function, since the displayed sum equals $\sigma(n)$.
