For each $n\geq 0$, let $w_n(t)$ be the colour-imbalance generating Laurent polynomial for checkerboard triangulations of the marked polygon $P_n$, and define the homogeneous polynomial

$$
Q_n(u,v)=\sqrt{uv}^{\,n}\,w_n\left(\sqrt{\frac{u}{v}}\right)\in\mathbb N[u,v].
$$

The coefficient of $u^\alpha v^\beta$ counts checkerboard triangulations involving $\alpha$ black and $\beta$ white triangles.

**Real-rootedness and positivity conjecture.** (i) The polynomials $Q_0(u,1),Q_1(u,1),\dots\in\mathbb N[u]$ have only real roots, all of which are non-positive, and the non-zero roots of $Q_n(u,1)$ interleave the non-zero roots of $Q_{n+3}(u,1)$. (ii) The symmetric polynomial $Q_n(u,v)+Q_n(v,u)$ is of the form $R_n(u+v,uv)$, where $R_n(e_1,e_2)\in\mathbb Z[e_1,e_2]$ involves only positive coefficients.

These are experimental conjectural properties concerning the root geometry and coefficient positivity of the triangulation polynomials. The source gives no proof or resolution for either assertion.
