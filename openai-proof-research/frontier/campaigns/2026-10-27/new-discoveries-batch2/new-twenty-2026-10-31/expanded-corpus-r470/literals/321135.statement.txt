Let $S_k$ denote the set of permutations of length $k$, let $\binom{S_k}{3}$ denote its three-element subsets, and let $\alpha_B(n)$ be the number of permutations of length $n$ avoiding every pattern in $B$. For a fixed pattern length $k$, define

$$
B=\{1 2 \ldots k,2 3 \ldots k 1,k 1 2 \ldots (k-1)\}.
$$

**Maximality conjecture for triples.** The pattern set $B$ is the maximal pattern set among sets of three patterns, in the sense that

$$
\alpha_B(n)\geq\alpha_{B'}(n)
$$

for all $B'\in\binom{S_k}{3}$ and all $n$. This conjecture is based on empirical evidence in the source, which gives no resolution.
