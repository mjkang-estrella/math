Let $G$ be a simple graph. For graphs $A$ and $B$, let $\operatorname{Hom}^{\mathrm{surj}}(A,B)$ be the set of graph homomorphisms whose vertex maps are surjective, and write $\hom^{\mathrm{surj}}(A,B)=|\operatorname{Hom}^{\mathrm{surj}}(A,B)|$.

**Surjective homomorphism swapping conjecture.** For every positive integer $i$,

$$
\hom^{\mathrm{surj}}(G\sqcup G,K_i)\leq \hom^{\mathrm{surj}}(G\times K_2,K_i).
$$

This is presented as a coefficientwise strengthening of the comparison between $P(G\sqcup G,q)$ and $P(G\times K_2,q)$, and the source notes that it would imply the conjecture that $K_q$ is strongly GT. The resolution status is not specified.
