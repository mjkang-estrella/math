           Let Λ𝑘𝑛 denote the class of (0, 1) square matrices containing in each row and in each column exactly 𝑘 1’s. The minimal value of 𝑘,
           for which the behavior of the permanent in Λ𝑘𝑛 is not quite studied, is 𝑘 = 3. We give a simple algorithm for calculation of upper
           magnitudes of permanent in Λ3𝑛 and consider some extremal problems in a generalized class Λ 𝑛 (𝛼, 𝛽, 𝛾), the matrices of which
           contain in each row and in each column nonzero elements 𝛼, 𝛽, and 𝛾 and 𝑛 − 3 zeros.
