# There is no mni clutter whose core is the projective plane of order 3

ImagePaulo TavaresImageJoão Luís Soares

Download full text [Button: Copy citation]

## Abstract

In 1998, Lütolf and Margot claimed, in a sidenote, that the projective plane of order $3$3, also called the ternary plane, or $PG(2,3$P G(2,3), cannot be the core of any minimally non-ideal clutter. In this paper, we show that, if $\operatorname{\cal C}$C is a minimally non-ideal clutter whose core is the $PG(2,3)$P G(2,3) then $\operatorname{\cal C}$C must be the blocker of the $PG(2,3)$P G(2,3). Since no projective plane of order $n \geq 3$n≥3 is minimally non-ideal, the nonexistence of $\operatorname{\cal C}$C is a consequence of the fact that a clutter is minimally non-ideal if and only its blocker is minimally non-ideal.

## Abstract

Lehman's theorem on the structure of minimally nonideal clutters is a fundamental result in polyhedral combinatorics. One approach to extending it has been to give a common generalization with the characterization of minimally imperfect clutters (Sebö, 1998; Gasparyan etal., 2003). We give a new generalization of this kind, which combines two types of covering inequalities and works well with the natural definition of minors. We also show how to extend the notion of idealness to unit-increasing set functions, in a way that is compatible with minors and blocking operations.
