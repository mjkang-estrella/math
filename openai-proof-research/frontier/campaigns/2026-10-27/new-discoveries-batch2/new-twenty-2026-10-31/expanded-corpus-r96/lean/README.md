## Modules (dependency order)

- `ASEPData`: the table `4x*`, the 8 tight cuts, and 5040 base-128 encoded certificates in 48 chunks. Generated by `scripts/asep_lean_data.py`.
- `ASEPCheck`: the decoder and the Boolean checker `chk`.
- `ASEPCover`: proves `certData.map (·.tl) = List.permutations' [1..7]` by kernel `decide`.
- `ASEPCerts0` … `ASEPCerts11`: 48 kernel `decide` theorems `chunkK_ok`.
- `ASEPDefs`: P_ASEP (matrix form), tours, vertices, adjacency, and the conjecture.
- `ASEPSound`: the generic lemma `not_extreme_of_dir`. A direction in the minimal face that is not parallel to x - T defeats extremality.
- `ASEPVertex`: `xs`, membership, the gap facts, uniqueness `eq_xs`, and `xs_extreme`. Generated by `scripts/asep_lean_vertex.py`.
- `ASEPBridge`: soundness of `chk` (`not_extreme_of_chk`).
- `ASEPMain`: every 8-cycle is the successor map of some certificate; this module holds the main theorems.
- `ASEPArcs`: the arc-space definitions and the transfer.

`PermOrder.lean` is a helper that exports the order of `List.permutations'` (`perm-order.txt`). It is not part of the proof.

## Replay

`python3 scripts/asep_replay.py NEW_DIR` (run from the lane directory) does the following:

1. Copies the sources into a fresh directory.
2. Sanitises the environment (`LEAN_PATH` holds only the pinned libraries plus the output directory).
3. Compiles every module with `-DwarningAsError=true` under `scripts/rsscap.py` (RSS ≤ 6 GiB, CPU 120 s, wall 140 s, `-j2`).
4. Runs `leanchecker` on every module.
5. Scans for `sorry`/`admit`/`axiom`/`unsafe`/`native_decide`/`ofReduceBool`/`implemented_by`/`extern`.
6. Audits the `#print axioms` output.

The current replay is `../lean-replay-3/result.json`. It reports `all_ok: true`, and every endpoint depends only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

Trust limits: mathlib and the other dependencies are not rebuilt (their prebuilt oleans are used). `leanchecker` runs per module without `--fresh`. Kernel `decide` proofs are checked by the kernel; no `native_decide` or compiler trust is involved.
