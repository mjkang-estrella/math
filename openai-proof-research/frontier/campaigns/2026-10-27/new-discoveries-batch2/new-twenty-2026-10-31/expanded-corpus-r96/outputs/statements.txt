===== 341896 Minuscule P-partition rowmotion orbit conjecture
Let

$$
(P,Q) \in \{(\Lambda_{\mathrm{Gr}(k,n)},T_{k,n}),(\Lambda_{\mathrm{OG}(6,12)},\Phi^+(H_3)),(\Lambda_{\mathbb{Q}^{2n}},\Phi^+(I_2(2n)))\}
$$

be a minuscule doppelgänger pair, and let $\mathrm{PP}^{\ell}(P)$ denote the $P$-partitions of height $\ell$.

**Minuscule P-partition rowmotion orbit conjecture.** For every $\ell\geq 1$, there is a bijection $\varphi$ between the $\mathrm{row}$-orbits of $\mathrm{PP}^{\ell}(P)$ and those of $\mathrm{PP}^{\ell}(Q)$ such that, for each orbit $\mathcal O\subseteq\mathrm{PP}^{\ell}(P)$,

$$
\#\mathcal O=\#\varphi(\mathcal O),
$$

and

$$
\sum_{T\in\mathcal O}\mathrm{ddeg}(T)=\sum_{T\in\varphi(\mathcal O)}\mathrm{ddeg}(T).
$$

The conjecture was checked computationally for several small cases, including $\ell=2,3,4$, but the paper states that the known doppelgänger bijection does not commute with rowmotion when $\ell>1$ and offers no proof.

===== 347539 Coefficientwise Hankel-total positivity conjecture for the GKP recurrence
Let $P_n(x;\bm{\mu})$ be the row-generating polynomials of the GKP recurrence, with parameters $x,\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\alpha',\beta',\gamma'$. A sequence of polynomials is **coefficientwise Hankel-totally positive** when every minor of its Hankel matrix has nonnegative coefficients in the indicated indeterminates.

**Coefficientwise Hankel-total positivity conjecture for the GKP recurrence.** The sequence

$$
\bm{P}=(P_n(x;\bm{\mu}))_{n\geq 0}
$$

of row-generating polynomials of the GKP recurrence is coefficientwise Hankel-totally positive, jointly in the seven indeterminates $x,\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\alpha',\beta',\gamma'$.

The conjecture had been confirmed computationally through the $8\times 8$ Hankel matrix $(P_{i+j}(x;\bm{\mu}))_{0\leq i,j\leq 7}$, but no proof or general resolution is given.
===== 349136 The graph positivity conjecture for regular bipartite graphs
Let $G$ be an $r$-regular bipartite graph with $v=2n$ vertices, let $m_i$ be its number of $i$-matchings, and define

$$
d(i)=\ln\left(\frac{m_i}{r^i}\right)-\ln\left(\frac{\overline{m}_i}{(v-1)^i}\right),
$$

where $\overline{m}_i=\frac{v!}{(v-2i)!\,i!\,2^i}$ is the number of $i$-matchings in the complete graph on the same vertices. Let $\Delta d(i)=d(i+1)-d(i)$, and call $G$ **graph positive** when $\Delta^k d(i)\geq0$ for all meaningful $k$ and $i$.

**The graph positivity conjecture.** As $n$ goes to infinity, the fraction of $r$-regular bipartite graphs with $2n$ vertices that are graph positive approaches one.

This conjecture is supported by numerical evidence, including that all graphs with fewer than $14$ vertices satisfy graph positivity. The paper studies the weaker graph positivity conjecture rather than proving this full assertion.
===== 349406 The symmetry-symbol conjecture for normalized non-exceptional PPSs
Let $v$ be odd. A normalized **propus parameter set (PPS)** is a parameter set $(v;k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4;\lambda)$ for which the associated PDF has $X_2=X_3$; it is **non-exceptional** when the four block sizes $k_i$ are not all equal. A PDF has symmetry symbols $(s{**})$ or $({**}s)$ when, respectively, its first or fourth block is symmetric and the other indicated blocks have arbitrary symmetry symbols.

**Symmetry-symbol conjecture.** For each normalized and non-exceptional PPS $(v;k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4;\lambda)$ there exist PDFs with symmetry symbols $(s{**})$ and $({**}s)$.

The conjecture is implicit in earlier work and had been verified there for odd $v\leq 53$; the source considers it in the context of constructing symmetric Hadamard matrices and lists it for the subsequent range of odd parameters.
===== 349717 Asymptotic strengthened convexity conjecture for d-th power partitions
Let $d\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq 2}$, and let $p_d(n)$ denote the number of partitions of $n$ into $d$-th powers, defined by

$$
\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-q^{k^d}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_d(n)q^n.
$$

A sequence $(a_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ is convex when $2a_n\leq a_{n-1}+a_{n+1}$ for $n\geq 1$. **Strengthened convexity conjecture.** There is an integer $N_d$ such that, for all $n\geq N_d$,

$$
2p_d(n)\leq\bigl(p_d(n-1)+p_d(n+1)\bigr)\left(1-\frac{1}{n^d}\right).
$$

In particular, $(p_d(n))_{n\geq N_d}$ is convex. This is presented as a generalization of the log-concavity behavior known for the classical partition function. The inequality was checked computationally for $d=2,3,4$ over substantial ranges, while the source gives no proof or general resolution and notes that the corresponding threshold for $d=5$ appears to be at least $10^6$.
===== 350489 Universal generator-set conjecture for circulant nut graphs
For a finite set $S$ of positive integers, call $S$ **universal** when $\operatorname{Circ}(n,S)$ is a nut graph for every even $n\geq 2\max(S)+2$. Here $\operatorname{Circ}(n,S)$ is the circulant graph with vertex set $\{0,1,\dots,n-1\}$ and edges $(i,i\pm s)$ modulo $n$ for $s\in S$.

**Universal generator-set conjecture.** For each odd $t\geq 3$, there exists $p_t$ such that $\operatorname{Circ}(n,\{1,\dots,2t+1\}\setminus\{p_t\})$ is a nut graph for every even $n\geq 4t+4$. For each even $t\geq 4$, there exist $q_t$ and $r_t$ such that $\operatorname{Circ}(n,\{1,\dots,2t+2\}\setminus\{q_t,r_t\})$ is a nut graph for every even $n\geq 4t+6$.

The conjecture extends the theorem covering many odd values of $t$ and is motivated by computational evidence through $t=1300$ for the remaining odd cases and for even $t$; a general proof is not supplied.
===== 350950 Sharpness conjecture for characteristic-polynomial congruence bounds of q-Seidel matrices
Let $\mathcal X_n(q,e)$ and $\mathcal X'_n(q,e)$ denote the sets of congruence classes of characteristic polynomials of $q$-Seidel matrices described in Theorems 1 and 2, respectively. For $e\in\mathbb N$ with $e\geqslant 3$ and $q$ a prime power, there exists $N\in\mathbb N$ such that for all $n\geqslant N$ the corresponding upper bounds in those theorems are attained with equality.

**Sharpness conjecture.** For $e \in \mathbb N$ with $e \geqslant 3$ and $q$ a prime power, there exists $N \in \mathbb N$ such that for all $n \geqslant N$ we have equality in the corresponding bounds in Theorem~ and Theorem~.

The conjecture is motivated by computer experiments for $n=21$ and $e=3,4$, which provide empirical evidence that the bounds are sharp for fixed $q$ and sufficiently large $n$ relative to $e$.
===== 363770 Balogh et al.'s linear path-separating-system conjecture
Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices. A **path separating system** of $G$ is a collection of paths in $G$ such that, for every ordered pair of distinct edges $(e,f)$, some path contains $e$ but not $f$. **Balogh et al.'s conjecture.** Every graph of order $n$ admits a path separating system of size $\operatorname{O}(n)$. This strengthened conjecture was confirmed in 2023, when a separating path system of size at most $19n$ was proved for every graph on $n$ vertices.
===== 366840 Hajdu–Sárközy's multiplicative irreducibility conjecture for perturbed shifted powers
Let $R$ be obtained from the set $\{x^k+1:x\in\mathbb{N}\}$ by changing $o(X^{1/k})$ elements up to $X$, where $k\geq 2$. A set $S\subset\mathbb{N}$ is **multiplicatively irreducible** if it cannot be written as a product set $AB=\{ab:a\in A,b\in B\}$ with $A,B\subset\mathbb{N}$ both having size at least $2$. **Hajdu and Sárközy's conjecture.** The new set $R$ is always multiplicatively irreducible. This is a multiplicative analogue of Erdős's conjecture on small perturbations of the squares. The paper's abstract says that a more general version is confirmed for $k\geq 3$, while the conjecture as stated here is not accompanied by a resolution for all $k\geq 2$.
===== 366914 Linear realizations for small $k$
Let $L$ be a multiset of linear lengths, where $\{1^a,(y-k)^b,y^c\}$ denotes the multiset containing $a$, $b$, and $c$ copies of $1$, $y-k$, and $y$, respectively. A multiset is **linearly realizable** if it is the multiset of linear lengths of a Hamiltonian path in a complete graph whose vertices are labelled consecutively. **Linear-realization conjecture for small $k$.** When

$$
k<y/2,
$$

multisets of the form

$$
\{1^a,(y-k)^b,y^c\}
$$

are linearly realizable for all $a\geq y$. The conjecture is presented as a possible next extension of the paper's methods and as a step toward proving the Coprime BHR conjecture for supports of size three; the source does not state that it has been resolved.
===== 367165 Fixed-perimeter inequality for parts divisible by and repeated at least k times
Let $FO_{j,k}(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ with exactly $j$ parts divisible by $k$, and let $FD_{j,k}(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ with exactly $j$ parts appearing at least $k$ times. For $j\geq 0$ and $k\geq 2$, **the fixed-perimeter inequality conjecture.**

$$
FD_{j,k}(n)\geq FO_{j,k}(n)
$$

for sufficiently large $n$.

The equality $FO_{j,2}(n)=FD_{j,2}(n)$ is known for all $n$, but for $k\geq 2$ the two quantities are not equal for all $n$ in general. The stated inequality is supported by computational evidence and concerns the eventual comparison for general $k$.
===== 368042 Chordal graph conjecture for square-free powers
Let $G$ be a chordal graph, let $I(G)$ be its edge ideal, let $I(G)^{[k]}$ denote its $k$-th square-free power, let $\nu(G)$ be its matching number, and let $\mathrm{aim}(G,k)$ be its $k$-admissible matching number. **Chordal graph conjecture.** For all $1\leq k\leq\nu(G)$, one has

$$
\mathrm{reg}(I(G)^{[k]})=\mathrm{aim}(G,k)+k.
$$

The equality is known for block graphs and for $k=2$ when $G$ is Cohen–Macaulay and chordal; computations also support it for chordal graphs with at most eight vertices. The assertion remains open in general.
===== 368627 The non-elementary abelian group obstruction to 2-designs
Let $G=\mathbb{Z}_{p^{t_{1}}} \oplus \mathbb{Z}_{p^{t_{2}}} \oplus\cdots \oplus\mathbb{Z}_{p^{t_{m}}}$ be a non-elementary $p$-abelian group, with some $t_i>1$. For an integer $k$ and an element $x\in G$, let $(G,\mathcal{B}_k^x)$ denote the associated incidence structure.

**Non-elementary group conjecture.** For any $(k,x)$ with $1\le k\le n-1$ and $x\in G$, the incidence structure $(G,\mathcal{B}_k^x)$ is not a $2$-design.

Computational verification via MAGMA suggests that non-elementary abelian $p$-groups fail to produce $2$-designs for all nontrivial parameter pairs, in contrast with the elementary abelian case. The conjecture excludes the trivial case $k=|G|$ and remains open.
===== 368807 The neighborhood-tour conjecture for ASEP vertices
Let $P_{\text{ASEP}}^{n}$ be the asymmetric subtour elimination polytope, let $\boldsymbol{x}$ be a vertex of this polytope, and let $\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{x})$ denote its neighborhood in the vertex structure considered by the paper. A **tour** is a Hamiltonian cycle of the complete directed graph on the node set. **Neighborhood-tour conjecture.** For every vertex $\boldsymbol{x}$, the neighborhood $\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{x})$ contains at least one tour. This is presented as a conjecture based on preliminary computational tests; the supplied text does not establish it or indicate a resolution.
===== 370189 The harmonious labelling conjecture
Let $T$ be a tree with $n$ edges, and let $\mathbb{Z}_n$ denote the cyclic group of order $n$. A labelling is a map $\phi\colon V(T)\to \mathbb{Z}_n$; edge sums are the values $\phi(x)+\phi(y)$ for $xy\in E(T)$.

**The harmonious labelling conjecture.** For every $n$-edge tree $T$, there exists a labelling $\phi\colon V(T)\to \mathbb{Z}_n$ such that the edge sums $\phi(x)+\phi(y)$ are all distinct, and exactly one label is used on two vertices.

This is the cyclic additive labelling conjecture of Graham and Sloane. The paper states that it is confirmed for bounded-degree trees, but the unrestricted conjecture remains open.
===== 375325 Refined scaffold exchange symmetry conjecture
Fix $n\ge1$, a diagonal multiset $m$ realizable by an area word of size $n$, $k\ge0$, $r\ge1$, a scaffold $\Xi$, and a matrix $g=(g_{t,\delta})$ recording per-gap, per-diagonal numbers of undecorated active rows. Let $\Phi^{(r)}_{m,k,\Xi,g}(u,v;q)$ be the corresponding refined class generating function. **Refined scaffold exchange conjecture.** For every such $n,m,k,r,\Xi,g$, with incompatible data and inadmissible $g$ assigned value $0$, the generating function $\Phi^{(r)}_{m,k,\Xi,g}(u,v;q)$ is symmetric in $u$ and $v$. This strengthens the scaffold exchange conjecture by retaining per-gap and per-diagonal data. Computations support it, including area-two cases, but it remains open.
===== 375831 Per-split sub-top coefficient conjecture for Miura-ori flip-graph counts
Let $d\ge5$, let $(a,b)$ satisfy $a+b=d$ and $a\le b$, and let $R_{(a,b)}(m,n)$ be the interior remainder. Denote by $[m^{d-3}]R_{(a,b)}(m,n)$ the coefficient of $m^{d-3}$; by symmetry, this is equivalently the coefficient of $n^{d-3}$. **Sub-top coefficient conjecture.**

$$
[m^{d-3}]R_{(a,b)}(m,n)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{8(1+\delta_{ab})}{(d-3)!}&\text{if }|a-b|\le1,\\[2pt]0&\text{if }|a-b|>1.
\end{cases}
$$

The conjecture refines the proposed degree bound by specifying the pure sub-top coefficient for every split; the stated vanishing has been checked in the finite cases reported in the source, but a general proof remains open.
===== 376197 GHV upper-bound conjecture for Stanley–Reisner ideals of matroids
Let $M$ be a matroid and let $I_{\Delta(M)}$ be its Stanley–Reisner ideal. Write $\widehat{\rho}(I_{\Delta(M)})$ for its asymptotic resurgence, $\omega(I_{\Delta(M)})$ for the largest degree of a minimal generator, and $\widehat{\alpha}(I_{\Delta(M)})$ for its Waldschmidt constant. **GHV upper-bound conjecture.**

$$
\widehat{\rho}(I_{\Delta(M)})\le\frac{\omega(I_{\Delta(M)})}{\widehat{\alpha}(I_{\Delta(M)})}.
$$

For matroid Stanley–Reisner ideals, $\omega(I_{\Delta(M)})$ is the largest size of a circuit of $M$. The bound is known for ideals of smooth subschemes and has been checked in the source for all simple matroids on ground sets of size at most $8$, but is conjectured in general.
===== 382492 Generalization of the Amdeberhan–Shareshian–Stanley conjecture for partition division maps
Let $\mu\vdash kn$. The symmetric function $\operatorname{rowDiv}_k(\mathrm{e}_\mu)$ is $\mathrm{e}$-positive with non-negative integer coefficients.

**Generalized Amdeberhan–Shareshian–Stanley conjecture.** For every partition $\mu$ of $kn$, the symmetric function obtained by applying the partition division map $\operatorname{rowDiv}_k$ to the elementary symmetric function $\mathrm{e}_\mu$ has a non-negative integer expansion in the elementary basis.

This conjecture extends the Amdeberhan–Shareshian–Stanley positivity phenomenon for partition division maps. The displayed computation provides an example, but the general assertion remains unresolved.
