 Multiplicative irreducibility of small perturbations of the set of shifted k -th powers

 Abstract. 
 
 
 Motivated by a conjecture of Erdős on the additive irreducibility of small perturbations of the set of squares, recently Hajdu and Sárközy studied a multiplicative analogue of the conjecture for shifted k -th powers. They conjectured that for each k\geq 2 , if one changes o(X^{1/k}) elements of M_{k}^{\prime}=\{x^{k}+1:x\in\mathbb{N}\} up to X , then the resulting set cannot be written as a product set AB nontrivially. In this paper, we confirm a more general version of their conjecture for k\geq 3 . 
 
 
 
 
 Key words and phrases: multiplicative decomposition, shifted powers
