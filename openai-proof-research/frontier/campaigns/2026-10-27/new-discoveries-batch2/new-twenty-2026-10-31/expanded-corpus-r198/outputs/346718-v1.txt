is characterized, 3) the so-called single and two-point algo-
rithms in [11] and [16] are extended to the multi-variable
case, and 4) a toolbox is developed to easily design and
apply the novel class of optimization algorithms to uncon-
strained optimization problems.                                                                    −∇ J
