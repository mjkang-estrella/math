                                               A complete subgraph of a given graph is called a clique. A clique Polynomial of a graph is
                                           a generating function of the number of cliques in G. A real root of the clique polynomial of a
                                           graph G is called a clique root of G.

                                           clique root in [−1, 0). As a generalization of their result, the author of this paper showed that

                                           A given graph G is called flat if each edge of G belongs to at most two triangles of G. In

                                           edges has r = −1 as one of it’s clique roots. We finally present some interesting open questions

2.2     Flat Graphs
Next, we introduce a new class of graphs which can be of special interest for our purposes.
They are triangulated graphs but they are not (necessarily) chordal. We call them flat graphs
[5].
Definition 2.2. A graph G is called flat if every edge of G belongs to at most two triangles of
G.
Example 2.2. The wheel W5 is a simple example of a flat graph which is clearly not a chordal
graph.
   For the sequel purposes, we need the following generalization of the concept of a degree of
a vertex.
Definition 2.3 (see [6]). Let G = (V, E) be a graph and e = {u, v} ∈ E be an edge of G. The
value of e in G denoted by valG (e) is defined as follows:

                                   valG (e) = NG (u) ∩ NG (v).

An edge e ∈ E with valG (e) = 0 is called an isolated edge of G.
