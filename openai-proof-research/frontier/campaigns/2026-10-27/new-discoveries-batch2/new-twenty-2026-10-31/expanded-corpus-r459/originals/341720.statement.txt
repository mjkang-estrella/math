Let $G=(V,E)$ be a multigraph. Define its density by

$$
\Gamma(G)=\max\left\{\frac{2|E(U)|}{|U|-1}:U\subseteq V,\ |U|\geq 3\text{ and odd}\right\},
$$

and call $G$ **elementary** when $\chi'(G)=\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil$.

**Elementary-multigraph conjecture.** Every multigraph $G$ with

$$
\chi'(G)\geq\Delta(G)+2
$$

is elementary.

The source presents this as a reformulation of the Goldberg–Seymour conjecture, which was proved in the paper; hence this formulation is solved as well.
