Let $\mathcal{G}(n,p)$ denote the Erdős–Rényi random graph, let $\mathcal{G}(n,p,d)$ denote the random geometric graph in dimension $d$, and let $p_0$ be the associated comparison parameter. Suppose that

$$
p\sim c_0/n \quad\text{for some } c_0>0,
$$

and

$$
d\sim c_1n\log^2 n \quad\text{for some } c_1>0.
$$

**Inclusion-divergence conjecture.** Under these assumptions,

$$
\operatorname{I-Div}\big(\mathcal{G}(n,p,d)\,\|\,\mathcal{G}(n,p_0)\big)\to 0.
$$
