Let $R_n$ be the squared Reed–Muller code matrix and $U^n=R_nX^n$. Consider an index $i$ that is the last row in a degree layer of $R_n$, excluding the last layer; then $i+1$ is the first row in the next layer.

**Layer-crossing conjecture.**

$$
H(U_i\mid U^{i-1},Y^n)\ge H(U_{i+1}\mid U^i,Y^n).
$$

Thus, conditional entropy should be non-decreasing when crossing from one degree layer to the next. By the symmetry of Reed–Muller codes, the source states that this conjecture implies the stronger twin-Reed–Muller ordering conjecture. It is presented as an open problem.
