Let $r$ be a positive integer and let $G$ be an $r$-locally $3$-connected graph, meaning that every separation of $G$ of order at most $r$ has the prescribed local $3$-connectivity property. An edge of $G$ can be deleted or contracted while preserving $r$-local $3$-connectivity. **Splitter theorem.** Every $r$-locally $3$-connected graph contains an edge to delete or contract such that $r$-local $3$-connectivity is preserved, unless $G$ is $K_4$. This is proposed as a splitter theorem for $r$-locally $3$-connected graphs, analogous to splitter theorems for other minor-closed classes and matroids; the source does not provide evidence that it has been proved or refuted.
