Let $D$ be the presheaf of hypergraphs and let $C\subset D$ be a **proper** presheaf, meaning that disjoint union preserves membership in $C$. For each finite vertex set $V$, let $\mathbf{d}C_V$ be the functor obtained from the disjoint deletion process, and call the isomorphisms in its image **weak hypomorphisms**. **Disjoint reconstruction conjecture.** Two hypergraphs in a proper presheaf $C\subset D$ are isomorphic if and only if they are weakly hypomorphic. Equivalently, $\mathbf{d}C_V$ is essentially injective. This conjecture is introduced as an analogue of the ordinary reconstruction conjecture after replacing the diagonal deletion data by disjoint-union deletion data; the source gives no resolution.
