Let $G$ be a graph, let $t$ be an integer, and for $v\in V(G)$ let $N_G[v]$ denote the closed neighborhood of $v$. **Local Hadwiger conjecture.** If $G$ is a graph without a $K_{t+1}$-minor, then there exists $v \in V(G)$ such that $\chi(N_G[v]) \leqslant t$. The source says this follows from the tree-chromatic weakening if that conjecture holds, and notes that it is still open even with the proposed constant-factor relaxation.
