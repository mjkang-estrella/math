Let $I_N=\{1,\dots,N\}$, let $\epsilon>0$, and let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a strictly convex function. For a set $I$, write $f(I)=\{f(i)\mid i\in I\}$. **Erdős's conjecture.**

$$
|f(I_N)+f(I_N)|\gg_{\epsilon}N^{2-\epsilon}.
$$

The conjecture formalises the expectation that convexity disrupts additive structure and therefore makes the sumset of the image of an interval nearly quadratic in size. The paper discusses estimates for convex sets that approach known lower bounds, but the conjectured bound is not stated as resolved here.
