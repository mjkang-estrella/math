Let $G$ be a graph. For an Abelian group $\Gamma$, write $G$ is $\Gamma$-colorable when it has a group coloring with group $\Gamma$; write $\mathbb Z_n$ for the cyclic group of order $n$.

**The $\mathbb Z_3$-coloring conjecture.** If $G$ is $\mathbb Z_3$-colorable, then $G$ is both $\mathbb Z_4$-colorable and $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$-colorable.
