* The McKay-limb construction in Theorem 3.2 supplies the triple spectral equalities. Lemma 3.3 transfers the equal total and root-conditioned geodesic counts of its particular rooted limbs through arbitrary common coalescence. Its syllable bijection preserves the start/end-root classes of nongeodesics. Its conclusion is equal growth, so applying this theorem to its own pair cannot furnish the proposed refutation.
