## 354898 Catalan array
Actual primary text: Teimoori Faal 2206.02758v1, (2.4), Definition 2.1, Section 4 and Conjecture 6.4 (not 6.3). The entire PDF was acquired, but the initial full-text print was truncated; only the explicitly revisited passages are claimed read. Definition has reversed triangular inequalities in one line; examples fix the lower triangular convention. No correction credit.

Route A: If C_{r,k} counts forests of k+1 ordered plane trees having total r-k edges, its column generating series is x^k C(x)^{k+1}, C=1+x C^2. Multiplying column k-1 by x C gives column k, hence C_{r,k}=sum_{l=k-1}^{r-1} C_{r-1-l} C_{l,k-1}. The first column is the Catalan sequence and diagonal is 1. This is an all-parameter constructive proof of vertical recurrence (2.4) under this forest/ballot convention: isolate the first tree to give the convolution, with l chosen by the remaining forest's edge count. It extends beyond a small-row check.

Route B: The source's adjacent admissible Catalan-number array in Section 4 is different: rows (1), (1,1), (2,3,1), (5,9,5,1), (14,28,20,7,1). If this is C_n, its first column forces lambda=(1,1,2,5,...), and (2.4) at (r,k)=(2,1) forces 1*1+1*1=2, contradicting its entry 3. The source instead prints associated sequence (1,2,5,14,...) for that example, which is not its own first column. This incompatibility cannot be silently normalized away. The ballot array starts (1),(1,1),(2,2,1), so Route A and Route B concern different arrays. A claimed solution must first fix which array Conjecture 6.4 means. No numerical enumeration was used.

Aigner 1999 is a concretely applicable companion defining admissible Catalan arrays, DOI 10.1006/jcta.1998.2945. Its institutional PDF GET failed hostname TLS validation, no bypass or retry. Search abstract/snippet is metadata, not a proof-body read. Remaining lemma: identify exact C_n in an authentic definition or authoritative correction and read applicable companion theorem bodies; only then decide proof/refutation and whether the result is routine. HOLD on fidelity, prior and significance; no accepted candidate from ambiguous notation.

## 360904 vertex deletion
Actual source definitions in Section 1.1: finite simple connected cyclic graphs C, valid orientations have positive indegree; DOM_t is the maximum of minimum total dominating set sizes. Read Proposition 2.3 and proof, Lemmas 2.5–2.10 and proofs/discussion. Conjecture 2.7 is exact. Lemma 2.5's old lower-bound proof is explicitly flawed; restating it is not a proof.

First route: start an extremal orientation D of G. If D-v remains valid, take its minimum TD-set T and add one in-neighbor u of v; T union {u} dominates all of D, so gamma_t(D)<=DOM_t(G-v)+1. This proves the easy case completely. But D-v may have zero-indegree vertices W whose only in-neighbor in D was v; G-v in C guarantees SOME valid orientation, not validity of this restriction.

Second route: orient paths in G-v into W to repair these sources. Reversing a directed path can fix one source but changes the in-neighborhood constraints along the path. There is no proved injection taking a TD-set of the repaired orientation back to a set for D with loss at most one. Multiple vertices in W all require v in D, but the new constraints may let the repaired orientation have a smaller TD-set by more than one. Exact missing repair lemma: for every valid D on G with G-v in C, find a valid D' on G-v with gamma_t(D')>=gamma_t(D)-1, or construct a graph for which every valid D' violates this. Connectivity/cycle existence alone does not supply it.

Third route: private neighborhoods of a minimum TD-set S are nonempty. If v is outside S, source Lemma 2.10 gives a good deletion; its existential v does not pay arbitrary prescribed v. Trying to exchange v out of S requires distinct compatible private-neighbor replacements and can increase size. Unicyclic graphs are paid by Proposition 2.3 when deletion remains connected cyclic: deletable vertices are leaves and the nonleaf count changes by at most one. This does not pay multicyclic graphs.

Actual later companion Blazsik–Nagy final DAM378 (2026), DOI10.1016/j.dam.2025.07.021, received Nov7 2024/revised and accepted July15 2025, was acquired and its definitions/main theorem read. It classifies DOM_t=order-1, not arbitrary vertex deletion. Its existence is not a blanket exclusion. Original paper's final DOI10.1016/j.dam.2025.11.031 has publication metadata March31 2026 but final body GET403; no retry. General route remains HOLD; no unverified certificate or finite sweep.
