### Exact substitution and the unpaid comparison

Set m=2^n, G=K_(m,m), H=Q_n. Both graphs are regular; deg(G)=m=|V(H)|. The graph has 2m clusters and 2m^2 vertices, and each actual vertex has one cross-edge. For base edge L_x R_y the physical port is y in L_x and x in R_y. For each fixed cluster these assignments are bijections from incident base edges to its m ports. These facts establish the compound-graph STRUCTURAL prerequisites only.

In L_x install all supplied T_i using tau_x(a)=a+x; in R_y install them using tau_y(a)=a+y. XOR translations are genuine automorphisms of H: two strings differ in precisely one coordinate before translation iff they do afterward. The actual cross-edge L_x R_y meets template coordinate y+x on the left and x+y on the right. Thus the transformed incidence labels are y -> x+y at L_x and x -> x+y at R_y, and both agree with the same template coordinate.

This is NOT a claim that independent cluster translations are automorphisms of the whole fixed dual-cube. Holding base cluster names fixed and translating the two physical endpoints can destroy their cross-edge pairing. One must transport the incident-port maps as well, or use the actual physical cross-edges after placing the local trees. A theorem asserting existence for freely chosen port wiring cannot simply be applied to this prescribed wiring. Whether the 2020 proof permits exactly this transport is the material unknown.

For supplied CIST let S_i=Int(T_i). The reviewed coordinate lemma gives s_j,t_j in S_i with s_j+t_j=e_j for each coordinate. Consequently the compatible base graph C_i, with edge L_x R_y iff x+y in S_i, contains the two-edge walk L_x,R_(x+s_j),L_(x+e_j). These walks connect its left class; nonempty S_i connects every right vertex. Distinct S_i make the C_i edge-disjoint. Choose a spanning tree B_i of each C_i. Install the translated local trees and select the ACTUAL cross-edges indexed by B_i. Connectivity lifts from B_i; every selected cross-edge is a bridge by its base-tree cut, so the union is acyclic. Added edges meet only already internal vertices, hence Int(U_i)={(b,x,y):x+y in S_i}. Common translations preserve local edge disjointness, and the B_i keep cross-edges disjoint. The path/internal-set equivalence yields k CIST. This identifies every bridge a subsumption proof must cover; it does not attribute any of them to the unread 2020 paper.

For k>=2 the coordinate lemma uses another tree: absence of an internal j-edge confines S_i to a half cube, domination makes it that full half and forces all j-matching edges into T_i; the other tree's disjoint internal set similarly forces the same matching, contradiction. Nonempty internal sets alone do not give C_i connectivity. No claim that degree/order equality alone proves the lifting is justified. k=1 and the zero-dimensional endpoint are covered in O1 and the frozen ordinary proof.
