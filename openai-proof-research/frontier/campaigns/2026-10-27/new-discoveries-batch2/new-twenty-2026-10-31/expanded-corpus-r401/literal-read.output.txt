346085 Let $G$ be a primitive permutation group of degree $n$, and let $D(G)$ be the subgroup generated by the derangements in $G$. A **Frobenius group** is a transitive permutation group whose nonidentity point stabilisers fix no point outside their stabilised point. **The primitive derangement index conjecture.** If $G$ is not a Frobenius group, then

$$
|G:D(G)|\leqslant\sqrt{n}-1;
$$

moreover, this bound is attained only if $G$ is an affine group. The bound is proved for non-affine primitive groups, while the remaining case reduces to primitive affine groups. 

346094 Let $G$ be a plane graph, and let $\overline{G}_{vef}$ denote its combined vertex-edge-face graph. The **Alon–Tarsi conjecture.** Every plane graph $G$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{AT}(\overline{G}_{vef})\leqslant 7.
$$

If true, this would improve the preceding Alon–Tarsi bound for $\overline{G}_{vef}$ and the known list-colouring bound $\operatorname{ch}(\overline{G}_{vef})\leqslant 8$; the source notes that the latter bound's tightness is unknown. 

346136 Let $T$ be a lobster, that is, a tree whose vertices are at distance at most two from a fixed path. An **antimagic labeling** of $T$ is a bijection $\tau:E(T)\rightarrow [|E(T)|]$ such that distinct vertices have different sums of labels on their incident edges. **Lobster antimagic labeling conjecture.** Every lobster admits an antimagic labeling. The paper proves that every lobster admits an antimagic orientation and presents this stronger-looking labeling question as an open problem; its truth is not established in the source. 

346143 Let $H_1$ and $H_2$ be graphs that are (strongly) sat-sharp, and let $H_1+H_2$ denote their disjoint union. **Disjoint-union conjecture.** If $H_1$ and $H_2$ are (strongly) sat-sharp graphs, then $H_1+H_2$ is (strongly) sat-sharp; equivalently, the class of (strongly) sat-sharp graphs is closed under taking disjoint unions. The preceding results establish this behavior for certain classes, including disjoint unions of cliques, but the general closure assertion is presented as plausible and remains open. 

346253 Let $N$ be a non-negative real and $k$ a positive integer. For a square matrix $A$ with non-negative real entries, define its **weight** by

$$
|A|:=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}.
$$

Assume that $A$ is nilpotent and has weight $N$. **Hajac–Tobolski's extremal-product conjecture.** The following three quantities are equal: the maximal weight of $A^k$, the maximal product of $k$ non-negative reals with sum $N$, and

$$
\left(\frac Nk\right)^k.
$$

This is the product formulation of the same nilpotent-matrix extremal claim stated immediately earlier in the source, so it does not add a distinct mathematical assertion; it connects the matrix problem with the balanced-product extremum. 

346311 Let $A$ be a $d$-dimensional matrix of order $n$, let $\Lambda_{d-1}(d,n)$ denote the set of $(d-1)$-permutation matrices, let $\operatorname{Hull}(\Lambda_{d-1}(d,n))$ be their convex hull, let $\operatorname{per_1}(A)$ denote the permanent, and let $J_n^d$ be the $d$-dimensional matrix of order $n$ with all entries equal to $1$. **Dow–Gibson's conjecture.** If

$$
A\in\operatorname{Hull}(\Lambda_{d-1}(d,n)),
$$

then

$$
\operatorname{per_1}(A)\geqslant(n!/n^n)^{d-1},
$$

with equality if and only if

$$
A=n^{1-d}J_n^d.
$$

Dow and Gibson proved the conjecture for order $2$, but the general claim remains open. 

346314 Let $\Omega_1(d,n)$ be the set of $1$-polystochastic $d$-dimensional matrices of order $n$, and let a $(d-1)$-permutation matrix be an element of the corresponding set of $(d-1)$-permutation matrices. **Taranenko's decomposition conjecture.** Every $1$-polystochastic matrix of even dimension can be represented as a non-negative linear combination of $(d-1)$-permutation matrices. The source reports no known counterexamples in even dimensions, so the conjecture remains open. 

346334 Let $\mathsf{HGK}_0$ be identified with some version of compactly supported differential forms on moduli spaces of curves with antisymmetrized marked points. For the forgetful map

$$
\pi:{\rM}_{g,n+1}\to {\rM}_{g,n},
$$

let pullback along $\pi$ act on the corresponding compactly supported differential forms.

**Geometric interpretation conjecture.** Under this identification, the operation $\Delta$ on $\mathsf{HGK}_0$ corresponds to pullback under $\pi$, and this pullback induces a well-defined operation on compactly supported differential forms.

The statement is explicitly presented as a conjectural geometric interpretation, and the source gives no resolution status. The notation is only partially specified in the source. 

346337 Let $\operatorname{\mathscr{P}}$ be a finite poset, not necessarily ranked, with no antichain of size $3$. Its **order polytope** $\operatorname{\mathcal{O}}(\operatorname{\mathscr{P}})$ is the polytope associated with $\operatorname{\mathscr{P}}$ by the order-polytope construction.

**Unranked graphic order polytope conjecture.** The order polytope $\operatorname{\mathcal{O}}(\operatorname{\mathscr{P}})$ is graphic.

This conjecture proposes dropping the ranked hypothesis from the preceding theorem characterizing a class of graphic order polytopes. The paper notes that rankedness is not necessary and gives non-ranked graphic examples, but does not establish the conjecture in general. 

346338 Let $\operatorname{\mathscr{P}}$ be a finite poset. Its **order polytope** $\operatorname{\mathcal{O}}(\operatorname{\mathscr{P}})$ is the polytope associated with $\operatorname{\mathscr{P}}$ by the order-polytope construction. A poset has an **antichain of size $3$** if it contains three pairwise incomparable elements.

**Antichain obstruction conjecture.** If $\operatorname{\mathcal{O}}(\operatorname{\mathscr{P}})$ is projectively unique, then $\operatorname{\mathscr{P}}$ has no antichain of size $3$.

This conjecture asserts that the absence of a three-element antichain is necessary for projective uniqueness of an order polytope. The paper presents it as a proposed necessary condition; its general validity remains open. 

346421 Let $b(n)$ denote the number of partitions of $n$ into distinct parts, and let $s(m)$ denote the number of partitions of $m$ into distinct parts. For integers $n$ and $k$, define the partition arguments in the usual convention that a partition-counting function is zero for a negative argument. **Merca's inequality conjecture.** For $k>0$,

$$
(-1)^{k-1} \left( \sum_{j=-(k-1)}^k (-1)^j b\big(n-j(3j-1)/2\big) -\frac{1+(-1)^n}{2} s\left( \frac{n}{2}\right) \right) \geqslant 0,
$$

with strict inequalities if $n\geqslant2+k(3k+1)/2$. The inequality is presented as a consequence of the preceding strengthened conjecture, so its status follows that conjecture and remains unresolved in the supplied text. 

346430 A **hypersimplex** is the matroid polytope of a uniform matroid, and its dual polytope is the polytope dual to that hypersimplex. A shelling is **extendable** if every partial shelling can be completed to a shelling of the whole polytope. **The hypersimplex conjecture.** The dual polytope of every hypersimplex is extendably shellable. This is presented as a geometric analogue of Simon's conjecture and as a potentially more tractable related problem; the source notes that the conjecture is unresolved and that its relation to extendable shellability of independence complexes is non-equivalent. 

346464 A **nice formula** is a formula whose fragments satisfy the niceness condition studied in the paper. A formula is $3$-avoidable if it can be avoided by an infinite word over a $3$-letter alphabet. **The 3-avoidability conjecture.** Every nice formula is $3$-avoidable. This would generalize the known result for nice formulas with at most three variables and the $3$-avoidability of doubled patterns. 

346476 Let $G=C_{n_1}\cup\cdots\cup C_{n_m}$ be a $2$-regular graph, where each $n_i\ge 3$, and let a graph be **prime** when it admits a labeling by distinct integers from $\{1,2,\ldots,|V(G)|\}$ such that adjacent vertices receive relatively prime labels. **Tout–Dabboucy–Howalla conjecture.** The graph $G$ is prime if and only if at most one of the integers $n_i$ is odd. The necessity is immediate from the independence number, and several partial cases are known; the full classification remains open in the source. 

346587 Let $w$ be a primitive word. A **bi-root** of $w$ is a primitive pair $\{x,y\}$ whose concatenations include $w$. **The half-length uniqueness conjecture.** The word $w$ has at most one bi-root $\{x,y\}$ such that

$$
|x|+|y|<\frac{|w|}{2}.
$$

The preceding theorem proves the analogous uniqueness statement under the stronger bound $|x|+|y|<\sqrt{|w|}$. The supplied text presents the half-length bound as a problem related to the first conjecture, but gives no resolution. 

346596 Let $X$ and $Y$ be iid $\mathbb{R}^d$-valued random variables with log-concave distributions and differential entropy $h$. Their difference $X-Y$ is also $\mathbb{R}^d$-valued.

**Madiman–Kontoyannis conjecture.** The entropy satisfies

$$
h(X-Y)\leq h(X)+d\log 2,
$$

with equality when $X$ and $Y$ have the $d$-dimensional exponential product distribution.

This is the entropic analogue of the Rogers–Shephard inequality for convex bodies. The claim is attributed in the source to Madiman and Kontoyannis; the source provides no resolution status. 

346597 Let $G=(V,E,L)$ be a looped simple graph. For a positive integer $k$, let $G^{[k]}$ be the graph obtained by adding $k$ new loops at every vertex, and call $G$ **$k$-tight** if every vertex subset $X\subseteq V$ induces at most $k|X|$ edges and loops and $|E\cup L|=k|V|$. **Cruickshank–Garamvölgyi–Jordán–Nixon conjecture.** If $d$ and $t$ are positive integers with $d\geq 2t$, then $G^{[d-t]}$ can be realised as an infinitesimally rigid linearly constrained framework in $\mathbb{R}^d$ if and only if $G$ has a $t$-tight looped simple spanning subgraph. This would extend the known characterisation of rigidity for sufficiently looped graphs to the general range covered by $d\geq 2t$; the source presents it as an open conjecture. 

346762 Let $G$ be a graph, and let $n(G)$ denote its order. The graph $G$ is **traceable** if it contains a Hamiltonian path, and $\gamma_g(G)$ denotes its game domination number in the Dominator-start game.

**Rall's 1/2-conjecture.** If $G$ is traceable, then

$$
\gamma_g(G) \le \left\lceil \frac{n(G)}{2} \right\rceil.
$$

The conjecture was proposed by D. Rall and first published by James et al. The paper proves it for unicyclic graphs and establishes it for several additional graph families, but the general case remains open. 

346798 Let $k$ and $l$ be positive integers. Write $P_k$ for the path with index $k$, $C_{2k}$ for the even cycle with index $2k$, and $C_{2l+1}$ for the odd cycle with index $2l+1$. A graph $H$ is **$C_{2l+1}$-Turán-good** when the corresponding Turán graph is extremal for copies of $H$ among $C_{2l+1}$-free graphs. **Path-and-even-cycle Turán-goodness conjecture.** The path $P_k$ and the even cycle $C_{2k}$ are $C_{2l+1}$-Turán-good. The supplied excerpt gives no resolution or supporting result for this conjecture, so its general status remains open. 

346849 Let $G$ be a connected graph. Write $\operatorname{rc}(G)$ for its rainbow connection number and $\operatorname{f}(G)$ for its forest number.

**Rainbow connection conjecture.** A connected graph $G$ has

$$
\operatorname{rc}(G) \leq \operatorname{f}(G)-1.
$$

This would improve the paper's bound $\operatorname{rc}(G)\leq \operatorname{f}(G)+2$ and would be tight up to additive factors for trees. The source states that proving it likely requires extensive further case analysis.

The CLI has no batch/list traversal or retry mode. Every redirect is precharged.
This guards this client only, not other tools or arbitrary Python networking.
"""
import contextlib
import datetime

import hashlib
import ipaddress

from pathlib import Path
import re
import sys
import urllib.error

ROOTS = frozenset([
    "https://github.com/openai/math",
    "https://github.com/LeanPool/lean-pool",
    "https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti",
    "https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures",
])
PER_ID_CAP = 12
GLOBAL_CAP = 48
MAX_BYTES = 24 * 1024 * 1024


class PolicyError(RuntimeError):
    pass


def key(url):
    p = urllib.parse.urlsplit(url)
    if p.scheme != "https" or not p.hostname or p.username or p.password:
        raise PolicyError("Only explicit credential-free HTTPS URLs are allowed")
    host = p.hostname.lower()
    if p.port not in (None, 443) or host in ("localhost", "api.github.com") or host.endswith((".local", ".internal", ".localhost")):
        raise PolicyError("Disallowed host/port")
    try:
        if not ipaddress.ip_address(host).is_global:
            raise PolicyError("Nonpublic IP address")
    except ValueError:
        pass
    return host + urllib.parse.unquote(p.path).rstrip("/")


def artifacts(url):
    s = urllib.parse.unquote(url).lower()
    found = set(re.findall(r"(?<!\d)\d{4}\.\d{4,5}(?!\d)", s))
    for doi in re.findall(r"10\.\d{4,9}/[-._;()/:a-z0-9]+", s):
        while any(doi.endswith(t) for t in (".full", ".pdf", ".abstract", ".html", ".xml")):
            doi = doi.rsplit(".", 1)[0]
        found.add(doi)
    return found


class Guard:
    def __init__(self, lane, opener=None):
        self.lane = Path(lane).resolve()
        self.ids = set(map(str, json.loads((self.lane / "reservation.json").read_text())["ids"]))
        self.denied = json.loads((self.lane / "inherited-denials.json").read_text())
        self.opener = opener  # Tests inject a local fake; CLI always uses urllib.
        self.ledger = self.lane / "guard-ledger.jsonl"

    @contextlib.contextmanager
    def locked(self):
        with (self.lane / "guard.lock").open("a") as lock:
            fcntl.flock(lock, fcntl.LOCK_EX)
            records, previous = [], "0" * 64
            if self.ledger.exists():
                for raw in self.ledger.read_bytes().splitlines(keepends=True):
                    record = json.loads(raw)
                    if record.get("seq") != len(records) or record.get("previous") != previous:
                        raise PolicyError("Broken guard ledger chain; no request permitted")
                    records.append(record)
                    previous = hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()
            yield records, previous

    def append(self, records, previous, event):
        record = dict(event, seq=len(records), previous=previous,
                      utc=datetime.datetime.now(datetime.timezone.utc).isoformat())
        raw = (json.dumps(record, sort_keys=True) + "\n").encode()
        with self.ledger.open("ab") as f:
            f.write(raw)
            f.flush()
        return record

    def note(self, event):
        with self.locked() as (records, previous):
            return self.append(records, previous, event)

    def reserve(self, url, routes, plan_sha):
        k = key(url)
        if url in ROOTS or k in {key(x) for x in ROOTS}:
            routes = sorted(self.ids)  # Shared roots can never be undercharged.
        if not routes or not set(routes) <= self.ids or len(routes) != len(set(routes)):
            raise PolicyError("Routes must be exact unique reserved IDs")
        with self.locked() as (records, previous):
            attempts = [r for r in records if r["event"] == "attempt"]
            failures = [r for r in records if r["event"] == "unavailable"]
            denied_keys = set(self.denied["blocked_keys"]) | {r["url_key"] for r in failures}
            denied_artifacts = set(self.denied["blocked_artifacts"])
            for r in failures:
                denied_artifacts.update(r.get("artifacts", []))
            if k in denied_keys or artifacts(url) & denied_artifacts:
                raise PolicyError("Inherited/current unavailable route or artifact: no retry")
            if any(r["url_key"] == k for r in attempts):
                raise PolicyError("Already attempted URL; use saved bytes/outcome, no refetch/query variant")
            if len(attempts) >= GLOBAL_CAP:
                raise PolicyError("Global GET-hop cap48 exhausted")
            for route in routes:
                if sum(route in r["routes"] for r in attempts) >= PER_ID_CAP:
                    raise PolicyError("Per-ID GET-hop cap12 exhausted: " + route)
            return self.append(records, previous, {
                "event": "attempt", "url": url, "url_key": k,
                "routes": routes, "plan_sha256": plan_sha,
                "ordinal": len(attempts) + 1,
            })

    def fetch(self, plan):
        if not isinstance(plan, dict) or set(plan) != {"url", "routes", "destination", "source_identity", "purpose"}:
            raise PolicyError("Exactly one explicit plan object required; no URL arrays/seed traversal")
        if not isinstance(plan["url"], str) or not isinstance(plan["routes"], list):
            raise PolicyError("Invalid URL/routes types")
        if not all(isinstance(x, str) for x in plan["routes"]):
            raise PolicyError("Route IDs must be strings")

            raise PolicyError("Concrete source identity and assigned-target purpose required")

        if not dest.is_relative_to(self.lane / "sources") or dest == self.lane / "sources" or dest.exists():
            raise PolicyError("Destination must be a NEW file under own sources/")
        plan_sha = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(plan, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
        current = plan["url"]
