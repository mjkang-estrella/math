# Candidate refutation of MathDB 365453

Investigator's result, not accepted. Source: Lau–Shiu, Utilitas Mathematica 124 (2025), 189–206, Conjecture 4.1, printed page 205. The version of record explicitly retains the conjecture. The graph below is the literal instance r = s = 1, m = 2, not the connected join of all six matching edges with one null graph.

Let H = 3P₂ ∨ O₂ and G = H ⊔ H. G has 16 vertices and 30 edges. Suppose a bijection from its edges to {1,...,30} is local antimagic and induces at most three weights. Each component contains a triangle, so there are exactly three weights A, B, C. Within either component, the two O₂ vertices have the same weight: both are adjacent to the two endpoints of a matching edge, which already have different weights. If its null-vertex weight is X, every matched pair has the other two weights. Write M_i for the sum of its three matching-edge labels. The join-edge label sum equals 2X. Therefore
  2 M_i = 3(Y+Z) − 2X,
where Y,Z are the other two weights. The total of all thirty labels is 465.

If both components have null weight C, their matching-label sums coincide: 2M_0 = 2M_1 = 3(A+B)−2C. Consequently 465 = 4C + M_0 + M_1 = 4C + 2M_0, impossible by parity.

Otherwise write X,Y for the different null weights and Z for the common endpoint weight. Put S = X+Y and M = M_0+M_1. Summing the two component equations gives 2M = S+6Z. Summing all edge labels gives 465 = 2S+M. Elimination yields
  5M = 465+12Z.
Since M consists of six labels at most 30, M ≤ 180, and hence 12Z ≤ 435.

There are six vertices of weight Z, three matching endpoints in each component. They form an independent set. Their incident edges consist of all six matching edges and twelve distinct join edges. Let Q be the sum of those twelve labels. Injectivity and positivity imply Q ≥ 1+...+12 = 78. Summing at these six vertices gives 6Z = M+Q, whence
  5Q = 18Z−465.
Thus 18Z ≥ 855. This contradicts 12Z ≤ 435 (multiply the first by 2 and second by 3). No such labeling exists, so χ_la(G) is not 3.

The argument does not need a proposed four-color labeling or an exact value of χ_la(G). Every graph without a P₂ component has a local antimagic labeling by Haslegrave's theorem, but that existence theorem is not needed to refute the source's asserted three-color labeling.
