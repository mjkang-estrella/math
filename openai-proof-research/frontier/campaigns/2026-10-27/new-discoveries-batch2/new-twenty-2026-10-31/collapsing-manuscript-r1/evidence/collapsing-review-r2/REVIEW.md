## A. Source fidelity — PASS

It agrees with arXiv `conj:col`, journal Conjecture 14.

The model is Δ = [12][13][23]. The three two-way margins define AΔ; P is their nonnegative column hull, in the redundant space of dimension pq + pr + qr. It is a cone, despite MathDB's informal use of “polytope.” An elementary collapsing merges a subset of states of one variable into one state. A collapsing is a sequence of these maps. A source facet obtained by collapsing is the inverse image, **within the source cone**, of a target facet. The conjecture specifically names target dimensions (p,q,q), under positive p ≤ q ≤ r. It does not assert that every facet comes from a binary table: Example 13 already disproves that different assertion at (4,4,4).

The Lean `Margin`, `Cell`, `margins` and `marginalCone` definitions implement those coordinates literally. `IsFace` uses a nonnegative supporting linear functional, and `IsFacet` uses codimension one of linear spans. For these finite-dimensional pointed polyhedral cones, that is the ordinary facet definition. It is not an ambient codimension-one requirement in the redundant coordinate space. `ObtainedByCollapsing` intersects the preimage with the source cone, requires a target facet, and targets exactly (p,q,q) in `CollapsingConjecture`.

The inductive `IsCollapsing` permits an elementary merge in any of the three variables, with composite state maps. Surjectivity and its fiber equivalence mean exactly one possible nonsingleton fiber at each step. Bijections, hence state relabellings, are also allowed. Every genuine source sequence induces such maps, and fiber sums compose. These observations supply the direct definitional correspondence; there is no missing mathematical implication needed by the proof. In fact, the proof discards both `IsCollapsing` and target facet hypotheses and proves the obstruction for arbitrary state maps and arbitrary target sets.

The source introduces S_p × S_q × S_r symmetry, not arbitrary linear maps or arbitrary cone automorphisms. Permuting states is covered. Even allowing variable permutations at this witness would not help: every target factor has four states, so the five-state factor must identify states. Collapsing to smaller intermediate tables cannot evade this pigeonhole obstruction. No source definition authorizes mixing coordinates by a general linear transformation. The endpoint refutes the universal conjecture, not just an altered endpoint or a restricted finite search.

## D. Prior art — HOLD

I found no explicit prior refutation in the bodies inspected. This is a bounded investigation, not a novelty clearance. Dated downloads, HTTP outcomes, full text and search receipts are retained. In particular:

1. [Develin–Sturmfels, Tropical convexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308254), Theorem 1 and its proof, identifies regular product-of-simplices subdivisions through the associated inequality polyhedron. This supplies relevant duality, not a bound on the number of maximal cells in a coarsest Δ3 × Δ3 subdivision. Its corrected injective-hull assertions are not used.
2. [Herrmann–Joswig, Splitting polytopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0774) develops splits and their decomposition with a split-prime remainder. A split has two maximal cells. Its machinery alone does not make a five-cell coarsest subdivision or this conjecture a published refutation.
3. [Herrmann–Jensen–Joswig–Sturmfels, How to draw tropical planes](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2383), Section 3, Proposition 3.3 and Proposition 3.7, provides the vertex-figure link and unbounded spread as n grows. That does not by itself establish spread greater than q at a fixed p,q, and counts of cells in a hypersimplex subdivision cannot simply be substituted for counts in its vertex figure. The initial EMIS HTTP route failed by connection refusal; arXiv was an accessible alternative, not a retry of that route.
4. [Herrmann–Joswig–Speyer, Dressians, Tropical Grassmannians, and Their Rays](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.1278), Sections 2–4 and 6–7, gives explicit lifting and rigidity machinery. Section 3 equates pseudovertices with maximal product-subdivision cells; Example 17 concerns Δ2 × Δ4. Its Dr(3,8) computation and examples with many tight-span vertices are genuine nearby prior art. They are not an explicit Δ3 × Δ3 counterexample. Question 38 asks about computing Dr(4,8). I have not cleared all subsequent computations or associated datasets.
5. [Rinaldo's 2006 thesis](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~brian/720-2007-source/nice%20materials/rinaldo-thesis.pdf), Section 4.3, repeats the exact assertion as Conjecture 4.3.5 with the same stabilization discussion. It does not retract or restrict it there. The separate [Computing Maximum Likelihood Estimates manuscript](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~arinaldo/papers/Computing_Extended_MLE.pdf), including Section 5.3, discusses collapsibility onto sets of variables; that is a different operation from merging states in this conjecture.
6. [Fienberg–Rinaldo (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.3618), especially its MLE facial-set theory and Examples 1–4, uses the earlier 4 × 4 × 4 facet count and an example at that size. It does not supply this 4 × 4 × 5 refutation in those passages. [Sullivant's Normal binary graph models](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1784), Theorem 1.2, classifies binary normality, not nonbinary facets. [Bernstein–Sullivant, Normal Binary Hierarchical Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05461), Definition 2.6 and Theorems 2.7–2.9, concern semigroup holes/normality. Failure of normality at 4 × 4 × 4 is not failure of the collapsing conjecture.
