A matching yields a chronological directed graph of indegree<=1 and outdegree<=k. Strict time makes it acyclic, hence components are rooted k-ary trees; conversely a tree partition matches each nonroot to a distinct copy of its parent. Thus width=n-maximum matching=max_S(|S|-k|N(S)|). Hall deficiency follows by adjoining d universal dummy supplies, where d=max_S deficiency: Hall gives a saturating matching, at most d of whose edges use dummies; reverse bound holds for every matching and S.
