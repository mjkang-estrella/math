403: Conjecture 9. Every 2-connected graph with maximum degree 3 has a MED decomposition
404: (a decomposition into a matching, a union of disjoint even cycles, and an independent set
405: of double-stars).
406: 
407:    If Conjecture 9 is true, then by Theorem 7 all 2-connected graphs with maximum degree
408: 3 are (7, 2)-edge-choosable. The argument does not extend to graphs with cut-vertices.
409: 
410: Example 10. There are 3-regular graphs having no MED decomposition. A small example
411: is the standard small 3-regular graph having no perfect matching. Let H be the graph
412: obtained from K4 by subdividing one edge. Form G∗ from 3H by adding one vertex v made
413: adjacent to the 2-valent vertices of 3H. Let x1 , x2 , x3 be the neighbors of v.
414:     Suppose that G∗ has a MED decomposition. Putting an xi on an even cycle leaves a
415: claw, which cannot be decomposed. Hence the decomposition must have each xi as a central
416: vertex in a double-star. Since a triangle can’t be decomposed, the other central vertex must
417: be v. Now the three required double-stars share v, yielding a contradiction.
