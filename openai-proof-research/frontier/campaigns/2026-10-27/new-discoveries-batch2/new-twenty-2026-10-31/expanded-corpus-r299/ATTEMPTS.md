## 318472: complete classical bridge, excluded, credit 0

Source Torii Conjecture 2 concerns finite simple polyhedral maps on closed surfaces, with vertex and facial degrees at least three. Assume every curvature is negative. Higuchi's local gap is Φ(v)≤−1/1806. Akama–Hua arXiv:1807.10762v3, page 2, explicitly gives the same curvature definition, finite-surface Gauss–Bonnet equation (2), and Proposition 1 citing Higuchi Proposition 2.1 with this gap. Double counting gives the equation directly: sum_v(1−deg(v)/2+sum_{f∋v}1/deg(f))=V−E+F=χ. Thus χ≤−V/1806, hence V≤1806|χ| when χ≤0. Contraposition proves exactly the assigned implication with c_M=1806, uniformly in M. If χ=0, all-negative curvature contradicts the sum immediately. No claim that 1806 is optimal is needed. This is a complete bridge between two printed classical facts and the exact requested quantifiers; zero credit is justified by the explicit reduction rather than brevity.

## 324269: quantum tomography, incomplete

Conjecture 8 immediately before it concerns improper classical marginalization, and is a different map. The displayed map for the first three sites is rho_{j,k}=sum_i Psi_{j,i} conjugate(Psi_{k,i}); its fibres, within the open-boundary TI MPS variety, must be generically exactly S¹ orbits. The two claims (three sites suffice and fewer do not) both need proof.

Attempt A: transfer the binary hidden-Markov birational recovery result. This fails because tracing amplitudes with conjugates is not summing amplitudes; source explicitly distinguishes these maps. Attempt B: apply generic purification uniqueness. Equal three-site density matrices imply a unitary action on the unobserved subsystem, but this unitary need not preserve the same translation-invariant bond-two MPS model. An arbitrary such unitary is therefore not an in-scope generic counterexample. Attempt C: use transfer-channel observability: rho of three sites gives moments of words of length3, and perhaps determines the shared local tensor plus boundary data modulo gauge. The precise missing lemma is injectivity of this real-algebraic moment map on a dense open subset of the TI open-boundary model modulo phase, with a separate positive-dimensional-fibre proof for two sites. No dimension count alone proves generic injectivity, and isolated GHZ-like coincidences lie on special loci. Generic fibre analysis, all N and boundary positioning remain open here. No solver plan substitutes for this missing theorem. Park; no Pi.
