## Exact source semantics

Clement–Lee-St. John–Sidman, arXiv:1308.3281v1 (the sole version listed in the live history) and CCCG 2013 paper 45, Definitions 2, 4, 7, 8, 11 and final Conjecture 1. There are disjoint sets A,B,U of sizes d,d,b; edges are the cliques on A and B and all A–U and B–U edges, with b extra A–B edges at distinct endpoints, d=2b, b≥2. The extra edges form a matching. This interpretation is also required by the source proof's inequality |E⁺(V′)|≤|V′₂|. Generic means maximum rigidity-matrix rank, not every embedding. The literal MathDB paraphrase omits the standing generic convention: a collapsed embedding is therefore a wording diagnostic, not a significant refutation.

The endpoint is nullity R(p) = binom(d+1,2)+binom(b,2) for every maximum-rank realization p of this graph. Rows of R(p) have p_i−p_j in block i and its negative in block j. Thus kernel vectors are precisely velocities satisfying (p_i−p_j)·(v_i−v_j)=0 on every edge.

## Proof

Work over the reals. On a nonempty open set of placements each bunch A∪U and B∪U is infinitesimally rigid and spans R^d. Indeed the d points of A are affinely independent in a hyperplane and the first U point is outside it. These d+1 points form a simplex. For a simplex, subtract the velocity at one vertex, interpolate the other d velocities by a linear map L, and use the edge equations first on basis vectors and then on their pairwise differences. They say that the symmetric part of L is zero. Thus its velocities are t+Ωp with Ω skew. Each further U vertex is attached to all d A vertices. Subtracting the unique rigid motion already found leaves a velocity w orthogonal to all d vectors p_u−p_a. These vectors form a basis when p_u is outside the A hyperplane, so w=0. This proves bunch rigidity directly, and likewise for B.

Subtract the unique global rigid motion supplied by A∪U. The remaining motion is zero there and on B is a rigid motion t+Ωp vanishing on U. Translate the origin to u₁ and set E=span{u_j−u₁:2≤j≤b}. For independent U, dim E=b−1. Vanishing gives t=0 and ΩE=0. Skewness then gives Ω(E⊥)⊆E⊥. Consequently relative motions are exactly skew maps on W=E⊥, of dimension d−b+1=b+1. Conversely each such skew map gives a kernel velocity before the extra edges are imposed. Global motions and relative motions give a direct sum: restriction to the spanning bunch A∪U determines the global motion uniquely. The unaugmented nullity is therefore binom(d+1,2)+binom(b+1,2).

Label the extra matching edges a_i b_i, 1≤i≤b. Write x_i,y_i for the orthogonal W projections of p_ai−u₁,p_bi−u₁. Their constraints become x_i·Ωy_i=0 (the sign is immaterial), since y_i·Ωy_i=0. These b functionals are generically independent on skew(W). To prove existence without a search, fix independent U in a coordinate (b−1)-plane. In the complementary coordinate space W with basis e₀,…,e_b, specialize x_i=e₀ and y_i=e_i. The functionals select the b independent skew entries Ω₀i, so their determinant on these b entries is ±1. This is a nonzero polynomial in the freely chosen A,B coordinates. The determinants guaranteeing bunch rigidity are also nonzero polynomials: each can be nonzero by choosing generic simplex and attachment positions. Their product with the displayed minor is nonzero, because a polynomial ring over R is an integral domain. A nonzero polynomial over R cannot vanish everywhere (induct on the number of variables using the univariate root bound). There is therefore a single placement satisfying all these conditions simultaneously. The deliberately degenerate specialization for the wedge minor is not asserted to be rigid; this product argument repairs that potential failure.

At this placement the augmented nullity is binom(d+1,2)+binom(b+1,2)−b = binom(d+1,2)+binom(b,2). On the entire nonempty open set where both bunches are rigid and U independent, b extra equations can remove at most b relative dimensions. Hence rank is at most d(2d+b)−binom(d+1,2)−binom(b,2) there. Every larger minor vanishes on that open set; a real polynomial vanishing on an open set is identically zero (the same induction applied on an open box). Thus no exceptional placement can have larger rank. The exhibited placement attains the bound, proving the maximum-rank endpoint for all b≥2.

## Failed shortcuts and repairs

Source Theorem 15 proves flexibility only, not exact nullity. Maxwell counting by itself cannot prove independence of the added edges. Calling the two bunches rigid alone leaves binom(b+1,2) relative degrees of freedom, not the desired binom(b,2). The missing rank-b step is supplied above by independent bivectors and the nonzero-polynomial product. A single special coordinate placement with repeated projections need not make the bunches rigid; simultaneous nonvanishing, not that placement, is the existence proof. An arbitrary nongeneric all-zero placement is out of intended source scope.
