350147 Laborde's path partition conjecture for digraphs 
 Let $D$ be a digraph, and let $\ell(D)$ denote the order of a longest directed path in $D$. For positive integers $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ satisfying

$$
\ell(D)=\ell_1+\ell_2,
$$

a partition of $D$ into two subdigraphs $D_1$ and $D_2$ is a partition whose parts contain all vertices of $D$ and whose longest directed paths have the indicated bounds. **Laborde's path partition conjecture.** For every digraph $D$ and any pair of positive integers $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ with $\ell(D)=\ell_1+\ell_2$, there exists a partition of $D$ into two subdigraphs $D_1$ and $D_2$ such that $\ell(D_i)\leq\ell_i$ for $i\in[2]$. This conjecture extends the Gallai–Roy–Vitaver theorem and is known for several generalizations of tournaments, but is open for general digraphs. 

350148 Bondy's strengthened path partition conjecture for digraphs 
 Let $D$ be a digraph, and let $\ell(D)$ denote the order of a longest directed path in $D$. For positive integers $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ satisfying

$$
\ell(D)=\ell_1+\ell_2,
$$

a partition of $D$ into two subdigraphs $D_1$ and $D_2$ is a partition whose parts contain all vertices of $D$. **Bondy's strengthened path partition conjecture.** For every digraph $D$ and any pair of positive integers $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ with $\ell(D)=\ell_1+\ell_2$, there exists a partition of $D$ into two subdigraphs $D_1$ and $D_2$ such that $\ell(D_i)=\ell_i$ for $i\in[2]$. This is stated as a stronger version of Laborde's path partition conjecture; the supplied text gives no resolution of the general claim. 

350149 Arroyo–Galeana-Sánchez's equivalent path partition conjecture 
 Let $D$ be a digraph, and let $\ell(D)$ denote the order of a longest directed path in $D$. For a positive integer $q<\ell(D)$, a partition $(A,B)$ of $V(D)$ determines the induced subdigraphs $D[A]$ and $D[B]$. **Arroyo–Galeana-Sánchez's equivalent path partition conjecture.** For every digraph $D$ and a positive integer $q<\ell(D)$, there exists a partition $(A,B)$ of $V(D)$ such that $\ell(D[A])\leq q$ and $\ell(D[B])\leq\ell(D)-q$. The source states that this formulation is equivalent to Laborde's path partition conjecture and notes that the conjecture has been proved for several classes of digraphs, while leaving the general case unresolved. 

350225 Higher-dimensional Stanley–Wilf conjecture 
 For $t\geq 2$, let $P$ be a $t$-pattern, and let $T_t(n,P)$ denote the set of all $t$-dimensional $n\times\cdots\times n$ $0$–$1$ matrices avoiding $P$. **Higher-dimensional Stanley–Wilf conjecture.** For every $t$-pattern $P$, there exists a constant $c=c_P$ such that

$$
|T_t(n,P)|\leq c^{n^{t-1}}.
$$

In the source statement, the final display writes $|T_2(n,P)|$, although the surrounding definition and the proposed higher-dimensional generalization use $T_t(n,P)$; the row follows that intended generalization. The conjecture extends the singly exponential bound in dimension two to growth exponential in the number of $(t-1)$-dimensional slices. 

350351 The extremal-free-word conjecture for avoidable patterns 
 Let $P$ be an **avoidable pattern**, meaning that arbitrarily long words avoiding $P$ exist over some finite alphabet, and let $\mu(P)$ be the least size of such an alphabet. An extremal $P$-free word is a $P$-free word for which every single-letter extension contains an occurrence of $P$.

**Extremal-free-word conjecture for avoidable patterns.** For every avoidable pattern $P$, there are no extremal $P$-free words over an alphabet of size $\mu(P)+1$.

This generalizes the four-letter square-free extension question to arbitrary avoidable patterns. The paper presents it as an experimental direction and gives no resolution. 

350377 Conjecture on chi-boundedness of graphs excluding induced k-fans 
 For a positive integer $k$, a **$k$-fan** is a graph consisting of an induced path and one additional vertex with at least $k$ neighbours on the path; a $3$-fan is called a **fan**. **The k-fan conjecture.** For every positive integer $k$, the class of graphs with no induced $k$-fan is $\chi$-bounded. This is known for $k\leq 3$, whereas the assertion for general $k$ remains open; it strengthens the Aboulker–Bousquet conjecture because a $k$-fan contains an induced cycle with exactly $k-2$ chords. 

350440 The finite-group extension conjecture for group-labeled graph lifts 
 Let $n\ge 3$ be an integer and let $\Gamma$ be a finite group. Let $K_n^{\Gamma}$ be the fully $\Gamma$-labeled complete graph, and let $\mathcal{M}_{n,\Gamma}$ denote the class of matroids considered in the paper. Write $M(K_n^{\Gamma})$ for the cycle matroid of the underlying graph. **The finite-group extension conjecture.** If $M\in\mathcal{M}_{n,\Gamma}$ and

$$
r(M)-r(M(K_n^{\Gamma}))>1,
$$

then there is a prime $p$ and an integer $j\ge 2$ such that

$$
\Gamma\cong \mathbb{Z}_p^j.
$$

The abelian-group version is proved in the paper, and the conjecture proposes the same conclusion for all finite groups. Its status remains open. 

350623 The integer-move conjecture for the fractional transversal game 
 Let $H=(V,\mathcal E)$ be a finite hypergraph. An **integer move** in the fractional transversal game is a move of the form $(v_{i_1},1)$. **Integer-move conjecture.** If each of the first $2k-1$ moves, for $k\geq 1$, was an integer move, then Staller has an integer move in the $(2k)^{\rm th}$ turn that is optimal in the fractional transversal game. This would mean that fractional moves are advantageous only for Edge-hitter. The conjecture is presented as open and would imply the weaker inequality $\tau_g^*(H)\leq\tau_g(H)$ for every hypergraph. 

355301 Partition sumset Bohr-set conjecture for finite-index endomorphisms 
 Let $G$ be a (not necessarily countable) abelian group, let $d\geq 3$, and let $\phi_1,\dots,\phi_d$ be endomorphisms of $G$ such that $[G:\phi_j(G)]<\infty$ for each $j$. Suppose that $S\subset [d]$ is non-empty and

$$
\sum_{j\in S}\phi_j=0.
$$

**Partition sumset Bohr-set conjecture.** For every finite partition $G=\bigcup_{i=1}^r A_i$, there exists $i\in [r]$ such that

$$
\sum_{j=1}^d\phi_j(A_i)
$$

contains a Bohr-$(k,\eta)$ set, where $k$ and $\eta$ depend only on $r$ and the indices $[G:\phi_j(G)]$. This extends the corresponding partition result and remains conjectural in the stated generality. 

355406 Pseudoperiodic word avoidance conjecture for binary words 
 Let $(a,b)$ be a pair of positive integers with $1\leq a<b$. An infinite binary word has **pseudoperiod** $(a,b)$ if each position is compatible with a repetition having periods $a$ and $b$; a word is **avoiding $3^+$-powers** if it contains no factor whose exponent is at least $3$. **Pseudoperiodic word avoidance conjecture.** For all pairs $(a,b)$ with $1\leq a<b$ and $b\ne 2a$, there exists an infinite binary word with pseudoperiod $(a,b)$ and avoiding $3^+$-powers. The conjecture is motivated by the observed critical exponents in the computed examples, all of which are at most $3^+$; its general case remains open. 

355462 Ferdinand's girth conjecture for the bipartite graph D(k,q) 
 Let $D(k,q)$ be the bipartite graph originally proposed by Lazebnik and Ustimenko, where $k$ is an integer not less than $2$ and $q$ is a prime power. The graph has girth at least $k+4$ for $k\geq 3$. **Ferdinand's girth conjecture.** $D(k,q)$ has girth $k+5$ for all odd $k$ and all $q\geq 4$. This conjecture concerns the exact girth of the highly symmetric graphs $D(k,q)$, which are also used in extremal graph theory, finite geometry, coding theory and related applications. Its resolution status is not established by the supplied text. 

356143 List steady-word conjecture for four-letter alphabets 
 A word $W=w_1w_2\cdots w_n$ is **steady** if it is square-free and remains square-free after deleting any single letter. Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $\mathcal{A}_1,\mathcal{A}_2,\ldots,\mathcal{A}_n$ be alphabets, each of size $4$.

**List steady-word conjecture.** There exists a steady word $W=w_1w_2\cdots w_n$ such that $w_i\in\mathcal{A}_i$ for every $i=1,2,\ldots,n$.

The paper proves the corresponding statement for alphabets of size at least $7$. The analogous conjecture for pure square-free words is discussed separately and is not this claim; no resolution of the four-letter steady-word version is supplied here. 

356304 The conjecture for extensions beginning with n1 and n2 
 Let $L(w)$ denote the lexicographically least infinite word on $\mathbb{N}$ beginning with $w$ whose only square factors are contained in the prefix $w$. **The conjecture for extensions beginning with $n1$ and $n2$.** For every integer $n\geq 3$,

$$
L(n1)=nL(1)\qquad\text{and}\qquad L(n2)=nL(2).
$$

These identities would extend the known formulas for prefixes $nn$ and $n_1n_2$ with letters at least $3$, and would describe the least square-free extensions of the mixed prefixes $n1$ and $n2$. 

356307 The fixed-size MSTD–MDTS conjecture for dihedral groups 
 Let $D_{2n}$ be the dihedral group of order $2n$. For a positive integer $m$, let $\mathcal S_{2n,m}$ denote the collection of subsets of $D_{2n}$ of size $m$. A subset is **MSTD** if its sumset has greater cardinality than its difference set, and **MDTS** if its difference set has greater cardinality than its sumset.

**Fixed-size MSTD–MDTS conjecture.** For every integer $n\geq 3$ and every $m\leq 2n$, the collection $\mathcal S_{2n,m}$ has at least as many MSTD sets as MDTS sets.

The source reports that this conjecture is known for $m=2$, $m=3$, and $m>n$; the general fixed-size assertion remains open in the supplied text. 

356385 The St. Ives matching conjecture for online path Ramsey numbers 
 For each $k\geq 1$, let $S_k$ be the St. Ives ordered matching defined recursively from two disjoint consecutive copies of $S_{k-1}$ by adding an edge whose endpoints lie respectively to the left and right of both copies. Let $r_o(S_k,P_n)$ denote the online Ramsey number for finding an ordered copy of $S_k$ or an ordered path $P_n$.

**St. Ives matching conjecture.** For all $k\geq 1$, there is a constant $c_k$ such that for all $n\geq 1$,

$$
r_o(S_k,P_n)\leq n+c_k.
$$

Every intersection-free matching on $k$ edges is a subgraph of $S_k$, so this statement is equivalent to the intersection-free matching conjecture. The source gives no resolution of the conjecture. 

356544 Conjectured relation between permutations generating two and three CNATs 
 Let $b(n,k)$ be the number of distinct permutations of $n$ letters that give rise to exactly $k$ CNATs. **CNAT-count relation conjecture.** For all $k\geq 1$, $b(n,2)=b(n+1,3)$. The statement is presented as a conjecture based on further numerical experimentation; no proof or resolution is given. The quantifier over $k$ appears inconsistent with the displayed equation, which does not depend on $k$. 

356792 Conjecture that every nontrivial tree is harmonious 
 A graph $G$ is **harmonious** when it admits a harmonious labeling, namely an injective vertex labeling whose induced edge labels are also injective modulo $|E(G)|$; for trees, the definition allows at most one repeated vertex label. A tree is **nontrivial** if it has at least one edge. **Harmonious-tree conjecture.** Every nontrivial tree is harmonious. Many classes of trees are known to be harmonious, and super edge-magic trees are known to be harmonious, but the assertion for all trees remains open. 

356860 The e-positivity conjecture for chromatic quasisymmetric functions of indifference graphs 
 Let $\gamma$ be an indifference graph, and write its chromatic quasisymmetric function in the elementary symmetric-function basis as

$$
X_{\gamma}(\mathbf{x};t)=\sum_{\lambda}a_{\lambda}(t)e_{\lambda}.
$$

**The e-positivity conjecture.** For each $\gamma \in \mathcal{I}\mathcal{G}$, $X_{\gamma}(\mathbf{x};t)$ is $e$-positive; equivalently, $a_{\lambda}(t)\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}[t]$ for every partition $\lambda$.

This conjecture generalizes the Stanley–Stembridge conjecture, whose $t=1$ case is known. The full polynomial positivity statement remains open. 

365573 Cichacz–Suchan irregular-labeling conjecture for digraphs 
 Let $\overrightarrow{G}$ be a digraph of order $n$ whose weakly connected components all have order at least $3$, and let $\Gamma$ be an Abelian group. A **$\Gamma$-irregular labeling** is a labeling in which the vertex weights are distinct, as defined in the surrounding discussion. **Cichacz–Suchan's irregular-labeling conjecture.** There exists a constant $K$ such that every such digraph $\overrightarrow{G}$ has a $\Gamma$-irregular labeling for every Abelian group $\Gamma$ satisfying

$$
|\Gamma|\geq n+K.
$$

This is presented as a conjecture based on bounds previously obtained for components of order at least $4$ and for groups of size greater than $(1+\varepsilon)n$; no resolution is given. 

366974 Positive matching decomposition conjecture for Cartesian products of cycles 
 Let $C_m$ and $C_n$ be cycles with $m,n\geq3$, and let $\mathrm{pmd}(G)$ denote the positive matching decomposition number. **Positive matching decomposition conjecture for Cartesian products of cycles.**

for all $m,n\geq3$ such that $m+n$ is even, or such that $(m,n)\in\{(3,4),(3,6),(5,6)\}$. The supplied text presents this exact-value assertion after an analysis of positive matchings in the relevant Cartesian products; no resolution beyond that analysis is provided.
