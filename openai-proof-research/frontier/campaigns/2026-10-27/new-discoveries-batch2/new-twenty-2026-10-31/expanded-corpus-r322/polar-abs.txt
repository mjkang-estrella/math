 Title: Polar Degrees of Matroids

 Authors: Clara Briand , Leonie Kayser , Julian Weigert 
 View a PDF of the paper titled Polar Degrees of Matroids, by Clara Briand and 1 other authors

 View PDF 
 HTML (experimental) 



 
 Abstract: We show that the polar degrees of the coordinate-wise inverse of a linear subspace $L \subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ are given by the coefficients of a substitution of the reduced characteristic polynomial of the associated matroid $\mathrm{M}(L)$. Our proof connects the geometry of conormal varieties of reciprocal linear spaces to the combinatorial conormal fan of $\mathrm{M}(L)$. As a corollary, we settle two open conjectures regarding matroid discriminants.
