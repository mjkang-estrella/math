## 375371: square rectairs, sustained mex route

CRIM deletes one whole row or column, merging the two remaining pieces. A row move deletes one part; a column-j move subtracts one from precisely the parts at least j, dropping zeros. These operations strictly decrease area. The source defines R(r,c,k)=[c^(r-k),c-1,...,c-k], 0<=k<min(r,c). For r=c>=2, rank |r-c|=0 is even. Definition3 balancedness says precisely r even OR k<r-1. Theorem3's entire proof was read: parallel/perpendicular response, small k=0,1, extremal k=r-1 or c-1, and intermediate k. Corollary1 gives normal SG=0 exactly for balanced rectairs. This is a complete SOURCE reduction of the zero branch, not a new result.

The remaining family is S_r=[r,r-1,...,1] for odd r>=3. Every row-i deletion produces D(r,i)=[r,...,r-i+2,r-i,...,1]. Conjugating a column move gives the same collection of SG values because S_r is self-conjugate and conjugation preserves moves. Thus the exact recurrence is G(S_r)=mex{G(D(r,i)):1<=i<=r}. Deleting the first row gives S_(r-1), a known zero position. Deleting the last row gives R(r-1,r,r-2). These first/last moves are NOT the same shape, and cannot be conflated; conjugation exchanges row/column moves, not arbitrary row indices. For odd r>=7, the proposed value2 would follow from (i) some D(r,i) has G=1 and (ii) no D(r,i) has G=2, together with the known zero option. For r=3,5 proposed value1 requires absence of any value1 option. Neither response pairing nor balancedness establishes these exact values: a two-move return to P proves an option is N, which permits SG1,2,3,... . The near-square R(r-1,r,r-2) itself belongs to the adjoining conjectural SG family. This is an explicit obstruction to a closed induction on square rectairs alone. No invariant controlling all internal deletions was found.

At r=1,k=0, the source game gives G([1])=1 while the displayed formula's otherwise branch gives2. This is only an endpoint correction. It is not submitted as a finite substantive solution. Definitions, Theorem3 proof, Corollary1 proof and Proposition15 proof were read; geometric figures were reviewed as captions/algebraic descriptions, not visually rendered. Thesis companion landing metadata was fetched; no companion proof body was available in this review. Hence literature/source qualification remains incomplete independently of the mathematical mex gap.

## 375373: Conway-pair classification, sustained induction route

Normal terminal value is0, misere terminal value is1; at any nonterminal partition both coordinates are separately mex of the corresponding option coordinates. A nonempty row of length c has options empty and, if c>1, row(c-1); induction gives CP=(1,0) for odd c and(2,2) for even c. Conjugation handles columns. For a hook H(r,c)=[c,1^(r-1)] with r,c>1, its options are the column(r-1), H(r-1,c), row(c-1), H(r,c-1), omitting duplicate shapes. Source Theorem19 handles boundary r=2 or c=2 and induction on r+c. With both r,c even the option pairs are(1,0),(3,3),(1,0),(3,3), so coordinatewise mex is(0,1). With both odd they are(2,2),(3,3),(2,2),(3,3), giving(0,0). With opposite parities the option set includes(1,0),(0,0),(2,2),(0,1), giving(3,3). Thus the hook direction is already fully public, not merely a familiar-family heuristic.

For the converse, suppose a nonhook has pair(0,1). Its options all have normal coordinate !=0 and misere coordinate !=1; some option has misere coordinate0. That option must have pair(a,0) for a>0, but the recurrence does NOT force a=1 or force it to be a row. Assuming only a classification of smaller(0,1) positions therefore does not close the induction. An arbitrary nonhook can have moves to hooks as well as to other nonhooks; hook-family mex induction is not a proof for this larger option set. The full game is explicitly neither returnable nor domestic, so treating all nonhooks as tame/symmetric is unsupported. A simultaneous structural classification of enough(a,0),(a,1) options is missing. The attempt was extended past the hook calculation to this precise failure of inductive closure; no nonhook counterexample is certified.

Empty partition has CP(0,1) by definition and is not an even-by-even hook. This literal exception is a terminal convention correction, excluded from credit. The meaningful nonempty converse remains held. No Pi implementation plan is appropriate without it.

## 375626: exact symmetry obstruction, endpoint not qualified

Take equal baselines mu>0, identical zero initial memory, zero diagonal kernels, and cross-kernels phi12=phi21=a exp(-beta t), 0<a<beta. The branching matrix has spectral radius a/beta<1. Swapping labels1 and2 preserves the process law (for example exchange the two identically distributed Poisson immigration/offspring constructions). Source area109 is antisymmetric under this swap, so integrability implies E[A12_T]=-E[A12_T], hence E[A12_T]=0 for EVERY T. The same is true for two independent Poisson channels with those baselines and zero excitation. Padding each system by independent identically initialized channels keeps every other cross-area expectation zero: exchanging two independent equal-mean channels, or factorizing the independent cross terms with equal mean paths, yields cancellation. Both models are sparse when dimension is padded, have identical baselines/self-excitation, and differ in directed SUPPORT (reciprocal pair versus no edge).

This is a complete ordinary mechanism AGAINST full-support recovery from signs alone; all horizons do not repair it. It is not a qualified refutation of the assigned conjecture because the source says DOMINANT directed excitation graph and supplies no tie/threshold/control definition. A dominance convention that discards equal opposing edges maps both models to the empty dominant graph, defeating this witness. Trying unequal a12 and a21 does not fix that gap: source Theorem10.2 gives leading-order sign(a21-a12), which is consistent with an asymmetric-dominance interpretation. Adding a reciprocal weak edge may preserve signs while changing support but likewise does not necessarily change dominant direction. No invented identifiability conditions were adopted. Standard antisymmetric-statistic blindness to symmetric interactions is also a classical mechanism, not established novelty. Source10.1/10.2 proof bodies were read, sole arXiv version metadata checked, bounded search found no new primary proof; this is not literature clearance.
