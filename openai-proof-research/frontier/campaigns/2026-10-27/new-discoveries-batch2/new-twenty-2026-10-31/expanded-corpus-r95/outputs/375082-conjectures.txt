 For given positive integers s and t , let n_{1}\geq n_{2}\geq\cdots\geq n_{s}\geq 1 and m_{1}\geq m_{2}\geq\cdots\geq m_{t}\geq 1 be integers. For each k\in[s+t]\backslash[1] , let \ell_{k}=\max\left\{n_{i}+m_{j}-1:i+j=k\right\} . Then 
 

 
 
 \hat{r}\left(\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{s}K_{1,n_{i}},\bigsqcup_{j=1}^{t}K_{1,m_{j}}\right)=\sum_{k=2}^{s+t}\ell_{k}.
