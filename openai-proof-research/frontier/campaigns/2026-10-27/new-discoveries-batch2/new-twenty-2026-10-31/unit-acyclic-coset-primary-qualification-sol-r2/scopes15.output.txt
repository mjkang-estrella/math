365710 Maximum sigma_t-irregularity conjecture for connected triangle-free graphs 
 Let $G$ be a connected triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices. Write $sigma_t(G)$ for its $sigma_t$-irregularity. Among such graphs, the extremal complete bipartite graphs are conjectured to have maximum $sigma_t(G)$.

**Triangle-free extremal conjecture.** Among connected triangle-free graphs on $n$ vertices, the extremal complete bipartite graphs have the maximum $sigma_t(G)$-irregularity.

The paper proves the corresponding extremal result among complete bipartite graphs and proposes this conjecture as the extension to all connected triangle-free graphs; its status is not resolved in the supplied text.
365943 Nonexistence conjecture for cubic 8-circulant nut graphs 
 A **cubic $8$-circulant nut graph** is a cubic nut graph admitting an $8$-circulant structure. **Nonexistence conjecture for cubic $8$-circulant nut graphs.** There exists no cubic $8$-circulant nut graph. This is the only unresolved case in the cubic $\ell$-circulant nut graph existence problem; computational results suggest that such graphs might not exist, but no proof is given here.
366371 The majorization characterization conjecture for ordinal spaces embeddable in the line 
 Let $(X,\delta)$ be an ordinal space with $|X|=n\in\mathbb N^+$, and let $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ be an enumeration of its points satisfying the majorization property. Write $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^1$ for embeddability of $X$ in the real line. **Majorization characterization conjecture.** If such an enumeration exists, then

$$
X\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^1.
$$

The paper proves that line embeddability implies the existence of an enumeration with the majorization property and states sufficiency for $n\leqslant4$, while explicitly saying that it cannot determine whether sufficiency holds for $n>5$; the general assertion is therefore open.
366974 Positive matching decomposition conjecture for Cartesian products of cycles 
 Let $C_m$ and $C_n$ be cycles with $m,n\geq3$, and let $\mathrm{pmd}(G)$ denote the positive matching decomposition number. **Positive matching decomposition conjecture for Cartesian products of cycles.**

\mathrm{pmd}(C_m\square C_n)=6
$$

for all $m,n\geq3$ such that $m+n$ is even, or such that $(m,n)\in\{(3,4),(3,6),(5,6)\}$. The supplied text presents this exact-value assertion after an analysis of positive matchings in the relevant Cartesian products; no resolution beyond that analysis is provided.
368807 The neighborhood-tour conjecture for ASEP vertices

A **tour** is a Hamiltonian cycle of the complete directed graph on the node set. **Neighborhood-tour conjecture.** For every vertex $\boldsymbol{x}$, the neighborhood $\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{x})$ contains at least one tour. This is presented as a conjecture based on preliminary computational tests; the supplied text does not establish it or indicate a resolution.
369421 The representation-number conjecture for larger toroidal grid graphs 
 Let $\text{TGr}_{m,n}$ be the toroidal grid graph formed from the Cartesian product of the cycle graphs $C_m$ and $C_n$, with $m,n\geq 3$, and let $\mathcal{R}(G)$ denote the representation number of a graph $G$. **Representation-number conjecture.** If $n,m\geq 3$ and $m+n\geq 8$, then

$$
\mathcal{R}(\text{TGr}_{m,n})\geq 4.
$$

The graphs $\text{TGr}_{3,3}$ and $\text{TGr}_{3,4}$ have known 3-representations, but no 3-representation is known for larger toroidal grid graphs; the conjecture predicts that all cases with $m+n\geq 8$ require representation number at least $4$.
372205 Existence of regular open and closed XOR-magic graphs of power-of-two order 
 Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $d$ be a degree. An **open XOR-magic graph** and a **closed XOR-magic graph** are graphs admitting, respectively, an open or closed XOR-magic labeling. The graphs below are required to have order $2^n$.

For each degree

$$
d\in\{5,7,\ldots,2^n-7,2^n-5\},
$$

there exists a $d$-regular open XOR-magic graph of order $2^n$. For each degree

$$
d\in\{4,6,\ldots,2^n-6,2^n-4\},
$$

there exists a $d$-regular closed XOR-magic graph of order $2^n$.

**Existence conjecture.** For every permitted degree $d$ in the two displayed sets, the corresponding regular open or closed XOR-magic graph exists. This would complete the existence picture apart from the degrees excluded by the previously stated nonexistence result and extend the constructions known for order $16$ and order $8$ to every order that is a power of two.
378395 The collapsing conjecture for facets of hierarchical model polytopes 
 Let $p\leq q\leq r$ be positive integers, and let $P_\Delta^{p,q,r}$ denote the polytope associated with the hierarchical model in the paper. A facet is **obtained by collapsing** if it arises from a facet of a smaller table through the paper's collapsing operation.

**Collapsing conjecture.** Suppose that

$$
p \leq q \leq r.
$$

Then all facets of $P_\Delta^{p,q,r}$ are obtained by collapsing from facets of $P_\Delta^{p,q,q}$.

The conjecture is motivated by computational results summarized in the paper's table, although the authors also exhibit a non-collapsible facet of $P_\Delta^{4,4,4}$, showing that not every facet arises by collapsing to a binary table. The conjecture remains open in the supplied source.
380362 The distance bound for loops with nonequivalent associator 
 Let $L=(G,\circ)$ and $M=(G,*)$ be Moufang loops on the same set $G$ of order $n$, and define their distance by

$$
d(\circ,*)=\left|\{(a,b)\in G\times G: a\circ b\ne a*b\}\right|.

 Suppose that $L$ and $M$ have nonequivalent associators. **Distance-bound conjecture.** Their distance satisfies

d(\circ,*)\ge 3n^2/8.
$$
 This conjecture asks for the minimum distance between Moufang loops with nonequivalent associator; the source gives no evidence of a proof or disproof.
381433 Cardoso–Luz conjecture on adverse graphs 
 An **adverse graph** is a graph $H$ without isolated vertices such that $upsilon(H)$ and $lambda_{min}(H)$ are integers, and, for every vertex $i\in V(H)$,

$$
\upsilon(H-N_H(i))=\upsilon(H),\qquad \lambda_{min}(H-N_H(i))=\lambda_{min}(H).
$$

Here $upsilon(H)$ is the optimal value of the quadratic stability-number bound and $lambda_{min}(H)$ is the least eigenvalue of $H$. A graph is a **$\mathcal{Q}$-graph** when it belongs to the class $\mathcal{Q}$ defined in the paper.

**Cardoso–Luz conjecture.** All adverse graphs are $\mathcal{Q}$-graphs.

Computational tests with adverse graphs supported the conjecture, but it remains open. Its resolution would clarify the polynomial-time recognition of $\mathcal{Q}$-graphs.
381805 Conjecture on the super edge-magic total strength of odd-cycle unicyclic graphs

and $k_i$ pendant vertices adjacent to each $a_i$, for $1\leq i\leq n$. Here $s m(G)$ denotes the super edge-magic total strength of $G$. **Super edge-magic total strength conjecture.**

The claim extends the known exact value for the uniform family $k_i=k$, while the paper presents computations for certain graphs in this broader family as supporting evidence; its general validity remains open.
