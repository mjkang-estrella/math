340049 Let $G$ be a triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices with $m$ edges, and let $\chi_\ell(G)$ denote its list chromatic number. **List-colouring conjecture.** There are constants $C_1,C_2>0$ such that

$$
\chi_\ell(G)\leq C_1\sqrt{n/\log n}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\chi_\ell(G)\leq C_2m^{1/3}/(\log m)^{2/3}.
$$

The paper describes these natural list-colouring versions as open problems, extending the fractional colouring questions to list chromatic number.
340077 Let $\underline{X}$ be the input random vector and let $\underline{X}_{{\mathcal M}_i}$ denote the model random vector obtained after $i$ cascade-tree stages. Write ${\mathcal D}(f_{\underline{X}}(\underline{x})\,||\,f_{\underline{X}_{{\mathcal M}_i}}(\underline{x}))$ for the Kullback–Leibler divergence between their distributions. **Convergence conjecture.** The KL divergence converges to $0$ as the number of cascade trees $i$ increases. The preceding theorem establishes only that this divergence decreases and converges to a finite value; whether the limiting value is $0$ is the conjectured strengthening.
340109 Let $G$ be an abelian group, and let $A,B\subseteq G$ be finite sets with $\#A=\#B$. Let $\mathcal{M}(A,B)$ be the set of matchings from $A$ to $B$, and let $F^{(t)}_{(A,B)}$ be the final nonempty set in the iterative acyclicity construction, where $t$ is the acyclicity index of $\mathcal{M}(A,B)$. **Terminal acyclicity-sequence conjecture.** If $A\cap(A+B)=\emptyset$, then every $f\in F^{(t)}_{(A,B)}$ is acyclic. This is presented as a proposed criterion for constructing acyclic matchings; its status is unresolved in the source.
340110 Let $G$ be an abelian group, and let $A,B\subseteq G$ be finite subsets with $A\cap(A+B)=\emptyset$. Write $\mathcal{M}(A,B)$ for the set of matchings from $A$ to $B$. An acyclicity sequence records the multiplicities of the support of a matching in non-increasing order. **Unit acyclicity-sequence conjecture.** There exists $f\in\mathcal{M}(A,B)$ such that $f$ is acyclic and its acyclicity sequence contains only $1$. The conjecture is motivated by simulation results and is intended as a constructive approach to the weak acyclic matching property; it remains open in the source.
340165 Let a **$d$-sphere** be a finite simple graph whose unit spheres are $(d-1)$-spheres and which becomes contractible after deleting a suitable vertex. An **edge refinement** is the operation of inserting a vertex into an edge and connecting it to the common neighbors of the edge's endpoints. A graph is **Eulerian** when the degrees of its relevant simplices are even.

**Eulerian edge-refinement conjecture.** $d$-spheres have edge refinements which are Eulerian.

The statement is proposed as a higher-dimensional extension of the result that every $2$-sphere admits edge refinements making it Eulerian. The source specifically says that the corresponding higher-dimensional bootstrapping remains to be established.
340172 Let an $athfrak{I}$-contractible graph be a graph reducible to a single vertex by $athfrak{I}$-contractible transformations. An expansion is the inverse of a collapse, and a graph is homotopically collapsible when its clique complex can be transformed to a point by a sequence of collapses and expansions. **Equivalence conjecture.** The family of $athfrak{I}$-contractible graphs is the same as the family of homotopically collapsible graphs. The supplied text provides no separate resolution evidence for this claim.
340212 Let $G$ be a connected $n$-vertex graph. Write $t_{par}(G)$ and $t_{seq}(G)$ for the parallel and sequential dispersion times, respectively, and let $t_{cov}(G)$ be the cover time.

**Dispersion-time conjecture.**

$$
t_{par}(G) \leq t_{seq}(G)+t_{cov}(G).
$$

This conjecture would imply that the parallel and sequential dispersion times have the same order up to the contribution of the cover time, and could resolve the preceding comparison problem for some classes of graphs. The source gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.
340268 Let $P$ be a simple $d$-polytope, and consider its vertex-edge graph. A subgraph is **non-separating** if its deletion does not separate the relevant graph, as in the source's formulation. **Perles' conjecture.** Every $(d-1)$-regular, induced, $(d-1)$-connected, non-separating subgraph of the vertex-edge graph of a simple $d$-polytope corresponds to a facet of $P$. This is a combinatorial characterization conjecture for facets of simple polytopes; the supplied source does not establish its resolution.
340389 Let $G=\prod_{i=1}^t K[a_i,b_i]$ be a direct product of balanced complete multipartite graphs, where $2\leq b_1\leq\cdots\leq b_t$ and $K[a_i,b_i]$ denotes the complete multipartite graph with $b_i$ parts of size $a_i$. Write $\operatorname{IR}(G)$ for the upper irredundance number and $\alpha(G)$ for the independence number. **Strengthened Defant–Iyer conjecture.** If $2\leq b_1\leq\cdots\leq b_t$, then

$$
\operatorname{IR}(G)=\alpha(G).
$$

The paper presents this as a strengthening suggested by methods of Defant and Iyer. The supplied text gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open here.
340479 Let $W_d(t)$ be a power series, and write $W_d[t^k]$ for the coefficient of $t^k$ in $W_d(t)$. Let $k\in\mathbb{N}$ and $d\geq 2k$. **Recurrence conjecture.** For fixed $k\in\mathbb{N}$ and $d\geq 2k$, one has

$$
W_d[t^{k}] = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \Big((-1)^{i+1} \cdot \binom{k}{i} \cdot W_{d-i}[t^{k}] \Big) + 1.
$$

The statement follows a theorem giving a related recurrence for $T(d+k,d)$; the supplied text gives no resolution status for this conjectural analogue.
340516 Let $w$ be a word of length $m$, and let $\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{hare}}}$ and $\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{tortoise}}}$ denote its hare- and tortoise-fertilities. **The hare–tortoise fertility gap conjecture.**

$$
\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{hare}}}-\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{tortoise}}}\leq\frac{m-5}{2}.
$$

The preceding theorem gives examples of length $2k+5$ attaining a gap of $k$, and the conjecture asserts that these examples are minimal with respect to length.
340517 Let $w$ be a word, and let $\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{hare}}}$ and $\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{tortoise}}}$ denote its hare- and tortoise-fertilities. **The hare–tortoise fertility comparison conjecture.**

$$
\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{hare}}}\leq 2\langle w\rangle_{\operatorname{\mathsf{tortoise}}}-2.
$$

This is proposed alongside the conjectured sharp bound on the difference between the two fertilities.
340524 Let $X$ be a smooth well-formed weighted complete intersection. A nef-partition is the toric partition required in the generalized Givental construction, and a good toric Landau–Ginzburg model is the corresponding Laurent-polynomial mirror model.

**Nef-partition conjecture.** The variety $X$ has a good nef-partition and a toric Landau–Ginzburg model.

The source explains that existence of nef-partitions for weighted complete intersections is expected but not proved in general. Thus the asserted existence remains open in the stated generality.
340525 Let $p=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_l)$ be a partition of $n$ into odd parts, and let $\operatorname{ind}_S(p)$ denote its associated seaweed-index statistic. **Parity conjecture.** The index $\operatorname{ind}_S(p)$ is even if and only if $l\equiv 1,2\pmod 4$, and it is odd if and only if $l\equiv 0,3\pmod 4$. This conjecture connects the parity of a seaweed-algebra statistic with the number of parts of an odd partition; its resolution is not supplied in the source.
340528 Let $G$ be a simple plane graph whose faces can be properly colored with two colors, with one color class consisting only of even faces.

**The face-coloring conjecture.** Every simple plane graph whose faces can be properly colored with two colors such that one color class contains only even faces is $3$-colorable.

This proposed extension arises from the paper's Alon–Tarsi argument for medial graphs and is presented as an affirmative conjecture. Its resolution is not given in the supplied text.
340531 Soit $M$ un matroïde et soit $r=\operatorname{rk}(M)$. Pour chaque entier $k$, $W_k(M)$ désigne le nombre de plats de rang $k$ de $M$. **Conjecture de Rota–Welsh.**

$$
(W_0(M),\dotsc,W_r(M))
$$

est une suite log-concave et donc unimodale; de plus, pour tout entier $k$ tel que $0\leq k\leq r/2$, on a $W_k(M)\leq W_{r-k}(M)$, et

$$
W_0(M)\leq W_1(M)\leq \dotsb \leq W_{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}(M).
$$

Cette conjecture porte sur les nombres de Whitney de seconde espèce et constitue une conjecture ouverte en combinatoire énumérative des matroïdes. Elle est démontrée dans le cas des matroïdes représentables.
340542 Let $H\colon\Omega\times\Omega\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ be a symmetric measurable weighted graph, or model. Call $H$ **antiferromagnetic** when all eigenvalues other than the top one are nonpositive, and call $H$ **biclique-maximizing** when complete bipartite graphs maximize the relevant homomorphism quantity. **Antiferromagnetic biclique-maximizing conjecture.** Every antiferromagnetic model is biclique-maximizing. This conjecture generalizes the semiproper-coloring result and complements the proved statement that every ferromagnetic model is clique-maximizing; the supplied status evidence indicates that the converse of this conjecture is false.
340552 Let $p\geq 5$ be an odd prime, let $\delta,a,c\in\mathbb{F}_p^{\times}$, and let $X_p(\delta,a,c)$ be Allen's Cayley graph on the specified subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(3,p)$. Let $\left(\frac{\cdot}{p}\right)$ denote the Legendre symbol. **Allen's conjecture.** If

$$

then $X_p(\delta,a,c)$ is Ramanujan. The source gives no resolution of this conjecture.

The problem is to maximize $\lambda_{max}(G)$ over $G\in {\cal K}(p,q,e)$. **Amitava's extremal spectral-radius conjecture.** Under these assumptions, an extremal graph solving this maximization problem is obtained from a complete bipartite graph by adding one vertex and a corresponding number of edges. This conjecture was proposed as a bipartite analogue of a conjecture of Brualdi and Hoffman, which was proved for nonbipartite graphs by Rowlinson; the supplied status evidence indicates that it has been resolved.
