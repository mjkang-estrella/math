## 340110: exact refutation of source Conjecture3.7
Primary: Aliabadi–Soleimany Dizicheh, arXiv1808.04975v3, pp1–5, especially preliminary standing equal-cardinality/nonempty assumptions and Conjecture3.7. The ambient group is any abelian group, not only prime cyclic groups. A matching is a bijection f:A→B with a+f(a) outside A, and acyclic means uniquely determined among matchings by the full labelled multiplicity function. Unit sequence means every positive multiplicity equals1.

Take G=(Z/3Z)×(Z/2Z), A=B=(Z/3Z)×{1}. These are nonempty equal-size finite sets, 0 is outside B, and A+B=(Z/3Z)×{0}, disjoint from A. Thus every bijection A→B is a matching. Suppose f has unit acyclicity sequence. Its three sums lie in the three-element set A+B and are distinct; therefore mf is exactly the indicator of A+B. Put t=(1,0) and define g(a)=f(a)+t. Translation by t permutes B, so g is also a matching and g≠f (indeed at every argument). For any x in G, mg(x)=mf(x−t)=mf(x), because A+B is invariant under t and mf is its indicator. Hence f is not acyclic. This applies to EVERY unit-sequence matching, proving that no matching satisfies both requirements of Conjecture3.7. Unit-sequence matchings do exist here: f(a)=a gives sums (2x,0), distinct since multiplication by2 permutes Z/3Z. Thus the obstruction is labelled-support symmetry, not absence of bijections or a missing cardinality hypothesis. More generally the same argument works for any nontrivial finite H with A=B=H×{1} in H×Z/2Z whenever unit sums occur; if none occur the existence claim fails anyway.

Adversarial self-check: adding t to the OUTPUT preserves B and admissibility; it is not translating the input or silently comparing supports instead of multiplicities. The literal claim quantifies over all abelian groups, so one admissible six-element group refutes it. The standing assumptions omitted in the MathDB record are satisfied. Not a claim to refute the weaker existence-of-an-acyclic-matching conjecture: constant-sum bijections exist and are acyclic in this example. Source Theorem3.5 does not apply: B∪{0} is not a subgroup. Source examples3.3/3.4 give some nonacyclic unit matchings but do not establish that ALL unit matchings of this coset pair are nonacyclic.
