   Partitions


    Definition
    A (k, `)-partition is a set partition of {1, 2, . . . , k`} with exactly ` blocks each of size k.

                                                               P = {P1 , P2 , . . . , P` }

    These are also called uniform set partitions.
    The set of all uniform-(k, `) partitions is denoted by U(k, `).

    Two set partitions

                                    P = {P1 , P2 , . . . , P` } and Q = {Q1 , Q2 , . . . , Q` }


       1    are intersecting if Pi = Qj for some i and j.
       2    are t-intersecting if Pi1 = Qj1 , Pi2 = Qj2 , . . . , Pit = Qjt
            for distinct i1 , . . . , it and distinct j1 , . . . , jt .
       3    are partially-t intersecting if |Pi ∩ Qj | ≥ t for some i and j.

   Derangement Graph


    Definition
    For any k, ` define the partition derangement graph, ΓG(k,`) .
            The vertices are the uniform (k, `)-partitions.
            Vertices P, Q ∈ U (k, `) are adjacent if and only if P and Q are not partially
            t-intersecting.


    A set of uniform partitions is a coclique (independent set) in ΓG(k,`) , exactly if the set is
                             a partially t-intersecting set of partitions.

    Graph Properties:
       1    The graph ΓG(k,`) is regular, denote the degree by dk,`
       2    The derangement graph is vertex-transitive, the group Sym(k`) acts transitively on
            the vertices of ΓG(k,`) .
       3    A resolvable balanced incomplete block t-design on k` points with blocksize k and
            index λ = 1, if it exists, is a maximum clique.
