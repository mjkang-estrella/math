Let $G$ be a triangle-free graph with chromatic number $k$, and let $\frac{}{ }$ denote a positive real parameter. **Erdős's conjecture.** For every $\frac{}{ } > 0$, there exists $k_0(\frac{}{ })$ such that, for $k \geq k_0(\frac{}{ })$, every triangle-free $k$-chromatic graph contains more than $k^{2-\frac{}{ }}$ odd cycles of different lengths.

The conjecture concerns how many distinct odd cycle lengths must occur in triangle-free graphs of large chromatic number. The paper proves a stronger result, namely that such graphs contain cycles of a quadratic-logarithmic number of consecutive lengths, so this conjecture is solved.
