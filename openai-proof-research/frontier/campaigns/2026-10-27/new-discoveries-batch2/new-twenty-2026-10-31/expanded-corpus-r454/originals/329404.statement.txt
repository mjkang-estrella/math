Let $n,r\in\mathbb N$ with $r$ dividing $n$. Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices with degree sequence $d_1\leq\dots\leq d_n$. **Balogh, Kostochka and Treglown's conjecture.** If

$$
d_i\geq (r-2)n/r+i
$$

for all $i<n/r$, and

$$
d_{n/r+1}\geq (r-1)n/r,
$$

then $G$ contains a perfect $K_r$-packing. A perfect $K_r$-packing is a collection of vertex-disjoint copies of $K_r$ covering all vertices of $G$. This conjecture proposes a degree-sequence analogue of the Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem, characterising sufficient degree conditions for a perfect clique packing; its resolution status is not specified in the source.
