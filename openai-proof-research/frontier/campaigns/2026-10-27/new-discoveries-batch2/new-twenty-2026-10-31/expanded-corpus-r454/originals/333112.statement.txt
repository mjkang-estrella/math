Let $\mathfrak{C}$ be the set of all pairs of non-empty sets of cycles, and let $R(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)$ be the corresponding generalised Ramsey number. For $(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)\in\mathfrak{C}$, let $\mathfrak{m}(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)$ be the number defined by

$$
\mathfrak{m}(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)=\max\!\big(5,\min(\ga^2+\ga_e^1/2-1,2\ga^2-1),\min(\ga^1+\ga_e^2/2-1,2\ga^1-1)\big).
$$

Theorem~ gives the lower bound $R(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)\geq \mathfrak{m}(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)$, with the stronger bound $R(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)\geq \mathfrak{m}(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)+1$ for the exceptional class specified there. **Equality conjecture.** Equality holds in the lower bound of Theorem~ for all pairs $(\Ga_1,\Ga_2)\in\mathfrak{C}$.

The conjecture extends the cases established in the theorem, including all pairs for which $\min(\Ga_1\cup\Ga_2)\leq 6$ and the specified classes $\mathfrak{C}_1\cup\mathfrak{C}_2$, as well as the special case where both shortest-cycle lengths are even. Determining whether equality always holds remains open in the source.
