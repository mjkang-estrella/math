Let $p$ be a prime and let $A,B$ be non-empty subsets of $\mathbb{F}_p$. For subsets $A,B\subset\mathbb{F}_p$, write $A+B=\{a+b\mid a\in A,\ b\in B\}$ and define the **restricted sumset** $A\dot{+}B=\{a+b\mid a\in A,\ b\in B,\ a\neq b\}$. **Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture.** For every $A\subset\mathbb{F}_p$, 

$$
|A\dot{+}A|\geqslant\min\left\{p,2|A|-3\right\}.
$$

The conjecture concerns the restricted analogue of the Cauchy–Davenport theorem and was later proved, so the database status is recorded as solved.
