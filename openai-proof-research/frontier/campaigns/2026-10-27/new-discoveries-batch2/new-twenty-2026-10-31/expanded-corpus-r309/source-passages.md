Definition 1.1.



We say that the equation  $P(x,y,z)=0$  is partition regular if for every finite partition of  ${\mathbb{N}}$  there exist distinct  $x,y,z$  on the same cell that satisfy the equation.





Rado’s theorem states that for  $a,b,c\in{\mathbb{N}}$ , the equation




 $ax+by=cz$
