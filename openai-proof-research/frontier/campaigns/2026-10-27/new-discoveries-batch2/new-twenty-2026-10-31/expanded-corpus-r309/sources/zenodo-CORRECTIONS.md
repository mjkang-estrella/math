# Version 3: stronger statements, shorter proof

Version 2 correctly established both conjecture disproofs. Its limitations
reported no arbitrary-order family. Version 3 now supplies two such families:

1. Complete graphs of every order n≥4, with two explicit integer conductances
   and all elementary inequalities strict.
2. Non-RN split-graph supports of every odd order n≥5, with explicit integer
   conductances, submodular capacity, and an unbounded number of negatively
   curved vertices.

The second family's support-level obstruction applies to every positive
weighting, so its universal quantifier is not inferred from one bad weighting.

The same scalar algebra gives an infinite hierarchy of networks that fail
submodularity while every proper Kron reduction passes. This prevents a
bounded retained-order characterization. Proper reductions here retain at
least two vertices; one-vertex resistance-capacity normalization is undefined.

The main exposition shrinks from twelve pages to six. Prior exact certificates,
sharp minimum witness sizes, uniqueness, and special-case intervals remain in
the supplements. Their earlier exploratory limitations should be read in their
specific version-2 context.

The original version-1 correction is retained: its assertion that the unweighted
five-vertex example did not settle Conjecture 7.7 was wrong. The spanning-tree
hub-degree identity proves that support is not RN for any positive weighting.

None of these extensions establishes independent external review or historical
priority. Negative-vertex count is unbounded; negative-curvature magnitude is
not claimed unbounded.
