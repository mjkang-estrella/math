Let $c$ be a $2$-coloring of the edges of $K_n$, let $E(c,H)$ be the set of edges contained in no monochromatic copy of $H$, and define

$$
f(n,H)=\max_c |E(c,H)|.
$$

Let ${\rm ex}(n,H)$ denote the Turán number of $H$. **Yuan's conjecture.** For any graph $H$ and all sufficiently large $n$, there exists a constant $C=C(H)$ such that

$$
f(n,H)={\rm ex}(n,H)+C.
$$

This conjecture proposes that the excess of the maximum number of NIM-$H$ edges over the Turán number eventually stabilizes at a constant depending only on $H$. It is motivated by Yuan's example showing that equality with the Turán number does not hold for every graph $H$; the supplied source gives no resolution of the conjecture.
