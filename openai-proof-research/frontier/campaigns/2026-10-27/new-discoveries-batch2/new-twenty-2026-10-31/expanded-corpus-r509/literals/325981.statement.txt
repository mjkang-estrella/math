Let $H$ be a strictly $2$-balanced, edge-color-critical graph. Write $e(H)$ for its number of edges, $\chi(H)$ for its chromatic number, and

$$
m_2(H)=\frac{e(H)-1}{v(H)-2},
$$

where $v(H)$ is the number of vertices of $H$. Let $\mathcal{F}_{n,m}(H)$ be the family of $H$-free graphs with vertex set of size $n$ and exactly $m$ edges. **Typical-partite structure conjecture.** There exists a constant $C$ such that, whenever

$$
m \geq Cn^{2-1/m_2(H)}(\log n)^{1/(e(H)-1)},
$$

almost all graphs in $\mathcal{F}_{n,m}(H)$ are $(\chi(H)-1)$-partite. This would extend known typical-structure results for unrestricted $H$-free graphs to the sparse fixed-edge setting; its status is open in the supplied source.
