For a graph $G$, let $\nu(G)$ denote its matching number and $\nu_{ur}(G)$ its uniquely restricted matching number. For every positive real number $\epsilon$ and every positive integer $\Delta$, consider graphs of maximum degree at most $\Delta$ and girth at least $g$. **High-girth approximation conjecture.** There is some positive integer $g=g(\epsilon,\Delta)$ such that

$$
\nu_{ur}(G)\geq (1-\epsilon)\nu(G)
$$

for every graph $G$ of maximum degree at most $\Delta$ and girth at least $g$. The conjecture asserts that, for fixed maximum degree, sufficiently large girth makes the uniquely restricted matching number asymptotically as close as desired to the ordinary matching number; the source gives no resolution, so it remains open.
