Let $k$-uniform hypergraphs be hypergraphs whose hyperedges all have cardinality $k$. Let $C_n$ denote the cycle on $n$ vertices, and let $mu^k_n$ be the minimum number of hyperedges in a $k$-uniform hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ with edge intersection hypergraph $EI(\mathcal{H})=C_n$.

**Asymptotic sharpness conjecture.** For any $k ge 3$ there exists an $n_k in \mathbb{N}$ such that for every $n ge n_k$,

$$
\mu^k_n = \left\lceil \frac{3n}{k} \right\rceil.
$$

The equality would show that the general lower bound is asymptotically sharp for every uniformity $k\ge 3$. The paper establishes the equality for sufficiently large $n$ when $k\in\{3,4,6\}$ and when $k=5$ with $n\equiv 0\pmod 5$, while the conjecture remains open for all $k>6$ and also for $k=5$ when $n\not\equiv 0\pmod 5$.
