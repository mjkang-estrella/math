Primary text lines 69–75 define word-representation by alternation of each distinct pair, uniformity, the minimum representation number and infinity for non-word-representable graphs. Lines 78–93 explain the complete-graph/one-copy and circle-graph/two-copy conventions. Positive k is intended: a word with one copy of every vertex represents a complete graph; allowing zero as the minimum would destroy that convention. The source's incidental statement about all cycles having representation number 2 includes the familiar C3 complete-graph exception; that does not alter the conjecture or this certificate.

Lines 370–388 define the toroidal graph by adding the other wrap edges to a cylinder, explicitly identify it as C_m □ C_n, give known words at (3,3) and (3,4), and quantify Conjecture 1 by m,n ≥ 3 and m+n ≥ 8.
