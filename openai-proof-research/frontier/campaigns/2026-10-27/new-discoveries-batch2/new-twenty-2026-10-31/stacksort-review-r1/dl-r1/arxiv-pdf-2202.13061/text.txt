                                           1         Introduction
                                           Recently, Defant and Propp [2020] defined the degree of noninvertibility of a function f : X →
                                           Y between two finite nonempty sets by
                                                                                                  1 X −1
                                                                                   deg(f ) =             |f (f (x))|

                                           as a measure of how far f is from being injective. Interested mainly in endofunctions (also called
                                           dynamical systems within the field of dynamical algebraic combinatorics), that is, functions
                                           f : X → X, they then computed the degrees of noninvertibility of several specific functions and
                                           studied the connection between the degrees of noninvertibility of functions and those of their
                                           iterates.
