1417:                                             8. Future Directions
1418:    Theorem 1.1 determines the orbit structure of toric promotion when G is a forest. It is still open
1419: to understand the dynamics of toric promotion for other graphs, including cycle graphs.
1420:    Theorem 1.3 determines the orbit structure of any permutoric promotion operator when G is a
1421: path. It would be interesting to gain a better understanding of TProπ when G is another type of
1422: tree, even in the special case when π −1 has 2 cyclic descents. A natural place to start could be the
1423: case when G is obtained from Pathn−1 by adding a new vertex that is adjacent to vn−2 (i.e., G is
1424: the Dynkin diagram of type Dn ).

1158: cannot be piecewise hereditary, since periodicity only appears for Dynkin module type,
1159: which cannot appear here, and for sheaf type where the possible Coxeter numbers — for

1161: be of Fuchsian type. In fact, by [23, Table 2] there are only 22 weight triples yielding a
1162: Fuchsian singularity category with periodic Coxeter transformation. For these 22 triples it
1163: is easily checked that they don’t belong to the green wall. Therefore, assuming the validity

1165: the piecewise hereditary case extends to the Fuchsian case as well. That is, under the
