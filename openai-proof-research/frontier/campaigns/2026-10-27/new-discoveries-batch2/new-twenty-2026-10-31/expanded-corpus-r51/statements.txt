357843 One-third conjecture for clustered sets in planar graphs

Write $\alpha_{\mathcal{P},2}(G)$ for the maximum size of a set $S$ whose induced subgraph has maximum degree at most $1$, equivalently, such that every vertex in $S$ is adjacent to at most one other vertex in $S$. **One-third conjecture for planar graphs.** Every planar graph has such a set $S$ containing at least one third of its vertices; equivalently,

$$
\alpha_{\mathcal{P},2}(G)=\frac{1}{3}.
$$

The octahedron gives the matching upper bound, so the conjecture asserts that this bound is best possible. It is implied by the Albertson–Berman conjecture that every planar graph has an induced forest on at least half of its vertices, but neither conjecture is resolved in the supplied text.

357876 Weighted logarithmic mean characterisation of harmonic functions
Let $D$ be a bounded domain in $\mathbb{R}^m$, with $m\geqslant 2$. Let $A_x(r_1,r_2)$ be an admissible annular domain centred at $x$, with $r_2>r_1>0$, and let $B_{r_2}(x)$ be admissible. Denote by $M^\circ(S_r(x),u)$ the spherical mean of $u$ over $S_r(x)$, and let $r$ either attain each value in $(r_1,r_2)$ or equal $r_i$ for $i=1$ or $2$. **Weighted logarithmic mean conjecture.** If $u\in C^0(D)$ satisfies, for every $x\in D$ and every such $A_x(r_1,r_2)$,

$$
\frac{m}{|A(r_1,r_2)|}\int_{A_x(r_1,r_2)}u(y)\log\frac{r}{|x-y|}\,\mathrm{d}y=M^\circ(S_r(x),u),
$$

then $u$ is harmonic in $D$. The identity extends the logarithmic weighted mean property of harmonic functions from balls to annular domains; the stated result is presented after the corresponding harmonic characterisations and its resolution is not established in the supplied text.

357894 Density threshold for typical Hodge loci in the Torelli locus
Let $g\geqslant4$ be an integer, let $S\subset\mathcal A_g$ be a closed Hodge generic subvariety of dimension $q$, and let $\mathbb V$ be the induced polarizable $\mathbb Z$-VHS on $S$. The **Torelli-locus typical-density conjecture.** The typical Hodge locus of $S$ for $\mathbb V$ is analytically dense if and only if $q\geqslant g-1$. When this holds, the Hecke translates of $\mathcal A_{g-1}\times\mathcal A_1$ in $\mathcal A_g$ intersect $S$ in an analytically dense subset. This predicts the precise dimension threshold for density in the Siegel modular variety and identifies the largest relevant strict special subvarieties.
