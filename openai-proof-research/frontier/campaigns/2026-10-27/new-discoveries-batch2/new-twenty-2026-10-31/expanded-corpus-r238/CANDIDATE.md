No negative coefficient, combinatorial interpretation, or uniform proof emerged. Evaluating the printed coefficient formula alone would also face the assignment's source-formula exclusion. A real continuation needs a nonroutine structural insight, not a bounded nonnegativity theorem.

3. **345516, simple polygon intersections.** Five independent side-count regimes received 18-second exact-predicate local searches with recorded seeds, proposal counts, maxima and integer coordinates. There is no violation. The source Figure 1 constructions and the published almost-optimal theorem were checked; no modification of a printed example is proposed.
