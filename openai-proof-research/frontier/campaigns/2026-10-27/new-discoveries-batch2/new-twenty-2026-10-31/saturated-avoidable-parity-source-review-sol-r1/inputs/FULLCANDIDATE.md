## Full proof
Write V=G/2G as a vector space over F2, and q:G→V. Let U be avoidable, U∩2G empty. Pick a coloring c:G→F2 such that x≠y and x+y∈U imply c(x)≠c(y).

For each u∈U put R_u(x)=u−x. This reflection has no fixed point: R_u(x)=x would imply u=2x. Thus c(R_u(x))=c(x)+1 for EVERY x.

Claim: there is no odd list u1,...,u_(2t+1) from U whose images sum to0 in V. Repetitions are allowed. Suppose there were such a list. Put
 A=u_(2t+1)−u_(2t)+u_(2t−1)−...−u2+u1.
The difference between A and the unsigned sum is twice a group element, so q(A)=0. Choose x with 2x=A. Successively apply R_u1,...,R_u_(2t+1). Induction on the length, using only commutativity, gives endpoint A−x=x. Each of the 2t+1 steps toggles c, giving c(x)=c(x)+1, impossible in F2. No step is a loop; repeated nonconsecutive vertices do not invalidate color toggling. This proves the claim without a simplicity assumption on the closed walk.

Let S=q(U). On span(S) define f(sum_j epsilon_j s_j)=sum_j epsilon_j in F2. This is well defined: two representations differ by a finite zero relation. An odd such relation, after collecting the coefficients1, is the forbidden odd list above. The zero relation of length0 has even parity. It follows directly from concatenating finite expressions that f is linear and f(s)=1 for s∈S. Since G is finitely generated, V is finite dimensional; extend a basis of span(S) to a basis of V and assign f=0 on added basis vectors. This gives a linear functional fbar:V→F2 with fbar(q(u))=1 for every u∈U.

For saturated U, U is nonempty. Indeed {0} is avoidable in every abelian group: partition each two-element orbit {x,−x} of negation into opposite colors; singleton orbits may receive either color since the two summands must be distinct. Thus the empty set is not maximal. Consequently fbar is nonzero. Set phi=fbar∘q and H=phi^−1({1}). Then U⊆H. H is avoidable: partition G into the fibers of phi; two elements in one fiber have sum with phi-value0, so their sum is not in H. Maximality of U among all avoidable sets implies U=H.

Finally, for the source decomposition of G, every odd-order cyclic factor has zero image under a homomorphism to F2. A Z or even cyclic factor maps by its coordinate modulo2 times a coefficient a_i∈{0,1}. Hence phi(x)=sum a_i x_i mod2; because phi is nonzero these coefficients are not all0. This is EXACTLY the printed necessary conclusion, including arbitrary rank, all torsion factors, finite and infinite G and potentially infinite U. If V=0 then U must be empty, so there is no saturated U under the hypotheses; this case was already excluded by the nonemptiness argument, not silently dropped.

## Separate sufficiency warning
The assertion preceding Conjecture1 that every listed parity hyperplane is saturated needs exceptions. In an elementary abelian2-group, adding0 changes no edge, since x+y=0 implies x=y. A parity hyperplane therefore is avoidable but not saturated. This is a source-side converse defect, not a counterexample to the literal necessity proved above; no extra credit is proposed for it. The theorem endpoint below deliberately does not assert the converse.
