 323251 The split exactness conjecture for matching-complex deletion sequences 
 Let $n\geq3$, let $e$ be an edge of the complete graph on $n$ vertices, and let $\mathsf{M}_n\setminus e$ be the matching complex with the vertex $e$ deleted. Consider the sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow\tilde{H}_d(\mathsf{M}_n\setminus e;\mathbb{Z})\longrightarrow\tilde{H}_d(\mathsf{M}_n;\mathbb{Z})\longrightarrow\langle e\rangle\otimes\tilde{H}_{d-1}(\mathsf{M}_{[n]\setminus e};\mathbb{Z})\longrightarrow0.
$$

**Split exactness conjecture.** This sequence, obtained by cutting the long exact $0$-$e$-$2$ sequence, is split exact for every $n\geq3$ and every $d$.

If true, the long exact deletion sequence would decompose into short split exact sequences, giving a direct-sum description of the homology groups. The supplied text gives no proof or resolution, so the conjecture remains open.

 323878 Symmetry conjecture for q,t-Narayana polynomials of parallelogram polyominoes 
 For positive integers $m,n$, let $\mathsf{Para}_{m,n}$ be the set of parallelogram polyominoes with parameters $(m,n)$. For $\mathcal P\in\mathsf{Para}_{m,n}$, let $\mathsf{area}(\mathcal P)$ and $\mathsf{parabounce}(\mathcal P)$ be its area and bounce-weight statistics, and define

$$
F_{m,n}(q,t)=\sum_{\mathcal P\in\mathsf{Para}_{m,n}}q^{\mathsf{area}(\mathcal P)}t^{\mathsf{parabounce}(\mathcal P)}.
$$

**q,t-Narayana symmetry conjecture.** For all positive integers $m,n$, the distribution is symmetric:

$$
F_{m,n}(q,t)=F_{m,n}(t,q).
$$

These polynomials extend the area and bounce statistics from Dyck paths to parallelogram polyominoes. The conjecture had been verified in the paper for all pairs with $m\leq11$ and arbitrary positive $n$, but no general proof is supplied.

 324030 The induced-subposet characterization of strict pattern-avoidance inequality 
 Let $P$ be a poset, and let $Av_P(\tau)$ denote the number of permutations of $P$ avoiding the pattern $\tau$. Let $R_1$ and $R_2$ be the two posets depicted in the source, with $R_1$ a four-element path and $R_2$ a five-element path, viewed as induced subposets.

**Pattern-avoidance inequality conjecture.** For every poset $P$,

$$
Av_P(\{1\}\{1,2\}\{2\}) \leq Av_P(\{1\}\{2\}\{1,2\}),
$$

with strict inequality if and only if $P$ contains either $R_1$ or $R_2$ as an induced subposet.

The conjecture proposes a universal comparison between two pattern-avoidance counts and characterizes exactly when equality is strict. The source presents it as an open problem; no resolution is supplied here.

 324127 Small-dilate least-eigenvalue and multiplicity conjecture for simplicial rook graphs 
 Let $SR(d,n)$ be the simplicial rook graph, let $\tau(SR(d,n))$ denote its least adjacency eigenvalue, and let $M(d,n)$ be the Mahonian number. **Small-dilate eigenvalue conjecture.** If $n\leq\binom{d}{2}$, then $\tau(SR(d,n))=-n$, and the dimension of the corresponding eigenspace is $M(d,n)$. The paper verifies this for all $d\leq 6$ using Sage; in general it remains unclear how to exclude a smaller eigenvalue or additional linearly independent eigenvectors with eigenvalue $-n$.

 331649 Bollobás's consecutive-order tree packing conjecture 
 Let $k\geq 1$ and let $T_n,T_{n-1},\dots,T_{n-k+1}$ be trees, where $T_{n-j}$ has $n-j$ vertices. The trees **pack into** $K_n$ when they can be found as pairwise edge-disjoint subgraphs of the complete graph $K_n$. **Bollobás's conjecture.** For every $k\geq 1$ there is an $n_0(k)$ such that if $n>n_0(k)$, then any set of $k$ trees $T_n,T_{n-1},\dots,T_{n-k+1}$ such that $T_{n-j}$ has $n-j$ vertices pack into $K_n$. This weakens Gyárfás's Tree Packing Conjecture, which asks for packing trees of every order from $1$ through $n$; the paper confirms the conjecture for certain trees and, as a consequence, for $k\leq 5$.

 357336 Bounded nonrepetitive connection conjecture for 2-connected graphs 
 Let $G$ be a graph, and let $\operatorname{nrc}(G)$ denote the least number of colors in a nonrepetitive connected coloring of $G$.

**Bounded nonrepetitive connection conjecture.** There exists a constant $t$ such that every $2$-connected graph $G$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{nrc}(G)\leq t.
$$

The paper establishes finite bounds for several more highly connected classes, including $4$-connected graphs, but leaves the existence of a uniform bound for all $2$-connected graphs open.

 366636 Reflection-symmetric Dyck-path wreath conjecture 
 Let $k$ be a positive integer. Let $\mathbb{Z}_{2k+1}$ be the integers modulo $2k+1$, let $\mathcal{D}_{k}$ be the set of Dyck $k$-paths, and let $C_k$ denote the $k$th Catalan number. For a permutation $\pi$ of $\mathbb{Z}_{2k+1}$, let $\mathcal{F}_{\pi}$ be its associated wreath. For $D\in\mathcal{D}_k$, let $D^{(R)}$ be the Dyck path obtained by reflecting $D$ in the line $x=k$.

**Reflection-symmetric strengthening.** There exists a set $\Pi=\{\pi_1,\pi_2,\ldots,\pi_{C_k}\}$ of $C_k$ permutations of $\mathbb{Z}_{2k+1}$, each fixing $0$, and a bijection $\varphi:\Pi\to\mathcal{D}_k$ such that

$$
\mathbb{Z}_{2k+1}^{(k)}=\bigcup_{i=1}^{C_k}\mathcal{F}_{\pi_i},
$$

and, for any $i$ and $j$, the $j$th step of $\varphi(\pi_i)$ is a rise if and only if $\pi_i(j)\in\{1,2,\ldots,k\}$, and

$$
\varphi^{-1}(D)(j)+\varphi^{-1}(D^{(R)})(-j)=0
$$

for every Dyck $k$-path $D$ and every $j\in\mathbb{Z}_{2k+1}$.

This is the stronger statement suggested by the authors' search through $k\leq4$. It adds a reflection symmetry relating the permutations paired by reflected Dyck paths; the source gives no proof or resolution.

 367277 The 6-edge-connectivity conjecture for strongly connected modulo 3-orientations 
 A graph is **\mathbb Z_3-connected** if, for every function $\beta:V(G)\to\mathbb Z_3$ satisfying $\sum_{v\in V(G)}\beta(v)\equiv 0\pmod{3}$, there is an orientation $D$ of $G$ such that $d_D^+(v)-d_D^-(v)\equiv\beta(v)\pmod{3}$ for every $v\in V(G)$. An orientation is **strongly connected** if, for every two vertices $x,y\in V(G)$, there is a directed path from $x$ to $y$. A graph is **$\mathcal S_3$-connected** if, for every $\mathbb Z_3$ boundary function $\beta$, it has a strongly connected orientation $D$ satisfying $d_D^+(v)-d_D^-(v)\equiv\beta(v)\pmod{3}$ for every vertex $v$.

**The 6-edge-connectivity conjecture.** Every 6-edge-connected graph has a strongly connected modulo $3$-orientation.

This conjecture strengthens the known result that every 6-edge-connected graph is $\mathbb Z_3$-connected. It was proved for 8-edge-connected graphs by Lovász, Thomassen, Wu and Zhang, while the 6-edge-connected case is therefore no longer open.

 367343 Keszegh–Pálvölgyi conjecture on 2-coloring one-headed directed hypergraphs 
 Let $H=(V,E)$ be a directed hypergraph, with each hyperedge partitioned into head-vertices and tail-vertices; write $h(e)$ and $t(e)$ for the head and tail of a hyperedge $e$. A proper 2-coloring is a coloring of the vertices with two colors such that every hyperedge contains vertices from at least two color classes.

**Keszegh–Pálvölgyi conjecture.** If every hyperedge has more tail-vertices than head-vertices, and for every $e_{1},e_{2}\in E(H)$ with $|e_{1}\cap e_{2}|=1$ the common vertex is a head-vertex of at least one of $e_{1},e_{2}$, then $H$ admits a proper 2-coloring.

This conjecture generalizes Lovász's sufficient condition for proper 2-colorability of hypergraphs. The source states that it is proved in the special case where every hyperedge has exactly one head-vertex; in that case the size condition means that every hyperedge has at least two tail-vertices. The general conjecture remains unresolved in the supplied text.

 368254 Exponential average-availability conjecture for local girth in planar graphs 
 Let $G$ be a planar graph and let $f$ be a local girth function for $G$. For an operation sequence $\sigma$, define its average availability by

$$
\overline a(\sigma)=\prod_{i=1}^n a(\sigma_i)^{1/n},
$$

where the availability of $\mathsf{Del}(G,f,v)$ is $f(v)+1$ and the availability of $\mathsf{DelSave}(G,f,v,w)$ is $f(v)-f(w)$. A sequence is **legal** if each operation is legal, and it removes every vertex when it certifies weak $f$-degeneracy.

**Exponential average-availability conjecture.** There exists a constant $c>1$ such that, for every planar graph $G$ and local girth function $f$ for $G$, there is a legal sequence of operations $\sigma$ with average availability at least $c$ that removes every vertex of $G$.

Together with the proposition preceding the conjecture, this would yield exponentially many local-girth correspondence colourings of planar graphs: the number of colourings would be at least $\overline a(\sigma)^{v(G)}$, hence at least $c^{v(G)}$. The supplied text gives no evidence that the conjecture has been resolved.

 368506 The multiplicative conjecture for star-pattern intervals 
 Let $s\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 4}$, and let $G=G^{(a)}$ be any graph pattern in the statement, with ambient edge multiplicity $a=2$. **Multiplicative conjecture.** If

$$
\Sigma_s(K_{1,4})\leq q<\min\left(\Sigma_s(K_{1,7}),\Sigma_s(K_{1,\infty})\right),
$$

then

$$
\operatorname{ex}_\Pi(s,q)=e^{\pi_{\operatorname{Petersen}}}.
$$

For all $k\geq 7$, if

$$
\Sigma_s(K_{1,k})\leq q<\min\left(\Sigma_s(K_{1,k+1}),\Sigma_s(K_{1,\infty})\right),
$$

then

$$
\operatorname{ex}_\Pi(s,q)=e^{\pi_{K_{1,k}}}.
$$

Finally, at $q=\Sigma_s(K_{1,\infty})$,

$$
\operatorname{ex}_\Pi(s,q)=e^{\pi_{K_{1,\infty}}}.
$$

The conjecture proposes the multiplicative extremal values on the remaining star-pattern intervals for ambient multiplicity $2$. The paper gives corresponding extremal constructions, but the full assertions are presented as tentative conjectures.

 368663 AENY spread-global dimension stabilization conjecture for grid posets 
 Let $[k]=\{0,\ldots,k-1\}$ be the standard $k$-element totally ordered set, and let $\mathrm{gl.dim}_{\operatorname{sprd}}$ denote the global dimension associated with the spread exact structure. For each $m\geq 1$, define

$$
g_m:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N},\qquad g_m(k)=\mathrm{gl.dim}_{\operatorname{sprd}}\left([k]\times[m]\right).
$$

**AENY spread-global dimension stabilization conjecture.** For every $m\geq 1\in\mathbb{N}$, the function $g_m$ is constant when $k\geq 2+m$.

The conjecture predicts stabilization of the spread-global dimension for Cartesian products of a fixed finite total order with increasingly large finite total orders. The spread-global dimension is finite for finite posets, but is not uniformly bounded over all two-dimensional grids; no explicit general bound on the global dimension is currently known.

 369619 The original inverse Gowers conjecture 
 Let $p\geq 2$ be a fixed prime, let $d\geq 1$, and let $F:\mathbb{F}_p^n\to\mathbb{C}$ be a bounded function with $\|F\|_{\infty}\leq 1$. If its Gowers norm satisfies

$$
\|F\|_{U^{d+1}}\geq\varepsilon,
$$

then there exists a degree-$d$ polynomial $P:\mathbb{F}_p^n\to\mathbb{F}_p$ such that

$$
\left|\left\langle F,e^{2\pi iP/p}\right\rangle\right|\geq\delta(p,d,\varepsilon).
$$

**Original inverse Gowers conjecture.** For every such $F$, a lower bound on the $U^{d+1}$ norm implies correlation with a phase polynomial of degree $d$, with correlation bounded below by a positive quantity depending only on $p$, $d$, and $\varepsilon$. This is the inverse problem for the Gowers norm: large uniformity norm should reveal low-degree polynomial structure. The precise validity and dependence of the bound are not established here.

 370344 The joint cpen-last distribution conjecture for Jacobi permutations 
 Let $\mathfrak{J}_{n}$ be the set of Jacobi permutations of length $n$. For a permutation $\pi$ of length at least $2$, let $\operatorname{cpen}(\pi)$ be the penultimate letter of the complement of $\pi$, and let $\operatorname{last}(\pi)$ be its last letter. Define

$$
j_{n,i,k}^{(\operatorname{cpen},\operatorname{last})}=\mathopen{}\mathclose\bgroup\originalleft|\{\,\pi\in\mathfrak{J}_{n}:\operatorname{cpen}(\pi)=i\text{ and }\operatorname{last}(\pi)=k\,\}\aftergroup\egroup\originalright|.
$$

Let $E_{n,k}$ denote the Entringer numbers.

**The joint cpen-last distribution conjecture.** For all $n\geq3$ and $i,k\geq1$, we have

$$
j_{n,i,k}^{(\operatorname{cpen},\operatorname{last})}=E_{n-1,n+1-i-k}.
$$

If true, this gives a symmetric joint distribution for $\operatorname{cpen}$ and $\operatorname{last}$ over $\mathfrak{J}_{n}$; the source presents it as supported by empirical evidence.

 371492 Ji's generic length-spectrum injectivity conjecture 
 For every $d\geq 2$, let ${\mathcal M}_d$ be the moduli space of degree-$d$ rational maps, let $E_d\subset {\mathcal M}_d$ be a subset defined over $\mathbb R$, and let $\overline g$ denote the complex conjugate of $g$. Two rational maps are **conjugate** when they represent the same point of the moduli space.

**Generic length-spectrum injectivity conjecture.** There is a Zariski closed proper subset $E_d\subset {\mathcal M}_d$ defined over $\mathbb R$ such that, for every $[f]\notin E_d$, if some $g\in\operatorname{Rat}_d(\mathbb C)$ has the same length spectrum as $f$, then either $g$ or $\overline g$ is conjugate to $f$.

This is the length-spectrum analogue of the generic injectivity theorem for the multiplier spectrum. It is presented as a conjecture of Ji and the author, and the supplied passage gives no resolution.

 372589 Refined decomposition conjecture for alternating sign matrix enumerations 
 Let $A_n(z,\rho):=A_n(z,\rho,1)$, and for odd order define

$$
B_{2m+1}(z,\rho)=\sum_{A\in\operatorname{HSASM}(2m+1)}z^{\frac{\mu(A)-m}{2}}\rho^{f(A)-2},
$$

where $\operatorname{HSASM}(2m+1)$ is the set of horizontally symmetric ASMs of order $2m+1$, $\mu(A)$ is the statistic used in the preceding enumeration, and $f(A)$ is the position of the $1$ in the first row. **Refined decomposition conjecture.** There exists a family of polynomials $B_{2m}(z,\rho)$ in $\{z,\rho\}$ such that, for every $m\geq 1$,

$$
A_{2m+1}(z,\rho)=\rho B_{2m+1}(z,1)B_{2m+2}(z,\rho),
$$

$$
A_{2m}(z,\rho)=\rho(\rho+1)B_{2m}(z,1)B_{2m+1}(z,\rho).
$$

This refines the previously conjectured decomposition by tracking the position of the $1$ in the first row. The source presents it as a proposal; no proof or resolution is supplied in the given text.

 373705 Petrov's balls conjecture for biregular labeled multigraphs 
 Let a bipartite labeled multigraph be a tuple $(L,R,E,u,v)$, where $u:E\to L$ and $v:E\to R$. Let $A_{n,k}$ be the set of biregular bipartite labeled multigraphs with $L=[n]$, $R=[k]$, and $E=[n]\times[k]$; let $I$ be the set of involutions in the permutation group of $[n]\times[k]$. For functions $u,v$ with common domain $E$, let $[u,v]$ denote the weighted $L\times R$ matrix whose $(x,y)$ entry counts edges $e$ with $u(e)=x$ and $v(e)=y$, and let $W_{[n],[k]}$ be the all-ones matrix. **Petrov's balls conjecture.** For every $(u,v)\in A_{n,k}$, there exists an involution $\iota\in I$ such that

$$
[u\circ\iota,v]=W_{[n],[k]}.
$$

In the balls-and-colors interpretation, this asserts that the balls can be paired or left fixed so that every girl ends with exactly one ball of each color, with every ball involved in at most one swap. The conjecture implies the weak balls conjecture, but the general statement remains unproved; special cases are established in the paper.

 374355 The colorful-complex extremal characterization conjecture 
 Let $G$ be a graph, let $\mathcal{U} = \{U_1, \ldots, U_m\}$ be a partition of $V(G)$, and let $k \ge -1$. Write $\mathbf{Col}(\mathcal{I}(G),\mathcal{U})$ for the colorful complex of independent transversals, and let $\widetilde{H}_j$ denote reduced homology over a fixed ring. Suppose that

$$
\widetilde{H}_k(\mathbf{Col}(\mathcal{I}(G),\mathcal{U})) \neq 0,
$$

while

$$
\widetilde{H}_j(\mathbf{Col}(\mathcal{I}(G-U_i),\mathcal{U}-\{U_i\})) = 0
$$

for every $U_i \in \mathcal{U}$ and every $-1 \le j \le k$, that $G$ is a disjoint union of complete bipartite graphs, and that $G$ has exactly $|\mathcal{U}|+k$ connected components. **The colorful-complex extremal characterization conjecture.** Then for every $U_i \in \mathcal{U}$, there exists a complete bipartite component $K_{A,B}$ of $G$ such that $A \subseteq U_i$.

 374421 The universal categorical spectrum conjecture for topological stabilizer codes 
 Let $\mathbf{TS}^*$ be a proposed categorical spectrum of topological stabilizer codes, with $\mathbf{TS}^n$ its degree-$n$ component, and let $\mathbf Q^*(\mathrm{pt})$ denote the unitary QCA spectrum at a point. Write $\mathbf{CatSp}$ for the category of categorical spectra and $\mathbf{ComAlg}$ for commutative algebra objects. **Universal categorical spectrum conjecture.** There is a categorical spectrum of topological stabilizer codes $\mathbf{TS}^*$ such that

$$
\mathbf{TS}^{n-1}\cong\Omega\mathbf{TS}^n
$$

for all $n\geq 1$, whose core is $\mathbf Q^*(\mathrm{pt})$, and such that for every other categorical spectrum $\mathbf C^*$ there is a natural-in-$\mathbf C$ isomorphism

$$
\pi_0\operatorname{hom}_{\mathbf{CatSp}}(\mathbf C^*,\mathbf{TS}^*)=\operatorname{hom}_{\mathbf{ComAlg}}(\mathbf C^0,\mathbf Q^0(\mathrm{pt})).
$$

This conjecture seeks a universal target category for topological stabilizer codes whose invertible part recovers the QCA spectrum. The proposed universal property would organize stabilizer codes in all dimensions and relate their bulk-boundary structure to the classification of QCAs.

 374945 Terminal stability conjecture for derivative discriminants 
 For $n\ge r$, let $\mathcal D_{n-r}^{(n)}=\operatorname{disc}(P^{(n-r)})$, where $P$ is a monic polynomial of degree $n$. For fixed $r$, let $G_1,\ldots,G_N$ be square multigraphs and let $\widetilde{G_\nu}$ denote their symmetrized graph monomials.

**Terminal stability conjecture.** For each fixed $r\ge2$ there exists a finite list of square multigraphs $G_1,\ldots,G_N$, depending only on $r$ and not on $n$, such that for all $n\ge r$

$$
\mathcal D_{n-r}^{(n)}=\sum_{\nu=1}^N c_\nu(n)\,\widetilde{G_\nu},\qquad c_\nu(n)\ge0,
$$

where the coefficients $c_\nu(n)$ are rational functions, or equivalently polynomials after a common positive normalization, in $n$.

This is a conjectural strengthening of the square-graph cone problem, asserting uniform finite graph support for each fixed terminal order. The cases $r=2$ and $r=3$ are settled in the note; $r=4$ is identified as the next concrete target, and $r=5$ is reduced to an explicit quintic polynomial. The general assertion remains open.
