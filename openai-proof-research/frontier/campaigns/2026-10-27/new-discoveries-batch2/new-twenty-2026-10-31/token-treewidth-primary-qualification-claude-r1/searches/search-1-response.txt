The search turned up a related result, but not a proof of a Θ(n^(k-1)) treewidth bound for token graphs of all trees.

- **Star graphs:** Fabila-Monroy, Gómez-Galicia, Hernández-Cruz and Trujillo-Negrete (arXiv 2402.17962, "On the Treewidth of Token and Johnson Graphs") prove that for the star S_n and fixed 1 ≤ k ≤ n, the treewidth of the k-token graph is Θ(n^(k-1)). That is the Θ(n^(k-1)) statement you're asking about, but it is established for stars only.
- **Paths and complete graphs:** The same paper treats token graphs of paths and complete graphs. It also gives an exact treewidth and pathwidth for the double vertex graph F_2(K_n). The authors conjecture that their upper bound for the treewidth of F_k(K_n) is tight.
- **Trees more generally:** The search returned no proof or conjecture for arbitrary trees. The closest hit is Fabila-Monroy, Leaños and Trujillo-Negrete's paper on connectivity of token graphs of trees (DMTCS 2022), which shows that the connectivity of F_k(G) equals its minimum degree when G is a tree. That concerns connectivity, not treewidth.
- **Background:** Token graphs also generalize Johnson graphs, since F_k(K_n) is the Johnson graph J(n,k). The double vertex graph is the F_2 case, defined by Alavi et al. in 1991.
- **Separators and symmetric powers:** Nothing in the results addressed separators of token graphs or their link to symmetric powers. A further search would be needed for those topics.
