                      |CB′′ | = |H| + |G/H| + DA (G/H) − 2 ≤ |G| + DA (G) − 1,
we see, in view of

              |H| + |G/H| − 1 ≤ |G|
              |CB′′ | + (|G| − (|H| + |G/H| − 1)) =
              |H| + |G/H| + DA (G/H) − 2 + |G| − |H| − |G/H| + 1 ≤ |G| + DA (G) − 1,

that
                                              |Σ∪     ′
                                                |G| (C )| = |G|.

Consequently, in view of Step 1, the theorem holds with subgroup H = G, α = γ and β = δ,
completing the final step of the proof.                                               
