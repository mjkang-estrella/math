## Independent ordinary verification

Put V=Z/12Z × {0,1,2,3} and join (i,a) to (j,b) precisely when j−i=±1 and a≠b. Symmetry and absence of loops are immediate. There are 48 vertices, degree 2·3=6, and 144 edges.

For v=(i,t), any two neighbors x=(j,a), y=(k,b) have a common neighbor w=(i,c), where c is chosen outside {a,b,t}. There are four labels and at most three exclusions, so w exists and differs from v. Every walk with endpoints outside v can have each segment x,v,y replaced by x,w,y. Alternatively x=y permits deleting the excursion. Each replacement reduces occurrences of v and never increases length. The resulting walk lifts to the induced graph on V minus {v}. Applied to a shortest original walk this proves the deletion distance is at most its original distance; inclusion of the induced graph proves the reverse inequality. This argument covers every surviving pair, including equal endpoints, and has no bounded-distance proxy assumption. Only one vertex is deleted at a time; simultaneous deletion is not claimed.

For no surrounding, let u=(i,a), v=(j,b) be distinct. If i=j then a≠b and (i+1,b) is adjacent to u but is neither v nor adjacent to v. If i≠j, the two-element layer neighborhoods S_i={i−1,i+1} and S_j={j−1,j+1} are unequal: equality on C12 would force i=j (the alternative swap would require 4=0 mod12). Pick k in S_i minus S_j, and c≠a; when k=j also require c≠b. At most two exclusions leave a label. Then (k,c) belongs to N(u) but not N[v]. This also verifies both possible directions of the surrounds relation and excludes weak and strong twins.

Projection to C12 sends edges to edges; a walk from layer 0 to layer 6 has length at least 6. Labels 0,1,0,1,0,1,0 along layers 0 through 6 give a six-edge path between (0,0) and (6,0). For connectedness and the upper diameter bound, let delta be the cycle distance of layers. For delta≥2, K4 has a label walk of every prescribed length delta≥2 between arbitrary labels: lengths 2 and 3 exist directly, and prepend a two-edge excursion to obtain larger lengths. Combine it with a shortest layer path. When delta=0 and labels differ, use two edges through a neighboring layer; when delta=1 and labels agree, use three layer steps i,j,i,j and a label triangle. Other cases use zero or one edge. All distances are at most 6. Finally 32≤48<64 proves the logarithm comparison. No minimality claim enters.
