## 356563: rainbow trees, three sustained routes

The literal endpoint is: for every finite graph G, every positive k,c,c' with c'≤c, and every integer t, P(T(c,k)≥t)≥P(T(c',k)≥t), under independent uniform edge colours. Trees are subgraphs with k vertices, not induced trees or just maximum rainbow-component size. Source arXiv:2210.11972v1, final section, Conjecture 5.3. The journal search excerpt labels the same assertion Conjecture 5.4 (European J. Combinatorics 127 (2025), 104154). The attempted institutional journal PDF GET returned 403; it was not retried. Its proof body and intervening corrections remain unread. Search results do not clear them.

Route A: if c=q c', sample independent uniform pairs (a_e,b_e) in [c']×[q]; project to a_e. Both marginals are exactly uniform and independent. Distinct projected colours imply distinct pairs, so every rainbow tree in the smaller colouring remains rainbow. Therefore all tails are monotone in this divisible case, pathwise. This is the complete elementary source bridge (the source mentions q=2); it earns zero. For adjacent palettes c' and c'+1 a deterministic colour projection cannot give uniform fibres unless c' divides c'+1. Randomly choosing a global projection does not fix independence: if h is shared, P(h(X_1)=a,h(X_2)=a)=E[(|h^{-1}(a)|/c)^2], generally greater than the square of the mean. Random maps independently at each edge destroy preservation of distinctness. This blocks that extension, not the conjecture.

Route B: put M=|E(G)|. A set partition π of the edges describes equal-colour classes. Its exact probability under c colours is (c)_{b(π)}/c^M, with falling factorial zero if b(π)>c. Let A_b(t) count partitions with b blocks for which at least t k-vertex tree edge sets meet each block in at most one edge. Then the target is the exact inequality sum_b A_b(t)(c)_b/c^M ≥ sum_b A_b(t)(c')_b/(c')^M. This is a finite reformulation, not a computation performed here. Refinement preserves each rainbow tree. Nevertheless block count alone does not specify tree counts; partitions with the same b can behave differently. Nonnegative coefficients alone do not prove the inequality, since each individual term with b<M eventually decreases with c. A missing lemma is stochastic refinement domination of these uniform occupancy partitions, or a weaker inequality restricted to the particular upper sets {T≥t}. No proof of either was obtained. FKG cannot simply be invoked: at two colours, two crossing two-block partitions can have a meet with more than two blocks, hence zero measure, while each original partition has positive measure. The requisite lattice log-supermodularity is absent.

Route C: balancing colour probabilities. Condition on all edges outside two colour classes; remaining edges become independent Bernoulli labels with bias p. One would need a Schur-concavity assertion for every tree-count tail, not merely monotonicity of the probability that ONE tree is rainbow. The expectation is exactly (#k-vertex trees)(c)_{k-1}/c^{k-1}, hence monotone by multiplying factors 1-j/c. This does not imply any tail inequality. A general symmetric cut-event balancing argument is false: on an abstract four-leaf star of inequality constraints, the probability all four constraints hold is p(1-p)^4+(1-p)p^4=x(1-3x), x=p(1-p). At p=1/2 this is 1/16, while at x=1/6 it is 1/12. This is NOT a counterexample to the MathDB problem: the constraint star is not automatically realizable as the whole relevant rainbow-tree-count threshold in a graph. The missing mechanism is a structural balancing theorem for these particular families of graphic tree edge sets. No such theorem was proved. A search located a balanced-colour random k-out paper with a colour-monotonicity section; its author-PDF TLS verification failed. No disabling of TLS or retry occurred. The visible excerpt concerns fixed near-equal multiplicities, not the iid model here, so transfer remains HOLD.

Disposition: PARK with explicit full-scope tail inequality unpaid. Novelty HOLD, source completeness HOLD, formal endpoint NOT STARTED. Divisible palettes, expectations, and partition reformulations are helpers only.

## 355473: derangements, exact algebra and public comparator

For every odd prime p let A have indices 1,…,p−1, A_ii=0 and A_ij=1/(i−j) for i≠j. Every denominator is a unit in Z_(p), so congruence modulo p² is well-defined. Terms with fixed points vanish. Thus the unsigned literal sum is per A and the signed literal sum is det A, with no missing term or sign. Sun's source v3 Conjecture 4.7, lines 449–465 of the extracted text, is exactly the pair in the corpus. The arXiv page records v1 July 26 2022, v2 August 10 2022, v3 February 27 2024 and Acta Math. Sinica Chin. Ser. 67 (2024), 286–295. All three arXiv PDFs were acquired; only v3's relevant statement was read in detail. Journal body and old-version full comparison remain incomplete.

Route A: simultaneous reversal of every cycle of odd length at least three cancels its contribution in a skew kernel, but this alone leaves even cycles. The stronger Cauchy identity eliminates the sum over ALL cyclic orderings on any fixed set of size s≥3. Here is its exact proof: regard that sum as a rational function of the last variable z. Each term decays as z^−2. Its only finite poles are simple at z=x_i. Removing the last vertex leaves a cycle on the other vertices; insertion immediately before or after i gives opposite residues with the same other factors. Hence all residues vanish and the rational function has no finite pole and vanishes at infinity, so is zero. Group permutations by cycle supports. Only disjoint transpositions survive. Each contributes −(x−y)^−2 to the permanent; in the determinant the permutation sign removes that minus. For N=2h this proves per A=(−1)^h det A EXACTLY. Since (−1)^((p−1)/2)=Legendre(−1,p), BOTH literal congruences reduce to det A≡1 mod p². This reduction is complete but not a new solution.

Route B: use the Vandermonde evaluation basis 1,X,…,X^(p−2) on F_p^×. Direct summation gives A(X^k)=k X^(k−1) for 1≤k≤p−2 and A(1)=−X^(p−2), modulo p. The weighted cycle matrix D has determinant (p−2)!≡1 mod p. This proves only the first modulus, not p². A lifting argument must compute tr(D^−1 E) for the actual rational matrix A=D+pE in this basis; skew symmetry or Fermat alone does not make it zero. To get det A=1 mod p², the required trace is W_p−1 mod p, with W_p=((p−1)!+1)/p, compensating det D≡1+p(1−W_p). That is the precise unpaid lemma in an independent proof attempt.

Current public primary comparator: Yang–Zhang, arXiv:2605.19502v3, Theorem 2 equations (11)–(12), states the EXACT two congruences for every odd prime. Actual source text read: Lemma 11 (cycle residue cancellation), Proposition 12 (matching expansion), Lemma 13 (interpolation conjugation), Lemma 14 (mod-p cyclic derivative), Lemma 15 (p² trace computation), and the proof of Theorem 2, extracted lines 417–617. It uses precisely the two routes above and states the required trace calculation. The comparator page gives v1 May 19, v2 May 23, v3 May 27 2026; all PDFs acquired. Earlier versions were not fully read. No journal reference or correction clearance is claimed. This is a direct public-resolution overlap, not novelty inferred from a title. Independent audit of every arithmetic step in that public paper is not claimed. Even independently repairing or reproving the same exact congruences would earn zero under this task.

Disposition: EXCLUDE FROM FRESH CREDIT as an exact publicly stated/proved resolution with relevant primary proof read; ordinary independent full proof not completed. Novelty FAIL for fresh credit; version/journal completeness HOLD. No candidate and no Pi plan.
