## Independent mathematical and source reasoning

The original JSONL record, normalized literal and raw-binding text have the same statement. The primary assumes finite simple undirected graphs, sets c0=1 globally, defines flatness repeatedly as every edge in at most two triangles, and states Conjecture 2 without extra restrictions. The words 'triangulated graphs' in surrounding prose do not override Definition 2.2: Definition 2.3 explicitly allows zero-triangle edges and later flat-graph theorems discuss them. No planarity, connectedness, K4-freeness, no-isolated-edge condition, or positive triangle count occurs in Conjecture 2. Theorem 3.1's stronger hypothesis must remain local. Its inference excluding K3,3 is not used as a premise. The source's claim D=1 when -1 is a clique root conflicts with c0-inclusive D=0; retaining both conventions resolves the ambiguity for this counterexample without repairing the conjecture.

Take the disjoint union of two three-element sets. All opposite-side pairs and only those pairs are adjacent. This relation is symmetric and has no loops. Six singletons are cliques; the unique empty set is a clique. Every pair clique has a unique left and right endpoint, giving a bijection from Fin 3 x Fin 3 to the unordered pair cliques. This counts nine subsets, not eighteen oriented tuples. If a clique contained three distinct vertices, two would be on the same side and nonadjacent. Thus no clique has size >=3. Every edge therefore lies in zero triangles, so the witness is flat nonvacuously (it has edges). The odd total is 6; the even total is 1+9=10, or 9 with only positive sizes. Integer differences are -4 and -3; their absolute values are 4 and 3, each >1. Natural truncated subtraction would give 0 and would be wrong, but it is not used: the casts precede subtraction in Z.

The Lean proof pays distinctness using erase membership for the three-vertex argument, cardinality two via card_eq_two, pair injection by disjoint sum constructors, and all higher sizes by K33_clique_card_le. It does not assume those counts as inputs. Flatness follows from proved empty triangle incidence sets. The finite range includes sizes 0 through 6. Rewrites establish actual sums; decide is used only for the now closed integer arithmetic of natAbs, not an externally supplied graph answer. omega/simp/elimination produce proof obligations under the trusted pinned library, with final execution evidenced by supplied logs. No native_decide or custom oracle appears in source.
