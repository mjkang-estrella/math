# Search coverage and explicit gaps

This is not an exhaustive technical investigation of all finite objects described by the reservation. All 20 literal statements were extracted with byte provenance; all 20 primary arXiv HTML bodies were retrieved; targeted passages, definitions and relevant proof sections were read. Review depth varies by ID in results.json. In particular, the following finite-computation obligations remain incomplete: new tangent-cone/patch ideal enumeration (326097), global manifold-complexity enumeration (326377), class-group computations (329002), and an exact formal derivability predicate for the Collatz formulation (330110). No source-only computational claim is presented as a fresh search. These are honest gaps, not negative search findings.

Executed new exact diagnostic searches:

Every computed h* vector unimodal. The even n=2r endpoint needs different dimension and was omitted explicitly.

Uses source subgroup-totient S(G), not the defective MathDB sum of element orders. No mismatch; source conjecture already proved in 2017/2018. This is NOT all finite groups through order32.
* 331705: 384 point/contraction pairs from y=(a,b), a=1..8, b=1/8..2 step1/8, t=1/4,1/2,3/4, with boundary sample s=1..16 step1/16. Positive diagnostic leads are retained. Sampling is not a continuum maximum proof. The chosen witness is separately proved for every real boundary parameter s>=1 in Lean.

Infinite or analytic source obligations are not replaced by irrelevant finite proxies. In particular finite group tables do not certify local lifting of formal power series, finite knot quotients do not refute existence of an arbitrary TFEA quotient, matrices in a bounded diagram degree do not decide a Heisenberg-category isomorphism, and a single finite point set cannot refute a lower-bound assertion with an existential positive asymptotic constant.
