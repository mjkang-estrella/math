16:                                                  In this series of papers, the primary goal is to enumerate Hamiltonian cycles
17:                                                  (HC’s) on the grid cylinder graphs Pm+1 × Cn , where n is allowed to grow
18:                                                  whilst m is fixed. In Part I, we studied the so-called non-contractible HC’s.
19:                                                  Here, in Part II, we proceed further on to the contractible case. We propose
20:                                                  two different novel characterizations of contractible HC’s, from which we con-
21:                                                  struct digraphs for enumerating the contractible HC’s. Given the impression
22:                                                  which the computational data for m ≤ 9 convey, we conjecture that the
23:                                                  asymptotic domination of the contractible HC’s versus the non-contractible
24:                                                  HC’s, among the total number of HC’s, depends on the parity of m.

2042:       For the initial values of m we have come to notice that the numbers of HC nc ’s
2043: are the dominant ones for even m, whereas the numbers of HC c ’s are such for odd
2044: m. That prompted us to make a conjecture about the asymptotic behaviour of
2045: the total number of HC’s in the graph Pm+1 × Cn . Moreover, certain matchings
2046: between the dominant characteristic roots of the sequences the numbers of HC c ’s
2047: in Pm+1 × Cn and Pm+1 × Pn for small values of m are noticed. This way, we have
2048: come to yet another conjecture regarding the asymptotic behaviour of the entries
2049: of these two sequences.
