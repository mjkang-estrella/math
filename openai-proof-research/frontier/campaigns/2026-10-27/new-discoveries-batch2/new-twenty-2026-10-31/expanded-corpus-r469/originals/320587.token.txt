"Let $X$ be an $n$-element set, and write $X^{(\\le m)}=\\bigcup_{0\\le i\\le m}X^{(i)}$ and $X_x^{(\\le m)}=\\{A\\in X^{(\\le m)}:x\\in A\\}$. A collection of $d+1$ sets is a **$d$-simplex** if its total intersection is empty but the intersection of every $d$ of its sets is nonempty. **The extremal family conjecture.** For $d\\ge 2$ and $1\\le k\\le n-d-1$, if ${\\cal F}\\subseteq X^{(\\le n-k)}$ is $d$-simplex-free, then\n\n$$\n|{\\cal F}|\\le 2^{n-1}+\\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}\\binom{n-1}{i}-\\sum_{i=0}^{k-1}\\binom{n-1}{i},\n$$\n\nwith equality if and only if ${\\cal F}=X_x^{(\\le n-k)}\\cup(X\\setminus\\{x\\})^{(\\le d-1)}$ for some $x\\in X$. The proposed family is motivated by the authors' calculations for higher-dimensional simplices, where the preceding method does not generally give a sharp bound; the conjecture concerns the cases covered by the displayed range of $d$ and $k$."
