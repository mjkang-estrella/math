Let $G$ be a finite solvable group, and let $L(G)$ denote its subgroup lattice. Choose a chief series

$$
1=N_{0}\subset N_{1}\subset\dots\subset N_{k}=G.
$$

A **complement** to a subgroup $N$ is a subgroup $H$ with $HN=G$ and $H\cap N=1$; a **chain of complements** to this chief series is a chain

$$
1=H_{k}\subset H_{k-1}\subset\dots\subset H_{0}=G
$$

where $H_i$ is a complement to $N_i$ for each $i$. **Shareshian's EL-labeling conjecture.** The lattice $L(G)$ admits an $EL$-labeling whose descending chains are precisely the chains of complements to a chief series. This conjecture seeks an explicit shelling-type labeling whose descending chains realize the complement-chain basis underlying the topology of solvable subgroup lattices; the paper presents an EL-labeling, resolving the conjecture.
