"Let $d$ be the ambient dimension and let $S\\subset\\mathbb{R}^d$. A set $S$ is a **non-uniqueness set** for the spherical mean transform in the space of compactly supported functions when there exists a nonzero homogeneous harmonic polynomial whose zero surface is denoted by $\\Sigma$, a rigid motion $\\omega$ of $\\mathbb{R}^d$, and an algebraic surface $\\Phi$ of dimension at most $d-2$. **Higher-dimensional non-uniqueness conjecture.** A set $S\\subset\\mathbb{R}^d$ is a non-uniqueness set if and only if\n\n$$\nS\\subset\\omega\\Sigma\\cup\\Phi.\n$$\n\nThis conjecture proposes the higher-dimensional analogue of the known planar characterization, in which non-uniqueness sets are contained in a rigid motion of a Coxeter system together with a finite set. The corresponding characterization in higher dimensions is presented as an open problem."
