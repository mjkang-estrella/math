"Let $p>1$ and $k\\geq 1$ be integers, and set $n=kp+1$. Let $\\mathsf M_p(n)$ denote the $p$-uniform hypergraph matching complex on $n$ vertices, and let $\\widetilde H_{k-1}(\\mathsf M_p(n))$ be its reduced homology group in degree $k-1$. Write $\\operatorname{ch}$ for the Frobenius characteristic, let $\\mathsf e_k$ and $\\mathsf h_p$ be the elementary and complete homogeneous symmetric functions, respectively, and for a symmetric function $\\mathsf f=\\sum_\\lambda a_\\lambda\\mathsf s_\\lambda$ define $\\mathsf f|_r=\\sum_{l(\\lambda)=r}a_\\lambda\\mathsf s_\\lambda$. **The top homology conjecture.** For integers $k\\geq 1$, $p>1$, and $n=kp+1$, \n\n$$\n\\operatorname{ch}\\widetilde H_{k-1}(\\mathsf M_p(n))=(\\mathsf e_k[\\mathsf h_p]\\mathsf h_1)|_{k+1}.\n$$\n\nThis conjecture describes the $\\mathbb C[S_n]$-module structure of the top homology of $\\mathsf M_p(n)$; the paper proves it for $p\\leq 3$ and for $(n-1)/p\\leq 2$, while the general case remains open."
