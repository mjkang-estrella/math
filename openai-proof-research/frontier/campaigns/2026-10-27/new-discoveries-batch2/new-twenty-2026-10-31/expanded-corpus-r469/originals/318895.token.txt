"Let $M$ and $M'$ be sixth-root-of-unity matrices representing the same matroid $\\mathcal{M}$. The lattices $\\ker_\\mathbb{C}(M)\\cap \\mathbb{E}^E$ and $\\ker_\\mathbb{C}(M')\\cap \\mathbb{E}^E$ are **isometric** if there is a bijection between them preserving their inner products.\n\n**Isometry conjecture.** If $M$ and $M'$ represent the same matroid $\\mathcal{M}$, then\n\n$$\n\\ker_\\mathbb{C}(M)\\cap \\mathbb{E}^E\n$$\n\nand\n\n$$\n\\ker_\\mathbb{C}(M')\\cap \\mathbb{E}^E\n$$\n\nare isometric.\n\nThe claim asks whether the ordinary isometry class of the Eisenstein flow lattice is independent of the chosen sixth-root-of-unity representation, even though the stronger $\\mathbb{E}$-isometry class detects representation equivalence. The paper gives an example in which two lattices are not $\\mathbb{E}$-isometric but seem to be isometric; the conjecture remains open in the supplied text."
