Let $\lambda,\mu,\nu,\lambda',\mu',\nu'$ be partitions, and define

$$
P^{\lambda,\mu,\nu}_{\lambda',\mu',\nu'}(n)=c(n\lambda+\lambda';n\mu+\mu',n\nu+\nu').
$$

Assume that $f(n)=c(n\lambda;n\mu,n\nu)$ is a polynomial of degree $d$, and that $c(\lambda';\mu',\nu')\neq 0$. **Generalised stretched Littlewood–Richardson polynomiality conjecture.** There exists a polynomial $g(n)$ of degree $d$ and an integer $m$ such that

$$
P^{\lambda,\mu,\nu}_{\lambda',\mu',\nu'}(n)=g(n)\quad\text{for }n\geq m.
$$

In particular, if $c(\lambda;\mu,\nu)=1$, then there exists an integer $m$ such that

$$
P(n)=P(m)\quad\text{for }n\geq m.
$$

The conjecture generalises eventual polynomiality from stretched to translated, or generalised stretched, Littlewood–Richardson coefficients. The surrounding discussion motivates it using the piecewise-polynomial structure of Littlewood–Richardson coefficients, but the source supplies no proof or resolution status.
