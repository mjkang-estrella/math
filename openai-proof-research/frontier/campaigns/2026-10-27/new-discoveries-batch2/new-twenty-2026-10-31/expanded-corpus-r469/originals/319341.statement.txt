Let $\tilde{K}_{\lambda,mu}(q,t)$ be the KostkaMacdonald polynomial, let $\mathcal{P}_+(lambda)$ denote the set of highest weight elements of the type $B^{1,1}$ paths of weight $lambda$, and let $\operatorname{inv}_mu(p)$ and $\operatorname{maj}_mu(p)$ be the associated path statistics. The formula

$$
\tilde{K}_{\lambda,\mu}(q,t)=
\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}_+(\lambda)}
q^{\operatorname{inv}_\mu(p)}
 t^{\operatorname{maj}_\mu(p)}
$$

**HaglundHaimanLoehr formula criterion.** The formula is valid if and only if at least one of the following conditions holds: (i) $mu_1\leq 3$ and $mu_2\leq 2$; or (ii) $lambda$ is a hook shape. This conjecture seeks to extend the known formula, which was previously established when $mu_1\leq 2$; determining the full range of validity gives a combinatorial interpretation of the KostkaMacdonald polynomials in additional cases.
