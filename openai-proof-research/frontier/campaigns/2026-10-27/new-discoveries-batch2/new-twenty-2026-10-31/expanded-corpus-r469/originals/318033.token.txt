"Let $NCP_k(n)$ denote the number of $k$-distant noncrossing partitions of $[n]$. For a moment sequence $(\\mu_n)_{n\\geq 0}$, write its associated monic orthogonal polynomials in the form\n\n$$\nP_{n+1}(x)=(x-b_n)P_n(x)-\\lambda_nP_{n-1}(x).\n$$\n\nHere $F_n$ denotes the $n$-th Fibonacci number, defined by $F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}$ and $F_1=F_2=1$.\n\n**Fibonacci recurrence-coefficient conjecture.** If $\\mu_n=NCP_2(n)$, then $b_0=b_1=\\lambda_1=1$, and for $n\\geq2$,\n\n$$\nb_n=3-\\frac{1}{F_{2n-1}F_{2n-3}},\\qquad \\lambda_n=1+\\frac{1}{(F_{2n-3})^2}.\n$$\n\nIf $\\mu_n=NCP_3(n)$, then $b_0=b_1=\\lambda_1=1$, $\\lambda_2=2$, and, for $n\\geq3$, $b_n=3$ and $\\lambda_n=2$.\n\nThe conjecture gives explicit recurrence coefficients for the orthogonal polynomials associated with the enumerative sequences of 2-distant and 3-distant noncrossing partitions. The preceding computations provide evidence for these formulas, but the source does not state a resolution, so the conjecture remains open."
