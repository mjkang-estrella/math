Let $\widetilde\Lambda_n$ denote the set of standardized Laplacian matrices of order $n$, and let $S(n)$ be the closed convex polygon whose vertices are

$$
\lambda_{0}(n)=0,\quad \lambda_{1}(n),\dots,\lambda_{n-2}(n),\quad \lambda_{n-1}(n)=1,\quad \overline{\lambda}_{n-2}(n),\dots,\overline{\lambda}_1(n),
$$

where, for $k=1,\dots,n-1$,

$$
\lambda_k(n)=\frac{1}{n}\left(k-e^{-2\pi\mathrm{i}/n}-\cdots-e^{-2k\pi\mathrm{i}/n}\right)=\frac{1}{n}\left(k-\frac{\sin\frac{k\pi}{n}}{\sin\frac{\pi}{n}}e^{-\frac{(k+1)\pi\mathrm{i}}{n}}\right).
$$

**The polygonal spectral enclosure conjecture.** All eigenvalues of every standardized Laplacian matrix $\widetilde L\in\widetilde\Lambda_n$ belong to $S(n)$.
The conjecture would identify the polygon $S(n)$ as a universal spectral region for standardized Laplacian matrices; the preceding results establish related inclusion and extremal statements, but the asserted enclosure is described in the source as still unproved.
