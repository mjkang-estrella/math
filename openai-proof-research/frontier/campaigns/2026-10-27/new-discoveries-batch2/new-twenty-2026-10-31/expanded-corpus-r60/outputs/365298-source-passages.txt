In this paper, we prove the conjecture. More generally, we prove the following theorem. Theorem 3. Let G be a non-planar graph which can be embedded on a surface of Euler characteristic 0 . Then \bn(G)=3 . We also investigate the relationship between genus and basis number for graphs of higher genus and show that the basis number is asymptotically much smaller than the genus of a graph.
