Notation and preliminaries The Boolean lattice, often denoted merely by 2^{[n]} , is the partially ordered set (2^{[n]},\subseteq) made up of all subsets of [n]=\{1\dots,n\} , together with the inclusion relation. We are considering subsets \mathcal{F} of the Boolean lattice. We imagine the layers to be arranged bottom to top, with the empty set \emptyset at the bottom and the full set [n] at the top.
