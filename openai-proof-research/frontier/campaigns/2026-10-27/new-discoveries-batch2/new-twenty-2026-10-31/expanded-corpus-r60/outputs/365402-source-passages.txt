[3] If G is a subcubic graph (graph with maximum degree three), then \chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\leq 6 . Many attempts to prove this conjecture have been made and the quest go on. In [1], Bezegová et al. obtained some bounds on the star chromatic index of subcubic outerplanar graphs, trees and outerplanar graphs. They showed that \chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\leq 5 , where G is a subcubic outerplanar graph. In [8], the star chromatic index of two-dimensional grid graphs, also confirmed this conjecture. In 2018, Lei et al. [4, 5] consider the maximum average degree of subcubic graphs and obtained some results. The maximum average degree of a graph G , denoted mad(G) , is defined as the maximum of 2|E(H)|/|V(H)| taken over all the subgraphs H of G .
