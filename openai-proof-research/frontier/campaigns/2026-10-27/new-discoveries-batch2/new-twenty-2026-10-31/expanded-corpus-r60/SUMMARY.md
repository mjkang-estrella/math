## Mathematical substance

Put D = n|E| - sum_v deg(v)^2. In a triangle-free graph D counts induced three-vertex subgraphs with exactly one edge. A connected triangle-free graph that is not complete bipartite contains an induced P4. Its two one-edge triples, together with at least one distinct triple from each outside vertex, give D >= n-2. This supplies a strict gap in the source bound sigma_t <= |E|(n^2-4|E|). A rational integer-bracketing argument places some complete bipartite graph within (n-3)^2 of the continuous upper bound n^4/16. Since n(n-2) > (n-3)^2, the full extremal assertion follows, with orders 4,5,6 handled explicitly and smaller orders immediate.
