
360848 The conjecture that reflexive simplices have no ML degree drops
Let $\Delta$ be a reflexive simplex, and let $\mathrm{mldeg}(\Delta)$ and $\mathrm{deg}(\Delta)$ denote its maximum likelihood degree and degree, respectively, under the standard scaling. **Reflexive-simplex ML degree conjecture.** Reflexive simplices do not exhibit ML degree drops with the standard scaling; that is,

$$
\mathrm{mldeg}(\Delta)=\mathrm{deg}(\Delta).
$$

The computations in the paper verify equality for several explicitly constructed families and their first instances, but no general proof is given.

360894 Generalized Chern-Hamilton conjecture for closed contact 3-manifolds
Let $(M,\zeta)$ be a closed contact $3$-manifold, where $\zeta$ is a contact distribution, and consider the Chern-Hamilton energy functional on the space of compatible metrics for $(M,\zeta)$. **Generalized Chern-Hamilton conjecture.** There exists a compatible metric that realizes the minimum of the Chern-Hamilton energy functional among all compatible metrics. 360899 Kaczynski's logarithmic-area conjecture for unrestricted trapezoids

360986 Majority 3-edge-coloring conjecture for graphs without pendant edges
A **majority $3$-edge-coloring** of a graph is an edge-coloring with three colors in which, at every vertex, the number of incident edges of its most frequent color is at most the number of incident edges having other colors. 361012 Hanany–Puder character bound conjecture for word maps on symmetric groups
Let $F_m$ be the free group of rank $m$. For a word $w\in F_m$, let

$$
\pi(w)=\min\{\operatorname{rk}H:H\leq F_m,\ w\in H,\text{ and }w\text{ is not primitive in }H\},
$$

with $\pi(w)=\infty$ if no such subgroup exists. For $k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 2}$ and a partition $\lambda\vdash k$, let $\lambda^{+}(n)$ denote the corresponding stable Young diagram, and let $\chi^{\lambda^{+}(n)}$ and $V^{\lambda^{+}(n)}$ be the associated irreducible character and representation of $S_n$. For independent uniformly random permutations $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_m\in S_n$, write $w(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_m)$ for the word-map evaluation.

**Hanany–Puder conjecture.** For any $k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq2}$, any $\lambda\vdash k$, and any $w\in F_m$,

$$
\mathop{\mathbb{E}}_{\sigma_{1},\dots,\sigma_{m}\in S_{n}}\left[\chi^{\lambda^{+}(n)}\left(w\left(\sigma_{1},\dots,\sigma_{m}\right)\right)\right]=O\left(\frac{1}{\left(\dim V^{\lambda^{+}(n)}\right)^{\pi(w)-1}}\right).
$$

This conjectures a substantially stronger decay estimate than the known bound $O(n^{-\pi(w)})$ for words that are not proper powers or the identity. It refines the study of distributions of word maps on symmetric groups by relating character expectations to the primitivity rank of the word and the dimension of the corresponding stable irreducible representation.

The exact value of $\mathrm{mader}_{\vec{\chi}}(F)$ is known for only a few digraphs, and $\overleftrightarrow{K_3}$ is the smallest digraph for which the value was unknown in the source. The conjecture is presented as an open problem.

361503 The 2-DIOMEGA-ORDERING NP-completeness conjecture
Let $T$ be a tournament, and let $\operatorname{\overrightarrow{\omega}}(T)$ denote the minimum, over all orderings of $V(T)$, of the clique number of the corresponding backedge graph. **The 2-DIOMEGA-ORDERING conjecture.** The problem of deciding whether $\operatorname{\overrightarrow{\omega}}(T)\leq 2$ is NP-complete. The cases $k=1$ and $k\geq 3$ are respectively polynomial and NP-complete; the source presents the case $k=2$ as the remaining question, but does not provide a resolution.

361549 Maximum-weight bisection bound for graphs of bounded maximum degree
Let $G=(V,E,w)$ be an edge-weighted graph with non-negative edge weights, and let $w(G)$ denote the total edge weight. A **bisection** is a cut whose two parts differ in size by at most $1$. Let $k$ be a positive integer and suppose that

$$
\Delta(G)\leq k.
$$

**Bounded-degree bisection conjecture.** If $k$ is odd, then $G$ has a bisection of weight at least

$$
\frac{k+1}{2k}w(G),
$$

and if $k$ is even, then $G$ has a bisection of weight at least

$$
\frac{k+2}{2(k+1)}w(G).
$$

The corresponding bounds are known for maximum cuts and are tight there for complete graphs; the conjecture asks whether they also hold for maximum-weight bisections. The paper notes that the conjecture holds for weighted subcubic graphs and almost all graphs, while the general case remains open.
SOURCE: Stefanie Gerke, Gregory Gutin, Anders Yeo and Yacong Zhou, “Lower Bounds for Maximum Weight Bisections of Graphs with Bounded Degrees”, arXiv:2401.10074 (2024). | https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10074

361559 The conjecture that random 4-regular graphs are globally rigid in the real line

361574 The real-intersection conjecture for Chebyshev T-curves
Let $A=[a_1~\cdots~a_n]\in\mathbb{N}^{1\times n}$, and suppose that three entries of $A$ are pairwise coprime. Let ${\cal X}_{A,T}$ be the associated Chebyshev $T$-curve. A hyperplane through the origin is a linear hyperplane in the ambient space containing the origin.

**Real-intersection conjecture.** Any hyperplane through the origin intersects ${\cal X}_{A,T}$ in at least

$$
\min_{j\in[n]}a_j
$$

real points.

The conjecture is motivated by computations for the example $A=[2~3~7]$, where hyperplane sections have at least two real points and some hyperplanes have the maximum of seven real points. The general lower bound is presented as conjectural in the source, and no resolution is supplied here.

361580 Auslander's super-prolongation conjecture for downstream structures

361584 Hriňáková–Knor–Škrekovski conjecture on extremal iterated-line-graph Wiener ratios
Let $G$ be a graph on $n$ vertices. The Wiener index $W(G)$ is the sum of distances over all unordered pairs of vertices. Let $L(G)$ be the line graph of $G$, define $L^0(G)=G$ and $L^k(G)=L(L^{k-1}(G))$ for $k\geq 1$, and set

$$
R_k(G)=\frac{W(L^k(G))}{W(G)}.
$$

**Hriňáková–Knor–Škrekovski conjecture.** For large $n$ and $k\geq 2$, among all graphs $G$ on $n$ vertices, $R_k(G)$ attains its maximum at $G=K_n$ and its minimum at $G=P_n$.

The minimum for $k=1$ is known to be attained by the star, while for trees and $k\geq 3$ the path is known to attain the minimum. The conjecture concerns the remaining extremal cases and the simultaneous maximum and minimum over all graphs.
SOURCE: Mohammad Ghebleh and Ali Kanso, “On the Second-Order Wiener Ratios of Iterated Line Graphs”, arXiv:2401.12370 (2024). | https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12370
