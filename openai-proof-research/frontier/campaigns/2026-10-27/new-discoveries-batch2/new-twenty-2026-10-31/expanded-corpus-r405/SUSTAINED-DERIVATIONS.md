First shortcut: taking the majority symbol everywhere only gives an unaligned circular palindrome, not the required matched half-subsequences. ## 342386: partition reconstruction beyond degrees
Primary pp.13–15: read Conjecture 3.18 and proofs of Propositions 3.19,3.21, Lemma 3.20, Theorems 3.22,3.23. The semidirect refutation in FULLCANDIDATE does not refute this distinct conjecture: its automorphism preserves every fiber. Initial degree separation works for some regular cases only; arbitrary f can increase connecting degree by varying preimage multiplicity. Hence uniform endpoint degree is no invariant identifying all inner edges.

Cycle-length reconstruction works if every H-edge lies in a cycle shorter than girth(G), or shorter than twice the girth when f is locally injective: any crossing cycle projects to a closed trail in G using distinct connecting edges, containing a cycle, so it has at least girth(G) crossing edges. Local injectivity also forces an inner edge between successive crossing edges. Those statements recover the source's proved cases with their exact extra assumptions. They do not settle all 2-connected H: a long cycle H can have no edge on a sufficiently short cycle.

A stronger attempted route is to recover fibers as blocks or minimal cuts. It fails as a general immediate invariant: when f is locally injective and G,H are both 2-connected, connecting edges may themselves lie on cycles and the whole product can have no cut vertex separating fibers. Merely declaring H to be a 2-connected block does not make it maximal. To finish one would need an intrinsic characterization of precisely the chosen H-copies under arbitrary f, or a concrete automorphism mixing edge types. None is proved. The source's Figure 7 uses non-2-connected H, so importing it as a counterexample drops a literal hypothesis. Its text description was read; its graphical layout was not visually inspected. Park, no candidate or exclusion of this full scope.
