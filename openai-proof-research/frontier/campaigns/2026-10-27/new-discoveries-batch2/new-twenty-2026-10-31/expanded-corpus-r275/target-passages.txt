11:                                                                               Abstract
12:                                                    The Hirsch conjecture was posed in 1957 in a letter from Warren M.
13:                                                Hirsch to George Dantzig. It states that the graph of a d-dimensional
14:                                                polytope with n facets cannot have diameter greater than n − d. That is
15:                                                to say, we can go from any vertex to any other vertex using at most n − d
16:                                                edges.

825: 3.3    The Klee-Walkup polytope Q4
826: In their seminal 1967 paper [43] on the Hirsch conjecture and related issues,
827: Klee and Walkup describe a 4-polytope Q4 with nine facets and diameter five.
828: Innocent as this might look, this first “non-trivial” Hirsch-sharp polytope is at
829: the basis of the construction of every remaining Hirsch-sharp polytope known to
830: date (see Section 3.4). It is also instrumental in disproving the unbounded and
831: monotone variants of the Hirsch conjecture, which we will discuss in Section 3.5.

671: constant ǫ > 0, an ǫ-separator S in a connected graph G is a set of vertices whose removal
672: yields at least two components of size at least ǫ|V (G)|. If for ǫ|V (G)| vertices v of G,
