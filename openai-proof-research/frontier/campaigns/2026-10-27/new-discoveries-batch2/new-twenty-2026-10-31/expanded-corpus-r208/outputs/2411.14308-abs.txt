[2411.14308] New results similar to Lagrange's four-square theorem Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Number Theory arXiv:2411.14308 (math) [Submitted on 21 Nov 2024 ( v1 ), last revised 5 Dec 2024 (this version, v3)] Title: New results similar to Lagrange's four-square theorem Authors: Zhi-Wei Sun View a PDF of the paper titled New results similar to Lagrange's four-square theorem, by Zhi-Wei Sun View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: In this paper we establish some new results similar to Lagrange's four-square theorem. For example, we prove that any integer $n>1$ can be written as $w(5w+1)/2+x(5x+1)/2+y(5y+1)/2+z(5z+1)/2$ with $w,x,y,z\in\mathbb Z$. Let $a$ and $b$ be integers with $a>0$, $b>-a$ and $\gcd(a,b)=1$. When $2\nmid ab$, we show that any sufficiently large integer can be written as $$\frac{w(aw+b)}2+\frac{x(ax+b)}2+\frac{y(ay+b)}2+\frac{z(az+b)}2$$ with $w,x,y,z$ nonnegative integers. When $2\mid a$ and $2\nmid b$, we prove that any sufficiently large integer can be written as $$w(aw+b)+x(ax+b)+y(ay+b)+z(az+b)$$ with $w,x,y,z$ nonnegative integers.
