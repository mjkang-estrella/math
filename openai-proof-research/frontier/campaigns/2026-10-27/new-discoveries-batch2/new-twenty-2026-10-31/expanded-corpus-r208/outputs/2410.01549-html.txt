Mao, Wang, Magnant, and Schiermeyer [Discrete Math., 2023], Song, Wei, Zhang, and Zhao [Discrete Math., 2020], and Zhao and Wei [Discrete Appl. Math., 2021] each proposed one conjecture on the Gallai-Ramsey numbers for fans, wheels, and kipases, respectively. We establish new lower bounds that disprove all three conjectures. Given k k graphs G 1 , … , G k G_{1},\ldots,G_{k} , the Ramsey number R ⁡ ( G 1 , G 2 , … , G k ) R(G_{1},G_{2},\ldots,G_{k}) is defined as the smallest positive integer n n such that for any edge coloring τ : E ⁡ ( K n ) → [ k ] \tau:E(K_{n})\to[k] , there exists a monochromatic subgraph G i G_{i} of color i i for some i ∈ [ k ] i\in[k] . When k = 2 k=2 and both G 1 G_{1} and G 2 G_{2} are complete graphs, we refer to R ⁡ ( K p , K q ) R(K_{p},K_{q}) as the classical Ramsey number, often abbreviated as R ⁡ ( p , q ) R(p,q) . Currently, only nine exact values of non-trivial classical Ramsey numbers are known: R ⁡ ( 3 , q ) R(3,q) for q ∈ [ 3 , 9 ] q\in[3,9] , R ⁡ ( 4 , 4 ) R(4,4) , and R ⁡ ( 4 , 5 ) R(4,5) . The most famous unresolved problem is R ⁡ ( 5 , 5 ) R(5,5) , which gained further popularity due to a famous joke by Erdős [ 3 ] . The best known bounds are 43 ≤ R ⁡ ( 5 , 5 ) ≤ 46 . 43\leq R(5,5)\leq 46\,. The lower bound was established by Exoo [ 5 ] , while the upper bound was recently obtained by Angeltveit and McKay [ 1 ] , utilizing 80 years of CPU time. McKay and Radziszowski [ 15 ] conjectured that the lower bound is the exact value and provided some experimental evidence. Conjecture 1 (McKay and Radziszowski, 1997) . R ⁡ ( 5 , 5 ) = 43 R(5,5)=43 . Ramsey theory has developed many variations. One such variation restricts the way of coloring, making it relatively easier to study the multicolor case. An edge coloring τ : E ⁡ ( G ) → [ k ] \tau:E(G)\to[k] is called a Gallai coloring of the graph G G if it does not contain a rainbow triangle, meaning no triangle has all three edges colored with different colors. This type of coloring was first studied by Gallai in 1967 [ 7 ] , who established a significant theorem in this field. Theorem 1 . Let ( G , τ ) (G,\tau) be a Gallai k k -colored complete graph with | V ⁡ ( G ) | ≥ 2 |V(G)|\geq 2 . Then, the vertex set V ⁡ ( G ) V(G) can be partitioned into nonempty sets V 1 , … , V p V_{1},\dots,V_{p} with p ≥ 2 p\geq 2 such that at most two colors are used on the edges in E ⁡ ( G ) ∖ ( E ⁡ ( V 1 ) ∪ ⋯ ∪ E ⁡ ( V p ) ) E(G)\setminus(E(V_{1})\cup\cdots\cup E(V_{p})) , and only one color is used on the edges between any fixed pair ( V i , V j ) (V_{i},V_{j}) under τ \tau , where E ⁡ ( V i ) E(V_{i}) denotes the set of edges with both ends in V i V_{i} for all i ∈ [ p ] i\in[p] . Accordingly, we define the Ramsey number under this restricted coloring scheme. The Gallai-Ramsey number G ​ R ​ ( G 1 , … , G k ) GR(G_{1},\ldots,G_{k}) is the smallest positive integer N N such that for any Gallai coloring τ : E ⁡ ( K N ) → [ k ] \tau:E(K_{N})\to[k] , there exists a subgraph of color i i isomorphic to G i G_{i} for some i ∈ [ k ] i\in[k] . When G 1 = ⋯ = G k = G G_{1}=\cdots=G_{k}=G , we abbreviate it as G ​ R k ​ ( G ) GR_{k}(G) .
