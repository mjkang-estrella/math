[2410.23009] RSK as a linear operator Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Rings and Algebras arXiv:2410.23009 (math) [Submitted on 30 Oct 2024 ( v1 ), last revised 20 Dec 2024 (this version, v2)] Title: RSK as a linear operator Authors: Ada Stelzer , Alexander Yong View a PDF of the paper titled RSK as a linear operator, by Ada Stelzer and 1 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: The Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence (RSK) is a bijection between nonnegative integer matrices and pairs of Young tableaux. We study it as a linear operator on the coordinate ring of matrices, proving results about its diagonalizability, eigenvalues, trace, and determinant. Our criterion for diagonalizability involves the $ADE$ classification of Dynkin diagrams, as well as the diagram for $E_9$. Comments: We thank S. Fomin for comments that led to a reformulation of the diagonalizability theorem in terms of Dynkin diagrams. 37 pages Subjects: Rings and Algebras (math.RA) ; Combinatorics (math.CO); Representation Theory (math.RT) Cite as: arXiv:2410.23009 [math.RA] (or arXiv:2410.23009v2 [math.RA] for this version) https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.23009 Focus to learn more arXiv-issued DOI via DataCite Submission history From: Ada Stelzer [ view email ] [v1] Wed, 30 Oct 2024 13:35:30 UTC (38 KB) [v2] Fri, 20 Dec 2024 17:19:45 UTC (39 KB) Full-text links: Access Paper: View a PDF of the paper titled RSK as a linear operator, by Ada Stelzer and 1 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) TeX Source view license Current browse context: math.RA < prev | next > new | recent | 2024-10 Change to browse by: math math.CO math.RT References & Citations SciX Google Scholar Semantic Scholar export BibTeX citation Loading...
