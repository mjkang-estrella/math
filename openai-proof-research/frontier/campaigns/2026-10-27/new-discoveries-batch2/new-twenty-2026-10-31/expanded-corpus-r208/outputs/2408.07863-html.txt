We show that interlacing triangular arrays , introduced by Aggarwal–Borodin–Wheeler to study certain probability measures, can be used to compute structure constants for multiplying Schubert classes in the K K -theory of Grassmannians, in the cohomology of their cotangent bundles, and in the cohomology of partial flag varieties. Our results are achieved by establishing a splitting lemma, allowing for interlacing triangular arrays of high rank to be decomposed into arrays of lower rank, and by constructing a bijection between interlacing triangular arrays of rank 3 with certain proper vertex colorings of the triangular grid graph that factors through generalizations of Knutson–Tao puzzles. Along the way, we prove one enumerative conjecture of Aggarwal–Borodin–Wheeler and disprove another. 1. Introduction The LLT polynomials are a 1 1 -parameter family of symmetric polynomials introduced by Lascoux, Leclerc, and Thibon [ 17 ] . They have close connections to Macdonald polynomials [ 8 , 9 ] and Kazhdan–Lusztig theory [ 7 ] , among other areas of representation theory and geometry. In recent work, Aggarwal, Borodin, and Wheeler [ 1 ] studied probability measures arising from the Cauchy identity for LLT polynomials. They show that these measures asymptotically split into a continuous part, given by a product of GUE corners processes, and a discrete part, supported on interlacing triangular arrays . They conjectured that these arrays are equinumerous with vertex colorings of the triangular grid graph in the “rank- 3 3 ” case, and with vertex colorings of another grid graph in the rank- 4 4 case. We construct a bijection between interlacing arrays and vertex colorings proving the first conjecture, and we disprove the second. Our bijection factors through intermediate objects which are certain edge labelings of the triangular grid graph. We recognize these edge labelings as cryptomorphic to certain Schubert calculus puzzles [ 12 , 13 ] . We apply various geometric interpretations of puzzles [ 10 , 15 , 19 , 20 ] to prove that the corresponding families of interlacing triangular arrays compute structure constants in cohomology H ∗ ​ ( Gr ⁡ ( d , n ) ) H^{*}(\gr(d,n)) and K K -theory K ⁡ ( Gr ⁡ ( d , n ) ) K(\gr(d,n)) of Grassmannians, in the (localized) cohomology H ℂ × ∗ loc ​ ( T ∗ ​ Gr ⁡ ( d , n ) ) H^{*\text{loc}}_{\mathbb{C}^{\times}}(T^{*}\gr(d,n)) of their cotangent bundles, and for the multiplication in the cohomology of partial flag varieties of classes pulled back from smaller partial flag varieties. 1.1.
