11:                                                                                   Abstract
12:                                                       Chudnovsky, Scott, Seymour and Spirkl recently proved a conjecture
13:                                                   by Kalai and Meshulam stating that the reduced Euler characteristic of
14:                                                   the independence complex of a graph without induced cycles of length
15:                                                   divisible by three is in {−1, 0, 1}. Gauthier had earlier proved that assum-
16:                                                   ing no cycles of those lengths, induced or not. Kalai and Meshulam also
17:                                                   stated a stronger topological conjecture, that the total betti numbers are
18:                                                   in {0, 1}. Towards that we prove an even stronger statement in the same
19:                                                   setting as Gauthier: The independence complexes are either contractible
20:                                                   or homotopy equivalent to spheres. We conjecture that it also holds in
21:                                                   the general setting.
