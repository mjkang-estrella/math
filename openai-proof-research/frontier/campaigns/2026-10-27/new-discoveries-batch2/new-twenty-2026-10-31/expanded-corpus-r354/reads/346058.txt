11:                                                  A BSTRACT. We define and study a model of winding for non-colliding
12:                                                  particles in finite trees. We prove that the asymptotic behavior of this
13:                                                  statistic satisfies a central limiting theorem, analogous to similar re-
14:                                                  sults on winding of bounded particles in the plane (Wen and Thiffeault, 2019).
15:                                                  We also propose certain natural open questions and conjectures, whose
16:                                                  confirmation would provide new insights on configuration spaces of
17:                                                  trees.
