9:                                                                                                    Abstract
10:                                                      We define a new cutting plane closure for pure integer programs called the two-halfspace closure. It
11:                                                  is a natural generalization of the well-known Chvátal-Gomory closure. We prove that the two-halfspace
12:                                                  closure is polyhedral. We also study the corresponding 2-halfpsace rank of any valid inequality and show
13:                                                  that it is at most the split rank of the inequality. Moreover, while the split rank can be strictly larger
14:                                                  than the two-halfspace rank, the split rank can be at most twice the two-halfspace rank. A key step of
15:                                                  our analysis shows that the split closure of a rational polyhedron can be obtained by considering the split
16:                                                  closures of all k-dimensional (rational) projections of the polyhedron, for any fixed k ≥ 2. This result
17:                                                  may be of independent interest.
