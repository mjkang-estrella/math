10:                                                    Abstract. The obstruction set for graphs with knotless embeddings is not
11:                                                    known, but a recent paper of Goldberg, Mattman, and Naimi indicates that it
12:                                                    is quite large. Almost all known obstructions fall into four Triangle-Y families
13:                                                    and they ask if there is an efficient way of finding or estimating the size of
14:                                                    such graph families. Inspired by this question, we investigate the family size
15:                                                    for complete multipartite graphs. Aside from three families that appear to
16:                                                    grow exponentially, these families stabilize: after a certain point, increasing
17:                                                    the number of vertices in a fixed part does not change family size.
