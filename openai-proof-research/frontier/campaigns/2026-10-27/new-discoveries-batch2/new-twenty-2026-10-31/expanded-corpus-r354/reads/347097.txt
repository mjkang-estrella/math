12:                                                                                            Abstract
13:                                                     The long-standing Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture states that every n-uniform linear hy-
14:                                                 pergaph with n edges has a proper vertex-coloring using n colors. In this paper we propose
15:                                                 an algebraic framework to the problem and formulate a corresponding stronger conjecture.
16:                                                 Using the Combinatorial Nullstellensatz, we reduce the Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture to the
17:                                                 existence of non-zero coefficients in certain polynomials. These coefficients are in turn related
18:                                                 to the number of orientations with prescribed in-degree sequences of some auxiliary graphs.
19:                                                 We prove the existence of certain orientations, which verifies a necessary condition for our
20:                                                 algebraic approach to work.
21:                                                     Keywords: coloring, hypergraphs, Erdős–Faber–Lovász, Combinatorial Nullstellensatz,
22:                                                 graph orientations
