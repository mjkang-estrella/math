12:                                                   Abstract. We establish a tantalizing symmetry of certain numbers refining the Narayana numbers.
13:                                                   In terms of Dyck paths, this symmetry is interpreted in the following way: if wn,k,m is the number of
14:                                                   Dyck paths of semilength n with k occurrences of U D and m occurrences of U U D, then w2k+1,k,m =
15:                                                   w2k+1,k,k+1−m . We give a combinatorial proof of this fact, relying on the cycle lemma, and showing
16:                                                   that the numbers w2k+1,k,m are multiples of the Narayana numbers. We prove a more general
17:                                                   fact establishing a relationship between the numbers wn,k,m and a family of generalized Narayana
18:                                                   numbers due to Callan. A closed-form expression for the even more general numbers wn,k1 ,k2 ,...,kr
19:                                                   counting the semilength-n Dyck paths with k1 U D-factors, k2 U U D-factors, . . . , and kr U r D-factors
20:                                                   is also obtained, as well as a more general form of the discussed symmetry for these numbers in
21:                                                   the case when all rise runs
