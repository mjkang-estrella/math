5613: 5.5    Graphic groups as linearly independent colored graphic bases
5614: Graphic groups have been investigated in [15, 27, 13]. A set F ({Gi }ni=1 ; ⊕) of colored graphs {Gi }ni=1
5615: admitting W -type colorings under the additive operation “⊕” is called an every-zero graphic group (also
5616: Abelian additive group) based on the W -type coloring if:
5617:     (i) Every graph Gk of F ({Gi }ni=1 ; ⊕) is as the “zero” such that Gj ⊕ Gk = Gj for any graph Gj of
5618: F ({Gi }ni=1 ; ⊕);
5619:     (ii) for each zero Gk , Gi ⊕ Gj = Gs ∈ F ({Gi }ni=1 ; ⊕);
5620:     (iii) Gi ⊕ (Gj ⊕ Gs ) = (Gi ⊕ Gj ) ⊕ Gs ;
5621:     (iv) Gj ⊕ Gs = Gs ⊕ Gj .
5622:     Let Gc = (G1 , G2 , . . . , Gn ) with Gi ∈ F ({Gi }ni=1 ; ⊕) be an linearly independent colored graphic base.
