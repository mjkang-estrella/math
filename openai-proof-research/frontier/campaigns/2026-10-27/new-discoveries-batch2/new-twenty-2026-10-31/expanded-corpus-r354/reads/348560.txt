11:                                                                                                Abstract
12:                                                     In 1972, Tutte posed the 3-Flow Conjecture: that all 4-edge-connected graphs have a
13:                                                 nowhere zero 3-flow. This was extended by Jaeger et al. (1992) to allow vertices to have
14:                                                 a prescribed, possibly non-zero difference (modulo 3) between the inflow and outflow. They
15:                                                 conjectured that all 5-edge-connected graphs with a valid prescription function have a nowhere
16:                                                 zero 3-flow meeting that prescription (we call this the Strong 3-Flow Conjecture). Kochol
17:                                                 (2001) showed that replacing 4-edge-connected with 5-edge-connected would suffice to prove
18:                                                 the 3-Flow Conjecture and Lovász et al. (2013) showed that the 3-Flow and Strong 3-Flow
19:                                                 Conjectures hold if the edge connectivity condition is relaxed to 6-edge-connected. Both prob-
20:                                                 lems are still open for 5-edge-connected graphs.
