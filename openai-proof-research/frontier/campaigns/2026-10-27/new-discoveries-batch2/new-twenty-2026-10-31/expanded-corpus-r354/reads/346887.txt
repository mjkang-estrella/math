10:                                                   Abstract. Oriented matroids (often called order types) are combinatorial structures that
11:                                                   generalize point configurations, vector configurations, hyperplane arrangements, polyhedra,
12:                                                   linear programs, and directed graphs. Oriented matroids have played a key role in com-
13:                                                   binatorics, computational geometry, and optimization. This paper surveys prior work and
14:                                                   presents an update on the search for bounds on the diameter of the cocircuit graph of an
15:                                                   oriented matroid.
16:                                                      We review the diameter problem and show the diameter bounds of general oriented ma-
17:                                                   troids reduce to those of uniform oriented matroids. We give the latest exact bounds for
18:                                                   oriented matroids of low rank and low corank, and for all oriented matroids with up to nine
19:                                                   elements (this part required a large computer-based proof). The motivation for our inves-
20:                                                   tigations is the complexity of the simplex method and the criss-cross method. For arbitrary
21:                                                   oriented matroids, we present an improvement to a quadratic bound of Finschi. Our discus-
22:                                                   sion highlights two very important conjectures related to the polynomial Hirsch conjecture
