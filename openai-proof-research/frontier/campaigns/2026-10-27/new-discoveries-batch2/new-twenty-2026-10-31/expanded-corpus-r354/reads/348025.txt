5:                                                  Abstract. Given a group-word w and a group G, the set of w-
6:                                                  values in G is denoted by Gw and the verbal subgroup w(G) is the
7:                                                  one generated by Gw . The word w is concise if w(G) is finite for all
8:                                                  groups G in which Gw is finite. We obtain several results support-
9:                                                  ing the conjecture that the word [u1 , . . . , us ] is concise whenever
10:                                                  the words u1 , . . . , us are non-commutator.

15:                                                                          1. Introduction
16:                                               If w = w(x1 , . . . , xk ) is a group-word in variables x1 , . . . , xk , we
17:                                           denote by w(G) the verbal subgroup of the group G generated by the
18:                                           set Gw of all values of w in G. A word w is concise if w(G) is finite
19:                                           for every group G such that Gw is finite. P. Hall had conjectured that
20:                                           every word is concise but the conjecture was refuted by Ivanov in [9].
21:                                           Further examples of words which are not concise were produced in [12].
22:                                               On the other hand, many words are known to be concise. P. Hall
