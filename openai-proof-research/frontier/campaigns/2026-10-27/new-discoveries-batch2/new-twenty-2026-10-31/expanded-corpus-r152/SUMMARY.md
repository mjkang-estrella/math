## Searches and their limits

All scripts and full successful search outputs are retained locally. Finite computations are diagnostics, not Lean certificates.

- **349310:** `words.c` and `words2.c` implement the literal fixed point by base-Q digit parity. The first sweep has 32 parameter cases with p<=q<=5, Q<=8, n=3..5 and Q^n<=4096. The second has 30 cases p<=q<=8, Q<=12, n=3. Prefixes reach 11,943,936 letters. For each case, the log records prefix length, number of differences, proposed bound, best observed run, difference and start. Difference sets are specified exactly by the C loops: an initial interval plus Q^n-1 and Q^n+1 when the interval is capped. No violation. This cannot exclude a later starting position or an untested difference. Some large-n first-pass prefixes are too short even to attain the conjectured lower bound; those are not evidence for equality.
- **367689:** source y_G is the cut deficiency max_A(2c(G-A)-|A|-1), NOT maximum genus as the MathDB paraphrase says. Exhaustively checked 773 applicable connected planar atlas graphs through seven vertices. Then independently optimized over all vertex partitions by subset DP for 100 seeded connected planar graphs, 20 at each order 8..12. Computed complement chromatic number exactly by clique partition. No source-conjecture violation. The DP uses y_G=max_partition(2*blocks+internal_edges-|E|-1); splitting disconnected blocks proves equivalence to the edge-cut definition. Companion arXiv:2204.08010 Theorem 2.2 proof read. No claim that the paraphrase itself is sound.
- **371286:** seeded greedy edge addition with a prescribed dominating set; every accepted addition checks every other gamma-subset. Gamma=3,4,5; orders 3gamma..3gamma+6; 50 trials per gamma3 case and 15 otherwise. Exact graph witnesses and checks retained. Stopped on public/source overlap.
- **367365:** all 1024 triangle subfamilies on five vertices, plus 16 sampled ten-triangle complexes on orders 6..9. Compared area-derivative ranks with Rado predictions modulo 1000003. No discrepancy. Specializations do not certify generic rank, and cannot prove a generic defect.
- **376304:** all 35 two-subspaces over F2 in dimension four; all 30 maximal intersecting families have Ore degree 3, equal to threshold. Monotonicity covers their subfamilies. At (q,n,k,s)=(2,6,2,3), the two source barriers give degrees 9 and 15 against threshold 15. No new obstruction.
- **355274:** exact edge-subset dynamic programming over every connected atlas graph of orders 2..6 (142 graphs). No colorable graph in this range needs more than three classes. Uncolorable graphs are distinguished from failures of the bound.
- **366300:** 1330 numerical cases, all connected atlas graphs of orders 5 and 6, alpha=0.50,0.55,...,0.95; no separation above 1e-7. The source proves sufficiently large n, leaving small-order scope; numerical Jacobi output is not exact proof.
- **354350:** twelve applicable odd-edge-count atlas graph/parameter cases through order seven, with short odd cycles excluded by DFS. No numerical violation. The even-edge-count nonintegral construction is a source-domain issue, not a discovery.
- **357156:** exact reciprocal tuples with di>=2, integral reciprocal sum and the logged denominator caps. Counts: 15,150,3477,5268,4105 at n=4..8. Cyclic-order maxima: 6,42,1806,546,630. The last two denominator caps miss the enormous known extrema, so this does not push the substantive verified range. Source already enumerated groups through seven. The group formula requires checking that no prime divides four denominators; an initial diagnostic criterion was corrected before final output.
- **367084:** exact maximum clique on all labelled spanning trees of K3,K4,K5, every meaningful positive t. Recorded the t=1 source-known failures rather than treating them as new results.

The remaining five targets (355998, 358614, 360775, 366209, 370754), together with the eventual formula 372295, contain geometric semantics, unknown constants/polynomials, or eventual thresholds. Small finite objects cannot refute those quantifiers. Their primary conjecture bodies and relevant neighbouring results were read; precise gaps are in results.json. In particular, the metadata claim that 370754 is solved quotes a different, pentagon-count theorem.
