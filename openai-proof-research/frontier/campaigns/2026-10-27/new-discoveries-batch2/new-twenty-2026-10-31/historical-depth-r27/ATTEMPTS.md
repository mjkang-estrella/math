## 384921: exact two-cycle EMT / VMT construction

**Restored source scope.** The AIM 2024 workshop report, pp.1–2, defines a bijection on V disjoint union E onto 1,...,|V|+|E| with constant edge weights. It identifies the even-n 2C_n question and the stronger VMT magic-constant 5n+2 conjecture, and reports residual subfamilies after the workshop. The original AimPL question itself remains unread. The report is not a proof of the residual classification. Its PDF was readable through web extraction although direct HTTP returned404; see SOURCE-DEPTH.md. The scanned McQuillan 2010 publisher PDF was actually inspected as rendered pp.130–134: Proposition1, Lemmas2–3, Theorems6–7, Corollary8 and their displayed proof bodies, including the summarized dependency on an older paper for Lemma3. Cichacz–Froncek–Singgih 2017 full proof body remains unread; its author publication listing links DOI rather than providing an actual downloadable manuscript.

**Exact semantic bridge.** A cycle has vertices v_i and edges e_i=v_iv_(i+1), indices modulo its length. An EMT assignment (a_i,b_i) obeys a_i+a_(i+1)+b_i=k. On the dual cycle, assign vertex e_i label b_i and edge joining e_(i-1),e_i label a_i. The VMT sum at dual vertex e_i is b_i+a_i+a_(i+1)=k. This is a bijection on exactly the same labels, separately on each cycle, so preserves component lengths and the shared magic constant. Thus the report's VMT constructions are relevant to the exact EMT target, but do not automatically supply a new answer.

**Attempt 1: use known odd multipliers.** McQuillan's theorem starts from a labeled disjoint union and replaces each component C_k either by t copies of C_k or by C_(tk), for odd t=2m+1. Its proof first constructs a Z×Z labeling with image B×{0,...,t-1} and then uses mixed-radix bijections. It yields magic constants th−3m and 6|V|m+h. For exactly two components, the operation that replaces a component by multiple copies cannot increase the number of components unless it exits the desired two-cycle class. The operation elongating both components multiplies both lengths by an odd number. Starting from one cycle, the copy operation produces an odd number of cycles, never two. Hence these operations alone cannot create the needed base 2C_(2^q), or reduce unequal lengths with no common odd divisor. This is a precise gap, not an exclusion of the original problem. A finite new seed would at best yield an already-known multiplier subfamily; such a variant earns no credit.

**Attempt 2: universal two-copy affine lift fails.** Suppose one tries to double a base VMT cycle with label f(x), placing 2f(x)−epsilon_x in copy0 and 2f(x)−(1−epsilon_x) in copy1, epsilon_x in {0,1}. Each pair then uses exactly {2f−1,2f}, ensuring global bijectivity. If the base vertex-plus-two-incident-edge sum is h, the two lifted weights at corresponding vertices are 2h−E_v and 2h−3+E_v, where E_v is an integer sum of three epsilons. Equality of the two weights forces 2E_v=3, impossible. This rules out the entire paired-complement offset construction, rather than one failed seed. It does not rule out label permutations differing between copies or an entirely different construction.

**Attempt 3: reduce the stronger magic-constant conjecture to a permutation design.** For N=2n total vertices in a two-cycle VMT labeling, write the edge-label sum E and vertex-label sum A. Summing weights gives Nk=A+2E, while A+E=N(2N+1). Consequently E=N(k−2N−1). For k=5n+2 this is E=2n(n+1), exceeding the sum of the smallest 2n labels by exactly n. Choose 2n distinct edge labels in [1,4n] with that sum, cyclically order them into two n-cycles, and require that k−b_(i−1)−b_i list exactly the complementary 2n labels. This is an exact sufficient-and-necessary construction formulation for the stronger fixed-k endpoint, with wraparound explicitly included. It leaves a substantial design lemma: construct these two permutations for every even n, and separately handle all unequal m≥n in the literal question. No proof of that lemma obtained; no claim that k=5n+2 is required by MathDB, and no finite seed promoted to a solution. A Lean packet would need the two arbitrary-length permutations, range/bijection, disjoint-label exhaustion, all wraparound sums, and dual-cycle isomorphism. Park this route on that exact gap.

## 384918: projection algebra and failure of available linearizations

**Bodies read.** Balla–Sudakov's published 2019 paper: principal-angle definitions pp.82–83; Theorem2.1 with determinant-polynomial independence proof p.84; Theorem2.2 construction pp.84–85; concluding equi-isoclinic discussion pp.88–89. Iverson–Rose arXiv2511.20642: Theorem5.1, Proposition5.2 and its Gram-matrix proof; corner spaces Definitions5.3/Proposition5.4; Proposition5.6, Theorems5.7–5.8 with surjectivity proof and exceptional alpha=0 branch; Example5.9 and subsequent warnings that the j≤3 pattern does not continue. This is an unversioned retrieved preprint, hash-bound; publisher equivalence not claimed. EulerSolve's October23,2026 landing page gives only a restricted boundary-parameter result (k=2, projection length1/2, reflection triangle) and explicitly disclaims general resolution. Its manuscript proof body was NOT read, and it is not treated as established novelty clearance or a full-scope bridge.

Write P_i for rank-k real orthogonal projections with P_i P_j P_i=lambda P_i for i≠j, lambda=cos²(alpha) in [0,1). Then tr(P_i P_j)=lambda k, tr(P_i²)=k. The Frobenius Gram matrix is k[(1−lambda)I+lambda J], positive definite. Thus P_i are independent in Sym_d, giving only M≤d(d+1)/2. This argument is the known Proposition5.2 mechanism; no new theorem claimed.

**Attempt 1: centered trace bound.** Set S=sum_i(P_i−kI/d). Expanding tr(S²)≥0 gives
0≤Mk(1−k/d)+M(M−1)k(lambda−k/d).
If lambda<k/d, solve to obtain M≤d(1−lambda)/(k−d lambda). For fixed positive lambda,k and d→infinity, the required inequality fails; the coefficient instead becomes positive. Thus the relative bound controls a different dimension regime and cannot yield the requested O_(alpha,k)(d). This explicitly explains the bridge failure, beyond simply saying the known bound is quadratic.

**Attempt 2: take more corner constraints.** Every P_j lies in K_J={X symmetric: P_i X P_i is a scalar multiple of P_i for all i∈J}. The read preprint proves dim K_J=d(d+1)/2−|J|k(k+1)/2+|J| for |J|≤3. For fixed k this remains quadratic. Extrapolating to |J| proportional to d² might appear to force a linear bound, but independence of those constraints is exactly unproved. It is false without additional conditions: the source's real (d,k,M)=(4,2,4) example has dim K4=4 whereas the extrapolated formula gives2. The exceptional pattern is documented in the actual body, not inferred from the abstract. Needed new lemma: sufficiently many independent corner constraints, or a different global structure theorem, valid for all fixed lambda and k. No such lemma established.

**Attempt 3: reduce to equiangular lines.** Normalizing vec(P_i) gives unit vectors in dimension d(d+1)/2 with pairwise inner product lambda. A fixed-angle equiangular-line bound in that ambient dimension gives at best O_(lambda)(d²), not O(d). Selecting one unit vector from each k-space also fails: cross inner products are sqrt(lambda) times entries of independently varying orthogonal k×k transition blocks, not a common absolute scalar. Simultaneous alignment is a substantive global condition absent from the hypothesis. The source's tensor/block lower construction has varying zero principal angles and cannot refute equi-isoclinism. The stronger second asymptotic bound would moreover need a leading coefficient depending on k only; an alpha-dependent O(d) lemma would not alone finish it. Park both exact claims; no endpoint/domain counterexample or bounded-d instance credited.

## 384964: two chromatic zero-free regions

**Actual primary comparison.** Sokal cond-mat/9904146v3 Theorem5.1 proof, Corollaries5.2–5.3, and Section7 plus Table2/footnote12 were read. The theorem applies a polymer criterion and tree counts; its constant does not imply 2Delta. Section7 explicitly proposes the 2Delta-disc possibility and gives the complete-bipartite Stirling expansion. Bencs–Regts arXiv2505.04366 Theorem1.1 and its concluding proof using Proposition4.5 were read: the actual numeric choice a=.333,b=.739 yields4.25Delta, again insufficient. This is not a full dependency audit of their analytic criterion. No public resolution of either literal region is asserted or excluded based merely on these bounds. The AimPL body remains unread.

**Attempt 1: dense complete-bipartite obstruction.** For K_(m,n), classify a proper q-coloring by the k distinct colors used on the m-side: its chromatic polynomial is sum_k S(m,k)(q)_k(q−k)^n. This is a polynomial identity because the counting equality holds at every sufficiently large integer q; each term chooses the partition, injective colors, then independent n-side colors. This elementary bridge explains why the exact coefficients in diagnostics.py concern the actual chromatic polynomial, though it has NOT been certified in Lean. The retained script forms every coefficient in integer arithmetic for 2≤m≤n≤10 (45 cases), crosschecks small integer evaluations against independent enumeration for m≤5, and finds no approximate root outside either target region. Full coefficients/root diagnostics are retained in diagnostics.log. Floating roots are not certified isolations or proofs even for these45 graphs. This reproduces a classical candidate family; it is not novelty evidence.

**Attempt 2: simple degree2 families.** For a cycle C_l, P(q)=(q−1)^l+(−1)^l(q−1). Every nontrivial root satisfies |q−1|=1, so |q|≤2 and Re q≤2. Trees have q(q−1)^(n−1), and disjoint unions multiply chromatic polynomials. Consequently degree≤2 examples cannot supply the desired counterexample (isolates and K2 handled by their factors). This is routine and receives no credit. It motivates looking outside sparse theta subdivisions of a single cycle; it does not prove a uniform result for higher maximum degree.

**Attempt 3: deletion/contraction and analytic criteria.** Deletion/contraction gives P_G=P_(G−e)−P_(G/e). Nonvanishing of the two terms cannot imply nonvanishing of their difference. Moreover contraction can raise degrees, so induction under the exact same Delta loses its hypothesis. An admissible induction needs quantitative control of ratios P_(G/e)/P_(G−e) on the entire proposed complex region. Sokal's polymer/tree criterion or Bencs–Regts' broken-circuit criterion supplies a ratio-control framework only at larger constants; substituting2 does not establish its hypotheses. No valid improvement to2 or a counterexample was found. The Re q>Delta+1 clause is separate: even a 2Delta modulus bound would not settle it when Delta>1. Necessary remaining result is either a degree-controlled exact graph polynomial with certified offending root, or a uniform analytic ratio estimate in each literal region. Park, do not formalize a weaker zero-free constant or use the bounded diagnostic as endpoint.
