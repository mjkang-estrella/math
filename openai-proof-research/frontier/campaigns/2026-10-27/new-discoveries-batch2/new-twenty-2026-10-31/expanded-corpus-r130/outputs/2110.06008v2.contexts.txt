One could wonder whether our result has analogs in higher dimensions and, if so, which
distinguished lattices they would be. The fundamental diﬀerence to the above works is that
our results relate to the theory of sphere coverings (not packings). This potentially excludes
the E8 lattice from being optimal for our problem as, e.g., the A∗8 Voronoi lattice (the dual
of the A8 root lattice) yields a thinner covering than the E8 lattice [28, Chap. 2.1.3]. The
currently best lattice covering in dimension 8 was found by Schuermann and Vallentin [84]. To
the best of our knowledge (see again [28, Chap. 2.1.3]), the Leech lattice Λ24 is still nominated
for being the optimal candidate, as it yields the best known covering in dimension 24.
