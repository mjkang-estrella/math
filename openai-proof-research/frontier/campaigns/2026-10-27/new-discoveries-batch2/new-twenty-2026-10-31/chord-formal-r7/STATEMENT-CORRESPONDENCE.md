# Statement correspondence

The source is Burns, Jonoska and Saito, Genus Ranges of Chord Diagrams, arXiv:1410.6148, Conjecture 3.4, as cached in expanded-corpus-r137/sources/329080.txt and its candidate proof. MathDB 329080 reproduces: “For any n ≠ 4, there is a unique (up to equivalence) chord diagram with genus range {1, 2}, and it is (123123)(44 ⋯ nn).”

The intended endpoint restricts to n >= 3, n != 4. The printed positive-integer scope fails at n=1,2, as the source table itself shows. Root's ruling authorizes the intended scope and separately requires the small-size refutation and the exactly-four n=4 classification.

All definitions are inherited without edits. Diagram n is a fixed-point-free involution on Fin (2*n), representing a double-occurrence word without chord names. Equivalent is the literal equivalence closure generated by cyclic rotation and reversal conjugating that involution; renaming is already suppressed. r3Block n has pairing 0<->3, 1<->4, 2<->5, then successive adjacent pairs.

Sides n assigns one Boolean to each endpoint, hence 4^n choices. boundary is the textbook cycle count of rho after theta on the three half-edges P,N,C per endpoint, with boundary=2 for the empty annulus. HasGenus D c g means 2*g+boundary D c=n+2. genusRange existentially quantifies c; Range12 is pointwise equivalence with g=1 or g=2. There is no integer division or rounding. The root clarification chord-root-clarification-r2.json says DartBridge is not required because the endpoint uses boundary throughout.
