## Exhaustion of the global tilde spaces

Fix one orientation and let L_ij be the sum of all its U_k(W) for which f(W,k)=(i,j). Lemma11.4 gives L_ij subset tilde V_ij. The local decompositions and V=directsum W show sum_ij L_ij=V. Source10.3 says V=directsum_ij tilde V_ij. For v in tilde V_ab, write v=sum_ij l_ij with l_ij in L_ij. Compare this with the global tilde-coordinate decomposition having sole coordinate v at(a,b). Uniqueness implies l_ab=v and all other l_ij=0. Hence L_ij=tilde V_ij for EVERY index. No dimension count or thinness is being assumed.

Now P_W of a vector in tilde V_ij is the sum of precisely its matching U_k(W) terms. Every other module is annihilated by P_W. For fixed W the placement is injective in k, so it is either a single U_k(W) term or zero. In particular the W-projection stays in the exact original tilde space and its flags. This proves the crucial equality and projection properties, rather than merely inclusion of selected eigenvectors.

## Whole-space orthogonality

Take arbitrary u in DD(i,j) and v in UU(r,s), with i+r!=D or j+s!=D. Expand them using the equality just proved. Terms from different W are orthogonal. In the same W take DD piece k and UU piece l. If k+l>d, their dual supports are disjoint: rho+d-k<rho+l. If k+l<d, their primal supports are disjoint: tau+k<tau+d-l. In either case the inner product of EVERY pair of vectors in those pieces is zero. If k+l=d, the placements give
 i+r=D+d-k-l=D, j+s=D-d+k+l=D.
This last case is impossible for the chosen noncomplementary global pair. Thus every term of the finite sesquilinear expansion vanishes, and <u,v>=0.

For DU(i,j), UD(r,s), compare pieces k,l in the same W. If k>l, their dual supports are disjoint: rho+d-k<rho+d-l. If k<l, their primal supports are disjoint: tau+d-l<tau+d-k. If k=l, the placements give i+r=D-k+l=D and j+s=D+k-l=D, again excluded. Distinct-module terms vanish as before. This proves the second whole-space assertion.

Empty index groups are zero by the equality, so no zero-space exceptions remain. The -1 cells used to define the orthogonal complements are zero as in the source. There is no endpoint outside [0,D] to check for the theorem; formulas with local indices outside[0,d] contribute no summand. No rho=tau, thinness, module multiplicity restriction, q real, new graph hypothesis or selected-vector-only conclusion has entered.
