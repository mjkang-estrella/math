## A. Definition fidelity — PASS

The primary comparison is the cached arXiv v2 body `../expanded-corpus-r127/sources/txt/1804.03322.txt`, with v1 checked at lines 4064–4076. This review uses cached primary text, not the abstract as a substitute for the body.

* **Cycle and processing.** Source Example 3.11, lines 1035–1050, advances the rotor before moving the chip. `Dynamics.lean:8–25` gives vertex-labelled chips on `ZMod n` and one Boolean rotor per vertex. True is the current right edge, so true fires left and becomes false; false fires right and becomes true. `Step` at line 27 requires a strictly positive chip count. For n ≥ 3 the two neighbours are distinct; there is no graph quotient by rotations. `SourceSemantics.lean:6–25` proves compatibility of natural processing with the signed lift under precisely the positivity condition needed for natural subtraction.
* **Legal relation and component.** Source Definitions 4.6–4.7, lines 1533–1553, use a common legal descendant and its equivalence class. `Dynamics.lean:27–29,104` uses exactly a common descendant for the reflexive transitive closure of legal steps. Empty executions are allowed in Reach. `recurrent_related_iff` at lines 98–102 supplies mutual reachability for recurrent representatives; `Cardinality.lean:48–55` proves equality of whole component sets from mutual reachability. These are sets of ambient signed configurations, not just recurrent SCC vertex sets.
* **Recurrence.** Source Definition 5.2, lines 2254–2262, is exactly `Dynamics.lean:31–32`: a nonempty legal execution exists, and every related signed configuration reaches the given configuration. Existence of a first step is equivalent to a nonempty finite execution. The universal quantifier is over all signed configurations. `CycleTest` is separate (`Reduction.lean:15–18`), and its equivalence is proved, rather than made the definition of recurrence.
* **Recurrent components and diversity.** Source Definition 4.8, lines 1625–1641, permits forward, backward and two-sided walks and requires infinitely many occurrences of every letter. `SourceSemantics.lean:28–48` represents all three with the correct direction and legal-step condition. On ℕ, the quantifier `∀ v T, ∃ t ≥ T` is equivalent to an infinite occurrence set; on ℤ, the literal infinite-set condition is used. `SemPeriodic.lean:28–71` constructs a periodic diverse forward walk; `Sem3Coverage.lean:44–55` proves its required all-vertex coverage. Thus recurrent-component status is not merely a nonempty cycle with no diversity check.
* **Level and weight.** `Dynamics.lean:34–36` uses signed total chips and positional weight plus one for each current left rotor. These agree with source §8.3, lines 4101–4121. Source Definition 5.17 and its formula, lines 2726–2731, together with Lemma 5.18(iii), line 2747, and Lemma 7.8(i), line 3842, identify rotor-network level with total chips (capacity zero, period vector of ones). `Reduction.lean:8–12` proves natural/signed agreement. `Dynamics.lean:38–94` proves invariance through firing, reachability and Related. The left-edge weight is 1, the right-edge weight 0; it is not reversed.
* **Canonical component and uniqueness.** `Reduction.lean:20–30` places m chips at 0 and sets left rotors precisely at positions 1 through i.val. At i = 0 that interval is empty; at i = n−1 it contains all nonzero vertices. `SemCanonical.lean:6–56` proves the cycle test and weight. `C` is its actual signed legal component. The final `∃! S : Set (SignedConfig n)` at `ChanLevineFinal.lean:14–16` is uniqueness of a component set, not uniqueness of a configuration. `Cardinality.lean:58–68` proves it from connected recurrent fibres; those fibres are now proved by sink normalization (`Sem5Sink.lean:17–83`, `Sem5Classify.lean:38–107`, `SemFinal4.lean:8`). Hence the earlier review's unproved-uniqueness objection is resolved in this packet.
* **Meaning of the count.** `Reduction.lean:29–30` counts exactly natural configurations whose lifts are literally recurrent and belong to C. `SemWords.lean:68–72`, via `Sem3Coverage.closedCoverage`, proves every signed recurrent state's chips nonnegative. Each such state has a unique natural preimage (coordinatewise Int.toNat with the same rotors); lifts are injective. Thus restricting the counting domain loses or duplicates no source recurrent configuration. Finiteness is independently proved in `Cardinality.lean:6–45`: each coordinate at level m is below m+1, giving an injection into a finite product. The Nat.card is not zero through infinitude. Canonical recurrence and reflexive Related give an inhabitant at every positive level and weight, so there is no empty-component vacuity.
* **Quantifiers and gcd.** `ChanLevineFinal.lean:11–16,23–27` has all n ≥ 3, m ≥ 1 and i,j : ZMod n. The explicit `[NeZero n]` is redundant under n ≥ 3 and is always constructible; it excludes no source parameter. `gcd(gcd(n,m),i.val)` is well-defined on residues since gcd(n,m) divides n. The proof explicitly handles reduction modulo that gcd (`CountArithmetic.lean:55–74`). It does not replace gcd equality by congruence modulo gcd(n,m). Zero residue is included and has gcd(n,m,0)=gcd(n,m).

The literal endpoint and proved component-set uniqueness required by root's adjudication are present. A precision boundary: the displayed unique-existence predicate characterizes component sets admitting a recurrent natural representative of the specified level and weight. It does not literally quantify over arbitrary sets satisfying only `RecurrentComponent`. Identifying those two descriptions uses source Lemma 5.19 (lines 2792 onward). The packet supplies the recurrence-to-diverse-component direction used by its endpoint, but does not package the full converse as an additional theorem. This is not a substitute recurrence definition or an unproved premise of the final endpoint; the manuscript should state the exact predicate and its source correspondence candidly. A standalone equivalence theorem for all diverse components would be useful strengthening, not a repair to the displayed endpoint.

## C. Independent semantic sanity — PASS

My new `scripts/sanity.py` constructs every vertex-labelled state and every source legal advance-then-move edge, then computes terminal strongly connected components by an iterative two-pass graph algorithm. It does not use the cycle test to select recurrent states. The fixed-level natural state space is finite and forward closed; Definition 5.2 holds precisely on terminal SCCs admitting an edge. If a state shares a descendant with such a recurrent state, that descendant is inside its terminal SCC and reaches it, even when the other state is signed and outside the enumerated space. Conversely a nonterminal SCC reaches a downstream terminal SCC and cannot return. Thus this is a definition-level diagnostic.

For all 16 cases n = 3..6, m = 1..4, terminal SCC counts agree exactly with an independent integer implementation of the Lucas-trace/denominator/Ramanujan formula used in the proof. They also agree configuration by configuration with the literal facing-pair/Hamiltonian CycleTest. Each case has exactly n recurrent SCCs, each of constant weight, with distinct weights. Every vertex has a legal internal edge in each terminal SCC, permitting a diverse repeated walk. Four additional forward-closed signed enumerations with chips ≥ −1 (n = 3,4 and m = 1,2) have exactly the same recurrent counts and no recurrent negative state. All checks passed. The complete outputs, not only a few sample values, are in `logs/sanity-results.json` and the simulation log.
