# Exact attempts and boundaries

No new resolution or Lean endpoint was obtained. All computations below are ordinary integer or finite-field diagnostics, not Lean proofs. Random counts are trials and may include repeats.

## 358525 and 358524: constrained invertibility

The actual source is Bjerkevik, arXiv:2305.15550v3, Definitions 6.6 and 6.9 and Conjectures 6.5 and 6.10. The field is fixed. The quantifier is existence of one odd constant independent of matrix size, not the assertion that c = 1 or c = 3 works for a particular size. Example 6.7 already gives a feasible size-three problem without a simple solution. Repackaging that example, including direct sums or padding, earns no credit. Theorem 6.17 already supplies the equivalence with upset matchings; that equivalence is not a new solution of either reserved record.

For every tested invertible matrix A, take the minimal support patterns P = supp(A) and Q = supp(A inverse). The source graph has u_i -> v_j when A[j,i] is nonzero and v_j -> u_i when B[i,j] is nonzero. Compute all directed shortest-path distances. For each successive odd c, keep mutual pairs at distance at most c, and compute a maximum matching by augmenting paths. This implements the simple-solution criterion without enumerating permutations. Enlarging supports cannot make a matching harder, so minimal supports are the appropriate search space for matrices over each tested finite field.

`ci.py` exhausts every binary matrix for sizes 1 through 4. Invertible counts are 1, 6, 168 and 20160. Size-three minima are c = 1 for 150 matrices and c = 3 for 18 matrices; size-four minima are 1 for 14496 and 3 for 5664. There are 4000 sparse random trials at each size 5 through 8. `ci-fields.py` separately exhausts sizes 2 and 3 over F_3, then performs 10000 random trials for each combination of field F_3, F_5, F_101 and size 4, 6, 10, 16. No tested feasible pattern requires more than c = 3. Full counts and reproducible seeds are in the JSON evidence and logs.

Attempted structural route: determinant expansion guarantees a matching in the forward support, but it need not guarantee inverse support in the reverse direction. The diagonal identity AB = I ensures an individual mutual neighbor, not Hall's condition for every subset. Allowing directed paths of length three enlarges the mutual graph substantially in the tests. The missing lemma is a dimension-independent Hall inequality for these enlarged mutual neighborhoods. The generic reachability bound, at most 2n - 1 for finite directed paths, depends on n and cannot settle the conjecture. No such uniform Hall lemma or family violating every fixed odd c was found. A single failure at c = 3 would not negate the reserved existential statement.

## 357880: bipartized triangulations

Crespo Ruiz, arXiv:2303.15776v1 and v2, Definitions 1.4 and 1.5, Theorem 3.3, and Conjecture 1.10 specify the object. Retain all k-irrelevant edges, choose k(n - 2k - 1) k-relevant diagonals, and reject sets containing k + 1 pairwise crossing diagonals. Bipartize (i,j) to (i,n-1-j) in zero-based indexing and restrict each side to n-k-1 vertices. Use the actual bar-and-joint matrix at moment-curve points (t,t^2,...,t^k), with independent distinct parameters for the two parts. Row independence is the diagnostic tested; no assertion that it is a literal full-scope basis theorem is made here.

`triangulations.py` exhausts all relevant-diagonal subsets at (k,n) = (3,9), (3,10), (3,11), (4,11). The facet counts are 30, 330, 4719 and 55. Every reduced matrix has full row rank modulo 1000003 at the specified seeded specialization. Extra samples: 20000 subsets at (3,11), 100000 subsets at (3,12), and 100000 subsets at (4,12). Of the latter two samples, 47 and 5396 pass the crossing filter (trials, not distinct facets); all pass the row-rank test.

The source's Theorem 1.7 already proves generic independence and the hyperconnectivity basis statement. That does not automatically specialize to points on a moment curve: a minor nonzero in unrestricted coordinates can vanish identically after specialization. Theorem 3.3 identifies the relevant specialized representations but does not prove their independence for all triangulations. Theorem 1.13 already prints a dependent K9-minus-three-edges configuration at special repeated-side parameters. It is not a counterexample to the generic-parameter conjecture. Theorem 1.14 concerns simultaneous fan realization and must not be confused with failure of generic independence. The missing step is a nonzero specialized maximal minor for every k-triangulation, or an identically rank-deficient triangulation with a symbolic dependence. No such dependence was found. Generic rank failure cannot be inferred from failure at one numerical specialization.

## 358018: symmetric simplicial circuits

Cruickshank, Jackson and Tanigawa, arXiv:2304.04693v1, Sections 2, 3, 5 and Conjecture 7.1 define a free simplicial involution and a circuit as a minimal nonzero mod-two homology cycle. The group Gamma_(1,3) is generated by diag(1,-1,-1). It asks for a fully infinitesimally rigid realization with that symmetry, not merely rigidity against symmetric velocities. The full ordinary rigidity matrix was therefore used.

Fix n/2 disjoint vertex pairs and forbid any triangle containing both vertices of a pair. Enumerate triangle orbits under the involution, compute the kernel of the orbit boundary map over F_2, and expand each selected orbit cycle into its triangles. A nonempty cycle is minimal exactly when the boundary columns on its support have nullity one. Require every vertex to occur. Test 4-connectivity by every deletion of up to three vertices. To avoid an unimplemented planarity oracle, retain only graphs with more than 3n-6 edges, which certifies nonplanarity but misses possible nonplanar graphs at smaller edge counts. Build the full rigidity matrix at independently seeded integer orbit representatives and their half-turn images and compute rank modulo 1000003.

For n = 6 and 8, all 1 and 255 nonzero invariant cycles were examined. They yield respectively 1 and 84 full-vertex minimal circuits; 0 and 36 pass the connectivity and density filters. For n = 10 the invariant cycle space has dimension 25, so exhaustive enumeration would contain 33554431 nonzero cycles. Initial 3000 trials yielded 58 full-vertex circuits and 57 eligible trials. A second seeded sample of 100000 trials yielded 1675 full-vertex circuits and 1655 eligible trials. Every eligible matrix had rank 3n-6. No counterexample appeared.

The source already prints the Bricard octahedron and the symmetric insertion construction. They do not meet the needed nonplanarity/4-connectivity combination in the required way; modifying them would additionally face the source-construction exclusion. The missing theorem is that every nonplanar 4-connected symmetric circuit admits a nonzero rigidity minor on the half-turn locus. Neither finite checks nor Fogelsanger's unrestricted generic rigidity theorem provides this specialization result. The accepted 2025 manuscript still states the same conjecture.

## Scope of the pass

The remaining deep targets received statement/source and follow-up screening, rather than uncontrolled exhaustive searches. In particular, finite evidence cannot address the infinite-poset, coarse-geometric, boundary-action, or strip-finiteness quantifiers. No enumeration of all groups, oriented matroids, bridgeless cubic graphs, ISK4-free graphs, polynomial coefficients, or odd strong immersions is claimed. Sources with known resolutions were stopped before search. This pass did not satisfy a literal exhaustive-small-instance-search requirement for every conceivable finite object in the reservation, and does not claim otherwise.
