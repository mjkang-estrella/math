##349224: convexity shortcut fails; unbalanced bipartite route closes but is public

Example3.9 distinguishes vertex energy from Shapley; the vertex-energy core theorem3.6 cannot be substituted for Shapley core membership. Conjecture3.10 is exactly the reserved universal statement.

A hand mechanism uses G=K_{2,m}. An induced coalition with r vertices on the small side and k on the other has adjacency eigenvalues +/-sqrt(rk), others zero: the subspace constant on each side has matrix [[0,k],[r,0]], and zero-sum vectors on either side are killed. Thus its worth is2sqrt(rk), including zero cases.

Let a be one small-side vertex and q=sqrt2, H=(m+1)(m+2). In a uniformly random permutation use independent continuous uniform priorities to represent predecessor sets. Conditional on a's priority t, a given k-element subset of the large side precedes with weight t^k(1-t)^(m-k). The other small vertex precedes with probability t. The marginal of a is2sqrt k if that vertex follows, and2(q-1)sqrt k otherwise. Integrating and multiplying by binomial(m,k), the two weights are respectively (m-k+1)/H and(k+1)/H. This follows either by integrating the integer beta polynomial or by counting permutations of the same predecessor sets; no approximation is used. Therefore
 phi_a=2/H sum_{k=0}^m [m+q+(q-2)k]sqrt k.

Write S1=sum sqrt k and S3=sum k sqrt k. Monotonicity of t^(1/2) on each unit interval gives S1>=integral_0^m sqrt t dt=(2/3)m sqrt m.  phi_a/[2sqrt m]-(q-1) >= [(13-9q)m^2/15+(1-4q/3)m-2(q-1)]/H.

##349178: reflection gauge, then exact pair-coincidence obstruction
Read primary2012.12582 Section2 definitions, Section4.1 pattern, Section4.3 propositions4,6,7,8 and Remark9, Section5.2.1 Conjecture10. A block with local row r,column c has color word f_ab(c-e_ab*r) modulo z, e_ab in{+1,-1}. Reflection reverses its direction. Independently reflecting every block preserves its internal rectangle condition but is not a global row/column permutation; a rectangle spanning blocks can be created. Thus this tempting shortcut is invalid.

A stronger isomorphism route seeks block-row signs u_a and block-column signs v_b in{+1,-1}. Reflect every local row coordinate throughout block row a by u_a and every local column coordinate throughout block column b by v_b. These ARE global row and column permutations and preserve all rectangles. Substituting r=u_a*r', c=v_b*c' gives, after absorbing v_b into the word, new sign e'_ab=e_ab*u_a*v_b. All signs can become+1 if e_ab=u_a*v_b. A necessary and sufficient condition on a fully populated rectangular block array is that every2x2 sign product equals+1: necessity cancels four row/column factors; sufficiency takes u_a=e_a1 and v_b=e_11*e_1b and uses that product condition. Hence the conjecture is proved for the subclass admitting such a sign factorization, including a single block row/column. This is a helper only, NOT the general statement. No reason was found that rectangle-free colorings force sign factorization; two colors per block disjoint from other blocks would already remove many such restrictions, though no finite counterexample to the conjecture is asserted.

Second route drops isomorphism and seeks recoloring with all signs equal. For any pair of distinct global rows R,S, rectangle-freeness is exactly: for each color d, at most one global column c satisfies chi(R,c)=chi(S,c)=d. Necessity is the two-column rectangle; sufficiency recovers any rectangle from its two rows. In two block rows a,a', within block column b, coincidences are pairs of evaluations f_ab(c-e_ab*r),f_a'b(c-e_a'b*r'). Reversing one sign changes the phase difference from e_ab*r-e_a'b*r' to its counterpart; the coupled requirements range over every pair(r,r'). This is why preserving within-block gap counts does not preserve cross-block constraints. The paper's Proposition8 bound for same-direction blocks is necessary, and Remark9 explicitly warns that the distribution constraints are insufficient.

Precise unpaid full-scope lemma: for every admissible block coloring with arbitrary signs, construct words g_ab so that every global row-pair/color has at most one coincidence when all signs are+1, also treating partial boundary blocks in general m,n. Neither sign-factorization nor color-distribution feasibility proves this lemma. No enumeration or solver was run. Current novelty screening was not spent on this target; general resolution status remains unqualified. Park rather than inflate the subclass proof into a full candidate. No proof or disproof of the universal conjecture.
