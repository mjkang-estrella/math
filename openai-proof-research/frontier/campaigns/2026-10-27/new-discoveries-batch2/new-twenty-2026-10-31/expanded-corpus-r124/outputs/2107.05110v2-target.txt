60:     In this paper we study a weaker property than the Virial Positivity conjecture. We work with
61: the Weak Virial Positivity Conjecture.
62: Conjecture I.2. For each i and k ≥ 2 one has
63: 
64:                                    Prob(∆k u(i) ≥ 0) −−−−→ 1.
65:                                                       n→∞
66: 
67: In fact, what we prove is
68: Theorem I.1. If r ≤ 10, i + k ≤ 100, 2 ≤ k ≤ 27, or i + k ≤ 29 all r, then
69: 
70:                                    Prob(∆k u(i) ≥ 0) −−−−→ 1.
71:                                                       n→∞
72: 
73:     The proof is presented in Section II. We here note that one relies heavily on the work of
74: Wanless, [4], and Pernici, [5]. We are also indebted to Robin Chapman, see Appendix C of [3],
75: for a combinatoric proof. The extension of the range of validity of the theorem to i + k ≤ 29 all
76: r is due to numerical computer computations presented in [7].
