57: Remark 1.4 (Addition of primes). More than once, some mathematicians (and physicists), among
58: them very eminent ones, have expressed the opinion that the two famous Goldbach conjectures are
59: completely devoid of interest since they concern the addition of primes whereas primes are created
60: to be multiplied, not added. Our note may be considered as a strong case for the interest, in certain
61: contexts, of the addition of primes and of numbers related to them (such as prime powers). By the
62: way, one of the Goldbach conjectures has already been proved by Harald Helfgott [23].
63: Notation 1.5 (Primes). Notation for primes depends on the context. We follow the tradition of

65: more than one prime number involved, as is the case, for example, in formula (1), then one of these
