                  5. Open Problems and Further Extensions
   Although the initial empty-board problem for Inverse Treblecross is resolved,
several structural patterns extend beyond our current framework. Since the “re-
duction move” strategy relies on immediate parity responses, it is insufficient to
characterize these broader phenomena. In this section we propose conjectures and
extensions, which likely require more sophisticated tools.

5.1. Coverage of Regular Positions. The regular positions defined in the main
theorem contain at most one piece, with the two exceptions [X 4̄X] and [X 9̄X].
Computational evidence suggests that R nearly exhausts the simple states of SG
value 0 or 1.
Conjecture 5.1. For all single-piece boards [aXb] with a, b ≥ 2, the regular set R
contains every state with G ≤ 1, except the isolated states
                             [1X1],    [1X4],    and       [4X4]
(up to left–right reflection), which have value 1 and lie outside R.
Remark 5.2. The restriction a, b ≥ 2 is necessary: for example [1X0] = [1X]
has value 1 but χ2 (1)χ2 (0) = 0, so it lies outside R; the same applies to [0X1].
Such boundary states of one piece are precisely the exceptional classes R∗ already
handled separately.
