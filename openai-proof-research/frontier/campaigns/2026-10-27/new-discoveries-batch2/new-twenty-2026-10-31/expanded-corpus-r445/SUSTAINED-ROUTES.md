Route2: use deletion maps f_{N,k} from the source subadditivity argument. Fix K of size k. Each relative permutation of K has (N-1)!/(k-1)! preimages whose first symbol lies in K: select k-1 later positions for the remaining K letters and permute the N-k other symbols. Lifting a balanced a-coloring of P_k therefore gives each lifted color k(N-1)!/a vertices. Combining with a disjoint palette from P_{N-k} with b colors yields (N-k)(N-1)!/b vertices per other color. For this construction to be equitable these integers must agree, since a+b<=N implies a+b divides N!, so every equitable class is exactly N!/(a+b). Thus k/a=(N-k)/b is necessary for this simple lift, in addition to optimality a+b=chi(P_N). Source subadditivity alone gives neither identity.
