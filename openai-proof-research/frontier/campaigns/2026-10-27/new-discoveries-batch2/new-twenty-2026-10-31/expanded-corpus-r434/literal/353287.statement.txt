Let $H$ be a graph. Write $\chi(H)$ for its chromatic number and $\chi_L(H)$ for its list chromatic number.

**Ohba's conjecture.** If

$$
|V(H)|\leq 2\chi(H)+1,
$$

then

$$
\chi_L(H)=\chi(H).
$$

This conjecture concerns when the list chromatic number agrees with the ordinary chromatic number for graphs whose order is bounded in terms of their chromatic number. The source attributes it to Ohba; its resolution status is not specified in the supplied text.
