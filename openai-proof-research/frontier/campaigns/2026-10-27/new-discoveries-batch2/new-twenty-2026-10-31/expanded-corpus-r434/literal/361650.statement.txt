Let $
C$ be a hereditary graph class. **Bounded-rank alternation conjecture.** The class $
C$ has bounded rank if and only if there is $k\in\mathbb{N}$ such that every first-order formula is equivalent on $
C$ to a formula of alternation rank $k$. This conjecture generalizes the theorem preceding it in the source and relates the structural notion of rank to a uniform bound on quantifier alternation; the source leaves it as a conjecture for future work.
