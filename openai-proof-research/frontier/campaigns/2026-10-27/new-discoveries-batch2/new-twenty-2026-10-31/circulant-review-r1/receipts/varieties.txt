(2020) : 05C12, 05C69, 05C30 1 Introduction General position and mutual-visibility are two fresh areas in metric and algorithmic graph theory. These concepts are complementary to each other, and together they represent a flourishing field of research. After general position sets were independently introduced (in a general setting) to graph theory in [ 5 ] and in [ 22 ] , research in this area has expanded rapidly, a recent review article [ 4 ] lists 115 references. These investigations include several interesting variations including edge general position sets [ 23 ] , monophonic position sets [ 26 ] , Steiner position sets [ 15 ] , vertex position sets [ 25 ] , mobile position sets [ 14 ] , and lower general position sets [ 10 , 20 ] . See also recent studies [ 1 , 13 , 27 , 31 , 32 , 33 ] . Given a set X X of vertices in a graph G G , two vertices u u and v v are X X -positionable , if for every shortest u , v u,v -path P P we have V ⁡ ( P ) ∩ X ⊆ { u , v } V(P)\cap X\subseteq\{u,v\} . (Note that if u ​ v ∈ E ⁡ ( G ) uv\in E(G) , then u u and v v are X X -positionable.) Then X X is a general position set , if every u , v ∈ X u,v\in X are X X -positionable. A largest general position set is a gp-set and its size is the general position number gp ⁡ ( G ) {\rm gp}(G) of G G . Based on the motivation of robotic visibility, the graph mutual-visibility problem was introduced in 2022 by Di Stefano [ 9 ] . Given a set X X of vertices in a graph G G , two vertices u u and v v are mutually-visible with respect to X X , shortly X X -visible , if there exists a shortest u , v u,v -path P P such that V ⁡ ( P ) ∩ X ⊆ { u , v } V(P)\cap X\subseteq\{u,v\} . The set X X is a mutual-visibility set if any two vertices from X X are X X -visible. A largest mutual-visibility set of G G is a μ \mu -set and its size is the mutual-visibility number μ ⁡ ( G ) \mu(G) of G G . Although only recently introduced, the mutual-visibility sets has already received a lot of attention, here we would like to point in particular to [ 2 , 8 , 17 , 21 , 24 , 28 ] . In [ 7 ] , the total mutual-visibility number was introduced, while the variety of mutual-visibility invariants was rounded off in [ 6 ] by adding to the list the outer mutual-visibility number and the dual mutual-visibility number. A set X ⊆ V ⁡ ( G ) X\subseteq V(G) is an outer mutual-visibility set in G G if X X is a mutual-visibility set and every pair of vertices u ∈ X u\in X , v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) ∖ X v\in V(G)\setminus X are X X -visible. X X is a dual mutual-visibility set if X X is a mutual-visibility set and every pair of vertices u , v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) ∖ X u,v\in V(G)\setminus X are X X -visible. Finally, X X is a total mutual-visibility set if every pair of vertices in G G are X X -visible. The cardinality of a largest outer/dual/total mutual-visibility sets are respectively denoted by μ o ​ ( G ) \mu_{\rm o}(G) , μ d ​ ( G ) \mu_{\rm d}(G) , μ t ​ ( G ) \mu_{\rm t}(G) . Following the pattern of mutual-visibility, the variety of general position invariants was presented in [ 30 ] . The definition of the outer/dual/total general position set in G G is analogous, we just need to replace everywhere “ X X -visible” by “ X X -positionable.” Largest corresponding sets are called gp o {\rm gp}_{\rm o} -sets , gp d {\rm gp}_{\rm d} -sets , gp t {\rm gp}_{\rm t} -sets and their sizes are the outer/dual/total general position number of G G , respectively denoted by gp o ​ ( G ) {\rm gp}_{\rm o}(G) , gp d ​ ( G ) {\rm gp}_{\rm d}(G) , gp t ​ ( G ) {\rm gp}_{\rm t}(G) .
