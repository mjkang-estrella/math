## A. Source and MathDB fidelity — PASS

The raw record `expanded-corpus-r108/raw/350979.jsonl`, its results entry, source arXiv:2106.06827v3 (8 February 2022), and the survey agree on the relevant quantifier: for every integer n ≥ 11, there exists a circulant of order n with diameter 2 and mp-number 2. This is not Theorem 4.4, which concerns unrestricted graphs. The source assumes simple undirected graphs, defines distance by shortest-path length, and defines a monophonic path as a path without chords. Its mp-set forbids more than two members anywhere on one induced path. The between-two-others formulation is equivalent: restrict any offending induced path to the first and last of three selected vertices; conversely a between-vertices violation gives a common induced path.

`InducedPath` requires injectivity and adjacency iff indices are consecutive for arbitrary natural path length. This is exactly a simple induced path. Length 0 and 1 cannot contain three distinct vertices. `TripleMP` forbids common-path containment; `MonophonicSet` quantifies all distinct member triples. In finite types, the filtered universe in `mpNumber` ranges over every finset; its cardinality supremum is a maximum, attained because the collection is finite and contains the empty set. There is no bounded-path surrogate in the endpoint.

The source itself does not define circulant beyond the conventional term. The survey expressly identifies it with a Cayley graph of a cyclic group. Mathlib's `circulantGraph J := fromRel (· - · ∈ J)` symmetrizes differences and removes loops. Arbitrary J, asymmetric J, and J containing 0 thus give precisely the same graph class as symmetric nonzero difference sets. `Bridge.represent` proves full coverage on ZMod 15; labelling an arbitrary order-15 circulant by Z/15 loses no graphs. `SourceClaim` has ∀ n and ∃ J, as required.

Mathlib's natural diameter is 0 for disconnected graphs. That convention causes no weakening here: diameter = 2 excludes disconnected graphs, and `radius_of_diam` explicitly obtains finite extended diameter before bounding distances. The finite witnesses at order 15 are not complete graphs (so radius ≤ 2 means diameter exactly 2). The integer-versus-natural domain makes no difference under n ≥ 11. ## B. Ordinary mathematics — PASS

A triangle is an mp-set. If its three vertices occurred at indices i, j, k on an induced path, all three pairs of indices would be consecutive, impossible in a line.

Three distinct vertices with identical open neighborhoods are an mp-set. Suppose they occur on an induced path. That path has positive length, so one selected vertex has a path neighbor q. Equality of open neighborhoods makes q adjacent to all three selected vertices. The induced-path condition would give q three different neighbors along a path, impossible. Equality of open neighborhoods also ensures the selected vertices are nonadjacent in a simple graph; no closed-neighborhood convention is being used.

Independent enumeration in `scripts/compute.py` constructs adjacency directly by modular differences, tests triangles at 0 and the two-step reachability set, and visits every one of the 128 masks. The result is 109 triangle masks, 17 distance-obstruction masks, and precisely masks 41 and 70 as residual cases. The zero-degree disconnected graph is in the distance-obstruction bucket. Translation invariance makes testing at 0 sufficient. The two residual jump sets are {1,4,6} and {2,3,7}, and each has identical neighborhoods at 0, 5, 10. See `classification.json`.

The residual graphs both have **exact mp-number 6**, not merely at least 3. Their five fibers are the cosets of {0,5,10}; the quotient is a C5 (edges between residues differing by ±1 for mask 41, and ±2 for mask 70). Hence they are C5 with every vertex replaced by an independent set of three vertices. The union of two nonadjacent fibers is an mp-set of size 6. No induced path can contain two vertices of one fiber and any vertex of a nonadjacent fiber: the twins would have to be the ends of a length-2 path, since extending farther gives a chord. For the upper bound, any three distinct quotient vertices lie on a common induced C5 path, whose vertices can be lifted to prescribed fiber members. An mp-set therefore meets at most two fibers, hence has size at most 6. The separate exhaustive path/subset computation agrees: 360 forbidden triples in each residual graph and maximum sets of size 6, recorded in `classification.json`.

Additional exact samples at order 15: empty graph 15; C15 with jump {1}, 2; complete graph 15; jump set {1,2}, 3. These are computed by exhaustively enumerating induced paths starting at 0, translating all forbidden triples, and searching subsets in descending size. Translation covers paths starting elsewhere. `exact-samples.json` retains maximum-set witnesses and enumeration counts.

Coverage of orders 11–40 is now exact as an existence question: an example exists at **every order except 15**. At 15 all 128 masks were excluded. At the other 29 orders there are explicit induced-path witnesses covering every triple, giving mp ≤ 2; any pair is an mp-set, giving equality. Two-step reachability and a missing edge give diameter exactly 2. The independent verifier checks every listed path's injectivity, every pair's adjacency iff consecutive, and coverage of all binomial(n,3) triples after translations. See `coverage-verified.json` and `scripts/verify_witnesses.py`. This uses selective search to find positive witnesses, not exhaustive mask search at those 29 orders.
