346885 Let $\mathcal{M}$ be an oriented matroid of rank $r$ on $n$ elements, and let $G^*(\mathcal{M})$ be its cocircuit graph.

**Linear diameter conjecture.** The cocircuit graph satisfies

$$
\operatorname{diam}(G^*(\mathcal{M})) \leq n-r+2.
$$

This is the oldest and most ambitious challenge concerning cocircuit-graph diameters, and it resembles the Hirsch conjecture for convex polytopes. It has been disproved, by examples related to Santos's counterexamples to the Hirsch conjecture. 

346926 Let $G$ be a reduced plabic graph, and let $\Sigma_G^S$ and $\Sigma_G^T$ denote respectively its source and target seeds. 346957 Let $k\in\mathbb{N}$ be fixed. For a rational polyhedron $P$, define the **$k$-halfspace closure** by

$$
\mathcal{H}_k(P):=\bigcap_{\substack{k\text{-halfspace rational }\\\\ \text{relaxation }Q\text{ such that }\\\\P\subseteq Q}} Q_I,
$$

where $Q_I$ denotes the integer hull of $Q$. **Polyhedrality conjecture for the $k$-halfspace closure.** For any fixed natural number $k\in\mathbb{N}$, and any rational polyhedron $P$, $\mathcal{H}_k(P)$ is a rational polyhedron. This asks whether the closure obtained by intersecting integer hulls of all $k$-halfspace rational relaxations remains finitely describable by rational linear inequalities; the source gives no resolution, so the conjecture remains open. 

346959 Let $k\in\mathbb{N}$ be fixed. For a rational polyhedron $P\subseteq\mathbb{R}^n$, define the **$k$-dimensional projection closure** by

\mathcal{P}_k(P):=\bigcap_{L\in\mathcal{L}_k}\bigl(P_{I,L}+L^\perp\bigr),

where $\mathcal{L}_k$ is the family of $k$-dimensional subspaces, $P_{I,L}$ is the integer hull in the corresponding projection, and $L^\perp$ is the orthogonal complement of $L$. **Polyhedrality conjecture for the $k$-dimensional projection closure.** For any fixed natural number $k\in\mathbb{N}$, and any rational polyhedron $P$, $\mathcal{P}_k(P)$ is a rational polyhedron. This asks whether the intersection over all $k$-dimensional projections is a rational polyhedron; the source poses the question without resolving it.

347253 A **bi-bracket** is an element of the space $\mathcal{BD}$ of bi-brackets, while a **mono-bracket** is an element of the space $\mathcal{MD}$ of mono-brackets. These spaces are generated over $\mathbb{Q}$ by their respective brackets.

347266 Let $G$ be a complex reductive group with Borel subgroup $B$, Weyl group $W$, and let $X_w=\overline{BwB/B}$ be the Schubert variety associated with $w\in W$. 347465 Let $\boldsymbol{d}=(d_1,\ldots,d_n)\in\mathbb{Z}_+^n$ and $\boldsymbol{t}=(t_1,\ldots,t_m)\in\mathbb{Z}_+^m$. The pair $(\boldsymbol{d};\boldsymbol{t})$ is **feasible** if

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n d_i=\sum_{j=1}^m t_j,
$$

and let $\chi(\boldsymbol{d};\boldsymbol{t})$ denote its Euler characteristic.

**Non-orientable realizability conjecture.** Every feasible pair $(\boldsymbol{d};\boldsymbol{t})$ with $\chi(\boldsymbol{d};\boldsymbol{t})\leq 0$ can be realized on a non-orientable surface.

This is a stronger surface-specific form of the general realizability conjecture. The paper notes examples distinguishing torus and Klein-bottle realizability, but whether every feasible pair of nonpositive Euler characteristic is realizable on some non-orientable surface remains open. 

347506 Let $S$ be a random fixed-length circular society with $n$ voters, approval set length $p$, and piercing number $\tau(S)$. For a positive integer $k$, **Extended probability formula.** The probability that $S$ has piercing number $k$ is

$$
P(\tau(S) = k) = \binom{n}{k} (1-kp)^{k-1}(kp)^{n-k}
$$

for $p$ in an interval $(0, \frac{1}{2k} + \delta)$ for some $\delta > 0$. The formula is known for $p < \frac{1}{2k}$; the conjecture proposes that it continues to hold on a larger interval, as suggested by simulations for certain values of $k$.

347660 Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the endpoints of an origami edge-path

$$
\{\alpha=\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_{n>2}=\beta\}.
$$

Assume that the successive intersection quotients satisfy

$$
\frac{|\beta\cap\alpha_1|}{|\alpha\cap\alpha_1|}>\frac{|\beta\cap\alpha_2|}{|\alpha\cap\alpha_2|}>\cdots>\frac{|\beta\cap\alpha_n|}{|\alpha\cap\alpha_n|}.
$$

**Intersection-quotient conjecture.** Under this condition, the edge-path is a quasi-geodesic.

The quotients can be viewed as slopes in the flat structure determined by the $(\alpha,\beta)$-origami. Strict decrease is intended to prevent zig-zagging near a geodesic. The source gives no resolution of the conjecture. 

347661 Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the endpoints of a shortest origami edge-path, and let $\alpha_i$ and $\alpha_{i+1}$ be consecutive curves on it, with $\alpha_{i+1}$ intersecting $\alpha_i$ once. **Intersection-minimization conjecture.** The quantity $|\beta\cap\alpha_{i+1}|$ is minimal among all curves $\alpha_{i+1}$ intersecting $\alpha_i$ once.

This conjecture formalizes the intuition that shortest paths should move monotonically toward the endpoint $\beta$, rather than zig-zagging. The source gives no resolution of the claim. 

347663 Let $J_{s,2}(A)$ denote the number of solutions to the quadratic Vinogradov system for a finite set $A$ of real numbers, with $s$ pairs of variables. Let $s\geq 3$ be a natural number, let $A$ be a finite, non-empty set of real numbers, and let $\epsilon>0$ be real. **Bourgain–Demeter's conjecture.** One has

$$
J_{s,2}(A)\ll_{\epsilon}|A|^{2s-3+\epsilon}.
$$

The conjecture asserts that the lower bound supplied by the structured sets $A_N=\{1,2,\ldots,N\}$ is sharp up to a factor of $|A|^{\epsilon}$. Its case $s=3$ was posed by Bourgain and Demeter, and the displayed estimate for general $s$ is equivalent to that case via Young's convolution inequality. 

347741 Let $4\mathcal{T}4$ be a plane triangulation graph, with EQ-type, VEL-type, and CP-type denoting the discrete types associated respectively with equilateral triangulation geometry, vertex extremal length, and circle packing. **The EQ-type conjecture.** For any plane triangulation graph, EQ-type coincides with VEL-type, and therefore with CP-type. For bounded-degree plane triangulation graphs, EQ-type and CP-type already coincide by quasiconformal comparison; the conjecture concerns the unbounded-degree case, where the relationship with VEL-type remains open.
