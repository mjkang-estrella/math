With truth defined by color(T), that internal vertex is opposite the majority of T,u1,u2, so its output is their OR. Chaining gives OR of any finite interval. The countable graph has path vertices v_i and, among outneighbors of v_i, outputs of every finite tail-prefix OR starting at v_{i+1}.
