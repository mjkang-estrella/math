367907 Let ${\cal F}$ be a family of sets. It is **subset-closed** if every subset of every member of ${\cal F}$ also belongs to ${\cal F}$. Recall that ${\cal F}$ is ${\sf EKR}$ when some star ${\cal F}_x$ has size at least that of every intersecting subfamily ${\cal H}\subseteq{\cal F}$.

**Chvátal's conjecture.** Every subset-closed family of sets is ${\sf EKR}$.

This conjecture asks for an Erdős–Ko–Rado-type extremal theorem for arbitrary subset-closed families. Its status is not specified in the source.
368264 Let $\operatorname{\mathcal{G}}=(V,E)$ be a bridgeless cubic graph. A **cycle double cover** of $\operatorname{\mathcal{G}}$ is a set of cycles in which every arc of $\operatorname{\mathcal{G}}$ is contained in exactly two cycles. **Cycle double cover conjecture.** Every bridgeless cubic graph has a cycle double cover. The conjecture concerns the existence of such covers for all bridgeless cubic graphs; arbitrary cycle double covers are not known to exist in general.
319963 Let $t$ be a positive integer and let $G$ be a finite simple undirected graph. A **list-assignment** of $G$ assigns a set $L(v)$ of colours to each vertex $v$; the graph is $k$-choosable if it is colourable from every list-assignment satisfying $|L(v)|\geq k$ for every vertex. The graph $G$ is **$K_t$-minor-free** if it has no minor isomorphic to $K_t$.

**Weak List Hadwiger conjecture.** There is a constant $c$ such that every $K_t$-minor-free graph is $ct$-choosable.

This list-colouring analogue is attributed in the source to Kawarabayashi and Mohar. The source presents it as a conjecture; no resolution is given.
318534 Let $G$ and $H$ be graphs, and let $\mathcal{S}_G$ and $\mathcal{S}_H$ be sets of pebble distributions on $G$ and $H$. For distributions $D_g$ on $G$ and $D_h$ on $H$, define their product $D_g\cdot D_h$ on $G\times H$ by

$$
(D_g\cdot D_h)((x,y))=D_g(x)D_h(y).
$$

Define $\mathcal{S}_G\cdot\mathcal{S}_H$ to be the set of all such products. **Generalized Graham conjecture for sets of distributions.** For all graphs $G$ and $H$ and all sets of distributions $\mathcal{S}_G$ and $\mathcal{S}_H$, 

$$
\pi(G\times H,\mathcal{S}_G\cdot\mathcal{S}_H)\leq \pi(G,\mathcal{S}_G)\pi(H,\mathcal{S}_H).
$$

This conjecture generalizes Graham's pebbling conjecture and implies the corresponding conjecture for individual distributions and several pebbling-number specializations.
319086 A family of finite graphs is considered, and $c_1(\mathcal F)$ denotes the supremum of $c_1(G)$ over graphs $G$ in $\mathcal F$, where $c_1(G)$ is the multi-commodity max-flow/min-cut gap for $G$. A graph family **forbids a minor** if there is a graph that is not a minor of any member of the family.

**Gupta–Newman–Rabinovich–Sinclair conjecture.** For every family of finite graphs $\mathcal F$, one has

$$
c_1(\mathcal F)<\infty
$$

if and only if $\mathcal F$ forbids some minor.

This conjecture characterizes precisely the graph families with uniformly bounded multi-commodity max-flow/min-cut gap, equivalently bounded distortion into distributions over trees. The supplied text does not state whether it has been resolved.
316755 Let $\bar{\mathcal K}_k$ be the class of all graphs that are not $k$-colourable. A class of graphs is **multiplicative** if it is closed under categorical products.

**Hedetniemi's multiplicativity conjecture.** For every positive integer $k$, the class $\bar{\mathcal K}_k$ is multiplicative.

316898 Let $G=(V,E)$ be a digraph. An independent set $A\subset V$ is a **quasi-kernel** if every vertex of $G$ can be reached from some vertex of $A$ by a directed path of length at most $2$; dually, $A$ is a **quasi-sink** if every vertex can reach some vertex of $A$ by a directed path of length at most $2$. For $W\subset V$, write $G[W]$ for the subgraph induced by $W$.

**Erdős–Soukup's conjecture.** Every digraph $G=(V,E)$ has a partition $(V_0,V_1)$ such that $G[V_0]$ has a quasi-kernel and $G[V_1]$ has a quasi-sink.

The finite analogue, that every finite digraph has a quasi-kernel, is due to Chvátal and Lovász, whereas the direct assertion fails for infinite digraphs, even for tournaments. The conjecture asks whether a simultaneous quasi-kernel/quasi-sink partition always exists.

The claim proposes that every simple eigenspace is reconstructible up to a permutation of coordinates. 378496 Consider the symmetric Potts channel $M$ on $q\geq 3$ symbols, with $b$-ary tree branching factor $b$ and second eigenvalue $\lambda_2(M)$. **Potts-model below-bound conjecture.** There exist $b$ and $\delta$ such that reconstruction is solvable on the $b$-ary tree, while

$$
b\lambda_2^2(M)<1.
$$

If true, this would show that the Kesten–Stigum bound is not the reconstruction threshold for these Potts models, and that census-only algorithms are inferior to optimal reconstruction.

321265 Let $r$, $s$, $t$, $k$ be positive integers. A **$t$-colouring** assigns one of $t$ colours to every edge of a complete $r$-uniform hypergraph, and an **$s$-coloured matching of size $k$** is a matching of $k$ edges whose edges use at most $s$ colours. **Gyárfás's conjecture.** For any $t$-colouring of the complete $r$-uniform hypergraph on

$$

vertices, there exists an $s$-coloured matching of size $k$.
The conjecture generalizes the known graph case to complete uniform hypergraphs; the paper proves the first nontrivial case for $3$-uniform hypergraphs and $3$ colours, supporting the conjecture.
321623 A **cubic graph** is a graph in which every vertex has degree three. A cubic graph $H$ is a **spanning minor** of a cubic graph $G$ if some subdivision of $H$ is a spanning subgraph of $G$. A **Kotzig graph** is a cubic graph with a 3-edge-coloring such that the union of any two color classes is a Hamilton circuit.

**Häggkvist–Markström conjecture.** Every $3$-connected cubic graph contains a Kotzig graph as a spanning minor.

The conjecture would provide a broad sufficient condition for the circuit double cover conjecture, because a cubic graph containing a Kotzig graph as a spanning minor has a $6$-even-subgraph double cover. The source also notes that $3$-edge-connectivity is insufficient and that cyclical $4$-edge-connectivity may be necessary.
321625 A **cubic graph** is a graph in which every vertex has degree three. A cubic graph is **3-edge-colorable** if its edges admit a proper coloring with three colors. A cubic graph $H$ is a **spanning minor** of a cubic graph $G$ if some subdivision of $H$ is a spanning subgraph of $G$. A **$6$-even-subgraph double cover** is a double cover consisting of six even subgraphs.

**Connected 3-edge-colorable spanning-minor conjecture.** If a cubic bridgeless graph contains a connected 3-edge-colorable cubic graph as a spanning minor, then $G$ has a $6$-even-subgraph double cover.

The source presents this as a strengthening of several earlier sufficient conditions, including those involving Kotzig and semi-Kotzig graphs. It is stated as a conjecture and no resolution is given in the paper.
320015 Let $N$ be a $\operatorname{GF}(q)$-representable matroid, and let $\operatorname{bw}(M)$ denote the branch width of a matroid $M$. **High-branch-width non-fragility conjecture.** There is an integer $l$, depending only on $N$ and $q$, such that, if $M$ is a $\operatorname{GF}(q)$-representable matroid with $\operatorname{bw}(M)>l$ and $N$ is a minor of $M$, then there exists an element $e\in E(M)$ for which both $M{-} e$ and $M/e$ have a minor isomorphic to $N$. 364721 Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, let $\alpha(G)$ denote its independence number, and let $K_n$ denote the complete graph on $n$ vertices. A graph $H$ is a **minor** of $G$ if it can be obtained by deleting vertices or edges and contracting edges.

**Weak Hadwiger conjecture.** Every graph $G$ with $\alpha(G)\leq 2$ contains

$$
K_{\left\lceil\frac{|V(G)|}{2}\right\rceil}
$$

as a minor.

Plummer, Stiebitz, and Toft showed that this conjecture is equivalent to Hadwiger's conjecture for graphs with independence number at most two. It remains unsolved and is presented as a possible route toward understanding, or disproving, Hadwiger's conjecture.
