11930: In this section, we determine the image of the Artin group of type F4 . This group contains
11931: in a natural way two groups isomorphic to AB3 . We will therefore use some results from the
11932: type B section. The F4 -graphs are graphs with two parameters since there are two conjugacy
11933: classes in type F4 . This makes the uniqueness conditions proven in the section on W -graphs
11934: fail. The irreducible representations are all in low dimension. We can therefore compute easily
11935: the bilinear forms for the self-dual representations and it is not necessary to use the conjecture
11936: on W -graphs associated to self-dual representations. It is unclear whether the conjecture is true
11937: in type F4 because of the lack of rigidity when there are two parameters. The field extensions
11938: involved in type F4 are quite complicated, we have to distinguish 15 cases which are described in
11939: the Appendix. Determining the image inside each representation uses arguments similar to the
11940: other cases. The main difference comes from the fact that AF4 is not the normal closure of AB3
11941: in AF4 , we use the fact that there are two copies of AB3 inside AF4 for the inductive arguments.
11942: The image inside the full Iwahori-Hecke algebra is also slightly more complicated because AF4
11943: is not perfect. This requires some additional computations for the proof of Theorem 9.1.
