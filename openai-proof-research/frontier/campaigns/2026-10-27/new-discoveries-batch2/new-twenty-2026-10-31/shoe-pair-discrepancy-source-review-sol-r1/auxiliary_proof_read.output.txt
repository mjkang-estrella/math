431:                      • It is true trivially for n = 0 and n = 1, and, by Section 5.2, for n = 2.
432:                      • By broad analogy, many symmetric payoff functions achieve their extreme values at points
433:                        with lots of symmetry. Indeed, Theorem 1 asserts that, for each n, D(p) achieves its
434:                        minimum value, 0, at the uniform distribution, corresponding to the maximum conceivable
435:                        symmetry in p, while the family in (11) corresponds to breaking symmetry somewhat,
436:                        but as little as possible.
437:                      • The one-parameter family (11) arises in other extremal problems which share the feature

440:                        inequality is sharp’; see Cover and Thomas [2, Equation (2.135), page 40].
