328763 Let $G$ be a plane graph. A proper colouring of $G$ assigns integers to its vertices so that adjacent vertices receive different integers. **Fabrici–Gö̈ring's conjecture.** Every plane graph has a proper colouring using the numbers $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$ such that every face contains a unique vertex coloured with the maximal colour appearing on that face. This conjecture would strengthen the Four Colour Theorem; the cited source proposed it, while the paper proves the analogous statement with colours $1,\ldots,5$.
328766 Let $S_n$ be the polytope of symmetric doubly-stochastic matrices, and let $h^*(S_n)$ denote its Ehrhart $h^*$-polynomial. A regular unimodular triangulation of $S_n$ should exist, and consequently $h^*(S_n)$ should be unimodal when $n$ is even. This would connect the combinatorial structure of $S_n$ with the unimodality of its Ehrhart series; the source gives this as a conjectural consequence of proving squarefreeness of the relevant initial terms, with no resolution stated.
328809 Let $\mathscr{V}^\lambda$ be the hyperbolic Poisson–Voronoi graph. For $r>0$, consider a geodesic in $\mathscr{V}^\lambda$ connecting the Voronoi cells containing $-r$ and $r$, and let $D_r$ be the graph distance from this geodesic to the cell containing $0$. **Geodesic-deviation conjecture.** The family of random variables $(D_r)_{r>0}$ is tight. This asks whether long graph geodesics between opposite points of the hyperbolic plane remain within a tight random distance of the cell containing the origin; it is presented as an open qualitative question about how geodesics in the tessellation compare with hyperbolic geodesics.
328829 Let $H$ be an $r$-partite hypergraph with matching number $
u(H)=k$. A set is **contained in a side or in an edge** if it is a subset of one of the sides or of one edge, respectively. **Generalized structured-cover conjecture.** There exist sets $S_1,\ldots,S_k$, each of size at most $r-1$ and contained in a side or in an edge, such that

$$
\bigcup_{i\leqslant k}S_i
$$

is a cover of $H$.

This would generalize the preceding structured-cover assertion and imply a strengthened form of Ryser's conjecture. The paper presents it as open.
328839 A permutation class is **well-partially-ordered (wpo)** if it has no infinite antichain under pattern containment. It is **strongly algebraic** if it and all of its subclasses have algebraic generating functions.

**Strongly algebraic characterization conjecture.** A permutation class is strongly algebraic if and only if it is wpo.
The source proves that every strongly algebraic class is wpo and presents the converse as the author's conjecture; it says that little is known about strongly algebraic classes.
328840 Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a proper permutation class, and let $\operatorname{Si}_n(\mathcal{C})$ be the set of simple permutations in $\mathcal{C}$ of length $n$. Let $\mathcal{C}_n$ be the set of permutations in $\mathcal{C}$ of length $n$.

**Atkinson's conjecture.**

$$
\frac{|\operatorname{Si}_n(\mathcal{C})|}{|\mathcal{C}_n|}\longrightarrow 0\qquad\text{as }n\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

The source attributes this conjecture to Atkinson and gives no resolution.
328841 Let $M$ be a $0/\mathord{\pm}1$ matrix, and let $\operatorname{Grid}(M)$ be its monotone grid class, consisting of permutations whose entries in each nonzero cell are monotone with the sign prescribed by $M$.

**Finite-basis conjecture for monotone grid classes.** Every monotone grid class has a finite basis.
The source notes that only limited cases are known, including skew-merged permutations and the monotone grid class of the $2\times2$ all-one matrix.
328852 Let $G_1$ and $G_2$ be connected graphs of the same genus $g$, and let $\Sigma_g$ be the genus-$g$ surface. Suppose that $G_1$ and $G_2$ have embeddings into $\Sigma_g$ such that their corresponding dual graphs are isomorphic as multigraphs.

**Higher-genus dual conjecture.** The quadratic forms over $\mathbb{Z}$ represented by the combinatorial Laplacians of $G_1$ and $G_2$ are equivalent.

This extends the proposed planar statement to embeddings in minimal-genus surfaces. The source presents it as an extension of the planar conjecture and gives no evidence of a resolution.
328925 A labelled poset $P$ has a $P$-partition enumerator $F_P({\mathbf{x}})$, which is always quasisymmetric; a labelling is **column-strict** when it has the column-strict property used for labelled posets, and a **skew Ferrers poset** is the poset associated with a skew Ferrers diagram $\lambda/\mu$. **Stanley's conjecture.** The function $F_P({\mathbf{x}})$ is symmetric, and not merely quasisymmetric, if and only if $P$ is a column-strict labelling of some skew Ferrers poset $\lambda/\mu$. This conjecture asks for a characterization of the labelled posets whose $P$-partition enumerators are symmetric; it is attributed to Stanley's thesis and is presented in the source as an open question.
328936 Let $d_1,d_2\in\mathbb{Z}^+$ and let $m$ satisfy the Grimm–Ballew procedure's conditions; let $g_1$ and $g_2$ be different bases obtained by that procedure for the same $d_1$, $d_2$, and $m$. **Grimm–Ballew isomorphism conjecture.** Then

$$
Y(g_1,m)\cong Y(g_2,m).
$$

The conjecture is motivated by examination of reverse multiples generated by the Grimm–Ballew procedure; no proof or resolution is stated.
328955 Let $A\subset\mathbb C$ be a finite set, and write

$$
\frac{A+A}{A+A}=\left\{\frac{x}{y}:x,y\in A+A,\ y\ne 0\right\}.
$$

**Quotient-of-sumsets small-doubling conjecture.** There exist absolute constants $c$ and $c'$ such that

$$
\left|\frac{A+A}{A+A}\right|\leq c|A|^2\ \Rightarrow\ |A+A|\leq c'|A|.
$$

The conjecture asks whether near-extremality of the quotient-of-sumsets bound forces $A$ to have small sumset. The paper proves weaker bounds in this direction and notes analogous conjectures for $(A-A)/(A-A)$, $A(A+A+A+A)$, and $(A-A)(A-A)$; the conjecture itself is presented as open.
328994 Let $G$ be a group, let $N$ range over the normal subgroups of $G$, and let stability and weak stability refer to the notions for systems of relator words introduced in the paper. **Stability conjecture.** A group $G$ is stable whenever every quotient $G/N$ is weakly stable. This would clarify the relationship between stability and weak stability in permutations and could provide a way to detect stability through the weak stability of group quotients; the source presents this as an unresolved question.
329047 Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a supersimple $2-(n,4,\lambda)$ design other than $\mathbb{P}_3$, let $\infty$ be a point in $\mathcal{D}$, and suppose that the hole stabilizer $\pi_\infty(\mathcal{D})$ is primitive. A permutation group is **2-primitive** if it is primitive and a point stabilizer is primitive on its non-trivial orbit. **2-primitivity conjecture.** Then $\mathcal{L}_\infty(\mathcal{D})$ coincides with a 2-primitive subgroup $H$ of $\operatorname{Sym}(n)$, and $\pi_\infty(\mathcal{D})$ is equal to the stabilizer in $\mathcal{L}_\infty(\mathcal{D})$ of the point $\infty$. This would show that the behaviour observed in all currently known examples is general and would aid the classification of Conway groupoids.
329132 Let $a_N$ denote the number of dimer packings of the odd lattice $(2k+1) \times (2k+1)$, and let $c_k$ be the integer in the factorization below. **Factorization and squarefreeness conjecture.** For the odd lattice $(2k+1) \times (2k+1)$,

$$
a_N=2^k c_k,
$$

where $c_k$ is an odd integer. Furthermore, when $k>1$, $c_k$ is squarefree: its prime decomposition contains no repeated factors. The conjecture identifies an arithmetic pattern in the number of dimer packings on odd square lattices; the accompanying congruence pattern for $c_k$ is stated separately.
329136 Let $G$ be an undirected graph. Write $\operatorname{gn}(G)$ for its guessing number, let $V(G)$ be its vertex set, and let $\kappa_f(G)$ denote the fractional clique-cover number of $G$. **Christofides–Markström conjecture.** If $G$ is an undirected graph then

$$
\operatorname{gn}(G)=|V(G)|-\kappa_f(G).
$$

This conjecture asserts that the fractional clique-cover lower bound is always sharp. It is known for several families, including perfect graphs, odd cycles, and complements of odd cycles, but the general case remains open.
329156 Let an $n\times n$ array whose rows and columns are permutations of $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ be a Latin square. The product of the signs of its columns is its column sign; call the square column even or column odd according as this product is $1$ or $-1$. **Column-sign Latin square conjecture.** If $n$ is even, then the number of column even Latin squares of size $n$ does not equal the number of column odd Latin squares of size $n$. This conjecture is stated as equivalent to the Alon–Tarsi conjecture in the paper, but the supplied text gives no separate resolution status.
329227 Let $\mathrm{GC}_3$ be the graph complex, and let $L_k$ denote its loop graphs. The partner classes are those classes killed by the loop graphs in the spectral sequence described in the source. **Trivalent partner conjecture.** The classes killed by the loop graphs $L_k$ are linear combinations of trivalent graphs; equivalently, the corresponding classes live in degree $-3$ in $\mathrm{GC}_3$. The conjecture concerns the currently unknown partners of the loop graphs; the source reports that computationally found non-loop classes occur in degrees $-3$ and $-6$, and gives no resolution.
