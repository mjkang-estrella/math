Introduction The starting point of this work is a conjecture of Ceballos and Pons ( [ 8 , Conjecture 1] , also Conjecture 1.2 below) stating that a certain combinatorial complex on s \s -decreasing trees can be geometrically realized as a polyhedral subdivision of a polytope. The family of s \s -decreasing trees is parameterized by weak compositions s = ( s 1 , … , s n ) \s=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{n}) where s i s_{i} are nonnegative integers for i = 1 , 2 , … , n i=1,2,\dots,n . Ceballos and Pons [ 8 , 9 ] showed that for every s \s , the set of s \s -decreasing trees admits a lattice structure called the s \s -weak order. In the special case when s = ( 1 , … , 1 ) \s=(1,\ldots,1) , the set of s \s -decreasing trees is in bijection with the set of permutations of [ n ] := { 1 , … , n } [n]:=\{1,\ldots,n\} , and the s \s -weak order is the classical (right) weak order on the permutations of [ n ] [n] . In the same way that the weak order on permutations restricts to the lattice on Catalan objects introduced by Tamari in [ 44 ] (as implied by a classical bijection of Stanley [ 42 , Section 1.5] ), Ceballos and Pons show in [ 9 , Theorem 2.2] that the s \s -weak order restricts to the s \s -Tamari lattice.
