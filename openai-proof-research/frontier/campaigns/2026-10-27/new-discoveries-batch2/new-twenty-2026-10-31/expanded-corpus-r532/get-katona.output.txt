                                                                                            Abstract
                                                         Let H be a family of graphs. The generalized Turán number ex(n, Kr , H) is
                                                     the maximum number of copies of the clique Kr in any n-vertex H-free graph.
                                                     In this paper, we determine the value of ex(n, Kr , {Pk , Km }) for sufficiently large
                                                     n with an exceptional case, and characterize all corresponding extremal graphs,
                                                     which generalizes and strengthens the results of Katona and Xiao [EJC, 2024] on
                                                     ex(n, K2 , {Pk , Km }). For the exceptional case, we obtain a tight upper bound for
                                                     ex(n, Kr , {Pk , Km }) that confirms a conjecture on ex(n, K2 , {Pk , Km }) posed by
                                                     Katona and Xiao.
                                                     Keywords: Generalized Turán number, extremal graph, path.


                                           1        Introduction
                                              Let H be a family of graphs. A graph is called H-free if it does not contain any
                                           member of H as a subgraph. The Turán number of H, denoted by ex(n, H), is the
                                           maximum number of edges in any n-vertex H-free graph. If we ask the maximum
                                           under the condition that the graphs are connected, then denote it by excon (n, H). In
                                           1941, Turán [12] proved that the unique extremal graph of ex(n, Kk+1 ) is the balanced
                                           complete k-partite graph on n vertices, denoted by T (n, k). Since then, the Turán
                                           problem has attracted a lot of attention and some related problems have been studied
                                           widely.

                                           respectively. For two disjoint graphs G and H, let G ∪ H denote the disjoint union of
                                           G and H and kG denote the disjoint union of k copies of G. Denote by G ∨ H the
                                           graph obtained from G ∪ H by adding edges between all vertices of G and all vertices

    Recently, Katona and Xiao [7] considered the Turán problem when a path and a
clique are forbidden at the same time, i.e., excon (n, {Pk , Km }) and ex(n, {Pk , Km }).
They obtained the following two results, where t(n, k) is the size of T (n, k).

Theorem 1.3. (Katona and Xiao [7]) For k > m and sufficiently large n,

                      excon (n, {Pk , Km }) = δk · n + t (δk , m − 2) − δk2 .

Moreover, T (δk , m − 2) ∨ In−δk is one of the extremal graphs.

Theorem 1.4. (Katona and Xiao [7]) For k > 2m − 1 and sufficiently large n,

                       ex(n, {Pk , Km }) = δk · n + t (δk , m − 2) − δk2 ,

Moreover, T (δk , m − 2) ∨ In−δk is one of the extremal graphs.

    Inspired by these results, Liu and Kang [10] considered a more general problem.
For any graph H, they determined the exact value of excon (n, {Pk , H}) and the value
of ex(n, {Pk , H}) up to a constant term.
    For a graph T and a family H, the generalized Turán number, denoted by ex(n, T, H),
is the maximum number of copies of T in an n-vertex H-free graph. Obviously, when
T = K2 , ex(n, T, H) = ex(n, H). And excon (n, T, H) is defined similar to excon (n, H).
This concept was first defined by Alon and Shikhelman [1] and has been a very active
problem in the last decade. For the path Pk , its generalized Turán number is also
studied widely. Let Nr (G) denote the number of copies of Kr in G. In 2017, Luo [9]
obtained the generalized version of Theorem 1.2.
Theorem 1.6. (Luo [9]) For n ≥ k ≥ 4,

          excon (n, Kr , Pk ) = max{Nr (K1 ∨ (Kk−3 ∪ In−k+2 )), Nr (Hn (k, k))}.

Moreover, the extremal graph is either K1 ∨ (Kk−3 ∪ In−k+2 ) or Hn (k, k).
