**Main match:** Hafidh and Baskoro's paper "On the Locating Chromatic Number of Trees" studies the locating chromatic number of palms, which are subdivided stars. Its abstract reports that the authors establish a Θ(n^{1/k}) bound for the equal-leg case, a sharper asymptotic for three legs, and an exact formula for olive graphs. They prove χ_L(S_n(k)) = Θ(n^{1/k}); χ_L(S_n(3)) = (1 + o(1))∛(4n); and χ_L(O_n) = ⌈log_3(n/4)⌉ + 3. The arXiv listing is https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.05775. The ar5iv version gives the same equal-leg result, stating it for the regular palm S_n(k,k,…,k) in its final section (index 9-13).
