## Headline: one candidate, 332037 (Smyth, arXiv:1512.04084, Conjecture 1.5), refuted by a finite certificate

Smyth conjectured that for all A ≥ B ≥ 0 and a ≥ b ≥ 0 there is an injection γ : U^A_b × U^B_a ↪ U^A_a × U^B_b with λ1μ1 ⊵ γ(λ1, μ1), where ⊵ is dominance of the non-increasing rearrangements. This is false at **(A, a, B, b) = (3, 20, 2, 19)**.

Let D be the dominance down-set generated by these 8 partitions of 39:

    (13,12,8,4,2) (13,13,7,3,3) (12,12,8,6,1) (14,12,6,4,3)
    (13,11,7,7,1) (14,11,6,6,2) (15,11,5,5,3) (16,10,5,4,4)

* D contains the rearranged concatenations of **1137** elements of U³₁₉ × U²₂₀.
* D contains those of only **1134** elements of U³₂₀ × U²₁₉.

Dominance is transitive, so any dominance-decreasing γ maps the first set into the second, and therefore cannot be injective.
