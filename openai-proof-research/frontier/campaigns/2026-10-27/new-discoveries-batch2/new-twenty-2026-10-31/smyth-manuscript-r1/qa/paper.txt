1. Source statement and conventions

   Throughout, N denotes the non-negative integers. Put [m] = {1,...,m}, with [0] empty. Smyth’s set U m
consists of maps from [m] to N; these are ordered compositions with m non-negative parts, so zero parts are
allowed. Write |λ| for the sum of the coordinates and U nm for the maps of weight n. The empty map has weight
zero. A partition is a non-increasing sequence of non-negative integers with ﬁnite support. Finite partitions are
padded with zeros when necessary.
   Write λ_p for the non-increasing rearrangement of λ. For partitions, mu dominates λ, written mu           λ
(equivalently λ mu), when their weights agree and each initial sum of mu is at least the corresponding initial
sum of λ. Thus
                                           j             j
µ dominates λ iff | µ | = | λ | and Σ mu(i) ≥ Σ λ (i) ( j ≥ 1).
                                         i=1            i=1

  For compositions, dominance means dominance of their rearrangements. Concatenation λ mu places the A
coordinates of λ ﬁrst and the B coordinates of mu second; its length is A+B and its weight is a+b when the
weights are a and b. Permuting coordinates preserves this dominance relation.
Conjecture 1.5 [1] (verbatim)

   For all integer A, a, B, b with A ≥ B ≥ 0 and a ≥ b ≥ 0 there is an injection
γ : U bA × U aB → U aA × U bB

   such that for all (λ 1 , µ 1 ) ∈ U bA × U aB , (λ 2 , µ 2 ) = γ ((λ 1 , µ 1 )) ∈ U aA × U bB satisﬁes λ 1 µ 1   λ 2 µ 2.
   The source’s dominance symbol means that the domain concatenation dominates its image. The injection
arrow above is printed as an ordinary arrow; its injectivity is asserted in the quoted sentence. The original TeX,
PDF, and exact MathDB raw line are included in the dossier.

2. An explicit down-set obstruction

   Theorem. For (A,a,B,b) = (3,20,2,19), the injection in Conjecture 1.5 does not exist. Consequently its
universally quantiﬁed assertion is false.
   Let G consist of the following eight non-increasing partitions, each of weight 39:
     (13,12,8,4,2)     (13,13,7,3,3)
     (12,12,8,6,1)     (14,12,6,4,3)
     (13,11,7,7,1)     (14,11,6,6,2)
     (15,11,5,5,3)     (16,10,5,4,4).

   Let D be the down-set of partitions of 39 dominated by at least one member of G. This deﬁnition allows
partitions of arbitrary ﬁnite support. Only its intersection with partitions having at most ﬁve parts is used in
the counts below. Set

   For a pair x = (λ,mu), put r(x) = (λ mu)_p, and deﬁne S = {x in X : r(x) in D} and T = {y in Y : r(y) in D}.
Literal enumeration gives
|X| = 210 ⋅ 21 = 4410, |Y | = 231 ⋅ 20 = 4620,

|S| = 1137, |T | = 1134.

    These are counts of ordered pairs, including all zero coordinates, not counts of partitions. One can
reproduce them without an optimization solver: enumerate the ﬁrst two coordinates of each three-part
composition, force the third by the weight, enumerate the ﬁrst coordinate of each two-part composition, force
its second, then sort all ﬁve entries and compare their initial sums against every generator. See Section 3.
Proof of the theorem

   Assume that an injection gamma as conjectured exists at these parameters. Take x in S. By deﬁnition some
g in G dominates r(x). The conjecture says that r(x) dominates r(gamma(x)). Dominance is transitive: total-
sum equalities compose, and each pair of corresponding initial-sum inequalities composes. Hence g dominates
r(gamma(x)), so gamma(x) is in T. Therefore gamma restricts to an injection from S to T. Finite injectivity
would give |S| at most |T|, contradicting 1137 > 1134. This proves the parameter refutation and thus the
negation of the universal conjecture.
   No maximum-matching assertion is needed for this proof. Nor is it necessary to enumerate the entire down-
set among partitions of unrestricted length: all domain and image concatenations here have exactly ﬁve
coordinates.

4. Supporting computations and true special cases

   The fresh audit obtains a maximum admissible matching of 4407 out of 4410 domain elements at the
counterexample parameters. The frozen review reports the same value from element-level Hopcroft-Karp and a
type-level maximum ﬂow; this dossier also recomputes those values. Reversing the dominance orientation
gives maximum matching 4050. This is a diagnostic against a possible reading error, not the conjecture being
refuted.
   The ﬂow groups elements by their sorted concatenation and retains their multiplicities. A type network may
use directed unit balancing transfers: move one unit from a coordinate at least two larger than another, then
sort again. Such transfers generate dominance among ﬁxed-length integer partitions. With capacities equal to
type multiplicities at the source and sink and large intermediate capacities, integral ﬂow equals admissible
matching. The audit checks the multiplicity formula against literal ordered enumeration at the counterexample
parameters.
   Supporting computation ﬁnds no failures for A,B,a,b at most 10 with A at least B, a at least b, and a+b at
most 16. The nontrivial region A > B at least 2 and a > b is checked by exact ﬂow; the remaining cases follow
from the constructions below or empty-domain cases. For (A,a,B,b) = (3,b+1,2,b), failures occur at every
tested b from 19 through 43. This is a ﬁnite observation, not an inﬁnite-family theorem, and neither sweep
contributes separate credit.
Explicit constructions

  When A = B, send (λ,mu) to (mu,λ). This is injective, and the concatenations have identical rearrangements.
When a = b, use the identity. When B = 1, write mu = (a), put delta = a-b, and send (λ,(a)) to (λ+delta e_1,(b)).
The ﬁrst coordinate recovers λ by subtracting delta; all other coordinates are unchanged, so the map is injective
and has the required target weights.
   To check dominance for this last map, write t = λ(1), so 0 at most t at most b at most a. The two-coordinate
vector (t,a) has the same sum as (t+delta,b), and each target coordinate is at most a. It therefore majorizes that
target pair. Appending the unchanged coordinates preserves majorization: the sum of the largest j entries of an
appended vector is the maximum, over the number chosen from each block, of the corresponding largest-entry
sums in those blocks. Thus the required inequalities persist.
   When B = 0, the domain is empty unless a = 0, which also forces b = 0; then both sides consist of their zero
maps. This covers A = 0 as well. These constructions prove special cases for all their allowed parameters; they
do not suggest that an untested range has been exhausted.

6. Motivation, retention and related work

  Smyth’s motivation is a combinatorial route to total nonnegativity. For a non-negative TN_2 sequence p, let
p_λ be the product of its entries at the coordinates of λ. Lemma 2.1(iii) of [1] says that λ dominating mu
implies p_λ at most p_mu. Theorem 3.1 explains how the proposed injection would therefore give
( p A )b ( p B )a ≤ ( p A )a ( p B )b ,

   where (pˆi)_j denotes the coefﬁcient of xˆj in p(x)ˆi. Nonnegative unmatched target terms make summing
legitimate. This would imply the k = 2 case of the source’s Theorem 1.4 about its coefﬁcient matrix. That
theorem is proved independently in the source. Theorem 3.1’s intermediate reversed product inequality is a
typographical error: the lemma and the ﬁnal comparison give the stated orientation. The submitted Order
manuscript [2] corrects that sign and states the implication as Theorem 7, following Conjecture 6.
   A down-set indicator weights tuples more freely than the product weights p_λ available from a TN_2
sequence. Hence the obstruction refutes the proposed injection, not the product inequality or Smyth’s
probabilistic characterization. It closes a particular proposed combinatorial route; it does not undermine an
established theorem.
   The cached author publications page links [2] as the Order paper. That submitted manuscript, created on 27
July 2016, retains the conjecture verbatim as Conjecture 6, with unchanged quantiﬁers and orientation. The
review matches its abstract, keywords and reference list with the published record [3]. This is positive retention
evidence. The post-refereeing printed body is UNREAD; neither this draft nor its dossier claims direct
veriﬁcation of retention in that body. Root’s ruling accepts this as a qualiﬁed limitation for proceeding, not a
novelty proof.
   The supplied zbMATH review describes the main characterization and its extension but does not discuss
this conjecture. Readable citing material examined in the review cites Smyth generically or concerns total
positivity; some other citing bodies were inaccessible or not read. Cached registry path screens and bounded
searches found no resolution. Such negative search results do not prove priority, and no priority claim is made
here. The root must decide acceptance; this lane has zero accepted credit.
