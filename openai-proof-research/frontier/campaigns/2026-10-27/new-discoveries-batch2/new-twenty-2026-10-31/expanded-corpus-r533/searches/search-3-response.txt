- **The series:** The theory paper by Cushing, Kamtue, Liu, Münch, Peyerimhoff and Snodgrass introduces a curvature flow on weighted graphs built on Bakry-Émery calculus, by adapting this flow to preserve the Markovian property, its limits turn out to be curvature sharp. It is Bakry-Émery curvature sharpness and curvature flow in finite weighted graphs: theory, published in manuscripta mathematica in 2025.
- **Implementation paper:** The companion paper is described as examples (random graphs, paths, cycles, complete graphs, wedge sums and Cartesian products of complete graphs, and hypercubes) and exhibit various properties of this flow. It focuses on asymptotic stability and instability of curvature flow equilibria. It appeared in Axioms in 2023.
- **Simple random walk on complete graphs:** The search results confirm that the simple random walk is treated as a non-degenerate example, with transition probabilities 1/d_x for all pairs x ∼ y of adjacent vertices.
