Let $F$ be a bipartite graph such that

$$
\operatorname{ex}(n,F)=O(n^{2-\alpha})
$$

for some $0<\alpha<1$. Let $G$ be an $n$-vertex graph, and write $v(F)$ and $e(F)$ for the numbers of vertices and edges of $F$. **Simonovits's supersaturation conjecture.** There exist positive constants $c$ and $c'$ such that if $e(G)\geq cn^{2-\alpha}$, then $G$ contains at least

$$
c'\frac{e(G)^{e(F)}}{n^{2e(F)-v(F)}}
$$

copies of $F$.

The bound has the order of magnitude predicted by a random graph with $e(G)$ edges. Simonovits proved the conjecture for even cycles, and Erdős and Simonovits proved it for paths and several other graphs, but the general bipartite case remains open.

Let $\mathbb{I}_n$ denote the $n$-dimensional hypercube graph, and let $\mathcal{VR}(\mathbb{I}_n;r)$ be its Vietoris–Rips complex at scale $r$. Let the **collapsibility number** of a simplicial complex mean the parameter used in the paper to measure its collapsibility. **Collapsibility-number conjecture.** For $n\geq r+1$, the collapsibility number of $\mathcal{VR}(\mathbb{I}_n;r)$ is

$$
2^r.
$$

The value is established in the paper for $r\in\{2,3\}$, and the boundary cases $r=1$ and $r=n-1$ are described explicitly; the conjecture proposes the same formula throughout the stated range.

For $n\ge 2$, let $P^s$ be the non-lazy simple random walk on the unmixed complete graph $K_{n+1}$, and let $DF(P^s)$ be the corresponding linearized curvature-flow matrix.

**Complete-graph spectral conjecture.** The simple random walk $P^s$ is asymptotically stable, and the eigenvalues of $DF(P^s)$ are

$$
-\frac{n-1}{n}\quad\text{with multiplicity }{n\choose 2},\qquad
-\frac{n+3}{n^2}\quad\text{with multiplicity }n,
$$

$$
-\frac{n+3}{n}\quad\text{with multiplicity }{n\choose 2}-1.
$$

As $n\to\infty$, these eigenvalues tend respectively to $-1$, $0$ (with $-\frac{n+3}{n^2}\approx-\frac1n$), and $-1$. Numerical verification is reported for $n=4,5,6,7$, while the general assertion remains open.

Let $d\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $\mathbf{N}=(N_1,N_2,\cdots,N_d)$, where $N_1,\dots,N_d$ are positive integers. Write $\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{N}}$ for the family of arithmetic progressions in the corresponding grid, and let $\disc(\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{N}})$ denote its discrepancy. **Sharp discrepancy conjecture.**

$$
\disc(\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{N}})=\Theta_d\left(\max_{I\subseteq [d]}\left(\prod_{i\in I}N_i\right)^\frac{1}{2|I|+2}\right).
$$

The conjecture asserts that the lower bound in Theorem~ is tight up to a constant depending only on $d$, removing the sub-logarithmic factor from the proved upper bound. The paper notes that the gap remains even for certain two-dimensional grids, including $\mathbf{N}=(N,\sqrt{N}(\log N)^k)$.

Let $G$ be a **$cn$-regular tripartite digraph** with three vertex classes, each of size $n$, meaning that every vertex has indegree and outdegree $cn$. A Hamilton cycle is a directed cycle containing all $3n$ vertices. **Tripartite approximate packing conjecture.** Let $\varepsilon>0$, $c>1$, and let $n$ be sufficiently large. Then $G$ contains at least $(1-\varepsilon)cn$ edge-disjoint Hamilton cycles. This is proposed as an approximate version of the conjecture that every regular tripartite tournament has a Hamilton cycle decomposition; neither the exact decomposition conjecture nor this approximate version is proved here.

Let $H$ be a tournament whose vertex set can be partitioned into two transitive sets $L$ and $R$ such that the bipartite graph on $L$ and $R$, joining $\ell\in L$ to $r\in R$ exactly when $H$ has an edge from $r$ to $\ell$, is a forest.
