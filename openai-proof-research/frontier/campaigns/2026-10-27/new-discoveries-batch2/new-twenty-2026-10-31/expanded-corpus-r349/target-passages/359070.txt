Using these notions we have the following conjecture for countably infinite digraphs:



Conjecture 21 ([9, 8]).



In any countable infinite directed graph GGG there exists a vertex partition V(G)=A\cup BV⁡(G)=A∪BV(G)=A\cup B such that in G[A]G⁡[A]G[A] there exists a quasi-sink and in G[B]G⁡[B]G[B] there exists a quasi-kernel.





Please observe, that the cut edges between the partition classes are not used.




There are several special classes of countable infinite digraphs where the conjecture has been proved. We give here some examples. Each of the following classes contain infinite digraphs which somewhat “resemble” the finite digraphs:

(1) 



A digraph whose in-degrees are finite has a Qk.

(2) 



A digraph with a finite chromatic number has a Qk.

(3) 



If for the infinite digraph GGG there exists an n\geq 2n≥2n\geq 2 such that the complement of GGG does not contains K_{n}KnK_{n}, then GGG satisfies Conjecture 21.

(4) 



Each infinite digraph GGG whose complement has finite chromatic number, GGG satisfies Conjecture 21.

(5) 



Each infinite digraph GGG whose complement is locally finite (that is, each vertex has finite degree), GGG satisfies Conjecture 21.
