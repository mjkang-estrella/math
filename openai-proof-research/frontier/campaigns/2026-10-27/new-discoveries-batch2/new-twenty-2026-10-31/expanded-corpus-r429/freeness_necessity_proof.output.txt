In this section, we introduce the ψ-graphical multiarrangements, which are
determined by a vertex-weighted graph over Z≥0 , and give a characterization

some definitions of multiarrangements.
    A multiarrangement (A, m) is a pair of an arrangement A with a map
m : A → Z≥0 , which is called multiplicity, and its defining polynomial is
