 Title: A Characterization of Claw-Free Graphs using Zero Forcing Invariants 
 Authors: Randy Davila , Houston Schuerger , Ben Small View a PDF of the paper titled A Characterization of Claw-Free Graphs using Zero Forcing Invariants, by Randy Davila and 2 other authors 
 View PDF 
 HTML (experimental) 



 
 Abstract: We prove that the \emph{standard zero forcing number} $Z(G)$ and the \emph{positive semidefinite zero forcing number} $Z_+(G)$ are equal for all claw-free graphs $G$. This result resolves a conjecture proposed by the computer program \emph{TxGraffiti} and highlights a connection between these graph invariants in claw-free structures. As a corollary, we show that a graph $G$ is claw-free if and only if every induced subgraph $H \subseteq G$ satisfies $Z(H) = Z_+(H)$.
