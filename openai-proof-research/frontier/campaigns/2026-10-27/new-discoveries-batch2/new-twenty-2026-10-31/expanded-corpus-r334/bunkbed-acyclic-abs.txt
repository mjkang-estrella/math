 Title: The acyclic directed bunkbed conjecture is false

 View PDF 
 HTML (experimental) 



 
 Abstract: We construct a simple acyclic directed graph for which the Bunkbed Conjecture is false, thereby resolving conjectures posed by Leander and by Hollom.
