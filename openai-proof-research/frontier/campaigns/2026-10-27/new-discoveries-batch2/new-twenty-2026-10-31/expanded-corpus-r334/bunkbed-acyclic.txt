 Abstract. 
 
 We construct a simple acyclic directed graph for which the Bunkbed Conjecture is false, thereby resolving conjectures posed by Leander and by Hollom. 
 
 
 
 1. Introduction 

 
 The Bunkbed Conjecture, introduced by Kasteleyn in 1985 [ 9 , Remark 5] , postulates a certain monotonicity of connectivity probabilities in Bernoulli bond percolation on an undirected graph. Being an intuitive, but surprisingly hard to prove, statement, a lot of effort has been put in proving its correctness. Despite that, the original conjecture turned out to be false, as shown recently by Gladkov, Pak and Zimin [ 2 ] who built on the work of Hollom disproving the hypergraph variant [ 4 ] . 
 
 
 Since the main conjecture does not hold, the problem of finding broad families of graphs, or other general models, where it does hold is of great interest.
One of the remaining promising general models considered is the setup of acyclic directed graphs put forward by Leander [ 5 ] , and highlighted by Hollom [ 4 ] . Nevertheless, in this work we prove that the Bunkbed Conjecture does not hold in this case either. 
 
 
 Let G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) be a (directed or undirected) graph, and let ( V − , E − ) (V^{-},E^{-}) and ( V + , E + ) (V^{+},E^{+}) be two copies of G G . For v ∈ V v\in V let v − v^{-} , v + v^{+} denote the copies of v v in V − V^{-} and V + V^{+} , respectively. The bunkbed graph G ¯ = ( V ¯ , E ¯ ) \overline{G}=(\overline{V},\overline{E}) of G G is the graph defined by V ¯ = V − ⊔ V + \overline{V}=V^{-}\sqcup V^{+} and E ¯ = E − ⊔ E + ⊔ E ~ \overline{E}=E^{-}\sqcup E^{+}\sqcup\widetilde{E} , where E ~ \widetilde{E} consists of bidirected edges ( v − , v + ) (v^{-},v^{+}) for all v ∈ V v\in V . We will call the edges in E ~ \widetilde{E} vertical , and the edges in E − ⊔ E + E^{-}\sqcup E^{+} horizontal . 
 
 
 The bunkbed percolation model is a probabilistic measure on the subgraphs of the bunkbed graph. The most commonly chosen measure is the p p -Bernoulli bond percolation ℙ p \mathbb{P}_{p} of the edges of the bunkbed graph, i.e., a random subgraph of the edges of the bunkbed graph, in which every edge is retained with probability p p , or deleted with probability 1 − p 1-p , independently of other edges. 
 
 
 The original Bunkbed Conjecture states the following: 
 
 
 Conjecture 1.1 . 
 
 For every undirected graph G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) and every u u , v ∈ V v\in V 
 

 
 
 ℙ p ​ ( u − ↔ v − ) ≥ ℙ p ​ ( u − ↔ v + ) , \mathbb{P}_{p}(u^{-}\leftrightarrow v^{-})\geq\mathbb{P}_{p}(u^{-}\leftrightarrow v^{+}), 
 
 
 where x ↔ y x\leftrightarrow y is the event that in the percolated bunkbed graph there is a path connecting vertices x x and y y . 
 
 
 
 This conjecture was disproved by Gladkov, Pak and Zimin [ 2 ] . Their counterexample is ingeniously based on a graph found by Hollom [ 4 ] , which in turn served as a counterexample to the Hypergraph Bunkbed Conjecture. The apparent intuition behind the conjecture is that vertices u − u^{-} and v − v^{-} are closer to each other than u − u^{-} and v + v^{+} for any conceivable notion of closeness , and so the chance of an open path between u − u^{-} and v − v^{-} should be at least as large as the chance of open path between u − u^{-} and v + v^{+} . There are certain instances where the conjecture does hold, notably on complete graphs [ 1 , 10 ] , for adjacent vertices in edge-transitive graphs [ 8 ] , for vertices u u , v v with the same neighbourhood [ 8 ] ; and also for the random cluster model with parameter q = 2 q=2 [ 3 ] ; for more thorough discussion see [ 2 ] . 
 
 
 Leander considered the Bunkbed Conjecture for directed graphs in [ 5 ] , in which case one compares the probability of existence of directed paths in a Bernoulli percolated bunkbed graph. This directed case was disproved by Hollom, who found a directed multigraph counterexample. Hollom conjectured that one can avoid the use of multiple edges. Further, Leander considered the conjecture for acyclic directed graphs. Interestingly, Hollom’s counterexample contains a lot of directed cycles and as such does not disprove Leander’s acyclic variant. The acyclic graphs seem promising to satisfy the conjecture as they might be amenable to some inductive argument. Despite that, we find a simple acyclic directed graph not satisfying the conjecture, thereby resolving the conjectures by Leander and by Hollom. 
 
 
 Theorem 1.2 . 
 
 There is a simple acyclic directed graph with distinguished vertices u u , v v such that 
 

 
 
 ℙ 1 / 2 ​ ( u − → v − ) < ℙ 1 / 2 ​ ( u − → v + ) . \mathbb{P}_{1/2}(u^{-}\rightarrow v^{-})<\mathbb{P}_{1/2}(u^{-}\rightarrow v^{+}). 
 
 
 
 
 
 Our counterexample is the graph G 2 k G_{2}^{k} depicted in Figure   3 . It is a blow-up of the graph G 1 G_{1} depicted in Figure   3 , with vertices 2 2 , 5 5 , 8 8 substituted with gadgets on k + 2 k+2 vertices. 
 
 
 The proof of Theorem   1.2 has two steps. Firstly, in Section   3 we prove that bond percolation on G 1 ¯ \overline{G_{1}} conditioned to have vertical edges ( 2 − , 2 + ) , ( 5 − , 5 + ) , ( 8 − , 8 + ) (2^{-},2^{+}),(5^{-},5^{+}),(8^{-},8^{+}) does not satisfy the Bunkbed Conjecture. Then, in Section   4 , we show that in unconditioned bond percolation on G 2 k ¯ \overline{G_{2}^{k}} the gadgets behave with high probability as if they were vertical edges at 2 2 , 5 5 , 8 8 in G 1 ¯ \overline{G_{1}} .
