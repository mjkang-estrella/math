14:                                                      Determining the optimal fidelity for the transmission of quantum information over noisy quan-
15:                                                  tum channels is one of the central problems in quantum information theory. Recently, [Berta-
16:                                                  Borderi-Fawzi-Scholz, Mathematical Programming, 2021] introduced an asymptotically converg-
17:                                                  ing semidefinite programming hierarchy of outer bounds for this quantity. However, the size of
18:                                                  the semidefinite programs (SDPs) grows exponentially with respect to the level of the hierarchy,
19:                                                  thus making their computation unscalable. In this work, by exploiting the symmetries in the
20:                                                  SDP, we show that, for a fixed output dimension of the quantum channel, we can compute the
21:                                                  SDP in time polynomial with respect to the level of the hierarchy and input dimension. As a
22:                                                  direct consequence of our result, the optimal fidelity can be approximated with an accuracy of ϵ
23:                                                  in poly(1/ϵ, input dimension) time.

138: Related work Algorithmic aspects of optimal channel coding have been extensively studied in
139: various settings. In classical channels, the problem was explored in [BF17] for point-to-point channels,
140: and a generalization was investigated in [BFGG22]. For entanglement-assisted classical channels,
141: the research can be found in [BFS16], and for the multiple-access channel, the study is investigated
142: in [FF23b], and for broadcast channels, it is discussed in [FF23a]. In terms of quantum channels, the
143: problem has been examined in [LM15, BBFS21, KDWW21, HSW23] for general quantum channels
144: and in [FSS19] for classical-quantum channels. In addition, in recent work [HSW23], authors exploited
145: the unitary covariance symmetry of the identity channel to reduce the complexity of the SDPn (N , M )
146: when n = 2 compared with the original form in [BBFS21].
147:     Exploiting symmetries to simplify convex programs, especially semidefinite programs, has been done
148: for various problems and applications [LM11, Chapter 9]. This approach has been seen in other surveys
149: 
150:

152: 
153: as well [Val09, DK10]. Specifically, it has been applied to the mutually unbiased bases problem [GP21]
154: and the classical (quantum) capacity of quantum channels in quantum information theory [FST22],
155: quantum separability problem [DPS04]. In coding theory, this method has been used to provide upper
156: bounds for nonbinary codes [Sch05, GST06, Lau07, LPS17, Pol19a]. Furthermore, this approach has
157: also proven beneficial in various other fields and problems, such as queueing theory [BLS21, Pol22],
158: crossing numbers for graphs [DKPS07, BP22], truss topology optimization [BdKPS09], and polynomial
159: optimization [GP04, RTAL13, RSST18]. Our approach in this paper is to exploit symmetries under
160: the symmetric group action, inspired by some prior works in [LPS17, GP21, FST22].
