329314 Let $G(X^{(n)})$ be the scale-free Gilbert graph on the point set $X^{(n)}$ in the torus $\mathbb{T}_n$, let $\mathsf{e}_1=(1,0,\ldots,0)$, and let $q(x)$ denote the closest point of $X$ to $x$. Assume $s>d$. **Linear chemical-distance conjecture.** There exists a constant $c=c(\beta,d)>0$ such that the chemical distance between $q(-n\mathsf{e}_1/4)$ and $q(n\mathsf{e}_1/4)$ is at least $cn$ with high probability as $n\to\infty$. The preceding theorem proves only a lower bound of order $n/(\log n)^{\alpha}$ for every $\alpha>0$; the conjecture asserts that this sublogarithmic loss is an artifact of the proof and that distances are instead bounded below linearly.
330222 Let $M\geq 2$. An equiangular tight frame (ETF) in $\mathbb{C}^M$ with $N$ vectors is denoted by $\operatorname{ETF}(M,N)$; the maximal possible number of vectors in the complex case is $N=M^2$. **Zauner's conjecture.** There exists an equiangular tight frame $\operatorname{ETF}(M,M^2)$ for every $M\geq 2$. These maximal ETFs are also called symmetric, informationally complete, positive operator-valued measures (SIC-POVMs) and are important in quantum Bayesianism, quantum state tomography, and quantum cryptography. Their existence is conjectured in every dimension, but the general existence question remains open.
333689 Let $G$ be a connected graph of order $n$, let $\mathcal{L}(G)$ denote the set of longest paths of $G$, and let $f(G,\mathcal{P})$ denote the distance parameter for a subset $\mathcal{P}$ of longest paths. A function $g$ is **sublinear** if $g(n)/n\to 0$ as $n\to\infty$. **Sublinear-distance conjecture.** There exists a sublinear function $g$ such that for every connected graph $G$ of order $n$ and every subset $\mathcal{P}$ of $\mathcal{L}(G)$ with $3\leq |\mathcal{P}|\leq 6$, $f(G,\mathcal{P})\leq g(n)$. The source states that this conjecture is equivalent to the common-vertex conjecture for three through six longest paths; it is not resolved in the paper.
335261 Let $m$ be a positive integer, and let $C(G)$ denote the number of cycles in a graph $G$. **Cubic-root-of-three lower-bound conjecture.** For all sufficiently large $m$, there exists a graph $G$ with $m$ edges and at least

$$
(1+o(1))^m(\sqrt[3]{3})^m
$$

cycles.

The conjecture proposes that the exponential growth rate supplied by the paper's multigraph construction is also achievable, up to a subexponential factor, by simple graphs. The source gives no resolution of this claim.
335300 Let $C$ and $D$ be different sets of nonnegative integers satisfying

$$
C\cup D=\mathbb N,
$$

$$
C\cap D=r+m\mathbb N,
$$

for integers $r\ge0$ and $m\ge2$, and let $R_C(n)$ and $R_D(n)$ denote their two-element representation functions.

**Chen–Lev strengthened conjecture.** If $R_C(n)=R_D(n)$ for every positive integer $n$, then there exists an integer $l\ge1$ such that

$$
C=H_0(1,2,4,8,\dots,2^{2l-1},2^{2l}-1,2^{2l+1}-1,2(2^{2l+1}-1),4(2^{2l+1}-1),8(2^{2l+1}-1),\dots),
$$

$$
D=H_1(1,2,4,8,\dots,2^{2l-1},2^{2l}-1,2^{2l+1}-1,2(2^{2l+1}-1),4(2^{2l+1}-1),8(2^{2l+1}-1),\dots).
$$

This is explicitly described as a stronger version of the Chen–Lev question and would identify the sets themselves, not only the parameters of their periodic intersection. The source supplies no resolution.
336936 Let $(a(n))_{n\geq1}$ be the sequence of integers used to form $A_N=\{a(1),\ldots,a(N)\}$, and let $E(A_N)$ denote its additive energy. For a real number $\alpha$, consider the sequence $(\{a(n)\alpha\})_{n\geq1}$ on the torus. **Pair-correlation obstruction conjecture.** If $E(A_N)=\Omega(N^3)$, then there is no $\alpha$ for which the pair correlations of $(\{a(n)\alpha\})_{n\geq1}$ are Poissonian. This strengthens the known result that non-Poissonian pair correlations occur for every $\alpha$ in a positive-measure subset of $[0,1]$.
338639 A **properly coloured** graph is an edge-coloured graph in which edges sharing a vertex have distinct colours. A subgraph is **rainbow** if all its edges have distinct colours. Let $K_n$ be the complete graph on $n$ vertices.

**Constant-error rainbow tree conjecture.** There is a constant $C$ such that every properly coloured $K_n$ has a rainbow copy of every tree on $n-C$ vertices.

The paper proves rainbow copies for every tree on $n-o(n)$ vertices and explains that the error term cannot be zero. The conjecture asks whether the error can be reduced to a constant.
340014 Let $G$ be an abelian group. It has the **weak acyclic matching property** if, for every pair of finite subsets $A$ and $B$ of $G$ with $\#A=\#B$ and $A\cap(A+B)=\emptyset$, there is at least one acyclic matching from $A$ to $B$.

**Weak acyclic matching conjecture.** There exists a prime $p$ such that $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ does not satisfy the weak acyclic matching property.

This is a weaker property than the acyclic matching property because the condition $A\cap(A+B)=\emptyset$ ensures that every bijection from $A$ to $B$ is a matching. The paper notes that infinitely many prime cyclic groups fail the acyclic matching property, but whether one fails the weak version remains open.
340080 Let $\mathcal{C}_{2k+1}$ denote the family consisting of the cycle $C_{2k+1}$, and let $\mathrm{sat}(\mathcal{C}_{2k+1};n)$ be its saturation number on $n$ vertices. **Odd-cycle saturation conjecture.** For all $k\ge 1$ there exists a $c_k>0$ such that

$$
\mathrm{sat}(\mathcal{C}_{2k+1};n)\le \left(\frac{1}{4}-c_k\right)n^2+o(n^2).
$$

This conjecture predicts a strict improvement over the general quadratic upper bound for saturation by every odd cycle. The source presents it as an expected stronger bound; no resolution is given.
342928 Let $n$ be the number of vertices, and let $0\le d_1\le d_2\le n-1$ be integers such that $d_1n$ and $d_2n$ are even. Write ${\mathcal G}(n,d_i)$ for the uniformly random $d_i$-regular graph. A **coupling** $(G_1,G_2)$ is a joint realization with these marginal distributions. **Monotone coupling conjecture.** Except for $(d_1,d_2)=(1,2)$ and $(d_1,d_2)=(n-3,n-2)$, there exists a coupling $(G_1,G_2)$ such that

$$
G_1\sim {\mathcal G}(n,d_1),\qquad G_2\sim {\mathcal G}(n,d_2),
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{\mathbb P}(G_1\subseteq G_2)=1-o(1).
$$

This conjecture asks for asymptotically almost-sure containment whenever the larger regular degree is at least the smaller one. The weakened sandwich results cited in the paper do not imply this assertion, even when the degree gap is large.
343024 Let $P$ be a planar poset, let $d\ge2$, and let $S_d$ denote the standard example of order $d$. The dimension of $P$ is denoted by $\dim(P)$. **The planar-poset standard-example conjecture.** For every $d\ge2$, there exists a constant $d_0$ such that if $P$ is a planar poset and $\dim(P)\ge d_0$, then $P$ contains $S_d$ as a subposet. This is a long-standing folklore conjecture about whether planar posets of sufficiently large dimension must contain arbitrarily large standard examples; its status is presented here as open.
344449 Let $d$ be an integer with $d\geq 2$. A graph is **$d$-degenerate** if every subgraph has a vertex of degree at most $d$. Let $f(H,p)$ denote the subgraph query complexity, and write $b$ for the relevant query parameter.

**Extremal graph existence conjecture.** For every integer $d\geq 2$, there exists a $d$-degenerate graph $H$ such that

$$
f(H,p)=b^{d-o(1)}.
$$

In the case $d=2$, the paper constructs such a graph, whereas the existence of such graphs for $d\geq 3$ remains open.
344879 Let $n\geq4$ be an integer. For positive integers $v_1<\cdots<v_n$, define

$$
\operatorname{ML}(v_1,\ldots,v_n)=\max_{t\in\mathbb{R}}\min_{1\leq i\leq n}\Vert tv_i\Vert.
$$

**Weak Loneliness Spectrum Conjecture.** For every integer $n\geq4$, there exists a function $f_n:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ such that, whenever $v_n>f_n(v_{n-1})$, either

$$
\operatorname{ML}(v_1,\ldots,v_n)=\frac{s}{ns+1}\quad\text{for some }s\in\mathbb{N},
$$

or

$$
\operatorname{ML}(v_1,\ldots,v_n)\geq\frac{1}{n}.
$$

This is a regime-specific refinement of the Loneliness Spectrum Conjecture, addressing sets of speeds with one speed sufficiently larger than the preceding one. The paper proves a stronger explicit bound for $n=4$ and $n=6$, but the assertion for every $n\geq4$ remains open.
347543 Let a **quintic graph** be a 5-regular graph, and let two 1-factorisations be **Kempe equivalent** if one can be obtained from the other by a sequence of Kempe switches. **Kempe-class conjecture.** Every quintic graph contains at least two Kempe equivalence classes. The paper proves the existence of infinitely many planar 5-connected quintic graphs with at least two Kempe equivalence classes, but the asserted statement for all quintic graphs remains open.
347986 Let $C_m$ and $C_n$ be cycles, and let $C_m\square C_n$ denote their Cartesian product.

**Holub–Lužar–Mihaliková–Mockovčiaková–Soták's conjecture.** There exists a constant $c$ such that for every integer $m\geq c$, there is an integer $n$ satisfying

$$
\chi'_{st}(C_m\square C_n)=6.
$$

The survey states this as an equivalent formulation of the path–cycle product conjecture. Since Cartesian products of two cycles have star chromatic index between $6$ and $7$, the assertion asks for a $6$-coloring for at least one cycle length $n$ for every sufficiently large $m$.
348150 Let $n,d$ be positive integers. Given a finite family of $nd$ absolutely continuous measures in $\mathds{R}^d$, a set of $n$ affine hyperplanes induces a chessboard coloring: each measure is divided according to the parity of the number of chosen positive half-spaces containing a point.

**Langerman's conjecture.** For every such family of measures, there exists a set of $n$ hyperplanes whose induced chessboard coloring splits every measure into two equal parts.

This conjecture removes directional restrictions imposed in earlier results on chessboard colorings by hyperplane arrangements. The source notes that the case $d=n=2$ was proved by Barba, Pilz, and Schnider, while the general statement remains unresolved.
353119 Let $G$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{F}_p$, and let $alpha\colon G^k\tomathbb{F}_p$ be an $r$-approximately symmetric multilinear form, meaning that the partition rank of $alpha-\alpha\circ\pi$ is at most $r$ for every permutation $pi\in\operatorname{Sym}_k$. A multilinear form is **symmetric** if it is invariant under all permutations of its arguments. **Tidor's conjecture.** There exists a symmetric multilinear form $sigma\colon G^k\tomathbb{F}_p$ such that

$$
\operatorname{prank}(\sigma-\alpha)leq O(r^{O(1)}),
$$

where the implicit constants may depend on $p$ and $k$. Tidor formulated this conjecture in the study of the inverse problem for the Gowers uniformity norm in low characteristic and proved it for trilinear maps; the general case remains open in the supplied source.
369196 Let $r$ be a positive integer. An $r$-edge-coloured complete graph is a complete graph whose edges receive one of $r$ colours, and the diameter of a component is measured in its monochromatic subgraph.

**Milićević's conjecture.** For every $r$, there is a constant $D(r)$ such that every $r$-edge-coloured complete graph can be covered by $r-1$ monochromatic components of diameter at most $D(r)$.

This strengthens the complete-graph case of Ryser's conjecture. The source reports that it is proved for $r\leq 4$, with bounds $D(3)\leq 4$ and $D(4)\leq 6$, while the general case remains open.
375408 Let $H$ be a **star forest**, meaning a vertex-disjoint union of stars. For a graph $G$, let $N(G,H)$ be the number of subgraphs of $G$ isomorphic to $H$, and let

$$
N(m,H)=\max\{N(G,H):e(G)=m\}.
$$

**Alon's star-forest extremal-graph conjecture.** For every integer $m>0$, or at least for all sufficiently large $m$, there exists a star forest $G_m$ with $e(G_m)=m$ such that

$$
N(m,H)=N(G_m,H).
$$

The conjecture describes the form of an extremal graph for counting a fixed star forest with a prescribed number of edges. The supplied text gives no resolution evidence, so whether the assertion holds for every $m$ or only eventually remains open.
382244 For a graph $G$, let $\chi(G)$ denote its chromatic number and let $\omega(G)$ denote its clique number. A graph is **$P$-free** if it contains no induced copy of the path $P$. **Path-free polynomial chi-boundedness conjecture.** For every integer $k$, there is a constant $C_k>0$ such that every graph $G$ satisfying

$$
\chi(G)\geq \omega(G)^{C_k}
$$

contains an induced path of length $k$. Equivalently, for every fixed path, the class of graphs excluding that path as an induced subgraph is polynomially chi-bounded. The general chi-boundedness of path-free classes is known only in limited cases; the paper notes a bound for $P_5$-free graphs, while the stated polynomial form remains open.
