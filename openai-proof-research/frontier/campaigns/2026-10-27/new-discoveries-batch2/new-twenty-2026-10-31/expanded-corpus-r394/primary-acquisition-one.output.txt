443: 3.1      Puzzles known to have no surjective strategies
444: Our richest collection of known puzzles without surjective strategies comes from
445: a theorem of Rabinovich, which models switches as a vector space over a finite
446: field.

448: faithfully on a collection of switches that behave like a vector space V over a
449: finite field Fq of characteristic p. Then the resulting puzzle has a surjective
450: strategy if and only if H is a p-group.
