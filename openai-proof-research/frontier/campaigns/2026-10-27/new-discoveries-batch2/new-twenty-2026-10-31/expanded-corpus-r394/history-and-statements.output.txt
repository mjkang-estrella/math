355596 Fix a positive integer $r$. For a metric graph $\Gamma=(G,l)$ with rational edge lengths, the $r^{th}$ Metric Divisorial Gonality problem asks whether $\operatorname{dgon}_r(\Gamma)\leq k$ for a given integer $k$. **Metric divisorial gonality conjecture.** The $r^{th}$ Metric Divisorial Gonality problem is NP-complete. Membership in NP is known when both $r$ and $k$ are fixed; extending the argument to variable $k$, for example with $k$ bounded by $r|V(G)|$, is left open.
355625 Let $\mathsf{M}$ be a loopless matroid of rank $k$ and cardinality $n$, and let $W_i$ be the number of flats of rank $i$. **Whitney-number coefficient conjectures.** The sequence $W_0,\ldots,W_k$ is unimodal, the sequence $W_0,\ldots,W_k$ is log-concave with $W_i^2\geq W_{i-1}W_{i+1}$ for $1\leq i\leq k-1$, and the normalized sequence $W_0/\binom n0,\ldots,W_k/\binom nk$ is log-concave, equivalently

$$
W_i^2\geq\frac{i+1}{i}\frac{n-i+1}{n-i}W_{i-1}W_{i+1}\qquad(1\leq i\leq k-1).
$$

These are the classical coefficient conjectures for the $\mathrm{W}$-polynomial, attributed in the paper to Rota and Mason. The source says that the unimodality conjecture remains open; no resolution status is supplied for the full combined list.
355716 Let $G$ be a connected simple compact Lie group with trivial center. A **non-constant word** is an element of $G\ast F_d$ that does not lie in $G$ itself; its evaluation on $G^d$ is the associated word map.

**Word-map nonconstancy conjecture.** Every non-constant word defines a non-constant word map.

The trivial-center hypothesis is necessary, since a nontrivial central element produces a non-constant word with constant evaluation. The claim also fails for certain semisimple, non-simple compact groups, while the conjecture asks for the corresponding nonconstancy property in the simple case.
355750 Let $P$ be a connected $n$-element poset with minimal elements $m_1,\ldots,m_s$. Define a coloring $c:P\to\mathcal{P}([s])$ by putting $i\in c(x)$ if $x\geq_P m_i$, where $[s]=\{1,\ldots,s\}$. Let $L_{n-2}$ denote the labeling obtained after $n-2$ applications of promotion, and let $\mathbb{P}$ denote the relevant probability distribution on labelings.

**Coloring probability conjecture.**

$$
\mathbb{P}\left(c(L_{n-2}^{-1}(1))=\{s\}\,\middle|\,L(m_{s-1})=n\right)\geq\mathbb{P}\left(c(L_{n-2}^{-1}(1))=\{s\}\,\middle|\,L(m_s)=n\right).
$$

The paper states that this inequality would imply the general bound for tangled labelings by showing that the number of tangled labelings increases when $m_{s-1}\lessdot m_s$; it remains open.
355767 Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a disconnected poset. A poset is **Frobenius** when its associated type-A Lie poset algebra has index zero, and it is **toral** when it has the toral construction form described in the paper. The disjoint sum of two posets is their union with no comparabilities between the two components.

**Disconnected contact-poset conjecture.** $\mathcal{P}$ is contact if and only if it is a disjoint sum of two Frobenius, toral posets.

The paper explains that this conjecture would follow from the toral-poset conjecture for Frobenius posets, together with known results on disconnected contact type-A Lie poset algebras. The source gives no resolution status.
355768 Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a connected poset, and let $\mathfrak{g}_A(\mathcal{P})$ be its associated type-A Lie poset algebra. The algebra is **contact** when it has index one and admits a contact form.

**Connected contact-poset torality conjecture.** If $\mathfrak{g}_A(\mathcal{P})$ is contact, then $\mathcal{P}$ is toral.

This conjecture proposes that the toral construction captures every connected contact type-A Lie poset algebra. The source gives no resolution status.
355806 Let $a$ and $b$ be relatively prime positive integers. Let $\operatorname{Tam}(a,b)$ be the rational Tamari lattice of lattice paths from $(0,0)$ to $(a,b)$ lying strictly above $y=(b/a)x$ except at their endpoints, and let $\operatorname{Row}$ denote its rowmotion operator.

**Thomas–Williams' conjecture.** The operator

$$
\operatorname{Row}\colon\operatorname{Tam}(a,b)\to\operatorname{Tam}(a,b)
$$

has order $a+b-1$.

This conjecture gives the period of rowmotion on every rational Tamari lattice. The paper proves it for the subfamily of $m$-Tamari lattices, while the general case remains open.
355825 Let $p\geq2$ be an integer, and let $P=\nabla(p)$, where

$$
\nabla(p)=\{(i,k)\in[p]\times[p]\mid i+k<p+1\}.
$$

Use the same notation as for the Delta-triangle: $f$ is a $[?]$-labeling of $\widehat{P}$, $R$ is birational rowmotion, and $a=f(0)$ and $b=f(1)$. **Periodicity conjecture for the Nabla-triangle.** The Delta-triangle periodicity statement should also hold when $P=\nabla(p)$ instead of $P=\Delta(p)$. Thus, with the corresponding transpose involution $x\mapsto x'$, one should have

$$
(R^pf)(x)=a\overline{b}\cdot f(x')\cdot\overline{a}b.
$$

If true, this would generalize the cited commutative result; no resolution is supplied in the given text.
355860 Let $\tilde{D}_n$ be the extended Dynkin graph with $n+1$ vertices, let $W(\tilde{D}_n)$ be its walk matrix, and set

$$
r=\left\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor.
$$

**Smith normal form conjecture.** The Smith normal form of $W(\tilde{D}_n)$ is

$$
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{diag}(\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{r-1},n-1,0,\ldots,0), & \text{if $n$ is even},\\
\operatorname{diag}\left(\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{r-1},\frac{n-1}{2},0,\ldots,0\right), & \text{if $n$ is odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

This conjecture gives the remaining Smith normal form data after the paper determines the rank of the walk matrix, and its verification would complete the proposed next step for the extended Dynkin graphs $\tilde{D}_n$.
355968 Let $G$ be a connected graph on vertex set $[n]$, let $K_m$ be the complete graph on $[m]$, let $J_{K_m,G}$ be the generalized binomial edge ideal, let $c(G)$ be the number of maximal cliques of $G$, and assume $m<n$. **Madani–Kiani's connected-graph conjecture.**

$$
\operatorname{reg}(S/J_{K_m,G})\leq \min\{(m-1)c(G),n-1\}.
$$

The source says that this bound was proved for chordal graphs, but does not provide a general proof or disproof for connected graphs.
356068 Let $a$ and $b$ be integers with $2\leq a\leq b\leq 2a$ and $a+b\equiv 2\pmod 3$. Let $n$ and $k$ be the integers such that

$$
(a,b)=(n+3k,2n+3k-1).
$$

**Propp's conjecture.** The $(a,b)$-benzel has

$$
\prod_{i=1}^k \frac{(2i)!(2i+2n-2)!}{(i+n-1)!(i+n+k-1)!}
$$

tilings by right stones, rising bones, and falling bones. This conjecture concerns the previously unresolved case $a+b\equiv 2\pmod 3$; the supplied paper presents it immediately before proving related enumeration results, but the provided context does not establish whether this particular conjecture is resolved.
356111 Consider the Trail of Lost Pennies on the finite trail $\llbracket -j-1,k+1\rrbracket$ in its symmetric standard form. **Finite-trail equilibrium-count conjecture.** The number of time-invariant Nash equilibria equals

$$
\max\{2(j+k)-5,1\}.
$$

The formula is presented as a conjecture about the effect of reflection symmetries on the finite-trail game; no proof or resolution is supplied in the text.
356278 Let $K_{2m}$ be the complete graph on $2m$ vertices. A **vertex-split** is a decomposition of its edge set into mutually edge-disjoint spanning trees; write the resulting group as $S_{paning}(K_{2m})$. **KSTH conjecture.** Each complete graph $K_{2m}$ can be vertex-split into a group $S_{paning}(K_{2m})$ of $m$ mutually edge-disjoint spanning trees, each with $2m-1$ edges, such that every pair of distinct spanning trees $T_i,T_j\in S_{paning}(K_{2m})$ forms a vertex-coincided graph $G=T_i[\odot_{prop}]T_j$ containing a Hamilton cycle and two perfect matchings. This asserts a structured decomposition of complete graphs into spanning trees with strong pairwise connectivity properties; the source provides no resolution, so the conjecture remains open.
356493 Let $G\wr H$ be a wreath product with state group $K$, and call a surjective strategy **minimal** when it has the form $\{k_i\in K\}_{i=1}^{|K|-1}$. **Minimal strategy conjecture.** Whenever $G\wr H$ has a surjective strategy, it also has a minimal surjective strategy $\{k_i\in K\}_{i=1}^{|K|-1}$. Minimal strategies would give the shortest possible guaranteed bound discussed in the source; the conjecture is based on the examples known to the authors and remains open.
356510 Let $X$ be a compact, connected, irreducible graph of graphs with circular edge spaces.

**Graphs of free groups with cyclic edge groups.** Then $\rho_+(X)\leq 0$. Furthermore, $\sigma_+(X)<0$ unless $\pi_1(X)$ has a Baumslag--Solitar subgroup.

The source expects this conjecture to be relatively easy to prove and presents it as a warm-up for more difficult examples; no proof or resolution is supplied.
356522 Let $t,n$ be positive integers and set

$$
m=(t-1)(n-1)+2n.
$$

Consider a $t$-coloring of the edges of the ordered complete graph $K_m$, whose vertices are linearly ordered. A matching is a set of pairwise vertex-disjoint edges; it is **non-nested** if no two edges are nested and **non-separated** if no two edges are separated in the vertex order. **Ordered matching conjecture.** Every such coloring contains (i) a monochromatic non-nested matching with $n$ edges, and (ii) a monochromatic non-separated matching with $n$ edges. This conjecture asks whether the Cockayne–Lorimer bound remains valid for these two ordered matching types; the source identifies this as the main open problem and notes positive answers in several special cases.
356551 Let $G$ be a cubic graph. A **linear forest** is a forest whose components are paths, and a two-colouring of $V(G)$ induces one subgraph on each colour class. **Abreu–Goedgebeur–Labbate–Mazzuoccolo's conjecture.** The vertex set of $G$ can be two-coloured so that the two colour classes induce isomorphic linear forests. The conjecture is known for large connected cubic graphs of large girth and for some specific cubic graphs, but remains open in general.
356664 Let a Costas sequence be a sequence whose difference triangle has no repeated entry in any row. A sequence is **singly periodical** when every circular shift of its terms is also a Costas sequence. A Welch sequence is one obtained from the Welch construction: for a prime $p$, a primitive element $\alpha\in\mathbb{F}_p$, and $c\in\mathbb{Z}$, the sequence $\alpha^{c},\alpha^{1+c},\dots,\alpha^{p-2+c}$. **Golomb–Taylor's conjecture.** A Costas sequence is singly periodical if and only if it is Welch. Only Welch sequences are currently known to be singly periodical, so the conjecture remains open.
356673 Let $B$ be an oriented graph, let $\overline{B}$ be its underlying undirected graph obtained by forgetting edge directions, and call $B$ directed-forcing when it has the directed forcing property. **Directed forcing conjecture.** If $B$ has a homomorphism to an edge and $\overline{B}$ contains a cycle, then $B$ has the directed forcing property. The paper proves that a homomorphism to an edge and a cycle in the underlying graph are necessary conditions for directed forcing. It conjectures that these two conditions are also sufficient, leaving the characterization open.
356691 Let $G$ be an $coverline{K_3}$-free graph on $n$ vertices. A set of vertices or edges is **pairwise adjacent** when every two members are adjacent, where adjacency between an edge and another member has the natural incidence/endpoint interpretation used in the source.

**Pairwise-adjacent vertices-or-edges conjecture.** Every $coverline{K_3}$-free graph on $n$ vertices has a set of

$$
\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil
$$

pairwise adjacent vertices or edges.

This conjecture is presented as an obstacle to reconciling two algorithmic approaches for proving restricted forms of Hadwiger's Conjecture. The supplied text does not state whether it has been resolved.
