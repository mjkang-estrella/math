  The aim of this paper is to investigate sumsets, difference sets, and balanced sets in dicyclic
groups. Before stating our main results, we introduce some notation. For k ∈ N, we denote by
                                    SDic4n (k),    DDic4n (k),     BDic4n (k)
the number of MSTD, MDTS, and balanced subsets of Dic4n of cardinality k, respectively.
  For a fixed subset A ⊆ Dic4n representing a given type, we write
                               SDic4n (k, A),     DDic4n (k, A),    BDic4n (k, A)
for the corresponding counts restricted to subsets of type A.
   Moreover, for 0 ≤ r ≤ n, we define the subsets
              R2r = {a2r , a2r+1 , . . . , a2r+2n−1 },   F2r = {a2r b, a2r+1 b, . . . , a2r+2n−1 b},
where exponents are taken modulo 2n. Note that Dic4n = R2r ∪ F2r .
  Our main results in this direction are stated as follows.
Theorem 1.4. Let n ≥ 2 be an integer. Then the following hold.
  (1) If n is even, then
               SDic4n (2) = 4n(n − 2),          DDic4n (2) = 0,        BDic4n (2) = 2n(2n + 3).
    (2) If n is odd, then
               SDic4n (2) = 4n(n − 1),          DDic4n (2) = 0,        BDic4n (2) = 2n(2n + 1).
