325531 Let $m$ be an odd integer with $m>7$ and $m\not\equiv 0 \pmod{3}$. In the additive group $({\mathbb F}_{3^m},+)$, let ${\rm P}$, ${\rm DY}(1)$, ${\rm DY}(-1)$, ${\rm RT}(1)$, ${\rm RT}(-1)$, $D_1$, and $D_{-1}$ denote the seven skew Hadamard difference sets constructed in the paper. **Pairwise inequivalence conjecture.** These seven skew Hadamard difference sets are pairwise inequivalent. For $m=5$ and $m=7$, the paper verifies pairwise inequivalence using the distributions, or the extrema, of their triple intersection numbers; the conjecture extends this distinction to all odd $m>7$ not divisible by $3$.
332780 A finite simple graph $G$ is **double-critical** if it is connected and, for every edge $uv\in E(G)$, $\chi(G-\{u,v\})=\chi(G)-2$. A graph $H$ is a **minor** of $G$ if it can be obtained from a subgraph of $G$ by contracting edges; in particular, $K_t$ denotes the complete graph on $t$ vertices. **Kawarabayashi–Pedersen–Toft conjecture.** For every integer $t\ge1$, every double-critical $t$-chromatic graph contains a $K_t$ minor. This is a weaker version of Hadwiger's conjecture, which asserts the same minor conclusion for every $t$-chromatic graph. The conjecture was verified in the cited paper for $t\le7$; its general status remains open.
334234 Let $G$ be a connected graph with chromatic number $t$. It is **double-critical** if for every edge $xy\in E(G)$, the graph $G\backslash\{x,y\}$ is $(t-2)$-colorable. The complete graphs are double-critical. **Erdős–Lovász double-critical graph conjecture.** If $G$ is a double-critical, $t$-chromatic graph, then

$$
G=K_t.
$$

Complete graphs are the only known examples of double-critical graphs. The conjecture has been verified for $t\le 5$ and remains open for $t\ge 6$.
377054 For a connected undirected graph $G$, let $T(G;x,y)$ denote its Tutte polynomial and let $\EuScript{K}(G)$ denote its critical group. **Non-determination conjecture.** There exist connected undirected graphs $G$ and $H$ such that

$$
T(G;x,y)=T(H;x,y)
$$

and

$$
\EuScript{K}(G)\not\simeq\EuScript{K}(H).
$$

Thus the critical group cannot be computed from the Tutte polynomial alone. The source notes that the converse relation already fails: the Tutte polynomial is not computable from the critical group, and it proposes the reverse failure as an open conjecture.

378894 Let $X$ be a non-empty set of permutations, and let $s_n(X)=\left|\operatorname{Av}(X)\cap\mathcal{S}_n\right|$, where $\operatorname{Av}(X)$ is the class of permutations avoiding every element of $X$. **Wilf–Stanley conjecture.** There exists a real number $c_X$ such that

$$
s_n(X)\leq c_X^n.
$$

The conjecture asserts exponential boundedness of the enumeration sequence of every nonempty permutation class defined by avoidance. It was resolved by Marcus and Tardos, so the claim is solved.
379052 Let $A\subset\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ have cardinality $n-2$, with exactly two missing elements $a_i,a_j\in\{1,2,\dots,n\}$. **The two-missing-elements perfect Skolem set conjecture.** The set $A$ is a perfect Skolem set if and only if: (1) if $n\equiv0$ or $1$ modulo $n$, then $a_i\equiv a_j$ modulo $2$; (2) if $n\equiv2$ or $3$ modulo $n$, then $a_i\equiv a_j-1$ modulo $2$; and (3) $A\notin\{\{3\},\{2,4\},\{2,4,5\},\{3,4,5,6\}\}$. This is presented as a special case of the perfect Skolem set conjecture, with the listed exceptional sets accounting for failure of the density condition. The source gives no separate resolution status for this special case.
379168 Let $A=\{a_1<a_2<a_3<\dots\}$ be an increasing sequence of positive integers, let $A(n)$ count its elements not exceeding $n$, and let

$$
S_A=\left\{\sum_{x\in B}x:\ B\subset A,\ |B|<\infty\right\}.
$$

The sequence $A$ is **subcomplete** if $S_A$ contains an infinite arithmetic progression.

**Folkman's conjecture.** There is a constant $c$ such that every increasing sequence satisfying

$$
A(n)\geq cn^{1/2}
$$

is subcomplete.

This is presented as a refinement of Erdős's completeness conjecture, removing the modularity condition. Folkman proved sufficiency of the stronger bound $A(n)\geq cn^{1/2+\epsilon}$, while the square-root threshold remains the conjectural case in the supplied text.

The preceding theorem gives the Euler-number generating series for the relevant moduli spaces in terms of this quotient, so the conjecture would provide an explicit simplification; its status is not resolved in the supplied text.
379275 Let $\Gamma$ be a web, and let $A$ and $B$ be its distinguished vertex sets. An $A$--$B$-separating set $S$ meets every $A$--$B$ path; $S$ is **linkable into $A$ in $\overleftarrow{\Gamma}$** when the reversed web contains a linkage from $S$ to $A$, and $A$ is linkable into $S$ in $\Gamma$ analogously. **Separating-set characterization conjecture.** If $\Gamma$ is unlinkable, then there exists an $A$--$B$-separating set $S$ which is linkable into $A$ in $\overleftarrow{\Gamma}$, but $A$ is not linkable into $S$ in $\Gamma$. The text presents this as a third formulation equivalent to the Erdős–Menger conjecture.

379904 Suppose $|q|<1$ and $z\ne0$. Here $E(q):=\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-q^n)$ and $(x;q)_\infty:=\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}(1-xq^n)$. Write $F(q,z)\succeq0$ to mean that the relevant Laurent series in $q$ and $z$ has nonnegative coefficients.

**Nonnegative product conjecture.**

(i) If $p\ge1$, then

$$
\frac{E(q)}{(z;q)_\infty(qz^{-p};q)_\infty}\succeq0.
$$

(ii) If $a,b,m,n\ge1$, then

$$
\frac{E(q^{ma+nb})}{(q^a;q^{ma+nb})_\infty(q^b;q^{ma+nb})_\infty}\succeq0.
$$

(iii) For $a\ge1$,

$$
\frac{[z^a;q]_\infty E(q)}{[z;q]_\infty[z^{a+1};q]_\infty}\succeq0.
$$

These are further positivity conjectures for $q$-products, following preceding propositions on analogous inequalities. The supplied text gives no evidence that any of the three assertions has been resolved.
