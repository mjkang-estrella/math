                                                   Abstract. We study a continuous interpolation between the type B and shifted type A Narayana
                                                   triangles. Using a direct Gegenbauer-moment proof and a continuant comparison criterion, we
                                                   show that the row polynomials form a coefficientwise Hankel-totally positive sequence for every

                                                   real θ ≥ 0, and establish the same property for the moment sequences associated with all tails
                                                   of the corresponding Jacobi matrix. Their γ-expansion, real-rootedness and Stieltjes moment
                                                   representation are explicit, as are the exact orders of vanishing and extreme coefficients of arbitrary
                                                   Hankel minors. This confirms and strengthens Rodelet–Causse–Tevlin’s log-convexity conjecture for
                                                   super Narayana polynomials. We further extend Wang–Yang’s total positivity of the type A and B
                                                   Narayana triangles and strict total positivity of the associated squares to every real θ > −1, using

                                                   continuous Narayana transformations preserve strong t-log-convexity for every θ ≥ 0, and that every
                                                   d-Hoggatt transformation has the same property for integers d ≥ 1. The preservation proofs employ
                                                   the Zhu–Sun criterion and a mixed-minor one-sign-change argument based on a hypergeometric
                                                   hitting-probability inequality.
