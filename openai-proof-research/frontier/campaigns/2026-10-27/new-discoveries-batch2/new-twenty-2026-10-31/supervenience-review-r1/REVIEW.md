## B. Independent mathematical check

Fix w and any S(w,u,v). Write G(A,B) = D(A; A ∧ B). If A is true at both ends, A-agreement is automatic and the consequent is exactly B-agreement. If A is false at both ends, the consequent is automatic. If A differs, the antecedent fails. Therefore G(A,B) asserts exactly B-agreement on those S-pairs inside the truth set of A. This is valid for an arbitrary relation; it does not take a transitive closure or assume all pairs inside a cell are edges.

The four cells are A ∧ C, A ∧ ¬C, ¬A ∧ C, ¬A ∧ ¬C. A pair agreeing on A and C belongs to exactly one common cell. Conversely any pair inside a cell agrees on both. Conjoining G(cell,B) over the cells is consequently equivalent to D(A,C;B). Repeated endpoints, loops, asymmetric relations and empty relations cause no exception. With an empty world set, there are no pointed obligations.

The recursive translation preserves atoms and Boolean constructors and replaces each arity-2 connective by the four guarded cells of its translated arguments. Its structural induction uses truth equivalence at **every world**, allowing replacement in all determining inputs and the dependent input even under further modal nesting. This proves uniform pointed-model equivalence and defeats the n = 1 strict step. No claim about expressivity follows from bounded model checks alone; the induction is the proof.

Independent `scripts/models.py` verifies all local pair relations and all truth sets for A, C, B on 0, 1, 2, 3 worlds: 1, 16, 1,024 and 262,144 cases respectively. These are full S(w,-,-) slices; arbitrary other slices cannot affect a one-step identity with fixed truth sets. It separately checks three genuinely nested formulas, including modal formulas in determining positions, on all 256 ternary frames and all 64 three-atom valuations on two worlds, at both points: 98,304 pointed comparisons. Total one-step cases: 263,185. No mismatch. Negative controls find counterexamples both to using unguarded D(cell;B) and to omitting each of the four guarded cells. Witness masks and the exact enumeration scope are in `logs/model-check.json`.

## D. Prior art and routine-ness: the unresolved decisive gate

Actual primary proof-body reads:

1. Fan, Section 6.1 / Proposition 16: Boolean cells eliminate finite determinacy arity over ordinary Kripke frames. The proof partitions equal determining valuations into cells, exactly the combinatorial step used here. This is the strongest routine-adaptation objection. Its stated noncontingency formula must not be transferred to arbitrary ternary frames without a bridge.
2. Goranko–Kuusisto, arXiv:1609.07398, Proposition 3.3 and proof (printed p. 13): universal-frame determinacy is represented by a disjunction of Boolean normal forms under a universal modality. The proof uses the unique complete input type and its truth-set union. This is a genuine antecedent, but does not itself assert arbitrary ternary-frame unary definability.
3. **Additional adverse/countervailing evidence:** Fan, Section 7.3, explicitly defines relativized agreement O(A,B), which has precisely the guarded semantics above. It gives the two-cell decomposition of unary supervenience into O(A,B) and O(¬A,B), then conjectures that O is strictly more expressive, treating O(p,q) as a candidate undefinable formula. Thus the bridge G(A,B) is exactly a counterexample to a second source conjectural undefinability claim. Section 7.4 already decomposes relativized supervenience into two restricted-agreement formulas. The restricted-agreement *semantic notion* is therefore old; its unary definition is the added observation. I found no statement of that definition in these inspected sections.
4. **Newly located later paper:** Humberstone, “Supervenience, Dependence, Disjunction,” Logic and Logical Philosophy 28 (2019), 3–135, DOI 10.12775/LLP.2018.007.
