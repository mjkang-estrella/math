Let $T$ be an oriented tree, let $n=|V(T)|$ be its order, and let $\operatorname{unvd}(T)$ denote its unavoidability.

**Sumner's conjecture.** Every oriented tree of order $n>1$ is $(2n-2)$-unavoidable; equivalently,

$$
\operatorname{unvd}(T)\leqslant 2n-2.
$$

This is a celebrated conjecture about tournaments and oriented trees. The source does not state a resolution, so its status remains open.
