**Spectral detection conjecture.** The sharp threshold for distinguishing the RGG from its Erdős–Rényi counterpart is determined by

$$
n^3\operatorname{tr}^2(\kappa^3)=1.
$$

Equivalently, the detectable regime should satisfy $n^3\operatorname{tr}^2(\kappa^3)\gg1$, while the undetectable regime should satisfy $n^3\operatorname{tr}^2(\kappa^3)\ll1$.

The conjecture is motivated by both the signed triangle statistic and the Kullback–Leibler expansion, where $\operatorname{tr}(\kappa^3)$ arises naturally. It unifies the conjectured threshold for hard RGGs with the proved thresholds for several smooth-kernel models, but the general sharp characterization remains open.
