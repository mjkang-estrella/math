341585 A poset is **Gorenstein*** when it is Cohen--Macaulay and Eulerian. Its $cd$-index is denoted by $\Phi_P(c,d)$; coefficientwise comparison means comparison of the coefficients of corresponding $cd$-words. **Gorenstein* poset conjectures.**

- The $cd$-index of every Gorenstein* lattice is coefficientwise greater than or equal to the $cd$-index of the Boolean lattice (simplex).

343849 Let $D$ be a weakly connected digraph. An edge set $F\subseteq E(D)$ is a **finitary dijoin** if it intersects every non-empty finite dicut of $D$.
