## Follow-up works actually used

- David Anderson, Greta Panova and Leonid Petrov, *Computation and sampling for Schubert specializations*, [arXiv:2603.20104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20104), Theorem 1.2. Explicit prior refutation of 366094.
- Qingsong Gu, Lu Hao, Xueping Huang and Yuhua Sun, *Sharp Criteria for the existence of positive solutions to Lane-Emden-type inequalities on weighted graphs*, [arXiv:2604.24932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24932), Corollary 1.7. Prior resolution of 353295.
- Nathan Dowlin, *A spectral sequence from Khovanov homology to knot Floer homology*, JAMS 37 (2024), 951–1010, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1039). Prior literal existence result for 340907.
- Luis Ferroni, *Symmetric edge polytopes are not gamma-positive*, [arXiv:2607.02424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02424). Cites the reserved 372128 source and supplies printed series-parallel negative-gamma examples. This is not itself cited as an explicit evaluated aggregate counterexample.
- *Existence of weak Landau-Ginzburg models for weighted complete intersections*, [arXiv:2610.38727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38727), Theorem 1.3, Corollary 1.4, Conjecture 1.6. The nice-nef-partition part of 335212 is settled; compactification remains a separate obligation.
- Gehao Wang, *The identification of the extended refined open partition function and the Kontsevich-Penner matrix model*, [arXiv:2511.16919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16919), Theorem 2 and Section 4. The original-model identification gap matters for 335254.
- *On the Randić energy of caterpillar graphs*, MATCH 87 (2022), 729–744, [journal PDF](https://match.pmf.kg.ac.rs/electronic_versions/Match87/n3/match87n3_729-744.pdf). Located recent citing literature around 341123; the search excerpt supplies the previously verified order-10 range.
- *On the R-Spectrum and Randić Energy of Kragujevac-like Trees*, Mathematics 14 (2026), 3338, [publisher page](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/14/18/3338). Partial-family confirmation, not an all-graph proof. Full proof body not audited here.
- Jiangdong Ai et al., *On Seymour’s and Sullivan’s second neighbourhood conjectures*, [arXiv:2306.03493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03493), and Ayman El Zein, *A Note on Sullivan’s Second Neighborhood Conjecture*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-025-02908-7). Located partial results; no claim of a full resolution.
- [Public August 2026 ProbXiv solution of Long–Wagner Conjecture 1.9](https://probxiv.com/problems/the-largest-projective-cube-free-subsets-of-mathbb-z-2-n-2). Public disclosure suffices to exclude rediscovery; its exact counting certificate was not independently rerun.
