21 349177 Nonexistence conjecture for dendrimer nanostars of at least four stages A **dendrimer nanostar** is the nanostar structure obtained by growing a dendrimer through a specified number of stages; a graph is a **$G_\phi$-graph** if it is realized as $G_\phi(A)$ for an appropriate set $A$, using the Euler $\phi$ function as in the paper. **Nonexistence conjecture for dendrimer nanostars.** There is no dendrimer nanostar which has grown at least four stages, as a $G_\phi$-graph. The paper proves that the two-stage nanostar $D_2$ is a $G_\phi$-graph, while reporting that attempts to prove nonexistence for nanostars grown through at least four stages have failed; the conjecture remains open.
22 349441 Estrada–Mugnolo's Kirchhoff-index ordering conjecture for hubs-biased resistance distances Let $G$ be a connected graph. Write $\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}_{\mathnormal{\alpha=1}}}(G)$ for the Kirchhoff index based on the hubs-repelling resistance distance, $\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}}_{\alpha=0}(G)$ for the standard Kirchhoff index, and $\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}_{\mathnormal{\alpha=-1}}}(G)$ for the Kirchhoff index based on the hubs-attracting resistance distance.

**Kirchhoff-index ordering conjecture.** For $\alpha\in\{-1,1\}$,

$$
\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}_{\mathnormal{\alpha=1}}}(G)\geq\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}}_{\alpha=0}(G)\geq\mathcal{\mathcal{\mathscr{R}}_{\mathnormal{\alpha=-1}}}(G),
$$

with equality if and only if $G$ is regular.

The conjecture was formulated after computational experiments on more than 12,000 connected graphs with $5\leq n\leq 8$, and predicts that hubs-repelling navigation gives the largest Kirchhoff index while hubs-attracting navigation gives the smallest. Its general validity is left open in the source.

**Non-IR conjecture for paths and cycles.** The path $P_n$ is not an IR-graph for each $n\geq 3$, and the cycle $C_n$ is not an IR-graph for each $n\geq 5$.

These are open problems concerning which graphs can occur as irredundance graphs. They are stated together in the paper's list of conjectures and open problems and are attributed there to Mynhardt (2019).
24 350721 Signed cycle-type restriction polynomiality conjecture Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $k$ be a fixed positive integer with $1\leq k\leq n$, and let $\mathfrak{S}_n$ denote the symmetric group on $n$ elements. is an element of $\mathbb{N}[x_1,\dotsc,x_n,y_1,\dotsc,y_n]$. This generalizes the derangement case by imposing a lower bound on the lengths of all cycles; the source provides no resolution or further evidence for the claim, so its status remains open.

27 352496 The Polynexus formula for nonzero products in the rook monoid Let $M_n$ be the monoid of rook diagrams and let $\mathbf{0}$ denote its zero element. Let $\psi(n)$ be the number of nonzero products arising from $M_n\setminus\{\mathbf{0}\}$.

**Polynexus conjecture.** For $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $\psi(n)$ equals the $(n+1)$th Polynexus number of order $7$, namely

The formula was suggested by numerical evaluation of the sextuple sum defining $\psi(n)$ and comparison with the Polynexus numbers. An analytic evaluation of that sum was not available in the source, so the conjecture remains open here.
28 352827 Unit-gap conjecture for diameters of Schrijver graphs Let $k$ and $r$ be integers with $2\leq r\leq k-2$, and let $D(G)$ denote the diameter of a graph $G$. For $n=2k+r$, write $\operatorname{SG}(n,k)$ for the Schrijver graph. **Unit-gap conjecture.** If $2\leq r\leq k-2$, then

$$

$$

The conjecture concerns the possibility of larger drops in the diameter as $r$ increases. It is based on computations and is open; the paper notes that the analogous differences are in $\\{0,1\\}$ for $k\leq 5$, while a larger gap occurs at the transition from $r=1$ to $r=2$ when $k\geq 6$.

31 354228 Even-arity complete full tree local antimagic chromatic number conjecture Let $T$ be a complete full $t$-ary tree, meaning a rooted tree in which every nonleaf has exactly $t$ children and all leaves have the same depth. 34 355956 Yip's maximal subfield clique conjecture for generalized Paley graphs Let $d>1$ be an integer. The subfield $\mathbb{F}_{p^r}$ is a clique in the generalized Paley graph $GP(q,d)=\operatorname{Cay}(\mathbb{F}_q^+;(\mathbb{F}_q^*)^d)$. **Yip's maximal subfield clique conjecture.** The subfield $\mathbb{F}_{p^r}$ forms a maximal clique in $GP(q,d)$. 35 356266 Hurlbert's ordering conjecture for universal cycles of permutations Let $S_n$ be the set of permutations of $[n]$, and let $\mathcal{O}(n)$ be the clustered graph of overlapping $n$-permutations: its edges are the permutations in $S_n$, and consecutive permutations overlap in their last and first $n-1$ entries up to reduction. **Hurlbert's ordering conjecture.** Given any Euler tour of the clustered graph of overlapping permutations (that is, an ordering of $S_n$ such that each consecutive pair of permutations overlaps), there is a universal cycle for permutations that covers $S_n$ in the same order. 38 360653 The GM-rule Nash equilibrium conjecture for exact slow NIM Consider the exact slow game NIM$(n,n-1)$ for $\ell$ players: a position is a non-negative $n$-vector, players move cyclically, and on each move a player keeps one entry unchanged and decreases each of the other $n-1$ positive entries by $1$. A position with at least two non-positive entries is terminal; the player whose turn it is loses, and the other $\ell-1$ players share the payoff $\frac{C-L(P)}{\ell-1}$, where $L(P)$ is the play length and $C$ exceeds the length of every play from the initial position. The GM-rule specifies a strategy for each player, including the move that keeps the largest entry when no entry is a multiple of $\ell$. **GM-rule Nash equilibrium conjecture.** The set of $\ell$ GM-strategies forms a uniform Nash equilibrium. The claim concerns the strategic behavior induced by the GM-rule in the multiplayer exact slow NIM game; the supplied text does not state whether it has been proved or disproved.
