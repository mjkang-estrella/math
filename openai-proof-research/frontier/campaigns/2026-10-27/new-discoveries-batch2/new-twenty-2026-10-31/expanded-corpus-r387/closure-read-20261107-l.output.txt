14:                                                Abstract
15:          Two boards are rook equivalent if they have the same number of non-attacking
16:       rook placements for any number of rooks. Define a rook equivalence graph on an
17:       equivalence class of Ferrers boards by specifying that two boards are connected
18:       by an edge if you can obtain one of the boards by moving squares in the other
19:       board out of one column and into a single other column. Given such a graph,
20:       we characterize which boards will yield connected graphs. We also provide some
21:       cases where common graphs will or will not be the graph for some set of rook
22:       equivalent Ferrers boards. Finally, we extend this graph definition to the m-level
23:       rook placement generalization developed by Briggs and Remmel. This yields a graph
24:       on the set of rook equivalent singleton boards, and we characterize which singleton
25:       boards give rise to a connected graph.
