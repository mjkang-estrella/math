journal target Conjecture 24. If Ka,b is a complete, bipartite graph that is the rook equivalence graph
of Ferrers board B, then a = b = 1.

    As we saw in the proofs of Theorems 11 and 12, cycles with odd length show up
frequently in the rook equivalence graphs. In fact, if value v1 occurs in two different
positions of ξ(B) which can both be exchanged with an entry with another value v2 , this
will induce a cycle of length three in the rook equivalence graph of B. It may be possible
to provide an argument why any rook equivalence graph on at least three vertices must
have an odd length cycle, or produce a Ka,b = G(B) to act as a counterexample.
