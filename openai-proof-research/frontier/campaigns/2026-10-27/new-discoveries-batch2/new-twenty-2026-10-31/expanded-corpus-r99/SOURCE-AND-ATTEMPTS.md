## Source-internal and published obstructions to credit

- **354691, dope matrices.** Source Conjecture 15 is not obtained from its Theorems 1 and 6: the former only treats two rows; the latter says that generic tuples maximize the pattern count. A post-source primary paper, Bisain, *Generic classification and asymptotic enumeration of dope matrices*, arXiv:2209.13811, Theorem 1.1, gives the exact claimed classification for all positive m,n. Its Section 2 proof reduces to the independence of almost-safe patterns (Lemma 2.3), then realizes safe patterns with no unintended zeros. This is a full-scope known resolution. Our already completed modular n=7 sweep adds no credit.
- **354964, rooted e-positivity.** The source's own rooted coloring definition gives
  `[x_0^k] X_0(G_*^r) = sum X_(G-S)`, over independent sets S containing r of size k. If P-r is (3+1)-free, every P-S in this sum is an induced subposet of P-r and is also (3+1)-free. Hikita's Corollary 1.8 (arXiv:2410.12758), with Conjecture 2.1's exact statement, makes every summand e-positive. Thus the requested rooted statement follows by the elementary independent-color-class decomposition from the now-proved unrooted theorem. Zero credit as a routine bridge. No novel rooted positivity theorem is asserted.
- **356988, PageRank.** The source's regular and biregular graph theorems do not imply coordinate monotonicity in general. Direct edge-space calculations found the four-vertex paw and the five-vertex house. At damping 17/20, the paw's hub has values 4593/12524, 3378741/9176122, and 6358233/17379386 at mu=1,10,100: an increase then decrease. An exact rational path and a Lean obstruction were produced. However, the primary preprint DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23580381, dated 23 October 2026, already proves this very result, including the same paw at the same damping (Proposition 5.3 and its order-four census). Its Section 5 proof and model were read, not just its abstract. STOP: known result, not a new Lean discovery. The source's printed spread inequality also disagrees with its accompanying directional prose; that wording issue is not used for credit.
- **381679, hook-length masses.** Bhowmik and Tsai, *Local Limit Theorems for Hook Lengths in Partitions*, DOI 10.1007/s00026-024-00737-8, repeats the exact source conjecture as Conjecture 1.4, explains the moving-lattice interpretation, and explicitly disproves it at t=4 and t=5 (Theorems 1.7 and 1.8). The t=5 subsequence limit is 25 sqrt(5)/(4 pi^2) times the limiting density, not that density. These theorems and the surrounding explanation were read. No domain-correction refutation is proposed. This is already publicly refuted.
- **349718, strengthened log-concavity.** O'Sullivan, arXiv:2205.13468, Theorem 1.3 and Section 5, especially Corollary 5.7, explicitly prove a stronger 1+n^(-2) margin and identify Ulas Conjecture 3.5. For d>=2 and n>=1 this margin dominates 1+n^(-d). The asymptotic expansion and the final implication to Ulas's conjecture are in the paper. Known full-scope resolution, not a new asymptotic bridge.
- **350434, C_u.** The updated source Remark 5 says the non-APN half is already proved by its small-field computations together with Bartoli-Timpanella for m>20. Its Remark 3 proves non-permutation for even m and seventh-power u. For odd m not divisible by 3, every nonzero u is a seventh power. For m=9,15,21 its Remark 3 reports all relevant u checked. The later paper arXiv:2410.23097 states and proves non-permutation for odd m>=23 in Theorem 6.3. Together these cover the remaining dimensions, although that later paper itself leaves the small odd dimensions as an open problem. This is a source/literature combination, not a new result; no independent audit of its Lang-Weil constants is claimed. Our m=4..8 linearized derivative diagnostics were redundant.
- **350451, jet graphs.** Galetto-Iammarino-Yu, arXiv:2407.01836, Theorem 5.4 proves exactly that every jet of every very well-covered graph is very well-covered. Its proof uses the cover-stacking theorem and Favaron's matching property, proving |N(U)|=|U| for the irreducible 2-cover case. The theorem and proof were read. No new general theorem or parameter variant is proposed.

## Searches and remaining mathematical gaps

- **354576, connected {2,4}-factor:** recover the actual conjecture from arXiv:2205.05044v1, Conjecture 7.12; current v2 removes that section rather than furnishing the claimed proof. The preceding Theorem 7.11 imposes the stronger minimum of two bounds, including an isolated-vertex term. Combining it with its proof does not yield the proposed half-|S|+1 condition. All 263 atlas graphs of orders 3..7 satisfying the condition passed: a Hamiltonian cycle was sought first, then a connected even degree-2/4 spanning subgraph if necessary. This only treats simple graphs; the source also allows parallel edges. The obstacle is controlling the odd-cycle components in the source's tree-connectivity deficiency argument without its extra bound. No general proof or counterexample.
- **349714, square values:** source Conjecture 5.2 is literal and has no small omitted endpoint to exploit. The source's genus-two reductions for n=6 do not solve the curves lacking Mordell-Weil bases. Exact dynamic programming tested every n=6..100 and x=2..200000, with no square value. The full source does not give a single uniform x-range for every n. The computation is an absolute coverage statement, not a claim to exceed all source computations. The unresolved generator/basis cases for n=6 remain material; an unrestricted finite sweep cannot prove the conjecture.
- **349716, power-partition residues:** the source's Conjecture 3.1 asserts infinite occurrence of each residue, not equal limiting frequencies (the latter is its Question 3.2). DP modulo lcm(2,...,12), d=2..6, n<=200000 gives exact residue counts over 100001..200000. This extends the source's stated 10^5 coefficient table, but cannot decide infinite occurrence. Need a genuine infinite arithmetic progression or modular generating-function argument; no missing residue in one finite window is a refutation.
- **349854, snake order polytopes:** source Conjecture 6.2 is existential in a regular triangulation and is restricted by the paper's word/poset framework. The ladder theorem, canonical twist invariance, and circuits do not furnish the noncanonical dual graph for each turn. No faithful regular-triangulation enumeration was implemented. This is an explicit search-coverage gap; arbitrary polytope or nonregular triangulation tests would not test the conjecture. Need a regular flip construction at an arbitrary turn with an invariant separating its dual graph from the canonical one. Parked.
- **350265, deeply critical oriented cliques:** Theorem 5 already excludes every even-order circulant, so searching that family cannot produce a discovery. Exhausted all oriented graphs on 2 and 4 vertices; then sampled 2000 deleted-vertex odd-circulant constructions at each even n=6,8,...,32, including sizes beyond the source's sporadic census through 17. None passed even the necessary deletion test: for every deleted arc, at least two vertex pairs must lose all adjacency/directed-2-path conflicts to save two colors. A failure of this necessary test is a rigorous diagnostic rejection; a survivor would still need a genuine oriented coloring certificate. There were no survivors. Need noncirculant constructions retaining critical witnesses under every arc deletion.
- **350368, entropoid hardness:** source Conjecture 5 is an asymptotic cryptographic assumption. Unrestricted exhaustive algorithms would only exploit the paraphrase's missing time/resource model. The source comparison with groups does not itself prove hardness or a root-extraction algorithm. Source-backed parked disposition only, as required; no tiny-prime 'refutation'.
- **350763, rainbow matchings:** source Theorem 1.2 proves the claim when n>=4k-4; it leaves a bounded-order problem for each k, not a finite problem for all k. The exceptional K4 is the proper 3-color one-factorization. For k=2, disjoint present edges must have the same color if there is no rainbow pair. Union-find over these forced equalities gives the maximum possible color-degree sum for each atlas graph on at most 7 vertices. Only K4 reaches 3n. For k=3, seven-vertex local search made 4,083,000 color/nonedge mutation proposals, with a penalty for rainbow triples; best zero-penalty sum was 25, below the required 35. This is heuristic, not exhaustive, and no optimum is claimed. A structured high-color-degree construction or a uniform argument for n<4k-4 is needed.
- **350840, facial coloring:** one planar embedding of each connected planar atlas graph of order <=7 was tested at ell=4..12, yielding 6966 coloring certificates using at most 3ell+1 colors. These simple-graph, one-embedding tests do not cover loops, parallel edges or all embeddings. Boundary walks were used, so for bridges this is a stronger conflict test than trail-only constraints. Jendrol'-Onderko's 2025 note was fetched and read: it handles subdivisions with 3-edge-connected reduction and bounded thread length, not all graphs. Need control of arbitrarily long threads and general reductions; no full implication from the inspected theorems.
- **351090, peak stability:** implemented the source's exact recurrences (5.16)-(5.18) for both peak polynomials. Floating roots were computed for r=2..20, n=1..80. High-degree positive-real-part alarms included source-proved r=2, hence were not accepted. Exact Fraction Routh checks completed r=2..5,n<=60 and r=6,n<=47. All nonconstant zero-factored polynomials had positive Routh pivots; n=1 becomes a positive constant after removing its zero and is trivially stable despite the script's empty-second-row flag. The source means weak Hurwitz stability, allowing zero roots. Source Proposition 5.12 concerns Turan expressions, not the requested T-polynomial. Theorem 5.3's reciprocal-interlacing hypotheses do not immediately cover the tilde family for r>2. Need a full M-versus-tilde-M interlacing theorem. The 2025 Stirling-codes paper inspected treats a different r=2 construction and does not supply that bridge.
- **351336, partially directed k-triangle:** read the source's orientation-removal containment convention and k-triangle definition. Enumerated all (k+2)^(k+1) templates on k+1 vertices for k=3,4, and deterministic prefixes of 20000 templates for k=5,6. Tested weighted blow-up densities at theta=(k/(k-1))^(k-1) on uniform weights and a finite two-class rational grid. No tested density exceeded 1. These sparse finite templates do not approximate every extremal configuration; no global optimum is asserted. Need a larger support or a structural density inequality. The source proves k=2 only for this sharp constant; its k<=5 SAT result is a weaker density statement and must not be substituted.
- **355906, gap-free regularity:** source Conjecture A quantifies both ordinary and symbolic powers for s>=2; its proofs only handle s=2,3. Using exact multigraded Koszul homology over F2, tested every isolate-free gap-free atlas graph of order <=5 at s=4 and 5 (27 graphs, 54 cases). All agree with max(reg I+s-1,2s) for both powers. Membership for symbolic powers used every minimal vertex cover; ordinary membership used exact capacity-constrained edge multisets. Exponents 0..s suffice by the Taylor lcm bound for minimal generators. These are characteristic-2 diagnostics, not a proof over all fields. Need a uniform higher-power degree-complex argument; source Lemma 1.4 and its low-power colon decompositions do not supply it.
- **381793, signed Wachs Mobius:** used the source's signed embedding into ordinary Bruhat order (Proposition 2.6), with sorted-prefix comparisons, and its exact adjacent-inverse-position definition. All 3072 n=7 elements and all 1,818,272 comparable intervals were checked by the Mobius recurrence; no value outside {0,+1,-1}. All 6144 n=8 elements and all 9,192,960 comparable intervals were also checked with no violation. Its complete census is retained in `outputs/wachs8.json`. Source Proposition 4.15 only treats intervals from identity. Theorem 4.13 and Lemma 4.14 provide a Boolean-fiber union property, but that alone does not control the alternating sum for arbitrary bottoms; no claimed one-paragraph generalization. A special matching or a fiber cancellation theorem for every interval is the remaining proof gap. No Lean surrogate for these bounded computations is offered.
