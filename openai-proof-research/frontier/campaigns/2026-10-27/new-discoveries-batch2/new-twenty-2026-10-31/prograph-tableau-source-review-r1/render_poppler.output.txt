Proposition 4.1. Take any G ∈ PCkx (n, m). Then G has precisely y = (n − m)(k −
1) + x output strands. In particular, y ≡ x mod (k − 1).

Proof. Observe that each k-ary coproduct increases the number of free edges by k−1,
while each k-ary product decreases the number of free edges by k−1. If we begin with
x free edges, after n coproducts and m products we have y = x + n(k − 1) − m(k − 1)
outgoing free edges.
