Lemma 2.4 (rank logic). Let (ar )r≥0 be real and klo , khi integers with ar ≤ ar+1 for r < klo , ar+1 ≤ ar
for r ≥ khi , and no valley at any k with klo < k < khi . Then (ar ) is unimodal.

Proof. If no strict fall ai+1 < ai is followed by a strict rise aj+1 > aj , j > i, the sequence is unimodal
(peak at the first strict fall, or at max(khi , 0) if there is none). Otherwise take such i < j and let k be
the least index greater than i with ak+1 > ak . If k = i + 1 then ak < ak−1 ; otherwise minimality gives
ak ≤ ak−1 . So there is a valley at k. The fall at i and the prefix give i ≥ klo , so k > klo ; the rise at k
and the tail give k < khi . Contradiction.
