261: We show that the Duval-Reiner conjecture on the majorization by the conjugate degree sequence of vertices fails in every dimension  r\geq 2 .
262: More precisely, for every  n\geq r+5 , we construct a pure  r -dimensional complex on  n  vertices that violates the conjectured inequality at the fifth partial sum.

279: An abstract simplicial complex, or simply a complex  K  on a finite set  V  is a collection of subsets of  V=V(K)  closed under inclusion.
280: Throughout the paper, all complexes or graphs are finite.
281: An element of  K  of cardinality  i+1  is called an  i -face or an  i -simplex of  K , and its dimension is  i .
310: Horak and Jost [13, 14] developed a general framework for weighted Laplace operators on simplicial complexes and established general eigenvalue bounds and interlacing inequalities.
311: Duval and Reiner [5] studied Laplacian spectra of shifted simplicial complexes, while Duval, Klivans, and Martin [4] proved the simplicial matrix-tree theorems.
312: Aharoni, Berger, and Meshulam [1] established inequalities relating the smallest eigenvalues of successive higher Laplacians of flag complexes and derived a spectral criterion for the vanishing of their reduced homology, and Lew [20] extended these inequalities to general complexes with bounded missing face dimension.
313: Lew [18] also obtained the lower bound for the smallest eigenvalue of the Laplacian for complexes with bounded missing face dimension and further conjectured the corresponding extremal construction.
314: Zhan, Huang, and Lin [24] subsequently proved this conjecture.
315:

318: 
319: Recent work has also studied the bound for the largest up-Laplacian eigenvalue of complexes.
320: Fan, Wu, and Wang [9] proved that the largest up-Laplacian eigenvalue is at most the spectral radius of the corresponding signless up-Laplacian, and characterized equality under  r -path connectedness.
321: Recently, Zhang and Fan [26] established a sharper upper bound for the largest up-Laplacian eigenvalue in terms of unions of neighborhoods of the  (r-1) -faces contained in an  r -face.
322: Signless Laplacians on simplicial complexes were introduced in [16] and have since been studied in several directions; see, for example, [8, 10, 21, 22, 25].
323: Normalized Laplacians and incidence balancedness were studied in [23].
