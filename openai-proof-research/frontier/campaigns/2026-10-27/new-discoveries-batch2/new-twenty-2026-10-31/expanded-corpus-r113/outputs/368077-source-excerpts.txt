656: 3.2. Saturated words
657: 
658: 
659: 
660: 
661: Given a number system  \mathcal{B}  over a finite set of digits, we say that a positive integer  n\geq 1  is saturated if its representation  \langle n\rangle_{\mathcal{B}}  in base  \mathcal{B}  is of length strictly less than that of  \langle m\rangle_{\mathcal{B}}  for all  m\geq n+1 . Accordingly, we say that  \langle n\rangle_{\mathcal{B}}  is a saturated word. For instance in base  10 , the saturated integers and corresponding words are the  n=10^{\ell}-1=9\cdots 9 .
