2011: According to Theorem 1.3,
2012:  {\mathscr{A}}_{G}  is free if and only if  G  is
2013: a path, a cycle, an almost path, or a path-with-triangle graph.
2014: For  G  a path graph,  {\mathscr{A}}_{G}  is supersolvable, by [CK25, Cor. 8.11], and supersolvable arrangements are inductively free,
2015: thanks to work of Jambu and Terao [JT84, Thm. 4.2].
2016: For  G  a cycle,
2017: Cuntz and Kühne show in
2018: [CK25, Prop. 3.1] that
2019:  {\mathscr{A}}_{G}  is inductively free.
2020: Thus in order to derive Conjecture 1.5 we need to show that  {\mathscr{A}}_{G}  is inductively free for
2021:  G  an almost path or a path-with-triangle graph.
