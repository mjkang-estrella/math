## Strongest sustained routes

**367758, stamp assignments.** Read the precise mountain/valley and same-side noncrossing definition, the deletion lemma, the original v2 experimental table and the current paper. Exhaustive generation counted every assignment through 18 stamps. At 18 stamps there are 184,940,388 legal layer orderings; the maximum count is 6,530. All maximizing assignments have the conjectured run structure and reproduce the original table. The authors already enumerated maximizers through 21, so this is replication. To go beyond that, exact fixed-assignment counts and single-bit neighborhood searches evaluated 42 assignments at size 22 and 96 at size 26, including assignments with forbidden runs. At size 30 the 105-second internal budget stopped after 62 completed assignment counts; the last neighborhood was incomplete. No refutation was established. A better invalid assignment than one allowed assignment would not establish a counterexample: all allowed assignments would still need to be excluded as global maximizers.

**375601, pinwheel scheduling.** Read Conjecture 8 and the finite-state interpretation. Complete DFS cycle decisions were made for every qualifying sorted instance in these boxes: 2–6 tasks with periods 2–12; 2–5 tasks with periods 3–20; 2–6 tasks with periods 4–16. There are also partial six-task and seven-task lexicographic sweeps, with exact stopping tuples in the logs. No counterexample was found. The decision algorithm explores age states and detects cycles; it does not mistake failure of a greedy schedule for unschedulability. The authors' experiments establishing their 5/6 theorem do not provide a simple scalar range against which these new boxes can uniformly be compared. The proposed minimum-period-dependent threshold remains unresolved.

**375764, design automorphisms.** Reconstructed Construction 4.1 and tested all 21 positive regular uniform labelled simple input hypergraph/e pairs with v = 2..5, e in {2,3}, ve <= 10 and the conjecture's k/e condition. Complete point-map backtracking using subset incidence counts found no fibre-breaking automorphism. A partial general argument recovers the fibres from pair codegrees for a 2-design input when the output is not a 2-design. That argument is a direct combination of the source's equations (9), (10) and Proposition 4.3 and is not eligible as a discovery. It leaves the important output 2-design case open. See `outputs/design-partial-proof.md`.
