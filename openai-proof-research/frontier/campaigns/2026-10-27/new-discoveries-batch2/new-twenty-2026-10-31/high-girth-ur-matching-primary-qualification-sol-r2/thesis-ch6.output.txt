  Again, (6.2) is tight for infinitely many trees [49]. In the next two chapters, we will
see that (6.2) essentially also holds for subcubic graphs.
  Note that the bound (6.2) is also a best-possible lower bound for the ordinary matching
number [49]. This suggests to study the ratio νur (G)/ν(G). We believe that, at least
asymptotically for large girth, the uniquely restricted matching number behaves like the
matching number.

Conjecture 6.4 ([37]). For every positive  and every positive integer ∆, there is some
positive integer g = g(, ∆), such that νur (G) ≥ (1 − )ν(G) for every graph G of
maximum degree at most ∆ and girth at least g.

from an old result from Bollobás [7] and Observation 1.3 (iii) that we can improve
Observation 1.3 (i) and (ii) by at most a logarithmic factor.
