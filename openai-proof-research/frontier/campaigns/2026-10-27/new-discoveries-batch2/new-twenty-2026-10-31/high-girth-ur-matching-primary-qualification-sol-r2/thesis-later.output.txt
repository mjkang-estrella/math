1.4 Upper bounds and approximation algorithms
Next, we briefly discuss upper bounds on the restricted matching numbers.

Observation 1.5. If G is a ∆-regular graph, then

  (i) νs (G) ≤ m(G)/(2∆ − 1) [87], and

 (ii) νac (G) ≤ (m(G) − 1)/(2∆ − 2) provided that ∆ ≥ 2.
