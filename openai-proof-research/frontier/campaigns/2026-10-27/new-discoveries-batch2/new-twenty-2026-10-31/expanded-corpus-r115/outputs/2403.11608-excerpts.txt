636: This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we shall introduce some basic definitions and notations for  q -series, Bailey pairs, Bailey lattices and integer partitions. In Section 3, by using Agarwal, Andrews and Bressoud’s Bailey lattice, we give the truncated forms for the Jacobi triple product theta series with odd and even basis, respectively. In Section 4, we will deduce the truncated versions for the three classical theta series and their corresponding partition function inequalities. In Section 5, we prove and extend Ballantine and Merca’s conjectures to  \ell -regular partitions with  \ell=3  or  \ell\geq 6 . We also show that the stronger conjecture on the truncated Jacobi triple product series holds when  R=3S  for  S\geq 1 . In Section 6, we remark the relations of truncated theta series with other Bailey chain and Bailey lattice.
