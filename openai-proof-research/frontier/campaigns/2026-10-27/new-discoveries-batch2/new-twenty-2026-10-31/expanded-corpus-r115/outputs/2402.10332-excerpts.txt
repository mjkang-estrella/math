652: A key consequence of Hogancamp’s Theorem 1.1 is that there is a categorification  Q_{n}  (the “symmetric projector”) of a ‘normalization’  (\prod_{k=2}^{n}(1-q^{2k}))p_{n}  of  p_{n}\in TL_{n} , by clearing denominators. This  Q_{n}  is a finite chain complex, obtained from  P_{n}  by taking the iterated mapping cone of all of the  U_{i}  for  1\leq i\leq n . The  \beta_{n,k}  obstructions do not all vanish, so that there does not appear to be a spectral version of these  Q_{n} . Indeed, since  \beta_{3,1}\not\simeq 0 , we have that  Q_{3}  does not have a lift to spectra in a suitable sense. However it seems natural to ask if there is a polynomial  f(q)  such that there is a natural finite  \mathcal{H}_{2n} -module spectrum, the decategorification of whose chain complex is  f(q)p_{n}\in TL_{n} . As a particular form of this, we conjecture:
