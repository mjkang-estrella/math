A uniform disk bound for wave-kernel Padé approximants

Let $$f(z) = \cosh\sqrt z = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^j}{(2j)!},$$ an entire function. For an integer $m \ge 1$ let $r_m = P_m/Q_m$ be its diagonal Padé approximant at $0$: $\deg P_m, \deg Q_m \le m$, $Q_m(0) = 1$ and $Q_m(z) f(z) - P_m(z) = O(z^{2m+1})$, with $r_m$ taken as a reduced rational function. Nadukandi and Higham base an algorithm for the wave-kernel matrix functions $\cosh\sqrt A$ and $\operatorname{sinhc}\sqrt A$, where $\operatorname{sinhc} z = \sinh z/z$, on these approximants. In their error analysis, the bound below lets the condition $\rho(A) \le 3$ on the spectral radius replace $\rho(I - r_m(A)) \le 2$. They proved, from the coefficients of $P_m$ and $Q_m$ computed symbolically, that for $1 \le m \le 20$ the approximant $r_m$ has no pole in the disk $|z| \le 3$ and satisfies the bound there.

Problem. Is it true that, for every $m \ge 1$, $r_m$ has no pole in $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| \le 3\}$ and $$|1 - r_m(z)| \le 2 \qquad (|z| \le 3)?$$

Source: Nadukandi and Higham (2018), Conjecture 4.6.
