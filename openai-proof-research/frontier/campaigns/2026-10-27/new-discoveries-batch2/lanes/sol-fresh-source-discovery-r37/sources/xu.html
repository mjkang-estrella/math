    I am a professor in the Mathematics Department at the University of Oregon. You can view a picture of me if you have the time.

    Research

    My research is in analysis (approximation theory, harmonic analysis, orthogonal polynomials, numerical analysis, special functions). My interest is mostly on higher dimensional problems, such as orthogonal polynomials of several variables, cubature formulas, and mutivariable approximation.
