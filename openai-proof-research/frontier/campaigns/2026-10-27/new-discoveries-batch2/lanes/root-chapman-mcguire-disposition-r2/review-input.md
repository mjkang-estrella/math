## Mathematical verdict: PASS for both routes

I wrote checker.py independently, importing neither scout nor root code and using only coefficient-list arithmetic over F3. It verifies 29 nonvacuous assertions. checker-evidence.json records exact residues, irreducibility certificates, gcds, and both sides of Eq. (12). The final successful execution is checker-run.log. Earlier reviewer mistakes and runs are retained separately in failed-attempts.md and the failed logs.

Route A is sound. For g=Z^7+Z^6-Z^4-Z^2-Z-1, successive Frobenius residues prove Z^(3^7)=Z modulo g. The gcd with Z^3-Z is 1. Every irreducible factor therefore has degree dividing 7; degree 7 is prime, and no linear factors exist. This proves irreducibility without a search assumption. Thus K=F3[Z]/g is a field of size 3^7. It embeds in F_(3^14), since 7 divides 14. The identity Z^11+Z+1=g(Z)(Z^4-Z^3+Z^2-1) is checked in F3[Z]. Hence for x=Z mod g, R_2(x)=x+x^11=-1. The explicit residues of alpha=x^8 and alpha^5 agree with PROOF.md. They show alpha!=0,1, alpha^5!=1, and U=1-alpha^4!=0. Also V=1-alpha!=0. Since g(0)!=0, x!=0.

The direct derivation avoids any converse of Theorem 3. R^3=R gives xU=x^3 V, so U=x^2 V. Raising to the ninth power gives U^9=x^18 V^9=alpha^2 x^2 V^9. Consequently U/U^9=alpha^(-2)V/V^9, literally Eq. (12) at q=3,d=2. Its denominators are all nonzero. Eq. (13) follows from alpha^((3^14-1)/8)=x^(3^14-1)=1. The checker also verifies Eqs. (5)–(11), including the nonzero Eq. (8) denominator (it is U^3). No source theorem converse or Hilbert 90 converse is used.

Route B is independently sound. Write T(A)=1+A+A^2+A^3 and H(A)=A^7+A^6+A^5-A^4+A^3-A^2-1. The exact factorization is

    T(A)^4-A = (A-1)(A^4+A^3+A^2+A+1)H(A).

H is irreducible by the same prime-degree Frobenius criterion, and gcd(H,A^5-1)=gcd(H,A^4-1)=1; H(0)!=0. A root a in F_(3^7) therefore avoids 0,1, all fourth roots, and all fifth roots. T(a)^4=a ensures T(a)!=0. A root x of X^2-T(a) exists in an extension of F_(3^7) of degree at most 2, hence can be placed in F_(3^14). Then x^8=a. The equality x^2(1-a)=1-a^4 yields R_2(x)^3=R_2(x) directly. Eq. (12) follows by the direct division argument above, Eq. (13) follows by the ambient field order, and a^5!=1. The elimination polynomial quoted by the scout is also divisible by g. Neither irreducibility of that degree-14 polynomial nor claims about all its components are needed.

## Substantive verdict: full routine bridge established

This exclusion is not based on proof length, computational ease, standard tools alone, or the fact that q=3 is small. The source already provides the entire compatibility reduction needed here, and the remaining construction has no unresolved bridge:

1. Eq. (6), p.4, specializes to x^2=T(alpha). Raising to the fourth power and using alpha=x^8 gives T(alpha)^4=alpha. More strongly, Section 6 p.14 explicitly performs the q+1 power step to obtain Eq. (16). Although that section assumes n=6 for subsequent restrictions, this power step uses no n=6 property. At q=3, Eq. (16) is alpha(alpha-1)^4=(alpha^4-1)^4. Dividing by (alpha-1)^4 gives precisely T(alpha)^4=alpha. Thus the compatibility polynomial is already the source's equation, rather than a new mechanism introduced by the scout.
2. Specialize that known equation to F3 and divide out (A-1)(A^4+A^3+A^2+A+1)=A^5-1. The residual polynomial is the displayed H. Polynomial multiplication and the two gcd computations establish that H has roots and none are forbidden. A new structural theorem or a conjectural factor pattern is unnecessary. Irreducibility identifies degree 7 and the specific n=14, but is unnecessary if only some even finite ambient extension is wanted.
3. Adjoin a root a of H and a square root x of T(a). These are finite algebraic extensions of F3, so some even finite extension contains both. With the checked irreducibility, F_(3^14) suffices exactly. x^8=a follows identically from T(a)^4=a; it is not an assumed compatibility condition.
4. x^2=T(a) supplies Eq. (5), hence the actual twisted-trace condition by direct Frobenius calculation. The checked gcds make every rational denominator legitimate. Finite-field multiplicative order supplies Eq. (13), so adding that equation does not obstruct the construction when n is free. The field embedding, allowed divisibility d|n, odd characteristic, and non-fifth-root conclusion are all now proved.

The source itself imposes a small ambient field in Section 6, then applies its n=6 norm restriction. Removing that particular bound while retaining the question's actual quantifiers and adjoining roots of its own equation gives a complete classical finite-field adaptation. The direct g certificate makes the same construction explicit; it does not add another substantive mechanism. In my judgment this meets the campaign's exclusion of routine corollaries/adaptations. It remains a correct refutation of an explicitly conjectured universal statement, and I do not claim the authors stated the refutation or that an earlier publication contains it.
