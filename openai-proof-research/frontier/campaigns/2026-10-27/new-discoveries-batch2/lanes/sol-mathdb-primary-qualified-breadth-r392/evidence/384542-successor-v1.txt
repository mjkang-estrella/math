                                                  A BSTRACT. In this note we establish an expression for the Steklov spec-
                                                  trum of warped products in terms of auxiliary Steklov problems for
                                                  drift Laplacians with weight induced by the warping factor. As an ap-
                                                  plication, we show that a compact manifold with connected bound-
                                                  ary diffeomorphic to a product admits a family of Riemannian met-
                                                  rics which coincide on the boundary, have fixed volume and arbitrar-
                                                  ily large first non-zero Steklov eigenvalue. These are the first examples
                                                  of Riemannian metrics with these properties on three-dimensional
                                                  manifolds.




                                                                            1. Introduction

                                              Let (M, g M ) be a smooth connected compact Riemannian manifold of
                                           dimension m ≥ 2 with boundary Σ = ∂M, and consider the Laplace oper-
                                           ator ∆ be acting on smooth functions on M. The Dirichlet-to-Neumann
                                           map D∆ : C ∞ (Σ) → C ∞ (Σ) associated to ∆ is defined by D∆ f = ∂ν f¯, where
                                           ν is the outward normal along the boundary Σ and where the function
                                           f¯ ∈ C ∞ (M) is the unique harmonic extension of f to the interior of M.
                                           Because it is an elliptic pseudodifferential operator, D∆ is essentially self-
                                           adjoint. Its closure (which we also denote D∆ ) has non-negative, un-
                                           bounded discrete spectrum σD∆ (M):
                                                       0 = σ0 (M, g M ) < σ1 (M, g M ) ≤ σ2 (M, g M ) ≤ · · · → ∞,
                                           where each eigenvalue is repeated according to its multiplicity. The eigen-
                                           values of D∆ are known as Steklov eigenvalues of M. Their interplay with
                                           the geometry of M is an active area of investigation. See [11, 7] for sur-
                                           veys.
                                             The general question that motivates this paper is to know how large
                                           the spectral gap σ1 (M, g ) > 0 can be for metrics g that are prescribed on
                                           the boundary ∂M. More precisely, let g M be the Riemannian metric of M.
                                           The question is to compute, or estimate, the following quantity:

For surfaces, the determination of the exact value of the supremum (1)
has been the subject of intense activity over the last ten years. See [7,

fold M of dimension at least 3, there exists a family of positive smooth

   For manifolds M of dimension at least 4 with boundary ∂M that ad-

terior derivative d ξ, Cianci and Girouard [3] constructed a family of met-
                                                  ε→0

these manifolds satisfy S(M, g M ) = +∞. The proof is inspired by work
of Bleecker [2], establishing the existence of Riemannian metrics with
fixed volume and arbitrarily large spectral gap on any closed manifold
of dimension at least three, admitting a Killing vector field satisfying the
above condition. Because the construction in [3] is adapted from that of
Bleecker on the boundary of the manifold, the dimension of the bound-
ary needs to be at least 3 in this construction.
  The purpose of this paper is to present two new families of examples
where S(M, g ) = +∞. Our method provides the first three-dimensional
examples, but also higher-dimensional examples that are different from
those of [3]. In our first result, not only the volume Vol(M, g M ) is pre-
served, but the volume element dVg M is also preserved.

Theorem 2. Let (M, g M ) be the Riemannian product of a compact mani-
fold B n with boundary and a closed manifold F k , where n > k ≥ 1. Then
for any ε > 0 there exists a Riemannian metric g ε on M that coincides
with g M in a neighborhood of ∂M, and satisfies dVg ε = dVg M in M and
σ1 (M, g ε ) → +∞ as ε → 0.

  It is noteworthy that the assumption on the dimension of the factors is
essential. For instance, if B is an interval and F is a circle, then the prod-
uct manifold M = B × F is a cylinder, and hence, it follows from Kokarev’s
inequality (2) that σ1 (M, g )L g (∂M) ≤ 8π.
   By establishing extensions of Theorem 2 involving the mixed Steklov-
Neumann spectrum, we are also able to study compact Riemannian man-
ifolds with connected boundary isometric to a Riemannian product. In
this setting, we show a result similar to Theorem 2, where the Riemann-
ian metrics have fixed volume instead of fixed volume element.

Theorem 3. Let (M, g M ) be a compact Riemannian manifold with con-
nected boundary ∂M that is isometric to the product of two closed Rie-
mannian manifolds of positive dimensions. Then for any ε > 0 there exists
a Riemannian metric g ε on M which coincides with g M on ∂M, satisfying
Vol(M, g ε ) = Vol(M, g M ) and σ1 (M, g ε ) → +∞ as ε → 0.

   It is worth to point out that Theorem 3 is applicable to a much larger
class of manifolds than Theorem 2. Indeed, no asumption is made on
the structure of the interior of the manifold and the only dimensional

constraint is positivity. For instance, Theorem 3 applies to any three-
dimensional Riemannian manifold M with boundary ∂M that is isomet-
ric to a torus.

1.1. The Steklov spectrum of warped products. Let B be a compact Rie-
mannian manifold of dimension n ≥ 1 with boundary ∂B and let F be a
closed Riemannian manifold of dimension k ≥ 1. Given a positive func-
tion h ∈ C ∞ (B), the warped product M = B ×h F is the product manifold
B × F endowed with the Riemannian metric g B + h 2 g F . The Laplacian on
a warped product is closely related to a diffusion operator L h on the base
manifold B determined by the warping function h ∈ C ∞ (B). Indeed for
f ∈ C ∞ (B × F ), we have that
                           ∆M f = L h f + h −2 ∆F f ,
where L h = ∆B − gradB ln h k . More precisely, this diffusion operator is
given by
                 L h f = ∆B f − g B (gradB ln h k , gradB f ).
Some relations between the spectra of these operators in the setting of
non-compact Riemannian manifolds have been established in [16, 15].
Motivated by them, we give an expression for the Steklov spectrum of
a warped product in terms of this diffusion operator, which is a natural
generalization of the standard description of the Steklov spectrum of a
Riemannian product.
  Denote by σ∆ (F ) = {λ j } j ≥0 the spectrum of the Laplacian on F . For
each λ ∈ σ∆ (F ), consider the auxiliary Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator
Dh,λ : C ∞ (∂B) → C ∞ (∂B) defined by Dh,λ f = ∂ν f¯, where this time f¯ is the

                     4. Large eigenvalues on product manifolds

   The aim of this section is to prove Theorem 2 and Theorem 3. In or-

2 involving the mixed Steklov-Neumann spectrum. Although it is not
assumed in Theorem 2, throughout most of this section, we focus on

for our purposes.
