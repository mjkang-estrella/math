OP-00880: Exact diameter of the leaf-slide graph on labelled trees — clopen.solutions clopen.solutions Problems Subjects Papers About Sign in Subjects › CO OP-00880 Open Exact diameter of the leaf-slide graph on labelled trees Submit a result For $n \ge 3$ let $G_n$ be the graph whose vertices are the trees on the labelled vertex set $[n] = \{1, \dots, n\}$, two trees being adjacent when one is obtained from the other by a single leaf slide: for a leaf $v$ with neighbour $w$ and a neighbour $u \ne v$ of $w$, delete the edge $\{v, w\}$ and insert $\{v, u\}$. Labels are fixed and each slide counts once, so the distance between two trees is the least number of slides transforming one into the other. $G_n$ is the $1$-skeleton of Kozlov's network realignment complex, which deformation retracts onto the complete graph $K_n$; in particular $G_n$ is connected. Klatt, Papadopoulos and Raffelsiefen proved $$\left\lfloor \frac{3n^2 - 4n}{8} \right\rfloor \le \operatorname{diam}(G_n) \le \frac{n^2}{2} - n \quad (n \text{ even}), \qquad \left\lfloor \frac{3n^2 - 6n}{8} \right\rfloor \le \operatorname{diam}(G_n) \le \frac{n^2 - 1}{2} - n \quad (n \text{ odd}),$$ so that $\operatorname{diam}(G_n) \le \lfloor n^2/2 \rfloor - n$ for every $n$, and found by computation that the upper bound is the diameter for $6 \le n \le 9$. Problem. Is $$\operatorname{diam}(G_n) = \left\lfloor \frac{n^2}{2} \right\rfloor - n$$ for every integer $n \ge 6$? Source: Kozlov (2025), Open Question 2.5, who asks for a formula for the diameter; the value is the upper bound of Klatt, Papadopoulos and Raffelsiefen (2026), whose computations suggest that it is attained for n ≥ 6.
