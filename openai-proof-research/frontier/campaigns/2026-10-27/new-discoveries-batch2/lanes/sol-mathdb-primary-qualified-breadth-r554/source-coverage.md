For k602 there is an elementary full-scope reduction, sufficient to reject it as low-hanging discovery work under the strict routine-adaptation standard. Let caustic semiaxes be A>B>0 and focal distance c with c²=A²-B². Each billiard side is tangent to it, at (A cos t,B sin t), with line x cos(t)/A + y sin(t)/B=1. Both foci are inside the caustic. Distances q+ and q- from (+/-c,0) to this same line have product
(1-c² cos²(t)/A²)/(cos²(t)/A²+sin²(t)/B²)=B²,
since the numerator equals sin²(t)+(B²/A²)cos²(t), which is B² times the denominator. Thus product_i q1,i product_i q2,i=B^(2N), for every N and every such polygon. This uses only tangent equation and point-line distance, with no periodicity machinery. Exact rational sanity check for A=5,B=3,t with cos t=3/5,sin t=4/5 gives 9. This is classical focal tangent algebra, not an eligible new result; no claim that an explicitly named k602 proof was found in a public body. No formal certificate, prior clearance, or distinct-family credit asserted.
