##### 315991 | Babai's problem | status=open type=open_problem complete=full sols=0 formal=None
URL: https://mathdb.com/p/315991/babai-s-problem
SOURCE: Wikipedia | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babai%27s_problem | stated:1979 doc:1979
Let $G$ be a finite group with identity $1$, and let
$\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote the set of irreducible complex characters of $G$.
For a generating subset $S \subseteq G \setminus \{1\}$, let
$\operatorname{Cay}(G,S)$ be the Cayley digraph with vertex set $G$ and arc set
$\{(g, sg) : g \in G,\ s \in S\}$; when $S = S^{-1}$ this digraph is identified
with the (undirected) Cayley graph of $G$ with respect to $S$. Two such digraphs
are isomorphic, written
$\operatorname{Cay}(G,S) \cong \operatorname{Cay}(G,S')$, if there is a
bijection of their vertex sets carrying arcs onto arcs. For a positive integer
$\nu$ put

$$
M_{\nu}^{S} = \left\{ \sum_{s \in S} \chi(s) \;\middle|\; \chi \in \operatorname{Irr}(G),\ \chi(1) = \nu \right\}.
$$

Then for every finite group $G$, every positive integer $\nu$, and all
generating subsets $S, S'$ of $G \setminus \{1\}$,

$$
\operatorname{Cay}(G,S) \cong \operatorname{Cay}(G,S') \implies M_{\nu}^{S} = M_{\nu}^{S'};
$$

that is,
$M_{\nu}^{S}$ depends only on the isomorphism type of the graph $\operatorname{Cay}(G,S)$.

A finite group
$G$ for which this implication holds for all inverse-closed generating subsets $S,
T \subseteq G \setminus \{1\}$ and all positive integers $\nu$ is called a
BI-group; determine the finite groups that are BI-groups.
