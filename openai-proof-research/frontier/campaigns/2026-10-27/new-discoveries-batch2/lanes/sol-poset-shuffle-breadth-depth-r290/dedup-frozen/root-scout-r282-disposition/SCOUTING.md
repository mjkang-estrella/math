## Complete applicable routine bridge for item9

Let A=[[1,1/2],[1/2,1]], D=diag(1,-1), C=A+epsilon I and B=DCD, with 0<epsilon<1. All are positive definite. Z commutes with D, so det(I+BZ)=det(I+CZ) and det(I+B)=det(I+C). Since A<C, the actually inspected B Proposition4.15 yields f_A triangleleft f_C=f_B in exactly the source order defined by chains of proper position and closure (Definition4.1). But B-A=[[epsilon,-1],[-1,epsilon]] has eigenvalue epsilon-1<0. This proves failure with unequal normalized polynomials (their expected size increases), not just reflexivity or a strict/non-strict wording issue. The identity is a complete source adaptation, so it does not receive credit. It also explains why merely inspecting a new source ID would have been misleading.
