                                               FROM BASEL PROBLEM TO MULTIPLE ZETA VALUES

                                                                            MASATO KOBAYASHI

                                                 Abstract. We show many consequences of the proof of Basel problem by Boo
                                                 Rim Choe (1987) to central binomial series, multiple zeta values and some other
                                                 multiple sums. The main idea is to evaluate integrals involving powers of arcsine
                                                 function.
