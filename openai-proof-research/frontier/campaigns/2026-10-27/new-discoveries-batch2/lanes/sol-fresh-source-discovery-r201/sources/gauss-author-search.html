A Riemannian metric and an affine connection determine three midpoint maps associated with the connection, its metric dual, and the Levi--Civita connection. We also give non-flat examples with a Euclidean metric and a parallel cubic form in every dimension larger than one. In dimension one, we characterize the invariance completely by using the Matkowski--Sutô equation. For Euclidean dual pairs, we conjecture that the invariance implies that the cubic form is constant.
