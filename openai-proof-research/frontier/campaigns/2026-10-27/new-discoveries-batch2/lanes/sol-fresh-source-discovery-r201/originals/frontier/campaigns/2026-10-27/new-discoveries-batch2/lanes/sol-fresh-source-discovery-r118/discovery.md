## Three attributed full-scope questions

1. **Melbourne–Tkocz, arXiv:2005.10930v1, Conjecture 5.1, Section 5.**
   For every finite positive monotone log-concave sequence x, is
   t -> log(t sum_i x_i^t) concave on the whole interval t>0?
   Actual source pp7–8 read, with its varentropy equivalence. Exact public
   exclusion: Melbourne–Palafox-Castillo, arXiv:2111.06997v1, Theorem 1.1
   covers monotone log-concave summable sequences, hence the finite claim.
   Actual theorem and Section 3 final proof assembly read: matched geometric
   p-moments, moment majorization, geometric concavity and interpolation.
   This was already screened in original r74 and r84. Not fresh.
   Its adjacent Conjecture 5.2 concerns positive monotone concave y and
   log((t+gamma) sum y_i^(t/gamma)) on t>-gamma, gamma>0. Actual successor
   Section 4 gives a two-term counterexample. At gamma=1 and y=(1/10,11/10),
   the midpoint log-concavity deficit K(1)^2-K(0)K(2) equals -39/25.
   checks.py compares the literal three values with the independently expanded
   polynomial 4 lambda²+4 lambda-2. This is verification of public work,
   with no correction/helper/endpoint credit. The source's displayed HTML
   counterexample equations have transcription issues; the direct literal
   calculation avoids relying on those typographical forms.

2. **Shannon/Rice–Massey Weak Simplex Conjecture**, precisely restated by
   Mulgund, arXiv:2607.14087: for every d>=1, energy E>0 and positive Gaussian
   noise variance, among d+1 equiprobable signals in R^d of equal squared
   norm E, the regular simplex maximizes correct maximum-likelihood decoding
   probability. This is the unrestricted dimension/noise claim, not a fixed
   instance or dimension-constrained replacement. Mulgund v2 Theorem 2.1,
   its actual Section 6 proof, and latest v4 Theorem 2.1/Corollary 2.6 were
   inspected. The covariance comparison assumes R-J/m positive semidefinite;
   adaptive tilting yields the common-threshold orthant inequality, and the
   coding reduction supplies this exact optimization assertion. Latest v4
   includes equality information. Su–Yang–Xu–I arXiv:2608.19093v1 was also
   frozen as the adjacent public equality-case source; no new uniqueness
   work is proposed. Public full-scope claims predate this lane. Exclude;
   the lane does not independently audit all auxiliary lemmas or formalizations.
   A defect in one announced proof would not by itself clear all other prior
   claims, nor give this lane a new proof. No smaller signal instance is credited.

3. **Strong Gaussian product question**, Russell–Sun arXiv:2205.02127v3,
   introduction (1.1), attributed there to Li–Wei: for every n, centered
   real Gaussian vector X (including singular laws) and alpha_i>=0, is
   E product |X_i|^alpha_i >= product E |X_i|^alpha_i?
   Actual introduction, known partial-scope distinctions and general statement
   read. Exact public exclusion: Ouimet–Greaves arXiv:2609.20234v1 Theorem 1
   proves all strictly positive exponents, arbitrary covariance, and equality
   iff independence when all marginal variances are positive. Zero powers
   remove coordinates, with the usual zeroth-power convention. Actual entire
   Theorem 1 proof was read: orthant minimizers of a strictly convex log-barrier,
   principal-minor orthogonality, branch weights with second-moment identity,
   Jensen's inverse-square kernel bound, positive integral recovery, and the
   singular-covariance limit. This is already a complete public claim covering
   the screened problem. It is not the log-concave-function product inequality
   in the preceding simplex paper. Exclude; no attempt to earn credit from
   all-even powers, finite SOS instances or formalizing the public proof.
