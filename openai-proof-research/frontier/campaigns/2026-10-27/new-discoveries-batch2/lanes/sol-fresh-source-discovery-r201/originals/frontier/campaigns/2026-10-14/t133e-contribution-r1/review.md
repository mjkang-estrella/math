## Verdict

**TECHNICAL** for the FULL unchanged T133E scope (scope-freeze.md, SHA256 `3b7f81a5…ba74`).

* **Not DUPLICATE.** No pinned statement and no primary statement I read asserts EXPTIME-hardness of supplied-pair k-WL equivalence on bounded-degree graphs with a uniform edge-expansion lower bound. The source's subcubic outputs have private paths of unbounded length, so they are not uniform expanders. The three companions are fixed-k on diameter-two graphs, identification over all mates, and parity lifts. Codenotti–Schoenebeck–Snook (CSS) give existential expander pairs for a hierarchy lower bound, not a complexity reduction. Lichter–Raßmann–Schweitzer (LRS) give coNP-hardness with no expansion statement. This is a finding within the stated reading limits, not a priority certificate.
* **Not INELIGIBLE.** The target is the frozen full statement. The freeze followed a construction outline, and this is disclosed. Chronology is consistent (§7). There is no earlier project chain on source 133 (§7). No narrowing is proposed or needed.
* **Not BLOCKED.** All identity checks pass (§1). The decisive source operations were read in their actual pinned bodies: the uncoloring, ownership, lifts, recognition, game interface, and the companion hub construction.
* **Not SUBSTANTIAL.** The new content beyond the source is a choice of color palette. The source encodes a sort number c by a private path of length c, read off by a forced pebble walk. T133E encodes it by adjacency to station c of one shared, degree-tagged Margulis–Gabber–Galil frame, read off by a forced pebble walk from a unique-degree root. Every other step is either a source operation used unchanged (block ownership, one-offset lifts, the generic game interface, reached-history recognition, the four-slot readout walk, dimension capping) or a classical tool (explicit expander, degree tags, bounded-cell expansion transfer). The two properties the palette must have do not interact anywhere in the proof. Station identification works for any connected, port-labelled, bounded-degree frame with a unique root; expansion is a property of the chosen frame plus the bounded-cell lemma. Neither argument uses the other. That is the signature of a routine combination of credited tools. It is not a new mechanism.

## 2. Exact target compared

The unchanged full T133E is the language E of triples (G,H,k). G and H are equal-positive-order, connected, simple, undirected, uncolored graphs given as adjacency matrices, with maximum degree ≤ 101 and edge Cheeger constant h ≥ 1/200000 (h of a singleton is infinity). k ≥ 2 is binary, and (G,H,k) is in E exactly when G and H are joint-update k-WL equivalent with common color names and separate histograms. Out-of-class inputs are rejected. The claim is that E is EXPTIME-complete under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions, and also with unary k. Every hardness output, on yes and no instances alike, satisfies the graph restrictions. The relevant comparison is therefore with the source's subcubic theorem. T133E replaces "maximum degree ≤ 3" by "maximum degree ≤ 101 and a uniform expansion lower bound" and keeps the same hardness mechanism.

## 3. What the pinned source family already pays (actual bodies)

Line numbers refer to the canonical blobs in the author packet's `private/preprints/`, which are byte-identical to the pinned tree. "VD" means the Variable-dimension manuscript.

* **Ownership and one-offset lifts for added vertices.** VD §06, lines 305–308: "Every new vertex made at v belongs to v's block part." Lines 336–367 build lifts Ψ_{B,ξ_B} on whole parts that depend only on B's offset. Lines 359–360 say: "Crucially, the third prescription does not require the neighboring block's offset." Lines 369–380 show that compatible unions are induced isomorphisms and that "edges leaving the union impose no condition on unchosen offsets."
* **The generic game interface.** VD §07, lines 17–56 (`prop:game-reduction`). Its hypotheses concern arbitrary graphs W^0 and W^1 with block partitions, offset-local maps and decoding sets. The forward proof (lines 66–120) extends the offsets separately for each inactive block, announces one whole bijection, and notes: "No second inactive block becomes active during this move." This is the "one-active-block whole-bijection quantifier at exact r" that T133E inherits. T133E only re-verifies the two hypotheses.
* **Exact width with bounded live slots, via reached-history tests.** VD §06, lines 394–402 (the principle) and 404–521 (`lem:graph-recognition`). Degree invariance with one extra slot is at lines 430–434. In sort recovery (lines 451–475), a forced walk along a private path of unbounded length uses three live slots and is "forced by adjacency and inequality". Underlying adjacency is at lines 477–491. The shared readout uses four slots along `graph:readout-walk` (lines 493–520). The source realization already preserves exact dimension r (`cor:subcubic-game`, lines 534–548).
* **Degree tags via private leaves.** VD §05, lines 29–34 and 99–108: a vertex of block j gets j(N+1) leaves, and the disjoint degree intervals identify blocks under every winning strategy.
* **A fixed shared frame vertex under announced bijections.** Fixed-dimension companion, `02-compression.tex` lines 534–575. A hub adjacent to every block part, together with block-owned marks, gives graphs of diameter two. The hub is identified by degree, and Duplicator "Match[es] the hubs". Marks map "to the mark with the same local number at its translated owner", and "any selection activates at most one new block." This is the closest family antecedent for T133E's frame vertices: fixed, shared vertices adjacent to whole invariant fibers, with leaves mapped by local index. T133E's stations are a bounded-degree, many-hub version of this pattern. The author's reading limits show that this file was not read (only `main.tex` lines 1–180).
* **Sort sizes and uniformity.** VD §08, lines 167–175: a channel has at most 2^6 vertices per level and boundedly many sorts. `lem:dimension-cap` and `prop:membership` (VD §02, lines 36–100) give the capped upper bound after checking the input graph conditions.

## 5. Primary comparison

Primary line numbers refer to my own `pdftotext` (default mode) extractions in `/var/tmp/t133e-contrib-r1/primary/`, made from byte-verified PDFs. They differ from the author's `.txt` line numbering.

* **CSS, arXiv 1401.0758.** Theorem 3.1 gives uncolored CFI pairs Y_f(G) and Y_g(G) over random 3-regular G that are 0.01-expanders and fool linear-level Lasserre (hence WL). Lemma 3.4 and §5 transfer expansion through bounded clustering and stretching (Lemmas 5.2–5.3; I read their proofs, lines 1082–1321), plus a twist argument. So "WL-hard pairs that are bounded-degree uncolored expanders" and "expansion survives bounded local replacement" are both paid. CSS remove colors by simply dropping them, which their CFI structure permits. They prove no statement about a supplied-pair decision problem.
* **Chen–Flum–Liu, arXiv 2507.01459v1.** The introduction (about lines 95–120) states that "coloring can be easily replaced by adding some extra graph gadgets … But this might come with some price", in size and degree. Proposition 9.1 says that for a rigid base G the automorphisms of the uncolored Y(G) preserve colors. Theorem 9.3 makes the same-color relation first-order definable when the minimum degree is at least 3. This is close in spirit: a rigid structure makes the colors implicit. They do not use a shared frame or block offsets.
* **LRS, CSL 2025.** Theorems 3 and 19 give NP- and coNP-hardness with k in the input, over uncolored simple graphs and graphs with 4-bounded color classes, through CFI over cubic graphs with the cubic treewidth hardness. Theorem 4 and Corollary 22 give P-hardness for fixed k with 4-bounded abelian classes, via one-way switches. No expansion statement appears in the read portions. This is the closest prior complexity result with bounded color classes and uncolored variants. It concerns a weaker hardness class and makes no expansion claim.
* **OWWZ, arXiv 1401.2436 (found in the bounded search, not in the author packet).** Footnote 1 and §1 (lines 155–176) say: "It is often stated that the colors can be replaced by gadgets while keeping the number of vertices O(n). We do not find this to be immediately obvious," and they remove colors through robust asymmetry of random graphs. This is the strongest literature evidence that parameter-preserving color removal can be nontrivial. It does not help T133E. Their difficulty is linear size with an Ω(n) SOS degree. T133E has a polynomial size budget, and its width is protected by the source's own slot accounting, so no size/width trade-off arises.
* **Rooted-expander color encoding.** Bounded web search found no paper that uses a degree-labelled explicit expander as a shared color palette in a WL or isomorphism reduction (queries are listed in `reading-limits.md`). This is search absence, not theorem absence. It is also not needed for the verdict, because the verdict rests on the operation decomposition against paid source and classical operations, not on priority.

## 6. Strongest case for SUBSTANTIAL, and why it does not prevail

1. *The source's uncoloring provably destroys expansion, so something new was required.* True: a private path of length c has a cut of size one around Θ(c) vertices. But the replacement needs no new insight about the game. Swap the private label for a shared, individualized label, and pick the shared structure to be a known expander. The candidate's own §8 lists every ingredient as credited or elementary.
2. *Synchronizing the frame with whole-bijection announcements is delicate.* No. The source isolates exactly this in a generic interface (VD §07). The source already discharges a harder instance, with offset-dependent port permutations at block boundaries (VD §06, lines 336–380). The companion hub construction discharges the fixed-shared-vertex instance. Here, frame vertices are fixed pointwise and fibers are permuted within themselves, so station–fiber edges are trivially preserved. Candidate §4's remark that "adding arbitrary expander edges between valuation vertices would not have this property" is correct, but it rules out a design no one needed. It is not evidence of a new mechanism.
3. *Exact width r is preserved with constant live slots and unbounded rounds.* This was already true of the source's subcubic realization (`cor:subcubic-game` at exact r). The station walk has the same three-slot structure as the source's path walk.
4. *OWWZ show that color removal is not always routine.* See §5. Their obstacle (linear size with linear SOS degree) is absent here.
5. *The headline is new and has some motivation (GI and WL on expanders: CSS, OWWZ, Spielman).* Under the campaign rule, a new headline or a classical-tool combination does not suffice, and recent publication of the source is not weighed.

**What would have changed the verdict.** This is an illustration of the threshold, not a replacement target. The verdict would change if a step needed the expansion and the game recognition to constrain each other. An example would be outputs where degree tags or private leaves are unavailable, such as regular or degree-3 expander outputs, which the scope explicitly excludes. The frozen candidate contains no such step.

## 8. Objections

### Required (numbered)

1. **R1 — Classification.** The full T133E residual is TECHNICAL (§§4, 6). No record may count it toward 20 or describe the frame/sort-fiber bridge as a new mechanism. At most it is a new-to-project technical strengthening of the source's subcubic theorem.
2. **R2 — Attribution of ownership and whole-bijection synchronization.** The candidate's §4 and comparison.md present block ownership, offset-local whole-part maps and the preserved one-active-block quantifier order as part of the "proposed additional operation". Any retained record must credit them to VD §06 (lines 305–308, 336–380), VD §07 `prop:game-reduction` (lines 17–120), and the fixed-dimension companion's hub/marks construction (`02-compression.tex` lines 534–575).
3. **R3 — Attribution of station recognition.** The "three live slots, unbounded rounds" forced walk must be credited as the source's sort-recovery walk (VD §06 lines 451–475), moved from a private path to a rooted frame. Degree invariance must be credited to VD §06 lines 430–434 and VD §05 lines 91–97.
4. **R4 — Missing family comparator.** comparison.md and reading-limits.md omit the fixed-dimension companion's uncoloring section. That section is the closest family antecedent for fixed shared vertices under announced bijections. It must be read and cited before any attribution sentence is retained.
5. **R5 — Novelty wording.** The strongest supportable status is "new to this project; no read source or primary states it". Bounded search absence (§5) must not be phrased as literature priority. OWWZ (arXiv 1401.2436) must be added to the comparison as a color-removal comparator.
