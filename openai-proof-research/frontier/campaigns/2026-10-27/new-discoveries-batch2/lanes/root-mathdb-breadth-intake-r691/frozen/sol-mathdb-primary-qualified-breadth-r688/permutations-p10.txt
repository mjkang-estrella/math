We make the following three, increasingly general, conjectures:
Conjecture 1 (Bevan, Dijon 2023). If every pattern in B ends in 1, then the
scaling limit of Av(B) is the increasing permuton.
Conjecture 2 (Bevan, Oberwolfach 2024). If every pattern in B ends in 1 or
begins with its largest value, then the scaling limit of Av(B) is the increasing
permuton.
Conjecture 3 (Troyka). If every pattern in B is skew decomposable, then the
scaling limit of Av(B) is the increasing permuton.
