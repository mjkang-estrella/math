Abstract

    

We study entropic versions of four-function inequalities and give transport proofs of the Euclidean Ahlswede–Daykin theorem and
a four-function version of the Prékopa–Leindler inequality due to Cordero-Erausquin and Maurey.
One of our main results shows that the sharp range for the four-function inequality for equally weighted power means of finite orders 
α
,
β

is 
0
≤

+

≤
2

, with the Ahlswede–Daykin min–max inequality obtained as a limit.
En route, we prove corresponding entropy inequalities under the quadratic optimal transport coupling,
including an entropy power inequality for the minimum and maximum in Euclidean space.
We also study the stability of four-function inequalities under convolution with log-concave product densities,
recovering the preservation of log-supermodularity established by Karlin and Rinott, and
explain its application to a conditional entropy power inequality for log-supermodular random variables.
