Title:Integer-valued polynomials satisfying growth constraints
Authors:Avner Kiro, Alon Nishry            
View a PDF of the paper titled Integer-valued polynomials satisfying growth constraints, by Avner Kiro and 1 other authors
    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We consider polynomials which take integer values on the integers (IVPs), and satisfy an additional growth condition on the natural numbers. Elkies and Speyer, answering a question by Dimitrov, showed there is a critical exponential growth threshold, such that there are infinitely many IVPs with growth above the threshold and finitely many IVPs below that threshold (of arbitrary degree). In this paper, we give more refined estimates for the number of IVPs having exponential growth thresholds. In addition, we consider a similar problem, where there is a (not necessarily symmetric) growth condition on the integers. Notably, the critical threshold is determined by the logarithmic capacity of an explicit domain.
