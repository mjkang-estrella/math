          Some Bounds for the Ratios of Hypergeometric
               Functions with Shifted Parameters

                                              D. Karp

                               Institute of Applied Mathematics
                            7 Radio St., Vladivostok, 690041, Russia
                                 E-Mail: dmkrp@yandex.ru


  Abstract. We demonstrate two approaches to finding bounds for the ratios of hypergeometric
functions and studying their monotonicity. One is based on generalized Stieltjes transform represen-
tation, the other uses some recent log-concavity results for series. Besides, a conjecture asserting the
monotonicity of a quotient of products of confluent hypergeometric functions is made.

vice versa which leads inevitably to some loss of precision.

and obtaining the bounds for the ratios of the generalized hypergeometric functions
(1). One is based on generalized Stieltjes transform representation satisfied by q+1 Fq
when its denominator parameters majorize those of numerator introduced in [8] and the
other is related to log-concavity in parameters of functions defined as power series whose
coefficients involve shifted factorials of these parameters. The latter approach has been
recently investigated in [9].
