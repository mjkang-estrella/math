     Title: Enumeration of Nondegenerate $2 \times (k+1) \times k$ Hypermatrices 
     Authors: Brandon Koprowski ,  Joel Brewster Lewis              View a PDF of the paper titled Enumeration of Nondegenerate $2 \times (k+1) \times k$ Hypermatrices, by Brandon Koprowski and Joel Brewster Lewis 
     View PDF 
     HTML (experimental) 



     
             Abstract: We consider the problem of enumerating hypermatrices of format $2 \times (k + 1) \times k$ over a finite field that have nonzero hyperdeterminant and whose nonzero entries are restricted to a plane partition. We conjecture an attractive product formula for the enumeration, and prove it in many cases. In general, we show that the enumeration is given (up to a power of $q - 1$) by a polynomial in $q$ with nonnegative integer coefficients, whose value at $q = 1$ enumerates a natural family of three-dimensional rook placements.
