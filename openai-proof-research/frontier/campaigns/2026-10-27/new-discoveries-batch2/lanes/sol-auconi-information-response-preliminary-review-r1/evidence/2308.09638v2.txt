                                                                  The invariant response was defined from a formulation of the fluctuation-response theorem in
                                                               the space of probability distributions. An inequality is here conjectured which sets the mutual
                                                               information as an upper bound to the invariant response, and its large information limit is proven.
                                                               Applications to the thermodynamics of feedback control and to estimation theory are discussed.
