                                                              ON A CONJECTURE OF BERNDT AND KIM

                                                                     KATHRIN BRINGMANN AND AMANDA FOLSOM




arXiv:1112.4727v1 [math.NT] 20 Dec 2011
                                                  Abstract. We prove a recent conjecture of Berndt and Kim regarding the positivity of the
                                                  coefficients in the asymptotic expansion of a class of partial theta functions. This generalizes
                                                  results found in Ramanujans second notebook, and recent work of Galway and Stanley.

Conjecture (Berndt-Kim [2]). For any positive integer b, for sufficiently large n, the coeffi-
cients an in the asymptotic expansion (1.3), have the same sign.
The purpose of this note is to prove this conjecture of Berndt and Kim.
Theorem 1. The Berndt-Kim conjecture is true. More precisely, if b ≡ 1, 2 (mod 4), then
an > 0 for n ≫ 0, and if b ≡ 0, 3 (mod 4), then an < 0 for n ≫ 0.
