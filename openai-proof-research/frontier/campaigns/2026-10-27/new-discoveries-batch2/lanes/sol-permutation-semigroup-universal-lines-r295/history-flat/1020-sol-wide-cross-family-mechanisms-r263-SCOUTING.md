## Ranked survivors (provisional eligibility)

**1. Friedland–Goldman–Yomdin, 1909.04918v1, Section 4 example 2 p9; Definition 1.1 p2.** For every C>0, does there exist K(C)<infinity such that for every integer p>=1 and every analytic f(z)=sum a_k z^k with radius>1 satisfying |a_k|<=C k^p max_(0<=i<=p)|a_i| for every integer k>=p+1, f takes each complex value at most K(C)p times in |z|<1/2? **Complete ordinary refutation at C=1**, offered for review in PROOF.md. For t>=1, p=3^t, m=pt, f=z+3^m z^(m+1) has domination and pt+1 distinct half-disc zeros. Independent real-rooted product construction also refutes it. Closest actual proofs: source Theorem 2.3/Remark 2.4; Batenkov–Yomdin 1301.6033v2 Theorem 2.3 and 1411.7629v1 Theorem 2.2; Friedland–Yomdin1503.00325v1 Theorem 2.1; Roytwarf–Yomdin1997 Lemma2.2.3/Theorem2.3.2. They provide upper bounds depending on the entire coefficient sequence or a shrinking radius. No inspected theorem gives the lower witness or linear fixed-radius endpoint. **HOLD**: bounded history/title/author search found no exact prior resolution, but old sharpness literature and live proof registries are not cleared. Strongest objection is a known elementary/generic lower-bound example already implying the source negation. Decisive next step is independent prior-art/subsumption/source review, not a larger calculation. Theorem3.1's Borel transform claim is untouched.

**2. Khan–Khan–Saha–Zhao, 1909.01306v1, Conjecture11 p11.** For every pair of positive integers 1<a<n-1 with gcd(a,n)=1, is n/2<V(a,n)<3n/4, where V counts primitive integer points strictly inside the parallelogram spanned by (1,0),(a,n)? Closest actual source bodies: Theorem7's explicit a=2,n-2 formulas and Proposition10's upper bound under both a and ceil(n/a) even. The general exact model is V=#{1<=k<n:gcd(ceil(ak/n),k)=1}. Mechanism: primitive-ray charging combined with modular inversion a<->a^-1; no proof here. Strongest objection: geometric visible-point inequalities may already imply the entire interval, and no specialist citing-body sweep is complete. Decisive calculation: establish an injection from nonprimitive points to primitive points, with strict excess except the excluded boundary slopes, or derive a residue-class obstruction. No enlarged finite sweep. Current narrow arXiv query finds the source, not proof of current openness. Same-paper irrational-limit Conj12 is not a second family and its extension outside the initially coprime definition requires care.

**3. Pourhadi–Faghani, 1909.02634v1, Conjecture2.9.** For every finite undirected connected quasi-lambda-distance-balanced graph, where one fixed rational lambda>1 makes |W_uv|/|W_vu| either lambda or 1/lambda for every edge uv, must the sets of vertex degrees and transmissions each have cardinality exactly two? W_uv consists of vertices closer to u; D(u) is the source total-distance transmission. Closest source proof Theorem2.2 claims bipartiteness; its cyclic counting argument would itself need scrutiny before reliance. Mechanism in the bipartite case: |W_uv|=(|V|+D(v)-D(u))/2, hence constant ratio forces a constant absolute transmission difference on each edge. This only forces a layered integer potential, not immediately two levels or two degrees. Strongest objection: later work on quasi-distance-balanced graphs or a standard transmission theorem may settle this; narrow author queries are not a citing-body clearance. Decisive next calculation: construct a connected bipartite graph with three transmission levels but constant edge transmission difference, then verify the degree/transmission counts exactly. No such witness claimed. Related Problems2.21–2.22 are retained as one family, not separately ranked.

## Compact remaining screens

**4. Wakhare–Vignat, 1909.01508v2, Conjecture1 Section8.** For every integer ell>=3 and n>=1, ell^n R_(2n)(1/sqrt(ell))/(2ell-2) is integral. Definitions1–3 and the actual Theorem1 proof express R as integral Bell polynomials in Schett self-convolutions; each odd Schett specialization has a kk' factor and pairing the convolution terms supplies an even factor (the central binomial coefficient is even). Thus the natural bridge is R_(2n) in 2s(1-s)Z[s], degree<=n, s=k². Rational specialization then routinely gives the displayed integrality. **Do not nominate this without checking the full bridge and follow-up; likely source-specific routine polynomial divisibility, not a fresh substantive outcome.** No extra congruence conjecture inflated into a separate target.

**5. Silva–Ibiapina, 1909.01918v1, Question6.** Does any finite graph G admit nonzero vectors in R³ on edges with incident edges orthogonal, while needing four ordinary edge colors (pi'(G)=3<chi'(G)=4)? Actual Theorem4 constructs the gap in dimension4 with nine Kochen–Specker bases, using odd order to obstruct a perfect matching. That proof does not descend to dimension3. Park: would need an exact orthogonal realization of a class-II subcubic graph or an equivalence theorem ruling it out; planar bridgeless cubic graphs already fall to the Four Color Theorem. No claimed fresh approach beyond that barrier.

**6. Miller–Newlon, 1909.01938v1, Conjecture1.10.** The normalized number of uniformly randomly chosen legal moves in the Fibonacci Quilt game is conjectured to have a Gaussian limit as starting integer n tends to infinity. Exact normalization and the source's reference to a Zeckendorf endpoint require clarification against its FQ-legal stopping rule before nomination. Actual Theorem1.8's proof uses a decreasing square-root-index monovariant (with one exceptional move), proving termination; Theorem1.9 counts shortest games. Neither supplies variance growth or dependency control. Park: no martingale/regenerative decomposition establishing a central limit theorem, and not another random-subtraction family claim.

**7. Bulutoglu, 1909.02123v5, concluding discussion of Babai–Ronyai Problem7.1.** Find a polynomial-time decomposition into irreducible rational subrepresentations for a rational representation of a finite group, in the input model of the cited problem. Actual Theorems9–10 show why rational, rather than complex, decomposition bounds integer-hull dimensions; averaging gives an invariant affine translate and irreducible multiplicities restrict dimensions. Complex randomized decomposition is explicitly weaker. Reject investment: no new rational splitting algorithm or bit-complexity analysis; original Babai–Ronyai body not separately retrieved. This is not a claim that the input-model details are cleared.

**8. Sagan–Tirrell, 1909.02593v1, Conjecture6.1.** For every positive integer n, are there infinitely many positive b with cyclotomic value Phi_n(b) prime? Source reduction formulas in Theorem5.1 and Lucas-atom coefficient Lemma6.2 prove algebraic identities/divisibility, not primality. Reject: includes the longstanding prime-polynomial-value barrier, no new sieve mechanism. No finite prime list nominated.

**9. Moss, 1909.02709v3, Conjecture9.1/9.3 (one family).** Under the literal automorphic setup of9.1 (small U, allowed unramified v0, non-Eisenstein maximal ideal m and f in S(U,k)[m]), must every irreducible submodule of the cyclic local module generated by f be generic? Actual Theorem9.2 gives the spherical Hecke eigencharacter; Corollary9.4 equates this to genericity of the cyclic module, and9.5 reduces the quasi-banal case to dimension n! of the Iwahori-generated module. Park: reduction does not prove genericity, no mechanism controlling global congruence submodules. Original CHT statement not separately fetched; no sweeping Ihara result claimed.

**10. Bibby–Odesky–Wang–Wang–Zhang–Zheng, 1909.03303v3, Conjecture5.6.** Every flag simplicial3-manifold Delta should satisfy gamma2(Delta)>=16 beta1(Delta). Source Section5 constructs flag connected sums/handle additions attaining the suggested slope; these are upper examples, not a lower theorem. Park: no local-to-global link charging that supplies beta1 uniformly; do not replace it by sphere-only Gal gamma2>=0 or a triangulation census. No new approach to the remaining manifold inequality.

**11. Bell–Hamidizadeh–Huang–Venegas, 1909.04023v1, Question4.1.** For every positive-characteristic field k and affine domain A of GK dimension1 in which k is inseparably closed, is A cancellative? Actual preceding Makar-Limanov/Hasse-Schmidt derivation analysis constrains a counterexample to an Azumaya algebra with center k'[x], nontrivial Brauer group, iterative invariant A but noniterative invariant different. Park: ordinary characteristic-zero slice argument cannot remove these restrictions; no explicit twisted algebra or descent theorem. Conjecture2.10 and Question5.7 not counted separately.

**12. Krásenský–Tinková–Zemková, 1909.05422v2, closing Open questions.** Can a totally positive integer indecomposable in the smaller real quadratic subfields Q(sqrt(m)) or Q(sqrt(s)) become decomposable in the ordered biquadratic extension, or is that possible only for the Q(sqrt(t)) subfield? Actual Theorem1.3 studies preservation of indecomposables; the universal ternary-form theorem is already proved and is not a target. Park: source parameter ordering and all hypotheses must be carried into any search; no new explicit decomposition or uniform trace obstruction supplied. Wider Kitaoka/universal-form problems are not fresh nominations.

**13. Kostov, 1909.05532v1, Problem2.** Classify, for every sign pattern of a monic polynomial with all coefficients nonzero, all realizable pairs of nonzero positive/negative simple-root counts satisfying Descartes parity and upper bounds. Actual source resolves the different hyperbolic root-modulus-order Problem1 in degree6; Theorem1 gives canonical realizations and restricted sign-pattern exclusions. Reject significant investment: unrestricted degree classification much broader than a degree6 deformation, no uniform necessary/sufficient criterion. Original historical problem body not retrieved; do not nominate a familiar forbidden instance.

**14. Amenta–Conde-Alonso–Cruz-Uribe–Ocariz, 1909.05987v1, Section3 density/dual question.** Characterize unbounded sequence exponents p(.) for which the source simple-function subspace S_(p(.)) is dense in ell^(p(.)); source Example3.19 shows it need not be. Actual Theorem2.1/Proposition2.3 separate the usual associate functionals from the germ-space dual using bounded-exponent restrictions and extension by zero. This does not characterize the residual quotient in the sequence space. Park: no exact tail summability criterion stable under arbitrary exponent partitions; generic Hahn–Banach decomposition alone is not a new resolution.

**15. Dressler, 1909.06707v2, Section5 dimension question.** Determine the dimension of the SONC face C_(n,2d)(Gamma) for prescribed real zeros Gamma, in particular when it is full-dimensional in the corresponding nonnegative-polynomial face. Actual source reduces zero constraints to agiforms for its special unit-modulus Gamma and counts the univariate case; Theorem3.1 already resolves the separate SOS-containment question. Park: general Gamma requires control of independent circuits, not just counting zero constraints. No universal matroid-rank formula derived; classical AM–GM alone does not determine face dimension.
