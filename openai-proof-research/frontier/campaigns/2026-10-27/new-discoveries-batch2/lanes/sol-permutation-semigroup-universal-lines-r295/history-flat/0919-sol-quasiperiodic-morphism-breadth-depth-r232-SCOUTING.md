# Breadth beyond words: primary-body screen, not novelty clearance

All linked arXiv versions below were downloaded in this lane on 30 October2026, hashed before PDF extraction and retained read-only; retrieval.jsonl records exact bytes, SHA256, URL, version and UTC. This is a set of separate primary research papers, not one survey's question list. Original attribution is distinguished from the nearest primary follow-up. Eight external exploratory families are rows1–8; row9 is a rejected family and is not an extra open lead. Together with the inherited word family these give ten family rows. They are NOT ten verified original-source/current-prior-cleared open questions. Several original attribution bodies and current registry comparison are missing. No rank implies eligibility.

1. **Strong blocking sets / minimal codes.** Bishnoi–Kakatelis, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.33219v1,23Oct2026, Section6 pp16–17: construct smaller explicit strong blocking sets in PG(k−1,q) without requiring the stronger line-avoidance property; codimension-two/hypergraph extensions are merged here. Definition: every hyperplane meets the set in a spanning set. Earlier Alon–Bishnoi–Das–Neri construction is attributed, not original-body cleared. Nearest actual body here Section3's field-sensitive partition parameter and Section4 line construction, not an unsolved existence assertion: O(qk) explicit existence is already public. Mechanism: replace line avoidance by direct hyperplane rank certification. Decisive calculation: identify an actual graph/code instance that fails avoidance while retaining all hyperplane ranks, then prove an asymptotic advantage. Objection: optimizing a known constant or a parameter instance is not eligible substantive resolution. **Low exploratory priority**, no quantified new bound invented.

2. **Minor-deletion kernelization beyond planar obstructions.** Ghazy–Greilhuber–Hartmann, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18719v1,13Oct2026, introduction p3 and Theorems1.1/1.2: does F-Minor Deletion parameterized by solution size admit a polynomial kernel for every fixed finite F, beyond families containing a planar graph? This broad question is stated as major open, not a newly created conjecture. Actual Theorem1.1 gives distance-to-class compression lower bounds from unbounded minimal F-blocking sets, under disjoint-copy closure and connected F; Theorem1.2 shows bounded ordinary blocking sets do not characterize all lower bounds. End-of-paper Lemma6.15/Cor6.1 proof uses tidy leaf blocks/pathwidth constructions. Mechanism: extended blocking-set gadgets, not ordinary ones. Decisive step would require a new structural parameter that handles nonplanar obstructions. Objection: this is a major complexity problem, unsuitable for an elementary finite certificate attack. **Defer hard**. Do not mistake a lower bound in a different parameter for a solution-size answer.

3. **Asymptotic binary covering density / saturating sets.** Davydov–Marcugini–Pambianco–Wu, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16078v2,11Oct2026, p4 quotes Green's Problem40: for fixed R let f(R) be the least asymptotic density constant for infinite sequences of binary linear covering codes of radius R; must f(R) tend to infinity as R tends to infinity? Its translation ell_2(r,R)=s_2(r−1,R−1) is Eq1.3. Green's original problem body is NOT fetched; attribution remains secondary in this primary construction paper. Theorems2.1 and5.4 and actual QM1 proof give improved fixed-R upper bounds, not the R→infinity answer. Mechanism: concatenation and parity-check partitions. Decisive calculation: obtain a bound uniform in R with the correct direction, rather than another R=2,3 constant. Objection: small-radius optimizations do not bear on divergence. **Defer**, no fixed parameter credited.

4. **Cameron–Liebler generator classifications.** D'haeseleer–Mannaert–Storme, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.08260v1,11Mar2025, Section1 Conjecture1.1 attributes an eventual classification to [21,Conj5.1], involving unions of point pencils, generators in nondegenerate hyperplanes, and versions excluding a fixed point. The printed n(q,k) quantification is insufficiently explicit here to promote a literal all-rank endpoint; original [21] is unread. Nearest actual body Section3, Proposition3.1 proof identifies Klein-quadric degree-one sets with point/plane sets in PG(3,q) having x or x+q incidence counts. This is a concrete finite algebraic classification setting; it does not prove the eventual all-polar-space conjecture. Mechanism: integral incidence eigenspaces plus Boolean constraints. Decisive calculation: read [21]'s actual quantified conjecture, then eliminate one proposed nontrivial parameter uniformly. Objection: spectral membership alone does not classify Boolean vectors. **Original-scope HOLD**, not ranked.

5. **Group-labeled matroid bases.** Liu–Oki–Xu, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32798v1,23Oct2026, Theorem1 ALREADY proves weighted proximity for one abelian-group labeling: every min-cost base lies within |F| exchanges of a min-cost F-avoiding base, when one exists. Do not rediscover that statement or its FPT consequence. The paper's Conjecture3, attributed to Schrijver–Seymour1990, remains the broader base-label cardinality inequality with stabilizer H: |ell(M)| >= |H| min(sum_(Q in G/H) r(E(Q))−r(E)+1,|G|/|H|). Its printed theorem2 is prime-cyclic; theorem4 adds a unique min-cost label-base condition. Multiple-label proximity is merged here. Original1990 body unread. Mechanism: Kemperman–Scherk plus exchange; decisive step remove the unique-label hypothesis without assuming the conjectured stabilizer inequality. Objection: the newly published proximity theorem explicitly bypasses, rather than proves, that old conjecture. **Defer hard**; closest theorem is a public resolution of the easier lead.

6. **Pattern-avoiding permutahedra root location.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.06096v2,29Oct2026 updated version (first5Sep2026), Theorems3/4 give real negative zeros of H_d=h* of conv of (132,213)-avoiding permutations for d<=1000 and d>=2^62. The untouched intermediate interval is not claimed solved. This is one all-d family, not separate dimensions/outcomes; Davis–Sagan's earlier general pattern-set question is an attribution, original body unread. Actual Lemma6 proof pairs cut constraints with path-Laplacian constraints to establish the cube model; Sections5–9 use compatible refined Eulerian polynomials and weighted tail coordinates. The naive nonnegative-coordinate argument fails at a_(7,1)=−1/15 in the paper itself. Decisive calculation: sharpen the uniform weighted-tail estimates enough to join ranges, not perform the impossible intermediate enumeration. Objection: extending a finite range alone is a parameter instance; existing asymptotics may make this a technical constant improvement. **Provisional low-rank lead**, substantial/new classification not established.

7. **Inverse Kazhdan–Lusztig coefficient log-concavity.** Braden–Ferroni–Matherne–Nepal, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.01086v2,22Oct2026 (first1Oct2025), Conjecture1.2(c), attributed to Gao–Xie2021: for every matroid M the coefficients of inverse KL polynomial Q_M are log-concave. Merge the P/Z/Y variants conservatively. Their Conjecture1.3, the binomial-Hadamard real-rootedness strengthening of Xie–Zhang, is ALREADY REFUTED by this same version's rank19 example; never use its conjecture label as openness evidence. Actual deletion formula Theorem1.4 is the nearest mechanism: deletion, restriction and contraction terms have correction coefficients; log-concavity is not automatically preserved under such sums. Original GX21/XZ26 bodies unread. Decisive calculation: read the rank19 Q coefficients and check the weaker inequalities exactly before investing in a recurrence induction. Objection: a nonreal root of the binomial transform does NOT refute coefficient LC. **Provisional lead only**, no literal witness produced.

8. **Ehrhart Taylor threshold classification.** Liu–Zhang, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03327v2,16Oct2026 (first3Sep2026), final paragraph p38: characterize natural classes with real Taylor threshold rho(P)=0, especially matroid base and smooth polytopes, and extremizers of tau(P)=floor((d−1)/2). Those are original author directions, not separate clauses counted as families. Actual Theorems1.5/1.7 ALREADY prove the universal INTEGER threshold floor((d−1)/2), including sharpness; do not nominate that bound. Formula1.4 expresses Taylor coefficients as L_P^(j)(k)/j!, equivalently weighted elementary-symmetric evaluations of the h* vector. Mechanism: exact signs in a structured h* family. Decisive step specify a genuinely unsolved natural class with an exact all-object endpoint; none selected here. Objection: an unspecified request to characterize natural classes is not an eligible theorem statement. **Defer vague endpoint**.

9. **Multiplicative Merino–Welsh.** Chen–Jin–Ma, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.27706v1,20Oct2026, actual Sections3–5: the all-matroid claim T(2,0)T(0,2)>=T(1,1)^2 is already false by Beke et al.2024; this paper proves all uniform q-thickenings with q>=3 satisfy it and the smallest doubled uniform counterexample has62 elements. Actual Lemma5.2 deletion–contraction proof shows every element of a minimal matroid counterexample lies in a two-element circuit or cocircuit, via the cross-term geometric-mean inequality. The graph version is a different surviving classical problem. **Reject the matroid target**; no graph theorem inferred. Not one of the eight exploratory open directions above; smallest-all-matroid enumeration is not promoted.

10. **Inherited quasiperiodic fixed-point converse.** Exact original/published bodies, hypotheses, two mechanisms, 92 positive lasso certificates, unresolved universal bridge and strongest objection: ATTACK.md. The only sustained attack; no full nomination.

Additional rejection controls, not extra families: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40496v1 Theorems1.1/1.2 publicly solve crown/wheel/whirl real-rootedness; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25863v2 already computes Birkhoff series through13 and World Cup through13, so those orders are unavailable; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.10513v2 publicly refutes IDP h*-unimodality and related stronger assertions. These downloaded bodies are negative screening evidence, not padding for new open targets.
