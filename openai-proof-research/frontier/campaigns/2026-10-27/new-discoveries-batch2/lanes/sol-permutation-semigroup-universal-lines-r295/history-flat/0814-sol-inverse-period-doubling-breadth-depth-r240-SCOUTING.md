1. **Minimal eventual periods of Fubini numbers.** Mező, *Periodicity of the Last Digits of Some Combinatorial Sequences*, VOL17/Mezo/mezo19.pdf, December5 2013, §6.1 p15 asks for m(k) modulo10^k after observing4,20,100,500. F_n counts ordered set partitions. Source's earlier modulo10 results do not supply the full minimal-period function. Mechanism: separate2-adic stabilization and5-adic exponential modes of sum j! S(n,j), use CRT and a witness excluding proper period divisors. Closest current retrieved body Asgari–Jahangiri arXiv1701.07276v1 January25 2017 gives residual r-Fubini period results: Theorem3.3/proof claims phi(p^m) after term m-1 for odd p, with separate §4 powers2. Its normalization uses (k+r)! and distinct distinguished elements; the r>=1 statement has not been bridged to the source's ordinary r=0 sequence, and exact minimality needs attention. This is a serious possible subsumer, not freshness clearance. Next step: compare ordinary normalization and all k thresholds against §4 before investment. Park.

2. **Distinct-displacement permutations with narrow slope range.** Larsson, *Wythoff Nim Extensions and Splitting Sequences*, VOL17/Larsson/larsson5.pdf, April4 2014, §5 p11 asks whether a permutation g of N with pairwise distinct g(i)-i can satisfy limsup g(i)/i - liminf g(i)/i <1. The paper answers negatively for involutions, not all permutations. Treat all permutations as the full scope. Mechanism: replace involution's paired points with arbitrary permutation cycles and derive a mass-transport packing inequality for the displacement graph; finite-cycle extremizers can falsify candidate inequalities. Closest retrieved later body Fokkink–Rust arXiv1904.08339v2 November18 2019 Theorems1–3/proofs code a specific split-move game's P-positions by Tribonacci letter positions. Those are not arbitrary distinct-displacement permutations, so these theorems do not settle §5. Older Knape–Larsson thesis and Hegarty–Larsson2006 original general-permutation bodies are not retrieved; a current narrow query is not historical clearance. Park with this exact gap.

3. **Kneading-cycle sum and caliber.** Smith, *Reducing Quadratic Forms by Kneading Sequences*, VOL17/Smith/smith5.pdf, December13 2014, Conjectures6–7 pp12–13 (one family) concern all reduced cycles of discriminant a²+4: for caliber l and sum n, n=(2r+1)l+1, r>=0, with r>0 imprimitive; the number at a given permissible n,l is (1/(2l))*sum_(d|l,d odd) mu(d)2^(l/d). Actual Lemmas4–5 prove the principal-cycle pattern and upper bound n<=a+1, not this classification. Mechanism: encode kneading cycles as primitive binary necklaces and isolate the sum-preserving expansion operation; Möbius inversion then explains the formula rather than verifying small sums. Current arXiv1408.4631v3 December8 2014 is a differently numbered prepublication body, with conjectures still present; main Theorem1 proves the reduction/kneading correspondence. It supplies no complete necklace bridge. The exact caliber/conjugacy interpretation for primitive versus imprimitive forms is missing. Current kneading query is contaminated by complex-dynamics papers. Park; same-family Conjecture9 not separately counted.

4. **Eventual division-free free-Fibonacci dynamics.** Avila–Khovanova, *Free Fibonacci Sequences*, VOL17/Avila/avila4.pdf, August5 2014, Conjecture33 p18: for every n admitting a division-free sequence, the proportion of starting pairs in[1,N]^2 whose n-free Fibonacci sequence eventually becomes division-free tends1 as N→infinity. At each step add the preceding two terms and remove every factor n. These are positive starting integers; “probability1” is the limiting box density stated in the source, not an unspecified random process. Conjecture31 for n=5 is part of this family, not another slot. Mechanism: deterministic residue-state absorption with a Lyapunov bound on repeated divisions, rather than independent-random-residue heuristics. Actual source §7 exhibits Lucas division-free behavior for5; §10's heuristic does not establish absorption density. Current arXiv query returns the original1403.4614v1 and irrelevant physics, with no closer proof body identified. Thus nearest-body/current clearance is incomplete. Next step: find a uniform contraction/tail estimate for nonabsorbed starting pairs; beware of Collatz-like difficulty. Park, no novelty claim.

5. **Maximum rectangular prisoners configurations.** Bullington–Eroh–Winters, *Prisoners and Guards on Rectangular Boards*, VOL17/Eroh/eroh4.pdf, July18 2014, revised July17/30 2015, §7 p18 asks whether other maximum configurations exist beyond the provided constructions (and distinguishes merely maximal configurations). A valid prisoner has at least as many king-neighbor guards as prisoners. Theorem6 p16 gives only P(6,n)>=floor(11n/3); §7's classification question remains separate. Mechanism: finite column-state transfer with a max-plus weight AND multiplicity, then classify equality transitions and boundary components for the fixed-width classes already optimized. Actual closest retrieved primary predecessor Howard–Ionascu–Woolbright arXiv0801.0833v1 January6 2008 analyzes square3x3 maximal cases and proves its unique maximum up to rotation; this is not all rectangular widths. Source's 2015 revision still asks the question. The source's informal “other configurations” does not specify a numbered universal uniqueness conjecture; any future result must state symmetry and board scope exactly. General all-width classification and current citing-body coverage are missing; no count for a single extra configuration or routine transfer algorithm.
