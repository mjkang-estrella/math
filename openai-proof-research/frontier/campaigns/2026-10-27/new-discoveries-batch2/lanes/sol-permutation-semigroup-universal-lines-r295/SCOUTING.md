1. **Bipartite spectral extremizers: public refutation.** Cheng–Liu–Weng, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.01124v1, Conjecture1.1 and Theorem2.6, pp2,7. BFP asks whether the spectral-radius maximum over e-edge subgraphs of K_(p,q), with no isolated vertices and not complete bipartite, is obtainable by adding one vertex with incident edges to a complete bipartite graph. The actual theorem refutes it for p>2,q>kp+2,e=p(q-k). Lemmas2.4–2.5 compare quotient-matrix characteristic polynomials against the proposed one-deficient-vertex forms; the displayed f(x)>g(x) root comparison and Theorem2.6 body were read. Mechanism already public; no credit. Do not confuse the conjecture with the true universal sqrt(e) spectral bound or the known near-complete edge range.

2. **Strong Euclidean complementability: public refutation of the strengthened question only.** Gowers–Wyczesany, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03023v1, Questions1.2–1.3, Theorem1.4 and Corollary3.2. Question1.3 asks for every k,C,epsilon an n forcing a k-dimensional simultaneously strongly(1+epsilon)-Euclidean and strongly(1+epsilon)-complemented subspace in every sufficiently high-dimensional strongly C-Euclidean space. Actual theorem excludes even dimension2 for an absolute epsilon. Strong complementability fixes the orthogonal projection, unlike arbitrary complementability. The proof computes support functionals of an ellipsoidal norm plus a small l1 term, then converts good vectors to sign vectors near PY+QY; the support-functional calculation was read. Original Milman Question1.2 remains explicitly unanswered BY THIS BODY. Retire the strengthened family; do not falsely claim a full resolution of the weaker question.

3. **Planar cycle Turán bound: public refutation.** Cranston–Lidický–Liu–Shantanam, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02043v1, Conjecture1, Theorems1,7, §§2–3. Ghosh–Győri–Martin–Paulo–Xiao propose for each ell>=7 and all sufficiently large n the bound ex_P(n,C_ell)<=3(ell-1)n/ell-6(ell+1)/ell. Actual Theorem1 beats it for every ell>=11 and every sufficiently large n. Theorem7 body substitutes C_ell-free triangulation gadgets into dense planar girth-(ell+1) skeletons; the explicit vertex/edge formulas and leading-coefficient inequality were inspected. This refutes the full universal assertion, not all individual ell=7,...,10 cases. A new gadget calculation would repeat public work; revised conjectures are not nominated.

4. **Khanin quantitative-return rigidity: public refutation.** Forni–Kanigowski, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.09584v1, Conjecture1.2, Theorem1.3, §2 Lemmas2.1–2.2. The statement assumes smooth diffeomorphisms satisfy dist(T_i^n x,x)>=C n^(-tau) for every x,n and are topologically conjugate, and concludes a smooth conjugacy. Actual theorem constructs two torus diffeomorphisms with the return bound and C0 but no C1 conjugacy. The proof uses a Diophantine rotation factor and a Liouville circle diffeomorphism; the product conjugacy limiting identity reduces a hypothetical C1 conjugacy to the impossible circle conjugacy. Krikorian's stronger Diophantine-vector hypothesis is explicitly distinct and not refuted. Public resolution; no nomination.

5. **Donkin tilting-module conjecture: public refutation, counted as one family.** Bendel–Nakano–Pillen–Sobaje, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.11615v3, Conjecture2.3.2, Theorem1.2.1 and §3.2. For every restricted lambda, the tilting module T((p^r-1)rho+lambda) restricted to G_rT is conjecturally the corresponding projective indecomposable Q-hat_r((p^r-1)rho+w0 lambda). Actual theorem supplies failures in all root-system types except A_n,B2 at specified primes. The inspected G2,p2 argument uses Ext^1(k,L(omega2))^(-1)=nabla(omega1), and Theorem3.1.2 forces a simple socle absent in the possible Weyl modules. Levi restriction propagates failures; its weight-space projection proof was also read. Jantzen's related filtration question is not counted separately. Do not interpret the theorem as failure at every prime, or every A_n. Public, retired.

6. **Matching Kneser chromatic formula: public refutation.** Iradmusa, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.04998v1, Conjecture1 and its complete one-page proof. Alishahi–Hajiabolhassan propose chi(KG(G,rK2))=|E(G)|-ex(G,rK2) for every connected G,r>=2, with edge-disjoint matching adjacency. For a snark on2r vertices, any two perfect matchings intersect (otherwise their union and complement give a3-edge-coloring), so this Kneser graph is edgeless, chi=1. The perfect-matching avoidance theorem and deleting a vertex's three incident edges give ex=|E|-3, so RHS=3. All proof steps were read. This is edge-disjointness, not vertex-disjointness; other Kneser families do not subsume its definition. Already public; no nomination.

7. **Laptev–Safronov eigenvalue inequality: public refutation of remaining parameter range.** Bögli–Cuenin, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06135v1, introduction inequality(1), Theorems1–3 and §2. The conjectural uniform estimate |z|^gamma<=D_(gamma,d) integral |V|^(gamma+d/2), for 0<gamma<=d/2 and nonreal Schrödinger eigenvalues, fails when d>=2,gamma>1/2. The actual construction gives z=1+i epsilon and |V_epsilon|<=epsilon on a box with longitudinal scale epsilon^-1 and transverse scale epsilon^-1/2. Thus its L^q mass tends to zero for q>(d+1)/2 while eigenvalue size tends to1. §2 localized resolvent operator mechanism was inspected; the full operator lemmas were not independently re-proved. The positive gamma<=1/2 endpoint is not refuted. Public, retired.

8. **Principal HK groupoid conjecture: public refutation integrally.** Deeley, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.01527v2, Conjecture0.1, Theorem0.2 and Corollary3.6 (proof read). For second-countable étale principal minimal ample groupoids the conjecture identifies reduced C*-algebra K-theory with the sum of even/odd groupoid homology. Actual body constructs a free odometer of a torsion-free flat-manifold group and distinguishes the orders of torsion subgroups: K-theory torsion is bounded by the manifold's, while the homology torsion survives the inductive limit. Theorem0.2 supplies the strict mismatch in dimensions>=9. Rational HK holds for these examples, explicitly by the Chern character; it is not refuted. Public integral endpoint retired; no nomination.
