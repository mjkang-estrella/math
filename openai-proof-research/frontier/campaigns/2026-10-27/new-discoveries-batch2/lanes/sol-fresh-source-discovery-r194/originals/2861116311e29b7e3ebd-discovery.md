For any field K and any finite collection P of unit lattice cells, let V(P)
be its corners and I_P the ideal in S=K[x_v:v in V(P)] generated by
x_(a,c)x_(b+1,d+1)-x_(a,d+1)x_(b+1,c) for every full cell rectangle
[a,b]×[c,d] contained in P. R=K[P]=S/I_P is standard graded. Write its
reduced Hilbert series h_R(t)/(1-t)^dim(R), h_R(1) nonzero. Rooks attack
only along a contiguous row/column of cells of P. A switch replaces two
opposite corner cells of a full contained rectangle with its other two
corner cells. Write q_k for the number of switch-equivalence classes of
k nonattacking rooks, q_0=1, and r=max{k:q_k>0}.

**Full claim:** for every such P and K, h_R(t)=sum_(k=0)^r q_k t^k
AND reg(R)=r. Collections may have holes, multiple connected components,
or components touching only at vertices. Source imposes no convexity,
primality, characteristic-zero or Cohen–Macaulay hypothesis. The empty
collection has R=K, h=1 and r=reg=0. Coordinates used in checks start at
zero; translate by (1,1) to the source's positive-coordinate convention.

This is distinct from the r117 Ferrers-board q-rook unimodality question.
History exact/title/author/statement alias screens locate that unrelated
record only. No qualified prior full investigation of this target found.

## Sustained all-order route: exact monomial quotient reduction

Put v=|V(P)|, c=|P|. For every integer m>=0 form a finite graph G_m:
vertices are all degree-m monomials of S. Join M U and M W whenever
U-W is one of the source inner 2-minors and M has degree m-2. Let C_m
be its number of connected components. Then, over **every field**,

    dim_K R_m = C_m.

Ordinary proof: S_m has its monomials as a vector-space basis. Since the
generators are homogeneous quadrics, (I_P)_m is precisely the span of all
edge differences e_U-e_W. On each connected component these differences
span the subspace whose coefficient sum is zero: path telescoping gives
every e_U-e_root, and they are independent by their non-root coordinates.
The quotient has one basis vector per component. The argument works in
every characteristic and needs neither primeness nor a term order.

Consequently the polynomial-equality half of the conjecture is equivalent
to the following universal finite-enumeration identity for every m>=0:

    C_m = sum_(k=0)^min(m,r) q_k binom(v-c+m-k-1,m-k).       (*)

Here v-c>0 for nonempty finite P: choose the lower-left corner for each
cell, an injection into V(P), and the top-rightmost extreme boundary
vertex supplies at least one extra corner.

Forward bridge: I has no linear forms, so H_R(1)=v. In the expansion
h_R(t)/(1-t)^d the coefficient of t is h_1+d. Since q_1=c, the equality
h=rook forces d=v-c. The formal expansion of (1-t)^-(v-c) now gives (*).
Reverse bridge: (*) gives HP_R(t)=q(t)/(1-t)^(v-c); q(1)>0, since its
constant coefficient is1 and all q_k are nonnegative. Thus the fraction
is reduced, dimension is v-c, and its unique reduced numerator is q.
This proves the equivalence; it does **not** prove (*).

Potential construction: partition each monomial-congruence class into a
switch class of k rooks and a free multiset of size m-k on v-c parameters.
No canonical map with the required independence and bijectivity was found.
The source's local corner-deletion/colon recursion proves such counts only
under its convexity and quadratic-basis assumptions. Gluing arbitrary
collections creates nonlocal reductions. This is the precise all-order gap.

Even a proof of (*) would not alone prove the full Conjecture2.4: h-degree
equals regularity under a Cohen–Macaulay theorem, not for every standard
graded ring. A proof must either establish adequate universal CM structure
or control the full graded resolution/local cohomology to get reg(R)=r.
The companion's Question4.19 cannot be imported as a theorem.

## Explicit obstruction to the most tempting source extension

Take the three cells with lower-left corners (0,0),(0,2),(1,1).
Only single cells are full inner rectangles. With descending coordinate
variable order and grevlex, the upper and right cell minors have leading
monomials x_03 x_12 and x_12 x_21. Their S-polynomial, up to sign, is

    x_02 x_13 x_21 - x_03 x_11 x_22.

Neither term is divisible by any of the three antidiagonal leading cell
monomials (the third is x_01 x_10). It is a nonzero element of I with
irreducible leading term. Thus those quadrics are not a Gröbner basis in
this order. This exactly blocks simply dropping the source's corner
conditions. It does not rule out other orders, more generators, a
different full proof or the conjecture itself. The board's rook polynomial
is (1+t)^3; all computed Hilbert coefficients agree. This is an easy known
method obstruction, not a fresh discovery nominee.

## Other fresh exact screen: complete source-based exclusion bridge

Hayk Karapetyan2503.22421v1 (28March2025), latest v2 (20August2025),
Conjecture1 p2: K_(a,n)=x^n+(1-x)^n+a^n, rational a different from0,-1,
is irreducible over Q. The surrounding FLT motivation explicitly discusses
odd n; the printed conjecture omits that restriction. Both versions read.

For literal even n=4,a=1 the exact identity is

    x^4+(1-x)^4+1 = 2(x^2-x+1)^2.

The two sides expand to coefficients (2,-4,6,-4,2), so this is a genuine
proper positive-degree factorization over Q; a=1 meets the printed
parameter condition. But this is **not eligible**: it is an elementary
even-domain correction, and the source itself already supplies the factors.
Complete source bridge: §3 sets K_n=K_(-1,n), establishes the sixth-root
factor x^2-x+1 and its multiplicity for n mod6; for even n one has
K_(1,n)=K_(-1,n) identically. In particular n=4 gives a double quadratic
factor. More generally let w satisfy w^2-w+1=0. Then w(1-w)=1,
w^3=-1,w^6=1; for even n congruent2 or4 mod6,
w^n+(1-w)^n=-1. Hence x^2-x+1 divides K_(1,n) for every such n,
and is proper when n>=4. This repeats source/classical algebra, and
does not address any intended odd-n irreducibility. No stronger theorem
or source novelty is asserted. No experiment on the hard odd-n problem.
