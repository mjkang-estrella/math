## Independent proof check

Let R_i=Σ_j Z_ij and E_i=Σ_j Z_ij²=(Z²)_ii. The diagonal term gives E_i≥1 and R_i≥1. Entry bounds give E_i≤R_i. Choose a real orthonormal eigenbasis v_j and eigenvalues λ_j≥0. On J={j:λ_j>0}, put w_ij=λ_j v_j(i)². Then w_ij≥0, Σ_J w_ij=Z_ii=1, and Σ_J w_ij λ_j=E_i. Also Σ_i v_j(i)²=1. Zero-eigenvalue terms disappear from these identities by their explicit λ factors; no invertibility is used.

For q<1, the function t↦t^(q−1) is convex on the positive half-line. Weighted scalar Jensen gives

    E_i^(q−1) ≤ Σ_J λ_j^q v_j(i)².

Summation and orthonormality yield Σ_i E_i^(q−1)≤Σ_J λ_j^q. Since q−1<0 and E_i≤R_i, the complete bound is

    Σ_J λ_j^q ≥ Σ_i E_i^(q−1) ≥ Σ_i R_i^(q−1).

Every sign, support restriction, row/column index, and exponent checks. For finite q<0, both score interiors equal n^(−q) times the corresponding unnormalized sums, and 1/(1−q)>0. Multiplication and the outer power therefore preserve the desired direction VS_q≥D_q. There is no reversed inequality when q is negative.

For −∞, nonemptiness and diagonal one force a positive eigenvalue. The least positive eigenvalue m satisfies m≤E_i≤R_i because E_i is the above convex combination of positive eigenvalues. Thus n/m≥n/min_i R_i, exactly the support VS/LCR limits. Arbitrary rank, repeated eigenvalues, n=1, and rank-one matrices cause no exception.

The proposed strictly-positive weighted-p extension also checks mathematically: for A=diag(√p)Zdiag(√p), weights λ_j v_j(i)²/p_i have sum one and mean Σ_k p_k Z_ik²≤(Zp)_i. Apply Jensen, multiply by p_i, and sum over i. This is one extension of the same result, not another count. A formal packet claiming it needs that separate matrix normalization bridge; the uniform bridge alone does not certify it.

## Adversarial prior-art screen

Scalar weighted Jensen and the equivalent Hölder–McCarthy argument are classical. In particular, the core follows by applying the negative-power vector-state inequality on the range of A to A^(1/2)e_i/√A_ii. The generic energy inequality is not claimed to be a new general operator inequality.

An actual accessible primary treatment is S.S. Dragomir, *Jensen type weighted inequalities for functions of selfadjoint and unitary operators*, Italian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics 32 (2014), 247–264, [primary PDF](https://ijpam.uniud.it/online_issue/201432/23-Dragomir.pdf). Its Theorem 1 gives scalar-convex functional calculus on a vector state, and Theorem 2(iii) gives the negative-power Hölder–McCarthy inequality for an invertible positive operator. Restricting to the positive spectral subspace and choosing the above vectors recovers our Jensen ingredient. I found no explicit trace-versus-row-sum support inequality or Vendi/LCR theorem in that paper; it contains neither trace nor row-sum formulations. Credit to classical Jensen/Hölder–McCarthy must remain prominent. A future primary source explicitly stating this full same comparison or the same complete stronger trace/energy assertion would be an exclusion, even if it does not use the Vendi name.

Pasarkar–Dieng, *Cousins Of The Vendi Score*, AISTATS 2024, [actual published primary PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/pasarkar24a/pasarkar24a.pdf), defines its order family for q≥0 and studies that range; it does not provide the missing negative-order comparison. The original VS q=1 definition and weighted-p construction are known background, not discoveries here.
