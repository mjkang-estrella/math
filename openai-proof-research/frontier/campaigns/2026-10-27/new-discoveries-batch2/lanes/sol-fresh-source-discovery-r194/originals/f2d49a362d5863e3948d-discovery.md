# Sustained mathematics and strongest falsifier

Initial route: replace singleton colors in an optimal total dominating
code by pairs or triples. In Q5, one pair has at most two common
neighbors instead of the five of a singleton, causing severe coverage
loss. But this capacity heuristic alone does not rule out all colorings,
and it is not a proof of optimality.

Second route: pick one representative per active color class to obtain
a total dominating set. This only gives the known lower bound gamma_t;
it leaves exactly the one- and two-color gap in the conjecture. Assuming
two unused residual parity classes would be circular, since an optimal
coloring need not arise from a singleton-code construction.

The successful route weights each active class by |C|+|CN(C)|. The source
common-neighborhood facts imply this is at most d+1. If a coloring had
at most gamma_t+1 classes, the representative argument permits at most
one inactive class. With none, all2^d vertices and their coverage must
fit in the active capacity, an impossibility. With one, it has size at
most2^(d-1)-1: equality at2^(d-1) would force a parity part, leaving its
complement uncovered. Thus active capacity must exceed3*2^(d-1), which
contradicts (d+1)gamma_t for every d=2^n+1>=5. Explicit formulas in
PROOF.md include the tight d=5 endpoint, without induction on sampled n.

Strongest falsifier to the mathematical proof: an active class violating
the local weighted bound, or a maximum independent inactive class that
does not force a parity part. Both are disposed of by complete ordinary
arguments in PROOF.md. Exhausting all nonempty neighborhood subsets for
d=5,9,17 checks the local inequality in those dimensions only. Explicit
Hamming-code colorings independently check the upper bound there.

Strongest objection to discovery credit: the entire route is an elementary
reorganization of source Fact5.1, not a substantive new structural method.
The proof gives the full source-to-conclusion bridge and adopts that
objection. It is not repackaged as a helper, general criterion, Q5 special
case, well-total-domination answer or extra family. The precise gap to a
qualifying outcome is substantive novelty. No proof gap is concealed by
the zero disposition.
