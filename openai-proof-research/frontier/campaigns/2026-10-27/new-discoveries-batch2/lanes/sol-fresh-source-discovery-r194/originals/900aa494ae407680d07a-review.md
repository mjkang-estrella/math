## Exact source statement and law bridge

Target: Asanjarani–Nazarathy, *The ANZIAM Journal* 64 (2022), 54–68, [DOI 10.1017/S1446181122000013](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446181122000013), published Conjecture 3.9; corresponding preprint arXiv:1905.01736v1 Conjecture 4.2. The quantifiers are every finite irreducible MMPP, every t >= 0, with time-stationary first-arrival survival at least event-stationary survival. It is not just a mean inequality or a variance claim.

The actual published HTML was parsed preserving its inline TeX, unlike lossy extraction that drops formulas. Section 2 gives row-state convention, Q=C+D, diagonal D for MMPP, pi Q=0, and arrival-embedded matrix K=(-C)^(-1)D. Proposition 2.2 gives survival eta exp(tC)1, with eta=pi for time stationarity and eta=alpha for event stationarity. The Palm weight is alpha=pi D/(pi D1). An event does not change the modulator state when D is diagonal, so no pre/post-arrival law discrepancy arises. These laws concern waiting forward from the observation/event epoch, not the length-biased entire straddling gap.

Neither reversibility nor strictly positive *every* off-diagonal entry is a substantive hypothesis. Irreducibility suffices. The dense random numerical experiment is not an assumption: the same source explicitly uses a sparse directed four-cycle in Example 3.3 and separately tests cyclic order four. Reversible/symmetric and order-two positive cases do not include this witness.

He, *J. Appl. Prob.* 58 (2021), 880–889, [DOI 10.1017/jpr.2021.18](https://doi.org/10.1017/jpr.2021.18), was checked in the actual cached published PDF and extracted body. Rendered pp.885,888,889 were inspected. His Theorems 3.1–3.2 establish the mean bound and event SCV bound, with equality exactly for constant D; Section 4.1 gives the Poisson conclusion. Section 4.3 separately leaves the stochastic order for future study. Thus the candidate does not refute He’s proved assertions. His reversible results cannot be transported to this directed generator. No credit is due for SCV, its equality characterization, or these standard laws.
