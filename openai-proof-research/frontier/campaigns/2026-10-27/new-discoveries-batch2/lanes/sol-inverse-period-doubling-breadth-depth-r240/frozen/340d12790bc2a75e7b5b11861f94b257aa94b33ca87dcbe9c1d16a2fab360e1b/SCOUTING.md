## Ranked leads

1. **Cube common-cover transitivity: full negative answer.** Hemminger–Landesman–Yao, [1501.04540v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.04540v2),10Dec2017, Question6.6 PDFp17, Definition1.4 p3. Exact question: does the linear automorphism group of the centered n-cube induce a CCT action on B_(2^n), for every n? **Result:** no for every n≥5. PROOF.md gives six vertices, an explicit signed coordinate permutation and a distance-profile obstruction valid under the full group. Closest actual result: Prop4.20 p15 refutes a different left-regular action; it does not refute the cube action. Source reports n≤3 checked; our full n4 sweep finds no obstruction and is not credited. **Mechanism:** make X and Y orbit-equivalent vertex deletions of a set Z with a distinguished metric vertex that prevents a stabilizer transport. **Decisive calculation:** completed by exact verification of all3,840 dimension5 symmetries and a hand-checkable profile table; uniform higher-dimensional embedding proved. **Classical objection:** generic deletion ambiguity or a known cube reconstruction result might subsume this; bounded arXiv/current phrase and immutable history checks do not settle that literature. No exact same-object classical bridge found. Independent review and priority checks still required. This is not a refutation of the main edge-poset Peck conjecture.

2. **Promotion sorting, the (n−2)! conjecture.** Bayer–Chau–Denker–Goff–Kimble–Lee–Liang, [2411.12034v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.12034v1),18Nov2024 submission (PDF header20Nov cache response is HTTP metadata, not used as submission date), Conj1.5 PDFp2. For every finite n-element poset n≥2 and x∈P, the number of tangled bijective labelings L:P→[n] with L(x)=n−1 is at most(n−2)!, with equality iff there is exactly one minimal y<x. Tangled means extended promotion first becomes natural after exactly n−1 applications. **Closest theorem:** Thm3.14 PDFp10 handles inflated rooted forests; Thm4.4 p12 handles shoelaces. Actual statements and local proof text inspected; full long proofs not audited. **Mechanism:** encode each tangled x-labeling by an ordering of the remaining n−2 labels plus a reconstructible certificate for the two distinguished labels; control collisions through minimal-element basins. **Decisive next calculation:** find a canonical injection valid across interacting basins, or a concrete collision obstruction in a poset outside source classes. **Objection:** deletion of the two labels need not be injective, and the source's basin/forest reductions do not apply to arbitrary posets. A finite test through n9 is already public and cannot count. History screen no exact hit; current arXiv query located source and predecessors, not a later exact resolution, but returned irrelevant stem matches and is not complete clearance. No fresh computation or theorem claimed here.

3. **Permutation Möbius correction under prepending.** Jason P. Smith, [1506.04406v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.04406v3),22May2017, Conj35 PDFp16: for indecomposable α,λ with EZ(α,λ)=0, EZ(α⊕λ^m,α⊕λ^n)=EZ(λ^m,λ^n) for source repetition indices (positive m≤n, with proper intervals when using its EZ sums). λ^m is the direct sum of m copies; EZ is the signed sum over collections of rightmost adjacency-class embeddings with empty intersection of zero sets (Def8 PDFp4, formula p8). **Closest actual theorem:** Prop33 pp15–16 proves this under the stronger “single” interval hypothesis; Prop32 p15 gives the right side explicitly as (−1)^(n−m−1) binom(n−1,m−1) for proper m<n and |λ|>1. **Mechanism:** a sign-reversing cancellation on embedding collections may replace the pointwise missing-letter property by aggregate EZ=0. **Decisive next calculation:** isolate collections missing a λ-block, compute their signed weight in terms of EZ(α,λ), and show it vanishes uniformly, or find a nonfactorizing collection yielding a counterexample. **Objection:** the source Prop33 proof uses a common missing letter, which numerical cancellation EZ=0 does not imply. Repeating its proof with that substitution is invalid. Exact whole-family history no hit; current dedicated later permutation-companion bodies not cleared. Provisional reserve only; do not invest heavily before that check. No all-repetition proof claimed.
