## Sustained strongest route: marked clique decomposition

The source extremizers are a central clique K_r, with a distinguished vertex u joined by one bridge to a distinguished vertex in each disjoint satellite K_s. A plausible universal route is (1) prove a connected extremal graph can be transformed to such a clique-star without losing maximal induced matchings, with equality tracking; (2) optimize the resulting two-product expression for every n. These are explicit combinatorial tasks, not numerical-limit inference.

Here is the complete restricted counting bridge, valid for all r>=1 and all satellites s_i>=3. Put a_i=binom(s_i,2), b_i=binom(s_i-1,2), A=product a_i, B=product b_i, D=sum_j product_(i!=j)b_i. Then

* r=1 or2: m=A+D.
* r>=3: m=binom(r-1,2) A+(r-1)B+D.

To verify: an induced matching uses at most one bridge. With bridge j selected, all other satellite edges avoid their distinguished vertices, giving product_(i!=j)b_i. With no bridge and r>=3, maximality forces one root-clique edge, because otherwise an edge between two non-u root vertices could be added. Root edges avoiding u give binom(r-1,2)A; edges incident to u give(r-1)B. For r=1, with no bridge every satellite needs its one edge and any such edge blocks its bridge. For r=2, no-root-edge configurations contribute A-B, while the root edge contributes B. Every counted configuration dominates all unused edges, so it is maximal; categories are disjoint and exhaustive. For satellite size2, residual K1 has one empty maximal induced matching: checks.py handles this boundary explicitly rather than using b_i=0 blindly.

There is also a uniform reduction INSIDE this restricted family. Any satellite of size s>=10 can be replaced by satellites5 and s-5, keeping the order and increasing the count strictly when all satellites have size>=3. The A factor increases, since

2[a_5 a_(s-5)-a_s]=9s^2-109s+300 >0 for s>=10

(at10 it is110 and its derivative18s-109 is positive). The B factor cannot decrease, since

2[b_5 b_(s-5)-b_s]=5(s-5)(s-10)>=0.

The D summands for other satellites scale by that B comparison, while the replaced satellite's summand scales from1 to b_5+b_(s-5)>=1. All coefficients are nonnegative; A has positive coefficient in both root cases. This proves strict improvement and bounds satellite sizes by9 in restricted optimizers. It does NOT classify arbitrary connected graphs or prove the all-n restricted optimization. It is an uncredited helper, not a source resolution.
