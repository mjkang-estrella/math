These are eleven distinct primary question families, **not eleven open problems**.
Journal pages are discovery locators only. Every entry below was checked against
its frozen PDF body and relevant proof/definition passages. All journal URLs have
prefix https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/ . Local PDFs, extracted bodies, hashes,
HTTP dates and response headers are in sources/ and source-receipts.jsonl.
Publication chronology comes from each body's closing publication line; filename

## Same-object follow-up before depth

HMT was stopped upon reading the actual 2022 Theorem21/Remark22, which explicitly
names the 2016 C27. The exact rational control p=3, a=3^84, x∈{3^85/85,
3^84/28,3^81} independently confirms a refutation; it earns no credit.
For the selected binary polynomial object the closest actual follow-up is
arXiv:2106.10008v1, *Factorization of cyclotomic polynomial values at Mersenne
primes*. Thm1.1's exceptional sets and Lemma2.3(ii)'s exclusion of 1+X+X²
were read. Latest author query bounded to nineteen entries and the actual
October2026 body arXiv:2606.08640v3 were inspected. The latter concerns
special perfect polynomials, not the asserted composition irreducibility.
No claim that these exhaust all prior proofs.

The single 2026 Fried paper was read only as a latest resolution control;
its ten resolved conjectures are not counted in the eleven-family breadth.
Other downloaded papers lacking a retained question are likewise not counted.
No 1990s primary family was reached. The qualifying bodies span 2002–2020,
with one printed 2008/index2009 chronology discrepancy explicitly preserved.

## Ranked unresolved leads (bounded, not novelty-cleared)

1. **Decimal polynomial pairs, C4 (2015; precursor arXiv:1210.7593v2,
   updated 15 August2014).** Literal statement above; closest actual body
   Proposition1 and source's carry-bearing Table1 examples all require a
   palindromic member. Mechanism: encode carries c_i by
   convolution_i+c_(i−1)=digit_i+10c_i and impose the reversed product.
   Next decisive calculation: exact search for a nonzero two-sided carry
   pattern with both digit vectors nonpalindromic, then realize all digits.
   Objection: a known reverse-multiplication construction may already refute
   C4; bounded title/phrase arXiv results do not exclude it. Not a cleared survivor.
2. **Fibonacci-coefficient irreducibility (2007), p.7.** Exact p_n above.
   Mechanism: (X²−X−1)p_n=X^(n+2)−F_(n+2)X−F_(n+1), plus reduction
   modulo Fibonacci divisors. Next calculation: analyze noncyclotomic factors
   of this sparse trinomial uniformly, or seek a first exact factor.
   Objection: Schinzel-type sparse-polynomial theorems or an unlocated later
   proof may subsume it; no all-n factor exclusion proved here.
3. **Dihedral bases, Q9 (2017).** Exact supremum above, all finite dihedral
   groups. Write B=A∪Cs and use rotation/reflection products to turn B²=G
   into cyclic sum/difference covers. Next calculation: find a uniform
   pair-of-subsets construction at size≤(3/2)√(2n), or a certified exact
   lower bound exceeding it. Objection: finite covers do not establish the
   all-n bound; resemblance to r229 word/abelian-combinatorics is insufficient
   to infer the same object. Kept low priority; no fresh-proof clearance.

These rankings are research directions only. **Zero novelty-cleared survivors,
zero eligible nominations.** No newly announced famous resolution is used as
the principal scouting pattern.
