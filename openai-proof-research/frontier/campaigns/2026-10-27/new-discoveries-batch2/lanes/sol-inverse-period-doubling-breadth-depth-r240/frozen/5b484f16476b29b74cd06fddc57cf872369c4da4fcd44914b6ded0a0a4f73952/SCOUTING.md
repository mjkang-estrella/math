# Eight additional finite/algebraic source families

These are eight genuinely different mathematical objects, not P/Z/Y variants. This is a rejection-aware breadth screen, **not eight cleared open problems**. Exact primary bodies were downloaded and hashed before extraction/reading; URLs, UTC, SHA256 and failures are in retrieval.jsonl. The original attribution body is distinguished from a successor's quoted question. The frozen breadth.xml supplies original publication/update dates; all entries below are October2026 source versions unless otherwise stated. history-hits.json and history-bindings.json record a compact-report alias screen, not universal semantic clearance. Reserved decimal-pairs and centers-of-distances targets were not adopted.

1. **All-ear-intersecting triangulations — sustained survivor, unresolved.** Leader–Tan, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39116v1,29Oct2026 (body dated30Oct), Sections1/4: every pair shares an ear; is the family size <=C_(n-3) for all convex n-gons? This is the authors' explicit subquestion. Kalai/Meagher's older original arbitrary-diagonal question is body-read at https://mathoverflow.net/questions/114646/intersecting-family-of-triangulations; do not conflate its generality with the selected subquestion. Nearest actual results: source Theorem2 gives the sharp bound if any one ear is excluded; Theorem3 gives C_(n-3)+C_(n-5) for all ears. Section3 proof and Borg–Feghali's actual separated-set shifting proof (2008.02342v2,5Dec2020,Theorem3) were read. Alias screen: no triangulation/intersection match in the bound corpus. Mechanisms: weighted separated sets, shifting with compensation, integer disjointness graph. Decisive next calculation: derive a uniform compensation inequality for weight lost by shifting {2,5} to {2,4} at n6. See discovery.md for sustained exact work, not merely a proposed plan. Novelty remains HOLD.

2. **Minimal-advantage dice maximum cycle — duplicate, retire.** Terry Easlick, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40321v1, first29Oct2026 (body dated30Oct), actual Section3 Conjecture1: is6 the largest n for which the minimal-strict-win directed graph of fair n-sided dice with labels in[n] has maximum directed-cycle length exactly n? For even n the threshold win count is n²/2+1; for odd n it is (n²+1)/2. Source Theorem1 already settles the smallest n and Section3 supplies longer-than-n cycles through n10. Finding a cycle of length n alone would not refute the largest-n statement, since its maximum may exceed n. Nearest proof uses exhaustive SCC/DFS. Historical alias screen positively identifies r206, whose SUMMARY/discovery already sustained this exact claim. Mechanism would be padding plus exact integer pair-win preservation; decisive calculation would be an in-scope new n with certified longest cycle, not another short cycle. No such calculation was invested after the duplicate was identified; no novelty rank.

3. **Filtered graphical zonotopal cycle congruence — duplicate, retire.** Shapiro–Smirnov–Vaintrob, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.38469v1,28Oct2026, actual Conjecture7.8: H_n(t) congruent to tr_n(t)-t² modulo t^ceil(n/2), for all n>=3 and nonzero quadratic coefficient. Theorem7.1 already proves the first3 stable coefficients; Theorem7.3 gives the top term. Proposition7.12's actual proof identifies cumulative filtration dimension with the rational rank of the integer matrix indexed by S and |m|<=d, entries product_(i in S)(m_(i-1)-m_i). Its generating-exponential proof was read; the transfer-matrix trace alone does not certify this rank. Alias screen positively recovers r171's sustained same-conjecture attempt. Mechanism: boundary-operator rank or interpolation filtration; decisive calculation is a uniform exact rank proof, not n18 enumeration. Retire as duplicate rather than relabeling it.

4. **Characteristic-free Segre resolutions / chessboard homology — publicly resolved, reject.** Limonchenko–Yu, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37827v1,27Oct2026, introduction quotes Reiner–Roberts Problem4.1: are all shifts of the at-most-four-factor Segre modules characteristic-free; equivalently is integral homology of generalized chessboard complexes with at most4 rows torsion-free? Original Reiner–Roberts body is not fetched, so this attribution is not independently original-body cleared. Current primary Theorem1.2(1) already supplies Sigma^(m-4) RP² summands for row capacities (2,2,2,m-5), m>=7; Theorem1.1 settles at-most3 rows positively. The relative-chain proof uses integral Smith quotients, with a mod2 functional detecting a nonzero class; the proof's explicit cyclic-quotient calculation was read. History screen: no exact chessboard/Segre-resolution hit. Mechanism: exact boundary/Smith forms. Decisive counterexample calculation is already public; no small rerun or torsion formalization is a discovery. Reject. This family is distinct from matroid-polynomial positivity.

5. **Nice signed-graphic arrangements — publicly resolved, reject.** Liang–Wang–Zhou, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.38412v1,28Oct2026, introduction attributes to Bailey [Conj4.5] that nice signed-graphic arrangements are inductively factored. Literal setting: finite signed graph, equations x_i=0 or x_i=±x_j, field characteristic !=2, a given nice partition. Original Bailey body remains unread; no independent original attribution clearance. Actual Theorem1.1 proves **every given** nice partition is inductive, stronger than existence. Section4.3 proof was read: choose a safe element, use bijective restriction and induced niceness, substitute coordinates to preserve signed-graphic form, induct on hyperplanes. History: no nice/signed-graphic match. Mechanism: safe-element deletion/restriction. Decisive calculation was already supplied by the theorem, so reject the exact target; general Terao freeness is not promoted as a fresh replacement.

6. **Capped ordered graph chromatic bound — publicly resolved, reject.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37578v1,27Oct2026, introduction states the Davies–Krawczyk–McCarty–Walczak conjectural polynomial chi-bound for graphs ordered so a<b<c<d and ac,bd imply ad. Actual main bound is chi(G)<=min(3^(omega-1),18omega³). Corollary2.3 already gives three colors for triangle-free capped graphs. The Section2 auxiliary bipartite graph/extension argument was read, including transfer of colors from contracted components and contact constraints; source has full constructive proof, not merely a numerical assertion. Original earlier conjecture body unread. History recovers r191's compact prior public-resolution screen of this paper; no new claim here. Mechanism: ordered edge-rank/forest decomposition. Decisive calculation would have to improve a genuinely open sharp endpoint, which is unspecified; do not promote constant optimization as a new source outcome. Reject.

7. **Coprime matching of divisor sets — publicly resolved, reject.** Alekseyev, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37512v1,27Oct2026, Section1: for n, N=tau(n), which N-term arithmetic progressions can be bijected to divisors of n with coprime pairs? Actual Theorem1.1 gives iff for each p|n at most N/(nu_p(n)+1) progression terms are divisible by p. Theorem1.2 resolves the attributed M-number strong-matchability characterization (p^p does not divide n for all primes p); Theorem1.3 addresses the original [1,N] matchability form. Earlier attribution body not fetched; no priority claim beyond this body. Section5 sufficiency proof was read: reduce to primitive progression, count prime multiples, isolate the possible high-exponent prime, and partition into shorter progressions. History has many lexical 'M-number' false positives, not a positive exact-title duplicate; these do not alter public settlement. Mechanism: Hall matching plus arithmetic progression partition. Decisive calculation is exactly the proved prime-count criterion, so retire rather than test examples. No universal arbitrary-integer-set extension is invented.

8. **Monomial immanant positivity — hooks publicly settled, full problem too broad.** McNulty, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39021v1,28Oct2026, actual Conjectures1.1/1.2 quote Stembridge/Haiman: for every partition lambda and totally nonnegative matrix, monomial immanant >=0; stronger at q=1 every monomial virtual character is nonnegative on every KL basis element. Original historical papers unread, attribution bounded. Actual Theorem1.3 proves all hook shapes; Theorem3.1 is the complementary-submatrix product positivity theorem and Section4's proof reduces marked-cycle functions through parabolic cosets and nonnegative localization multiplicities (proof end was read). History: no hook/immanant match in reports. Mechanism: character expansion and hyperbolic-localization products. Decisive next calculation would be an actual nonhook coefficient outside the paper's generated positive semiring, then an all-shape bridge or literal counterexample. General Haiman/Stembridge is a familiar hard problem, so **defer hard**, not sustained target and not two separately counted families.
