## Strongest target: exact convex formulation of Conjecture 3

Scale a=1 and put B=(0,0), C=(1,0), A=(x,h), with **all x real and h>0**.
Then b=sqrt((x-1)^2+h^2), c=sqrt(x^2+h^2),
m_b=sqrt((x+1)^2+h^2)/2, m_c=sqrt((x-2)^2+h^2)/2. Write

K=(sqrt((x+1)^2+h^2)+sqrt((x-2)^2+h^2))/sqrt(h^2+9/4).

For P=(u,v) in the closed triangle T=conv{A,B,C}, the sum of side distances is
the affine function D(P)=d0+g·P, where

d0=h/b, g=(h/c-h/b, 1+(x-1)/b-x/c).

Indeed the three signed distances inside T are v,
[h(1-u)+(x-1)v]/b, and [hu-xv]/c. Their sums give the formula. D is positive
even on the boundary: at each vertex two terms vanish and its opposite
altitude is positive. Thus Conjecture 3 is equivalent, by continuity and
density of the strict interior, to F(P)>=0 on T, where
F(P)=sum_{V=A,B,C} |P-V|-K(d0+g·P).

F is strictly convex. Equality in convexity of each Euclidean norm along a
nontrivial segment would force that segment and all three vertices onto a
common line, impossible for h>0. Hence F has a unique minimizer on compact T.
In the interior it is smooth; its Hessian is
sum_V [I-e_V e_V^t]/|P-V| with e_V=(P-V)/|P-V|. It is positive definite:
a null vector would have to be parallel to each P-V. This supplies a valid
fixed-shape convex optimization problem, not a universal sign certificate.

There is an exact compact dual. For each vertex V choose a vector z_V with
|z_V|<=1 and put w=sum z_V-Kg. Then

min_{P in T} F(P) = max_{|z_V|<=1}
 [min_{W=A,B,C} w·W - sum_V z_V·V - Kd0].

For the <= direction of each proposed lower certificate, use
|P-V|>=z_V·(P-V), and minimize the resulting affine form on T at a vertex.
For equality, at a minimizing P choose norm subgradients z_V so that
w·(W-P)>=0 for all W in T, the usual convex first-order optimality condition.
Such choices exist by subgradient sum calculus for the everywhere finite norm
sum and the closed convex constraint T. Then z_V·(P-V)=|P-V|, and
min_W w·W=w·P, making the certificate exact. This is ordinary convex duality,
not a new theorem or a source resolution.

**Precise missing uniform step:** for every x in R and h>0, construct three
unit-ball vectors z_A,z_B,z_C satisfying, simultaneously for W=A,B,C,

  (sum z_V-Kg)·W - sum z_V·V >= Kd0.

No such all-shape construction, equivalent primal lower bound, or certified
universal elimination was obtained. A numerical minimizer, finite mesh, strict
convexity, or verified boundary values cannot replace that quantified step.
Even vertex checks alone cannot lower-bound a convex function in the interior.

## Exact falsifiers of tempting shortcuts

For Conjecture 1 put y_i=6/[T(m_j+m_k)] with T=1/R+1/(2r). Theorem 1.1 gives
sum y_i^2<=3; the conjecture requires sum y_i^3<=3. Positive numbers
(6/5,6/5,1/10) have square sum 289/100<3 and cube sum 3457/1000>3.
This is a countermodel to that implication, **not geometrically realized
medians** and not a source refutation. Full median constraints remain needed.
Writing p=sum m, q=sum pair-products, t=product m, D=pq-t and E=p^2+q,
one has exactly sum 1/(p-m_i)^3=(E^3-6pED+3D^2)/D^3.
Newton identities for the reciprocals prove this formula. No sign certificate
for its source comparison was obtained. Source median-duality/Gerretsen
adaptation remains the strongest classical-objection route, not a claimed
exclusion without a complete bridge.

K=2(sqrt5+sqrt2)/sqrt13>2. In the relaxation retaining only aR_1>=br_3+cr_2
and its cyclic analogues, let epsilon=1/1000,
