## Sustained full-scope proof attempt

The target is all ranks and all thresholds, not just rank2 or asymptotic thresholds. Factor Sigma as a Gram matrix of unit vectors in R^r. For G=RU with chi_r radius independent of uniform U, the exact tail is the radial average of the surface measure of {U: min_j|<U,u_j>|>=t/R}. This is the source Theorem4.2 mechanism. Thus a full C4.1 proof would close all ranks of C3.1 at once. It also shows why a covering theorem alone is insufficient: knowing the set is nonempty below a critical alpha supplies no sharp measure lower bound.

Planar slice attempt: identify antipodal angles modulo pi, order perpendicular normal lines, and let gaps d_i>=0 sum to pi (duplicates allowed). The uncovered arc fraction for half-width alpha is

    (1/pi) sum_i (d_i-2alpha)_+.

Convexity of positive part gives >=(1-2n alpha/pi)_+, attained by equal gaps. Integrating conditional planar Gaussian radii gives the rank<=2 comparison. This is a routine parameter instance, explicitly excluded from discovery credit. For normals spanning higher dimension, arbitrary great-circle intersections have unequal effective widths and fewer/shifted projected zero sets. The equal-width slice formula cannot be integrated as if widths stayed alpha. A localization/planarization theorem preserving this union objective is missing. Merely projecting and renormalizing normals supplies no justified monotonicity.

The public cyclemath identity offers another possible all-rank route for p=2, but its nonnegative correction does not bound V2 in the required direction by the cyclic V2. Controlling ordinary volume and dropping that correction does not produce the full tail ordering; even a sharp p=2 proof would not resolve C3.1.

Covariance interpolation attempt: put n=3, C_ii=1,C_ij=-1/2, and Sigma_s=I+s(C-I). C is sign-conjugate to the cosine covariance. It is feasible on[0,1], det Sigma_s=(1-s)(1+s/2)^2. Let F_t(Sigma)=P(all |g_i|>=t), q=P(|Z|>=t), c=E[(Z²-1)1{|Z|>=t}]=2t phi(t). Smooth Gaussian density differentiation at s=0 gives F'_t(0)=0 by parity and

    F''_t(0) = (3/4)c² q >0     (t>0).

For A=C-I, the second density derivative is phi_3(x)[tr(A²)/2-x^T A² x+(x^T A x)²/4]. Odd integrands vanish; integration gives (3/4)q³-(3/2)(q+c)q²+(3/4)(q+c)²q=(3/4)c²q. Differentiation is justified near0 by uniform positive definiteness and Gaussian domination.

On the other hand source Proposition2.1 gives

    F_t(C)=(6/pi) integral_0^(pi/6) exp[-t²/(2sin²theta)] dtheta.

Differentiate with u=cot(theta) to obtain

    dF_t(C)/dt=-(6/pi)e^(-t²/2) integral_(sqrt3 t)^infty e^(-y²/2)dy.

Hence F_t(C)=1-6t/sqrt(2pi)+(3sqrt3/pi)t²+O(t³), whereas F_t(I)=q³=1-6t/sqrt(2pi)+(6/pi)t²+O(t³). Since sqrt3<2, F_t(C)<F_t(I) for all sufficiently small positive t. Yet along the feasible line F initially INCREASES. Therefore F is not convex along that line and a monotone straight interpolation to the cyclic optimizer cannot prove the conjecture. This is an analytic route obstruction, consistent with the source's discussion of spurious local minima. It is NOT an actual source counterexample, and receives no credit.

## Strongest objection, precise gap, falsifier

Nothing above compares the full higher-rank zone union with the cyclic union at every alpha, or establishes all-rank Gaussian tail comparison at every t. Covering existence, rank2 Jensen, p2 identities, and local covariance derivatives are all insufficient. The strongest objection to a proposed proof is its unjustified passage from existential extremizers or planar slices to a global surface-measure comparison. No full ordinary result survives.

A genuine falsifier of C3.1 would be an explicit admissible PSD diagonal1 Sigma and a positive t with a rigorous tail bound strictly BELOW the cyclic formula. For C4.1 it would be actual unit normals and alpha with rigorous union measure ABOVE the cyclic union. Our interpolation failure is neither. Finite checks cannot settle either universal claim.
