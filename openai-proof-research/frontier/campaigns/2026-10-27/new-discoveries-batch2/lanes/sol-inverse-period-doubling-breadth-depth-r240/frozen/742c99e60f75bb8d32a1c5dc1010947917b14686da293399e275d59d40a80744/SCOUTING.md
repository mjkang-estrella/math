## Ranked investment decisions and outcomes

1. **T Conj5, full R/C residual compression.** Source/quantifiers #5. Closest actual proof body P1.2 and T11 control exact equalities. Mechanism: pointed arrangement cells, bounded vertices, recession removal, then real-part projection. Decisive step was proving boundedness of EVERY cell vertex, including multiple constant rows; completed in PROOF B. Full target deduction completed. Objection: this is a complete classical polyhedral/cofactor bridge, with no independent novelty basis; do not count as a fresh discovery merely because r214 left it unresolved.

2. **T Conj3, full arbitrary additive G.** Source/quantifiers #3. Closest actual proof body T12 uses its Lemma4; P1.2 covers only rational existence. Mechanism: Smith normal form, finite-denominator lattice, all maximal augmented minors. Decisive step was retaining membership in G while shrinking: completed in PROOF D. Objection: original1989 integer-bound body is denied; theorem statement read in T, not independently re-proved, and full standard-theorem bridge makes discovery credit doubtful. This and rank1 are ONE family, not two prospective counts.

3. **U Conj4, all uniquely determined rational configurations.** Source/quantifiers #10. Closest proof bodies U's own constant-merging/cofactor reductions and P2.3/Thm1.1. Mechanism: split coordinates at a putative >2 gap, form relations between large variables whose differences equal small coordinates, and seek an independent rescaling/translation preserving all equalities. Decisive next step: prove a nonzero deformation exists despite cycles of difference constraints, or exhibit one exact uniquely determined rational configuration refuting the source. Objection: a global bound on relative magnitude does not exclude one internal gap; no universal deformation invariant is established. Lower priority reserve, not a promised fresh lead.

4. **A division-graph injectivity.** Source/quantifiers #14. Closest actual body Thm4.1 recovers many invariants but not multiplication. Mechanism: encode for each cyclic subgroup all coset-orbit incidence maps across subgroup inclusions, then compare a concrete nonisomorphic same-mark pair. Decisive step: an exact full-D isomorphism certificate, not matching partial invariants. Objection: no such pair selected/certified or current specialist clearance. Defer before expensive group enumeration.

5. **V adaptive IV/ML, optional inherited follow-up.** Source/quantifiers #16. With IV weight t, the source's two generic bounds give lambda(t)=max(1-t/2,(1+t)/2), minimized at t=1/2 with value3/4. Indeed for q<=1/2 the ratio is tq+1-t, maximized at q=1/2; for q>=1/2 it is tq+(1-t)/(2q), convex in q and maximized at an endpoint. This only shows the source's separate inequalities cannot yield a better UNIVERSAL constant weight; it is not a feasible common-profile metric certificate and does not obstruct adaptive weights. Frank's lower family itself gives asymptotic distortion 2+t and can be avoided there by t=0. No concrete all-t, same-profile certificate appears, so pivoted to ranks1/2. No claim about unrestricted randomized optimum.
