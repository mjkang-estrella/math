Abstract.
    

We present analysis of some new means recently introduced by M. Raïssouli and A. Rezgui.
We establish comparison relations and results on  [MATH (K,N)] (K,N)(K,N)-sub/super-stabilizability where  [MATH K] KK and  [MATH N] NN belong to the class of power means, denoted by  [MATH B_{p}] BpB_{p},
and  [MATH M] MM is one of the classical or recently studied new means.
Assuming that means  [MATH K] KK,  [MATH M] MM and  [MATH N] NN have asymptotic expansions, we present the complete asymptotic expansion of the resultant mean-map. As an application of the obtained asymptotic expansions and the asymptotic inequality between  [MATH M] MM and  [MATH \mathcal{R}(B_{p},M,B_{q})] ℛ⁡(Bp,M,Bq)\mathcal{R}(B_{p},M,B_{q}), we show how to find the optimal parameters  [MATH p] pp and  [MATH q] qq for which  [MATH M] MM is  [MATH (B_{p},B_{q})] (Bp,Bq)(B_{p},B_{q})-sub/super-stabilizable.
  



Key words and phrases: Resultant mean-map; Stable mean; Sub-stabilizable mean; Super-stabilizable mean; Asymptotic expansion; Inequalities; Power means

(1)
