12 otherwise. The open content is the lower bound λ ≥ 12 when 12 ∤ n.

## Theorem (certified here; stronger than the conjecture)

For every n ≥ 19, λ(3,2,1)(C_n(1,3)) = 11 if 12 | n, and λ ≥ 12 otherwise. For every
even n ≥ 142 with 12 ∤ n, λ = 12.

## Mechanism

### Window lemma (n > 6t, here n ≥ 19)

- A walk of length ≤ 3 in C_n(1,t) lifts to Z with displacement e, |e| ≤ 3t.
- For n > 6t, each residue d has at most one such representative e, because two of
  them would differ by a nonzero multiple of n, which exceeds 6t.
- So dist_{C_n}(i, i+d) ≤ 3 iff d ≡ e (mod n) for some e with dist_Z(e) ≤ 3, and
  then the two distances are equal.
- The constraints are |f(i) − f(i+e)| ≥ 4 − dist(e), for e in the set
  {1:3, 2:2, 3:3, 4:2, 5:1, 6:2, 7:1, 9:1}. Offset 8 is at distance 4 and carries no
  constraint.

Hence span-(K−1) labelings of C_n correspond exactly to cyclic words of length n
over {0, …, K−1} in which every cyclic 10-window satisfies these constraints. These
in turn correspond to closed walks of length n in the graph whose vertices are the
valid 9-windows, with an edge whenever the windows overlap in a valid 10-window.

### Lower bound

- Every closed walk lies inside one strongly connected component (SCC).
- In an SCC of period p, every closed-walk length is ≡ 0 (mod p).
- 12 labels: 152 states, 128 edges. The only nontrivial SCCs are two directed
  12-cycles: f(i) ≡ c + 5i and f(i) ≡ c + 7i (mod 12). So span 11 forces 12 | n.
- 11 labels: 6 states, 4 edges, no cycle. So span 10 is impossible for all n ≥ 19.
  This independently replays Proposition 5 for n ≥ 19.

### Upper bound (independent of the source)

- The 12-cycle gives span-11 labelings for every n ≡ 0 (mod 12) with n ≥ 19.
- With 13 labels, from s0 = (0,5,10,3,8,1,6,11,4), there are closed walks through s0
  of length 12 and of every even length in [142, 164].
- Closed-walk lengths through s0 are closed under addition. So every even n ≥ 142 is
  a length: n = n' + 12k with n' ∈ [142, 152].
- Hence λ ≤ 12 for all even n ≥ 142, matching Theorem 10 independently. Reconstructed
  witness words were re-validated as cyclic labelings.

## Certificate size and formal burden

- 152 states and 128 edges: small enough for a hand-checkable listing of the two
  cycles plus the 11-label graph.
- Lean route: decidable window-validity predicate; a list of valid windows checked
  by `decide` (12^9 enumeration is too large for the kernel, so it needs a
  structured DFS enumeration proof); the closed-walk ↔ labeling lemma for n > 6t;
  the potential-function argument.
- Formal burden: moderate. No Lean work was done.
