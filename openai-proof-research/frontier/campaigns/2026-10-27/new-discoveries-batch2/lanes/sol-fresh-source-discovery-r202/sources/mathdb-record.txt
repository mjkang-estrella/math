Olson's conjecture on regular-sequence additive bases


Let G be a finite abelian group, let p be the smallest prime divisor of ∣G∣, and let c0​(G) be the least integer t such that every regular sequence over G of length at least t is an additive basis. Olson's conjecture asserts that
c0​(G)=⎩⎨⎧​p∣G∣​+p−2,p∣G∣​+2p−3,​G≅Cp​⊕Cpn​,G≅Cp​⊕Cpn​,​

for every finite abelian group G and every positive integer n. The unresolved cases are chiefly groups of the form Cp​⊕Cpn​ with p≥5.References



On additive bases of finite abelian groups — arXiv — Weidong Gao, Hanbin Zhang

Open · progress reported

A new paper claims major progress for higher-rank groups and several rank-two families, but the general conjecture remains open.

Olson’s conjecture predicts a formula for the regular-sequence threshold c0​(G) of finite abelian groups. The rank-two case G=Cp​⊕Cpn​ remains the central unresolved case.

Known results


G=C3​⊕C3q​, with q≥5 prime: c0​(G)=3q+3 (2021).

For G=Cn1​​⊕Cn2​​, the conjecture holds when p≥3, n1​≥2p, and n1​n2​≥72p6 (2021).

Earlier work covers cyclic groups, groups of even order, finite abelian p-groups, and several higher-rank families.


October 27, 2026 claimed advance

Gao and Zhang’s paper was reported as proving c0​(G)=∣G∣/p+p−2 for all groups of rank at least three and determining several rank-two families. The remaining cases are described as mainly Cp​⊕Cpn​ with p≥5, but the supplied evidence does not independently verify the full classification.

Current status (as of October 2026): Several substantial families are settled, while the full conjecture remains open, especially for Cp​⊕Cpn​ with p≥5; the latest broader classification is an unverified claim.Sources
