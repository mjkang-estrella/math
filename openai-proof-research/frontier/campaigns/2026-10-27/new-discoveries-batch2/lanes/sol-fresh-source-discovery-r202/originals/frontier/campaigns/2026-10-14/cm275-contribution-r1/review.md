## Scope assessed

The assertion is QMA membership for every distinct rational unit-nuclear
configuration, every positive unary electron count, and the stated rational
threshold promise with gap at least inverse total input length. The energy is
the infimum of the full spinful antisymmetric Coulomb form on all of R^(3N),
with nuclear repulsion omitted. Source hardness then makes this same promise
problem QMA-complete. There is no supplied orbital basis, bounded physical
diameter, separation, binding, ground eigenvector, confinement, neutrality,
selected-spin, regularity or preparation promise. This is the statement used
for the contribution decision; a bounded-box or bound-state subclass would
not receive this verdict in its place. Exponentially large binary charges
remain outside the statement.

## What is already paid

The stipulated family 275 source proves full-continuum hardness for unit
nuclei. Its full many-body proof already uses fiberwise Hardy, form cores,
all-spin antisymmetry, a complementary-space lower bound, and square
completion. Its density proof establishes the required fixed gap for the
constructed separated well modes. Its implementation caps Coulomb kernels
and integrates explicit fixed-dimensional orbital densities. These are real
antecedent operations, not an absence of analytic tools. The new proof gets
no credit for hardness or for naming Hardy, IMS or complement elimination.

The binary-charge companion supplies a different engineered-well hardness
construction. Its read introduction explicitly distinguishes that target and
does not assert general membership. This omission alone contributes nothing
to the present verdict. The present polynomial bounds use the explicit
unit-nucleus list and would not establish binary-charge membership.

O'Gorman–Irani–Whitfield–Fefferman's published Section V and Appendix D
retain a supplied finite Gaussian orbital space. Appendix D computes
Gaussian/nuclear matrix entries, discards controlled separated blocks, and
bounds the resulting finite Fock-space perturbation by a polynomial number
of coefficient errors. It adds unit nuclei to that specified family; it
does not give an input-uniform basis or a lower comparison for arbitrary
continuum states. The supplied-basis QMA verifier remains an antecedent.

Zheng–Leng–Liu–Wu Theorem 3.3 is the closest positive upper-bound result.
Its full proof discretizes smooth few-body potentials on Dirichlet boxes
whose side lengths are polynomially bounded above and below, then uses a
unary coordinate embedding and diagonal penalties to obtain StoqMA
membership. This already pays bounded smooth-box membership. It cannot be
substituted directly here: the permitted box diameter may be exponential,
rescaling multiplies derivative bounds by that diameter, and its unrestricted
scalar minimum does not impose the fermionic sector.

Kivlichan et al.'s actual Fourier Cauchy–Schwarz, discretization and sampled-
norm proofs use pointwise wavefunction bounds and subsequent volume factors.
Their polynomial-spacing corollary adds derivative assumptions, and their
dynamic comparison requires cutoff control during both evolutions. Childs
et al.'s Fourier-tail proof similarly retains a high-derivative parameter;
its Coulomb discussion describes a periodic-box replacement and softened
interaction. Those are useful algorithms, but not the uniform spectral-
infimum certificate required here. Schuch–Verstraete's read passages supply
finite Hubbard membership and a designed-potential realization, not the
arbitrary nuclear input interface.

## Residual operation and why it is substantial

The substantive object is a uniform two-sided continuum-to-register energy
comparison whose lower bound is valid for every state that the verifier can
accept. The proof pays it with a linked construction, not merely the claim
that a smooth approximation should work.

First, the product IMS partition retains every inner particle number k.
The lower bound discards only nonnegative exterior kinetic energy and
repulsion and controls the exterior attraction. Conversely, disjoint free
packets are wedged with an arbitrary correlated k-electron state to restore
exactly N particles. This produces one explicit box with error polynomially
small independently of binding and of the existence of a minimizing vector.
The box can be exponentially large, so this step alone does not reduce to
the polynomial-box upper-bound theorem.

Second, Hardy capping gives a separation-free form error controlled by
kinetic energy. After capping, a bounded-potential eigenvector in the box
has a bounded kinetic **operator norm**, giving a physical-frequency cutoff
independent of box volume. This cutoff argument is standard spectral
compression in its own right; it is not separately credited as a discovery.
An abstract complementary-gap square-completion argument could also serve
this part. Neither choice by itself establishes a sound sampled verifier.

The decisive finite-model step is the exact isometry of every shifted
sampling map on the retained band. Translating within one cell and comparing
the two normalized sample vectors gives, uniformly over normalized states,

    |A_0(f) - integral V |f|²|
      ≤ Lip(V) sqrt(D) h + 2 ||V|| W D h.

The proof uses translation in L2 and exact Fourier orthogonality, not a
supremum bound times the exponentially large configuration-space volume.
It controls the entire Hermitian compression, including malicious witnesses,
without a high-derivative premise on the original Coulomb eigenfunction.
Odd reflection identifies the grid's accepted parity sector with the
Dirichlet sine space; binary frequency/position registers retain polynomial
qubit cost even when the physical box and mode count are exponential.

Finally, the explicit cutoff, oddness and sign-representation projections
make that analytic comparison apply to arbitrary supplied quantum states.
Antisymmetrization, QFT, reversible arithmetic and amplification are standard
operations. Their role is to close the certificate's validity conditions;
the claim is not that any of these circuit primitives is newly invented.

I considered the strongest technical-reduction objection: cap the potential,
put it in a box, invoke the known box theorem and add an antisymmetry check.
The published box proof's unary grid and polynomial-side assumptions do not
cover this substitution. Retaining physical coordinates requires a different
binary-grid verification interface; rescaling to its unit box moves the
exponential size into derivative/kinetic scales. A generic pointwise
quadrature estimate also fails to supply the needed polynomial dependence
in variable dimension. The supplied source's orbital quadrature and fixed
well gap do not fill those holes. CM275 supplies explicit replacements with
all error constants in the right input parameters and with both energy
inequalities. This is more than coefficient transfer, a closure property,
or rephrasing a known upper bound.

The elementary nature of the individual tools is not itself a reason for
SUBSTANTIAL or TECHNICAL. The positive decision concerns their concrete
joint conversion with no remaining binding, regularity, geometric-size or
witness-validity promise. I found no reduction of that conversion to an
already supplied source/companion operation or to the actual compared
primary upper-bound proof. This is a bounded operation-level assessment,
not an inference from failure to locate a literally identical theorem.

## Eligibility and objections

I searched the project Markdown record, including root proposals, earlier
iterations and reviews, with the precise problem and input vocabulary.
`history-search.json` records the pattern, exact scanned file identities and
matches. The results outside this fresh candidate/current review chain were
catalogue copies, not a developed membership proof. This search is evidence,
not a claim that every historical file was read semantically.

The old Coulomb-positive target was checked directly in its frozen scope:
family 373 concerns a fixed three-marginal optimal-transport cost, positive
Schwartz densities, and exclusion of all deterministic Monge optimizers.
The I220/I223 quantitative chain concerns BV/fractional complexity of
preserving maps. Neither concerns fermionic Coulomb operators, input
encoding, or QMA membership. I137's crystalline branch is also a different
energy/coherence problem. No excluded chain is being narrowed or renamed.

There are **no required contribution/eligibility repairs** against the sealed
scope. Nonblocking publication requirements: credit the smooth-box upper
bound prominently; retain the source-hardness premise and original full
input model; describe the new contribution as the uniform verification
interface; do not claim new Hardy/IMS/Fourier/projection methods, full
binary-charge membership, efficient state preparation, or worldwide priority.
