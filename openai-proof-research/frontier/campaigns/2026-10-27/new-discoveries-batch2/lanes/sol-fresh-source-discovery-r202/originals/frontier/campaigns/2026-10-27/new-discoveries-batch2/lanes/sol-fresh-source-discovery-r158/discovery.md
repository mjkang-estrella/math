Let A_(rho,nu)=[m_rho]p_nu and E_(nu,lambda)=[p_nu]e_lambda, invertible constant rational matrices indexed by partitions of n. Plethysm acts diagonally in power sums, with D_(nu,nu)=product_(r in nu)(q^(kr)-1). Therefore the exact full coefficient vector is

c_(mu,k) = (q-1)^(-n) E^(-1) D A^(-1) h_(mu,k).

## Why generic Schur positivity does not close the gap

Consider G[X;q]=s_(2)[X]+q^2 s_(1,1)[X]. This is Schur positive and G[X;1]=p_1^2, the expected degree-two modified-Macdonald specialization. Nevertheless

G[(q-1)X;q]/(q-1)^2 = -q e_(1,1)+(q+1)^2 e_(2).

Proof: s2=(p1^2+p2)/2, s11=(p1^2-p2)/2; substitute p1 -> (q-1)p1 and p2 -> (q^2-1)p2, then use p2=e1^2-2e2. The numerator cancels (q-1)^2 exactly, leaving the displayed polynomial identity. Thus even Schur positivity plus polynomial divisibility and q=1 specialization are insufficient. G is NOT asserted to be an actual Macdonald polynomial. This is a method falsifier only. The simpler individual s2 test also has negative e2 coefficient -(q^(2k)-1)/(q-1)^2; eight exact q=2 evaluations are saved, but the stronger polynomial example avoids relying on poles as the obstruction.
