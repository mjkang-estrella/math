## Exact source and independent mathematical adjudication

The actual Bernoulli article, 27(3) (2021), pp2011–2039, DOI 10.3150/20-BEJ1299, uses saturated trees over a finite alphabet with at most countably many finite/infinite contexts. Definition 2.5 requires every q_c(letter)>0. Definitions 2.7/2.10 give longest internal *strict* suffix s, preceding letter alpha and the product of q-factors before alpha s. Equation (5) groups finite contexts of length |alpha s|+n−1 in kappa_(alpha s)(n). Proposition 3.1(i)/Definition 3.2 define stability by sigma(T) subset T. Conjecture 4.2 on p2037 asserts: for every such non-null probabilisation, absence of an infinite shift-stable subtree implies convergence of every cascade series **and geometric rates**. No finite-S or minimal-context premise is stated. This is the same rate clause as preprint Conjecture 5.2, not a claim about process mixing.

Here is a self-contained all-order verification of the candidate's substantive bridges. Let p_m be the prefixes of b=(01)^infinity, a_k=(01)^k00, N_0=0, N_k=k²(N_(k−1)+k+10). Make internal precisely the p_m together with all a_kv of length less than N_k, then include both children of every internal node. Anchors are prefix-incomparable: the first shorter anchor's terminal defect disagrees with the longer one's alternating spine. The internal set is prefix closed, so this is saturated. Every departure from b ends immediately or within one finite bush; b is the unique infinite branch. There are countably many contexts. Assign both letters probability 1/2 at every finite and infinite context.

An infinite rooted finitely branching subtree would have an infinite branch, necessarily b. Shift stability would then require all prefixes of (10)^infinity, whereas 1 is a leaf of T. Thus no infinite shift-stable subtree exists. This proof uses node stability, the literal source condition, without requiring the subtree to be saturated. A descendant subtree off the spine is finite, and a spine-rooted alternating branch has the same missing shifted-partner obstruction.

For k>=3 set H=N_(k−1)+3, R=N_k−2k−2−H=kB+r, 0<=r<k. The 2^((k−2)B) leaves

    a_k (11u_1)…(11u_B) 1^(r+H),    |u_i|=k−2,

all have depth N_k. A nonempty spine-internal suffix is impossible because every suffix ends with at least three ones (length-one/two suffixes are 1 and 11). A suffix internal in an earlier bush j<k has length <N_j<=N_(k−1), so lies inside the terminal ones and cannot start with a_j. Any later anchor j>=k needs an alternating prefix of length 2k. In the tail every window of 2k letters contains a complete forced 11 pair or two terminal ones: forced pairs start k apart, and the distance from the last forced pair to the terminal run is at most k. Inside a_k, any proper start either begins at 1 or encounters its 00 defect before 2k alternating letters; its last zero can alternate for at most two letters into the tail. At the original start, only j=k matches, and that is the whole word, not a strict suffix. This excludes every nonempty internal strict suffix against **all** bush indices, not a finite truncation.

Thus alpha-LIS=1 is fixed. Fair q gives cascade 2^(1−N_k). Write D_k=N_k−1−(k−2)B. The selected leaves yield kappa_1(N_k)>=2^(−D_k), while

    0 <= D_k/N_k <= 2/k + N_(k−1)/N_k + (3k+5)/N_k -> 0.

Here N_(k−1)/N_k<=1/k² and N_k>=k²(k+10). Also kappa_1(n)<=1: at most 2^(n−1) length-n words end in 1 and each relevant cascade is 2^(1−n). Hence limsup kappa_1(n)^(1/n)=1. No C*r^n term bound with r<1 exists; a geometric nonnegative tail bound would imply one. Equation (5)'s length index is exactly n for alpha-LIS1. Arbitrary re-enumeration is not used.

All finite leaves are prefix free; disjoint fair cylinders give sum_c 2^(−|c|)<=1. Therefore sum_n kappa_1(n)<=2. Bare convergence is not refuted or resolved in general. A redundant iid representation is explicitly permitted by the read source definitions. The effect is about representation-specific cascade weights, not slow mixing of the iid process.

## Classical objection and closest-body comparison

The strongest concrete objection remains: the construction disguises a standard slow prefix-code distribution in an iid chain. Duchi's actual Theorem 2.4.2, printed p39 and its countable extension/converse, supplies arbitrary Kraft-admissible lengths and a prefix code. It does **not** allocate its codewords to one prescribed alpha-LIS or exclude all infinite shift-stable subtrees after saturation. A code constructed from a constant spine, for example, contains the corresponding stable comb. Conversely, merely replacing that spine by an alternating one does not establish the fixed suffix property in large bushes. The forced-pair and terminal-run argument above supplies that missing all-index bridge. Kraft and iid stationarity are classical, uncredited tools; neither alone is a complete theorem-to-target implication.

Ferreira–Gallo–Paccaut arXiv:1907.02442v2, Theorem 3/Corollary 4, and Appendix A Lemmas 3–4 with their proofs were inspected. The appendix's countable sets with near-full finite-word growth make the broad entropy/growth idea classical. Those statements and constructions do not put the resulting many leaves in one alpha-LIS class while excluding a stable subtree. Their growth function counts prefixes/suffixes of an infinite set, not the literal graded fixed cascade family. Their compatibility/weak-Bernoullicity criteria concern the process law. No complete subsumption bridge follows from them.

The 2012 arXiv:1007.2986 bamboo body, Section 4.2.1, equation (19), gives repeated q_1(0)<1 factors and hence geometric bounds. That reasoning uses the sparse bamboo's specific contexts; it does not control the arbitrarily large finite bushes here. The 2019 companion's Proposition 0.4.5/Theorem 0.4.6 require the relevant stability/finite-S conditions and do not establish this nonstable geometric assertion. These preprints are not asserted byte-identical to later chapter versions.

New closest-body coverage: Gallo, *Chains with unbounded variable length memory: perfect simulation and a visible regeneration scheme*, AAP43 (2011), pp735–759, DOI10.1239/aap/1316792668, actual publisher PDF. Section 4 defines finite reference-string depth functions and Figure 3(a) explicitly allows superlinear finite bushes; Theorem 1 on p747 separately requires a linear depth function for its exponential simulation-tail conclusion. Sections 6.3–6.5 derive the sufficient renewal bounds; they do not prove a converse about a fixed cascade series. These examples are an important precedent for deep-bush constructions, not a discovered novelty here. Figure 3(a) has a constant infinite spine; Figure 3(b) includes constant infinite contexts too. Saturation around a constant infinite branch contains an infinite shift-stable comb, excluding both as counterexamples to Conjecture4.2. Their simulation/return-time conclusion also differs from literal kappa_1(n). This body gives no complete same-object exclusion.

I therefore retain **scoped substantive PASS** for the explicit universal rate refutation. It is not credited for summability, code construction, iid existence, helpers or formalization. Elementary length alone is not an exclusion criterion. A short older theorem supplying the simultaneous bridges would overturn this judgment.
