Implementation notes, not a manuscript. The mathematical proof is LastSplit.lean. A high-token board with no duplicate above index one is bounded by count(1) copies of index one plus one copy of each index 2..n; hence it has at least four ones. Two C1 moves make S2 legal. Along a legal success word of length k<=3 each reciprocal factor is >=a=(2n)^-1, so its product is >=a^k>=a^3. The normalized actual continuation cylinder supplies exactly that ratio, even for k=1. This cylinder is included in the split-soon event. Disjoint complementary finite sums yield the one-block contraction. Equal-length prefixes have disjoint complete-word cylinders. Summing their actual masses gives the finite prefix-tree contraction and induction. Remaining endpoint gaps are listed in SUMMARY.md.
