The sharp weighted Bessel K tail bound

Problem

R. E. Gaunt, Inequalities for some integrals involving modified Bessel functions, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 147 (2019), 2937–2951, DOI 10.1090/proc/14433, arXiv:1806.00524v2, Conjecture 2.4, manuscript page 7. The source states:

“Conjecture 2.4. Let n < 1. For ν ≥ 1 − n + √(2(1 − n)), define α_{ν,n} := ν + n − √((ν + n)² − 2ν − 1). Then, for all x > 0, ∫x^∞ K{ν+n}(t)/t^ν dt > K_{ν+n−α_{ν,n}}(x)/x^ν, ν ≥ 1 − n + √(2(1 − n)). (2.17) We have equality in (2.17) when n = 1, and this equality is valid for all ν ∈ ℝ. Let α′{ν,n} := ν + n + √((ν + n)² − 2ν − 1), so that ν + n − α′{ν,n} = −(ν + n − α_{ν,n}) and thus K_{ν+n−α′{ν,n}}(x) = K{ν+n−α_{ν,n}}(x). Then, the index ν + n − α_{ν,n} (and equivalently ν + n − α′{ν,n}) in inequality (2.17) is best possible, in the sense that, for any β ∉ {α{ν,n}, α′{ν,n}}, either K{ν+n−β}(x)/x^ν < K_{ν+n−α_{ν,n}}(x)/x^ν for all x > 0, or there exists a y > 0 such that ∫y^∞ K{ν+n}(t)/t^ν dt < K_{ν+n−β}(y)/y^ν.”

Motivation

The source note is D5/L/Asymptotics/gaunt2019besselintegrals. The reused integral representation is recorded in D5/L/Asymptotics/nist2026dlmfbesselkintegral. The question asks for the largest absolute order that can give a universal lower expression with the fixed weight x−ν.

Gap

The sharp order is fixed by q2=p2−2ν−1. The required comparison is not an ordinary monotonicity statement in the order: it also compares a weighted tail with a point value. The strict lower bound, sharpness and n=1 identity are separate clauses of claim.

Route

The parameter range gives q≥0, q<ν and q+1<p. For u>0, the function s↦cosh(us​) is strictly convex on [0,∞). Its second derivative has the sign of vcoshv−sinhv, which is positive for v>0. Adjacent secants at (q−1)2,(q+1)2,p2 give, when q>0,

cosh(pu)>cosh(qu)coshu+qν​sinh(qu)sinhu.

At q=0, the tangent at s=1 gives cosh(pu)>coshu+νusinhu. Put Sr​(u)=sinh(ru)/r for r=0 and S0​(u)=u, and define

hr​(u)=cosh(pu)−cosh(ru)coshu−νSr​(u)sinhu.

Integration by parts gives Kr​(t)=t∫0∞​e−tcoshuSr​(u)sinhudu. Differentiation under the integral and the fundamental theorem give

T(x)−x−νKr​(x)=∫x∞​t−ν∫0∞​e−tcoshuhr​(u)dudt.

A tilted Gaussian dominates each differentiated kernel. Weighted Bessel functions vanish at infinity and are integrable on every positive tail. The strict convexity comparison makes the right side positive at r=q.

For r>q, hr​(0)=hr′​(0)=0 and hr′′​(0)=q2−r2<0. Thus hr​<0 on a right-hand interval. A fixed inner subinterval gives a negative contribution with a slower exponential decay rate than any positive contribution outside that interval. Consequently the inner integral, and then the tail difference, are negative for all sufficiently large arguments. For ∣r∣<q, strict order monotonicity gives Kr​<Kq​. For n=1, the hyperbolic addition formula makes hν​ identically zero.

Falsifier
