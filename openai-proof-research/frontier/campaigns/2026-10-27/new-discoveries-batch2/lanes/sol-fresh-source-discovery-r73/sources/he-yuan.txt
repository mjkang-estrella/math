              Results on some conjectures about binomial coeﬃcients
                                          by

                                             Abstract
          Z. W. Sun, V. J. W. Guo and C. Krattenthaler proposed several conjectures about
      arithmetic properties of binomial coeﬃcients. In this paper, we aim to give new results
      for these conjectures or their extensions. In particular, we partially solve a conjecture
      of Z. W. Sun.

      Key Words: Binomial coeﬃcient, p-adic valuation, greatest common divisor,
      congruences.
      2020 Mathematics Subject Classiﬁcation: Primary 11B65; Secondary 05A10,
      11A07.
