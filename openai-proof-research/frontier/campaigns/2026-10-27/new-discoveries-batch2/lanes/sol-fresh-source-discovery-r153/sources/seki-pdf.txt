                                            Irreducibility of truncations of the Catalan generating function
                                                                                         Shin-ichiro Seki

                                                 Abstract. We prove that, for every positive integer n, the polynomial
                                                                                      C 0 + C 1 x + · · · + C n xn
                                                 is irreducible over Q, where Ck denotes the kth Catalan number.

otherwise.
    Coleman [1] gave an alternative proof of this theorem using Newton polygons.
    Katz and Rivin [2] used Magma to carry out systematic computations of the Galois groups of
truncations of a wide range of formal power series with rational coefficients. Among other results,
they verified computationally that the Galois group of Pn (x) over Q is isomorphic to the symmetric
group Sn for 1 ≤ n ≤ 2000; see [2, §4]. In particular, the irreducibility of Pn (x) over Q was already
known in this range. Sun conjectured in OEIS A224416 that the Galois group of Pn (x) over Q is
isomorphic to Sn for every positive integer n. We do not address this stronger conjecture in the
present paper.
