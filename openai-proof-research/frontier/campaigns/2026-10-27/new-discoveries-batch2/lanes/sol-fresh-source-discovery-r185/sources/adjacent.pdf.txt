George E. Andrews1 · Mohamed El Bachraoui2

Abstract
Han and Xiong defined Gaussian binomial coefficients with positive rational upper
index and conjectured that the integer-exponent part is coefficientwise largest at the
parameter 1/2. Ono reduced the conjecture to the primitive parameters 1/(2 m). We
prove the primitive comparison for every m ≥ 2 and k ≤ 6 m + 2, together with the
additional case (m, k) = (2, 15). The proof compares each primitive summand with
two adjacent summands in the 1/2-trace. All three local estimates are proved by finite
product decompositions. As a noncomputational consequence, the conjecture holds
for every positive rational parameter when k ≤ 15. Moreover, the remaining primitive
range for fixed k is reduced to 2 ≤ m ≤ (k − 3)/6, with (m, k) = (2, 15) removed.

Keywords Gaussian binomial coefficient · Integer trace · Coefficientwise positivity ·
Q-series

this finite computation: it gives a uniform analytic region in (m, k) and consequently
proves infinitely many cases with k > 200.

whenever
