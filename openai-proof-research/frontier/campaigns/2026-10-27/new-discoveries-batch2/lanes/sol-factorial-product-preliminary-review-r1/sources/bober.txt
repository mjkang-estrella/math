                                                  Abstract. We give a complete classification of a certain family of step func-
                                                  tions related to the Nyman–Beurling approach to the Riemann hypothesis
                                                  and previously studied by V. I. Vasyunin. Equivalently, we completely de-
                                                  scribe when certain sequences of ratios of factorial products are always inte-
                                                  gral. Essentially, once certain observations are made, this comes down to an
                                                  application of Beukers and Heckman’s classification of the monodromy of the
                                                  hypergeometric function n Fn−1 . We also note applications to the classification
                                                  of cyclic quotient singularities.

argument of a complex number to be in [0, 2π).
