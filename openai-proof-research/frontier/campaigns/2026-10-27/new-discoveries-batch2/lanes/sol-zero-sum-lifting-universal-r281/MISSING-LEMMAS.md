# Precise unresolved obligations

**Prime incidence endpoint (necessary and sufficient).** For every prime p, r≥1, N=2r(p−1)+1 and nonzero a_i∈F_p^r, using canonical integer representatives, let Z be all nonempty residue-zero subsets and c_j(I)=sum_I a_ij/p modulo p. Prove that for at least one j there exists λ∈F_p^Z with sum_I λ_I 1_I=0 in F_p^N and sum_I λ_I c_j(I)≠0. Equivalently, not all r carry vectors lie in the column space of the complete incidence matrix. No such universal lemma is proved here.

**Polynomial sufficient replacement (stronger, not asserted necessary).** With D and C_j defined in discovery.md in the Boolean algebra, prove some deg(D C_j)>r(p−1)+1 for every spanning list at that N, or find an alternative separator whenever the degree criterion fails. The universal strict inequality is unproved and might be too strong. Constants and multiplier degrees below N−r(p−1)−p cannot carry the required signal by degree alone.

**Composite propagation premise P(r,p,k).** For every prime p, r≥1 and k≥1, any length p k+(2r−1)(p−1) sequence of integer r-vectors nonzero modulo p has k disjoint nonempty blocks with sum zero modulo p and nonzero modulo p². The conditional implication from all P to the full all-n endpoint is proved in discovery.md. P(k=1) is the prime endpoint; the k>1 strengthening is unproved. A bounded-length greedy extraction proof fails on the rank-four ternary cap construction.

A counterexample alternative must specify p or general n, r, and all integer vector lifts, and verify every nonempty residue-zero subset is also zero modulo n². No such example is supplied. The cap construction is explicitly not claimed as one.
