arXiv:0804.0144v7 [math.NT] 20 Aug 2008

                                                  Abstract. We prove that the ratio of the Newman sum over numbers
                                                  multiple of a fixed integer which is not multiple of 3 and the Newman sum
                                                  over numbers multiple of a fixed integer divisible by 3 is o(1) when the
                                                  upper limit of summing tends to infinity. We also discuss a connection
                                                  of our results with a digit conjecture on primes.
