                                                  Abstract. This paper establishes an extension of Wolstenholme’s the-
                                                  orem to the ring of Gaussian integers Z[i]. For a prime p > 7, we prove
                                                  that the sum Sp of inverses of Gaussian integers in the set {n + mi |
                                                  1 ≤ n, m ≤ p − 1, gcd(p, mi + n) = 1} satisfies the congruence Sp ≡ 0
                                                                                                                      (k)
                                                  (mod p4 ). We further generalize this result to higher-power sums Sp ,
                                                  demonstrating structured divisibility patterns modulo powers of p. We
                                                  propose some conjectures generalising the connections between classical
                                                  Wolstenholme’s theorem and binomial coefficients. Special cases and
                                                  irregularities for small primes (p ≤ 1000) are explicitly computed and
                                                  tabulated.
