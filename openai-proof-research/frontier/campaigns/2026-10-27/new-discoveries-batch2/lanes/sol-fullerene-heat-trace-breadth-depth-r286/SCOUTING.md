# Breadth with a sustained fullerene route

Fifteen distinct source-question families across spectral graph theory, algebraic geometry, operator theory, optimization, learning, ordered spaces, logic, semigroups, complex analysis, convex geometry and nonlinear dynamics. Fourteen are additional to the assigned fullerene lead, not extra claims about that lead. Primary bodies span 2009–2024; older attributions are not counted as independently read originals. All dispositions are bounded screens, not universal novelty clearance.

## fullerene: Bille–Buchstaber–Spodarev

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.19322v1, Conjecture 3. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-3db898413dd9-fullerenes.txt`.

For every fixed alpha,beta in (0,1), character equality iff dual-fullerene isomorphism; feasible original orders may differ.

FULL mathematical refutation; rank 1. Exact crossing below. Entire-function equality is a different assertion.

## free-curves: Dimca

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.07591v1, Conjecture 3.1. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-9d35643980f3-2312.07591v1.txt`.

Freeness of complex projective line arrangements is determined by the intersection lattice.

Park: Terao endpoint, 40-year problem; no new universal mechanism. Do not count singularity or weak-combinatorics variants.

## quantum-walk: Coutinho–Guo

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.02236v1, 2.1 Open Problem. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-73d6f8c5009a-2404.02236v1.txt`.

For graphs with perfect state transfer, improve the universal cubic edge-versus-diameter growth to m=Omega(D^k) for some k>3.

Hold: finite spectral bounds do not supply a supercubic universal exponent. Strong cospectrality alone is not sufficient.

## friedrichs: Caruso–Michelangeli

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.05777v1, QUESTION 2. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-1f0669ae684f-2406.05777v1.txt`.

Identify additional assumptions on closed realizations of joint abstract Friedrichs operators under (4.1) that transfer normal-equation Krylov solvability to standard Krylov solvability.

Park: sufficient-conditions endpoint is open ended; scalar symmetric-part and compatible smooth-vector case is already provided in the body, lines 606–611. Cached quantum Krylov-complexity proof concerns another object.

## gradient: Kornowski–Shamir

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.13888v1, Open Problem 1. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-fee2908f1cfd-2406.13888v1.txt`.

Does one infinite stepsize sequence give a dimension-free anytime T^(-alpha) error bound with alpha>1 uniformly over all smooth convex functions?

Hold: a schedule tuned to final horizon does not answer the anytime quantifier. Current successors not exhaustively screened; not ranked.

## bandits: Mussi–Drago–Metelli

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.06321v1, 3.2 Open Problem 2. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-f6b7e8f65797-2407.06321v1.txt`.

Find concentration bounds tight for Bernoulli observations under the bounded-RKHS and bounded-kernel hypotheses of Assumption 2.1.

Park: tightness and estimator not pinned to one exact minimax endpoint; do not count ordinary subgaussian concentration already printed in Eq.(2).

## greedy: Andrea García

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08935v1, Question 3. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-669b508ade88-2408.08935v1.txt`.

Is 1-almost-greediness equivalent to 1-semi-greediness for Banach and quasi-Banach spaces?

Hold: bi-monotone 1-semi-greedy to 3^(2/p)-almost-greedy Theorem 4.20 does not establish constant 1. No full-scope basis construction or proof here.

## schrodinger: Cuenin–Frank

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.11285v1, Question 1.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-54947a605826-2409.11285v1.txt`.

For d>=1, 0<gamma<1 (gamma>=1/2 if d=1), sigma>d/2, does the displayed weighted complex-eigenvalue Lieb–Thirring estimate hold for every V in L^(gamma+d/2), uniformly in h>0?

Park: universal analytic estimate; tensor or scalar examples do not give a complete sharp estimate.

## ordered-banach: Jochen Glück

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.19768v1, Open Problem 2.5.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-5641ed82ba0b-2409.19768v1.txt`.

If every increasing net with a supremum converges to it in norm in an ordered Banach space, must every order-bounded increasing sequence converge in norm (Levi property)?

Rank 2, provisional only. Attempt a cone whose bad bounded nets have no supremum. Normality is already forced by Remark 2.6; a nonnormal-cone example fails. Need all-net proof and successor/registry clearance.

## toric: Goel–Hess–Kuhlmann

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.06468v1, Open Problem 1.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-44e7a0cdace2-2411.06468v1.txt`.

Give a membership test for every strictly separating intermediate cone Ci in filtration (6), preferably via Gram matrices.

Park: intrinsic membership characterization is not supplied by ordinary SDP; source states such cones are not spectrahedral shadows. No uniquely specified complexity target.

## fixed-point: Masato Fujita

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.14661v1, Theorem 1.1.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-1b61cc663a2c-2411.14661v1.txt`.

Does definable completeness and strong Banach fixed point property imply o-minimality?

Exclude as publicly resolved: source gives a negative answer using definably complete locally o-minimal non-o-minimal expansions, Theorem 1.1 and curve-selection proof. This is not an unresolved survivor.

## semigroup: Trevor Jack

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.16284v1, Problem 9.4. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-d49954db2530-2411.16284v1.txt`.

Is there a finite semigroup whose Otto conjugacy is a congruence but whose quotient is not cancellative?

Exclude exact cached proof duplicate. FiniteOttoCongruence.lean defines the literal witness relation, uses idempotent powers, and proves result : claim. Infinite example does not refute the finite theorem.

## fermat: Xu–Mandal–Biswas

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.19338v1, Open problem 1.1.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-f0be43b2420c-2412.19338v1.txt`.

Do the source Fermat-type PDDEs (1.3),(1.4) have finite-order transcendental entire solutions on C^2 when n>2,m=1?

Exclude: this paper expressly resolves this question in its main theorems; Theorem 2.2 has a no-solution branch m1>=3. No result is claimed from a bare open-problem label.

## bregman: Bauschke–Macklem–Wang

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.3127v1, Problem 3.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-9fb0ce7ced93-1003.3127v1.txt`.

Does a full-domain nonquadratic Legendre function exist such that every nonempty closed convex subset is right-Bregman-Chebyshev?

Park pending successor and classical affine-convexity-preserver screening. Source explicitly ties the question to a nonaffine gradient preserving convexity; dimension and one-dimensional degeneracies must be checked, no discovery claim.

## quadratic-stochastic: Ganikhodzhaev–Rozikov

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.4207v1, Problem 2.. Frozen body: `frozen/ref-a0a65de6f633-0902.4207v1.txt`.

Find necessary and sufficient conditions on heredity coefficients P_ij,k for a quadratic stochastic operator to be transversal.

Park: source already gives the even-principal-minor condition in the Volterra subclass. Repeating it does not solve the general QSO question; no fresh full classification mechanism.
