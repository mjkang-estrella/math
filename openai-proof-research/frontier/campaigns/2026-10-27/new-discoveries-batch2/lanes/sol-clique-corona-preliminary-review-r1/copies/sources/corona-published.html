Unimodality of the independence polynomial of clique corona graphs

Authors

  • Do Trong Hoang Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Vietnam https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9875-6516
  • Vadim E. Levit Ariel University, Israel https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4190-7050
  • Eugen Mandrescu Holon Institute of Technology, Israel https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3533-9728
  • My Hanh Pham Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8565-8957

DOI:

https://doi.org/10.26493/1855-3974.3383.3c7

Keywords:

Independence polynomial, unimodality, clique corona graph, well-covered graph

Abstract

For a positive integer p, a graph G belongs to the class Wp if |V(G)| ≥ p and any p disjoint independent sets can be extended to p
disjoint maximum independent sets. In this paper, we establish several inequalities for the coefficients of the independence polynomial of graphs in Wp. We then prove that the clique corona graph G ∘ Kp, formed from a graph G and a complete graph Kp, also belongs to Wp. As an application, we examine the unimodality problem for the independence polynomial of G ∘ Kp. In particular, we prove that the independence polynomial of Sn ∘ Kp, where Sn is a star graph, is unimodal.
