                                                  adapted to additional symmetries, weights, and non-Euclidean radial structures.
                                                  First, an improved Brascamp–Lieb inequality for even functions yields a strength-
                                                  ened Prékopa–Leindler theorem for even strongly log-concave functions. We also
                                                  record a Legendre-transform proof of a generalized log-Sobolev inequality for log-

  In the remaining sections of this paper, we present several extensions and vari-
ants of the Brascamp–Lieb inequality. Our interest comes from the connection of
these inequalities to convex geometry, and in particular to the B-conjecture. In [7],
Cordero-Erausquin, Fradelizi and Maurey proved that if γ is the standard Gaussian
measure on Rn and K ⊆ Rn is an origin-symmetric convex body, then
(5)                                   t 7→ γ(et K)
is log-concave. This answered a question which Latała attributes to Banaszczyk;
see [15]. Interestingly, the same result was proved by Brascamp and Lieb in [5],
decades before Latała even suggested the problem, but this result remained largely
unnoticed until lately – see the short discussion after Theorem 1.7. In any case, the
authors of [7] conjectured that (5) remains true with the Gaussian measure replaced
by any even log-concave measure on Rn . This conjecture became known as the
B-conjecture.
   Very recently, after this manuscript was mostly complete, the B-conjecture was
presumably resolved by OpenAI using an internal model. More precisely, their
preprint [17] claims a proof of the celebrated log-Brunn–Minkowski conjecture, orig-
inally raised and solved in dimension n = 2 by Böröczky, Lutwak, Yang and Zhang
                   REMARKS ON THE BRASCAMP–LIEB INEQUALITY                            5

[2]. A result of Saroglou [20] shows that the log-Brunn–Minkowski conjecture im-
plies the B-conjecture in full generality. We would also like to mention the paper
[9], where the B-conjecture was previously resolved when the measure is a certain
Gaussian mixture.
   While this recent result is a (yet to be peer reviewed) major achievement, it is
actually not very relevant to the functional inequalities discussed in this paper and
does not make them redundant in any way. Much more relevant to us is the work [8],
where the B-conjecture was proved for rotation-invariant log-concave measures. The
proof relied crucially on an improved Brascamp–Lieb inequality for even functions.
