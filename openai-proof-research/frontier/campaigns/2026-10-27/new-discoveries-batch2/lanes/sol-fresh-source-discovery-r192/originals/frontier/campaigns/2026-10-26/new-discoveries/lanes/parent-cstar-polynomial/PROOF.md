## Witness and universal factorization

Take A=M2(C), denote I its identity, and put

T=[[1,2],[0,−1]], (a1,a2,a3)=(T,−2T,T), (b1,b2)=(I,−I).

T²=I and Σa_j=0. The root matrices pairwise commute, but T is nonnormal; the C*-algebra generated by it is noncommutative. For every z∈M2(C), expand the ordered deletion derivative, without commuting z past T:

P′(z)=(z+2T)(z−T)+(z−T)²+(z−T)(z+2T)
     =3z²−3T²=3(z−I)(z+I).

All zT/Tz terms cancel separately. Thus the source's full factorization hypothesis holds on the entire algebra, with central, distinct, self-adjoint critical factors. Each a_j is a genuine zero of the displayed ordered cubic, and each b_k is a genuine zero of P′.

## Source inequality failures

Write H=TT*=[[5,−2],[−2,1]], J=T*T=[[1,2],[2,5]]. Then

Σa_j a_j*=6H, Σ(a_j a_j*)²=18H², Σa_j²=6I,
Σb_k b_k*=Σ(b_k b_k*)²=2I,

with the adjoint-reversed versions obtained by H→J. All these are exact finite-matrix identities.

In each case define the defect as the conjectured right side minus its left side.

- Conjecture2.1 defects:2(H−I)=[[8,−4],[−4,0]] and2(J−I)=[[0,4],[4,8]].
