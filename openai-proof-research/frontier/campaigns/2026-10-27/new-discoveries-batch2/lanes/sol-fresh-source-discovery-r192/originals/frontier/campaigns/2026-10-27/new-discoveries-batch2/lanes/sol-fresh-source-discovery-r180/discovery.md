## C. Fresh local source; public refutation — sustained source comparison

Aaron Potechin and Hing Yin Tsang, *A Conjecture on Induced Subgraphs of Cayley
Graphs*, arXiv:2003.13166v1, DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2003.13166, submitted
30 March 2020 (PDF header dated 31 March). Current live abstract lists v1 only.
Definitions 1.1–1.3, literal Conjecture 1, Theorem 2.1 and its two-stage proof
were read from actual PDF bytes. All groups are finite. S excludes identity,
is inverse-closed, and may be assumed generating. For EVERY such G,S and EVERY
U subset G with |U|>|G|/2, it asserts Delta(Cay(G,S)[U])>=sqrt(|S|/2).

Closest classical companion read early: Noga Alon and Kai Zheng, *Unitary signings
and induced subgraphs*, author-hosted PDF (arXiv alias 2003.04926).
Theorem 1.1 and its complete proof pull back a majority through the surjection
F_2^d -> F_2^n defined by distinct generators, apply Huang on the cube, and map
the distinct coordinate neighbors back to distinct neighbors. Potechin–Tsang
Theorem 2.1 uses products of cycles and the analogous abelian quotient map to
obtain sqrt((|S|+t)/2), t the involution count. These are explicit abelian
results. Commutativity is essential to that construction; there is no all-group
classical bridge to recommend as a fresh proof.

The decisive closest body is Florian Lehner and Gabriel Verret,
*Counterexamples to “A Conjecture on Induced Subgraphs of Cayley Graphs”*,
arXiv:2004.01327, DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2004.01327. Live abstract lists
v1 3 April 2020 and latest v2 21 May 2020. Actual v2 full body read, including
Sections 3–4 and all of Lemma 4.1. It explicitly cites precisely source
Conjecture 1 and gives both dihedral covers and a wreath-product infinite family.
The former alone refutes the literal quantifiers. This predates r180 by six
years. No journal VoR is claimed from an arXiv-only record. A distinct published
sensitivity companion copy returned 404; that limit does not undo the explicit
accessible public refutation. No other copy of that failed object was pursued.

### Complete public bridge, with the drawing replaced by an exact table

Write D_(18m) as pairs (i,e), i mod 9m and e mod 2, with multiplication
(i,e)(j,f)=(i+(-1)^e j,e+f). Take S={b,ab,a^3 b}. Its three elements are distinct
involutions, exclude identity, and generate since b and ab generate a and b.
For m=1 choose
U={(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(2,1),(3,0),(4,0),(5,1),(6,1),(7,0),(8,1)}.
Every selected vertex has exactly one selected neighbor. The five pairs are
{(0,0),(0,1)}, {(1,0),(2,1)}, {(3,0),(6,1)}, {(4,0),(5,1)},
{(7,0),(8,1)}; the formula for the other two neighbors at (i,e) is
(i+(-1)^e*j,e+1), j=0,1,3, making this finite base directly inspectable.
Thus |U|=10>9 and Delta=1<sqrt(3/2).

For EVERY integer m>=1 reduction i modulo 9 is a surjective homomorphism
D_(18m)->D_18. Each vertex has exactly the three neighbors indexed by 0,1,3,
whose images remain distinct; hence reduction bijects each neighborhood.
The inverse image U_m therefore has 10m vertices, each of induced degree 1,
inside an 18m-vertex cubic Cayley graph. Inversion identifies the right Cayley
convention used in checks.py with the source's left convention. This proves
the universal cover statement without extrapolating finite computations.
It is Lehner–Verret's Section 3 construction, fully public, not our result.
Their Lemma 4.1 additionally chooses one majority fiber and half-size parity
fibers in Z_2 wr G; within each nonzero fiber there are no selected neighbors,
and only the lamp-toggle edge exits the fiber, preserving maximum degree one.
Iterating gives unbounded valencies. That stronger construction reinforces the
scope match; it is not needed for the exclusion.

checks.py searches until the first ten-vertex base witness (5,033 subsets), then
checks m=1..12 exactly. Those are diagnostics of the public proof only. There
is no unresolved universal gap for this refutation, but no eligible new theorem.
