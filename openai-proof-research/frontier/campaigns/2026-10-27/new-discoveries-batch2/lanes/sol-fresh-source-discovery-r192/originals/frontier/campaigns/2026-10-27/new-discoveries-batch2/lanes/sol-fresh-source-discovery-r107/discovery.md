## Sustained full-scope attack on Conjecture9

Let A=Z_(p)G and let P be an arbitrary principal indecomposable F_pG-module. Over the complete ring Z_pG, idempotents lift, so choose its projective lift P_hat. Write W=Q_p tensor P_hat. To prove the missing implication it suffices to realize W by a rational QG-module V, for each such P.

Indeed, if Q_p tensor V is isomorphic to W, regard V as a dense rational subspace of W and set L=V intersect P_hat. Starting with a G-stable Z_(p)-lattice L0 in V, two p-adic lattices are commensurable, so p^a L0 is contained in L and L is contained in p^(-a)L0 for some a. Thus L is a finite free Z_(p)-lattice. Density shows its completion is P_hat; L is G-stable. The extension Z_(p) -> Z_p is faithfully flat and finite presentation allows projectivity to descend. Consequently L is an A-projective lifting of P. Source Proposition1.4 would finish the full conjecture if such rational realizations were constructed uniformly. This is a sufficient bridge, not a claimed new theorem or a necessary-only shortcut.

The hypothesis supplies only some positive rational V with

    alpha([V]) = beta([P]) in G(F_pG).

It does not identify Q_p tensor V with W. The decomposition map forgets ordinary character information on p-singular elements and extension structure of reductions. No argument was obtained that selects a rational witness having the correct entire Q_p representation. Character rationality, local/global Schur indices, and nonnegative principal-projective multiplicities cannot simply be assumed. Nor does choosing an arbitrary stable lattice in V make its reduction projective.

The failure of the naive shortcut is uniform, not just sampled. For every odd prime p, take G=C_p. QG has two simple rational representations: the trivial representation of dimension1 and the cyclotomic representation of dimension p-1. F_pG is F_p[t]/(t^p), with t=g-1, and has one simple module S. Its regular projective P has class p[S]. Both rational witnesses

    V_bad = trivial^p,    V_good = trivial plus cyclotomic

have decomposition class p[S]. Yet V_bad has character p on each nonidentity group element, while the regular lift has character0; V_bad is not its rational realization. Every lattice in V_bad has trivial G-action, so its reduction is S^p, never P: t acts as zero on S^p and as a length-p nilpotent Jordan block on P. Nonprojectivity also follows because finitely generated projectives over the local algebra F_p[t]/t^p are free, while S^p is not its rank-one regular module.

This does **not refute Conjecture9**: V_good supplies precisely the correct lift in this classical group. It invalidates the proposed inference that an arbitrary positive K0 witness is enough. The source conjecture only needs existence of one good choice, and could conceivably force it by an additional argument. No family of finite groups was found satisfying the whole inclusion while lacking one required rational/projective lift. Normal Sylow reduction and the source A5 calculations remain excluded cases.
