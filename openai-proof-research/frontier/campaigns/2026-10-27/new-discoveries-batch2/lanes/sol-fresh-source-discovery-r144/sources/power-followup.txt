The multiplication of H H extends to its non-empty subsets by A B := { a b : a ∈ A , b ∈ B } AB:=\{ab:a\in A,\ b\in B\} . The resulting power semigroup is a classical construction in semigroup theory, going back at least to the works of Tamura and Shafer in [ 20 ] . One of its basic structural questions asks to what extent the power semigroup determines the original semigroup . This question led to the study of globally determined classes and related isomorphism problems, see for example [ 14 , 11 , 10 , 18 , 16 , 17 ] . Standard background on semigroups and their representations may be found in [ 12 ] . For a monoid H H , 𝒫 fin , 1 ​ ( H ) = { A ⊆ H : A ​ is finite and ​ 1 H ∈ A } \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{fin},1}(H)=\{A\subseteq H:A\text{ is finite and }1_{H}\in A\} is also a monoid, and we call it the reduced finitary power monoid of H H . Power monoids have recently been studied from the viewpoints of factorization theory and arithmetic combinatorics [ 9 , 5 , 6 , 7 , 19 ] . For numerical monoids, the ambient additive order supplies particularly effective tools, where the required background on numerical monoids is given in [ 2 ] . Automorphism groups and rigidity phenomena for finitary or reduced power monoids have been determined in several important cases, including ℕ \mathbb{N} , ℤ \mathbb{Z} , finite abelian groups, and numerical monoids [ 15 , 21 , 24 , 22 , 26 ] . Related results concerning torsion groups and the Bienvenu–Geroldinger conjecture appear in [ 23 , 25 ] . The Kleisli construction is one of the standard categorical constructions associated with a monad, and it is the origin of Kleisli categories, which were initially introduced for the study of adjoint functors, see [ 13 , 8 , etc] . The main purpose of this paper is to show that power semigroups of groups, and more generally reduced finitary power monoids, are naturally instances of convolution in Kleisli categories.
