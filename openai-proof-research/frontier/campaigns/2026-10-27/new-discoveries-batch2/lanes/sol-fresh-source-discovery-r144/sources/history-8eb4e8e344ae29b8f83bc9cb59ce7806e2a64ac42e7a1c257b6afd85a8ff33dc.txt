## Why the residual exists at all (credited to the author)

The issue the author identified is real and not trivial to state. In Γ, a word of length n reaches stage depth up to about 2n. The coefficient map φ = iρ(−⊗q) (envelope `algebra.tex` 258–265, q = e₀−e₁ at 224) wraps each basis element in a raw sector around a canonical code. Literal code length grows by at least the factor D₀ per application (`bootstrap.tex` 111–119). Literal K/H words for φ^r(b) are therefore exponentially long in r. A routine audit of the literal source normalizer alone does not give PSPACE for S. The source's own literal recursion is polynomial-space by its strict dependency m = M⌊n/D₀⌋ < n (`bootstrap.tex` 204–212); the scope correctly says this does not suffice. Wächter–Weiß (abstract, local text line 35) show that a group with a PSPACE-complete word problem can have an EXPSPACE-complete compressed word problem. So some argument is required.

The author also found and repaired a real trap: the native singleton raw(w) is not c([w]) unless w is H-irreducible (`bootstrap.tex` 396–410; compiler 469–472). Recognizing the compression issue and avoiding this trap are the strongest points in the candidate's favour. They show that care is needed. They do not show that the repair needs a new mechanism; the repair is the source identity raw(w)·raw(ε) = raw(f(w)) (`bootstrap.tex` 79–80 with v = ε).

## Strongest case for SUBSTANTIAL, and why it does not prevail

1. *The source has no structured word algorithm for S at all.* That is true (envelope `action.tex` 282–301; companion introduction 46–56). It shows that the theorem is not printed and that some algorithm had to be written. But writing that algorithm from the construction's own normal forms (N_η, the rule tables R_n, the mapping-torus normal form, brick refinement) is the expected work of a complexity audit. Each component already exists in decision form in the sources.
2. *Exponential compression is real, and compressed word problems can be harder (Wächter–Weiß).* That result concerns arbitrary straight-line-program inputs to a fixed group. The decorations here are the construction's own designed units: whole quiver sectors and self-synchronizing code runs. The source introduced those units to prove context invariance, and the companion already evaluates them semantically for termination. The candidate expressly disclaims arbitrary compressed inputs and arbitrary shared circuits. The warning shows that a check is required; it does not show that the check is deep.
3. *The companion uses semantic labels only in proofs, whereas the candidate makes them algorithmic.* The companion's algorithm already retains original expressions and compares them by evaluation, never searching for representatives (`encoding.tex` 398–405, 461–465). Its proof-only device is the minimum-cost label (431–465), which the candidate does not use either. The candidate's atoms are the canonical values themselves, compared by recursive evaluation. That is the companion's algorithmic stance, carried through a resource bound.

The verdict would change only if some step needed a new structural invariant that the source normal forms do not control. Examples: a rule that can straddle an atom boundary in a way the source's alignment does not exclude, or sharing (DAG) compression that forces a genuinely new compressed-evaluation technique. Neither occurs in the frozen candidate.
