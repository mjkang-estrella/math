Title:Arithmetic pseudo-Frobenius numbers and determinantal numerical semigroup rings
    Authors:Do Van Kien            View a PDF of the paper titled Arithmetic pseudo-Frobenius numbers and determinantal numerical semigroup rings, by Do Van Kien
    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)



    
            Abstract:Let $H=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_n\rangle$ be a numerical semigroup. A conjecture on numerical semigroup rings predicts that the defining ideal $I_H$ has a determinantal presentation if and only if the set of pseudo-Frobenius numbers of $H$ has form $\{h+\alpha,h+2\alpha,\ldots,h+(n-1)\alpha\}$ for $h\ge 0, \alpha>0$. We prove this conjecture under the condition that $h$ has a unique factorization in $H$. We also give characterization this unique-factorization condition. As an application, we consider the family of affine-orbit numerical semigroups determined by $m=cA_{n-1}+1$, $1\le c\le a$. We prove that the relevant element $h$ has a unique factorization and the defining ideal is generated by the 2-minors of a $2\times n$-matrix. In particular, the Eagon--Northcott complex of the matrix mentioned above gives the graded minimal free resolution of $k[H]$.
