                                                   Abstract. In this paper, we prove a sharp Minkowski-type inequality in Cartan-
                                                   Hadamard 3-spaces using harmonic mean curvature flow and improve the known es-
                                                   timates for total mean curvature in hyperbolic 3-space. In particular, we sharpen
                                                   Ghomi-Spruck’s result in [11] by retaining the volume contribution in the monotonic-
                                                   ity argument. As a corollary, we obtain a comparison theorem relating the total mean
                                                   curvature of convex surfaces in Cartan-Hadamard 3-spaces to their enclosed volume.

   Inequality (1.3) is a refinement of (1.2). Compared with the previously known inequal-
ities, (1.3) appears to give the sharpest lower bound of total mean curvature currently
known in Cartan-Hadamard 3-spaces, including hyperbolic 3-space.
   Total mean curvature enters naturally both in convex geometry and in variational
problems for area. It is the main term of one of the quermassintegrals for convex bodies
in space forms, which are important in convex geometry and Brunn-Minkowski Theory
[23]. It also plays an important role in the definition of Brown-York quasi-local mass
in general relativity [6]. A longstanding problem related to the Minkowski inequality
(1.1) is the still-open question of its validity for general mean-convex domains. Guan-Li
[13, Theorem 2] proved that (1.1) holds provided that Γ is star-shaped and mean-convex.
G. Huisken showed that (1.1) holds for outward-minimizing surfaces, see [13, Theorem
6] and also [16]. Dalphin-Henrot-Masnou-Takahashi [7, Theorem 1.1] established (1.1)
in the case where Γ is axially symmetric and such that Γ ∩ P is connected for every
affine plane P orthogonal to the axis of symmetry.

   A sharp inequality between total mean curvature M and surface area S remains
unknown. Santaló conjectured [21] that (see [22, p. 78]), in hyperbolic space H3 (a) with

(1.5)                           M (Γ) ≥       16πS(Γ) − 4aS(Γ)2 .

The proposed right-hand side of (1.5) is exactly the value attained by a geodesic sphere
with area S(Γ). Thus, if (1.5) holds, geodesic spheres minimize total mean curvature
among convex surfaces with fixed area. However, an example by Naveira-Solanes [22, p.
815], see [20, p. 109] or [11, Note 1.3], shows that (1.5) is false in general. They showed
that a flat double disk, which is isometric to a geodesic sphere in a totally geodesic
plane H2 embedded in H3 , gives a counterexample to (1.5) when its surface area S(Γ)
is large enough. Its two flat faces are counted in the surface area and its circular edge
is counted in the singular total mean curvature.
   In H3 , for a given area, the minimizer of the total mean curvature among convex
surfaces with fixed surface area exists by Blaschke selection theorem. The optimal horo-
convex minimizer of total mean curvature M with fixed surface area S has been proved to
be the geodesic sphere [9, Theorem 6.1]. Yet, the shape of the general convex minimizer
is not known. Hence Santaló’s problem, which asks for the optimal convex surface with
the minimum total mean curvature M among convex surfaces with fixed surface area
S, is still open. Recently the author [15] showed that there exists a new family of
counterexamples to (1.5) in addition to flat double disks. Santaló’s problem is of interest
not only in geometry but also in general relativity. For example, the total mean curvature
in hyperbolic space is also used in the definition of Wang-Yau’s quasi-local mass in
[26, Theorem 1.3].

                                      2. Preliminaries

2.1. Notation and Definitions of Convexity. Here we list some notation and defi-
nitions for convexity used in this paper. By smooth we mean C ∞ . The term curvature
means sectional curvature unless specified otherwise. A domain is a connected open set
with compact closure. Assuming smoothness, a convex hypersurface Γ of an ambient
manifold N is a closed embedded submanifold of codimension one which, when prop-
erly oriented, has positive semidefinite second fundamental form IIΓ . A strictly convex
hypersurface Γ of N is a convex hypersurface with positive second fundamental form.
