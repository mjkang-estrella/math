Abstract
Rotation symmetric Boolean functions have important applications
in the design of cryptographic algorithms. In this paper, the Conjecture
about rotation symmetric Boolean functions (RSBFs) of degree 3
proposed by Cusik and Stănică is proved. As a result, the nonlinearity of such
kind of functions is determined.
Index Terms: Boolean functions, Rotation-symmetric, Fourier Transform,
Nonlinearity
I Introduction
A Boolean function fn​(x0,⋯,xn−1)f^{n}(x_{0},\cdots,x_{n-1}) on nn variables is a
map from 𝔽2n\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n} to 𝔽2\mathbb{F}_{2}, where
𝔽2n\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n} is the vector space of dimension nn over the two
element field 𝔽2\mathbb{F}_{2}. Rotation symmetric Boolean functions
(Abbr. RSBFs) are a special kind of Boolean functions with
properties that the evaluations on every cyclic inputs are the
same, thus could be used as components to efficiently implement a
message digest algorithm in cryptography, such as MD4, MD5. These
functions have attracted attentions in these years (see [2-7]).
One focus is the nonlinearity of these kind functions
(see [6, 7]). It is known that a hashing algorithm
employing degree-two RSBFs as components cannot resist the linear
and differential attacks ([4]). Hence, higher degree
RSBFs with higher nonlinearity are attractive to protect the
cryptography algorithm from differential attack. Cusik and
Stănică ([1]) investigated the weight of a kind
of 3-degree RSBFs and proposed a conjecture based on their
numerical observations.
Conjecture 1
The nonlinearity of
F3n​(x0,⋯,xn−1)=∑0≤i≤n−1xi​xi+1​(m​o​d​n)​xi+2​(m​o​d​n)F_{3}^{n}(x_{0},\cdots,x_{n-1})=\sum\limits_{0\leq i\leq n-1}x_{i}x_{i+1(mod\ n)}x_{i+2(mod\ n)}
is the same as its
weight.
As claimed in [1], if Conjecture 1 could be proved, then
significant progress for kk-degree (k>3k>3) RSBFs might be
possible. To prove the Conjecture, we factor F3nF_{3}^{n} into four
sub-functions, and discover some recurrence relations, which are
different from Cusik’s[1]. The technique used in this
paper may be also applied to the study of RSBFs of degree k>3k>3.

III Conclusion
In this paper we proved the Conjecture proposed in [1],
i.e. the nonlinearity of F3n​(x0,⋯,xn−1)F_{3}^{n}(x_{0},\cdots,x_{n-1}) is the same as its
weight. It is interesting to note that [7] has proved that the nonlinearity of F2n​(x0,⋯,xn−1)=∑0≤i≤n−1xi​xi+s⁡(m​o​d​n)F_{2}^{n}(x_{0},\cdots,x_{n-1})=\sum\limits_{0\leq i\leq n-1}x_{i}x_{i+s(mod\ n)} is the same as its weight if
ng​c​d​(n,s−1)\frac{n}{gcd(n,s-1)} is even. These properties indicate that
rotation symmetric Boolean functions have nice cryptographic
applications. Whether higher degree RSBFs have these properties is
an interesting topic for further research.
