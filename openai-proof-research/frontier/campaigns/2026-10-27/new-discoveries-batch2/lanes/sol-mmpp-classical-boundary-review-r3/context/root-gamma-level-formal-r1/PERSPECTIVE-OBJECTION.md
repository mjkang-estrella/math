# Concrete correction to preliminary substantive reasoning

The ordinary proof is sound and the root strict Jensen component compiles.
No discovery is accepted. The preliminary review's claim that the mechanism
is not covered by standard perspectives needs correction BEFORE promotion.

Let g(u)=-log(1-u^-2), extended by +infinity for u<=1. This is a proper
closed convex function. The classical perspective theorem gives convexity of

    p_m(x,s)=x g((s+m)/x), x>0, s>x.

Each p_m is the ordinary perspective (eta,y)->eta g(y/eta) composed with
the affine shift (x,s)->(x,s+m). Nonnegative sums preserve convexity, so

    P(x,s)=sum_m p_m(x,s)=x F(x,s)

is jointly convex. Therefore {F<=log c}={P-x log c<=0} is convex. On each
nonconstant segment with distinct x values, at least one of m=0,1 is strictly
convex, by the ratio-equality subtraction already proved; hence the strict
level-boundary/inverse argument follows. If x is unchanged but s differs,
strict convexity of g itself gives a strict term as well.

Thus the assertion "F is not itself a perspective sum" is literally true but
does not answer the subsumption objection: multiplying a level inequality by
the positive variable x supplies precisely the missing perspective sum. The
root weighted-Jensen proof is the standard proof of perspective convexity
with adjusted weights. It must not be advertised as a new convexity tool.
