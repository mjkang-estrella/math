## Mathematical interpretation and exact coverage

The complete 477-line O217 source, all four own source/audit files, five deliberately false mutant sources, all 43 freshly emitted support declaration types/axiom reports, all 10 author-control declaration types/axiom reports, and every coverage entry were read. There are 28 support theorem declarations, with the remaining public support declarations being definitions, abbreviations, and one instance. The source contains no bespoke axiom, sorry/admit, unsafe implementation, native_decide, or imported O217 conclusion. The fresh axiom reports contain only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` (or subsets). Ordinary kernel-checked algebra/finite decision tactics are used.

1. **Concrete model and gauge.** `Edge n` is precisely `(i,j) : Fin n × Fin n` with `i<j`, hence one canonical copy of each unordered edge, no loops or doubled pairs. Relabeling by +1 gives the human convention. Bool false/true represents +1/−1. The actual flip changes one vertex and is proved involutive, yielding a constructed equivalence; `sum_flipSpin` reindexes the finite counting sum by that equivalence. The incident-edge sign theorem explicitly excludes the impossible equal-endpoint branch using `i<j`. `hamiltonian` contains the off-diagonal interaction sum, while q occurs once in `exp(q * hamiltonian)`. There is no field or prior weight. Thus setting q=β/√n agrees exactly with the human model, without a sign, factor-of-two, or normalization discrepancy.

2. **Positive partition and calculus.** The spin index is nonempty, even at n=0, because the all-false function exists. Every exponential is strictly positive; `partitionSum_pos` proves a strict positive sum. The actual partition and log identities are derived from Hamiltonian reindexing, not assumed. `logFam_contDiff` proves C∞ in the replaced real coordinate by finite sums/products/exponentials and positivity before taking logarithms. It does not establish joint mixed derivative estimates or cumulant bounds.

3. **Deleted cubic sign and incidence.** `flipStar i e` negates exactly the incident edges different from e; `Function.update ... e t` removes the original value at e. With the explicit premise `incident i e`, the actual family satisfies `logFam q (flipStar i e x) e t = logFam q x e (-t)`. Three ordinary total derivatives at zero produce the negative sign. I inspected mathlib `deriv_comp_neg` in Deriv/Shift and `deriv.neg` (with generated fun version) in Deriv/Add: these equalities have no differentiability premise and respect the total-derivative-zero convention at nondifferentiability. The arbitrary-Ψ observable theorem is therefore exact, including square and scaled test observables, but it supplies neither C6 regularity nor derivative bounds for arbitrary Ψ. No moment cancellation or expectation-zero statement follows merely from this oddness. The distinction is accurately disclosed.

   Independently formalized boundary controls prove uniqueness of the n=2 edge, identity of its empty remaining-star flip, and **zero actual deleted cubic for both log partition and every observable**. This is a consequence of the exported oddness theorem and actual definitions, not a mock family. A new nonincident n=3 edge control gives +1 after flipping the unrelated vertex, and its false −1 mutant fails at `⊢ False`. The incidence condition in the termwise sign/reindexing reasoning is meaningful. This control does not claim that every nonincident log-family identity fails for every disorder.

4. **Square remainder.** The universal identity is correct by expansion: `u²−b1²z²−b1b2z³ = 2b1z(u−b1z−b2z²/2)+(u−b1z)²`. The scalar bound follows from the two supplied lower-order remainders and `|b1|≤d1`, with explicit `c2≥0`. The H0 version combines this with the supplied cubic Taylor remainder for u, producing coefficient `2|H0|c3+2d1c2+c1²` times `|z|⁴`. It does not assume the desired square remainder as a premise. No extra positivity premise on c1/c3 is required: their hypotheses already force the needed nonnegative right-hand sides at nonzero z, and z=0 is harmless. The bounds use absolute z and cover negative z. A reviewer endpoint check uses u(z)=z+z², b1=1,b2=2,z=−1, where the square-remainder bound is attained. There are no random variables in this theorem; it proves the algebraic ingredient avoiding a fifth-power remainder, not the probabilistic assertion that fourth moments suffice.

5. **Bootstrap and centering.** For actual real scalars M,V,C≥0, the assumed inequality `M≤V+C√M+C` is solved to `√M≤√V+C+√C`. The proof completes the square and bounds square roots; it is noncircular. `bootstrap_variance` substitutes that bound into a supplied scalar |A−V| estimate. `centered_variance` adds the square of a supplied mean-offset bound; the combined coefficient is exactly `[C(C+√C+1)+B²](1+√V)`. The probabilistic interpretations of A, M, V and mean are expressly outside the file. The standalone centered correction is valid as a real inequality without a V≥0 premise, whereas the bootstrap assembly retains M,V,C≥0; this broader standalone type is not a claim about negative variance. A reviewer control instantiates C=0 and checks that the theorem reduces to square-root monotonicity. The author negative-C and smaller-constant mutants independently rebuild and fail mathematically.

6. **Cardinality and powers.** The exact count is the natural-number n(n−1)/2, obtained from finite ordered-pair counting; n−1 uses natural subtraction and remains correct at n=0,1. The real bounds use actual Fintype.card, not a free scalar m with the needed bound assumed. For n>0, `(β/√n)^4=β⁴/n²` and the sixth power is β⁶/n³; 2m≤n² yields both mq⁴≤β⁴/2 and mnq⁶≤β⁶/2. The positive-real-cast n premise covers every human n≥2; β may be any real because only even powers occur. Independent controls also check cardinalities 0,0,1 at n=0,1,2. The exact remaining-star count n−2 is **not** separately formalized; the n=2 empty-star consequence is checked in this review only.
