## 2. What is actually new at the operation level (conditional on the proof)

I re-derived the operation's exponent bookkeeping independently from `candidate.md` §§2–8 (no proof verdict implied):

* Ordinary two-moment Lindeberg on F(x)=log Σ exp(q Σ x_eσ_iσ_j), q=β/√n, leaves a cubic term per edge of size |Δμ₃|·|E∂_e³U|≤Cq³L. Summed over ≍n² edges this is ≍n^{1/2}L, which at the needed scale a≍n^{1/6} gives n^{1/3}→∞. This is the barrier every SK source read below avoids by **assuming** E J³=0 (or accepting a density-level error).
* The candidate pays the cubic term by an extra, structural estimate: the deterministic vertex gauge F(T_i x)=F(x) makes h=∂_e³U|_{x_e=0} exactly odd under reflection of the remaining star S of vertex i; therefore E_P h = ½(E_P h − E_{P⁻} h), and P vs P⁻ (star reflected coordinatewise, exterior unchanged, still a product law) differ only in odd moments. A *second* Lindeberg over the |S|=n−2 star coordinates, Taylor to degree two, has remainder governed by the mixed **sixth derivative** ∂_f³∂_e³U≤Cq⁶L integrated against only |x_f|³. Hence |E h|≤Cnq⁶L, and n²·nq⁶=β⁶: the cubic barrier disappears with no third-moment matching and no moment beyond four.
* Variance: identity (18) Taylor-expands the square without introducing |z|⁵; one/two deleted coordinates are restored by Lipschitz continuity; the finite hybrid class {law(J_e),law(−J_e),N(0,1)}^E closes M≤V+C√M+C. I regard this variance bridge as **technical** in isolation (standard bootstrap and Taylor algebra; parent-assisted pre-freeze). It is not counted separately; it is needed to carry the cubic cancellation to the unbounded observable (F−c)² at only fourth moments, and that integration is assessed together with §2's core.

Per the brief, I do not credit separately: the stipulated Gaussian fluctuation theorem; ordinary matched-three-moment transfer; gauge symmetry as such; Taylor algebra. The candidate contribution is the **integrated asymmetric cubic cancellation** (gauge-oddness converted into an n^{-1/2} gain by a reflected-star product-law replacement, derivative order six against moment order three) together with the fourth-moment-only variance/smooth-test closure that makes it deliver (1)–(3) uniformly over nonidentical arrays and all hybrids.

Theorem 1.1/Cor. 1.2/Thm 1.3: two-moment invariance with λ₃ error, applied to the SK free-energy *density*; explicit N^{-1/2} density rate under third moments. Same cubic barrier; no cancellation. math/0508519 not re-read by me beyond the packet's account (limit).
* **Cheng–Liu–Shao–Xu, arXiv:2609.07446v1** (lines 1–420 and 760–1030 read). Theorem 2.2 / Prop. 3.2: quantitative Lindeberg of the free energy at a diverging scale s_N, with conditional log-cosh derivative bounds (21)–(22), Taylor through order four, smoothing. Hypothesis (6) **imposes E ξ_i³=0 for every coordinate**; the cubic term is simply absent. This is the closest antecedent: the smooth-test architecture of O217 (3) is theirs; the third-moment removal is not.
* **Chen–Lam, arXiv:2302.04158v1** (lines 1–230 read). Theorems 1–2: o(N) / O(N/log N) variance under finite third moment via monotone transform f(g); not a comparison to O(√V). **Remark 2** states that matching the first and second moments of the free energies by approximate IBP is available "under the assumption that the first four moments of h agree with those of g and h has a finite fifth moment". O217's variance comparison uses two matched moments and a fourth-moment bound. This is direct evidence that the reduction is not regarded as routine in the SK literature, though it is not proof of substantiality.
* **Choo–Han–Lee, arXiv:2605.07050v2** (lines 1–140, 600–660 read). High-temperature multigraph expansion; Prop. 2.13's orthogonality uses symmetric V_ij with odd/even coefficient functions. Symmetric, β<1; not the operation.
* **Yu-Ting Chen, arXiv:1612.09250v3**: grep only by me (third-moment remarks at lines 114–119, 1192); I rely on the packet's account for the generic mixed-p-spin spin-law interface. Not a pressure-fluctuation comparison; pure SK excluded by genericity.
* **Pinned family 217** (both manuscripts; all acquired `.tex` grepped for universality/non-Gaussian/Rademacher/Lindeberg/third moment/gauge). No non-Gaussian claim. Gauge is used for Gaussian pinning/augmentation (`02-foundations.tex:189`, `04-reduction.tex:117`, `fluctuations.tex:526–534`), not for an asymmetric product-law comparison. The complete tree lists no further 217 files beyond those acquired.
* **Pinned family 227** (critical SK autocorrelation universality, `09-replacement.tex` of the quench paper and the functional paper's comparison section, local project copies, grep-level): their Gaussian→Rademacher transfer uses a *symmetric* sign, so the cubic term vanishes. Not the operation.

**Additional antecedent the packet does not credit (found in this review).** Erdős–Yau–Yin, *Rigidity of eigenvalues of generalized Wigner matrices*, arXiv:1007.4652v4, §6.1 discussion and Lemmas 6.5–6.6 (read in full in the extracted text, lines 2370–2745). Their edge Green-function comparison needs only two matched moments; they say explicitly that the third-order term has size N^{-2+1/6}, "still larger than N^{-2}", and that "the leading term … has actually almost zero expectation". Lemma 6.6 proves |EF′(x)R_ijR_jaR_bi|≤N^{-4/3+Cε} by removing the a-th row/column (the star of vertex a) and observing that the leading term is linear in that row's mean-zero entries, independent of everything else. That is the same *conceptual* move—a two-moment telescoping whose cubic coefficient is shown to have an extra-small expectation by a second-level expansion in a vertex star—in random matrix theory, 2010/2012.

Why EYY does not already supply O217's step: their mechanism needs a local law (|G_ij|≤N^{-1/3+ε}, diagonal entries ≈m_sc) and resolvent/Schur-complement identities that isolate a mean-zero linear term. SK pressure at β>1 has no analogous local law or cavity linearization (∂_e³F=−2q³⟨s_e⟩(1−⟨s_e⟩²) is a nonlinear Gibbs functional with order-one typical size of ⟨σ_iσ_j⟩ at low temperature). O217 replaces the local-law input with exact gauge oddness plus a reflected product-law comparison that uses **only** uniform cumulant derivative bounds, so it holds at every β including the replica-symmetry-breaking regime and for nonidentical arrays. The import from EYY is therefore the principle, not the operation; the operation is a genuinely additional SK interface. The credit, however, is required (objection R1).

I also considered and rejected two shortcuts that would make the step routine: (i) a random vertex gauge J_e↦ε_iε_jJ_e preserves the law of F but leaves conditional third moments ε_iε_jμ_e correlated with the hybrid law, so averaging over ε returns exactly the star-flipped-law comparison the candidate performs (the author's adversarial item 2 says the same); (ii) for iid μ₃ one could relate Σ_e⟨s_e⟩ to magnetization, but Σ_e⟨s_e⟩³ and nonidentical μ_e are not controlled that way. Neither shortcut supplies the estimate.

## 5. Verdict

**SUBSTANTIAL** (moderate strength), for the full unchanged frozen target, **conditional on the independent proof gate passing the complete proof**; not DUPLICATE, not INELIGIBLE, not BLOCKED.

Justification at the operation level: the decisive content is a structural cancellation lemma—gauge-odd cubic Lindeberg coefficients of a zero-field bounded-spin pressure have expectation O(nq⁶) rather than O(q³), by a reflected-star second product-law replacement with derivative order six against moment order three—combined with a fifth-moment-free square remainder and finite-hybrid bootstrap that carry it to the variance and to diverging-scale smooth tests uniformly over nonidentical arrays. No SK pressure source read (Carmona–Hu 2004, Chatterjee 2005, Chen–Lam 2023, Cheng–Liu–Shao–Xu Sept 2026, Choo–Han–Lee 2026, pinned 217/227) supplies or can be specialised to give that interface; the most recent close comparator explicitly imposes E ξ³=0 and Chen–Lam's Remark 2 describes four matched moments plus a fifth moment as the known route to variance matching. The nearest conceptual antecedent (EYY Lemma 6.6) relies on local-law inputs unavailable for low-temperature SK and does not transfer. The strength is moderate rather than high: the proof is short, uses classical tools, its headline β>1 consequences are inherited wholly from pinned 217, and the variance half is technical in isolation. Brevity is not the basis of the verdict either way.

## 6. Objections

Required (must be honoured in any integration/finding text; none changes the frozen scope or asks for narrowing):

* **R1 — antecedent credit.** Credit Erdős–Yau–Yin arXiv:1007.4652 §6.1 and Lemmas 6.5–6.6 as the RMT antecedent of the principle "with only two matched moments, the mismatched cubic term has an anomalously small expectation, obtained by a second expansion in a vertex row/star". Describe O217's step as a new SK-specific, local-law-free gauge/reflected-star implementation of that principle, not as the first appearance of the idea. `candidate.md` §10 and `217-prefreeze-comparison.md` omit this.
* **R2 — accounting.** The counted contribution is the integrated finite-size comparison (1)–(3) with unrestricted third moments. The β>1 prefactor and law ν_β are credited entirely to pinned family 217; the variance bridge (identity (18), Lipschitz restoration, finite-maximum bootstrap) and the parent's pre-freeze variance note are author-side technical assistance, not separate contribution. Integration text must not present the Gaussian law, gauge symmetry, or matched-moment Lindeberg as new.
* **R3 — conditionality.** This SUBSTANTIAL verdict is void if the proof gate finds an unrepaired gap in §3 (inner star replacement), §7 (cubic square coefficient, inequality (22)–(25)) or §8 (bootstrap); any repair that adds a third-moment, symmetry, or higher-moment hypothesis would be a scope change and require a fresh contribution review rather than inheriting this verdict.

Nonblocking:

* **N1.** The external search is bounded; September–October 2026 arXiv activity on SK fluctuations is dense, and a parallel non-Gaussian low-temperature or two-moment SK comparison could exist unindexed. No worldwide priority certificate is given.
* **N2 (reach, not credited).** The constants in (1)–(3) are visibly polynomial in β, hence locally uniform; the same argument would appear to remove the E ξ³=0 hypothesis from off-diagonal coordinates in Cheng–Liu–Shao–Xu-type critical-window transfers. This is outside the frozen scope, unreviewed, and not counted; it only illustrates that the lemma is a reusable interface.
* **N3.** The packet's chronology is candid: the cancellation idea predates the freeze and the variance repair is parent-assisted. Neither affects eligibility, because the substantial core (§2 cancellation) is the author lane's own pre-freeze feasibility idea and the frozen scope was not altered afterwards.
* **N4.** The packet's antecedent map is SK-only; RMT Green-function comparison (Tao–Vu, EYY, later edge works) was not screened by the author. Covered by R1; I read only EYY.
