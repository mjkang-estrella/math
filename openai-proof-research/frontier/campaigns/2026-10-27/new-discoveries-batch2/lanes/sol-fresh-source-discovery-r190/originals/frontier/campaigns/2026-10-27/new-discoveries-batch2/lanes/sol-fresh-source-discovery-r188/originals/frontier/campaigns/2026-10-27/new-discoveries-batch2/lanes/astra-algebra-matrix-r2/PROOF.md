# Candidate refutation: two-step nilpotent algebras need not have a multiplicative basis

Status: complete ordinary argument below, but NOT nominated for discovery credit. A subsequent public-prior-method check found the serious objection detailed in PRIOR_METHOD.md. Full literal Lean is also absent. Credit 0.

## Exact target

Heib–Bruschi, arXiv:2601.16161v2 (20 October 2026), Definition 15, equation (6), and Conjecture 91(a), printed p.62. The conjecture says every finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra of class at most two is minimal-graph-admissible. Definition 15 requires an actual basis whose pairwise brackets are scalar multiples of basis vectors; redundant spanning sets do not suffice. The same target is MathDB 372720 (v1 labels it Conjecture 106).

We refute the assertion over both the real and complex numbers, with a 12-dimensional algebra of class exactly two. Our coefficients are algebraically independent real numbers. This is an existence counterexample, not an explicitly computable rational multiplication table.

## Construction and theorem

Choose 112 real numbers t[k,a,b], with 1 <= k <= 4 and 1 <= a < b <= 8, algebraically independent over Q. Such a finite family exists: inductively the algebraic closure of a field generated by finitely many chosen real numbers over Q is countable, whereas R is uncountable.

For K=R or C let L=K^8 direct-sum K^4 with basis x_1,...,x_8,z_1,...,z_4. Define

    [x_a,x_b] = sum_k t[k,a,b] z_k  (a<b),
    [x_b,x_a] = -[x_a,x_b],
    [x_a,x_a]=0, [z_k,L]=0,

and extend bilinearly. Every iterated bracket of length three is zero, so Jacobi holds. The bracket is nonzero, hence the nilpotency class is exactly two.

**Theorem.** This L has no multiplicative basis over K.

## Proof

1. The derived algebra D=[L,L] is exactly the span Z of the four z_k. Indeed the four bracket columns indexed by (1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(1,5) form a 4-by-4 matrix of distinct algebraically independent entries. Its determinant is a nonzero polynomial over Q (specialize this submatrix to the identity). It therefore cannot vanish on t. Consequently these four brackets span Z. We do not need to identify the entire center.

2. Suppose B is a multiplicative basis. Every nonzero bracket of two B-vectors is a nonzero scalar multiple of a B-vector. Let B_D be the subset of B-vectors which occur as such bracket outputs. Expanding arbitrary brackets in B shows D=span(B_D). Independence of B and dim D=4 imply B_D has exactly four elements. Reorder B as u_1,...,u_8,v_1,...,v_4 with B_D={v_1,...,v_4}. The v_k are central because D is central. The images of the u_a form a basis of L/D. Therefore

    [u_i,u_j] = c[i,j] v_{delta(i,j)}  (i<j),

for some c[i,j] in K and delta(i,j) in {1,2,3,4}. When the bracket is zero choose any delta and c=0. There are at most 28 scalar c-parameters.

3. Write x_a=sum_i R[i,a] u_i modulo D and v_l=sum_k Q[k,l] z_k. There are 64 entries of R and 16 entries of Q. Since D is central, all central corrections to the x_a drop out of brackets. Thus, by bilinearity and skew-symmetry, for every k,a<b,

    t[k,a,b] = sum_{i<j} Q[k,delta(i,j)] c[i,j]
                   * (R[i,a] R[j,b] - R[j,a] R[i,b]).                (*)

In particular the field Q(t[k,a,b]: all k,a<b) is contained in the field generated over Q by the 64+16+28=108 scalar entries of R,Q,c.

4. A field generated by 108 elements over Q has transcendence degree at most 108, and every subfield containing Q has at most that transcendence degree. But the field generated by t has transcendence degree 112 by algebraic independence. This is a contradiction. The argument allows completely arbitrary complex changes of basis when K=C: it makes no assumption that R,Q,c lie in Q(t) or in R. Only the number of their generators is used. QED.

## Scope and stronger form

The same argument works for q alternating forms on a p-dimensional space whenever q<=binom(p,2) and

    q*binom(p,2) > p^2+q^2+binom(p,2).

It does not depend on graph connectedness, a nice-basis contraction condition, or a fixed starting basis. No first-batch family is involved. All helpers and all parameter choices comprise at most one outcome.

One can also read (*) as a finite family of polynomial parametrizations (one per delta), each with at most 108 variables and 112 outputs. Each image lies in a proper Q-algebraic subset. Hence a nonempty Zariski-open set of type-(8,4) brackets has no multiplicative basis. This generic formulation is not required for the existence proof above.

## Strongest objections / open gates

- Decisive new objection: the public 2016 rank argument for commutative associative nil algebras transfers directly to give an explicit rational 20-dimensional class-two Lie counterexample. PRIOR_METHOD.md gives the complete transfer. The generic 12-dimensional proof does not erase this substantive-novelty concern. Recommend no credit under the campaign bar; reduced dimension is not nominated as a separate discovery.
- The transcendence-degree bound and the alternating-form parametrization are classical. The proposed new content is their use to obstruct *every* multiplicative basis for the actual two-step source conjecture. Whether this meets the campaign's substantive bar must be reviewed; no credit is presumed.
- Nice bases impose an additional disjoint-support condition. A theorem about absence of nice bases alone does not imply this result. Prior literature on multiplicative bases itself must be checked, especially Calderon Martin and Gorbatsevich.
- No full literal Lean endpoint yet. Required bridges are generic coefficient existence; the central-extension Lie algebra; independence/surjectivity of the four brackets; derived-basis adaptation; equation (*); cardinality obstruction. A polynomial-parametrization obstruction alone is not the source theorem.
- The suggested 10-dimensional improvement using column normalization is not needed here and is not claimed as a verified endpoint.
