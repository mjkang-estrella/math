Stable Quadratic Polynomials over ℚ⁡(i)\mathbb{Q}(i)



Jermain McDermott



Abstract.
    

Let ff be a polynomial or a rational function over a field KK. A basic question is, if ff is a polynomial, are its iterates irreducible or not? We wish to know what can happen when considering iterates of a quadratic f=x2+r∈K⁡[x]f=x^{2}+r\in K[x]. If the number of factors of fnf^{n} is bounded by a constant independent of nn, then ff is said to be eventually stable. This paper is an extension to ℚ⁡(i)\mathbb{Q}(i) of the paper [6], which considered ff over ℚ\mathbb{Q}. The progress towards the conjecture "if f2f^{2} is irreducible, then fnf^{n} is irreducible for all nn" is affected by the lack of a linear ordering on ℚ⁡(i)\mathbb{Q}(i), and consequently a new function involved in a lower bound on the nn to check. Showing stability for c≡1mod2c\equiv 1\mod 2 (as a ℤ⁡[i]\mathbb{Z}[i] equivalence class) is not as easily handled as over ℤ\mathbb{Z}, however, the elusive case of c≡2mod4c\equiv 2\mod 4 (as a ℤ\mathbb{Z} equivalence class) is shown to be stable over ℤ⁡[i]\mathbb{Z}[i], offering more evidence for [6, Conjecture 1]. 

  




1. Introduction




Suppose KK is a field with algebraic closure K¯\overline{K}. Let f⁡(x)=x2+r∈K⁡[x]f(x)=x^{2}+r\in K[x], and denote its iterates by




fn​(x):=(f∘f∘…∘f)​(x)​ (n compositions)  with ​f0​(x)=x.f^{n}(x):=(f\circ f\circ...\circ f)(x)\text{ ($n$ compositions) }\quad\text{ with }f^{0}(x)=x.






Fix α∈K\alpha\in K and denote by f−n​(α):={β∈K¯:fn​(β)=α}.f^{-n}(\alpha):=\{\beta\in\overline{K}:f^{n}(\beta)=\alpha\}. If fn​(x)−αf^{n}(x)-\alpha is separable, the disjoint union Tf,∞​(α):={α}⊔f−1​(α)⊔f−2​(α)⊔⋯T_{f,\infty}(\alpha):=\{\alpha\}\sqcup f^{-1}(\alpha)\sqcup f^{-2}(\alpha)\sqcup\cdots is a rooted tree with root vertex α\alpha, and edges assigned according to the action of ff. Denote by Aut​(Tf​(α))\text{Aut}(T_{f}(\alpha)) the tree automorphisms of Tf​(α)T_{f}(\alpha). Since the natural action of the Galois group GK:=Gal​(K¯/K)G_{K}:=\text{Gal}(\overline{K}/K) on f−n​(α)f^{-n}(\alpha) commutes with f∈K⁡[x]f\in K[x], we obtain a homomorphism Gal​(K¯/K)→Aut​(Tf​(α))\text{Gal}(\overline{K}/K)\to\text{Aut}(T_{f}(\alpha)) called the arboreal Galois representation associated to (f,α)(f,\alpha). One of the central problems in arithmetic dynamics is whether the image of this homomorphism has finite index in Aut​(Tf​(α))\text{Aut}(T_{f}(\alpha)); a dynamical parallel of the Serre open image theorem. This question has generated a large literature, see [1] for a summary of this and other current research in the field. 





In this article, we attempt to characterize factorizations of fn​(x)−αf^{n}(x)-\alpha for α=0\alpha=0.



Definition 1.



Let KK be a field, f∈K⁡[x]f\in K[x], and α∈K\alpha\in K. For n≥1n\geq 1 let knk_{n} denote the number of irreducible factors of fn​(x)−αf^{n}(x)-\alpha over KK. We call the pair (f,α)(f,\alpha) (resp. ff) eventually stable over K if there is a constant C⁡(f,α)C(f,\alpha) (resp. C⁡(f,0)C(f,0)) such that for all nn,




kn≤C⁡(f,α).k_{n}\leq C(f,\alpha).



The pair (f,α)(f,\alpha) is called stable over K if C⁡(f,α)=1C(f,\alpha)=1, i.e. fn​(x)−αf^{n}(x)-\alpha is irreducible over KK for all n≥1n\geq 1.





Eventual stability is applied in some finite-index results for certain arboreal Galois representations [3, 4], and is equivalent to a bound independent of nn on the number of Galois orbits on f−n​(α)f^{-n}(\alpha) [8, Proposition 2.2]. However, this is a straightforward consequence of the image of the arboreal representation associated to (f,α)(f,\alpha) having finite index, so finite index of the arboreal representation implies eventual stability. The reverse implication is much less clear. Other applications include finiteness of SS-integer points in backwards orbits; see [8, Section 3] for a discussion of these and other results concerning the topic. 





The following theorem of Hamblen, Jones, and Madhu [7] establishes eventual stability of a large class of polynomials over number fields (using ideas resembling Eisenstein’s criterion):



Theorem 1.1.



(Hamblen, Jones and Madhu, [7, Theorem 5])
Let d≥2d\geq 2, let KK be a field of characteristic not dividing dd, and let f⁡(x)=xd+r∈K⁡[x]f(x)=x^{d}+r\in K[x]. If there is a discrete non-archimedean absolute value on KK with |r|<1|r|<1, then ff is eventually stable over KK.





The following corollary is an extension of [7, Corollary 6] considered by DeMark et al.:



Corollary 1.1.



Let f⁡(x)=xd+r∈ℚ⁡(i)​[x]f(x)=x^{d}+r\in\mathbb{Q}(i)[x], and suppose that rr is non-zero and is not the reciprocal of an element of ℤ⁡[i]\mathbb{Z}[i]. Then ff is eventually stable over ℚ⁡(i)\mathbb{Q}(i).

2. Background




We first list some work-saving facts:



Proposition 2.1.



If p​(x)∈ℚ​(i)​[x]p(x)\in\mathbb{Q}(i)[x], then any factorization of pp into irreducible polynomials over ℚ⁡(i)\mathbb{Q}(i) has the same number of factors as p¯​(x)\bar{p}(x), p⁡(x)p(x) with conjugated coefficients.
