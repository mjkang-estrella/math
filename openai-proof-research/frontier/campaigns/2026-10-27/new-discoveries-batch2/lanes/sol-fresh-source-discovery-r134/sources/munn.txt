                                                                             1. Preliminaries
                                             Building on the idea of Rees [14] who presented 0-simple semigroups by matrices
                                          over groups, Munn introduced [12] Munn algebras in 1955 for the finite dimensional
                                          case. In this article, we generalize some results in matrix algebras to that in Munn
                                          algebras.
                                             Let R be a ring. Recall that an element e ∈ R is called an idempotent if e2 = e.
                                          The commutator of two elements x, y ∈ R is defined as [x, y] := xy − yx. In
                                          Section 2, we show some results in matrix algebras that Mn (R) can be generated
                                          by idempotents or commutators. Then we investigate the generalization of these
                                          results to some Munn algebras.
                                             In 2009, Brešar, Grašič, and Ortega [6] introduced the concept of zero product
                                          determined algebras. The property of zero product determinacy is widely used to
                                          explore derivations, homomorphisms, and preserver problems. Brešar summarized
                                          the relevant research of the past 15 years in [3]. In Section 3, we generalize the
                                          results of the matrix algebra Mn (R) being zero product determined and zero Jordan
                                          product determined to some Munn algebras.

     2. Munn algebras generated by idempotents or commutators
Definition 2.1. Let R be a ring. For any x, y ∈ R, define the Jordan product by
x ◦ y = xy + yx. If R is equal to its additive subgroup generated by elements of the
form e ◦ f where e, f ∈ R are idempotents, then we say that R is additively spanned
by Jordan products of its idempotents.

  In [5], Brešar and Godoy proved that if R is a unital ring containing 21 , then
Mn (R) (for n ≥ 2) is additively spanned by Jordan products of its idempotents.
We present the following generalization:

Theorem 2.2. Let D be a division ring, P be an n×m matrix over D with rank P =
r, and A = M(D, m, n, P ), where 2 ≤ m, n < ∞.
      • If r ≥ 1, then A is generated by idempotents as a D-algebra.
      • If r ≥ 2, then A is generated by idempotents as a ring.
      • If r ≥ 2 and char D 6= 2, then A is additively spanned by Jordan products
        of idempotents.

Remark 1. Let R be a unital Banach algebra, I and Λ be arbitrary index sets,
and the sandwich matrix P be a non zero Λ × I matrix over R such that kP k∞ =
sup {kpmi k : m ∈ Λ, i ∈ I} ≤ 1. The
                                 P vector space M(R, P, I, Λ) of all I ×Λ matrices
over R with ℓ1 -norm k(aim )k = i∈I,m∈Λ kaim k < ∞ and product A • B = AP B
is a Banach algebra that called the ℓ1 -Munn Banach algebra, see [8, 7].
   Using [10, Theorem 4.1] and [3, Theorem 5.14], it is easy to prove the following
theorem.
Theorem 3.6. Let F be a field of real or complex numbers, I and Λ be ar-
bitrary index sets, P be a non zero Λ × I matrix over F such that kP k∞ =
sup {kpmi k : m ∈ Λ, i ∈ I} ≤ 1. If I or Λ is finite and P is invertible over the
group of all invertible elements of R, then M(R, P, I, Λ) is a zpd Banach algebra.
