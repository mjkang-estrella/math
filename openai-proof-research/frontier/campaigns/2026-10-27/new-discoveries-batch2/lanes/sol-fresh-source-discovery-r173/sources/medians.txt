        TWO INEQUALITIES FOR THE MEDIANS OF A TRIANGLE

                                                   JIAN LIU1


         Abstract. With the help of software Maple, we establish two geometric inequalities
         involving medians, circumradius and inradius of a triangle. One of them is the best
         possible inequality in the strong sense. We also propose four related conjectures checked
         by the computer.
