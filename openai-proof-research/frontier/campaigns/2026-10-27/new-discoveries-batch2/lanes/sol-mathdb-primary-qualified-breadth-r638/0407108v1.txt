                                                                                Introduction
                                               In [5], Dieter Happel asked the following question: If the Hochschild coho-
                                            mology groups HHn (Γ) of a finite dimensional algebra Γ over a field k vanish
                                            for all sufficiently large n, is the global dimension of Γ finite? We give a neg-
                                            ative answer to this question. Indeed, consider the finite dimensional algebras
                                            Λ = Λq = khx, yi/(x2 , xy + qyx, y 2 ) for some field k and q in k. These algebras,
                                            intensely studied already by Rainer Schulz [9] to exhibit other pathologies, are all
                                            four dimensional, of infinite global dimension, and, for q 6= 0, selfinjective. If q
                                            is not a root of unity in k and non-zero, then we show that the total Hochschild
                                            cohomology of Λq is of dimension 5, thus answering negatively Happel’s question as
                                            promised. Moreover, if q is a root of unity, another interesting phenomenon occurs:
                                            the Hochschild cohomology vanishes on large, but finite intervals, whose lengths
                                            depend on the multiplicative order of q.
                                               None of these phenomena can occur for commutative algebras as is shown in
                                            the companion paper [1]. Indeed, there it is shown, as a consequence of a more
                                            general result, that a commutative finite dimensional algebra R is of finite global
                                            dimension as soon as Hochschild cohomology vanishes just in one even and one odd
                                            degree, thus giving a strong affirmation of Happel’s question in the commutative
                                            case. Let us point out that the family {Λq } contains a few (graded) commutative
                                            specimens. For q = −1, the algebra Λ−1 is the commutative complete intersection
                                            k[x, y]/(x2 , y 2 ) and for q = 1, the algebra Λ1 is the (graded commutative) exterior
                                            algebra in two variables, isomorphic to the group ring k[Z2 × Z2 ] in characteristic
                                            two. For q = 0, that is Λ0 = khx, yi/(x2 , xy, y 2 ), we discuss the structure of its
                                            Hochschild cohomology in section 2.6.
                                               The Hochschild cohomology ring HH∗ (Λ) has a ring structure given by the
                                            Yoneda product. If R → A and S → A are two ring homomorphisms, we de-
                                            note the fibre product (pullback) by R ×A S. More precisely, in this paper we prove
                                            the following.
