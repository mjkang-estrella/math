Title:A Note on Inequalities for Three Domination Parameters
    
Authors:Dickson Y. B. Annor            
View a PDF of the paper titled A Note on Inequalities for Three Domination Parameters, by Dickson Y. B. Annor
    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)



    
            Abstract:In this short paper, we establish relations between the domination number $\gamma$, the total domination number $\gamma_t$, and the connected domination number $\gamma_c$ of a graph. In particular, we prove upper and lower bounds for $\gamma_t$ in terms of $\gamma$ and $\gamma_c$.
