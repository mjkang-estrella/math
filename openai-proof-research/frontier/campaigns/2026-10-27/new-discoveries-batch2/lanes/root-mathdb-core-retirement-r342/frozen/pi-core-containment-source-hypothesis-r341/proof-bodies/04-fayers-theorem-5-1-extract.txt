Abstract: "We also give a new proof of a result of Vandehey" (the containment result).
Intro: "...has been answered affirmatively by Vandehey in an unpublished thesis [V]; in the
present paper ..." ; "Finally in Section 5 we examine the largest (s, t)-core, and prove ..."

    "Theorem 5.1. [V, Theorem 2.4] Suppose s and t are coprime positive integers, and λ is
     an (s, t)-core. Then [λ] ⊆ [κ_{s,t}].

     This theorem answers a question of Olsson and Stanton [OS, Remark 4.11], who prove the
     theorem in the case t = s + 1. Our aim in this section is to give a new proof of this
     theorem."

Complete proof retained in the paper (Proposition 5.5): every s-point p of the level-t
rhomboid R^s_t has its s-core λ satisfying [λ] ⊆ [κ_{s,t}]. Proof: Lemma 5.2 (origin and ▽ lie
on opposite sides of every hyperplane H_{ijk} meeting R^s_t), Lemma 5.3 (if b ≤ a+1 then
[σ̃_i(λ)] ⊇ [λ], via beta-numbers: "the jth beta-number for σ̃_i(λ) is either equal to or one
greater than the jth beta-number for λ; ... [σ̃_i(λ)] ⊇ [λ]"), Lemma 5.4, then an alcove path
from p to the vertex ▽ giving a chain
    [λ] = [λ(0)] ⊆ [λ(1)] ⊆ · · · ⊆ [λ(d)] = [κ_{s,t}].
"Since p_ν ∈ R^s_t for every (s, t)-core ν, Proposition 5.5 implies Theorem 5.1."

Identification of κ_{s,t}: "Following [OS] we denote this partition κ_{s,t}. ... it was shown
by Kane [K] that its size is (s^2 − 1)(t^2 − 1)/24, and this is known to be the largest size of
any (s, t)-core." So Fayers' κ_{s,t} = Olsson-Stanton κ_{s,t} = MathDB κ_pq.

Scope note: "We continue to assume that s, t are coprime positive integers with s ⩾ 2."
(wlog s = min(p,q); the s = 1 case is trivial: the only 1-core is the empty partition, since
any nonempty partition has a corner cell of hook length 1, so κ = ∅ uniquely and containment
is trivial; likewise q = 1.) s-core convention: partition with no hook-length divisible by s;
[λ] = Young diagram; [λ] ⊆ [κ] is rowwise containment λ_i ≤ κ_i. Conventions match MathDB
(cross-checked against the frozen r339 enumeration for 15 pairs: sources/crosscheck-maximal-beta-set.json).
