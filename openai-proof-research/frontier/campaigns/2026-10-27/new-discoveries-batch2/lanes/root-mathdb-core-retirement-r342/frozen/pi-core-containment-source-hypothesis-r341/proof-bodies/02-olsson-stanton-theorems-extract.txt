Theorem 4.1 (identification of kappa_{s,t} as the UNIQUE maximum-size (s,t)-core):
    "Theorem 4.1 Let s and t be relatively prime positive integers. If λ is a
     partition of n which is both an s-core and a t-core, then n ≤ (s^2 −1)(t^2 −1)/24.
     Moreover there is exactly one such partition for n = (s^2 −1)(t^2 −1)/24, which
     is κ_{s,t}."

Proposition 4.5 (the t = s+1 case proved there):
    "Proposition 4.5 If µ is any (s, s + 1)-core, then the Ferrers diagram of µ
     is contained in the Ferrers diagram of κ_{s,s+1}."

Remark 4.10 (explicit warning that beta-set SET inclusion is NOT diagram containment):
    "Remark 4.10 If λ and µ are (s, t)-cores, β(λ) ⊂ β(µ) does not imply λ ⊂ µ.
     In this case one can have the size of µ less than the size of λ."

Remark 4.11 (the general question — same question as OWR 2006/15 p.913):
    "Remark 4.11 In view of Lemma 4.5 it seems reasonable to ask: If µ is any
     (s, t)-core, is the Ferrers diagram of µ contained in the Ferrers diagram of
     κ_{s,t} ?"

Remark 4.12: "Theorem 4.1 improves a result of Puchta [9], who stated that an (s, t)-core has
at most s^2 t^2 nodes. However his proof does not appear to be correct."

Conclusion: Olsson-Stanton prove existence/uniqueness/max size of κ_{s,t} and containment only
for t=s+1 (Prop 4.5); they POSE the general containment question (Remark 4.11). They do NOT
prove it in general. The MathDB claim is exactly Remark 4.11 / the OWR question.
