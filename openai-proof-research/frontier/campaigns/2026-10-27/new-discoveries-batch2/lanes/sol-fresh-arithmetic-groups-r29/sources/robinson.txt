 Abstract 
    
 Let  G G  be a finite group and  p p  be a prime. Let  ( 𝕂 , R , 𝔽 ) (\mathbb{K},R,\mathbb{F})  be a (splitting)  p p -modular system for  G G . 
    
 We let  G p G_{p}  denote the set of  p p -elements of  G G  and  G p ′ G_{p^{\prime}}  denote the set of  p p -regular elements of  G G . In this note, we examine the generalized character  Ψ 1 , p , G \Psi_{1,p,G}  of  G G  which vanishes on all  p p -singular elements and whose value at each  p p -regular  y ∈ G y\in G  is the number of  p p -elements of  C G ​ ( y ) . C_{G}(y). 
We examine cases when  Ψ 1 , p , G \Psi_{1,p,G}  is a character, and when it is a character afforded by a projective  R ​ G RG -module. We conjecture that, in fact,  Ψ 1 , p , G \Psi_{1,p,G}  is always a character, and may always be afforded by a projective  R ​ G RG -module. We discuss some properties of  Ψ 1 , p , G \Psi_{1,p,G} , and relate it to a truncated version of the  R ​ G RG -module afforded by the conjugation action of  G G  on itself. 
    
 Since the virtual character  Ψ 1 , p , G \Psi_{1,p,G}  vanishes on  p p -singular elements, it corresponds to a unique virtual projective module  P 1 , p , G P_{1,p,G}  in the Green ring for  R ​ G RG , and we obtain a uniform explicit expression for  P 1 , p , G P_{1,p,G}  which is valid for all finite  G G . It is however not immediately obvious from this expression whether or not  P 1 , p , G P_{1,p,G}  is always a module. On the other hand, letting  R R  denote the trivial  R ​ G RG -module, we give a general explicit formula expressing  P 1 , p , G − R P_{1,p,G}-R  (in the Green ring for  R ​ G RG ) as a virtual module induced from (proper)  p p -local subgroups of  G G  when  O p ​ ( G ) = 1 .  Among other examples, we prove for each prime  p p  and each prime power  q q  (not necessarily divisible by  p p ) that  P 1 , p , G P_{1,p,G}  is a genuine projective  R ​ G RG -module when  G ≅ PSL ⁡ ( 2 , q ) G\cong{\rm PSL}(2,q)  and when  G ≅ SL ⁡ ( 2 , q ) . G\cong{\rm SL}(2,q).
