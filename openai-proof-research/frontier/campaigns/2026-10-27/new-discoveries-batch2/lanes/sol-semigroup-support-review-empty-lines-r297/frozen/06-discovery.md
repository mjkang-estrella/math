## Claim A: reflection of the exact Apéry table

Set s=2t-a, so 0<2s<t and gcd(s,t)=1. The nonnegative t-coefficients attainable from2t,3t are exactly0 and all integers>=2. In residue jt modulo a, 0<=j<a, the minimal representative is jt except j=1, where it is(a+1)t. Adding a to the coefficient j raises that minimum; adding a-generator copies cannot lower it. Hence the exact Apéry table is

    A[jt mod a] = jt (j=0 or 2<=j<a),
    A[t mod a] = (a+1)t.

Its maximum is (a+1)t and F=(a+1)t-a. Every positive gap has exactly the form

    F - i*a - j*t,  i>=0, j in {0,2,3,...,a-1},

provided that expression is positive. For j=0 use the exceptional Apéry representative; for j>=2 use the representative indexed a+1-j. This is a complete reflected gap table.

Suppose s>=3. The ratio reduction gives t>=5s. Up to and including offset a+3t the smallest gap offsets from F are, in increasing order,

    0, a, 2t, 3t, 2a, a+2t, 4t, a+3t.

Proof of exhaustiveness: j=0 contributes0,a,2a before that cutoff (3a>a+3t since2a>3t). j=2 contributes2t,a+2t; j=3 contributes3t,a+3t; j=4 contributes4t; j>=5 starts at5t>a+3t; all other i-values are larger. The first stated inequality holds because t>=5s, a=2t-s. Thus there are no missing gaps. The intervening distances are

    a, s, t, t-2s, s, s, t-s.

All are at least3. Consequently every gap through offset4t has two consecutive positive integers on each side; F-4t-3>0 because a>=12 and t>=15. Required boundary distances include s,t,t-2s. They would all be1 modulo3. But t=1 and t-2s=1 imply s=0 modulo3, contradicting s=1. This excludes EVERY s>=3 remaining after the prefix test.

If s=1, initial residues require t=1 modulo3, giving t=3k+1,a=6k+1. If s=2, initial residues require t=2 modulo3; primitivity forces t odd, giving t=6k+5,a=12k+8. With a>=12 the source parameter k is positive. These are exactly H2 and H15; source lemmas supply sufficiency. This proves Claim A, with the explicit rational-computation dependency above.

## Claim B: three Apéry layers simplify and give top obstructions

Set u=2t-a. Again 0<2u<t, gcd(u,t)=1, and the ratio reduction forces t>=5u on prefix survivors. For a fixed t-coefficient J the least a-coefficient is J mod3, achieved with that many copies of a+t and the rest copies of3t. The r294 formula minimizes over J=j+la, l=0,1,2. In fact l=0 ALWAYS minimizes here: increasing l once changes the candidate by at plus an a-coefficient difference at least-2a, which is positive since t>2 (indeed t>=7). Therefore

    A[jt mod a]=(j mod3)*a+j*t, 0<=j<a.

This is a universal simplification, not an assumption about residues. Initial permutation forces a=t modulo3, both nonzero. We separate these two cases to locate the top Apéry element correctly.

If a=t=1 modulo3, the maximum is at j=a-2 and

    F=a+(a-2)t.

To check the maximum, the representatives with each fixed j mod3 increase with j, and comparison of their final three indices gives this value because t<2a. Put h=a-2-j. For h>=0, reflected offsets from F are (h mod3)*a+h*t+l*a, l>=0; the remaining j=a-1 gives offsets2a-t+l*a. Retain only positive gaps. For u>=3,t>=5u, the top offsets through3a-t are EXACTLY

    0, a, 2a-t, a+t, 3t, 2a, 3a-t.

For completeness, h=0 gives multiples of a; h=1 starts a+t and its next offset2a+t already exceeds3a-t; h=2 starts2a+2t, already too large; h=3 starts3t and its next a+3t exceeds3a-t by2u; h>=4 starts still larger. The extra j=a-1 gives2a-t,3a-t. Their order follows by substituting a=2t-u. Distances through and immediately after2a are

    a, t-u, u, u, t-2u, t-u.

They are all>=3. F-2a-3>0 under a>=12,t>=15, so the isolated-gap boundary lemma applies to all these gaps through offset2a. In particular u,t-u,t-2u must all be1 modulo3. Subtracting the last two requires u=0 modulo3, contrary to u=1. This excludes the first residue case for every u>=3.

If a=t=2 modulo3, the top Apéry index is j=a-1 and F=(a-1)t. Comparing the final three indices uses2t>a. For h=a-1-j, reflected offsets are

    h*t + (1-((1-h) mod3))*a + l*a, l>=0.

The four smallest are0,u,a,a+u: h=0 contributes0,a; h=2 contributes u,a+u; h=1 starts a+t>a+u and h>=3 starts at least3t>a+u. The offset2a is also larger than a+u. For u>=3,t>=5u all neighboring distances u,a-u,u are>=3, and the relevant gaps are positive. Thus the distance u between the two highest gaps must equal1 modulo3. But u=2t-a=2 modulo3 in this case, an immediate contradiction. No assumption about long runs across a double gap is used.

The u=1,2 cases now follow exactly as in Claim A from initial residues and primitivity. They give respectively H3 and H16. Source Lemmas4.3 and4.16 prove their sufficiency. This proves Claim B with the same explicit computational reduction dependency.
