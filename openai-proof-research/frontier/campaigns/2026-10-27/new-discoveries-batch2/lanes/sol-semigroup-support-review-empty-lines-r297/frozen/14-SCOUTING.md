1. **Sensitivity versus block sensitivity.** Huang, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00847v1, Theorems1.1,1.4,1.5; source Conjecture1.2 attributes the question to Nisan–Szegedy. For every Boolean function, block sensitivity is at most twice the fourth power of sensitivity. Actual proof of Theorem1.1 and Lemmas2.2–2.3 read: recursively signed cube adjacency squares to nI; Cauchy interlacing and maximum row degree bound give a sqrt(n) induced-degree lower bound. The Gotsman–Linial equivalence and existing degree/block-sensitivity inequality supply the stated universal consequence. Retire the polynomial relation, not a claim about optimal exponent. This is separate from semigroup permutation words.

2. **Kadison–Singer / Weaver partition family, counted once.** Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.3969v1, Theorem1.2, Corollary1.3, final proof at end of §5. Independent finitely supported random vectors with sum of expected outer products I and expected squared norms<=epsilon have positive probability of operator norm <=(1+sqrt(epsilon))^2. Actual final proof read: mixed characteristic polynomial gives the expectation, Theorem5.1 controls its top root, Theorem4.4 provides interlacing, and Theorem3.3 selects an outcome. The corollary partitions a bounded-norm isotropic family into two bounded frame operators. The pure-state/paving equivalences are attributed to the cited earlier sources, not independently reproved here. Retire this established partition/extension family, not general optimal paving constants. The downloaded v1 body internally bears an August2018 date despite its2013 arXiv header; retain that provenance discrepancy.

3. **Erdős homogeneous discrepancy.** Tao, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.05363v1, Theorem1.1 and Corollary1.2. Every unit-vector-valued sequence in a real or complex Hilbert space has unbounded sums on homogeneous arithmetic progressions; ±1 is the scalar special case. Actual theorem statements, reductions around Theorems1.7–1.8, and Remarks1.10–1.11 read. Mechanism: stochastic completely multiplicative reduction, logarithmically averaged Elliott theorem, pretentious-character obstruction. The deep analytic theorem is a cited dependency, not independently audited here. The stronger arbitrary real matrix formulation in Remark1.11 is expressly left unresolved BY THIS BODY; we do not claim its resolution or nominate it without current-source checking. Retire the ordinary discrepancy question only.

4. **Exponential cap-set bound.** Ellenberg–Gijswijt, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.09223v1, Proposition2, Theorem4, Corollary5 (complete proofs read). For A in F_q^n with only diagonal solutions to alpha*a1+beta*a2+gamma*a3=0 and gamma!=0, |A|<=3m_((q-1)n/3); the F_3 cap-set consequence is o(2.756^n). The body’s monomial splitting bounds a diagonal evaluation matrix rank, and the maximal-support polynomial dimension argument supplies the set bound; the final large-deviation estimate is explicit. Retire the proposed near3^n growth scenario, not the exact exponential growth constant or integer Roth bounds. This is not a duplicate of the semigroup AP support: objects and quantifiers differ.
