# Additional arithmetic/algebra/finite-geometry breadth

Eight distinct question families beyond r231's eleven are listed below. They are source questions, not eight certified open problems. The primary URLs share prefix https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/ . Each PDF was saved and hashed before reading; source versions, HTTP receipts, extracted text and final hashes are retained. Closing publication lines determine dates. No newly announced resolution is used to pad the list.

1. **Moody, Arithmetic Progressions on Edwards Curves**, 2011-02-08, VOL14/Moody/moody2.pdf, final paragraph p3: find an Edwards curve with a rational x-coordinate arithmetic progression of length>=10. The source constructs length9 on x²+y²=1+d x²y², d rational; its introduction explicitly writes d!=1 (the usual nonsingular elliptic interpretation also excludes0). Do not infer an extra source restriction silently. Actual follow-up González–Jiménez arXiv:1304.4361v2 (2015-04-07), introduction and Theorems1.1–1.3, proves the specific progression 0,±1,…,±5 impossible and treats symmetric cases; it explicitly says the general length10 question is unanswered there. Actual Çelik–Sadek–Soydan arXiv:1903.07132v1 (2019-03-17), introduction and construction scope, concerns arbitrary sets of size4–6 and twisted/general models, not an ordinary Edwards length10 witness. Park: high-genus rational point problem, no uniform construction. Do not promote the special impossibility result to a full answer.

2. **De Koninck–Doyon–Luca, Powerful Values of Quadratic Polynomials**, 2011-03-25, VOL14/DeKoninck/dek.pdf, Section3 pp7–8: for every integer k!=0, n²+k is powerful for infinitely many positive n (powerful means every prime divisor occurs to exponent>=2). Actual Theorem1 gives density-one k, not every k. The introduction's generalized Pell bridge turns one nonsquare powerful value into infinitely many. Proposition2's explicit sufficient criterion and Pell proof do not establish a first value for every k. Current author/title queries are noisy and did not produce a later actual same-object proof; those intervals are uncleared. Park: finding the first seed is the genuine obstruction; a table instance or classical Pell propagation earns no credit.

3. **Weingartner, On the Solutions of sigma(n)=sigma(n+k)**, 2011-05-03, VOL14/Weingartner/wein3.pdf, p1: for which k>=1 are there infinitely many positive n satisfying that equality? Source also recalls Sierpiński's k=1 question and Erdős's stronger consecutive-run question; these are one family here. Proposition1 and proof derive the even-k infinitude only under Schinzel H, using four simultaneous linear primes. Proposition2 is an enormous finite existence range, not unconditional infinitude. Current author/sigma and sum-of-divisors queries did not locate a qualified actual later proof. Park immediately: prime-pattern bottleneck; no relabelled famous open problem or parameter credit.

4. **Applegate–LeBrun–Sloane, Dismal Arithmetic**, 2011-11-21, VOL14/Sloane/carry2.pdf. Digits add by max and multiply by min, with no carries. Conjecture26 p30: for every integer n>1, the ordinary number d_10(n) of dismal divisors is strictly less than the integer sigma_10(n) formed by their dismal sum. Read definitions and actual Theorems24–25/proofs: they bound that sum and classify the binary sum, but do not bound the decimal divisor count as C26 needs. Conjectures10,12–14 on primes/maximal counts are not extra families. Current dismal/arithmetic and math.NT queries return arXiv:1107.1130v2 (2011-10-26), the same paper, not a new proof. Provisional research lead; no latest-literature completeness claim.

5. **Kronenthal, An Integer Sequence Motivated by Generalized Quadrangles**, 2015-07-16, VOL18/Kronenthal/kron3.pdf, Conjecture1 p3, attributed to Dmytrenko–Lazebnik–Williford: for every odd prime power q, every monomial graph over F_q of girth>=8 is isomorphic to Gamma_3(q)=G_q(xy,xy²). Both parts are F_q³; adjacency means x_i+y_i=f_i(x_1,y_1), i=2,3. Theorem2 reduces to G_q(xy,x^k y^(2k)) with permutation-polynomial constraints; the paper's actual main result closes a congruence-derived sequence, not the whole classification. Actual arXiv:2101.09448v1 is classification over **the real numbers**, a different field scope. Current author query found that body; broad monomial queries are noisy. Park: odd-finite-field exponent exclusion still requires a complete Hermite–Dickson bridge, not several tested congruences. No assertion of present openness without earlier/later finite-field classification bodies.

6. **Gross–Harrington, Special Numbers in the Ring Z_n**, 2015-11-25 (author order changed2016-01-11), VOL18/Harrington/harr5.pdf, Question1 p2: when not every residue is the sum of two nonzero squares, how badly does that fail? This recalls the Harrington–Jones–Lamarche question. Actual Theorem7 completely characterizes sums of two **unit** squares, with separate local conditions at2,3,5 and primes3mod4; actual Theorem22 treats nonzero square summands at p^k for p>=7, p=3mod4. The body explicitly says the unit restriction prevents a full answer to Q1. Actual arXiv:1404.0187v1 is the earlier *Representing Integers as the Sum of Two Squares in Z_n*, not a later resolution; query results about polynomial rings/endomorphisms are different objects. Park as likely classical local/CRT classification, not freshness-cleared. A new formula needs both summands globally nonzero modulo composite n; local nonzero assumptions cannot simply be imposed at every component.

7. **Borisov, Iterations of Integer Polynomial Maps Modulo Primes**, 2013-10-12, VOL16/Borisov/borisov3.pdf, Question10 p4: does an integer polynomial map with two different fixed points exist such that modulo every prime every initial point eventually reaches one fixed point or the other according as its initial first coordinate is zero or nonzero? Dimension is not bounded in the question, and time may depend on the prime/point. Actual Theorem2 proves the additive trap (x²y,x²y+xy²) sends every F_p² point to the *same* fixed point, through ratio translation. Theorems4 and6 cover multiplicative/power traps; their proofs impose generator/order conditions. Theorem8 closes nilpotency loci under intersection, not this two-basin construction. Current title query returned unrelated integer-part multicorrelations, so no same-object follow-up body or current-clear claim. Provisional research lead; Q11's arbitrary nilpotency loci is not an extra family.

8. **Cooper–Riasanovsky, On the Reciprocal of the Binary Generating Function for the Sum of Divisors**, 2013-01-26, VOL16/Cooper/cooper3.pdf, Conjecture29 p10: the support of (1+sum_(n>=1)(sigma(n) mod2)q^n)^(-1) in F_2[[q]] has natural density1/32. The constant coefficient convention is essential. Read Definitions1–3, Theorem25's proof, Proposition26, Theorem27 and Corollary28: the residual 7mod8 series is q^7 T(q)^16, giving support in7mod16 and upper density<=1/16, not density1/32. Actual arXiv:1409.2909v1, posted2014, is a later deposit of the same 2013 article, not a resolution. Current exact-title query returns that deposit only. Park: parity-density/equidistribution of the residual series is an additional all-order problem, not a consequence of its support. Distinct from the ordinary sigma equality family in item3 and from partition parity, which is not counted separately.

## Extra controls, not additional qualifying families

Read frozen ASM valuation paper VOL14/Moll/moll2.pdf: Observation4 p3 requests a general finite periodic-expansion procedure, but is too underspecified to nominate. arXiv:0908.0149v1 supplies the older Fourier/Mellin–Perron ASM valuation approach, not a full response to that method-design request. Inverse football pools VOL14/Brink/brink3.pdf already proves the low-dimensional uniqueness conjecture in its own body; no new credit. Vector partitions VOL18/Sissokho/sissokho3.pdf, full subsets VOL14/Mirzavaziri/mirza4.pdf, and sigma/phi ratio VOL16/Broughan/broughan26.pdf were retrieved but are not counted as fresh question families. Stoneham strong-normality and Young-graph families were screened as extra controls, not used to inflate the eight; Young graphs directly inform decimal reversal's standard-mechanism objection.

## Ranked research survivors: three provisional, zero novelty-cleared

1. **Borisov Q10**, exact version/date/URL above. Closest actual proof: additive trap Theorem2. Mechanism: add a persistent branch coordinate to ratio translation so first-coordinate zero and nonzero produce distinct absorbing states over every F_p. Next decisive calculation: produce integer coordinate polynomials and prove both absorption and fixed-point distinctness, including p=2. Strongest objection: the known trap loses exactly the information one needs to preserve; no valid polynomial indicator uniform over all primes has been supplied. Not labelled fresh.
2. **Dismal C26**, exact version/date/URL above. Closest actual proof: Theorem24 and the digit-divisibility tableau. Mechanism: inject divisors into ordinary integers below the digitwise maximum divisor, with strictness for n>1; alternatively exhibit a finite exact violation. Next calculation: derive a complete allowed-prefix recursion for divisor tableaux and compare the divisor count to sigma_10(n). Strongest objection: local digit bounds neglect simultaneous witness-quotient constraints and may either fail or be an already-known routine combinatorial inequality. No exclusion routine or full bridge is claimed.
3. **Edwards length>=10**, exact version/date/URL above. Closest actual proof: González–Jiménez2015 symmetric nonexistence results, plus 2019 S-sequence constructions of smaller length. Mechanism: work on the simultaneous-square genus-five model with free rational start and step, rather than Moody's fixed symmetric progression. Next decisive calculation: exact rational-point construction yielding ten coordinates, followed by substitution into a nonsingular ordinary Edwards model. Strongest objection: known special-case obstructions and unlocated later progress; no small-height enumeration can prove general nonexistence.

These are priorities for further source qualification, not accepted or literature-cleared survivors. None merits Lean/manuscript work yet. The decimal C4 full refutation is reported separately with a strong automatic-extension objection, rather than ranked as a new discovery.
