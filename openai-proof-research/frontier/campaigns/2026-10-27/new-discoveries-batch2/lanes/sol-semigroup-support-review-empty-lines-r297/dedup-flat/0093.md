## Ranked research leads (not resolved outcomes)

1. **Grytczuk–Stankiewicz, Problem 1**, [2011.12822v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.12822v1), submitted 25 November 2020, printed p8: does there exist a finite word W on a three-letter alphabet with exactly 80 distinct terminal square-reduction descendants? A reduction is UXXV -> UXV for nonempty X; a terminal descendant is square-free. Length is unrestricted. Actual source Theorem 3, pp5–7, constructs every positive cardinality over FOUR letters; Theorems 1–2 give unbounded/exponentially many ternary reducts, not exact cardinality. Both construction bodies were inspected before sustained work. The source's explicit length49 example has81 reducts; independently reproduced by two exhaustive closure traversals, 2928 reachable words. Two routes are in discovery.md: exact deletion-DAG construction and separator/product transfer. A capped 163-trial local search found no80; the product route cannot remove the fourth letter without a new reduction-reflecting code. **Next:** construct and prove such a ternary synchronizing code, or search structured families whose complete reduct DAG admits a small symbolic decomposition. A larger unstructured sweep is not the proposed next step. History exact-ID and square-free-reduct aliases had no hits in the bounded report sample; the arXiv phrase query found the source, not a demonstrated solution. Current full proof registries and exhaustive citing literature remain unavailable/uncleared. Unknown optimum is irrelevant: one genuine 80 witness would completely answer Problem1, not partially answer it. The infinitude conjecture about missing cardinalities is NOT solved by such a witness.

2. **Machacek, Conjecture 4.6**, [2010.11112v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.11112v2), version14 March2021, printed p12: for every n>=1,d>=0, the domination number gamma_n(d) equals the independent domination number i_n(d) in G_n(d). Vertices are exponent vectors in N^n summing to d; distinct vertices are adjacent iff their L1 distance is2. Actual Proposition4.5 proof, pp11–12, shows G_n(d) is induced-K_(1,r)-free iff d<r or n<r. Together with the explicitly cited Allan–Laskar claw-free theorem, this clears only n<=2 or d<=2, not the full scope. Original Allan–Laskar body was not retrieved; no extra theorem is inferred from its citation. A floating MILP diagnostic over ten pairs agrees, and integer-rounded upper witnesses were independently checked against graph incidence constraints; these are not universal proofs or exact lower certificates. The prior n4,d6 solver failure was preserved and the range reduced, not the caps raised. **Mechanism:** transform a minimum dominating set into an independent one by transfers of single exponent units; induced claws show why the classical claw-free replacement cannot simply apply. **Next:** identify an exchange preserving domination of the boundary simplex faces, or produce one finite pair with certified gamma<i. Exact-title arXiv search found only this paper; broad triangular-grid/hexagon-chess literature and the original Geramita–Gregory–Roberts/Wagon predecessors remain a prior-art gate. No certified current-open status is claimed.
