in PROOF.md, every rational nonincreasing s and every core member in every
finite consistent pseudometric. The sharp lower construction is already public.
Mechanism: split the witnessing matching into plurality tokens except those
of the comparison candidate b, retain b's entire column, and retain the cost H
of voters whose favorite is b. The flow intersection bound

independent adversarial review of the flow splitting and universal scalar

2. **Recursive Copeland distortion 5**, Kizilkaya–Aggarwal–Micha,
[2608.21790v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21790), Section 3 and conclusion.
Question: can the upper bound 7 for the recursive ranking rule in the paper's
identical position-weight setting be reduced to the conjectured 5? Mechanism:
retain correlations between the successive restricted-election matchings rather
than charging their voter-distance terms separately. Next: formulate one
all-prefix charging inequality with the source's monotone positional weights.
No result proposed; the present lane read the weighted-recursion definitions,
stated guarantees and concluding target, but did not authenticate all auxiliary
proof dependencies. The exact restriction to identical weights must be retained.

3. **Two-entry uniform lottery beating 3**, Jia–Peng,
[2608.26854v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26854), Introduction and Section 6.
For every profile, can a deterministic choice of two candidates (repetitions
allowed), followed by an equal coin flip, have worst-case distortion strictly
below 3? Their body proves only 2<=N*<=164. Mechanism: a pair whose joint metric
LP dual covers each other's bad metrics, rather than compressing a lottery by
a concentration inequality. Next: characterize pairwise dual feasibility on
three/four-candidate profiles before claiming universal existence. High risk:
there is no universal pair-selection lemma here, and the source benchmark 5/2
is not asserted to be the current unrestricted optimum.
