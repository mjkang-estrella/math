                                                   Abstract. In this paper, we shall first introduce the Pascal determi-
                                                   nantal array of order k as a generalization of the standard Pascal array.
                                                   We also present an algorithm to produce determinantal arrays of any
                                                   order. Then we investigate geometric properties of these new determi-
                                                   nantal arrays. As a by product, we give a proof of the correctness of
                                                   our proposed algorithm. Then we give a geometric interpretation of the
                                                   determinant of any k by k subarray of the Pascal array. Finally, we will
                                                   give a generalization of Rahimpour’s determinantal identity, using the
                                                   above geometric interpretation.


                                                                            1. Introduction
                                              In [1], the first author and M. Bayat proved a conjecture by Rahim-
                                           pour about determinants inside the Pascal array. After more investigations
                                           around the previous project, the second author of this paper made a general-
                                           ization of the Rahimpour’s determinantal identity. Here, we first introduce
                                           a new infinite class of squared arrays which are called the Pascal determi-
                                           nantal arrays as a generalization of the standard Pascal array and then we
                                           recall the Rahimpour’s identity. We then state it’s generalization.
                                           In section 3, we present an algorithm to produce a Pascal determinantal ar-
                                           ray of order k from the given Pascal array. In section 4, we quickely review
                                           Dodgson’s condensation formula for determinants. We also give another re-
                                           cursive formula for Pascal determinantal arrays based on this formula. The
                                           weighted version of the star of David rule will be the subject of section 5.
                                           We also show that this rule is true for the class of Pascal determinantal
                                           arrays. In section 6, we give a proof of the correctness of our algorithm,
                                           using the weighted version of the star of David rule and the condensation
                                           formula for determinants. Finally in the last section, after giving a geomet-
                                           ric interpretation of the entries of the Pascal determinantal array of order
                                           k, we present a simple geometric proof of our main result.
