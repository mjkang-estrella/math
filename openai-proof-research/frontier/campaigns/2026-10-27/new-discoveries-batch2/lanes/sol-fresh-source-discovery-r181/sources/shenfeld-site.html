Currently I work on generative modeling. Prior, I worked on optimal transport, high-dimensional probability, and convex geometry.

I am an Assistant Professor of Applied Mathematics and an Affiliated Faculty with the Data Science Institute at Brown University. Previously, I was a C.L.E. Moore instructor and an NSF postdoctoral fellow in the Mathematics department at MIT. I completed my PhD at Princeton University under the supervision of Ramon van Handel, and received my B.Sc. from MIT.
