Structural Properties, Complete
Monotonicity, Bounds, and Analytic
Approximations with Bounded Remainders
for a Wallis-Type Gamma Ratio
Mansour Mahmoud and Cristinel Mortici

     Abstract. In this paper, we investigate several completely monotonic func-
     tions involving the Psi function, which is defined as the logarithmic deriv-
     ative of the Gamma function. By exploiting the analytic and monotonic
     properties of these functions, we develop three new approximation formu-
     las for a Wallis-type ratio with bounded remainder terms. These approx-
     imations lead to a number of new inequalities that are better than some
     existing bounds in the literature. Finally, an open problem is proposed
     concerning the monotonic behavior of the remainder functions arising
     from the new approximations.

     Mathematics Subject Classification. 33B15, 41A60, 26D15, 41A30.

     Keywords. Wallis’ ratio, Gamma function, Psi function, Asymptotic ap-
     proximation, Speed of convergence.

5. Conclusion
This work has examined several completely monotonic functions constructed
from the Psi function and explored their analytic implications. Building on
these properties, we introduced three new approximation formulas for a Wallis-
type ratio in (17), (19) and (21), each accompanied by bounded remainder
terms. As a consequence, a collection of new inequalities was obtained, pro-
viding reﬁned estimates compared with earlier results. Our analysis also shows
that the newly established bounds outperform previously some recent inequal-
ities throughout the positive real axis. To conclude, we raised an open question
concerning the monotonicity of the remainder functions associated with the
new approximations. Addressing this question may lead to a deeper under-
standing of the ﬁne behavior of these ratios and could yield further improve-
ments in related inequalities and approximation formulas.
