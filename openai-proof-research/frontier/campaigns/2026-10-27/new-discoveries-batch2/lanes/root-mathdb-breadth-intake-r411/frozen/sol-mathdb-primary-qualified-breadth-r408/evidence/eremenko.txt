Unsolved Probelms in Function Theory
My favorite unsolved problems
"In re mathematica ars proponendi quaestionem pluris facienda est quam solvendi"
(Georg Cantor, a thesis in his PhD defense, Dissertation Berlin 1867, Theses. III. In: Gesammelte Abhandlungen, Hrsg. Ernst Zermelo, Verlag von Julius Springer, Berlin 1932, S. 31)

In summer 2026 I decied to withdraw them, in view of the AI revolution which
is going on.

this required daily updates, sometimes more than that.
