For every cofinite additive submonoid S⊆N minimally generated by four positive integers m=n1<n2<n3<n4, write F=max(N\S), PF={f∉S: f+n_i∈S for all i}. Assume m+F−f∈S for every f∈PF. Is |PF|≤4? ## Mechanism A: arithmetic boundary reflection, with exact certificates

Let A=Ap(S,m)={s∈S:s−m∉S}, W=max A and order x≤_S y iff y−x∈S. Its maximal elements are exactly {m+f:f∈PF}; F=W−m. For a maximal w≠W, W−w∉S: otherwise w<_S W, contradiction. Therefore canonical reduction is equivalent to

  W+m−w ∈ A for every ≤_S-maximal w≠W.             (R)

Indeed its membership in S is precisely m+F−f∈S, and subtracting m yields W−w∉S. The omitted w=W maps to m∈S and satisfies canonical reduction automatically. Conversely (R) supplies all remaining source conditions. This is an exact elementary equivalence, not a new theorem answering the question.

Exact universal missing lemma A: for EVERY numerical semigroup S minimally generated by m and three further integers, if (R) holds, then A has at most FOUR ≤_S-maximal elements. A finite fixed-m certification cannot discharge this quantifier. In Kunz variables w_i=mk_i+i, k_i≥1 for1≤i<m, k_0=0, enforce k_i+k_j+floor((i+j)/m)≥k_((i+j) mod m). Exactly three nonzero Apéry elements must be primitive minimal generators. Maximality and (R) become finite membership/disjunction tests at each m; m remains unbounded. Decisive next step: prove a staircase boundary classification forcing at most three maxima besides W, or construct a symbolic infinite family violating it. Existing L-shape arguments in H count corners coarsely and do not yet supply the bound4.

Rigorous countermodels to relaxations: reflection alone without embedding dimension four is insufficient. The ordinary semigroup <6,7,8,9,10,11> has A={0,7,8,9,10,11}, W=11, all five nonzero elements maximal, and reflection17−w interchanges7↔10 and8↔9. It has canonical reduction and type5. Conversely four generators alone are insufficient: <9,10,12,13> has A by residues [0,10,20,12,13,23,24,25,26], PF={11,14,15,16,17}, F=17 and type5. Reflection of w=20 is15∉S, so canonical reduction fails. Thus neither deletion of the reflection premise nor deletion of the exact embedding hypothesis is valid.

Bounded construction check: all minimal four-generator gcd-one tuples with entries in [4,42] were checked by shortest paths on residues; 35,545 semigroups, 10,467 canonical, with type counts1:3159,2:4297,3:2992,4:19. No type≥5 canonical witness in this box. This is not a universal claim. Independent dynamic-programming membership checks through twice the largest Apéry element verify retained witnesses and the PF criterion. No randomized search or floating arithmetic.

## Mechanism B: RF support elimination and its missing arithmetic packing step

For f∈PF\{F}, canonical reduction provides a factorization

  F−f = −m + a2*n2+a3*n3+a4*n4,  a_j≥0.

No m term is necessary: a factorization of m+F−f with a positive m coefficient would imply F−f∈S, contradicting maximality of the two distinct PF elements. For every i=2,3,4, an RF+ row has

  f = −n_i + sum_(j≠i) b_ij*n_j.

If a_i>0, then necessarily b_i1=0. Otherwise adding these two equations gives a nonnegative factorization of F, impossible. This is the one-row part of the zero-product mechanism in N Lemma2.3; it is justified here directly and receives no novelty credit.

Exact missing lemma B: for every minimally four-generated S with canonical reduction, the distinct non-F pseudo-Frobenius elements admitting these one-row factorizations and their compatible RF+ rows number at most THREE. A finite support-pattern pigeonhole argument alone is inadequate: repeated support patterns must be excluded by integer coefficient comparisons and PF-antichain constraints. The actual Theorem2.4 proof uses TWO additional NG rows to eliminate coefficients; canonical reduction supplies no such rows. Decisive next step: a new arithmetic collision lemma for repeated one-row support patterns that does not assume the missing NG rows.

Concrete countermodel to the stronger transfer: the SOURCE'S OWN S=<16,17,19,39> has F=79, PF={37,59,61,79}, and A=[0,17,34,19,36,53,38,39,56,57,58,75,76,77,78,95]. Canonical values16+79−f are58,36,34,16, all in S. Boundary reflections are53→58,75→36,77→34. It is not nearly Gorenstein: for generator17 every possible NG choice p∈PF fails. Explicit obstructing q values: p=37,q=79 gives−25; p=59,q=79 gives−3; p=61,q=79 gives−1; p=79,q=37 gives59∉S. Hence importing the existing type≤3 theorem is rigorously invalid. This known example is not a counterexample to the desired type≤4 bound.

Strongest classical objection: N already isolates the exact canonical-reduction question after proving the nearly-Gorenstein result. Apéry reflection is a natural rewriting of a canonical ideal condition, and RF zero products are existing tools. A proof merely translating them or checking the known witness would be routine scaffolding. A full arithmetic boundary/packing lemma, or a genuine type≥5 canonical construction, is required. No such result is obtained here.
