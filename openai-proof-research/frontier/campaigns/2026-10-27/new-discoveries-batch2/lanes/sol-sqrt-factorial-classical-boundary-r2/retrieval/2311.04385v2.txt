                                                              and zeros of Hankel transforms

                                              Abstract. It is proved by the method of partial fraction expansion and
                                          Sturm’s oscillation theory that the zeros of certain Hankel transforms are
                                          all real, simple and distributed one by one between consecutive zeros of
                                          Bessel functions. As an application, we obtain a list of sufficient conditions
                                          as well as necessary conditions on parameters for which 1 F2 hypergeometric
                                          functions belong to the Laguerre-Pólya class.
                                                  Keywords. Bessel functions, Fourier transforms, Hankel transforms,
                                                  Laguerre-Pólya class, partial fraction expansions, transference principle.
                                                  2020 MSC. 30D10, 33C10, 34C10, 44A15.

     We organize the present paper as follows.
     In section 2, we establish the aforementioned partial fraction expansions
based on Cauchy’s residue theorem. In section 3, we revisit the method of
Hurwitz and Pólya. In sections 4, 5, we describe the nature and distribution
of zeros of Hankel transforms and identify Hν (f )(z) as a member of the

7, we consider 1 F2 hypergeometric functions of the above type and deal with
the problem of identifying parameters for the Laguerre-Pólya class.
