        ESTIMATE OF THE NUMBER
    OF RESTRICTED INTEGER-PARTITIONS
                                      Joel Ratsaby
      Using the saddle-point method an estimate is computed for the number
      wm,N (n) of ordered m-partitions (compositions) of a positive integer n under
      a constraint that the size of every part is at most N . The approximation
      error rate is O(n−1/5 ).
