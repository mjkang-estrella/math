Abstract
The existence of qq-ary linear complementary pairs (LCPs) of codes with q>2q>2 has been completely characterized so far.
This paper gives a characterization for the existence of binary LCPs of codes. As a result, we solve an open problem proposed by Carlet e​t​a​l.et~al. (IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 65(3): 1694-1704, 2019) and a conjecture proposed by Choi e​t​a​l.et~al. (Cryptogr. Commun. 15(2): 469-486, 2023).
Keywords: Linear complementary pair of code, linear ℓ\ell-intersection pair, the security parameter
Mathematics Subject Classification 94B05 15B05 12E10
1 Introduction
Let qq be a prime power and let 𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q} denote the finite field with qq elements. The (Hamming) weight wt⁡(𝐱){\rm wt}({\bf x}) of 𝐱=(x1,x2,…,xn)∈𝔽qn{\bf x}=(x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{n})\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n} is defined by wt(𝐱)=|{i|xi≠0,1≤i≤n}|{\rm wt}({\bf x})=|\{i~|~x_{i}\neq 0,~1\leq i\leq n\}|. A linear [n,k,d]q[n,k,d]_{q} code CC is a kk-dimensional linear subspace of 𝔽qn\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}, where dd is the minimum weight of all nonzero codewords of CC.
The (Euclidean) dual code C⟂C^{\perp} of a linear [n,k]q[n,k]_{q} code CC is defined by
C⟂={y∈𝔽qn|⟨x,y⟩=0​for​all​x∈C},C^{\perp}=\{\textbf{y}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}~|~\langle\textbf{x},\textbf{y}\rangle=0~{\rm for\ all}\ \textbf{x}\in C\},
where ⟨x,y⟩=∑i=1nxi​yi\langle\textbf{x},\textbf{y}\rangle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}y_{i} for 𝐱=(x1,x2,…,xn){\bf x}=(x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{n}) and y=(y1,y2,…,yn)∈𝔽qn\textbf{y}=(y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{n})\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}.
A pair of linear codes (C1,C2)(C_{1},C_{2}) of length nn is called a linear ℓ\ell-intersection pair of codes if dim(C1∩C2)=ℓ\dim(C_{1}\cap C_{2})=\ell. This notion was introduced by Guenda e​t​a​l.et~al. [19]. In the special case when dim(C1)+dim(C2)=n\dim(C_{1})+\dim(C_{2})=n and ℓ=0\ell=0, (C1,C2)(C_{1},C_{2}) is called a linear complementary pair (LCP) of codes. This notion was introduced by Carlet e​t​a​l.et~al. [12]. If dim(C1)=k\dim(C_{1})=k, then (C1,C2)(C_{1},C_{2}) is called an [n,k][n,k] LCP of codes. In the more special case when (C,C⟂)(C,C^{\perp}) is an LCP, then CC is called linear complementary dual (LCD). This notion was introduced by Massey [28] in order to provide an optimum linear coding solution for the two-user binary adder channel.
LCPs of codes have been used in the framework of direct sum masking, which was proposed as a countermeasure against side channel attacks (SCAs) and fault injection attacks (FIAs) [11, 10]. If an LCP of codes (C1,C2)(C_{1},C_{2}) is used in this framework, then the minimum distance d⁡(C1)d(C_{1}) of C1C_{1} measures the protection against FIAs, whereas the minimum distance d⁡(C2⟂)d(C_{2}^{\perp}) of the dual code of C2C_{2} measures the protection against SCAs. It has been shown that the level of resistance against both SCA and FIA depends on min⁡{d⁡(C1),d⁡(C2⟂)}\min\{d(C_{1}),d(C_{2}^{\perp})\}, which is referred to as the security parameter of the LCP of codes. Note that for an LCD code CC, (C,C⟂)(C,C^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes. Then the security parameter of (C,C⟂)(C,C^{\perp}) is simply d⁡(C)d(C), which has been studied extensively (see [3, 2, 4, 5, 16, 17, 22, 26, 9, 18, 21, 14, 27]). Hence we can define the optimal security parameter of [n,k][n,k] LCPs of codes for given nn and kk as follows:
dL​C​P​(n,k)=max⁡{min⁡{d⁡(C1),d⁡(C2⟂)}|(C1,C2)​is​an​[n,k]q​LCP​of​codes}.d_{LCP}(n,k)=\max\{\min\{d(C_{1}),d(C_{2}^{\perp})\}~|~(C_{1},C_{2})~{\rm is~an}~[n,k]_{q}~{\rm LCP~of~codes}\}.
Let dL​(n,k)d_{L}(n,k) denote the largest minimum distance among linear [n,k]q[n,k]_{q} codes. Then we clearly have dL​C​P​(n,k)≤dL​(n,k).d_{LCP}(n,k)\leq d_{L}(n,k).
Carlet e​t​a​l.et~al. [13] showed that for any qq-ary linear code CC with q>2q>2, there is a monomial transformation σ\sigma on 𝔽qn\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n} such that (C,(σ⁡(C))⟂)(C,(\sigma(C))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes. They proposed an open problem for the existence of qq-ary LCPs of codes: is there a monomial transformation σ\sigma on 𝔽qn\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n} such that (C1,(σ⁡(C2))⟂)(C_{1},(\sigma(C_{2}))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes for any two qq-ary linear [n,k][n,k] codes C1C_{1} and C2C_{2} with q>2q>2? This open problem has been settled by Anderson e​t​a​l.et~al. [1]. This means that the problems of the existence and the optimal security parameters of qq-ary LCPs of codes have been settled for q>2q>2.
For the binary case, the same article [13] showed that for any binary linear code CC, there is a linear transformation σ\sigma on 𝔽2n+1\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n+1} such that ({0}×C,(σ⁡({0}×C))⟂)(\{0\}\times C,(\sigma(\{0\}\times C))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes. They also proposed a similar open problem for the existence of binary LCPs of codes.
Moreover, their result also yields that dL​C​P​(n,k)≥dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)\geq d_{L}(n,k)-1. Hence an interesting topic is to study the best security parameter problem for binary LCPs of codes. For this topic, Choi e​t​a​l.et~al. [15] determined the best security parameters of binary [n,k][n,k] LCPs of codes for n≤18n\leq 18 or k≤4k\leq 4. Further, they also described a sufficient condition for dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1, and gave a conjecture on its necessary condition. Very recently, Gu¨{\rm\ddot{u}}neri [20] constructed an infinite family of optimal binary LCPs of codes from Solomon-Stiffler codes.
In this paper, we study the problems of the existence and the optimal security parameters of binary LCPs of codes. We show that for two binary linear [n,k][n,k] codes C1C_{1} and C2C_{2}, there is a linear transformation σ\sigma on 𝔽2n+1\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n+1} such that ({0}×C1,(σ⁡({0}×C2))⟂)(\{0\}\times C_{1},(\sigma(\{0\}\times C_{2}))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes. This solves an open problem proposed by Carlet e​t​a​l.et~al. [13]. Further, we present a more general sufficient condition for dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1 than that given by Choi e​t​a​l.et~al. [15], and show a conjecture proposed in the same paper. As a result, we construct an infinite family of optimal binary LCPs of codes from Solomon-Stiffler codes, whose parameters cover those constructed by Gu¨{\rm\ddot{u}}neri [20].
Further, these results also imply that the conjecture proposed by Guenda e​t​a​l.et~al. [19] is not valid for the binary case.
The paper is organized as follows. The next section gives some notations and preliminaries. In Section 3, we show that there is a linear transformation σ\sigma on 𝔽2n+1\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n+1} such that ({0}×C1,(σ⁡({0}×C2))⟂)(\{0\}\times C_{1},(\sigma(\{0\}\times C_{2}))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes for two binary linear [n,k][n,k] codes C1C_{1} and C2C_{2}. In Section 4, we present a characterization for dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1. In Section 5, we conclude the paper.
2 Preliminaries
Throughout this paper, let OO denote an appropriate zero matrix and let 𝟎{\bf 0} denote an appropriate zero row (resp. column) vector.
A generator matrix of a binary linear [n,k][n,k] code CC is any k×nk\times n matrix GG whose rows form a basis of CC. A parity check matrix of a binary linear [n,k][n,k] code CC is any generator matrix of C⟂C^{\perp}. A binary vector 𝐱=(x1,x2,…,xn){\bf x}=(x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{n}) is even-like if ∑i=1nxi=0\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}=0 and is odd-like otherwise. A binary linear code is said to be even-like if it has only even-like codewords, and is said to be odd-like if it
is not even-like. The Griesmer bound [25] is defined on a binary linear [n,k,d][n,k,d] code CC as

where ⌈a⌉\lceil a\rceil is the least integer greater than or equal to the real number aa.
A binary linear [n,k,d][n,k,d] code CC is said to be a Griesmer code if nn meets the Griesmer bound, i.e.i.e., n=g⁡(k,d)n=g(k,d). Assume that SkS_{k} is a matrix whose columns are all the nonzero vectors in 𝔽2k\mathbb{F}_{2}^{k}.
It is well-known that SkS_{k} generates a binary simplex code, which is a one-weight [2k−1,k,2k−1][2^{k}-1,k,2^{k-1}] Griesmer code for k≥3k\geq 3 (see [25]).

which generates the first order Reed-Muller code ℛ⁡(1,k)\mathcal{R}(1,k) (see [25]).
It can be checked that ℛ⁡(1,k)\mathcal{R}(1,k) is a binary linear [2k,k+1,2k−1][2^{k},k+1,2^{k-1}] code containing the all-one vector 𝟏{\bf 1}.
A permutation π\pi is an arrangement of nn elements without repetition, and the corresponding permutation matrix is denoted by PπP_{\pi}.
The permutation group PAut(𝔽2n)(\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}) of 𝔽2n\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n} is the set consisting of all permutations. For i=1,2i=1,2, let CiC_{i} be a linear [n,ki]q[n,k_{i}]_{q} code with generator matrix GiG_{i} and parity check matrix HiH_{i}. If C1C_{1} and C2C_{2} form an ℓ\ell-intersection pair, then

Conjecture 4.5.
[15, Conjecture 10]
Let n>k≥1n>k\geq 1. Then, there exists a unique binary optimal [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)] code CC which is even-like and contains 𝟏{\bf 1} if and only if
dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1.d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1.
In fact, we see in the subsequent proof that dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1 if and only if every binary optimal [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)] code is even-like and contains 𝟏{\bf 1}.
Theorem 4.6.
Let n>k≥1n>k\geq 1. Then, dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1 if and only if every binary optimal [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)] code is even-like and contains 𝟏{\bf 1}.
Proof.
(⟸)(\Longleftarrow) Suppose that dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k), then there exists a binary [n,k][n,k] LCP of codes (C,D)(C,D) with security parameter dL​(n,k)d_{L}(n,k). Hence C∩D={𝟎}C\cap D=\{{\bf 0}\}, CC and D⟂D^{\perp} have parameters [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)].
By hypothesis, every binary optimal [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)] code is even-like and contains 𝟏{\bf 1}. This implies that both CC and D⟂D^{\perp} are even-like and contain 𝟏{\bf 1}. Since D⟂D^{\perp} is even-like, we have 𝟏∈D{\bf 1}\in D. Then 𝟏∈C∩D{\bf 1}\in C\cap D. This contradicts the condition that C∩D={𝟎}C\cap D=\{{\bf 0}\}. Hence, dL​C​P​(n,k)≤dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)\leq d_{L}(n,k)-1. On the other hand, by [13, Corollary 9], we have dL​C​P​(n,k)≥dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)\geq d_{L}(n,k)-1. Therefore, dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1.
(⟹)(\Longrightarrow) Suppose that dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1. If there exists a binary linear [n,k,dL​(n,k)][n,k,d_{L}(n,k)] code CC such that CC is odd-like or does not contain 𝟏{\bf 1}, then both cases imply that 𝟏∉Hull⁡(C){\bf 1}\notin{\rm Hull}(C). By Lemma 4.3, there exists a mapping σ∈𝐏𝐀𝐮𝐭⁡(𝔽2n)\sigma\in{\rm{\bf PAut}}(\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}) such that (C,(σ⁡(C))⟂)(C,(\sigma(C))^{\perp}) is an LCP of codes. This implies that dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k), which contradicts the condition that dL​C​P​(n,k)=dL​(n,k)−1d_{LCP}(n,k)=d_{L}(n,k)-1.
∎
Corollary 4.7.
If there exists a binary linear [n,k,d][n,k,d] code CC with 𝟏∉Hull⁡(C){\bf 1}\notin{\rm Hull}(C), then there exists a binary [n,k][n,k] LCP of codes with security parameter dd.

By Lemma 4.3, there exists a mapping σ∈𝐏𝐀𝐮𝐭⁡(𝔽2n)\sigma\in{\rm{\bf PAut}}(\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}) such that (C,(σ⁡(C))⟂)(C,(\sigma(C))^{\perp}) is a binary [n,k][n,k] LCP of codes. Together with the fact that d⁡(σ⁡(C))=d⁡(C)d(\sigma(C))=d(C), the result holds.
∎
4.2 Binary LCPs of codes from Solomon-Stiffler codes
Very recently, Gu¨{\rm\ddot{u}}neri [20] constructed an infinite family of optimal binary LCPs of codes from Solomon-Stiffler codes. Here, we construct an infinite family of optimal binary LCPs of codes from Solomon-Stiffler codes, whose parameters cover those constructed by Gu¨{\rm\ddot{u}}neri [20]. We first recall the concept of Solomon-Stiffler codes.
Assume that SkS_{k} is a k×(2k−1)k\times(2^{k}-1) matrix whose columns are made up of all nonzero vectors of 𝔽2k\mathbb{F}_{2}^{k}.

Conjecture 4.11.

There exists a linear ℓ\ell-intersection pair of [n,k1,d1]q[n,k_{1},d_{1}]_{q} and [n,k2,d2]q[n,k_{2},d_{2}]_{q} codes for every ℓ\ell satisfying k1+k2−n≤ℓ≤min⁡{k1,k2}k_{1}+k_{2}-n\leq\ell\leq\min\{k_{1},k_{2}\} provided that there exist [n,k1,d1]q[n,k_{1},d_{1}]_{q} and [n,k2,d2]q[n,k_{2},d_{2}]_{q} codes.

Guenda e​t​a​l.et~al. [19] and Huang e​t​a​l.et~al. In fact, Corollary 4.10 implies that this conjecture does not always hold for the binary case.
5 Conclusion
In this paper, we have pushed further the study of the characterization of binary LCPs of codes. We have characterized the existence of binary LCPs of codes. As a consequence, an open problem proposed by Carlet et al. in [13] has been solved. We have also settled the best parameter problem of binary LCPs of codes. As a result, a conjecture proposed by Choi et al. in [15] has been solved. Further, we have constructed an infinite family of optimal binary LCPs of codes with more general parameters, which cover the parameters constructed by Gu¨{\rm\ddot{u}}neri in [20]. Further, these results also imply that the conjecture proposed by Guenda e​t​a​l.et~al. in [19] does not always hold for the binary case.
