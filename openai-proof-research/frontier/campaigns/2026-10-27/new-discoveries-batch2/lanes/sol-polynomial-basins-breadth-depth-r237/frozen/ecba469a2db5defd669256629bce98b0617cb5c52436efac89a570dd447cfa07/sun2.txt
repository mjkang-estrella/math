                                             Abstract
         In this note we introduce a new class of refined Eulerian polynomials defined by
                                              X
                                 An (p, q) =     podes (π) q edes (π) ,
                                                         π∈Sn

      where odes (π) and edes (π) enumerate the number of descents of permutation π in
      odd and even positions, respectively. We show that the refined Eulerian polynomials
      A2k+1 (p, q), k = 0, 1, 2, . . . , and (1 + q)A2k (p, q), k = 1, 2, . . . , have a nice symmetry
      property.


1    Introduction
Let f (q) = ar q r + · · · + as q s (r ≤ s), with ar 6= 0 and as 6= 0, be a real polynomial. The
polynomial f (q) is palindromic if ar+i = as−i for any i. Following Zeilberger [7], define the
darga of f (q) to be r + s. The set of all palindromic polynomials of darga n is a vector
space [6] with gamma basis
                                          Γn := {q i (1 + q)n−2i | 0 ≤ i ≤ ⌊n/2⌋}.
   Let f (p, q) be a nonzero bivariate polynomial. The polynomial f (p, q) is palindromic of
darga n if it satisfies the following two equations:
                                                     f (p, q) = f (q, p),
                                               f (p, q) = (pq)n f (1/p, 1/q).

                                                             1
See Adin et al. [1] for details. It is known [4] that the set of all palindromic bivariate
polynomials of darga n is a vector space with gamma basis

                   Bn := {(pq)i (p + q)j (1 + pq)n−2i−j | i, j ≥ 0, 2i + j ≤ n}.

   Let Sn denote the set of all permutations of the set [n] := {1, 2, . . . , n}. For a permutation
π = π1 π2 · · · πn ∈ Sn , an index i ∈ [n − 1] is a descent of π if πi > πi+1 , and des (π) denotes
the number of descents of π. The classic Eulerian polynomial is defined as the generating
polynomial for the statistic des over the set Sn , i.e.,
                                                  X
                                        An (q) =      q des (π) .
                                                π∈Sn


Foata and Schützenberger [3] proved that the Eulerian polynomial An (q) can be expressed
in terms of the gamma basis Γn with nonnegative integer coefficients. A polynomial with
nonnegative coefficients under the gamma basis Γn is palindromic and unimodal [5].
    Ehrenborg and Readdy [2] studied the number of ascents in odd position on 0, 1-words.
We define similar statistics on permutations. For a permutation π ∈ Sn , an index i ∈ [n − 1]
is an odd descent of π if πi > πi+1 and i is odd, an even descent of π if πi > πi+1 and i is
even, an odd ascent of π if πi < πi+1 and i is odd, an even ascent of π if πi < πi+1 and i is
even. Let Odes (π), Edes (π), Oasc (π) and Easc (π) denote the set of all odd descents, even
descents, odd ascents and even ascents of π, respectively. The corresponding cardinalities
are odes (π), edes (π), oasc (π) and easc (π), respectively. Note that we can also define the
above four statistics on words of length n. The joint distribution of odd and even descents
on Sn is denoted by An (p, q), i.e.,
                                            X
                                An (p, q) =     podes (π) q edes (π) .
                                             π∈Sn


    The polynomial An (p, q) is a bivariate polynomial of degree n − 1. The monomial with
degree n − 1 is p⌊n/2⌋ q ⌊(n−1)/2⌋ only. If p = q, then An (q, q) = An (q) is the classic Eule-
rian polynomial. Thus An (p, q), n = 1, 2, . . . , can be seen as a class of refined Eulerian
polynomials. For example, we have

           A1 (p, q) = 1,
           A2 (p, q) = 1 + p,
           A3 (p, q) = 1 + 2p + 2q + pq,
           A4 (p, q) = 1 + 6p + 5q + 5p2 + 6pq + p2 q,
           A5 (p, q) = 1 + 13p + 13q + 16p2 + 34pq + 16q 2 + 13p2 q + 13pq 2 + p2 q 2 ,
           A6 (p, q) = 1 + 29p + 28q + 89p2 + 152pq + 61q 2 + 61p3 + 152p2 q
                       + 89pq 2 + 28p3 q + 29p2 q 2 + p3 q 2 .
