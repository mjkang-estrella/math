                                               Abstract
    We extend and generalize an identity of Apostol, involving Bernoulli numbers, to
every sequence of complex numbers. Moreover, our result allows us to obtain other
relations involving Appell polynomial sequences and second-order linear recurrence
sequences.

                       This paper is dedicated in memory of professor
                              Tom M. Apostol (1923 – 2016)

The mapping T is called a binomial transformation [4], and the sequence u∗ is called the
dual sequence of u. It is easy to prove that (u∗ )∗ = u, and if u is linearly recurrent over C,
then u∗ is also linearly recurrent over C. Moreover, if C(x) is a characteristic polynomial
of u, then C(1 − x) is a characteristic polynomial of u∗ . The sequence u is called invariant
(resp., inverse invariant) under the binomial transformation   if u∗ = u (resp., if u∗ = −u).
