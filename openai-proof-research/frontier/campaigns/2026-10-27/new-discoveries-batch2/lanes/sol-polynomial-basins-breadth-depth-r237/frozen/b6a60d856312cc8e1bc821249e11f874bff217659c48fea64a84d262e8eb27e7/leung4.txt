             Another Identity for Complete Bell
             Polynomials Based on Ramanujan’s
                       Congruences

                                                                Abstract
          Let p(n) be the number of partitions of a positive integer n. We derive a new
       identity for complete Bell polynomials based on a generating function of p(7n + 5)
       given by Ramanujan.
