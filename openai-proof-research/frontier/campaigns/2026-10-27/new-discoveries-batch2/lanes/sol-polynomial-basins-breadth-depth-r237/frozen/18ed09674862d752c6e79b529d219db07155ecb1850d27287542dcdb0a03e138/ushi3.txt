                                                       Abstract
        Let cq (n) be the Ramanujan sum and let S(m, n; q) be the Kloosterman sum. We
     study the eigenvalues of q × q matrices whose (m, n) entry is cq (m − n) or S(m, n; q),
     where q is a fixed positive integer. We also study the eigenvalues of matrices whose
     entries are sums of Ramanujan sums or sums of Kloosterman sums.
