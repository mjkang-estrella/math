         We obtain factored closed-form expressions for the sums of cubes of Fibonacci and
      Lucas numbers.


1     Introduction

the recurrence relations Fn = Fn−1 + Fn−2 , F0 = 0, F1 = 1 and Ln = Ln−1 + Ln−2 , L0 = 2,
L1 = 1, with F−n = (−1)n−1 Fn and L−n = (−1)n Ln .
