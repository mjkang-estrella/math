Two sustained proof routes:

1. **Finite block coupling / convolution.** Divide Bin(3n) into independent blocks, optimize a stable approximation for each block and convolve. A fixed block size b cannot establish o(n) unless its PGF Q satisfies log Q(e^t)=(3b)log((1+e^(2t/3))/2) for every t. Indeed a coupling of distance o(n) forces normalized log MGFs to differ by o(1) at each t, from exp(-|t|d)E exp(tA)<=E exp(tB)<=exp(|t|d)E exp(tA). Repeated fixed blocks have a fixed nonzero normalized discrepancy unless those analytic functions coincide. Coincidence is impossible: as z->0 the right PGF ((1+z^(2/3))/2)^(3b) has a nonzero z^(2/3) term, whereas Q is an ordinary polynomial. Thus fixed-block iteration, including a good small-n coupling, is genuinely blocked. The source's binomial and uniform-parameter attempts are specific failures of the same all-tilt mismatch. Allowing b to grow would require the original missing construction, not solve it by recursion.

2. **Continuum odds spectrum / tilted tails.** Construct a nonuniform empirical odds measure whose continuum Stieltjes transform is z^(2/3)/(1+z^(2/3)). Integrate to match the full limiting log MGF. Midpoint odds quantiles give explicit2n parameters. Exponential tilting proves both strict left/right tail exponents; a compactness argument comparing extreme quantiles then proves W_infinity/n->0.
