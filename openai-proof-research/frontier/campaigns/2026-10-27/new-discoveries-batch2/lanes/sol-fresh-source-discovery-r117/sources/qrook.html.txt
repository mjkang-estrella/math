Morales Note: corresponding author Affiliation: LACIM, Département de Mathématiques, Université du Québec à Montréal ahmorales.math@gmail.com September 2025 Abstract We show by an explicit example that the Garsia–Remmel q q -rook numbers of Ferrers boards do not all have unimodal sequences of coefficients. This resolves in the negative a question from 1986 by the aforementioned authors. Dedicated to the memory of Adriano Garsia. keywords: q q -rook numbers, rook placements, Ferrers boards, unimodality, counterexamples, q-Stirling numbers The central objects in rook theory are the placements of (nonattacking) rooks on a board . Formally, a board B B is a finite subset of ℤ > 0 × ℤ > 0 \mathbb{Z}_{>0}\times\mathbb{Z}_{>0} , and a rook placement on B B is a subset w w of B B such that no two elements of w w agree on either coordinate. Analogously to the case q = 1 q=1 , one says two partitions are q q -rook equivalent if they have the same q q -rook numbers. Surprisingly, two partitions are rook equivalent if and only if they are q q -rook equivalent [ 10 , p. 257] . In the particular case λ = δ n \lambda=\delta_{n} , the polynomial R k ​ ( λ , q ) R_{k}(\lambda;q) is a q q -Stirling number of the second kind [ 10 , Eq. I.9] ; for more on these numbers, see [ 22 , 31 ] and Remarks 8 and 10 below. The same polynomials R k ​ ( λ , q ) R_{k}(\lambda;q) also arise in enumerative linear algebra, when counting matrices of rank k k over a finite field whose nonzero entries all belong to B λ B_{\lambda} [ 18 , Thm. 1] ; this connection has led to applications in coding theory, since up to a power of q q , the R k ​ ( λ , q ) R_{k}(\lambda;q) give the weights of certain rank-metric codes (e.g., see [ 25 , 11 , 15 ] ). The q q -rook numbers and the related q q -hit numbers have also recently appeared in the expansions of chromatic symmetric functions of graphs corresponding to certain Dyck paths called abelian [ 1 , 7 , 23 , 16 ] . Based on an assortment of empirical and circumstantial evidence, Garsia and Remmel conjectured [ 10 , p. 250] that the polynomials R k ​ ( λ , q ) R_{k}(\lambda;q) always have unimodal sequence of coefficients , i.e., that if R k ​ ( λ , q ) = a N ​ q N + a N − 1 ​ q N − 1 + … + a 1 ​ q + a 0 R_{k}(\lambda;q)=a_{N}q^{N}+a_{N-1}q^{N-1}+\ldots+a_{1}q+a_{0} then for some m m it holds that a N ≤ a N − 1 ≤ ⋯ ≤ a m + 1 ≤ a m ≥ a m − 1 ≥ ⋯ ≥ a 1 ≥ a 0 a_{N}\leq a_{N-1}\leq\cdots\leq a_{m+1}\leq a_{m}\geq a_{m-1}\geq\cdots\geq a_{1}\geq a_{0} .
