. This NP-complete problem is one of the recent topic of research which has wide applications in navigation of autonomous robots [
13
]. Butterfly network is one of the most significant and widely researched interconnection architecture. Previously, we have studied the general position problem of butterfly network [
22
]. In this article, we solve the general position problem of butterfly derived architectures such as augmented butterfly network, enhanced butterfly network and compute their gp-number.

Introduction
We refer [
2
] for the definitions and terminologies. A path of shortest length 
d
(
u
, 
v
) for 
u
, 
v
 ∈ 
V
(
G
) is known as a 
geodesic
. If 
d
H
(
u
,

d
G
(

V
(

isometric subgraph
 in
 G
. If every block in

 is complete, then

 is called a 
block graph. x
 is a 
simplicial vertex
 in 
G
, if the neighbors of 
x
 form a complete subgraph in 
G
. 
 A general position set 
S
 of 
G
 is a set in which no three vertices lie on a shortest path in 
G
. A gp-
set
 is a largest general position set and the cardinality of such a set is called the gp-
number,
 denoted by gp(
G
). The general position problem is to obtain its gp-set of 
G
, this NP-complete problem was introduced in [
13
] due to the motivation of the no-three-in-line [
3
, 
11
, 
17
, 
21
] and general position subset selection problems [
4
, 
20
].
 Butterfly network is an important interconnection network [
12
] possessing attractive topologies of interconnection networks but it has some weaknesses too. Its toughness is less than one, it is non-pancyclic and non-hamiltonian. But most of the desirable properties in the butterfly network are preserved by the augmented butterfly network [
15
]. Paul Manuel et al. in [
14
], has determined the gp-number for Beněs networks using isometric path cover and left the general position problem of butterfly networks as an open problem. In [
22
] Prabha et al. has solved the above open problem by using dual min-max invariant problem. In this article, we compute the gp-number of augmented and enhanced butterfly networks. 
2 
Literature Survey
After its introduction, the general position problem has drawn the attention of several researchers and has been explored extensively. An equivalent concept of the above problem named geodesic irredundant number was introduced and studied in [
26
]. In [
1
], the gp-number is deduced for the complement of a bipartite graph, grids, trees and hypercubes. Ghorbani et al. in [
5
] determined the gp-number for some families of Kneser graphs, coronas of graphs, joins of graphs and line graphs of complete graphs. In [
19
], the result of kneser graphs obtained in [
5
] were improved and the best possible bound is determined. In [
6
], 
k
-steiner general position set is introduced and studied. In [
7
], the general position problem is solved for cartesian graph powers. In [
9
], the problem is studied for integer lattices. In [
10
], results that connect the above problem with the strong resolving graphs are established. Also the general position problem is studied for strong products, generalized lexicographic product, different rooted product graphs. In [
14
], Paul Manuel et al. has determined the gp-number for a large class of subgraphs of the infinite diagonal grid and infinite grid graph. In [
18
], the general position problem is investigated on complementary prisms. The general 3-position is introduced as a main component for characterization of the gp-number for an extremal case of complementary prisms. In [
8
], general d-position set is introduced as an extension of general position sets. Also the relation between general 
d
-position sets with the dissociation number and strong resolving graphs is demonstrated. In [
23
], the classes of all graph with gp(
G
) = 
n
-2 is characterized. In [
24
], a lower bound on the gp(
G
) for Cartesian products of graphs is proved. The general position problem is solved for the Cartesian products of a complete graph with a path and complete multipartite graph with a path. In [
25
], the gp-number is investigated on the Cartesian product of two trees.

5 
Conclusion
General position problem is one of the recent problems which has gained wide attention from researchers and is applied in the field of robotics mainly in the navigation of autonomous robots. Augmented and enhanced butterfly networks are important interconnection networks derived from butterfly network. In this article, we have computed the gp-number of augmented butterfly and enhanced butterfly networks. Further, the general position problem of several networks such as wrapped butterfly, cube connected cycles are under study.
