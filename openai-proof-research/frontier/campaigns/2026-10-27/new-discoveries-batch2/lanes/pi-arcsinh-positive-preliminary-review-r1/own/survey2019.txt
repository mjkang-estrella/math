Abstract. The generalized trigonometric functions which have a short history, were introduced
by Lindqvist two decades ago. Since 2012, many mathematician began to study their classical
inequalities, general convexity and concavity, multiple-angle formulas and parameter convexity
and concavity. A number of results have been obtained. This is a survey. Some new refinements,
generalizations, applications, and related problems are summarized.

useful tool. Because of practical constraints, we have to exclude many fine papers and
