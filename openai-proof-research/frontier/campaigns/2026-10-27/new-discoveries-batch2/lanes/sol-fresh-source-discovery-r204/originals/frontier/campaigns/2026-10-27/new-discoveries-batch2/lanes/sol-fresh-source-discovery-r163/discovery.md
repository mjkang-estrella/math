## Mathematics and disposition

The full Širola conjecture follows from the standard one-segment Newton
polygon argument. This conclusion rests on a complete bridge, not shortness.
For the first kind negate ω; for the second retain it. Both then obey the min
valuation rule, with integer values, and the coefficient assumptions become
n v(cj)≥(n−j)v(c0). For slope λ=v(c0)/n, the smallest and largest indices
attaining min(v(pi)+iλ) add under products. At each extremal index a unique
lowest-valued term prevents cancellation. Since f has both endpoints minimal,
any factor has its own constant and leading coefficients minimal. A degree-s
factor must therefore satisfy n|s v(c0); coprimality forces n|s, a contradiction.
This proves the entire conjecture, including arbitrary non-UFD domains.
