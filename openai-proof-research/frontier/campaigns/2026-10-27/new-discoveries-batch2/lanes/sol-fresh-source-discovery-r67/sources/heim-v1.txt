                                                  series. Andrews also discovered an elementary proof. Sun conjectured that this happens in
                                                  a certain way for n ≥ 6:            p          p

                                                  dently obtained a second proof. In this paper, we follow an approach by Rota. We consider
                                                  p(n) as special values of the D’Arcais polynomials, known as the Nekrasov–Okounkov poly-
                                                  nomials. This identifies Sun’s conjecture as a property of the largest real zero of certain

                                                  purpose of this paper is to offer a uniform approach.
