Polynomization of Sun’s conjecture

Bernhard Heim1,2 · Markus Neuhauser3,4

Abstract
Let p(n) denote the number of partitions of a natural number n. As n → ∞, the nth
root of p(n) tends to 1, which is related to the Cauchy–Hadamard test for power series.
Andrews also discovered an elementary proof. Sun conjectured that this happens in a
certain way for n ≥ 6:                        
                                 n
                                   p(n) > n+1 p(n + 1).
The conjecture was proved by Wang and Zhu; shortly thereafter, Chen and Zheng inde-
pendently obtained a second proof. In this paper, we follow an approach by Rota. We
regard p(n) as special values of the D’Arcais polynomials, known as the Nekrasov–
Okounkov polynomials. This identifies Sun’s conjecture as a property of the largest
real zero of certain polynomials. This leads to results toward k-colored partitions, over-
partitions, and plane partitions. Moreover, we also consider Chebyshev and Laguerre
polynomials. The main purpose of this paper is to offer a uniform approach.
