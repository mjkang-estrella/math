Mechanism A: for a fixed s-target S, J_t=|H_t∩S| is birth–death, with upward probability a_j=(r−j)(s−j)/[r(n−r)] and downward b_j=j(n−r−s+j)/[r(n−r)]. Its stationary mass π_j=binom(s,j)binom(n−s,r−j)/binom(n,r). Writing h_j for expected time to J=s and d_j=h_j−h_(j+1), first-step recursion gives a_j d_j−b_j d_(j−1)=1. Detailed balance telescopes to π_j a_j d_j=Σ_(i≤j)π_i, so h_0=Σ_(j<s)(Σ_(i≤j)π_i)/(π_j a_j). The final term is (1−p)/q and all earlier terms are O(n^(s−1)), yielding h_j~1/q for every j<s. This extends the source's r=s scalar recurrence but does not prove cover time: Matthews' theorem for vertex cover cannot simply be applied to overlapping target SETS. A disjoint-target reduction has only order n targets and loses the factor s in log binom(n,s). This mechanism was sustained to its precise obstruction, not mistaken for the full theorem.
