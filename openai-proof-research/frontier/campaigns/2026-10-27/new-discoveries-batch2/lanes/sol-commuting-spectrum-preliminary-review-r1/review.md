## MATH gate: PASS — complete independent audit

Let A,B be arbitrary finite abelian groups and b:A×A→B be biadditive and alternating. Expanding b(a+c,a+c)=0 gives b(c,a)=−b(a,c), without dividing by two. Choose a cyclic direct sum A=⊕ C_{n_i}, with generators e_i. Set c_ij=b(e_i,e_j) for i<j. Both n_i c_ij=0 and n_j c_ij=0 follow by biadditivity. Define

    f(a,c)=Σ_{i<j} a_i c_j c_ij.

Changing a_i by n_i or c_j by n_j changes every affected summand by zero. Thus f is well defined on the cyclic factors and is biadditive. Expanding b in coordinates, its diagonal terms vanish and its lower-triangular terms are the negatives of its upper-triangular terms. Therefore f(a,c)−f(c,a)=b(a,c), including characteristic two, where negation may equal the identity. No assumption on the exponent of B is needed.

On R=A⊕B define (a,u)(c,v)=(0,f(a,c)). Both distributive laws follow from biadditivity. Products lie in 0⊕B, which annihilates R on both sides, so (xy)z=x(yz)=0 for every triple; R is associative. The additive group is abelian and [x,y]_R=(0,b(a,c)). Every pair (a,c) has exactly |B|² lifts. Hence

    Pr_c(R) = #{(a,c) in A² : b(a,c)=0}/|A|².

This works for zero groups, empty cyclic decompositions, cyclic groups, zero b, and extraneous B components. Such a ring usually has no identity; the sources permit that. No unital conclusion is asserted.

For any finite G with [G,G]≤Z(G), take A=G/Z(G), B=Z(G), using addition for their abelian laws. The map b(gZ,hZ)=g⁻¹h⁻¹gh is independent of lifts: multiplying either lift by a central element cancels it in the commutator. Central commutators give [gg',h]=[g,h][g',h] and [g,hh']=[g,h][g,h']; thus b is biadditive and alternating. Its zero set is exactly the commuting coset pairs. Every coset has |Z(G)| elements, so Pr_c(G) is the same displayed quotient and equals Pr_c(R). Here |R|=|G/Z(G)||Z(G)|=|G|, though order preservation is unnecessary for spectrum equality. Abelian and trivial G give probability one. This proves the entire previously missing inclusion, for every finite class-at-most-two G.

The classification-free variant is also valid. Put N=|A|, enumerate every a_i in A, let F=(Z/N)^{|A|}, and let p(x)=Σ x_i a_i. Lagrange gives Na_i=0, so p is well defined and surjective. N need only annihilate b(A,A), since N b(a,c)=b(Na,c)=0; it need not annihilate B. Define f(x,y)=Σ_{i<j}x_i y_j b(a_i,a_j). This is well defined and f(x,y)−f(y,x)=b(p(x),p(y)). On F⊕B use the preceding product. Every p-fiber is a coset of ker p and has |ker p| elements; every pair in A² has |ker p|²|B|² lifts. Since |F|=|A||ker p|, division by |F⊕B|² gives exactly the same probability. If A is trivial, N=1 and F is trivial despite having one coordinate; all formulas still hold. B trivial is also harmless.

For the reverse inclusion, take an arbitrary finite possibly nonassociative distributive ring S. Set T=S⊕S, (a,u)(c,v)=(0,ac). Distributivity in S suffices; every triple product in T vanishes, so T is associative. Define x∘y=x+y+xy. Expanding either association gives x+y+z+xy+xz+yz, since the triple terms vanish. Zero is the identity. The two-sided inverse is −x+x², as x x²=x²x=0. All products and x² lie in the annihilating second summand. Expanding the commutator with the convention x⁻¹∘y⁻¹∘x∘y gives xy−yx; this is central under ∘, so the group has class at most two. Its two elements commute precisely when their first coordinates commute in S. There are |S|² second-coordinate lifts of each first-coordinate pair, hence Pr_c(T,∘)=Pr_c(S). This proves the reverse inclusion independently and matches the already printed Juráš–Ursul bridges. Together the two inclusions prove the literal target, even for possibly nonassociative rings.

The discarded alternating product b itself has commutator 2b. For A=C2², B=C2 with nonzero determinant b, that ring is commutative, although the b-zero probability is 5/8. This failure is real and retained; it does not affect triangular f. No assertion about higher-class nilpotent groups follows.

## SUBSTANTIVE gate: FAIL; recommendation EXCLUDE

The strongest objection survives independent audit. The full bridge is: (i) the source already constructs the alternating commutator bimap on G/Z(G) with central output and proves the exact fiber-counting identity; (ii) choose cyclic generators, put the strictly upper-triangular entries of that bimap into f, and use annihilation by the two cyclic orders to descend it; (iii) put its outputs into a square-zero summand, so associativity is automatic and its commutator is b; (iv) reuse the source's reverse inclusion. The preceding section gives every step rather than invoking an unnamed correspondence.

The only missing mathematical idea is taking the upper-triangular part of an alternating biadditive form. The characteristic-two issue is removed by that standard elementary polarization, rather than by a new structural theorem, classification, invariant, or exceptional construction. The finite-free-cover variant is the same mechanism on redundant generators and supplies no independent substantive contribution. Buckley 2015 further confirms that probability invariance and zero-triple-product realizations are established machinery; it does not need to have printed this endpoint for the campaign's exclusion of routine consequences to apply.

This is a substantive-credit judgment, not a proof of bibliographic priority. I found no inspected earlier body explicitly asserting the full even-order endpoint. An authentic published open question can still have a short routine resolution under this campaign's stricter rule. I therefore recommend preserving the proof uncounted and stopping formal/manuscript investment for discovery credit. Root alone adjudicates this recommendation.

## FORMAL gate: feasible, NOT IMPLEMENTED

The free-cover proof avoids the finite-abelian decomposition theorem at the cost of a larger finite type. A literal endpoint must quantify over arbitrary finite class-two groups and produce a finite nonunital associative ring with equality of rational pair counts, plus cover arbitrary nonassociative source rings for the reverse implication. Required bridges are quotient commutator biadditivity, finite ZMod coordinates, triangular sums, equal-size fibers, the explicit ring structure, and the adjoint group/inverse calculation. None requires division by two. No residual mathematical conjecture remains, but library availability and implementation effort have not been verified. No Lean file, axiom audit, certificate, replay, or formal PASS is claimed.

`check.py` is independently written finite diagnostics, not a universal proof or the scout's checker. `checks.json` records exhaustive admissible triangular coefficient arrays for eight small cyclic-product settings (including mixed orders and trivial A), a 256-element free cover of C2² with all fibers size 64 and exact probability 5/8, and the full associativity/inverse/central-commutator/probability check on T of size 16 built from an explicitly nonassociative four-element ring. All pass. Universal justification is the algebra above.
