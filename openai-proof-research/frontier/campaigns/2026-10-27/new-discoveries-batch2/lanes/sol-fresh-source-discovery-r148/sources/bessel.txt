                                                     We conjecture a quadratic critical-value evaluation of 0 K0 (t)5 dt for a weight-three,
                                                                                                                                     √
                                                 level-60 newform. We prove that its twisted symmetric-square value at 2 equals 15π 2
                                                 times its Petersson norm, and give exact norm and coefficient-twist identities. These
                                                 modular theorems identify the companion norm formula with Lim–Tu–Yu’s conjecture; they
                                                 do not prove the Bessel comparison. Chuang’s period formulas relate the even and odd
                                                 determinants. Directed intervals bound the individual-period discrepancy by 10−358 . Full
                                                 proofs and reproducible certificates accompany the note.
