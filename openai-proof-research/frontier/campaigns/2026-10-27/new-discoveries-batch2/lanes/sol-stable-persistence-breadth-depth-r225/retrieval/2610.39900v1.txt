                                           Abstract
                                           We provide a sharp estimate for the asymptotic number of lattice zonotopes
                                           inscribed in [0, n]d as n tends to infinity. Our estimate refines the logarithmic
                                           equivalent established by Bárány, Bureaux, and Lund when the sum of the
                                           generators of the zonotope is prescribed. As we shall see, the exponential part
                                           of our estimate is a polynomial of degree d in n1/(d+1) , plus a term involving
                                           the non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Our analysis is based
                                           on a correspondence between power sums over primitive vectors (vectors
                                           with coprime coordinates) and Eulerian polynomials. We also analyse some
                                           combinatorial properties of lattice zonotopes. In particular, we compute the
                                           asymptotic mean and variance of the diameter of the graph of a random
                                           lattice zonotope inscribed in [0, n]d as n goes to infinity, and show that this
                                           diameter satisfies a central limit theorem.
                                           Keywords: zonotopes, lattice polytopes, asymptotic enumeration,
                                           saddle-point method, Mellin transform, Eulerian polynomials, Riemann
                                           zeta function, graph diameter

1.1. Moments of the diameter of lattice zonotopes
    Once the combinatorial description of zonotopes and Theorem 1.1 have
been established, one can compute moments of combinatorial parameters of
zonotopes. In this paper, we are interested in two parameters: the diameter
of the graph of a zonotope and the number of occurrences of a given generator
in a random zonotope.
    The diameter (of the graph) of a polytope of a given size is a key com-
binatorial parameter about which little is known. Besides its own scientific
interest (see for instance the Hirsch conjecture), it is connected to the com-
plexity of the simplex algorithm, which makes upper bounds on this quan-
tity highly sought after (see [11] for more references). In this perspective,
the largest possible diameter of a lattice zonotope contained in [0, n]d is a
natural quantity to consider.
   We compute asymptotic estimates of the mean and the variance of the
diameter of a random lattice zonotope inscribed in [0, n]d .

behavior of our result when n and d grow large.

7. Estimates of the moments of some parameters
    In this section, we establish the asymptotic behavior of the first two
moments of two parameters of random lattice zonotopes. To do so, we add a
variable u (a marking variable, in the terminology of the symbolic method)
which counts the parameter, and we study the coefficients of the resulting
generating function for u = eη , with η real and close to 0. This gives the
moment generating function of the parameter, conditionally on the bounding
box, and hence its moments.
    We begin with a result similar to [4, Lemma 4.3], which gives the mean
number of generators of a zonotope contained in a given cone and ending at
a given point, under the Boltzmann distribution. In the case of zonotopes
inscribed in a hypercube, we compute the mean and the variance of the
number of generators under the uniform distribution. The diameter of the
graph of a zonotope is equal to its number of pairwise non-collinear generators
[25], that is, to the size of the support of the associated function ω. This
gives Theorem 1.4.
