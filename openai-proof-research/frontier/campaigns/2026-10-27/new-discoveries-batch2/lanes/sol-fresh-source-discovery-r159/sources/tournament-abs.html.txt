 Title: Random Tournaments Are Asymptotically Almost Surely Link-irregular 
 Authors: Lily Zhang View a PDF of the paper titled Random Tournaments Are Asymptotically Almost Surely Link-irregular, by Lily Zhang 
 View PDF 
 HTML (experimental) 



 
 Abstract: Bastien and Khormali (2025) conjectured that a nontrivial link-irregular tournament with $n$ vertices exists if and only if $n \ge 6$. Their work establishes the conjecture for $n \le 100$. We present a probabilistic argument for the existence of link-irregular tournaments with $n$ vertices when $n \ge 9$ to resolve the conjecture. In fact, we show that a random tournament is asymptotically almost surely link-irregular.
