                                                 A NOTE ON CONSEQUENCES OF MAHLER’S INEQUALITIES

                                                                                 YANLU LIAN AND FEI XUE*


                                                   Abstract. We derive Makai’s inequality voln (K) voln ((K − K)◦ ) ≥ (n + 1)/n! in every
                                                   dimension from the symmetric Mahler theorem in one higher dimension, with equality
                                                   precisely for simplices. The proof uses a classical two-layer construction, an identity for its
                                                   polar volume, and a Rogers–Shephard volume estimate. We then record basic consequences
                                                   of the Mahler and Makai inequalities for polar successive minima, successive minima at




arXiv:2610.34396v1 [math.MG] 25 Oct 2026
                                                   the Santaló point, critical determinants, first covering minima, and non-separable lattice
                                                   arrangements. We also combine Alexandrov–Fenchel with the volume-product inequalities
                                                   to obtain sharp bounds for paired mixed volumes.
