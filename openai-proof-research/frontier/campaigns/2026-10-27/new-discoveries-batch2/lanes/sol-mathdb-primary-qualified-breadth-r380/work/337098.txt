9 (or [11], Conjecture 7), which provides a broad family of possible further identities, and is
still wide open to this day. It would be interesting to investigate whether our combinatorial
approach via the KOH theorem, which appears to be new in this context, might also help
with the Reiner-Stanton conjecture.
