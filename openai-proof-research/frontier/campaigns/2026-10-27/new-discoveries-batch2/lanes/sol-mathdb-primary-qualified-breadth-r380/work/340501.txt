                                           Abstract
                                           We give a counterexample to a conjecture of Wang and Hou related with the
                                           sum of the k largest Laplacian eigenvalues of signed graphs.
                                           Keywords: signed graph, sum of the eigenvalues, Laplacian matrix
                                           2010 MSC: 05C50


                                           1. Introduction
                                               Let G be a simple graph. A signed graph Gσ is obtained from G by
                                           a sign function σ : E(G) → {+1, −1}. The adjacency matrix A(Gσ ) is a
                                           (0, ±1)-matrix where

                                           For a positive integer n, we denote by [n], the set {1, . . . , n}. The Laplacian
                                           matrix of a simple graph G, denoted by LG , is D(G) − A(G), where D(G)
                                           is a diagonal matrix of the degree sequence of G and A(G) is the adjacency
                                           matrix of G. Similar to the LG , the Laplacian matrix of a signed graph Gσ is
                                           defined as D(G) − A(Gσ ) and is denoted by LGσ . The following well-known
                                           conjecture states an upper bounds for the sum of the largest eigenvalues of
                                           LG .
