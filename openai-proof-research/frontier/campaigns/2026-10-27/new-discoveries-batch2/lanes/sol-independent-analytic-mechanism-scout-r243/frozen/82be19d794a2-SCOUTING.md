## Ranked next decisions

1. **S linearized family**: attacked in ATTACK.md. Exact reduction to a primitive point in a specified F_p-subspace; generic character bound quantitatively fails. Next: actual specialized primitive-subspace theorem proof or sharper sum on this subspace. Objection: sparse-subspace primitive existence can be hard, and n=2 support is only60 instances.
2. **W homogeneous coloring**: each cubic vertex selects a pair of its neighbors required to be equal. Form the equivalence closure of all selected pairs. It yields an admissible coloring iff no original edge collapses and, at every vertex, the third neighbor stays outside the pair's equivalence class. This is an exact existential reformulation, not a proof of four-colorability or finite exceptions. To get≤4 one must color the quotient compatibly without merging the two neighborhood classes. A triangle forces its external neighbor to match one of the other two triangle colors; a diamond forces its tips equal. Finite boundary signatures suggest a two-edge-sum obstruction search. They do not prove that a noncolorable two-pole extends to infinitely many bridgeless examples. Read original2017 theorem before sustained gadget work.
3. **W non-nested matching**: actual current Turán paper retains an additive binomial gap. Its long/short-edge counting proof and Ramsey consequence are concrete mechanisms; decisive next step is strengthening the short-edge bound without creating a forbidden ordered matching. No new invariant established, so reserve.
4. **W strong coloring of cycle unions**: independent-transversal/recoloring mechanism; decisive prerequisite is original Haxell2004 proof read, unavailable in this bounded pass. Five colors on K5 blocks does not close four on K4. Reserve rather than sustained investment without proof-body comparison.
5. **W almost-group-connectivity**: boundary-flow elimination exploiting bicriticality, preserving nonzero labels. Need the actual Jaeger–Linial–Payan–Tarsi group-connectivity proof and a reducible boundary configuration; neither is supplied by finite checks. Low-priority reserve.
