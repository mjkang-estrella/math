## Sustained construction and exact proof

Time-stationary survival S(t)=pi exp((Q-D)t)1 and event-stationary survival
S_event(t)=alpha exp((Q-D)t)1 satisfy S'(t)=-lambda* S_event(t), since
pi(Q-D)=-pi D. Thus the conjecture demands S'(t)+lambda*S(t)>=0.
This suggests testing a nonreversible phase cycle: surviving phases can move
into a high-intensity region after spending time in a low-intensity region.
Dense random matrices rapidly mix and obscure this mechanism. A directed
cycle with triangular intensities yielded a strict crossing. This is a
construction heuristic, not part of the proof or a theorem about all cycles.

The literal witness has Q_(i,i+1)=1,Q_(i,i)=-1 modulo12 and D diagonal
(9,8,7,6,5,4,3,4,5,6,7,8)/60. pi=1/12, alpha=a/72. PROOF.md supplies the
complete source-law bridge, positive substochastic uniformization and explicit
integer recurrence certificate. An order80 uniformization sum is less than-2
before its positive exp(-276/25) factor; its absolute remainder is less than.01.
