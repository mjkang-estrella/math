# T197O: all odd coefficient characteristics

Status: full unchanged frozen target proved by a characteristic audit of a pinned companion construction. Author-side classification: TECHNICAL extension; **NO SUBSTANTIAL NOMINEE**. No independent review or campaign acceptance is claimed.

For every odd prime p there are r≥1, a finitely generated group G, and a,b∈F_(p^r)[G] such that ab=1 and ba≠1. The proof actually retains finite presentation, but that is not a replacement target or a separate contribution.

The principal dependency is the September 23, 2026 pinned manuscript *A Counterexample to Kaplansky's Direct Finiteness Conjecture in Characteristic Two*, at OpenAI/math commit fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb. Its entire sections 01–05, including 02a, were read. We do not infer an odd-characteristic theorem from its stated characteristic-two theorem. We check its construction and proof over an arbitrary fixed odd characteristic. Every operation below except the field parameters, rounded degree, and sign bookkeeping is already present there. This text is original explanatory prose; third-party copies remain private.

## 1. Parameters and the geometric input

Fix an odd prime p. Let q=p^h tend to infinity with h. Choose the least prime ell≡−1 (mod q^100), and put D=ell+1, s=floor(log_q D), b=s+20, n=10b, N=q^n. The coprime progression has such a prime with ell≤C q^(100L), for absolute C,L; this is the usual Linnik bound. The primary statement in Xylouris, *Acta Arithmetica* 150 (2011), Theorem 1.1, even gives L=5.18. Only existence of fixed C,L is used. Thus 100≤s≤S for a fixed S independent of h; n is bounded. Also q divides D, ell is odd and distinct from p, and ell²=1 as a scalar in characteristic p. Let Q=F_q and d=floor((q−2)/2)=(q−3)/2. For q≥9, q/3≤d<q and 2d≤q−2. For any enumeration a_0,...,a_(q−1) of Q, set

S_d={(a_i1,...,a_in): i_1+...+i_n≤d}⊂Q^n.

We prove that for sufficiently large h there are holes O⊂S_d, with 2N/D²≤|O|≤7N/D², and full affine Q-lines F in V=Q^n\O, such that each point of V lies on exactly D lines and the incidence graph has girth at least 12. The following construction is precisely the pinned companion's geometric construction, with q and d as above.

Partition D indices into D/q bundles. In Q^n choose two-dimensional spaces W_r, one per bundle, and (b−1)-dimensional spaces E_j, one per index, so any ten distinct spaces have direct sum. For existence identify Q^n with (Q')^10 where [Q':Q]=b, assign distinct parameters ξ∈Q' and use the Q'-lines generated by (1,ξ,...,ξ^9). The Vandermonde determinant proves ten-wise independence. There are enough parameters because D+D/q<q^(s+2)≤q^b. Inside each W_r choose one direction e_r and assign its other q directions as d_j to the bundle indices. Put U_j=d_j⊕E_j. Then distinct U_j have zero intersection, U_j∩e_r=0, and U_j∩W_r=d_j if r is j's bundle and zero otherwise.

Write M=N/D². The simplex has binom(d+n,n)≥q^n/(3^n n!)≥c_S N points, with fixed c_S>0. Select its points independently with probability θ=5M/|S_d|=O(D^−2). The selected size lies between 4M and 6M with probability 1−o(1), since its variance is at most 5M and M>q^(8s+198)→∞. With probability 1−o(1), every C+W_r, where C is a U_j-coset, contains at most 100 selected points: the union bound is at most

D²N(θq^(b+2))^100=O(q^(2402−88s))=o(1).

Delete selected points involved either in a pair in a common W_r-coset, or in four distinct witnesses of an exceptional rectangle. Here an exceptional rectangle has alternating directions e_r,e_r' with r≠r'; a witness for a side is a point in its W_r-plane but off the side. There are at most N(D/q)q² collision pairs before selection, and at most ND² rectangles with at most q^8 witness tuples each. The expected number of marked selected points divided by M is

O(q/D+q^8/D^4)=o(1).

Consequently some outcome leaves between 3M and 6M holes after removing at most M points, and has these three properties: each W_r-coset has at most one hole; each C+W_r has at most 100 holes; no exceptional rectangle has four distinct hole witnesses. These imply the looser required bounds 2M≤|O|≤7M.

For each hole o and bundle r, designate as replacement lines all q−1 lines parallel to e_r in o+W_r except o+e_r. Such lines miss O and are disjoint within a replacement label r. An exceptional rectangle of replacement lines would have four distinct hole witnesses: opposite sides lie in distinct W_r-cosets, and adjacent supporting planes meet only at their shared corner, which is not a witness. Hence it cannot occur.

Independently for every j and U_j-coset C choose a uniform two-dimensional subspace P_(j,C)⊂E_j. The cosets of d_j⊕P_(j,C) partition C into three-dimensional slabs. A fixed nonzero vector in E_j lies in P_(j,C) with probability

p_*=(q²−1)/(q^(b−1)−1)≤2q^(3−b).

Bad events first include two holes in a common slab; the E_j-component of their difference is nonzero by the W-coset restriction. Other bad events are closed sequences of k=3,4,5 nonzero steps, with cyclically adjacent labels different, using at least one generic step. A generic step G_j has vector in U_j with nonzero E_j-component; a replacement step R_r has vector in e_r and both endpoints in the union T_r of the hole W_r-planes. The bad event says every generic step's E_j-component lies in its assigned P_(j,C).

Ten-wise independence classifies all such sequences. Each generic label occurs twice: its E_j-components must sum to zero, and cyclic nonadjacency allows at most two occurrences in at most five steps. With one generic label, the distinct directions involved force a mixed alternating rectangle. With two generic labels there is either an alternating generic rectangle or a pentagon with two copies of each generic label and a single replacement step; in the pentagon both generic indices belong to the replacement bundle. Opposite vectors in a rectangle are **negatives** of each other. This is the sign-sensitive reading of the companion's word “paired”; no equality of a vector with its negative is used. In a pentagon, the replacement vector and one vector of each generic label determine the remaining two vectors by directness of U_j⊕U_j'. The two occurrences of a generic label belong to distinct U_j-cosets, because between them in one cyclic direction there is one step outside U_j. A polygon involving g generic labels therefore uses 2g independent variables and has probability p_*^(2g).

For a fixed variable P_(j,C), the sum μ of probabilities of its bad events is bounded by

100²p_* + O(Dq^(3b)p_*^4 +(D+q³)p_*² +q^(3b+2)p_*^4)=O(q^−7).

To check the counts: at most 100 holes lie in C; a generic rectangle has at most Dq^(3b) label/starting-point/vector choices; a mixed rectangle uses two endpoints in C∩T_r, of size at most 100q in j's bundle and 100 otherwise, and one exceptional vector, totaling O(100²(q³+D)); a pentagon has at most q choices of the other generic index, q^b starting points, q^(2b) generic vectors, and q exceptional vectors. Rotations and orderings contribute fixed constants. Substituting b=s+20 and D<q^(s+1) yields exponents −s−17, −7, −s−33, −2s−31, −s−6. Every event uses at most four variables. For large h, μ≤1/16. The asymmetric local lemma applies with x_A=2Pr(A): the sum of neighboring x_B is at most 1/2, so Pr(A)≤x_A product_neighbors(1−x_B). Therefore a simultaneous choice avoids all bad events.

Each slab now has at most one hole. A hole-free slab is partitioned by parallel lines of any nonzero P-direction. In a slab with hole o, choose δ spanning d_j and a Q-linear map J on its two-dimensional P with no Q-eigenvector (multiplication by an element of F_(q²)\Q). Retain the axis o+d_j and, for every nonzero linear functional γ on P, use the line

{o+x+γ(Jx)δ : γ(x)=1}.

For each x≠0 the pair (x,Jx) is a basis, so for each height c exactly one γ has γ(x)=1, γ(Jx)=c. Thus these q²−1 lines partition the slab off the axis; every nonaxis direction has nonzero E_j-component. This argument is valid in odd characteristic without alteration.

Each j gives a partition of Q^n. At each hole and bundle remove its q axes o+d_j and insert the q−1 replacement lines. A point off o+e_r loses and gains one incidence, a point of that axis other than o loses and gains none, and o loses q. Hole separation prevents duplicate removals; direction intersections and replacement disjointness prevent duplicate additions. Thus all holes disappear and every remaining point has degree D. Distinct full affine lines meet in at most one point. An incidence cycle of length below 12 would consequently give a 3-, 4-, or 5-step polygon with cyclically distinct adjacent labels. A polygon with generic steps was excluded by the slab events. With replacement steps only, independence forces an alternating rectangle, excluded by the hole witnesses. This proves the geometric input in every fixed characteristic p.

## 2. Pairing vectors

Newton polynomials on the index simplex give an invertible triangular evaluation matrix: for tuple i use product_k product_(u<i_k)(X_k−a_u). Its diagonal values are nonzero. For each hole o choose a polynomial f_o of total degree ≤d taking value 1 at o and 0 at all other simplex points. Put m=|O|, t=|V| and x_v=(f_o(v))_(o∈O).

Every product f_o f_o' has degree ≤2d≤q−2, so its sum on each full affine Q-line is zero. Indeed constant monomials sum to q=0 and each degree k with 1≤k<q−1 has zero power sum by multiplying the parameter by c with c^k≠1. Parallel lines give zero sum over Q^n. At holes the evaluation vectors are coordinate vectors. Hence

Σ_(v∈V) x_v x_v^T=−I_m,   Σ_(v∈f) x_v x_v^T=0 for every line f.

The size bounds give 0<m<t and t/m=N/m−1≤D²/2−1≤(D−1)²=ell². There is no compatibility problem: D=0 as a scalar in characteristic p.

Take K=F_(p^(h(ell−1))). It contains Q and an ell-th primitive root ζ because ell≠p and ell divides p^(ell−1)−1. All denominators ell and its powers below are therefore invertible.

## 3. Embedded local groups and rectangular matrices

At every v use a copy A_v of the additive group F_ell². Assign its ell+1 one-dimensional subgroups to incident lines, identifying copies of the additive F_ell along each line f. The resulting universal group N is presented by generators s_(f,c), c≠0, and local zero-sum relations; length-at-most-three multiplication-table relations suffice. It is finitely presented.

For completeness, local injectivity follows from the companion's planar argument, which concerns groups of order ell and has no dependence on the coefficient field. A hypothetical nontrivial local generator becoming 1 gives a planar bipartite graph with point labels at black vertices and line labels at white vertices, nonzero local edge weights, zero balances everywhere except one white vertex. Minimize vertices, then edges. Parallel edges merge by adding weights. Two cyclically consecutive neighbors with the same label can be merged across their empty sector, contradicting minimality. Black degrees are at least 3 because distinct local line subgroups have zero intersection; ordinary white degrees are at least 2 and the exception has degree at least 1. Every face except possibly the exceptional face maps without backtracking into the incidence graph, so has length at least 12. With E edges, numbers of black vertices, white vertices, faces satisfy B≤E/3, W≤(E+1)/2, F≤E/6+1. Their sum is at most E+3/2, contradicting Euler's E+2. Thus each A_v embeds. This is precisely the paid local-injectivity lemma, not a new topology result.

Set κ=ell², P_v=κ^−1 Σ_(u∈A_v)u, and P=diag(P_v). Each P_v is idempotent. Since incident order-ell subgroups B_f partition A_v outside 1,

Σ_(u∈A_v)u=1+Σ_(f∋v)(Σ_(u∈B_f)u−1).

Define U_(−,v)=−κ x_v P_v and T_(v,−)=P_v x_v^T. Then UP=U, PT=T, and the two pairing sums give UT=I_m. The calculation uses subtraction as written; it does not replace a minus sign by a plus sign in odd characteristic.

## 4. Packing and a surviving reverse defect

Let H=N×C_ell with central generator z. Fix A_* and coordinate isomorphisms φ_v:A_v→A_*. Assign distinct pairs (slot(v),α_v), where slot(v)∈{1,...,m} and α_v:A_*→F_ell is linear; this is possible because t≤mκ. Adjoin stable letters τ_v imposing

τ_v(uz^a)τ_v^−1=φ_v(u) z^(a+α_v(φ_v(u))).

Call the group L. These are isomorphisms between finite subgroups A_v×C_ell and A_*×C_ell, so H embeds. Explicitly its regular action on itself restricts to equal numbers of regular orbits for the two associated subgroups; pair these orbits to define a permutation implementing each isomorphism. The assignments for the different stable letters have no further relations and preserve the faithful base action. The group L is finitely presented and z remains central.

In K[L] set e=ell^−1Σ_a ζ^−a z^a. It is central, nonzero and idempotent, with ez=ζe. For each linear form α on A_*, put Q_α=κ^−1Σ_u ζ^(α(u))u. Character summation gives Q_αQ_β=0 for α≠β and Q_α²=Q_α. Conjugation gives τ_v eP_v τ_v^−1=eQ_(α_v).

Define an m×t matrix C by C_(slot(v),v)=τ_v eP_v, all other entries zero, and a t×m matrix J by J_(v,slot(v))=eP_v τ_v^−1. Orthogonality gives JC=eP. Now let A=UJ+(1−e)I_m and B=CT+(1−e)I_m. Their product is I_m. For Δ=BA−I_m,

JΔC=e(TU−P).

The right side lies in K[H]. Apply only there the homomorphism ε(nz^a)=ζ^a. It sends e and each P_v to 1. If X has columns x_v, then ε(U)=−κX=−X, ε(T)=X^T and XX^T=−I_m; consequently

ε(e(TU−P))=−X^T X−I_t.

Since t>m, choose 0≠y∈ker X. The displayed matrix takes y to −y≠0. Hence the sandwich, Δ, and the reverse defect are nonzero. This is the one essential sign correction relative to the characteristic-two printed specialization. An explicit kernel witness can be any nonzero column of I_t+X^T X: X(I_t+X^T X)=0, and the matrix is nonzero by rank. We have not extended ε to the stable-letter group L.

## 5. Scalarization

Use the same paid finite matrix block as the companion. Let B_0=(Z/ell)^m and F=B_0⋊S_m. For each coordinate i put e_i=|B_0|^−1Σ_(w∈B_0) ζ^(−w_i)w. Distinct coordinate characters give nonzero orthogonal idempotents. Let σ_i swap coordinates 1 and i and define e_ij=σ_i e_1 σ_j^−1. Then e_ij e_kr=δ_jk e_ir and these elements are linearly independent. No division by |S_m| occurs, even when p divides it. With G=L×F the map Φ:Mat_m(K[L])→K[G], Z↦Σ_ij Z_ij e_ij is an injective algebra map with identity image f=Σ_i e_i. Tensor product with the group basis of L proves injectivity. Set a=1−f+Φ(A) and b=1−f+Φ(B). The complement annihilates the image, so ab=1 and ba−1=Φ(Δ)≠0. The finite constructions make G finitely presented, in particular finitely generated. K has the required finite exponent r=h(ell−1).

This proves the exact frozen target for every odd prime, including p=3. Only the extension degree h is required to be sufficiently large for each fixed p. No restriction to sufficiently large primes, unproved hyperstar protection, or numerical extrapolation enters this proof.
