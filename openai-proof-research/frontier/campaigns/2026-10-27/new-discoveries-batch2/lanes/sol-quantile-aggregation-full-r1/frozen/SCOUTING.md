## Ranked shortlist

1. **Tyaglov–Atia positive-polynomial zero count, complete refutation.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.04951, v5, Conjecture3 p24, Eq3.20. For EVERY real p of even degree n>=2 with no real zero and p' entirely real-rooted: Z_R(Q_kappa[p])=n for kappa>(n-1)/n; n-2<=Z_R<=n for 1/2<kappa<=(n-1)/n; Z_R=n-2 for kappa<=1/2. All counts include multiplicity. ONE family, not three nominations. PROOF.md gives a positive sextic, kappa3/4, and eight disjoint integer sign-change intervals. Strongest mechanism: integrate an asymmetric product of five real linear factors and choose the additive constant just above its global-minimum threshold. Closest actual source: 2024 Katkova–Tyaglov–Vishnyakova Sections9/12/16, including their odd-degree three-crossing example and its different denominator convention. Current bounded searches found those related results but no matching positive-sextic resolution. Qualified history has no exact identifier or full-title hit; broad Tyaglov hits refer to a different C*-moment problem. **Decisive next step:** independent adversarial ordinary-math/source/prior-art review of PROOF.md, especially the 2024 same-object subsumption objection; no Lean/manuscript investment authorized here. Limits: publisher VoR body not obtained, public proof registries denied, no exhaustive citing/literature clearance. Do not claim priority from shortness or a clean identifier screen.

2. **Bressan–Floater–Sande asymptotic n-width phase.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.13736, v2, Conjecture1 p3. For EVERY integer r>=1 and interval a<b, let d_n be the least constant over all n-dimensional X_n subset L2(a,b) such that min_(v in X_n)||u-v||_2<=d_n||u^(r)||_2 for EVERY u in H^r(a,b). Claim: d_n^(-1/r)-(n-(r-1)/2)pi/(b-a) tends to0 as n tends to infinity. Actual Theorem1 and its proof give only the bracket ((n-r+1)pi/(b-a), n pi/(b-a)). Section3 identifies d_n^(-2) with the (n+1-r)-th positive eigenvalue of the clamped 2r-th derivative operator. **Mechanism:** asymptotic exponential boundary determinant for the clamped ODE, together with Sturm/min-max indexing to remove the integer ambiguity in phase. **Decisive next step:** obtain an actual classical high-order separated-boundary eigenvalue theorem specifying BOTH the phase and eigenvalue indexing; substitute j=n+1-r explicitly. Merely saying “Birkhoff” or observing r=1/2 cases is not a complete exclusion. No full applicable classical bridge was established here, so retained with a serious likely-old spectral-theory objection. Exact ID/title/author history screen clean; current query mostly unrelated widths, not a clearance. No expensive computation run.

3. **Chen–Liu–Liu–Wang quantile-mixture aggregation inclusion.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.12338, v2, Conjecture1 p25. For EVERY n, EVERY doubly stochastic Lambda in Q_n, and EVERY n-tuple F of distributions with decreasing densities (source M_D), D_n(F) subset D_n(Lambda tensor F), where D_n is the set of all sum distributions over arbitrary couplings and component inverse cdfs of Lambda tensor F equal Lambda F^(-1). Non-strict inclusion. Source definitions, Theorem1 proof and Theorem3 proof read. Theorem1 uses random permutations/Birkhoff for DISTRIBUTION mixtures; it does not preserve quantile-mixture marginal laws. Theorem3 proves only worst-case VaR ordering via a variational formula, not inclusion of every sum law. Uniform subcase is public and uncounted. **Mechanism:** a coupling-lifting kernel for elementary two-coordinate averaging, retaining the given sum almost surely; compositional proof would cover all doubly stochastic matrices if the kernel is universal. **Decisive next step:** solve the two-coordinate lifting problem for two decreasing piecewise-constant densities with rational breakpoints, or give a separating bounded test payoff and exact transport certificate refuting it. No finite example alone proves universal inclusion. Exact identifier/object-alias history screen clean; current exact topic query returns original only, not proof absence. No broad arbitrary-distribution extension invented from source's informal questions.

## Other body-grounded families

4. **Berge–Berge–Luef, affine Wigner positivity.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.03112v1, Section9 pp25–26. For EVERY psi in the source L2(R+) (right Haar dr/r), nonnegativity of W_Aff^psi on Aff implies psi(r)=C r^(-i(x+ia)) exp(i(y+ib)r), C complex, (x,a),(y,b) in Aff (positive a,b). Zero vector included by C=0. Actual source establishes positivity of this family using covariance; necessity remains the stated conjecture. Classical Hudson theorem is for the usual Euclidean Wigner transform; the affine transform's nonlinear logarithmic-mean coordinate does not permit a direct substitution. Defer: a full infinite-dimensional classification with no new necessity mechanism, rather than a tractable low-dimensional problem. No routine exclusion claimed. Closest source Proposition9.2 controls concentration only and does not prove positivity necessity. Bounded title/object screen clean; cited earlier positivity proof and all later citation bodies not independently audited.

5. **Cardon–Sorensen–White, Hermite multiplier coefficient interlacing.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.13055v4, Problem8.3 p15. For which sequences gamma_n such that H_n maps to gamma_n H_n preserves real-rootedness do the coefficients Q_n of T=sum Q_n D^n/n! have interlacing roots? Actual source Problem8.3 is a classification REQUEST, not a universal interlacing conjecture. It must not be recast as “all sequences work” for a cheap counterexample. Proposition2.1 specifies Q_n=sum_(k=0)^n binom(n,k)T[x^k](-x)^(n-k); source's nearest quoted Forgacs–Piotrowski theorem proves only real-rootedness of each Q_n. Defer generic classification; Problem8.4 asks for a more natural proof of an already proved Laguerre recurrence and is ineligible as a new target. Problems8.1/8.2/8.5 are broad classifications/qualitative questions and earn no extra slots. Current exact Hermite/interlacing query empty is no literature clearance.
