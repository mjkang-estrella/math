* **383871:** Huneke, *Hilbert-Kunz multiplicity and the F-signature*, arXiv:1409.0467v1, dated 1 September 2014 on the arXiv header. Read introduction definitions, Theorems 3.14–3.16 and proofs, Examples 3.13, 3.17–3.18, Section 6 F-signature setup/Definition 6.1, Theorem 6.2 and beginning of its lemma argument, bibliography entries MS and S. Exact URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.0467v1 . This is the record's documented source link number (ar5iv route not requested). A survey is evidence of the stated definitions and public overlap, not independent verification of every cited calculation.
* **383871 closest directly identified workshop response:** Miller–Swanson, *Hilbert-Kunz functions of 2 x 2 determinantal rings*, arXiv:1206.1015v1, 5 June 2012. Read introduction, workshop acknowledgment, diagonal-order and staircase definitions, **entire Theorems 2.2 and 2.4 proof bodies**, Remarks 2.3 and beginning of 2.5. Exact URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.1015v1 . This paper predates the survey; “response” means the explicitly acknowledged AIM question, not chronology after the survey. No F-signature formula for arbitrary determinantal rings follows from these sections. No abstract treated as proof. Formula extraction contains typesetting artifacts, so no new formula certificate is claimed.
* **384223 related existing construction:** Hacking–Prokhorov, *Smoothable del Pezzo surfaces with quotient singularities*, arXiv:0808.1550v1, 11 August 2008. Read introductory deformation definitions/Theorem 1.1/Corollary 1.2; Section 2.2 local cyclic-quotient smoothing and Proposition 2.8 proof; **entire Proposition 3.2 proof**; Theorem 4.1 tables, mutation explanation, and proof through the converse T-singularity check (not the full remaining classification proof). Exact URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.1550v1 . In characteristic zero, the proof kills H²(T_X) using Serre duality, a nonzero anticanonical section, and orbifold Hodge vanishing, then uses unobstructed local Q-Gorenstein deformation. The local equation is a quotient of xy=z^(dn)+t. This is not by itself an explicit global projective family. No complete audit of cotangent-complex, Hodge, vanishing or classification dependencies was performed.
