## Independent reconstruction

1. **Partition and physical eligibility.** In a legal word every adjacent S_2,C_1 is disjoint from every other such pair. If the initial heights are (h_1,h_2,h_3), S_2 is possible when h_2>=2, and the following C_1 is possible precisely when h_1+1>=2, or h_1>=1. Their net change is (-1,-1,+1), identical to C_2. Replacing the pair by C_2 preserves legality and all later boards. Since the replacement letter is neither S_2 nor C_1, it creates no new compressible pair. In the reduced word, mark exactly the C_2 slots with pre-move h_2>=2. Each may expand independently; its endpoint agrees exactly. Across a slot boundary an expansion starts S_2 and ends C_1, so it cannot create a cross-boundary S_2,C_1 pair. Reduction and expansion are inverse, proving exhaustive disjoint cubes, including zero-slot cubes. Testing eligibility after C_2 would be wrong; the proof does not do that.

2. **Actual conditional law.** At slot i the initial choice of C_2 or S_2 has the same reciprocal legal-type factor. Expansion introduces exactly one extra factor 1/b_i at the board after S_2. All slot endpoint boards are fixed independently of other choices. Thus a word with expansion set E has weight P_N(R) product_{i in E} b_i^{-1}. Summing gives mass P_N(R) product_i(1+b_i^{-1}) and normalized independent Bernoulli parameters 1/(1+b_i). In particular 1/(2n+1)<=p_i<=1/2 and V=sum p_i(1-p_i)>=m/(6n). This is a probability-factorization argument, not an assumption of unconditional independence.

3. **Abundance and boundary survival.** At block j<=floor(N/6), h_1>=N-6(j-1)>=6. All three moves exist and the prescribed triple C_1,C_1,C_2 has conditional probability at least (2n)^-3. Its final C_2 survives. The second C_1 survives because preceded by C_1. If an S_2 just before the block absorbs the first C_1, replacing that pair leaves the board before the second C_1 unchanged, hence also the board before the C_2. That board has at least two F_2 tokens. Distinct successful blocks provide distinct marked slots. Conditional success probabilities bounded below by p suffice: sequential uniforms couple the indicators above independent Bernoulli(p), or one can iterate conditional exponential moments. Raw triple indicators are not independent. With mu=floor(N/6)/(8n^3), P(m<mu/2)<=exp(-mu/8). Binet and N>=F_n imply mu/2>=(3/2)^n eventually, uniformly over N in the nth interval.

4. **Anti-concentration and averaging.** For a conditional Bernoulli sum, the characteristic modulus is <=exp(-2V sin^2(t/2)). On [-pi,pi], sin(|t|/2)>=|t|/pi. Fourier inversion and the whole-line Gaussian integral give maximum atom <=sqrt(pi/(8V)). An interval of length w contains at most floor(w)+2 integer points, even with arbitrary endpoint conventions. On good cells V>=c^n/(6n), so averaging over every cell yields Q(T,w)<=e_N+(floor(w)+2)sqrt(3*pi*n/(4c^n)). This is uniform in the interval location: different cell centres introduce no error. At w=3n^6 the expression tends to zero; merely citing exploding component variance would not establish it.

5. **Hazard.** In any value-N board H>n+2 implies either an immediate legal S_j with j>=2, selected with probability >=1/(2n), or all higher bins have height <=1 and h_1>=4. In the latter case C_1,C_1,S_2 is a legal three-step path, with probability >=(2n)^-3. Positive value prevents tokens beyond n. The conclusion remains valid if an alternative choice terminates early: such a path cannot contribute to q full high-token no-split blocks.

6. **Last split is not a stopping time.** For a fixed deterministic t, let E_j mean that the first j length-three blocks after t contain no split and have H>n+2 at their starts. Conditioning on the deterministic filtration at t+3j gives P(E_{j+1})<=(1-p_n)P(E_j). If t=L and T-t>3q+n+3, all q blocks exist and their starts have H=Z(N)+T-(t+3j)>n+2, since all remaining moves are combines. The event is therefore contained in the union of E_q over t=0,...,d_N. This gives (d_N+1)(1-p_n)^q without conditioning at L. With q=n^5 this is <=(3N+1)exp(-n^2/8), tending to zero since N<2^n. No-split games are covered by t=0. The resulting suffix length b_n=3n^5+n+3 is <=n^6 eventually, whereas n^6 is still negligible in the exponential concentration scale. Small n are irrelevant to the limit.

7. **Quantile and measurable stopping event.** Let m be the least integer with P(T<m)>=1/2. The previous integer has probability <1/2, and its single intervening atom is <=Q_N, giving P(T<m)<=1/2+Q_N. Removing [m,m+2n^6) leaves P(T>=m+2n^6)>=1/2-2Q_N. With suffix control this gives P(L>=m+n^6)>=1/2-o(1). Every complete game has C=N-Z(N) combines, including C_1. If T>=m, m-S_m counts actual combines and is <=C; if T<m, padding gives m-S_m=C+m-T>C. Thus {T<m}={m-S_m>C}, also at T=m, where both strict conditions are false. The prefix event is observable despite padding. B={L>=m+k} is a finite union of suffix events. A and B are disjoint, and covariance of A-complement with B is P(A)P(B).

8. **Endpoint and quantifiers.** At k=n^6 the lower bound tends to 1/4. Universal indicator Cauchy–Schwarz bounds every covariance by 1/4, so alpha_N(n^6)->1/4. The same events work for every 0<=k<=n^6 (their suffix event remains measurable in the enlarged suffix algebra), giving alpha_N(k)>=1/9 eventually. For r=delta/(4+delta)>0 the weighted sum is >=9^-r sum_{j=1}^{n^6+1}j^2, tending to infinity. Neither the eventual threshold nor delta needs to be uniform over all delta: the literal negation fixes arbitrary delta first.
