2. **Silva–Ibiapina, [1909.01918v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.01918v1), Question6.** Is there a finite simple G with orthogonal edge index3 and chromatic index4, meaning nonzero incident-edge vectors in R³ are orthogonal? 3. **Miller–Newlon, [1909.01938v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.01938v1), Conjecture1.10.** The literal text says the number of moves of a uniformly random legal-move game decomposing n into its “Zeckendorf expansion” converges to a Gaussian as n grows.
