85: 2    Zarankiewicz Numbers for Quadrilateral
86: In 1951, Kazimierz Zarankiewicz [15] asked what is the minimum number
87: of 1’s in a 0-1 matrix of order n × n, which guarantees that it has a 2 × 2
88: minor of 1’s. In the notation introduced above, it asks for the value of
89: z(n) + 1.
90:     The results and methods used to compute or estimate z(n) are similar to
91: those in the widely studied case of ex(n, C4 ), where one seeks the maximum
92: number of edges in any C4 -free n-vertex graph. The latter ones may have
