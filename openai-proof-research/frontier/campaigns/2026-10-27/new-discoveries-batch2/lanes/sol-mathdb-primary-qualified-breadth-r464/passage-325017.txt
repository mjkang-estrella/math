10:                                                   Abstract. We are interested in studying the asymptotic behavior of the zeros of
11:                                                   partial sums of power series for a family of entire functions defined by exponential
12:                                                   integrals. The zeros grow on the order of O(n), and after rescaling we explicitly
13:                                                   calculate their limit curve. We find that the rate that the zeros approach the curve
14:                                                   depends on the order of the singularities/zeros of the integrand in the exponential
15:                                                   integrals. As an application of our findings we derive results concerning the zeros
16:                                                   of partial sums of power series for Bessel functions of the first kind.
