10:                                                  Abstract. In the case of some fractals, sampling with average
11:                                                  values on cells is more natural than sampling on points. In this
12:                                                  paper we investigate this method of sampling on SG and SG3 . In
13:                                                  the former, we show that the cell graph approximations have the
14:                                                  spectral decimation property and prove an analog of the Shannon
15:                                                  sampling theorem. We also investigate the numerical properties of
16:                                                  these sampling functions and make conjectures which allow us to
17:                                                  look at sampling on infinite blowups of SG. In the case of SG3 , we
18:                                                  show that the cell graphs have the spectral decimation property,
19:                                                  but show that it is not useful for proving an analogous sampling
20:                                                  theorem.
