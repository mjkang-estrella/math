11:                                                   Abstract. Doubly non-negative matrices arise naturally in many setting including Markov random
12:                                                   fields (positively banded graphical models) and in the convergence analysis of Markov chains. In
13:                                                   this short note, we settle a recent conjecture by C.R. Johnson et al. [Linear Algebra Appl. 435
14:                                                   (2011)] by proving that the critical exponent beyond which all continuous conventional powers of
15:                                                   n-by-n doubly nonnegative matrices are doubly nonnegative is exactly n − 2. We show that the
16:                                                   conjecture follows immediately by applying a general characterization from the literature. We prove
17:                                                   a stronger form of the conjecture by classifying all powers preserving doubly nonnegative matrices,
18:                                                   and proceed to generalize the conjecture for broad classes of functions. We also provide different
19:                                                   approaches for settling the original conjecture.
