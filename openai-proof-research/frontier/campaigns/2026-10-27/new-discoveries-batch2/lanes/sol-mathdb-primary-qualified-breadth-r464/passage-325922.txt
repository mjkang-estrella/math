11:                                                    Abstract. We consider a group of agents connected by a social network
12:                                                    who participate in majority dynamics: each agent starts with an opinion in
13:                                                    {−1, +1} and repeatedly updates it to match the opinion of the majority of
14:                                                    its neighbors.
15:                                                        We assume that one of {−1, +1} is the “correct” opinion S, and consider
16:                                                    a setting in which the initial opinions are independent conditioned on S, and
17:                                                    biased towards it. They hence contain enough information to reconstruct S
18:                                                    with high probability. We ask whether it is still possible to reconstruct S from
19:                                                    the agents’ opinions after many rounds of updates.
20:                                                        While this is not the case in general, we show that indeed, for a large
21:                                                    family of bounded degree graphs, information on S is retained by the process
22:                                                    of majority dynamics.
23:                                                        Our proof technique yields novel combinatorial results on majority dynam-
24:                                                    ics on both finite and infinite graphs, with applications to zero temperature
25:                                                    Ising models.
