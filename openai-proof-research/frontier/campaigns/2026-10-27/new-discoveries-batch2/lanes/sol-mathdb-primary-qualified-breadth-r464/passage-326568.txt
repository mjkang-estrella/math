29:                                                  Abstract We give an upper bound on the number of perfect matchings in simple graphs with
30:                                                  a given number of vertices and edges. We apply this result to give an upper bound on the
31:                                                  number of 2-factors in a directed complete bipartite balanced graph on 2n vertices. The upper
32:                                                  bound is sharp for n even. For n odd we state a conjecture on a sharp upper bound.
