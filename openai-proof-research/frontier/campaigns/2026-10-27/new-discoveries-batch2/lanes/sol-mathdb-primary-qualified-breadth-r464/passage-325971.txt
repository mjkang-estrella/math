16:                                                                                             Abstract
17: 
18:                                                     In this paper we relate umbral moonshine to the Niemeier lattices: the 23 even unimodular
19:                                                 positive-definite lattices of rank 24 with non-trivial root systems. To each Niemeier lattice we
20:                                                 attach a finite group by considering a naturally defined quotient of the lattice automorphism
21:                                                 group, and for each conjugacy class of each of these groups we identify a vector-valued mock
22:                                                 modular form whose components coincide with mock theta functions of Ramanujan in many
23:                                                 cases. This leads to the umbral moonshine conjecture, stating that an infinite-dimensional
24:                                                 module is assigned to each of the Niemeier lattices in such a way that the associated graded
25:                                                 trace functions are mock modular forms of a distinguished nature. These constructions and
26:                                                 conjectures extend those of our earlier paper, and in particular include the Mathieu moonshine
27:                                                 observed by Eguchi–Ooguri–Tachikawa as a special case. Our analysis also highlights a cor-
28:                                                 respondence between genus zero groups and Niemeier lattices. As a part of this relation we
29:                                                 recognise the Coxeter numbers of Niemeier root systems with a type A component as exactly
30:                                                 those levels for which the corresponding classical modular curve has genus zero.
