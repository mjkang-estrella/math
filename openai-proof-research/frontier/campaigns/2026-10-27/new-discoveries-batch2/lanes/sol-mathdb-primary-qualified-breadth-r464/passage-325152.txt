11:                                                  Abstract. Milgram constructed a 28-vertex cubic graph of genus 4
12:                                                  that disproved Duke’s conjecture relating Betti number to minimum
13:                                                  genus. We apply Milgram’s method to construct to find graphs of higher
14:                                                  genus violating Duke’s conjecture, which gives a sharper bound on that
15:                                                  relationship. These graphs are also counterexamples to a related con-
16:                                                  jecture of Nordhaus et al. on the relationship between minimum and
17:                                                  maximum genera of graphs. As a side note, we give a simpler proof of
18:                                                  correctness for Milgram’s method and we show that Duke’s conjecture
19:                                                  is true for genus at most 3.

24:                                                                          1. Introduction
25:                                             All graphs in this paper are finite and undirected, and all surfaces in this
26:                                           paper are orientable. Let G = (V, E) be a graph, possibly with self-loops and
27:                                           parallel edges. A natural question regarding the topological properties of a
28:                                           graph is determining into what surfaces that graph embeds. The Edmonds-
29:                                           Heffter correspondence states that any 2-cell embedding Π onto a surface
30:                                           can be specified up to equivalence (that is, an orientation-preserving home-
31:                                           omorphism of pairs) by a cyclic ordering of the edge-ends incident with each
32:                                           vertex. For more background on topological graph theory, refer to Gross
33:                                           and Tucker [3]. Let Sk denote the orientable surface of genus k, where k
34:                                           denotes the number of handles. The (minimum) genus γ(G) is defined to
35:                                           be the minimum value k such that G embeds in Sk . Similarly, we define
