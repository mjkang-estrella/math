10:                                                                                          Abstract
11:                                                      We consider packings of congruent circles on a square flat torus, i.e., periodic (w.r.t.
12:                                                  a square lattice) planar circle packings, with the maximal circle radius. This problem
13:                                                  is interesting due to a practical reason — the problem of “super resolution of images.”
14:                                                  We have found optimal arrangements for N = 6, 7 and 8 circles. Surprisingly, for the
15:                                                  case N = 7 there are three different optimal arrangements. Our proof is based on a
16:                                                  computer enumeration of toroidal irreducible contact graphs.

20:                                          1.1      History
21:                                          A class of problems of an optimal (in different senses) placing of equal balls in different
22:                                          containers is a huge class of interesting research problems in discrete geometry. The best
23:                                          known variations are the following: to find the densest packing of equal balls in Rn , to find
24:                                          the minimal covering of Rn by equal balls, to find the maximal number of equal balls that
25:                                          can touch another one with the same radius, and to find the maximal number of equal
26:                                          balls that can fit into a prescribed container. One can also allow balls of different radii or
27:                                          even replace the balls with something like simplices or other objects, or study the universal
28:                                          properties of packings of convex bodies, giving rise to many other similar problems that are
29:                                          also of interest. These problems have been widely studied and appeared to be very complex
