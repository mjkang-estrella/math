18:                                           Abstract
19: 
20:                                           We study graphs where each edge adjacent to a vertex of small degree (7 and 9,
21:                                           respectively) belongs to many triangles (4 and 5, respectively) and show that these
22:                                           graphs contain a complete graph (K6 and K7 , respectively) as a minor. The second
23:                                           case settles a problem of Nevo (Nevo, 2007). Morevover if each edge of a graph
24:                                           belongs to 6 triangles then the graph contains a K8 -minor or contains K2,2,2,2,2
25:                                           as an induced subgraph. We then show applications of these structural properties
26:                                           to stress freeness and coloration of graphs. In particular, motivated by Hadwiger’s
27:                                           conjecture, we prove that every K7 -minor free graph is 8-colorable and every K8 -
28:                                           minor free graph is 10-colorable.
