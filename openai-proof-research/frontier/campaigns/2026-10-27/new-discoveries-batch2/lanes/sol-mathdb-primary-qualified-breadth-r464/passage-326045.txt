28:                                                                                             1. I NTRODUCTION
29:                                              Let a and b be coprime positive integers. In this paper we will examine integer partitions that are
30:                                           simultaneously a-core and b-core (i.e. have no cells of hook length a or b). Jaclyn Anderson proved that
31:                                           the number of such “(a, b)-cores” is finite and has a nice closed formula generalizing the Catalan num-
32:                                           bers. Moreover, her proof was elegant and bijective. Since then, it has become obvious that simultaneous
33:                                           core partitions are at the intersection of some very interesting algebra, combinatorics, and geometry. We
34:                                           will state some intriguing conjectures about (a, b)-cores, we will generalize work of Fishel and Vazirani
35:                                           on Shi arrangements from type A to type C, and we will investigate a “major index”-type statistic on
36:                                           simultaneous cores. Please enjoy.
37:                                              The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2 we introduce definitions and background material on
38:                                           core partitions. We also state some conjectures and open problems that motivate the rest of the paper.
39:                                           Section 3 introduces precise definitions of abacus diagrams, which serve as the basis for the proofs of
40:                                           our results. The focus of Section 4 is alcoves in m-Shi arrangements of types A and C. The key result is
41:                                           Theorem 4.5, which characterizes m-minimal and m-bounded regions as a simultaneous core condition,
42:                                           generalizing the result of Fishel and Vazirani [FV10] through a unified method. Theorem 5.4 gives a
43:                                           major index statistic on simultaneous core partitions to find a q-analog of the Catalan numbers of type A
44:                                           and type C using abacus diagrams and their bijection with lattice paths; this is the main goal of Section 5.
45:                                           We conclude in Section 6 with a few more open problems motivated by this paper.
