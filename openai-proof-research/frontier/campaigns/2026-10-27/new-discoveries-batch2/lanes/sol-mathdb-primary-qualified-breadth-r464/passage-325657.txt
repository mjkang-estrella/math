22:                                                                                   1. Introduction
23:                                              Many results in the theory of classical elliptic modular forms are concerned with study-
24:                                           ing the extent to which a modular form is determined by its Fourier coefficients. In this
25:                                           paper we focus our attention on the signs of the Fourier coefficients of Eisenstein series
26:                                           with real coefficients and, in particular, those that are Eisenstein newforms. This is equiv-
27:                                           alent to studying the Hecke eigenvalues of the newforms in question, as it is well-known
28:                                           that the p-th Hecke eigenvalue of an Eisenstein newform is equal to its p-th Fourier co-
29:                                           efficient. These Fourier coefficientsP are in turn given by the values of a variant of the
30:                                           sum-of-divisors function, σ(n) := d|n d. The variant that we consider introduces signed
31:                                           terms, weighting each divisor of n by the values of certain Dirichlet characters. We exploit
32:                                           properties of Dirichlet characters and the sum-of-divisors function in order to obtain gen-
33:                                           eralizations of several well-known theorems for cusp forms. Owing to the explicit nature
34:                                           of Eisenstein series, many of our results are “best possible”. We now develop the notation
35:                                           necessary to describe our results in greater detail.

60: many for which λf (p) < 0 [10, page 173]. In analogy with the problem of determining the

62: prime p for which λf (p) < 0 (or, more generally, the least integer n coprime to N for

64: strongest result in this direction is due to Matomäki [13, Theorem 1], who has shown
65: that λf (n) < 0 for some integer n ≪ (k 2 N)3/8 . In a different direction, Kowalski, Lau,

68: sequence has signs coinciding with {εp } for all primes p ≤ x.
