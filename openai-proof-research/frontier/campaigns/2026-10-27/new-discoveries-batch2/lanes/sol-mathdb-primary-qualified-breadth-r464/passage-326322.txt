12:                                           1     Introduction
13:                                           One of the first mentions [6] of the union-closed sets conjecture calls it “a much-
14:                                           travelled conjecture”. This is indeed so. Geographically it has spread from
15:                                           Europe to at least North America, Asia, Oceania and Australia. Mathematically
16:                                           it has ventured from its origins in extremal set theory to lattice and graph theory.
17:                                           In this survey we strive to trace its journey.
18:                                               The main attraction of the conjecture is certainly its simple formulation. A
19:                                           family A of sets is union-closed if for every two member-sets A, B ∈ A also their
20:                                           union A ∪ B is contained in A.
21:                                           Union-closed sets conjecture. Any finite union-closed family of sets A 6= {∅}
22:                                           has an element that is contained in at least half of the member-sets.

24:                                           omitted commas and parentheses. There, one may count that the elements 1, 2, 3
25:                                           appear each in only 12 of the 25 member-sets, which is less than half of the sets.
26:                                           Each of the other elements 4, 5, 6 however is contained in 16 sets, more than
27:                                           enough for the family to satisfy the conjecture. Power sets are other examples
