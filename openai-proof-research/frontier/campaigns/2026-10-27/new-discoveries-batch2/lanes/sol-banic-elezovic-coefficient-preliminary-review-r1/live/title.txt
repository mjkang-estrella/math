An exact decomposition splits $\mathrm{Var}(d)$ into per-edge, adjacent-pair and disjoint-pair terms and yields, for fixed $n$ as $N\to\infty$, $\mathrm{Var}(d)/N=α(n)-β(n)/N+γ(n)/N^2+o(N^{-2})$. For every $n\ge 2$, $Nα(n)$ is the classical normal-theory variance (Hoel, Kamat) of the sum of absolute successive differences of $n$ independent $N(0,N/n)$ variables; the correction $β(n)$ is obtained in closed form for $n\ge 3$ (the case $n=2$ is treated separately). Two disjoint edge differences $D_1=X_2-X_1$ and $D_3=X_4-X_3$ are exactly uncorrelated and asymptotically independent, yet $\mathrm{Cov}(|D_1|,|D_3|)=-1/(πn)+O(1/N)$: a correlation of relative order $1/N$, invisible in the Gaussian limit and absent under Poissonized sampling, whose constant is proportional to the exact joint fourth cumulant $κ_{2,2}(D_1,D_3)=-4N/n^2$ and is proved here on the lattice. The adjacent pair contributes the lattice-Edgeworth constant $a_*=-2\sqrt{3}/(9π)$, of which one quarter is a smooth Edgeworth term and three quarters a lattice-corner term; the disjoint skeleton $h(m)$ is closed through a Bessel generating function; and $γ(n)$ ($n\ge 5$) is expressed through a single triangular-lattice constant whose closed form we conjecture. Together with the mean of Part 1, the variance supplies the two moments used by moment-matched approximations to the null law of $d$. This is Part 2 of a series; the cyclic statistic $d^{c}$ will be treated in Part 5. &#9651; Less Submitted 12 October, 2026; originally announced October 2026. We&hellip; &#9661; More We study the expectation of the discrete total variation statistic $d=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}|X_{i+1}-X_i|$ of counts $X_1,\dots,X_n$ over $n$ ordered bins with sample size $N$, under the uniform multinomial null. In the notation used throughout the series, a reference distribution $\mathbf r$ defines $d_{\mathbf r}(x)=\|x-N\mathbf r\|_V$; here $\mathbf r=\mathbf u$ is uniform and we write simply $d$. We obtain an exact single-sum formula, a closed form in central trinomial coefficients valid for every $n\ge2$, with one-term evaluations at $n=2,4$ and a complete D-finite characterization for $n=3$. The per-edge expectation $W_N=E|X_2-X_1|$ satisfies an exact three-term recurrence, from which we derive its complete $1/N$ expansion with the $n$-independent constant $E(d^\circ/\sqrt{Nn})\to 2/\sqrtπ$ for the cyclic statistic $d^\circ=d^\circ_{\mathbf u}$, independent of the number of bins; the complementary sparse regime (fixed $N$, $n\to\infty$) is treated in a separate paper. Conditioning on a two-bin total reduces each edge to a symmetric random walk; as the subsequent parts of the series will show, this method carries the analysis to non-uniform reference laws. &#9651; Less Submitted 9 October, 2026; originally announced October 2026.
