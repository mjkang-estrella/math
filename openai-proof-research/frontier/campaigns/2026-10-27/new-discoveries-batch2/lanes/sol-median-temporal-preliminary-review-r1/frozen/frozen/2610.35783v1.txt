                                                     Weak Hurwitz stability of extended Turán expressions for
                                                      Stirling–Whitney–Riordan and Laguerre polynomials

                                                                                                 Abstract
                                                        In this paper, we establish a sufficient condition for weak Hurwitz stability of extended
                                                     Turán expressions for the Stirling–Whitney–Riordan polynomials. The proof uses polynomial
                                                     transformations that preserve zeros in a real interval and in the unit disk. As an application,
                                                     we prove that the extended Turán expressions for the Bell polynomials are weakly Hurwitz
                                                     stable. We also prove the corresponding result for the reflected Laguerre polynomials using
                                                     polynomial approximations. These results settle two conjectures of Chasse, Grabarek, and
                                                     Visontai.

                                                     2020 Mathematics Subject Classification. 05A15, 26C10, 05A20.

                                                     Keywords. Turán expression, weak Hurwitz stability, Stirling–Whitney–Riordan polynomial,
                                                     Laguerre polynomial

                                               Turán expressions for polynomial sequences and their weak Hurwitz stability have been widely
                                           studied. Craven and Csordas [5] studied the connections between Jensen polynomials and the
                                           Turán and Laguerre inequalities. Borcea and Brändén [3] developed a theory of linear differential
                                           operators preserving polynomial stability. For combinatorial polynomials, Liu and Wang [10]
                                           gave criteria for real zeros and interlacing, while Liu and Zhu [12] studied strong q-log-convexity.
                                           Gao and Liu [7] investigated weak Hurwitz stability of strong Turán expressions for Eulerian
                                           and Narayana polynomials. Chasse, Grabarek, and Visontai [4] proved weak Hurwitz stability of
                                           ordinary Turán expressions for several polynomial sequences. For further results, see [9, 11].
                                               Recently, Zhu [16, Theorem 3.2] proved weak Hurwitz stability of ordinary Turán expressions
                                           for the Stirling–Whitney–Riordan polynomials under a strict balance condition. Subsequently,
