arXiv:1405.6315v1 [math.CO] 24 May 2014

                                                   Abstract. The reciprocal Pascal matrix is the Hadamard inverse of the sym-
                                                   metric Pascal matrix. We show that the ordinary matrix inverse of the recip-
                                                   rocal Pascal matrix has integer elements. The proof uses two factorizations of
                                                   the matrix of super Catalan numbers.

                                              The n × n reciprocal Pascal matrix is the initial segment of this matrix, Ri,j
                                          for 0 ≤ i, j < n. (All matrices in this paper are indexed starting at 0.) The
                                          determinant of the inverse of the n×n reciprocal Pascal matrix is sequence A060739
                                          in the Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences [2]. Katz asked about a formula
                                          for the determinant of this matrix in 2001[3]. Israel stated the formula for the
                                          determinant of the reciprocal Pascal matrix, and conjectured that the inverse of
                                          the reciprocal Pascal matrix is an integer matrix [4]. The author suggested a proof
                                          that the inverse of the reciprocal Pascal matrix is an integer matrix in 2003 [5],
                                          using an ad hoc factorization of the reciprocal Pascal matrix.
                                              This paper gives a proof that the inverse of the n × n reciprocal Pascal matrix is
                                          an integer matrix, using the matrix of super Catalan numbers [1]. The new proof
                                          is simpler than the previous proof, and it has the advantage of showing the formula
                                          for the determinant of the reciprocal Pascal matrix.
