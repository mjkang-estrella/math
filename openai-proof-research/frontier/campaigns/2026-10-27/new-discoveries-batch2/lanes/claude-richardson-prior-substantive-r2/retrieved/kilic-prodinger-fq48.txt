                         A GENERALIZED FILBERT MATRIX

                             EMRAH KILIC AND HELMUT PRODINGER

       Abstract. A generalized Filbert matrix is introduced, sharing properties of the Hilbert
       matrix and Fibonacci numbers. Explicit formulae are derived for the LU-decomposition,
       their inverses, and the Cholesky factorization. The approach is to use q-analysis and to
       leave the justification of the necessary identities to the q-version of Zeilberger’s celebrated
       algorithm.
