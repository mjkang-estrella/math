## Fully literal simplification that actually removes work

Eliminate W, the Banach space and operator-norm identity, without changing any innovation, x, sigma, alpha or rate. Work only with nonnegative measurable functions and kernel integrals. If the already defined literal sampled block K obeys `K w<=b w`, positivity gives `K^k 1<=b^k w`: base `1<=w`; inductive step `K(K^k 1)<=K(b^k w)=b^k K w<=b^(k+1)w`. The actual Markov property identifies the left side with k-block survival. To obtain b, prove the **same** scalar weighted estimate `sup_x (K_m w-Q_T w)/w ->0` and Q decay; then `K_m w <= (C exp(-T/2)+epsilon_m)w`. Taking logs of the literal survival probabilities, using R, yields exactly r225 (9)–(10) and its final limit. Thus this encoding provably removes construction/completeness of the weighted Borel Banach space, operator-valued convergence and its norm equality. It does not weaken the scope or assume the desired conclusion. All integral measurability, positivity, finite moments and block Markov identities remain to be proved. This is an ordinary proof of the encoding reduction, not an emitted formal lemma or source closure.

A second definitional simplification is to state the endpoint universally over existing iid innovations satisfying the literal characteristic-function predicate, rather than choose a canonical stable law. This matches the source's universal innovation formulation and can avoid a separate canonical-choice wrapper. It does **not** permit assuming stable density, a cadlag extension, heat-kernel bounds or R; those must follow for every such law. This audit has not checked a complete alternate process-free derivation. Rational-grid-only killing, alpha>1 restrictions, Gaussian subordination without an exit-law proof, or conditional assumptions H/K/R are not accepted substitutes. No other fully checked encoding that removes the major scientific packages is claimed.
