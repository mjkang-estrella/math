                                          Abstract

                                          We present some results about the number of rational points on a certain family of curves
                                          defined over a finite field. In a small number of cases the curves have more rational points
                                          than expected. Fibonacci numbers make an appearance, as do cyclotomic polynomials.

1 Introduction

Let p be a prime number and let q = pr where r is a positive integer. Throughout this
paper Fq will denote the finite field with q elements.

Let n ≥ 4 be a positive integer, and let d be a divisor of n with 1 < d < n. Let C = Cq,n,d
be the projective plane curve defined over Fq by the homogenization of the affine equation
                  2               n−2          n−1                                2           d−2          d−1 +q d −1
     y + yq + yq + · · · + yq           + yq         = x + xq + xq + · · · + xq                     + xq                 . (1)
It is straightforward to show that the genus of C is g(C) = (q n−1 − 1)(q d−1 + q d − 2)/2,
and that C has one singular point which is the unique point at infinity on the curve.

Let Trqn :q denote the trace function from Fqn to Fq , which is
                                   2                   n−2            n−1
      Trqn :q (x) = x + xq + xq + · · · + xq                   + xq           .
The reader may have noticed that the lefthand side of (1) is a trace, and the righthand side
is almost a trace. The equation of C = Cq,n,d can be written

      Trqn :q (y) = x + xq + xq + · · · + xq                   + xq                       .                               (2)

      Rd (x) := x + xq + xq + · · · + xq                   + xq                       .                                   (3)

in Lemma 2. For example, when n = 6 and d = 3 we will show that there are no bonus
points for any q. We will also find some values of q, n, d where there are a number of
unexpected bonus points. For example, when n = 4 and d = 2 we will show that there
are bonus points if and only if q ≡ 3 mod 5.

In the remainder of this article we present some results of our investigation into which
values of q, n, d bring bonus points, and which values do not. We also attempt to find the
exact number of bonus points.
