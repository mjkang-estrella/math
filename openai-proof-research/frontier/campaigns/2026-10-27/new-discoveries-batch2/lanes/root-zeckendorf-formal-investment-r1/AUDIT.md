## Integer termination/length route checked in ordinary mathematics

There is a direct finite-game route to the needed linear bound without formalizing the source's sharp phi bound. Put, for source indices i>=1,

    w_i = i+2+2F_i-F_(i+1),    W(h)=sum_i h_i w_i.

Use integer arithmetic initially. The Fibonacci recurrence implies F_(i+1)<=2F_i, so w_i>=i+2>0; w_1=3,w_2=5,w_3=6. The W decrease is 1 for C_1, 1 for S_2, 1 for S_k (k>=3), and k for C_k (k>=2). The higher-index identities follow because both F_i and its shift conserve value under those moves; the linear i+2 part gives k or 1. Exceptional moves are checked using 3,5,6. Thus every move strictly reduces nonnegative integer W by at least one. Initially W=3N; since value N>=1 remains, the final W is positive. Every legal prefix has length <3N, giving termination and d_N<3N after finite-word construction. Terminality plus Zeckendorf uniqueness supplies Z(N); telescoping token counts gives C=N-Z(N). This is an implementation route proved here in ordinary arithmetic, not a Lean lemma or separate scientific outcome.

## Endpoint integration checks

The reviewed candidate's local S_2,C_1 diamond has equal endpoint with C_2 precisely when pre-move h_2>=2 and h_1>=1. Cell weights depend on fixed endpoint boards, so other expansions cannot alter intermediate b_i. Successful triples and q_j bounds are under P_N, not mu_N. Abundance via 4/nu is sufficient; Chernoff and Fourier can both be removed. See FINITE-CONCENTRATION-REVIEW.md for full constants and edge cases.

For the suffix, at a deterministic t restrict to q no-split blocks whose starts have H>n+2. Iteration yields (1-p_n)^q without independence. If T-L>3q+n+3 and t=L, every relevant block start meets that threshold since each remaining combine reduces H by one and terminal H>=1. Union over deterministic t includes t=0. q=n^5 and N<2^n give the vanishing (3N+1)exp(-n^2/8) bound. No postulated short final clock is acceptable.

Let Q_N bound every length-3n^6 interval and choose least integer m with P(T<m)>=1/2. Minimality gives P(T<m)<=1/2+Q_N; removing [m,m+2n^6) leaves P(T>=m+2n^6)>=1/2-2Q_N. The suffix bound then yields P(L>=m+n^6)>=1/2-o(1). These estimates also ensure the chosen cut is within the finite source row eventually. Prefix A={T<m} and suffix B={L>=m+k} are disjoint. A-complement gives positive covariance P(A)P(B). For arbitrary events, the 2x2 probability table implies covariance <=1/4, so the true supremum tends to 1/4. This must be established for all events, even if an implementation computes the supremum by enumerating finite image subsets.

For k<=n^6 the same witness gives alpha_N(k)>=1/9 eventually. With r=delta/(4+delta)>0, the finite partial weighted sum is at least 9^(-r)(n^6+1)(n^6+2)(2n^6+3)/6. Zero padding implies alpha_N(k)=0 for k>d_N, so the source infinite sum can be proved equal to a finite sum; Lean real tsum must not be used without this summability step. The lower bound diverges along all N via n(N)->infinity.
