                                                                                                  Abstract
                                                      We prove the sharp Berezin–Li–Yau inequality for Riesz means of order at least one for Aharonov–
                                                  Bohm operators on bounded planar domains with Dirichlet boundary conditions. This confirms
                                                  a conjecture posed by Frank and Hansson in 2008. We also obtain an improved lower bound for
                                                  individual eigenvalues and extend it to the case of magnetic potentials with bounded magnetic field.
                                                  These results are then applied to deduce estimates for Riesz means of orders between zero and one,
                                                  and for Aharonov–Bohm eigenvalues on product domains.
                                           MSC (2020): Primary 35P15. Secondary 35P20, 81Q10.
                                           Keywords: Aharonov–Bohm operator, magnetic Laplacian, Berezin–Li–Yau inequality, eigenvalue bounds.
