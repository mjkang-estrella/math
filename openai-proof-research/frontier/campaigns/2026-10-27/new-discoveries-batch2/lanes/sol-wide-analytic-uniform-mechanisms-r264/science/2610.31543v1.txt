                                                   Abstract. It is well known that u0 ∈ L2 (R) \ {0} is an extremizer for the
                                                   L8t L4x -Strichartz estimate if and only if it is a Gaussian. We show that ev-
                                                   ery extremizing sequence of even functions {un } ⊂ L2 (R) \ {0} approaches
                                                   a centered Gaussian in an appropriate sense. This result follows from a cer-
                                                   tain inequality for coordinate-wise even L2 functions on R4 conjectured by
                                                   Gonçalves (2019) which we prove in this paper with the optimal constant.
