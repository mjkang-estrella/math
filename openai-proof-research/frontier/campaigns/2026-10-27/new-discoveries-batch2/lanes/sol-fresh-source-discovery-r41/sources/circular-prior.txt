     SUN’S CIRCULAR PRIMITIVE-PERMUTATION
   CONJECTURE: ONE EXCEPTION AND A COMPLETE
                 CLASSIFICATION

                                    ANONYMOUS


        Abstract. In 2013 Z.-W. Sun conjectured that for every finite field
        Fq with q > 7 and every a0 ∈ Fq , the nonzero elements of Fq can
        be arranged in a circle so that a0 + xy is primitive for every pair x, y
        of neighbours. A recent preprint (arXiv:2610.06289) proved this for
        q > 18 888 871 and for a0 = 0. We show that the conjecture is false for
        q = 11, a0 = 2, and that this is the only exception: for every prime
        power q > 7 and every a0 ∈ Fq with (q, a0 ) ̸= (11, 2) the required
        circular permutation exists. The proof combines a sieved form of the
        character-sum criterion, which leaves 510 prime powers q ≤ 92 821, with
        exhaustive coverage of their nonzero parameters. The supplied com-
        putational records report 3 472 147 successful cycles; replay regenerates
        the witnesses by heuristic search and checks them deterministically with
        separately implemented arithmetic, rather than checking retained cycle
        streams.




                                 1. Introduction
   For a prime power q and a0 ∈ Fq , let Gq,a0 be the graph on F∗q in which
distinct x, y are adjacent when a0 +xy is a primitive element of Fq . A circular
permutation (a1 , . . . , aq−1 ) of F∗q with all a0 + ai ai+1 primitive (indices mod
q − 1) is exactly a Hamilton cycle of Gq,a0 .
Conjecture 1 (Sun [5, Conjecture 3.8, arXiv v9], dated 2013-09-17). For
every finite field Fq with q > 7 and every a0 ∈ Fq , the graph Gq,a0 is Hamil-
tonian.
   Luo and She [4] proved this for q > 18 888 871 (all a0 ) and for a0 = 0
whenever q > 4, using the Chvátal–Erdős theorem and a character-sum
discrepancy bound.
   The contribution here is the explicit exception, the corrected exact classi-
fication, the overlapping-set independence estimate and computational clo-
sure of the finite remainder. The graph model, character bound and infinite
tail are inherited from [4]; the sieve is classical [1, 2]. We also record a

Theorem 2. Let q > 7 be a prime power and a0 ∈ Fq . Then Gq,a0 is
Hamiltonian if and only if (q, a0 ) ̸= (11, 2).
Proposition 3. G11,2 is not Hamiltonian.
Proof. The primitive roots modulo 11 are 2, 6, 7, 8. Hence distinct x, y ∈ F∗11
are adjacent in G11,2 if and only if xy ∈ {0, 4, 5, 6}, that is, xy ∈ {4, 5, 6}.
The neighbours of 2 are 3 (2 · 3 = 6) and 8 (2 · 8 = 16 ≡ 5); the value 2 · 2 = 4
would give a loop. The neighbours of 9 are 3 (27 ≡ 5) and 8 (72 ≡ 6); here
9 · 9 = 81 ≡ 4 is again a loop. Thus 2 and 9 both have degree two with
neighbourhood {3, 8}. A Hamilton cycle uses both edges at each vertex of
degree two, forcing the 4-cycle 2, 3, 9, 8. These four edges also exhaust both
cycle incidences at 3 and 8, so none of the other six vertices can enter the
cycle. This proves nonexistence without using failure of the search.           □

                         4. Proof of Theorem 2
   The case a0 = 0 and the range q > 18 888 871 are covered by [4, The-
orem 1.2]. Proposition 3 gives the exception. For the remaining 1 205 121
prime powers 8 ≤ q ≤ 18 888 871, exact computations test Theorem 6 over
products of subsets of the prime divisors of n. They leave a set R of 510

Separate implementations of the same mathematical criterion, using differ-

Remark 8. The independence bound α ≤ M + c uses I × I rather than
                                                                        √
splitting I. The seed’s literal bound is 2B + 1, with B = W (q − 1) q;
applying its splitting argument to our sieved discrepancy would give 2M +1.
These are different constants. Together with the sieve, the improvement
lowers the largest unresolved q from 18 888 871 to 92 821.

    The classification removes all finite-range uncertainty left by the imported
theorem and corrects the literal conjecture. It does not supply an efficient
uniform construction, classify all obstructions in other arithmetic graph fam-
ilies, or show that the deletion bound is sharp. Those would require further
mathematics beyond this completed specialist classification.

                          Appendix A. Computations

   fails.c lists the 2 693 fields failing the seed criterion φ(n) ≥ 3W (n) q +

Both use the sign guard L ≥ 0 before comparing L2 with 4W (e)2 ∆2 q, where
L = φ(n) − 1 − 1/(θ(e)δ). Agreement is implementation diversity, not an
independent mathematical theorem or external peer review.
   ham.c uses exponential/logarithm tables and randomized Pósa rotations.
The deterministic verify.c uses separate schoolbook polynomial arith-
metic, although it receives the same field parameters and modulus. It
checks the characteristic, field size, permutation and every primitive ad-
jacency. This is internal generator/checker separation, not formal verifica-
tion. exact small.py separately uses exhaustive dynamic programming for
q = 11, 13.
   For extension fields, write R = Fp [X]/(h) with monic h of degree f .
This ring has q = pf elements before irreducibility is assumed. Checking
X q−1 = 1 makes X a unit; checking X (q−1)/ℓ ̸= 1 for every prime divisor
ℓ | q − 1 gives exact order q − 1. Its distinct powers exhaust all nonzero
elements of R, which are consequently units. Thus R is a field and h is
irreducible. Prime fields use arithmetic modulo the checked prime p.
   The archive retains programs, parameter lists and execution records, not
all historical cycle streams. Replay regenerates and immediately verifies
those streams. From the repository root, with Python 3, SymPy 1.14.0 and
a C compiler available as cc (or selected through CC), run
python3 code/replay.py --out ../replay-fresh-01 --workers 4
The output directory must not already exist. No shell pipeline is used.
The driver records both generator and verifier statuses in each field’s JSON
record, and records Python/platform/source hashes in receipt.json. Suc-
cessful ordinary jobs have both statuses zero. The expected q = 11 job has
generator status zero and verifier status two, with exactly missing a0=2;
this exception is checked explicitly. A complete successful receipt must cover
510 fields, the exact totals above, and both residual routes. Selected-field or
cycles-only diagnostics are not complete replays. The receipt reports actual
execution; the command and expected totals alone do not certify that a run
completed.
