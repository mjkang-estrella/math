                                              Abstract
          We show that the distance measure implied by the recently proposed Chatterjee
       coefficient of correlation can violate the triangle inequality, both in theory and in
       practice.

Keywords: Triangle inequality, Transitivity, Distance measure

5.2     Maximum Violation
For a dataset consisting of random variables X1 , . . . , XN , we compute the worst possible
additive violation of the triangle inequality, i.e., for a distance D ∈ {d, d′ , d′avg , d′min },

                                                    min         D(Xi , Xj ) + D(Xj , Xk ) − D(Xi , Xk ).
                                                 i,j,k∈[N ],
                                               i̸=j,i̸=k,j̸=k

Note that if this value is positive, the dataset satisfies the triangle inequality. We report in
Figure 2 the results for all the datasets. Note that distance d′ , that uses max to symmetrize
the coefficient, reaches a violation of almost −1, the worst possible, for two datasets.
