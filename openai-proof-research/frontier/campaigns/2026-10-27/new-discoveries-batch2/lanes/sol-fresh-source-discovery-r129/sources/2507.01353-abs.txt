Title:Unimodality for Radon partitions of random vectors
Authors:Swee Hong Chan, Gil Kalai, Bhargav Narayanan, Natalya Ter-Saakov, Moshe White 
View a PDF of the paper titled Unimodality for Radon partitions of random vectors, by Swee Hong Chan and 4 other authors
 View PDF
 HTML (experimental)
 Abstract:Consider the (almost surely) unique Radon partition of a set of $n$ random Gaussian vectors in $\mathbb R^{n-2}$; choose one of the two parts of this partition uniformly at random, and for $0 \le k \le n$, let $p_k$ denote the probability that it has size $k$. In this paper, we prove strong unimodality results for the distribution $(p_0,\dots,p_n)$.
