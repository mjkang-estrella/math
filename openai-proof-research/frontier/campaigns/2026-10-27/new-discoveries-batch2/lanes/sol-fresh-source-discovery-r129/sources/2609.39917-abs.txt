Title:Log-concavity and Approximate Counting for Totally Unimodular Polytopes
Authors:Jonathan Leake, Maryam Mohammadi Yekta 
View a PDF of the paper titled Log-concavity and Approximate Counting for Totally Unimodular Polytopes, by Jonathan Leake and 1 other authors
 View PDF
 HTML (experimental)
 Abstract:We present a new lower bound on the number of lattice points of all totally unimodular polytopes, generalizing previous lower bounds on contingency tables, integer flows, and beyond. Our bound is based on the Gurvits capacity convex optimization problem, and thus our result implies an efficient deterministic algorithm for approximate counting of the lattice points up to an explicit exponential factor. We achieve our bounds by showing that the associated generating polynomials fit into a new general class of log-concave polynomials called VLC ("variable-wise log-concavity''). This also implies a conjecture of Ferroni and Higashitani on the evaluations of the Ehrhart polynomials of unimodular polytopes. The essential ingredient for these results is the resolution of Barvinok's log-concavity conjecture for contingency tables on lines, which was proven using ChatGPT 6 Astra. We conjecture a generalization of Barvinok's conjecture, which we believe will lead to stronger and more general bounds.
