<P ALIGN="CENTER"><STRONG>Stephen G. Simpson
<BR>
Department of Mathematics
<BR>
Pennsylvania State University
<BR>
  <TT>simpson@math.psu.edu</TT></STRONG></P>
<P ALIGN="CENTER"><STRONG>December 15, 1999</STRONG></P>
<P ALIGN="LEFT"></P>

<P>
The basic reference for reverse mathematics is my recently published
book <EM>Subsystems of Second Order Arithmetic</EM> [<A
 HREF="node10.html#sosoa">32</A>].  The
web site for the book is <TT>www.math.psu.edu/simpson/sosoa/</TT>.
This article is a write-up of some representative open problems in
reverse mathematics.  It was originally a handout for my 45-minute
invited talk at the AMS-INS-SIAM conference <EM>Computability Theory
  and Applications</EM>, June 13-17, 1999, Boulder, Colorado.
