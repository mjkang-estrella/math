## Reconstructed exact graph certificate

`check.py` verifies that every row of the PROOF.md neighbor table equals the corresponding JSON edge list. For each graph it checks 32 labeled vertices, 90 distinct simple edges, and degree counts twelve 5 and twenty 6. It checks all 60 supplied oriented triangles, every directed edge exactly once, opposite directions paired, and each vertex link a single directed circle. It also checks the supplied rotations against those oriented links. This goes beyond degree-sequence recognition.

These glued oriented triangles form a connected closed orientable surface: edge interiors have disk neighborhoods, the circular links give disk neighborhoods at vertices, and no boundary remains. Euler characteristic is 32−90+60=2, hence the surface is a sphere. Its cellular dual has 60 vertices and 90 edges; the code checks it is simple and cubic. Vertex stars give the dual facial cycles with lengths twelve 5 and twenty 6. Exhaustive deletion tests, including deletion of zero, one, or two vertices, pass for both original graphs (529 tests each) and both duals (1831 each). Thus both dual graphs are 3-connected planar cubic graphs. Steinitz realizes them as convex 3-polytopes; uniqueness of the spherical embedding for 3-connected planar graphs preserves the certified facial structure. Their planar duals are the supplied G,H. This is a full mathematical source-class bridge, not just a matrix/degree surrogate.

The checker uses Python integers and Fraction only; no NumPy, NetworkX, floating eigensolver or imported verifier. It starts with the identity and iterates integer left multiplication through power 90. At beta=1/50 the integer pencil is 45A+D divided by 50. At beta=9/10 it is 9A+9D divided by 10. Both strict intervals are independently reproduced:

- 1/1000 < f(1/50) < 1/500.
- −2 < f(9/10) < −1.

The full exact rational lower/upper endpoints and tail constants are in `independent-evidence.json`. Here f is the G minus H heat trace at alpha=9/10. The row-sum bounds are R=138/25 and 54/5, respectively. For nonnegative M, tr(M^k) is nonnegative and at most 32 R^k. With K=90 the one-graph remainder is bounded by E=32·3^ceil(R)·R^91/91!: use (91+j)!≥91!j!, then exp(R)≤exp(ceil R)<3^ceil R, where exp(1)<3 follows directly from its scalar series. Consequently the difference lies between S_G−S_H−E and S_G−S_H+E; no factor 2 is required because each individual remainder lies in [0,E]. Even using 2E would be safe, but is unnecessary.

The independently computed sixth integer-pencil traces are 540051518791920 and 540051567999420. A graph isomorphism simultaneously permutes A and its actual degree diagonal D, so these unequal moments prove nonisomorphism. Additional pure-adjacency moments through degree 10 differ as well; this pair is not adjacency cospectral.

Uniform convergence of the exponential series on bounded parameter intervals follows from this same row-sum majorant. Thus f is continuous on [1/50,9/10]; its opposite strict signs give an interior beta* by IVT. Both parameters lie strictly inside the source interval.
