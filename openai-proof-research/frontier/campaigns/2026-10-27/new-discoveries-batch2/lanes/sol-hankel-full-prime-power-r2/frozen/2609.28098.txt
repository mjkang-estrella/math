                                                Abstract. We establish weighted bilinear summation identities for two families of Apéry-
                                           like polynomials gn (x) and vn (x). The identities express weighted sums in terms of consecutive
                                           endpoint values and, when necessary, lower moments. For gn (x)2 we obtain identities with
                                           weights (2n + 1)r for 1 ≤ r ≤ 4; for vn (x)2 we treat the cubic and quintic weights. Combining
                                           these formulas with congruences for the endpoint values gives supercongruences modulo p3 and
                                           p4 , together with special evaluations modulo p5 and p7 , where p is a prime greater than 3. In
                                           particular,

                                           confirming a congruence conjectured by Sun. The proofs use explicit quadratic telescoping
                                           identities and p-adic endpoint expansions.
