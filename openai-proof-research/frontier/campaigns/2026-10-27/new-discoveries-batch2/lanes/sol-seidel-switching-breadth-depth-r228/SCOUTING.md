# Breadth: eight distinct additional families; no nomination
These are eight finite/algebraic/combinatorial families beyond r226's signed-spectral/tensor/matching/Costas list and r227's analytic frame/convex/density routes. They are research screens, not eight currently open tractable survivors. Actual downloaded primary PDF bodies and extracted proof passages were read, not just search snippets. The original historical paper behind each attribution was not independently obtained unless stated. Newness is bounded to the verified historical screen; no worldwide priority claim.

1. **Borodin--Kostochka coloring.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40279v1 (29 Oct2026), Introduction literal conjecture: every finite simple graph with Delta>=9 satisfies chi<=max(Delta-1,omega). Theorem1.1 and final proof prove it for (diamond,L4)-free or (diamond,K5+)-free graphs. Body includes the Delta9 hereditary reduction, vertex-criticality and induced-clique obstruction. Closest mechanism: force K5+ and L4 using maximum-clique structure. Decisive gap: removing both extra forbidden subgraphs leaves no such contradiction. Defer famous broad problem; two hereditary subclasses are ONE family, public and uncredited. Kostochka historical hits concern spectral saturation, a different object.

2. **Hibi--Li face numbers.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23629v2 (metadata query retained), Theorem1.1: for every finite n-element poset P, f_k(O(P))<=f_k(C(P)) for 0<=k<=n-1. Actual transfer-map proof and Theorem8.2 proof read: successive chain/order coordinate transfers share a base, with fibre [L,U] replaced by [0,U-L]; non-affine branching gives strictness in dimensions>=2. Public claimed full resolution, exclude. This is face enumeration, distinct from linear-extension probabilities below. Generic 'Hibi' substring history hits are mostly words such as 'prohibited', not evidence of this family.

3. **Murty--Simon diameter criticality.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39032v1 (29 Oct2026), Theorem1.1: every n>=3 diameter-2-critical graph has <=floor(n^2/4) edges, equality only balanced complete bipartite. Main deduction from Lemmas1.2/1.3 and actual critical-nonedge bipartite incidence/switch-exit proof bodies read. Mechanism: charge nonmatching edges to nonedges, analyze equality via triangle-freeness. Public claimed full resolution; exclude, no independent re-proof or endorsement of every lemma.

4. **Balanced linear extensions.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30888v2, source Introduction's 1/3--2/3 conjecture: every finite nonchain poset has a pair with uniform-linear-extension ordering probability in [1/3,2/3]. Theorem1.2 proves a fixed epsilon improvement over (5-sqrt5)/10, not the full conjecture. Actual BFT-triple proof passages and Appendix packed-sequence inequalities read; small-range, large-width and large-range/small-width regimes are separated. Mechanism: log concavity plus order-polytope conditioning. No decisive full 1/3 mechanism; defer, not a finite-sweep extrapolation. Large-width and rate questions remain ONE family.

5. **Wilson categorical Engel groups.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.15664v3 (28 Oct2026 header; internal date30 Oct2026), Conjecture1.4: every omega-categorical n-Engel p-group is nilpotent. Source proves 3-Engel and 4-Engel5-group cases and odd-exponent 4-Engel corollary. Actual associative/Lie transfer proof and final group consequences read. Mechanism: exceptional Lazard correspondence plus categorical nilalgebra results. Missing all-n/all-p bridge; exceptional characteristics prevent naive extrapolation. Defer; no new survivor ranked.

6. **Finite binary Fuglede.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36417v1 (26 Oct2026), Theorem1.1 for nonempty E subset F2^d, d=7 or8: spectral iff tiles iff has a linear-subspace tiling complement. Actual Fourier tiling/projection proof and Section7 support-subspace obstruction proof read. Source uses Walsh-support plane and quarter-density argument; nine-dimensional obstruction shows why the plane/subspace method cannot simply be iterated. Public dimension7/8 proof claim, exclude as a discovery. Full all-d assertion not claimed. Historical Fuglede hit is a geometric stability estimate, not this finite spectral-set question.

7. **Andreadakis filtration.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36302v1 (26 Oct2026), literal original equality gamma_c(IA(F_n))=I_(c+1)A(F_n) all n>=2,c>=1. Theorem1 supplies strict inclusion for all n>=3,c>=4. Actual matrix-homomorphism proof and concluding Steinberg-symbol obstruction proof read: symbol order c>=3 contradicts 2-torsion in the older K2 group. Public refutation claim, exclude. Old rank3 counterexamples explicitly credited; no finite-parameter re-count.

8. **Distinct partial-sum rearrangements in finite abelian groups.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34424v1 (25 Oct2026), Conjecture1.3 attributed Alspach--Liversidge: for every finite abelian G, every S subset G\{0} admits an ordering with all nonempty partial sums distinct. Definitions and actual Appendix local--remote-chain conditional proof read. It does NOT require the zero empty sum to differ from the final sum. Body credits a sufficiently-large-prime resolution of Graham's cyclic-prime case; source's higher-additive-dimension mechanism uses nested-chain anti-concentration and rainbow Cayley paths. All finite abelian subsets remain a larger scope. Defer: no small-dimension bridge, no finite check promoted to universal proof.

Additional comparisons, NOT needed to pad the eight: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39883v1 presents a public pure-semisimplicity counterexample (quantum Laurent division rings, dimension-sequence criterion, actual Sections4/6 proof inspected); https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28536v2 presents a public Brandt binding-number proof for k>=6 (actual final deduction/sharpness proof inspected). Both are public claims and uncredited, without certifying their entire proof chains. Pure semisimplicity generic history hits are retained, not misrepresented as clearance.
