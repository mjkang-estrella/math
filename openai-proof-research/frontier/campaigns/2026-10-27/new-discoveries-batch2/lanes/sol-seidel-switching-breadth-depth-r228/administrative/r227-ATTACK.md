# Strongest provisional survivor: the annular frame interval

Source: Marcin Bownik and Ziemowit Rzeszotnik, arXiv:1905.08230v1 (20 May 2019), Problem 2.7, PDF p7. The original attribution is Bownik–Weber, Studia Mathematica 159 (2003), 453–479, reference [21]. For every b in (1/8,1/6], determine whether the dyadic integer-translation system generated by hat(psi_b)=1_{(-1,-b) union (b,1)} is a frame for L2(R). The quantifier is existence of constants 0<A_b<=B_b<infinity such that the frame inequalities hold for EVERY f in L2(R). Uniformity in b is not required. Fourier convention exp(-2 pi i x xi).

Status: NOT SOLVED. The 2003 original proof of the adjacent ranges has not been retrieved; the actual 2019 primary report contains the adjacent-range table, definitions and some other proof bodies, but does not prove this interval classification. No claim of current worldwide openness. This is a fresh provisional research target under the bounded history screen, not a discovery nomination.

## Route A: prove coercivity directly from the exact energy

Put E=(-1,-b) union (b,1), g=hat(f). Periodizing each wavelet coefficient and applying Fourier-series Parseval gives

 Q_b(g)=sum_j 2^j integral_0^1 |sum_{k in Z} g(2^j(x+k))1_E(x+k)|^2 dx.

Initially use bounded, compactly supported g away from zero, then density and the upper bound below. Because E has width two and b>0, each summand has at most TWO entries almost everywhere. The only overlaps are a positive normalized frequency y in (b,1-b) and y-1. Thus the exact expression is a sum of pair energies |g(x)+g(x-2^j)|^2, integrated over x in 2^j(b,1-b), plus singleton energies for |x|/2^j in (1-b,1). Endpoints have measure zero. There is no omitted negative-frequency partner: each pair is indexed once by its positive member.

Equivalently, with d_b(x)=#{j:b<|x|/2^j<1},

 Q_b(g)=integral d_b(x)|g(x)|^2 dx
       +2 Re sum_j integral_{2^j(b,1-b)} g(x) conjugate(g(x-2^j)) dx.

For the whole assigned range, d_b(x)<=3 almost everywhere. Every frequency participates in at most three pairs/singletons. Applying |u+v|^2<=2(|u|^2+|v|^2) proves Q_b(g)<=6||g||_2^2. Hence the entire source problem is the positive LOWER bound, not Besselness.

Give each frequency the edges x <-> x-sign(x)2^j for b<|x|/2^j<1-b. Edges reverse sign and preserve Lebesgue measure. Singleton terms are anchors. A uniform bounded-length, bounded-multiplicity assignment of every frequency to an anchor would prove coercivity by telescoping along paths and Cauchy–Schwarz. More generally a measured graph Poincare inequality would suffice. A graph of rational sample frequencies alone does NOT prove this measurable inequality.

Actual obstacle: anchors are not present at every frequency; a naive diagonal dominance proof has zero residual on open frequency intervals. Nor is the existence of one path enough: path lengths or congestion can diverge. The missing universal step is a measurable Poincare estimate for ALL g, for each b in the requested interval, or a demonstrated failure of that estimate. Adjacent-range frame bounds cannot be carried across a discontinuous indicator cutoff by pointwise convergence.

## Route B: disprove coercivity by a cancellation construction

Because the graph is bipartite by sign, assign opposite phases on positive and negative frequencies. Internal pair energies then cancel. For a finite orbit patch V with equal amplitudes, the formal discrete Rayleigh quotient is (anchor mass + boundary edge count)/|V|. This is an exact identity for that FINITE GRAPH, not yet an L2 construction. The script checks edge reciprocity and sign reversal with rational arithmetic. At b=1/6 the seven computed graph balls do not establish vanishing quotient; at 13/100 and 1/7 the boundary stays extensive. These failures are preserved in checks.json. They refute neither the frame statement nor a more sophisticated obstruction.

To turn this route into an actual counterexample requires finite positive-measure frequency patches with consistent translations, bounded overlaps and endpoint separation, whose anchor-plus-boundary measure divided by their total measure tends to zero. Rational orbit points have measure zero. Thickening each finite patch individually works when a positive minimum endpoint separation exists, but the overlap bookkeeping and uniform small quotient are still missing. A single cycle of cancellations is not enough if its vertices have extra edges or anchors.

Next decisive calculation: derive a finite interval coding of the overlap maps and prove a uniform expansion/anchoring alternative, including parameter changes inside (1/8,1/6]. If the coding has arbitrarily large low-boundary measured patches, write the actual normalized L2 witnesses; otherwise extract an explicit Poincare constant. This is a genuine proof/counterexample fork, not a finite search presented as a universal argument.
