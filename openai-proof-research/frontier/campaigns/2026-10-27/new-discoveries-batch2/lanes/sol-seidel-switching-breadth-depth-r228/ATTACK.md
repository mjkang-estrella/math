## Actual matrix, independent of missing source labels
Let x,y be three-bit vectors and V={(x,y):x !=0, x dot y=0 in F2}, in numeric lexicographic order. There are 7*4=28 vertices. Define S_vv=0 and S_(x,y),(u,z)=(-1)^(x dot z+u dot y) for distinct vertices. This is explicitly symmetric with off-diagonal signs. Exact charpoly computation gives (t+5)^14(t-3)^7(t-7)^7; checks/matrix-exact.json supplies every entry.

Direct row-sum proof: for fixed x!=0, sum over all y' with u dot y'=0 of (-1)^(x dot y'+u dot y) vanishes unless u=x, since the character x on u-perp is nontrivial unless x belongs to span(u). The u=x term is 4, because x dot y=0. Subtracting the diagonal contribution 1 gives row sum 3. Hence A=(J-I-S)/2 is a 12-regular graph. This is a complete argument for this displayed matrix only.

Exact RREF of S-lambda I has respectively 14,7,7 free coordinates. Every sign eigenvector is uniquely determined by their signs. Fix the first free sign to +1, enumerate only 8192,64,64 assignments and use integer-scaled rational pivot equations to test whether each remaining coordinate is +/-1. Counts including global negatives: 0,128,0. No floating nullspaces or LP, no unbounded 2^28 search. Independent verifier uses its own Fraction elimination and directly checks the cubic polynomial, traces, multiplicities and all signs. The graph already displayed witnesses the source condition via lambda=3. Two empty eigenspaces here emphatically do not refute Question A.

## Universal projector route and precise obstruction
For each eigenvalue nu with complementary lambda,mu,
 P_nu=(S-lambda I)(S-mu I)/((nu-lambda)(nu-mu)).
Question A requires a cube vertex in at least one image. Equivalent exact certificate: ||Ss-nu*s||^2=0 for some sign s and one nu. Rationality and constant diagonal projectors alone are too weak: let R=[[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]], Q=[[0,R],[R^T,0]], P=(I+Q)/2. P and I-P are rational rank2 orthogonal projectors with every diagonal 1/2, but neither image contains a sign vector. Indeed a sign vector (a,b) in a +/-1 eigenspace of Q would require a=+/-Rb; for b in {+/-1}^2 the coordinates of Rb have absolute values 1/5 and 7/5. This is an obstruction to an overgeneralized projector argument, NOT a Seidel counterexample: Q has zero off-diagonal entries and non-unit entries. Three-eigenvalue Seidel off-diagonal rigidity remains the missing universal ingredient. One cannot discard it.

Strongest classical-adaptation objection: the finite-field graph and deletion of a maximal clique from a regular two-graph are classical constructions; constant row sum already proves regularity before any search. Source RREF/projector techniques and these finite computations are routine tools and do not earn discovery credit. A universal projector theorem must use the actual Seidel constraints; merely rational orthogonal decompositions fail. Next decisive work needs a different switching class with the spectrum, sourced from actual construction data, or a new all-order integrality/Seidel rigidity theorem. Neither is supplied.
