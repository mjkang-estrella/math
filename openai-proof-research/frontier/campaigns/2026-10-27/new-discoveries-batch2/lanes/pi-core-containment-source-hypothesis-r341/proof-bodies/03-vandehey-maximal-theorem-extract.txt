VANDEHEY — first complete proof of the containment claim (2008, preprint)
Retained: sources/vandehey-0809.2134-containment.pdf, arXiv:0809.2134 [math.CO],
"A GENERAL THEORY OF (s,t)-CORE PARTITIONS", Joseph Andrew Vandehey, thesis, University of
Oregon, May 2008 (Honors College B.S. thesis; cited in later literature as [Van08] and as
"Containment in (s,t)-core partitions, arXiv:0809.2134, 2008").
Passages verbatim (pdftotext).

Abstract: "We introduce the idea of (s,t)-closure and delta-sets and show that (s,t)-closed
beta-sets which are contained set-wise in (s,t)-closed delta-sets are also contained
partition-wise. This implies the maximal (s,t)-core partition theorem of Olsson and Stanton."

Introduction (p. 6-7): "Olsson and Stanton [5] expanded on this work ... They also conjectured
that there exists a maximal (s,t)-core partition; that is, that if P = (P1,...,Pn) is an
(s,t)-core partition and Q = (Q1,...,Qm) is the maximal (s,t)-core partition, then Pi ≤ Qi for
i ≤ n. In their paper [5], they proved their conjecture for the (s,s+1)-core case.
In this paper, we will be proving Olsson and Stanton's conjecture in the general case."

Definition (p. 14): "given two partitions P = (P1,...,Pn), Q = (Q1,...,Qm), we write P < Q
(read P is contained partitionwise in Q) if n ≤ m and for all i ≤ n, Pi ≤ Qi."

Theorem 2.4 (Maximal Theorem) (p. 14):
    "There exists a maximal (s,t)-core partition under partition-wise containment. In
     particular, if β is any (s,t)-core beta-set, then β ≺ β_{s,t} = {st − s − t}."
(here β_{s,t} denotes the (s,t)-closure of {st-s-t}; the proof of the general case is carried
out via delta-sets in Sections 4-5, including Lemma 4.1 "β ≺ β + k", Theorem 3.1 on delta-sets
{i} for i in N, Theorem 4.2 "Second generalization of the maximal theorem", and the chain
argument {b} ≺ {b+1} ≺ ... ≺ {a}.)

Scope: s,t relatively prime positive integers (the (s,s+1) case of Olsson-Stanton is reproven;
general coprime case proven). This is the first full-scope proof; it is a preprint (not
peer-reviewed journal publication), superseded in the journal record by Fayers 2011.
