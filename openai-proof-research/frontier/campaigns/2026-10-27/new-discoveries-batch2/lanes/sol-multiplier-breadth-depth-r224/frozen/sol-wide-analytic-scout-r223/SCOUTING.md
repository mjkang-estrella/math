## Ranked provisional survivors

1. **Exceptional cylinder interval monotonicity**, first row: exact full scope Q2 p28 and definitions pp8–11. Mechanism: keep positive square energy in the envelope derivative. Next decisive calculation: prove or refute the all-μ,all-fixed-phase,all-n energy-versus-boundary inequality in mechanisms.md. Strongest objection: source already owns the Wronskian/Lommel/completion method; retaining a term is not a result. The source's crude extension is rigorously false, but the target remains open here. Current title search returns source v1. No exact object match in bounded immutable history and compact screens; unrestricted literature/registry clearance absent.

2. **Square-root factorial multiplier sequence**, second row: Section3.1 p9 and Problem39 p16,1505.00355v2. Full multiplier endpoint requires every real-rooted real polynomial to retain real-rootedness under coefficient multiplier √k/k!, or the complete entire LP+ characterization; CZDS is stronger and remains separate. Mechanism: either a certified Jensen/Toeplitz obstruction with algebraic square-root coefficients, or a real-zero preserving integral/operator construction for the entire exponential generating function. Next step: contemporary citing-body clearance before any large-degree search, then an exact determinant/Sturm certificate if a negative minor appears. Strongest objection: degree≤30 numerical Jensen checks in the source already support nearby sequences; no bounded verification proves LP+. The existing polynomial-multiplier and integer-power tools do not automatically extend through a square root. Current author-title API query found only original; that is weak coverage, not proof of absence. Historical journal/citer intervals remain unverified; no HEAD loop or expensive investment.

3. **Curvature of first-kind optimal constants**, same oscillatory family as item1, no separate family credit:2609.39008v1 Q1 p28, βμ,n=min[jμ,n<x<jμ,n+1](1−Jμ−1Jμ+1/Jμ²), μ>0,n≥1. Determine sign of β″ and whether it changes exactly once for each n. Actual Theorem2.1 pp4–7 gives β′<0 and nodal increase, not curvature. Mechanism: second envelope derivative ∂μμΦ−(∂μxΦ)²/∂xxΦ, with ∂xxΦ=2β² at the minimum, plus differentiated order-Wronskian integrals. Next decisive calculation: a sign-variation bound for that exact expression uniform in μ for each n. Objection: positivity of first-order integral energy gives no second-order sign-variation theorem; local small/large-order signs cannot prove uniqueness. Rank below Q2; no high-order sweep performed.
