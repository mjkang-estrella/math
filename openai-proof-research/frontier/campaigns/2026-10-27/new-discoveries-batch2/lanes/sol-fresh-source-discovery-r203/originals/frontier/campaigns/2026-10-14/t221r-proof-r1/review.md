## Re-derivation of the argument, by obligation

Notation: C(n,q) is the configuration law with a uniform q-set A of
cavities, one uniform missing port at each, and a uniform matching of the
other dn−q ports. P(n,q,s) is the expected root log with source
perturbations (intensity s, sites uniform in A).

1. **LO upper bound: trial law, exponents, physical disorder.** LO (2)–(4)
   hold for exp(βJst)=cosh(βJ)(1+tanh(βJ)st), f(s)=s, p=2 even, with
   E[(−tanh βJ)^n]=0 for odd n and ≥0 for even n by symmetry; (3) needs
   bounded J,H (truncation pays the rest); (5)–(8) are trivial for
   d-regular, dn even. LO's nested chain ζ^(r+1)→…→ζ^(1)→x with
   F_j generated by all physical disorder and ζ^(r),…,ζ^(r−j) is the
   source chain with η_j=ζ^(r−j); T_l uses (E_l(T_{l+1}W)^{m_{l+1}})^{1/m_{l+1}},
   identical to `model:power-means`. Independent labels per (p,i,ℓ).
   The site term log T_0 Σ_σ exp(Σ_{i≤d}U_i(σ)+βHσ) equals
   log 2+log T_0 V_s by homogeneity of T_0, and E[d]E[(p−1)/p log T_0⟨E_2⟩_x]
   = (d/2)E log T_0 V_e. LO's F_N is the uniform-port-matching law
   (LO §4.1: E F(E^N,0)=N F_N with F defined by uniform complete matchings),
   which is the scope's configuration model with usual loop/multi-edge
   scores. Hence limsup ≤ B_d for each finite trial; r=0 is LO Thm 1. ✔
2. **Uniform matching deletion and root variance.** Preimage count of a
   target matching under delete-and-splice is 1+2|μ′|, constant (replayed
   exactly). (2.1) follows with O(q) edge and O(s) factor changes, uniformly
   in u,L. (2.2) controls the whole root log X_0: E[X_0|A,ports] is
   constant by symmetry; the exposure martingale of the matching has
   O(n) steps with differences bounded by a two-edge switch; given the
   matching, Efron–Stein over couplings, fields, perturbation labels and
   Lipschitz dependence on Poisson counts give O(n+s). ✔
3. **Inverse-history density and normalization.** Each inverse history of
   bond completion from C(n,q+2d) is equiprobable precisely because
   missing ports are uniform; histories biject with M_d-sets. I replayed
   this with *all* missing ports random (including final holes): the final
   law is ∝M_d and the (A,ports) marginal is uniform. (3.6) is linearity of
   expectation; replayed for k=1,2 at several (n,d,q). ✔
4. **Loops, E, M_d, covariance.** Identity (3.2) verified algebraically and
   on every enumerated matching. Var E_hh=O(q) by cavity-port exposure;
   E L_ff²=O(1); invalid ordered tuples O(n^{d−1}); Lipschitz x^d gives
   Var M_d=O(n^{2d−2}(q+1)); E M_d≍n^d; Var W=O((q+1)/n²). With
   E[W|A,ports]=1, Cauchy–Schwarz against the total variance O(n) gives
   O(√((q+1)/n)) = O(n^{−1/20}). No TV×O(n) step is used. ✔
5. **Star completion and sequential switch.** Inverse histories (delete the
   two new vertices) are equiprobable, so the law is C(n+2,q) given A⊂old,
   conditioned on G. At step j the legal set is d(n−q−j+1) ports, none
   frozen; the switched-preimage weight is (U−1)/(ℓM), independent of the
   target. Replayed exactly through all 2d steps (n=4,q=0 and n=6,q=2,
   d=2): output uniform on G, and the illegal probability equals the
   illegal-port fraction at every step. Expected edits O((q+1)/n). ✔
6. **Perturbation repair.** Thinning the reservoir perturbations to A and
   augmenting gives Poisson(s) uniform on A, independent of the completion
   matching and of the filled set; expected changed factors
   O(s/q+|s_{n+2}−s_n|). The insertion identity (`tree:insertion`, with the
   new disorder adjoined as root variables, trees.tex 135–140) is applied
   to the ORIGINAL reservoir C(n,q+2d) with perturbations on I. ✔
7. **Bad increments, density, parity.** q_{n+2}−q_n∈{0,2} eventually;
   O(N^{9/10}) bad indices; bad increments bounded via (4.2)'s argument at
   fixed q plus q→q+2 deletion. Telescoping inside one parity class (only
   even n for odd d; both classes separately for even d) contradicts the
   class liminf unless infinitely many good n have low averaged increment. ✔
8. **Thermal constants, Palm/colored identities on I, decorrelation in R^I.**
   `pert:concentration` uses only: score bounds, X_0′/X_0″ (graph-free;
   constant 4(1+|I_ν|)/(m_1|I_ν|λ) with m_1=1/L fixed), Var G=O(n) (from
   2.2) and |E G′|≤Cλ. With λ=c_ν n^{3/4}, ρ=n^{−1/8}: √n/(ρλ)=O(n^{−1/8}).
   Palm/Mecke with sites uniform in I leaves the reservoir law unchanged;
   averaging the inserted site gives R_E over I; `pert:analytic` and
   polarization/mark selectors are spatially generic. `decor:*` uses only
   the prescribed-tree identities, tree sampling and the normalized inner
   product on a finite coordinate set; R^I with |I|^{−1}Σ_{i∈I} satisfies all
   of it (norms ≤1, contractions, three-copy PSD bound). The source itself
   allows different sequences for different L (decorrelation.tex 6–8). ✔
9. **Simultaneous selection.** Markov on Σ_{ν≤j}∫e≤j^{−3}, conditional mean
   ≤(2f+ε+Cj^{−2})/(1−j^{−2}), diagonal subsequence for multioverlap
   moments over I; (i)–(iii) of `pert:selection` hold in this setting. ✔
10. **Encoding, patterns, logarithmic expansion, factors.** Uniform i∈I in
    the reservoir root; independent copies of the entire root/prefix per
    label; E∏τ^a=E R_S over I. Filled cavities are uniform in I and
    independent of the reservoir; distinct→iid labels costs O(1/q)·2C.
    A_*, A_b ∈ [e^{−C},e^C], C=2H+2dB; z=cosh C, c=tanh C; fixed-order
    terms use ≤2d labels; geometric tails uniform. Terminal averaging gives
    V_s^{(1)}V_s^{(2)} and ∏_j V_e^{(j)}; disjoint label groups with
    independent physical disorder factor through every power mean, so
    trial increment = 2log2+2E log T_0V_s−dE log T_0V_e = 2B_d. ✔
11. **Simple graphs.** P(simple)→exp(−(d²−1)/4)>0 (Λ=(d−1)/2); uniform
    simple = configuration conditioned on simplicity ((d!)^n port labelings
    per simple graph); |E[log Z|S]−E log Z|≤√(Var/P(S))=O(√n). ✔
12. **Truncation.** Clamping preserves symmetry; |F_n−F_{n,K}|≤(d/2)δ_J+δ_H
    in both models (loops included); |B_d−B_{d,K}|≤(3d/2)δ_J+δ_H uniformly
    over all depths, messages, exponents (site: d couplings + field; edge
    weight d/2). B_d is finite without message moments. ✔
13. **Silent graph-universal extensions.** None found. The Poisson
    `cavity:increment`, `pert:root-variance` physical part and
    `pert:selection` sizes are replaced explicitly; every other invoked
    source step is a proof operation re-checked on I, not an appeal to the
    Poisson theorem.
