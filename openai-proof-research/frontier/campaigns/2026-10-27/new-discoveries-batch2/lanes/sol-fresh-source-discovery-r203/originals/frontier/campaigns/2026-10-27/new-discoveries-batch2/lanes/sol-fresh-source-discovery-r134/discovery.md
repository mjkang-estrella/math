## Sustained route for the full equivalence E

Let T=A⊗_F A as a vector space, μ(a⊗b)=ab, τ(a⊗b)=b⊗a, σ=μ(1+τ). Put K=span{a⊗b:ab=0} and J=span{a⊗b:ab+ba=0}. Both spans mean finite algebraic sums, with no closure.

By scalar separation, zpd(A) iff K=ker μ and zJpd(A) iff J=ker σ. Indeed K is always contained in ker μ; a tensor in the difference can be separated by a scalar functional vanishing on K, producing a prohibited bilinear f. Conversely equality makes every zero-product-vanishing functional factor through μ. The Jordan argument is identical. Surjectivity uses 1 and invertibility of 2. This reformulation is classical, not a proof of the desired implication.

The ordinary proof in Brešar Proposition2.2 gives additional structure: identifying T with A⊗A^op as an algebra, K is a LEFT IDEAL. Multiplying a zero-product tensor by c⊗d gives ca⊗bd, whose product is c(ab)d=0. Hence the relations a⊗1−1⊗a generate all balancing relations under left multiplication. It is tempting to transfer this mechanism to J.

**That transfer is false, even when BOTH desired properties already hold.** In M2(F), choose u=diag(1,−1), v=E12, e=E11. Then uv+vu=0, so u⊗v belongs to J. But (e⊗1)(u⊗v)=e⊗v and σ(e⊗v)=E12≠0. Since J⊆kerσ, the product does not belong to J. Thus J is not a left ideal. This is a route falsifier, not a counterexample to E. It works over every field of characteristic≠2.

There is also no naïve flip transfer: K need not be invariant under τ. In M2(F), E12 E11=0 whereas E11 E12=E12. Symmetrizing a generating ordinary zero tensor need not create a Jordan zero tensor. The map 1+τ supplies σ=μ(1+τ), but supplies no inclusion (1+τ)K⊆J.

**Exact remaining universal task:** prove for every A that K=kerμ implies J=kerσ, and conversely J=kerσ implies K=kerμ, or give a single unital separating algebra. Neither equality has been deduced from the other. Constructing some anticommuting elements or spanning commutators by their products is insufficient: the companion's Theorem4.8 already has this weaker consequence and explicitly rejects its converse. No infinite-dimensional example with one equality and not the other was found.

Finite diagnostics enumerate all 6561 ordered pairs in M2(F3): both zero-tensor spans have rank12 in tensor dimension16; each product map has rank4. This confirms this familiar algebra satisfies both properties, not just the displayed route falsifiers. The dual numbers F3[t]/(t²) have zero-tensor span rank1 but product-kernel dimension2, for BOTH products. They are not a separating example. The checker exhausts actual zero pairs, uses incremental Gaussian elimination, and never enumerates families of tensor decompositions. Runtime and memory are far below the supplied caps.

## Full-scope generation G: missing bridges

The source's finite proof has three essential finiteness uses: local division-algebra analysis with reduced trace, nilpotent radical with a nonzero first radical layer if the radical is nonzero, and lifting idempotents modulo arbitrary ideals. An arbitrary unital A has no supplied Artin–Wedderburn finite block decomposition; its radical need not be nilpotent; a division algebra need not be finite over its center. Extending the existing trace calculation to centrally finite division algebras would still leave the arbitrary infinite-center-dimension case, and would be an excluded parameter/helper result.

General idempotent lifting is unavailable without additional hypotheses. F[t]→F[t]/(t(t−1)) has a nontrivial idempotent represented by t, while the domain F[t] has only 0 and1 as idempotents. This refutes the unrestricted lifting lemma, not G: F[t] is not zJpd. No argument was found showing that zJpd itself supplies all the lifting or structural hypotheses needed in the finite proof.

Nor may one assume the property passes to finitely generated subalgebras and then use finite results. The subalgebra F[ε] with ε²=0 embeds unitally in M2(F), via a+bε↦aI+bE12. The ambient algebra is generated by idempotents and has both properties; the subalgebra has neither. For the latter claim, f(a+bε,c+dε)=ad vanishes on zero products: ac=0 and ad+bc=0 force ad=0 by a case split. Because the algebra is commutative, Jordan zeros are the same. But f(1,ε)=1 while f(ε,1)=0, forbidding either factorization. This is an exact arbitrary-characteristic≠2 counterexample to subalgebra inheritance, not a new source result. Also, finitely generated algebras need not be finite dimensional.

For a direct infinite example of the same obstacle, F[t] is not zpd or zJpd: f(p,q)=p(0)q'(0) vanishes when pq=0 because F[t] is a domain, but is not symmetric. In contrast M2(F[t]) is generated by idempotents: fE12=(E11+fE12)−E11 and similarly fE21; multiplying by matrix units obtains fE11 and fE22. Thus the ambient matrix trick resolves only known examples, not the general converse.

**Exact missing G endpoint:** given a completely arbitrary zJpd A and a∈A, produce a finite F-linear expression in products of idempotents equal to a, or construct an actual zJpd algebra where no such expression exists. No such construction or universal expression is supplied. The strongest objection is that every closed step is source/classical machinery and the source's hard general gap remains untouched.
