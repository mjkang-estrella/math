Title:On a question by Roggenkamp about group algebras
Authors:Dylan Johnston, Dmitriy Rumynin
View a PDF of the paper titled On a question by Roggenkamp about group algebras, by Dylan Johnston and 1 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:We investigate whether the group algebra of a finite group over a localisation of the integers is semiperfect. The main result is a necessary and sufficient arithmetic criterion in the ordinary case. In the modular case, we propose a conjecture, which extends the criterion.
