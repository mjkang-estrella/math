## Literal target and scope

For each positive integer N, start with N copies of F1=1, F2=2. At each state choose uniformly among legal move **types**, rather than among distinguishable token instances. C1 replaces two F1 by F2. Ck, k≥2, replaces F(k−1)+Fk by F(k+1). S2 replaces two F2 by F1+F3; Sk, k≥3, replaces two Fk by F(k−2)+F(k+1). Let P_N be this path law; the different uniform law on complete move sequences is not interchangeable with it.

Write d_N for the longest game length and X_(N,i)=1 for a split at time i, padded with zero after termination. Let alpha_N(k) be the supremum of |P_N(A∩B)−P_N(A)P_N(B)| over temporal cuts m and all events A in the sigma algebra generated by X_(N,i), i≤m, and B generated by X_(N,i), i≥m+k. Using absolute values agrees with the source supremum without them because sigma algebras are closed under complements. Source Question6.5 asks whether there exist delta>0 and C>0, independent of N, such that

    sum_{k≥0} (k+1)^2 alpha_N(k)^(delta/(4+delta)) < C

for every N. This precise sufficient-condition question is the sustained target, not an asserted proof of Gaussianity.

The source Conjecture1.7 also informally says expectation and variance approximately0.215N. Literal mean length cannot have coefficient0.215: source Theorem1.3 gives T≥N−Z(N), and Z(N)=O(log N). This is a formulation warning excluded from discovery credit, not a new result or reason to alter the stochastic target silently. Mean, variance and normalization must be specified separately for any eventual CLT.

## Mechanism 1: regeneration across empty digit bands

Attempt: an empty band of Fibonacci bins separates left and right randomness; repeatedly regenerated bands might give mixing. The necessary universal step is a bound on the probability that information from a prefix survives for k further **moves**, uniform in N and in the cut, with control of arbitrary sigma-algebra events, not just one pair of indicators.

A deterministic empty band is insufficient. For any width w≥1, take F_(w+1) copies of F1 on the left and one token F_(w+2) on the right, with every intervening bin empty. The left tokens can be combined to a token F_(w+1), immediately adjacent to the right token, without initially touching the right token. To prove this for all indices, a combine tree for F_j is obtained by concatenating the combine trees for F_(j−1) and F_(j−2) and applying their final C_(j−1); base F1 needs no move and F2 needs C1. Thus arbitrarily wide spatial emptiness does not prevent later interaction when the left mass is unrestricted. Proposition2.1's actual reverse-combine proof also shows such configurations are reachable from the all-F1 start. This is a method counterexample, not a negative answer to mixing.

The runnable check constructs widths1–8 exactly. It also verifies conservation of Fibonacci value for every enumerated move. No probability lower bound uniform in width or N follows merely from existence of these paths. Missing: a quantitative barrier including left mass, random scheduling and lifetime in the temporal grading, then a coupling of complete prefix/suffix event laws. Next decisive step is to bound mass-conditioned crossing probability before the next barrier regeneration; repeating larger finite length histograms would not close it.

## Mechanism 2: a length martingale on the exact game DAG

For a reachable state s let h(s)=E_s T_remaining and q(s)=E_s T_remaining^2 under P_N. At terminal states both vanish; for nonterminal s with b(s) legal types,

    h(s)=1+average_{s→t} h(t),
    q(s)=1+2 average h(t)+average q(t).

These follow from conditioning on the first move. Since the finite game terminates, backward induction justifies them without a stationary-chain assumption. If S_j is the state after j moves and T the terminal time, M_j=min(j,T)+h(S_min(j,T)) is a martingale. Its increment at a live state is D_(j+1)=h(S_(j+1))−average_(s→t)h(t). After termination it is zero. Thus T−h(S0)=sum D_j, and orthogonality gives

    Var_s(T)= E_s sum_{j<T} Var(h(S_(j+1)) | S_j).

Equivalently the right side v satisfies v(s)=average v(t)+Var_uniform(h(t)). The exact check independently recurses q and v and verifies q−h²=v for all548 visited states from inputs2–18. Its single-indicator covariance calculations are lower bounds on alpha, not upper bounds or exhaustive sigma-algebra computations.

To obtain a normalized CLT by this route one still needs diverging sigma_N²=Var(T), convergence in probability of the predictable quadratic variation divided by sigma_N² to1, and the conditional Lindeberg condition at scale sigma_N. No such uniform estimates are proved here. Even a completed CLT through this martingale would not answer the literal stronger mixing Question6.5. The next decisive calculation is a uniform bound on the oscillation of h among legal successors, coupled with concentration of the accumulated conditional variance. Termination, confluence and finite moment identities alone give neither.

## Exponent and event-class objections

The source says its Question6.6 bound C/k^(2+epsilon) on specified realization events would suffice for Question6.5. This cannot be used as a generic summability bridge. Put r=delta/(4+delta)<1. Even an alpha bound O(k^(−a)) gives the required weighted summability only when a*r>3. With a=5/2 (epsilon=1/2), the numerical envelope (k+1)^(−5/2) satisfies the proposed decay yet its weighted r-power sum diverges for every r<1. This is an elementary numerical countermodel to the inference, not the mixing coefficient of a constructed game. For a>3 one can choose delta>12/(a−3). Moreover Question6.6's fixed-realization cylinder events do not by themselves bound arbitrary unions in the two sigma algebras without a union-complexity estimate. Both quantifier gaps remain explicit. No source correction is nominated as a discovery.
