## 330259 The list colouring conjecture for chromatic index
An instance of list edge-colouring consists of a graph $G$ and an assignment of lists $L:E(G)\rightarrow\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{N})$, where each edge $e$ receives a list $L(e)$ of colours.
