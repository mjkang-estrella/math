 (a) For any n, the casino can trick you at least as much in the card

Harder case

  ▷ The “card version” of this problem still gives a convergent hierarchy of
    upper bounds.

  ▷ A “deck of cards” is determined by values

                                X1 , . . . , Xn ∈ R+ .

                               Xi + Xj + Xk < 2Xℓ

    is satisfied for as many tuples ijkℓ ∈ {1, . . . , n} as possible.

  ▷ This is a problem of finding a maximal feasible subsystem of
    inequalities.
