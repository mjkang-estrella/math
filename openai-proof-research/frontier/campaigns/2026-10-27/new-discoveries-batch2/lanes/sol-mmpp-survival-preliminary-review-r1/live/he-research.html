Qi-Ming HE's Research Interests

Main Research Interests

1. Applied Probability

1.1 Algorithmic probability
1.2 Special stochastic processes
1.3 Matrix analytic methods

2. Operations Research

2.1 Modelling of stochastic systems (Telecommunications Networks and Manufacturing Systems)
2.2 Queueing theory and its applications
2.3 Inventory theory and its applications
2.4 Design of manufacturing systems

My current research project is supported by NSERC. Research topics are:

1) Representations of phase-type distribuitons
2) Design of queueing systems with multiple types of customers
3) Design of inventory systems with multiple-item demands
