## Attack on the proof

An unrestricted assertion that every open-window point lifts would be false in the presence of endpoint residue obstructions. The proof uses a∈x(1−x)Z[x] and adjusts endpoint derivatives by multiples of m(0),m(1) before approximation. Uniform unweighted approximation alone cannot preserve strict interior positivity near endpoints. Section 2 explicitly supplies the weighted bound. The selected embedding projection includes whole complex pairs and leaves at least one omitted coordinate. Integral trace duality applies to any full-rank lattice, including the ideal J; it does not require J to be the full ring of integers. The selected physical annulus ensures a nonzero contraction.

Strongest mathematical falsifier: failure of this precise weighted lifting lemma or of the proper-coordinate projection density lemma. Strongest eligibility objection: a known same-object approximation theorem could make the final implication classical or routine. This lane cannot clear that objection by noting that the source still calls the conjecture open.
