                                            Abstract. Given A ∈ Zm×n , rankA = m, b ∈ Zm . Let d be the maximum
                                          of absolute values of the m × m minors of the matrix (A b), M = {x ∈ Zn |Ax =
                                          b, x ≥ 0}. It is shown that if M 6= ∅, then there exists x0 = (x01 , ..., x0n ) ∈ M, such
                                          that x0i ≤ d (i = 1, 2, .., n).

                                                                             Introduction
                                            Let A(m × n) be a matrix of rank m with integer elements, b be an integer
                                          vector, d be the maximum of the absolute values of the m × m minors of
                                          (A b), M be the set of nonnegative integer solutions for the system Ax = b,
                                          N = {1, ..., n}.
                                            In [1] the conjecture was made that the following theorem is true:
                                          Theorem 0.1. If M is nonempty, then there exists x0 ∈ M such that
                                          x0i ≤ d, i ∈ N.
                                             This conjecture was considered in [1-3], howerever, full and strict answer
                                          had not given. The complete proof of the theorem was given in [4]. See also
                                          [5]. In this paper I state the translation of the proof from [4].
                                          Notation. Let H denotes the matrix which rows are the lattice basis for
                                          integer solutions of system Ax = 0;
                                             h1 , ..., hn be columns of H;
                                             a1 , ..., an be columns of A;
                                             x1 be any vector of M ;
                                             M 1 be the set of integer solutions of the system H T y + x1 ≥ 0.
                                                                      The proof of Theorem 1
                                            Without loss of generality, assume that g.c.d of all m × m minors of A is
                                          equal to 1. It is clear that the relation x = H T y + x1 determines one-to-one
                                          mapping between M and M 1 . We should use next result from [6]:
                                          Lemma 0.2. . Let I ⊆ N, | I |= m, A1 be the matrix that consists the
                                          columns of matrix H with index from N \ I. Then | det A1 |=| det H 1 | .
                                            The theorem can be proved by induction on n. Case n = m is obvious.
                                          Assume that theorem is true for n ≤ n0 and prove it is true for n = n0 + 1.
