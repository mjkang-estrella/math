## Exact construction for finitely many complementary intervals (uncredited diagnostic)

Let M>0 and 0≤a<b≤M. Define the gadget

G(a,b)=[0,6M] \ ((3M-b,3M-a) ∪ (3M+a,3M+b)).

For every t∈[0,M], t∈C(G(a,b)) iff t∉(a,b). Exclusion is witnessed by x=3M. For inclusion, suppose x∈G and neither x-t nor x+t lies in G. If one is outside [0,6M], the other lies in the untouched boundary strip [0,2M] or [4M,6M], contradiction. Otherwise both are in holes. They cannot be in the same hole because their midpoint x would lie in that open interval. In distinct holes, their distance2t lies strictly between2a and2b, so t∈(a,b). This also handles a=0: the middle point is retained, and the two holes touch only there.

Suppose C⊂[0,M] contains0 and M and has finitely many complementary open intervals (a_i,b_i). Take one translated copy of each G(a_i,b_i), and at least two anchor blocks [0,2M]. Place these finitely many blocks at offsets 100M·10^j. All blocks have width≤6M; all pairwise block-distance ranges are mutually disjoint and lie above6M (the differences of the distinct powers of10 differ by at least1). For t≤6M, partners must lie in the same block. The anchors exclude every t>M, using their midpoint; the gadgets give exactly C for t≤M. For t>6M, at most one pair of blocks can supply partners, so a point in a third block has none. Thus the finite union has exactly center C. It is compact. In particular [0,1]∪{3/2} IS realizable; it is not an obstruction. intervals.py checks a continuous finite-union realization using exact rational endpoint arrangements, not a pixel grid. C=[0,M] is realized by [0,2M]. Every finite prescribed center also falls under the finite-complement construction. These facts do not imply realization for arbitrary compact C.

## Why the two limiting constructions fail

1. Given arbitrary compact C, exhaust its relatively open complement in [0,M] by finitely many whole component gaps. This produces decreasing finite-complement C_n with intersection C. The separated-gadget construction realizes each C_n, but its diameter grows with the number of gaps; hence it supplies no bounded hyperspace subsequence. Rescaling would alter the prescribed distances. Packing fixed-width gadgets into a bounded region introduces cross-block partners; the exclusion proof no longer works.

2. Aligning all hole midpoints makes a single bounded set K=[0,6M] minus the holes 3M±([0,M]\C). It supplies every exclusion witness at3M, but can lose wanted partners elsewhere. With M=3 and C={0,1,3/2,3}, the point x=10.5 belongs to K, while x±1=9.5,11.5 do not. Hence 1∉C(K). This is an exact compact finite-C diagnostic, not a counterexample to the full problem (the separated construction realizes that C).

3. Hausdorff convergence preserves positive obligations in the following precise direction: if K_n→K, a_n→a and a_n∈C(K_n), choose x_n→x for each x∈K; compact boundedness gives a convergent subsequence of partners y_n, so |x-y|=a. Thus prescribed C survives if it belongs to all C(K_n). It does NOT exclude new distances. Explicitly K_n={i/n:0≤i≤n}∪{1/(2n+1)} has C(K_n)={0} for n≥3, but tends to [0,1], whose center is [0,1/2]. At the extra point every positive grid candidate k/n fails: its displacement to any grid point is not an integral multiple of1/n (2n+1 is coprime to n). The endpoint0 forces all candidates into K_n; the remaining candidate1/(2n+1) fails at endpoint1. checks.py verifies n=3,5,9,17 exactly; the preceding argument is uniform.

For a robust negative obligation one needs a witness x_a and a positive margin dist(x_a±a,K)>0. On a compact subset of the complement, finite covers of such margins would suffice under Hausdorff perturbation. The challenge is simultaneous construction with all positive partner obligations, especially as complementary gaps shrink toward a∈C. Checking only rational excluded distances does not prevent an irrational accidental center unless margins extend to a covering of the entire complement.

## Sustained next step and disposition

The finite-gadget route handles arbitrary locations and widths of finitely many excluded intervals. The remaining full-scope obstacle is bounded composition of infinitely many gadgets while controlling both signs of every potential partner. No invariant accomplishing that, no necessary structural condition excluding ALL compact K for an explicit C, and no classical full bridge was proved here. A fractal prescription such as the ternary Cantor set is not refuted by failed aligned holes or a finite-grid search.
