1. **Airy/GUE commuting differential eigenfunctions.** Literal source H, Barhoumi-Andréani Questions1–2 pp2–3. For each real s, study eigenvectors on L²([s,∞)) of L_s=d/dx((x-s)d/dx)-x(x-s), and find a differential operator commuting with the restricted finite-n Hermite projection K_n whose edge-rescaling tends to L_s. The latter is ONE related family, not another count. History aliases Airy/Tracy–Widom hit r74, whose actual frozen report concerns Chernoff-density sharp log-curvature through Airy zeros; that is a different object, but the lexical hit is retained. Closest actual statement read: H derives the shifted Airy Hankel-square identity and attributes commutation to Tracy–Widom1994 §IV-B p165. Original1994 proof NOT read here. Mechanism: impose commutator kernel identity L_xK=L_yK plus vanishing boundary concomitant; solve coefficients before treating the spectral domain. Next decisive calculation: show the restricted Hermite kernel admits such a self-adjoint differential expression and boundary conditions uniformly in n. Eligibility objection: “study eigenvectors” lacks a single source endpoint; the identity operator is vacuous, and ordinary Sturm–Liouville existence is routine. DEFER, unranked.

Objection: a sufficient subclass is easily routine or already known; no full new universal condition was established. DEFER.

Objection: radial/translated radial examples are routine; no such extension proved. DEFER.

Objection: limiting-envelope inclusion need not control union over finite n or all four clauses. DEFER.

6. **Ranges of subsum representation cardinalities.** Literal source G §6 Problems29–31 p11, plus Marchwicki–Żmija, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.13016v1,18August2025, actual §2 Lemma2.1 and proof. Closest actual proof read: Lemma2.1 separates finitely many translated tail achievement sets by scaling a finite prefix past the tail diameter, giving multiplicative ranges F·C=C and analogous finite/Cantorval/multi-interval statements. Objection: this is exactly the missing universal control, so the finite-prefix lemma alone is routine and no new outcome. DEFER; one range family, no separate counts for ω/c variants.
