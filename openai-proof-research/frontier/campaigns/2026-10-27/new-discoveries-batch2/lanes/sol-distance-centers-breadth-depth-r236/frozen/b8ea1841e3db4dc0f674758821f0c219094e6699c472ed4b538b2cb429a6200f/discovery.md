## Uniform route investigated, and its exact gap

Theorem 9.2 gives for all x,h

E_K(x;h)=2K(h,(D_hK)(h,h))+5(D_{K(h,h)}K)(h,h)−4(D_hK)(h,K(h,h))=0.

At a fixed cubic K this is a linear map L_K on the derivative tensor T∈R^d*⊗Sym³(R^d*), obtained by replacing D K with T. If L_K is injective, all first derivatives vanish at that point. A potential all-order route is a dimension-independent kernel/stratum theorem for L_K, followed by a proof that a smooth field satisfying E_K=0 cannot move within its exceptional strata. That would use the source's necessary condition and cover the full midpoint hypothesis without checking arbitrarily many Taylor coefficients.

Exact checks.py constructs every coefficient of E in h and uses rational-domain elimination. In d=2 the cubic coordinates are (C000,C001,C011,C111), derivative coordinates ordered first by differentiation direction and then by that list. At K=(1,0,0,0), L_K has rank 7 of 8 and kernel generated by the single derivative ∂₂C222. At (1,0,0,1), (1,2,3,5), and (0,1,1,0) it has rank 8. Two specified d=3 tensors have rank 30 of 30. These are exact finite algebra, not a uniform injectivity or PDE theorem.

The rank-one obstruction has a simple full algebraic explanation. Set K(v,w)=v₁w₁e₁ and T_v(w,z)=v₂w₂z₂e₂. Then K(h,T_h(h,h))=0, T_{K(h,h)}(h,h)=0 and T_h(h,K(h,h))=0 for every h. Thus L_K(T)=0 despite T≠0. Consequently the unrestricted pointwise injectivity lemma is FALSE, even for nonzero K. This is not a counterexample to Conjecture 9.1: no smooth field on a neighborhood with these prescribed jets and exact midpoint identity is constructed. For example the tempting field C=dx₁³+x₂dx₂³ has this jet at x₂=0 but fails E=0 away from that line; the second scalar block contributes 3x₂h₂⁴. A jet kernel does not establish integrability of a nonlinear PDE solution.

Residual quantifiers: all dimensions d≥2; all smooth tensor fields, including fields whose values lie entirely in degenerate algebraic strata; all base points, even zeros of K; all sufficiently close pairs in a genuine open neighborhood; exact midpoint identity, not finitely many jets. No proof that E=0 forces constancy is available. No proof that the higher even-order conditions overcome the kernel is available. Smooth flat remainders also prevent replacing exact local identity by a formal Taylor series without a separate argument. The finite diagnostic was stopped here; no search expansion substitutes for this missing uniform bridge.
