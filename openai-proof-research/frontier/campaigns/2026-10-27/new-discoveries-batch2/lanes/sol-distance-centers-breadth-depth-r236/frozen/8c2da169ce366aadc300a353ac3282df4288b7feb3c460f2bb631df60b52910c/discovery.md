## Sustained route: lift the cyclic decomposition integrally

Assume inductively that DU_(n-1) has integral canonical form. Even if D_n is surjective, choose a Z-linear section s and split ZP_n = s(ZP_(n-1)) direct-sum K, K=ker D_n. Write U_(n-1)(x)=(Ax,Hx), where A=D_n U_(n-1). Then UD_n has block matrix [[A,0],[H,0]], so DU_n has [[A+rI,0],[H,rI]]. An integral cyclic decomposition must glue cyclic factors of A to the new kernel coordinates through H. Rational splitting or characteristic-polynomial knowledge does not prove the primitive gluing that this requires. Shah's hypotheses provide precisely additional input for this step; the universal statement does not provide those hypotheses.

A concrete exact obstruction to the tempting spectrum-only route: T=diag(1,3) and T'=[[2,1],[1,2]] are symmetric positive definite integral matrices with the SAME rational spectrum. For T+tI, the ideal generated by all entries is (t+1,t+3), a proper ideal (reduce at t=1 modulo2). Hence it cannot have the predicted factors 1,(t+1)(t+3). T' DOES have a primitive cyclic vector e1: det[e1,T'e1]=1. Modulo2 the kernel of T-I has dimension2, while that of T'-I has dimension1. T is not asserted to be a differential-poset DU. This falsifies only a generic algebraic shortcut, not Conjecture1.1.

Next attempted falsification uses the literal combinatorics. For a current cover matrix A, an upper incidence B must satisfy B^T B=A A^T+rI. Pair requirements are decomposed into cliques of common-upper elements; singleton upper elements fill remaining degree requirements. The checker verifies the FULL Gram identity for every successful generated extension. Wagner then extends each valid prefix indefinitely. A genuine mismatch with Eq(2.1) over any finite field would refute the full source.
