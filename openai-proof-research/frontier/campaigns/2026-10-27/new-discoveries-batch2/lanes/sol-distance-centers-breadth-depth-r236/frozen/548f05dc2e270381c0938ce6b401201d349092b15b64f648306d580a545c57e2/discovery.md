## Universal route and missing lemma

Put m=floor(d/2). Write F=Σ_e c_e x^e with coefficients polynomial over Z in the coefficient variables a_ij of its factors. The middle catalecticant C_m has columns indexed by derivative exponents u with |u|=m and rows by v with |v|=d−m. Its entry is

c_(u+v) ∏_j (u_j+v_j)!/v_j!.

A full-column-rank minor establishes Ann(F)_m=0. Since Ann(F) is a homogeneous ideal in a polynomial ring, if a nonzero D of degree k<m annihilated F, multiplying D by any monomial of degree m−k would give a nonzero middle annihilator. Thus middle injectivity implies injectivity at EVERY k≤m; symmetry of the perfect apolar pairing gives the formula in every degree above m. Conversely the conjectured formula forces this middle injectivity. Rank is open because minors are polynomial functions in the a_ij. Consequently, for each n,d and each allowed characteristic, one full-rank coefficient specialization over the algebraic closure suffices for a nonempty open set. For sufficiently large finite fields a nonzero minor polynomial has a nonvanishing rational parameter point by the usual polynomial root bound; the threshold can depend on n,d and the chosen minor. This is a reduction, not a full result.

Plausible uniform route: construct a family of coefficient specializations/degenerations whose selected maximal minor has a distinguished noncancelling term, with integer content supported only on primes ≤d. Nonzero over Q alone would leave possible exceptional primes >d; an integral or characteristic-specific construction is necessary for the full source scope. No signed-matching/noncancellation lemma covering all n,d is proved. There is no induction across the middle-degree jump and no control of the contents of all maximal minors.

A concrete first family was ℓ_t=Σ_(j=0)^(n−1) t^j x_(j+1), t=1,…,d. Its uniform matroid property follows from Vandermonde determinants in characteristic zero and in the tested primes, all larger than d. But for n=4,d=8 the middle 35×35 matrix has rational rank exactly 34. A universal full-rank theorem for this specific specialization is FALSE. For n=5,d=10, modular rank is 119/126 at each of three primes; no rational rank claim is made there. The prescribed integer perturbations in checks.json have full ranks 35 and 126 modulo1009, so these failures do not refute the generic conjecture. They show why a pleasant-looking explicit family is insufficient. No broad parameter search was used to turn these finite examples into a claimed theorem.
