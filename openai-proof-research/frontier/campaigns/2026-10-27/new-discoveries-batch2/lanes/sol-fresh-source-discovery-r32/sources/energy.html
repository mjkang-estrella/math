A degree-two spectral gap for Brownian energy exchange

Submit a result

Fix $n \ge 2$, positive numbers $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n$, and symmetric nonnegative weights $w_{xy}$ with $w_{xx} = 0$ whose positive-weight graph on $\{1, \dots, n\}$ is connected. On the simplex $\Delta_{n-1} = \{\eta \in [0,1]^n : \sum_x \eta_x = 1\}$ let $\mu = \operatorname{Dirichlet}(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n)$, and for real polynomials $f$ on $\Delta_{n-1}$ let $$\mathcal{E}(f,f) = \sum_{x < y} w_{xy} \int \eta_x \eta_y \bigl((\partial_x - \partial_y) f\bigr)^2 \, d\mu,$$ the Dirichlet form of the Brownian energy process, the diffusion on $\Delta_{n-1}$ with generator $\sum_{x < y} w_{xy} \{ \eta_x \eta_y (\partial_x - \partial_y)^2 - (\alpha_y \eta_x - \alpha_x \eta_y)(\partial_x - \partial_y) \}$, which is reversible with respect to $\mu$. Let $$\lambda = \inf_{\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f) > 0} \frac{\mathcal{E}(f,f)}{\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f)}, \qquad \lambda_{\le 2} = \min_{\substack{\deg f \le 2 \\ \operatorname{Var}_\mu(f) > 0}} \frac{\mathcal{E}(f,f)}{\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f)},$$ the first infimum over polynomials of every degree, so that $\lambda$ is the spectral gap of the process. The generator preserves polynomial degree and is intertwined with the symmetric inclusion process, whose spectral gap equals $\lambda$; polynomials of degree at most $k$ correspond to $k$ particles. Kim and Sau proved that $\lambda$ is attained at degree one when every $\alpha_x \ge 1$, that this can fail otherwise, and that when $\alpha$ is replaced by $\varepsilon\alpha$ with $\varepsilon \to 0$, the spectral gap of $k$ particles is asymptotic to that of two particles, for every $k \ge 2$. Caputo, Quattropani and Sau proved that the spectral gap is always attained at degree at most two for the Kipnis–Marchioro–Presutti model, in which the energies on a block are resampled from the conditional law of $\mu$ at random times, on arbitrary weighted graphs and hypergraphs.

Problem. Is $\lambda = \lambda_{\le 2}$ for every $n \ge 2$, every choice of positive $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n$ and every choice of weights as above?

Source: Kim and Sau (2026), §1.3, as the two-particle spectral gap identity for the symmetric inclusion process, after a conjecture of Caputo for the Beta-binomial splitting process; asked for the Brownian energy process by Caputo, Quattropani and Sau (2026), §1.4.6.
