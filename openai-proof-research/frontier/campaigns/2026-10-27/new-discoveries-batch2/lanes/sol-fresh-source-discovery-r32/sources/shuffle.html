Hypergraph shuffles and the one-particle spectral gap

Submit a result

Let $n \ge 2$ and give each subset $B \subseteq [n]$ with $|B| \ge 2$ a weight $w_B \ge 0$, such that the graph joining two vertices whenever they lie in a common subset of positive weight is connected. Place one distinct label at each vertex; independently, each $B$ rings at rate $w_B$ and then permutes the labels it carries uniformly at random. Let $L_{\mathrm{IP}}$ be the generator of this interchange process on $S_n$. The position of a single label is the random walk with generator $$L_{\mathrm{RW}} f(x) = \sum_{B \ni x} w_B \left( \frac{1}{|B|} \sum_{y \in B} f(y) - f(x) \right).$$ Both chains are reversible; let $\lambda_{\mathrm{IP}}$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{RW}}$ be the smallest positive eigenvalues of $-L_{\mathrm{IP}}$ and $-L_{\mathrm{RW}}$, so that $\lambda_{\mathrm{IP}} \le \lambda_{\mathrm{RW}}$. When only sets of size two have positive weight, $\lambda_{\mathrm{IP}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{RW}}$: this is Aldous' spectral gap conjecture, proved by Caputo, Liggett and Richthammer. Bristiel and Caputo proved the equality when $w_B$ depends only on $|B|$, and Alon, Kozma and Puder proved it for further classes of hypergraphs.

Problem. Is $\lambda_{\mathrm{IP}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{RW}}$ for every $n \ge 2$ and every choice of weights as above?

Source: Caputo (by 2010; recorded in Piras (2010), Conjecture 3, according to Alon and Puder); see Cesi (2016), Aldous, Caputo, Durrett, Holroyd, Jung and Puha (2021), Bristiel and Caputo (2024), Conjecture 1.7, and Alon and Puder (2026), Conjecture 1.2 ("Caputo's hypergraph conjecture"), in this form.
