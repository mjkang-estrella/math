# 608. A degree-two spectral gap for Brownian energy exchange

**Area:** Interacting diffusions and sampling

**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL

**Last checked:** 2026-09-24

## Problem statement

Fix $`n\ge2`$, positive numbers $`\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n`$, and symmetric nonnegative weights $`w_{xy}`$, with $`w_{xx}=0`$ and connected positive-weight graph. On

```math
\Delta_{n-1}=\{\eta\in[0,1]^n:\ \sum_x\eta_x=1\},
```

let $`\mu=\mathop{\mathrm{Dirichlet}}\nolimits(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)`$. For real polynomials restricted to this simplex, define

```math
\mathcal E(f,f)=\sum_{x<y}w_{xy}\int\eta_x\eta_y\bigl((\partial_x-\partial_y)f\bigr)^2\,d\mu,
```



```math
\lambda=\inf_{\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits_\mu(f)>0}\frac{\mathcal E(f,f)}{\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits_\mu(f)},\qquad
\lambda_{\le2}=\min_{\substack{\deg f\le2\\\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits_\mu(f)>0}}\frac{\mathcal E(f,f)}{\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits_\mu(f)}.
```

The first infimum runs over polynomials of every finite degree; equivalently it is the gap of the closed Brownian-energy Dirichlet form generated by these polynomials.

Is $`\lambda=\lambda_{\le2}`$ for every choice above?

## Application

A positive answer would reduce the slowest relaxation mode of a continuous energy-redistribution diffusion to a finite quadratic eigenvalue problem.

## Status review

**Known cases:** When every $`\alpha_x\ge1`$, degree one suffices. Two-particle reduction is also known asymptotically as all site weights are scaled toward zero.

**Remaining target:** An exact degree-two reduction at every positive parameter vector. The September source explicitly leaves this diffusion question open despite proving a corresponding result for jump exchange dynamics.
