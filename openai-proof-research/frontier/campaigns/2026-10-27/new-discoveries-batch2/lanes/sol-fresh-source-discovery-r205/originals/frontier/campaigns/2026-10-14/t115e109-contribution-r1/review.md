## Reproduction of the composition at the claimed scope

Here is the decisive reduction, in the candidate's actual bit model. It also identifies exactly what remains author-written rather than literally stated in a cited theorem.

1. Put q=sum ceil(log2(b_ij+1)). There are at most 2^q leaves and fewer than 2^(q+1) feasible prefix nodes. Lower-interval translation keeps each counter input polynomial in complete L. With eta inverse polynomial in q and confidence 1/4, fix a single polynomial B(L) bounding the clipped counter's fair-bit tape, work and rational output size on all these inputs. Enumerate the 2^B tapes separately at each prefix. This exposes the entire scalar distribution, including failures, in 2^poly(L) bit work. No source counter internals are rederived.

2. For sorted scalar values v_i let h_i/2^B be their cumulative base probability. With r=24(k+q+20)+1, the median probability at v_i is the difference of two binomial tails

       F_r(v_i) = 2^(-Br) sum_{j=(r+1)/2}^r
                    binom(r,j) h_i^j (2^B-h_i)^(r-j).

   The formula follows by counting which repetitions fall below that threshold. It applies to repeated scalar values and arbitrary rational scalar outputs; it makes no accuracy assumption about a failed value. Its numerator has O(Br) bits. Polynomially many arithmetic operations in r and B per support value suffice, with an absolute polynomial exponent. The support size does not grow with k.

3. JVV's rejection principle is applied at fixed relative precision, rather than demanding eta exponentially small. Conditional on a fully assigned good estimate tree, Q(x) lies between 1/(2M) and 2/M and the root estimate lies between M/2 and 2M. Thus Q(x) min(1,1/[4 Zhat Q(x)])=1/(4 Zhat) at every leaf. The full-tree good event has failure <=2^(-k-19); its integrated accepted mass is a uniform submeasure of mass at least (1-beta)/8. Bad assignments contribute an additional nonnegative measure of mass <=beta. Independent retries normalize by their actual total success s>=1/16. Their exact law is p_k(x)=s_x/s and has TV <=2^(-k). This uses no ability to recognize a bad estimate.

4. For tabulation enumerate each path and its at most 1+2q queried scalar values. The number is at most E=2^[q+(1+2q)B], independent of k. Multiply the median masses, actual rational branch probabilities and clipped terminal acceptance. This computes s_x including all failed-estimate mass. Integrating each rational-coin rejection loop gives its rational coin probability exactly; dividing by s integrates all retries. The full-tree assignment is an analysis device, not a computational enumeration step.

5. A single history term has bit length O(poly(L)(k+1)). Even summing E terms with unreduced product denominators gives only O(E poly(L)(k+1)) bits. Schoolbook summation, multiplication, normalization over at most 2^q leaves, and common-denominator formation still cost 2^poly(L)(k+D+1)^c for an absolute c. This is the needed bit bound. Neither polynomial-bit dyadic full laws nor bounded generator runs have been assumed.

6. Apply the already-paid residual mixture with D=16(a(L)+c+q+10)^2, k=2D and delta=2^(-D). Since M<=2^q and p_k(x)<=1/M+delta^2, the residual [1/M-(1-delta)p_k(x)]/delta is nonnegative. Its exact rational categorical draw is affordable on the rare branch. The candidate's displayed estimate 2^(-D+a)(3D+1)^c<=1 pays even the conservative arithmetic majorant. Exact feasibility and the direct empty-dimension/unique cases retain the full target.

This reproduction does not establish a full independent correctness PASS. It establishes that the identified contribution interface can be assembled without a new capped-chain, trace or learner theorem. The only algorithm-specific hard input in this assembly is the stipulated bounded counter.

## Strongest defense and why it does not change this verdict

The defense is correct about a real distinction: enumerating the r amplified tapes would cost 2^(Theta(Br)), and a generic FPAUS need not have an affordable actual-law oracle. An FPRAS headline alone is also insufficient. Here the candidate deliberately uses JVV's median-powered scalar interface, and the source supplies the unconditional finite base-tape bound. Those two features make a binomial-tail computation immediately available. This is more than setting a cap parameter in the uncapped sampler.

There is also a precise limit to the GLMP comparison. Its Section 6.2 enumerates learned-weight possibilities with size of the form exp(poly(n) log(S+1)); the number depends on the confidence-dependent sample count S. It does **not** prove the candidate's confidence-independent support or an absolute exponent c in poly(k). Nor is enumerating all histograms of a scalar support of size 2^B a valid substitute: that could be far too expensive. The median-threshold CDF is the specific simplification that avoids that issue.

Nevertheless, after choosing the already-paid median statistic, that simplification is an elementary one-dimensional repeated-sample law. Its output is consumed by only polynomially many scalar queries. Ordinary finite marginalization supplies the macro-history formula, and elementary geometric normalization supplies the unbounded-retry law. Allowing a singly exponential bit-size denominator is enough to make these pieces compatible with GLMP correction; it demands no new cancellation or spectral estimate. The entire effect therefore follows from an existing rejection generator and an existing law-compression/correction strategy, with routine exact-probability bookkeeping. This is the affirmative operation-level reason for TECHNICAL; it is not a judgment based solely on the simplicity of individual formulas or on a stronger printed headline.

It is conceivable that the assembled generic compiler has not previously been stated in this form. This review has not established that claim, and it is not needed for the campaign assessment. A first formulation of a compatible generic corollary can remain a technical deduction. Conversely, a future argument identifying an essential operation not supplied by the above reproduction would justify reopening this gate. No such operation is identified here.
