OP-00853 Open

The happy-edge conjecture for convex plane spanning trees

Submit a result

Let $P \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be a finite set in strictly convex position. A plane spanning tree on $P$ is a spanning tree with vertex set $P$ whose edges are straight segments, no two crossing. A flip removes one edge and inserts another so that the result is again a plane spanning tree; the removed and inserted edges may cross. The edges in $E(T) \cap E(T')$ are the happy edges of $T$ and $T'$.

Aichholzer, Ballinger, Biedl, Damian, Demaine, Hesterberg, Korman, Lubiw, Lynch, Tkadlec and Uno proved that every shortest flip sequence keeps the happy edges on the boundary of the convex hull, checked by computer that the property in the problem holds for $|P| \le 10$, and showed that its analogue for edge slides fails. Aichholzer, Dorfer and Vogtenhuber proved that for compatible flips, in which the removed and inserted edges do not cross, no shortest sequence of compatible flips removes a happy edge, and that the analogue for rotations fails. Bjerkevik, Kleist, Ueckerdt and Vogtenhuber constructed, for $|P| \ge 3$ and every pair of trees, a flip sequence of length at most $\tfrac53 |E(T) \setminus E(T')| + \tfrac23 b - \tfrac43$ that flips no happy edge, where $b$ is the number of happy edges on the boundary of the convex hull.

Problem. Prove or disprove that for any two plane spanning trees $T$ and $T'$ on $P$ there is a shortest flip sequence $T = T_0, T_1, \ldots, T_k = T'$ with $$E(T) \cap E(T') \subseteq E(T_i) \qquad (0 \le i \le k).$$

Source: Aichholzer, Ballinger, Biedl, Damian, Demaine, Hesterberg, Korman, Lubiw, Lynch, Tkadlec and Uno (2024), the Happy Edge Conjecture (Conjecture 17), stated for convex point sets in the arXiv version (2022); Aichholzer, Dorfer and Vogtenhuber (2025) also credit a personal communication of F. Hurtado (CCCG 2003).
