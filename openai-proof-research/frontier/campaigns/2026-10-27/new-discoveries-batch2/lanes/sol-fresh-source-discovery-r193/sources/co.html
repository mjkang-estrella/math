Title: Average eccentricity in k-connected tournaments
Fadekemi Janet Osaye
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

The average eccentricity $\avec(D)$ of a strong digraph $D$ is the mean eccentricity of all vertices in $D$. A tournament $T$ is an orientation of a complete graph. In this paper, we prove that the average eccentricity satisfies $2 \le \avec(T) < \frac{n}{2k} + \frac{11}{2} + \frac{k}{8n}$ for a $k$-connected tournament $T$ of order $n$. We further examine the case for strong tournaments where $k = 1$, and show that $\avec(T) \le \frac{n+11}{2} + \frac{1}{8n}$ for any strong tournament $T$. Moreover, we show that the lower bound $2$ is sharp and that the upper bounds are best possible apart from an additive constant.

Title: On Hamilton cycles in quasiprimitive graphs of order $2pq$
Hao Yu, Tianlei Zhou
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO); Group Theory (math.GR)

Let $p>q>5$ be primes. We determine the socles and corresponding actions of quasiprimitive imprimitive groups of degree $2pq$, all of which are almost simple. Using this classification, we prove that a connected graph of order $2pq$ admitting a quasiprimitive automorphism group contains a Hamilton cycle, except possibly for three specified families of actions with socle $\PSL(2,p)$ or $\PSL(2,q^2)$.

Title: A generalization of pseudocomplementation in semilattices, lattices and posets with 0
Ivan Chajda, Helmut Länger
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

Pseudocomplemented lattices satisfy a lot of nice properties and were studied by a number of algebraists. However, even when a poset P with 0 is not pseudocomplemented, we can assign to every element a of P the set $a^p$ of all maximal elements being orthogonal to a. This assignment $^p$ is a non-trivial operator provided P satisfies the so-called Ascending Chain Condition (ACC). In particular, we investigate this operator when the poset P in question is a semilattice or a lattice. We characterize this operator and describe its properties that turn out to be similar to the corresponding properties of pseudocomplementation. Moreover, we introduce an equivalence relation $\Theta_p$ on P induced by $^p$ similarly as it was done by V. I. Glivenko for distributive pseudocomplemented lattices. Finally, we investigate for which lattices L with 0 satisfying the ACC this equivalence relation turns out to be a congruence and when the quotient lattice $L/\Theta_p$ is complemented respectively modular.

Title: Existence of uniformly most reliable two-terminal graphs with distance constraints
Pablo Romero
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

A two-terminal graph is a simple graph equipped with two distinguished vertices, called terminals. Let $T_{n,m}$ be the class consisting of all non-isomorphic two-terminal graphs on $n$ vertices and $m$ edges. Let $G$ be any two-terminal graph in $T_{n,m}$, and let $d$ be any positive integer. For every $\rho\in [0,1]$, the \emph{$d$-constrained two-terminal reliability of $G$ at $\rho$}, denoted $R_G^d(\rho)$, is the probability that $G$ has some path of length at most $d$ joining its terminals after each of its edges is independently deleted with probability $\rho$. We say $G$ is a \emph{$d$-uniformly most reliable two-terminal graph} ($d$-UMRTTG) if for each $H$ in $T_{n,m}$ and every $\rho \in [0,1]$ it holds that $R_{G}^d(\rho)\geq R_{H}^{d}(\rho)$. Previous works studied the existence of $d$-UMRTTG in $T_{n,m}$ when $d$ is greater than or equal to $n-1$, or equivalently, when the distance constraint is dropped. In this work, a characterization of all $1$-UMRTTGs and $2$-UMRTTGs is given. Several structural properties of 3-UMRTTGs are established. For every $d\geq 3$, we prove the existence of a unique $d$-UMRTTG in $T_{n,\binom{n}{2}-1}$. In contrast, for every $d\geq 4$ and every $n\geq 11$, no $d$-UMRTTG exists in $T_{n,m}$ whenever $20\leq m\leq 3n-9$ or $3n-5\leq m\leq \binom{n}{2}-2$. Additionally, for each $d\geq 5$, it is proved that no $d$-UMRTTG exists in $T_{n,m}$ when $n\geq 11$ and $m=3n-8$.

Title: Trace-minimal graphs maximizing the number of spanning trees
Pablo Romero, Louis Petingi
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

A classical problem in spectral graph theory is to determine, among all simple graphs with a fixed number of vertices and edges, those that maximize the number of spanning trees. Such graphs are called $t$-optimal. Petingi and Rodríguez [Discr. Math. 244 (2002), 351-373] reduced the construction of infinitely many $t$-optimal graphs to the determination of almost-regular graphs with a minimum number of induced $3$-paths, known as almost-regular $\nu$-min graphs. They conjectured that the class of $d$-regular $\nu$-min graphs is invariant under the addition or deletion of copies of the complete graph $K_{d+1}$, which would provide a method to construct large families of $t$-optimal graphs. This conjecture was recently verified for $d\in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ by Gong et al. [Discr. Math. 349(4), 2026].
In this work, we establish an asymptotic version of this conjecture. We prove that the class of $d$-regular $\nu$-min graphs on at least $n$ vertices is invariant under the addition or deletion of copies of $K_{d+1}$ whenever $d\geq 3$ and $n\geq d^3$. In addition, we introduce a new method to construct $t$-optimal graphs based on the concept of trace-minimal graphs, originally introduced by Ábrego et al. [Linear Algebra Appl. 412 (2006), 161-221]. As a consequence, we obtain infinitely many graphs that are both trace-minimal and $t$-optimal.

Title: Mutation-acyclicity in rank four is decidable but not semialgebraic
Kymani T. K. Armstrong-Williams, Edward Hirst, Blake Jackson, Kyu-Hwan Lee
Comments: 30 pages
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO); High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th); Representation Theory (math.RT)

We prove that mutation-acyclicity of quivers on four vertices is decidable. If a four-vertex quiver $Q$ is mutation-acyclic, then some sequence of mutations takes it to an acyclic quiver without any arrow multiplicity exceeding $\operatorname{max}\{\operatorname{ht}(Q),m(Q)\}$, where $\operatorname{ht}(Q)$ is the largest arrow multiplicity of $Q$ and $m(Q)$ is the Markov invariant of Fomin and Neville evaluated on the totally proper wiggle class of $Q$. A search within a computable form of this bound decides mutation-acyclicity of four-vertex quivers with $O(h^{24})$ states for input height $h\ge1$. Together with a theorem of Caldero and Keller, it also decides mutation equivalence of two four-vertex quivers up to relabeling whenever one of them is mutation-acyclic. We conjecture that $\operatorname{ht}(Q)$ alone suffices as the bound, which would reduce the search to $O(h^{6})$ states, and we verify this for all four-vertex quivers of height at most three. In the other direction, for every $n\ge4$ we construct an affine family of connected $n$-vertex quivers whose nonnegative even members are mutation-acyclic and whose positive odd members are mutation-cyclic. Consequently, unlike in rank three, no fixed finite Boolean combination of polynomial equations and strict polynomial inequalities in the arrow weights (i.e., a semialgebraic criterion) decides mutation-acyclicity of $n$-vertex quivers, even allowing polynomials that are not mutation-invariant. Finally, we correct the criterion for four-vertex quivers of height at most two stated in our earlier paper, and recover its classification counts with short certificates. The calculations in the proof of the height bound are verified by computer for all values of their parameters.

Title: On the diameter and distribution of Sidon sets
Daniel Carter, Kevin O'Bryant, Terence Tao
Comments: 33 pages, 9 figures
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

A Sidon set is a set $\mathcal{A}$ of integers with the property that if $0\neq a-b=c-d$ and $a,b,c,d\in\mathcal{A}$, then $(a,b)=(c,d)$. Our main result is an improved and explicit bound on the diameter of a Sidon set: if $\mathcal{A}$ is a Sidon set with $k\ge 1$ elements, then
\[\operatorname{diam}(\mathcal{A}) \ge k^2 \left(\frac{1}{1 + 0.945/\sqrt{k}}-\frac{0.945}{\sqrt k}\right) -1;\] in particular, $\operatorname{diam}(\mathcal{A})\ge k^2-1.89 k^{3/2}$. This implies that if $\mathcal{A}\subseteq[1,N]$ is a Sidon set, then $|\mathcal{A}| \le N^{1/2}+0.945 \, N^{1/4}+0.447$. Our technique also gives an improved bound for the case of large $k$, namely $\operatorname{diam}(\mathcal{A}) \ge k^2-1.88607 k^{3/2}$ for $k\ge 483$. A $\gamma$-Golomb ruler is a set $\mathcal{A}$ of integers with the property that there are not $\gamma+1$ distinct pairs $(a_i,b_i)\in\mathcal{A}^2$ with $0\neq a_1-b_1=\cdots=a_{\gamma+1}-b_{\gamma+1}$. Our diameter bounds extend in a natural way to the case of Golomb rulers. Our techniques can also be adapted to address a number of questions on the distribution of Golomb rulers: we provide upper bounds on the two quantities $\left|a_\ell-a_1 -\frac{\ell-1}{k-1}(a_k-a_1)\right|$ and $\max_{1\le i<k} (a_{i+1}-a_i)$, where $\mathcal{A}=\{a_1<\dots<a_k\}$ is a $\gamma$-Golomb ruler with $\operatorname{diam}(\mathcal{A})\le (k^2-bk^{3/2})/\gamma$ (where $b\in \mathbb{R}$ is a free parameter).

Title: Characterizing $3$-graphs with Degenerate Codegree Turán Density
Dylan King, Simón Piga, Marcelo Sales, Bjarne Schülke
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

Determining the Turán densities $\pi(F)$, uniform Turán densities $\pi_{\therefore}(F)$, and codegree Turán densities $\gamma(F)$ of $3$-graphs $F$ is a notoriously difficult problem in combinatorics. A landmark result by Erdős characterized the $3$-graphs whose Turán density is zero. Half a century later, Reiher, Rödl, and Schacht characterized the $3$-graphs whose uniform Turán density is zero. Recently, Ding, Lamaison, Liu, Wang, and Yang suggested a possible characterization of $3$-graphs whose codegree Turán density is zero, proved one direction of it, and conjectured the other. Title: Edge complexity of complete bipartite and complete multipartite graphs
S. Ferguson, V. Gupta, A. Iosevich, J. Iosevich, C. Kelly, G. Smith, S. Yang
Comments: 68 pages; computational verification programs and recorded results included as ancillary material
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

We study edge complexity, the minimum Fourier $\ell^1/\ell^2$ ratio of a graph's adjacency matrix over cyclic vertex labelings. For $K_{m,n}$, with $1\leq m\leq n$, we reduce the problem to minimizing the Fourier $\ell^1$-norm of indicators of $m$-element subsets of $\mathbb Z_{m+n}$. We prove the sharp bound $\operatorname{FR}_{\min}(K_{m,n})\geq\frac{4n^2}{(m+n)\sqrt{2mn}}$, with equality precisely when $m\mid n$, and classify all labelings attaining it. We determine all Fourier ratios and minimizing labelings for $K_{2,n}$, and all minimizing labelings for $K_{3,n}$. Put $N=n+3$. The optimal three-element sets are precisely the cosets of the subgroup of order $3$ in $\mathbb Z_N$ when $3\mid N$, and precisely the arithmetic progressions with invertible common difference otherwise. The triple classification uses polynomial minorants and additive energies. We prove that attainment of the graph-energy bound is invariant under uniform independent set blow-ups. Edge complexity itself can decrease by an arbitrarily large factor under a twofold blow-up, and we give a finite example. For complete multipartite graphs, we give an exact labeling formula and sharp lower bound, and classify all part sizes and labelings attaining that bound. This bound strictly improves the graph-energy bound when the part sizes are unequal. We prove a lower bound for arbitrary simple graphs in terms of their average degree and maximum absolute degree deviation. For Laplacian projectors, we apply the equality criterion for the multiplicity bound to unbalanced complete bipartite graphs, whose top-projector minimum reduces to the same set minimization. Finally, we apply existing Fourier recovery and sampling theorems to approximate a graph from random adjacency samples under a fixed known labeling with bounded Fourier ratio, bounding the number of incorrectly recovered edges.

Alexander Clow
Comments: 23 Pages
Subjects: Combinatorics (math.CO)

In 2019, Sivaraman conjectured that every $P_k$-free graph has cop number at most $k-3$ for $k\geq 5$. Chudnovsky, Norin, Seymour, and Turcotte proved $P_5$-free graphs have cop number at most $2$. For $k\geq 6$ Sivaraman's conjecture remains widely open. There is little evidence to believe Sivaraman's conjecture is optimal, making the discovery of substantially better bounds interesting, even for subclasses of $P_k$-free graphs. We consider this problem for graphs with no long holes, which generalizes $P_k$-free graphs, that also have some forbidden shorter holes. Specifically, we show that for $t\geq 13$, $(C_4,\dots, C_{t}, C_k,C_{k+1},\dots)$-free graphs have cop number at most $\lceil \frac{k-2}{\lfloor \frac{t}{4}\rfloor }\rceil+1$. Additionally, for $t\geq 2$ we show that $(C_{t+2},C_{t+3},C_k, C_{k+1},\dots, )$-free graphs have cop number at most $(t-1)\lceil \frac{(k-2)}{t}\rceil$. We first prove that the Fourier support of a nonempty proper subset of density different from one half contains a two-dimensional subspace. Consequently, every spectral set of density one quarter has a subspace tiling complement. We count Boolean functions represented as the sign of a real polynomial of bounded degree on the $n$-dimensional cube.
