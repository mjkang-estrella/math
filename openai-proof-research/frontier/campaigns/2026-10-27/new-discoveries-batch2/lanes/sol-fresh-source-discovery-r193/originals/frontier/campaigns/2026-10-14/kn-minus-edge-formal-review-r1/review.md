## Mathematical audit

The row-by-row assessment of all eleven `coverage.json` rows is in `coverage-audit.json`. The following explains the substantive checks and limits.

1. **Tensor kernel and actual functional.** `tensor_kernel_iff` proves exactly the intersection of the two finite-coordinate contraction kernels equals sums of tensors with both factors in `ker phi`. Its projected-basis construction uses the explicit witness `contract phi e=1`; the reverse implication checks both slots. It is not a characterization of all zero crossing tests. `missingFunctional` is the actual antisymmetric polynomial evaluation, and its normalized witness `(a-b)^(-1) x` is valid over any field with `a!=b`. The finite bounded-polynomial coordinate identification, including placing the witness in a particular degree space, is uninstantiated and disclosed. The historical necessity/iff overstatement is not repeated in the formal package.

2. **Split dimensions and mixed dimension.** `slotEval` is an actual linear map with target `iota -> K`. Its right inverse proves surjectivity, so its kernel has dimension `d^2-d`, not the two-slot intersection dimension `(d-1)^2`. `split_kernel_dimension` is valid with finite-dimensional ambient domain; surjectivity supplies finiteness of the target. In `mixed_dimension_bound`, the ambient space is finite dimensional and nondegenerate, both maps are injective, and mixed orthogonality puts the second range in the first range's orthogonal complement. Neither self-isotropy nor a direct-sum assumption appears. Although the source type does not assume finite dimensionality of A/B, injection into finite V supplies it; this is not misuse of `finrank=0` on an infinite-dimensional space. `mixed_coordinate_bound` uses the concrete weighted diagonal form and retains **both** injections. The actual symmetric bases, `d=binom(s,2)`, actual images, and slot-swap application remain gaps.

3. **Concrete form and mixed polynomial tests.** `diagonalForm` is the displayed weighted sum; its nondegeneracy proof tests individual coordinates and requires every weight nonzero. `delta` has exactly the stated six differences in the `x,u,y,v` convention. Its degree budget, both within-pair sign reversals, and nonvanishing on four distinct values are proved. `mixed_test_degree` and `mixed_test_missing` use the actual product `P Q Delta` and the first/second split hypotheses in the correct slots and both endpoint orders. These results do not establish graph mixed orthogonality. The extraction of four equal endpoint-orientation contributions, the nonzero drawing coefficients, whole-edge order multiplicity, and `c(D)>=|T|/2` remain explicitly unformalized.

4. **Rectangular twisted detector.** `orderPair` is the explicit finite weighted evaluation sum. With `d=s-2`, `RectangularNondegenerate` states **right** nondegeneracy on `P_(d+1,d) x P_(d,d+1)`. `rectangle_twist` places `(v-c) A` in the second rectangle. Commutativity of evaluated products transfers all twisted tests to the first rectangle, forcing `(v-c)A=0` as a polynomial. The final step cancels a nonzero polynomial in an integral domain, never inverts its value at a node. This is a genuine deduction from the stronger rectangular premise, not a restatement of a square detector conclusion.

   `twisted_detector_from_U4` uses `2(d+2)` **real** nodes, their actual reciprocal derivative weights, and signs `+/-1/2` from an arbitrary permutation order. Every finite prescribed linear order can be represented this way. Its explicit `UpstreamDetectorPremise` matches the part of pinned U4 used with `s=d+2>=2`. It is a defined proposition supplied as a theorem argument, not a new axiom or a proof of upstream U4. The generic theorem's arbitrary field/weights/signs do not purport to generalize U4 itself.

5. **Local product and localization.** `localTest` includes all factors `P (u-v)(u-y)(v-x) U(x) (y-a)V'(y) (v-a)B'(u,v)`. The actual separate-degree budgets are `P:(d,d,d,d)`, `U:(d+2,0,0,0)`, `V':(0,0,d+1,0)`, and `B':(0,d+1,0,d)`. Every resulting degree is at most `2d+3=n-2`. Both first-slot missing substitutions use P; the two second-slot orders vanish through different twist factors. No identification of pointwise vanishing with polynomial vanishing is hidden here.

   `linear_zero_of_products` proves the span by splitting each bounded monomial. `localize_away` constructs the full Lagrange isolator, divides by `X-t_a`, and derives the degree bound from `card iota<=r+t+2`. With `r=s,t=s-1`, this gives the required `2s+1` nodes. Its conclusion is only at `i0!=a`, without an assumption about sigma at a or b.

   `localized_twisted_vanishing` then restricts rectangular detection and `hidentify` to indices outside **both** endpoints and uses **all** summed equations `htests`. In particular, `localNodes` are abstract and are not proved to enumerate global nodes other than i, nor is the distinguished global node proved to be a local node. The generic detector permits any twist constant, so the conditional deduction is still valid. Connecting the abstract data to the actual complete-star nodes, weights, signs, and diagonal specialization remains part of the disclosed drawing bridge. **`hidentify` and `htests` are genuinely unproved new bridge obligations, not upstream premises.** They contain substantive missing work but do not assume the final conclusion `A i=0`. No endpoint order is assumed. The theorem does not need `a!=b` for this conditional implication; the later root theorem does require it.

6. **Roots, division, cancellation, and descent.** `diagonal_root_bound` really counts the two erased distinct endpoints, leaving `2s-1` distinct constant-polynomial nodes; for `s>=2` this exceeds `2s-4`. It works in `Polynomial (MvPolynomial (Fin 2) K)`, a domain. Building this univariate polynomial from `P(x,u,x,v)` and transporting its degree/evaluations is absent. `collision_divisibility` is only the univariate factor theorem; it does not establish the four distinct prime factors in the original multivariate polynomial.

   The four-factor collision polynomial has degree two in each variable, both swap invariances, and nonzero values on independent coordinates. Its missing specialization is a **nonzero polynomial even when a=b**, but can vanish at some points. The cancellation in `quotient_preserves_missing` is therefore sound. Symmetry and evaluation cancellation and the exact degree loss all retain their necessary nonzero/factorization assumptions. `degree_descent` proves well-founded termination for a predicate W only after a caller supplies `hdivide`: every nonzero W-member factors by C with a quotient still satisfying W. That is a usable generic tool; its main obligation is uninstantiated for this evaluation kernel. The `d=0,1` obstruction handles `s=2,3` only **conditional on collision divisibility**. The zero polynomial has `natDegree=degreeOf=0`; every exact additive degree claim appropriately excludes zero factors. No negative degree space is silently used.

7. **Scope and assumptions.** All generic tensor/polynomial results use the stated field/domain hypotheses; no infinitude of K is silently used. Root counting needs distinct supplied nodes, so the hypotheses themselves restrict a finite field's possible size. The skew self-pairing lemma explicitly assumes `CharZero`; without a characteristic restriction, skew need not mean alternating. The U4 wrapper alone specializes to real nodes. Nothing asserts graph topology, cycles, interpolation-weight cancellation for cycles, star-pairing identification, diagonal substitution, four-prime-factor divisibility, either evaluation injection, the even responsibility argument, upper drawings, all-n assembly, or the external product theorem in Lean.
