   MONOTONICITY OF TURÁN-RATIOS OF SYMMETRIC POLYNOMIALS

                                                       SUVRIT SRA*


        Abstract. We study the Turán-ratio Sλ,i (x)2 /(Sλ,i−1 (x)Sλ,i+1 (x)), where Sλ,i (x) := sλ (1[n−i] , x[i] ),
        and sλ is the Schur polynomial in n variables (indexed by partition λ) with i arguments equal to
        x and n − i of them equal to 1. Specifically, we prove that this Turán-ratio is strictly increasing
        for x > 1 and 1 ≤ i < n (unless all n padded parts of λ are equal, then it is identically one).
        For one-row shapes, our result answers a question of Ait-Haddou about complete homogeneous
        symmetric polynomials. For this case, we also offer a second proof through the zeros of Meixner
        polynomials that gives an explicit positive formula for the logarithmic derivative. We conclude with
        a Turán-ratio conjecture for Macdonald polynomials.
