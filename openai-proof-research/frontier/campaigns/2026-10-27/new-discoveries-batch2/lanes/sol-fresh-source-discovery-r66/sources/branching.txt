2. Preliminaries on Macdonald polynomials

with X = {x1 , . . . , xn } (and refer to X as an alphabet) instead of f (x) with x = (x1 , . . . , xn ), the latter

also use f ( X + Y ) where X + Y denotes the (disjoint) union of the alphabets X and Y , and f ( X + z)
