[1712.03422] On the saturation number of graphs Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Combinatorics arXiv:1712.03422 (math) [Submitted on 9 Dec 2017] Title: On the saturation number of graphs Authors: Saeid Alikhani , Neda Soltani View a PDF of the paper titled On the saturation number of graphs, by Saeid Alikhani and Neda Soltani View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Let $G=(V,E)$ be a simple connected graph. A matching $M$ in a graph $G$ is a collection of edges of $G$ such that no two edges from $M$ share a vertex. A matching $M$ is maximal if it cannot be extended to a larger matching in $G$. The cardinality of any smallest maximal matching in $G$ is the saturation number of $G$ and is denoted by $s(G)$. In this paper we study the saturation number of the corona product of two specific graphs. We also consider some graphs with certain constructions that are of importance in chemistry and study their saturation number.
