[2610.22142] Wild number for spanning trees in complete and complete bipartite graphs Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Combinatorics arXiv:2610.22142 (math) [Submitted on 15 Oct 2026] Title: Wild number for spanning trees in complete and complete bipartite graphs Authors: Aleyah Dawkins , Alexander Nappo , Jessie Tong , Christopher Zimbizi View a PDF of the paper titled Wild number for spanning trees in complete and complete bipartite graphs, by Aleyah Dawkins and 3 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Given an edge-colored graph, the wild number measures how close the graph is to having a spanning tree in every color. We investigate this parameter for complete graphs and complete bipartite graphs, with particular emphasis on minimum proper colorings and equitable $2$-colorings. For complete graphs, we determine the full range of wild numbers attainable by equitable $2$-colorings and show that, although every value in this range occurs, the expected wild number of a uniformly random equitable $2$-coloring tends to $0$ as the order of the graph grows. For minimum proper colorings of $K_n$, we establish a general lower bound, determine the exact wild number for $3 \le n \le 12$, and conjecture that the lower bound is sharp for all $n$. We also develop structural conditions on ideal wild sets and use them to obtain upper bounds and analyze the performance of a natural greedy algorithm. For complete bipartite graphs, we prove a sharp upper bound for equitable $2$-colorings and determine the wild number exactly for minimum proper colorings. Finally, we apply these results to Cartesian products of complete graphs and complete bipartite graphs, obtaining lower bounds for the wild number under minimum proper and product colorings.
