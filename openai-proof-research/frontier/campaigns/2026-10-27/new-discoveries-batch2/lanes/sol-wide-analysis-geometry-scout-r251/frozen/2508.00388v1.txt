Therefore the main objective of this paper is to supply the possible answers of the above
proposed queries (Q(a) to Q(c)). To reach our objectives first we give an affirmative answer
to the question Q(a). That is we prove that the range of α from {0}∪[1/3, 1) can be extended
to a larger one [0, 1) for which the improvement of (1.8) is still possible. Consequently, we
strengthen the result of Gupta [5]. When providing an answer to Q(b), we have seen that
for any decreasing sequence {qn }, the improvement of (1.4) is attainable for α ∈ [1/3, 1).
But we couldn’t manage to answer this question for an arbitrary increasing sequence {qn }.
Rather, we have shown that there exists improvements of (1.4) for some strictly increasing
sequences {qn } such as qn = n, and qn = n3 , n ∈ N. We prove that the Copson’s inequality
(1.4) for qn = n achieves an improvement when α ∈ [17/50, 1), whereas for qn = n3 , Copson’s
inequality (1.4) gets an improvement when α ∈ [0, 1/2].
The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we establish an improved Hardy’s inequality
with power weights nα for α ∈ [0, 1). Section 3 deals with the improvement of the Copson
inequality (1.4) for an arbitrary sequence {qn }. In fact, in first subsection we have shown an
improved Copson inequality (1.4) for an arbitrary decreasing sequence {qn }, and in another
subsection, we have proved an improved Copson inequality (1.4) for some increasing sequences
{qn }.
