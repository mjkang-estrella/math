(2022), and are known to be unimodularly equivalent to strength-one flow polytopes for acyclic directed graphs strongly dual to generalized snake posets. Lee, Vindas-Meléndez, and Wang (2026) conjectured that the Ehrhart $h^*$-polynomials of these order polytopes are real-rooted. We prove this conjecture using a connection between these $h^*$-polynomials and non-nesting rook polynomials, which were recently introduced by Alexandersson and Jal (2024+) in connection with $P$-Eulerian polynomials for width two posets. Comments: 11 pages, 1 figure.
