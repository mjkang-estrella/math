                                                    Abstract. For n ≥ 2, we study the optimal function φn in the Brunn–
                                                    Minkowski inequality for θ-convolution bodies
                                                                      |K +θ L|1/n ≥ φn (θ) |K|1/n + |L|1/n
                                                                                                           

                                                    for any convex bodies K, L ⊂ Rn . We improve the known lower bound for every
                                                    0 < θ < (3/4)n and determine φn on an interval strictly containing [(2/3)n , 1].
                                                    For θ < 1 in this interval, equality holds if and only if K and −L are translates
                                                    of the same n-dimensional simplex. The proof reduces the geometric problem
                                                    to a sharp one-dimensional estimate for nonnegative concave functions under
                                                    a weighted integral constraint.

                                 2. Preliminaries
   We recall the notation and the classical facts used in the proof. A convex body
is a compact convex subset of Rn with nonempty interior, and | · | always denotes
n-dimensional Lebesgue measure. We write K − K = K + (−K) for the difference
body of K.
   For convex bodies K, L, the generalized covariogram is the convolution of their
indicator functions:

and attained. For a fixed pair K, L, we write
