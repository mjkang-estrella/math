2.1. Notation. Given a convex body K ⊆ Rn with 0 ∈ intK, we will denote by
ρK the radial function ρK : S n−1 → [0, ∞) given by ρK (u) = max{λ ≥ 0 : λu ∈
K}, where S n−1 denotes the (n − 1)-dimensional Euclidean sphere in Rn , that is,
S n−1 = {u ∈ Rn : kuk2 = 1}. It is well known that given two convex bodies
K1 , K2 ∈ Rn we have that K1 ⊆ K2 ⇔ ρK1 (u) ≤ ρK2 (u) for every u ∈ S n−1 . k · kK
will denote the Minkowski gauge, defined as
                             kxkK = inf{λ > 0 : x ∈ λK}.
Notice that for every u ∈ S n−1 we have that kukK = ρK1(u) . χK will denote the
characteristic function of a convex body K and K − K will always denote the
difference body K + (−K). Whenever K ⊆ Rn is a k-dimensional set contained
in an affine k-dimensional subspace, |K| will denote its k-dimensional Lebesgue
measure. B2n will stand for the Euclidean unit ball, k · k2 for the Euclidean norm
and ∆n will stand for the regular simplex.
