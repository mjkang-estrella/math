Title:
Improved Brunn--Minkowski inequality for $θ$-convolution bodies

    
Authors:
Javier Martín Goñi
            
View a PDF of the paper titled Improved Brunn--Minkowski inequality for $\theta$-convolution bodies, by Javier Mart\'in Go\~ni

    
View PDF

    
HTML (experimental)




    

            
Abstract:
For $n\geq 2$, we study the optimal function $\varphi_n$ in the Brunn-Minkowski inequality for $\theta$-convolution bodies $|K+_\theta L|^{1/n}\geq\varphi_n(\theta)(|K|^{1/n}+|L|^{1/n})$ for any convex bodies $K,L\subset\mathbb{R}^n$. We improve the known lower bound for every $0<\theta<(3/4)^n$ and determine $\varphi_n$ on an interval strictly containing $[(2/3)^n,1]$. For $\theta<1$ in this interval, equality holds if and only if $K$ and $-L$ are translates of the same $n$-dimensional simplex. The proof reduces the geometric problem to a sharp one-dimensional estimate for nonnegative concave functions under a weighted integral constraint.
