Exact hypotheses matter. A tournament is a bounded sequential randomized match algorithm on n players, with fixed pair probabilities P in M_n = {P in [0,1]^(n*n): P+P^T=1}. Doubly monotonic D_n permits ties and probabilities 0 and 1: entries decrease in the player index and increase in the opponent index. Symmetry means equivariance of winning probabilities under every relabeling for every P. Honesty means that, at every state with the next pair announced and every P, forcing one's next match to be a win gives at least the winning probability obtained by forcing it to be a loss, with subsequent play governed by P. It is not merely effort preference at one displayed strength matrix. Remark 2.1 requires attention to internal randomness timing.
