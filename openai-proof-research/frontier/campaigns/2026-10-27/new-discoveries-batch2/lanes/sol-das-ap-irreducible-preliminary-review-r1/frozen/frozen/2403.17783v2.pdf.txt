                                                                                  1. Introduction
                                              Let G be a finite transitive permutation group on Ω . A pair of permutations
                                           x, y ∈ G is said to intersect if
                                                                   {(ω, ω x)| ω ∈ Ω } ∩ {(ω, ω y )| ω ∈ Ω } 6= ∅,
                                           in other words, the ratio xy −1 fixes some point in Ω.
                                           Definition 1.1. A subset S of a transitive permutation group G is said to be an
                                           intersecting subset if any pair of elements in S intersect.
                                              Clearly, a point stabilizer Gω and its cosets are all intersecting subsets. An EKR-
                                           type problem in permutation group theory is to study the cardinality and the struc-
                                           ture of intersecting subsets in transitive permutation groups, where ‘EKR’ standards
                                           for Erdős-Ko-Rado, refer to [14]. An intersecting subset is said to be maximum if it
                                           is an intersecting subset of the maximum possible size.
                                              A transitive permutation group G on Ω has the EKR property if its point stabiliz-
                                           ers are maximum intersecting subsets. There have been many papers published in
                                           literature that study groups with EKR property, refer to [1, 5, 13, 14, 19, 22, 26, 27]
                                           and references therein. In particular, it was shown in [27] that all 2-transitive
                                           permutation groups have the EKR property. A recent result [25] shows that the
                                           characteristic vector of any maximum intersecting subset of 2-transitive groups is
                                           a linear combination of characteristic vectors of cosets of point stabilizers. How-
                                           ever, the results in [20] show that a permutation group in general does not have the
                                           property.
                                             Key words and phrases. EKR property, semiregular subsets, intersecting subsets.

   In this paper, we explore intersecting subsets of permutation groups in more
general case. It is not hard to show that an intersecting subset S of a transitive group
G ⩽ Sym(Ω) can be much larger than Gω , for instance, in a transitive permutation
representation of G = PSL(2, 2e ) of degree |G|/2, each Sylow 2-subgroup is an
intersecting subset. This shows that not only a transitive permutation group is in
general “far from satisfying the EKR property”, but also the ratio |S|/|Gω | can be
arbitrarily large.                                                                 p 
