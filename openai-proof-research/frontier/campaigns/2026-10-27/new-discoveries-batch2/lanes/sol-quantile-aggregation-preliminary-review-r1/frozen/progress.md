# Routes and outcome

1. Source-fidelity and later-resolution screen: froze scout and source PDF/text before reading. Read literal definitions, Theorem 1 proof, quantile-mixture definitions, Proposition 1/2, Conjecture 1 and Example 3. Current arXiv version remains v2. Narrow quantile-mixture and quantile/aggregation/mixability searches found no complete later resolution; not exhaustive clearance.
2. Random permutation construction: source Birkhoff proof cannot transfer to quantile averages because the marginal laws are distribution mixtures. Rejected as a universal construction.
3. Constant-sum/joint-mixability route: mean-length condition survives averaging in source Example 3, but that checks a single point-mass sum law. Later negative-association/decomposition theorems also control constant sums, not arbitrary H. No universal lift obtained. Convex-order comparisons have the same limitation for nonconvex payoffs.
4. Two-coordinate exact transport obstruction: randomly sampled convex piecewise-linear quantiles and 1-Lipschitz polygonal payoffs, comparing finite assignment optima. 2,400 trials at N=100 produced a positive diagnostic gap but not one exceeding the initially conservative error estimate. Rather than treating that failure as evidence for inclusion, refined the best candidate at N=200 and generated a rational dual feasible certificate.
5. First dual solver aborted (exit 134); kept caps unchanged and reran with HiGHS threads=1, which succeeded. Converted dual to rational upward rounding, then checked all inequalities exactly.
6. Independent standard-library checker verified the explicit permutation, density monotonicity, 40,000 rational inequalities, totals, and strict continuous margin. Complete refutation obtained; no breadth expansion or arbitrary-DS construction needed. The full universal assertion fails at an elementary 2x2 averaging matrix. A proof only for products of T-transforms would therefore already be false; no unsupported factorization claim is used.
