        A convergent cascade series without geometric decay
                                            Research manuscript

                                          29 October 2026
                          Credit 0; pending final review; no priority claim


                                                      Abstract
           We give an explicit binary saturated context tree, with exactly one infinite branch and no
      infinite shift-stable subtree, equipped with fair probabilities at every finite and infinite context.
      Its cascade series for the fixed alpha-LIS 1 converges with sum at most 2, but admits no eventual
      bound Crn with C > 0 and 0 < r < 1. The index is the length grading of equation (5) of
      Cénac, Chauvin, Noûs, Paccaut and Pouyanne [1]. This contradicts the explicit geometric-rate
      clause of their Conjecture 4.2, under this literal rate interpretation. It does not refute the universal
      bare-convergence conjecture. Classical prefix-code counting and Kraft’s inequality receive no
      novelty credit. A closed Lean certificate accompanies the construction; formal checking supplies
      no literature-priority conclusion.


1    Source conventions and exact claim
A word is a finite string over {0, 1}; ϵ is empty. Prefixes and suffixes retain the written left-to-right
order. A rooted context tree T is prefix closed and saturated: every internal node has both children.
Finite contexts are leaves; infinite contexts are branches all of whose finite prefixes are nodes. We
require at most countably many infinite contexts and a nontrivial tree. A probabilisation assigns to
each finite and infinite context c a positive law qc (0) + qc (1) = 1.
     For a nonempty noninternal word w, its LIS s is its longest internal strict suffix. Write w =
β1 · · · βp αs. The alpha-LIS is αs. For any noninternal word v, let cont(v) be its unique context
prefix. With σ deleting the first letter, the source cascade is

                                                       ∏
                                                       p
                                        casc(w) =            qcont(σi w) (βi ).
                                                       i=1

The minimality implicit in LIS makes each context in this product well defined. For a context alpha-
LIS t, the source series has terms
                                         ∑
                          κt (n) =                     casc(c),     n ≥ 1.
                                      c finite context: α-LIS(c)=t
                                               |c|=|t|+n−1

The certificate also sets the n = 0 term, which is zero for t = 1 here.

2      Statement and tree construction
A stable tree has σ(T ) ⊆ T . By an infinite shift-stable subtree we mean an infinite prefix-closed node
set contained in T and closed under σ; our exclusion below even allows subtrees without saturation.
Define geometric decay to mean

                           ∃C > 0 ∃r ∈ (0, 1) ∃n0 ∀n ≥ n0 ,       κt (n) ≤ Crn .

The source’s phrase “geometrical rates” is not a separate formal definition. We use this standard
length-based meaning, tied to its equation (5). An exponential bound on nonnegative series tails
implies this term bound, so that stronger interpretation is excluded too.

Theorem 1. There is a non-null probabilised binary context tree with countably many contexts and
no infinite shift-stable subtree, for which 1 is a context alpha-LIS and
              ∑
                    κ1 (n) ≤ 2,      ¬∃C > 0, r ∈ (0, 1), n0 : ∀n ≥ n0 , κ1 (n) ≤ Crn .
               n≥0

      Let b = (01)∞ and let pm be its prefix of length m. Put

                 N0 = 0,     Nk = k 2 (Nk−1 + k + 10),          ak = (01)k 00      (k ≥ 1).

Set
                                ∪
         I = {pm : m ≥ 0} ∪          {ak v : |ak v| < Nk },   T = I ∪ {wa : w ∈ I, a ∈ {0, 1}}.
                               k≥1

The ak are prefix incomparable: for j < k, their letters at position 2j + 2 (numbering from one)
disagree. Each ak is the off-spine child of p2k+1 . The recurrence gives strictly increasing depths and
Nk > 2k + 2; in particular N1 = 11 and N2 = 92. The set I is prefix closed. Completing it by both
children gives precisely I as internal set: an internal descendant of a node outside I would contradict
prefix closure of I. Thus T is saturated. Each bush has all its bottom leaves at depth Nk .
     A branch leaving b either reaches an ordinary leaf immediately or enters one finite bush. Con-
sequently b is the unique infinite context. Finite words are countable, so all contexts are countable.
Define qc (0) = qc (1) = 1/2 on every context, including b. This is normalized and uniformly positive.
It represents a fair iid process; no new process-mixing property is claimed.

3    Excluding stable subtrees and fixing one alpha-LIS
Every word of T of length at least three starts with 01. Indeed spine nodes and all bushes do, and
the only shorter off-spine possibilities terminate by depth two. In particular 10, 11 ∈ / T . If a prefix-
closed, shift-closed S ⊆ T contained a word of length at least three, its three-letter prefix 01a would
belong to S. Shifting gives 1a ∈ S ⊆ T , a contradiction. Such S therefore has only words of length
at most two, hence is finite.
    Fix k ≥ 3 and put

               H = Nk−1 + 3,        R = Nk − (2k + 2) − H = kB + r,            0 ≤ r < k.

Here R ≥ 0: using Nk = k 2 (Nk−1 + k + 10), subtracting Nk−1 + 2k + 5 leaves a positive integer
for k ≥ 3. For each independent choice of words ui of length k − 2, take

                                w = ak (11u1 )(11u2 ) · · · (11uB )1r+H .

A block 11ui has exactly k letters; there is no trailing extra letter. These words have length Nk and are
leaves in bush k. Fixed block boundaries recover every free bit, so their number is exactly 2(k−2)B .

Lemma 1. Every nonempty proper suffix of every selected w is noninternal. Consequently its LIS is
ϵ and its alpha-LIS is 1.

Proof. The terminal run has at least H ≥ 3 ones. Suffixes of length one and two are 1, 11, neither
internal. A longer suffix cannot be a spine prefix, since an alternating spine prefix cannot end in 111.
    An internal suffix in bush j < k would have length less than Nj ≤ Nk−1 . It would lie wholly in
the terminal ones, and could not begin with aj , which starts with zero.
    For j ≥ k, an anchor occurrence requires an initial alternating segment (01)k of length 2k. No
such segment starts in the tail after ak . To see this explicitly, the forced 11 pairs begin k positions
apart. A start inside a block before the next block boundary encounters the next complete pair within
the next k + 1 letters. If it is in the last block and no next block exists, it encounters two terminal ones
within the next k + 1 letters. A start at a boundary already encounters 11, and a start in the terminal
run does as well. A 2k window, whenever it fits, thus contains equal adjacent letters. This also covers
B = 0, when the whole tail is ones.

Completion of the suffix proof. A proper start within ak at a letter 1 cannot start an anchor. A start
at even offset 2h, 1 ≤ h < k, leaves only 2(k − h) alternating letters before the 00 defect. A start
at its penultimate zero meets that defect at once. A start at its last zero meets a tail beginning 11 and
alternates for at most two letters. None admits the required 2k letters. At offset zero, j > k is blocked
by the same 00 defect; j = k gives w itself rather than a proper suffix. These possibilities exhaust
both kinds of internal nodes. The empty suffix is internal, so the LIS is empty and the preceding letter
is the final 1.

    Also 1 itself is a leaf with alpha-LIS 1, so the chosen index belongs to the source’s set of context
alpha-LIS.


4    Counting and failure of geometric decay
Fair probabilities give one factor 1/2 for each letter preceding the alpha-LIS. Thus a selected leaf
has cascade 2−(Nk −1) . Since |1| = 1, the equation (5) index is exactly its length, not an arbitrary
enumeration of contexts. Define

                              Fk = (k − 2)B,            D k = N k − 1 − Fk .

The selected leaves give
                                    κ1 (Nk ) ≥ 2Fk −(Nk −1) = 2−Dk .
We have Fk ≤ Nk − 1, so Dk ≥ 0. Euclidean division gives B ≥ R/k − 1 and hence

                                    Dk  2 Nk−1 3k + 5
                               0≤      ≤ +     +
                                    Nk  k   Nk      Nk
                                        2  1     3k + 5
                                       ≤ + 2+ 2           −→ 0.
                                        k k    k (k + 10)

For the first inequality use Nk − 1 − (k − 2)B ≤ 2Nk /k + (2k + 2) + H + k. The recurrence gives
the other bounds, as well as Nk → ∞.
    Suppose an eventual Crn bound existed. The subsequence lower bound would imply

                                          Dk         log C
                                      −      log 2 ≤       + log r
                                          Nk          Nk
for all sufficiently large k. The left side tends to zero and the right side to log r < 0, a contradiction.
This proves the rate obstruction with all constants and cutoffs quantified. We do not need an optimal
rate or an exact root-limit statement.

5    Convergence and literal formal scope
Finite contexts form a prefix-free family. For any finite subfamily, the fair binary cylinder probabil-
ities 2−|c| sum to at most one, because the cylinders are disjoint. Taking the supremum over finite
subfamilies gives the classical countable Kraft bound. In the subfamily with alpha-LIS 1, the cascade
is 21−|c| . Grouping nonnegative terms by their lengths therefore gives
                                  ∑               ∑
                                      κ1 (n) =            21−|c| ≤ 2.
                                  n≥0           α-LIS(c)=1

This completes the theorem. Kraft and its application here are classical helpers, not additional pro-
posed outcomes.
    The nine immutable mathematical Lean modules are DepthDefs, SourceDefs, TreeConstruction,
DepthArithmetic, DepthAnalysis, SelectedFamily, SelectedCounting, KraftSummability and Cas-
cadeTheorem. Booleans encode 0, 1 as false,true. SourceDefs defines the actual context prefix, LIS,
alpha-LIS, probability laws on both context kinds, finite cascade product, and the exact finite length
sum. Its default definitions outside valid noninternal inputs are irrelevant: the factor-validity lemmas
prove all consumed suffix inputs valid. TreeConstruction supplies the admissible source tree and
exclusion of stable subtrees. The final closed endpoints, in namespace CascadeRate, are
      source_geometric_rate_refuted :
        ¬ SourceGeometricRateClaim

      convergent_non_geometric :
        Summable (kappa sourceTree (fairAssignment sourceTree) [true])
        ∧ (tsum of that same series ≤ 2)
        ∧ ¬ GeometricDecay (that same series).

The accompanying inventory prints actual complete types, including generated, private and instance
declarations, and collects transitive axioms. The only allowed axioms are propext, Classical.choice
and Quot.sound. Compilation uses Lean 4.34.1 and the pinned eight-package environment. There are
no residual source-existence, counting, suffix, convergence or asymptotic hypotheses in these end-
points. The formal universal rate claim quantifies saturated nontrivial source subtrees; the ordinary
proof above excludes even the broader node-subtree class. No theorem about all nonfair probabilisa-
tions’ bare convergence is claimed.

     The supplement’s stable comb examples already allow slow cascades; they do not satisfy ab-
sence of an infinite stable subtree. Similarly, classical prefix codes prescribe lengths, but do not by
themselves supply both a single fixed alpha-LIS and the exclusion of stable subtrees. The proposed
content is this simultaneous realization. Deep finite bushes, Kraft counting, fair iid stationarity and
auxiliary arithmetic are explicitly uncredited.
     The strongest remaining substantive objection is subsumption: a pre-existing construction, or
short explicit implication from one, supplying these same two bridges would defeat discovery credit.
The supporting reviews compare classical coding, stable comb and deep-bush examples, but are
bounded literature screens rather than global novelty certificates. Their registry observations date
to 29 October 2026 around 07:05 UTC; no present-current registry assertion or exact MathDB iden-
tifier is made here. A separate final review must assess fresher evidence. Exact current MathDB
solution bodies and an exhaustive specialist citation-database audit remain outside coverage.
     The strongest mathematical falsifiers are a selected leaf with a nonempty internal proper suffix,
or an infinite shift-stable subtree. The proof above addresses all indices rather than a finite numerical
sample. A source clarification assigning a different rate grading, or imposing an unstated minimal-
context condition, would require reassessment of the source mapping. The read source does not
impose minimality of the iid representation. Formal proof success alone does not settle these source
or priority issues.
