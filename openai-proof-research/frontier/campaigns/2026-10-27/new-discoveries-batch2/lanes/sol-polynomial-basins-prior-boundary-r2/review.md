## Classical boundary: complete bridge and substantive judgment

Here is an independently checked complete bridge, rather than the assertion that an elementary proof must be routine.

**A. Basin invariance.** At each good p the prescribed partition is invariant in both directions under applying F once: an image in the other class would give the same eventual orbit two distinct fixed endpoints. Consequently its first coordinate P satisfies P(a,b)=0 iff a=0 for all prime-field points. This is the standard forward-orbit basin identity, applied literally to the given partition.

**B. Schur-specialization rigidity.** Integer-lattice evaluation gives P(0,y)=0 identically: every integer value is divisible by infinitely many primes. Thus P=x^k H with k>=1 maximal, H(0,y) nonzero and P nonzero. If H depends on yi, choose integers for x and the other variables with x=a!=0 and leading coefficient in yi nonzero. This is possible because x times that coefficient is a nonzero polynomial on the integer lattice. The specialized nonconstant integer polynomial has roots modulo infinitely many primes by Schur's prime-divisor theorem; avoid E and divisors of a to contradict the zero-preserver hypothesis. Hence H=h(x). If h is nonconstant, h(0)!=0 and Schur again supplies a root at a prime outside E and divisors of h(0). That root is nonzero, again impossible. Therefore P=c x^k, c!=0; every prime divisor of c lies in E. This covers arbitrary finite dimension by the same specialization, without induction on dynamical dimension, estimates, or an auxiliary-coordinate theorem.

For completeness the Schur ingredient itself is sufficient without any prime-value conjecture: if only S divides nonzero values of f, take A=f(t0)!=0, M=product(S). Then g(t)=f(t0+AMt)/A is an integer polynomial congruent to 1 modulo every prime of S. A value |g(t)|>1 has a prime divisor outside S dividing a nonzero f-value, contradiction. Nonconstant f has such a value by growth. No irreducibility or density estimate is hidden here.

**C. Torsion survives powering.** Choose an odd prime q not dividing k, let N=q product(E), and choose a prime divisor p of 1+N+...+N^(q-1). It is outside E and not q. The element z=N modulo p has z^q=1 and z!=1, hence exact order q. This construction already supplies z; neither cyclicity, Cauchy nor Dirichlet is needed. Under u↦c u^k, two initial first coordinates 1 and z have ratio z^(k^t) at time t. This is never 1, so the corresponding full orbits cannot both be absorbed at B_p. c cancels in the ratio; arbitrary auxiliary coordinates cannot affect it. This also covers k=1. The fixedness of B_p allows comparison after the larger of the two absorption times.

The exact classical ingredients are Schur's prime-divisor argument, the integer-lattice polynomial identity principle, elementary basin invariance, and finite-order powering with an elementary geometric-sum prime construction. Sha's [2011 power-dynamics body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.3482v2), Proposition3.1 pp3–4, states periodic-point/order formulas for x↦x^k on units modulo odd prime powers; it accounts for the dynamics ingredient, not the multivariate reduction or the multiplier c. The c-removal identity above is exact. None of its asymptotic results is needed.

I judge this a **routine adaptation of these precise ingredients** under the campaign standard. Once the literal partition is expressed as the invariant hypersurface, the only algebraic task is excluding an extra factor from one coordinate. Every possible extra factor is excluded by a direct specialization of Schur, and the remaining dynamics is the standard survival of prime-to-k torsion. No new classification machinery, stronger local-global principle, estimate, or synchronization construction remains. In particular the arbitrary-dimensional generality carries no additional dynamical argument after step B. This is stronger grounds for exclusion than shortness alone, and supplies the complete bridge rather than mistaking a vaguely similar theorem for it.

Thus exceptionality is neither an automatic hypothesis nor a legitimate shortcut to step B. Later proof bodies are not cleared.

For the system P=0, xt−1=0, such a certificate would preclude nonzero-x zeros modulo almost all primes. Our hypothesis is the reverse starting direction: no such **prime-field** zeros. A proof recovering complex emptiness would need an additional specialization/splitting argument. The arithmetic Nullstellensatz statement is not itself that reverse implication. The inspected proof uses generic coordinates and traces to construct certificates, not the requested classification across varying primes. No full-proof clearance is claimed. The elementary Schur specialization above supplies all the needed reverse-direction work for this special hypersurface without invoking that theorem.

It cannot be silently substituted for zero-preserver rigidity.
