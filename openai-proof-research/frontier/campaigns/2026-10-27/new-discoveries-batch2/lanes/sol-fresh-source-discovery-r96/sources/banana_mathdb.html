Higher-degree ULC failure conjecture for the polynomials fmf_mfm​ and hmh_mhm​

For each integer m≥4m\geq 4m≥4, let fmf_mfm​ and hmh_mhm​ be the polynomials defined in the paper, and let the ULC inequality refer to the ultra-log-concavity inequality for their coefficients. Higher-degree ULC failure conjecture. Both fmf_mfm​ and hmh_mhm​ are not ultra-log-concave, and every coefficient of degree jjj with

4≤j≤m−24\leq j\leq m-24≤j≤m−2

fails the ULC inequality. This conjecture is based on computer computations for m=1m=1m=1 through m=100m=100m=100; the stated higher-degree failure pattern is not proved in the supplied passage.

References
  1. Stephanie Chen, “Log-Concavity of the Grothendieck Classes of Banana Graphs and Clasped Necklaces”, arXiv:2503.16296 (2025).

Open · solution reported

The conjecture has a posted complete-proof claim, but nobody has independently checked it, so the degree-by-degree assertion remains unconfirmed.

Stephanie Chen formulated the conjecture in 2025: both polynomial families should fail the strengthened coefficient inequality, with every degree from 444 through m−2m-2m−2 failing. The paper reports computational evidence through m=100m=100m=100 but does not prove this pattern.

Known results

  • Chen (2025) proves that both fmf_mfm​ and hmh_mhm​ are not ultra-log-concave for m≥4m\ge4m≥4.
  • For odd mmm, Chen proves failures for fmf_mfm​ at degrees 111 and 333, and for hmh_mhm​ at degree 333.
  • For even mmm, Chen proves failures for fmf_mfm​ at degree 222, and for hmh_mhm​ at degrees 111 and 222.

Posted attempt

A posted argument claims a complete proof of the remaining assertion: for every m≥4m\ge4m≥4 and 4≤j≤m−24\le j\le m-24≤j≤m−2, both families violate the ULC inequality strictly. The argument has not been independently verified, and no corroborating proof or published correction was found.

Current status (as of August 2026): Overall non-ULC is proved for m≥4m\ge4m≥4, while the asserted failure at every degree 4≤j≤m−24\le j\le m-24≤j≤m−2 has only an unverified complete-proof claim.
