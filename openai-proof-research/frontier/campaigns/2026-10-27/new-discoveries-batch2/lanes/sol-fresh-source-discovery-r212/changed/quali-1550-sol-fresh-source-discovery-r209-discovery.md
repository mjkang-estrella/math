## Closest obtained proof bodies: complete applicability limits

The source itself proves all-p strict local minimality and global minimality on some neighborhood of p=2. Its full Section 9 proof reduces arbitrary lattice shapes to nonnegative Selling parameters of an obtuse superbase, normalizes the largest basis length to one, and uses the energy lower bound to bound the determinant away from zero on minimizing sequences. The resulting compact set contains global minimizers. Local uniform convergence E_p→E_2 there forces minimizers to approach the unique BCC representative as p→2. The positive Hessian then makes that representative the unique minimizer for p sufficiently near 2. This is a complete bridge to a neighborhood of 2, not to all p. At another parameter, a distant competing minimizer could tie BCC despite BCC retaining a positive local Hessian. No uniform global energy gap for all parameters is supplied. Increasing L^p norms for each fixed lattice does not compare different lattices.

External closest obtained body: Senping Luo and Bingbing Xie, *On sharp stability of the BCC quantizer*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26553 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26553, actual v1 dated 19 October 2026. ## Sustained all-scope route: normalized radial tails

Normalize det Λ=1 by dilation. Let X_A be uniform on V(A), R_A=|X_A|, S_A(t)=P(R_A>t), and H_A(t)=E(R_A−t)_+=integral_t^infinity S_A(u)du. Each V(A) is bounded, so all these integrals are finite, for each nonsingular A. Tonelli gives

E_p(A)=p integral_0^infinity t^(p−1) S_A(t)dt.

For every p>1, integrate pointwise r^p=p(p−1) integral_0^r t^(p−2)(r−t)dt, then use Tonelli:

E_p(A)=p(p−1) integral_0^infinity t^(p−2) H_A(t)dt.

At p=1, E_1(A)=H_A(0). Therefore the following exact missing lemma would prove the FULL source question, including p=1:

For EVERY covolume-one three-dimensional Bravais lattice Λ and EVERY t≥0,
    integral_(V(Λ)) (|x|−t)_+ dx ≥ integral_(V(BCC)) (|x|−t)_+ dx.   [UNPROVED]

This integrated-tail order is sufficient; no equivalence with the conjecture is claimed. Failure of this stronger lemma would not itself refute the all-p statement. The source's Selling reduction makes it enough to prove it for all nonnegative six-tuples whose Gram matrix is positive definite, after determinant normalization. Zero Selling coordinates on valid nonsingular boundary strata are included. Fedorov's five combinatorial types reduce incidences, not the continuous parameter space: each type contains infinitely many shapes. There is no proof here that symmetrizing those parameters reduces H_A. A plausible full route is an incidence-compatible smoothing inequality for these truncated radial integrals, with equality at the equal Selling tuple. That uniform inequality remains missing.

The easier pointwise tail order S_A(t)≥S_BCC(t) is FALSE. For unit-covolume BCC the nearest-neighbor length is 2^(1/3) sqrt(3)/2, hence packing radius r_B=2^(1/3)sqrt(3)/4 and r_B^6=27/1024. For FCC, represented by D3={z∈Z³:sum z_i even} of covolume2 scaled by 2^(−1/3), the shortest vector has length2^(1/6); its packing radius r_F=2^(1/6)/2 and r_F^6=1/32. These shortest lengths follow directly by enumerating the integer/half-integer cosets for BCC and the nonzero even-sum integer vectors for D3. For EVERY t∈(r_B,r_F), the radius-t ball lies inside V(FCC), whereas it crosses a BCC nearest-neighbor bisector and leaves a positive-volume cap outside V(BCC). Thus

P(R_BCC≤t)<vol(B_t)=P(R_FCC≤t), hence S_BCC(t)>S_FCC(t).

The rational witness t=11/20 satisfies 27/1024<t^6<1/32 exactly. This disproves pointwise stochastic domination as a proof route, not global moment minimality. It is geometric and holds on a whole open interval; the capped checker only controls the rational witness arithmetic. Integrated tails could still have the required ordering because later tail contributions can compensate.

A separate exact abstract control shows why the already known endpoint orders cannot be interpolated without geometric input: let R_A equal0 with probability2/3 and2 with probability1/3, and R_B≡1. Then E R_A²=4/3>1, max R_A=2>1, but E R_A=2/3<1. A neighborhood of p=2 has the same moment ordering, since (1/3)2^p>1 for p>log_2(3)<2. These are NOT lattice-radius distributions or a source counterexample. They isolate the invalid abstract inference from a neighborhood of 2 and the infinity limit to every p≥1.

## Reconnaissance: Dajani's literal perturbation separation and its full bridge

Adam Dajani, *Maximum Diversity and Support Transitions on Disks and Three-Dimensional Balls*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.38031, actual v1, submitted 27 October 2026 22:33:42 UTC per current abstract. DOI identifier10.48550/arXiv.2610.38031. Read Theorem1.1, the Fourier positivity and variational criterion Section2, Section3 through the boundary-collar argument Lemma3.7, Discussion and AppendixB. The already-public disk/ball solutions are excluded as reconnaissance, without independent re-proof. The discussion p28 asks whether the interior support stays separated from the outer boundary under small smooth perturbations of a supercritical three-dimensional ball.

The following supplies the full classical bridge for the literal separation reading; no exclusion is inferred merely from shortness. Write E_f={rω:0≤r≤R+f(ω)}, with smooth f on S² and ||f||_infinity small (thus covers all sufficiently small smooth radial perturbations; smooth perturbations of the ball boundary are radial after shrinking the neighborhood). Let μ_f minimize exponential energy on E_f. Existence follows from weak compactness; uniqueness follows from the strictly positive Fourier multiplier in source Section2. As f→0, μ_f converges weakly to the ball equilibrium μ_0: all measures lie in one compact ball, every subsequential limit is supported on B_R, and pushing μ_0 by radial scaling x→(1−||f||_infinity/R)x gives an admissible recovery measure on E_f. Continuity of the kernel proves convergence of energies; uniqueness identifies every limit.

For the ball, Lemma3.7 gives ∂_r U_0(R)<0, and μ_0 is atomless (Lemma3.6). On a fixed collar of the radius R, radial slopes of U_f approach those of U_0 uniformly in the following sufficient sense. Split the directional derivative kernel into distances≤δ and>δ. The latter, with a continuous cutoff, is an equicontinuous family on a fixed compact set, so weak convergence gives uniform convergence of its integrals. The former is bounded in absolute value by the measure of a δ-ball. Atomlessness and compactness imply sup_x μ_0(B_(2δ)(x))→0; weak convergence plus a finite covering gives the corresponding uniform bound for μ_f. The kernel gradient is bounded by1, so the split error tends to zero. Along every radial line the potentials are Lipschitz and their a.e. derivatives therefore are uniformly strictly negative on a smaller collar. Integration gives strict decrease along radial outward segments. Any interior point in that collar has an outward segment within E_f to its boundary, ending at strictly lower potential. Since the variational criterion says U_f≥I(μ_f) on E_f and equality on support, no such interior point lies in supp μ_f. Thus the entire support inside E_f is uniformly separated from ∂E_f by a positive distance. Boundary mass is nonzero: μ_0 has positive mass on ∂B_R, hence weak convergence puts positive mass in the collar, whose interior was just excluded. Interior mass is nonzero: take a small ball inside the source's central positive-density component and apply weak convergence with a continuous nonnegative test function there. This proves a persistent inner support part and boundary support separated by an empty collar.

This bridge does NOT prove connectedness/smoothness of the interior component, that every boundary point carries support, positivity of two smooth surface densities, or the author's suggested moving-interface construction. If the informal question intends that stronger free-boundary structure, those parts remain unresolved and cannot be claimed solved. For literal collar separation alone, the above is a classical variational continuity consequence; no discovery nomination, PROOF.md or credit is requested. This reconnaissance does not replace the exact BCC target.
