 A nilpotent version of the first Kac conjecture 
 
 
 Lydia Gösmann
 

 Abstract 
    
 We derive a formula for the nilpotent Kac polynomial and define an explicit generalized Kac-Moody Lie algebra (by generators and relations) and conjecture that its graded dimensions recover the constant terms of the nilpotent Kac polynomial. We outline empirical evidence supporting the conjecture and prove it for two special classes of quivers: quivers without oriented cycles except loops and quivers with ”negative enough” Euler form. 
  
 
 
 1  Introduction 

 
 For a dimension vector  𝐝 \mathbf{d}  of a quiver  Q Q  let  A 𝐝 A_{\mathbf{d}}  be the Kac polynomial attached to Q and  𝐝 \mathbf{d} . For a finite field  𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q}  the integer  A 𝐝 ​ ( q ) A_{\mathbf{d}}(q)  is equal to the number  A 𝐝 ​ ( 𝔽 q ) A_{\mathbf{d}}(\mathbb{F}_{q})  of isomorphism classes of absolutely indecomposable representations of  Q Q  of dimension vector  𝐝 \mathbf{d}  over  𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} . The polynomial  A 𝐝 ​ ( t ) ∈ ℤ ​ [ t ] A_{\mathbf{d}}(t)\in\mathbb{Z}[t]  is monic and of degree  1 − ⟨ 𝐝 , 𝐝 ⟩ 1-\langle\mathbf{d},\mathbf{d}\rangle  and has nonnegative coefficients. 
 Furthermore, one can also ask for the number of absolutely indecomposable nilpotent representations. This question is addressed in work of Bozec-Schiffmann-Vasserot  [ 2 ] . There they developed a formula for the nilpotent and 1-nilpotent Kac polynomial counting nilpotent or 1-nilpotent absolutely indecomposable representations, respectively. In particular, they prove that the constant term of the 1-nilpotent Kac polynomial is the dimension of a generalized Kac-Moody algebra in the corresponding degree. The question of a Lie algebra corresponding to the nilpotent Kac polynomial remained open. In this paper we derive an explicit formula for the nilpotent Kac polynomial and propose an explicit presentation (by generators and relations) of a Lie algebra  𝔤 Q n ​ i ​ l \mathfrak{g}_{Q}^{nil}  and conjecture that its graded dimensions recover the constant terms of the nilpotent Kac polynomial. We give computational evidence, prove consistency with the generalized Kac–Moody case as well as the special case of quivers with negative enough Euler form. Moreover, we observe that the generators are governed by the Le Bruyn–Procesi classification of dimension vectors of simple-representation and we interpret the conjecture in the context of BPS theory. A proof of the conjecture will appear in a forthcoming joint paper with L. Hennecart.

 3  Conjecture: Nilpotent Kac-Moody algebra 

 
 Definition 3.1 . 
 
 For a quiver  Q Q  the (positive part) of the nilpotent Kac-Moody algebra  𝔤 Q n ​ i ​ l \mathfrak{g}^{nil}_{Q}  is given by the following generators: 
 
 •  
 
 e 𝐬 i e_{\mathbf{s}_{i}}  for  𝐬 i ∈ I \mathbf{s}_{i}\in I , if the vertex  i i  has no loops, and 
 
 •  
 
 e 𝐝 e_{\mathbf{d}}  for any dimension vector  𝐝 \mathbf{d}  such that  supp ⁡ ( 𝐝 ) \supp(\mathbf{d})  is a strongly connected subquiver and  ⟨ 𝐝 , 𝐬 i ⟩ ≤ 0 \langle\mathbf{d},\mathbf{s}_{i}\rangle\leq 0  and  ⟨ 𝐬 i , 𝐝 ⟩ ≤ 0 \langle\mathbf{s}_{i},\mathbf{d}\rangle\leq 0  for all  1 ≤ i ≤ n 1\leq i\leq n . 
 
 
 The generators are subject to the following relation: 
 
 •  
 
 [ e 𝐟 , e 𝐠 ] = 0 [e_{\mathbf{f}},e_{\mathbf{g}}]=0  for every pair of generators with  ( 𝐟 , 𝐠 ) = 0 (\mathbf{f},\mathbf{g})=0  and 
 
 •  
 
 ( ad ⁡ e 𝐬 i ) 1 − ( 𝐬 i , 𝐟 ) ​ e 𝐟 = 0 (\ad e_{\mathbf{s}_{i}})^{1-(\mathbf{s}_{i},\mathbf{f})}e_{\mathbf{f}}=0 , for  𝐟 ≠ 𝐬 i \mathbf{f}\neq\mathbf{s}_{i} , if  𝐬 i ∈ I \mathbf{s}_{i}\in I  and the vertex  i i  has no loops. 
 
 
 
 
 
 Remark 3.2 . 
 
 The construction above defines (the positive part of) a Borcherds algebra  [ 1 ] . The size of the corresponding Cartan square matrix is the number of generators of the above construction, that means all dimension vectors satisfying the conditions above, so it is typically infinite. Moreover, the entry  c 𝐝 , 𝐞 c_{\mathbf{d},\mathbf{e}}  is the symmetrized Euler form  ( 𝐝 , 𝐞 ) (\mathbf{d},\mathbf{e}) . If  𝐝 ≠ 𝐞 \mathbf{d}\neq\mathbf{e}  then  c 𝐝 , 𝐞 ≤ 0 c_{\mathbf{d},\mathbf{e}}\leq 0  and  c 𝐬 i , 𝐬 i = 2 c_{\mathbf{s}_{i},\mathbf{s}_{i}}=2 , if  𝐬 i ∈ I \mathbf{s}_{i}\in I  and the vertex  i i  has no loops. All other  c 𝐝 , 𝐝 c_{\mathbf{d},\mathbf{d}}  have an value  ≤ 0 \leq 0 . A direct calculation shows, that the construction of the Borcherds algebra with this Cartan matrix, reconstruct exactly the above relations.
