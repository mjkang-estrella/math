Title:On a conjecture of Andrews and Bachraoui
    
Authors:Koustav Banerjee, Kathrin Bringmann, William J. Keith            
View a PDF of the paper titled On a conjecture of Andrews and Bachraoui, by Koustav Banerjee and 2 other authors
    View PDF
    HTML (experimental)



    
            Abstract:Recently, Andrews and Bachraoui considered a generating function $F_{k,m}(q)$ associated with certain two-color partitions, and conjectured that this function has non-negative coefficients for $m=1$. They showed this property for $1 \leq k \leq 4$. In this note, we prove that $F_{k,1}(q)$ has non-negative coefficients for $5 \leq k \leq 10$. Moreover, we show that, as $k\to\infty$, $F_{k,1}(q)$ is related to Ramanujan's third order mock theta function $\omega(q)$ and to quotients of certain $q$-binomial coefficients.
