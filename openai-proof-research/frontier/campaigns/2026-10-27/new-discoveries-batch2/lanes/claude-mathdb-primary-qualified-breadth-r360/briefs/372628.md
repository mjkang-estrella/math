# Brief 372628: λ(3,2,1)(C_n(1,5)) = 14 for odd n ≥ 105

Status: computer-certificate proof candidate for the open lower bound. The upper bound
relies on the source's Theorem 24; this lane's independent replay of it is recorded
separately (see "Upper bound" below). No credit or novelty claim. Needs independent
review.

## Primary source (retained, body read)

Same paper as brief 372627: arXiv:2601.12574v2.
- Conjecture 25: "Let n ∈ N, n ≥ 105 odd, and let G = C_n(1,5). Then
  λ(3,2,1)(G) = 14."
- Theorem 24: λ ≤ 14 for odd n ≥ 105. It is proved from λ(C_45) = 14 (Table 5) and
  patterns of lengths 12 and 14, via n = 45 + 12k + 14ℓ.
- The authors tested odd n ≤ 151 and found no labeling with labels 0..13.

## Theorem (certified here)

For every odd n ≥ 31, C_n(1,5) has no L(3,2,1)-labeling with labels {0, …, 13}, so
λ ≥ 14. Combined with source Theorem 24, λ = 14 for every odd n ≥ 105.

## Mechanism

- Window lemma as in brief 372627, with t = 5 and n > 30. The constraints are
  {1:3, 2:2, 3:1, 4:2, 5:3, 6:2, 7:1, 9:1, 10:2, 11:1, 15:1}.
- States are the valid 15-windows over 14 labels: 110,672 states and 111,010 edges.
- The nontrivial SCCs are:
  - one SCC of 4,848 states with period 2;
  - 26 twelve-cycles;
  - 48 fourteen-cycles.
- Every period is even, so there is no closed walk of odd length, and hence no
  span-13 labeling of any odd C_n with n ≥ 31.
- The local edge certificate (SCC rank decreasing across SCCs, φ(v) ≡ φ(u)+1 (mod p)
  inside an SCC) was checked edge by edge. Canonical certificate sha256:
  `d12240c7…3446`. It is regenerated deterministically by the verifier; the file is
  not stored.

## Independent cross-checks

`checks/lab321_bruteforce.py` runs backtracking on the actual circulant, with no
window reduction:
- Odd n ∈ {31, 33, 35, 37, 41, 45, 105} with 14 labels: no labeling. n = 105, the
  smallest case in the conjecture's scope, took 21.3M search nodes.
- Even n = 34 with 14 labels: a labeling exists, consistent with the even-n λ = 13
  of Table 4 and Theorem 23.
- The case n = 45 with 15 labels (expected to exist, per Table 5) hit the CPU cap and
  is inconclusive. It is recorded in failures.md and is not load-bearing.

## Certificate size and formal burden

- 110,672 states: machine certificate only.
- A Lean proof would need a verified enumeration of the valid 15-windows. The
  certificate can be compressed by symmetry (label reversal x ↦ 13 − x), but kernel
  checking of a 10^5-state certificate without native_decide is heavy.
- Formal burden: moderate to high. No Lean work was done.
