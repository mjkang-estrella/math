Log-behavior of the root sequences of P-recursive sequences
Qing-hu Hou and Zhongjie Li
Affiliation: School of Mathematics Tianjin University Tianjin 300072, China qh_hou@tju.edu.cn, lizhongjie@tju.edu.cn
Abstract
In recent years, Sun has proposed numerous conjectures regarding the log-concavity of root sequences {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1}. We establish criteria for the asymptotic log-concavity of {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1} and the asymptotic ratio log-convexity of {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1} for PP-recursive sequences {an}n⩾0\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant{0}}. Additionally, by the aid of symbolic computation, we present a systematic approach to determine the explicit integer NN such that the sequence {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant{N}} is log-concave and the sequence {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant N} is ratio log-convex.
Keywords: log-concavity, ratio log-convexity, PP-recursive sequences.
1 Introduction
A sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} is called log-convex (log-concave, respectively) if the inequality an2⩽an+1​an−1a_{n}^{2}\leqslant a_{n+1}a_{n-1} (an2⩾an+1​an−1a_{n}^{2}\geqslant a_{n+1}a_{n-1}, respectively) holds for all n⩾2n\geqslant 2. For a non-negative sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1}, we define its root sequence as {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1}. Recently, Sun [13, 14] proposed several conjectures regarding the log-concavity of the root sequences of combinatorial sequences. Some of these conjectures have been confirmed, as shown in [5, 10, 12, 17]. In particular, Xia [16] developed a systematic method to prove the log-concavity of {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1} when ana_{n} satisfies a second-order linear reucrrence.
We consider the general case where {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} is a P-recursive sequence. Recall that a PP-recursive sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} of order dd satisfies a recurrence relation of the form
p0​(n)​an+p1​(n)​an+1+⋯+pd​(n)​an+d=0,∀n⩾1,p_{0}(n)a_{n}+p_{1}(n)a_{n+1}+\cdots+p_{d}(n)a_{n+d}=0,\quad\forall\,n\geqslant 1,
where pi​(n)p_{i}(n) are polynomials in nn. Wimp and Zeilberger [15] (see also [3, Sec. VIII.7]) showed that a PP-recursive sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} is asymptotically equal to a linear combination of terms in the form of
eQ⁡(ρ,n)​s​(ρ,n),e^{Q(\rho,n)}s(\rho,n),
(1.1)
where
Q⁡(ρ,n)\displaystyle Q(\rho,n)
=μ0​n​log⁡n+∑j=1ρμj​nj/ρ,\displaystyle=\mu_{0}n\log{n}+\sum_{j=1}^{\rho}\mu_{j}n^{j/\rho},
(1.2)
s⁡(ρ,n)\displaystyle s(\rho,n)
=nr∑j=0t−1(logn)j∑s=0∞bs​jn−s/ρ,\displaystyle=n^{r}\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}(\log{n})^{j}\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}b_{sj}n^{-s/\rho},
(1.3)
with ρ,t\rho,\ t being positive integers and μj,r,bs​j\mu_{j},\ r,\ b_{sj} being complex numbers. We focus on the case where ana_{n} asymptotically takes the form of (1.1) and t=1t=1 in (1.3), which encompasses the majority of combinatorial sequences.
Based on the asymptotic expression of ana_{n}, we establish a criterion for ana_{n} such that the root sequences {ann}\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\} is asymptotically log-concave, i.e.,
ann2⩾an−1n−1​an+1n+1\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}^{2}\geqslant\sqrt[n-1]{a_{n-1}}\sqrt[n+1]{a_{n+1}}
holds for all sufficiently large nn. We define a sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} to be ratio log-convex if {an+1/an}n⩾1\{a_{n+1}/a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} is log-convex. By a similar discussion, we provide a criterion for ana_{n} such that the root sequence is asymptotically ratio log-convex.
Our next objective is to identify a specific integer NN that guarantees the log-concavity of the sequence {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant N}. We solve the problem by establishing bounds for both rn=an+1/anr_{n}=a_{n+1}/a_{n} and ana_{n}. Notably,
Hou and Zhang [6] have developed an algorithm to compute NN and provide bounds for the ratio rnr_{n} when n⩾Nn\geqslant N. Combing with the asymptotic expression of ana_{n}, we are enable to give an upper bound of ana_{n} as intended. Then we can use Maple or Mathematica to find the derivative and limiting value in order to prove the constant positivity or negativity of the rational function.
This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, by studying the asymptotic expressions of PP-recursive sequences, we give a criterion of the asymptotic log-concavity for the sequence {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1} and the asymptotic ratio log-convexity for {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1}. In Section 3, by studying the log-behavior of {ann}n⩾1\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant 1} and the monotonicity of {an+1nann}n⩾1\left\{\frac{\sqrt[n]{a_{n+1}}}{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}}\right\}_{n\geqslant 1}, we extend the result of [6] to give a systematic method to find NN such that the sequence {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant{N}} is log-concave and {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant N} is ratio log-convex.

3 Finding the explicit NN
In this section, we aim to determine a specific value for NN such that {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant{N}} is log-concave or ratio log-convex. In their work [6], Hou and Zhang introduced an algorithm for computing an integer NN along with lower and upper bounds for rn=an+1/anr_{n}=a_{n+1}/a_{n} when n⩾Nn\geqslant N. Our approach relies on these bounds together with an upper bound for ana_{n}. It is worth to note that deriving these bounds for rnr_{n} requires the sequence {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant 1} to be bound preserving, as defined in [6].
Firstly, we give a criterion on the log-concavity of the root sequences.
Theorem 3.1.
Let {an}n⩾1\{a_{n}\}_{n\geqslant{1}} be a positive sequence. Suppose we can find an upper bound hnh_{n} of ana_{n}, a lower bound fnf_{n} and an upper bound gng_{n} of an+1an\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}, such that
2​log​hn+n⁡(n+1)​log​gn+1−n⁡(n+3)​log​fn⩽0,∀n⩾N.2\log h_{n}+n(n+1)\log g_{n+1}-n(n+3)\log f_{n}\leqslant 0,\quad\forall n\geqslant N.
(3.1)
Then the root sequence {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant{N}} is log-concave.
Proof.
From the definition, the log-concavity of {ann}n⩾N\{\sqrt[n]{a_{n}}\}_{n\geqslant{N}} is equivalent to
log⁡an+2n+2+log⁡ann−2​log⁡an+1n+1⩽0.\dfrac{\log{a_{n+2}}}{n+2}+\dfrac{\log{a_{n}}}{n}-\dfrac{2\log{a_{n+1}}}{n+1}\leqslant{0}.
(3.2)
Denote rn=an+1/anr_{n}=a_{n+1}/a_{n}.
Substituting an+2=an​rn​rn+1a_{n+2}=a_{n}r_{n}r_{n+1} and an+1=an​rna_{n+1}=a_{n}r_{n} in (3.2), we get an equivalent inequality
2​log​an+n⁡(n+1)​log​rn+1−n⁡(n+3)​log​rn⩽0.2\log a_{n}+n(n+1)\log r_{n+1}-n(n+3)\log r_{n}\leqslant 0.
Notice that
2​log​an+n⁡(n+1)​log​rn+1−n⁡(n+3)​log​rn\displaystyle 2\log a_{n}+n(n+1)\log r_{n+1}-n(n+3)\log r_{n}
⩽\displaystyle\leqslant\
2​log​hn+n⁡(n+1)​log​gn+1−n⁡(n+3)​log​fn.\displaystyle 2\log h_{n}+n(n+1)\log g_{n+1}-n(n+3)\log f_{n}.
The proof follows immediately.
∎
