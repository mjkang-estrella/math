<H1><A NAME="SECTION00030000000000000000">&#160;</A>
<A NAME="sec:banach">&#160;</A>
<BR>
Banach Space Theory
</H1>

<P>
Regarding the reverse mathematics status of well known theorems of
functional analysis, much is known, but many questions remain.

<P>
For example, the open mapping theorem for separable Banach spaces is
known to be provable in a system called 
<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{RCA}_0^{+}$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="49" HEIGHT="39" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img42.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{RCA}_0^{+}$">
which is of the same
strength as 
<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="45" HEIGHT="33" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img2.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{RCA}_0$">
and indeed conservative over 
<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="45" HEIGHT="33" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img2.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{RCA}_0$">
for <IMG
 WIDTH="25" HEIGHT="36" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img43.gif"
 ALT="$\Pi^1_1$">sentences.  But whether it is provable in 
<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="45" HEIGHT="33" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img2.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{RCA}_0$">
or even 
<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="49" HEIGHT="33" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img7.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{WKL}_0$">remains unknown.  See Brown/Simpson [<A
 HREF="node10.html#bs2">5</A>], Mytilinaios/Slaman
[<A
 HREF="node10.html#my-sl">24</A>], Simpson [<A
 HREF="node10.html#partial">31</A>].

<P>
As another example, consider the Krein/Smulian theorem for
separable Banach spaces.  This is a somewhat lesser known but still
basic theorem of functional analysis.  It says that a convex set in
the dual of a separable Banach space is weak-
<!-- MATH: $\textstyle{*}$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="14" HEIGHT="17" ALIGN="BOTTOM" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img44.gif"
 ALT="$\textstyle{*}$">-closed if
and only if it is bounded-weak-
<!-- MATH: $\textstyle{*}$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="14" HEIGHT="17" ALIGN="BOTTOM" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img44.gif"
 ALT="$\textstyle{*}$">-closed.  It is known
from Humphreys/Simpson [<A
 HREF="node10.html#hs1">16</A>] that this statement is provable in

<!-- MATH: $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ -->
<IMG
 WIDTH="45" HEIGHT="33" ALIGN="MIDDLE" BORDER="0"
 SRC="img41.gif"
 ALT="$\mathsf{ACA}_0$">,
but the exact strength is unknown.
