                          SOME REMARKS ON OPEN PROBLEMS
                                  FOR NIELSEN’S BETA FUNCTION


                        K. J YOTHI , B. R AVI ∗ AND A. V ENKATA L AKSHMI


      Abstract. Using the convolution properties of the Laplace transforms, the generalized Schwartz
      inequality, and the monotonicity of the Nielsen beta function, we address the open problems
      related to the Nielsen’s beta function. Consequently, we improve the validity of the existing
      inequalities.
