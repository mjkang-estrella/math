# Eight additional attributed families: all retired as publicly answered

Early semantic deduplication bound 1000+ compact lane reports before scanning author names, titles and defining vocabulary (history-bindings.json and dedup.json). Gerbner stability and stable Kneser were excluded immediately because actual historical hits already cover them. None of the selected eight has a matching hit in that compact corpus; this does NOT prove complete historical absence, present openness, or novelty. Active/unreported targets cannot be ruled out by this corpus alone. The decisive retirement of every entry below is a public body, so no unresolved survivor is inflated from absent hits. No expensive solve was started on these targets.

Every following URL is an actual primary counterexample/successor body downloaded, hashed, text-extracted and hashed BEFORE inspection. The original attributed question is compared with the actual successor statement/construction below. Older original articles were not separately retrieved, so attribution is as quoted in the inspected successor and that original-source coverage stays incomplete. This is sufficient to retire a rediscovery; it is not full historical bibliographic clearance. No search claim that later work is absent is made. The current registry's complete theorem bodies remain unread, as in the preceding lane.

1. **Stable-set consecutive intersections — retire, public counterexample.** Redmond McNamara, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.08544v2 (25 September2025), pp1–4. Literal attributed Hildebrand Conjecture1.3: every stable S with positive lower density has positive lower density intersection S∩(S+1)∩...∩(S+k-1), for every positive integer k. Stability means density of {n:1_S(n)!=1_S(pn)} is0 for every prime p. Closest actual theorem: Theorem1.6 refutes even nonemptiness at k=3 with density1/2; Theorem1.7 gives prime k and density1-1/(k-1). Body §2 chooses phases p^(iT) approximating a character mod3, then varies T across scales; the displayed construction and Lemma2.1 torus argument were read. Mechanism for a new solve would simply repeat public scale gluing. Objection: stability is asymptotic, not exact multiplicative closure; finite modular-set samples do not address it. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit, but fully public resolution. No nomination.

2. **Regular high-girth occupancy extrema — retire the refuted family; do not promote its residual questions.** Cambie–Jooken, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.05542v2 (28 November2023), §§1,3–5. One literal representative of Perkins–Perarnau's merged extremal family: for every cubic G of girth>=7 and every λ>0, α_G(λ)<=α_H3,8(λ), where α_G=λP'_G/(|V(G)|P_G) and P counts independent sets. Closest actual theorem3.1 gives a 38-vertex cubic graph with α_G>α_H3,8 for every λ>17, with both independence coefficient vectors printed. Theorem3.2 similarly refutes the 4-regular girth>=5 endpoint for λ>37. Body derives the rational-function sign from an explicit polynomial and Descartes' rule, rather than mere numerical plotting. Section4 retires lower-extremum variants; these are merged as one family. Source Question5.1 about all graph homomorphism counts and restricted fugacity/Moore-graph residuals are distinct and not claimed solved or cleared. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; public refutation. Mechanism: coefficient comparison already works publicly. Objection: a maximum independent-set ratio alone does not settle all λ. No nomination.

3. **Dombi increasing triple representation counts — retire, public counterexample.** Bell–Shallit, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.12473v3 (version header28 December2022/internal2 January2023), §§1–2,4. Literal attributed question: no co-infinite A⊂N has r(3,A,n) eventually nondecreasing, with r counting ORDERED triples summing to n. Closest actual Theorem1 proves eventual strict increase for A=N\F whenever 0∉F and |F∩[0,n]|=o(n^α), α<(k-2)/k, k>=3. Actual proof expands (1-X)(1/(1-X)-F(X))^k; the positive n^(k-2) main coefficient dominates all error terms. Theorem2 makes F={2^(n+2)-1:n>=0} explicit and proves strict increase from the start using an automatic difference sequence. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; publicly refuted. Mechanism: sparse deletion generating functions; repeating it is uncredited. Objection: unordered representation functions are a different problem. No nomination.

4. **Hendrickson connectivity/redundancy sufficiency — retire, public counterexample.** Georg Grasegger, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.11818v1 (22 December2022), pp1–4. Literal conjectural reverse of Theorem1: every (d+1)-connected redundantly d-rigid graph on at least d+2 vertices is globally d-rigid. Generic rigidity is essential. Closest actual successor body recalls the higher-dimensional bipartite counterexamples and deterministically identifies K5,5 as the smallest in dimension3; Theorem3 proves sufficiency through n<=d+6. Its computational section explicitly distinguishes probabilistic tests from symbolic checks and flexible-circuit classification; the cone/edge-count proof mechanism was read. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; public resolution of the unrestricted implication. Mechanism: stress/rigidity rank and coning already yield the counterexample, not a new discovery. Objection: numerical embeddings do not establish generic global rigidity or minimality. We did not replay the published enumeration and do not claim to have independently certified its minimality. No nomination.

5. **Directed KKL inequality — retire, public counterexample.** Dubroff–Nadimpalli–Narayanan, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.02035v1 (5 October2022/internal6 October), Conjecture1/Theorem5 and §2 pp3–4. Literal proposed universal assertion: an absolute c>0 exists such that every Boolean f on n bits has a coordinate with negative influence >=c ε(f)log(n)/n, where ε is distance from monotonicity. Closest actual Theorem5 constructs functions on2n bits with all negative influences O(1/n) but ε(f)=Ω(1). Body uses f(x,y)=OR_i[(AND_{j∈T_i}x_j) AND (1-y_i)] with random |T_i|=log n; the first coordinates have zero negative influence and the others are relevant with probability1/n. A variance lemma and constant-probability single active tribe establish distance. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; public refutation (and Remark6 records an independent Khot–Minzer construction). Objection: ordinary undirected KKL remains true and is not refuted. No nomination.

6. **Pseudo-2-factor cubic bipartite classification — retire, public counterexample.** Abreu–Funk–Labbate–Romaniello, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.10961v1 (22 July2022), Conjecture1.1 and §2 pp2–3. Literal assertion: every essentially4-edge-connected pseudo2-factor-isomorphic cubic bipartite graph is K3,3, the Heawood graph or the Pappus graph. Pseudo means parity of cycle count is identical for every2-factor; essential connectivity excludes nontrivial3-edge cuts. Closest actual body gives Goedgebeur's30-vertex graph and a construction from residues of Fano and Möbius–Kantor incidence structures. It removes quadrilateral incidences and reconnects four deficient points/lines in each residue. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; earlier computational counterexample and this structural successor are both public. Mechanism: incidence gluing is already published. Objection: the construction is explicitly NOT an established infinite family, and ordinary2-factor isomorphism is stronger than parity. No nomination.

7. **Berge α-diperfect forbidden-cycle characterization — retire, public counterexample.** de Paula Silva–da Silva–Lee, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.08007v2 (28 July2022), Conjecture1.5, discussion pp2–4, Theorem2.4. Literal assertion: D is α-diperfect iff it has no induced anti-directed odd cycle. α-diperfect requires that in every induced subdigraph, EVERY maximum stable set has an orthogonal path partition (exactly one stable vertex per path). Actual successor characterizes orientations of complements of odd cycles that fail this property; it explicitly notes these have no forbidden induced odd cycle of length>=5. The source distinguishes these from Begin-End-diperfectness: its examples contain blocking odd cycles, and do not refute Conjecture1.6. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; public infinite refutation family. Mechanism: complement-cycle path partitions already supply the obstruction. Objection: Gallai–Milgram's existence of one suitable maximum stable set does not imply the every-maximum-set endpoint. No nomination.

8. **Equality of independence and annihilation number — retire, public counterexample.** Michaela Hiller, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.07529v1 (15 February2022), Theorem1.1/§§2–3. Literal attributed Larson–Pepper characterization at a(G)>=n/2: α(G)=a(G) iff α'(G)=a(G), with α' the critical independence number. The odd-order a=(n-1)/2 branch is part of the same family. Actual source Lemma1.2 retains sufficiency; §2 refutes necessity by C3 plus t isolated vertices: α=a=t+1 but α'=t, for t>=1. Source Conjecture3.2's König–Egerváry/maximal-annihilating characterization also loses its only-if direction in connected examples. Closest theorem/body therefore refutes the broad implication while retaining bipartite/connected-claw-free restrictions in §4. Duplicate status: no compact-history exact hit; public answer. Mechanism: isolated-vertex or connected-gadget construction is already published. Objection: maximal and maximum annihilating sets are not interchangeable, nor is α' ordinary α. No nomination.
