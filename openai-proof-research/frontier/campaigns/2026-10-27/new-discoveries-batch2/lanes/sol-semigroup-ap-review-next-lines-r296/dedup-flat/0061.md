Source bodies were frozen and hash-compared before extraction; source-receipts.jsonl binds versioned URLs and times. Dates below are first submission/latest retrieved version, not a claim of first historical formulation. |1. **Friedgut–Kalai–Kindler / Lionel Levine**, [2103.01541v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.01541v3), first2Mar2021/version9Mar2021, Conj1.1: for t players with independent fair infinite hat stacks, each player sees all other stacks and chooses one own hat using a prearranged strategy; optimal probability all choose black tends to0 as t→∞. Finite-stack optima and their supremum define the infinite limit.|**Park.** Actual blocker Lemmas3.2–3.3 and their proof read: give strict p(t+1)<p(t), but blocker size grows as a tower and gives no uniform loss at a positive putative limiting probability. Strict decrease does not imply zero limit. Mechanism would require a positive decrement depending only on p(t), not t; decisive calculation is a blocker bound uniform at densityα>0. Strongest objection: this is the original hard asymptotic core, with no new uniform mechanism; current status not independently cleared. Related independent-set questions are not separate breadth entries.|
|2. **Hilton–Zhao as posed by Cao–Chen–Shan**, [2103.05171v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05171v1),9Mar2021, Conj1: splitting any vertex of an n-vertex class1 Δ-regular graph with Δ>n/3 into two adjacent vertices with inherited incident edges gives a Δ-critical graph.|**Park.** Theorem1 proves only Δ≥3n/4 in original-order notation (source theorem calls original order n−1). Multifan/Kierstead recoloring and the concluding swap argument inspected. Overfullness establishes class2 but not criticality of every edge. Mechanism: retain an elementary missing-color set after deleting any edge in the lower-density range. Decisive calculation: exact missing-color balance that avoids the 3n/4 counting threshold. Strongest objection: the source's density loss is substantive, and current universal status is uncleared.|
|3. **Bonamy–Heinrich–Legrand-Duchesne–Narboni**, [2103.10684v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.10684v2),19Mar2021/13Mar2025, Question1.6: infimum c such that for every sufficiently large t some graph admits a quasi-K_t minor but no K_ct minor; quasi bags need only have connected pairwise unions.|**Park, not a theorem-announcement count.** Actual construction and Claims2.1–2.3 proof comparison: paired vertices with one random missing cross edge give c≤2/3, while the elementary pairing bridge gives c≥1/2. The same body explicitly refutes the older Las Vergnas–Meyniel conjectures; those are not offered as unresolved. Mechanism would reduce attainable minor density below2/3; decisive test is a bag gadget with >2 vertices and controlled pair-union connectivity. Objection: larger bags can themselves generate the forbidden minors, and no new gadget was produced.|
|4. **Aliabadi–Filom**, [2103.11432v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.11432v2),21Mar2021/30Jan2022, Q1.4: A,B⊂Z/pZ, p prime, |A|=|B|, A∩(A+B)=∅; must an acyclic matching A→B exist? A matching is a bijection with a+f(a)∉A; acyclic means its sum-multiplicity function uniquely determines it.|**Reject: public complete proof.** Actual Aliabadi–Taylor [2404.02178v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.02178v2) (firstApr2024/version6Aug2025), Lemma2.1 and Thm2.3 proofs read. Order the possible sums and greedily take every available pair of each sum; cancellation in both coordinates makes each sum class disjoint. Lexicographic maximality forces uniqueness step by step. Applies to every abelian group. The lemma's general wording mentions only left cancellation; the group application has both needed cancellations, so this wording issue does not obstruct the exact bridge. Also read the classification proof [2402.08008v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.08008v3), which concerns unrestricted A,B and does not confuse weak with ordinary acyclicity. No threshold variant substituted.|
|5. **Brooke Logan Ogrodnik**, [2103.14594v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14594v2),26Mar2021/21Nov2021, Conj1.6: every sufficiently large admissible integer is the trace of an element of Γ(2)′, the commutator subgroup of the principal level2 subgroup of SL2(Z). Admissible means occurring modulo every positive integer.|**Park.** Theorem1.5 gives exact local residues mod256 in{2,18,66,146} and mod9 in{0,2,3,6,7}; Theorem3.2 supplies the strong-approximation decomposition. Local theorem statements and proof portions were compared, not a complete independent audit of its long proof. None supplies integral representatives for all sufficiently large admissible traces. Mechanism: explicit bounded-width commutator trace parametrization; decisive calculation is covering these residues integrally, not just modularly. Strongest objection: genuine thin-group local-global obstruction and no surjectivity mechanism. Negative finite exceptions cannot refute an eventual claim.|
|6. **Y. Jang–J. Nešetřil–P. Ossona de Mendez**, [2103.14687v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14687v1),26Mar2021, Conj1.1: every fixed t-pattern P has extremal ones count f_t(n,P)=O_P(n^(t−1)); a t-pattern has any two ones differing in at least two coordinates, and avoidance is ordered submatrix containment.|**Park.** Actual Theorem3.1 and Lemma3.2 proofs read: full-division selection treats free patterns, slicing inducts on sunflower-core size, and dyadic block contraction turns the extremal bound into the exponential counting consequence. Mechanism: produce a nonsunflower obstruction tensor or a compatible slice induction; decisive calculation is a pattern with repeated coordinates lacking a single fixed core. Objection: the existing division argument controls blocks, not simultaneous selections with equal coordinate requirements. Counting conjecture is the same family, not another screen.|
|7. **Ekhad–Zeilberger**, [2104.01731v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.01731v2),5Apr2021/27May2021: exact critical exponents and rationality for the displayed generalized three-dimensional ballot enumeration sequences (the fifteen source table models).|**Reject displayed 3D questions: actual public answer.** Michael Wallner [2105.12155v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.12155v1),25May2021, bijection to large tandem walks, Theorem2.3, Lemma3.2 and Theorem3.3 proof comparison. The correlation parameter c=−B/√((A+B)(B+C)) yields exponent −1−π/arccos(−c), with excursion periodicity treated; Table1 explicitly gives every displayed model. Rational-square cosine classification explains irrationality except the ordinary tandem case. No numerical estimate is a certificate, and no new higher-dimensional question is substituted.|
|8. **Brešar et al. as posed by Bell–Driscoll–Krop–Wolff**, [2104.05665v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.05665v1),12Apr2021, Conj1: every pair of finite graphs G,H satisfies γ_gr(G⊠H)=γ_gr(G)γ_gr(H), where γ_gr is the longest closed-neighborhood sequence each step footprints a new vertex.|**Park.** Actual Proposition2.1 proof and forest-labeling/critical-caterpillar proof portions read. Simplicial fiber projection yields a recurrence; the forest theorem supplies equality when one factor is a forest. The lower product inequality is general; the missing upper bound is not. Mechanism: exact subset-DP for footprint sequences seeking a strict-product witness; decisive calculation would need a certified full pair and exact optima. Objection: cyclic fibers break the simplicial exchange, and no such witness or all-graph exchange was produced. Current later status uncleared.|
|9. **Brightwell–Felsner–Trotter as posed by Chan–Pak–Panova**, [2104.09009v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.09009v3),19Apr2021/10Mar2022, Conj1.3: in any finite poset and any three distinct x,y,z, if F(k,l) counts linear extensions with L(y)−L(x)=k and L(z)−L(y)=l, then F(k,l)F(k+1,l+1)≤F(k,l+1)F(k+1,l), all k,l≥1.|**Park.** Actual Thms1.4/1.6 give width2; characteristic-matrix recurrence and its deletion proof portions read, with two-chain encoding explicit. Decisive calculation is the sign of the two-by-two F minor once the transfer has more than one frontier coordinate. Strongest objection: two-chain total-positivity structure does not survive automatically; modern full status not cleared.|

Strongest objection: no full current registry audit and no UNSAT archive replay; finite Kempe failure is not a source refutation.|
