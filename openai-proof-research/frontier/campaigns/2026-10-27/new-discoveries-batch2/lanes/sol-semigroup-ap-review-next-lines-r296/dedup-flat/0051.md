## 1. Finite-difference floor sums

Thanatipanonda–Wong, *Curious Bounds for Floor Function Sums*, VOL21/Wong/wong7.pdf, published Feb23 2018, Section4 p14 and C17 pp16–17. Define S_m(A,K)=sum_(j=0)^K sum_(T subset [n]) (-1)^(n-|T|) floor((j+sum_(i in T)a_i)/m), with m>=1 and indexed entries 0<=a_i,K<=m-1; repeats are permitted despite set notation. A precise testable source subquestion is S_m(a1,a2,a3,a4;K)>=-3 floor(m/3) for every such tuple. This is part of one all-order extremal floor-sum family, not multiple clause credits. Actual Theorem15 proof decomposes into lower-order sums and gives only -2 floor(m/2)-floor(m/3); Theorem16 supplies the opposite parity bounds. Thus neither proves the requested lower bound. Mechanism: sum the periodic sawtooth finite differences by interval breakpoints, reducing to a finite chamber optimization in a_i/m. Next decisive calculation: exact four-variable chamber minimization with endpoint floors retained. Objection: classical floor-sum inequalities or the 2017 Onphaeng–Pongsriiam body may already cover it; only its source-restated theorem was read here. Current author/floor query returns arXiv:1708.03190v2, the same work. Park pending full prior theorem comparison; no formal work.

## 2. Minimal Lucasnomial denominator multipliers

Ballot, *Lucasnomial Fuss-Catalan Numbers and Related Divisibility Questions*, VOL21/Ballot/ballot30.pdf, published Aug22 2018, Questions1–3 p6. For regular fundamental Lucas U=U(P,Q), a>=2,k>=1, let m(U,a,k) be the least positive multiplier making m*[an choose n]_U/U_((a-1)n+k) integral for every n>=1. Regular means the source's nondegenerate fundamental Lucas sequence with coprime P,Q (its definition and zero-discriminant identity case apply); do not broaden to arbitrary recurrences. Q1 asks for all such triples with m=3. Actual Theorem38 and its proof give existence and m=product p^alpha_p, where alpha_p is the maximal nonnegative valuation deficit and only finitely many primes of rank<ak contribute. Theorem30 classifies m=1, not m=3. Mechanism: classify rank/valuation deficits with only the prime3 allowed. Next decisive calculation: derive necessary exclusions at primitive prime divisors and a finite remaining parameter list, then prove all-n sufficiency. Objection: source's valuation formula may make the requested classification routine or older Lucas literature may complete it. Current Lucasnomial/Ballot arXiv query has zero entries, which supplies no prior clearance. Park.

## 3. Hyperquadratic irreducibility over rational function fields

Lasjaunias, *On Particular Families of Hyperquadratic Continued Fractions in Power Series Fields of Odd Characteristic*, VOL21/Lasjaunias/lasj5.pdf, published Nov24 2018; precursor arXiv:1803.01739v1 March5 2018. C10 p16 asks: for every odd prime p, 1<=k<p/2, 1<=j<=2k-1 and epsilon in F_p^* excluding epsilon_A,epsilon_B, is H_V irreducible over F_p(T)? Here omega_k=(-1)^(k-1) product_(i=1)^k(1-1/(2i)), w1=(2k-1)/(2k omega_k), w_(i+1)w_i=(2k-2i-1)(2k-2i+1)/(i(2k-i)); K_(n,m) is the continuant of w_n T,...,w_m T, with K_(n,n-1)=1,K_(n,n-2)=0. The actual definition p12 is

H_V=K_(j+2,2k) X^(p+1)-K_(j+1,2k) X^p+epsilon(K_(1,j)X+K_(1,j-1)).

epsilon_A=(-1)^(k+j+1)[w_(j+1),...,w_(2k),omega_k], epsilon_B=(-1)^(k+j+1)[w_(j+1),...,w_(2k)]. Proposition9's proof identifies roots through the continued-fraction continuants and special quadratic factors at extremal k; it does not exclude all other factors. A displayed intermediate proof line has inconsistent indices/X compared with the defining formula; use the definition, not that line. Mechanism: projective Frobenius action and factor degrees via its semilinear orbits. Next calculation: derive the exact stabilizer criterion for those continuants uniformly in p,k,j. Objection: projective-polynomial factorization literature may subsume it. Actual later arXiv:1910.02648v1 (Oct7 2019) body discusses low-degree differential-quadratic conditions and constant-coefficient examples p=11,17, not this all-parameter F_p(T) criterion. Its references identify further uninspected factorization bodies; park, not cleared.

## 4. Regularity of zero positions in inverse period doubling

Rampersad–Stipulanti, *The Formal Inverse of the Period-Doubling Sequence*, VOL21/Stipulanti/stip8.pdf, published Dec5 2018, Problem27 p20. Let d_n=v2(n+1) mod2 and let u be coefficients of the compositional inverse over F2 of its generating series. The increasing enumeration b of {m>=0:u_m=0}, beginning0,2,3,4,6,... (A317544), is asked to be k-regular for some integer k>=2. This asks about the index sequence, not the binary indicator, and not reciprocal inversion. Actual Proposition25/Corollary26 prove nonregularity for the *one*-positions using reduction mod3, a Fibonacci-associated morphism, and multiplicative independence. Remark28 supplies a Baum–Sweet alternative for that same one-position sequence, without answering P27. Mechanism: derive the gap/substitution system for complementary zero positions and either produce a finite module for its k-kernel or a nonautomatic modular image. Next calculation: exact automaton-to-index transducer and a closed gap recurrence. Objection: automaticity of the indicator does not imply regularity of its increasing enumeration; related Merta bodies may already settle it. Broad current period-doubling query is dominated by physics and supplies no freshness evidence. Park for targeted author/body follow-up.

## 5. Multiplicity in digit-sum reversal products

Niţică, *About Some Relatives of the Taxicab Number*, VOL21/Nitica/nitica10.pdf, published Dec9 2018, Q18 p4: are there infinitely many base-b multiplicative Ramanujan–Hardy numbers having at least two distinct multiplicative multipliers? Here b>=2 is fixed, N,M are positive integers, s_b(N) is its base-b digit sum, and N=M s_b(N)*(M s_b(N))^R, reversal in that base. The source introduces arbitrary b; an answer restricted to b=10 or odd b is not silently full scope. Actual Theorems12/15 give many additive multipliers; their N are explicitly not MRH. Theorem27 gives an odd-base multiplicative family, not two distinct multipliers for each N. Actual follow-up *Infinite sets of b-additive and b-multiplicative Ramanujan-Hardy numbers*, arXiv:1811.10035v1 Nov25 2018 (earlier than final journal publication), Theorems1–3 and proofs provide infinite families, divisibility by palindromes and multiplier phenomena; the displayed multiplication proof does not supply two different M for the same N. Mechanism: construct two distinct reversal-factor pairs X*X^R=Y*Y^R=N with s_b(N) dividing both X,Y. Next calculation: parametric digit identity satisfying these divisibilities for all lengths. Objection: this intersects known reverse-product constructions and r234 decimal-pair work, but the shared-digit-sum/two-multiplier condition is different; no separate credit for a plain reverse-product witness. Park pending same-object follow-up qualification.

## 6. Shifted Fibonacci gcd spectra

Rahn–Kreh, *Greatest Common Divisors of Shifted Fibonacci Sequences Revisited*, VOL21/Kreh/kreh6.pdf, published Aug22 2018, Problems13–15 p11. Precise target P14: for all integers |a|>=2, find sufficient conditions for a divisor d of a²±1 to occur as gcd(F_n+a,F_(n+1)+a), with the source's parity classes G0(a) (even n) and G1(a) (odd n). Theorem3's actual proof uses entry points e(d), Cassini and F_(ke(d)+1) congruences to derive necessary conditions. TheoremD from Spilker, stated in the body, is already sufficient at the maximal divisors a²±1; Theorems1–2 calculate only a=±3. Mechanism: exact local conditions on Fibonacci matrix powers, then CRT on the index n, retaining the exclusions that make the gcd exactly d rather than a multiple. Next calculation: characterize admissible residue classes of n at each prime power dividing a⁴-1 and their compatibility. Objection: full Pisano-period enumeration is a routine finite algorithm, and a mere algorithm restatement may not qualify as a new theorem. Current shifted-Fibonacci query locates lcm/Zeckendorf papers, not a demonstrated same-object answer. Park.

## 7. Domino defects in memory configurations

Young, *The Number of Domino Matchings in the Game of Memory*, VOL21/Young/young2.pdf, published Sept30 2018, C6 pp11–12. N_(2,k)(k-l) counts pairings of 2k cells, modulo pair relabelling, with exactly k-l pairs adjacent in the 2-by-k grid. For all l>=1 source predicts F_l(x)=sum_(k>=l)x^k N_(2,k)(k-l)=x²Q_l(x)/((1-x)^l(1-x-x²)^(l+1)), where Q_l=(l+1)x^(3l-2)+c_(l,1)x^(3l-3)+...+c_(l,2l)x^(l-2), with its low-l convention stated separately. Actual source proves l=0,1 and reports a rook-polynomial computation for l=2, with empirical l=3–5. Actual later *Generating Functions for Domino Matchings in the 2×k Game of Memory*, arXiv:1905.13165v2 Dec28 2019 (v1 May30), Theorem2/proof and Section4 supply the general D(y,z,z) series and explicitly explain recovery of these formerly conjectured generating functions. This is a positive existing-method/subsumption hit. Mechanism: extract fixed defect by z^(-l) after y->y/z in the explicit general series. Next decisive calculation, if needed for exact disposition: carry that coefficient extraction through to the proposed numerator degree/leading coefficient. Do not nominate the generating-function question as fresh. The exact all-l numerator-shape bridge was not separately completed here; no assertion that a cited abstract proves every shape clause. This is one family, not also counting its Poisson question.

## 8. Bounded-altitude cubic-lattice walks

Flórez–Junes–Ramírez, *Further Results on Paths in an n-Dimensional Cubic Lattice*, VOL21/Florez/florez4.pdf, published Dec20 **2017** although indexed in vol21 (2018), C3 p23. For every k>=1, the number of walks of length k from the origin with unit axial steps in Z³, staying at z>=0, ending at z=0, and attaining maximum z exactly1 is (3^k+5^k)/2-4^k. Definitions of C3^+(k) impose the endpoint, which must not be dropped. Actual Theorems13/17 and surrounding proofs treat unrestricted altitude via colored Motzkin correspondences, not this stated maximum condition. Mechanism and decisive calculation are immediate: the two allowed heights have transfer matrix [[4,1],[1,4]], so its (0,0) entry after k steps is (5^k+3^k)/2; subtract the 4^k entirely horizontal paths. This completes an ordinary all-k bridge using the definitions; exact checks k<=20 are only diagnostics. Recommend routine exclusion, not nomination. C1/C2 planar counts are the same family and not extra breadth. Current cube/author query produces an unrelated finite-group paper; no historical clearance follows. Its strong classical transfer-matrix objection is already sufficient to avoid investment.
