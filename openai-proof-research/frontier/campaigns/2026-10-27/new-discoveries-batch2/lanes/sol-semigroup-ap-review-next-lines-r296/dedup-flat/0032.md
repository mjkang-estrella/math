This is an eight-family body-read screen, not eight eligible open nominations. Original paper question bodies were read after retrieval/hash, distinct from later abstracts. history-bindings.json binds 750 compact top-level batch reports before reading; history-hits.json preserves aliases and excerpts. 1. **Needleman, Boggle labeling subgraphs.** Original https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.04173v1, 11 June2015, body May21, Section3.1 Open Question1: “Are there any 3×3 puzzles with nine distinct letters that have only 11 two-letter words?” Definition1 fixes the universe B_3 of boards labeled1..9; Section2.2 identifies puzzles with labeling subgraphs of the king graph, requiring every embedding, including self-isomorphisms, to extend to board symmetry. Original Theorem3.1 quotes the six-three-letter-word lower bound; its cited original proof was not fetched, so do not claim to have read that proof. The actual graph equivalence argument on pp3–4 was read. Mechanism: finite exact hitting set against all alternative labelings. Calculation: breadth_checks.py enumerates all9!=362880 labelings, finds8 board automorphisms and30335 distinct nonempty edge-difference masks, and tests all binom(20,11)=167960 clue subsets. Twelve survive; ALL have support8. boggle_witness.py independently tests the first list by direct adjacency over every permutation and finds exactly8 embeddings. Thus fixed-known-symbol-universe witnesses exist, while an all-nine-symbols-in-the-clues reading has none. This ambiguity prevents promotion as a substantive discovery, rather than hiding a domain correction. Current original-title/category search returns the original paper; latest broad hit https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02117v1 is about word-dictionary scoring, not labeling-puzzle uniqueness (abstract-only comparison). Alias hits0. **HOLD scope; not ranked.**

2. **Tripathy, Kostka/Schur divisibility.** Original https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.07982v1, 23Nov2016, Definition1/Conjecture2: with p prime and positive e<f, m=p^e,n=p^f, k=n/m,

       g(m,n)=<s_(m^(n-m)) s_((n-m)^m), s_(m^m)^(2(k-1))>,
       val_p(g(m,n))=f-e.

The source defines a^b as b rows of length a. Conjectures4/5 are stronger termwise expansions; merge into this one family. Actual Theorem10 proof counts paths in a rectangle and uses Kummer to settle p2,f=e+1; Section4 Theorem11 settles p2,e1. Read these scopes and the start of its elementary-basis expansion. Exact edge checks e1..6 give valuation1 and reproduce known results only. Current author+divisibility query returns only this original; no live registry clearance. Alias hits0. Mechanism: LR coefficient extraction plus p-adic orbit action, ideally distinguish fixed orbits from p-divisible ones. Decisive next computation: exact g(3,9) in Schur multiplication and comparison with the stronger termwise bound, BEFORE investing in a universal modular-representation claim. **Survivor rank2; substantial but high algebraic cost.**

Direct composition calculation for n3, steps2,4,6 gives V6+V2 V4/4+V2³/64. Mechanism: finite recurrence over visited vertices, then singularity/asymptotic analysis; a raw recurrence alone may be routine, not a satisfactory new closed formula. Current narrow query returned the original plus an unrelated quantum-graph paper; alias hits0. Decisive next step: a uniform m3 recurrence with enough asymptotic control to give the actual fixed-m n→infinity endpoint, resolving the indexing ambiguity first. **Survivor rank1, qualification HOLD.**

5. **Banica–Pittau, regular complex Hadamard matrices.** Original https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.4712v1,17Jul2013. Read Theorem4.1 proof's row-graph strategy, noting its detailed order6 classification is cited elsewhere. Mechanism: exact cyclotomic equations with row graph and phase normalization. Decisive calculation: compatibility of a fourth row with each Theorem3.6 branch, followed by a uniform7-row classification; a numerical random matrix search is insufficient. Current keyword queries are severely polluted by unrelated Hadamard well-posedness papers and do NOT establish status. History14 hits are broad Banica/Hadamard false positives, including Banica–Nechita; no exact regular-order7 hit in their recorded excerpts. **Survivor rank3, status/prior-art HOLD.**
