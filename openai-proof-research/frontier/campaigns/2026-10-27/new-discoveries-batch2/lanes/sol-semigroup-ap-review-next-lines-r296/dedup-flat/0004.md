# Twelve distinct primary-source families

This is a twelve-family screen, not twelve new unresolved questions. Primary PDFs and derived text were frozen before body inspection. Sources span2000–2023; chronology distinguishes deposition from internal dates. Campaign repetitions and resolved statements are explicitly removed. Only one actionable survivor is ranked. Sources are in primary/ and content-addressed copies in frozen/; primarybindings.jsonl and access.jsonl bind hashes and retrieval dates. Page numbers below are printed pages unless called PDF pages.

1. **Karp: Pochhammer Toeplitz zero preservation.** Kryvonos(ed.), arXiv:2312.07754v1, Karp contribution, Conjecture1 p3: if Σ_(k=0)^n f_k z^k has only negative real zeros, must P_n(z)=Σ binom(n,k)f_k f_(n−k)[(z)_k(z)_(n−k)−(z+1)_k(z−1)_(n−k)] also have only negative real zeros? The printed known theorem gives degree n−2 and positive coefficients, which does not imply real-rootedness. Campaign r236 already screened this collection; no freshness from rediscovery. Cached KarpZhangNinh/Result and KarpQuadraticTruncations actual bodies concern parameter Turán coefficients of rising-factorial series, not Toeplitz root locations. No all-n root-preserving mechanism; park. Higher determinant clauses are not additional families.

2. **Karlovych–Shargorodsky: backward shift norm.** arXiv:2308.04072v1, Open problem5.4 p15: determine ||B|| on H^p for every1≤p<∞, Bf(e^it)=e^(−it)(f(e^it)−f̂(0)). The identity ||B||=||I−K_0|| is printed there; p=∞ norm2 is already known and p=2 follows from orthogonal coefficient deletion. Theorem5.1 p13 bounds convolution complements on L^p; this is not an exact Hardy norm formula. Park: exact all-p extremizer mechanism absent; no credit for p=2 or printed identities.

3. **Novak: geometry-uniform integration upper bounds.** arXiv:2307.06787v1, Open Problem p4 following Theorem1: characterize volume-one domains D_d admitting e(n,F_d^k(D_d))≤c_k d n^(−k/d), and construct optimal algorithms. F_d^k controls all partial derivatives of total order≤k. Theorem1 p3 already treats the cube using product rules and supplies all-domain lower bounds. Domain classification is open ended; grids on cubes do not resolve it. Park with no new geometry mechanism.

4. **Gesmundo/AGATES: minimal apolar regularity.** arXiv:2304.10570v1, Problem4 p5 with definitions p4: for EVERY homogeneous f∈S^dV and EVERY minimal-degree apolar zero-dimensional scheme X (degree=cactus rank of f), must reg(X)≤d? Nonredundant is explicitly weaker than minimal, and known counterexamples to the nonredundant version are not witnesses here. Reduced schemes and degree-two schemes are reported positive nearby. Park: no verified minimality certificate or all-scheme proof. Problem5 is not counted separately.

5. **Grätzer: congruences of SPS lattices.** arXiv:2303.00699v1, Section5, with Sections4/6–9. Characterize finite distributive D representable as Con L for a slim planar semimodular finite lattice L. Theorem2 p3 gives every D for planar semimodular lattices without slimness; the two-cover and later structural properties restrict SPS representations. A generic distributive-lattice realization therefore loses a source hypothesis. Park: broad characterization, no sufficient complete obstruction list or new mechanism.

6. **Li–Shi–Ling: binary complementary code optimum.** arXiv:2312.09482v1, Conjecture4.5 p11 attributed to Choi et al.: uniqueness of an optimal even-like binary code containing1 iff d_LCP(n,k)=d_L(n,k)−1, all n>k≥1. Theorem4.6 immediately supplies the actual criterion: EVERY optimal code must be even-like and contain1; its two-direction proof is body-read. This paper advertises and proves a settlement, so do not promote its quoted conjecture to an unresolved lead. Exclude as source-resolved; no extra family for its intersection-code refutation.

7. **Schmid: uniform realization of narrow length sets.** arXiv:1511.08080v1, Problem6.6 p18: is there f:N→N such that for EVERY finite abelian G with |G|≥f(M), EVERY finite L⊂N_≥2 with maxL−minL≤M occurs as a set of factorization lengths in B(G)? Lemma6.7 p18 explicitly realizes arithmetic progressions via atoms g^n,(−g)^n,g(−g); arbitrary finite sets are not implied. Park until successor clearance and a width-uniform zero-sum construction. Not a new version of previously scouted fixed-group zero-sum questions.

8. **Csordas–Forgács: quadratic factorial CZDS.** arXiv:1505.00355v1 and separatelyv2, Definition4 p2, Problem37 p16. EVERY real polynomial must lose no real-root count under γ_k=(k²+k+1)/k!, equivalently must not gain nonreal roots. Rank1, full ordinary negative endpoint in PROOF.md. This is not Problem39(a), the square-root factorial multiplier family invested in r224; actual r223/r224 bodies distinguish multiplier from CZDS and do not prove this endpoint. Their nearby source Proposition35 p15 proves the weaker multiplier property only.

9. **Boon–Boxma–Winands: deterministic large-switch polling limit.** arXiv:1408.0142v1, internal January2011, deposited1August2014, Conjecture4.2 p5 and Open problem2 p7: cyclic polling with general renewal arrivals converges to a deterministic cyclic system as deterministic switch-over times grow. The Poisson/branching-service limit and numerical renewal tests are printed; a renewal fluid-law proof must preserve queue discipline, stability, scaling, and steady-state passage. Campaign r227 already screened the distinct gated/one-limited joint-PGF target in this same paper. Park this limit endpoint: no interchange-of-limits proof here, and no known Poisson theorem claimed as new.

10. **Larcher/Moser: small total continued-fraction coefficients.** arXiv:1407.2380v1, Conjecture2 p6: exists absolute B such that EVERY N has gcd(a,N)=1 with sum of continued-fraction coefficients of a/N≤B logN. Zaremba's stronger bounded-partial-quotient conjecture implies it, as explained on that page; density-one results cited there do not cover every N. Famous all-denominator barrier; park without a new mechanism. No counting separate lattice/digital-net variants.

11. **Baseilhac: current q-Onsager isomorphism.** arXiv:1512.02975v1, Problem1-a p2 asks isomorphism of the full current algebra A_q, q-Onsager algebra and the coideal realization. Early successor check: Terwilliger arXiv:2103.03028v1, actual Theorem9.14 pp27–28 and Theorems10.2–10.3 pp28–29, proves A_q≅O_q⊗F[z1,z2,…], with a polynomial center and the two-generator subalgebra O_q. This distinguishes full extension from a central quotient; its proof bodies settle the structural issue, so exclude this as a fresh isomorphism target. Do not infer abstract nonisomorphism merely from notation; the centers supply the obstruction under the paper's field/q hypotheses.

12. **Smarandache: radical rationalization.** arXiv:math/0010143v1 (2000), Unsolved problem4, unnumbered body following problem3. Rationalize the reciprocal of a finite sum of radicals. In the ordinary algebraic interpretation (rational radicands, positive integral indices, nonzero sum), this is routine finite-field linear algebra: let α be the denominator, take its nonzero minimal polynomial m(t)=a0+a1t+…+a_dt^d over Q; a0≠0 and α^(−1)=−(a1+…+a_dα^(d−1))/a0. This explicitly exhibits the full classical bridge and excludes it. Undefined radicand/index conventions cannot be converted into a new theorem by weakening the question. The essay's other problems are not additional screen entries.
