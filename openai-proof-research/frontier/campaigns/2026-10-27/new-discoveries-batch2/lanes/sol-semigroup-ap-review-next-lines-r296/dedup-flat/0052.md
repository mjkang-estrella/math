1. **Leinster–Willerton, Euclidean convex magnitude.** [0908.1582v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.1582v2), p4, text178–196: every compact convex K⊂R^m has magnitude Σ_i V_i(K)/(i!ω_i). Also implies the source's stronger asymptotic proposal; these are one family here. **RETIRE:** actual Barceló–Carbery [1507.02502v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.02502v2), Theorem3, text447–465, computes |B_R^5|=R⁵/120+(3R⁵+27R⁴+105R³+216R²+72)/(24(R+3)), which differs from the conjectured polynomial; pp32–33 solve the radial boundary-value coefficient system. The located r239 Leinster–Willerton alias is graph-magnitude Whitney twisting, a different object/endpoint, not this Euclidean question. No new counterexample claimed. Source first2009, v2 August2012; successor first2015, v2 July2016.

2. **Linusson/Kasteleyn, fixed-post bunkbed connectivity.** [0811.0949v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.0949v2), Conjecture2.1, text93–110: every graph G, vertex set T of retained posts, edge-probability vector(p_e), and u,v satisfy P(u₀↔v₀)≥P(u₀↔v₁), with independent horizontal copies of each edge. **RETIRE this precise scope:** Gladkov–Pak–Zimin [2410.02545v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02545v1), Theorem1.2 pp1–2, constructs a planar7222-vertex graph with three retained posts violating the inequality at horizontal p=1/2; proof uses robust hyperedge replacement, §3.2 onward. This directly matches the fixed-post source scope. The source's Conjecture1.1, in which vertical edges also have probability p, is not silently equated to it: Proposition2.2 only gives the one-way implication from the fixed-post statements. No fresh source counterexample/credit. Source2008/v2 November2009; successor October2024.

3. **Herzog–Vladoiu–Zheng/Stanley, depth inequality.** [0712.2308v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.2308v1), introduction text20–39: for every finitely generated Z^n-graded module M over K[x₁,…,x_n], depth(M)≤sdepth(M). **RETIRE:** actual Duval–Goeckner–Klivans–Martin [1504.04279v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.04279v3), Theorems3.3–3.5 construct nonpartitionable Cohen–Macaulay complexes; text470–482 explicitly identifies the counterexample to this depth inequality, with depth4 and Stanley depth3. Proof of3.3 forces vertex5 into incompatible intervals; three-copy gluing gives3.5. The ring/module target is not replaced by a claim about all ideals alone. Source December2007; successor firstApril2015, v3 June2016. No independent partition enumeration performed.

4. **Haglund–Remmel–Wilson, Delta identity.** [1509.07058v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.07058v4), Conjecture1.1 equations(7),(8), text171–183: for every n>k≥0, Δ′_(e_k)e_n equals each of the displayed labeled-Dyck-path rise and valley generating functions. **PARTIALLY RESOLVED / PARK remaining endpoint:** actual D’Adderio–Mellit [2011.11467v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.11467v1), Eq(1), Theorem5.3 and §6 proves the compositional rise version, hence the rise formula after summing compositions. Remark1.1 explicitly excludes the valley version from that proof. No inference that the full two-formula source conjecture is either resolved or currently open: later valley literature was not cleared. Generic Dyck-path-algebra resemblance is not a full valley proof. Source2015/v4 September2017; successor November2020. No ranking without a new mechanism/current valley proof screen.

5. **Brändén/Neggers–Stanley, descent-polynomial real roots.** [math/0408312v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0408312v2), Conjecture1: for every labeled finite poset P on[p], its descent enumerator W(P,t) has only real zeros. **CAMPAIGN HIT / RETIRE, not a fresh candidate:** alias history r59 already mentions this family. Same actual body Theorem1 and pp2–3 proves arbitrarily many nonreal zeros in P_(m,n) via Bessel-limit/Hurwitz arguments, and gives explicit examples. Remark1 says these examples are not naturally labeled; no claim that this alone settles the naturally labeled restriction. The source is itself a primary resolution body, so no invented successor is needed for this exact unrestricted statement. August/September2004 chronology.

6. **Alon–Lubetzky, delayed Shannon-capacity growth.** [cs/0608021v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0608021v1), introduction questions1–2, text68–95. For a_k=α(G^k)^(1/k), can early values stay polynomially below c(G), and can a_k have arbitrarily many significant jumps at prescribed stages? **RETIRE same-body resolution:** Theorem1 gives, for every fixedν and ε>0, a graph on N vertices with a_k≤c_k log₂N for k<ν but a_ν≥N^(1/ν); Theorem2 gives prescribed successive jump locations. Proofs in §§2–3 use finite-field/algebraic graph constructions. These are answered existence questions, not a new conjecture that all graph capacities are computable. Source August2006; current API stillv1. No computation or new graph is nominated.

7. **Razborov/Fon-der-Flaass, sharp hypergraph density.** [1008.4707v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.4707v1), §2.2 Conjecture2: π^FDF(ρ₃)≥4/9 for every oriented-graph limit with no induced oriented four-cycle; definitions of the FDF interpretation are part of the source scope. Concretely, FDF declares a triple an edge when its induced orgraph is one of the source Figure1 types I₃, P̄₃, K̃₁,₂ or transitive T̃₃. The conjecture asks asymptotic density at least4/9 for these FDF images. This is the FDF subclass of the Turán(3,4) minimum-edge problem, not arbitrary tetrahedron-free maximum density. **PARK:** source Theorem2.2 proves only7/16; Theorem2.3 adds structural hypotheses. Actual successor [1210.4605v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.4605v1), introduction Eq(2) and ensuing forbidden H₁,H₂,H₃, proves π_min(I₄³,H₁,H₂,H₃)=4/9. Its additional induced exclusions are not a complete subsumer of arbitrary FDF inputs. No general reduction removing them supplied. No rank: substantial extremal gap and no new mechanism. Source2010; successor2012 identifier with internalJune2018 date retained as a version-date anomaly rather than guessed publication chronology.

8. **Andrews–Robbins q-TSPP, primary proof by Koutschan–Kauers–Zeilberger.** [1002.4384v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.4384v2), Theorem in §1: for every n≥0, Σ_(π∈T(n))q^|π/S₃|=∏_(1≤i≤j≤k≤n)(1−q^(i+j+k−1))/(1−q^(i+j+k−2)). T(n) is the totally symmetric plane partitions in[n]^3; exponent counts S₃-orbits, not cubes. **RETIRE same-body complete published proof:** §2 reduces to Okada's determinant and the three holonomic certificate identities, which the paper proves/computationally certifies. This is the completed2010/2011 proof body (v2 December2012), rather than the2008 predecessor explicitly conditional on remaining calculations. No new enumeration, certificate claim or rerun of external certificates. Direct lane report source/alias scan found no hit. The1983 original posing documents are cited by the body but were not separately retrieved; this is an attributed-question screen using a primary proof body, not a claim to have retrieved the first posing document.
