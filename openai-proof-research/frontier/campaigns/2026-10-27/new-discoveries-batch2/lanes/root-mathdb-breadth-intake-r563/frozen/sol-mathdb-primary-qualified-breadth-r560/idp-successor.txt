In this note, we investigate some of the fundamental algebraic and geometric properties of s s -lecture hall simplices and their generalizations. We show that all s s -lecture hall order polytopes, which simultaneously generalize s s -lecture hall simplices and order polytopes, satisfy a property which implies the integer decomposition property. This answers one conjecture of Hibi, Olsen and Tsuchiya. By relating s s -lecture hall polytopes to alcoved polytopes, we then use this property to show that families of s s -lecture hall simplices admit a quadratic Gröbner basis with a square-free initial ideal. Consequently, we find that all s s -lecture hall simplices for which the first order difference sequence of s s is a 0 , 1 0,1 -sequence have a regular and unimodular triangulation. This answers a second conjecture of Hibi, Olsen and Tsuchiya, and it gives a partial answer to a conjecture of Beck, Braun, Köppe, Savage and Zafeirakopoulos. Introduction Let s = ( s 1 , … , s n ) s=(s_{1},\ldots,s_{n}) be a sequence of positive integers. An s s -lecture hall partition is a (lattice) point in ℤ n \mathbb{Z}^{n} living in the s s -lecture hall cone C n s := { λ ∈ ℝ n : 0 ≤ λ 1 s 1 ≤ λ 2 s 2 ≤ ⋯ ≤ λ n s n } . The s s -lecture hall partitions are generalizations of the lecture hall partitions , which correspond to the special case where s = ( 1 , 2 , … , n ) s=(1,2,\ldots,n) . Lecture hall partitions were first studied by Bousquet-Mélon and Eriksson [ 4 ] who proved that ∑ λ ∈ C n ( 1 , 2 , … , n ) ∩ ℤ n q λ 1 + ⋯ + λ n = 1 ∏ i = 1 n 1 − q 2 ​ i − 1 . \sum_{\lambda\in C_{n}^{(1,2,\ldots,n)}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{n}}q^{\lambda_{1}+\cdots+\lambda_{n}}=\frac{1}{\prod_{i=1}^{n}1-q^{2i-1}}. In [ 11 ] , the s s -lecture hall simplex is defined to be the lattice polytope P n s := { λ ∈ C n s : λ n ≤ s n } . P_{n}^{s}:=\{\lambda\in C_{n}^{s}:\lambda_{n}\leq s_{n}\}. A d d -dimensional lattice polytope P ⊂ ℝ n P\subset\mathbb{R}^{n} is the convex hull of finitely many points in ℤ n \mathbb{Z}^{n} whose affine span is dimension d d . For a positive integer k k , we define k ​ P := { k ​ p ∈ ℝ n : p ∈ P } kP:=\{kp\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:p\in P\} . The generating function 1 + ∑ k > 0 | k ​ P ∩ ℤ n | ​ x k = h 0 ∗ + h 1 ∗ ​ x + ⋯ + h d ∗ ​ x d ( 1 − x ) d + 1 , 1+\sum_{k>0}|kP\cap\mathbb{Z}^{n}|x^{k}=\frac{h_{0}^{\ast}+h_{1}^{\ast}x+\cdots+h_{d}^{\ast}x^{d}}{(1-x)^{d+1}}, is called the Ehrhart series of P P , and the polynomial h 0 ∗ + h 1 ∗ ​ x + ⋯ + h d ∗ ​ x d h_{0}^{\ast}+h_{1}^{\ast}x+\cdots+h_{d}^{\ast}x^{d} is called the (Ehrhart) h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomial of P P . The h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomial has only nonnegative integer coefficients, and for the s s -lecture hall simplex P n s P_{n}^{s} it is called the s s -Eulerian polynomial . In the case that s = ( 1 , 2 , … , n ) s=(1,2,\ldots,n) , the s s -Eulerian polynomial is the classic n t ​ h n^{th} Eulerian polynomial, which enumerates the permutations of [ n ] [n] via the descent statistic. One remarkable feature of this generalization is that every s s -Eulerian polynomial has only real zeros, and thus they each have a log-concave and unimodal sequence of coefficients [ 12 ] . Identifying large families of lattice polytopes with unimodal h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomials is a popular research topic with natural connections to the algebra and geometry of the toric varieties associated to lattice polytopes. Showing that an h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomial is real-rooted is a common approach to proving unimodality results in geometric and algebraic combinatorics [ 3 , 6 , 12 , 13 ] . However, the applicability of this proof technique to families of h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomials does not obviously relate to the algebraic structure of the associated toric variety for the underlying polytopes. Consequently, research into the algebraic properties of the s s -lecture hall simplices and their generalizations that can be used to verify unimodality of the associated h ∗ h^{\ast} -polynomials is an ongoing and popular topic [ 1 , 2 , 5 , 7 , 9 , 10 , 11 , 12 ] . In this note, we prove some fundamental algebraic properties of s s -lecture hall simplices and their generalizations. We show that all s s -lecture hall order polytopes [ 5 ] , a common generalization of s s -lecture hall simplices and order polytopes, have the integer decomposition property. This result positively answers a conjecture of [ 7 ] . As an application of this result, we then give an explicit description of a quadratic and square-free Gröbner basis for the affine toric ideal of families of s s -lecture hall simplices. To do so, we relate s s -lecture hall polytopes to alcoved polytopes [ 8 ] . The identified Gröbner basis is purely lexicographic and can be constructed for any toric ideal associated to an s s -lecture hall simplex for which the first-order difference sequence of s s is a 0 , 1 0,1 -sequence. This answers a second conjecture of [ 7 ] in a special case that they noted to be of particular interest, and it provides a partial answer to the conjecture of [ 2 ] . 2. Corollary 1 . Let s s be a weakly increasing sequence of positive integers whose first order difference sequence of s s is a 0 , 1 0,1 -sequence. There exists a regular, flag, and unimodular triangulation of P n s P_{n}^{s} . Proof. By Theorem 3 we know that the toric ideal of the polytope A n s A_{n}^{s} has a quadratic Gröbner basis for some term order that has a square-free initial ideal. It follows from [ 14 , Theorem 8.8] and [ 14 , Corollary 8.9] that A n s A_{n}^{s} has a regular, flag, and unimodular triangulation whose minimal non-faces are indexed by the lexicographically non-minimal sets of s s -lecture hall multisets.
