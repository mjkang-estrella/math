     Ah interesting sequence of polynomials which were first introduced by A. I. Yablonskii and A. P.
     Vorobiev to describe rational solutions of the Painlev›      equation is considered. It also gives a
     special rational solution of the Toda equation. Al1 our polynomials have integral coefficients. By
     computer calculation we proved rigorously that the first 24 of our polynomials are irreducible. It
     is natural to suppose that all our polynomials are irreducible. But our numerical results show that
     it is a very difficult mathematical problem to determine whether or not all our polynomials are
     irreducible or not. E. R. Berlekamp's algorithm was used for numerical calculation instead of
     usual Kronecker's algorithm.

     Key words: Toda equation, Painlev›         equation, rational solution, irreducible polynomial



1.    Introduction

      Recently many authors discovered a misterious link among the KdV equation,
the modified KdV equation, the Toda equation and the Painlev› equation. Al1 the
above equations are very important and fundamental in soliton theory. Some special
rational solutions of the equations are given by a sequence of special polynomials,
P,(t) (n=0, 1, 2, . . . ) , which are given by (1.1) and (1.2). We call them Yablonskii-
Vorobiev polynomials because A. I. Yablonskii [1] and A. P. Vorobiev [2] first
introduced this sequence of polynomials to describe the rational solutions of the
Painlev›     equations. At that time they did not mention the Toda equation. It was
discovered by Toda in 1966 [3]. But, to our great surprise, the Toda equation with
two time variables can be seen in the famous book of G. Darboux [4].
      Our polynomials, P,(t), all have integral coefficients. The problem of irreduci-
bility of P,(t) is treated especially by the aid of computer calculation. Our results
show that E. R. Berlekamp's algorithm [5] is very efficient when we wish to determine
whether or n o t a given polynomial with integral coefficients is irreducible. But when
we wish to determine whether or not all P,(t) are irreducible our results (computer
calculation) show that it is a very difficult mathematical problem. In general it is very
hard to show theoretically that all members of a certain sequence of infinitely many
