 Abstract. 
    
 We present analysis of some new means recently introduced by M. Raïssouli and A. Rezgui.
We establish comparison relations and results on  ( K , N ) (K,N) -sub/super-stabilizability where  K K  and  N N  belong to the class of power means, denoted by  B p B_{p} ,
and  M M  is one of the classical or recently studied new means.
Assuming that means  K K ,  M M  and  N N  have asymptotic expansions, we present the complete asymptotic expansion of the resultant mean-map. As an application of the obtained asymptotic expansions and the asymptotic inequality between  M M  and  ℛ ⁡ ( B p , M , B q ) \mathcal{R}(B_{p},M,B_{q}) , we show how to find the optimal parameters  p p  and  q q  for which  M M  is  ( B p , B q ) (B_{p},B_{q}) -sub/super-stabilizable. 
  
 
 
 Key words and phrases:  Resultant mean-map; Stable mean; Sub-stabilizable mean; Super-stabilizable mean; Asymptotic expansion; Inequalities; Power means

 Definition 1.1   ( [ 9 ,  11 ] ) . 
 
 Let  K K ,  N N  be two nontrivial stable means.
Mean  M M  is called

 Some interesting results regarding these notions can also be found in papers  [ 1 ,  12 ] .
Motivated by the results on sub/super-stabilizability for bivariate means studied in the paper  [ 11 ]  in combination with the general algorithms for the coefficients in the asymptotic expansion of the resultant mean-map and consequently of the stabilizable mean, obtained in the paper  [ 8 ] , we present the results on sub/super-stabilizability in the context of the asymptotic expansions.
