.s; p/-Valent Functions

Omer Friedland and Yosef Yomdin




Abstract We introduce the notion of .F ; p/-valent functions. We concentrate in
our investigation on the case, where F is the class of polynomials of degree at
most s. These functions, which we call .s; p/-valent functions, provide a natural
generalization of p-valent functions (see Hayman, Multivalent Functions, 2nd ed,
Cambridge Tracts in Mathematics, vol 110, 1994). We provide a rather accurate
characterizing of .s; p/-valent functions in terms of their Taylor coefficients, through
“Taylor domination”, and through linear non-stationary recurrences with uniformly
bounded coefficients. We prove a “distortion theorem” for such functions, com-
paring them with polynomials sharing their zeroes, and obtain an essentially sharp
Remez-type inequality in the spirit of Yomdin (Isr J Math 186:45–60, 2011) for
complex polynomials of one variable. Finally, based on these results, we present a
Remez-type inequality for .s; p/-valent functions.
