                                              4. Conclusion
   Theorem 1.2 leads to various natural questions regarding the distribution function F (x).
For example, the continuity of F , the rate of convergence in distribution as well as the tail
of the distribution are but a few problems arising out of our study. Another direction is to
understand the distribution of L(k, f ) without any restriction on the parity of Erdős functions.
In this course, one is led to study the analogue of higher dimensional Dedekind sums for the
digamma function. Since these investigations are far afield from our current focus, we relegate
them to future research.
