For independent uniform I,J in {0,...,n-1} and independent U,V,W uniform(0,1), n>=2, set

    A=(I+U)/n, B=(nI+J+V)/n², C=(nJ+I+W)/n².

The n² boxes have side lengths n^-1,n^-2,n^-2 and mass n^-2 each. Their interiors are disjoint and their density is n³; total mass n² n³ n^-5=1. Summing box sections gives marginal densities1 for A,B,C on (0,1). Therefore this is a jointly absolutely continuous law, not merely three continuous marginals or a singular copula with added informal noise.

Conditioning on A reveals I and U but not J,V,W. B or C reveals I,J and its own residual uniform, but neither of the other residual uniforms. These are exact product-law conditional kernels; boundaries can be assigned arbitrary kernels. Consequently B|A is uniform in its I coarse bin; A|B and A|C are uniform in the I coarse bin; B|C and C|B are uniform in their known fine bins.
