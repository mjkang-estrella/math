 Sharp permutation bound for triangular parts of correlation matrices 





   Submit a result 


 For an integer $n \ge 1$ let $B \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$ be Hermitian
positive semidefinite with $B_{ii} = 1$ for every $i$. For a permutation
$\sigma$ of $\{1, \dots, n\}$ with permutation matrix $P_\sigma$, let
$L_\sigma$ be the strictly lower triangular part of $P_\sigma B P_\sigma^*$, and
write $\|\cdot\|_2$ for the spectral norm. The ratio $\|L\|_2/\|B\|_2$ for the
strictly lower triangular part $L$ of $B$ itself enters convergence bounds for
SOR methods; over all $n \times n$ matrices its supremum is asymptotic to
$(\ln n)/\pi$, and Oswald and Zhou note similar lower estimates for positive
semidefinite $B$ with unit diagonal. Following a suggestion of Kashin, they
proved that every Hermitian $B$ has a permutation with
$\|L_\sigma\|_2 \le C \|B\|_2$ for an absolute constant $C$. Their correction
gives $C = 245$ for Hermitian and $C = 122.3$ for positive semidefinite $B$,
and withdraws the value $32.42$ claimed earlier for unit diagonal. The all-ones
matrix is fixed by every permutation; a direct computation shows that its strictly lower triangular part has
norm $1/(2\sin(\pi/(4n - 2)))$, so the ratio tends to $2/\pi$ as
$n \to \infty$. 
 Problem.  Does every such $B$, for every $n \ge 1$, admit a permutation
$\sigma$ with
$$\|L_\sigma\|_2 \le \frac{2}{\pi}\, \|B\|_2 \,?$$
Equivalently, is
$$\sup_{n \ge 1}\ \sup_{B}\ \min_{\sigma} \frac{\|L_\sigma\|_2}{\|B\|_2} = \frac{2}{\pi},$$
the inner supremum taken over all such $B$ of size $n$? 


   Source: Oswald, in Oswald and Zhou (2023), the correction to Oswald and Zhou (2017), who conjectures that 2/π is the best possible constant.
