## A source-specific necessary test

Cancel cyclotomic factors and write G=-N/Q, where N,Q are monic, coprime
integer polynomials. In the cases below Q is squarefree. Set L=105,
R=q^L-1, A=gcd(Q,R), B=Q/A, C=R/A. Since Q is squarefree, gcd(B,R)=1.
Define the unique degree<L polynomial

    P = N C B^(-1) mod R.

Then P/(1-q^L) retains exactly the periodic-tail frequencies of G whose
orders divide L. Proof: at every root of A its residue equals that of G,
because R=A C and Q=A B; at roots of C, P vanishes. Both denominators have
only simple roots. Partial fractions therefore identify all retained Fourier
coefficients. The discarded polynomial part affects only finitely many
series coefficients. Equivalently, P's coefficient vector is obtained by
averaging the eventual coefficient sequence along steps of L over a common
period. This is an identity about finite-period sequences, not an asymptotic
numerical approximation.

If 5.4 holds, its tail is a sum of nonnegative functions of periods
d=1,3,...,2n+1. Averaging along L changes each such function to a nonnegative
function of period gcd(d,L). When 2n+1<105, these gcds are proper divisors of
105 and hence each depends on at most two coordinates in Z/3 x Z/5 x Z/7.

Choose two residues in each coordinate. On the resulting eight CRT vertices
put weight -1 on one opposite pair and +1 on the other six. Every fibre sum
over a proper-divisor coordinate system is nonnegative: after fixing two
coordinates the sum over the remaining pair is 0 or 2; coarser fibres are
sums of these. The functional is therefore nonnegative on each nonnegative
proper-period function. A negative value on P would refute 5.4, even if all
coefficients of G were positive. It would not automatically refute 5.1.

This tests the literal source product, unlike the artificial rational
function used to exclude a generic proof route in root-bbk-periodic-cone-r2.

## Exact results and independent checks

All 2520 cube functionals were checked exactly for each of (k,n)=(20,17),
(30,25),(60,51). All are positive. Their minimum values divided by the mean
coefficient are respectively

- 3909708498978800 / 977427131894447;
- 4094967908034007866119418 / 1023741977016267575365507;
- 4.

Thus this particular projected-tail attack found no counterexample. It is
not a proof of a full tail decomposition, let alone an all-coefficient or
all-parameter proof. Other periods, other duals, and the finite initial
segment remain outside the test.

The projection calculation was independently cross-checked at every
cyclotomic divisor c of 105, directly from the original factors. If the
denominator contains exactly one more multiple of c than the numerator,
the value modulo Phi_c is

    105 * prod(numerator multiples of c)/prod(denominator multiples of c)
        * prod(1-q^h, h not divisible by c)
        / prod(1-q^d, d not divisible by c).

Otherwise it is zero. This follows by dividing each vanishing factor by
1-q^c and evaluating its geometric sum, giving h/c or d/c. The separate
verifier also checks 420 actual eventual coefficients in four cases whose
entire period divides 105, generated by direct formal-series multiplication.
All 444 comparisons pass. Both scripts use exact rational arithmetic and
enforce 512 MiB address-space and a 90-second CPU cap; no cap was raised.

## Remaining full-task gap

There is still no uniform construction of nonnegative numerators, no proof
of the full product's coefficient positivity, and no source counterexample.
The three cases sit well inside these necessary half-spaces; expanding this
same small test grid is lower priority than finding an all-parameter mechanism
or a demonstrably stronger obstruction. No Lean, manuscript or candidate
acceptance is warranted. Current public registry clearance was not redone;
this method-level diagnostic does not establish novelty.
