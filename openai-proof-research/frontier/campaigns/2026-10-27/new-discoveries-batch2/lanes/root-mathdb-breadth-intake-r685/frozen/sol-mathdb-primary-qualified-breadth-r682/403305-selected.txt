   Finally, we make the following observation which shows that the exponent of
the Schur multiplier of a finite group of prime power exponent q can be bounded in
terms of the exponent of the Schur multiplier of R(2, q). (For any positive integer
d we let R(d, q) be the largest finite d generator of exponent q.) If M (R(2, q)) has
exponent e then M (R(d, q)) has exponent e for all d ≥ 2, and if G is any finite

The Coset Intersection problem takes two subcosets of the symmetric group and
asks if their intersection is non-empty. It is polynomial-time equivalent to other

summarized in the title, of relatively recent progress on the asymptotic worst-case
complexity of this problem.
