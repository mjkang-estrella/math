                                                   Abstract
           Let F0 = 0, F1 = 1, and Fr+1 = Fr + Fr−1 . We prove that m+4
                                                                                
                                                                               4  F,q
                                                                                      has a symmetric
       weakly unimodal coefficient sequence for every integer m ≥ 1. Thus the fixed fourth-order
       slice of the q-Fibonomial unimodality conjecture holds for all parameters. The proof reduces
       every lower-half first coefficient difference to four values of the {1, 2, 3}-part denumerant.
       A six-residue formula gives uniform positivity for m ≥ 7, and the remaining six cases are
       verified by exact integer coefficient recomposition. No assertion is made about the unrestricted
       two-parameter conjecture.
