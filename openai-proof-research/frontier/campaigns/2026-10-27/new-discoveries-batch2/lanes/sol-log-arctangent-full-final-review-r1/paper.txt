                                               Abstract
          We give an exact computer-assisted proof of the two displayed inequalities in Mihoković–
      Raïssouli’s published Conjecture 37. A classical Stolarsky stabilization identity reduces the
      problem to strict increase of a centered logarithmic ratio. The new comparison is established
      by a finite, exact 24th-derivative certificate,
                                                 √ Abel summation and ordinary calculus. All
      finite arithmetic is reproducible over Q( 17); no sampled inequality is used. A separate
      Lean certificate proves the literal two-branch statement without residual hypotheses. This
      is a credit0 manuscript pending final review, with no acceptance or priority claim.
