Classification of generic spherical quadrilaterals
Andrei Gabrielov
Date: August 24, 2026
Abstract.
Generic spherical quadrilaterals are classified up to isometry.
Condition of genericity consists in the requirement that the
images of the sides under the developing map belong to four distinct circles
which have no triple intersections.
Under this condition, it is shown that the space of quadrilaterals
with prescribed angles consists of finitely many open curves. Degeneration
at the endpoints of these curves is also determined.
Keywords: positive curvature, conic singularities, quadrilaterals,
conformal map.
MSC 2010: 30C20, 34M03.
