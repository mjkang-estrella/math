ПРОБЛЕМЫ МАТЕМАТИЧЕСКОГО АНАЛИЗА 

2024  Выпуск 128 

  • Editorial Board: N. Uraltseva (Editor-in-Chief), A. Arkhipova, N. Ivochkina, A. Laptev, V. Maz’ya, V. Osmolovskii, B. Plamenevskii, G. Rozenblyum, T. Rozhkovskaya (Responsible Secretary),  O. Safronov.

 

  • Published in Russian since 1966.
  • Published in English since  1973. The  second issue of  Journal of Mathematical Sciences launched in 1973  J. Math. Sci., vol. 1, No. 2, 1973  is an English translation of  Problems in Mathematical Analysis, No. 3, 1972. Currently,  Problems in Mathematical Analysis is a source journal of Journal of Mathematical Sciences. Series B  published by Springer.

 

  • Since 1995, all issues of PMA (starting with No. 13) have been  translated into English and prepared for Springer by Tamara Rozhkovskaya.
  • Since 2001, all issues of PMA (starting with No. 24) have been publihsed in Russian by the private publisher «Tamara Rozhkovskaya Publisher», with approximately 6 issues per year from 2010 to 2024.

 

  • The last PMA-128 is published in English as J. Math. Sci., vol. 286, No. 2, December 1, 2024.
  • The only papers recommended by members of Editorial Board and papers by invited authors are considered. All submissions are carefully reviewed.
