# Exact palette transfer and scope

## Source and current disposition

Mattiolo–Mazzuoccolo–Mkrtchyan, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.09241v1 (19 April 2021), §3 Question 1 pp8–9: if a bridgeless cubic graph admits a proper coloring E→{1,…,5} with at most two abnormal edges, must it admit a normal five-edge-coloring? With S(v) the three incident colors, uv is abnormal exactly when |S(u)∪S(v)|=4. Parallel edges are allowed, loops excluded; surjectivity onto all five colors is not required. Proposition 3 rules out exactly one abnormal edge; its Petersen-cycle pullback proof was read. A positive answer is not the universal Petersen conjecture.

His theorem identifies the precise 112-vertex graph by normalized sorted JSON edge hash dc16cc18600cf77c8661b7baf89c7019f265299308541961ff884ea7187b4e8b and says it has no normal five-edge-coloring. The public proof uses two SAT encodings and checked DRAT derivations; our work does not claim to replay those derivations.

## Exact state and composition lemma

For a cubic multipole every vertex has degree three when semiedges count as incidences. For each ordered port retain (c,M), where c∈{0,…,4} is its color and M is the three-bit mask of all colors at its incident vertex. Retain the minimum number d of abnormal **internal** edges for that state, with a reconstructing coloring. All states whose minimum exceeds 2 may be omitted because every later contribution is nonnegative.

Joining ports (c,M),(c',M') is permitted iff c=c'. Its defect contribution is exactly 1[popcount(M OR M')=4]. The join does not alter either vertex palette: a semiedge is replaced by an edge with the same color. Therefore internal defects stay fixed and the total is the sum of child internal costs and these join contributions. Repeated ports at the same vertex retain their full common palette; local properness is checked before composition. The exterior sees a child only through its ordered ports and these palettes. Replacing a coloring by a smaller-cost coloring of the same state preserves every external join and its cost. These observations prove sufficiency and the min-plus composition rule in both directions, by restriction of a whole coloring and by gluing compatible child colorings.

S5 acts simultaneously on all port colors and palette bits and preserves properness and all union cardinalities. For a complete local coloring, rename colors in order of first appearance. The resulting restricted-growth color sequence is a unique representative of its global color orbit. Exhaustive recursion allowing an old color or the next new color (up to five) therefore misses no coloring. For ordered boundary states use the lexicographically smallest image under the 120 permutations; retain one minimum witness. This quotient is valid only for simultaneous relabeling. At joins expand the full relative S5 orbit, not independently canonicalized individual ports. The claw may be fixed to a global orbit representative because the entire assembled coloring can be renamed simultaneously.

## Complementary cycle-pullback invariant

Use target P=KG(5,2). Let X(v) be the two colors missing at v. For any color c present at v define t(X(v),c) as the unique target edge incident with X(v) whose complementary singleton color is c. At a source vertex its three local t-edges form a complete target star. For every target cycle Z the number of these local incidences belonging to Z is even. Summing over source vertices modulo2, every normal source edge cancels, because its two local t-edges agree (poor or rich). Thus the XOR, over abnormal uv, of the two coordinate unit vectors at t(X(u),c(uv)) and t(X(v),c(uv)) is orthogonal to every target cycle, and therefore lies in the binary cut space of P. This last assertion follows by choosing a spanning tree: orthogonality to each fundamental cycle determines every chord coefficient from tree coefficients, exactly the coefficients of a vertex cut.

For one defect the signature consists of two distinct target edges, incompatible with Petersen edge-connectivity3. For two defects the XOR signatures must be a cut; zero is allowed, so no handshaking contradiction follows. verify_math.py exhausts the 1024 vertex subsets,15 target edges and60 unordered abnormal local types; each type has four compatible partner types (240 ordered pairs). The actual two defects have identical signatures, giving XOR0. This is a precise necessary invariant, not a sufficient gluing theorem or a global repair. It explains why pairing the odd pullback endpoints alone cannot force repair. The r253 K3,3 obstruction to one/two Kempe moves remains preserved and is not repeated as a discovery.
