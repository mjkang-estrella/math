                                                                                        Abstract
                                                   Let A1 , . . . , Am be families of k-subsets of an n-set. Suppose that one cannot
                                                choose pairwise disjoint edges from s + 1 distinct families. Subject to this condition
                                                we investigate the maximum of |A1 | + . . . + |Am |. Note that the subcase m = s + 1,
                                                A1 = . . . = Am is the Erdős Matching Conjecture, one of the most important open
                                                problems in extremal set theory. We provide some upper bounds, a general conjecture
                                                and its solution for the range n ≥ 4k 2 s.

2    The proof of Theorem 1.3
In this section, we prove an upper bound for fp~ (n, k, s) in the case p~ = (p, 1, . . . , 1).
First we recall some definitions. Let σ = (x0 , . . . , xn−1 ) be a cyclic permutation on [n],
1 ≤ k < n an integer. Define the arc Ak (xi ) on σ as the k-set (xi , xi+1 , . . . , xi+k−1 ) where
the computation is modulo n. Let

                             C(σ, k) = {Ak (xi ) : i = 0, 1, . . . , n − 1} .

The cyclic permutation method was invented by Katona [12] to give a simple proof of the
Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem [5]. Various applications of the cyclic permutation method in
extremal set theory can be found in a recent paper [8]. By the cyclic permutation method,
Theorem 1.3 is implied by the following lemma.

    In the proof of Lemma 3.1 we also need some facts concerning shifting, an important
