 Abstract 
    
 In this paper, we prove: Let  𝔸 \mathbb{A}  be a nonnegative primitive tensor with order  m m  and dimension  n n . Then its primitive degree  γ ⁡ ( 𝔸 ) ≤ ( n − 1 ) 2 + 1 \gamma(\mathbb{A})\leq(n-1)^{2}+1 , and the upper bound is sharp. This confirms a conjecture of Shao  [ 7 ] . 
    
 AMS classification:  05C50; 15A69 
    
 Keywords:  tensor; product; primitive tensor; primitive degree. 
  
 
 
 1  Introduction 

 
 In  [ 1 ]  and  [ 2 ] , Chang et al investigated the properties of the spectra of nonnegative tensors. They defined the irreducibility of tensors, and the primitivity of nonnegative tensors, and extended many important properties of primitive matrices to primitive tensors. Recently, as an application of the general tensor product defined by Shao  [ 7 ] , Shao presented a simple characterization of the primitive tensors in terms of the zero pattern of the powers of  𝔸 \mathbb{A} . He also proposed the following conjecture on the primitive degree. 
 
 
 Conjecture 1.1 . 
 
 When  m m  is fixed, then there exists some polynomial  f ⁡ ( n ) f(n)  on  n n  such that  γ ⁡ ( 𝔸 ) ≤ f ⁡ ( n ) \gamma(\mathbb{A})\leq f(n)  for all nonnegative primitive tensors of order  m m  and dimension  n n . 
 
 
 
 In this paper, we confirm the conjecture by proving the following theorem. 
 
 
 Theorem 1.2 . 
 
 Let  𝔸 \mathbb{A}  be a nonnegative primitive tensor with order  m m  and dimension  n n . Then its primitive degree  γ ⁡ ( 𝔸 ) ≤ ( n − 1 ) 2 + 1 \gamma(\mathbb{A})\leq(n-1)^{2}+1 , and the upper bound is sharp.
