This paper asks a simple arithmetic question: which primes, and to which powers, occur in the denominators of these coefficients? We prove that the odd part of the common denominator of $\kappa_r$ divides $\operatorname{lcm}(1,3,\ldots,2r-1)$, and that the power of $2$ is at most $2^{E_r}$ with $E_r=3r-1+\nu_2((r-1)!)$, where $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation. The study of the leading coefficient leads to the formula \[ \nu_2\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^{m}\binom mj\frac1{2j+1}\right)=m+\nu_2(m+1)\qquad(m\ge0), \] which we prove by showing that the series $\sum_{k\ge0}k!/(2k+1)!!$, whose real sum is $\pi/2$, converges to $0$ in the field $\mathbb Q_2$ of $2$-adic numbers. Finally, we show that the statement ``the exponent $E_r$ is attained for every $r$'' is equivalent to an integrality property of the Taylor coefficients of a single explicit power series in one variable. That property is established in a separate paper, and is confirmed here independently, by exact computation, for $r\le48$.
