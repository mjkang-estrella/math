                                                             Abstract
             Jacobsthal was the first to study a certain kind of sum of floor functions. His work
          was followed by that of Carlitz, Grimson, and Tverberg. More recently, Onphaeng and
          Pongsriiam proved some sharp upper and lower bounds for the sums of Jacobsthal and
          Tverberg. In this paper, we devise concise formulas for these sums, which we then use
          to prove the upper and lower bounds claimed by Tverberg. Furthermore, we present
          conjectural lower and upper bounds for these sums.
