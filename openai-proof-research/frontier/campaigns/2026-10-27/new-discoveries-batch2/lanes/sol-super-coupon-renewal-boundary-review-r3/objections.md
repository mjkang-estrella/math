Exact counterexamples (no floating-point evidence):

* o→a with probability ε, o→o otherwise, a→o deterministically: success iff L=2. Length/mark independence is false.
* o→a probability ε³, a→o probability ε²: π(o)=1/(1+ε)→1, but return from a has mean ε^(−2). High atom mass alone gives no uniform exponential moment.
* o→a probability ε, a→b surely, b→a probability1/2 and b→o probability1/2, remaining o transition a self-loop: with A={a}, E V=2h exactly. Repeat-entry control is essential.

Repairs are the explicit uniform deletion minorization, stopped rare-addition estimate and deterministic survival inclusions proved in review.md. No counterexample satisfies all of the reviewed model-specific hypotheses.
