个人简介：

   袁博君，浙江科技大学讲师。 2021年毕业于安徽大学，获得博士学位。研究方向为代数图论与组合。已在电子组合等国内外知名期刊上发表SCI论文9余篇。 主持完成一项国自然科学基金。具体情况如下：

一、主要科研项目

1.国家自然科学基金青年项目，12201559，基于极端谱参数的Hermite谱刻画问题研究，2023/01-2025/12, 30万，主持，已结题。

二、近期代表性论文

1. Wenjun Yu, Bo-Jun Yuan, Moshe Schwartz, On Zero Skip-Cost Generalized Fractional-Repetition Codes From Covering Designs, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 2026, 72(4): 2123-2132.

2.Bo-Jun Yuan, Ni Yang, Hong-Yan Ge, Shi-Cai Gong, The number of dissociation sets in connected graphs, Discrete Applied Mathematics, 2025, 373: 196-203.

3.Bo-Jun Yuan, Zhao-Yu Yang, Lu Zheng, Shi-Cai Gong, Maximal and maximum induced matchings in connected graphs, Applied Mathematics and Computation, 500 (2025) 129432.

4. Jia-Min Zhu, Bo-Jun Yuan, Yi Wang, On the Diﬀerence Between the Skew-rank of an Oriented Graph and the Rank of Its Underlying Graph, Acta Mathematicae Applicatae Sinica, English Series, 40, 1 (2024), 129-136.

5.Yi Wang, Bo-Jun Yuan, On graphs whose orientations are determined by their Hermitian spectra,the Electronic Journal of Combinatorics, 27(3) (2020), #P3.55.
