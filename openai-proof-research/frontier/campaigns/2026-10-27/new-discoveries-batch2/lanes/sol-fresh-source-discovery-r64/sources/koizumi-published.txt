                                     Abstract
We show that sequences of positive integers whose ratios a2n /an+1 lie within a
specific range are almost uniquely determined by their reciprocal sums. For in-
stance, the Sylvester sequence is uniquely characterized as the only sequence with
a2n /an+1 ∈ [2/3, 4/3] whose reciprocal sum is equal to 1. This result has applica-
tions to irrationality problems. We prove that for almost every real number α > 1,
                              n
sequences asymptotic to α2 have irrational reciprocal sums. Furthermore, our
observations provide heuristic insight into an open problem by Erdős and Graham.
