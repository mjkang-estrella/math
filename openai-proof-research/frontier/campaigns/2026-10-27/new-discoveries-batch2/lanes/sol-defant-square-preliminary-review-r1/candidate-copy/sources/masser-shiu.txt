     We end this introduction with a word about the related set F* of
highly totient numbers n > 1 with the property that φ(m) < φ(n)

probable that there are no other elements in F*; furthermore this can in
fact be established with the help of a suitable gap hypothesis (see also (3)
of [1], p. 465). So the set F* seems comparatively uninteresting.
