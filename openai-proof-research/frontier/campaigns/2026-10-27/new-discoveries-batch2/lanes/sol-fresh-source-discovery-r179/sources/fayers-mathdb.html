Fayers' divisibility conjecture for (s,s+t,s+2t,… )(s,s+t,s+2t,\dots)(s,s+t,s+2t,…)-core polynomials

1 year old · traced to 2025

Let sss and ttt be positive integers with gcd⁡(s,t)=1\gcd(s,t)=1gcd(s,t)=1, and let ft(s)f_t(s)ft​(s) be the polynomial appearing in Fayers' formula for the number of (s,s+t,s+2t,… )(s,s+t,s+2t,\dots)(s,s+t,s+2t,…)-core partitions. Fayers' divisibility conjecture. Suppose t≥2t \geq 2t≥2. Then ft(s)f_t(s)ft​(s) is divisible by s+t+(−1)ts+t+(-1)^ts+t+(−1)t.

This is the second part of Fayers' conjecture and remains open according to the source.

References
  1. William Keith, Rishi Nath and James Sellers, “On simultaneous (s, s+t, s+2t, )-core partitions”, arXiv:2508.00074 (2025).

Fayers conjectured that, for coprime positive integers s,ts,ts,t with t≥2t\geq2t≥2, the polynomial ft(s)f_t(s)ft​(s) counting these core partitions is divisible by s+t+(−1)ts+t+(-1)^ts+t+(−1)t. ft(s)=∑m=1tt!m!(t−1m−1)(s+1)m−1‾∈Z[s].f_t(s)=\sum_{m=1}^{t}\frac{t!}{m!}\binom{t-1}{m-1}(s+1)^{\overline{m-1}}\in\mathbb Z[s].ft​(s)=m=1∑t​m!t!​(m−1t−1​)(s+1)m−1​∈Z[s].

The associated generating function was already recorded by Vladeta Jovovic in 2003 in OEIS A079638. The new step below is its parity-dependent negative-diagonal extraction, which proves the previously open divisibility.
