- **325487**, Amdeberhan–Dixit–Guan–Jiu–Moll Conjecture 6.1: **already proved** by
  Dixit–Moll–Pillwein, *A hypergeometric inequality*, Theorem 1.2, Annals of
  Combinatorics 20 (2016), 65–72, DOI 10.1007/s00026-015-0294-5.
  The actual author/RISC PDF was downloaded and its page 2 rendered and read.
  Its theorem reproduces all four terminating hypergeometric polynomials and
  the x >= 1/2 range exactly; the text explicitly says it proves the earlier
  conjecture. The later author-hosted version of the original paper replaces
  the conjecture by a discussion citing that proof. Primary PDF:
  https://www.risc.jku.at/publications/download/risc_4965/hyperineq.pdf .
  A coefficient-ratio/one-sign-change route independently noticed here is
  uncredited. The m=1 equality at x=1/2 is also explicitly acknowledged by the
  published-proof manuscript (page 4); it is not a new endpoint discovery.
- **325243**, Loczi–Ketcheson Conjecture 2.11: the intended degree-at-most-s
  version is a routine positive-coefficient argument. Normalize to coefficients
  a_k of P(z)/(1+z)^s, with a_0,...,a_(s+1) >= 0. The vanishing coefficient of
  z^(s+1) in P gives
  0 = sum_(i=0)^s binom(s,i) a_(s+1-i).
  Every weight is positive, so a_1=...=a_(s+1)=0 and P=p_0(1+z)^s.
  The printed unused p_(s+1) is a formulation issue, not an eligible refutation.
