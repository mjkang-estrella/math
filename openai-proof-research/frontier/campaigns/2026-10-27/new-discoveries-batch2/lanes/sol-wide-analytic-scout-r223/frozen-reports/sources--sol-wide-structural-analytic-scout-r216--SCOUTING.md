2. **S SNT congruence preservation**, literal#16. Nearest ACTUAL theorem: Prop2.17 proves rank1 and rank2 cases using rk<=st+, rank<=2 equality and st+(XAXᵀ)<=st+(A). General mechanism: pull a minimal cone factorization of XAXᵀ back through X restricted to cone(col A). If X has a nonnegative left inverse Y, then A=Y(XAXᵀ)Yᵀ proves equality immediately for all A; that FULL classical sufficient bridge receives no credit. Singular X can still preserve a specific A, so invertibility is not necessary. The restricted condition Y>=0, YXA=A is also sufficient: Y(XAXᵀ)Yᵀ=A. It is NOT necessary: A=I3 and X=[[1,1,0],[0,1,0],[0,0,1]] preserve st+=3 by ordinary full rank, but YXA=A forces the non-nonnegative inverse of X. Thus even this proposed iff is closed by a full explicit classical bridge. Decisive next calculation: work with rank3 A having st+(A)>3 and characterize whether the minimum cone generators survive X; ordinary full-rank examples have no information about this gap. It remains unproved that this yields a useful full criterion rather than a tautological rephrasing of factorization. Bounded exact identifier/SNT-rank screen has no match; author/invariant aliases and current citing literature remain gaps.

Decisive next calculation: seek a simultaneous support-face/rank certificate for a rank3 matrix with nonnegative rank>=4; arbitrary support patterns cannot be substituted for actual nonnegative factorizations.
