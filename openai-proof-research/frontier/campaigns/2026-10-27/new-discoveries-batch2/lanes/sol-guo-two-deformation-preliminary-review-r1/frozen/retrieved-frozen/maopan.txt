                                                   Abstract. We prove many congruences modulo p2 for the truncated hypergeometric series
                                                   in a unified way. For example, for any odd prime p and two p-integers α, β, we have the p-adic
                                                   Gauss identity

                                                   provided that p is less than the sum of the least non-negative residues of α and β modulo
                                                   p. Furthermore, the p-adic analogues of some common hypergeometric identities, including
                                                   the balanced 4 F3 transformation and Whipple’s 7 F6 transformation, are established. We also
                                                   confirm a conjecture of Deines et al.
