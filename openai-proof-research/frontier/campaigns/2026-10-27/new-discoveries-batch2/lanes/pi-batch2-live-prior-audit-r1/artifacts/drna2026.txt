Abstract
In this article, we present the best possible proof of the conjectures posed by re-searchers Li Yin et al. and
Riku Klen et al., along with the appropriate bounds involv-ing generalized trigonometric and hyperbolic
functions. These inequalities are further generalized with the best possible bounds. Alternative proofs of
the Cusa-Huygens inequality were given using particular cases.

Conclusion

In this paper, we provided proofs of the inequalities involving generalized hyperbolic and trigonometric functions with one
parameter establishing the best possible bounds. Additionally, we made efforts to address the conjectures posed by researchers.
