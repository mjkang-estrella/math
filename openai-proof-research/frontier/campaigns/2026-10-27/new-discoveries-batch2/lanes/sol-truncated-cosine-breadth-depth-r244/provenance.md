# Provenance and execution discipline

All writes are inside this assigned lane. Administrative AGENTS/ledger/prior context were snapshot, not scientifically frozen. freeze.json binds the r243 discovery, scouting, failure and numerical reports plus the literal1003.1278v1 PDF/text and selected integer-shift comparison. Copies were hash-verified against originals before scientific use. The r243 history index is reused by immutable hash; historical trees were not recursively duplicated. history-exclusion-screen.json records bounded keyword matches against hash-matched historical reports.

Every new retrieval is through retrieve.py under the user-specified helper_guard.py, with OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1. The guard fixes address space and groupRSS512MiB, CPU210s and wall230s; no cap was increased. Native bounded reads and apply_patch were used as permitted. New primary bytes are saved in primary, copied to frozen and verified BEFORE PDF text extraction/reading; freeze-new.jsonl and http.jsonl preserve the URL, UTC retrieval time, status and SHA256. XML metadata is also frozen before inspection. No search result or abstract substitutes for an asserted proof comparison.

source-map.json binds both PDF bytes and extracted text. chronology.json records the publication/update dates returned by the saved Atom bodies when identifiers match, and separately records printed arXiv version banners/interior dates where metadata was not returned. A later revision date is not silently treated as original publication. This is a bounded arXiv primary-source survey on30Oct2026, not a full citation-index, public-registry or priority audit.

All Python, retrieval, computation, metadata assembly, hashes and verification run through the fixed guard. cluster.log and leading.log contain guard receipts. Checks are described in checks.md. Final manifest excludes itself and receipt.json. verify.py is then run in a SEPARATE guarded invocation after the other writes, verifies every manifest member, checks original/copy bindings before and after a deterministic exact leading replay, and writes only receipt.json. Source changes after freeze make verification fail.
