                                                                 1. A Bernstein type inequality
                                           A classical theorem due to Bernstein [2] states that every even trigonometric
                                         polynomial T of order M satisfies the inequality

                                                                             kT ′ kL∞ ≤ M kT kL∞ .

                                         His result was extended to all trigonometric polynomials by Fejér [3]. Another
                                         proof was given by M. Riesz [4], [5]; this also shows that

                                         (1.1)                           kT ′ kLp (I) ≤ M kT kLp(I)

                                         for every interval I of length |I| = 2π and for every exponent 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞.
                                            For p = 2 this inequality follows easily by applying Parseval’s formula. Indeed,
