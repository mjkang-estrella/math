                                              ON A FAMILY OF POLYNOMIALS RELATED TO ζ(2, 1) = ζ(3)

                                                                                   WADIM ZUDILIN




arXiv:1504.07696v2 [math.NT] 31 Mar 2017
                                                  Abstract. We give a new proof of the identity ζ({2, 1}l ) = ζ({3}l ) of the mul-
                                                  tiple zeta values, where l = 1, 2, . . . , using generating functions of the underlying
                                                  generalized polylogarithms. In the course of study we arrive at (hypergeometric)
                                                  polynomials satisfying 3-term recurrence relations, whose properties we exam-
                                                  ine and compare with analogous ones of polynomials originated from an (ex-)
                                                  conjectural identity of Borwein, Bradley and Broadhurst.
