PMC Copyright notice PMCID: PMC9361280 PMID: 35966718 Abstract Let I ν x be the modified Bessel function of the first kind of order ν . Motivated by a conjecture on the convexity of the ratio W ν x = x I ν x / I ν + 1 x for ν > - 2 , using the monotonicity rules for a ratio of two power series and an elementary technique, we present fully the convexity of the functions W ν x , W ν x - x 2 / 2 ν + 4 and W ν x 1 / θ for θ ≥ 2 on 0 , ∞ in different value ranges of ν , which give an answer to the conjecture and extend known results. As consequences, some monotonicity results and new functional inequalities for W ν x are established. As applications, an open problem and a conjectures are settled. Finally, a conjecture on the complete monotonicity of W ν x 1 / θ for θ ≥ 2 is proposed. Keywords: Modified Bessel function, Convexity, Monotonicity, Inequality Introduction It is well-known that the second-order modified Bessel differential equation x 2 y ″ x + x y ′ x - x 2 + ν 2 y x = 0 , 1.1 has linearly independent solutions, that are, modified Bessel functions of the first and second, as usual, denoted by I ν and K ν , respectively, see [ 1 , p. 77]. The modified Bessel function of the first kind I ν x can be represented explicitly by the infinite series as I ν x = ∑ n = 0 ∞ x / 2 2 n + ν n ! Γ ν + n + 1 , x ∈ R , ν ∈ R \ { - 1 , - 2 , … } , 1.2 while the modified Bessel function of the second kind K ν x is given by K ν x = π 2 I - ν x - I ν x sin ν π , where the right-hand side of the formula above is replaced by its limiting value if ν is an integer. It is known that I ν x satisfies the recurrence relations x I ν ′ x + ν I ν x = x I ν - 1 x , 1.3 x I ν ′ x - ν I ν x = x I ν + 1 x , 1.4 which imply that I ν - 1 x - I ν + 1 x = 2 ν x I ν x 1.5 and y ν x : = x I ν ′ x I ν x = W ν - 1 x - ν = x 2 W ν x + ν , 1.6 where W ν x = x I ν x I ν + 1 x for ν > - 2 and x > 0 . 1.7 The ratio W ν x = x I ν x / I ν + 1 x plays an important role in finite elasticity [ 2 , 3 ] and epidemiological models [ 4 , 5 ], while another ratio R ν x = I ν + 1 x / I ν x appeared in probability and statistics [ 6 – 8 ], and was applied in chemical kinetics [ 9 , 10 ], optics [ 11 ] and signal processing [ 12 ]. For this reason, the properties of the ratio W ν x have attracted many scholars’ interest. In 1983 Watson [ 13 ] showed that W ν x is increasing on ( 0 , ∞ ) for all ν ≥ 0 , which was used in the study of a Bayesian estimation problem by Robert [ 7 ]. This result was proven again in 1984 by Simpson and Spector [ 2 ] using another way. Baricz and Neuman [ 14 , Theorem 2.2 (d)] in 2007 proved that W ν x is increasing on ( 0 , ∞ ) for all ν > - 2 . Another proof of this monotonicity property for ν > - 1 can be seen in [ 15 , Theorem 2.2 (i)]. In this paper, Baricz [ 15 , Theorem 2.2 (i)] proved that the function x ↦ x μ - ν I ν x / I μ x is strictly increasing on ( 0 , ∞ ) for all μ > ν > - 1 . In [ 13 ] Watson also showed that the ratio R ν x = I ν + 1 x / I ν x is increasing and concave on ( 0 , ∞ ) for all ν ≥ - 1 / 2 . Using this result, Baricz [ 16 , Eq. (2.12)] proved that the ratio y ν x ( = W ν - 1 x - ν ) satisfies y ν ″ x < 1 / ν + 1 for ν ≥ - 1 / 2 , which implies that the function x ↦ W ν - 1 x - x 2 / 2 ν + 2 is strictly concave on 0 , ∞ for ν ≥ - 1 / 2 . In 1984 Simpson and Spector [ 2 ] proved that the ratio W ν x is convex on ( 0 , ∞ ) for all ν ≥ 0 . Based on numerical experiments, Baricz [ 15 , p. 591] made the following conjecture. Conjecture 1 The function W ν x is strictly convex on 0 , ∞ for all ν > - 1 . Remark 1 In a recent paper [ 17 ], Yang and Tian pointed out that Baricz’s conjecture may be modified as follows: W ν x is strictly convex on 0 , ∞ for all ν ≥ - 1 / 2 and concave on 0 , ∞ if - 3 / 2 ≤ ν < - 1 / 2 . It is worth mentioning that, as early as in 1979, Ismail and Kelker [ 18 ] showed a more deep result which states that the function x ↦ x ν - μ / 2 I μ x / I ν x is strictly completely monotonic on 0 , ∞ for all μ > ν > - 1 . This, by choosing μ = ν + 1 , implies that the function x ↦ 1 / W ν x is strictly decreasing and convex on 0 , ∞ for ν > - 1 . Motivated by Baricz’s conjecture and the consequence of Ismail and Kelker’s result, the aim of this paper is to further investigate the convexity of the ratios W ν x and W ν x 1 / θ on 0 , ∞ for ν > - 2 instead of ν > - 1 with θ ≥ 2 . The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In the next section, we recall some tools which will be used in this paper and establish an asymptotic expansion of W ν x . Our main results are presented in Sect. 3 . In the first subsection, we present the monotonicity of the function x W ν ′ x - θ W ν x for θ = 1 and θ ≥ 2 ; in the second subsection, we find that the ratio W ν x is strictly convex on 0 , ∞ if and only if ν ≥ - 1 / 2 , which gives answer to Baricz’s conjecture; while the functions x ↦ W ν x 1 / θ for θ ≥ 2 and x ↦ W ν x - x 2 / 2 ν + 4 are both strictly concave on 0 , ∞ for ν > - 2 , the former concavity improves a consequence of Ismail and Kelker; in the third subsection, we give necessary and sufficient conditions for which the functions x ↦ W ν x - p / x where p = ν + 1 / 2 and 2 ν + 1 are respectively convex and concave on 0 , ∞ . Some consequences of main results are given in Sect. 4 , which include the monotonicity of three functions involving W ν x and several new functional inequalities for W ν x . In Sect. 5 , as applications, we give answers to Gaunt’s open problem and Baricz–Neuman’s conjecture. Finally, we make a conjecture that the function x ↦ W ν x 1 / θ for θ ≥ 2 and ν > - 1 is a Bernstein function. Conclusions In this paper, using the monotonicity rules for a ratio of power series and an elementary auxiliary function we proved several convexity results: For ν > - 2 , the functions x ↦ W ν x and x ↦ W ν x - ν + 1 / 2 / x are both strictly convex on 0 , ∞ if and only if ν ≥ - 1 / 2 . For θ ≥ 2 and ν > - 2 , the functions x ↦ W ν x 1 / θ and x ↦ W ν x - x 2 / 2 ν + 4 are both strictly concave on 0 , ∞ , while x ↦ W ν x - 2 ν + 1 / x is strictly concave on 0 , ∞ if and only if ν ≥ - 3 / 2 . As direct consequences of the above main results, we describe the monotonic pattern of the difference W ν x - x on 0 , ∞ for ν in the intervals [ - 1 / 2 , ∞ ) , [ - 3 / 2 , - 1 / 2 ) and - 2 , - 3 / 2 , and establish several new inequalities for the ratio W ν x . Finally, as applications, we give answers to an open problem and a conjecture. Finally, let us recall that an infinitely differentiable function f : I → [ 0 , ∞ ) is called a Bernstein function on an interval I if f ′ is completely monotonic on I (see [ 61 , Definition 3.1]). Now, inspired the facts that W ν x , W ν ′ x > 0 and W ν x 1 / θ ″ < 0 for x > 0 and ν > - 1 with θ ≥ 2 , we propose a conjecture as follows. Conjecture 3 Let τ ∈ ( 0 , 1 / 2 ] and ν > - 1 . The function x ↦ W ν x τ is a Bernstein function on 0 , ∞ .
