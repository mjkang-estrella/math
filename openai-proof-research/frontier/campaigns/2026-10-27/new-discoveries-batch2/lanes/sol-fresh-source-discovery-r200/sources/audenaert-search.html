These notions can be seen as generalising their natural counterpart for scalar means, and as a relaxation of the notion of geodesity. We exhibit two classes of non-trivial means that are monotonic with respect to the Euclidean metric. We also show that all Kubo-Ando means are monotonic with respect to the trace metric, which is the natural metric for the geometric mean. △ Less
