## Independent proof audit

**Projector geometry and lower bound.** Trace multiplication gives distance squared 2−2|u*v|², and compactness plus injectivity identifies CP² with X. Hermitian 3-by-3 matrices have nine real parameters; trace one has affine dimension eight. For a nonempty arbitrary covering member of diameter d<sqrt(2), its relative open (sqrt(2)−d)/4-neighborhood has diameter at most (d+sqrt(2))/2. Empty members receive empty neighborhoods. This argument depends only on distances. Each neighborhood is proper, and distance to its nonempty closed complement is positive exactly on the neighborhood. Normalizing their sum gives the required continuous partition without a hidden measurable-selection assumption.

H(w)=||w||²f([w]), with H(0)=0, is continuous at zero since its l1 norm is ||w||². Continuity of the PSD square root and compact local parameter neighborhoods justify continuity of the integral extension at changes of rank. Unitary invariance gives integral uu*=I/3; the factor three is correct, not six. For any nonzero w in ran Q the inverse square root restricted to the range provides a unit u mapping to a positive multiple of w. Positivity of the integrand at this u persists on an ambient open spherical neighborhood of positive measure, including when Q has rank one or two. At Q=0 both supports are empty. This establishes the exact support identity without assigning ambient positive measure to a lower-dimensional range.

Positive and negative spectral ranges are orthogonal. Their extension vectors therefore have disjoint positive-coordinate supports, so ||E(A+)−E(A−)||1=tr|A|>0 for all nonzero Hermitian A, including semidefinite and singular A. The resulting continuous odd map S8→S(m−1) contradicts Borsuk–Ulam when 1≤m≤8. The empty family cannot cover X. Since every subset of X has diameter at most sqrt(2), the stated arbitrary-cover lower assertion follows. The real spectral method is correctly credited and its more difficult equality-case obstruction is not silently imported.
