## Universal mechanism considered and exact obstruction

For Conjecture7.4, take a boxed occurrence at positions i<j<k<l in pi, with values b<a<d<c. Its four odd entries in J survive as a boxed occurrence exactly when no gap t=i,...,l-1 receives an even value 2rho_t strictly between 2b-1 and 2c-1. Equivalently, it is destroyed exactly when some such t has b<=rho_t<c. Proof: the original unselected odds cannot block this source occurrence; the inserted even points are the only new possible interior blockers, and the displayed inequalities are exactly their rectangle membership. Thus a necessary condition on rho is a family of interval-rectangle hitting constraints on its permutation matrix.

That condition is insufficient, not merely unproved: pi=(2,1,4,3), rho=(1,3,2) gives J=(3,2,1,6,7,4,5). It blocks the only inherited occurrence, but positions(1,2,4,6) have values(3,2,6,4), a new boxed2143. The other intervening values1,7 lie outside(2,6). New mixed odd/even occurrences defeat the inherited-only hitting reduction. Increasing rho already fails at pi=(2,3,1), output(3,2,5,4,1). Decreasing rho fails at pi=(1,2,3,5,4), output(1,8,3,6,5,4,9,2,7). Neither is a source counterexample: good alternative completions exist. Precise gap: find, for every pi, a bijective gap assignment which meets inherited constraints and forbids every mixed occurrence, or prove some pi has no such assignment. No uniform invariant accomplishing this was found. Conjecture7.5 additionally permits a different linear-length injection; failure of strict completion would not refute factorial growth.

For Conjecture8.5, insertion of the new maximum at gap j does not change existing boxed12 pairs: its value is outside their vertical ranges. New pairs correspond exactly to right-to-left maxima of the prefix preceding that gap. Hence b(new)=b(old)+r_j. This is the public source's insertion mechanism, not a new theorem. The total b does not determine the insertion multiplier: pi=123 and pi=132 both have b=2, but sum_j q^(r_j) is respectively1+3q and1+2q+q^2. A scalar recurrence depending only on b therefore fails at n3. Precise gap: a refined state space with a proved preservation of coefficient unimodality, or another universal coefficient comparison, is needed. Real-rootedness/log-concavity are not assumed: the source already rules those out.

For critical triangles, the source's at-most-eight active box constraints would imply the bound if every optimum had at least2n+1 active constraints. That premise is not supplied by isolatedness, LICQ or ordinary nonlinear optimization. Indeed maximize z subject to z<=-sum_(i=1)^d x_i^2 in a box: the unique interior optimum has one active nonlinear constraint for arbitrarily many variables. LICQ gives an upper count, not the required lower count. This generic falsifier is not a Heilbronn counterexample. Precise gap: exploit determinant-specific bilinear constraints to prove sufficient blocking/rank despite possible second-order rigidity, or construct an actual globally optimal counterexample. The source's finite configurations and numerical upper bounds cannot fill that gap; the paper explicitly distinguishes exact lower bounds for n9/10 from numerical optimality evidence.
