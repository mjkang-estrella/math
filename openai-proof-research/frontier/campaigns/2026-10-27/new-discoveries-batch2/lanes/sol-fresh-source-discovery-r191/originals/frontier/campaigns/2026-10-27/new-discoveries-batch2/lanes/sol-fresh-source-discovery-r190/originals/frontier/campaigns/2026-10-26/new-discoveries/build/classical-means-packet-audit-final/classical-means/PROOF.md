# Complete proposed exponent classification

The last two are already the source's proven statements; our Lean code reproves them from the literal definitions, without treating the source as an axiom. These facts and classical increasing two-point convexity imply the desired inequality for every n≥1. For n≤0 apply increasing convex exp to the weak-majorized pairs (n logG,n logA) and (n logH,n logQ). Their largest-coordinate comparison uses H≤G and the sum comparison uses QH≤AG, with multiplication by n reversing the logarithmic inequality. Thus sufficiency covers n≤0 or n≥1.
