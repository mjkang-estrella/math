## Complete bridge, checked independently

The literal source is Asanjarani–Nazarathy, ANZIAM64 (2022),54–68, [Conjecture3.9 and Proposition2.2](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446181122000013). It asserts for every finite irreducible MMPP and every t>=0 that pi exp(t(Q-D))1 >= alpha exp(t(Q-D))1, with pi Q=0, lambda=pi D1 and alpha=pi D/lambda. The cached published body and prior source audit support these quantifiers. Positive off-diagonal entries everywhere and reversibility are not required. D diagonal and strictly positive is more than sufficient. This is a forward survival statement, not SCV, an entire straddling gap, or event-hazard monotonicity.

Let U be uniform modulo2pi and conditionally take an inhomogeneous Poisson process on the whole line with f(U+s), f(x)=mu+epsilon cos x, 0<epsilon<mu. Uniform rotation proves stationarity; bounded positive f ensures a simple locally finite process of mean intensity mu. Write A=integral_0^t f(U+s)ds. Conditional Poisson void probability yields T=E exp(-A). The joint Mecke formula, tested with an arrival at x and a void in the open future interval (x,x+t], gives P=E[f(U)exp(-A)]/mu. The added atom at x is outside that interval, and rotating U justifies evaluating the environment at0. This derives the normalized Palm law rather than assuming time and event initializations coincide.

Set V=U+t/2 and a=2epsilon sin(t/2). Then A=mu t+a cos V. In the weighted integral cos U=cos V cos(t/2)+sin V sin(t/2); the sine term integrates to zero. Under V->V+pi the odd-cosine integral is

    E[cos V exp(-a cos V)] = -J(a),
    J(a)=E[cos V sinh(a cos V)].

Consequently G=T-P=(epsilon/mu) exp(-mu t) cos(t/2) J(a). For a>0, x sinh(ax)>=a x² for every real x: for x>=0 use sinh y>=y, and use parity for x<0. Averaging gives J(a)>=a/2>0. This sign computation, not the Mecke identity alone, creates the crossing. In fact its sign is negative for pi<t<2pi and positive for0<t<pi. This observation receives no extra credit beyond one refutation family.

Take mu=1, epsilon=1/2 and t=3pi/2. Then a=sqrt2/2 and cos(t/2)=-sqrt2/2, giving G<=-exp(-t)/8. Since t<5 and e<3, exp(t)<243, so G<-1/1944. The elementary e<3 bound can be obtained from sum through degree3 plus (1/24)/(1-1/5); checks.py verifies this rational upper bound. No floating-point evaluation enters the sign.

For a finite MMPP choose integer N>=3, h=2pi/N, j=floor(U/h), and an independent Poisson clock Y of rate1/h. The modulator is (j+Y(s)) moduloN, with directed-cycle forward rate1/h and diagonal -1/h. Its stationary law is uniform and it is irreducible. Let D_jj=f(hj). The finite geometric-series identity sum_j exp(2pi i j/N)=0 gives exactly mean arrival rate1, not an approximate normalization; hence alpha_j=f(hj)/N. Rates lie in[1/2,3/2]. Conditional on the modulator path the arrivals are Poisson, so its two survivals are E exp(-A_N) and E[f(hj)exp(-A_N)], where A_N integrates f(hj+hY(s)). This is also the source's killed semigroup with C=Q-D, by first-step conditioning (diagonal killing at f(hj)).

Unwrap the clock phase. The initial floor error is<h; E hY(s)=s and Var(hY(s))=hs. Cauchy–Schwarz gives E|hj+hY(s)-U-s|<=h+sqrt(hs). Periodicity causes no difficulty because f is globally epsilon-Lipschitz on the unwrapped line. Tonelli yields

    E|A_N-A| <= epsilon F,
    F=ht+(2/3)sqrt(h)t^(3/2).

Since A_N,A>=0, exp(-x) is1-Lipschitz there. The time-survival error is <=epsilon F. For Palm survival split the product into the initial weight difference and the void difference: the errors are <=epsilon h/mu and (mu+epsilon)epsilon F/mu. Thus

    |G_N-G| <= epsilon h/mu + epsilon(2+epsilon/mu) F.

This controls both laws at the same threshold. No general MAP-density theorem or unproved preservation of Palm limits is being invoked.

For N=10^12, h<7/10^12, t<5 and sqrt(ht)<6/10^6. Hence F<35/10^12+(10/3)(6/10^6), and |G_N-G|<100000189/4000000000000<1/3888. Therefore G_N<-1/3888.
