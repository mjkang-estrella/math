## 1. Cullinan–Gajek-Leonard / Yamamura

Primary: *On the discriminants of truncated logarithmic polynomials*, Integers 25 (2025), A39, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15283716, Conjecture 1, journal p2. Define F_n(X)=1+sum_{k=1}^n X^k/k. For **every** integer n>=1 the claim is Gal_Q(F_n) isomorphic to S_n. The n=1 endpoint is part of the claim. This is a secondhand precise attribution to Yamamura's MathSciNet review MR3976464 of Shokri–Shaffaf–Taleb (2019); that original review was not accessed.

Actual journal pp2–9 read, especially Proposition 1, Theorem 4/Corollary 1 and Theorem 5's proof. The source reduction uses irreducibility and containment of A_n; n>1 then requires a nonsquare discriminant. The argument for n=mq requires q>m and coprimality to the two explicitly defined rational quantities X(m),Y(m). It does **not** clear all composite n congruent to 1 mod 4, particularly squares. Theorem 2 says the residual integer product is coprime to p for n=p^(2e); it does not say the product is nonsquare.

Chronology: arXiv:2401.14138v1, 25 Jan 2024; journal revised 2 Mar 2025 and published 25 Apr 2025. Both current arXiv body and journal proof read. No universal result derived here. Extending finitely many m or checking more discriminants would be an excluded partial-family result. Strongest falsifier of a prospective full claim: an n>1 with square discriminant, after verifying the literal polynomial; no such witness found or claimed. The inaccessible original review and original 2019 proof are source gaps. No novelty PASS.

## 2. Adutwum–Clark–Emerson–Sheydvasser–Sheydvasser–Tougouma

Primary: *Distributions of Ulam Words up to Length 30*, Integers 25 (2025), A102, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17535327. Journal Conjecture 3 p5, arXiv:2410.01217v2 Conjecture 1.3. Ulam words start with 0,1 and admit exactly one ordered concatenation of **distinct** shorter Ulam words. With pi the binary-value interpretation, for every **fixed** N>1 and every a modulo N the proportion of length-n words with pi(w)=a mod N should tend to 1/N.

Read the literal recursion, symmetry statements, Section 3 growth proofs, Conjecture 3 and Section 6 modular discussion. Complement symmetry makes the N=2 comparison elementary, but proves neither all prime powers nor general fixed N. The source's claim that prime-power marginal convergence suffices by CRT needs a joint-distribution bridge; CRT alone does not establish it. This observation is not a refutation or eligible wording correction. The all-modulus conjecture remains unresolved here.

Chronology: v1 2 Oct 2024, current v2 17 Jul 2025, journal revised 2 Jul 2025, published 5 Nov 2025. Current v2 and journal retain the same target despite different theorem numbering. Finite recursion through length 14 is a definition diagnostic only. Strongest prospective falsifier: a fixed modulus and a residue whose limiting frequency differs from 1/N; a finite discrepancy is insufficient. No uniform tail estimate or such limiting obstruction obtained. Earlier Bade–Cui–Labelle–Li arXiv:2008.02762v2 (1 Nov 2020) Theorem3.1 symmetry induction was read, including the second-representation contradiction; no full earlier-paper or Mandelshtam companion audit, so no novelty clearance. Journal Theorem11 has an apparent length convention issue in its complement exponent; the literal recursion and fixed-modulus conjecture above, not that printed helper, control this lane. No correction credit is claimed.

## 3. Di Nasso–Jin

Primary: *From Schur's Theorem to the Pairwise Sum Theorem*, Integers 25 (2025), A105, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17711561, final Q12 p7 and property (dagger) p5. Let T={U tensor U: U is a nonprincipal ultrafilter on N}, and S={W in beta(N^2): pi_1(W)=pi_2(W)=Sum(W)}. The precise printed question compares Sum(closure(T) intersect S) and closure(Sum(T intersect S)); closure placement matters. The final display was visually checked from the retained journal page. The preceding separation question asks whether a PR-witness for infinite singleton-and-pair sums can fail to be a PR-witness for infinite finite sums.

Read all of Sections 2–3, including actual Lemma 2.1, Theorem 2.2 and Propositions 3.3, 3.5, 3.6 proofs. The theorem on infinite disjoint A,B with A union B union (A+B) monochromatic is already proved in this source; reproving it would earn no credit. The closure equivalence and tensor construction are also public. Neither the ordinary Pairwise Sum Theorem nor its easy implication from Hindman's theorem answers the separation problem. No new separating ultrafilter or equality proof obtained.

Chronology: received 13 Sep 2025, accepted 3 Nov, published 25 Nov. The later Ramsey's Witnesses arXiv:2503.09246v2 (19 Feb 2026) was retained; actual Proposition2.8 construction and Lemma4.3 valuation proof read, with Theorem4.1/Corollary3.9 scopes compared. These selected bodies do not certify a full current companion audit or resolve the final equality. No freshness PASS. Strongest objection: a proposed construction must have **every member** pair-sum-rich, not merely one infinite pair-sum configuration, while some member must contain no infinite finite-sums configuration.
