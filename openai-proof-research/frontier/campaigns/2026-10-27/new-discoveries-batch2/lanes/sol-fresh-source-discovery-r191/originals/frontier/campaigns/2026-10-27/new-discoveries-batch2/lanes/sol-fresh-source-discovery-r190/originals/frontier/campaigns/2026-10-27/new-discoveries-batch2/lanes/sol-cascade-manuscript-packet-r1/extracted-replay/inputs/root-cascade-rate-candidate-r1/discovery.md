## Failed route retained

The first attempted proof direction was to define a finite “stable kernel” of words all of whose factors are internal, hoping its bounded depth would force a uniform escape probability and geometric cascade decay. This fails to control the sum over many leaves. An internal word can contain many noninternal factors, so leaving the internal-node set while successively prepending letters does not prevent later return for a general, nonstable tree. Finite full bushes can contain exponentially many leaves at huge depths even with just one infinite branch. An individual path probability bound cannot be summed without accounting for that multiplicity. The final construction exploits exactly this defect. No proof of the claimed stable-kernel equivalence or its sufficiency is being reported.

An earlier single-tail candidate a_k1^M fails: its cascade is 2^(-(n_k−1)) but it has only one leaf, so it decays geometrically. The repair is a high-cardinality block-coded tail. Forced11 pairs every k positions prevent later-bush anchors, while a final n_{k-1}+3 run of ones prevents earlier-bush internal suffixes. This is the key fixed-series bridge.
