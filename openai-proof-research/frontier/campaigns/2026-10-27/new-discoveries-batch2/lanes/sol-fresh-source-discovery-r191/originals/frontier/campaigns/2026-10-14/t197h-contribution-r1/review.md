## Required objections

1. **Paid disk-filling template (decisive).** The CAT(0) companion's `thm:diagram` (Section 3, read in full here, lines 1–671) proves a linear disk isoperimetric inequality from a bounded-fragment estimate, a planar separator, an outside polygon with no interior map, and outside contacts charged to the word length. Its remark says the single finite event controls all disk words, and its sphere and disk cases come from the same estimate. The T197H residual is this operation transplanted to source197's bounded-pattern exclusion. The author's operation ledger, reading limits and candidate do not mention this companion, although the project read it in I89 (`frontier/iterations/i89-geometric-group-discovery.md`, lines 17, 50 and 206, cites its "letter filling constant at most 60/kappa").
2. **Sections 3–5 follow source197 almost line for line.** The fixed-outer-word minimization is source197's rooted case with the outer surgeries removed (`topology.tex` 142–243). The interval lemma is `lem:paired-intervals` with the exceptional boundary read as a cyclically reduced word. The extraction is `thm:no-arrangements` with one extra deficit term. Sections 3–5 contain no new topological move, estimate or probabilistic input.
3. **The local-to-global step is the paid mechanism.** Source197's bounded-pattern theorem (an ε fraction of unpaired positions must occur in every bounded near-paired system, with ε fixed first) is already a local linear boundary inequality. Source196 `paper.tex` 90 and source197 introduction 97–101 describe the separator extension "however large". Turning this into a disk inequality is the standard Gromov/Ollivier local-to-global pattern, executed in the CAT(0) companion. The new hyperbolicity conclusion follows from paid operations plus bookkeeping.
4. **No substantiality from the consequence.** As far as this review can see, a torsion-free hyperbolic nonsofic group would be a notable consequence. The brief rules out weighing it, and I did not.
5. **Chronology gives no presumption.** `parent-boundary-defect-r1/conditional-interface.md` (commit `debe619f`, 23:37:42) already contains the whole residual: fixed-word inner minimization, the formal outer disk with no lift, 6(N'+1) intervals, the |v| deficit, H ≥ 32|v|/ε, and 25H/32 remaining. It states that the author independently proposed the same route. `original-scope.txt` (file time 23:36) was fixed after that exploration and before `candidate.md` (23:39), exactly as disclosed. The freeze is therefore post-mechanism. This does not make the packet ineligible, but it gives no pre-derivation weight.
