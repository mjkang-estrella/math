    A subset D of vertices in a graph Γ is called an efficient dominating set (also called a
perfect code) if D is an independent set and each vertex not in D is adjacent to exactly one
vertex in D. By the definitions of a circulant graph and an efficient dominating set, it is easy
to see that if Cay(Zn , S ) admits an efficient dominating set D, then n = (|S | + 1)|D|. A subset
D of vertices in a graph Γ is called an efficient open dominating set (also called a total perfect
code) if each vertex in Γ is adjacent to exactly one vertex in D. Similarly, one can easily see
that if Cay(Zn , S ) admits an efficient open dominating set D, then n = |S ||D|, and D induces a
matching in Cay(Zn , S ), which implies that |D| is even.
    The existence of efficient dominating sets in some families of graphs was first studied by
Biggs [1] and Kratochvı́l [9]. It was proved in [11] that a Cayley graph on an abelian group
admits an efficient dominating set if and only if it is a covering graph of a complete graph. In
[3], a method for constructing E-chains of Cayley graphs was given and was used to construct
infinite families of E-chains of Cayley graphs on symmetric groups, where an E-chain is a
countable family of nested graphs each containing an efficient dominating set. In [14], Terada
showed that there exists no efficient dominating set in any Cayley graph on special linear group
SL(2, 2 f ) ( f > 1) with respect to a conjugation-closed connection set.
    During the past two decades, efficient dominating sets in circulant graphs have attracted
considerable attention. For example, Kumar and MacGillivray [10] characterized the efficient
dominating sets in circulant graphs with domination number two and three, and Deng [4] fur-
ther characterized the existence and construction of efficient dominating sets with domination
number prime. In [12], Obradović, Peters and Ružić gave necessary and sufficient conditions
for the existence of efficient dominating sets in connected circulant graphs of degree 3 and 4.
Applying the tool of cyclotomic polynomials, Feng et al. [7] generalized the results in [12]
by considering the case of degree pl − 1 (p prime and l positive integer). Almost at the same
time, using the subset direct sum method, Deng et al. [5] independently gave necessary and
sufficient conditions for the existence of efficient dominating sets in connected non-complete
circulant graphs Cay(Zn , S ) of degree |S | = p − 1, pq − 1 and pm − 1 (p, q primes and m positive
integer) or order n = pk q, p2 q2 , pqr, p2 qr, pqrs (p, q, r, s distinct primes and k positive integer)
satisfying that |S | + 1 divides n and |S | + 1 is relatively prime to |S n|+1 . Efficient open dominating
sets have also received much attention in recent years. In [8, Corollary 3.5 (b)], Huang et al.
gave a necessary and sufficient condition for a subgroup to be an efficient open dominating set
in circulant graphs. Feng et al. [7] gave a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence
of efficient open dominating sets in connected circulant graphs Cay(Zn , S ) of degree |S | = pl

(p prime and l positive integer) satisfying that |S | divides n and |S | is relatively prime to |Sn | .
In terms of the order of a cyclic group and the cardinality of an efficient dominating set (resp.
an efficient open dominating set), Feng et al. [6] gave a necessary and sufficient condition for
the existence of efficient dominating sets (resp. efficient open dominating sets) in circulant
graphs. Wang et al. [16] studied efficient dominating sets in circulant graphs of degree pl − 1,
where p is a prime and l ≥ 1, which extended known results in [7] and provided insight on the
general problem on the existence and structure of efficient dominating sets in circulant graphs.
Recently, Cameron et al. [2] generalized some results in [7] to general abelian groups.
    In this note, we generalize the results in [2], [5], and [7] by proving that the similar result
holds for any circulant graph Cay(Zn , S ) of general degree |S | and general order n satisfying
that |S |+1 divides n and |S |+1 is relatively prime to |S n|+1 . Furthermore, for any circulant graph
which admits an efficient dominating set, the present paper gives some results about the set
of all its efficient dominating sets. We further give a necessary and sufficient condition for a
general subset to be an efficient dominating set in circulant graphs. Similar results for efficient
open dominating sets are also obtained.


2      Preliminaries
    Let G be a finite abelian group with additive notation, and let M, N be two subsets of G .
If every g ∈ G has a unique representation in the form g = m + n with m ∈ M, n ∈ N, then G is
called the direct sum of M and N. In this case, we write G = M ⊕ N. For two positive integers
m, n, we use (m, n) to denote the greatest common divisor of m and n. If (m, n) = 1, then m
and n are relatively prime.

Proposition 2.1 ([15, Proposition 2.1]) Let G be a finite abelian group and let M, N be subsets
of G . Then G = M ⊕ N is equivalent to the conjunction of any two of the following conditions:

    (i) G = M + N;

    (ii) (M − M) ∩ (N − N) = {0};

    (ii) |G| = |M||N|.

     If S is a subset of a finite abelian group G, then we write S 0 = S ∪ {0}.

Lemma 2.2 ([4, Lemma 2.3]) A subset D of finite abelian group G is an efficient dominating
set of Cay(G, S ) if and only if G = S 0 ⊕ D.

                                                  3
    For any subset M of an abelian group G, the set of all g ∈ G satisfying g + M = M is a
subgroup of G , called the stability subgroup of M, denoted by G M . It is easy to see that M is a
union of cosets of G M . Moreover, if 0 ∈ M, then G M ⊆ M. A subset M ⊆ G is called periodic
if G M , {0}. Otherwise, a subset M ⊆ G is called aperiodic if G M = {0}.

Lemma 2.3 ([4, Lemma 2.4]) Let S and D be two subsets of finite abelian group G , and let
A = GS 0 . Then G = S 0 ⊕ D if and only if G/A = S 0 /A ⊕ (D + A)/A and A ∩ (D − D) = {0}.

Lemma 2.4 ([13, Theorem 3.17]) Let G be a finite abelian group, and let M, N be subsets
of G . If G = M ⊕ N, then G = kM ⊕ N for any integer k relatively prime to |M|, where
kM = {km: m ∈ M}.

    The following result was first stated in [12, Remark 1] and also appears in [4].

Lemma 2.5 ([12, Remark 1]) Let n = |S 0 |d, where d is an integer. Then Cay(Zn , S ) admits
an efficient dominating set D which is a coset of some subgroup of Zn if and only if s − s′ .
0 (mod |S 0 |) for any distinct s, s′ ∈ S 0 , where S 0 = S ∪ {0}.

   By the definitions of a Cayley graph and an efficient open dominating set, similar to
Lemma 2.2, one can easily obtain the following result.

Lemma 2.6 A subset D of a finite abelian group G is an efficient open dominating set of
Cay(G, S ) if and only if G = S ⊕ D.

    Similar to Lemma 2.5, we have the following result.

Lemma 2.7 Let n = |S |d, where d is an integer. Then Cay(Zn , S ) admits an efficient open
dominating set D which is a coset of some subgroup of Zn if and only if s − s′ . 0 (mod |S |)
for any distinct s, s′ ∈ S .
