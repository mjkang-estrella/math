## Actual fixed-channel proof comparison

For Yu arXiv:2402.07660v5 Theorem3, fix finite alphabets, a memoryless channel K=P_{Y|X}, target P_Y, and assume at least one P_X induces P_Y. The code is a map `f_n:[exp(nR)]→X^n`; the output is the uniform mixture of `K^n(.|f_n(m))`. For reverse divergence order `q>1`, including infinity, the normalized and unnormalized vanishing-divergence rates equal

`max_QY min_{QX|Y: D(QY|X||K|QX)<=q' D(QY||P_Y)} I_Q(X;Y)`.

This is cached HTML eq42–44; the PDF labels the rate expression eq24. PDF and HTML display-number assignments differ, so comparison here is by the literal formula and theorem, not by assuming matching printed numbers. The proof is not hiding an arbitrary-block synthesis converse. Appendix B-E, PDF pp35–36, defines the codeword set A, excludes outputs jointly typed with codewords at mutual information above R+epsilon, and lower-bounds the reverse divergence by a minimization involving `D(QY|X||K|QX)`. Its eq73 expands each summand using the product-channel likelihood; eq74–78 use type-class counts and fixed single-letter log K. Appendix D Statement2 obtains the rate by rewriting the resulting max/min inequality as `forall Q_Y exists Q_X|Y` satisfying both bounds. It does not introduce the synthesis seed posterior or bound block conditional mutual information of arbitrary A_m,B_m.

The tempting substitution is output letter `Z=(X,Y)` and a channel letter w with `K(z|w)=a_w(x)b_w(y)`, reverse order q=1+beta, and q'=1+1/beta. For one fixed product-output channel, conditional channel KL decomposes into CMI plus nonnegative marginal KL terms. However its code components are then products **over coordinates**. An admissible synthesis processor need only factor between X^n and Y^n, and may correlate coordinates inside each block. Such processors are absent from this restricted code class. Even infimizing over all single-letter product-output K does not show their inclusion. Embedding each entire block as a channel letter changes alphabet/channel with n, outside this fixed-channel asymptotic theorem and its type-count proof. A new uniform reduction is still required.

The candidate supplies that precise missing bridge. For arbitrary `P_n=M^-1 sum_m A_m B_m`, finite reverse divergence E_n and any Q, the variational identity yields `D(Q^n||P_n)<=cnD(Q||pi)+E_n`. Independence of U_i=(X_i,Y_i) under Q^n gives `sum I(U_i;M,U_<i)<=log M`; conditioning monotonicity gives `sum I(X_i;Y_i|M,U_<i)<=I(X^n;Y^n|M)`. Time sharing then proves `log M/n>=C(Q,cD+E_n/n)`.
