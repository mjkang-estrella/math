The strongest proposed contribution is the primitive top-class lift. The relevant primary version is [Xin Li, arXiv:2209.08087v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.08087v2). Here are the hypotheses and the functor, rather than an appeal to the word “natural.” Regard Gamma as a discrete one-object groupoid H, and let G=X⋊Gamma (with the source's equivalent action convention). The projection

    phi:G→H,  (x,gamma)↦gamma

is a continuous surjective groupoid homomorphism. Each range fiber, and likewise each source fiber, maps bijectively to Gamma: the missing endpoint is determined uniquely by the specified endpoint and gamma. It is proper: a compact subset of discrete Gamma is finite, and its inverse image is a finite union of compact copies of X. For the unit-space actions choose Z=H^(0)={*}, Ztilde=G^(0)=X and psi:X→{*}. The anchor maps are identities, so their local-homeomorphism condition holds; psi itself need not be a local homeomorphism. It is proper, equivariant, and bijective on the anchor fibers required in Li §3.1.2. The Hausdorff-open-cover requirement is satisfied by the whole spaces.

Li §3.1.2 defines a **permutative functor by inverse image** for precisely this situation. Its source B_H has finite lists of points as objects and Gamma-labeled permutations as morphisms. Equivalently, its n-object automorphism group is Gamma wr Sym(n). The functor sends n points to n copies of X. A morphism specified by a permutation sigma and labels gamma_i sends

    (i,x) ↦ (sigma(i),x+gamma_i).

If the following morphism has permutation rho and labels delta_j, composition has permutation rho sigma and label gamma_i+delta_(sigma(i)). These are exactly the composite translations. The empty list, concatenation, identity labels and block permutations are preserved. Thus this is a compatible functor on objects, all morphisms, tensor product and symmetry, with no clopen partition choices. Its spectrum map is paid by Li §2.6 functoriality. The classical free-symmetric-monoidal-category K-theory operation identifies K(B_H) with Sigma^infinity(BGamma)_+; Li Lemma4.5 invokes this operation via Thomason. Equivalently one can use the submission's degreewise free finite-set model, so no new equivalence is being imposed on the submitted mechanism. I did not directly reread Thomason's underlying paper and do not present Li's invocation as a direct Thomason-body read.

This is the **same** whole-X functor as the submission, not a repaired mechanism. Reorderings are the block symmetries just described. The source's full grid proof retains all horizontal exchange maps and uses constant-vertical-string inclusions and the ladder formula. The integral map can also be followed through Li's actual proof of Theorem4.18. In resolution degree p, the composable-arrow projection identifies with X×Gamma^p→Gamma^p. Pullback takes a finite-support singleton indicator at a label tuple to the indicator of the **entire** compact slice X×{tuple}. In the finite-partition proof of Theorem4.14, this is the constant multiplicity function 1 on every partition atom, hence 1_X, not a sum of atoms weighted by their areas or an orbit count. Pullback commutes with the face maps: the first face acts by translation on the X coefficient, the inner ones compose labels, and the last forgets a label. Indicator functions are therefore sent by the chain map

    [gamma_1|...|gamma_p] ↦ 1_X[gamma_1|...|gamma_p].

The §2.6 spectrum maps commute with the finite-based-set operations. At every homogeneous resolution stage the same inverse-image operation commutes with the augmentation and face functors.
