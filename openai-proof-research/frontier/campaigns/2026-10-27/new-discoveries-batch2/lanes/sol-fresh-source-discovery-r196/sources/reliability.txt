                                           1     Introduction
                                           Let Tn,m be the set of all non-isomorphic two-terminal graphs on n vertices and m edges. Let G ∈ Tn,m
                                           and let d be a positive integer. A spanning subgraph of G is called a d-pathset if it contains a path of
                                           length at most d joining its terminals. For each ρ ∈ [0, 1], the d-constrained two-terminal reliability of G
                                                              d
                                           at ρ, denoted by RG  (ρ), is the probability that the resulting spanning subgraph is a d-pathset after each
                                           edge of G is independently deleted with probability ρ.
                                               Given positive integers d, n, and m such that n ≥ 2 and 1 ≤ m ≤ n2 , we investigate whether there
                                                                                                                        
                                                                                               d         d
                                           exists a two-terminal graph G ∈ Tn,m satisfying RG    (ρ) ≥ RH  (ρ) for every H in Tn,m and every ρ in [0, 1].
                                           Such a two-terminal graph G is called a d-uniformly most reliable two-terminal graph (d-UMRTTG).
                                               In this work, we study the existence of d-UMRTTGs in each nonempty class Tn,m as a function of the
                                           distance constraint d. This article is organized as follows. Section 2 introduces the basic concepts and
                                           notation, while Section 3 reviews related work. Section 4 characterizes all 1-UMRTTGs and 2-UMRTTGs
                                           in each nonempty set Tn,m . Section 5 establishes structural properties satisfied by all 3-UMRTTGs and
                                           proves that there is a unique d-UMRTTG in Tn,(n)−1 when d ≥ 3.
                                                                                                    2
                                               Whereas d-UMRTTGs always exist when d ∈ {1, 2}, Section 6 shows that no d-UMRTTG     exists in
                                           Tn,m whenever d ≥ 4, n ≥ 11, and either 20 ≤ m ≤ 3n − 9 or 3n − 5 ≤ m ≤ n2 − 2. Additionally,
                                                                                                                        

                                           it is shown that no d-UMRTTG exists in Tn,3n−8 when n ≥ 11 and d ≥ 5. Finally, Section 7 presents
                                           concluding remarks and open problems.
