34:                                                                                 1. Introduction
35:                                              S. Gelfand wrote in 2004 (cf. [10]) : “The problem of solving quadratic equations
36:                                           for matrices seems to be absolutely classical. It would be natural that such a
37:                                           problem should have been at least formulated, or even solved, in the 19th century
38:                                           at the latest. Still, I asked many people about this problem, and they directed me
39:                                           to various sources, but nowhere could I find even a mention of this problem”.
40:                                              Let n ∈ N≥2 . In the present paper, we deal with polynomial equations where
41:                                           the coefficients are generic n × n matrices and the unknown is a n × n matrix;
42:                                           the underlying field is assumed to have characteristic 0. Questions about generic
43:                                           matrices are solved in [1] and [2] or about formal matrices in [16]. More generally,
44:                                           C. Procesi described, in [20], properties of the algebra of generic matrices.
45:                                           Let Q be the field of rational numbers. If M is a n × n matrix, then χM denotes
46:                                           its characteristic polynomial, σ(M ) its spectrum and tr(M ) its trace.
