19:                                                                                    ABSTRACT
20:                                               In this paper, we study distribution of the zeros of the Alexander polynomials of knots
21:                                           and links in S 3 . We call a knot or link real stable (resp. circular stable) if all the zeros of
22:                                           its Alexander polynomial are real (resp. unit complex). We give a general construction
23:                                           of real stable and circular stable knots and links. We also study pairs of real stable knots
24:                                           and links such that the zeros of the Alexander polynomials are interlaced.
25: 
26:                                           Keywords: Knot, Link, 2-bridge link, Alexander polynomial, half-plane property, real
27:                                           stable polynomial, circular stable polynomial, interlacing zeros

110:      The purpose of this paper is to provide a recent study on various stabilities of
111: the Alexander polynomials of knots or links in S 3 . The study was motivated by our
112: desire to answer a question (later called conjecture) posed by Jim Hoste in 2002.
113: He asks if the real part of each zero of the Alexander polynomial ∆K (t) of an alter-
114: nating knot K is larger than −1. It is exactly a question whether ∆K (−(t + 1)) is
115: (strongly) Hurwitz stable for an alternating knot K. The question leads us to other
116: problems on stabilities of the Alexander polynomial of a (not necessarily alternat-
117: ing) knot. For example, since the sequence of the coefficients of a stable univariate
118: real polynomial under a certain condition is unimodal, we see immediately that the
119: stable Alexander polynomial of an alternating knot satisfies Trapezoidal Conjec-
120: ture, one of the outstanding conjectures that still remains open. In [22], it is shown
121: that many 2-bridge knots or links satisfy Hostefs Conjecture. Further, a few more
122: subtle theorems on Hurwitz stability and real stability of the Alexander polynomials
123: of 2-bridge knots or links are proven.
