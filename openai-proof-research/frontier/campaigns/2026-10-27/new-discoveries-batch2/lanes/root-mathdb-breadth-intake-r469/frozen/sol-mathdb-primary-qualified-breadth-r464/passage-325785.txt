20:                                                                                           Abstract
21: 
22:                                                     The maximum drop size of a permutation π of [n] = {1, 2, . . . , n} is defined to
23:                                                 be the maximum value of i − π(i). Chung, Claesson, Dukes and Graham obtained
24:                                                 polynomials Pk (x) that can be used to determine the number of permutations of [n]
25:                                                 with d descents and maximum drop size not larger than k. Furthermore, Chung and
26:                                                 Graham gave combinatorial interpretations of the coefficients of Qk (x) = xk Pk (x)
27:                                                 and Rn,k (x) = Qk (x)(1 + x + · · · + xk )n−k , and raised the question of finding a
28:                                                 bijective proof of the symmetry property of Rn,k (x). In this paper, we establish
29:                                                 a bijection ϕ on An,k , where An,k is the set of permutations of [n] and maximum
30:                                                 drop size not larger than k. The map ϕ remains to be a bijection between certain
31:                                                 subsets of An,k . This provides an answer to the question of Chung and Graham.
32:                                                 The second result of this paper is a proof of a conjecture of Hyatt concerning the
33:                                                 unimodality of polynomials in connection with the number of signed permutations
34:                                                 of [n] with d type B descents and the type B maximum drop size not greater than
35:                                                 k.
