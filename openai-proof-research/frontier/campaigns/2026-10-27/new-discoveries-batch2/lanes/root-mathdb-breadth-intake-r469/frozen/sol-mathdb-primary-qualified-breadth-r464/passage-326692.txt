16:                                                     When Γ is a directed acyclic graph, the dynamics monoid of every update system supported on
17:                                                  Γ naturally arises as a quotient of the Hecke-Kiselman monoid associated with Γ. In the special
18:                                                  case where Γ = Γn is the complete oriented acyclic graph on n vertices, we exhibit an update
19:                                                  system whose dynamics monoid coincides with Kiselman’s semigroup Kn , thus showing that the
20:                                                  defining Hecke-Kiselman relations are optimal in this situation. We then speculate on how these
21:                                                  results may extend to the general acyclic case.
