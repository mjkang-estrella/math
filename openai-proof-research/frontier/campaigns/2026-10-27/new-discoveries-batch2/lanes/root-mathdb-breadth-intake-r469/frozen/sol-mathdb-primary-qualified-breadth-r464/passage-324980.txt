10:                                                                                                  Abstract
11:                                                                                                             (k)
12:                                                       A k-uniform linear cycle of length ℓ, denoted by Cℓ , is a cyclic list of k-sets A1 , . . . , Aℓ such
13:                                                   that consecutive sets intersect in exactly one element and nonconsecutive sets are disjoint. For
14:                                                   all k ≥ 5 and ℓ ≥ 3 and suﬃciently large n we detemine the largest size of a k-uniform set family
15:                                                   on [n] not containing a linear cycle of length ℓ. For odd ℓ = 2t + 1 the unique extremal family
16:                                                   FS consists of all k-sets in [n] intersecting a ﬁxed t-set S in [n]. For even ℓ = 2t + 2, the unique
17:                                                   extremal family consists of FS plus all the k-sets outside S containing some ﬁxed two elements.
18:                                                   For k ≥ 4 and large n we also establish an exact result for so-called minimal cycles. For all k ≥ 4
19:                                                   our results substantially extend Erdős’ result on largest k-uniform families without t + 1 pairwise
20:                                                   disjoint members and conﬁrm, in a stronger form, a conjecture of Mubayi and Verstraëte [23].
21:                                                   Our main method is the delta system method.

24:                                           1       Introduction
25:                                           The delta system method is a very useful tool for set system problems. It was fully developed in a
26:                                           series of papers including [11] and [8]. It was successfully used for starlike conﬁgurations in [8]
27:                                           and [14] and recently also for larger conﬁgurations (as paths and trees) in [12] and [13]. In this
28:                                           paper we apply the delta system method, particularly tools from [11] and [8], to determine, for all
29:                                           k ≥ 5 and large n, the Turán numbers of certain hypergraphs called k-uniform linear cycles. This
30:                                           conﬁrms, in a stronger form, a conjecture of Mubayi and Verstraëte [23] for k ≥ 5 and adds to the
31:                                           limited list of hypergraphs whose Turán numbers have been known either exactly or asymptotically.
32:                                               We organize the paper as follows. Section 2 and 3 contain deﬁnitions concerning hypergraphs.
33:                                           Section 4 gives a rough upper bound establishing the correct order of the magnitude. Section 6
34:                                           contains the statements of the main results. Section 7 introduces the delta system method and
35:                                           lemmas needed for the linear cycle problem and Sections 8–10 contain proofs.
