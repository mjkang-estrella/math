10:                                                   Abstract. We show that, if k and ℓ are positive integers and r
11:                                                   is sufficiently large, then the number of rank-k flats in a rank-r
12:                                                   matroid M with no U2,ℓ+2 -minor is less than or equal to number
13:                                                   of rank-k flats in a rank-r projective geometry over GF(q), where
14:                                                   q is the largest prime power not exceeding ℓ.

18:                                                                        1. Introduction
19:                                              Let Wk (M) denote the number of rank-k
20:                                                                                  flats in a matroid M. For
21:                                           example, we have Wk (PG(r − 1, q)) = kr q , the q-binomial coefficient
22:                                           for r and k. The following conjecture appears in Oxley [4 p. 582],
23:                                           attributed to Bonin:
24:                                           Conjecture 1.1. If q is a prime power, k ≥ 0 is an integer
25:                                                                                                 r  and M is
26:                                           a rank-r matroid with no U2,q+2 -minor, then Wk (M) ≤ k q .
27:                                             Unfortunately for k = 2, r = 3 this conjecture is false for all q ≥ 13;
28:                                           we discuss counterexamples due to Blokhuis (private communication)
29:                                           soon. Our main theorem, on the other hand, resolves the conjecture
30:                                           whenever r is large compared to q and k. In fact we show more, ob-
31:                                           taining an eventually best-possible bound on Wk when excluding an
32:                                           arbitrary rank-2 uniform minor:
33:                                           Theorem 1.2. Let ℓ ≥ 2 and k ≥ 0 be integers. If r is sufficiently large
34:                                           and M is a rank-r matroid with no U2,ℓ+2 -minor, then Wk (M) ≤ kr q ,
35:                                                                                                              

37:                                           where q is the largest prime power so that q ≤ ℓ.
38:                                              This was shown for k = 1 in [1]. The bound is attained by projective
39:                                           geometries over GF(q), so cannot be improved.
40:                                              Our theorem does not resolve Conjecture 1.1 in the case where r is
41:                                           not too large compared to k; in particular, the conjecture remains open
42:                                           in the interesting case when k = r − 1 (that is, where Wk is the number
43:                                           of hyperplanes).
