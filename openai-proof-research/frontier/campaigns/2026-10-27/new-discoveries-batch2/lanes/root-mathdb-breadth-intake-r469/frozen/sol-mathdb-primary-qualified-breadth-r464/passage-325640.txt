10:                                                   Abstract. We investigate the rank-generating function Fλ of the poset of partitions con-
11:                                                   tained inside a given shifted Ferrers shape λ. When λ has four parts, we show that Fλ is
12:                                                   unimodal when λ = hn, n − 1, n − 2, n − 3i, for any n ≥ 4, and that unimodality fails for
13:                                                   the doubly-indexed, infinite family of partitions of the form λ = hn, n − t, n − 2t, n − 3ti, for
14:                                                   any given t ≥ 2 and n large enough with respect to t.
15:                                                      When λ has b ≤ 3 parts, we show that our rank-generating functions Fλ are all unimodal.
16:                                                   However, the situation remains mostly obscure for b ≥ 5. In general, the type of results that
17:                                                   we obtain present some remarkable similarities with those of the 1990 paper of D. Stanton,
18:                                                   who considered the case of partitions inside ordinary (straight) Ferrers shapes.
19:                                                      Along the way, we also determine some interesting q-analogs of the binomial coefficients,
20:                                                   which in certain instances we conjecture to be unimodal. We state several other conjectures
21:                                                   throughout this note, in the hopes to stimulate further work in this area. In particular,
22:                                                   one of these will attempt to place into a much broader context the unimodality of the
23:                                                   posets M (n) of staircase partitions, for which determining a combinatorial proof remains
24:                                                   an outstanding open problem.

110:    In the next section, we will consider the case when λ has four parts. First, we show that
111: Fλ is unimodal for all “truncated staircases” λ = hn, n − 1, n − 2, n − 3i. Notice that, unlike
112: in many other instances of nontrivial unimodality results in combinatorics, in this case Fλ is
113: never symmetric (for n > 4) nor, as it will be clear from the proof, log-concave.
114:    Our second main result is the existence of a doubly-indexed, infinite family of nonunimodal
