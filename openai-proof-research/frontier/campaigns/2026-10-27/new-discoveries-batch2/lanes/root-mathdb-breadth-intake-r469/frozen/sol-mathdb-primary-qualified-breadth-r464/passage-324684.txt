7:                                                  Abstract. We investigate the problem of counting the real or complex Hadamard ma-
8:                                                  trices which are circulant, by using analytic methods.P Our main observation is the fact
9:                                                  that for |q0 | = . . . = |qN −1 | = 1 the quantity Φ = i+k=j+l qqijqqkl satisfies Φ ≥ N 2 , with
10:                                                  equality if and only if q = (qi ) is the eigenvalue vector of a rescaled circulant complex
11:                                                  Hadamard matrix. This suggests three analytic problems, namely: (1) the brute-force
12:                                                  minimization of Φ, (2) the study of the critical points of Φ, and (3) the computation of
13:                                                  the moments of Φ. We explore here these questions, with some results and conjectures.
