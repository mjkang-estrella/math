11:                                                  Abstract. Given a finite non-cyclic group G, call σ(G) the smallest number
12:                                                  of proper subgroups of G needed to cover G. Lucchini and Detomi conjectured
13:                                                  that if a nonabelian group G is such that σ(G) < σ(G/N ) for every non-trivial
14:                                                  normal subgroup N of G then G is monolithic, meaning that it admits a unique
15:                                                  minimal normal subgroup. In this paper we show how this conjecture can be
16:                                                  attacked by the direct study of monolithic groups.
