12:                                                  Abstract. Let a = a1 < · · · < ar be a sequence of positive integers, and let Ha
13:                                                  denote the semigroup generated by a1 , . . . , ar . For an integer k ≥ 0 we denote by
14:                                                  a + k the shifted sequence a1 + k, . . . , ar + k. Fix a field K. We show that for all
15:                                                  k ≫ 0 the tangent cone of the semigroup ring K[Ha+k ] is Cohen–Macaulay and
16:                                                  that it has the same Betti numbers as K[Ha+k ] itself.
17:                                                     As a consequence, we show that the number of defining equations of the tangent
18:                                                  cone of a numerical semigroup ring is bounded by a value depending only on the
19:                                                  width of the semigroup, where the width of a numerical semigroup is defined to
20:                                                  be the difference of the largest and the smallest element in the minimal generating
21:                                                  set of the semigroup. We also provide a conjectured upper bound of the above
22:                                                  number of equations and we verify it in some cases.
