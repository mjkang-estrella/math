11:                                                   d-degree which forces a perfect matching in a k-uniform hypergraph on n vertices. We also
12:                                                   extend bounds of Bollobás, Daykin and Erdős by asymptotically determining the minimum
13:                                                   vertex degree which forces a matching of size t < n/2(k − 1) in a k-uniform hypergraph on n
14:                                                   vertices. Further asymptotically tight results on d-degrees which force large matchings are also
15:                                                   obtained. Our approach is to prove fractional versions of the above results and then translate
16:                                                   these into integer versions.
