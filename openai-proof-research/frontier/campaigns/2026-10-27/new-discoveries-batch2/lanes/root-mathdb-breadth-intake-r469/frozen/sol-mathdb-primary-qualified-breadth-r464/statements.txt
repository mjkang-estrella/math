SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.0649
Let ${\mathbb T}^2$ be the square flat torus, and consider arrangements of 9 points representing the centers of 9 congruent circles. Let $d$ denote the maximal possible minimum distance between two points in the torus.

**Nine-point packing conjecture.** There is one unique, up to isometry, optimal arrangement of 9 points in ${\mathbb T}^2$, shown in the paper's Figure 9, with

$$
d=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{3}}}\approx0.3437.
$$

This conjecture extends the paper's proved optimal-packing results for $N=6$, $7$, and $8$ and predicts both the exact optimal separation and uniqueness of the arrangement for $N=9$.

ID 324641: Fujita–Nakamigawa conjecture on the balanced decomposition number
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.2308
Let $G$ be a connected graph. Its **balanced decomposition number** is the minimum integer $s$ such that, for every balanced coloring $V(G)=P_1\uplus P_2\uplus X$ with $|P_1|=|P_2|$, there is a partition $V(G)=V_1\uplus\cdots\uplus V_r$ in which every induced subgraph $G[V_i]$ is connected, $|V_i\cap P_1|=|V_i\cap P_2|$, and $|V_i|\leq s$. A graph is **2-connected** if it has vertex-connectivity at least $2$.

**Fujita–Nakamigawa's conjecture.** If $G$ is $2$-connected, then its balanced decomposition number is at most

$$
\left\lfloor\frac{|V(G)|}{2}\right\rfloor+1.
$$

This conjecture connects the balanced decomposition number with vertex-connectivity and was motivated by pebble motion on graphs. It was announced as solved by G. J. Chang and N. Narayanan.

ID 324737: Chang–Narayanan conjecture on strong edge-colorings of degenerate graphs
SOURCE https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.6093
A graph $G$ is **$k$-degenerate** if every subgraph of $G$ has minimum degree at most $k$. Let $G$ be a $k$-degenerate graph with maximum degree $Delta$, and let $c7_s'(G)$ denote its strong chromatic index.

**Chang–Narayanan conjecture.** There exists an absolute constant $c$ such that

$$
\chi_s'(G)\le ck^2\Delta.
$$

Furthermore, the factor $k^2$ may be replaced by $k$.

Chang and Narayanan proved the corresponding linear bound in $Delta$ for $2$-degenerate graphs with $c7_s'(G)\le 10\Delta-10$, and subsequent work improved this to $8\Delta-4$. The conjectured dependence on $k$, particularly the replacement of $k^2$ by $k$, remains open in the source context.
