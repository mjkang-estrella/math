                                           holomorphic function that maps the disk to the punctured disk is true.

n = 3 case was proved by J. Hummel, S. Scheinberg and L. Zalcman [5]; they
also proved Lemma 3.3 below, and that (1.3) is a strict local minimum for
(1.2) (after normalizing so that f (0) and fˆ(n) are both positive). The n = 4
