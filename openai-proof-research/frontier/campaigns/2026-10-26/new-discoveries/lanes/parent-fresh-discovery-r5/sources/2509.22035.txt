                                                    Abstract. We study the constant Cd,p defined as the smallest constant C
                                                    such that ∥P ∥p∞ ≤ C∥P ∥pp holds for every polynomial P of degree d, where we
                                                    consider the Lp norm on the unit circle. We conjecture that Cd,p ≤ dp/2 + 1
                                                    for all p ≥ 2 and all degrees d. We show that the conjecture holds for all p ≥ 2
                                                    when d ≤ 4 and for all d when p ≥ 6.8.

Lemma 3. Let 1 < p < ∞ and d ≥ 1. Then there exists a unique solution of (1).
Proof. Let (Pn )n be a sequence in Pd such that Pn (1) = 1 and (∥Pn ∥pp )n converges
