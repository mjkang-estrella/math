                                                   We prove complete monotonicity of sums of squares of generalized Baskakov
                                                basis functions by deriving the corresponding results for hypergeometric func-
                                                tions. Moreover, in the central Baskakov case we study the distribution of
                                                the complex zeros for large values of a parameter. We finally discuss the
                                                extension of some results for sums of higher powers.

                                          Butzer-Hahn basis and the Baskakov basis. As there is a huge amount of literature on
                                          this subject we only refer to [3] for a general introduction. Apart from being of general
                                          interest, one motivation for the study of analytic properties of sums of squares of such
