They observed in [3, eq. (2.1)] that this series gives the generating function for
the difference of the enumerations of two disjoint subsets of a special family
of bicolored partitions. Along this line, they conjectured the positivity of this
series, which implies an inequality for the two partition enumerations.
Conjecture 1.1 (Andrews–El Bachraoui, [3, Conjecture 2.8]). For every pos-
itive integer k, the series Fk,1 (q) is positive.
  This conjecture was confirmed by Andrews and El Bachraoui [3, eqs. (2.2),
(2.3) and (2.5), and Theorem 2.6] for 1 ≤ k ≤ 4. In a subsequent paper,
Banerjee, Bringmann, and Keith [4, Theorems 1.2 and 1.3] extended the
study to the cases where 5 ≤ k ≤ 10, and established a weaker result that
Fk,1 (q) is a nonnegative series.
  The main objective of this note is to confirm this conjecture of Andrews
and El Bachraoui.
