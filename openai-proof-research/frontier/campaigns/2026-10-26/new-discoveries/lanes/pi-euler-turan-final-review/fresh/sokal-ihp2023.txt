will introduce a generalization: a matrix M of polynomials (in some set of indeterminates) will be called {\em
coefficientwise totally positive}\/ if every minor of M is a polynomial with nonnegative coefficients. And
a sequence (an )n≥0 of polynomials will be called {\em coefficientwise Hankel-totally positive}\/ if the Hankel matrix H = (ai+j )i,j≥0 associated to (an ) is coefficientwise totally positive. It turns out that many
