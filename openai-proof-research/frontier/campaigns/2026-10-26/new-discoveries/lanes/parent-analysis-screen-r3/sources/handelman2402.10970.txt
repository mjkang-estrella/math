                                           Question 1. If h : (0, ∞) → (0, ∞) is a log convex function that is integrable on (0, ∞) and

                                           we will prove the following result:
                                           Theorem 1. There is a function h : (0, ∞) → (0, ∞) that is log convex, integrable on (0, ∞),

                                           any r > 0.

                                           terexample.
