                                                 Abstract. We answer three questions posed by Bourin and Lee on symmetric moduli and




arXiv:2602.01463v1 [math.FA] 1 Feb 2026
                                                 related orbit inequalities in [6]. First, we show that the exponent 2 in their unitary-orbit
                                                 estimate for the quadratic symmetric modulus is optimal in every dimension n ≥ 2 by an
                                                 explicit 2 × 2 counterexample for p > 2. Second, we construct a compact operator for which
                                                 the corresponding singular-value inequality fails for every p > 2 (indeed for all p > 0 at a
                                                 fixed pair of indices). Finally, we obtain isometry-orbit refinements of Euler’s quadrilateral
                                                 identity for matrices and derive several Clarkson–McCarthy type inequalities for Schatten
                                                 p-norms and related consequences.

Must we necessarily have p ≤ 2?
  Our first result answers Question 1.2 affirmatively in every noncommutative dimension n ≥ 2

Theorem 1.3. Let n ≥ 2 and p > 0. If for every Z ∈ Mn there exist unitary matrices

Remark 1.4. For n = 1, the statement of Question 1.2 is different: for a complex number
