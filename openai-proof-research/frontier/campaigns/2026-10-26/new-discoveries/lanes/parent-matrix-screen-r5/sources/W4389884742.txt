                                                                         1. Introduction and preliminaries

                                             Let B(H) denote the C ∗ –algebra of all bounded linear operators on a Hilbert space H, with
                                           zero element O.
                                             An axiomatic approach for connections and means of positive operators in B(H) was given
                                           in detail in [11]. However, some partial discussion was known earlier for the operator harmonic
                                           and geometric means, as found in [14, 15], respectively. In this context, an operator T ∈ B(H)
                                           is said to be positive if, for all nonzero x ∈ H, one has hT x, xi > 0. Such an operator will be
                                           denoted by T > O. On the other hand, we will write T ≥ O if hT x, xi ≥ 0 for all x ∈ H. The
                                           set of all positive operators in B(H) is denoted by B(H)+ .
