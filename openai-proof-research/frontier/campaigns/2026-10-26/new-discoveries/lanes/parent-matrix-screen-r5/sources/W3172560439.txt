                                                                                            Abstract
                                                        The purpose of this paper is to establish new log-majorization results concerning
                                                    eigenvalues and singular values which generalize some previous work related to a
                                                    conjecture and an open question which were presented by R. Lemos and G. Soares in
                                                    [11]. In addition, we present a complement of a unitarily invariant norm inequality
                                                    which was conjectured by R. Bhatia, Y. Lim and T. Yamazaki in [4], and recently
                                                    proved by T.H. Dinh, R. Dumitru and J.A. Franco in [5] for the Schatten p-norm
                                                    with 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞.

                                           keywords.          Positive semi-definite matrix; Hermitian matrix; Log-majorization; Eigenvalues; Singular
                                           values; Norm Inequalities


                                           2010 Mathematics Subject Classification.                     15A45, 15A60, 47A64



                                           1        Introduction
                                           Let Mn be the space of n × n complex matrices where its identity matrix is denoted by
                                           In . If A is positive semi-definite, then we will write A ≥ 0 to indicate so. If, in addition,
                                           A is invertible, then we will write A > 0. For positive semi-definite matrices A, B ∈ Mn ,
                                           we write A ≥ B if A − B ≥ 0. For any n × n matrix X, we shall assume that its singular
                                           values, which are the eigenvalues of the positive semi-definite matrix |X| = (X ∗ X)1/2 ,
                                           are taken in the decreasing order: s1 (X) ≥ s2 (X) ≥ · · · ≥ sn (X) ≥ 0. In addition, if
                                           the eigenvalues λ1 (X), λ2 (X), . . . , λn (X) of X are real, then we will always assume that
                                           they are also arranged in decreasing order, that is,

                                                                               λ1 (X) ≥ λ2 (X) ≥ · · · ≥ λn (X).

     Our main objective in this paper is to present new log-majorization results related
to the above two conjectures as well as to prove an interesting norm inequality. More
explicitly, the rest of the paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we first disprove
Conjecture 1.2 in its current setting by providing a counterexample in the case t = 0 and
r, s being two real numbers such that s−1 ≤ 0 and 2r +s−1 ≥ 0. Then, we give a further
generalization of (3) as well as of (4) in the cases where its has been shown to be valid.
In Section 3, new results related to Conjecture 1.1 are established, and a new proof for
a refinement of inequality (1) is presented. In the last section, we present a complement
of a unitarily invariant norm inequality which was conjectured by R. Bhatia, Y. Lim and
T. Yamazaki [4] and recently proved by T.H. Dinh, R. Dumitru and J.A. Franco [5] for
the Schatten p-norm with 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞.
