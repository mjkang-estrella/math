# Positive-definite tensor uniqueness: source-version mismatch

Discovery credit **0**. Do not promote the simple counterexample below as a resolution of the current author's conjecture.

The actual arXiv1501.02546v1 Conclusion, Conjecture1p10, asks for uniqueness of NCP(q,A) for all symmetric positive-definite tensors. Its definitions use ordinary positive definiteness of the homogeneous form. A simple exact construction violates that old statement: in order4 and dimension2 take pure entries a1111=a2222=6, all six permutations of a1122=7, and all other entries0. Then the full form is6x^4+42x²y²+6y^4>0 off0, whereas the tensor-vector map is(6x³+21xy²,6y³+21x²y). For q=(-90,-90), both(2,1) and(1,2) are strictly positive exact zeros and hence distinct complementarity solutions.

However, the actual later author-hosted manuscript NCP-0608.pdf has a materially different conjecture: **Conjecture5.1p11 requires A∈D_(m,n)**. Definition2.5p4 explicitly defines D_(m,n) to be tensors congruent to a diagonal tensor through an invertible common mode transformation. The original broad symmetric class is no longer asserted. This is an additional substantive hypothesis; the construction above has not been shown to satisfy it and must not be represented as refuting that current question. The existence theorem and generalized uniqueness theorem are likewise revised in that manuscript. This version change was found before Lean/paper investment.

The closest actual predecessor Bai–Huang–Wang1508.06012v1 was read, especially Examples3.2and4.3. Their P-tensor counterexamples are nonsymmetric and do not directly supply the old symmetric counterexample. That difference does not establish novelty or restore applicability to the revised author's question. No separate discovery is claimed from this comparison or the old-source example.
