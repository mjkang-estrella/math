                                             Abstract
            We investigate the size of the largest entry (in absolute value) in the inverse of
       certain Vandermonde matrices. More precisely, for every real b > 1, let Mb (n) be
       the maximum of the absolute values of the entries of the inverse of the n × n matrix
       [bij ]0≤i,j<n . We prove that limn→+∞ Mb (n) exists, and we provide some formulas for
       it.

Conjecture 12. Let b > 1 and n0 = dlogb (1 + 1b )e. Then, for all sufficiently large n, we have
Mb (n) = |ci,i,n | for some i, 0 ≤ i ≤ n0 .
