                                          5. Conclusions
   In this paper, we extended [5, Theorem 1.1] to Theorem 2 and corrected the
errors in the proof of [5, Theorem 1.1]. Precisely, Theorem 2 holds not only for the
n-tuples p[n] = (p1 , ..., pn ) and q [n] = (q1 , ..., qn ) ∈ Nn0 but also for p[n] , q [n] ∈ In ,
where I ⊆ R is an interval. Correspondingly, [5, Propositions 4.1, 5.1 and 6.1]
                                        n           n               n
are also valid for p[n] , q [n] ∈ (1, ∞) , (0, ∞) and (−a, ∞) , respectively. In other
words, [5, Propositions 4.1, 5.1 and 6.1] have be extended to Propositions 2, 4 and
5, respectively, where Propositions 2 and 4 provide applications of Theorem 2 in
the Hurwitz zeta and alternating Hurwitz zeta functions. Moreover, Proposition 6
offers an application in probability theory.
   Finally, we emphasize that Lemmas 1 and 2 play important role in the proof
of Theorem 2, which can also be used to deal with a class of problems involving
