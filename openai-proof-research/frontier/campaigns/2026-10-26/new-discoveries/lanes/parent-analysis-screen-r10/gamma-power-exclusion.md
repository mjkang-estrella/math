Actual primary arXiv2609.33959v1, Valmir Krasniqi, 27September2026,
*The Guo–Zhang–Qi conjecture and positive Laplace kernels for a digamma power
family*, was downloaded and read at body level: definitions(1.3),(1.4),
Theorem1.1, Corollary1.2, kernel identities in Sections2–3, positivity in Section4
and the proof in Section5.1. Corollary1.2 gives exactly strict logarithmic complete
monotonicity of t^[t*(psi(t)-log(t))-gamma] on the entire positive half-line.

The old1601.00124 corrected paper only proves the unit interval. That limitation
does not survive the new paper. The new proof uses a positive Laplace density
constructed from h(u)=1/u^2-1/(4*sinh(u/2)^2), its strict decrease, and a
regularized logarithmic convolution. Root independently derived this same
mechanism before locating the successor, but that supplies no rediscovery credit.

The displayed family includes the original at alpha=gamma. It gives an explicit
sufficient constant alpha0=-1/2+exp(-gamma-1)/12 and a variational exact threshold;
neither the family nor its nonsharp explicit bound is available as a new outcome.
