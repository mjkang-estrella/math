                                                    Abstract. We study logarithmic complete monotonicity of a power family
                                                    built from the digamma function. An explicit Laplace representation of its

                                                    obtain uniform lower bounds for all alternating logarithmic derivatives. In

Remark 5.4. Proposition 5.3 separates the explicit sufficient bound from the actual
threshold. It does not evaluate the minimum in (5.5) in closed form, and it does not
assert uniqueness of its minimizer. In particular, the necessary condition α ≥ −1/2
discussed in [3, Section 4] is not sufficient. There is also a direct check at the
endpoint: since H(t) > 0,
                                               
                    f−1/2 (t) = exp −H(t) log t < 1       (t > 1),
while (5.2), whose asymptotic derivation holds for every fixed real α, gives f−1/2 (t) →
1 as t → ∞. Such a function cannot be decreasing on (1, ∞).
Remark 5.5. The construction shows why an additive parameter is effective: changing
α translates the entire Laplace density vertically. The estimates of Section 4 yield
an explicit admissible interval without having to locate its minimum. A closed-
form evaluation of (5.5), or sharper explicit estimates for it, would require a more
precise analysis of the regularized integral in (5.4). No extension to discrete or basic
