C∗ -algebraic Smale Mean Value Conjecture and Dubinin-Sugawa
                 Dual Mean Value Conjecture

                                                   K.M. Krishna∗

School of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, Chanakya University Global Campus, NH-648,
Haraluru Village, Devanahalli Taluk, Bengaluru North District, Karnataka State, 562110, India

   Abstract. We develop C∗ -algebraic analogues of two central problems in complex analysis: the Smale Mean Value
   Conjecture and the Dubinin-Sugawa Dual Mean Value Conjecture. Building on Smale’s classical mean value theorem
   and its dual formulation, we extend these conjectures to the setting of commutative C∗ -algebras. The proposed
   framework defines algebraic differentiation for polynomials over C∗ -algebras and formulates both standard and
   strong versions of the conjectures. We further introduce higher-order and dynamical extensions, including a C∗ -
   algebraic analogue of the Miles-Leighton-Pilgrim dynamics conjecture. Rigorous verification is provided for degree
   two polynomials, establishing the validity of these conjectures in this foundational case. The results highlight the deep
   interplay between operator-algebraic structures and analytic inequalities, offering new perspectives on long-standing
   problems in polynomial theory. This work opens pathways for generalizations to Banach and von Neumann algebras,
   while emphasizing the challenges posed by noncommutative settings.
       Key words: C∗ -algebra, Smale mean value theorem, Smale mean value conjecture, Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean
   value theorem, Dubinin-Sugawa dual mean value conjecture.

Conclusion

   For 45 years, Smale’s mean value conjecture has remained an important problem
in the theory of polynomials. The Dubinin–Sugawa dual mean value conjecture has
been an important problem for 17 years. In this article, we formulated a far-reaching
general formulation of both conjectures and verified them for degree 2 polynomials over
commutative C*-algebras.
