                                           Abstract: Using a result of Robertson [Proc. Edinburgh Math. Soc. (2), 1976], we introduce a notion of
                                           differentiation of maps on certain classes of unital commutative C*-algebras. We then derive C*-algebraic
                                           Gauss-Lucas theorem and formulate C*-algebraic Sendov’s conjecture. We verify C*-algebraic Sendov’s
                                           conjecture for polynomials of degree 2.
                                           Keywords: Sendov’s conjecture, C*-algebra.

                                                                                         1. Introduction

                                           Let a be a complex number, r > 0 be a real number and D(a, r) be the closed disc in C centered at a of
                                           radius r. Let C[z] be the set of all polynomials over C. In 1958, Sendov made the following conjecture
                                           which became known as Sendov’s conjecture.

                                           Conjecture 1.1.       [21, 25, 33] (Sendov’s conjecture) Let n ∈ N \ {1} and p(z) = (z − a1 )(z −
                                           a2 ) · · · (z − an ) ∈ C[z] be such that a1 , a2 , . . . , an ∈ D(0, 1). Then for each aj , 1 ≤ j ≤ n, there
                                           exists a zero b of p′ such that b ∈ D(aj , 1).

                                           A direct calculation says that Conjecture 1.1 holds for degree two polynomials. For n = 3, Conjecture 1.1
                                           is proved in [6]. For n = 3, 4, Conjecture 1.1 is proved in [36]. For n = 5, Conjecture 1.1 is proved in [26].
                                           For n = 6, Conjecture 1.1 is proved in [3, 24]. For n = 7, Conjecture 1.1 is proved in [9]. For n = 8,
                                           Conjecture 1.1 is proved in [10]. Conjecture 1.1 is proved for all polynomials after certain (unknown)
                                           degree in [40]. On the other hand, Conjecture 1.1 is proved for various special cases of polynomials and
                                           position of roots (inside the closed unit disc) in [1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 11–15, 17–20, 22, 23, 27–32, 34, 36–39, 41].
                                           Non-Archimedean version of Conjecture 1.1 has been solved in [16]. In this paper, we derive Gauss-Lucas
                                           theorem for polynomials over certain C*-algebras. We then formulate Sendov’s conjecture for polynomials
                                           over C*-algebras.

      2. C*-algebraic Gauss-Lucas Theorem and C*-algebraic Sendov’s Conjecture

Let A be a unital commutative C*-algebra and let G(A) be the set of all invertible elements of A. Let
A[z] be the set of all polynomials over A. We assume G(A) is dense in A. Such C*-algebras exist
and a characterization of such C*-algebras is given by Robertson [35]. We now introduce the following
definition.

Definition 2.1. (C*-algebraic differentiation) Let A be a unital commutative C*-algebra and
G(A) be dense in A. Let f : A → A be a function and ω ∈ A. We say that f is C*-algebraic
differentiable at ω if there exists an L ∈ A satisfying the following: for each ε > 0, there
exists a δ > 0 such that if z ∈ A satisfies kz − ωk < δ and z − ω ∈ G(A), then

                                         k(z − ω)−1 (f (z) − f (ω)) − Lk < ε.

In this case, we write f ′ (ω) = L.

We note that, because G(A) is dense in A, Definition 2.1 is well-defined. Now we derive C*-algebraic
Gauss-Lucas theorem.
