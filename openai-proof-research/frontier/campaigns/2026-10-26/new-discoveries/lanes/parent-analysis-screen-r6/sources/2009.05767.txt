                                                          Abstract

                                               In the abstract of [1] we read: “We obtain so far unproved properties
                                           of a ratio involving a class of Hermite and parabolic cylinder functions.”
                                           However, we explain how some of the main results in that paper were al-
                                           ready proved in [2], namely the ‘universal bounds’. An error in reference
                                           [2] was discussed in [1] which does not affect the proof given there for those
                                           ‘universal bounds’; we fix this erratum easily. We end this note proposing
                                           a conjecture regarding the best possible upper bound for a certain ratio of
                                           parabolic cylinder functions.

same.
