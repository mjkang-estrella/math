The sharp top-mean index
in dimensions at least five
Research proof and formalization packet

Abstract
For a probability vector in dimension d, form all d2 arithmetic means and all d2 geometric means
of its ordered coordinate pairs. Sort the two arrays independently. We prove that, for every d ≥ 5,
the sum of their respective largest k entries is at most d for every probability vector if and only if
k ≤ 2d − 5. This establishes the value proposed by Caro and Graswald in that dimension range. The
proof combines two incidence bounds for arbitrary sets of ordered pairs with Cauchy–Schwarz. An
explicit probability vector proves sharpness in every dimension. The complete statement, including
the independently sorted arrays, is certified in Lean.

Formal certificate and scope

The certificate comprises 76 declarations in twelve Lean modules, checked with Lean 4.34.1 and a pinned mathlib
revision. Its endpoint MaxIndex.source_index_classification is precisely the equivalence in Theorem 1, quantified over all nonnegative functions p : Fin d → R of sum 1. The strict suﬀiciency theorem also covers d ≥ 3. The
development proves the literal sorted-array/finite-selection equivalence, the loop and pair incidence counts, the
geometric square-mass identity, the largest-entry exchange, and the sizes and sums of both sharpness selections.
Reindexing the explicit witness onto Fin d is proved as well. No combinatorial or numerical certificate remains as
an extra hypothesis.
The strict replay checks every declaration type and allows only propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound.
The elementary incidence and Cauchy–Schwarz ingredients are standard; the source-question conclusion is the
combined sharp index theorem for d ≥ 5. Small-dimensional classifications are outside this certificate’s maximality
statement. Source and bounded literature-search records accompany the development; they do not certify absolute
worldwide priority.
