Corollary 2.4. Let k ≥ 1 be an integer, p ∈ [0, ∞); and let A, B ∈ Mn (C) be Hermitian. Then we
have
1. trace[(A2 + (AB)k (BA)k )p ] ≥ trace[(A2 + (BA)k (AB)k )p ], for p ≥ 1;

Proof. Since f (x) = xp is a convex function for p ≥ 1 and concave for 0 ≤ p ≤ 1, Corollary 2.4
follows from Corollary 2.3 and a general property of majorization (See [5, p. 56]).
