The parent parametrizes real projectors by two orthonormal frames. Rank
constraints force rankP=rankS=a and rankQ=rankR=b. Fixing the first frame to the
coordinate frame loses no real examples. Polar orthogonalization of [U W] and
[V Z] represents the actual Moore--Penrose inverse-square-root expressions
on their ranges. Gram eigenvalue checks exclude intersecting image pairs.
