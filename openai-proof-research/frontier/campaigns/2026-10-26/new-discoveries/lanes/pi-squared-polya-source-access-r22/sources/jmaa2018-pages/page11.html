The following lemma is well known. However, we give the proof to keep the present paper self-contained. Lemma 2.2. Let f : (0, ∞) → (0, ∞) be a given function and let α ≥ 1. a) If f is operator monotone, then (2.16) f (αt) ≤ αf (t) . b) If g is operator monotone decreasing, then g (αt) ≥ 1 α g (t) . Proof. (b) If g is operator monotone decreasing, then 1 g is operator monotone.
