The question studied in this paper is: what are the prime factors of den κr , and in
particular what is the exact power of 2? The odd part turns out to be easy. The power of
2 is the substantial part: its exponent 2, 5, 9, 12, . . . is given by Er = 3r − 1 + ν2 ((r − 1)!).
The purpose of the present paper is to prove that Er is an upper bound for every r, to
isolate the exact obstruction to its sharpness, and to verify sharpness in a substantial
finite range; the proof of sharpness for every r is completed in [17].
