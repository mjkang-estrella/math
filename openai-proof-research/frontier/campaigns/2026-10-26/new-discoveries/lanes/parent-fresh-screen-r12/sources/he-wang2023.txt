                                                                        HAIHONG HE AND XIAOXIA WANG∗




arXiv:2304.00960v1 [math.CO] 3 Apr 2023
                                                 Abstract. We prove two single-parameter q-supercongruences which were recently con-
                                                 jectured by Guo, and establish their further extensions with one more parameter. Crucial
                                                 ingredients in the proof are the terminating form of q-binomial theorem and a Karlsson-
                                                 Minton type summation formula due to Gasper. Incidentally, an assertion of Wang, Li
                                                 and Tang is also verified by establishing its q-analogue.
