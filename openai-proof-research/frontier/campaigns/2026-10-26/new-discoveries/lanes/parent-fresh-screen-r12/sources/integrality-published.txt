Some Congruences from the
Karlsson-Minton Summation Formula
Junhang Li, Yezhenyang Tang, and Chen Wang

     Abstract. Let p be an odd prime. In this paper, by using the well-known
     Karlsson-Minton summation formula, we mainly prove two supercongru-
     ences as variants of a supercongruence of Deines-Fuselier-Long-Swisher-
     Tu, which conﬁrm some recent conjectures of V.J.W. Guo.

     Mathematics Subject Classification. Primary 33C20, Secondary 05A10,
     11B65, 11A07, 33E50.

     Keywords. Truncated hypergeometric series, supercongruences, Karlsson-
     Minton summation formula.
