On a Coeﬃcient Inequality for
Carathéodory Functions
Pawel Zaprawa

     Abstract. In the paper we consider a general inequality |pn−1 pn+1 −pn 2 | ≤
     4 − |p1 |2 involving coeﬃcients of functions with a positive real part. We
     prove this inequality for n = 2 and n = 3. Consequently, the relative
     inequalities involving coeﬃcients of Schwarz functions are obtained. As
     an application, the two sharp estimates of the Hankel determinants H3,1
     and H2,3 are proved for functions in S ∗ (1/2) and M, respectively.

     Mathematics Subject Classiﬁcation. 30C50, 30C45.

     Keywords. Carathéodory functions, Schwarz functions, coeﬃcient prob-
     lems, Hankel determinant.
