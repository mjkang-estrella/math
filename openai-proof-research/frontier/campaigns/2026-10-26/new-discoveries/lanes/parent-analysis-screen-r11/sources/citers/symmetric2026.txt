                                         Abstract
                                         This paper presents a new approach to the third-order Hankel determinant problem for
                                         the family of starlike functions associated with a symmetric H-domain. Motivated by
                                         a recently proposed sharp bound, we develop an analytical method that substantially
                                         improves the best known estimate. The obtained upper bound is reduced from 0.104485
                                         to 0.062581, which is remarkably close to the conjectured sharp constant 1/16 = 0.0625.
                                         This significant improvement provides strong evidence supporting the validity of the
                                         conjectured bound while introducing a systematic technique for deriving refined coefficient
                                         estimates. Furthermore, the proposed approach is sufficiently flexible to be extended to
                                         related extremal problems involving Hankel determinants and other subclasses of analytic
                                         functions in geometric function theory.

                                         Keywords: analytic functions; starlike functions; convex functions; coefficient bounds;
                                         Hankel determinants

                                Zaprawa [4] investigated the third Hankel determinant and obtained non-sharp
                          bounds, namely |H3,1 ( f )| ≤ 1 for starlike functions and |H3,1 ( f )| ≤ 49/540 for convex
                          functions. Since the formula of H3,1 ( f ) involves higher-order coefficients, deriving exact
                          estimates is technically demanding, and achieving sharpness becomes essential. Despite
                          these difficulties, Kowalczyk et al. [5] succeeded in 2018 in establishing the sharp bound
                          |H3,1 ( f )| ≤ 4/135 for the convex class. For starlike functions, the same upper estimate
                          |H3,1 ( f )| ≤ 4/135 was later reported by Kwon et al. [6]. In the same year, Lecko et al. [7]
                          demonstrated that the sharp bound |H3,1 ( f )| ≤ 1/9 holds for starlike functions of order
                          1/2. The following Table 1 provides a survey of results concerning the computation of
                          the third-order Hankel determinant for various classes of functions. It indicates whether
                          the proposed bound is sharp, and if not, whether a conjecture for the sharp bound was
                          formulated and subsequently proved by another researcher. We highlight the cases in
                          which the bound was not sharp but a conjecture for the sharp bound was proposed, yet
                          remained unproven. In this paper, we establish the proof of one such conjecture, while
                          leaving our methodology available to researchers for addressing the other conjecture, which
                          likewise remains an open problem.

                          3. Conclusions
                               In conclusion, our findings refine the previously known bound for the third-order
                          Hankel determinant. While the earlier result provided an upper bound of approximately
                          0.104485 and conjectured the sharp estimate to be 0.0625, our work establishes a sub-
                          stantially improved bound of 0.062581. Although this result does not fully confirm the
                          conjecture, it comes remarkably close and offers strong evidence supporting its validity.
                          Moreover, the methodology developed in this study may serve as a promising tool for
                          improving bounds in other open problems of geometric function theory.
