                                           1     Introduction
                                               Let Mn (C) denote the space of n × n complex matrices, where n ≥ 2. The Böttcher–Wenzel
                                           inequality [1, 9] states that
                                                                           ∥XY − Y X∥2F ≤ 2∥X∥2F ∥Y ∥2F
                                           for all X, Y ∈ Mn (C), where ∥X∥2F = tr(X ∗ X) denotes the squared Frobenius norm. Mayumi,
                                           Kimura, Ohno, and Chruściński [7] introduced weighted versions using

                                                                                    ∥X∥2A = tr(AX ∗ X),

                                           where A ∈ Mn (C) is positive definite Hermitian and X ∗ denotes the conjugate transpose of
                                           X ∈ Mn (C). Throughout the paper, the eigenvalues of A are ordered as

                                                                                  0 < λ1 ≤ λ2 ≤ · · · ≤ λn ,

                                           with multiplicity. In particular, λ1 and λ2 are its two smallest eigenvalues.
                                              Fang and Cheng [5, Theorem 2.4] proved

                                           when one factor is normal, by Kimura, Mayumi, Ohno, Lee, and Chruściński [6, Supplementary
                                           Information, Theorem 1].
