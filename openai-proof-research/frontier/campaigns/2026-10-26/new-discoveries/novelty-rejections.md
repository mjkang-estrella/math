# Strict novelty exclusions

##336488 premagic diagonalizability

Not credited as a new discovery. The earlier Lean certificate is mathematically valid, but the substantive non-diagonalizable positive doubly-stochastic construction is old. David E. Speyer gave an explicit positive3×3 counterexample and the Jordan block construction on January13,2011, with Gerry Myerson supplying a two-parameter family later the same day. These matrices are premagic automatically. Ben Grossmann gave the rank-one nilpotent perturbation form λI+(1−λ)J/3+αuvᵀ in2020, essentially the same construction as the old packet up to transpose/rescaling.

Primary original sources:
- https://mathoverflow.net/questions/51887/non-diagonalizable-doubly-stochastic-matrices (Speyer and Myerson answers,2011)
- https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/3940146/prove-or-disprove-a-left-pk-mid-k-in-mathbbn-right-has-a-finite-number (Grossmann answer,2020)
- https://abel.math.harvard.edu/~knill/teaching/math19/math19b_2011.pdf (OliverKnill2011course, cites the MathOverflow example)

Consequently applying this old phenomenon to the source's terminology does not satisfy the user's genuine-new-mathematical-discovery standard. No count awarded. Earlier frozen archives are preserved as formalization artifacts, not retrospectively rewritten or claimed novel.
