# Contribution and prior-art limits

The counted contribution, once final review and packet verification pass, is one full resolution of Gerhold–Simon Conjecture 3.2 / MathDB 352508, including the stronger biased form from the same proof. The Gamma-ratio convexity, finite log-sum closure, integer anchors, endpoint cases and source sharpness example receive no independent credit.

The actual published 2023 source and arXiv:2111.02747v2 explicitly pose the full sharp lower bound for real exponents above 2. The source's first arXiv version does not contain this conjecture. Hino–Namba's latest arXiv:2408.12011v3 and published 2026 Theorem 2.9 give the substantially weaker lower bound F(a)≥a^(−a²), uniformly in the degree and input weight. The present result gives F(a)≥2^(1−a).

Alzer's 2018 complete-monotonicity theorem concerns the exact individual summand. Its logarithmic strengthening is recorded by Qi–Guo, and Ouimet reproduces and generalizes its three coefficient-inequality corollaries. Those classical results and the earlier MathOverflow Gamma-curvature discussion are credited. None of the inspected primary texts states the full sharp finite-sum bound. A short application of classical ingredients can resolve an explicitly open conjecture; shortness alone is neither evidence for nor against prior publication.
