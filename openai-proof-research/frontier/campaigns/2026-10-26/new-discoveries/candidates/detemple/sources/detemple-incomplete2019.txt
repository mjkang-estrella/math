                                      2. Some lemmas

     In order to prove our main results, we need some preliminary lemmas. The fol-
lowing lemma is called L’Hospital Monotone Rule, for short, LMR (see [33]), which
is an efficient tool to deal with the monotonicity of the ratio between two functions. A
monotonicity rule for ratio of two power series was presented in [34]. A similar mono-
tonicity rule for the ratio of two Laplace transforms was established in [35, Lemma 4]
(see also [36]).

continuous functions that are differentiable on (a, b), with f (a) = g (a) = 0 or f (b) =
g (b) = 0 . If f  /g with g (x) = 0 for each x in (a, b) is increasing (decreasing) on
(a, b), then so is f /g .

                                            4. Conclusions

     We proved in this paper the monotonicity of the three ratios Ri (x) ( i = 1, 2, 3 )
given by (1.5), (1.7) and (1.8) on (0, ∞). These monotonicity yield several best bounds
