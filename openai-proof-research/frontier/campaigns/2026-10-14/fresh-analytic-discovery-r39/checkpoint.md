The source's expected coordinatewise median need not be a probability flow. On a three-leaf star with root o, use three leaf measures (1/2,1/2,0), (1/2,0,1/2), (0,1/2,1/2). The deterministic coordinate median is (1/2,1/2,1/2), whose mass exceeds one. For independent uniform choices of these measures, each coordinate of the expected median is (1/2)phi(2/3)=10/27; total mass is10/9. This is a genuine failure of the direct source transfer, not a counterexample to T332.

Two routes under investigation: (a) an ambient signed-flow retraction uniform over all weighted trees; (b) a constrained replacement of the source median smoothing directly in the Markov-cotype inequality, preserving all parent/children flow constraints. Naive sequential corrections can propagate mass through arbitrarily many levels. A pointwise distance-to-feasible-set bound alone does not provide the pairwise Lipschitz bound needed for the chain energy.
