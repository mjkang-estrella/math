Applying the theorem to every nonzero integer multiple of h shows its maximal assembly image is non-torsion. The source's reduced vanishing therefore yields a nonzero maximal-to-reduced comparison kernel, not maximal group-assembly noninjectivity. No full primary proof reread is claimed; the theorem and the opening almost-flat-bundle mechanism were read.
