# T133E scope freeze

Frozen 2026-10-18, following pre-freeze exploration, source/companion interface reading, and bounded primary comparison, before assembling the final proof. The author exploration already contains the proposed construction and proof outline; this is not a claim that the scope preceded discovery. Clock observation immediately preceding this freeze was 2026-10-18 15:55:55 UTC. No independent review or contribution acceptance has occurred.

Let h(G)=min_{0<|S|<=|V(G)|/2}|E(S,V(G)\S)|/|S|, with h of a singleton defined as infinity. Define E to be the language of triples (G,H,k), encoded by explicit adjacency matrices and a binary integer k>=2, such that G,H are finite simple undirected uncolored connected graphs of the same positive order, both have maximum degree at most101 and h at least1/200000, and G,H are equivalent under joint-update k-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman refinement with common color names and separate-graph tuple histograms. Malformed triples and violations of the graph conditions are excluded.

Full target: E is EXPTIME-complete under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions. The same holds with unary k. All graph restrictions hold on every output of the hardness reduction, on both yes and no source instances. No promise that k is bounded or fixed, no colored outputs, no regularity or girth claim, and no restriction to an existential family of hard graphs substitutes for this language statement.

Proposed bridge: replace the source's growing-length sort paths by one rooted port-labelled expander frame with constant-size degree encodings and bounded fibers. Prove an exact same-dimension realization for every source product scalar-image instance of address length r>=8, including arbitrary winning-strategy histories, whole bijections announced before the next vertex, and uniform cut expansion. The source supplies the computation-to-instance compiler and exact-projection/ring-transfer theorem; classical Margulis–Gabber–Galil expansion supplies the frame. These are credited inputs, not new claims.

No separate fixed-k lower bound, generic transformation of every colored graph, all-mates identification theorem, high-girth or regular-graph theorem is part of this scope. These exclusions were decided before final proof assembly, not as repairs to a failed frozen theorem. The bounded-degree expander conclusion and exact-width mechanism still require a genuine substantial-contribution determination; a new statement alone does not establish it.
