Actual primary comparison rejects separate credit for this route. Those interface checks cannot be replaced by an unqualified citation, but they do not constitute a new discovery mechanism. No full K-theoretic theorem is claimed proved in this packet.

A quantified quantum version would be genuinely different. The pinned source is explicitly nonquantized. Its `stable-pairs.tex`, proof of `prop:kernel-inclusion`, chooses a nonpositive-character step in a neutral convolution word and moves it first using commutativity. Its `preliminaries.tex`, `prop:minuscule`, likewise states that no loop rotation shifts the coefficient functions. Neither operation remains verbatim valid with the quantum parameter. The existing ordinary coefficient-kernel equality only determines the zero-parameter fiber; it does not identify the required shifted quantum relations.

This is not fixed by asserting flatness alone. Over C[h], the quotients C[h,x]/(x^2) and C[h,x]/(x^2-h) are both free of rank two, have the same special fiber at h=0, and have distinct kernels under the specified coefficient map C[h,x]. Thus even flatness and matching special fibers do not prove coefficient-compatible equality. This elementary algebra example is only a warning against that inference, not a counterexample to quantum Hikita. A viable full quantum route needs an ordered convolution test with the actual shifted coefficient action, or an independently justified rigidity/uniqueness theorem for those coefficient ideals. No such operation has been obtained here. The quantum target was not frozen or nominated.
