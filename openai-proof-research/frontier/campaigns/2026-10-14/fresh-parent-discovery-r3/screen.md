Global scalar rescaling gives the product of L3 norms; it does not replace those norms with Lp norms of unequal exponents. Applying nonlinear power maps to individual inputs changes the cyclic product itself. A new asymmetric energy or a suitable localized extrapolation theorem would be needed for the tested route. No such theorem has been proved or imported here.
