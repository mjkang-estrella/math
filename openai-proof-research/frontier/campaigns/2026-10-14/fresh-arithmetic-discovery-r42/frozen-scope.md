# Frozen scope T172R42

Frozen after exploratory factorization and before writing the complete proof. The claims below retain every quantifier. A failed claim will be recorded as failed, not replaced by a narrower target.

Write p(t)=((1-t²)/(1+t²),2t/(1+t²)). A finite set T of distinct real parameters is projectively algebraic if |T|≤3, or, after choosing three distinct a,b,c in T, every cross-ratio ((t-a)(b-c))/((t-c)(b-a)), t in T minus {a,b,c}, is algebraic over Q. Equivalently all cross-ratios of four distinct members are algebraic.

(1) For every two finite projectively algebraic sets T1,T2 of real numbers, p(T1 union T2) is Ramsey: for every integer r≥2 there is D≥1 such that every arbitrary coloring R^D→{1,...,r} has a monochromatic congruent copy at its original scale. Empty blocks and overlaps are allowed.

(2) For every finite set T of distinct real numbers containing at least five algebraic numbers, p(T) is Ramsey if and only if its nonalgebraic subset T minus Qbar is projectively algebraic. There is no bound on cardinality or transcendence degree and no algebraic independence assumption.

The six-point consequence of (1) is already I17 and is not a new finding. The proposed new scope is the unbounded two-block construction and exact anchored converse. Source family172 thm:classification is stipulated; priority and contribution are not assumed. The elementary Möbius invariance observation is not separately nominated.
