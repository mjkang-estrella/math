## Convexity control

Convexity of B with B(-2) = 1, B(-1) < 1/4 and B(0) = 0 gives only B < (3|t|-2)/4 on [-2,-1] and B < |t|/4 on
[-1,0]. Both bounds exceed t^2/4 in the interior, so S1 is not a convexity corollary of the two pinned papers.
It is, however, a parameter substitution in their printed proofs.
