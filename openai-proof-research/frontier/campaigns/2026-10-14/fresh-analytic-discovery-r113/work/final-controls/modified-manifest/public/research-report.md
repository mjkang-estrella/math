## The principal research result of the screen

For source148, finite-state dependence initially appeared to obstruct the independent-block entropy argument. Reading the actual `types.tex` and `windows.tex` shows precisely which independence is needed: conditional interior blocks, and recovery of the unobserved future law after conditioning through the end of a chosen segment. The final window count expressly needs stationarity, not independence between selected windows.

For a Markov chain, recording block boundary states and transition counts makes the interiors independent and fixes the signed contraction. The entropy charge is logarithmic in block length plus one finite-state boundary charge per block. This exposes a compatible adaptation of the existing architecture; it is not a new inverse-entropy mechanism. A separate uniform nonsaturation statement for the finite collection of state-conditioned coding laws would still need to be written if this route were completed. No such finished theorem is claimed here.

More strongly, Comets–Fernández–Ferrari, arXiv:math/0009204v3, Corollary 4.18 and its Section 8 proof, already construct mutually independent word blocks, identically distributed away from the origin block, when their regeneration parameter beta is positive. Theorem 4.3(iv) supplies exponential return-tail control for exponentially decaying continuity defects. This is an actual primary regenerative construction, not an inference from a mixing slogan.

The remaining apparent obstruction was that exact collisions of affine maps erase word boundaries. The calculation in `regenerative-calculation.md` removes that obstruction directly: products at two clocks reconstruct each other from the intervening finite word and the clocks. The entropy cost is at most the clock entropy plus expected intervening length times log alphabet size. No recovery of hidden regeneration marks is needed. Under exponential word tails the cost is O(sqrt(k))+O(log k), hence sublinear, and the deterministic-time map entropy and iid word-map entropy have the expected mean-length relation.

This combines with the existing r26 countable-alphabet feasibility analysis to identify a stronger compatible route than a new correlated entropy argument. The round does NOT certify the full countable-IFS theorem, the Gibbs-to-chain normalization theorem, or a completed Gibbs dimension formula: those contracts and their entire assembly were not independently proved here. Their uncompleted verification is not labelled a novel operation. In particular, no finite-state, regenerative, or Gibbs dimension theorem is submitted for review merely because the precise wording was not found earlier.

Its proof uses a high-q Lq estimate to obtain a uniform small-ball bound for a carefully weighted block Bernoulli measure, then compares cylinder masses.
