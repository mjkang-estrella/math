T118S — full intended Sherali–Adams statement, frozen before final proof assembly

For every pair of integers r,c >= 0, there exist an integer B >= 1 and an explicitly finite rational bin-packing instance I consisting of exactly 5B individually indexed item copies, all with sizes strictly greater than 1/6 and at most 1, such that OPT(I)>B+c but SA_r(I)=B.

The integer program has one Boolean variable y_H for EVERY feasible subset H of the individual physical item copies (the empty subset may be omitted), constraints sum_{H containing i} y_H >= 1 for every copy i, and objective minimize sum_H y_H. Thus patterns cannot repeat a physical copy; equal numerical sizes remain distinct items. No restriction to a supplied fractional support, marked subclass, or five-item pattern is part of the relaxation.

Convention: SA_r uses a normalized linear functional L on multilinear polynomials of degree <= r+1; L of every product of at most r+1 distinct literals y_H or (1-y_H) is nonnegative. For every covering inequality g_i=sum_{H containing i}y_H-1 and every product P of at most r literals, impose L(P g_i)>=0 after Boolean multilinearization. The first moments minimize the objective. Thus r=0 is the covering LP with bounds 0<=y_H<=1. The proposed witness will satisfy lifted item EQUALITIES, which is stronger than required. This is an existence theorem, not a uniform efficient construction in r or c, not a growing-round-rate claim, and not a claim about the unrestricted integer-multiplicity type formulation.
