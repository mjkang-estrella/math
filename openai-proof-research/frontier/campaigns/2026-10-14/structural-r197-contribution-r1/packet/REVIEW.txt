                   dim ker D=dim E0+dim E*=dim Fix(U).   (3.4)

4. Spectral multiplicities give the lower bound for ALL representations

Let b=dim E-. Since n=2^(m-1), the roots satisfying lambda^n=-1 are
exactly the primitive 2^m-th roots. Conjugacy of A and A^5 preserves
multiplicities along the permutation of their exponents by 5. One can
see this without any character-theoretic semisimplicity theorem: for a
row eigenvector wA=lambda w, (wT)A=wA^5T=lambda^5 wT, and T is an
isomorphism. Thus every eigenspace in an orbit has the same dimension.

For j>=0, v2(5^(2^j)-1)=j+2. The base case is 5-1=4. At a squaring
step the extra factor 5^(2^j)+1 has valuation one. For an odd positive
k, the quotient (y^k-1)/(y-1) is an odd sum of odd terms, hence odd.
It follows that v2(5^ell-1)=2+v2(ell). The least positive ell with
5^ell=1 modulo 2^m is therefore d=2^(m-2), including m=3, when d=2.
An odd exponent is invertible modulo 2^m, so every primitive-root orbit
has precisely that length. Consequently d divides b.

If r=q d+s with q=floor(r/d) and 0<=s<d, then r-b>=s, since r-b is
nonnegative and congruent to s modulo d. Equation (3.6), together with
h2(X;V|G)>=0, gives

                         h2(Z;V)>=s.                    (4.1)

No old-generator action was required to preserve E-. We use only the
nonnegative old homology term. The elementary orbit computation therefore
controls EVERY representation, including nonunitary, infinite-image and
nonsemisimple representations. It is not merely a restriction on the
particular induced witness constructed next. This closes parent question 4
and is the necessary lower half of the proposed exact profile.

6. EVERY cover, exact cyclic attainment, and the geometric consequence

Let H<=Gamma be ANY finite-index subgroup of index e and let L be ANY
right CH-character, with no normality, finiteness or unitarity requirement.
The right induced module is L tensor_CH C Gamma, of dimension e. Choose
representatives for the decomposition Gamma=disjoint union H g_i;
they give a basis of C Gamma as a free left CH-module. On the common
universal cover, tensor associativity gives the chain isomorphism

 (L tensor_CH C Gamma) tensor_CGamma C_*(universal cover;C)
       -> L tensor_CH C_*(universal cover;C),
             (l tensor g) tensor c |-> l tensor (g c).

Its inverse sends l tensor c to (l tensor 1) tensor c. These maps respect
the balancing relations and every cellular boundary. The right side is
the cellular complex of the corresponding cover W with L. Finite-support
chains are unchanged, and normality is irrelevant. This is exactly the
classical proof operation read in Friedl--Nagel Lemma 2.7, written with
the present coefficients. If H2(W;L)=0, a rank-e representation downstairs
has zero H2, so (4)--(5) imply d divides e. It applies to EVERY cover in
the frozen statement and not just the following normal example.

Define phi:Gamma -> Z/d by t -> 1 and every other marked generator -> 0.
Every old relation, f and j maps to zero, so this is an epimorphism.
Let H=ker phi. In R_m, old generators and a are diagonal and only t
has a nontrivial permutation part. Thus every element of H is diagonal.
The row line C e_0 is H-invariant; let chi(h) be its scalar action.
Its image is finite and chi(x1)=-1. Row multiplication by T sends e_j
to e_(j+1), consistently with the specified column action.
The intertwiner

                   e_0 tensor g |-> e_0 R_m(g)

is well-defined by the character property; on 1,t,...,t^(d-1) it maps

attainment and least degree d without a classification of covers or

7. Why the entire integrated result is technical under the given criterion

After the source is fixed, the information needed from it is quite small:
its split d3, its literal involution 2-cell, H2-zero ordinary coefficients,
and its H2-zero finite character taking value -1 on that involution.
The universal part of the proposed residual is then a two-row cellular
calculation followed by the norm inverse 1/2 on Fix(U). The exact extremal
profile is an elementary optimization: the negative eigenspace dimension
is a multiple of d at most r, so at least r mod d invariant dimensions
remain; one length-d monomial orbit and trivial blocks attain that bound.
No classification of the old group or of its representations intervenes.

These operations are compatible simultaneously, not merely separately:

* The extra relators create a finite-order operator A but do not remove
  the source 3-cells or its split identity. Base change of that identity
  pays all coefficient conditions without injectivity or flatness.
* The very same literal E that remains in the source CW complex kills
  the relative connecting class. It can be used in a lift even though
  the plus construction kills its relative homology class over B.
* Only A is diagonalized; the old group action and T may be nonsemisimple.
  The lower bound uses an old H2 dimension as a nonnegative term rather
  than assigning it an unwarranted value.
* The chosen monomial orbit extends the precise source character, so the
  old H2 term vanishes at the lower-bound ranks. Trivial source coefficients
  provide exactly one unit of H2 per leftover dimension, also compatible
  with the two new relators.
* The chosen orbit has a transitive permutation part on d lines. Its
  line stabilizer is the specified cyclic-cover subgroup, making Shapiro
  both the all-cover lower-bound transfer and the sharp rank-one witness.
* Source torsion applies on that finite cover of a hypothetical finite
  2-model, so a stronger tower or a new dimension obstruction is unnecessary.

This is the requested complete strongest compatible composition, with
no residual left merely named. The identity, spectrum and cover minimum
are genuine deductions, but no nonroutine operation survives the explicit
composition. The grade does not follow just from each ingredient being
familiar, from the number d being large, or from an absence in the read
literature; it follows from the full construction above at all quantifiers.

Stabilization must still be paid in its strongest applicable form.
Hambleton's Lemma 2.1 gives a finite 2-model of Z_m wedge S^2 wedge S^2.
For each rank-r local system that wedge adds two copies of V in degree
two, with zero boundary; the twisted H2 dimension increases by 2r.
Thus its minimum is (r mod d)+2r. The stabilization does not preserve the
unstabilized profile or provide a cancellation to a 2-model of Z_m.
That limitation does not manufacture a nonroutine residual: sections
2--6 already derive the unstabilized claims by the other compatible
operations. Conversely stabilization alone would have been an inadequate
TECHNICAL argument for this exact theorem.

The literal word a^n has length growing with m. A constant number of
relator cells can encode an arbitrarily large torsion order and hence a
long automorphism orbit. The proof makes no bounded word-length,
triangulation, bit-complexity, or minimal-cell assertion. There is therefore
no unpaid compression or complexity theorem hidden in the eighteen-cell
count. X is fixed independently of m and r throughout, and no one-pair
variant of the source plus construction replaces it.
