                     Uniform interior gradient estimates for ﬁnite p
                            through a constrained aﬃne lift
                                                  Myeongjin Kang∗

                                                  October 14, 2026



                                                       Abstract
              Assuming the interior regularity and ﬁtted-class Liouville results of the pinned OpenAI
          inﬁnity-harmonic manuscript, we give an argument for a dimension-dependent interior C 1,α
          estimate common to all ﬁnite p ≥ 3. The argument uses the exact equation for the aﬃne lift
          u(y) + b, including at critical points of u. Its anisotropic limits have an eﬀective viscosity. Finite
          viscosity is handled by ﬁtted-class rigidity, whose positive-viscosity proof is included. Inﬁnite
          viscosity is handled by a uniform small-solution estimate and a quadratic comparison between
          diﬀerent ﬁtting scales. The exponent is nonexplicit. Supporting Lean ﬁles certify the zero-jet
          vector ellipticity algebra, not the analytic theorem.

     The classical analytic inputs are the local logarithmic gradient estimate of Kotschwar–Ni [2],
scalar regularity from Uhlenbeck [3], weak/viscosity equivalence [5], Savin’s small-solution theorem
[4], and linear weak-solution Harnack [6]. The latter is used in Appendix A. Their conclusions are
not new claims of this manuscript. A companion audit records exact source versions, read scopes
and the compact-parameter choices in Uhlenbeck’s proof.
     Appendix A supplies the positive-viscosity argument: the cone inﬁmum, viscosity-to-weak pas-
sage, contact propagation and rigidity. No estimate uniform in the positive viscosity is used.

Relation to existing estimates. Peng–Zhang–Zhou [8, Theorem 1.1] establish C 1 convergence
for potentials on convex rings, with geometric and nonvanishing-gradient structure. Bungert [9,
Theorem 1] gives value-convergence rates under a value-Hölder hypothesis. Neither statement
supplies a common gradient Hölder estimate for arbitrary interior weak solutions. The qualitative-
to-quantitative theorem of Sobral–Teixeira–Urbano [10, Theorem 1.1] gives constants depending
on p. Its large-shift compactness at ﬁxed p produces a linear operator with matrix I + (p − 2)e ⊗ e,
whose ellipticity ratio diverges in the joint large-p limit. The eﬀective-viscosity analysis below
addresses this simultaneous degeneracy.

    Lee–Lee [11] prove improved homogeneous regularity and the inhomogeneous C p conclusion
for p suﬃciently close to two. Their Theorem A transfers a homogeneous exponent strictly larger
than 1/(p − 1) to the sharp bounded-forcing exponent. If Theorem 1.1 is established, that transfer
applies for each ﬁxed p > max{3, 1 + 1/αn }. It is a corollary of the present claim, with constants
allowed to depend on p; the intervening range is not settled. In particular the full inhomogeneous
conjecture is not claimed here.

recovers v = u there. The contact set is consequently open. It is closed by continuity, nonempty
by hypothesis, and all of Ω by connectedness.
