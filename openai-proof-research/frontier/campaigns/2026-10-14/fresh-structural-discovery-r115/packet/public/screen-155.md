# 155: an individual kernel period survives the explicit lift

The source's theorem excludes fully periodic tilings in dimension three. It explicitly does not assert absence of each individual nonzero period. The prefreeze direction examined here was whether its construction and an existing connectedization could yield that stronger property. No such full target was frozen or claimed.

In `07-three-dimensions.tex`, π:Z³→Z²×(Z/QZ) has kernel Zw with w=(0,0,Q). The source takes representatives U, modifies a transversal by moving one marked representative by mw, and forms the rigidified tile T from translated component shapes. The true source `geom:existence` proof supplies a tiling from a quotient translation set A: B=π⁻¹(A) satisfies B+w=B, and the translation set mB tiles T. It therefore has the explicit nonzero period mw. This proves that this exhibited tile does not have the proposed stronger all-tilings property. It is not an impossibility theorem for other tiles or constructions.

`geom:grid` uses a hypothetical fully periodic tiling and density in a bounded fundamental domain to obtain grid coverage. `geom:kernel-invariance` then descends that tiling. I do not drop the full-periodicity hypothesis and claim every tiling has a kernel period. The period cited above comes directly from the existence tiling, where it is unconditional.

The strongest inspected connectedization is Kim, [arXiv2508.11725v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.11725v1), §2 through Theorem2.5 and proof. The center correspondence scales the old translation centers in the construction P'_i=(3m+6)P_i⊕S_l. Consequently it preserves an existing individual period after scaling; it does not remove the period mw. The actual r78 exploration already treated connectedization as an existing compatible operation. Its history was read, rather than inferring fresh work from changed terminology.

Demaine–Langerman [arXiv2610.12392v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12392v1) was acquired and its opening theorem/overview read only. Its proof/Lean repository was not read, and its headline undecidability claim supplies no asserted all-period-elimination bridge in this record. No connectedness-only variant is proposed. Combining different quotient directions would need a single simultaneous tiling construction and a decoder excluding every nonzero period while preserving existence; that operation was not supplied here. This is a stopped feasibility screen, not a frozen famous-headline conjecture.
