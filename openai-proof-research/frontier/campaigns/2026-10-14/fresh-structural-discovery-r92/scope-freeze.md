# Prospective full scope: finite punctures of the Turing degrees

For every finite set E of Turing degrees, every order automorphism of the induced poset D_T \ E is the identity. The automorphism is arbitrary: no Borel, arithmetic, measurable, or definability hypothesis. E may include the computable degree, minimal degrees, jump degrees, and degrees of any complexity. The empty set is included. Work in ZFC, accepting the exact pinned source241 statements at fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb.
