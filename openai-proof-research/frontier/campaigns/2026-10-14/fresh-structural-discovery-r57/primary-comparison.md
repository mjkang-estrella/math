# Primary and source operation comparison

This is a bounded operation comparison, not a claim to have searched all literature. Bodies and extracted-text hashes are in primary-receipts.json; exact semantic reads are in reading-limits.md.

| Input operation | Actual antecedent | Assessment here |
|---|---|---|
| Tighten capacities; choose maximum-capacity forest; keep large capacities binary | Cryan–Dyer–Randall, *Approximately Counting Integral Flows and Cell-Bounded Contingency Tables*, SICOMP2010, §2; stipulated source115 `scales.tex` | Paid. CDR's read introduction distinguishes large tight capacities and arbitrary capacities with fixed rows. Source115 supplies the arbitrary-dimension construction. |
| Arbitrary tabulated log-concave entry weights | Chen–Liu, arXiv2610.06724v1, Proposition C.20 and Corollaries C.21–C.22 with full C.9 reduction | Paid for unary capacities and polynomial log coefficient range. The actual reduction creates Σc_i labels; the table corollary requires Σu_ij and log R polynomial. W152 does not satisfy that representation hypothesis in general. This difference is not itself a contribution. |
| Product-activity weighted table normalizers | Barvinok, arXiv0709.3810v3, Theorem1.3 and Lemma1.4 | Already explicitly studied, including forbidden cells. The read theorem bounds the normalizer between a convex optimum and N^(−γ(m+n)) times that optimum; it is not arbitrary-relative-accuracy counting. Its entropy/dual variables are also prior weighted normalization ideas. No new weighted model is claimed here. |
| Exact reduced costs, complementary slackness and polynomial optimization | Orlin1993, §§1–2 and §5, Theorem5 | Paid. The read body explicitly uses node potentials, zero reduced-cost arcs for the optimum and a capacity-to-uncapacitated transformation. Rational-data bit conversion is ordinary scaling. The packet's coordinate gap identity is elementary dual algebra. |
| Tail truncation at optimum plus log of a cardinality bound | Elementary finite Gibbs estimate, proved in weighted-interface.md | No external novelty claimed. Every omitted state has weight ≤2^(−K) times one optimum's weight; at most N₀ states exist. The useful combination is preserving a coordinate box after using nonnegative reduced costs. |
| Unary exponential factors, two signatures and log-concave endpoint marginals | Source115 `integer-signatures.tex`, `signatures.tex` | Paid. Additional geometric unary factors already satisfy the source finite laminar hypothesis. No new signature closure is proved. |
| Convex scaled bins, conductance, population correction, bounded fair-bit evaluator | Source115 `evaluation.tex` | Paid once range and Lipschitz constants are polynomial. The localization supplies those constants without inventing a new chain. |
| Enlarged defect transport, warm trace restarts and capped annealing | Source115 `transport.tex`, `algorithm.tex` | Paid; the new P=D+g+G satisfies the same abstract input estimates. |
| Interval-conditioned weighted sampling and compact arithmetic | Source115 `flows.tex` operationalizes Jerrum–Valiant–Vazirani self-reduction; certified logarithm series and stable logistic evaluation are elementary | Technical arithmetic adaptation. No exact-sampling claim. |

The exact-uniform companion's Theorem1.1 concerns uncapped, unweighted tables and gives a bounded approximate sampler plus an expected-time exact correction. Its complete introduction was read and its internal weight-related passages were located; its entire proof was not reread in this packet. Nothing from its exact-correction mechanism is used in W152.

The only original interface assembly claimed here is reduced-cost localization before source115's large-cell bins, with the full parameter check. It closes the particular I152 obstruction author-side, but adds no new mixing, signature or flow optimization theorem. The author therefore records TECHNICAL/no nominee. This is not an independent contribution verdict, and it does not assert that an exact statement of W152 has already appeared in one of these references.
