A plausible quantitative direction was to turn rational nonvanishing into a representation-sized lower bound. This quickly meets an established mechanism. Symonds, The orbit space of the p-subgroup complex is contractible, Comment. Math. Helv.73 (1998), 400–405, https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/42802, proves that the Brown complex quotient is contractible when p divides |G|. The complete six-page primary text was read, including its lift-changing proof and relative remark. The quotient chain complex is the rational coinvariant chain complex: subgroup-chain orientations are preserved, quotient cells are chain orbits, and rational coinvariants are exact by averaging. Consequently its reduced homology is the coinvariants of reduced Brown-complex homology. Symonds therefore makes every rational reduced homology degree have zero trivial isotypic part. Combined with source nonvanishing and the elementary-/all-p-subgroup equivalence cited in the source introduction, a nonzero degree has dimension at least the smallest dimension of a nontrivial rational irreducible representation of G.
