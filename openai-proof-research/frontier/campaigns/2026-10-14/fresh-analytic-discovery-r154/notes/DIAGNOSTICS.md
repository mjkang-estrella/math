## Do not confuse affine-plane dimension and direction dimension

Let H=R3 times{0} in R4 and let F be its closed unit3ball. Let L be a small relatively open family of affine2planes in H meeting the radius1/2 ball. Each intersection with F contains an open2disc, so its Hausdorff dimension is2. The manifold A(3,2) has dimension3; hence dim L=3, while dim F=3.

The exploratory spread formula s+t/2 would give3.5 if one inserted affine-family dimension t=3 in place of directional dimension. That assertion is false. The direction family is contained in G(H,2), of dimension2, for which the paid formula gives exactly3. Any projective change of coordinates, restricted to valid bounded charts, maps the projective closure of H to another projective3space; its2plane directions in an affine chart still have dimension at most2. Thus a generic projective transformation cannot restore the lost direction parameter in this example. This is the geometric obstruction discussed in BD after its hyperplane projective-transfer proposition, now written explicitly for the selected codimension-two calculation. It does not refute a theorem with an additional geometric nonconcentration hypothesis, nor does it construct such a theorem.
