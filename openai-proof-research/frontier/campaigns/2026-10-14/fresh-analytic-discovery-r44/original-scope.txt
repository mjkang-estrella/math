For every integer d>=3, every beta>0, every symmetric real coupling law J
with E|J|<infinity, and every real field law H with E|H|<infinity, consider
independent couplings on edges and independent fields on vertices, all
independent of a uniform simple labelled d-regular graph on n vertices.
Take n to infinity through all dn-even admissible sizes. Its expected log
partition function divided by n converges to

  inf_{r>=0} inf_{zeta,m} B^{(d)}_{r,beta}(zeta,m).

Here use exactly family 221's nested independent-label message laws and
conditional power means, with theta(s,t)=beta J st and h=beta H. Replace
the Poisson site count in its functional by the deterministic count d and
its edge coefficient alpha(p-1) by d/2. Explicitly

  B^{(d)} = log 2 + E log T_0 V_s - (d/2) E log T_0 V_e,
  V_s=(1/2) sum_{epsilon=+-1} exp(beta H epsilon)
            product_{j=1}^d sum_{s=+-1} exp(beta J_j s epsilon)q_{x_j}(s),
  V_e=sum_{s,t=+-1} exp(beta J st)q_{x_1}(s)q_{x_2}(t),
  q_x(s)=exp(xs)/(2 cosh x).

For r>=1, zeta is a probability on r-fold nested probability measures on
R, as in source M_{r+1}; 0<m_1<...<m_r<1. For r=0, zeta is a probability
on R and T_0 is the identity. Different message labels have independent
entire nested processes; physical couplings/fields are held fixed in each
power mean and averaged afterward. No moment condition on messages.

The same formula is required for the d-regular configuration multigraph
model. Loops and multiple edges use their usual Ising interaction scores.
There is no high-temperature, small-field, discrete-coupling, fixed-depth,
replica-symmetry, subsequence, or attainment restriction. The target does
not claim physical-state ultrametricity or a constructed infinite hierarchy.
