# Scoped formalization plan — no formal verdict

The author proof is a technical screen and was not nominated for formal
review. A meaningful Lean split would isolate (1) bounded Hochschild
cochains and δ²=0, (2) uniform primitive estimates from the stipulated
source on bounded products, (3) the exact one-step conjugacy identity and
associator covariance, (4) the geometric iteration, finite stopping and
operator-product convergence. The source vanishing theorem must remain
an explicit imported premise, not be replaced by finite matrix tests.

`diagnostic.py` checks algebraic signs over ℚ² and the failure of an
approximate module differential to form a complex. It proves neither
the infinite-dimensional primitive estimates nor convergence. Its
negative controls deliberately use a wrong sign and a false square-zero
claim. A full all-von-Neumann formalization was neither written nor run.
