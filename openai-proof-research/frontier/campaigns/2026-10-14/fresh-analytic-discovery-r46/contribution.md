Kochanek's displayed theorem uses an exponent 1−η, not our exact linear
residual statement. We do not misquote that theorem. The comparison is
at the operation level: its actual recurrence has an additive fixed
associator term, and finite stopping at a constant multiple of that term
is already the natural operation. The source supplies the replacement
primitive estimates, not a new nonlinear scheme.

No source-companion duplicate is concealed: the canonical catalogue entry
for295 contains one manuscript, and the complete tree supplies 11 non-PDF
blobs plus its PDF. The blobs were authenticated; the PDF was not acquired.
Other cohomological papers appearing in history involve different algebraic
settings and are not evidence of either newness or exact duplication.
