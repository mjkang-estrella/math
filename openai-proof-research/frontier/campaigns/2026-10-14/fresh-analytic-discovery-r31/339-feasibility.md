# Family 339: positive entropy deficit and the unpaid geometric interface

Disposition: no nominee. This is a distinct actual-body feasibility screen after the family-352 barrier. No full quantitative rigidity theorem is proved. Exact entropy rigidity in the pinned source is stipulated, including all rank-one types; it is not audited against external literature.

## Exact target and comparisons

Let (M,g0) be any fixed closed connected negatively curved locally symmetric manifold of dimension n>=3, and fix Vol(g)=Vol(g0). A natural full local quantitative target is a C^N neighborhood and C>0 such that, for every smooth nearby metric g, after the usual diffeomorphism slice,

    ||phi_g^*g-g0||_{H^{3/2}(g0)}^2 <= C [h_top(g)-h_L(g)].

The target includes all nearby perturbations and all real, complex, quaternionic and Cayley rank-one backgrounds. The slice removes diffeomorphism invariance; the volume condition removes scale, which otherwise preserves equality. No projection away from an unknown finite-dimensional physical kernel is added to make the target pass. This is a screening target, not a frozen theorem claim.

Humbert, arXiv:2409.11197v3, MainTheo already proves exactly the displayed estimate, with an additional squared volume-error term, on the complement of the kernel of Pi_sol Q Pi_sol. It supplies all-type ellipticity, the generalized X-ray estimate, the slice, Taylor expansion and interpolation absorption. Solenoidal injectivity is proved for real and complex hyperbolic backgrounds. Its proof reduces the complex four-tensor equation to an explicit two-tensor W(S), estimates its spherical-harmonic components using raising/lowering operators and Pestov inequalities, and ends with a Weitzenbock coercive bound. The main theorem itself already discusses conversion to C^k powers by interpolation. These are paid operations, not proposed contributions here.

Quaternionic and Cayley solenoidal injectivity is explicitly a conjecture in that primary. The pinned global equality theorem does not prove Hessian injectivity: an isolated nonnegative zero can have zero Hessian, as x^4 shows. No inference from exact equality to quadratic coercivity is valid without an additional argument. Restricting to the finite-kernel complement would simply repeat Humbert.

A compact-class global power estimate obtained by entering these known local neighborhoods using the source's equality theorem plus compactness, followed by interpolation, would be a routine transfer in the real/complex cases. It is explicitly excluded as a nomination. A merely unspecified compactness modulus is also excluded.

## The source operation and positive-error debt

The source preliminaries use P(-J)=0 and h_L=int J dm_L. Equality of h_L with h_top makes m_L the equilibrium state for both -J and the constant -h_top. Comparing the two Gibbs estimates on repeated periodic orbits gives exact vanishing of every period integral of J-h_top. Livsic then gives the exact identity J=h_top+XF. The conormal forms constructed from F have exact opposite flow multipliers and constant nonzero wedge product.

Small entropy deficit alone does not let one compare two Gibbs estimates for the same measure: at positive deficit, m_L is not the equilibrium measure of the constant potential. Repeating an orbit and dividing its error by the number of repetitions is therefore not an available approximate version of the source proof.

A useful but standard diagnostic is the pressure identity. Put p(t)=P(-tJ), so p(1)=0 and p'(1)=-h_L. Where the usual twice-differentiable pressure formalism applies,

    delta=h_top-h_L=p(0)-p(1)+p'(1)=int_0^1 t p''(t) dt.

This is only the fundamental theorem of calculus together with pressure variation; p'' is an asymptotic variance in the corresponding equilibrium state. It does not supply a uniform C^r representative F or residual r in J=h_top+XF+r. The topology and the bound needed for such an approximate Livsic statement remain unpaid. The identity is not nominated.

The actual hull operation takes the exact real Zariski closure of the actual plaque-state germ; finite monomial evaluation spans and their exact annihilators define relation ideals. Noetherian stabilization, exact rank tests and exact invariant weights then supply formal affine fields. These are exact operations. To perturb them one needs bounds for finite-array conditioning, ranks and the relevant domains. The elementary matrix A_epsilon=diag(1,epsilon) illustrates the logical issue: ||A_epsilon e2||=epsilon but dist(e2,ker A_epsilon)=1 for epsilon>0. All matrix entries remain bounded; a small residual by itself does not control distance to the exact kernel without a singular-value bound. This is not a realizable geometric counterexample, and says nothing against the stipulated source.

The realization stage has a promising explicit tradeoff. Source smoothness Proposition smooth:propagation produces finite Taylor approximants p_{n,R} with

    ||p_{n,R}-q||_C0 <= C_{R,epsilon} exp(-(b(R+1)-epsilon)n),
    ||p_{n,R}||_Cj <= C_{R,j,epsilon} exp((B_j+epsilon)n),

where B_j is independent of R. This is a genuine lower operation, already paid by the source. If a new perturbation construction supplied an additional error eta exp(Cn) with uniform constants, balancing it with exp(-a_R n) would give eta^{a_R/(a_R+C)} at n=log(1/eta)/(a_R+C). The exponent arithmetic is elementary. The missing content is the estimate eta exp(Cn), with eta controlled by entropy deficit, for actual approximate conormal forms/plaque states and their finite jets. Source typical-set and finite-order constants are not stated uniformly on a positive-deficit class, and its exact formal relation kernels cannot simply be reused. This conditional tradeoff is not a quantitative rigidity proof.

Thus two potentially substantive mechanisms remain: (i) solve the all-type solenoidal kernel problem by a new quaternionic/Cayley geometric estimate, or (ii) construct an approximate cohomology/plaque mechanism with controlled finite-jet errors that directly yields a quantitative conclusion. Neither is achieved in r31. The source and Humbert bodies make these precise debts; no positivity, injectivity, rank stability or uniform radius is assumed to dispose of them.
