# Frozen target T022BA43

Selected after checking actual source022 and current primitive-counterexample statements, and before assembly of the two attempted proof routes. No theorem is asserted at freeze.

For every badly approximable real γ (there exists cγ>0 such that ||hγ||≥cγ/h for every integer h≥1), and every function ψ:N→[0,1/2] satisfying Σq φ(q)ψ(q)/q=∞, prove that for Lebesgue-almost every x∈[0,1] there are infinitely many integer pairs (a,q), q≥1, with gcd(a,q)=1 and |qx−a−γ|<ψ(q).

No monotonicity, support-density, extra divergence, uniform Diophantine constant, fixed congruence class, or quantitative counting conclusion is assumed. Ordinary numerator coprimality is mandatory; shift-adapted gcd conditions are not substitutes. Arbitrary badly approximable γ must be retained, not only a quadratic irrational. Failure to establish this target will remain unresolved rather than be replaced by the weak unreduced source theorem or a narrower positive subcase.

Two routes are being pursued: (A) determinant separation and pairwise/aggregate overlap estimates; (B) a primitive restriction of the source's weighted projected arrays, preserving first mass and its variance budget. Known rational/Liouville counterexamples were excluded before selecting this target and are not a disproof of it.
