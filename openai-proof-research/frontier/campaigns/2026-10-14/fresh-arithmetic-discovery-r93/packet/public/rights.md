# Rights and separation

Public originals in `packet/public` are this round's prose, code and provenance metadata. Original prose may be used under CC BY4.0 and original code under MIT, subject to the parent campaign's author and release workflow. No release is performed here. Candidate authorship and publication metadata are for the parent to integrate.

`packet/private` contains pinned upstream source bodies, primary PDFs/OCR/page renders, copied earlier history/context and search excerpts. These remain under their original rights. They are evidence for local review, not public-original payloads; no blanket license or republication authorization is asserted over them. Copying a complete original volume for local access does not imply semantic reading of its other papers.

Do not publish the whole packet or its private subtree. The public proof cites original primary URLs. The exact packet manifest authenticates private evidence for the parent but supplies no right to redistribute it. Publication remains STOPPED.
