# T195D93 — Frozen full quantitative deficiency statement

Let X be any smooth integral projective surface over C, and let H be any ample globally generated Cartier divisor on X. Put h=H^2 and Delta=K_X^2-2c_2(X). Let T=direct_sum_{n>=0} H^0(X,O_X(nH)) be the full section ring, let n=T_{>0}, and let (R,m) be the n-adic completion of T_n.

For every nonzero finitely generated reflexive R-module M, of arbitrary rank r=rank_R(M), H^2_m(M) has finite length and

    length_R H^2_m(M) >= r * max(0, (Delta-3h)/24).

The constant is exactly 1/24. There is no assumption that K_X is numerically proportional to H; no grading or algebraizability assumption on M; no restriction on rank, HN length or denominators; no asymptotic, subsequence, semistability, local-in-slope or large-parameter replacement. There is no effectiveness claim. All geometry is over C. The completion and full section ring above are part of the statement. No stronger assertion about other normal singularities, positive characteristic, lim Cohen–Macaulay sequences, equality classification or nonconical deformations is frozen.
