The remaining quantitative integration developed here is explicit. For E over C[[x₀,x₁,x₂]], the composition length L of Ext¹(E,A) supplies exactly L dual Koszul factors. For EVERY lattice of the same punctured bundle the functional Φ=2ch₂−c₁ costs at least −2 for each such factor and 0 for a free atom. Saturated extension of the stable filtration preserves restriction exactness. Since Φ(O)=0, the added free summands cancel exactly, without a rank-dependent loss. The bound therefore applies to the SAME pushforward produced by the fixed-interval surface transforms. Its upper bound is rh(1/4−Δ/(12h)), yielding the frozen formula.
