# Full problem retained after the operative screen

Recorded UTC: 2026-10-18T04:10:39.582240+00:00

For every integer n>=2, does there exist a function omega_n:(0,1)->[0,infinity), with omega_n(epsilon)->0 as epsilon decreases to zero, such that every closed connected smooth Riemannian n-manifold (M,g) with diam(M,g)=1 and inj(M,g)>=1-epsilon has Gromov--Hausdorff distance at most omega_n(epsilon) from some standard compact rank-one symmetric space of real dimension n, also normalized to diameter one?

No sectional/Ricci curvature bound, volume bound, fixed topology, fixed differentiable manifold, Zoll hypothesis, bound on derivatives, or assumed precompactness is included. No explicit power rate is required. All Riemannian manifolds in the displayed class are quantified, and model choice can depend on the manifold. This is a full unresolved problem retained for the feasibility screen, not a proved nominee. The exact epsilon=0 case is stipulated source344.

Chronology: this record is POST exploration of source344 and Durumeric's actual examples. The initial proposed interface was almost-Blaschke metric rigidity. Before this record, the exact-period/rank obstruction had been identified and a conformal-bump route to demonstrate failure of curvature bounds had been planned. No full target proof was completed. The auxiliary bump construction is not a replacement target and earns no count.
