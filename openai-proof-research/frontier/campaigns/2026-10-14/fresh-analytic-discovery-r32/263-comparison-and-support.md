## Primary operations actually inspected

**Solovej, Annals 2003.** [Published primary PDF](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v158-n2-p04.pdf). Theorem 1.5 concerns neutral Hartree–Fock minimizers and outer radii. Section 6 localizes an HF minimizer and compares its exterior energy with trials. Section 7 uses its HF equation for the unbounded exterior count and explains why allowing extra electrons at infinity defeats an energy-only mass bound. Lemma 8.2 constructs coherent trial density matrices; it does not identify the actual density. Lemma 4.4/Remark 4.5 give nonradial Sommerfeld comparison. These operations are credited. They do not supply the present arbitrary correlated form-state event estimate and its joint-scale weighted closure. Exact neutrality is essential here; the candidate does not contradict the variable-particle-number escape observation.

**Lieb–Simon, Advances in Mathematics 1977.** [Author-hosted primary PDF](https://math.caltech.edu/SimonPapers/53.pdf). Section III.1 defines approximate ground states by energy excess o(Z^{7/3}) and proves density convergence on the bulk Z^{-1/3} scale. Its actual argument perturbs the potential by a fixed test, uses Rayleigh–Ritz and convergence of perturbed energies, and then uses differentiability/concavity to identify the density. That operation is a clear precedent for approximate-state density convergence. Our observation scale m^{-1/3}, with m/Z→0, moves to infinity in their rescaled coordinates; its normalized mass is m, not Z. Their inspected theorem/proof supplies no uniform moving-tail error at that scale. We do not claim that approximate-state TF convergence itself is new.

The two primary comparisons are bounded. Searches also located reviews and unrelated mean-field/relativistic papers; these were not relied on. No worldwide novelty certificate is claimed. The source E fixed-sector energy asymptotics, R iterated radius asymptotics, and F arbitrary-offset/fixed-offset interfaces were compared directly; no companion theorem inspected already states the candidate full near-ground joint-scale conclusion.

## Adversarial checks and limitations

* Form energy, not operator expectation, is used for every form-domain vector.
* D includes the uniform neutral-to-global energy offset; δ alone is not used as an offset above E_Z.
* Both likelihood and its complement are needed for IMS. Jensen for a log^5(e/a) is not assumed; the actual likelihood-weighted tail gives the bound.
* No fixed-power smallness δa^7≤a^.02 is inferred from an arbitrary little-o. Formula (6) retains θ and (7)–(9) absorb it through the adaptive width.
* A net of the original r² mesh would destroy the slow-error estimate. The adaptive mesh has cost T^{-15}, explicitly charged to α.
* A pointwise density bound would unnecessarily introduce a packet-volume loss in the barrier budget. Formula (12) instead uses the integrated mass and the common bad-band event.
* The nuclear initialization includes the growing neutral mass: λu_0^{α_0}→0, not just P(bad)→0.
* The neutral spherical identity is valid without spatial symmetry; antisymmetry remains required throughout the quantum inputs.
* The exterior proof takes the sequence limit with R fixed before R→∞. It does not sum √(Dr) over all dyadic r or use the source priced eigenstate cutoff.
* Arbitrary slow e→0 and λ→∞ are independently allowed. Constants for each fixed requested accuracy/band precede the sequence threshold; the final increasing band is chosen by a slow diagonal.
* Only expected density and radius are claimed. No empirical-particle concentration, fixed-m limit, eigenstate existence proof, gap, or literature-priority conclusion is claimed.
