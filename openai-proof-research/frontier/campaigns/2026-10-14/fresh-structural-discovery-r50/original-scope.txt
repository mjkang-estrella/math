T239A — full original target, fixed before complete proof assembly.
For every fixed real p with 0<p<1, let A_n be the adjacency matrix over the real numbers of the simple loopless Erdos–Renyi graph G(n,p): A_ii=0 and the A_ij=A_ji, i<j, are independent Bernoulli(p) bits. Then
    lim_(n→infinity) P(det A_n=0)^(1/n) = max{1-p, p^2+(1-p)^2}.
The full statement includes p=1/2 and both sides of1/2. No uniformity for p depending on n, no leading prefactor, no conditional kernel classification, no sparse-regime limit and no deterministic algorithm are asserted. If the proof fails, this full scope is retained; a restricted bias range or a merely conditional column estimate will not replace it as the nominee.
