## Primary comparisons beyond the supplied manuscript

All PDFs are private reference inputs, with original author rights retained. Exact bytes and extracted text hashes are in primary-receipts.json. These were read as layout-preserving extracted text, not visually inspected for page layout.

**Campana–Claudon, arXiv:1107.0168v1**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.0168v1. Theorem 2.2 treats special orbifold surfaces using the two-dimensional klt/uniformizability situation. Theorem 4.1 extends virtual abelianity through fibrations when both fibre and base groups are already virtually abelian: it first obtains an almost polycyclic group, uses linearity and Selberg to remove finite torsion, then passes to a cover/resolution and the manifold mixed-Hodge argument. This does not supply an unrestricted special-manifold realization of an unknown orbifold group. Section 5 uses log MMP and signed-Ricci orbifold metric/splitting arguments for surfaces, not an arbitrary-dimensional auxiliary abelian fibration. Exact reads: extracted lines 180–220, 360–435, 475–575, 577–690. No complete appendix or full-paper reading claim.

**Campana, arXiv:0705.0737v8**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.0737v8. Corollary 11.29 and Theorem 11.31 use residual finiteness to separate finitely many local inertia elements and obtain finite covers whose resolutions realize finite-index groups. This is a positive prior operation, and it explains why the residual-finiteness-free hypothesis matters. Remark 11.30 explicitly warns that resolution can change the group. Conjecture 12.10 states orbifold abelianity; Proposition 12.11 reduces it conditionally through orbifold Iitaka and other orbifold cases, rather than the single manifold input. Question 12.14 asks for a commensurable group/universal-cover realization. OA057 only supplies an epimorphism from a higher-dimensional manifold, not that stronger realization. Exact reads: lines 4260–4350 (specialness/Bogomolov criterion), and all 5520–5840 (including 11.29–11.34 and 12.10–12.14). No full 160-page reading claim.

**Campana survey, arXiv:1001.3763v1**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3763v1. Exact read lines 1760–1810, Proposition 11.1, Conjecture 11.2 and Remarks 11.3: stability and proposed orbifold reductions are antecedents, not an all-order special-manifold realization. This is contextual, not a new premise.

**Belmans–Demleitner–Núñez, arXiv:2411.14814v1**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.14814v1. Equations (21)–(23) give the standard linear/translation cocycle description of affine actions. Proposition 32 realizes quotients using an exact 2-torsion translation in one factor and inversion in another; its proof is read in full. Example 33 and Remark 34 discuss C_m squared actions and order-4/order-6 restrictions of the particular elliptic scalar construction. This supplies a concrete operation comparison, not a claim that all higher-order affine actions are new. OA057 uses permutations on E^M and only excludes the designated boundary inertia; it does not construct a free action of the whole group. Exact reads: lines 340–392, 675–788 (Lemma 29, Proposition 30, Example 31, Proposition 32), 808–880 (Example 33, Remark 34, start of Proposition 35 only). Publisher numbering differs; this comparison uses the frozen v1 numbering.

**Kresch–Vistoli, arXiv:math/0301249v1**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0301249v1. Entire seven-page extracted text read, including proofs. Theorem 2.1 constructs a smooth finite-flat scheme cover of a smooth quotient DM stack with quasi-projective coarse space. Its proof takes enough products of projectivized bundles, makes the nonrepresentable locus have small dimension, then cuts by high-degree generic hypersurfaces until the map is finite. This realizes smooth projective sources when the coarse space is projective, but supplies neither connected abelian general fibres nor the divisorial submersions needed to preserve specialness. Finite-flat ramification need not preserve specialness: a finite branched cover of P^1 can be a curve of genus at least two. Keeping the projective bundle instead leaves stack stabilizers; cutting away stabilizers introduces the complete-intersection geometry. Thus this actual general realization operation does not directly replace the OA057 package. It is not asserted that no other realization theorem exists. The attempted v2 PDF returned 404; the author-hosted PDF timed out and was interrupted without saving bytes. Only the successful v1 is evidence.
