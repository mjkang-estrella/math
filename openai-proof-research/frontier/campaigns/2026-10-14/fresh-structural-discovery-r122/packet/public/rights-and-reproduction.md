# Rights and reproduction

Original research prose and original/adapted integrity code only are public under this directory. Project attribution: Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation; prose CC BY4.0 and original code MIT, following the campaign convention. This is a local research archive, not a publication or license grant over third-party material. Upstream, external primary PDFs/extractions and copied historical bodies remain private under their original rights. No third-party body is relicensed by the public metadata.

The verifier/control runner adapt the original r121 integrity implementation; its mathematical research is not copied into public prose. Files under research-tools/ preserve acquisition/search scripts as historical text, with their original root-relative assumptions; they are historical work tools, not verification commands, and need not be rerun. The verifier performs no network access and no writes.

From the repository root, invoke Python with -B, the owned packet/public/verify.py, --root set to this owned directory, --tree sources/preprints-tree.json, and externally supplied --finalhash and --reporthash. The two required SHA256 arguments are reported outside packet and in the final handoff. It verifies exact packet file/directory membership, regular single-link files, modes, bytes, source Git blobs/raw URLs/tree selection, primary hashes/versions, selected history copy hashes and running verifier identity. It does not recurse into live historical origin packets or authenticate an external origin beyond the bound snapshot identities.

packet/public/run_copied_controls.py takes the same four arguments. It executes the original verifier against disposable copies under controls/, with wrong/missing anchors, content/inventory/path/special-file changes, coherent resealing against the old anchor and isolated provenance mutations under explicitly NEW TEST anchors. Only the baseline should pass. Original protected-root inventories before/after must agree. controls/integrity-controls.json and final-verification.json are outside the sealed packet to avoid circular hashing. No mathematical verdict follows from these tests.
