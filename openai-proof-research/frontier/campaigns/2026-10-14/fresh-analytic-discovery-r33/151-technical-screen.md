# Family151 finite-regularity screen: technical only

Myeongjin Kang. Original prose CC BY4.0. All five substantive source files main, construction, orbits, entropy and homology were read in full. Clock-figure and README are acquired but not claimed read.

The tempting strengthening is a C^r zero-entropy endomorphism with homological eigenvalue2 for each prescribed finite integer r. The source construction already leaves its register gain L arbitrarily large above a fixed lower bound. Its smooth prediction map G is independent of L. On the compact quotient, for each fixed finite r, the derivatives of G^m through order r have a bound C_r K_r^m: prove this inductively using the composition chain rule, the exponential lower-order bounds and a discrete geometric-sum bound for the highest derivative. The angular functions have order-j derivative O_r((2^(m+1))^j), so the source summands have C^r norm at most C_r L^(1-m) K'_r^m for another finite K'_r. Choosing L>K'_r gives uniform convergence of all derivatives through order r.

The source orbit/readout proof uses only L>1 for its partial-register bound and exact cancellation L^(1+i-j)L^(j-i-1)=1; its entropy proof uses the monotone saturation radius up to3L, allowed for each chosen L; its homology argument has no upper bound on L. Thus higher fixed finite regularity is paid by a standard derivative estimate and parameter choice in the existing construction. It is classified TECHNICAL and not nominated. The constants depend on r and the resulting map depends on r. No single C-infinity map is asserted, and no limit in r is taken. This is not an audit or challenge to any stipulated source result.
