## Actual merged-root repair

Fix a good prefix and a vertex v. Sample one or two edges (v,x) independently and uniformly from its d_v incident edges. For two roots, the same edge is chosen with probability 1/d_v. Put this case into the error. Otherwise identify their common v in the formal initial graph, which is a two-edge star. Introduce a new formal vertex at each child gluing as in the source. The initial graph is triangle-free; each noninitial vertex has exactly the two older neighbors of its attachment edge. Every formal edge is focused only at its birth call. The proof of Lemma witness applies verbatim to this merged graph: a repeated triangle type or a first repeated noninitial vertex label forces an earlier extra edge on the union of two ancestral paths. The intentional identification of the original center is already in the formal graph and is not a collision. Any formal triangle still has a noninitial latest vertex and is one of the gluing triangles.

For a short witness pattern with r=1 or2 used roots and ell<L nonroot calls, distinguish the center v and leave all other vertices free. There are r+ell free vertices and r+2ell+1 tested edges. Every nonempty subset of free vertices has a first member with at most one earlier neighbor in the distinguished set; all others have at most two earlier neighbors. Before adding the extra edge its induced edge count is at most 2f-1, and afterward at most2f. Thus every same-root subtemplate has scaling at least n^f p^(2f)=D^f≥1. The source's large-template bound gives

    kappa_n n^(r+ell) p^(r+2ell+1)
      = kappa_n (np)^r D^ell p.

The root matching probability is d_v^(-r) and d_v~np uniformly, so this is the same normalized extra-edge saving kappa_n p. There are at most103 vertices, below H0=8001. As in the source, kappa_n p≤D^(-B), B=50.

For ell≥L, choose the last L births. The labels before them have at most d_v^r(2D)^(ell-L) choices. The source's last-L template keeps all2L backward edges plus the extra edge, with an independent boundary root set of size≤2L+1. Its ancestor-closure proof uses only the one/two path structure and attachment rule; initial center identification changes neither. Its total scaling is D^L p<1, and each proper free subset retains at most twice its cardinality edges. Hence the small-template bound gives≤kappa_n completions and the normalized bound (2D)^ell D^(-B) again.

Coupling failure is bounded uniformly in v and the good prefix by

    Delta_n=O(D^(-35)+1/d_v)=o(q²).

For k=1 the repeated-leaf term is absent. Sampling with replacement retains exact ordinary moments. Given the terminal graph, the chance every root survives is (deg_H(v)/d_v)^k. In the independent model it is the kth power of the average q_e over the star, namely (1+o(1))q^k. The two moments give

    E[(deg_H(v)/(d_v q)-1)² | prefix]=o(1)

uniformly in v and the good prefix. Since d_v q/sqrt(n)→1/sqrt2 uniformly, and the source's bad-prefix probability is superpolynomially small while deg_H(v)≤n, it follows that

    sup_v E[(deg_Hn(v)/sqrt(n)-1/sqrt2)²]→0.

Summing expectations also gives convergence to zero in L1 of the empirical average squared discrepancy. This is a complete technical deduction relative to the stated source interfaces, not an independent proof gate or a new accepted result.

## Why this does not settle extremes

The statement does not control max_v |deg_H(v)/sqrt(n)-1/sqrt2|. A union bound multiplies the second-moment bound by n. The retained collision term D^(-35), relative to q², is D^(-34)=n^(-.034+o(1)); it is insufficient. The qualitative independent-model error has no all-vertex tail assertion either. Growing/high centered moments, concentration of the complete priority dependency exploration, or another extremal mechanism would be needed. Merely applying the same uncentered k-root union bound gives an additive collision error and does not establish the required centered cancellation. No impossibility theorem or counterexample to uniform regularity is claimed.
