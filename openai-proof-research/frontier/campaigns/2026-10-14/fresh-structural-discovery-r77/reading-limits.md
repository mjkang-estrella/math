Diagnostics.py was written, read, then executed. It uses exact Gaussian rationals and reduced free-product moment recursion for six four-point spectral laws, including non-Haar real and non-real traces and scalar endpoint controls. It tests first-order response for word lengths 1–6, nonidentity-degree trace vanishing on seeded finite words, and prefix multiplicities for lengths 1–30. All 2,892 assertions pass; three intentional wrong formulas are detected. These are finite algebraic checks, not finite-factor models of the theorem, and do not certify freeness in an arbitrary factor, all nonlinear degrees, spectral clipping, norm Baire genericity, or contribution significance. The full proofs of those assertions are in candidate.md.

Verify.py was adapted from the fully read r75 integrity helper. It requires an externally supplied manifest hash, verifies exact file/directory membership and payload hashes, rejects symbolic and hard links, anchors the unchanged scope and complete source tree, and checks all nine canonical blob identities and both forms of each primary receipt. It is an integrity verifier only. Copied-package replay and deliberate corruption controls are to be reported externally after the immutable seal; the manifest does not self-certify its own external anchor.
