Family206 is the current pre-freeze screen. The pinned characterization companion's full Diophantine section explicitly proves undecidability by computing a bound on ALL representable lattice witnesses below a syntax-dependent cardinality ceiling. It never computes a lattice from an arbitrary input circuit. The literal weighted template is constructed only AFTER choosing a positive integer solution, as an ordinary interval [S_Z × Y, S_(Z union units)]. Thus a direct many-one reduction from positive-circuit solvability is a genuinely missing operation; merely restating Turing completeness or the noncomputable witness bound is technical.

Two precise issues to investigate before selecting a full target:
1. Uniform size bound Phi(t) is not an algorithm listing exactly the possible literal tables for a fixed unsolved circuit. Such a list requires a solution-independent syntactic construction and soundness for every listed table.
2. Inserting finitely many distinguished meet/join constraints only constructs a sublattice/diagram. It does not exhaust an ambient algebra's congruences. Since every finite lattice embeds in a finite partition lattice, even a nonrepresentable finite lattice's entire positive finite diagram embeds in a representable lattice. Therefore this local-diagram strategy cannot alone enforce the missing fullness condition.
