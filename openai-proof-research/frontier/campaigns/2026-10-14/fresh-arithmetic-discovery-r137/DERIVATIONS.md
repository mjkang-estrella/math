## 2. Endoscopic dimensions, square-zero multiplication, and the actual remaining algebra

For gamma of order m>1, let r=n/m and let pi:C'->C be its connected cyclic etale cover. Then g'=m(g-1)+1. The relative Hitchin base has trace-zero H^0(K_C') part of dimension g'-g, and components H^0(K_C'^i), 2<=i<=r. Thus

    s_gamma = (g'-g) + (r^2-1)(g'-1)
            = r^2(g'-1)+1-g
            = (n^2/m-1)(g-1),
    d_gamma = R-s_gamma = n^2(1-1/m)(g-1).

This formula includes r=1 (the sum is empty). It comes from the actual canonical relative base, not a formal substitution into a large-twist formula.     2d_gamma1+2d_gamma2 >= 2n^2(g-1) > 2R.

## 3. Canonical multiplicative splitting is already paid

There is a useful compatibility calculation that eliminates another apparent candidate. Let H denote total ordinary cohomology with its graded mixed Hodge structure. H^2 is generated by the rational class e of Hodge type (2,2); cup by e raises half-weight by two and satisfies curious hard Lefschetz centered at R. Define a decreasing rational filtration directly from the nilpotent operator e:

    M^a(e) = sum_{i,j>=0, i-j=a} (Im(e^i) intersect Ker(e^(j+1))).

Only finitely many distinct subspaces occur. Mellit's unique Lefschetz-filtration and split-Tate mechanism (published Propositions 2.12–2.20) gives F^k H_C=M^(k-R)(e)_C. Thus

    G_k = W_{2k}H intersect M^(k-R)(e),
    H = direct_sum_k G_k,
    W_{2k}H = direct_sum_{j<=k}G_j.

This is rational, not merely the complex Deligne splitting. It is multiplicative: x in G_a and y in G_b have product in W_{2a+2b} intersect F^(a+b)=G_{a+b}. It is Gamma-compatible because e and W are. Via NAH and P=W it splits the perverse filtration multiplicatively as well.

On a primitive e-string v,e v,...,e^ell v, with v in G_(R-ell), the canonical lowering is

    f_can(e^j v)=j(ell-j+1)e^(j-1)v.

Then h=[e,f_can] acts by k-R on G_k. It agrees with the source lowering. Indeed the difference D=f-f_can has grading components of h-weight at most -2 and satisfies (ad e)^2 D=0. In every finite-dimensional sl_2 summand of End(H), raising twice is injective on weight <=-2. Hence every such component of D is zero. This also proves independence, at the cohomology-operator level, from auxiliary moving-pole choices used to construct f.

Although h itself has the shifted eigenvalue k-R, the operator h+R id is a derivation of the ring by the multiplicative grading. This conclusion must not be confused with f being a derivation; its lowering behavior does not imply that. ## 4. Concrete one-node module calculation

    delta(u+eta v)=(1-lambda)u+c v.

Its kernel is represented by

    theta_kappa v = (eta-c/(1-lambda))v,

For an invariant class represented by a+eta b, descent gives c b=0. Direct multiplication, retaining the projective-bundle relation, yields

    p_*((a+eta b)theta_kappa v)

The constant coefficient agrees as well because the difference is a multiple of c^2 b=0. Thus this nodal step is linear over the descended invariant action, after the specified identification, and is not merely a vector-space isomorphism. ## 5. Family normalization: explicit Chern correction

On a fine local chart with labeled disjoint nodes over a base B, write u:tilde{S}->S for simultaneous normalization. For a line bundle N on S there is an exact sequence

    0 -> N -> u_*u^*N -> direct_sum_j Q_j -> 0,

where Q_j is a line on the node section. Push to C times B and let E,E' be the resulting rank-n bundles. Write i_j:B->C times B for the graph of its projected node, delta_j=i_{j*}1, q_j=c_1(Q_j), t_j=c_1(N_{i_j})=x_j^*c_1(TC), a=c_1(E), D=sum delta_j. Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch gives

    c_1(E')=a+D,
    ch_2(E')-ch_2(E)=sum_j i_{j*}(q_j-t_j/2).

For the normalized character ch_2- c_1^2/(2n), integration over C therefore changes by

    sum_j(q_j-t_j/2) - (1/n) int_C a D - (1/(2n)) int_C D^2.

When the graph sections are disjoint, this is

    sum_j [q_j - i_j^*a/n - (n+1)t_j/(2n)].

Twisting by a determinant-correcting root line does not change the normalized character; it therefore does not by itself erase this expression. Moreover vanishing on every fixed Prym fiber does not imply vanishing on the family. Evaluation of the Poincare line at a fixed curve point has zero rational first Chern class on the Jacobian, whereas the universal Poincare line on C times Jac(C) has a nonzero H^1(C) tensor H^1(Jac(C)) component. This explains why a fiberwise argument alone cannot discard the family correction.

This calculation is local on the line-bundle locus; compactification adds the projective-bundle tautological terms of section 4.
