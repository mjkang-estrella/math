# T054L136 — exact author-proof candidate

There exists an integer B0>=2 with the following property. Let B>=B0 be an integer. Let a>max(B,3) be an odd integer all of whose prime divisors are congruent to1 modulo3. Assume that for each integer n with1<=n<=B there is an odd prime p for which at least one of the following holds:

(i) v_p(a)=1 and the Legendre symbol (-n/p)=-1;

(ii) v_p(a+1)=1 and the Legendre symbol (n/p)=-1.

Let A_a be the positive definite integral lattice with ordered basis H,sigma,t and Gram matrix

    [3, 1, 0]
    [1, (2a+1)/3, 0]
    [0, 0, 2(a+1)].

For EVERY smooth complex cubic fourfold X with an isometry A_a -> A(X)=H^4(X,Z) intersect H^{2,2}(X) carrying H to h_X^2, and with End_Hdg(T(X) tensor Q)=Q, where T(X)=A(X)^perp in H^4(X,Z), X is irrational and its Kuznetsov component is equivalent to the bounded derived category of an untwisted smooth projective K3 surface.

For each a satisfying these arithmetic hypotheses, the locus of cubic fourfolds admitting a primitive embedding A_a -> A(X) carrying H to h_X^2 is a nonempty irreducible closed algebraic subvariety of dimension18 in the moduli space of smooth cubic fourfolds. Its very general member has A(X) isometric to A_a and End_Hdg(T(X) tensor Q)=Q, hence has the asserted irrationality and geometric K3 category. For each integer B>=2 there are infinitely many a satisfying the arithmetic hypotheses above; the corresponding dimension18 loci are pairwise distinct.

B0 is existential and is not asserted effective. “Very general” means outside a countable union of proper closed algebraic subsets. This statement asserts neither irrationality of every member of the closed locus, nor irrationality of its ambient Hassett divisor, nor any arithmetic field of definition, effective size bound, stable irrationality, or new categorical criterion.
