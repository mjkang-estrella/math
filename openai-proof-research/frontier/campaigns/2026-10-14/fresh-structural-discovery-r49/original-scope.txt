T197H — original full scope, recorded before final proof assembly.
There exist a finitely presented torsion-free word-hyperbolic discrete group G, a finite two-dimensional classifying CW complex K(G,1), and finite-support elements a,b,c in the ordinary group algebra F_2[G] such that ab=1, ac=0 and c is nonzero. In particular ba is not1 and G is not sofic. Hyperbolicity means Gromov hyperbolicity of a Cayley graph for a finite generating set, with no prescribed numerical hyperbolicity constant. The assertion is existential; it does not claim an executed explicit finite presentation, a uniform algorithmic complexity bound, a statement in odd or zero characteristic, or a classification of hyperbolic groups.
The source-essential proposed operation is a linear ordinary Dehn-area bound for cone presentations selected by source197's bounded-path exclusion, using a formal exterior boundary with an arbitrary freely reduced rose word. Algebraic one-sided inverses, asphericity, torsion-freeness and the sofic/direct-finiteness implication are prior inputs, not new operations.
