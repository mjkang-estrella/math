## Scoped formal support plan

A feasible formal component would encode the rational budget inequalities and a finite combinatorial interval-pairing lemma for ribbon maps with explicit disk/region data. A fuller development would require a formal relative cone-picture theorem, band surgery preserving a fixed outer word, planar separators and the linear-Dehn hyperbolicity criterion. None is currently implemented in Lean. The supplied finite checks exercise ribbon-map cut transport and rational constants only; they cannot certify those general topology statements or the new theorem.
