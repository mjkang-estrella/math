# Prefreeze: finite central descent is already supplied

Fix a finite subgroup H of Z_G and the central isogeny G -> G/H. On a smooth projective complex curve, the obstruction to lifting a G/H-bundle is a class in H^2(X,H). On the zero-obstruction substack, the fiber of the lifting map is the full Picard groupoid Bun_H, with isomorphism classes H^1(X,H) and automorphisms H^0(X,H). The adjoint determinant descends canonically. Smooth/etale descent of twisted D-modules is therefore groupoid descent, not merely taking invariants under the finite set H^1(X,H).

Source069 §13 explicitly gives coherent compatibility on that groupoid. It follows that equivariant descent data on the G side transport to equivariant data for tensor by the dual transgression systems. This is an ordinary transport of descent, already paid once the source's central theorem has been used. No distinct operation is introduced.

For example, an object with central character eta can carry untwisted Bun_H-equivariance only if eta restricted to H is trivial: automorphisms h of the identity torsor must act as the identity on that equivariance unit. Source sectors send (eta,p) to (p,-eta). The target tensor u_Q^(-1) acts under h by eta(h), exactly the same constraint. A computation using only H^1(X,H) would miss this condition even if every permutation of connected components were right.

For H=mu_n, H^1(X,H) has n^(2g) elements, H^0(X,H)=mu_n and H^2(X,H) is cyclic of order n (with the complex orientation convention retained). Counting or projecting by these finite characters is standard finite Fourier algebra. Nonzero H^2 classes cannot be supplied by pretending every quotient bundle has a lift. They require twisted lifting data; alternatively the source already treats G/H itself with its actual global form and every component. Neither route is an additional theorem nominee.

This calculation uses the actual proofs of Source069 equivalence:sectors and equivalence:central, including relative lifting obstructions, Weil restriction section covers through collisions, and the sign checks on torsor automorphisms. Bogdanova v1 §7, fully read, already constructs the corresponding gerbe/2-Fourier–Mukai framework in her Betti setting. Her result is a functor with that compatibility, not the full all-D-module irrational equivalence of Source069. Neither stronger finite descent nor a change of terminology earns a new claim here.
