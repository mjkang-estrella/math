# Frozen target TFS: torsion-free solutions of nonsingular systems

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. 2026-10-16.
Original prose CC BY 4.0; original code MIT. Third-party inputs remain private with their original rights.

For EVERY torsion-free group G, with no restriction on cardinality, finite generation, presentation, residual properties, solvability, orderability or local indicability, for EVERY pair of integers 1 <= m <= n, and EVERY ordered list w_1,...,w_m in G * F(x_1,...,x_n), form the integer matrix A=(a_ij), where a_ij is the total exponent of x_j in w_i. Assume rank_Q A=m.

TFS asserts that there exist a TORSION-FREE group H, an injective homomorphism i:G -> H, and elements h_1,...,h_n of H for which every w_i(i(G),h_1,...,h_n)=1.

No uniqueness of solution, finite presentation of H, relative asphericity, or torsion-freeness of the universal presented quotient is asserted. All torsion-free G, all nonsingular rectangular and square systems, all signs, and all positive invariant factors of A are retained. The trivial group G is included. A solution within a previously known class is not a replacement for TFS.

The stipulated input is family 256, openai/math fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb, Nonsingular systems of equations over arbitrary groups, Theorem 1. It supplies injectivity G -> U=(G*F_n)/<<w_1,...,w_m>>, hence an unrestricted solution overgroup. It does not state the torsion-free requirement on H.

The unpaid operation is to choose a quotient of U that removes all torsion while preserving every coefficient, or another construction with exactly that effect. The full assertion is frozen before sustained proof work. It will not be narrowed to layered systems, unimodular matrices, solvable groups, or residual classes to claim success. No new-conjecture, worldwide novelty, independent PASS, or count increase is claimed.
