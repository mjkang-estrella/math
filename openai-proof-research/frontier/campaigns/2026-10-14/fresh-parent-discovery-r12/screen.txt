2. Disjoint input/output extension: false, known obstruction
Screened stronger assertion: replacing Gamma by arbitrary nonempty disjoint open input and observation patches still determines the metric and connection up to a patch-fixing diffeomorphism and unitary gauge. It already fails for the trivial connection on a ball; no uncertain source result is needed for this negative conclusion.

Take the closed Euclidean unit ball M in dimension n>=3. Choose nonempty disjoint open boundary caps D,N such that a third open cap O is disjoint from their closures. Choose a nonzero smooth nonnegative boundary bump chi supported in O. Let c be the harmonic extension of 1+epsilon chi, epsilon>0. Smooth elliptic Dirichlet theory gives c smooth on the closure; the maximum principle gives c>1 in the interior and c=1 on D union N. Set g_tilde=c^(4/(n-2)) g and A_tilde=A=0 on the trivial rank-two Hermitian bundle.

The metric volume is c^(2n/(n-2)) dV_g, so Vol(g_tilde)>Vol(g). The two metrics are not isometric by ANY diffeomorphism. In particular they are not equivalent under the source's smaller patch-fixing gauge group.

For clarity use the source's density-valued energy convention. For smooth scalar v,w one has the pointwise sesquilinear identity
 c^2 <d(v/c),d(w/c)> = <dv,dw> - <dc,d(v conjugate(w)/c)>.
Since Delta_g c=0, integration yields
 E_gtilde(v/c,w/c) = E_g(v,w) - integral_boundary (v conjugate(w)/c) partial_nu c dS_g.
Equivalently, for arbitrary boundary f,h and their g-harmonic extensions of cf,ch,
 <Lambda_gtilde f,h> = <Lambda_g(cf),ch> - integral_boundary c f conjugate(h) partial_nu c dS_g.
Harmonicity of v/c for g_tilde follows directly from
 div_g(c^2 grad_g(v/c)) = c Delta_g v - v Delta_g c = 0.
These identities apply componentwise to C^2 with the zero connection.

If f is supported in D and h in N, then cf=f, ch=h and f conjugate(h)=0. Therefore all measured cross forms agree exactly. This proves the negative screen at full disjoint-data scope, rather than proposing a restricted positive replacement.

Negative control against overclaim: for nonzero f=h supported in D, the boundary term does not vanish. Hopf's boundary lemma gives partial_nu c<0 on D, since c attains its strict boundary minimum there. Hence the same-patch quadratic forms differ. The counterexample does not conflict with the stipulated same-patch theorem.

3. Nontrivial rank-two bundle extension: screened, not proved/nominated
A separate natural question is joint recovery when the two Hermitian rank-two bundles over M may be topologically nontrivial, with a prescribed unitary identification over the measured patch. Do not confuse this with I112's developed unknown-potential or quantitative-stability targets.

The read source interfaces already work substantially in local frames:
- its value/two-jet proof uses dual-bundle point distributions, scalar ellipticity and unique continuation;
- matching is defined as a local isometry plus a unitary map between fibers;
- joint surjectivity of source evaluations forces the point map and fiber map to be unique on overlaps;
- local conformal removal uses harmonic frames and parallel transport;
- global boundary extension uses normal parallel transport.
For a nontrivial bundle the exterior cap need only be trivial near a smaller patch. One can compare cap minimizers by retaining the common cap section and taking the separate interior harmonic extensions of its boundary trace; an identification of the entire bundles is unnecessary for that competitor construction. Compactness of fiber maps near limiting points can be expressed in local unitary frames.

These observations indicate a routine local-frame adaptation, not an identified new substantial mechanism. They are not a complete proof: source sections03–06 and the remainder of contact construction have not been audited under the changed bundle hypotheses, and global source transfer outside local trivializations has not been checked here. No generic claim that all local source theorems automatically globalize is made.
