# Primary interfaces and unpaid transfers

Ruelle, On manifolds of phase coexistence (1977), actual six-page article body read
in the IHES PDF (printed pp.24–29; surrounding articles are incidental extraction).
Sections 3–5 develop convex perturbation methods for coexistence, including
multiple phases and lattice pair interactions. This is an antecedent for generic
coexistence/repair claims, not a radial continuum realization theorem. Section 7
explicitly qualifies the proposed manifold picture. No original Israel1975 body
was read; credit to Israel here is through Ruelle, not independent theorem checking.
URL: https://www.ihes.fr/~ruelle/PUBLICATIONS/%5B48%5D.pdf

He–Jauslin–Lebowitz–Peled, arXiv:2510.24825v1, actual extracted lines240–625 and
680–745 read, plus HTML opening through line121. Equation1.13 depends on box
membership; equations1.14–1.16 give the exact density-spin representation.
Theorems1.3–1.4 establish coexistence in that box model under the stated
mean-field nonconvexity and convergence assumptions. Assumption1 and Theorem1.6
require separated good sets and normalized single-site mass estimates. This is
not a theorem for a general radial difference-only interaction.
URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.24825v1

For source228, the available O(sqrt(R0/R)) pressure comparison is a volume-scale
free-energy estimate. It does not identify the microscopic conditional measures
with the box-spin model, supply its reflection positivity, or bound probabilities
of mixed good/bad blocks. Thus the boxed finite-range result cannot be imported
by citing small pressure error. The unpaid route would need a conditional-block
or contour estimate stable under the actual inter-box radial interaction, with
constants uniform across the required competing phases. No such estimate is
proved here. No claim that all possible radial adaptations fail is made.

Simple truncation, schedule changes, coexistence-to-thermodynamics translations,
and generic small-perturbation repair are not nominated. The new calculation in
cluster-obstruction.md is a screening exclusion only, not a claimed new-to-literature
result. It does not establish nonexistence of multiple transitions for radial potentials.
