# Full prospective target T328A90

For every real reflexive Banach space X, every nonempty norm-closed bounded convex C contained in X, and every map T:C→C for which there are real numbers k_n≥1 with k_n→1 and ||T^n x−T^n y||≤k_n||x−y|| for all n≥1 and x,y∈C, prove that T has a fixed point in C.

No superreflexivity, normal structure, weak continuity, demiclosedness, asymptotic regularity, rate or summability of k_n−1, or additional compactness hypothesis is allowed. The given norm is retained. This is the classical asymptotically nonexpansive extension of the source328 theorem, not an assertion for arbitrary asymptotically nonexpansive-type or intermediate-sense maps. No restricted case may replace this target.
