# Recorded full target H (2026-10-16)

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.

For every finite simple cubic bipartite planar 3-vertex-connected graph G=(V,E), the affine hull over R of the incidence vectors of its Hamiltonian cycles is exactly

    {x in R^E : sum_{e incident with v} x_e = 2 for all v in V}.

In particular its dimension is |E|-|V|+1=|V|/2+1, so G has at least |V|/2+2 distinct Hamiltonian cycles.

The affine-hull assertion is the target, not merely the lower bound, Hamiltonicity, any fixed graph family, or numerical ranks. No claim of proof or novelty is made at nomination. All sizes, all real weights and all graphs in the class are retained. The equivalent annihilator assertion is that an edge weighting constant on Hamiltonian cycles must have the form w_uv=a_u+a_v for vertex potentials a.

Screening rejection: a proposed exponential counting target was discarded BEFORE target selection, because even prisms have only linearly many Hamiltonian cycles. A fresh primary working note (Austin B. Gibbons, THEORY.md, dated 2026-07-21) already proposes the lower bound and equality classification, without a proof; its exact scope and obstructions must be read. The affine-hull target is stronger. This is evidence of a real gap, not a novelty certificate. No major named unsolved theorem is being described as an elementary consequence of pinned Hamiltonicity.
