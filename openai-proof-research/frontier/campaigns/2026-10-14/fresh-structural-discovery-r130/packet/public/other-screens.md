# Other screens and exclusions

These families were checked against actual source text and project history. Each exclusion concerns operative overlap or a missing closable step. None is an impossibility claim.

## Family choice

I ranked combinatorial families 096–250 by the history counts in arithmetic r95's `work/family-history-counts.txt`, then checked candidates by content search. Low-count families do not have to be unexplored. The counts miss hits under other names (170 and 171 both had real history). Families on the r130 CLOSED list (175, 099, 089, 183, 156, 165 and the r128-stopped families) and the active analytic and arithmetic work were not touched.

## Excluded by history after source reading

- **171, hypercube Ramsey.** I read the source introduction and `02-reduction-and-notation.tex` in full. The bipartite cross-edge form is built into `lem:counterexample-sequence`. Subgraphs of cubes with bounded loss and Cartesian powers of spanning cube subgraphs are explicit source consequences. For grids such as P_3^(box k), embedding in a cube costs (4/3)^k. That is the classical subgraph-embedding obstruction for grids, so a new host argument would be needed. Content search then found structural r114 (`screen-171-247.md`, read in full). It records that structural r32 (`screen-171.md`) and r48 (`mechanism-check.md`) already treat multicolour cubes, P_3 Cartesian powers and the forced Q_(2d) containment cost. I did not re-read the r32 and r48 bodies themselves. Proposal notes in `proposals/combinatorics.md` reject the immediate cube-subgraph consequences as too thin. I added no new mechanism.
- **160, van der Waerden lower bounds.** I read the source introduction and `02-setup.tex` in full. The bound k^(c k floor(log2 r)) is already r^(c' k log k) uniformly in r, and the cyclic version is the source's own construction. Multidimensional versions follow by projection. Structural r81 (`working.md`) read the whole source and the Fox–Hunter robustness lemmas, and left its fixed-proportion target open. Not reopened.
- **164, Hindman sums and products.** I read the source introduction in full. Any ring containing N inherits the theorem by restriction, so the only non-restriction extensions are rings of positive characteristic or mixed read-once expressions. Structural r35 (`mixed164.md`) keeps the mixed problem open, and arithmetic r94 excluded the family by history. A positive-characteristic version would need the whole nilsequence apparatus over function fields. That is an unbuilt central step.
- **166, higher-dimensional distinct distances.** Structural r42 froze the full pinned target and left it unresolved. Not reopened without a new common-pin mechanism, and I found none.
- **176, second Kahn–Kalai.** Structural r113 (`screen-176.md`) shows that the rainbow lift is paid by the source tree theorem together with Han–Yuan. Not reopened.
- **159, quasipolynomial Szemeredi.** Proposal notes in `proposals/combinatorics.md` reject weighted, harmonic-tail and dense-prime variants and the vdW colouring consequence as already in the companions. Freiman-isomorphic box versions are routine. No source body was read for 159 in this round.
