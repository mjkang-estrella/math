## Exact primary distinction

Gupta-Talwar's shortest-path completion cannot by itself control Euclidean proximity of realized edge interiors. Semmes's weighted path metric is a different metric from the restricted ambient Hilbert norm needed for Dvoretzky placement. Schul's beta-sum construction controls length but the cited conclusion does not give the required output ambient doubling bound in terms of input doubling. The concrete new operation is height-separated ambient realization with all-scale ball estimates, followed by ordinary tours. These are positive operation comparisons; absence of a search hit is not evidence of novelty.
