# Exact scope of the separate Lean support

The formal dependency is cdf098-formal-v1, namespace CDFLocal. Its final external manifest anchor is recorded in formal-dependency.json and rechecked by support/verify.py. The source hash, coverage, exact type/axiom report and validation logs are explicit external inputs. This manuscript task read every line of CDFLocal.lean (343 lines), coverage.json, source-contracts.json, README.txt, the entire 120-line first-build type/axiom report, and the complete read-only verifier. It inspected build/mutation receipt headings but did not independently run Lean or rederive every mutation test. The formal implementer's build/control reports and the separate formal audit remain distinct.

Thirty support theorems and nine definitions are declared. The exact type report gives the following interfaces:

- Covers A c r: each point of A is within closed distance r of a member of an actual finite center set c. CoverBound existentially quantifies c with a cardinality bound. DoublingBound is defined using positive-radius closed balls.
- refine_cover composes finite center families with N*M count. iterated_cover gives D^q centers at radius r/2^q.
- recenter_pullback returns a finite representative set **inside A**, cardinality no greater than the original center set, and radius 2Kr under the stated metric pullback inequality. doubling_transfer requires its forward/backward metric inequalities and 4K<=2^q. subset_doubling gives D² for every metric subset. bilipschitz_doubling requires surjectivity and dist(x,y)<=dist(fx,fy)<=2dist(x,y), and gives D⁴.
- packing_bound and doubling_packing apply to actual finite strictly s-separated sets. The cover diameter condition is 2t<=s, or 2R/2^q<=s. They do not construct the net sets used in the manuscript.
- dyadic_window_card assumes a **finite** index set with values in (L,U] and U<=2^qL. Specializations supply eight intermediate, four high and five cluster scales. The existence/finite nature of the relevant constructed index sets remains part of the written proof.
- segment_cover gives at most **five** centers on an actual real normed-space segment of length <=2r. It does not formalize the written two-center optimization, nor extract a segment's endpoints from intersection with an arbitrary ball. Five suffices for the retained safe bounds of eight edge centers and five spine centers.
- parent_edge_projection and edge_height_window assume a lineMap parameter in [0,1] and the indicated parent-length/height inequalities. Hilbert-product membership implications remain external.
- endpoint_group_diameter uses actual metric triangle inequalities. bottom_group_bound and fine_edge_bound are conditional on squared-distance bounds whose Hilbert identities remain external.
- cluster_separation assumes metric closeness to two roots, root separation and b<=a/2. meeting_cluster_scale assumes two points in one r-ball. Identifying those roots with a unit-vector ray remains external.
- low_count, high_count, graph_count and curve_count prove count arithmetic. graph_count assumes a suitable count N; it neither constructs a graph cover nor proves the graph is doubling. constant_identities preserves lambda=76800, D=lambda², Delta=256D^10 and Lambda=10+6Delta^8.

The complete geometric construction, tree connectivity and abstract edge sum, all-regime cover assembly, finite-witness compactness, Dvoretzky existence, shrinking union, infinite tours, compactness, Lipschitz limit and nonembedding conclusion are **not formalized**. No source theorem was added as a Lean axiom. The type/axiom report lists only standard propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound dependencies. The formal implementation is not an independent formal PASS, and this manuscript asserts none.
