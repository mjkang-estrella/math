# Why the initial global parameter idea is already paid

Let X be locally compact Hausdorff, and let P,Q as in ideal-transport.md carry nondegenerate C_0(X)-algebra structures. Write I_x and J_x for their fibre ideals. Suppose an isomorphism F:T(Q)→T(P) of the full extended trace cones is compatible with these ideals in the exact sense F(τ_{J_x})=τ_{I_x} for every x, where τ_J is zero on J_+ and infinite elsewhere.

The pinned source realizes the prescribed F by an isomorphism φ:P→Q. Pullback of τ_{J_x} therefore has kernel I_x, so φ(I_x)=J_x. Let φ_x:P/I_x→Q/J_x be the induced fibre map. For f∈C_0(X), a∈P,

    (φ(fa))_x = φ_x(f(x)a_x) = f(x)φ_x(a_x) = (fφ(a))_x.

Fibre quotients separate points of a C_0(X)-algebra, whence φ(fa)=fφ(a). Thus the realization is automatically C_0(X)-linear. No continuous-field assumption or new parameter-selection construction is needed.

This is a direct corollary of prescribed global cone-isomorphism realization and its ideal weights. It makes no assertion that an arbitrary family of fibrewise cone isomorphisms assembles to such a global F. The original compact-parameter thought was screened out before a research target was frozen.
