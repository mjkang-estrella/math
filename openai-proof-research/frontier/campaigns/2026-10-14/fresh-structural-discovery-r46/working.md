## Provisional weighted interface (unproved)
For weights w_i∈[1,C], use the fractional greedy allocation at mass τ: points below the quantile fully selected, at most one partially selected. Its endpoint distance is half the ℓ1 difference. A bend may transfer arbitrarily tiny mass, so moved-mass counting alone is invalid. A possible repair shifts τ by Θ_C(b) to convert b distinct active vertices into Θ_C(b) endpoint mass. Boundary partial vertices require an explicit error argument.

A greedy batch crossing endpoint mass a overshoots by at most C. If lost/gained masses l_i,g_j total A∈[a,a+C], candidate exchange edge weights a l_i g_j/A have total aA and bounded edge weight. Measurable token transport might replace perfect matchings while keeping common layer mass a across batches. None of the activity, limiting, or recurrence extension has been proved. In particular, arbitrary denominators cannot be removed merely by cloning rational weights: the clone count diverges.
