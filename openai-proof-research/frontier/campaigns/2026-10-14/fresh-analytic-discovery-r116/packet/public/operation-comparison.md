The claimed additional integrated deduction is rough-density switching with exact retained-law domination AND the unchanged all-scale summable budget, including boundary. The substantive challenge is compatibility: a changed pair experiment would destroy the independent graph bound, a density-dependent scale subsequence would not supply the frozen auxiliary theorem, a density smoothing conjugacy could destroy smooth dynamics, and a collar union bound could fail without a tail rate. The proof supplies these interfaces simultaneously, using the already strong paired switch.

This description does not imply that the extra work is substantial. Its components are elementary L1 approximation, total-variation contraction, atomless conditional thinning, and one extra use of the paid stationary lemma. A reviewer may determine that their compatible assembly is routine. Conversely, the fact that the components are elementary does not by itself decide the integrated contribution. No independent gate is assigned here.

## Prior and late antecedents

I52 §2D is a direct same-target antecedent: rough equivalent-volume realization was explicitly screened and left at density regularity. It was read before sustained development and before freeze. This work does not claim discovery of that question. It shows why a quantitative modulus is unnecessary for this route: the density error is used only for fixed retention, not as a summable scale cost.

After freeze, a broader construction-string search located cross-family conditional-quantile work in I33 and I35. The operative passages were read: I33 §1 and §3.3 describe midpoint domination and its obstruction for weighted Brenier cells; I35 §3 gives measurable conditional CDF/quantile maps on physical source fibers and separates their transverse-law gap. These reinforce that conditional-quantile mechanics are paid classical material. They do not give this paired switch, its boundary clearance, or its original-law budget compatibility. They are explicitly late reads; the frozen target has not been edited. I33 display was truncated after the operative §§1–3.3, so no whole-report reading is claimed. I35 §3 was visible in full; unrelated sections were not read in full.

Foreman–Weiss's introduction/equivalent-volume formulation was read prefreeze. Its actual §9 realization reduction and numerical-growth requirements were read only after freeze. That timing falls short of a claim that every potentially related primary construction had been read before freeze. The prefreeze note disclosed the pending check. The later reading did not change the target or proof, and is not silently backdated as clearance. The downloaded v2 refers its underlying realization theorem to an earlier paper, whose complete proof was not acquired/read here. Thus the comparison is limited to the actual §9 interface, not an exhaustive audit of approximation-by-conjugacy literature.
