# R156 frozen author-proposed statement

Let X be a smooth connected complex projective variety of complex dimension four, and let D be a nonempty smooth connected divisor on X. Let R = sqrt[2](X,D) denote the order-two root Deligne–Mumford stack. Its fundamental group means the topological stack fundamental group, equivalently pi1(X minus D) modulo the normal closure of the squares of all meridians of D.

There exist a smooth connected complex projective variety Y of complex dimension eight and a representable morphism f:Y -> R, with coarse composite g:Y -> X, such that:

1. f induces an isomorphism pi1(Y) -> pi1(R), up to the usual base-point conjugacy.
2. The smooth general fibre of g is a product of four smooth connected genus-one curves. In particular its cotangent bundle is trivial.
3. There is an effective Cartier divisor E on Y with g^*D = 2E. For a general point of every prime divisor of X other than D, g has a point at which it is a submersion; over a general point of D, f has a submersive local lift to the order-two root chart.
4. If R is special in the differential-line sense used in the pinned fourfold-pair source (no rank-one coherent subsheaf L of Omega_R^p with Kodaira dimension p, for any 1 <= p <= 4, with saturation and sections interpreted on root charts), then Y is Campana-special.

Y may be chosen by the four-factor diagonal-quadric construction of the pinned fourfold-pair source, with a sufficiently high base twist and a general coefficient tuple additionally satisfying the curve conditions specified in the author proof.

No uniform degree, index, polarization, canonical choice, simultaneous relative construction, finite/etale manifold uniformization, or assertion for other root orders, boundary configurations or dimensions is part of this statement. No claim of contribution, correctness, acceptance, campaign count or literature priority is assigned here.
