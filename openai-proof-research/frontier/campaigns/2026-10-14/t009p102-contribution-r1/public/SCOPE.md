## Definitions

For a prime p, an integer n >= 1 and a field F of characteristic p, put A_n = Z/p^n Z and
V_n(F) = F^times/(F^times)^(p^n), written additively. Let R_n(F) be the A_n-submodule of
V_n(F) tensor_{A_n} V_n(F) generated by [x]_n tensor [1-x]_n, for all x in F minus {0,1}.
Its quotient W_n(F) is K_2^M(F)/p^n, with the product denoted by a dot. A compatible
isomorphism Theta: V_n(K) -> V_n(L) is an A_n-linear isomorphism with
(Theta tensor Theta)(R_n(K)) = R_n(L). No valuation, rational-subfield, differential,
choice of p-basis, lift to a larger modulus, or inverse-limit datum is supplied.

## Theorem T (finite prime-power reconstruction)

For every prime p, every integer n >= 1, every pair of algebraically closed fields k,l
of characteristic p, and every pair of finitely generated field extensions K/k,L/l
with trdeg(K/k) >= 2 and trdeg(L/l) >= 2, each compatible isomorphism
Theta: V_n(K) -> V_n(L) has the form

    Theta([f]_n) = a [alpha(f)]_n    for every f in K^times,

for a unique pair consisting of a unit a in (Z/p^n Z)^times and a field isomorphism
alpha: K -> L satisfying alpha(k)=l. Conversely every such pair induces a compatible
isomorphism. Equivalently the canonical map

    Isom(K,k;L,l) -> Isom_M(V_n(K),V_n(L))/(Z/p^n Z)^times

is bijective. No additional Frobenius identification is taken. The n=1 case is the
pinned source theorem; the claimed extension is all finite n >= 2. There are no
unspecified constants, thresholds, or auxiliary regularity hypotheses.

## Proposition I (the proposed core operation)

For every prime p and every field F of characteristic p with
p^2 <= [F:F^p] < infinity, let delta: V_1(F) -> V_1(F) be any F_p-linear endomorphism.
Assume, for every x in F minus {0,1}, the linearized Steinberg identity

    delta([x]_1) dot [1-x]_1 + [x]_1 dot delta([1-x]_1) = 0 in W_1(F).

Then delta = c id for a unique c in F_p. Conversely every such scalar satisfies the identity.
No injectivity, surjectivity or finite-dimensional-image assumption is imposed on delta.
