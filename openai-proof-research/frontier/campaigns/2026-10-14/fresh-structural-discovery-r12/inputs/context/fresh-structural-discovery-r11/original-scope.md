# Frozen original target R: root closure of every Artin parabolic

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.
Frozen before sustained proof work in this target, 2026-10-16.

For EVERY finite set S (including the empty set), EVERY symmetric Coxeter
matrix M with m_ss=1 and m_st in {2,3,...} union {infinity} for s!=t,
let A_M be the group on sigma_s with the alternating Artin relation of
length m_st for every finite off-diagonal label. For EVERY X subset S,
EVERY u,g in A_M, and EVERY nonzero integer m, put P=u A_X u^{-1}.
The full target is

    R: g^m in P implies g in P.

No restriction to spherical, FC, large-type, two-dimensional, right-angled,
complete diagrams, positive words, standard-only input, or bounded exponent
is permitted. Equivalently, under stipulated existence of parabolic closure,
PC(g^m)=PC(g) for every g and every m!=0. The cases X=empty and X=S are
included. A conditional criterion, subclass theorem or obstruction does not
solve R. If proof fails, retain this entire target as UNRESOLVED.
