# Substantive mathematical checkpoints

1. Frozen R is equivalent to torsion-freeness of N_A(Q)/Q for every
parabolic Q. If g^m lies in P, Q=PC(g^m) lies in P and is normalized by g,
by equivariance of parabolic closure. Torsion-freeness gives g in Q.
The converse applies R directly to any torsion coset in N_A(Q)/Q.
This is an exact reformulation, not a proof.

2. Every proper inclusion of parabolics has infinite index under the
source inside-parabolic conclusion. For standard A_R<A_Y choose s in Y-R.
The source makes A_s intersect A_R either 1 or A_s. The latter is
excluded by the Coxeter quotient: W_R fixes V/span(alpha_r:r in R)
pointwise while s negates the nonzero alpha_s class. Thus A_s intersect
A_R=1, and all sigma_s^k give different cosets. Conjugate inside the
larger parabolic to cover every inclusion.

3. Therefore finite SETWISE stabilizers of finite sets of transported
unit framing objects would solve R: their pointwise subgroup is an
intersection of parabolics, normal and finite index. If the setwise
stabilizer were parabolic too, the infinite-index fact forces equality.
Conversely full R forces each permutation to be trivial because a power
fixes every object. So this proposed extension is equivalent to the full
target once source stabilizers/intersections are granted.

4. Attempted orbit direct sum: aggregate nonnegative generator counts do
preserve exterior invariance and folding. But null:hom and null:injection
use actual Hom for two family members with the SAME transport x. Direct
sums of distinct transports have cross Hom terms, and those need not
satisfy middle-column vanishing. This is an unpaid premise, not licensed
by additive counts. A concrete rank-one calculation follows in candidate.md.

5. With S={s}, X=empty, simple labels, the source algebra has f,s,o and
one arrow on f-s. Let P=P_f. F_s(P) is [P_s<1> -> P_f] in degrees -1,0.
F_s^2(P) has minimal model [P_s<3> --token--> P_s<1> --arrow--> P_f]
in degrees -2,-1,0. The token is nonzero by invertibility/indecomposability.
Its upper layer is P_s<3>[2], level 1. Linear Hom from Y^0(P)=P_f to
that layer has a nonzero length-one class at (cochain,internal)=(-2,4).
Thus the blanket sum-family middle-vanishing extension is false. This
pair is not an actual periodic orbit of an element fixing a power of P;
it rejects the unrestricted extension, not R or all finite-orbit arguments.

6. Torsion-freeness of A does not give unique roots. In B3=<s,t|sts=tst>,
(st)^3=(ts)^3=(sts)^2 but st!=ts, detected in S3. Full R is consistent
with this: both elements lie outside the same rank-one parabolics and
root equality of arbitrary elements is stronger than what is requested.

7. General intersection-lattice axioms alone do not suffice: in Z^2,
the family {0, 2Z x {0}, Z^2} is a finite-height intersection-closed
family, all strict inclusions have infinite index, and Z^2 is torsion
free, but (1,0) has double in 2Z x {0}. This is not a family of Artin
parabolics; it only separates the indicated abstract properties from R.
