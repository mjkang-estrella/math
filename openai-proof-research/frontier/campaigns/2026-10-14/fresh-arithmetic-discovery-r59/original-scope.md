# T019S59 — two-prime geometric section question

Status: frozen full working question, not a theorem or nomination.

For every prime p, every odd prime ell different from p, every finite extension k/Q_p, and every smooth proper geometrically connected curve X/k of genus at least two, put Sigma={p,ell}. Let Delta=pi_1(X over bar k), let Delta^Sigma be its maximal pro-Sigma quotient, and put Pi_X^Sigma=pi_1(X)/ker(Delta -> Delta^Sigma). The full target is that the point-section map

    X(k) -> {continuous sections G_k -> Pi_X^Sigma}/Delta^Sigma

is bijective. The arithmetic quotient remains the full absolute Galois group G_k. No rational point, good reduction, prescribed genus, finite constant extension, or lifting to the full fundamental group may be added as a hypothesis. Surjectivity and injectivity are both retained. A theorem only for sections already lifting to the full group is not a solution.

The source's full profinite p-adic section theorem is stipulated true. The full target above is stronger than a statement about the images of its sections. The original source and its exterior-cover companion were read before this working scope was recorded. The exploratory selection first considered solvable exterior monodromy; actual RNS primary reading showed that operation is already paid, so it is not part of the proposed contribution.

This scope is recorded after exploratory feasibility and comparison, before any unified proof assembly. The current state still has genuine gaps in prime-controlled collar lifting and localization. No successful proof assembly exists at this timestamp.
