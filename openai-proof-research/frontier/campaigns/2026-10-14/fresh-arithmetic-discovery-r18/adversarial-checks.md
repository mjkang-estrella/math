# Adversarial mathematical checks

These are author checks of the proof's logical interfaces. They are not independent review or finite computation masquerading as a geometric proof.

1. **Nonzero virtual classes need not be effective.** The proof only uses that a nonzero cohomological action requires a nonzero cycle class, that a refined-intersection representative has support in the proper incidence composition, and that a nonzero cycle of dimension m requires support dimension at least m. Signs and cancellation cause no inference of positivity.
2. **A single nonempty chain is insufficient.** The source's equality incidence lemma alone supplies only that. The candidate retains the whole algebraic correspondence support and its pure dimension n+Σ(d−1).
3. **Do not choose one family from one point of a virtual cycle.** All finitely many full components in each selected degree are included. They retain the selected numerical classes and degree bound. No subfamily is substituted for a full component in an unsplitness theorem.
4. **The support is closed before applying the quotient.** It is the proper image of the ordered incidence fiber product. It is not the closure of pairs known to be related only on a generic open subset.
5. **One bad endpoint is not allowed in the support decomposition.** Saturation means a pair related to a point of X^0 has both endpoints in its entire class inside X^0. Thus the remaining support lies in E×E. If only E×X were available, the 2n−1 argument would fail. This is the main exact classical contract to audit.
6. **Equidimensionality is obtained without losing saturation.** Shrink the base and replace X^0 by its inverse image. The new fibers remain entire equivalence classes; X^0 stays dense and open. The proof does not use a dimension formula for arbitrary jumping fibers.
7. **The quotient is never extended across its boundary.** For a curve quotient, a proper fiber is already a closed compact codimension-one subvariety of X. Any prime component is Cartier because X is smooth. Choose a fiber avoiding finitely many representative curves, not all curves at once, and use numerical constancy thereafter.
8. **Spanning curve classes alone do not give chain connectedness.** On the blowup of P^2 in six general points, the six exceptional curves and the strict transform of a line through the first two points are seven independent isolated unsplit classes. Their finite union misses general points. This illustrates the need for the large correspondence-support hypothesis. It is not a new counterexample or a premise of the proof.
9. **The degree cutoff is strict.** Degrees up to i+1 are below 2i even for i=2. At degree 2i, a stable map may split into two minimal components. For example, the diagonal class on P^1×P^1 has anticanonical degree four and can degenerate to the two ruling lines. No argument at equality d=2i is used.
10. **Dominating is stronger than horizontal.** The proof of Section 5 excludes the finitely many proper base images of horizontal families before choosing a fiber. It uses irreducibility of each full family to ensure that existence of two points with distinct base images is a generic horizontal condition. If every curve meeting the chosen compact fiber stays in it, arbitrary chains stay in it, contradicting full chain connectedness.
11. **The minimal horizontal families may differ from the correspondence families.** Only a degree upper bound is transferred through the minimization property. Unsplitness is re-established for each newly selected full component. Fujita's Construction 3.5 and Corollary 6.4 are then used at their exact hypotheses.
12. **Pseudoindex need not be preserved by the contraction.** The base has pseudoindex at least i. The induction is at the base's actual invariants, and the possible increase for r=2 is handled by Fujita's P^i bundle classification. No constant-pseudoindex induction is smuggled in.
13. **Small quadrics and products.** Q^2 has Picard number two, Q^1 is P^1, and P^i alone has pseudoindex i+1. The fixed list explicitly excludes mismatched parameter readings; zero and negative product exponents are distinguished.
14. **No source inconsistency is used.** The independent-correspondence proposition and its lower spectral theorem are stipulated. The source's internal trace identity is not audited or improved here, and no statement from the root's reserved family122 route is invoked.
15. **No false formal-completion claim.** The feasible Lean support in formal-interface.md covers finite degree, set-relation, and linear algebra reasoning. Algebraic geometry, virtual classes, quotient existence, Mori contractions, and the full classification remain prose and external mathematical inputs.

The most demanding independent review questions are (i) the full-class/saturation reading of P1, (ii) the actual refined-intersection support representative, and (iii) the generic-horizontal and dominating-family step. These are proved explicitly in proof.md rather than replaced by a conjectural bridge.
