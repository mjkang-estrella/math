# Feasible scoped formal support; not performed

No Lean declarations or formal PASS are claimed in r61. A useful subsequent limited artifact could formalize (1) characteristic-two alternation vanishing on more than dim(E) vectors in a finite-dimensional subspace of an associative algebra; (2) the h_{j+1}=h_j² prefix-sum bound and domination of the two-resource recurrence; (3) injectivity of polynomial localization and extraction of powers from total-length chunk sums. Those would support exact interfaces only.

They would NOT formalize the finite-rule compiler, Kleene program, ownership/forged-state rollback, token grammar, palette orbit partition, or ordinary finite presentation. Full T247G is therefore not reducible to a handful of arithmetic checks, and a scoped formal artifact must not be advertised as the complete theorem.
