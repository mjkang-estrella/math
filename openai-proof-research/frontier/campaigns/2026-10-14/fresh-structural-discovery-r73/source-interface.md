# Exact source interface and finite operations

For nonzero unital separable complex A and every free ultrafilter omega, absence of characters on F_omega(A)=A_omega intersect A' is equivalent to A being Z-stable. Nuclearity, simplicity and comparison are not extra hypotheses. The finite tensor theorem applies to every nonzero unital complex D without characters, even nonseparable D: some m=m(D) admits a unital *-homomorphism I(2,3) to the maximal m-fold tensor power. It neither asserts an injective homomorphism nor finite-power Z-stability. Its image is characterless and subhomogeneous. For separable D the infinite minimal identical tensor power is Z-stable and contains Z unitally.

The finite proof's operative steps were read. A finite real selfadjoint witness space V detects noncommutativity in every representation. An incidence/Sard argument chooses r labels after n tensor legs when 2^n>2mn+r-1, where m=dim V. Factor representations and algebraic tensor independence ensure the labels retain independence. Two labelled sums s,t have nonzero commutator in every irreducible representation. The selfadjoint double commutator z=[s,[s,t]] has both positive and negative parts full by the bounded convex/concave orbit argument. Fullness bridges give c_i with c_i c_j=0; a further labelled sum yields a full square-zero element. Polar decomposition gives a cone over M2 with a full endpoint image.

A full cut c admits [1]<=N[c]. Tensoring L times with 2^L>=N gives N[e]<=[1]<=N^L[e]. The cube/simplex construction supplies exactly equivalent positive elements and defect X, with k[X]<=N[Y] for k=N^L. Auxiliary e eliminates the multiplicity by [X tensor 1]<=k[X tensor e]<=N[Y tensor e]<=[Y tensor 1], without assuming Cuntz cancellation. The dimension-drop criterion then gives I(2,3). The application uses separable commutator witnesses and commuting copies in the central sequence algebra; the infinite-power corollary passes from maximal to minimal tensor product and invokes the classical Dadarlat–Toms subhomogeneous criterion.

These steps are paid by the source. Their constants depend on D. Replacing identical factors by varying witness dimensions would require a new uniform mechanism (even the incidence inequality would involve their sum), and the full-cut constant N also varies. Failure of these estimates is not itself a counterexample. No such new uniform mechanism was proved here.
