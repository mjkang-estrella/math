# Screened construction and decisive primary comparison

This is an author sketch retained to explain rejection, not a new theorem or a completed independent proof audit.

HRW Example 4.8 constructs rank-two homogeneous factors B_j=End(1 direct-sum a Hopf-line tensor product) over products of two-spheres. The multiplicities are m_1=m_2=1 and m_j=2^(j-2) thereafter. Projection inequalities 4[f]<=3[g] coexist with f not below g: f has a trivial line, while the Euler class of g remains nonzero. Expansion into line summands and an assignment of distinct sphere coordinates to each summand establish the Euler obstruction. The untwisted infinite product is not Z-stable despite its CAR fibers.

DHTW, The Jiang-Su algebra does not always embed, converts the obstruction to absence of unital I(3,4) maps into sufficiently late finite tail products. Any finite subproduct embeds unitally in a larger prefix. Semiprojectivity of I(3,4) then excludes maps into the infinite product or an inductive limit of the same finite blocks. Since Z contains I(3,4) unitally, these limits cannot contain Z unitally. DHTW also provides the simplicity operation: coordinate isometries homotopic to the identity preserve the bundles up to unitary identification, and tail-dense orbits make a diagonal map with an identity branch and a twisted branch eventually full.

Exploratory stronger-factor sketch: partition the tail indices into disjoint infinite sets I_i (for instance via the 2-adic valuation). On the successive products D_(i,n) of factors in I_i, use the DHTW dense-isometry twisting maps. The expected limits A_i are unital separable nuclear simple infinite-dimensional AH algebras. Every finite tensor rectangle of their stages is a product of distinct original B_j and embeds unitally in an original tail prefix. Hence it admits no unital I(3,4) map. Cofinal finite tensor rectangles present the infinite tensor product of the A_i; semiprojectivity would exclude an I(3,4) map there too. Each A_i has no characters, and each contains its initial characterless rank-two homogeneous stage unitally. Thus Dadarlat–Toms Theorem 1.1 already makes each A_i's identical infinite tensor power Z-stable. The source299 result is not essential to that last assertion. The sketch's simplicity adaptation was not independently reviewed because the following stronger primary antecedent makes nomination inappropriate.

Robert–Rørdam, Divisibility properties for C*-algebras, Theorem 7.17 proves for every integer N>2 the existence of unital simple infinite-dimensional AH algebras A_n with w-Div_2(infinite tensor product A_n)>N. In particular the product is not Z-stable and has no unital Z embedding. Its proof, not just its headline, was checked: Lemma 7.7 supplies simple AH limits with bundle projections q_n=1 direct-sum p_J1 direct-sum 2p_J2 direct-sum ... direct-sum 2^(n-1)p_Jn. Evaluation branches at dense points ensure simplicity. Lemma 7.16 chooses the disjoint coordinate supports J_j^(k) in an order by k+j, with sizes dominating the weighted counts of tensor summands assigned to the largest support. Distinct-coordinate matching keeps the relevant Euler classes nonzero in every finite tensor rectangle. Theorem 7.17 passes the weak-divisibility obstruction through the two inductive limits using Proposition 3.6. This pays the desired stronger construction and its operative topological mechanism. No claim is made to have rechecked the full proof of Proposition 3.6 or every cited comparison lemma.

Gardella–Thiel Theorem 4.1 and Example 4.4 also clarify a different issue: characterless unital algebras have finite algebraic commutator witnesses, but no universal uniform witness budget is available for arbitrary sequences. RR Corollary 8.6 produces simple infinite-dimensional factors whose product and free-ultrafilter ultraproducts have characters. These are not by themselves tensor-product counterexamples; RR Theorem 7.17 is the actual tensor statement.

A quantitative bundle-growth variation was not frozen. Trivial homogeneous bundles M_r(C(X)) already contain matrix units regardless of base dimension, and their tensor tails can supply UHF absorption. Thus a dimension/rank-only necessary criterion over arbitrary bundles would be false. A meaningful topological threshold would need additional hypotheses and a genuinely unpaid operation beyond the HRW/DHTW/RR Euler constructions. None was found in this bounded screen.
