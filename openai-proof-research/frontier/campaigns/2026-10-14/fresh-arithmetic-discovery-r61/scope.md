# T031C61: unchanged full working target

Frozen after exploratory source and primary-interface reading, before assembling the proof attempts. UTC: 2026-10-18T18:41:45.509103+00:00.

For every number field F, choose its maximal prosolvable extension F^sol in an algebraic closure and put P_F=Gal(F^sol/F). Every continuous injective endomorphism alpha:P_F→P_F is surjective.

No openness, normality, open normalizer, cyclotomic compatibility, decomposition preservation, or finite-generation condition is assumed. A proof only for Q, for normal images, or for images with open normalizer does not settle this target. Such supporting statements will not be nominated as replacements. The target is a question here, not a proved theorem.

Exploration before this freeze identified two approaches: reconstruct enough local prime coverage to restore the source's character method, or prove the image has open normalizer and use intrinsic automorphism rigidity. The latter approach has a routine supporting reduction; the former faces a quantitative character-dimension obstruction. Neither has settled the target.
