## The additional operation submitted for review

Uniform death in each fiber does not itself kill homology in the parameter total space. The proof handles that difference directly. A common fine open-star cover is used at a finite sequence of cut levels. Local inclusions factor through a reference fiber. Below all relevant essential births they kill absolute homology; above all such births they kill pair homology. The Cech chain resolution has p+1 columns, so p+1 maps which vanish on its E1 diagonal kill the map on total homology. This is classical homological machinery, proved in the packet; no novelty is claimed for the spectral sequence or Mayer--Vietoris themselves.

The proposed additional content is their integrated, uniform application to this geometric family filtration, tracking the transition interval sharply enough to obtain the coefficient exactly lambda_+-lambda_- in every degree. The constants in FROZEN.md include the full cover-filtration loss and the constants-relative projection. The theorem does not require differentiability in the parameter, nondegeneracy, regular energy cuts or a finite sublevel count. All of those points are part of the frozen scope.

The smooth two-ended scaling family is carried through Mayer--Vietoris at open critical cuts and attains this coefficient. It rules out the tempting stronger conclusion of degree-independent death for arbitrary compact families, even though each round fiber has injective ordinary sublevel homology. A continuously normalized, nonhomothetic conformal family demonstrates that the constant-frequency regime asserted by the upper bound is not empty or forced to be a homothety family. This checks actual freedom in the joint hypotheses, rather than treating a vacuous regime as evidence.
