## Common smoothing, in both actual versions

Fine relative PL approximation uses Hamilton's relative three-dimensional theorem on transported PL structures, retaining a closed neighborhood where the map is already PL. Euclidean open domains have empty manifold boundary; this does not assume regularity of their boundary as subsets of R^3. Edwards-Kirby supplies supported small ambient extensions near fixed compact source configurations, with no derivative estimate. The insertion map G^{-1}h is controlled in value by the original locally bi-Lipschitz H, not by an unproved inverse bound for intermediate G.

Fine target control and positive output Jacobian give a proper degree-one map: the straight homotopy remains inside Lambda if its displacement is below half the distance of H(x) from Lambda's complement. Boundedness of Lambda makes the inverse images of compact sets stay in compact subsets of Omega. Positive local degrees then give exactly one preimage. This supplies the exact image for each approximant, not only for a limit.

Homogeneity near vertices reduces to a smooth sphere diffeomorphism. A fixed sphere isotopy, positive radial interpolation and a slow logarithmic transition give a nonsingular linear center. Support radii are then reduced to pay summable errors. These local bounds also explain why fixed PL geometry can carry inverse budgets.

## High exponents: p>2

Preparation first obtains a.e. differentiability through sphere estimates and openness. At p>=3 it obtains N via the sphere oscillation bound and covers. At 2<p<3, exceptional target assignments with null source preimage remain and are treated separately. The transported decomposability bundle contains the image of Df. S_0 collapses to graph sheets; S_kappa has no uniform inverse bound as kappa tends to zero. Paired wells create exact affine supports at the endpoints of small normal segments. Their random endpoint distribution has an actual three-dimensional density proportional to the spherical radius squared. The two-stage carrier installs exact target cores before later residual budgets. Regular implantation charges the affine model energy to the original forward energy; exceptional null-source assignments get marked cubes.
