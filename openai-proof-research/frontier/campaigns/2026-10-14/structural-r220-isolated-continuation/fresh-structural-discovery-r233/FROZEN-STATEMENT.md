# Frozen statement — r233

For every integer n >= 2, every real K, every complete, separable, geodesic noncollapsed RCD(K,n) metric measure space (X,d,H_d^n), with full-support reference measure exactly the normalized n-dimensional Hausdorff measure H_d^n finite on bounded sets, every point p in X for which ALL pointed Gromov-Hausdorff tangents are pointed-isometric to (R^n,|.|,0), every eta > 0 and every R > 0, there exist r in (0,R), an open set V in R^n containing 0, and a homeomorphism F:B_r^d(p) -> V such that F(p)=0 and

    (1+eta)^(-1) d(x,z) <= |F(x)-F(z)| <= (1+eta) d(x,z)

for every x,z in B_r^d(p).

Here RCD means the full CD(K,n) condition and quadratic Cheeger energy, as in Source357. Hausdorff measure is normalized to agree with Lebesgue measure on Euclidean n-space. Every ball and both distance comparisons use the original ambient distance d restricted to the stated domain. The radius may depend on X,p,K,n,eta,R. No uniform radius, convergence rate, chart compatibility, harmonicity, Laplacian bound, differentiable coordinate structure, regularity of the pulled-back metric, or openness of the exact regular locus is asserted.
