# Operational contribution comparison

This is a full author proof claim for the inherited T024W52 target, subject to independent proof assessment. Author recommendation: TECHNICAL contribution; no nomination or count increment. A full proof does not itself establish campaign substantiality.

Quantitative prime number theory already supplies the prime counts in intervals of relative length comparable to 1/log Y whenever the prime scale is a fixed power of Y (and also at the smaller source collision scale used here). Ford Theorem 4 already supplies a short-interval upper order bound for distinct totients. Ford's coefficient-uniform upper sieve survives translation of the interval; its determinant and singular factors are unchanged. The rough-number sieve similarly uses a standard remainder bound. The split at p=sqrt Y is the classical hyperbola decomposition: the smaller factor is summed against the existing local totient order bound, and the larger factor against the translated bad-prime sieve. Ford's reciprocal suffix sums, simplex volumes, coordinate-tail bounds and terminal geometric sums are retained.

What this packet supplies beyond the literal statements of those references is an explicit uniform local exceptional-preimage estimate, valid before choosing a good preimage, and the consequent local discarded-witness estimate. These are necessary interface statements: applying a global L1 error to a short window would not prove them. Their proofs, however, presently use the classical operations just listed. No new sieve estimate, cancellation bound, structural construction, or stronger geometric inequality has been identified. The enlarged collection of proof modules is not evidence of novelty. Independent contribution assessment may judge the assembled localization bridge differently, but the author does not claim a demonstrated substantial new mechanism or new-to-literature theorem.
