# Bounded shortlist and selection

2026-10-14. Full original campaign count remains 2/10; this assignment makes no acceptance decision.

The catalogue's 372 family headings were screened. Exact theorem statements, rather than abstracts alone, were then read for the following three possibilities. `source-manifest.json` authenticates the retained sources against the pinned preprint tree. This is the complete shortlist, not three proposed findings.

1. **Uniform permanence under intermittent reactions.** Fix any finite weakly reversible reaction network, a positive stoichiometric class P with compact closure, and K,T,α>0. For measurable rates 0≤k_e≤K and ∫ₛ^{s+T}k_e≥α on every sliding window and every edge, ask for one eventual compact subset of P, uniformly over all such rate paths and with entry time uniform on compact sets of initial states. Source149, *Uniform Permanence…*, `thm:main`, supplies autonomous fixed-positive-rate classwise permanence even for unbounded classes. Its actual finite affine-label operation already handles all measurable rates in a fixed positive interval. The source introduction and the existing I138/I141 reports were inspected. **Rejected as a fresh target:** the arbitrary intermittent bounded-class problem is already the exact old P138 frontier; bounded-positive time variation is already paid. A reaction-diffusion transfer was not silently substituted.

2. **Positive regularization of a nonliftable hyperbolicity cone.** For the fixed source095 Hankel data, set Q_δ(y)=UᵀG(y)gG(y)U+δ‖y‖²I and use the source determinant construction K_δ. Ask whether some δ₀>0 makes every 0<δ<δ₀ nonliftable by every finite affine SDP. Source095 `thm:main` gives nonliftability only at its original Q; its actual introduction/companion introductions and I198/I200 records were inspected. **Rejected as a fresh target:** this is already I200, with an unpaid positive-definite patch bridge. Merely making a cone strictly hyperbolic or globally smooth is not a substitute: the source explicitly records positive-lift theorems under those hypotheses. No claim of stability of arbitrary nonliftability under smoothing is made.

3. **Entropy rigidity for the full Anosov Randers class.** For every integer n≥3, every closed connected smooth Riemannian (Mⁿ,g) of strictly negative sectional curvature, and every smooth 1-form β with sup_M|β|_g<1, let F(v)=|v|_g+β(v). Assume the F-unit-speed geodesic flow Φ is Anosov, and let ν_F be its normalized canonical contact-volume probability. The proposed full statement is

   h_{ν_F}(Φ₁)=h_top(Φ₁) iff ∇R_g=0 and β=df for a globally defined smooth f.

   No dβ=0, small C² norm, conformal-class restriction, negative flag curvature, or prior smoothness of the Anosov distributions is imposed. Source339 `thm:main` supplies exactly the β=0 Riemannian statement, including every rank-one symmetric type. The full source main file and dynamical preliminaries were read, followed by the exact algebraic and geometric passages that obstruct an automatic extension. **Selected.** The nonclosed magnetic perturbation is the missing bridge, not another parameter-uniform version of R023/R024 or a paid equality/stability mechanism. The closed-β argument is only a benchmark and is not nominated.

Why selection 3 is credible but not proved: the same contact/entropy formalism produces a weighted unstable-Jacobian cohomology identity, and the source's proof identifies where it must become geometric rigidity. However, the source uses a genuine reversal symmetry to obtain both dynamical signs in an intrinsic formal plane. For dβ≠0, reversal changes the magnetic field. Establishing the corresponding rigidity would be a substantial bridge; neither a source citation nor a finite check pays it. The final report preserves the full statement above and records the outcome honestly.
