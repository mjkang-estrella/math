# Possible scoped formal support after both independent initial gates

No formal implementation or formal acceptance is claimed in this packet.
The full theorem includes complex representation theory, stipulated source
maps, LR/Pieri, Kirillov's formula and exact finite bases. A small formal
lemma must never be represented as formalizing those inputs or the full
T205M theorem.

Two meaningful feasible interfaces are:

1. **Unique-rank ideal cross-comparability.** For a finite poset P and its
   unique order ideal A of cardinality r, prove every a∈A and b∉A satisfy
   a≤b. A direct proof swaps a chosen incomparable pair in a linear extension,
   or uses ideal augmentation. With the monotone bottom-four column shape,
   formalize the rank-cut contradiction and the separated-chain count.
   Coverage would be the finite-poset combinatorial core, not source-module
   multiplicities or the whole high-capacity theorem unless the partition
   encoding and exceptional cases are included explicitly.
2. **Vacancy shifts for one-cell perturbations.** For finite nonincreasing
   integer sequences q and q'=q+e_u−e_v, u<v, show prefix differences are1
   on[u,v) and0 elsewhere. Deduce exact first/second vacancy changes −2,+1,
   preserving other vacancies, plus nonnegative admissibility under the
   stated deficit bounds. Add the elementary product lower bound
   ∏(1+a_i)≥1+∑a_i and endpoint quadratic inequalities if desired.
   Coverage would justify exact algebra in the configuration estimate;
   it would not prove Kirillov's representation formula or the full case split.
