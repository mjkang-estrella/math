## Source operation actually available

The complete wrapper, bibliography and seven included sections were read before these deductions. The source is not just a specialization assertion. It constructs a divisorial cocycle and proves its vanishing on fourfold Mori spaces over points, then forces a nonzero value under a rationality assumption.

For a degree d and any subcollection S of Picard-number-one K3 surfaces with trivial automorphism group, pre:test assigns u(K)=1 when the MRC base of the field K is birational to a member of S, and zero otherwise. On finite etale sets, it sums once per field orbit, not once per geometric point. Thus rational representations of permutation sets cannot replace actual field orbits. A nontrivial automorphism of an extension excludes its being a tested K3 function field. Projective bundles have the same test value as their base. The cocycle c_u counts acquired minus lost divisorial valuations; on smooth blowup factorizations it is additions minus removals of tested centers.

The operator theorem gw:blowup proves, for a smooth surface center Z in a rationally connected fourfold M,

    T_BlZ(M) = T_M orthogonal-sum T_Z(-1)  over Z,
    G_BlZ(M) = G_M x Q id_(T_Z).

Both factors act independently, including when their Hodge structures happen to be isomorphic. Point and curve centers add no T. The integral assertion comes from the classical blowup embeddings and intersection with the integral lattice. The operator assertion uses connected genus-zero invariants with two variable transcendental inputs, diagonal ordinary inputs, and descendants. Its positive projected-degree vanishing, relative reconstruction, exceptional-line projector and recovery of the old algebra were read, not replaced with a general quantum-cohomology slogan.

The whole-block lemma starts at P4. Primitive central idempotents in Q^m force a contraction to remove an entire existing block. Equality of rational projector images identifies saturated integral lattices, not merely rational Hodge structures. A rank-21 surface block with geometric genus one is birational to a Picard-one K3: the minimal surface identity b2=22-8q-K^2 together with rho>=1 forces q=K^2=0 and rho=1 in the source classification. For degree greater than two and Hodge endomorphism field Q, discriminant gluing excludes the minus action of an automorphism and Torelli excludes a nontrivial plus action. Thus every member of the source's full lattice-class test has trivial automorphisms.

This gives c_u(P4 -> M)=1 for the whole class of the final T_M under the rank-21, h31=1, End_Hdg=Q hypotheses (gw:net-center). It does not attach a distinguished K3 surface, polarization embedding, or cubic A2 embedding to that block.

## The uniform vanishing is stronger than one numerical test

The source's sl:vertical, sl:surface-identity and sf:intrinsic give, on an ordinary Sarkisov link,

    c_u(link) = Delta(v+s).

For common-base dimension two, the horizontal calculation is over the actual field k=C(Q). Two-ray surface diagrams, including the rank-two conic cases, produce actual Galois-set identities. The small conic degree is r=8-K_W^2<=7. The del Pezzo weights are the five-pencil set in degree five and the three-pencil set in degree six. The exceptional conic weight for r=3 is half the field-orbit sum on the eight component choices; the complement involution accounts for integrality. The conic horizontal correction is -u(Delta), since component covers have a nontrivial involution.

For a threefold conic base with function field L and quaternion class alpha, each eligible embedded surface subfield k supplies

    sigma_k = u(Delta_k) + 1_(r_k=3) u(P_k)/2.

The proof does not silently identify abstractly isomorphic subfields. If a candidate is nonzero, every eligible embedded subfield is that same subfield. It uses the first departure in an auxiliary log Sarkisov factorization, with fixed c<1/4 on the original visible ramification support. The log generic curve degree -2+cr is negative. Residue covers have degree at most fourteen and are etale off bounded marked support; degree alone is insufficient. The original support is retained even if residue classes disappear after extending constants. For a low-dimensional ordinary endpoint base the proof first makes a suitable relative model, bounds low-discrepancy terminalization, and compares outputs, rather than assuming that an arbitrary eligible k contains the ordinary base field.

The threshold uses bounded ordinary diagrams, a subsequent choice of c, bounded auxiliary log diagrams, bounded marked covers and surface/pencil bounds. On a degree-d Picard-one K3, a pencil nL-sum m_i E_i satisfies sum m_i^2=n^2 d and 2g-2=sum m_i >= n sqrt(d). These are used with bounded genus, not with a numerical BAB constant. The finite-type and Noetherian bounds are ineffective. There is no computable value of d0 in this packet.

At Mori-space-over-point endpoints v=s=0. Thus sf:vanishing applies to every birational map between the stipulated terminal Q-factorial rationally connected fourfold endpoints, uniformly in the chosen degree-d test subcollection. This includes singleton subcollections. In particular, replacing the full lattice class by singleton tests is already paid by the source; it is not a new theorem.
