The source companion's mechanism is more specific: large cells form a complete rectangle, padding gives dense unrestricted completions, a defect repair injection bounds full graph variance, exact translations give overlap of completions, and a finite bin/offset law is propagated. Its law-tabulation proof enumerates small states, bins and offsets independently of accuracy; it does not tabulate scalar output distributions of an arbitrary bounded counter via medians. The capped manuscript only asserts an inverse-TV-cost sampler in `flows.tex`; it does not provide the claimed exact sampler as a stated companion corollary. Nevertheless, obtaining a stronger conclusion than a source's printed theorem is not sufficient evidence of a substantial contribution.

GLMP Section 6.2 already compresses confidence-dependent sampling histories into distributions of learned weight vectors, evaluates multinomial probabilities and pays for exponential tabulation with rare correction. JVV already uses median-amplified approximate counts in a rejection-based generator. Thus the present bridge may be judged a technical composition of these operations. The author does **not** assert that changing the scalar sufficient statistic from empirical counts to medians is automatically substantial, nor that the larger exact-capped headline earns credit. This specific issue should be the first contribution-review question.

The proof differs from a literal parameter substitution in the uncapped algorithm: that algorithm's rectangle, repair and dense completion contracts do not hold for arbitrary forbidden positions. The replacement is a generic counter-to-tabulated-generator construction. It would apply to other polynomial-depth binary self-reductions with exact feasibility and bounded fixed-confidence counters. That generality is a consequence of the written argument, not a separate nomination or an assertion of literature priority.

## Prior campaign operation comparison

I152's W152 and r57 concern positive rational product activities and compact normalizer encoding, with inverse-TV accuracy cost. r57 localizes the weight range by min-cost duality before source bins, then uses source transport and weighted self-reduction. Its full frozen scope explicitly disclaims exact sampling and polynomial-in-log-TV sampling. Here the law is unweighted, the target is exact, and the new interface concerns tabulation of the implemented counter-to-generator law. No weighted theorem is inherited or claimed. Restricting W152 to activity one alone would not yield this conclusion; the present proof needs Sections 4–6.

The provisional overlap approach mentioned in the freeze was abandoned as a **method**, not as a scope. Source capped transversal variance controls the trace and observables; it does not supply the full all-state gap of the uncapped repair chain. A shared-free-coordinate comparison by itself does not pay high-accuracy trace returns, random balancing, and law tabulation. No such bridge is asserted in the final proof. Instead C's already-bounded output is used as a whole. This avoids importing an unpaid full-state invariant.
