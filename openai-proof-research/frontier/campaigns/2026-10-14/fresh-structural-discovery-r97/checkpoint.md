The logarithm identity follows from the norm-convergent log(Ad u) series. This yields local logarithmic contractions, but a differentiable Banach-Lie quotient chart must not be asserted merely from this observation: complementedness of the normalizer Lie algebra in B(H)_skew is not established. No claim that every such quotient is a split principal Banach-Lie bundle is made.

The qualitative source modulus delta(epsilon) need not dominate epsilon squared; blindly iterating that construction is unjustified.
