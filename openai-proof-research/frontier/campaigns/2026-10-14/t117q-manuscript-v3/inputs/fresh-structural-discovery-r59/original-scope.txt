# Original scope: T117Q

Frozen at 2026-10-18 during author-side discovery, after actual source/primary comparisons and the matrix-selection derivation, BEFORE the full parameter-compatible proof and final manuscript assembly. This is not a claim that scope preceded exploration. The initial Cayley thought was never frozen. No independent gate or nomination is implied by this scope receipt.

There exist an absolute constant c>0, integers n_j tending to infinity, and genuine n_j-point metric spaces (V_j,d_j) of negative type such that, for EVERY complex Hilbert space H and EVERY map F:V_j -> S_1(H) satisfying

    ||F(x)-F(y)||_1 <= d_j(x,y) for all x,y in V_j,

one has

    (1/n_j^2) sum_{x,y in V_j} ||F(x)-F(y)||_1
       <= [c^-1 (log log n_j)^(9/2)/sqrt(log n_j)]
          (1/n_j^2) sum_{x,y in V_j} d_j(x,y).

Here S_1(H) consists of trace-class operators with the unnormalized trace norm; H can be finite- or infinite-dimensional, with no bound on dimension or rank in terms of n_j. Both sums are ordered uniform-pair averages including zero diagonal terms. Negative type means d_j(x,y)=||z_x-z_y||_2^2 for Hilbert vectors z_x, while d_j itself satisfies all metric axioms, including positivity for distinct points and all triangle inequalities. There is no unit-vector constraint on z_x.

Equivalently these finite negative-type metrics have first-average distortion into trace class at least c sqrt(log n_j)/(log log n_j)^(9/2), and hence at least this bi-Lipschitz distortion. The existence statement makes no polynomial-time construction or rational-coordinate claim. The claimed constants are uniform over j, the target Hilbert space and F; finitely many initial sizes may be omitted so logarithms are positive.

Intended source-essential construction: source117 multichart negative-type metrics, with more charts and adjusted auxiliary amplitude/mesh; uniform finite scalar moment matching; operator spectral chart selection using the positive Gaussian dual pairing. The ordinary L1 theorem, Gaussian Wick identities, operator monotonicity/concavity, smoothing/coarea and uniform-copy conversion are credited antecedent tools, not independently counted results.

If any part fails, this full scope remains the target. No bounded-dimension, commutative, type-2, weighted-only, or weaker-exponent replacement is authorized as a rescue or nomination.
