# Remaining full selected diagonal estimate

Keep the r65 real vertical square partition Σ_w P_w²=Id. Its preselection offdiagonal bound B0 has already been credited to source083/DPGTZK; it is not a new contribution. It reduces the dyadic long maximum to

    ||sup_k |Σ_w P_w H_a^(δ_k)P_w f|||₂ ≤ C||f||₂,       (D)

uniform in the number of widths, their ratio, and the number of scales. Estimate (D) is still unpaid. Adding (SSα) of short-cutoff-route.md would pay the hard continuum maximum by its exact inequality (1). No arbitrary-mask commutator O(a) is used.

For precision, the corresponding diagonal short primitives are B_k(s)=Σ_w P_w A_k(s)P_w. Writing R_t=V_t−Σ_w P_w V_tP_w, the paid ||R_t||≤C|t| gives A_k−B_k=∫_1^s(R_(δ_k r)−R_(−δ_k r))dr/r. Its endpoint derivative has L² norm ≤Cδ_k; Minkowski gives the difference norm ≤Cδ_k|s−r|. Consequently the integrated W^{α,2} norm of this error is ≤C_α δ_k²||f||² for every α<1, summable in k. Thus (SSα) is equivalent up to this bounded error to the diagonal fractional square estimate. This is a bounded technical reduction from B0, not a closure of (SSα).

The individual smooth-selector commutator from r65 B1 still costs Cℓ_s per packet. No global Bessel/Carleson estimate summing those errors over all tiles and widths has been derived. B2 remains the source-style, summable logarithmic disparity estimate for arbitrary finite width families and arbitrary measurable selected cutoff. One restricted weak estimate alone does not imply the desired strong L² endpoint. The parent-adopted caveat is retained explicitly.

The source's deterministic finite subcollections do not include arbitrary spatial cutoff masks.
