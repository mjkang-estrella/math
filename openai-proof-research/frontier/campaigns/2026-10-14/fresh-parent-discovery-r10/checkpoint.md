## Question under examination

For a Kadison–Kastler continuous path t↦M_t of unital von Neumann algebras on one Hilbert space, t in [0,1], can one choose a norm-continuous unitary path U_t, U_0=I, with U_t M_0 U_t*=M_t for every t? ## Why the immediate interpolation argument is incomplete

Choose fine-grid pointwise implementing corrections v_i of norm displacement at most epsilon, then interpolate by principal logarithms. Even if the conjugacy error at every grid point is zero, the elementary conjugation estimate only bounds interior error by O(epsilon) plus the path modulus. A subsequent application of a qualitative stability modulus need not give corrections summable over successive refinements. Merely taking finer grids does not replace the O(epsilon) interpolation term by an arbitrarily smaller target error. A proof needs a controlled compatible selection or a normalizer adjustment along each interval. This is a diagnosis of that construction, not a counterexample to path lifting.

Classical local-section/selection results may already supply that missing operation from known normalizer structure. Their hypotheses must be read before any substantiality claim. In particular, an algebraic factorization or pointwise distance bound is not itself a continuous choice. No conclusion is inferred from the absence of a literal search hit.
