# T025O54 — full target freeze

There exist absolute constants C>0 and b0>=3 such that, for every odd integer b>=b0 and every integer a with 1<=a<b, there exist an integer k<=C log log b and distinct odd integers 3<=n1<...<nk with

    a/b = sum_{j=1}^k 1/nj.

Fractions need not be reduced. There is no restriction on maximum denominator. Every numerator is covered; no exceptional set, selected subsequence of odd b, repetitions, or average-length substitute is allowed. Parity forces k congruent to a modulo2 after clearing the odd denominators, but no particular exact k is prescribed.
