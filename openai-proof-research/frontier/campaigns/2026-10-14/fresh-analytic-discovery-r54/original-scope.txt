T373N — all particle numbers for Coulomb/Riesz Monge nonattainment

Scope recorded 2026-10-18 09:05 UTC, after feasibility derivation and actual source/primary comparisons, before final full proof assembly. This is a post-exploration freeze, not a claim of pre-exploration selection. No independent gate or contribution verdict.

For every integer N≥3, every integer d≥2 and every real s>0, there exists a nonnegative g∈C_c^∞(R^d), with ∫g²=1, such that, for μ=g²dx and c_s(x_1,…,x_N)=Σ_(i<j)|x_i−x_j|^(−s) (infinite on collisions):
(1) the minimum E_s(μ) over all N-fold couplings with every marginal μ is finite and attained;
(2) every (N−1)-tuple of Borel maps T_2,…,T_N:R^d→R^d, each preserving μ exactly, has ∫c_s(x,T_2(x),…,T_N(x))dμ>E_s(μ), allowing an infinite left side;
(3) the infimum over those preserving map tuples equals E_s(μ), and finite-cost tuples approach it.
In particular ρ=g² and √ρ=g belong to C_c^∞. No uniform constants or common marginal across N,d,s; no positivity on all space, connected support, unique optimizer, cyclic map, quantitative cost-complexity bound, or ground-state realization is claimed. All Borel maps, including arbitrary irregular ones, are excluded as optimizers.

N=3 is source373's theorem and not new. The proposed extension is every N≥4. Earlier three-marginal cost-complexity and everywhere-positive-density targets remain untouched; this does not answer the latter by weakening positivity. The proposed mechanism is two disjoint rotated regular(N−1)-gons with a common central point, strict circle-energy discrimination, and an N-variable calibration whose local branches have every coordinate diffeomorphic to the shared central component. No spectator padding or conditional substitute is asserted.

The actual circle-energy inequality is classical (e.g. Cohn–Kumar, Theorem1.2/Table1). The local IFT, finite-pattern calibration, exact marginal obstruction and separated-cell recovery all have explicit source/primary antecedents. The contribution question is whether their all-N realization is substantial; this is NOT presumed from a missing verbatim theorem. A complete proof may still be technical under campaign rules.
