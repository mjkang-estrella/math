# Evidence separation

`packet/public/` contains original author prose, original diagnostic/verifier code, and explicitly named provenance metadata. It is a local discovery packet, not a manuscript or release, and no publication is authorized or performed by this task.

`packet/private/` contains upstream bodies, primary PDFs/text extracts, copied historical originals, search hits and navigation metadata. These remain private/local under their originals' rights. Hashes and acquisition do not transfer rights or certify semantic reading. Working notes and control fixtures are local evidence only. No source/primary/history body is presented as newly authored public content.

Only the exclusive r143 root was intentionally written. No git operation, external write, submission, upload, contact, browser UI, formalization, downstream manuscript or release work was undertaken. Parent alone integrates, reviews and counts.
