## Actual primary operations

**Iyengar–Ma–Walker, 2104.10140v2** (retained PDF and text; published ANT 16 (2022), 1213–1257). Theorem 3 / Corollary 7.2 gives exactly the all-Betti conclusion for localizations at homogeneous maximal ideals of standard graded algebras over fields of positive characteristic. Theorem 5.16 gives the general inequality conditional on a lim Ulrich point. Its proof uses two Koszul homology comparisons: one converges to beta_i and the other is bounded by binom(d,i) times the intersection pairing. Theorem 7.1 produces the top-dimensional point [R]_d/e(R) using Proj(A), finite linear projection, pushforwards from a product of projective lines, and Frobenius. This is not a proof for every complete local ring. The new argument does not infer existence of a lim Ulrich point or sequence.

Read in the retained pdftotext file: lines 1–220 (complete introduction); 265–340 (definitions and flat-base-change facts); 940–1075 (complete Theorem 5.16 proof, Corollaries 5.18/5.19); 1330–1376 (Theorem 7.1 and Corollary 7.2); 1588–1785 (Theorem 7.15 complete proof, Lemma 7.17 complete proof, and Theorem 7.1 complete proof); 1785–1855 (Conjecture 8.1 and only the initial portion of Proposition 8.3). The full paper is not claimed read. Conjecture 8.1's all-characteristic scalar bound is not our full target.

**Iyengar–Ma–Walker, 2209.03498v2**, *Lim Ulrich sequences and Boij–Söderberg cones* (Forum Math. Sigma 11 (2023), e115; retained v2 dated 2024). Read lines 1–190, 742–784, 910–946 and 1140–1184. This includes Theorem 4.17 and its full proof, Theorem 6.2's precise graded hypotheses and its complete positive-characteristic proof in §6.13. Its operation tensors by lim Ulrich graded modules, compares Betti tables over a linear Noether normalization and uses closedness in the finite topology. The uniform regularity support bound is graded. The characteristic-zero proof and other supporting lemma bodies were not read here, and no consequence of those unread bodies is used. This broader graded result prevents claiming that only standard graded cases were previously known; it does not furnish the unrestricted complete-local target.

**Iyengar–Ma–Walker, 2510.02698v1**, *Ulrich modules over local rings of dimension two* (2025). Read lines 1–160, 435–570 and 570–698. Theorem 5.2 and its complete proof give all three rank bounds for ordinary Euler characteristic in dimension two when the completion is equidimensional and Proj(gr_m R) is geometrically reduced. The proof constructs an Ulrich module free of constant generic rank, identifies its numerical class and repeats the Koszul comparison. Theorem 6.9 and its complete proof replace ordinary Euler characteristic by Dutta multiplicity and require the one-dimensional connected components of Proj(gr_m R) to be geometrically reduced. Passing to R/j(R) supplies the reduction. Their blowup/Ulrich extension characterization is a genuine predecessor, credited by the pinned source. These geometric assumptions are not imposed in AB194. The full Ulrich-bundle existence proof was not read or imported here.
