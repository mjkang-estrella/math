The historical question is structural-r31/screen-065.md's full implication V(M),V(S) => V(Bl_S M), for smooth projective complex M and smooth center S of codimension at least two, in every genus and individual integral curve class, with arbitrary descendants and cohomology. Its source-paid numerical transport is not a new result. Arithmetic-r11's projective-bundle converse is also excluded. Actual new check: retain the common pushforward homology grading. All families in the blowup row of source sew:configurations map to M: U by blowdown; D,C,Q through S; every expansion and normal-cone modification through the original target. Thus the pushforward of a stable-map class is locally constant and additive at every gluing. On each open-and-closed fixed-pushforward component, the obstruction theory, refined gluing and projection formulas are the restrictions of their ungraded versions. This is classical, not an inference that numerical equality alone refines.

The precise closest primary is Liu–Yau, arXiv:math/0408147v1, Section2 pp5–8, especially Lemma2.1: the normal-cone moduli split by the postcomposition class in the fixed X, and the Cartier-divisor/virtual-cycle formulas restrict separately. Actual PDF retrieved from https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0408147 (identifies v1 in its header). Read extraction lines1–100 and285–420, including full Lemma2.1 proof; no entire-paper reading claim. It explicitly treats the operation sought here. Its displayed assumption that Z is connected is for notation; no disconnected theorem is attributed without iterating components.

The source cap inverse presents no nonformal obstruction. Work over C[H_2(M,Z)] in the same bounded counting-degree/genus completion. Record a cap class beta by the monomial z^(push beta). In Q, counting degree zero implies projection to S constant and hence push beta=0. Its fiber matrix M0 is therefore unchanged and invertible over C((hbar)); the finite Neumann inverse sum(-B)^j M0^-1 works over the group ring, even though that ring may have zero divisors from torsion. No inversion of arbitrary group-ring elements is needed. In P, zero counting degree corrections are vertical over S, also of pushforward zero, and strictly lower contact; the same finite nilpotent inverse works. Positive counting orders use only finite convolutions and the already-paid bounded-row finiteness. No uniform bound, new cap estimate or new denominator is required. External inverse coefficients carry the compensating group-ring grading exactly as the existing divisor fugacities do; they are not acted on by the Virasoro operator. The original contact kernel has degree zero. Consequently its graded cap replacement matches degree by degree before contraction with the outside tensors. At a first idle switch the common map to M preserves the outside grading; the same grouped cancellation applies.

Class separation must not confuse rational with integral statements. For algebraic curve classes, numerical equality implies equality in rational homology: hard Lefschetz sends their rational Poincare dual difference to a rational (1,1) class, hence a rational divisor by Lefschetz(1,1); the Hodge-index form on NS_Q is nondegenerate. Thus only torsion remains, not an arbitrary topological H_2 kernel. Rational separation does NOT eliminate that torsion. Retaining the entire pushforward H_2(M,Z), rather than silently discarding torsion, avoids this issue. The integral blowup homology decomposition has kernel of p_*:H_2(Bl_S M,Z)->H_2(M,Z) freely generated by the exceptional fiber lines, one for each connected component of S. Intersections with the individual exceptional divisors are -1 on the corresponding line and zero on the others. Therefore full pushforward class plus the source's final exceptional degrees is injective, including torsion. Alternatively finite-character inversion can separate the torsion after rational degrees have been fixed; this is elementary Fourier inversion, not new geometry.

These checks supply a plausible technical completion of the historical class-label interface. They are not a fully assembled unrestricted theorem proof: coherent spanning of all insertion generators and the refined evaluation-correspondence formulation would still have to be written if such a proof were desired. No need to manufacture that packet, because the operation is formal functorial grading, with the exact normal-cone refinement already in Liu–Yau. There is no substantial nominee.
