\paragraph{Formal support and its limits.}
The accompanying Lean~4.34.1 project contains eleven supporting theorems
and seven control theorems, with mathlib pinned to revision
\begin{center}\small\texttt{d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612}.\end{center}
The implementation's fresh-source build and type/axiom receipts report only
\path{propext}, \path{Classical.choice} and \path{Quot.sound} as kernel
axioms. No upstream theorem or new conclusion is installed as an axiom.
These are implementation checks; independent formal audit is separate.

The three boundary theorems prove the integer consequences of the assumed
identity $\delta_D=\delta_H+q+b-a$, including the complete indicator case
split. Three aggregate theorems treat arbitrary finite index sets, a
distinguished subset of at least $m-1$ improved fibers, $nm$ fibers in total,
and the bound $\sum c\leq-m<0$ for $m=n^2+2$. Two fiber theorems convert
negative total finite-fiber defects into strict sums and tagged-family
cardinalities. Three closure theorems prove complement cardinality gain and
objective improvement from explicitly assumed disjointness, strict image
inequalities, retained subsets, deletion budget and maximality.

The full graph theorem is \emph{not formalized}. The graph-to-boundary
identity, minimum-counterexample argument, protected subset deficit,
strong connectivity after deletion, transitive-blowup image estimate,
compatibility and anchor exclusion, coordinate-image interpretation,
pruning existence, diagonal retention, charge-set bounds and admissibility
remain in the paper proof. The control theorems expose false weakenings;
they are not graph counterexamples or extra frontier results. The package's
\texttt{formal/coverage.json} lists every declaration and its exact assumptions.
