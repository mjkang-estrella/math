# Alternative routes and exact limitations

These observations explain the abandoned routes. They are not obstructions to T197O, which is proved by the different companion construction in proof.md.

## Symmetric first-augmentation annihilation in characteristic three

Consider only the following template. A vertex i of a graph has an elementary abelian subgroup U_i=(C_3)^(s_i). An idempotent's regular-module image is killed by u−1 for every u∈U_i; denote its normalized rank by ρ_i. Thus the image lies in the U_i-invariant subspace of the regular module and ρ_i≤1/|U_i|=3^(−s_i). Suppose each incoming (respectively outgoing) edge uses a distinct order-three line in U_i, so degree_i≤2(3^s_i−1)/2=3^s_i−1. Suppose the compression matrix is a graph fitting matrix with nonzero diagonal and zero entries off edges. On an independent vertex set its principal submatrix is diagonal and nonsingular. Its rank is therefore at least α(graph). A random vertex ordering produces an independent set consisting of vertices earliest in their closed neighborhoods, whose expected size is Σ_i 1/(degree_i+1). Consequently

rank(fitting matrix) ≥ α ≥ Σ_i 1/(degree_i+1) ≥ Σ_i 3^(−s_i) ≥ Σ_i ρ_i.

There is no strict rank saving for this precise template, even allowing varying s_i. The p=3 specialization of the September 26 symmetric exponent floor((p−1)/2)=1 annihilation is of this type when its incident edge labels are represented by distinct lines. This does not cover different local modules, asymmetric powers, extra twists, different gluing constraints, or the characteristic-free semisimple construction used in proof.md. It is not a nonexistence theorem about group-algebra counterexamples.

Extending the coefficient field alone does not increase the number of order-p directions in a fixed (C_p)^3. Replacing p by p^k in a cyclic-module formula also does not inherit three-way independence of the moment-curve subgroups: equality modulo p can produce small torsion intersections. These are failed automatic-transfer arguments, not exhaustive obstruction proofs.

## The hyperstar route

hyperstar-interface.md is preserved byte-for-byte at its original hash. Its H3 gives a complete conditional p-uniform hyperedge identity ab=1 and ac=0 from H1 and root protection H2; H2 was not proved in this packet. Its H4 shows why the literal squared-word-decay argument of the October 4 torsion-free source does not transfer: the restricted two-step matrix already has row sum

R=(p−1)(q²+q−(p−1)(p³−1))/(q+1)²>1,  q=p^4.

Equivalently the guaranteed ordinary-letter count is p^8+p³+p; the expression uses |O|−1. The bound holds even if the socket partition is changed: at most (p−1)|E| ordinary letters can share classes with extra letters. It defeats that decay certificate, not root protection itself. The separate auxiliary research was delegated by the parent and is not imported as a theorem or review in this packet.
