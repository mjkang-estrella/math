## Hostile contribution comparison

A reader with the September 23 companion already has the low-degree pairing, exact regular punctured line system, high-girth proof, local-subgroup preservation, HNN character packing, defect detection, and scalarization. Nothing in T197O's proof supplies a new geometric, probabilistic, topological, or ring-theoretic mechanism. The apparent strength of extending from one characteristic to every odd characteristic does not by itself make the bridge substantial: every nontrivial engine was written out in the companion and is field-independent. The only additional work is to establish that independence carefully and retain minus signs.

The September 26 companion separately states a counterexample for a particular odd prime, using modular annihilation and low-rank fitting matrices. Our result is broader in the coefficient-prime quantifier, but avoids rather than solves that companion's small-prime module bottleneck. A full scope gain can still be a technical corollary of another companion. Neither the all-prime phrasing nor the proof length is a credible unpaid-mechanism claim.

Those operations do not duplicate this precise prime-uniform statement, but absence from that packet is merely new-to-project evidence. No claim of new-to-literature status is made. The September 23 source proof itself is the decisive nearest comparison.
