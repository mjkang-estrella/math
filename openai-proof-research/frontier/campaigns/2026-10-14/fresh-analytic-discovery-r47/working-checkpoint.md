# T355E working checkpoint

Myeongjin Kang, original prose CC BY 4.0. Author-side, unproved full target.

The fixed-shrinker logarithmic convergence-rate route was rejected as
technical before scope freeze. Source355 has an actual power gradient
inequality and a delayed-radius finite-length recurrence; CM2015 Lemma6.22
is the operative discrete-energy/rate antecedent. A rate restatement is
not nominated.

The current target is exact in original-scope.txt. Sun–Wang1811.05387v2
is by Ao Sun and Zhichao Wang (the local filename wang2020 is merely a
short label). Its Theorem3.1 allows cylindrical limit ends to be replaced
by disks. Proposition5.7 studies such a cap at distance d_i→∞ and finds
bowl-tip scaling and the intervening cylinder scaling
sqrt(1−ρ_i²/d_i²). This is a structural asymptotic, not yet the needed
quantitative coefficient matching.

Proposed bridge, NOT proved: let p_i=(b_i,x_i) be source355's compact
observation coordinates relative to the limiting shrinker. For a changing
cylindrical end at transition distance d_i, show its source opening has
size comparable to d_i^−2, with signs negative for a cap and positive for
a widening cone, and control the normalized finite-jet equation to any
fixed algebraic order in that opening. The source finite-power certificate
could then exclude nonzero openings for exact nearby critical surfaces.

Two debts remain explicit. First, source charts over S_b remove independent
end tilts based on compact observations; local convergence alone does not
control (angular mismatch)×d_i. Second, the growing cylindrical Jacobi mode
can amplify an exponentially small core discrepancy before distance d_i.
Sun–Wang's topological classification does not by itself bound that mode
or identify the compact opening coefficient. Full matching must control
all ends together, not just the angular average on one round cylinder.

The source main theorem is about fixed-center first-time tangents. Its
stationary lower operations use a fixed finite end list, bounded geometry,
and separated links/directions. The target covers all embedded shrinkers
in the stated class; matching their hypotheses is a genuine scope check,
not permission to restrict the target to known tangents or rotational ends.
