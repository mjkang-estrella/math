T355E — end stability under local convergence of self-shrinkers.
Author-side exploratory target frozen 2026-10-18 06:46 UTC, after actual
source/localized/finite-jet and Sun–Wang primary comparison, before any
complete proof or asserted matching estimate. No nomination or priority
claim. This is not a uniform decay-rate claim.

For every finite Λ and nonnegative integer g, and every sequence Σ_i of
connected, smooth, complete, properly embedded self-shrinking surfaces
without boundary in R³ with genus at most g and entropy at most Λ,
if Σ_i converges locally smoothly with multiplicity one in the fixed
ambient frame to such a connected self-shrinker Σ, then all its ends
persist with their type: after choosing a fixed sufficiently large ball
and disjoint conical neighborhoods of the finitely many ends of Σ,
for all sufficiently large i the portions of Σ_i outside that ball
are in one-to-one correspondence with those ends, and each is an annular
end asymptotic to a regular cone respectively a round cylinder exactly
when its corresponding end of Σ is. There are no compact caps replacing
cylindrical ends and no other escaping components. If Σ is compact,
Σ_i is compact and globally diffeomorphic to Σ for sufficiently large i.

The conclusion does not prescribe equal cone links/axes, a convergence
rate, a uniform entrance radius, or a uniform neighborhood of Σ.
It does not assume rotational symmetry, a nondegenerate Jacobi operator,
fixed end number/type on the approximating sequence, or a priori weighted
convergence. No such restrictions may be added to rescue the target.

Source355 is a stipulated input only in the scope of its actual finite-end
stationary and localized estimates. Extending the stationary arguments
from a tangent section to other shrinkers, when needed, must be justified
from the actual proof hypotheses rather than assumed from the headline.
Sun–Wang already supplies fixed-genus compactness, an end-number bound,
and conical-side topological persistence. Their cylindrical alternatives
permit annuli or disks. The missing quantitative bridge is not asserted.
