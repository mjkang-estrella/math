# Analytic r47 — T355E unresolved, no nominee

Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0; code MIT.
Author-side work only. No independent verdict or count change: 13/20.

The unchanged end-stability target T355E in `original-scope.txt` remains
unproved and unrefuted. No narrower radial, fixed-end-type, weighted-
convergence, or known-tangent substitute is offered. The packet preserves
one concrete sufficient matching interface and an exact obstruction to
inferring it from local elliptic convergence alone.

The source's finite-power opening estimate would exclude a changing end
if one proved BOTH a complete graph to a radius polynomial in its transition
distance D and a polynomial lower bound on its compact opening coordinate.
It would then give a contradictory exponential upper bound on that same
coordinate. The entire graph must include all ends in the source's common
normalized chart; an isolated axial calculation is insufficient.

The actual primary comparison is essential. Sun–Wang already proves
fixed-genus compactness, uniform bounds on end number, and topological
persistence for conical limit ends. Its cylindrical alternatives explicitly
allow a disk/cap or a conical end. Its proof gives a moving-point cylinder
profile with scale sqrt(1−ρ²/D²) in the cap case, and a bowl-tip limit.
It does not identify source355's compact opening coordinates or provide
the long-region estimate required here.

The missing match cannot come just from a qualitative compact-core limit.
The cylindrical Jacobi equation has a second solution asymptotic to
2 exp(z²/4)z^−3. Normalized at z=D, it is exponentially small on every
fixed compact region, has zero coefficient of the polynomial slow mode,
and has value one at D. This is an exact linear calculation, not an actual
family of complete shrinkers and not a counterexample to T355E. The
primary's macroscopic profile excludes this pure-mode behavior for its
actual caps; a quantitative nonlinear match, with all angular and chart
modes, is still required to exploit that exclusion.

`355-opening-interface.md` proves the scalar statements and conditional
contradiction, and identifies where the source's real comparison retains
an annular term. The source's Taylor cutoff constructs a comparison graph;
it cannot be used to assert that the actual surface is graphical to the
same radius. No estimate controlling that gap was obtained.

Before the end-stability target was frozen, a fixed-shrinker logarithmic
flow-convergence-rate idea was discarded as technical. The source already
has a power gradient inequality and delayed-radius length summation;
Colding–Minicozzi2015 supplies the actual discrete-gradient/rate antecedent.
The source's nonuniform finite-jet order cannot be declared uniform in
entropy/genus by invoking compactness alone. Neither rate idea is counted.
