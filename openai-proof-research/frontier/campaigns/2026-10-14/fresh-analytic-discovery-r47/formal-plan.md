# Scoped formal plan and limits

Only the scalar reduction-of-order identity, Wronskian, elementary
asymptotic, and exponential-versus-polynomial contradiction are currently
ready for isolated formalization. None is a full T355E proof.

A complete formal target would require the actual family of complete
embedded shrinkers, all graph-chart hypotheses, Gaussian area/defect
comparison, and an independently proved joint matching theorem. Treating
the matching statement as an unexplained axiom would merely formalize a
conditional reduction. No Lean proof was written or run.

The diagnostic is exact symbolic algebra for the scalar operator with
an abstract integral variable and its stipulated derivative. It checks
the two fundamental solutions, Wronskian, leading integral asymptotic
ratio, and deliberately wrong drift/exponent variants. It does not check
nonlinear geometric matching, existence of caps, or any source truth.
