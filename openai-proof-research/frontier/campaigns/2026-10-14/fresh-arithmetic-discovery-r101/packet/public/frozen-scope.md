# DEG-r101: frozen author-proof scope

For every integer k >= 2 there exists a real c_k > 0 such that, for every h in Z[x] of degree exactly k with positive leading coefficient and with a root in Z_p^* for every prime p, there exist real C_h > 0 and integer N_h >= 1 such that for every integer N >= N_h and every A subset {1,...,N},

    (A-A) intersection {h(p): p is a positive prime} subset {0}
    implies |A| <= C_h N^(1-c_k).

The exponent is independent of every coefficient, height, content, discriminant, root choice and local bad-prime data of h. C_h and N_h may depend on all of h. No restriction to simple roots, a rational root, primitive polynomials, bounded height, or any degree subclass is allowed. Prime-intersectivity is equivalently solvability of h(x)=0 mod q by a unit for every positive integer q.
