# Rights and separation

Public files contain original r101 research prose and diagnostic code. No upstream or third-party license is changed, and no license is inferred from access. They are working materials for the user's project, not a publication or release.

`private/upstream`, `private/primary`, `private/controls`, `private/render`, and private search/retrieval records contain source, primary, history or derivative bodies under their original rights. They are retained solely as private research evidence. Metadata is stored with the corresponding private evidence rather than used as an excuse to redistribute protected bodies. Parent/user-provided controls retain their original provenance. Public references point to primary locations and identify versions.
