## Geometric operation being tested: a finite singular vertex set

Let G be a countable connected weighted undirected graph, with every edge length≥1. Only vertices are points; d is its shortest-path infimum. Suppose S is a nonempty finite vertex set containing basepoint o and the graph H=G\S is locally finite (finite degree at every vertex). The internal weighted path metric of each H component has finite bounded balls: every path of length≤t has at most floor(t) edges, and local finite branching gives finitely many vertices at that combinatorial distance. Infimum paths need not be attained for the full graph; d remains a metric since every nonconstant path costs≥1. The full graph is connected and countable, hence d is finite and globally 1-separated; it is complete. Do not confuse internal H distances with full d.

Let X=F(M), and let P:X→F(S) be a bounded projection, C=||P||. For a finite E⊂M\S, choose D≥1 and D≥max_{x∈E}d(x,S). Define t(x)=dist_H(x,E), with infinity on components not meeting E. A logarithmic cutoff φ(x)=max(0,1−η log(1+t(x)/D)) has finite support in H. Set φ=0 where t=infinity. Define a pointed Banach-valued map

  f(x)=Pδ_x+φ(x)(I−P)δ_x  (x outside S), f(a)=δ_a (a in S).

It fixes E∪S. Its range spans a finite-dimensional space: F(S) plus the finite set of δ_x where φ(x)>0. For an edge x–y outside S of length l, either both t are infinity, or both finite. Assume t(x)≤t(y). Then t(y)−t(x)≤l, φ(x)≥φ(y), and

  f(x)−f(y)=[φ(y)I+(1−φ(y))P](δ_x−δ_y)
             +(φ(x)−φ(y))(I−P)δ_x.

The first term has norm≤max(1,C)l. Since P fixes all δ_a for a∈S,
  ||(I−P)δ_x||≤(1+C)d(x,S)≤(1+C)(D+t(x)).
The logarithmic difference is≤η(t(y)−t(x))/(D+t(x)), even if clipping at zero occurs. Thus the second term is≤η(1+C)l. For an edge from x to a∈S, directly
  f(x)−δ_a=[φ(x)I+(1−φ(x))P](δ_x−δ_a),
so the bound max(1,C)l holds. Edges within S are fixed. Concatenation along all finite paths and taking the infimum gives Lip(f)≤max(1,C)+η(1+C). Linearization gives a finite-rank T with that norm fixing the prescribed finite evaluations. No vector-valued extension theorem or metric retract is assumed: f is explicit.

Choosing η=ε/(1+C) and rescaling T by max(1,C)/(max(1,C)+ε) gives operators of norm≤max(1,C) with arbitrarily small error on any given finite evaluation set. Density plus finite compact nets gives max(1,C)-BAP (details to retain later). Auerbach plus Hahn–Banach supplies a projection P with C≤dim F(S)=|S|−1; for S={o}, P=0. Consequently the prospective bound is max(1,|S|−1)-BAP, and MAP for |S|≤2. In particular one infinite-valence vertex cannot support Source330's AP/no-BAP phenomenon in a graph with edge lengths bounded below. This is a geometric obstruction, not yet a substantial nominee.
