R197 IMMUTABLE AUTHOR-PROOF NOMINEE
Exact twisted Betti profiles with bounded cell count

Assume Source321 and all its operative proofs at openai/math commit fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb. Fix the finite complex X constructed in its Section 4 by Proposition prop:plus using t1=x1^2 and t2=x2^3, retaining both added 2-cells and both 3-cells. The marked generators are x1,a1,x2,a2,s, and the distinguished added 2-cell E has attaching word x1^2.

For every integer m>=3 put n=2^(m-1) and d=2^(m-2). Define Z_m by adjoining to X two based 1-cells a,t and two 2-cells with attaching words

                   a^n x1^(-1),     t a t^(-1) a^(-5).

Then all the following hold.

(1) Z_m is a finite connected 3-dimensional CW complex with cell counts (c0,c1,c2,c3)=(1,7,8,2), infinite fundamental group, and Euler characteristic zero. Its universal cover has H_i(-;Z)=0 for every i>2; H^3(Z_m;M)=0 for every left Z[pi1(Z_m)]-module M.

(2) For every integer r>=1, among ALL complex local systems V of rank r on Z_m (no unitarity, finite-image or semisimplicity restriction),

       min_V dim_C H_2(Z_m;V) = r - d*floor(r/d).

The minimum is attained by a unitary local system with finite monodromy image. In particular the positive ranks admitting H_2=0 are precisely d,2d,3d,..., and the same ranks are precisely those admitting an acyclic local system in all degrees.

(3) If a finite connected e-sheeted cover W->Z_m admits a rank-one complex local system L with H_2(W;L)=0, then d divides e. The cyclic d-sheeted cover defined by t->1 in Z/d and x1,a1,x2,a2,s,a->0 admits such an L with finite monodromy and with L(x1)=-1 on the lift of the marked x1 loop. That loop has order exactly two in pi1(W). Consequently the least degree e of any such cover is exactly d.

(4) Z_m is not homotopy equivalent to any finite CW complex of dimension at most two.

All assertions concern ordinary cellular homology with finite-support chains and right coefficient modules (equivalently the specified local systems). The cohomology assertion uses left modules. The cell bound counts CW cells, not word length, simplices, bit complexity or minimal cells. No asphericity, finite fundamental group, classification of all representations, or classification of all finite covers is asserted. The fixed source choice is made once, independently of m and r.
