# Assembly, strongest payment, and the strict interface

These remain prefreeze calculations. In particular no model-structure corollary below is nominated as a new substantial result. Note02 closes the full homotopy comparison (BC); this note checks its strongest routine consequences before identifying the additional algebraic operation that has NOT been obtained.

## 1. Direct genuine equivariant transfer does not require a strict pushout claim

Let F be any set of subgroups of a small discrete group G, closed under conjugation. On strict G-n-categories test weak equivalences and fibrations with R_H=Ex^2N_n((- )^H), H in F. Its left adjoint is

  L_H(K)=(G/H) copower (c_n Sd^2 K).

Copower means disjoint union of categories, not Gray tensor. The underlying G-object category is locally presentable: add the small set of unary G-action operations to the source finitary essentially algebraic presentation, with the action equations. There is a common regular presentability bound for the SET of domains of L_H I and L_H J. This does not require infinite H to be finitely generated or its fixed-point functor to preserve arbitrary filtered colimits.

Every generating attachment is an invariant Dwyer-poset attachment of the type covered by note02. Its old inclusion is a full sieve; successive attachments and their transfinite composites remain inclusions, computed as unions on cells. Fixed points commute with these unions for ANY H: if an element in an earlier invariant subobject is fixed in the union, injectivity shows it was already fixed there. This is a special union assertion, not a filtered-colimit assertion for general arrows.

For an orbit horn attachment, (10) identifies its fixed nerve with the homotopy pushout of the fixed pieces. Pure orbit fixed pieces are (G/K)^H copies of U(A),U(B); the horn map between these nerves is a monic weak equivalence. Thus the attachment is a weak equivalence on every tested fixed nerve. Transfinite horn attachments remain weak equivalences by the preceding union statement and source filtered-nerve preservation. The small-object/retract argument from source section7 now gives both weak factorization systems, with no strict fixed-pushout assumption. Right properness follows from the R_H limits; left properness follows by (10) for boundary attachments, then unions and fixed-source retracts. These are exactly the source transfer steps applied to a set of adjoints (compare Stephan AppendixA's framework, not claimed separately read in full).

The unit comparison is also paid, but requires an actual check. Every simplicial G-set is built from orbit boundary cells, as Stephan2.15 proves. Subdivision preserves the monomorphic boundary maps and the cell pushouts. Since fixed points of simplicial G-sets commute with monomorphic pushouts and unions, cellular induction identifies

  (Sd^2 K)^H = Sd^2(K^H)

canonically. This is not an inference that c_n commutes with all limits. On orbit simplex and boundary cells the source poset unit is a weak equivalence. On an arbitrary successor attachment, compare spans of fixed nerves using note02(10), just as source subdivided-unit does. At limit stages use unions. Thus the canonical

  (Sd^2 K)^H -> N_n((c_n Sd^2K)^H)

is a weak equivalence for every K and H. Combine it with the unit of Sd^2 dashv Ex^2, now on K^H, to obtain the actual adjunction unit

  K^H -> Ex^2 N_n((c_n Sd^2K)^H)

as a weak equivalence. The simplicial mate/last-vertex identities are those proved in source section7. Right detection of weak equivalences then gives the Quillen equivalence with genuine simplicial G-sets. This construction includes all positive finite n and omega. It is source/classical model-category assembly, not a nominee.

## 2. The orbit-diagram comparison is paid even with only homotopy pushouts

Assume F also contains the trivial subgroup. Let O_F be the orbit category. On O_F^op-diagrams of n-categories take the projective model structure. Let E evaluate at G/e and let Phi take the fixed-point diagram. Stephan2.9 gives E dashv Phi with isomorphic counit. The right adjoint detects the weak equivalences just defined.

Stephan2.10 proves its unit is an ISOMORPHISM on projectively cofibrant diagrams when strict cellularity holds. We do not import that strict conclusion. Instead let D be built by projective cells O_F(-,G/K) copower L_n d, d a boundary. At H the attachment has (G/K)^H copies of U(A)->U(B). Evaluating at e attaches G/K copies. After applying Phi, note02(10) compares the fixed nerve of the latter actual categorical attachment with the same simplicial homotopy pushout.

Inductively the maps N_n D(G/H)->N_n((E D)^H) are weak equivalences: the comparison of the upper and lower span uses the induction hypothesis on D and identities on the orbit pieces; the source attachment comparison supplies the upper canonical nerve map, note02 supplies the lower, and one monomorphic leg suffices for simplicial gluing. Initial objects agree. At transfinite limits the maps are unions of full sieves, so fixed points and nerves have the needed continuity. Retracts preserve the conclusion. Hence the actual projective unit is an objectwise WEAK EQUIVALENCE on every projectively cofibrant D. The counit and detection criterion give the Quillen equivalence.

This closes the homotopy-level orbit comparison without solving the strict cellularity assertion. Standard projective diagram extensions or left Bousfield localizations cannot be nominated by relabeling this operation. Stephan2.12 already explains those extensions when its hypotheses apply; the corresponding source model-category transport is paid once its concrete compatibility is verified.

## 3. Dimension change is compatible, and is also paid

For 1<=m<=n<=omega let i_m^n include m-categories into n-categories and let tau_m^n be intelligent reflection. The exact identities

  R_n i_m^n=R_m,             tau_m^n L_n=L_m

hold by the oriental adjunction and source conventions. The first still holds on fixed categories, since i simply adjoins identity cells and commutes with those fixed limits. Thus tau_m^n dashv i_m^n is a Quillen adjunction for the just-described equivariant structures. The triangle of left derived functors with L_n and L_m and their Quillen equivalences shows it is a Quillen equivalence.

For l<=m<=n, both tau_l^m tau_m^n and tau_l^n are reflections into the same full subcategory. Their canonical comparison is the unique one commuting with the units, and uniqueness gives the associativity coherence for any longer chain. The right inclusions compose literally. This pays this particular coherence assertion; it does not prove that arbitrary fixed-point limits commute with reflection, that Gray tensor is a Quillen bifunctor, or an unrelated relative-realization theorem. No old full scope is replaced by these familiar consequences.

## 4. Testing the stronger STRICT fixed-pushout operation

The exact possible strengthening of (BC) would make its canonical functor an ISOMORPHISM, for the SAME arbitrary G,H,n,A,B,X,a, not merely a weak equivalence. It is not a frozen claim. The following reduction makes clear what the unproved part actually is.

Objects already agree by source pushout-objects. Pure old fixed cells agree by fullness of X in Y; pure new cells are controlled by the invariant cosieve C=B\A and note02(1). Only cells with old 0-source and new 0-target can present a problem. In the source guarded presentation, such cells are equivalence classes of defined terms in X-cells and tuples of B, modulo source boundary relations, attachment equalities, strict composition/interchange, and intelligent top-dimensional reflection. For any such term t, being a fixed CELL means

  h(t) ~ t for every h in H,

not that t is itself an invariant syntax tree. To prove surjectivity in (BC) one needs a representative of every fixed class assembled from X^H and tuples in B^H. To prove injectivity one must show that any equality between such fixed representatives has an equality derivation using the fixed presentation. These are different obligations. Merely averaging chain coefficients fails positivity/integrality and does not establish either assertion.

The strongest read low-dimensional presentation is Ara--Maltsiniotis2014 complete section7.2, with section7.1's interface and proofs. This was a LATE read after notes01--02, before this strict-interface analysis. Its mixed hom category has objects represented by triples (f2,a,f1), with f1 in X and f2 in the attaching category. Morphisms are words of pairs of 2-cells, quotiented by vertical composition/identity relations and a sliding relation R3 through OLD 2-cells. The whole universal-property proof is read, not just the formula. It supplies an actual quotient presentation, but not a theorem that fixed classes lift to fixed words or that all equality witnesses can be chosen fixed. The primary proof's R3 is precisely an interface at which applying a limit to a quotient needs justification. This is not an objection to that valid primary construction.

In dimension1 a Dwyer-poset retraction reduces a mixed morphism to one in Hom_X(x,a(r(y))) followed by the unique arrow r(y)->y (and no such mixed morphism exists when y is outside the upward closure). For fixed x,y the element r(y) is fixed, so the normal form commutes with H. This is the classical Dwyer normal-form payment. It does not establish the all-higher-dimensional statement by itself. Nor does the pure-piece target-1-path support theorem apply directly to the mixed quotient: X can have loops, arbitrary parallel cells and relations, and the attaching functor can erase old boundaries.

A direct attempt to push all mixed terms to r(y) has the following defect in dimension>=2. It uses the triangle (a,r(y),y) to replace the crossing arrow [a,y] by [r(y),y][a,r(y)]. In U_2(B) that triangle is a 2-CELL, not an equality of arrows. Thus it gives a homotopical comparison and cannot serve as a rewrite of the underlying 1-cell normal form. The source stationary-cylinder proof is designed to make exactly such homotopical replacements. It does not identify those cells. Iterating this rewrite at all dimensions would additionally have to control interchange and intelligent reflection of the resulting equalities. No convergent, H-compatible relative normalization achieving that was constructed here.

The derived orbit equivalence in section2 does NOT pay this strict assertion: a weak equivalence of fixed nerves forgets the difference between these presentations. Conversely strict (BC) is not necessary for the derived equivalence, as sections1--2 already show. No general fixed-pushout impossibility is claimed, and no counterexample to strict (BC) was found.
