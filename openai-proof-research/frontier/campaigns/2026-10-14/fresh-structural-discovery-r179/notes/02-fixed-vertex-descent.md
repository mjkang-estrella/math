# Fixed-vertex descent for arbitrary equivariant poset attachments

Prefreeze calculations; not an author theorem, grade or nominee. Let G be any small discrete group, H any subgroup, and n any positive integer or omega. All posets and categories are small in the fixed universe. Write U(P)=c_n N_ord(P), N=N_n. Fixed categories are formed cellwise in EVERY dimension. All constructions use the source's strict globular conventions.

## 1. The strongest primary support lemma pays the pure fixed pieces

Ara--Maltsiniotis2015,10.3 identifies 1-cells of O(P)=U_omega(P) with nonempty finite chains S in P; composition is concatenation (union at the common endpoint). Lemma10.5 and Proposition10.6 prove, by induction on a whole composite expression, that every higher cell with iterated 1-target S lies in O(S), and the corresponding hom category is unchanged on embedding S into P. The proof treats composition at dimensions0,1 and >=2 separately. This is much stronger than knowing only the vertices of an oriental.

Consequently, for EVERY group action on P,

  U_n(P^H) -> U_n(P)^H                                      (1)

is a canonical isomorphism, as follows.

For n=1, U_1(P)=P and this is immediate. For n>=2, 1-cells are still finite chains because intelligent reflection changes only cells in top dimension n. An H-fixed 1-cell S is a finite linearly ordered set invariant under H. Every order automorphism of a finite chain is the identity, so S is pointwise fixed. For any fixed higher cell its iterated 1-target S is therefore a chain in P^H. Before reflection, the support lemma puts the cell in O(S), which is pointwise fixed by H.

The top quotient at finite n must be checked, not ignored. For fixed parallel 1-cells S',S, intelligent reflection gives

  Hom_{tau_n O(P)}(S',S)
     = tau_{n-2} Hom_{O(P)}(S',S),                           (2)

including tau_0, the quotient of objects by the equivalence relation generated by arrows, when n=2. The primary uses exactly this identity in its proof10.9. The target-1-path support isomorphism identifies the right side with the same expression for O(S). It also identifies it with the hom in O(P^H) when S',S are chains in P^H. Thus no new top-dimensional identification between these cells is introduced by vertices outside P^H. Objects and 1-cells already inject, and these hom isomorphisms prove injectivity in every other dimension. Every fixed cell is in the image, and every image cell is fixed. This proves (1) including finite-dimensional reflection.

This argument is an application of the PRIMARY SUPPORT THEOREM, not a new invariant-chain normal-form theorem. It avoids the false general principle that invariant positive chains are generated by invariant basis elements. That principle fails for permutation modules (as Stephan's chain-complex discussion explicitly warns).

Naturality of (1) follows from its canonical comparison map: it is the unique factorization of U(P^H)->U(P) through fixed cells. It is compatible with subgroup restrictions and conjugation. In particular,

  N_ord(P^H) -> N(U(P)^H)

is the source poset unit under (1), hence a weak equivalence. Gagna's stronger explicit nerve homotopy is available, but no assumption that it is a strict stationary Gray cylinder is needed.

## 2. A simplicial replacement with explicit fibers

Use the following classical lemma, with its proof, as the gluing operation (Dugger22.9). If D:I->sSet maps to K and the category of lifts of every simplex z in K to D is contractible, then diag B(D)->K is a weak equivalence, where

  B(D)_{p,q}=coprod_{i0 -> ... -> ip} D(i0)_q.                (3)

The first horizontal face transports the simplex along i0->i1; other faces compose or omit indexing arrows; degeneracies insert identities. Vertical maps are the simplex operators of D. For fixed q, the fiber over z is exactly the nerve of its lift category. Thus each horizontal row maps by weak equivalence to the discrete set K_q. The bisimplicial diagonal lemma gives the assertion. Objectwise weak equivalences of D give weak equivalences of diag B(D), using the other direction and the same diagonal lemma. All simplicial sets are cofibrant; no projective-cofibrancy assumption on this diagram is hidden.

For a constant diagram K0, (3) is K0 times N_ord(I). In particular its augmentation to K0 is a weak equivalence if I has an initial object. We will identify an ACTUAL initial object in every simplex fiber. No compatibility of local homotopies is assumed.

## 3. Couniversality after arbitrary fixed points

First suppose A subset E is a G-invariant sieve and i:A->E has a right adjoint retraction r:E->A. It is automatically G-equivariant: r(e) is the uniquely determined greatest member of A below e, so r(ge)=g r(e).

For an arbitrary equivariant a:U(A)->X set Y=X amalg_{U(A)} U(E). The source object and sieve formulas give

  Ob(Y)=Ob(X) disjoint-union (E\A),
  X -> Y a full sieve.                                    (4)

These identifications respect G even if a identifies old objects or cells. The goal is to show X^H->Y^H is a Street weak equivalence.

Let T=(E\A)^H and form the poset I={*} disjoint-union T, with * below every element and the induced order on T. For e in T set

  E_e=A union downward-closure(e),
  Y_e=X amalg_{U(A)} U(E_e),
  D(*)=N(X^H), D(e)=N(Y_e^H).                              (5)

E_e is an H-invariant sieve of E. Source labelled attachments identify Y_e with an ACTUAL full sieve of Y, not just an equivalent object. The relation e<=f induces the literal inclusion Y_e subset Y_f. Thus D is a strict diagram of simplicial subsets of N(Y^H).

Every map D(*)->D(e) is a weak equivalence. Indeed E_e\A has greatest element e; its retraction value t=r(e) is H-fixed. Source greatest-new uses the maps

  k(x)=x for x in A, k(x)=t otherwise;
  g(x)=x for x in A, g(x)=e otherwise.

They are H-equivariant and obey id<=g and k<=g. The exact source positive prisms therefore commute with H, as their chain prescriptions apply the same equivariant f,g to every tuple. Source pushout-cylinder naturality gives H-equivariant stationary cylinders on Y_e and an equivariant retraction to X.

Restriction of these cylinders is legitimate without any tensor/fixed-point exchange. Compose Y_e^H tensor D1 -> Y_e tensor D1 with the equivariant cylinder. Since H acts trivially on the first input and on D1, the composite factors uniquely through Y_e^H by the equalizer property. The endpoint and strict stationarity equalities restrict. Source Gray-nerve conversion gives the required homotopy zigzag on fixed nerves.

Now identify all simplex fibers of D->N(Y^H). If z is an m-simplex whose last vertex is in X^H, the full sieve property puts the WHOLE simplex in X^H. It belongs to every D(i), and the lift category is I, with initial object *. If its last vertex is a new object e, then e lies in T; the simplex lies in D(d) precisely when e<=d. Necessity follows from the object formula. Sufficiency follows because Y_d^H is a full sieve and every oriental vertex has an arrow to its last vertex. The lift category is the upper set {d in T:e<=d}, with initial object e. This includes m=0 and all degenerate simplices.

All fibers are contractible. The lemma of section2 gives

  hocolim_I D -> N(Y^H) a weak equivalence.                 (6)

The canonical transformation const N(X^H)->D is objectwise a weak equivalence by the local contractions. Since I has initial *,

  N(X^H) <- hocolim_I const N(X^H) -> hocolim_I D
           -> N(Y^H)                                      (7)

has weak-equivalence arrows. The composite triangle with the augmentation of the constant diagram is the ACTUAL N(X^H)->N(Y^H), by its restrictions to every summand in (3). Two-out-of-three proves that actual map is a weak equivalence.

This proves the full arbitrary-poset, arbitrary-group couniversality comparison. There is no finite-template hypothesis, finite-orbit hypothesis, bound on |H|, or use of commutation of H-fixed points with a general filtered colimit. Nonfixed vertices may occur inside E_e; the H-equivariant contraction handles them. If T is empty, (4) and fullness already give Y^H=X^H, and I={*}. If X^H is empty and T nonempty, r(e) is an H-fixed old object and its image would be in X^H, a contradiction; thus the empty case is consistent.

## 4. Arbitrary equivariant Dwyer-poset attachment comparison

Let A subset B be an invariant sieve, Z its upward closure, with right adjoint r:Z->A. Put C=B\A and T=Z\A. All are invariant, and r is equivariant by uniqueness. For the arbitrary attaching functor a define Y_B and Y_Z as usual.

Source cosieve covers give ACTUAL invariant full cosieves

  Y_B=Y_Z union U(C),   Y_Z intersect U(C)=U(T)             (8)

on objects and cells in their full intersections. These identities of pieces remain identities after fixed points. The fixed full cosieves cover Y_B^H on objects. Source nerve-cover therefore gives an ordinary simplicial pushout

  N(Y_B^H)=N(Y_Z^H) amalg_{N(U(T)^H)} N(U(C)^H).             (9)

The corresponding source decomposition holds for N(U(B)^H). Every specified intersection-to-C leg is monic. Section3 proves X^H->Y_Z^H a weak equivalence, and applied with old target U(A) also proves U(A)^H->U(Z)^H a weak equivalence. The latter is monic because it is a full inclusion. Thus

  P_Z=N(X^H) amalg_{N(U(A)^H)} N(U(Z)^H)
       -> N(Y_Z^H)

is a weak equivalence by two-out-of-three. Reattaching N(U(C)^H) along the monomorphism from N(U(T)^H) and using simplicial left properness gives the canonical weak equivalence

  P_B=N(X^H) amalg_{N(U(A)^H)} N(U(B)^H)
       -> N(Y_B^H).                                       (10)

This is exactly source section6 with the new couniversality calculation inserted. No strict preservation of a categorical pushout by fixed points was used.

To compare with a genuine categorical pushout of FIXED posets, note that

  upward-closure_{B^H}(A^H)=Z^H.                            (11)

If b is fixed and belongs to Z, r(b) is fixed and r(b)<=b; the reverse inclusion is immediate. Restrict r to get the right adjoint retraction on Z^H. Source attachment comparison applied to A^H subset B^H and a^H gives

  P_B -> N(V_H) a weak equivalence,
  V_H=X^H amalg_{U(A^H)} U(B^H),                            (12)

using the isomorphisms (1) to identify its domain with P_B. The canonical comparison V_H->Y_B^H fits (10)--(12) into a commuting triangle, so it is a Street weak equivalence.

All maps in this triangle are the universal comparison maps; their naturality in a, subgroup restriction and conjugation is strict and independent of the chosen proofs of weak equivalence. This is a CLOSED homotopy comparison, not a named gap. It is also a compatible SOURCE/CLASSICAL COMPOSITION, not submitted as a substantial result.
