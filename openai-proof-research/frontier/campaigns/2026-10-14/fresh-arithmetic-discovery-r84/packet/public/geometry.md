## 035: normal abundance versus conductor descent

The actual source035 main theorem is already for NORMAL projective log-canonical threefold pairs in characteristic p>3 with nef Q-adjoint divisor of numerical dimension one. It is not limited to terminal threefolds or a zero boundary. Its preparation includes dlt/boundary operations and its cycle obstruction is stronger than the final headline. The torsion section supplies extension across nonreduced cycles and finite exceptional sets: finite local H2 produces a common thickening that kills the obstruction, and Frobenius removes the nilpotent discrepancies. The terminal companion further provides Laurent Cartier descent, restriction-compatible horizontal projectors and Picard p-divisibility with an integral degree contradiction. Those are available source operations, not missing gluing just because they involve several stages.

The direction tested was semilog-canonical threefold abundance at the same numerical dimension. Pulling the adjoint to the normalization gives the conductor in the boundary, so the main normal theorem is the first strong reduction. Even when all normalized components are semiample, global sections must satisfy conductor identifications and their residue linearizations. When components have different numerical dimensions, compatible treatment of all components is additionally required; no purity of numerical dimension on components is silently assumed.

The strongest actual primary inspected was [Posva, gluing, 2110.09387v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.09387v2), Corollary5.2.26 and §5.3. Its source/spring adjunction is real operative machinery. The threefold gluing proof uses ampleness of the log-canonical divisor to control finite automorphism groups of the relevant springs and then descends line bundles. Replacing that ample divisor by a semiample divisor of numerical dimension one does not verify the same hypothesis. Remark5.2.27 explicitly retains a B-representation issue in a surface-source/curve-center situation. Also its threefold characteristic bound >5 is narrower than the source's >3. Neither difference proves a failure of the desired statement.

[Posva, slc surface abundance, 2110.10543v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.10543v4), Claims3.8–3.10, gives a more specific compatibility model: at surface Kodaira dimension one, the horizontal conductor curves over the base have degree at most two in the relevant rational-fiber case. Isomorphisms/double covers and different-preserving involutions give finite relations using automorphisms of log-general-type curves. For a threefold the conductor is a surface; no analogous degree-two curve argument was constructed here. A base curve by itself does not establish finite conductor holonomy on the line bundle.

A precise general-line-bundle diagnostic separates the obligations. Work over k=an algebraic closure of F_p(t), with p>5, and choose lambda=t of infinite multiplicative order. Let C be a projective irreducible rational nodal curve with normalization P1 and branches 0,infinity at its node. Glue the trivial bundle on P1 by the scalar lambda to get a degree-zero line bundle L_lambda on C. For every m>0 its global sections are constants a satisfying a=lambda^m a, hence vanish. This bundle is nef and its normalization pullback is trivial.

Take a smooth projective curve B with an ample line bundle A and set X=C times B times P1. This is a projective slc threefold. The line bundle M=pr_C^*L_lambda tensor pr_B^*A is nef of numerical dimension one: its numerical class is pulled back from the positive-degree A, its square is zero, and its intersection with an ample squared class is positive. The normalization pullback of M is semiample, but Kunneth gives H0(X,M^m)=0 for all m>0. This is a direct algebraic calculation; no numerical test was used.

M IS NOT ASSERTED TO BE AN ADJOINT BUNDLE. The canonical residue identification does not allow arbitrary gluing scalars. Thus the example refutes only a proposed generic line-bundle descent shortcut. It neither refutes slc abundance nor supplies a nominated theorem. Raising lambda to p^e does not kill it, so the source's nilpotent-thickening Frobenius operation alone is not a replacement for the actual conductor linearization. A proof would need finite compatible residue holonomy, or an alternative full gluing argument. None was completed. No normal-only or characteristic-restricted repair was frozen.

## 051: singular hyperbolicity and the metric interface

Source051 proves canonical ampleness on every smooth compact Brody-hyperbolic Kahler manifold. Its `nef:disc-bound` controls the derivative of disks in a fixed positive metric k. A maximum of derivative, curvature and area terms yields a uniform curvature-potential estimate through the bounded trace tr_k R_k. This builds bounded positive canonical potentials and nefness. `prop:volume-lower-bound` then handles every positive metric h with Ric_h>=-h and gives the explicit positive h-volume bound in terms of k and its disk constant D. Negative Monge–Ampere approximation supplies positive canonical volume; nef+big and absence of rational curves give ampleness. These analytic operations and its effective pluricanonical consequence are paid. Changing only a pluricanonical coefficient would overlap source038.

The exploratory singular direction cannot simply pull this theorem to a resolution pi:Y->X. If k_0 is the pullback of a projective metric on a singular hyperbolic X, it is only semipositive on Y. Replacing it by k_epsilon=k_0+epsilon omega_Y does not preserve a finite disk-derivative constant: when an exceptional rational curve is present, compose its nonconstant parametrization with z->Rz at a regular point. The vertical derivative for each fixed epsilon>0 is unbounded as R grows. Hyperbolicity of X only bounds the composite pi circ f. Nor is tr_(k_0) R_(k_0) defined as the source's smooth positive-metric trace across the exceptional locus. This is a conditional method obstruction when such an exceptional curve exists, not a claim that every resolution has one.

A horizontal semipositive calculation may still be possible on the regular locus. It would need compactness/extension and discrepancy control compatible with the source's determinant and volume operations. No global singular Monge–Ampere argument or singular volume estimate was completed. A local regular-locus estimate was not promoted to the full target.

The stronger primary [Hu–Meng–Zhang, 1407.5694v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.5694v1), Lemma2.6, Theorem3.1 and the proof of Theorem1.4, already gives minimal-model reduction over a hyperbolic target. Its alternatives retain absolutely minimal Calabi–Yau varieties, potentially singular, finite over the target. Even if abundance is supplied separately, the smooth source051 does not eliminate that singular alternative: a resolution need not be hyperbolic. This actual reduction was tried before claiming new missing work. No contemporary global open-status inference is made from the paper's dated conjecture statements, and no singular headline was frozen.
