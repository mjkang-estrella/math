# Integrated calculation: prime-inverted towers, intermediate covers and flat coefficients

PREFREEZE. These are author calculations relative to the stipulated source and the actual primary interfaces identified privately. ## 1. Parameters and the stronger available rank reduction

Fix an integer d>=3. Put N=2d-1 and n=N-1=2d-2. Choose a sufficiently large odd prime q, fixed independently of congruence depth. Let R=F_q[t], A_m=t^m R, and

    Gamma_a(m)=ker(SL_a(R) -> SL_a(R/(t^m))),
    Lambda=Z[1/q].

Use the same cyclic root groups as the source: x_i=e_(i,i+1)(1) for 1<=i<N and x_0=e_(N,1)(t), U the upper-unitriangular preimage at t=0, and H_i generated by all x_j except x_i. Let L be the common-intersection coset complex of U and the H_i. All orientations are given by the type order. The local pattern groups have entries in F_q or t F_q and are unchanged by reduction modulo t^m for m>=2. Put M=q^(N(N-1)/2).

The source uses N=2d+1 because it establishes trivial conjugation by leaving two coordinates unused after ordinary stabilization. The primary already proves direct pro-triviality of ambient conjugation at rank a>=2j+r-1, with r=2 here. Its proof uses two block embeddings into rank 2a, pro-surjectivity to move a class to the complementary block, commutation there, then pro-injectivity to return to rank a. We must use this stronger available operation before claiming a rank improvement.

The source's relative stable-range verification for t^m R works without change: a unimodular row congruent to e_1 is shortened with a t^m-divisible correction chosen by the Chinese remainder theorem at divisors of its first entry. It gives sr(A_m)<=2. The integer-unitization identification GL_a(A_m)=Gamma_a(m) is exact because a polynomial unit congruent to 1 modulo t^m is 1. The primary Tor-unitality and pro-stability therefore apply at N>=2j+1, in particular for every 1<=j<=d-1. This is compatible composition, not a new stability theorem.

## 2. Integral geometry and prime-inverted homology

The primary opposition cone is an integral chain homotopy through the required degree. For a block of the source codistance filtration, with F a simplex on the descent types and O the spherical opposition complex, the oriented relative chains are

    C_k(F*O, boundary(F)*O;Z) = Ctilde_(k-|F|)(O;Z)

up to the usual fixed degree sign in the differential. Every surviving face contains all vertices of F; deleting such a vertex vanishes in the relative complex. The integral cone identity on O makes the relative homology zero for k<n. Long exact sequences and the increasing union with the contractible building give

    Htilde_j(L;Z)=0 for j<n-1.

This uses the same geometric operation as the source; adding orientations does not constitute a new construction. In particular it holds after tensoring with the flat ring Lambda. Every nonempty simplex stabilizer is a finite q-group. Its order is a unit in Lambda, so the averaging idempotent splits the trivial module and all permutation chain modules are projective over Lambda[U]. The source's partial-resolution argument then gives

    H_j(U;Lambda)=0,                 0<j<n-1,
    H_j(Gamma_N(m);Lambda)=H_j(T_m;Lambda),  0<=j<n-1,

where T_m=Gamma_N(m)\L. The first orbit chain complex is a simplex; the second uses trivial congruence stabilizers. No stabilizer index multiplies the oriented face boundary.

For l>=m, [Gamma_N(m):Gamma_N(l)] is a q-power, so transfer gives a split surjection on homology with Lambda coefficients. Direct pro-triviality of ambient conjugation, localized from the integral primary statement, now gives ordinary triviality at every level. More explicitly, given m, choose l late enough that

    inc_*((g-1)y)=0 in H_j(Gamma_N(m);Lambda)

for all deeper y and all g in the constant ambient GL_N(R). For x at level m choose y mapping to x, possible by the transfer surjection. Equivariance gives (g-1)x=0. This argument uses surjectivity of the homology transition maps, not the false general claim that every pro-zero system is levelwise zero.

The finite quotient U/Gamma_N(m) is a q-group. Exact coinvariants, together with the just-proved trivial action, give

    H_j(Gamma_N(m);Lambda)
      = H_j(Gamma_N(m);Lambda)_(U/Gamma_N(m))
      = H_j(U;Lambda)=0,             1<=j<=d-1.            (1)

The geometric range is sufficient: d-1<2d-3=n-1 when d>=3. The source's extra two coordinates have been removed using an actual stronger primary operation; this numerical improvement is paid, not nominated.

Let A be ANY Lambda-module. For the finite free Lambda-chain complex of T_m, the universal coefficient sequence over the PID Lambda gives

    0 -> Ext^1_Lambda(H_(j-1)(T_m;Lambda),A)
      -> H^j(T_m;A)
      -> Hom_Lambda(H_j(T_m;Lambda),A) -> 0.

Both ends vanish for 1<=j<=d-1, including j=1 because H_0=Lambda is free. Thus H^j(T_m;A)=0 in that range, uniformly in A. The d-skeleton X_m has the same cochains and differentials needed in these degrees. This does NOT claim the result for F_q coefficients or for arbitrary abelian groups in which q is not invertible.

## 3. Actual covering maps and arbitrary intermediate subgroups

It is insufficient merely to say that congruence simplex stabilizers are trivial. We check stars. At the base vertex of type i, chambers are indexed by H_i. The faces in its link are quotients of H_i by its intersections with other local groups. Reduction is injective on H_i and preserves all these intersections. Consequently L->T_m is an isomorphism on each vertex star. Translating by U checks every vertex. Thus this is an honest simplicial covering map, not just a rigid quotient.

Fix l>=m>=2 and ANY subgroup H with

    Gamma_N(l) <= H <= Gamma_N(m).

No normality of H in Gamma_N(m) or U is required. Put T_H=H\L and X_H=skel_d T_H. The map T_H->T_m is a covering, and T_l->T_H is a regular cover with deck group

    Q=H/Gamma_N(l),

a finite q-group. The latter normality follows from normality of Gamma_N(l) in U; it does not require normality of H in U. Quotients are simplicial because all these actions are subactions of Gamma_N(m), whose stars already inject. Links of T_H are the fixed source links, every vertex is incident to M chambers, and every panel has q chambers. Therefore

    degree_d(X_H) <= M binom(N-1,d).                      (2)

Growth is inherited along m->infinity, since T_H covers T_m. The elementary local-ring lifting count also gives |X_m(0)|=N q^((m-1)(N^2-1)); this count was already present in the earlier original, so is not a new contribution. The weaker source lower bound would suffice here.

Coverings preserve the intrinsic chamber-induced weights by pushforward. Each top simplex has the same number of lifts; every lower face has the same local incidence count at each lift. In particular pullback is an isometry on weighted support norms and on the real L2 spaces. The graph Markov operator intertwines pullback. The global one-sided spectral bound of T_l therefore descends to T_H, even when T_H->T_m is nonregular. The other link spectral bounds hold by the star isomorphisms. This avoids incorrectly inferring uniform expansion of arbitrary covers from expansion of their base graph: the expanding deeper congruence cover is essential.

## 4. Flat coefficients trivialized by the congruence cover

Let A be a Lambda-module, of arbitrary size or rank, and let rho:Q->Aut_Lambda(A) be any action. Let E be the associated flat local coefficient system on T_H. Equivalently, E-cochains are Q-equivariant A-valued cochains on T_l. A nonzero value remains nonzero under every transport isomorphism, so weighted support norms are intrinsic and their pullbacks agree.

There is a chain-level identification

    C^*(T_H;E) = C^*(T_l;A)^Q.

Because |Q| is a unit in Lambda, invariants are exact via |Q|^(-1) sum_(g in Q) g. Thus cohomology commutes with invariants. Equation (1) and the universal coefficient argument give

    H^j(T_H;E)=H^j(T_l;A)^Q=0,       1<=j<=d-1.          (3)

The same holds for X_H. No assumption that rho is the trivial representation is used. No semisimplicity claim over arbitrary Lambda-modules is needed: the explicit averaging operator proves exactness even if A is not free or finitely generated. The local system is restricted to one trivialized by a finite congruence cover; arbitrary representations of the fundamental group have not been covered.

Algebraic averaging alone gives a poor support estimate. If a cochain b upstairs fills an equivariant cocycle, the average is equivariant but its support can lie in the union of |Q| translates of supp(b). The elementary estimate loses |Q|. One cannot call that an order-independent cofilling inequality. The next step uses the actual primary sheaf theorem to pay this loss.

## 5. Uniform quantitative expansion with flat coefficients

Write beta>0 for the minimum of 1 and the finitely many local coboundary constants supplied by integral opposition cones and chamber symmetry in dimension at most n-1. These constants depend on n, and are independent of sufficiently large q and of A. An integral cone matrix tensors with any abelian group and its support cannot increase on a basis face; the same matrix, not separate unrelated cones in every component, supplies the rank-independent bound. Alternatively, for an arbitrary A, the finitely many values of a given cochain lie in a finitely generated abelian subgroup, and a local correction there suffices.

For each nonempty face u of T_H, the closed star is contractible. Flat transport trivializes E on that star. The restricted sheaf E_u, including its augmentation value at the empty link face, is consequently the constant sheaf with coefficient A on the link. This pays BOTH local hypotheses of the primary simplicial sheaf theorem: in degree k, they ask for local coboundary expansion in degrees k-dim(u)-1 and k-dim(u). Degree -1 has constant 1 under the diagonal augmentation. The largest link degree needed is below its top dimension because n>=k+2. In our range 1<=k<=d-1 this follows from 2d-2>=d+1.

Let K_k,K'_k in (0,1] be the constants in that actual primary theorem. Choose q once so large that the source local spectral bound lambda satisfies

    lambda <= min_(1<=k<=d-1) (K_k beta)^(2^(k+1)).       (4)

This is possible because beta is independent of q and lambda tends to zero with q. For a real weighted graph with second eigenvalue <=lambda, the indicator identity gives w(E(S))<=lambda w(S)+w(S)^2. Thus (4) implies the required skeleton-expansion hypotheses for the global graph and all relevant links. No-intersection hypergraphs are not an extra missing hypothesis here: the primary's simplicial specialization explicitly reduces them to these link graphs.

Applying that primary theorem to the full n-complex T_H yields, for 1<=k<=d-1,

    ||delta_E f||_(T_H,k+1)
       >= c_k dist_(T_H,k)(f,Z^k(T_H;E)),
    c_k=K'_k (K_k beta)^(2^(k+2)-1)>0.                  (5)

Constants are independent of m,l,H,A,rho. Equation (3) replaces Z^k by B^k. This is an existing general sheaf local-to-global operation applied to the source's fixed links, not a newly invented twisted-coefficient theorem. In particular, the loss from naive averaging is fully paid here, rather than retained as a purported nonroutine gap.

## 6. Degree zero, including nontrivial monodromy

The primary simplicial sheaf statement above is for k>=1. Degree zero requires an additional check: constants downstairs are parallel sections, not arbitrary A-values. There is an elementary uniform repair.

Lift f to an equivariant function F on vertices of T_l. Let p_a be the probability that F=a under the vertex weights. There are only finitely many nonzero p_a even when A is infinite. The one-sided graph spectral bound gives, for the indicator of each value class,

    <1_(F=a),P 1_(F=a)> <= p_a^2+lambda p_a(1-p_a).

Sum over all a and complement. With r=||delta F||,

    r >= (1-lambda)(1-sum_a p_a^2)
      >= (1-lambda)(1-max_a p_a).                       (6)

If r<(1-lambda)/2, some value a occurs on weight>1/2. For each g in Q, equivariance and invariance of the measure imply p_(rho(g)a)=p_a. Two disjoint value classes cannot both have weight>1/2, so rho(g)a=a for every g. The constant a upstairs therefore descends to a parallel section downstairs. Its distance from f is at most r/(1-lambda). If r>=(1-lambda)/2, distance to the zero section is at most 1. In both cases,

    ||delta_E f|| >= ((1-lambda)/2) dist(f,Z^0(E)).        (7)

One may set B^0(E)=Z^0(E), the parallel sections, as the reduced-degree-zero convention. This explicit convention is necessary; setting B^0 to arbitrary constants is not meaningful under nontrivial monodromy. The same argument works for an arbitrary flat A-bundle trivialized by the chosen cover and does not require a vector-space norm or a coefficient-rank bound.

## 7. Skeleton norms, cofilling, and the limits of algorithmic claims

For s=d, each s-face of T_H lies in between 1 and M chambers. Put

    Lambda_H = binom(N,d+1)|T_H(n)|/|T_H(d)|.

The source double count, unchanged for E because support is a face property, gives for 0<=i<=d

    (Lambda_H/M)||f||_(T_H,i) <= ||f||_(X_H,i)
       <= Lambda_H ||f||_(T_H,i).

The same bounds hold for distances to any fixed subgroup of cochains. Cochains and differentials computing H^i for i<d agree on both skeleta. Thus (5)-(7) imply a common constant

    epsilon = M^(-1) min{(1-lambda)/2,c_1,...,c_(d-1)} >0

for the corresponding inequalities on X_H. Again the normalization cancels; no square of M or unbounded cover-degree factor is needed.

For a coboundary z in degree i+1, choose any f with delta f=z. A closest cocycle b exists because the finite face set admits only finitely many possible support weights. Then a=f-b satisfies

    delta a=z,       ||a||<=epsilon^(-1)||z||.           (8)

For i>=1, (3) makes b a coboundary. At i=0 it is a parallel section. Equation (8) is nonlinear cofilling; it is not a linear choice of all fillings, and it is not automatically a polynomial-time global minimum computation.

For a fixed finite coefficient alphabet and fixed local structure, the actual primary local correction algorithm gives its stated small-error recovery. It changes assignments on bounded stars while decreasing the defect. The number of iterations and the needed local optimization are paid in the primary with constants depending on the alphabet and local incidence. These constants are NOT independent of unbounded coefficient rank if local search enumerates all assignments. Nor does cocycle recovery output a primitive for every exact cocycle without a further solving step. No new alphabet-uniform algorithm is nominated.

## 8. Why uniform linear cofilling is a different demand

Even the trivial binary degree-zero case shows that (8) cannot generally be replaced by a uniformly support-bounded LINEAR section. This elementary calculation is a limitation, not a substantial result.

On a connected complex with v vertices, let S:B^1->C^0 be an F2-linear right inverse to delta_0. Then P=S delta_0 is a linear projection onto a complement of the constants. There is a linear functional ell:C^0->F2 with ell(1)=1 such that

    P(f)=f+ell(f)1.

Hence some vertex indicator e_x has ell(e_x)=1. For this x, P(e_x)=1+e_x. In the chamber-induced weights of T_m, every vertex has weight 1/v, because its chamber incidence is M. The edge support of delta e_x is its incident edges, whose total weight is exactly 2/v by the marginal identity for edge weights. Consequently

    ||S(delta e_x)||/||delta e_x|| = (v-1)/2.             (9)

This diverges as m grows. For the d-skeleton's intrinsic weights, the source norm comparisons still give a lower bound (v-1)/(2M). Thus no uniform linear section of this kind can exist for the growing family. A nonlinear small-support selection, an algorithm returning a cocycle, and a bounded linear section are three different objects. This calculation rules out the last proposal at its stated scope; it does not rule out efficient nonlinear correction or higher-degree phenomena.

## 9. Outcome of the integrated reduction

The combined construction closes as an ordinary consequence of the stipulated source, actual stronger primary stability and sheaf operations, and explicit transfer/cover/majority calculations. It supplies no established nonroutine operation warranting nomination. Some basic coefficient-universal scopes are already the subject of an immutable older disposition; the excluded old packet is not reopened, and none of these consequences is offered as a renamed retry.

No arbitrary noncongruence monodromy, no characteristic-q coefficient extension, no all-alphabet uniform-time primitive algorithm, and no uniform linear cofilling section are proved. The last is directly obstructed by (9). For noncongruence monodromy the missing ingredient is a quantitative and cohomological control of a trivializing cover outside the supplied congruence tower; ordinary exact averaging and the deeper-cover spectral argument no longer supply it. For characteristic q the transfer composite is multiplication by a q-power, hence zero, and the earlier full target remains intact. These are precise limits of this calculation, not a frozen conditional bridge and not claims that neighboring research is impossible.
