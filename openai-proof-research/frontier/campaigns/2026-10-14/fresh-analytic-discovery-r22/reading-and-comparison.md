## Exact operation comparison and disposition

Pinned source: delocalized replacement plus a separate, temperature-uniform localized-slice comparison for small centered Gaussian variance mixtures. It is stronger than a headline fixed-temperature pressure identity and already explains why moment matching alone is insufficient.

r22: explicit asymmetric smooth compactly-supported centered-variance-one law, deterministic strictly feasible axes, strip-correlation lower bound on strictly feasible surface volume, cubic-moment replacement on a small-coordinate region, Efron–Stein concentration, and low-temperature fixed-coordinate Gibbs localization. This is a mathematically explicit obstruction to broad moment-only universality. It does not settle universality for an eligible narrower symmetric class or offer such a class as a replacement nominee.

The displayed full deductions in localization-obstruction.md are offered for parent checking as technical mathematics. No frozen candidate, author PASS, independent certification, contribution acceptance, or campaign count increment is asserted. The operational ingredients are classical and the source already singles out localization; this is not represented as the sought seventh substantial finding.
