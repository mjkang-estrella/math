      The results of this paper are an extension of the Nash-De Giorgi-Moser results on the
regularity of solutions of single non-linear equations to solutions of certain types of systems.
We also allow a weakening of the ellipticity condition, so our results are new when applied
to single equations. The method of proof is to exhibit an auxiliary function for the system
which is subharmonic. Then estimates of Moser [7] for subsolutions and supersolutions are
used to get a strong maximum principle. From here a perturbation theorem similar to
theorems of Almgren [1] and Morrey [6] gives sufficient continuity for the linear theory of
the regularity of solutions of elliptic systems to be applicable.
