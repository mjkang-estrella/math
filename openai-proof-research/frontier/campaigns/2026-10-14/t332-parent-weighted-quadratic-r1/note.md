# Arbitrary diagonal quadratic weights still fail uniformly

Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. Original prose CC BY4.0; original code MIT. Parent-side continuation of the unchanged T332 target, not an independent review, a new nominee, or a count. This extends the earlier fixed-coordinate-p projection obstruction to every choice of positive diagonal quadratic weights, including weights chosen separately for each tree. Non-diagonal metrics and nonquadratic retractions are not covered.

## Statement

Take one unit stem and a complete binary tree of depth h below it. Number the edge levels a=0,...,h, with 2^a edges at level a. For an edge vector x use the transport norm N(x)=sum_e |x_e|. Let K be the positive vertex-measure cone, equivalently q_e>=sum_{f child of e}q_f at every edge e, with leaves q_e>=0. For ARBITRARY positive numbers alpha_e, let

    Pi_alpha(x)=argmin_{q in K} sum_e alpha_e(q_e-x_e)^2.

The objective is strictly convex and coercive, so this minimizer exists uniquely. If L is its Lipschitz constant for N on domain and range, then

    L^2 >= (h+1)/(2 H_{h+1}),
    H_m=sum_{r=1}^m 1/r.                              (1)

Thus no tree-dependent choice of diagonal quadratic weights provides a uniform retraction constant. The same lower bound holds if the minimization is over probability flows P={q in K:q_stem<=1}. Neither conclusion refutes a different retraction or T332.

## Local linear projections at faces

For a nonroot vertex v let a_v be the indicator of its rooted path. These vectors form a basis: the coefficient of a_v is q_e-sum_child q_f for the edge entering v. Hence K is a simplicial cone, and every subset of the a_v generates a face.

Fix a nonempty subset S of vertices. Set F=cone{a_v:v in S}, V=span F, and let D be the positive diagonal matrix with entries alpha_e. Choose q in the relative interior of F. Because the path vectors are a basis, a vector z exists with

    <z,a_v>=0 for v in S,    <z,a_v>=1 otherwise.

Put x=q-D^{-1}z. Then D(q-x)=z belongs to the dual cone and <z,q>=0. The quadratic first-order condition proves Pi_alpha(x)=q. Let P_V denote the D-orthogonal projection onto V. For all sufficiently small perturbations u, q+P_V u remains in the relative interior of F, and

    D[(q+P_V u)-(x+u)]=z-D(I-P_V)u

annihilates V and stays strictly positive on every excluded a_v. The same first-order condition shows

    Pi_alpha(x+u)=q+P_V u.                            (2)

Consequently L is at least the N-operator norm of P_V. This is an actual local behavior of the cone projection, not an optimization restricted without justification to one face. For P, choose q with total off-root mass strictly less than1. The same neighborhood has q+P_V u in P; the cone minimizer is then also the polytope minimizer. Thus every lower bound below applies to P as well.

## Subtree-face test

Let A=max_e alpha_e. Fix an edge e at level a. Select the face generated by all leaves below e, together with the parent vertex of e if that parent is not the root. Its span permits arbitrary signed leaf coefficients and an independent multiple of the parent's path vector. For input 1_e, the optimal projected flow vanishes along the prefix above e: the independent parent coefficient can cancel the prefix total, and zero minimizes its quadratic cost. All other branches vanish too.

Below e the span is exactly the space of flows conserved at every internal vertex. Define the effective resistance recursively by

    R_e=alpha_e                                  if e is a leaf,
    R_e=alpha_e+R_f R_g/(R_f+R_g)                 otherwise,       (3)

where f,g are its children. To verify (3), for a fixed entering flow s, the least downstream energy is obtained by splitting s=s_f+s_g to minimize R_f s_f^2+R_g s_g^2. Differentiation gives s_f=R_g s/(R_f+R_g), s_g=R_f s/(R_f+R_g), with value R_f R_g s^2/(R_f+R_g). The minimizing split is positive whenever s is positive; induction proves the claim on the whole finite subtree, allowing signed competitors.

For the input 1_e, the energy to minimize is

    alpha_e(s-1)^2+(R_e-alpha_e)s^2.

Its minimum occurs at s=alpha_e/R_e>0. All descendant flows are nonnegative, and at each of the h-a+1 levels their sum is s. Therefore (2) gives

    L >= (h-a+1) alpha_e/R_e.                         (4)

Since alpha_e<=A, recursion (3) gives R_e<=A sum_{j=0}^{h-a}2^{-j}<2A. Thus, for EVERY edge at level a,

    alpha_e <= 2 A L/(h-a+1).                        (5)

No symmetry or depth-only assumption on the weights has been made.

## Ray-face test and combination

Choose an edge e_* attaining A and any terminal leaf v below it. The face generated only by a_v has span a line. Its D-orthogonal projection sends 1_{e_*} to

    [A/S_v] a_v,    S_v=sum_{e in [o,v]}alpha_e.

The path has h+1 unit edges, so (2) gives L>=(h+1)A/S_v. Applying (5) at each level of this path yields

    S_v<=2 A L H_{h+1}.

Combining and canceling A>0 proves (1). Since H_{h+1}<=1+log(h+1), the lower bound tends to infinity. The argument quantifies over every choice of weights afresh at every h; it does not merely hold for one fixed profile.

## Interpretation and evidence limits

The earlier parent note t332-parent-positive-projection-r1/projection-obstruction.md excluded ordinary coordinate-p projection for each fixed p>1. Its electrical p=2 case has alpha_e equal to the edge lengths. A possible escape was to choose geometry-dependent diagonal weights. The two incompatible local face tests above close that escape even on unit-length trees. They do not cover general positive-definite quadratic forms, adaptive objectives, arbitrary Lipschitz maps, or a direct cotype argument.

The domain points constructed in (2) are arbitrary signed ambient flows, which the sufficient retraction route permits. No claim is made that the inputs are probabilities or have nonnegative edge coordinates. Projection fixes all feasible inputs. The probability-flow version is justified by the inactive root-budget constraint, not by assuming it is globally the cone projection.

This proof uses elementary finite-dimensional quadratic minimization and the rooted-path basis. It invokes no new source theorem or primary literature result. Earlier source332 truth remains stipulated, but this route-exclusion lemma is not itself a new source-essential result. No priority, independent proof gate, formal proof or campaign acceptance is claimed. The full original T332 quantifiers remain unresolved.

The exact rational diagnostic checks the resistance recursion, all subtree-face column lower bounds and all ray-face bounds for several chosen weight profiles, including nonsymmetric ones. It is a diagnostic for the formulas, not the all-weight/all-depth proof, and it does not compute the full projection's exact Lipschitz constant. The proof above supplies the general quantifiers.
