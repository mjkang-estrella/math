# Selection and bounded operation screen

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.

All numbered catalogue headings were extracted, and selected descriptions
and actual text interfaces in 204, 210, 254 and 310 read. The complete
preprint listing was parsed for all selected companion paths. Abstracts
were discovery aids only. No claim to read all 719 manuscripts is made.

Decisions before freezing R:

* Family 254 word/membership algorithms and arbitrary finite intersection
  witnesses are paid by its own full statements and algorithm proof.
* Family 310 change from rational nonvanishing to every field is already
  in its introduction; no new target was selected there.
* Family 204 supplies three-weight saturation, including spin weights.
  A multifactor extension would need an integral gluing argument and was
  not adopted. No claim that such an extension is new or automatic.
* Family 210 supplies a quadratic stabilization bound in the canonical
  direction. The project already records technical power-cancellation
  thresholds and unresolved seventh-seed/quotient work in I198, I199,
  I202 and I206. Their complete selection/literature records were read.
  This family was rejected before nomination; no historical technical
  result or seventh-seed target is repackaged.
* Family 314 cyclic length appeared as a discovery lead, but its group
  congruence operations overlap the vetoed poly-Zp history. No source
  theorem from it was used and no target was pursued.

R requires finite-orbit fixation, rather than only identification of
pointwise stabilizers. CMV's actual large-type proof uses systolic
finite-orbit geometry. HMS credits the two-dimensional root theorem.
GHLY gives a normalizer-quotient classifying-space theorem in spherical
ambient type and explicitly conjectures its general extension. These
stronger antecedents were compared at their actual operation interfaces,
not by searching for a matching title. R's source-category sum route has
an explicit unproved cross-Hom step, tested in the mathematical report.

General finite-group wreath/cyclic-series searches were selection-only
and supplied no premise. Secondary index sites and search snippets were
not used as theorem evidence. The current source-family choice is Artin
parabolics; neither those group-theory leads nor Foulkes were sustained
proof targets.

This was not an exhaustive MathSciNet/Zentralblatt or every-language
search. No primary statement settling full R was found in the read
interfaces, but this is not a global novelty or current-open-status
certificate. Since R remains unproved, no contribution nomination is
made. Supporting reductions and one explicit method obstruction are
technical research, not campaign results.
