# Rights and sharing boundary

The new public research notes and factual registers are original prose/metadata. The outside verify.py and run_controls.py are original integrity programs. These are the shareable portion for parent integration; no public upload is authorized by this packet.

Everything in private/ containing pinned upstream, primary PDFs/text/renderings, copied history/context or search bodies retains its original rights and attribution. It is not part of a public-original grant or publication payload. Private preparation code is archived for provenance only. Do not copy private evidence bodies into a public package merely because they are integrity-bound.

No mathematical theorem, eligibility certification or contribution verdict is conferred by a file's placement in public/.
