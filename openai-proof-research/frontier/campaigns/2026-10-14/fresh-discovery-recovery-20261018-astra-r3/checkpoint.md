New exact source-specific obstruction: if two independently sampled roots from the same prefix star have distinct adjacent outer endpoints, the same triangle is exposed in BOTH initial candidate lists. On every good prefix the probability of this event is (1+o(1))p, with p~n^(-1/2+1/2000). Therefore a raw full-vector coupling bound that charges every exposed collision cannot furnish the o(q^(2r)/n) error needed for a 2r-th moment plus all-vertex union bound, for ANY fixed r. This persists even if the source witness exponents are improved. It is an obstruction to this bound, not a lower bound on total variation or a counterexample to T188U.

A new exact one-shared-triangle calculation keeps that initial collision and produces its pair covariance under arbitrary independent exterior lifetime laws. For source-shaped lifetime tails its covariance is asymptotic to -2q^3. This is a solvable finite cavity model, not an assertion that different branches are independent in the finite triangle-removal process. The distinction is essential.

Two full-target routes remain under examination: a collision-cluster centered-moment expansion that keeps shared priorities, or a local weighted concentration theorem for the actual greedy matching. Current primary average-query bounds do not by themselves supply either missing estimate.
