# Author checks on the full proof

These are reasoning checks, not an independent review or mathematical gate.

* The higher-mode estimate includes arbitrary orthogonal-coordinate dependence. Its product-Hermite comparison explicitly covers planar degrees0 and1, rather than assuming all degree lives in the propeller plane.
* The tail first-chaos estimate uses all degree-one coefficients, including those normal to the propeller plane. Its coefficient tends to zero using integrable one-dimensional Gaussian tails; no dimension factor or independence of mismatch events is assumed.
* The cone probability formula pairs reflected sectors exactly. The restriction x>=rho s has CDF factor>=1/2 because the winning-sector inequality is b>=-s/sqrt(3). Both active comparisons and zero-score inactive labels are covered.
* The lower weight is allowed to vanish along full lines extending the boundary rays, but their Gaussian measure is zero. It therefore suffices for the equality case; no assertion that those full lines are actual interfaces is made.
* The nearest score frame is chosen for the TOTAL partition, not silently reselected after deleting tails. Lemma4.1 pays the frame change quadratically in the tail centroid. Its same-support conclusion uses a fixed positive distance between different three-label supports, independent of k.
* The truncated object is a full measurable partition, so the inherited source inequality and calibration apply. It is not a signed or fractional relaxation.
* All constants are chosen in the order: historical constants and local frame radius; fixed Gaussian smoothing constants; tail radius R; then rho_0. No choice depends on d,k or masses. The proof only requires rho to lie in a single full interval, not along a subsequence.
* An arbitrary contender is treated directly; existence, regularity or first-variation identities for a noise optimizer are not assumed. Parseval gives global entry and the source gives the first-moment inequality for the truncated partition.
* The exact quadratic expansion uses T_rho-Pi_0, matching the subtraction of squared cell masses. The first-chaos deficit identity is Delta(A^N)=2B J_N-||z(g)||². The two-indicator mismatch factor is included in the definition of kappa.
* The original failed uniform linear-tail comparison remains preserved. The full proof replaces that method, not the frozen target. No frozen file was edited.
