**Cyclic divisibility and individual nonrealization in dimension four.**
For every integer q>=3 there exist a finite connected oriented aspherical integral Poincare duality complex Q of dimension four and an epimorphism eta:G=pi_1(Q)->Z with the following properties.

1. G is a torsion-free word-hyperbolic group. For every positive integer d, let G_d=eta^(-1)(dZ), and let Q_d be the connected d-sheeted cover of Q corresponding to G_d. There exists a closed connected topological four-manifold homotopy equivalent to Q_d if and only if q divides d. Whenever such a model exists it may be oriented so that its homotopy equivalence to Q_d preserves orientation.

2. For every closed connected topological four-manifold N and every homotopy equivalence u:N->Q_q, identify pi_1(N) with G_q using u, up to inner automorphism. Choose gamma in G with eta(gamma)=1. Conjugation by gamma defines a homomorphism
   iota_u:Z/qZ -> Out(pi_1(N)), a mod q |-> [Ad(gamma^a)|G_q],
   transported through u. This homomorphism is injective. Every class in its image is represented by an orientation-preserving self-homotopy equivalence of N (with the orientation transported through u). No nonidentity class in its image is induced by any self-homeomorphism of N, without any restriction on that homeomorphism's order. Changing the chosen gamma does not change this homomorphism; changing the marking conjugates it by the induced outer isomorphism.

In particular, there is such an N with an orientation-preserving self-homotopy equivalence f for which [f] has exact order q, and for every integer a not divisible by q the map f^a is not homotopic to any homeomorphism. Powers with negative exponent mean powers of a homotopy inverse in the group of homotopy classes.

The quantifiers q>=3, all d>=1, every model N, all its markings u, and unrestricted-order homeomorphisms are part of the frozen scope. No extension to a noncyclic finite group, no comparison of unmarked homeomorphism types among different models, and no smooth-manifold realization is asserted.
