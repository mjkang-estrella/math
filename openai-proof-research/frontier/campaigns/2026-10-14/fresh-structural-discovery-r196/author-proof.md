## 2. The finite q-color incidence system

Fix q>=3. Put m=5q(q-1), and take the same smooth plumbing blocks as N's `ch:four-tip-model`, now indexed by

    (s,c,k), 1<=s<q, 1<=c<=q, 1<=k<=5.

The resulting plumbing neighborhood X has the source graph homotopy type with free generators tau_(s,c,k) and these extra loops. The product port collars, interchange of meridian and longitude under plumbing, and absence of self-plumbing of either individual core are exactly those of the original block. Define height on this graph by

    w(tau_(s,c,k))=s,    w(l_i)=0.

In particular height is onto, since s=1 occurs.

For each body choose two ordered lists of q ports, one of each color in each list, with all 2q ports distinct. At every slot choose independently a sign and a conjugator from C={1,l_1,...,l_9}. Install every such choice. Thus the number of bottom bodies can be taken to be

    k_q=[M(M-1)]^q 10^(2q) 2^(2q).

Different choices may give equivalent words; they are still installed as distinct separated parallel bodies. Finiteness, rather than minimality, is what is required. Each body has a bottom punctured torus F, with root curves a,b. Above a and b place a rooted dyadic comb with q leaves in color order 1,...,q. A dyadic vertex is a punctured torus with its two outgoing symplectic curves. Denote its iterated group commutator by B_q. The bottom word has the form [B_q(x_1,...,x_q),B_q(y_1,...,y_q)], with the marked conjugations and signs at the leaves.

Here is the finite incidence calculation needed later. For d>=q let Gamma_(q,d) have vertices Z/d and edges (v,s,c,k) joining v to v+s. Even if an underlying unoriented pair occurs more than once, these are different edges. There are no loops because 0<s<q<=d. At a vertex the two ports of any base block lift to different incident edges, so all distinct-port bodies give distinct-edge law sets. Let the law family consist of every set of 2q incident edges with two of each color.

**Incidence lemma.** An endpoint assignment avoiding all law sets exists exactly when q divides d.

**Proof.** Suppose one exists. For each vertex v let D(v) be the set of colors for which v receives at most one edge of that color. If D(v) were empty, choosing two received edges of every color would give a received law; hence D(v) is nonempty. If u,v differ by s with 1<=s<q, they are joined by at least five distinct edges of every color. A color in both D(u) and D(v) would permit at most two of those five edges to be assigned, impossible. Thus D(u) and D(v) are disjoint. Any q consecutive vertices form a clique. Their q nonempty pairwise disjoint deficiency sets in a q-element color set must be singletons and exhaust the colors. Write c(v) for the unique deficient color. Comparing the consecutive cliques beginning at v and v+1 yields c(v+q)=c(v). Lift this coloring to Z. It has periods q and d and therefore period gcd(q,d), by Bezout's identity, including negative translations. If gcd(q,d)<q, two positions in a q-block have the same color, contrary to the clique condition. Thus q divides d.

If q divides d, set c(v)=1+(v mod q). The endpoints of every edge have different chosen colors. At least one endpoint has chosen color different from that edge's own color; assign the edge there. Each vertex receives no edge of its own chosen color, hence receives no law. This proves both directions. The argument includes d=q and all multiple edges. ∎

## 6. Full reflected covers and the all-degree spectrum

Choose a finite flag-no-square triangulation L of S as in N's triangulation operation, and use the independent label group A_0=(Z/2)^(vertices L). Take the full finite reflection

    Q=R_(A_0)(P).

By `refl:input` it is a finite connected oriented aspherical integral PD4 complex. Folding the chambers gives a map rho:Q->P whose restriction to each chamber is identity. A chamber inclusion splits rho, so eta=w rho_*:G=pi_1 Q->Z is onto. The character agrees with the chosen chamber height, and gamma may be the image of any height-one plumbing generator.

For d>=1, Q_d is reflection of P_d using the ORIGINAL labels repeated on each lift of a panel. This follows directly by lifting the folding map: the pullback of P_d->P restricts to P_d in every chamber, and the gluing identifies the same boundary point across the same original coordinate. It is connected since chamber height is onto. Adjacent lifted panels still have distinct original labels.

For the implication from a manifold model, distinguish all lifted panel labels. Write A_d for the group with one coordinate per vertex of the lifted triangulation. The map A_d->A_0 sends each lifted coordinate to its original coordinate. On every simplex it is injective, since its original vertices are distinct. Therefore its kernel acts freely on the full reflected development, and full reflection of P_d is a finite cover of this repeated-label Q_d. A manifold model of Q_d lifts to a closed manifold model of that full reflection. The covered chamber satisfies every verified hypothesis of `refl:input`, so such a model forces a marked filling of S_d. This argument is necessary: the repeated labels are not silently treated as new independent labels.

If q|d, the filling V_d of §4 replaces each P_d chamber using its exact boundary inclusion. Gluing by the original panel labels gives a closed oriented TOP four-manifold M_d. Near a point on r panels, independent local coordinates give the 2^r orthants of R^4; independence on a face was checked above, so there is no residual local isotropy. At points in a chamber interior the assertion is immediate. Product collars make all gluings locally flat. The relative equivalences V_d->P_d induce an equivalence of these finite homotopy pushouts, hence M_d is homotopy equivalent to Q_d. The alternating label orientation is well defined and makes this equivalence orientation preserving. Asphericity of Q_d implies asphericity of M_d.

If q does not divide d and d>=q, the preceding implication and §5 exclude a model. For 1<=d<q choose an integer L>=1 such that D=d(1+qL)>=q. Then q does not divide D, because D is congruent to d modulo q. If Q_d had a closed manifold model, lift it along G_D subset G_d. The result would be a closed manifold model of Q_D, already excluded. This proves the exact spectrum for ALL d>=1, including the degree-one case without attempting to apply a loopless graph theorem to its loop graph.

## 7. Hyperbolicity and torsion-freeness

We check the support proof's applicability instead of assuming that asphericity alone gives hyperbolicity. Let H=pi_1 P, let the lifted boundary nerve be L_tilde, let W(L_tilde) be its right-angled Coxeter group, and set E=W(L_tilde) semidirect H. H acts by the boundary covering transformations. The homomorphism chi:E->A_0 sends each Coxeter generator to its original panel label and H to zero. Its kernel is the reflected group G. It has finite index in E. The height character on G is w composed with E->H, in agreement with the folding definition.

The input to N's `hyp:cubical-model` is exactly a finite graph model for P, the equivariant map from the lifted boundary to that graph's universal tree, and the finite three-dimensional flag-no-square nerve. The boundary marking is onto, so the lifted boundary is connected; its dual panel data and deck action are the same data used in reflection. The finite boundary and finite graph give finitely many endpoint-support types. Enlarging their finite connected subtrees gives a uniform finite support bound. The source support construction then gives a proper cocompact CAT(0) cube action of E, with the same bounded local cube dimension. It uses no relation specific to four-tip bodies or to the numerical values of w.

In that action, the proof of `hyp:endpoint-supports` identifies the endpoint supports of commuting labels. The absence of induced four-cycles in L bounds a square grid by the source uniform bound expressed in its finite support constants. Those constants may grow with q and k_q; the theorem asserts no uniform bound in q. The median-graph argument `hyp:grids-to-triangles` converts this grid bound to a finite thin-triangle constant. Thus E is word-hyperbolic by exactly the stipulated source proof, and so is its finite-index subgroup G. No additional metric-curvature assertion about M_d is made.

Finally Q is finite dimensional and aspherical. Its universal cover gives a finite-length free ZG-resolution of Z. ## 8. Every model and every nonidentity cyclic class

Let N be ANY closed connected TOP four-manifold with a homotopy equivalence u:N->Q_q. Give it the transported orientation and identify its fundamental group with G_q up to inner automorphism. Since Q_q is aspherical, N is aspherical. Conjugation by gamma preserves G_q, and its qth power is inner by gamma^q in G_q. Hence it defines a homomorphism iota_u:Z/q->Out(pi_1 N). Replacing gamma by gamma' with the same height changes it by an element of ker eta subset G_q, so gives the same outer class and the same homomorphism. Changing the marking conjugates the construction by the marking's induced outer isomorphism, as asserted.

For each a its class is represented by the deck transformation of Q_q->Q induced by gamma^a, transported through u and a homotopy inverse. This is an orientation-preserving self-homotopy equivalence: the cover orientation comes from oriented Q and its deck transformations preserve it. The statement concerns homotopy classes, so a chosen homotopy inverse supplies negative powers.

It remains to exclude EVERY homeomorphism representing a nonzero residue a, not merely periodic ones. Take 0<a<q and suppose h:N->N represents the corresponding outer class. Choose a lift U to the universal cover N_tilde whose exact conjugation on its deck group G_q is Ad(gamma^a); this is possible by composing an arbitrary lift with an appropriate element of G_q. Put K=ker eta. It is characteristic under that conjugation, so U descends to a homeomorphism h' of X=K\N_tilde. The element gamma^q gives the deck translation T of X->N. The exact conjugation identity gives h'T=Th'.

Choose a map N->S^1 representing eta/q on G_q and lift it to tau:X->R, so tau(Tx)=tau(x)+1. This tau is proper. Indeed, take a compact set C whose T-translates cover X. If tau(C) is in [b_0,b_1], then any compact height band [A,B] is contained in the union of those T^j C with A-b_1<=j<=B-b_0; only finitely many j occur. The band is closed in this finite compact union.

The continuous function tau(h'x)-tau(x) is T-invariant, hence bounded in absolute value by c, since X/T=N is compact. Choose an integer R>c+1 and set F=T^R h'. Then

    R-c <= tau(Fx)-tau(x) <= R+c.

The lower bound is positive. Positive iterates tend to +infinity in height and negative iterates to -infinity, uniformly on compact sets. Thus F acts freely and properly discontinuously: for any compact set, its height range gives a finite bound on powers whose image can meet it. Every orbit has a first iterate of nonnegative height, and that iterate has height at most R+c. The compact band [0,R+c] therefore meets every orbit. The quotient Y=X/<F> is a closed connected TOP four-manifold. Since h represents an orientation-preserving homotopy class, its degree is +1 and it preserves the orientation of the connected oriented manifold N. Its lifts and T also preserve orientation, so Y is oriented.

The universal cover of Y is the contractible N_tilde. Its deck group is generated by K and the lift V=gamma^(qR) U. Conjugation by V on K is Ad(gamma^(qR+a)). No nonzero power of V belongs to K, since its descended action is a nonzero power of the free action F. The group is therefore K semidirect Z with that conjugation. Since eta splits by gamma, G itself is K semidirect <gamma>; the map fixing K and sending the new generator to gamma^(qR+a) identifies pi_1 Y with G_(qR+a). Consequently Y is a closed manifold model of the aspherical complex Q_(qR+a). But qR+a is positive and not divisible by q, contradicting §6.

This excludes any such h. If iota_u had a nonzero kernel element, its class would be represented by the identity homeomorphism, which has just been excluded. Hence iota_u is injective. Choosing a representative f of its generator gives exact order q in homotopy classes, f^q homotopic to identity, and the asserted nonrealization for every positive or negative exponent not divisible by q.
