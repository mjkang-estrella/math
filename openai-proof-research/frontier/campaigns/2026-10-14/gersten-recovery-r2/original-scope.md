# Frozen eligible target G4: an integral even-degree local Gersten kernel

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. 2026-10-16. Original prose CC BY4.0;
original code MIT. Third-party materials remain private with original rights.

There exist a two-dimensional Noetherian regular local domain (B,n) of
mixed characteristic (0,5), with 5 in n^2, and an integral Quillen class
 x in K_4(B) such that x != 0 and x|Frac(B) = 0.

There are no restrictions on the residue field, completeness, presentation,
finite-type status, ramification index, support, class order or construction.
An existence proof must provide one such pair or prove its existence. A
refutation must show K_4(B)->K_4(Frac B) injective for EVERY such B.
A nonlocal ring, coefficient-only class, odd-degree example, conditional
survival statement or failed-template obstruction does not solve G4.

This is a NEW eligible freeze following the parent-directed exclusion of
family161 for prior source-family use. excluded-buc-scope.md preserves that
ineligible question unchanged; G4 is not advertised as solving BUC.

Operation identified before sustained work: preserve the supported integral
K3 class after degree-one Laurent-symbol multiplication AND passage to an
actual two-dimensional regular LOCAL ring. The specific model is
 B0=A[z]_(m_A A[z]), residue F5(z), where A is the source quintic local
ring, and the candidate is the image of c*[z]. Standard Bass suspension
only supplies the NONLOCAL Laurent ring; the crucial local survival is an
additional operation. Source Bott multiplication changes degree by TWO
and alone cannot reach K4. Alternative constructions are allowed.

Primary-operation screen: Weibel V.8 gives a split Laurent suspension,
not survival under arbitrary further localization. Feld arXiv:2608.05005v2
Theorem1.1/Corollary5.1 distinguish a mod3 kernel from an integral kernel:
an integral lift can have nonzero divisible generic image. The invertible-
coefficient hypotheses in arXiv:2608.18789v1 do not apply to mod5 detection
on a mixed-(0,5) local ring. No primary result read supplies G4.
