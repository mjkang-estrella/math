# T111I110 — frozen research target, not a theorem claim

Frozen 2026-10-19 UTC after the pre-freeze source/body screen and before development of the two-matroid proof. This file is not to be weakened if the proposed method fails.

There exists an absolute real c>0 such that for every finite labelled ground set E and every pair of matroids M1=(E,I1), M2=(E,I2), known to the rule before observations, there exists one measurable randomized online rule A_(M1,M2) with the following guarantee. For every collection of Borel probability laws (D_e)_(e in E) on finite nonnegative real numbers, take mutually independent variables S_e,V_e with S_e and V_e both of law D_e. Assume E[OPT(V)]<infinity, where OPT(v)=max{sum_(e in J) v_e:J in I1 intersection I2}. The rule receives exactly one sample S_e per element, no distribution description, and uses an independent initial random seed R. At each arrival it sees that element's label and actual value and irrevocably accepts or rejects it. For every measurable permutation chosen as a function of M1,M2, all laws, all S,V, and the entire R, the accepted set is in I1 intersection I2 at every prefix and has expected weight at least c E[OPT(V)]. The expectation includes all samples, values, rule randomness and any independent randomization of the order chooser.

The same single rule for a fixed matroid pair must work for every family of laws; c is independent of |E|, ranks, numerical values, matroid representation and the laws. Empty sets, loops, rank zero, ties, atoms and unbounded supports with finite expected optimum are included. No polynomial-time or oracle-query bound, numerical value of c, per-element retention, fixed-order restriction, hidden-randomness restriction, or third matroid is asserted.
