For the trivial line bundle on B², unweighted generation is trivial already (section 1). Its conclusion does NOT supply a section bounded by exp(phi/2), which can decay at arbitrarily deep boundary wells.

## Proposed additional operation, and contribution risk

The source constructs phi from a sum of regularized logarithms to forbid a SINGLE nonvanishing weighted scalar function. The additional operation is to scalarize those same factors into a family of infinite products while retaining a finite GLOBAL weight constant: the optimal reference coefficient makes the per-factor loss quadratic in an ℓ² perturbation. This supplies a rich basepoint-free weighted family despite the scalar no-minorant obstruction. The generic finite-generator and embedding operations then apply.

This is not automatically substantial. Cauchy–Schwarz, normal infinite products, and generic finite generation are elementary/classical individually. An independent contribution gate must decide whether their weighted assembly is a substantive extra construction or merely a routine consequence of the explicit source regularization. No claim of optimal dimension, general resolution for all negatively pinched manifolds, or worldwide novelty is made. A negative search is not the justification.
