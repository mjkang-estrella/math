# Pre-freeze mechanism: family 359 bounded higher-codimension coordinates

This is an exploratory operation, not a frozen theorem or a contribution verdict. Source truth is stipulated at the campaign pin.

The source permits any sufficiently large finite amplitude L, so integer L is available BEFORE selecting its metric parameter. Its potential is phi=psi+L sum_j U_j, with psi=-log(1-|z|²) and U_j=log((|1-aP_j|²+epsilon²)/(1+epsilon²)). Let c0=a/(1+epsilon²). For a complex c, elementary Cauchy–Schwarz gives

|1-cP|² <= [1+(1+epsilon²)² |c-c0|²/(a²epsilon²)] (|1-aP|²+epsilon²)/(1+epsilon²).

Select c_{j,l}=c0+2^{-j}t_l, l=1,2,3. The perturbation costs have a uniformly finite product on bounded t_l. The products q_l=product_j(1-c_{j,l}P_j) converge locally, since P_j decay geometrically on compact subballs. Their only zeros are zeros of factors. Three parameters t_l can avoid all simultaneous triples of factors: for each triple j1,j2,j3, the three polynomials P_j in two variables satisfy a nonzero polynomial relation; substituting independent rational targets 1/(c0+2^{-j_l}t_l) gives a proper parameter hypersurface. Countably many such hypersurfaces can be avoided. Taking f_l=q_l^L should give no common zero and |f_l|² <= C exp(phi-psi).

Then F(z,w)=(z1,z2,w f1(z),w f2(z),w f3(z)) is bounded, injective, immersive, and has a continuous local inverse on its image by division by a nonzero f_l. Its boundedness follows from |w|² exp(phi)<1. This could produce a bounded embedding into C5 of a source threefold which admits no everywhere nonsingular bounded map into C3. It would also imply a nondegenerate Caratheodory infinitesimal metric. These are prospective, not accepted claims.

Contribution risk: the generic zero-avoidance part is classical finite-generation/transversality in dimension two; the weighted infinite-product construction may itself be a standard scalarization of logarithms of Hilbert norms. Actual primary comparison must distinguish the new application from mere routine transfer before any nomination. No claim of optimal embedding dimension, proper embedding into a bounded target, Caratheodory completeness, or quantitative metric equivalence.
