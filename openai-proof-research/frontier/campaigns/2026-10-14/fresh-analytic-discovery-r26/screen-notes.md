The exact criterion is union over one fixed d, intersection over crowding j, limsup over n of finite unions [r-4^-n,r). It remains unchanged with liminf. The retained center r is an algebraic unit whose neighboring degree-dn polynomial coefficient prefixes occur in at least 2^n/d orthants, each containing at least j(2r)^n distinct vectors. Realization makes each given near-cancelling prefix into an irreducible polynomial root just above the *fixed* lambda. The converse obtains the many prefixes from singularity of mu_lambda. It does not produce fresh singular parameters or assert any retained interval is made of singular parameters.

Potential constructive mechanism examined: nested intervals avoiding successively listed algebraic numbers, while keeping the same d and enforcing every crowding level. Exact unpaid interface: after a finite avoidance stage, there is no proved retained-root density/branching statement inside the surviving interval, uniformly in the fixed d and later j. Thus no transcendental-singularity nomination, no weakened biased/degree variant, and no claimed impossibility theorem.
