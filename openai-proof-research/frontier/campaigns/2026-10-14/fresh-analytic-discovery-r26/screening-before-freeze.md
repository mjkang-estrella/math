## Positive operation comparison

The stipulated unit-charge source's introduction, full many-body section and full implementation section were read. Neither gives a uniform discretization of arbitrary inputs or arbitrary quantum witnesses. Binary-charge companion introduction was read in full: it explicitly disclaims QMA membership. Binary charges can be exponentially large; the present unit-charge target does not silently extend to them.

O'Gorman–Irani–Whitfield–Fefferman 2103.08215v1 Definition 1 and Theorem 1 proof use a supplied finite orbital basis with efficiently computable integrals. Published PRX Quantum 3 (2022) 020322 Section V distinguishes complete space. Published Appendix D, read through its proof ending, adds unit nuclei to their specially chosen Gaussian basis, proving a finite-matrix perturbation bound. The basis is retained. This is not an unrestricted-space lower bound or membership proof.

Zheng–Leng–Liu–Wu 2411.05120v1, full Section 3.1/Theorem 3.3 proof (extracted lines 350–486), is a closer upper-bound antecedent: C1 polynomially bounded O(1)-body potentials on Dirichlet boxes with all side lengths between inverse-polynomial and polynomial have a StoqMA ground-energy algorithm. Its finite-difference estimate is attributed to classical finite elements, then its unary embedding uses polynomially many spatial grid sites per coordinate. It does not treat Coulomb singularities, full-space spectral infima, the fermionic restriction, or exponentially long boxes. Smooth bounded box membership and grid encoding themselves receive no novelty credit. A binary representation of an exponentially long grid alone is also not claimed new.

Kivlichan–Wiebe–Babbush–Aspuru-Guzik 1608.05696v2 Theorem 4/Corollary 5 and operative Lemmas 9–15 estimate real-space evolution assuming momentum cutoff along both evolutions. Their general supnorm/volume bound contains (kmax L/pi)^(eta D/2); the improved polynomial-spacing corollary assumes additional uniform derivative bounds. The prospective energy-form argument instead uses exact shifted L2 sampling on a bandlimited space, so it does not require unknown high derivatives of the Coulomb eigenfunction. Childs–Leng–Li–Liu–Zhang 2203.17006v3 Section 2.2 and 2.5 supply Fourier simulation for smooth periodic solutions with a high-derivative parameter; Section 2.5's periodic replacement is heuristic for sufficiently large boxes and modifies Coulomb at short distance. Neither compared statement supplies the uniform nonbinding compactification or all-witness form bound. Schuch–Verstraete 0712.0483v2 main text distinguishes finite Hubbard membership and its designed-field realization; this is not an all-nuclear-input membership theorem.

These are bounded actual-primary comparisons, not a worldwide novelty certificate. The proposed integrated uniform interface is retained for independent contribution review; standard Hardy, IMS, spectral truncation, Fourier sampling, QFT, and QMA amplification remain credited tools. The fact that the source omits membership is not evidence of novelty by itself.
