                                                   Abstract
          We construct a sequence of genuine ﬁnite negative-type metrics  √    with n points whose ﬁrst
      uniform-average distortion into the trace class S1 (H) is at least c log n/(log log n)9/2 , uniformly
      over all complex Hilbert spaces H. The construction uses the multichart Hilbert kernel of the
      pinned uniform-sparsest-cut source. Its scalar coordinate selection is replaced by a dimension-
      independent matrix selection: universal scalar moment matching gives spectral cutoﬀs for all
      matrix tuples, and a positive Gaussian dual pairing averages these cutoﬀs without requiring
      them to commute. We track the enlarged chart number through the kernel, rounding mesh and
      triangle repair. Finite-rank compression, Hermitian dilation and uniform copies preserve the
      full statement. The result is new to this project; a bounded search found no antecedent, but
      literature priority is not claimed.


1    Statement, conventions and source dependence

8    Comparison with other trace-class operations
The precise claim is new to this project, with no antecedent found in a bounded search. The
individual tools above are standard; no global priority claim follows from the search or from the
shortness of the matrix bridge. We record the operations that distinguish the statement, rather
than claiming that alternative methods cannot ever apply.

                                                  10
8.1   Dimension reduction, snowﬂakes and quantum expansion
Naor–Pisier–Schechtman [6] study dimension reduction for subsets already embedded in trace class,
including embeddings into arbitrary low-dimensional subspaces. Their discussion explains why
scalar coordinate sampling does not automatically have a matricial version. It does not provide the
all-target-dimensions obstruction for our negative-type source family. Naor–Young [7], Question 13
and its discussion, concern Heisenberg groups and trace-class nonembedding. Our metrics are
diﬀerent; no conclusion about those groups is proved here.
    Kush–Nikolov–Tang [8], Theorem 34 of the cited preprint, embed the p/2-snowﬂake of Schatten
p into Hilbert space with constant average distortion for 1 ≤ p ≤ 2. At p = 1 the source distance has
been replaced by its square root. Composing
                                       √         with a trace-class map of our negative-type metric
therefore controls a Hilbert map for d, which is already Euclidean. This does not supply the
needed contraction of ﬁrst averages for d itself.
    Escudero Gutiérrez–Muguruza [14] compare expansion notions for compressed blocks of bis-
tochastic matrix tuples. Their Section 3.2 distinguishes that operation from metric Poincaré in-
equalities for arbitrary maps. We do not derive an arbitrary-map inequality from channel expansion,
and we do not claim to settle their general Question 2.
