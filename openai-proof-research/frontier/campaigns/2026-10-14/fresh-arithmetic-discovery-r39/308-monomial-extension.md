# Prefreeze monomial extension: technical, not selected

After the ideal-power transfer was assessed as technical, a broader finite-colength strongly stable monomial ideal J in Q[t0,...,tn] was considered. Strong stability means that replacing any factor ti in a monomial of J by tj for j<i stays in J. No target was frozen and no proof or contribution gate was requested.

In the same relative de Rham model, Dti=-delta bi only uses earlier tj. Thus J is differential-stable. The triangular Koszul coordinate changes have ti mapped to a unit times ti plus a sum of earlier tj with coefficients in the earlier exterior algebra. Strong stability preserves J under both the change and its triangular inverse. The finite monomial complement gives a finite-cell model exactly as in 308-positive-transfer.md. The universal calculation is Koszul(X+t) over Q[X,t]/J(t), whose homology is Q[X]/J(-X); its finite monomial filtration has residue-field layers. This is again the same regular-sequence base-change mechanism.

Actual source 07-cobordism.tex lines1–139, lemma cob:regularity and its proof, were then read. They give image(xi)=unit*vi plus a polynomial in earlier generators, and ideal membership in (p,v1,...,v(i-1)). Strong stability likewise preserves J under this triangular substitution. Consequently the coordinate issue does not provide a new unpaid mechanism: it is elementary triangular invariance of a strongly stable ideal.

This extension is not nominated. It broadens the same classical finite de Rham transfer and adds an elementary monomial-invariance check, rather than supplying the substantial new construction sought by the campaign. The whole finite-diagram/all-degrees descent discussion in 308-positive-transfer.md remains a necessary interface; no unconditional reviewed realization theorem is asserted here. No general classification of invariant BP ideals, fixed-prime simultaneous construction, or infinite tower is claimed. Primary reading limits remain those of the preceding note; no literature search or novelty clearance for arbitrary strongly stable thickenings was completed.
