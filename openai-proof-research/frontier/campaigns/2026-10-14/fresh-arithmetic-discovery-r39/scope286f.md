# Frozen scope T286F: finite fusion subcategories of twisted lattice factors

For every countably infinite ICC group Gamma abstractly commensurable with a lattice in a finite nonempty product of groups H_i(k_i)^+, where each k_i is R, C, or a finite extension of Q_p (primes may differ), each H_i is connected adjoint absolutely simple over k_i, and each H_i(k_i)^+ is noncompact and has property(T), and for every normalized scalar cocycle mu:Gamma×Gamma→T, put M=L_mu(Gamma).

Claim to prove: every full replete unitary fusion subcategory D of the category of bifinite M-M correspondences is group-theoretical, meaning categorically Morita equivalent to a pointed unitary fusion category. Here D contains the tensor unit, is closed under finite direct sums, all subobjects, Connes fusion and conjugation, and has finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects. There is no irreducibility, cocompactness, torsion-freeness or simple-ambient-factor restriction on the lattice, and no finite-type or finite-order restriction on mu.

Consequently, for every finite-index finite-depth inclusion P⊂M of II_1 factors (with the usual normal unital inclusion), both even fusion categories of its standard invariant are group-theoretical. No irreducibility assumption on the inclusion is added. This consequence is part of the chosen scope. The statement does not claim all subfactors have finite depth, all bifinite correspondences have finite image, or all integral fusion categories are group-theoretical.
