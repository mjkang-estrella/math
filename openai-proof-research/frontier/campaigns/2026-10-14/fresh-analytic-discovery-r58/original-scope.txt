T337E — exact sharp filling equality in proper CAT(0) spaces
Scope frozen at 2026-10-18T10:18:53.247434+00:00

For every integer n>=2, every proper CAT(0) space X, and every nonzero compactly supported integral n-current T in X with boundary zero, set
  V_X(T)=inf{M(S): S is an integral (n+1)-current in X, boundary S=T}.
Integral currents here have finite mass and finite boundary mass; compact support of candidate fillings is not imposed in this infimum. Let omega_(n+1) be Euclidean unit-ball volume and c_n=((n+1)^((n+1)/n) omega_(n+1)^(1/n))^(-1).

The target is the equivalence:
  V_X(T)=c_n M(T)^((n+1)/n)
if and only if there are R>0 and an isometric embedding F of the closed Euclidean (n+1)-ball of radius R into X such that
  T=F#(boundary [[B_R]]).
An orientation reversal is permitted through F and the choice of orientation on the Euclidean model. Necessarily R=(M(T)/((n+1)omega_(n+1)))^(1/n).

There is no ambient-dimensional, smoothness, multiplicity, connectedness, manifold-support, local-geodesic-completeness, or topological assumption. The conclusion in particular forces unit multiplicity. No claim about stability at positive deficit is included. No proper-CAT(0) assumption is weakened or extra lower-curvature bound inserted.
