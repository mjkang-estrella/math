# Feasible formal scope

No Lean theorem has been built or claimed here. Formal escalation is for the parent after separate full-proof and contribution gates.

A useful small first target is the scalar certificate, over real r≥0 and w in a real Hilbert space: from ||w||²=r²−r⁴/4 conclude that (w,r²/2) has distance1 from (0,1) and norm r. This can be proved by ring identities and square-root nonnegativity. A second small target is the two-sided derivative squeeze: a_++a_−≤0 together with ell≥−a_+/r and −ell≥−a_−/r, r>0, forces equality. These are genuine scoped algebra, not a formal proof of T337E.

A broader formal interface could import explicit classical contracts for the completed CAT(0) tangent cone/log contraction and strong chart differential, parallel-set splitting, integral-current chart/pushforward/constancy, compact minimum fillings and Stadler's density-one rigidity. The new endpoint construction would then need a real formal measure-theoretic noncancellation argument, including chart density, all-sequence rescaling and control of the off-chart mass. It must not be replaced by an axiom that directly asserts nonzero spherical degree or the target equality theorem.

The n>2 branch needs explicit source contracts for radial variation, subthreshold minimizer and endpoint chord estimates; an axiom saying simply 'sharp equality implies sphere' would hide the claimed deduction. Any conditional formal theorem must list all imported contracts and distinguish them from compiled proof. No numerical or integrity diagnostic counts as a formal mathematical gate.
