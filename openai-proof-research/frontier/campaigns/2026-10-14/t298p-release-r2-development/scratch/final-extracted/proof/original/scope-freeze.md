# Full two-variable finite-entropy separation target

Frozen before unified proof assembly, after actual source/companion and primary compression screening. Earlier discussion of relative compression and many-coordinate smoothing was exploratory screening, not a frozen target.

There exist a von Neumann algebra M with faithful normal tracial state tau and exactly two bounded self-adjoint elements X,Y in M such that

    -infinity < chi(X,Y) < chi-star(X,Y) < infinity.

Here chi is the original operator-norm-cutoff, limsup-over-matrix-sizes microstates free entropy, and chi-star is the standard nonmicrostates free entropy, with the same normalization as source298. The two elements need not generate the ambient algebra. No relative entropy, ultrafilter-only entropy, additional hidden coordinates, infinite-entropy gap, or changing number of variables replaces this assertion.

Source298 supplies a strict finite gap for a large fixed tuple, with explicit construction using p=2^64 signal coordinates and one spectral label. Source287 does not change the length or joint law of transported tuples. Classical matrix-entry compression pays a relative-entropy operation, not the required unconditional pair gap. The unpaid mechanism is quantitative preservation of the gap when removing scalar matrix-unit conditioning, or a different two-coordinate entropy witness. This target is not proved or nominated.
