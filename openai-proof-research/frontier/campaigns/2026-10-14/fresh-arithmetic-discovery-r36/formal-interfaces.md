# Feasible scoped formal interfaces

No formal implementation or formal gate is claimed. These are proposed interfaces for a later authorized scoped check, not replacements for the full theorem.

1. Matrix-word differentiation over a characteristic-zero field: derive (6.1), the relation derivative (Ad_A−Ad_C)S, and (6.4) for commuting A,B. This can be expressed with dual numbers and finite matrix products; no analytic geometry is required.
2. Principal-minor differential lemma: from stationary minors of sizes1,2,3 and nonzero off-diagonal/triangle factors derive E=[h,M]. Formalize the pair antisymmetry and triangle coboundary separately, with the r=3 determinant case explicit.
3. Vandermonde extraction: distinct nonzero subset products and zero weighted sums for finitely many consecutive powers imply each principal-minor derivative vanishes. The finite extraction is exact; infinite powers are a convenience in the exposition.
4. Generic joint-system witness: conditional linear kernel reduction can be formalized algebraically. The existence of a nonempty parameter open uses complex compactness plus symmetric/skew intersection and must remain an explicit external analytic contract unless fully implemented.
5. Ribbon certificate: formalize noninterleaving turn pairs in the stated cyclic rose order, and the boundary permutation. A theorem connecting this combinatorial certificate to embedded curves and boundary-fixing Dehn twists is a separate topology contract, not discharged by a Python list check.
6. Arithmetic selection: Pisot/Galois independence, dense integral elementary subgroups, constructible nonempty-open intersection, and finite spectral dominance are distinct obligations. No test of finitely many algebraic units substitutes for them.
7. Global bridge: irreducibility of the determinant-one surface variety, smooth irreducible quotient, Goldman symplectic/twist contracts, finite spectral-cover/Baire argument, ideal-preserving local flows and submersion are external geometry interfaces for any modest scoped formalization. A passing matrix certificate alone would not certify T027O.

The full paper proof remains responsible for all seven interfaces at every frozen g,r. No narrowing to finite rank, marked representations, the tangent lemma alone, or an assumed-generic arithmetic point is permitted.
