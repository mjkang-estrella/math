   for unmeasurable colourings). Downloaded PDF and pdftotext extraction.
   Read extracted lines 2670–2693: complete Lemma B.1 and the start of B.2.
   Additional HTML excerpts were inspected, not the whole paper. Lemma B.1
   supplies exactly the fixed-density-point compactness operation.
2. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18907v1 (Mikhail Koshelev, Andrey Kupavskii,
   Vsevolod Voronov, Ordinary and measurable colorings of Euclidean three-space).
   Read extracted lines 1–120 (introduction/statement/scope), 679–815
   (complete Section 7 Lemma 7.1 and start of consequences), and 900–1100
   (appendix material on amenability, weak compactness, conditional
   expectation, joint Borel representatives and beginning of character
