Starting from the stipulated self-dual partitional source pair, one elongation
followed by one truncation gives two connected matroids, still self-dual on
the same labeled countable set. They admit no independent covering, no
packing/covering partition, and no intersection witness. Their finite subsets
remain independent, as do those of their duals. The proof does not claim
finitary, cofinitary, tame, graphic, or representable examples.

The construction is mathematically explicit, but the remaining operations
are supplied by antecedents: standard elongation permits at most one error
relative to old independence; standard truncation makes old bases circuits;
the complement-symmetric single-exchange family preserves self-duality; and
the source ordinal rank is already invariant modulo an ideal containing
finite sets. Its original descent therefore survives those errors directly.
