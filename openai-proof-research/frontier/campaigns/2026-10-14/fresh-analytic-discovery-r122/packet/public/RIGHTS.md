# Rights and separation

`public/` contains original r122 research prose, project-derived integrity code, and bibliographic/integrity metadata. The verifier follows the existing r121 integrity pattern, with r122 registers, exact modes, and a dedicated copied-control runner. It does not contain upstream or external article bodies.

`private/context`, `private/sources`, `private/primary`, and `private/history` retain existing project, pinned upstream and third-party materials under their original rights. Their presence is for local inspection and reproducibility; it grants no new license or publication authorization. Public source registers identify those bodies without republishing them. Parent integration must preserve this separation.

No release, public upload, submission, contact or browser UI action occurred. This is a research archive, not a manuscript or a mathematical acceptance package.
