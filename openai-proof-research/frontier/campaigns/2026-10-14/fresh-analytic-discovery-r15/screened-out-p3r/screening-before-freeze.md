## Concrete unpaid operation

Investigate a **reversal of three-channel phase ordering in the full negative spectral trace**, with every two-channel subsystem unitarily identical between the two models. Take three equal scalar optimizer wells with rank-one polarizations. Change the sign of exactly one Gram overlap while preserving all overlap magnitudes. At coalescence the channel Gram eigenvalues control the trace. Far apart, two-body terms cancel between the two configurations; a resonant triangle survives at a smaller exponential scale. Its sign need not match the coalesced sign.

This operation is more specific than asking for a stronger Lieb–Thirring constant, deleting a famous hypothesis, or adding an error bound to equality classification. The obstacle is extracting the complete spectral trace difference at order R^2 exp(-4aR), including actual smooth overlapping tails and excluding shallow extra eigenvalues. An O(exp(-aR)) eigenvalue splitting theorem is insufficient.

## Plausible route identified before freeze

Use gamma=1, r=2, w_a=3a^2 sech^2(2ax). Choose positive-definite real 3x3 Gram matrices G+ with all off-diagonals rho and G- with g12=g23=rho,g13=-rho, 0<rho<1/2. At coincident centers, their eigenvalue triples are (1+2rho,1-rho,1-rho) and (1-2rho,1+rho,1+rho). Factorization evaluates the full trace, and a strictly negative third derivative gives the first sign.

For separated centers 0,R,2R use a three-component Birman–Schwinger operator whose (i,j) block is g_ij sqrt(w_i)(-d^2+k^2)^(-1)sqrt(w_j). Gram bounds by positive scalar multiples of I should control the **entire number** of negative eigenvalues. Factor its determinant into three local determinants and a small off-diagonal interaction determinant on a fixed k-contour about a. Phase switching cancels every even closed walk. The first surviving walk is the triangle, with length 4R and three local simple poles. Its contour residue gives R^2 exp(-4aR). Prove explicit Hilbert–Schmidt errors for replacing cross-well kernels by their separated rank-one versions; do not replace the actual potentials by compactly supported wells or point interactions.

This is a plausible full proof route. It does not imply a contribution verdict. Standard determinant/loop operations have already been credited; independent contribution review must decide whether the resulting reversal is substantial or technical.
