# Mathematical checkpoints and proof-risk audit

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.

These are author deductions, not independent verdicts. The original full
scope remains in original-scope.md, authenticated by freeze-record.json.

1. **Selection checkpoint.** Rejected finitary critical IID coding as an
   attractive but differently unpaid certificate problem. Rejected scalar
   normality alone as insufficiently distinct from Yu–Polyanskiy. Selected
   actual posterior space/time convergence with an increasing-depth path
   replacement as its unpaid operation. See screening-before-freeze.md.
2. **Normalization checkpoint.** With Q=E Y^2, R=E Y^4, the fourth recursion
   gives R=3Q^2+O(t^(3/2)). The exact second recursion contains the child
   conditional mean theta E[tanh(Y)psi(Y)]. It gives
   ((b-1)/b)Q^2=t+O(t^(3/2)), hence q(t)=kappa sqrt(t)+O(t).
   Omitting this extra theta changes the sibling term and is invalid.
   All Taylor remainders are global bounds paid by the source sixth moment.
3. **Path checkpoint.** Fixed-time moments alone do not establish path
   tightness. Source component locality and continuity make M a martingale
   in its own filtration; Doob plus the own-observation Gaussian tail give
   E sup|Y|^p=O(t^(p/4)). This pays L2 sup-norm nonlinear errors. Full-tree
   filtration martingality of a component posterior is never asserted.
4. **Growing-depth checkpoint.** A fixed-depth CLT limit would be circular.
   One explicit n=floor(log_b(1/epsilon)/8) pays expansion and dependence
   at once: sqrt(N)epsilon^(1/4)->0 and N epsilon^(1/4)->0. Conditional
   cut-root spins may be arbitrarily correlated. The product law is used
   only after the history-density total-variation estimate.
5. **Martingale-array checkpoint.** The summand distribution changes with
   epsilon. Uniform fourth maxima bound the second bracket moment by BDG.
   Independent bracket averages concentrate; monotonicity upgrades a finite
   grid to uniform clock convergence. DDS and the Brownian modulus then give
   C convergence, including t=0. No unsupported fdd-to-tightness step remains
   in the proposed proof.
6. **Spatial checkpoint.** Individual-site Gaussianity is insufficient for
   joint Gaussianity. A finite hull represents the full vector as a finite
   deterministic combination of disjoint outward cavity processes. Their
   entire histories decouple in TV. Boundary-sum conservation b theta^2=1
   makes the limit independent of the chosen hull and yields phi_d.
7. **Completion checkpoint.** The candidate provides the full frozen PCW
   proof, not a narrowed scalar theorem. Every source-specific premise has
   an exact label and hypothesis contract. No mathematical experiment,
   historical program, manuscript or Lean package was run or created.

Remaining validation obligations: independent proof review of the entire
path argument and an independent operation-level contribution review,
especially against older branching Gaussian perturbation methods. The
author claims neither gate. A full proposed proof can still be incorrect
or technical. Integrity verification establishes neither mathematical
truth nor substantiality.
