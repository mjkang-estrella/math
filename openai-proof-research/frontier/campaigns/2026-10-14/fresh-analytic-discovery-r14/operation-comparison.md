# Primary operation comparison

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0. No independent
contribution verdict or worldwide-priority assertion is made.

The complete proposed theorem is PCW, including its actual posterior-path
coupling, all fixed finite vertex sets, uniform path topology and time zero.
The scalar constant, scalar Gaussianity, and the Gaussian-wave covariance
are not separately nominated. A stronger statement than the pinned title
does not by itself establish a substantial contribution.

## Established operations that pay much of the argument

**The pinned body already pays small fields.** P's proof of
`lem:critical-tail`, not merely its abstract, uses b theta^p<1 for p>2 to
propagate the critical second-moment scale to all orders. Its posterior
and continuous-version sections pay the component recursion and martingale
versions. PCW does not claim these as new. Doob gives its maximum moments.

**The scalar perturbative core is established.** Yu–Polyanskiy,
*Broadcasting on Trees Near Criticality: Perturbation Theory*, Theorem1,
Lemmas2–4 and Remark2, establish a Gaussian boundary-LLR limit when the
channel approaches the reconstruction threshold from the reconstructible
side. Their moment recursion and repeated linearized branching are clear
antecedents of candidate Section3 and its scalar normality interpretation.
The weak Gaussian survey here changes the forcing balance; that change of
scalar parameter is not the proposed substantial contribution. [Author
body](https://people.lids.mit.edu/yp/homepage/data/isit2023_uniqbp.pdf).

**Survey fixed points are an established subject.** Yu–Polyanskiy,
arXiv:2211.15242v2, Theorems1 and15, give uniqueness and convergence for BP
with nontrivial symmetric survey distributions. The read proof of Theorem15
uses comparison and monotone sandwiching. It does not identify a joint
small-survey observation-time process. Its fixed-point distribution can
identify individual-time laws without specifying their coupling as survey
strength changes. [Versioned body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.15242v2).

**The observation framework is established.** Nam–Sly–Zhang,
arXiv:2012.09484v2, §2.2 and Lemma2.1, use Brownian Gaussian observations
and posterior drift. Propositions2.2–2.3 give finite-volume/root comparisons
in their strict parameter subrange. PCW uses the same observation model;
neither that model nor its posterior martingale is a new construction.
[Versioned body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09484v2).

**The Gaussian spatial law is established.** Elon,
arXiv:0907.5065v2, Theorem1.1 and Lemmas2.1–2.2, construct invariant Gaussian
waves and calculate their covariance. The spectral-edge specialization is
exactly phi_d in PCW. Candidate Section7 constructs its finite-hull version
to establish consistency but does not claim a new Gaussian-wave covariance.
[Versioned body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.5065v2).

**Averaged martingale brackets and Brownian time change are standard.**
Once an independent average with uniformly bounded fourth maxima is
available, Section4 is a standard concentration/time-change argument. It
must not be advertised as a new martingale CLT. Likewise finite-hull
linearization and critical boundary-sum conservation are elementary once
the component functional limit is available.

## The operation the candidate actually has to supply

The actual cavity posterior paths are correlated through their planted
spins. Independently replacing them at a single time, or taking a fixed
depth then claiming CLT, does not prove the frozen process statement.
Candidate Section5 supplies one depth n(epsilon) increasing with vanishing
observation time, with N=b^n, and simultaneously establishes

    L2 uniform-path error <= C sqrt(N) epsilon^(1/4),
    TV joint leaf-path dependence <= C N epsilon^(1/4).

Both vanish with N comparable to epsilon^(-1/8). The second estimate is a
whole-history spin-likelihood argument, valid despite arbitrary correlations
of the cut spins. The first estimate controls accumulated own-noise and
nonlinear message errors on the whole interval. This permits bracket
self-averaging for independent component posterior martingales with changing
law; it pays tightness at the singular time clock. Section6 then obtains
the joint vertex/time law from disjoint outward histories, rather than
inferring joint Gaussianity from individual-site normality.

This is the concrete operation identified before freeze and carried through
in the proposed proof. The bounded primary-body screen did not find that
operation already supplied for this critical forced-posterior process. That
bounded observation is not a novelty certificate. The proof uses standard
ingredients throughout; an independent contribution reviewer must decide
whether their simultaneous critical path replacement is substantial or a
technical extension of the older scalar branching normal-form method.
No exact-duplicate exclusion is substituted for that decision.
