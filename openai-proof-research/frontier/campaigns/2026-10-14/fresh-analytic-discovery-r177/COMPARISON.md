## Source and classical payments

Source308 already pays ordinary finite first-power Smith–Toda realization at every height with a varying prime, the canonical comodule and degree-zero generator, and the explicit companion at p=1009. These are not project discoveries in this round. Replacing the finite realization by an abstract BP-module presentation would discard an operative part of the source proof.

BSS, *Chromatic homotopy theory is asymptotically algebraic*, §6, supplies actual compact-operad descent: finite operadic diagrams descend through filtered colimits, and the argument can be made over every nonprincipal ultrafilter to obtain a cofinite prime statement. Its own Moore spectra are in an E_(n,p)-local category. Source308 supplies a different ordinary finite realization interface. Once that interface is present, descending a fixed finite package of multiplications, units and homotopies is routine. An arbitrary-ultrafilter argument for a fixed finite package cannot give one fixed prime for all heights, a numerical cutoff of 1009, or all infinitely many coherence conditions at once. None of these finite-diagram refinements is offered as a nominee.

Hopkins–Smith, *Nilpotence and stable homotopy theory II*, proves existence and compatibility of suitable powers of periodic self-maps. Its actual generalized Moore construction, Proposition 5.14, uses a sequence of prime-power exponents. The proof passes to powers using nilpotence and then takes a cofiber. It cannot be read as first-power realization at a prescribed prime; nor does eventual commutation of suitable powers provide a product on the fixed cofiber X. The relevant original proof pages, not just its theorem headline, were read. The full transitive nilpotence proof was not reread and is not a new premise in the attempted product extension.

Lee–Ravenel's original beta-nilpotence proof reads the modified May page, its explicit 24 exterior classes, the differential delta_(2p−1)B=h12 A−h11 C, and the module action on V(3). It eliminates candidate differentials using that action; it does not assert that every same-weight candidate in a larger fourth-coordinate problem vanishes. The seven-page author version was read in full. Its lower bound and Toda's upper bound also make clear why a scalar annihilation calculation is not a new discovery by itself.

Toda 1968 supplies the extended-power relations, including p gamma=0 implying alpha gamma^p=0 in its stated setting. This was read from the actual original article and the crucial formulas were checked in a rendered page. The article itself says that details of an earlier lemma are omitted; this is recorded rather than claiming a complete reading of an unavailable transitive proof. The companion supplies a complete finite three-cell proof of its needed Y-valued relation. The stronger sphere-valued carry null-map in DERIVATIONS.md follows directly from the companion's integral, free coefficient computation and is compatible with Toda's sphere relation. It removes the need for a product on the target Z in the retained scalar annihilation operation, but does not evaluate the secondary product obstruction.

Strickland, *Products on MU-modules*, was read late when the cofiber-square approach became relevant. Section 3 gives actual quotient-product operations: in the derived category of modules over a strictly commutative ring spectrum R with even pi_*R, a regular element x has null action on R/x; the cofiber then splits in the required way, yielding products. The proof of the null action uses pi_(|x|+1)(R/x)=0, and associativity uses another odd-group vanishing. Its later commutativity correction uses division by two only after a product exists. Here Y has not been made into such a strictly commutative even ring spectrum, and BP_*Y being even is not pi_*Y being even. Thus this proof cannot simply be imported to turn X into an ordinary ring spectrum. Reading this actual mechanism prevents treating odd-prime averaging as an automatic existence theorem for a quotient product.
