# T369H: frozen full quantitative question

For every nonempty bounded simply connected open set Omega in R^2 with C^infinity boundary, let mu>0 be its first positive Neumann eigenvalue and let V be its real eigenspace. Assume dim V=2. For every ordered real basis (u,v) of V, put F=(u,v), using the smooth extensions to the closure.

Is there a finite constant C=C(Omega,u,v)>0 such that, for every x,y in closure(Omega),

    |x-y| <= C |F(x)-F(y)|^(1/2)?

All pairs, all boundary points and the full stated smooth simply connected class are included. No convexity, symmetry, real-analytic boundary, pointwise positive boundary-Jacobian derivative, compact interior restriction, or spectral-gap-to-third-mode hypothesis is imposed. No uniform domain-independent C is requested.

Status at freeze: UNRESOLVED. This is a research question, not an asserted theorem. The qualitative global diffeomorphism/homeomorphism and smooth star-shaped boundary are already the technical I214/I218/I219 package and are not a new nomination. Pinned source thm:main is stipulated; no audit of its truth occurs.

The proposed unpaid operation is quantitative control of the inverse at the Neumann-degenerate boundary. A weaker exponent, an interior-only estimate, a local analytic countermodel, or merely re-proving I214 does not solve T369H. The new local countermodel to a proof route is supporting work, not a counterexample to this global first-mode question.
