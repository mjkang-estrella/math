\section{Precisely scoped formal support}\label{app:formal}
The completed Lean implementation~\cite{formalimpl} and separate independent
review~\cite{formalreview} support only the deterministic real algebra at
Proposition~\ref{prop:B}. The reviewed files are supplied unchanged, with
origin SHA-256 hashes and detached package anchors in the support metadata.
There are 21 implementation support declarations (three definitions and
18 theorems), 20 implementation control theorems, and 15 independent
semantic, type and use-site audit declarations.

\paragraph{Exact universal interface.}
For all real $\alpha,\beta,p,q,d,P,N$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:formal-hypotheses}
\begin{gathered}
 \alpha>0,\quad\beta>0,\quad\alpha+\beta=2,\quad
 0<p<1,\quad q=1-p,\\
 d\ge0,\quad P>0,\quad N>0,\quad 2dp^2<P,\quad 2dq^2\le N,
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
\path|T088P133.rational_mixture_lt_one| proves exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:formal-bound}
 (1+d)\left[\frac{p\alpha}{\alpha+P/p}
             +\frac{q\beta}{\beta+N/q}\right]<1.
\end{equation}
The declaration \path|rational_mixture_le_one| replaces the strict first
moment premise and conclusion by non-strict inequalities.
The algebraic declaration \path|conditional_means| derives $q>0$, $P/p>0$,
$N/q>0$, $p(P/p)=P$ and $q(N/q)=N$; it is not a theorem about conditional
expectations. The two \path|moment_lt_iff| and \path|moment_le_iff|
declarations convert $2dp^2<P$ or $\le P$ to $2dp<P/p$ or $\le P/p$
when $p>0$. Denominator positivity is derived in the mixture proofs.
There is no division by $d$, and $d=0$ is included.

\paragraph{Brackets and the unchanged paper proof.}
Write $A=P/p$, $B=N/q$. The definitions \path|firstBracket|,
\path|secondBracket| and \path|mixture| are respectively
\begin{align*}
 F&=pA\beta+qB\alpha-d\alpha\beta,\\
 G&=AB-d(p\alpha B+q\beta A),\\
 M&=(1+d)\bigl[p\alpha/(\alpha+A)+q\beta/(\beta+B)\bigr].
\end{align*}
The declarations \path|firstBracket_decomposition| and
\path|secondBracket_decomposition| prove the polynomial identities
\begin{align}\label{eq:formal-residuals}
 F&=p\beta(A-2dp)+q\alpha(B-2dq)+d(2p-\alpha)^2,\\
 G&=\bigl[\alpha B(A-2dp)+\beta A(B-2dq)\bigr]/2.\notag
\end{align}
The first uses $\alpha+\beta=2$ and $q=1-p$; the second uses only the
sum normalization. These are identities without sign premises.
The residual hypotheses give $F\ge0$ and $G>0$, since
$\alpha B(A-2dp)>0$. This is Lean's polynomial residual/square route
to the same first-bracket sign proved by Cauchy--Schwarz in the unchanged
paper proof. The names \path|firstBracket_nonneg|,
\path|secondBracket_nonneg| and \path|secondBracket_pos| record these signs.
The identities \path|cleared_slack| and \path|slack_identity| give
$1-M=(F+G)/[(\alpha+A)(\beta+B)]$; the latter takes nonzero denominators.
The declarations \path|mixture_le_one| and \path|mixture_lt_one| derive
the corresponding bounds using positive denominators.

The independent declarations in namespace \path|T088P133Review|
prove \path|original_first_bracket|, \path|original_second_bracket| and
\path|original_normalized_slack|. They identify $F/(\alpha\beta)$ and
$G/(\alpha\beta)$ with the two terms of \eqref{eq:two-brackets}, including
the weight $AB/(\alpha\beta)$. All nonzero divisors are explicit.
The eight exact interface contracts and \path|definition_contract|
fix the mixed wrappers, moment conversions, mean algebra, endpoint
interfaces and the three definition bodies.

The audit's \path|scalar_majorization_use| assumes, in addition to
\eqref{eq:formal-hypotheses}, an arbitrary real $L$ and the premise
\[
 L\le p\alpha/(\alpha+P/p)+q\beta/(\beta+N/q).
\]
It proves $(1+d)L<1$ by multiplication with $1+d\ge0$ and
\eqref{eq:formal-bound}. It does not define $L$ as an expectation or prove
this premise for one. In the paper that premise is \eqref{eq:mixture},
proved analytically using Lemma~\ref{lem:HNBUE}.

\paragraph{Endpoints and controls.}
The implementation derives $\alpha,\beta<2$ in
\path|positive_sum_two_bounds|. For all real $a,d,M$ with
$0<a<2$, $M>0$ and $2d\le M$, \path|one_sided_lt_one| proves
$(1+d)/(1+M/a)<1$. It validly requires no $d\ge0$.
The declarations \path|p_one_bound| and \path|p_zero_bound| instantiate
the reduced expressions in Proposition~\ref{prop:B}; they never substitute
a zero probability into the mixed-case rational denominators.
The independent \path|endpoint_expression| checks the equivalent fraction
$(1+d)a/(a+M)$, and \path|endpoint_negative_d| checks $d=-2$.

All 20 implementation controls have explicit elaborated types. They include
the asymmetric value $12/13$, $d=0$ value $1/3$, a non-strict equality
example, and countermodels after specified assumptions are removed.
The review independently checks ten exact rational configurations.
Two added-premise semantic mutations compile the support but are rejected
by the exact type contracts. Both historical eleven-case artifact suites
passed; those are integrity tests, not proofs of algebraic meaning.
These bounded controls do not assert that every hypothesis is individually
necessary, and they do not construct probability or polytope realizations.

\paragraph{Entirely unformalized obligations.}
No Lean declaration here proves actual-facet geometry or the nonzero
middle-vector exclusion; mixed-volume attainment, coarea or Gamma--Dirichlet;
distributions, log-concavity, HNBUE or expectation-to-mixture majorization;
the ten-pattern certificate or its probabilistic interpretation;
continuity, zero/repeated-knot assembly or the derivation of a strict moment;
simplex equality, every-six-facet geometric strictness or
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. The analytic and geometric proof remains necessary
in full. The adopted scoped formal PASS and this integration infer no
new mathematical, contribution, manuscript, acceptance, count or release gate.
