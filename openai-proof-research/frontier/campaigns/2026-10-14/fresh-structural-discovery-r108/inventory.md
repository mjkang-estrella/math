# Operation-level screening and selection

This is a bounded research screen, not a priority certificate. The complete
13,223-entry pinned tree was parsed, all paths indexed, and the complete
catalogue accessed and parsed for family navigation. The catalogue's entire
prose was not semantically read. Actual source passages and historical proof
operations, rather than title novelty, determined the exclusions below.

1. **Family114, common integer polymatroid bases.** The source counts each
   full integer vector once with binary capacities. Its
   `lem:polyhedral-controls` already obtains tight-chain spans, integral
   faces and bounded normal-cone descriptions. I29 combines precisely this
   active-normal rank with the previously paid covariance-trace entropy
   argument. That is an existing technical chain, not a new separator
   theorem. Projecting away coordinates changes multiplicities; I13 already
   exposes the missing uniform-support/fiber control. Neither entropy
   specialization nor projected counting was selected. Read actual
   `polyhedra.tex` proof passages and I29's argument; later checked the full
   main statement in `introduction.tex`. No new FPRAS proof is claimed.

2. **Family124, three-machine unit-job precedence scheduling.** The source's
   bounded-capacity list argument injects a relevant entry into a bounded
   number of locations. Historical r35 already extends the same injection
   accounting to fixed m using m+2. A new processor parameter alone would
   repeat that operation. Release constraints are excluded by the actual
   source model and would need additional structure, which was not supplied.
   Read `paper.tex`'s actual theorem/model and `lists.tex` through the
   capacity-three injection proof, plus r35's scheduling analysis.

3. **Family138, subset-sum algorithms.** The improved decision algorithm
   uses solution isolation and a phase checksum; the checksum proof has a
   specific importance-sampling variance calculation. The low-space
   companion preserves decision, not multiplicity counting. I17 already
   proves the exact relative-variance obstruction `(3/2)^k-1` for the
   squared-phase estimator on paired powers of three, even with an exact
   checksum. That obstruction does not rule out a high-support hybrid;
   this assignment found no new variance argument for that regime. Signed
   input/target encodings would be ordinary transfers. No counting target
   was frozen here. Source statement/variance passages and the actual I17
   calculation were read; the latter was cross-checked during final intake.

4. **Family184, correspondence coloring.** The full main statement gives
   `C_r Delta/log Delta` for fixed forbidden clique size; the round uses a
   global palette scale and entry-conflict potentials. I22 and r44 already
   investigated linear DP coloring in the Hadwiger number. Arbitrary edge
   matchings do not preserve a vertexwise palette-bin partition, and the
   fixed-r maximum-degree theorem does not substitute for a minor-order
   bound. These are concrete existing missing interfaces. The round setup
   and conclusion, main statement, and r44's preliminary DP screen were
   read. No local-degree or Hadwiger target was selected.

5. **Family104, selected exact stochastic game target T104AS.** The complete
   stochastic expected-liminf proof supplies discounted stabilization,
   gain/bias certificates, and exact binary quasipolynomial numerical
   labeling. The complete deterministic parity proof supplies uniform
   prefix bounds, strategy combination, extraction, bounded dominions and
   the one-full-branch cost accounting. I113/I116 already compare those
   operations and prove exact Blackwell policy/value extraction as technical
   work. We do not nominate policy extraction, a threshold shift, or a
   randomized-to-deterministic substitution. The unpaid proposed interface
   was the stochastic almost-sure conjunction with unrestricted priorities.
   The full scope was frozen in `scope.md`, before bridge development.

The fifth choice was motivated by two complete available engines and the
primary stochastic parity decomposition, rather than by claiming its central
step was easy. Development exposed a real asymmetric-dominion obstruction.
The selection did **not** produce a full proof. The target remains exactly
as frozen; its difficulty is not converted into a significance claim.

Family181 was an additional historical exclusion: r33's balanced-fold and
unbalanced-expander attempts, and r52/r54's continuation records, already
show that short linking is not the missing orientation-universal directed
cycle operation. No fresh family181 source theorem is used here. The
forbidden families092,101,174,188,197,224,277,298,300,369 were not developed.
In particular, r107's T174D and r71's T101G69 were not resumed.

## What was actually searched

`history-search.json` records content-search expressions, roots, exclusions,
and hashes of the returned passages. The searches cover actual Markdown
content under campaigns, iterations, and experiments; selected reports were
then opened for proof-operation comparison. Repeated frozen/release copies
were excluded. This is not an exhaustive semantic read of every historical
file, nor evidence that an unmatched operation has never appeared elsewhere.

No separate contribution or proof gate has been commissioned or fabricated.
