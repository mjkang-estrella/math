## A concrete quaternionic invariant calculation

Let g_0 be a quaternionic hyperbolic metric, in the curvature normalization of Humbert (sectional curvatures between -4 and -1), real dimension n=4m. For a local admissible orthonormal quaternionic frame I,J,K define on symmetric real two-tensors

    C_I S(v,w)=S(Iv,Iw),   T=C_I+C_J+C_K.

Each C_I is a self-adjoint isometric involution for the pointwise Hilbert–Schmidt inner product. For distinct I,J, C_I C_J=C_K=C_J C_I: the sign of IJ versus JI occurs in both tensor arguments and cancels. Therefore

    T^2=3 Id+2T,   P_+=(T+Id)/4,   P_-=(3Id-T)/4.

The projections are orthogonal, with eigenvalues 3 and -1 respectively. This conclusion is algebraic, with no choice of commuting differential operators. Under an SO(3) rotation J'_a=sum_b O_ab J_b, the sum of S(J'_a v,J'_a w) equals the old sum. Thus T is globally defined. The quaternionic rank-three bundle is parallel, so differentiating in a local frame gives an antisymmetric connection matrix whose two sums cancel: nabla T=0. Hence the rough Laplacian preserves these eigensubbundles. Trace(TS)=3 trace(S), so every minus tensor is tracefree, and Tg=3g.

Write S=S_+^0+S_-+hg, with S_+^0 tracefree in the plus bundle. This is an orthogonal parallel splitting. Applying Humbert's actually read Weitzenbock inequality (2.51) separately gives

    ||nabla S_+^0||^2 >= n ||S_+^0||^2,
    ||nabla S_-||^2 >= (n+12)||S_-||^2.

No assertion that the projections preserve divergence is needed here: the cited inequality is for arbitrary tracefree tensors. Denote the two-tensor part of the quaternionic operator by

    A S = (1/4)nabla^*nabla S -(1/2)S +(1/2)(tr S)g +(1/2)T S.

Using the standard Hilbert–Schmidt pairing (and Riemannian volume; multiplying all pairings by the same normalization changes nothing) gives the fully explicit lower estimate

    <AS,S> >= (n+4)/4 ||S_+^0||^2 +(n+8)/4 ||S_-||^2
               + n/4 ||dh||^2 + n(n+2)/2 ||h||^2.

Proof: the zero-order coefficients on the two tracefree sectors are +1 and -1, and on hg are (n+2)/2. The preceding rough-Laplacian lower bounds give exactly the displayed constants; parallel orthogonality removes cross terms. This is a genuine bound for A, not for Pi_sol Q or the entropy Hessian.
