# Scope fixed at the selection point — round 4

Author: Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. Original prose: CC BY 4.0.

Research target T2: There is a finite absolute constant C such that for every
integer d,N >= 1, every family v_1,...,v_N in R^d with ||v_i||_2 <= 1,
and every two permutations pi,rho of [N], there is ONE epsilon in {−1,1}^N
such that

    max_{sigma in {pi,rho}, 0 <= k <= N}
        || sum_{j=1}^k epsilon_{sigma(j)} v_{sigma(j)} ||_2 <= C sqrt(d).

The constant must be independent of N, d, both orders and the input vectors.
No zero-sum, rationality, generic-position, lower-norm, commuting-projector,
order-width, displacement or orientation restriction is allowed. All prefixes
use the same signs. This is an existence target, not an online/algorithmic one.
A disproof would need an unbounded normalized discrepancy family, rather than
failure of a particular filter construction. A proof for one order, bounded
width, finite direction palettes or independent occurrence signs does not
settle this target.

The target is distinct from the accepted campaign packages and from the
excluded poly-Z_p family. This scope is fixed before the detailed proof attempt;
the preliminary screen inspected the one-order source and the causal nature of
its construction. It does NOT assert that T2 is new to the literature, true, or
an established named open problem. The scalar weighted two-permutation and
network-rounding antecedents must be compared at their actual interfaces.

Alternative screen (not attempted targets): the catalogue's Foulkes stabilization
and semidefinite-lift families were examined for companion coverage. Both have
strong companion statements making easy extensions poor nominations. No theorem
from those families is relied on. At most this one target is selected for
mathematical work. A precise unresolved bridge will be retained if the full
claim cannot be proved; a technical obstruction will not be renamed a discovery.
