Primary texts actually retrieved and used for comparison are listed with
byte hashes in retrieval-literature.json and read extents in
source-contracts.md. They are Knuth's author TeX, Reis's institutional thesis,
MNT arXiv v2 PDF and Bérczi et al. arXiv v1 HTML. SIAM's Knuth publication
page was opened for metadata; arXiv HTML introduction was read through the
actual definitions and hypotheses. Crawl ages were not used as publication
dates. In particular the Reis thesis is dated 2023 on its title page.

Discovery-only results included prefix-discrepancy papers, weighted chairman
assignment, online discrepancy, weighted matroid work and factorization norms.
Some results concerned coordinate permutations of each vector, rather than
two prescribed index orders; those are different quantifiers. Search noise
also included ranking distances, machine learning weighting and permutation
metrics. No mathematical premise is taken from those snippets, Wikipedia,
ResearchGate or an abstract without a read primary interface.

There was no MathSciNet/Zentralblatt-wide, every-language or exhaustive
citation-network search. The bounded screen does not certify T2 as new, open,
true or false. The recent weighted-carpooling counterexample is not transferred
to T2 by assertion. No literature-only completion is claimed: candidate.md
contains the actual proof work and explicitly leaves T2 unpaid.

Repository selection screen: all numbered family headings were extracted from
the retained catalogue; selected structural family abstracts were read. The
complete preprint tree was parsed for companion paths. The pinned Steinitz
family's actual theorem and operation-level proofs were retrieved and read at
the documented extents. Foulkes and hyperbolicity lifts were discarded at the
catalogue stage, with no proof or novelty conclusion based only on abstracts.
