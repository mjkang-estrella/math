## Complete intersections: the actual bridge

## Scope explored, without freezing a theorem

The distinct operation tested here is a geometric relative Ward action for a smooth pair (X,D), with X and D in the source-paid full-Virasoro class, using ordinary descendants at interior marks and arbitrary cohomology-weighted contact profiles at D. It must work in every genus and actual integral curve class, with all primitive/odd states and the empty profile, and glue by the ordinary relative degeneration pairing. Its boundary correction must be constructed from the normal cap and must be independent of cap row choices. A tautologically conjugated unlimited reconstruction system is not the proposed Virasoro extension. No implication from absolute to relative constraints has been asserted.

The calculations in Note 02 construct the forced grading correction, isolate the full cap-image obstruction, and expose the strict-transform rubber term that must enter a quadratic sewing calculation. They are prefreeze feasibility calculations. A failure of this inference is not a counterexample to existence of a relative Ward action.
