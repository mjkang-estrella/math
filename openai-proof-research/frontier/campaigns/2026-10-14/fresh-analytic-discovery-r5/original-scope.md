# Original scope fixed before the detailed proof attempt

Author: Myeongjin Kang. No affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.

Target A: For every real f in W^{1,1}(R^2) and every sequence of real
f_j converging to f in the full inhomogeneous W^{1,1}(R^2) norm,

    || gradient M f_j - gradient M f ||_{L^1(R^2;R^2)} -> 0,

where M is the centered Hardy--Littlewood maximal operator over ALL Euclidean
disks, averaging |f|, and gradients are vector weak gradients. No radiality,
compact support, positivity, common support, regularity, sign or optimizer
uniqueness assumption is imposed. The result must include spatial tightness
at infinity and the contact set {M|f|=|f|}; local continuity, norm-only
convergence, positive-radius truncation or smooth-input continuity is not a
substitute. The pinned endpoint norm theorem is accepted as true. Its stronger
signed finite-band estimate, not merely its abstract, is available as a premise.

Preliminary screen: pinned source introduction explicitly separates continuity
from boundedness. Its extension proves BV bounds but does not assert gradient
continuity. Full signed finite-band control and square mean subtraction are
stronger premises than a bare norm bound. The intended new bridge must handle
vanishing maximizing radii at order zero using local affine comparison; whether
that is a substantial contribution rather than a technical continuation must
be assessed against actual primary continuity proofs. No worldwide novelty or
substantiality verdict is presumed.

The r4 original scope was read: it pursues two-order vector balancing, wholly
separate from this target. Poly-Zp, accepted gradient-p/kinetic/matrix/torus
contact and listed unresolved families are excluded. A few preliminary catalogue
alternatives (Brenier stability and Mumford--Shah tail refinements) were screened
out without proposed theorems, because immediate transfers look technical or
are already covered by earlier campaigns. No change of target has occurred.

If the global vector-gradient assertion cannot be proved, retain this exact
scope and identify the unpaid estimate. No Lean, manuscript or acceptance work
before a complete credible substantial candidate. No subagents, commits,
