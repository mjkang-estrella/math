## What was actually obtained

For 0<->A and 2A<->3A+B with unit rates, start the embedded chain at (N,0). For every fixed k>=1,

    E[A_k-N] = 1-(k+1)/N+O_k(N^-2)  if k is odd,
    E[A_k-N] = 2/N+O_k(N^-2)        if k is even.

The complete finite-deviation argument tracks the terminal phase and accounts for lost baseline probability. The positive even coefficient is a final-time boundary excursion effect; it does not accumulate at each cycle.

At the shifted point (N,1), h=1/N, every active slope in the actual pinned affine construction has r_B=0 and r_A<-1/2. The global supporting inequality consequently forces positive expected increment of its convex deficit over every such block. It also gives a positive continuous-time generator lower bound. The entropy increment is positive as well: for even k its leading term is 2(log N)/N. Taking a larger fixed number of jumps therefore does not repair the proposed transfer.

An explicit product-Poisson detailed balance computation, including finite stationary jump intensity and nonexplosion, proves that the example is positive recurrent. This is an essential logical control: the obstruction concerns particular Lyapunov constructions only. The proof and arithmetic diagnostic are technical support; neither is offered as a substantial campaign finding.

## Operations already paid, and exact unpaid step

The two pinned149 manuscripts give finite affine slopes with arbitrary tolerance, all-active comparisons, directed complex-level cut domination, and compact deterministic plateaux. The October version adds strict drift and finite scale-to-scale entry. Positive monomial weights and instantaneous return sources are part of those operations. At a stochastic boundary, falling-factorial weights can vanish. Both the generator sign and every fixed-jump-block repair fail in the example above.

Anderson–Kim2018 develops stochastic tier/Foster conditions. Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim2020 proves a fixed-block embedded-chain criterion when every stochastic top source is a deterministic top source, using uniformly bounded path-weighted entropy increments plus one negative path of probability bounded away from zero. The example has deterministic top complex 3A+B, disabled at (N,0), and stochastic top complex 2A. The primary hypothesis is genuinely absent. No source theorem was audited or contradicted.

The alternative is to average over complete boundary excursions or solve a boundary Poisson corrector. For this example the nonlinear reactions preserve A-B, so a completed excursion to B=0 cancels their A displacement. But A-B is not coercive in the full quadrant, and bounding an excursion requires a different argument than the fixed jump-block calculation. In a general network, fast boundary processes can be infinite, overlap other species scales, and involve multiple linkage classes. No uniform return-time integrability, globally coercive corrected Lyapunov function, or compatible estimates across those regimes were obtained.

Agazzi's primary talk body describes fiber exit-time averaging and Poisson-tail comparison in two dimensions; its title and final slide explicitly identify that scope and higher-dimensional patching debts, despite an unqualified intermediate theorem slide. The current project publication list labels the two-dimensional manuscript “in preparation.” The actual outline is therefore a relevant antecedent to the proposed correction, not a source of a proved arbitrary-dimensional bridge. No attempt is made to claim that generic averaging or Poisson comparison is new.
