# Working deductions and rejected operations

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. CC BY 4.0 original prose.

Full P remains fixed and UNRESOLVED. All new files are inside this attempt.

1. Rechecked old leakage and averaged transform arguments as support. A
   Gaussian admixture gives the desired density class and strict selection
   deficits but has no certified common optimal calibration. The compact
   binary-minimum projection R can still contain an unproved contact defect.

2. Attempted smooth-contact route. A smooth minimizer of a six-variable
   pair problem is only a map into R^6; neither three-dimensional block of
   its derivative is automatically invertible. The report gives an actual
   Coulomb calibration near (0,e1,-e1) whose positive pair Hessian yields
   DY=diag(8/3,0,0). This blocks that automatic inference without claiming
   positive-measure rank failure. No generic rank theorem was assumed.

3. Paid the global analytic gluing step conditionally, rather than silently
   inserting density regularity. Pick good flat bump squares on countably
   many compact chart patches, symmetrize their plans, and use weights
   summable simultaneously against every Schwartz seminorm and every
   relative-derivative constant A_(n,alpha,m)^m. Holder then gives
   |D^alpha f| <= C f^(1-1/m). Taking m>=4|alpha| makes all square-root
   derivatives bounded by C f^(1/4), proving Schwartz decay. The open cover
   proves positivity at every point, not merely almost everywhere. This
   works on all space if the charts exist. Their existence is the unpaid
   geometric operation, not an extra hypothesis of a claimed P theorem.

4. Considered common-translation convolution as a direct global filling.
   For a source support triple A, A+v is also in the smoothed support. Take
   v perpendicular to its two differences from coordinate 1. Exchanging
   first coordinates strictly increases both distances and reduces all
   affected Coulomb terms, with the third pair unchanged. Neighborhood
   exchange gives an exact same-marginal finite-cost competitor. Gaussian
   smoothing therefore does not preserve this plan's optimality, even
   though it produces a normalized everywhere-positive Schwartz square.

5. Examined superharmonicity and binary-minimum geometry as possible global
   obstructions. The source quadratic patches have negative Laplacian and
   small neighborhoods, so no total-dual-charge contradiction was obtained.
   No theorem rules out every possible positive marginal or every preserved
   extension. Full P is not refuted. No harmonic-open-region assertion about
   the actual fixed point is made.

6. A reservoir with entropy, caps or derivative penalty does not yet solve
   the inverse problem. Its KKT/regularization terms need an inactivity or
   compatibility proof to retain source contacts. Measurable selections
   can have singular other pushforwards. No density estimate or smooth
   fixed-point theorem has been obtained.
