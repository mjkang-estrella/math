## A prime-power model calculation, not a nomination

One early alternative was to vary coefficient order. The geometric arithmetic is already compatible with the source. Let p be odd, q=p^r, L=Q_p(zeta_q), pi=1-zeta_q, and V its integer ring. Choose a monic irreducible h(T)=T^q+sum_(i<q) h_i T^i over F_p and arbitrary lifts h_i to V. Consider

    P=(X-Z)^q+zeta_q Z^q+sum_(i<q) h_i X^i Y^(q-i).

At m=[0:0:1] the local affine equation has linear term -pi modulo (pi,x,y)^2; the quotient is a two-dimensional regular local ring. All intervening binomial coefficients of q are divisible by p. Its reduction is the irreducible homogenization of h, independent of Z. Thus points of the generic curve specializing away from m have residue fields containing F_(p^q), and their residue degrees are divisible by q.

The divisor y=0 is V[t]/((t-1)^q+zeta_q). This polynomial is Eisenstein at pi: its constant term is -pi, all other nonleading coefficients are divisible by p and hence pi. Its fraction field D is totally ramified of degree q, with N_(D/L)(t)=pi and (1-t)^q=zeta_q. These are exactly the source's geometric/norm interfaces, with q replacing5. For a coefficient lift beta with boundary[zeta_q], the integral boundary of beta_D[t] is plus or minus q{1-t,t}=0 by Steinberg. The separate DVR lift again uses K2(F_p)=0.

To finish the coefficient nonvanishing argument at general q one must use the full mod-q field-comparison isomorphism and norm compatibility for every finite extension of L. The source proves that interface at5; its motivic/cohomological proof indicates the same classical extension to odd prime powers. That primary general-coefficient chain was not newly read here, so this note stops short of presenting a separate full general-q theorem. Even if that routine comparison is supplied, all displayed construction operations are the same source/classical composition. This is not an identified nonroutine operation, a solution of G4, or a nominee.

Also, a detector taking value1 modulo q would imply that a finite-order integral class has order divisible by q; it would not determine its exact order, prove torsion, or give a fixed ring with unbounded-order kernels. Different q give different rings. Neither rational nonvanishing nor a coherent fixed-ring tower was established. Those stronger statements were not frozen or developed as replacement targets.
