In dimension at most four, in the global Weil \(\mathbb Q\)-factorial category with canonical sheaf a rational line bundle, every negative extremal ray has a projective contraction to a normal compact Kähler space, of relative line-bundle rank one, with \(-D\) relatively ample and \(f^*\mathrm{BC}(Z)=R^\perp\).
