\paragraph{Scoped formal support.}
A separate Lean package proves 29 supporting theorems among 35 public
declarations. It covers finite degree budgets and strict numerical cutoffs;
integer composition-dimension and quotient-dimension inequalities; the
two-endpoint set inclusion from saturation and constancy for a map defined
on the open subtype; preservation under actual reflexive-transitive chain
closure; linear-functional vanishing on a spanning set and contradiction
with a positive witness; and Picard, pseudoindex, and parameter arithmetic.
The relevant saturation, support containment, numerical dimension bounds,
spanning, positive witness, and generator-preservation properties are
\emph{supplied hypotheses} at these interfaces. Their geometric realization
in this paper is not formalized.

The full five-type classification remains a human proof conditional on S1,
essential S2, and the cited classical inputs. Lean does not construct Fano
manifolds, rational-curve moduli, virtual cycles, refined intersections,
proper equidimensional quotients, horizontal dominating families, Mori
contractions, or the classified bundles. It does not verify their geometric
dimension or positivity arguments or the converse invariant calculations.
Neither S1 nor S2 is introduced as a Lean axiom.
