# T012D62 full working scope

Frozen UTC 2026-10-18T18:49:52.522678+00:00 after actual binary-source and primary-interface exploration, before unified proof-attempt assembly.

For every fixed a0,a1,a2 in (0,1), prove that, through all real X tending to infinity,

    X^(-1) #{2≤n≤X : P+(n+i)≤n^(ai) for i=0,1,2}
      → rho(1/a0) rho(1/a1) rho(1/a2),

where P+(m) is the largest prime factor and rho is the Dickman–de Bruijn function.

This is ordinary natural density at every sufficiently large scale. No exceptional set of scales, logarithmic averaging, average over shifts, partially smoothed third coordinate, or restricted threshold chamber is a replacement. A stronger three-bin-phase statement is an attempted sufficient mechanism, not an added theorem obligation. The target is unresolved at freeze; exact stripping calculations have been explored but no full proof exists.
