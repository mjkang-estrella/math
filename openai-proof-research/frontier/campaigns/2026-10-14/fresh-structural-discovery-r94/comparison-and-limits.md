No nominee and no novelty verdict. No full target has been frozen. This is exploratory author work, not independent review.

The actual I12 campaign report was read in full. Its ultraproduct argument already supplies a dimension-free unspecified modulus for radial distance distortion of onto sphere ε-isometries. Its classical Kalton/Matoušková spiral comparison already rules out dimension-free approximation by a linear map in all dimensions. An explicit radial rate was specifically left as a possible additional direction there. R94 therefore cannot claim eligibility merely from selecting family322. The only potentially unpaid operation pursued here is a quantitative replacement of the source maximal-face/chord-alignment argument. That operation is incomplete.

Vestfrid, Near-isometries of the unit sphere, Ukrainian Mathematical Journal72 (2020), DOI10.37863/umzh.v72i4.6049: downloaded publisher PDF; theorem statements, radial Hilbert proof, spiral sharpness, and cube proof have been read. The complete raw extraction has607lines; first combined layout extraction was truncated and was supplemented by raw190–430 and430–607. Theorem1 has C log(n+1)ε linear approximation for self-maps of the Euclidean sphere; Theorem2 has universal Cε for self-maps of the cube sphere. Its Hilbert proof uses inner products and its cube proof identifies coordinate faces and pastes discrete signed-permutation isometries. Neither operation provides an arbitrary-Banach estimate. The direct Hilbert radial ε bound in working-geometry.md is elementary paid support, not a proposed contribution.

Rohman–Eryılmaz, A note on the extension of ε-isometries on the unit sphere of Banach spaces, Honam Mathematical Journal46(2024),38–46, DOI10.5831/hmj.2024.46.1.38: downloaded publisher PDF. Raw1–340 read separately; theorem3.3 and its proof/conclusion/references were read in the untruncated tail of the earlier combined layout output. The actual theorem imposes exact additivity on sums of unit vectors as the condition equivalent to linearity of the homogeneous extension. It is not an unconditional uniform radial stability theorem, despite the broader abstract wording. No theorem or proof of this paper is used as a black box. In particular its Lemma3.1 asserted global Lipschitz estimate cannot be substituted for additive ε-isometry control: a map that swaps two nearby sphere points and fixes all others is an onto additive near-isometry but is discontinuous. This observation concerns use of an external comparator, not the stipulated canonical source.
