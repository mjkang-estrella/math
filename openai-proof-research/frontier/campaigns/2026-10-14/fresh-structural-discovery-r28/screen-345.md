A quantitative lower bound N_g(L) in the full arbitrary-metric class is not paid by merely making the infinitude contradiction finite. The source fixes a hypothetical finite prime list, then chooses root orders D_i from monodromy root-of-unity eigenvalues, one polygon order for all selected root crossing intervals, and B as the maximum of those root ranks. The index support A includes nN0+1+max I_(i,s). None is bounded there in terms of the number of prime images alone. The transient argument also uses the minimum positive gap between distinct slopes in {0, alpha, Delta_i/ell_i}. Adding more primes below a finite cutoff need not preserve that gap. Hence replacing the infinite finiteness hypothesis by a cutoff-dependent prime count loses the uniform constants required by the contradiction. This is an obstruction to that proof conversion, not a counterexample to any quantitative growth conjecture. No bounded-degeneracy or bumpy subclass is substituted for the full target.

A possible extension to complex projective spaces was screened at the operation level: the sphere's two families and evaluation-cup pairing need a different global reflection module and actual full-group lifts. Current primary comparison found Chen--Deng--Zhang, arXiv:2610.21054v1 (15 October 2026), whose Theorem1.1 already claims all simply connected compact rank-one symmetric spaces. Theorem5.1 and its paired-algebra/global-annihilator arguments provide the projective operation; Proposition6.15 proves the doubling equality condition using dimensions after restriction, retaining overlaps of degree towers. Thus this is an explicit competing full target and proof mechanism, not a title-based absence claim. I do not re-present it as a discovery, and I do not certify that external manuscript's entire proof.
