# Family358: paid geometry and a parameter screen

No target frozen and no nomination. The stipulated source is the exact pinned manuscript in source358-receipt.json. All eleven non-PDF blobs were authenticated. I read introduction.tex, topology.tex, punctured-torus.tex, global.tex, and cometric.tex in full. I did not read variations.tex or turning.tex in full; the no-conjugate-points conclusion is stipulated, not re-audited.

The apparent strengthening from absence of an NPC metric to absence of any proper cocompact CAT(0) action is already the explicit theorem thm:no-cat0-action. The source also states the locally CAT(0) and no-focal-points obstructions. No such statement is new.

The cometric uses a fixed cutoff, diagonal perturbation of size delta, moving shear epsilon=delta^2, and periods B1=delta^2 B2. Its displayed first and second derivative formulas bound both P' and P'' by O(delta), with P uniformly bounded and uniformly positive. Thus the neck curvature is O(delta) by differentiating Q=P^{-1}; all other pieces have nonpositive sectional curvature. The collar has k between zero and one on [-1,0], hence 1<=ell(-1)<=e. A fixed sufficiently small B2 fits both cusp boundaries for every small delta. Truncation depths are log(c/(Bi ell(-1))), hence O(1+|log delta|), with the compact hyperbolic cores fixed. Consequently retaining the source parameter should give diam(g_delta)^2 max(sec(g_delta),0)=O(delta(1+|log delta|)^2). This is recorded as a feasibility observation, not a fully audited new theorem: the precise uniform no-conjugate-points parameter range in the later turning analysis was not independently read. More importantly, the operation is direct parameter retention in the existing construction, so it does not meet the substantial-operation gate by itself.

A current literature search returned Ivanov--Kapovitch, Leeb and Buyalo--Svetlov primary papers as comparison leads. Their proof bodies were not retrieved/read in this screen and no external theorem is asserted from those results. In particular, I do not claim that every graph manifold admits an almost-NPC metric or that this refinement is new in the literature. No priority verdict is made. Focused history queries had no relevant prior-developed358 hit, but that is not an exhaustive semantic eligibility certificate.
