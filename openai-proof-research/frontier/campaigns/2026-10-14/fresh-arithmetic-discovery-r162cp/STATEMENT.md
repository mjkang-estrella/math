# Frozen author statement — R162CP

## Exact source family and conventions

Fix any integer d>=3. Put N=2d+1, and fix any odd prime q satisfying all the largeness requirements of Source177, **Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension**, at pin fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb. In particular q>=5 and q>=2^(2d-1). Constants below may depend on d,q, but not on m or any cochains, sections or laws.

Let U be the inverse image of the upper unitriangular subgroup of SL_N(F_q) under evaluation at t=0 in SL_N(F_q[t]). For i=1,...,N-1 let x_i=I+E_(i,i+1), and let x_0=I+t E_(N,1). Let H_i be generated by the x_j with j different from i. For each integer m>=2 let Gamma_N(m) be the kernel of reduction modulo t^m, G_m=U/Gamma_N(m), T_m the common-intersection coset complex of the tagged left cosets of the images of the H_i, and X_m the d-skeleton of T_m. These are the original Source177 complexes, without subdivision, cover replacement or change of degree bound.

Write V_m=|X_m(0)|. All cochains and coboundaries in this statement have coefficients F_2. For an i-face sigma use the intrinsic weight

  w_i(sigma)=#{tau in X_m(d): sigma subset tau}/[binom(d+1,i+1)|X_m(d)|],
  ||f||_i=sum_(sigma: f(sigma)=1) w_i(sigma).

The source bound on the number of d-faces incident to a vertex is D=q^(binom(N,2)) binom(N-1,d). Let epsilon>0 be any fixed common source coboundary-expansion constant at these weights, decreased to at most 1 if necessary. Set Z_m=im(delta_1:C^1(X_m;F_2)->C^2(X_m;F_2)); these are all degree-two coboundaries. For z in Z_m let F_z={f in C^1:delta_1 f=z}, and let mu_z be its uniform probability law. Put

  Q_m(z)=min{||f||_1:delta_1 f=z}.

A section is an arbitrary function s:Z_m->C^1 with delta_1 s(z)=z for every z. It need not be linear and s(0) need not be zero. A probability law P on the finite set of all sections specifies one common random section for all syndromes. Define

  L_m(P)=max_(z!=z') E_(s~P)||s(z)+s(z')||_1 / ||z+z'||_2.

Let S_m be the infimum of L_m(P) over every such law. Let U_m be the infimum over those laws additionally satisfying s(z)~mu_z for every z. Equivalently U_m optimizes over all joint couplings of the entire family (mu_z)_(z in Z_m). For these joint couplings also put

  R_m=inf_P max_(z!=z') E_(s~P)||s(z)+s(z')||_1 / W_1(mu_z,mu_z'),

where W_1 uses the cost (f,f')->||f+f'||_1 on C^1, and the infimum in R_m is over the same exact-uniform-marginal laws as in U_m. Logs below are natural.

## The theorem

There exist c>0, C<infinity and an integer m_0>=2, depending only on d,q, such that for every integer m>=m_0,

  c log V_m <= S_m <= U_m <= C log V_m,
  c log V_m <= R_m <= C log V_m.

The upper bound for S_m can be attained by a law supported on F_2-linear sections. The upper bounds for U_m and R_m can be attained by a law supported on affine sections, formed from those same linear sections by adding ONE independent uniform element of im(delta_0) to all outputs simultaneously.

For every m>=2 and every z,z' in Z_m, the exact two-law transport identity and source-scale bounds are

  W_1(mu_z,mu_z')=Q_m(z+z'),
  (1/3)||z+z'||_2 <= Q_m(z+z') <= epsilon^(-1)||z+z'||_2.

In particular each pair of uniform primitive laws separately admits a coupling of constant source-scale cost, although every single joint coupling of ALL those same laws has the sharp logarithmic worst-pair loss. The lower bound on S_m applies even when marginal uniformity is dropped completely.

This is an existence and optimal-sensitivity theorem on finite spaces. It makes no runtime lower bound, no polynomial enumeration claim for the exponentially large full family, no all-path exact-randomness bound, and no change of coefficient group or monodromy assertion.
