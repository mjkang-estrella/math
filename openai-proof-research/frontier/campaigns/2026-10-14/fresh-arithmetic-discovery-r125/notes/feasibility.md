Working input: commutative unital finite-dimensional F_p-algebra A, structure constants and unit supplied; dimension D; polynomial f dense. Nilpotents are allowed. Provisional integrated calculation (before primary proof reading): ker(Frob^e) for p^e>=D is nilradical J. Its quotient is a product of finite fields; Berlekamp fixed algebra splits components by factoring degree<=D minimal polynomials of basis elements over F_p. This avoids an unsupported reduction from extension-field factoring and avoids a primitive element for a product with more components than p. CRT projectors lift uniquely through J. This part looks routine and fully source/classical paid.

For roots, simple residue roots lift uniquely by Newton through J^D=0. Pure p^e powers on A are F_p-linear. On units, prime-to-p exponent extraction reduces to residue units plus an invertible exponent on 1+J. These are promising complete paid interfaces, not substantial nominees. A binary exponent n is not a dense degree-n input; do not advertise an n-sized factorization call as polynomial in log n.

The possible genuinely different burden is nonunit singular roots: for A=F_p ⊕V⊕W with V*V given by arbitrary symmetric beta into W and (V⊕W)W=0, A is associative/local with J^3=0. Solving x^2=a in W over odd p forces the scalar of x to be0 and leaves beta(v,v)=a with w arbitrary. This is simultaneous quadratic solvability, not solved by merely listing residue roots or by the source dynamic evaluation in reduced algebras. Need strongest primary comparison and exact constructive reduction before deciding what this means. Failure of Hensel alone is not contribution evidence.
