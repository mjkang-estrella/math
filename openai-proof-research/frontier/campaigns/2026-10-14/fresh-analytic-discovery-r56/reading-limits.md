## Validation and limitations

`diagnostic.py` uses only standard-library exact rational arithmetic to check fixed-N factorial cumulants, finite connector powers and elementary weighted discrete insertion cases. It is not a proof of the continuum estimates or a gas limit. Negative controls reject dropping the canonical covariance, treating a cycle as a leading tree, and treating a negative conditioned-error exponent as positive.

No Lean development is justified for this unproved, non-nominated full theorem. Feasible scoped formal statements would be the finite cumulant recurrence, graph edge count and partition-ratio product bound; formalizing those would not settle the missing marked hierarchy/spatial traces/remainders or improve the contribution classification.

`verify.py` and `controls.py` are adapted from r55's read integrity programs, changing only the source directory in the mutation control. They check exact files AND directories, root and ancestor lstat chains, regular single-link files, external manifest anchor, source identities and primary hashes. Controls act on disposable copies and reject wrong anchor, modified/missing/extra files, extra empty directory, payload/directory/root/ancestor symlinks, hardlinks, FIFO and modified manifest. Integrity is separate from mathematics.
