# Multiple actions: exact reduction and the unpaid construction

Prefreeze only. No universal equality stated below is claimed proved unless explicitly derived. This note retains the stronger concrete work after the one- and two-action calculations, rather than leaving only a family title or a named gap.

## 1. Necessary scalar tests

Let p be arbitrary, d>=1, 1<=b_1<...<b_d, and a_i>b_i. Put
  B=direct-sum_i C_(p^a_i), delta=diag(p^b_1,...,p^b_d).
Let L(B,delta) denote the exact minimum defined in note02 Section1. Define
  S_0=max_(1<=j<=d) ceil((a_j+sum_(i<j)b_i+(d-j)*b_j)/b_j).   (3)
Every admissible filtration projects to Q_j=B/(delta-p^b_j)B. Its j-th summand has exponent a_j, and its i-th summand has exponent min(b_i,b_j) for i!=j, because v_p(p^b_i-p^b_j)=min(b_i,b_j). On Q_j, delta=p^b_j id. Each cyclic factor in the projected chain has order at most p^b_j, so multiplying orders proves L>=S_0. The generator lower bound d is redundant here, since the j=1 test is at least d+1 under a_1>b_1.

These inequalities are necessary only. No sufficiency is inferred from the source: Source314 turns an actual optimal group chain into chromatic loss, but does not optimize this prescribed module filtration.

A constructive upper bound is sum_i ceil(a_i/b_i), by coordinatewise filtration. It improves by partitioning coordinates into pairs and singletons and using note02(2) for each pair before concatenation. With repeated b-values one can also form whole equal-action blocks and use note02 Section2. All these are compatible filtrations of the same module, so they are legitimate upper bounds, not an assumption of product additivity. Note02 Section5 goes beyond even this blockwise payment.

## 2. Exact matrix formulation of the missing operation

A length-at-most-s filtration is equivalent to the following explicit finite congruence problem. Find a d by s matrix V, with its i-th row interpreted modulo p^a_i, and an integral strictly upper triangular s by s matrix N such that
  diag(p^b_i) V = V N  (row i modulo p^a_i),
  rank_(F_p)(V mod p)=d.                                  (4)

Proof of equivalence: given a filtration, choose one lift v_j of a cyclic generator at each step, padding with zero columns if necessary. Then delta(v_j) is an integer linear combination of v_1,...,v_(j-1). These coefficients give N, and the v_j generate B. Conversely, take columns of V and their successive additive spans. Equation(4) gives the delta condition, and the factors are cyclic. The rank condition implies that the columns generate B: if their span C surjects onto B/pB, then B/C=p(B/C); a finite p-group with this property is zero by iteration. Thus (4) is sufficient as well as necessary. N^s=0 is automatic, but requiring merely nilpotence without the simultaneous integral triangular basis would not be this exact interface.

The prospective full operation would construct (V,N) at s=S_0 for ALL these parameters, or identify and prove the missing additional obstruction and an attained replacement optimum. Neither construction nor obstruction classification is obtained here. This is a quantified, finite compatibility problem, not a claim that an arbitrary conjecture has been solved or an old frozen target has been weakened.

## 3. Why the two-action orbit does not automatically give the full construction

For two coordinates, note02 takes g=(1,p^(c-b)) and an orbit under delta; the determinant of [g,delta g] has valuation c. This makes the remaining quotient cyclic and killed by delta, exactly what the last step needs.

For d>=3 a single orbit in the free p-adic lattice has a different obstruction. If g=(p^w_i)_i with w_i>=0 and distinct eigenvalues lambda_i=p^b_i, the orbit lattice generated by g,delta g,...,delta^(d-1)g has determinant valuation
  sum_i w_i + sum_(i<j) min(b_i,b_j).
Solving for delta e_i in this lattice by interpolation requires the degree<d polynomial f with
  p^w_h f(lambda_h)=lambda_i if h=i, and0 otherwise.
Its leading coefficient is lambda_i/(p^w_i product_(h!=i)(lambda_i-lambda_h)), of valuation
  b_i-w_i-sum_(h!=i)min(b_i,b_h).
For i=1 this is -(d-2)b_1-w_1<0. Hence the polynomial is not integral and delta e_1 is not in that free lattice. The direct generalization “one orbit plus a delta-trivial remaining quotient” therefore has no automatic justification. This is an obstruction to this particular lattice construction, not a proof of impossibility for finite quotients or for (4). Finite exponent relations, more than one orbit, and a nontrivial filtration of the residual quotient can change the answer.

The original finite group can also have cyclic quotients with order larger than every individual p^b_i. For p=2, a=(3,4), b=(1,2), the explicit shortest chain has a factor of order8. Thus replacing every factor by an order bound p^max(b_i) is invalid in mixed actions. The scalar quotient tests above remain valid because they first change the module to one on which delta is a single scalar.

## 4. Finite calculations and their scope

The own-root Python programs enumerate additive subgroups by adjoining an element g only when delta g is already in the current subgroup. Every successor is exactly S+<g>; breadth-first search consequently computes the minimum for that finite module. It does not enumerate an unjustified restricted class of coordinate subgroups. All loops are over explicitly finite sets; the largest isolated run was guarded by a25-second shell timeout and finished in about2.1seconds.

The retained runs comprise11 one-active-factor tests,7 initial mixed tests,79 bounded sorted two/three-coordinate tests of total exponent at most7, and one three-action test of order1024. Overlaps are intentional, and these are not claimed as98 distinct parameter cases. The79-case batch found no discrepancy with its scalar lower bound; its generator-rank term and tests include trivial-action coordinates. It is supporting evidence only. It cannot establish universal sufficiency of(3), independent correctness, contribution, or acceptance.

The order1024 test led to note02 Section5's prime-independent five-step chain, which is checked by displayed algebra, not accepted solely because the program found it. This demonstrates useful concrete progress beyond title selection and pairwise concatenation. It does not close the all-parameter operation(4).

## 5. Decision before freeze

No full-scope substantial nominee is frozen. The exact calculations in note02 are retained as elementary or small explicit reductions; they are not offered as a restricted successful replacement for the general multi-action operation. The source and actual Kuhn--Lloyd mechanisms already supply the chromatic conversion, induction/restriction, type-function realization, Smith-to-Floyd amplification and existence of finite witnesses. Appending those consequences to a small chain calculation would not create a separate nonroutine operation.

The outstanding obstacle is construction(4) at a proved optimal all-parameter value for multiple genuinely different actions, after the explicit scalar tests, mixed orbit construction and blockwise bounds have been paid. This is not a global impossibility or exhaustion claim, and the unproved S_0 equality is not a finding. No old frozen theorem is altered, and no correctness/contribution grade, acceptance or count is assigned.
