## Developed route: cyclic filtrations of a module

For an abelian finite p-group B with an action alpha of a cyclic p-group H, every overgroup of the complement H in B semidirect H is M semidirect H for an alpha-invariant subgroup M of B. For nested such terms, normality is equivalent to (alpha-1)M_next being contained in M_prev; the quotient is then the abelian group M_next/M_prev. Thus the source loss at this embedded complement is exactly the minimum length of a filtration of B by cyclic factors killed by alpha-1. This uses full relative cyclic length, not elementary-abelian rank.

A controlled family is B=C_(p^a) direct-sum (C_(p^r))^k, with alpha=1+p^b on the first factor and identity on the remaining factors. Take 1<=b<a; for p=2 use b>=2 for the simple order convention, or take alpha=-1 and b=1 for inversion. Complement order must be an actual p-power order of alpha; choose a sufficiently large cyclic p-group with that action. The unamplified filtration length is ceil(a/b). The action on the extra factors is trivial. Kuhn--Lloyd Example5.15 is the parameter (p,a,b,r,k)=(2,3,2,1,1), with length2 instead of3.
