Family121 cyclic edit-distance question: for all arbitrary explicit finite integer strings x,y, including empty and unequal-length strings, put CED(x,y)=min_j ED(x,rot_j(y)), with the empty string having its unique empty rotation. ED has unit-cost insertions, deletions and substitutions. Seek one uniform randomized program returning a numerical estimate D with CED<=D<=(1+epsilon)CED with probability at least2/3, in worst-case expected N^(1+o(1)) logarithmic-word RAM time for every fixed rational epsilon in(0,1), N=|x|+|y|. Symbols have polynomial magnitude in N+2. All input processing, numerical precision and random-bit work are charged. The fixed-accuracy asymptotic convention is the source's; no polynomial dependence on1/epsilon is implicit.

The full provisional question was recorded before this packet's final report, but only after exploratory source/primary reading and route testing. No full construction emerged, so no target was frozen for proof assembly. Empty/equal strings, unequal lengths, small/intermediate/large distances, highly repetitive strings and growing integer alphabets remain in the question. A restricted scale, fixed alphabet, periodic-only, equal-length-only, exact-threshold, or slower-time result is not offered as a replacement nominee.
