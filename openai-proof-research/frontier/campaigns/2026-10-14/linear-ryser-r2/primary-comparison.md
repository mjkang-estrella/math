# Operation comparison and honest contribution limits

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.
Third-party rights retained privately.

The target remains the negation of the established linear intersecting
Ryser assertion. It is not a newly proposed conjecture. No new-to-literature
or substantial-result claim is made for the supporting work here.

The pinned balanced source's actual operation merges two parallel lines
and splits two others into opposite pairs, restoring lost pair intersection
through merged blocks. Its core/pencil argument proves the complete
cover lower bound for those partitions. The current K_4 trade instead
creates four fresh triple blocks, each at noncollinear centers, and
replaces twelve old pair agreements by twelve fresh agreements. It is
not a partition refinement: those fresh triples introduce agreements
absent in the old directions. The three opposite pairs keep their old
joining direction. All fifteen pairs are proved, rather than accepted
from the source or assigned colors independently of equivalence closure.

The extension-field companion's actual paper.tex overview describes a
nonlinear coordinate gadget, but its uninspected included construction
body is not used as an antecedent for our proof. Both pinned paper.tex
files already contain the ordinary tree-cover transfer. That transfer
is neither a new operation nor counted here.

The primary FHMW degree restrictions and their explicit 13-partite
9-cover remain constraints, not discoveries. Their Lemma 2.1 already
explains why a degree-one vertex gives an edge-minus-one cover. The
additional work here is NOT that elementary cover observation: it is
the saturated two-coordinate K_4 realization/classification and the
proof that no further permitted exception can share a gadget fresh
label, hence that private old labels persist in arbitrary permitted
extensions. Their Latin-square construction is a different actual
two-coordinate operation with its own proved explicit cover. No primary
computation is rerun, and its restricted bounds are not universalized.

Bahmanian's inspected detachment operation fairly splits hinges using
two laminar families. It does not keep the unique meeting of every
pair of distinct edges. We invoke no such nonexistent consequence.
The source-specific refinement/lift obstruction from r15 is accepted
and was not retried with a different splitting order.

Stinson's actual weighted substitution replaces each master block by a
specified GDD, and its subsequent disjoint-block selection constructs
a particular parallel class. It does not supply arbitrary full
resolutions or lower cover bounds. Our synchronized resolvable TD
screen proves those resolutions under explicit stronger ingredients;
it then gives c_output<=m c_master<=R-b. This is elementary established
design substitution with a paid upper-cover calculation, not a claim
to have invented Wilson's construction or a new substantial family.

No new global lower-cover mechanism emerged. The realized K_4 trade
fails globally even after all allowed two-coordinate repairs, and the
synchronized inflation fails by a mixed-class cover. The proofs have
precise limited scopes. They neither refute every two-coordinate
construction nor prove the full universal linear Ryser assertion.
Escaping them still requires a genuine finite witness with an independent
proof against ALL r-1 covers, or a universal structural cover proof.

The primary extents and exact source identities are recorded in
`source-contracts.md`. The search was bounded; related trade or finite
geometry results could subsume these elementary observations. There is
no exhaustive novelty search, independent PASS, nomination, manuscript,
Lean package, count increase, publication or submission.
