# Separation of original work and protected evidence

public/ contains original research prose and the frozen statement; it is the
reviewable original component of this local research packet. No new license
is imposed on user-owned original prose by this assistant.

private/ contains pinned upstream bodies, primary papers/text/renderings,
project history copies, context/catalogue copies and search evidence under
their existing rights. Acquisition metadata concerning those originals is
also kept private. None of these third-party or pre-existing bodies is
relicensed or cleared for publication. scratch/ contains local acquisition
scripts; controls/ and verify.py contain original integrity code. They are
not mathematical formalizations or a publication/release bundle.

All artifacts remain local. Publication, uploads, submissions and contacts
remain stopped. Parent alone integrates and requests subsequent gates.
