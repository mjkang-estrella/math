# Conditional identification of the physical null-current generator

This module assumes the coherent local Hilbert representation construction in integration-route.md has been completed. It is not a replacement for that construction or the original bounded-net target. The small-neighborhood smoothing argument below was sharpened in parent author collaboration; that reading is not independent review.

Fix a source null plane and a double cone O compactly on one side, with a positive margin. Let D_O be compact polynomial vacuum words from O, including any one finite jointly reconstructed descendant family needed in an expression. Reeh–Schlieder makes this space dense. The source C:K-domains gives locally uniform membership in Dom(K_n^r) and one-sided graph boundary continuity. The printed charge identity C:charge-identity is on the damped domain mathscrD; it must not simply be quoted on D_O.

For a word ψ=AΩ in D_O, take ψ_ε=U(iεη)ψ for a future timelike η. The charge identity holds for ε>0. The source's one-sided graph boundary continuity gives ψ_ε→ψ and K_n ψ_ε→K_n ψ in H as ε decreases to zero. Testing the charge identity against dense compact words and taking the same joint-distribution boundary limit yields the undamped identity. Its vector action agrees with −i δ_(k_n)A Ω: the complete vacuum flux is zero and the remaining commutator is exactly the compact-flux Ward insertion. For a finite later-admitted family this passage requires the same joint-product/adjoint/affiliation facts; these are supplied by R:centered-families and R:compatible-families. One must carry this finite-family passage explicitly rather than treat one-field Π as the whole Hilbert space.

Suppose π is the integrated representation from Route A. Differentiating its exact word transformation gives Q_n ψ=−i δ_(k_n)A Ω, where π(exp(sk_n))=exp(isQ_n). Therefore K_n and Q_n agree on D_O. Equality on this dense domain alone is insufficient.

Choose a fixed relatively compact group neighborhood V of identity so small that every g in V transports the closure of O into a compact region still on the selected side. For f∈C_c^∞(V), π(f)ψ is a finite joint polynomial vector: the transformed finite field family is fixed, the transported joint tests depend smoothly on g, and their integral is again a smooth compact joint test. The source graph estimates are uniform in this compact support placement. Closedness therefore passes K_n through the group integral, and the word-level Ward calculation identifies its result with Q_n π(f)ψ. Equivalently Q_n π(f)=π(L_n f), with the appropriate Haar left-derivative convention; this is bounded on H with norm at most ||L_n f||_1. The finite-field transformation and graph-continuity details are necessary here.

These vectors form a graph core for Q_n. Indeed smooth representation vectors are a core for every derived self-adjoint generator. Convolve such a vector with an approximate identity supported in V to approximate it in Q_n graph norm. For a fixed smoothing kernel, approximate its unsmoothed input in H by D_O; both π(f) and Q_n π(f)=π(L_n f) are bounded, so the smoothed approximants converge in graph norm. A diagonal choice proves the core assertion. Since the graph of K_n is closed and contains this Q_n core, Q_n⊂K_n. Both are self-adjoint, so Q_n=K_n.

The conclusion is conditional on checking the full Route A construction and the finite-family boundary passage just described. It does not identify the actual parabolic-cut generator H_p with K_n. Nor does it identify the source's time-Möbius representation with π merely from common affine subgroups.
