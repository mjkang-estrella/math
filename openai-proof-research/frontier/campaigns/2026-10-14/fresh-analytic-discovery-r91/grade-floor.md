# Finite centered towers — supporting author derivation only

Use source282 Assumption F:framework and R:centered-families, at the stipulated pin. This is not the full global-net target and not a nominee.

Let F be any nonzero homogeneous finite-component field of real grade d admitted by the source's intrinsic criterion, not necessarily an original label. For a component, the positive-type tempered two-point distribution has a positive locally finite spectral measure μ (Bochner–Schwartz). The unitary dilation covariance, with no mixing between grades, gives μ(λE)=λ^(2d)μ(E). If d<0 and μ(B_R)>0 for some centered finite ball, then for 0<λ<1,

    μ(B_R) >= μ(B_(λR)) = λ^(2d) μ(B_R),

contradiction. Thus μ=0, including any origin atom. The corresponding component annihilates Ω distributionally. Positivity/local finiteness suffice for this elementary argument; the positive-energy support restriction is not needed here.

To conclude F=0 as a field, fix a compactly smeared F(f), choose an open double cone spacelike to its support, and commute F(f) through all original polynomial words localized there. The shared invariant domain, adjoint compatibility, graded locality and vacuum annihilation make it vanish on these vectors. The original Wightman Reeh–Schlieder argument supplies a dense set of such vectors. A closed affiliated realization extending F(f) has closed kernel containing this dense set; hence its kernel is the entire Hilbert space. Therefore the insertion itself is zero. No unique-vacuum assumption is used. For d=0, disjoint-annulus scaling instead shows μ is supported at zero, hence derivatives vanish; this observation is optional and not used for truncation.

For an original homogeneous multiplet A of grade Δ and N_A components, source R:centered-families constructs every fixed depth r, with grade Δ−r and at most 4^r N_A components, jointly with all old fields. The preceding argument kills all depths r>Δ. Consequently the union of depths 0 through floor(Δ) is a finite-dimensional component space, with dimension at most N_A(4^(floor(Δ)+1)−1)/3; any negative-grade original field is already zero. Include adjoints. No infinite simultaneous reconstruction is needed: construct the finitely many depths through floor(Δ)+1 and use the last-depth zero identities. The remaining centered special transformations map the finite space into itself and lower its grading.

The source Lie identities imply that the four centered lowering matrices commute (evaluate the commuting special-conformal Ward bracket after subtracting the coordinate and Lorentz terms). Their products of length greater than Δ vanish. The finite fiber also carries Lorentz component matrices and the diagonal grade matrix. Ordinary translations remain differential operators in the spacetime variable, rather than finite fiber matrices.

Credit and boundary: positivity of homogeneous spectral measures and the Reeh–Schlieder zero-field argument are standard. The source supplies the nontrivial joint reconstruction of descendants. Finite closure is useful for a possible integration argument, but it does not alone prove any bounded-net covariance or an unbounded-generator integration theorem.
