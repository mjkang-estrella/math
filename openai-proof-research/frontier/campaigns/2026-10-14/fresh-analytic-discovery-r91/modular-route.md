# Route B: actual modular groups and the off-axis compatibility gap

Source C:cone-modularity identifies actual future/past cone modular generators with ±D. C:time-mobius constructs a positive-energy time-Möbius representation for every timelike line (and translates thereof), with actual modular groups as interval stabilizers. Its geometric conclusion is restricted to diamonds whose tips lie on that line.

Brunetti–Guido–Longo1994, actual body lines460–535 and700–744, uses a stronger geometric hypothesis. Its conformal criterion requires each modular group to act geometrically on ALL other local algebras, not only coaxial ones. The paper's central-extension splitting then integrates that existing modular covariance. Invoking this theorem now would assume the missing cross-line relation. The1993 paper lines470–535 likewise starts from a local bounded-algebra action; its later global extension is not a theorem upgrading infinitesimal fields to arbitrary bounded operations.

There is a concrete geometric obstruction to recovering the missing relation using only intersections and unions of coaxial diamonds. Every diamond with tips on a fixed timelike line is invariant under its spatial rotation subgroup SO(3). Every union/intersection of such regions retains that invariance. A double cone centered off the line is not invariant. Consequently no exact off-axis region reconstruction from these coaxial diamonds follows by these set operations. Nor can shrinking coaxial outer/inner bounds approximate an arbitrary off-axis double cone: its entire rotational orbit is forced into every invariant outer bound. This disproves only this isotony-only inference, not conformal covariance.

Changing the timelike line by a Poincare transformation conjugates the corresponding actual modular construction. It does not establish the mixed products between two such constructions. Equality of their affine subgroups also does not suffice: inequivalent positive-energy lowest-weight Möbius modules can have the same positive affine restriction (the source itself records this at C:time-primary-waves). A proof must exploit additional source charge identities or the reconstructed field action.

A possible junction with Route A is to identify its integrated null generators with the actual self-adjoint K_n via small-support Gårding cores. The original source cone modular group already agrees with its dilation subgroup. What remains is a theorem or proof taking those identifications to the actual finite-diamond modular groups, or directly to their action on all off-axis bounded algebras. Adding Haag duality, a split property or bounded-field generation would change the full target and is not being done.

The primary1993 duality theorem cannot shortcut this: it assumes conformal bounded-net covariance first. It also explicitly distinguishes essential duality on Minkowski space from double-cone Haag duality on the covering compactification. No Minkowski Haag-duality premise is inferred from a prospective field representation.

## A concrete positive defect, not just missing notation

Source F:parabolic-generator gives the actual geometric cut generator

    H_p=b(|Y|²+h),    M(W_p)=Ad(exp(iH_p)) M(W).

Source N:measure-proposition constructs the quadratic current moment

    K_n=b(|Y|²+κ),    0<=κ<=h,

and explicitly does not assert κ=h. Thus the positive commuting defect is

    H_p−K_n=b(h−κ).

The proposed null-core-interface, even if successful, identifies K_n with the field-derived conformal generator, not H_p. The actual bounded cut geometry is attached to H_p. Treating the two as equal would discard a stated source distinction. Source C:canonical-Pi only gives its specified one-field reducing-space identification; original polynomial vacuum generation does not say that this one-field space exhausts H.

A concrete sufficient next estimate for this particular route would be

    <ψ, b(h−κ)ψ> = 0

on a form core for the nonnegative difference, with a proof that the relevant field-word approximants are such a core. Its positivity then forces the difference to vanish. Merely showing zero on selected one-field waves does not do this. Neither finite grade closure nor local Ward identities supply this form-core saturation estimate in the work completed here. Alternatively, a direct bounded-net comparison could avoid proving this defect vanishes. Thus its vanishing is an unpaid sufficient mechanism, not asserted necessary for existence of some conformal action on the original net.
