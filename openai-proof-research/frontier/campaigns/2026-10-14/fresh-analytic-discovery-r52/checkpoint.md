## Verification and formal scope

It is not a PDE proof. `verify.py` authenticates exact files/directories against an EXTERNAL manifest hash, rejects links/nonregular entries/hardlinks and checks source Git blob hashes against the sealed complete-tree copy. It does not authenticate that tree independently of the external anchor; the parent can compare canonical bytes separately. `controls.py` runs positive and negative mutations on disposable copies within private storage and removes them. Run controls on a disposable packet copy when treating the original as frozen.

No Lean claim is made. A possible later formal scope is finite-dimensional square completion, the scalar Gaussian exponent identity and the Riccati bound after its analytic hypotheses are assumed.
