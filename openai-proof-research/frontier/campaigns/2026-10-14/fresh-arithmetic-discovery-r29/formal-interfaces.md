# Feasible scoped formal interfaces

No Lean file has been written and no formal pass is claimed. The following is a candidate plan only, contingent on independent proof/contribution gates.

1. Natural-number ceiling lemma: for n>=2 and r>=1, the least k with r<=nk is ceil(r/n); this k equals1 exactly when r<=n. Formalize the maximum equality-index invariant assuming the ideal staircase and I²<I.
2. LND order arithmetic: for m>=1,n>=2, successive order loss 1+(n−1)m takes order nm to m−1 and then (2−n)m−2<0. Prove the two-case numerical interface explicitly, not as a finite test.
3. Sparse polynomial identities (2.1), ΔH=p^(n+1), the determinant-one F,J change, and (2.4). Use a multivariate polynomial ring with symbolic t=p^n when convenient, then substitute powers. The ring/localization divisibility interfaces must be named rather than hidden.
4. Abstract invariant transfer: an algebra isomorphism identifying intrinsic S, the distinguished principal ideal, and reduced contraction ideals preserves the maximum in (7.2). This is the formal endpoint for pairwise parameter separation, conditional on those mathematical interfaces.

Not discharged by this scope: complete classification of LND kernels, torsor principalization and line-bundle linearization, source cubic rigidity, smooth affine geometry, the actual associated graded isomorphism with all cancellations, or full construction of every algebra isomorphism used in the manuscript. A supporting scoped certificate cannot be represented as a full formalization of the theorem. The real-valued Euler-characteristic comparison is ancillary and is outside this scope.
