## Actual primary operation comparison

Ghosh–Gupta, *On the triviality of a family of linear hyperplanes*, arXiv:2206.15210v5, 4 June 2023, Theorem4.1 and its full proof, Theorems4.2–4.3 and Corollary4.4 were read. They recover a polynomial coefficient subalgebra and exponent data from DK/ML invariants and contracted ideals for equations product(x_i^r_i)y=F. Their positive-characteristic corollary gives infinite nonisomorphic cancellation families. Thus recovering exponents by these invariants is a prior strategy. Our A_n has the explicitly computed S_n as DK, not a polynomial coefficient algebra. Its Euler characteristic is two, as checked in candidate §8. The current proof uses its own complete reduced contraction staircase; no arbitrary-r contraction formula is imported from this paper. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.15210v5 .

Ghosh–Pal, *A new family of counterexamples to the Zariski Cancellation Problem in positive characteristic*, arXiv:2404.13803v1, 22 April 2024, Theorem3.2 and proof were read. This extends the same invariant/exponent strategy to general coefficient polynomials α. Its hypotheses explicitly identify DK and ML with polynomial subalgebras. Those hypotheses are not our established interfaces. This is a close antecedent, not a theorem furnishing T047N directly. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.13803v1 .

Blanc–van Santen, *Embeddings of affine spaces into quadrics*, author-hosted 2017 preprint, introduction and §5.2 through the proof of Lemma5.10 were inspected as specified in reading-limits.md. Proposition5.8 recovers a fibration using uniqueness of A¹-fibrations on generic surface levels; Lemma5.10 then distinguishes embedded planes. Its ambient SL₂ is not the product Q₂×A¹ containing our center. It did not close the initial moving-cubic screen. T047N instead varies thickness and leaves the cubic fixed; it does not assert a classification of these centers. URL: https://algebra.dmi.unibas.ch/blanc/articles/embeddingsquadrics.pdf .

The source's other cited classical results were read only as deployed in its complete proof, not all in their original primary bodies. In particular no independent full read is claimed of Kaliman–Makar-Limanov, Brion, Traverso, Mason, Finston–Maubach or the residual-coordinate papers.

## Narrow author claim and independent gate

Residual work is the full specific complex family together with the exact intrinsic S_n/divisor/staircase calculation proving unmarked pairwise nonisomorphism. Generalizing the source identities, n=2 to n>=2, would alone be technical. Recovering exponents via Derksen/contraction invariants is also a known strategy. No claim of inventing either is made. Whether their specific combination and exact staircase constitute substantial new work requires independent contribution review; the author makes no favorable verdict, global novelty certification or acceptance count.
