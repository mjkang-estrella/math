For EVERY closed smooth orientable surface M (possibly disconnected) and EVERY
smooth positive definite Riemannian metric g on M, there exists a smooth

makes the latter automatic once injectivity and immersion are proved. There are
no curvature, genus, analyticity, smallness, or prescribed conformal-class
hypotheses. No estimate or universal numerical constant is asserted. The target
is exactly four-dimensional, not R^5, C^1, local, or merely immersed. It does not
claim approximation of a prescribed map or a prescribed knot class. Orientability
is part of the original target, not a subsequent scope reduction.

and boundary-geometry propagation. The unpaid operation is global injectivity
through those large derivative changes. Existence of an immersion and C^0
