## Campana–Claudon, 1107.0168v1

[Fixed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0168v1), *Abelianity Conjecture for special threefolds*. Read extracted lines 180–220, 360–435, 475–690. These include Theorem 2.2, the fibration exact sequence, Theorem 4.1 with its complete reduction proof, the metric argument through its ending, the surface MMP statements and the complete final proof of Theorem 2.2. The appendix classification proof was not read.

Theorem 2.2 is the special geometric orbifold **surface** theorem. Its proof uses the minimal model, orbifold elliptic/rational fibres, and Ricci-flat or Fano alternatives. The text itself explains the two-dimensional local uniformizability issue. It is not the all-dimensional manifold-to-root construction.

Theorem 4.1 starts with virtual abelianity of both base and fibre groups, obtains an almost polycyclic and hence linear group, removes torsion by Selberg, then uses a finite cover, resolution and the manifold mixed-Hodge statement. It cannot be invoked on the presently unknown orbifold group without its input hypotheses. Its exact-sequence and quotient operations are antecedents to the endpoint, but its proof does not duplicate the candidate's geometric realization.

## Campana, 0705.0737v8

[Fixed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0737v8), *Orbifoldes géométriques spéciales et classification biméromorphe des variétés kählériennes compactes*. Read extracted lines 4260–4350 and 5520–5840. This includes the Bogomolov-sheaf equivalence, full Corollary 11.29 and Theorem 11.31 proofs, Remark 11.30, the subsequent group criteria, Conjecture 12.10, Proposition 12.11 with proof, and Question 12.14. No claim to have read the complete long monograph.

The finite-uniformization construction separates finitely many local inertia elements in a finite quotient. Residual finiteness is a sufficient input. The resolution warning in Remark 11.30 is real: one must not assume that resolving a finite orbifold cover preserves the required group. OA057 instead descends root bundles before resolution and proves a quotient map, so it does not make that assumption.

Conjecture 12.10 contains the full orbifold abelianity expectation. Proposition 12.11 reduces it through additional orbifold inputs and an extension argument, rather than from the sole stipulated manifold theorem. Question 12.14 asks for commensurability and an analytic universal-cover realization. OA057's epimorphism from a higher-dimensional special manifold is weaker than that realization question. The conjectural status or significance of these primary statements does not determine the substantiality of a deduction relative to the much stronger stipulated 2026 proof operations.

## Belmans–Demleitner–Núñez, 2411.14814v1

[Fixed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14814v1), *Albanese morphism for hyperelliptic varieties*. Read extracted lines 340–395 and 670–885: affine decomposition and cocycle equations (21)–(23), Lemma 29, Proposition 30, Example 31, Proposition 32 including proofs, Table 1, Example 33 and Remark 34, and only the beginning of Proposition 35.

The affine linear/translation decomposition is standard. Proposition 32 uses an exact 2-torsion translation with inversion to realize a chosen Albanese fibre. Example 33 uses particular elliptic complex-multiplication scalar actions for small orders; Remark 34 explains the failure of that specific strategy at orders four and six. These statements neither provide nor forbid the OA057 all-M permutation action. The candidate allows its abelian dimension to grow with M and only demands freeness for designated inertia, not for every nonidentity element. Thus no free-action classification can be silently applied.

The technical assessment instead comes from the explicit conjugate-permutation calculation in review.md, compared with the pinned pairwise involution recipe. I do not attribute that exact OA057 formula or the whole all-order theorem to this primary paper.

## Kresch–Vistoli, math/0301249v1

[Fixed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0301249v1), *On coverings of Deligne–Mumford stacks and surjectivity of the Brauer map*. Entire seven-page extracted text read, including all proofs.

Theorem 2.1 constructs a finite-flat local-complete-intersection scheme cover of a quotient stack with quasi-projective coarse space, smooth when the stack is smooth. Proper projective coarse data yield a smooth projective source in the setting at issue. The proof starts with projectivized bundles, takes enough products to make the bad stabilizer locus relatively small, descends a power of an ample line bundle, and cuts by generic high-degree hypersurfaces to obtain a finite cover avoiding that locus. Lemma 3.2 is part of the descent comparison.

This is a genuine general manifold-source construction, but it does not give connected abelian general fibres or specialness preservation. A finite branched cover of a special P¹ can have genus at least two. Retaining the ambient projective bundle leaves the unwanted stack stabilizers; cutting them away changes the fibres to complete-intersection geometry. Thus Theorem 2.1 alone does not prove OA057 from manifold abelianity. The review does not label the candidate DUPLICATE on this basis.
