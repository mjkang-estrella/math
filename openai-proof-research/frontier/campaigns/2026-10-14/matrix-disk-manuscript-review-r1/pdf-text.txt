                     Disk rigidity for attained complete polynomial
                                     Crouzeix equality
                                                 Myeongjin Kang∗

                                                October 14, 2026



                                                      Abstract
              Relative to the pinned complete Crouzeix inequality and its exterior Faber operations, and
          to Jin’s geometric circle criterion, we prove that actual factor-two equality for one ﬁnite matrix-
          valued polynomial forces the numerical range to be a nondegenerate disk in every ﬁnite di-
          mension. We retain the Jensen deﬁcit in ﬁnite diagonal dual tests. Strong exterior boundary
          convergence and a triple-variance Fatou argument then make the diagonal compressed resolvents
          constant, without a rank-one assumption. An ordered constant test and equality in Parseval
          produce a ﬁxed matrix polynomial channel that is unitary on the boundary. Its rational inverse
          is holomorphic in the exterior and vanishes at inﬁnity. The determinant therefore realizes the
          boundary as a full polynomial level set. The numerical-range Hermitian tangent pencil supplies
          the additional hypothesis of the credited circle criterion. The argument allows coeﬃcient rank
          greater than one and assumes no initial boundary smoothness.
