Family 128: full reverse-complement shortest common superstring factor-two target
Author: Myeongjin Kang (no affiliation).
Original prose license: CC BY 4.0.

For every explicitly encoded finite alphabet Sigma, every explicitly encoded involution cm: Sigma -> Sigma, and every finite explicitly listed family S of finite words over Sigma, construct a deterministic algorithm of polynomial bit complexity in the total encoded input size that outputs a finite word T containing, for each s in S, either s or rho(s) as a contiguous substring, where rho reverses the word and applies cm letterwise, and whose length is at most 2 OPT_RC(S). OPT_RC is the minimum length of any such word. The algorithm and guarantee must be uniform in both the alphabet and involution. Fixed letters, fixed words (reverse-complement palindromes), arbitrary duplicates, substring redundancy, empty words, and the empty family are all included. If all requirements are empty, output the empty word. No fixed alphabet, absence-of-palindromes, bounded-length, or promised-period restriction is part of the target.
