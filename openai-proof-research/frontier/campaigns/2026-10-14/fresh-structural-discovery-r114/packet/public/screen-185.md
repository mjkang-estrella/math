# 185 — descriptive regularity is not supplied by the ordinal assembly

No target was frozen. The direction examined was a countable-ground-set packing/covering counterexample whose two independent-set families are Borel subsets of Cantor space. No such pair was constructed here. Nor is nonexistence asserted.

The source's stronger contracts were inspected, not only its counterexample theorem. In `03-filters.tex`, `lem:columns` produces continuum many columns with eventual exact finite-pattern densities. `lem:ultrafilter` extends a specified proper filter, avoids the larger ideal K, and imposes a finite-column condition. Its rank rho lists all large sets by ordinals below the continuum. `lem:thinning` raises that rank within a prescribed small/large interval, and `lem:reservations` permits fewer than continuum simultaneous reservations. The latter distinction matters: positive block density and rank invariance use different ideals. These are stipulated available operations.

The full `06-counterexample.tex` uses a local matroid Q on two copies of D, places Q on successive adjacent bundles of a double ray, and takes the two parity direct sums. For every independent covering, choose a large part of the central bundle, extend local independent sets to bases, and propagate outwards. Equation `eq:ordinal-descent` gives

    rho(x_k) >= rho(u_k) > rho(y_k) >= rho(x_(k+1)).

No independent covering can produce this infinite strict ordinal descent. Finite subsets of either matroid are independent; the same holds for their duals. The full source consequences section already gives the separate universal covering/packing failures by the Bowler–Carmesin equivalences. Those are not new transfers.

A direct Borelization of the *large/small classifier* cannot preserve its exact ultrafilter contract. A free ultrafilter U on a countable D is unchanged by finite modifications. If U were a Borel event under the fair product measure, its membership would be a tail event, hence have probability zero or one. Complementing every bit preserves measure and sends U to its set-theoretic complement in 2^D, because exactly one of X and D\X belongs to U. It would therefore have probability one half, a contradiction. Freeness here follows from avoidance of K, which contains every finite set. This classical zero-one observation concerns the auxiliary classifier. It does NOT prove that the final family of matroid bases or independent sets is non-Borel; no reduction recovering U from those families was proved.

The strongest alternative actually examined was the circuit-game construction in Bowler–Carmesin, *Infinite Matroids and Determinacy of Games*, [arXiv:1301.5980v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.5980v1), Section 6. Lemma 6.2 constructs a precircuit from the unique play reaching each tree node under a strategy. Lemma 6.4 transfers a strategy between the circuit and cocircuit games using local orthogonality and the local (O2) alternative. Corollaries 6.5–6.7 obtain (O2) and the Borel-ended overlap-one tree construction from determinacy. The source's bundle overlap is an entire countably infinite D, and its local Q is not the finite local input of the most immediate tree construction. More generally, the primary does not turn a source counterexample into a Borel independent-set family while preserving the covering obstruction. A Borel set of ends is also not, without an additional argument, a statement that the induced independent-set family is Borel.

Two attempted routes therefore remain incomplete: retaining the source classifier with a Borel presentation fails at the classifier; replacing it by a determined circuit game lacks a covering-obstruction transport and the required overlap/local-matroid interface. No general obstruction to the full descriptive question follows. The local-basis/assembly files were acquired but not semantically read in full, so no strongest-worldwide-operation or complete-family clearance is claimed.

Neither connectivity nor finite/cofinite modification is a new direction here. No tame, finitary, locally finite, or partial definability result is substituted for the contemplated Borel pair.
