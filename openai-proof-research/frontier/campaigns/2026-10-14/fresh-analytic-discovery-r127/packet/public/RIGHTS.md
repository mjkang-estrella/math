# Rights and distribution boundary

The files in public/ are original research prose and original verification code produced for this task. They contain mathematical formulas, source citations and bounded operation comparisons, not copied source-paper bodies. No publication or upload is performed or authorized by this packet.

Everything in private/ is evidence or preparation retained for local review: upstream TeX, external PDFs and extracted text, private renders, copied project history/context and search records. Those bodies retain their original rights and provenance. They are not relicensed by the public originals and must not be included in a public package merely because they are manifest-bound here. Parent integration controls any future distribution and attribution.
