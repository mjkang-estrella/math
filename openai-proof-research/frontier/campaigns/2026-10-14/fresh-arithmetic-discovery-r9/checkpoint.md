## Exact obstruction to the standard quadratic continuation route

This calculation is an original route diagnostic, not a nominated research theorem. It applies to every smooth quintic in the pinned source, because it uses its common four-dimensional numerical coordinates. Put `delta=u−1/8>0`. In coordinates twisted by `B=−H/2`, write

`p=c+r/2`, `q=d+c/2+r/8`, `s=e+d/2+c/8+r/48`.

The elementary identities `t_0^2=3/4−u` and `(Re(lambda)−1)/5=−u/2` rewrite the source charge as

`Z_G/5 = −s + (1/24−u)p + i t_0(q+delta r)`.

Consequently the nonzero **real numerical vector** `v_*` with twisted coordinates `(r,p,q,s)=(1,0,−delta,0)` lies in `ker Z_G`. In untwisted coordinates it is

`v_*=(1,−1/2,1/8−delta,−1/48+delta/2)`.

No object, integral class, or semistability is asserted for this vector. Negative definiteness of a support form on the real kernel must nevertheless test it.

For any real twist `beta`, put `A=−1/2−beta`. The coordinates of `v_*` twisted by `beta H` are

`(1,A,A^2/2−delta,A^3/6−delta A)`.

In the normalized coordinates, the BMS family is, up to the harmless positive factor 25,

`Q_K^beta = K Delta + Nabla_beta`,
`Delta=c_beta^2−2r d_beta`, `Nabla_beta=4d_beta^2−6c_beta e_beta`.

Direct expansion gives

`Delta(v_*)=2delta`,
`Nabla_beta(v_*)=2delta A^2+4delta^2`,
`Q_K^beta(v_*)=2delta(K+A^2+2delta)>0` for every real `beta` and every `K>=0`.

Thus **none** of the standard BMS forms with nonnegative discriminant coefficient is negative definite on `ker Z_G`. Changing the twist, adjusting a positive coefficient, or taking a nonzero finite nonnegative combination of these forms cannot repair this obstruction. The statement is about this specified family only, not all possible support forms. The Gepner source already supplies a different support form indirectly through its norm estimate.
