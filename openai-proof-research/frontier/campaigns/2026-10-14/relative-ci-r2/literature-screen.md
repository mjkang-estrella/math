Only primary texts support the mathematical operation comparisons. Search
snippets and secondary catalogue records were discovery aids. The direct
primary documents newly retained are:

1. Gasharov--Horwitz--Peeva, *Hilbert functions over toric rings*,
   https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~nhorwitz/toric.pdf . Read extraction lines
   115--205, including Proposition 2.6 and its complete proof. The proof
   homogenizes both ideals, preserves regularity of the parameter and its
   translate on both quotients, and specializes an actual free resolution.
   This operation already pays the simultaneous-pair degeneration route.
   It does not make the pinned module-only initial construction an algebra
   degeneration. This direct reading strengthens provenance of a known
   dependency; it is not a new theorem.

2. Caviglia--Kummini, *Poset embeddings of Hilbert functions and Betti
   numbers*, https://www.math.purdue.edu/~gcavigli/Articles-PDF/%5BM13%5D.pdf .
   Read lines 1--145, 198--242 and 525--600: notation, embeddings interface,
   Theorem 3.1, Lemma 3.2 proof, Theorem 4.1 and proof. Its Betti comparisons
   in Theorems 3.1 and 4.1 have ambient polynomial superscripts A,B. Their
   discussion before Theorem 3.1 explicitly distinguishes the unknown
   relative assertion from the ambient one. The extension theorem does not
   replace an arbitrary regular-sequence base by pure powers. No long
   full-paper proof audit is claimed.
