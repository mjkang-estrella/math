# T143D45: the global quintic Duffing–Liénard two-cycle question

For every real β≥0 and every real polynomial F of degree at most five, consider on the whole plane

x'=y−F(x),  y'=−x(1+βx²).

The proposed assertion is that this system has at most two geometrically distinct isolated periodic orbits. No restriction is made on the even coefficients of F, amplitude, hyperbolicity, stability, coefficient size, or the size of β. Periodic center orbits are not isolated and are not counted. In addition, for each fixed β≥0 there should exist such an F with two isolated periodic orbits, so the maximum is exactly two for each β.

This is a working question, not a proved candidate. It is frozen after exploratory source/history/primary reading and coordinate/kernel feasibility calculations, before assembly of the attempted full proof. The β=0 case is paid by the pinned source. No parity, small-damping, small-amplitude, compact-energy or β-small substitute may replace the global upper-bound target after a failure. The generic finite bound B(5) from the companion is not the proposed conclusion. T022BA43 remains unresolved and is not pursued in this packet.
