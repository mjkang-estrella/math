# Frozen full scope — TS132

Frozen 2026-10-16 after actual pinned source reading and bounded primary/history comparison; before the complete proof and adversarial checks are written. No later reduction to a subclass, spectral sensitivity, partial functions, numerical examples, or symmetry only of row indices is allowed.

For every real C>0 there exist an integer n>=1, a nonconstant total Boolean function f:{0,1}^n->{0,1}, and a subgroup G<=S_n acting transitively on all n input coordinates, such that f is invariant under G, f(0)=0, and bs(f,0)>C s(f)^2, where s is the maximum ordinary single-bit sensitivity over every input and bs(f,0) is the maximum number of disjoint nonempty sensitive blocks at zero.

The complete target also includes a fixed-power consequence: there exist alpha>2 and nonconstant total coordinate-transitive Boolean functions F_m, m>=1, with F_m(0)=0, bs(F_m,0)>=s(F_m)^alpha for every m, and bs(F_m,0) tending to infinity. Neither alpha nor a dimension-optimal construction is claimed optimal. No claim about cyclic invariance, full symmetric invariance, graph properties, sensitivity o(n^(1/3)), or minterm-transitivity is included.

Proposed operation: an affine-equivariant labelled lexicographic tournament on F_p^4 for p=3 mod4. The label is the first nonzero coordinate of the oriented difference, lying in the nonzero quadratic residues H. Every head-constant-label subset has at most 3^4=81 rows. Translations and H-scaling act transitively on the complete row-position set F_p^4 x H. This supplies both a uniform forbidden-label bound and coordinate transitivity for the source's nested-predicate recursion. The complete argument remains to be independently validated.
