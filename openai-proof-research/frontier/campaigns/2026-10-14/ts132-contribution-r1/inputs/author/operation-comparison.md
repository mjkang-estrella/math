# Bounded operation and contribution comparison

This is an author comparison for independent review, not a substantiality verdict or worldwide priority certification. The full TS132 scope remains fixed. The candidate is proposed because of a concrete compatible-label construction, not because searches returned no duplicate.

## Closest supplied proof

The pinned family132 manuscript proves superquadratic separation for total Boolean functions without transitivity. Its Lemma `lem:labelling` uses independent uniform labels on a regular tournament; the union bound excludes16-row head-constant configurations. Its recursive clauses need a single fixed label assignment through every depth. Nothing in that union bound preserves the automorphism action required to move every row-position pair. Independent labels cannot simply be declared equivariant. Averaging Boolean values need not stay Boolean; taking an OR over group translates has no source bound controlling the new zero-side sensitivity.

TS132 replaces precisely this random-label interface. First nonzero coordinate differences on F_p^4 give a deterministic head-constant-set bound81, independent of p, while square-scalar affine maps act transitively on F_p^4×H and transport every gate label. Both conclusions hold for the *same* labels. The asymptotic ratios r/M→0 and k/M² bounded above1 leave the original four-profile argument effective. Source base predicates, four recurrences, balancing and amplification remain credited. The changed constant16→82 is not itself the proposed contribution.

## Closest external antecedents

**Meiburg (2026), arXiv:2608.00851v1.** The complete mathematical main body §§1–6 was read. Gated tournament certificates and Paley structure are direct antecedents. The rigorous argument uses independently random labels and local-lemma exclusion of radius-one/two patterns to control spectral sensitivity. Section6.1 explicitly suggests cyclotomic labels with transitive block symmetry and gives empirical spectral values. Thus algebraic labels or symmetry alone cannot receive contribution credit here. TS132 uses a different, proved first-difference bound through fixed lexicographic depth, then the source's ordinary/joint profiles. The inequality λ(f)≤s(f) does not turn his spectral upper bound into the ordinary upper bound required here. No audit of his numerical or formal certificate is claimed. [Primary body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00851v1).

**Chakraborty–Kayal–Paraashar (2025), journal PDF carrying arXiv:2103.12355v7.** The actual §4 encoding/transitivity proof moves raw bits by coordinated cell and pointer actions, with a total decoder including invalid encodings. Its complexity transfer concerns the stated pointer-function measures. The introduction expressly distinguishes this from a general transitive conversion; Table1 retains the quadratic Rubinstein example for bs versus s. This supplies concrete context for the stronger TS132 scope, not an argument that every symmetry upgrade is substantial. No reduction from their decoder to the needed four ordinary/joint profiles was found in the read proof. [Primary body](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/15992/pdf).

**Chaubal–Gál, ECCC TR20-134.** The read portions give sensitivity bounds for specified DNF/CNF subclasses. Lemma5 and Theorem1 use certificate size and term counting; they do not construct a transitive superquadratic separator. They make clear that transitivity and stronger representation restrictions are different conditions. The later2023 publication was located, but its complete body was not read and is not asserted identical to this retained2020 version. [Primary preprint](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/134/download/).

## What independent contribution review should decide

The proposed residual contribution is the simultaneous deterministic label exclusion and coordinate transitivity, sufficient to cross the quadratic ordinary-sensitivity threshold within the researched transitive class. This goes beyond merely choosing a new parameter in the old random construction, but its sufficiency and substantiality are separate questions. A technical disposition would require showing that this exact compatible-label operation is already a routine instance of an inspected general construction; a duplicate disposition should identify a sufficient earlier ordinary-sensitivity theorem. Conversely, absence of a search hit, a short proof, or the parent's desire for two remaining results is not evidence of substantiality. The scoped evidence above is offered without a self-awarded verdict.
