## Actual primary interfaces screened before choosing LR

1. Family185, infinite matroids: full202-line introduction read. Theorem
thm:main uses a countable ground set, two labelled self-dual matroids,
and ordinal descent to obstruct EVERY independent covering. Corollary
cor:intersection already pays packing/covering-to-intersection, and the
local uniform matroid existence is explicitly included. Neither that
transfer nor restating its uniform-matroid consequence is a new target.
Changing to finitary matroids abandons its mechanism and meets known
countable positive results. No concrete new construction was selected.

2. Family206, finite lattice representation: actual negative-solution
introduction and companion m:main(i)-(iv) read. One must distinguish the
GLOBAL Palfy-Pudlak equivalence from a pointwise equivalence. The source
already has a self-dual subgroup obstruction and a mathematically specified
minimal negative congruence lattice using a Busy Beaver constant. A
self-dual-product trick, subgroup-to-field transfer, or noncomputable
witness-size consequence would add no substantial operation. An actually
evaluated small obstruction would require a different quantitative
construction; no credible concrete mechanism emerged in this screen.

3. Family247, periodic residually finite groups: complete155-line
introduction read. Theorem thm:main gives ordinary finite presentation,
infinitude, residual finiteness and2-power torsion simultaneously. Passing
to its finite-index subgroup is already part of the source; unbounded
orders already follow from restricted Burnside. Repackaging those as new
corollaries was rejected. No new controlled center/quotient construction
was developed or pursued.

4. Family162, selected: both actual paper introductions/statements and
assembly read, followed by balanced construction.tex IN FULL. Balanced
thm:main gives r=q+1, tau=r, exactly r nonisolated vertices per part,
for every sufficiently large prime q. Extension thm:main gives
q=s^n with s sufficiently large prime congruent2mod3 and every odd
n>=n0(s); it does not assert balance. Both permit multiple intersections.
Both already contain cor:tree-cover with proof, so the monochromatic
transfer is rejected as a discovery. Linearity instead requires actual
unique pair agreement, which arbitrary coloring cannot enforce because
each color's connected components must be cliques/blocks.

Primary FHMW Lemmas2.1-2.2 and Theorem2.3 proofs were read BEFORE freeze;
its construction section was read further during work. These already
supply substantial necessary bounds, but no general operation removing
duplicate intersections while preserving covering. The selected refinement
mechanism would preserve the cover lower bound for free if it could preserve
intersection. Its all-configuration failure is now proved, rather than
hidden as an assumed bridge. A different pencil-based addition was then
carried through and rejected by an exact side-count argument.

Only LR was frozen and pursued. No second target was substituted after its
mechanisms failed. The two checkpoints are support, not new accepted results.
