# Rights

The public prose, registers and code in `packet/public` and `packet/code` were written for this research round. The parent may integrate them.

`packet/private` holds third-party and upstream material under its original rights:

- pinned openai/math source files;
- arXiv PDFs and their `pdftotext` extractions;
- copies of project context and history files;
- search outputs;
- the acquisition record.

This material is kept only as evidence for reading and provenance. No redistribution or publication is implied.
