# Family 190: the canonical examples do not supply an arbitrary-query lower bound

Status: pre-freeze screen; no nominee or new tester theorem.

Let A_h be the pinned 66-by-66-pattern construction, m=2^h and n=(386h+2)m. The source gives distance at least m^2 cells from H-freeness and an exponential lower bound for uniform independent row/column sampling. This does not lower-bound arbitrary entry-query algorithms.

There is a nonadaptive correlated-query procedure, knowing h and the source position layout, with at most 4h*66^2+2 entry queries. Sample exactly the path and the positions from source Proposition prop:distance. Query the two signed V frames and the two signed W frames at each depth, including each frame's 64-by-64 anchor, and the two root cells. Reject only if a queried frame is exactly H. The queried locations depend only on the sampled path and internal randomness, not on observed bits.

For every binary B differing from A_h in at most alpha*m^2 entries, where 0<=alpha<1, this procedure rejects with probability at least 1-alpha. Indeed each changed protected cell (one endpoint anchor/dummy, or both endpoints root) is selected with probability m^-2. The source union bound says that with probability at least 1-alpha every selected protected entry is unchanged. Conditional on that event, if every queried frame avoided H, the four source propagation implications at each level would force the shared leaf cell to be both 0 and 1. Thus one queried frame is H. On every H-free input the procedure accepts, since rejection requires an explicit full H copy. It is not a tester for all inputs far from H-freeness: only the displayed neighborhood has a rejection guarantee.

Consequently no distribution supported on a constant-alpha edit neighborhood of this known template can witness a superpolynomial lower bound against all one-sided entry-query procedures. Query complexity is O(h)=O(epsilon_h^(-1/2)), even without adaptivity. Hiding the layout, or constructing a different hard family, needs a further argument. This deduction is already implicit in the source's distance proof and is technical support, not a contribution candidate.
