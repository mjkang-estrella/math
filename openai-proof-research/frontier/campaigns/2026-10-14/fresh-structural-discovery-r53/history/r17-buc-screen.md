Actual preliminary mathematical deductions: for a finite pattern F with m
edges and a symmetric signed kernel U with |U|<=1, expansion gives
 t(F,(1+U)/2)+t(F,(1-U)/2)
 =2^(1-m) sum_{A subset E(F), |A| even} t(F[A],U).
Consequently the nonempty even-subset sum must be negative. An even number
of edges only preserves the full-subset term under sign reversal; it does
not control the proper even subsets. Scaling the nonnegative mean-one
source kernel into [0,1] leaves its complementary density uncontrolled.

The tentative vertex-sign product lift U'((x,s),(y,t))=st U(x,y) retains
only spanning edge subsets with even degree at EVERY vertex: independent
sign averaging gives the factor product_v E s_v^deg_A(v). Since every
face vertex of source H has degree3, its full 66-edge term disappears.
This rejects that lift, not BUC. An endpoint-sum lift (s+t)U/2 instead has
an even-orientation expansion; it was considered but not developed here.
No source rank-layer modifications, biregular pattern construction or
I13 collapsed-map/sign-average repair were attempted. No mathematical
