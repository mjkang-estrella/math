Family 099 — unrestricted distance-scale embedding target.

For all finite alphabets Sigma with at least two symbols, all integers n>=1 and K>=1, seek one finite-dimensional real l1 map F:Sigma^{<=n}->l1^M, with M allowed to depend on Sigma,n,K, such that after one common rescaling

 min(ED(x,y),K) <= ||F(x)-F(y)||_1
 <= exp(C sqrt(log(K+2) log log(K+3))) min(ED(x,y),K)

for every x,y in Sigma^{<=n}, including the empty word. C is an absolute constant independent of Sigma,n,K. ED permits unit-cost insertions, deletions and substitutions, with no restriction on intermediate lengths. No finite query subset, periodic-free hypothesis, bound n<=poly(K), special alphabet size, or promise ED(x,y)<=K may replace these simultaneous capped-metric inequalities. No efficient computation or dimension bound beyond finiteness is requested. K>=n is source-paid; the intended new mechanism must address arbitrary n/K and arbitrary repetitive strings.

Chronology: the exact family mapping, bounded old/new authored history, all three source introductions and relevant upper/finite-tree operations, and the closest CGKK2018 section5 have been read before this scope freeze. The capped-scale interface and obstacles to globally noncontracting blocks and naive periodic-offset amortization were explored before this file was written. No complete construction or theorem has been found. The freeze is before any complete proof assembly; it is not represented as preceding all exploration. Failure of a proposed repair will remain failure at this full scope, not be rescued by a periodic-free, finite-query, weaker-distortion or bounded-aspect variant. No new stronger side-target of094 is involved.
