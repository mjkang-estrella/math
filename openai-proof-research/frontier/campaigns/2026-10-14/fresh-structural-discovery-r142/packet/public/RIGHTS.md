# Rights boundary

Files under packet/public are newly authored explanatory prose and original code, except explicitly named bibliographic metadata and links. Nothing in this directory authorizes publication of the research packet. Publication remains stopped.

Files under packet/private retain their upstream, primary-author or project-history rights. They include source/primary bodies, extracted text, copied originals, search results and navigation data. They are local reading evidence and are not public prose. Hashing or copying a body does not change its rights. Work scripts are original local acquisition, navigation and diagnostic scratch; their generated private bodies stay private.

No source prose is offered as newly authored mathematics. The copied integrity fixture is explicitly labelled as a copy of this run's own verifier and code; it is not an independent proof checker.
