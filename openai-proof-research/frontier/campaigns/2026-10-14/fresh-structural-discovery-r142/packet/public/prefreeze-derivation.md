# Interval quotients: prefreeze derivation

Status: PREFREEZE / no nominee. These are feasibility calculations and supporting deductions, not a frozen author theorem, an independent grade, or an acceptance request. Original scopes elsewhere remain unchanged. In particular this is not I13 or relative-ci-r2.

## Full operation investigated

Let k be any characteristic-zero field, S=k[x_1,...,x_n], n≥1, and F=(f_1,...,f_n) a homogeneous regular sequence with 2≤a_1≤...≤a_n, deg f_i=a_i. Set P=(x_1^{a_1},...,x_n^{a_n}). For each homogeneous ideal H containing F, let L(H) be its canonical lex-plus-P ideal: outside P take the lexicographically greatest monomials in each degree in the number prescribed by dim H_d−dim P_d. The stipulated source makes this an ideal. Inclusion of Hilbert functions implies inclusion of these ideals.

The trial full operation is to replace every member of any finite flag
F⊆I_0⊆...⊆I_m⊆S by L(I_t), preserving each Hilbert function and increasing every ambient graded Betti number of every interval I_b/I_a (0≤a≤b≤m), in every homological degree p≥0 and internal degree j∈Z. No degree, dimension, flag length or intermediate Betti row is discarded. This is equivalent to the corresponding assertion for every pair I⊆J: canonical replacements are already nested, so all pairs in a finite flag would then follow at once. The finite-flag packaging itself is not an additional contribution.

This question is NOT asserted as a theorem. No exact statement was frozen because its full feasibility did not close. Its base ring remains S throughout; the old relative-ci-r2 target instead changes complete-intersection base rings and asks for their infinite Tor. The present work neither repairs nor weakens that old scope.

## Paid reduction to a common monomial flag

Use the source's commuting division-coefficient forms for F once, independent of the ideals. After the source extension K of C, its bimodule N=Delta[x] is free (hence flat) over the left polynomial ring S_C, and free of finite rank dim_K Delta over the right polynomial ring T=K[y] generated by the commuting forms, with generators in degree zero on the right. Write q=dim_K Delta. Tensor a minimal S-free resolution of ANY graded S-module M with N. Left freeness preserves exactness, right freeness makes each term T-free, and positive-degree entries preserve minimality. Thus the right-T Betti dimensions are q times the original ones. This argument applies to M=I_b/I_a, not only to cyclic quotients.

Filter all I_t N by the same monomial order. The source leading-coefficient argument gives 0 or the entire Delta at each monomial. Inclusion is preserved, hence produces a monomial flag M_t containing P. Use the induced filtrations on I_b N/I_a N: finite-dimensional degree pieces give strict exactness and associated graded Delta tensor (M_b/M_a). In each internal degree the filtered Koszul complex has the same chain dimensions and its associated graded differential cannot have higher rank than the original. This gives simultaneous Betti domination for every interval, after cancelling q. This is a compatible application of the actual source operations, not an unpaid representation principle.

The finite coefficient field of the regular sequence and generators of the whole flag embeds into C. Faithfully flat base changes preserve regularity, inclusions, Hilbert functions and Koszul homology. Monomial outputs descend by their exponents. This extends the common construction from C to arbitrary characteristic-zero k, using the source's actual coefficient-field argument. It does not assert an arbitrary-field characteristic-p version.

## Paid simultaneous stabilization

For each failed box transfer use the same root-of-unity distraction on all flag members and then take the same initial order. Distraction preserves inclusions and the pure powers. The ratio-entry transformation of multigraded free complexes preserves exactness and Betti tables, including the subquotient complexes and their maps; the BCR exact-functor proof supplies this compatibility. Initial degeneration then increases each interval's Betti numbers.

The source's lex-prefix potential is nondecreasing for every member under each such move, and strictly increases for a selected member failing the transfer. The sum over the finite flag increases strictly and is bounded because the box is finite. Therefore a common sequence reaches a box-stable flag. A claim that common stabilization itself requires a new mixing or compatibility theorem would be incorrect.

Here box-stability means: for each box monomial u in the ideal, if x_h divides u and i<h, then x_i u/x_h is in the ideal whenever it remains in the box. Transfers landing in P hold automatically. The argument below uses the resulting coefficient-slice condition m_R I_s⊆I_{s−1}.

## Exact recursion for stable interval quotients

This supporting identity works over any field for a pair of box-stable monomial ideals P⊆I⊆J. Write S=R[z], R=k[x_1,...,x_{n−1}], z=x_n, a=a_n, and I_s={u∈R:u z^s∈I}, J_s likewise. These increase with s and equal R for s≥a. Let m_R be the homogeneous maximal ideal. Stability gives m_R I_s⊆I_{s−1} and m_R J_s⊆J_{s−1} for 1≤s<a. To see this, multiply a monomial of I_s by z^s and transfer one z to an earlier variable; a transfer outside the earlier box is already in P. Thus I_s/I_{s−1} and J_s/J_{s−1} are annihilated by m_R.

Write B_R(M;t,u)=sum beta^R_{p,j}(M)t^p u^j and H_V(u)=sum dim_k V_j u^j. For 1≤s<a put

K_s=(I_s intersect J_{s−1})/I_{s−1},
C_s=J_s/(J_{s−1}+I_s).

Then

B_S(J/I;t,u)
= B_R(J_0/I_0;t,u)
+ t u^a B_R(J_{a−1}/I_{a−1};t,u)
+ (1+tu)^{n−1} sum_{s=1}^{a−1} u^s [H_{C_s}(u)+t H_{K_s}(u)].                 (1)

Proof. Multiplication by z is injective on I and J, as they are ideals in a polynomial domain, including the zero ideal. Thus the natural derived base-change isomorphism identifies Tor over S of I and J with Tor over R of I/zI and J/zJ, including the map induced by inclusion. Their decompositions by z-exponent are

I/zI = I_0 direct-sum [sum_{s=1}^{a−1}(I_s/I_{s−1})(−s)] direct-sum (R/I_{a−1})(−a),

and similarly for J. These are R-module decompositions and are natural for inclusion. Applying derived tensor with k, the cone of the inclusion computes the Tor of J/I. The zero slice is the cone of I_0→J_0 and hence J_0/I_0. The top slice is the cone of the surjection R/I_{a−1}→R/J_{a−1}; its only homology is J_{a−1}/I_{a−1} in homological degree one. Each middle slice is a map between graded vector spaces killed by m_R. It splits over k into kernel, image and cokernel; the same splitting is R-linear. Its cone is C_s plus K_s shifted by one in homological degree. For such a vector space V, its R-Betti series is H_V(u)(1+tu)^{n−1}. Accounting for the internal shifts gives (1). All splittings here are of these annihilated middle modules, not an unjustified splitting of a general long exact Tor sequence. For n=0 the terminal recursion is simply the vector-space dimension in degree zero.

This derivation retains map kernels and cokernels that cannot be recovered from two unrelated Betti tables. It is still a composition of source endpoint decomposition and classical derived-base-change/mapping-cone operations; no substantial-contribution assertion is made for it.

The original code interval_betti.py implements (1) for standard-monomial downsets A of S/I and B of S/J. Its contract requires stable ideals; it is not a universal algorithm for arbitrary pairs. In that notation the middle standard sets are
K_s=(A_{s−1} minus A_s) minus B_{s−1},
C_s=(B_{s−1} intersect A_s) minus B_s.

## What earlier-variable compression already pays

Compress every coefficient ideal in R to its canonical box lex ideal, retaining its Hilbert function. The coefficient chains and cross-inclusions remain compatible, and the source shadow comparison supplies the stability condition. In each fixed degree the two relevant lex subspaces have intersection dimension equal to the minimum of their dimensions, the largest possible intersection. Since

dim K_s = dim(I_s intersect J_{s−1})−dim I_{s−1},
dim C_s = dim J_s−dim J_{s−1}−dim I_s+dim(I_s intersect J_{s−1}),

both middle contributions in (1) can only increase. If the full interval assertion in n−1 variables were available, it would handle the two endpoint pairs. Thus slice-wise compression is a compatible inductive step. It does not move dimensions among the z-slices to the globally canonical lex pair.

## Paid minimal-generator and top-row comparisons

For the original arbitrary pair F⊆I⊆J, the complete minimal-generator row already follows from the Hilbert-function source. For d≥1,

beta^S_{0,d}(J/I)=dim J_d−dim(I_d+S_1 J_{d−1}).

Let H=F+(J_{d−1}) be the homogeneous ideal generated by F and the whole degree d−1 subspace of J. Then H_{d−1}=J_{d−1} and H_d=F_d+S_1J_{d−1}. Its canonical lex-plus-P replacement has degree d−1 piece L(J)_{d−1}; idealness therefore gives

dim H_d ≥ dim(P_d+S_1 L(J)_{d−1}).

The space I_d+S_1 J_{d−1} contains both I_d and H_d, so its dimension is at least the maximum of their dimensions. Modulo P_d, L(I)_d and P_d+S_1L(J)_{d−1} are lex segments; the latter assertion is the usual box-lex shadow property for sorted powers, used in the source box proof. Their sum consequently has the larger dimension. Since dim I_d=dim L(I)_d, this proves

dim(I_d+S_1J_{d−1}) ≥ dim(L(I)_d+S_1L(J)_{d−1}),

and hence beta_{0,d}(J/I)≤beta_{0,d}(L(J)/L(I)). Degree zero is immediate from the same Hilbert functions. The reasoning works in the quotient box: it does NOT assert that the full polynomial-ring shadow of a powers ideal is a lex segment. This resolves the caution in the earlier working note.

For the top row let A=S/F, with socle degree s=sum a_i−n. The graded Frobenius pairing identifies Hom_A(J/I,A) with
N=(F:I)/(F:J) and with (J/I)^vee(−s).

For completeness, apply Hom_A(−,A) to 0→J/I→A/I→A/J→0; A is self-injective, so the resulting quotient of annihilators is exactly N. Graded finite-length duality over S gives

beta^S_{p,j}(J/I)=beta^S_{n−p,sum a_i−j}(N).

The colon ideals contain F. Their Hilbert functions are determined individually by those of I and J through the perfect pairing on A. For the monomial complete intersection S/P, exponent reflection u↦(a_1−1−u_1,...,a_n−1−u_n) reverses lex order in complementary degrees. A monomial is in P:L exactly when its reflected box monomial is outside L. Thus P:L(I) and P:L(J) are again the canonical lex-plus-P ideals for the residual Hilbert functions. Apply the just-proved beta_0 comparison to (F:J)⊆(F:I), then dualize back. This gives the complete beta_n row of the trial inequality. These two endpoint rows are supporting paid deductions, not a reduced-scope nomination. For n=1 they settle the trial, but no one-variable substitute is proposed.

## Exact remaining obligation

For every n, every sorted box and every box-stable pair I⊆J containing P, prove coefficientwise that the right side of (1) is bounded by the same expression for the globally canonical pair L(I)⊆L(J). In particular the unresolved intermediate rows are 1≤p≤n−1, with all degrees j. This is a sufficient residual obligation after the compatible reductions above. No proof or counterexample is supplied for it.

Separate endpoint containment is insufficient for a termwise proof. In four square variables set J=S and let the standard monomials of S/I be {1,x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4,x_1x_4,x_2x_4,x_3x_4}. The lex standard set replaces x_1x_4 by x_2x_3. At z=x_4, the zero-slice quotient is R/m_R^2 with beta_{1,2}=6, whereas its global lex counterpart has beta_{1,2}=5. The complete comparison in this example is already paid by the source, since J=S. Thus a correct full argument needs compensation between terms of (1). Failure of this particular termwise shortcut is not novelty evidence or a counterexample to the full trial.
