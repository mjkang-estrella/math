## Closest primary operations actually inspected

**Gaboriau2000**, *Coût des relations d'équivalence et des groupes*, Invent.Math.139,41–98, DOI10.1007/s002229900019, author PDF at https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/Cout/Cout.pdf. Original pp89–91 were rendered and inspected. Proposition VI.21 proves minimum-cost attainment using diagonal products and lifted graphings; VI.23 gives the commuting-marker construction used for B; VI.24 records normal/intersection propagation criteria. These are credited. Product attainment alone retains any asymptotically invariant factor of the input actions and therefore does not establish our gap. We do not assert failure of every classical criterion for Γ: the required hypotheses have not been independently classified. The candidate supplies an action-specific construction.

**Abért–Weiss**, arXiv1103.1063v2, https://arxiv.org/html/1103.1063v2, introductory Theorem1 and Corollary2, with the surrounding weak-containment definitions, were read in the actual body. Bernoulli actions have maximal cost among free actions, and all free factors of i.i.d. have that cost. This comparison prevents misdescribing Y as a Γ-factor of i.i.d.: its Bernoulli restriction is to H only. Their result does not itself construct a minimum-cost action with a prescribed spectral gap. Theorem1's local random-label proof was also read; it supplies weak containment, not our quotient-dependent graphing.

**Hutchcroft–Pete**, arXiv1810.11015v2, https://arxiv.org/html/1810.11015v2, Theorems1.2/3.1, Proposition2.1's connecting construction, the terminal proof of1.2, and the closing discussion of weakly mixing limits were inspected. Their method extracts sparse connected structures via percolation and property(T), with a relative-property(T) extension requiring an infinite normal subgroup. No such hypothesis is used or established for T259S. Their weakly-mixing-limit condition is explicitly identified as equivalent to property(T), not as an automatic consequence of one spectral-gap action. Their full percolation proof was not read.

**Kechris**, *Global Aspects of Ergodic Group Actions*, Chapter10, remains a partially retrieved comparison lead: search/mirrored excerpts point to minimum-cost density and upper semicontinuity, but local PDF retrieval failed404 and mirror-body retrieval failed403. No full chapter read is claimed, and no unverified theorem from it is a premise. Minimum-cost attainment itself was checked in Gaboriau's original proof above. Generic weak-mixing refinements, if invoked in a later comparison, require their exact hypotheses to be read; weak mixing alone is not claimed as our residual contribution.

## Remaining comparison limits and proposed credit

The source bibliography also cites Khezeli2509.08325v4, Bevilacqua–Bowen2510.05459v1, and Slutsky2607.20273v1. Their bodies were not read here and none is a premise. No completeness claim about modern cost criteria is made. Levitt1995, Kechris–Miller and Kesten originals were not read; standard elementary gap estimates are proved internally instead of being attributed to an unread exact statement.

The narrow proposed contribution is an explicit quotient satisfying two simultaneous requirements: a row graphing propagates from an arbitrarily small measurable set, and a free rank-two subgroup acts Bernoulli on the entire extension. The same source group's cost variation then persists among spectral-gap actions. The source supplies cost variation itself, and classical work supplies graphing, cost and Bernoulli tools. Whether this simultaneous construction is substantial rather than technical is deliberately left to an independent contribution review. No global novelty claim, acceptance or count is made.
