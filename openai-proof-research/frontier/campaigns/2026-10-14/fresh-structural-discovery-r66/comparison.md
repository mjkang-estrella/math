# Operation-level comparison and contribution risk

This is an author comparison, not an independent contribution verdict or worldwide-priority certificate.

1. **Source314** supplies the entire chromatic conversion r_h=ell, all heights including0, and the existential finite-spectrum witnesses. It does not supply the particular finite-field groups or the minimum scalar-commutator-rank lower bound for ell. Its profinite theorem additionally requires homomorphism integrality and subgroup congruences; sectional rank alone is not its hypothesis. We neither audit nor broaden source truth.
2. **de las Heras–Traustason**, *Powerfully solvable and powerfully simple groups*, arXiv2006.12593v1, §6, supplies the exact type-(2,…,2) group/algebra framework. Its pth-power identification and correspondence between powerful subgroups and alternating subalgebras are credited. Theorems6.8–6.9 and Corollary6.10 concern powerful composition chains and powerful solvability. They do not, in the inspected section, state the bound ell≥d+rho for arbitrary cyclic subnormal chains allowing order-p steps and subgroups not of type(2,…,2). Treating all subgroups as alternating subalgebras would be incorrect: the correct general data are U⊆W with beta(U,U)⊆W. The proposed new step explicitly handles this difference.
3. **Gow–Quinlan**, *Galois extensions and subspaces of alternating bilinear forms with special rank properties*, arXiv0709.0741v1, Lemmas1–3/Theorem1, supplies precisely Tr(a(x sigma(y)−sigma(x)y)), the radical equation, and constant rank d−1 for a cyclic odd-degree extension with generating automorphism. Our choice sigma(y)=y^p is a literal instance. Neither the forms nor the rank computation is claimed new.
4. **Kuhn–Lloyd**, *Chromatic fixed point theory and the Balmer spectrum for extraspecial 2-groups*, arXiv2008.00330 retrieved PDF, Definitions2.5–2.6 and §5, supplies the r_minus/r_plus perspective, quotient reductions and cyclic-chain upper bound. Example5.13 already has arbitrarily large gaps r_plus(G,H)−r_minus(G,H) for dihedral groups and a noncentral H. Example5.15 warns that cyclic lengths need not add under products. Accordingly we claim neither the first unbounded gap in general nor an unproved product amplification. The present full target concerns H=1, every fixed odd prime, exponent p²/class2/powerful groups and exact sectional rank, with a linear quantitative excess established by a commutator-rank obstruction.
5. **Classical operations** include the cocycle realization of class-two groups, Frattini/Burnside generator counting, finite-field trace duality, cyclic multiplicative groups, and maximal isotropic dimension. They are proved or explicitly credited in the candidate. The substantive hinge proposed for review is candidate§3: each order-p² factor creates a fresh projected direction, and one functional at the last such factor controls all these directions despite arbitrary intervening order-p steps. The chromatic consequence is a source application after that hinge, not an additional independent finding.

A routine-reduction verdict remains possible. The two old constructions plus source314 are all established inputs; a different statement alone is insufficient. A contribution reviewer should test whether the arbitrary-series/last-factor argument is already implicit in primary group theory or reduces to an existing lower bound with no real new operation. Bounded web searches of cyclic series, character degrees, powerful groups and constant-rank forms did not establish an antecedent, but their mostly irrelevant returns do not prove absence. No complete literature audit is claimed.
