# SAT covariance discovery — status

Date: 2026-10-14. Campaign directory only. No acceptance, no ledger edit, no count change, no publication.

## Result

The central limit theorem for the first unsatisfiable prefix is not proved, and it is not refuted. For every fixed \(k\ge 8\) and every fixed cap density \(B>U_k\), the missing \(L^2\) concentration of predictable variation is equivalent to one explicit pre-window sum. That sum is still only known to be \(O(n^2)\).

The original target remains open: in the i.i.d. uniform proper signed \(k\)-clause model with replacement, for fixed \(k\ge 4\) and \(B>U_k=\log 2/[-\log(1-2^{-k})]\),
\[
\frac{T-\mathbb E[T]}{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(T)}}\Rightarrow N(0,1),
\]
where \(T=\min(H,\lfloor Bn\rfloor)\), \(H\) is the first unsatisfiable prefix, centering is the finite-size mean, and \(\operatorname{Var}(T)=\Theta(n)\) is the pinned variance theorem. No limit of \(\operatorname{Var}(T)/n\) is assumed.

## What is proved

Notation matches `proofs.md`. The natural filtration is \(\mathcal F_i=\sigma(C_1,\ldots,C_i)\), \(M_i\) is the Doob increment of \(T\), \(v_i=\mathbb E[M_i^2\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}]\), and \(Q=\sum_{i\le L}v_i\). Then \(\mathbb E[Q]=\operatorname{Var}(T)\). The requested convergence \(Q/\operatorname{Var}(T)\to 1\) in probability is exactly Major’s conditional-variance condition (1.2). I347 already checked conditional Lindeberg for \(k\ge 4\). Repeating Lindeberg is not the remaining step.

**Theorem A** (\(k\ge 4\)). With \(w_n=\lceil n^{1/2}(\ln n)^2\rceil\) and \(s_n=\lfloor\mathbb E[T]-w_n\rfloor\),
\[
\sum_{i>s_n}\mathbb E[M_i^2]=O\bigl(n^{1/2}(\ln n)^2\bigr).
\]
The predictable energy on \(\{1,\ldots,s_n\}\) is \(\operatorname{Var}(T)-O(n^{1/2}(\ln n)^2)\), and \(s_n\ge c(k,B)\,n\). The tail \(Q_{>s_n}\) tends to 0 in \(L^1\) after division by \(n\), so \(Q/\operatorname{Var}(T)\to 1\) in probability if and only if the same limit holds for the pre-window sum of \(v_i\). This is an \(L^1\) truncation. It does not bound \(\operatorname{Var}(Q)\).

**Theorem B** (\(k\ge 8\)). Let \(X=D_d-D_c\) be the difference of omission durations for two independent copies of clause \(j\), let \(T_0\) be the hitting time with clause \(j\) omitted, and let \(\mathcal G\) be the \(\sigma\)-field generated by the prefix before \(j\), the two copies, and the shared clauses through time \(s_n\). Set
\[
\mathcal A_n
=\sum_{j\le s_n}
\mathbb E\Bigl[\operatorname{Cov}_Y\bigl(\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G],\mathbb E[T_0\mid\mathcal G]\bigr)^2\Bigr].
\]
Then
\[
\operatorname{Var}(Q)\le 8\mathcal A_n+C_{k,B}n^{7/4}(\ln n)^3,
\]
and \(\mathcal A_n\le 2\operatorname{Var}(Q)+C_{k,B}n^{7/4}(\ln n)^3\). Hence the following are equivalent: \(\mathcal A_n=o(n^2)\), \(\operatorname{Var}(Q)=o(n^2)\), and \(Q/\operatorname{Var}(T)\to 1\) in \(L^2\).

The rate \(n^{7/4}(\ln n)^3\) comes from the fourth-moment expansion of the post-\(s_n\) martingale of \(T_0\). Its dominant piece is the window of width \(w_n\), which contributes \(O(w_n^3)=O(n^{3/2}(\ln n)^6)\) to that fourth moment. The ratio of the error to \(n^2\) is \(O((\ln n)^3 n^{-1/4})\). This tends to 0. For unit implicit constants it is still larger than 1 at \(n=10^6\) and smaller than 1 by \(n=10^{24}\). The little-o is analytic; it is not a moderate-\(n\) numerical bound.

The same decomposition is valid for \(5\le k\le 7\), but the post-window fourth-moment majorant does not fall below order \(n^2\). At \(k=7\) it is \(O(n^2)\). At \(k=6\) it is \(O(n^{9/4})\). At \(k=4\) the uniform fourth duration moment is not available. No reduction to \(\mathcal A_n\) is claimed below \(k=8\). A weaker tail estimate that uses only the fourth-moment Markov bound, instead of the paid moment of order \(k-1\), reaches little-o only for \(k\ge 10\).

## The inequality that remains

For each fixed integer \(k\ge 8\) and each fixed \(B>U_k\),
\[
\mathcal A_n=o(n^2).
\tag{R}
\]
Cauchy–Schwarz and the fourth-moment bounds give only \(\mathcal A_n\le C_{k,B}n^2\). The sum is nonnegative, so there is no cancellation across \(j\) in that upper bound. Each of the \(\Theta(n)\) pre-window indices still contributes up to \(O(n)\). Removing the post-window indices from I347’s identity saves \(O(n^{7/4}(\ln n)^3)\) and does not improve the \(O(n^2)\) ceiling.

If (R) were proved, Major’s conditions (1.2) and (1.3), the Lindeberg estimate already checked in I347, and the late-cap transfer would give the central limit theorem for \(T\) and then for \(H\). That implication is conditional on (R). It is not a theorem of this campaign.

## What was not used as a proof

- The I347 two-root moment (4.1), and the explicit formula in which cooperative killing has disjoint marked roots and every root equal to a non-root witness. The covariance is not localized on root collisions.
- A stopping time at the death index \(T_0\). The duration difference is already measurable there, so the split has to be made at a deterministic time before \(\mu\).
- R022. Its statements (S) and (P) are small-information stability on the discrete cube. They do not constrain this hitting time.
- Wilson’s quantile gap and Carenini’s window bounds. They are overlap for the variance scale already pinned. They are not lemmas in Theorems A or B.
- Any simulation. `exponent_check.py` checks only the powers in the majorants. It draws no clauses.

I347’s bounded-kernel target (G2) is still a sufficient route for every fixed \(k\ge 4\), including \(k\le 7\). It is not proved.
