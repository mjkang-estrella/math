## Two different Source359 interfaces

The threefold is the Hartogs domain over B^2 with radius exp(-phi/2). Its complete metric uses potential lambda psi-log(1-exp(phi)|w|^2), with the real metric convention twice the Hermitian potential form. The base potential is psi plus a large finite multiple of the regularized polynomial logarithms. The all-real-plane curvature argument uses centered-chart fourth-order control and a mixed-block cancellation, not just holomorphic sectional curvature. It supplies finite positive pinching constants. The domain is contractible; its Stein property is paid by the actual strictly plurisubharmonic exhaustion in the source proof. No additional boundary regularity is inferred from being a domain in C^3.

The determinant obstruction concerns bounded holomorphic maps into C^3 whose Jacobian would be nowhere zero. The zero-section determinant estimate and a global logarithm would produce a forbidden pluriharmonic majorant, contradicted by the positive representing measures. The proof does not say that every such determinant is identically zero, or that every bounded function is constant. In particular z_1,z_2 are bounded nonconstant functions. The measures are representing measures for holomorphic values, not Jensen measures that could automatically test every plurisubharmonic function.

The companion is a tube over all of C^(n+1), in dimension m=n+2 chosen sufficiently large and fixed. Its complete base metric has nonpositive real sectional curvature. The logarithmic tube metric has real sectional curvature <=-1, with no factor of two in its real metric convention. It is diffeomorphic to Euclidean space and has only constant bounded holomorphic functions. The source explicitly supplies a negative base plane, so the tube's exact horizontal formula makes its sectional curvature unbounded below. This is not the threefold, and smooth product coordinates are not a biholomorphic product identification.

The frozen conjunction adds a bounded embedding into C^5 to the two-sided threefold's obstruction to a bounded everywhere-nonsingular C^3 map. It does not assert C^4, optimal dimension, properness, or completeness of the Caratheodory metric.

Its operative mechanism matters. For source polynomial factors P_j, the parameterized products q_t use coefficients c_0+2^(-j)t. The exact one-factor Cauchy–Schwarz bound compares |1-cP_j|^2 to the regularized logarithmic weight with a square-summable parameter loss. Local convergence and the global product bound follow. Algebraic dependence for three polynomial equations on C^2, followed by a Baire choice, gives three q_t with no common zero. With the source amplitude taken as a sufficiently large integer L, the powers f_l=q_t^L obey the needed weight bound. The five bounded functions (z_1,z_2,w f_1,w f_2,w f_3) embed: locally a nonzero f_l recovers w, and the base coordinates recover z. Thus finite bounded separation and these weighted-product/Baire primitives are already paid project operations. No alteration of that frozen target is proposed.

## Actual primary proofs consulted

**Wu–Yau, Invariant metrics on negatively pinched complete Kahler manifolds**, JAMS 33 (2020), published PDF, DOI 10.1090/jams/933. The operative Kobayashi comparison proof and Bergman construction were read, including the L2 holomorphic top-form separation and derivative estimates. Under the applicable two-sided hypotheses this supplies invariant metric comparisons; it does not identify a bounded global coordinate map. Top forms and their ratios cannot silently be replaced by globally bounded functions. The preceding quasi-bounded-geometry/smoothing proofs and later complete Kahler–Einstein proof were not fully read; those transitive ingredients are used only as the displayed primary interfaces. The Source359 counterexample remains stipulated.

**Hwang–Singer, A Momentum Construction for Circle-Invariant Kahler Metrics**, arXiv math/9811024v1. Actual definitions, reconstruction, metric and curvature differentiation, and the fiber completeness proof were read (physical pp.1–3,8–18). A positive momentum profile and the horizontal positivity interval are part of the hypotheses. The length integral at an infinite fiber endpoint is an explicit obstruction, not a license to preserve completeness after arbitrary reparameterization. These formulas support the scalar ansatz calculation; a scalar-curvature solution does not by itself control all real sectional planes. The broader classification and most subsequent applications were not read.

**Hao–Chen–Wang, On the negatively pinched properties of the disc bundles over negatively pinched Kahler manifolds**, arXiv 2509.04179v1, all 13 pages read. The bundle curvature determines the base metric used in its formulas. The complete bundle construction, full curvature tensor estimates, pinching statements and iteration are actual paid primary operations. The source threefold, by contrast, permits a controlled perturbation h separately from the lambda-scaled hyperbolic base contribution. The one-sided tube base has no asserted two-sided negative pinching. Neither identification may be inserted without proof.

**Hao–Chen–Wang–Zhang, The curvature estimation of the complete Kahler–Einstein metrics on the disk bundles**, arXiv 2609.14482v1. Physical pp.1–15 were read; pp.3–13 were a late read after the two calculation notes were first written, to complete the internal curvature/ODE comparison. Theorem 2 and its real sectional proof require the complete Kahler–Einstein base induced by the dual line-bundle curvature, with the stated Ricci and sectional inequalities (in particular lambda_0<-m and the required upper bound 2 lambda_0/m). The ODE normalization is fixed by that base; it is not a freely adjustable source weight. Its Lemma 3 invokes Ebenfelt–Xiao–Xu for existence/completeness. That transitive paper was not read here, so this is not a certificate of complete primary/transitive coverage. Later classification pp.16–19 was not developed. The arXiv header is 13 September 2026, while the PDF body displays 15 September 2026; this exact v1 was used.

**Shcherbina–Zhang, A Kobayashi and Bergman complete domain without bounded representations**, arXiv 2003.06728v1. The actual statements and full Section 6 Liouville argument were read, together with the preceding completeness passage. The proof uses analytic multifunction slices, the bounded subharmonic maximum, approximation by leaves and an identity argument. Its smooth strictly pseudoconvex C^2 domain supplies neither a negatively pinched real sectional metric nor the source tube's large sheared-disk construction. That missing geometric operation cannot be replaced by its Kobayashi/Bergman completeness. The main construction pages and the invoked Slodkowski theorem were not read in full.

**Seshadri, Negative sectional curvature and the product complex structure**, arXiv math/0602289v1, all six pages read. The product obstruction uses the holomorphic product structure, Schwarz-lemma comparison, and the incompatibility of a product with the resulting hyperbolicity. It does not apply just from the smooth (z,u,b) coordinates on the tube. The final entire-space example has curvature approaching zero and does not supply the uniform negative upper bound here.
