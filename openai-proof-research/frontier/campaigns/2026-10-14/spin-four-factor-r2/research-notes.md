# Global routes investigated and their unpaid points

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0; code MIT.

1. **Existing arity reduction.** Read KKM's proof and followed original
   polygon/path references before the new construction. Real polygon
   transfer and binary Hecke/tensor support equality are paid. The
   special-vertex realization at unscaled labels is not supplied by
   the read proofs. Broad all-arity attribution prose in Sam/Hong--Shen
   does not establish the extra integral-point claim.

2. **Stay inside one fixed polytope.** This approach is actually false
   as a universal local prescription, even for genuine D4 schedules
   and external root-congruent labels: the explicit fixed singleton
   has only a wrong-coset junction. Its scaled selected diagonal is
   genuine, and its unscaled invariant exists through other schedules.

3. **Demand every coefficient integral.** This is unnecessarily strong.
   The proved graph criterion permits a correct junction when two odd
   cycle contributions both occur in one segment. It does not guarantee
   a combined root basis, nor cover all non-basis systems.

4. **Add a bridge and move half a relation.** Carried this out completely
   in one example, including the new initial frame at the reset,
   first-segment reordering, time inequalities, moving-junction gap
   terms, endpoint identity, and limiting boundary switches. The graph
   relation alone would not have paid these signs. No arbitrary bridge
   is asserted to work. The general coefficient-to-gap bounds do extend
   algebraically; forcing a feasible link in every obstructed input does not.

5. **Global area maximum across the reset.** The one-shape source
   argument suggests optimizing dominant path area with independently
   decreasing chains in two segments. Endpoint-preserving variations
   away from the reset are available, but a full proof must control
   folding at a moving junction, generic endpoint freedom and limiting
   covers, and then obtain the precise minimal-schedule reduction.
   None of these is assumed proved here. The fixed bad system is not
   a counterexample to such a globally optimized construction, since
   another realization uses fewer switches. Even a global basis
   statement would still need the link-existence step.

6. **Optimize the common diagonal directly.** Maximizing a convex
   spectral functional over the intersection can force active faces,
   but rational active faces need not carry a point of the specified
   root coset. No integral face recursion or type-D lattice vertex
   theorem was obtained. Calling a rational extremum an integral one
   would repeat the same missing operation.

7. **Copy the three-orbit strict count.** Remains invalid. Rechecked
   R12's analytic odd-parity sums, lengths and trace separation; these
   data also have the required external root congruence. They still
   do not establish an exceptional minimal two-segment schedule.
   Their equal self-dual external labels have pair contractions.

These are exact limits of attempted routes, not proofs that no global
argument can work. Full Q4 remains UNRESOLVED unchanged. No exhaustive
representation search was run; the only checker concerns one explicit
schedule construction and a symbolic affine interval.
