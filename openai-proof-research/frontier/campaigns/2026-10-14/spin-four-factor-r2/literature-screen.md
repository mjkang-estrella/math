This is a bounded primary comparison. I104/S104 and I226 already
screened the arbitrary-arity compatibility operation; this round does
not invent or rename that conjectural extension. The current author
report is not an independent mathematical or contribution gate.

KKM's original saturation proof pays a triple Hilbert-basis computation.
KLM pays real arbitrary polygons, refined transfer, an affine fixed
vertex, special vertices after a root-system scale, and tensor-to-Hecke
support. KM's path theorem pays the additional conversion from special
triangles to tensor occurrence after its scale, with a minuscule-sum
exception under its stated hypotheses. Sam pays arbitrary-factor
scale two for SO/Sp and four for Spin; Hong--Shen pays folding and
odd-spin scale two. The stipulated family-204 theorem pays full type-D
TRIPLE saturation, including all spin weights.

The special-vertex support equivalence in this round is an elementary
assembly of those paid interfaces and positivity. It clarifies what
an arity reduction would still have to establish. It is not a new
substantial geometric mechanism or a scale-one polygon theorem.

The signed-graph determinant/parity calculation is source antecedent.
Projecting it onto first-segment edges gives an exact criterion for
the junction coset, a technical refinement of which coefficients matter.
The explicit D4 bad fixed system and bridge family add an actual
feasibility calculation at a free reset. They neither classify all
minimal systems nor force a link in every original case. One cannot
infer a four-orbit strict-count theorem from this example.
