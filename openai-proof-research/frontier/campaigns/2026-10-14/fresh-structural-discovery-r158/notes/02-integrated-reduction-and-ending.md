## Partial-label experiment and exact local calculation

Identify four states with G=(Z/2)^2 and its three nontrivial characters chi1,chi2,chi3=chi1 chi2. A known mark J_v in{1,2,3} selects the observed bit chi_(J_v)(sigma_v). Define a_n(J) as expected total variation of the FULL four-state root posterior from uniform, conditional on the deterministic marks, with expectation only over spins/channel randomness. For nonconstant marks, these coarsened level observations need not be degraded versions of earlier ones. No convergence of a_n(J) is assumed; use liminf/limsup explicitly.

A single noisy bit of strength a has posterior coordinates x=+/-a e_i with equal probabilities. Combining two conditionally independent such experiments of strengths a,b gives exactly

  same axis:      m = (a²+b²-2a²b²)/(1-a²b²),      ab<1;
  different axes:m = a²+b²+a²b².

At a=b=1 the same-axis value is1, with zero-normalizer combinations discarded by their zero observation probability. In the different-axis case Z=1 and the combined vector has coordinates (sign1*a,sign2*b,sign1*sign2*ab) after a coordinate permutation. Thus its newly generated third coordinate is explicitly retained.

For independent uniform axes, the same-axis case has probability1/3. When ab<1 the excess over a²+b² is

  a²b²(a²+b²-2a²b²)/(3(1-a²b²)) >0   for a,b>0.

This does not contradict the source: a pure-bit law has H=-3/25, and its uniform-axis symmetrization also lies outside E H>=0. The source itself states that H value. This is a diagnostic against using the cone without its hypothesis, not a nominee or a new cone obstruction.

A more informative exact identity follows without imposing coordinate exchangeability. Fixed-character tree experiments are invariant under the four group translations, which act as even sign changes on x. Their coordinate means and off-diagonal covariances vanish, and their only potentially nonzero cubic monomial is x1x2x3. Write

  u_i=E x_i², v_i=E y_i², a=sum u_i, b=sum v_i,
  alpha=E x1x2x3, beta=E y1y2y3,
  s=x.y, N_i=x_i+y_i+x_j y_k+x_k y_j, Z=1+s.

Using the actual product marginal weight Z gives

  m_product = a+b+ab-3 sum_i u_i v_i
              -4(alpha b+a beta)-6 alpha beta

Indeed E|R|²=ab-u.v, E s²=u.v, E[s x.R]=2 alpha b, E[s y.R]=2a beta, and E[s|R|²]=6alpha beta; expand1/Z=1-s+s²/Z. All expectations before tilting use independent marginal laws. ## A full-scope quenched extrapolation fails by paid operations

Consider the tempting implication: for every locally finite deterministic ferromagnetic four-state tree with positive weighted capacity, independent uniform known character marks give liminf_n a_n(J)>0 almost surely. The following calculation rules out that implication at its stated scope. It is not a replacement nominee; every ingredient is source/classical composition.

Define q_k=floor((3/4)log2(k+1)), k>=0, and N_k=2^(k+q_k). Since q_k-q_(k-1) is0 or1, there is a spherically symmetric core tree with N_k vertices at depth k and2 or4 children at each vertex. Every core edge has lambda=1/sqrt2. The equal unit flow has theta=1/N_k at depth k and R_k=2^(-k). Its four-state weighted potential is

  V3=(3/4) sum_(k>=1) 2^(-2q_k)<infinity,

because2^(-q_k) lies between (k+1)^(-3/4) and2(k+1)^(-3/4). Hence the fixed-channel source gives full-label reconstruction. For the binary character process the L2 potential of equal unit flow is sum 2^(-q_k)=infinity. Spherical symmetry makes equal flow optimal (average over finite-level automorphisms, or recursively maximize the chosen edge flow), so binary capacity is zero. PP10 Theorem2.1 applies at its exact bounded positive interaction hypothesis and gives binary advantage b_k→0.

If all observed depth-k characters are the same j, the four-state root likelihood is constant on each chi_j coset. The posterior splits the binary probabilities evenly within those two cosets. Its four-state TV from uniform is exactly the binary TV from(1/2,1/2), hence a_k=b_k. This is stronger than merely saying that one root bit is unrecoverable; the observations contain no information distinguishing the other root bit.

Now insert L_k=k²*3^(N_k) perfect unary edges after every active core stage k, identically at every vertex of that level, before the next branching stage. Every L_k is a finite integer; the construction remains a deterministic locally finite tree with at most4 children. Its channels lie in{1/sqrt2,1}. Perfect edges do not change the core joint spin law and contribute zero to the R009 weighted potential. Each plateau contains at least L_k different levels with the same core depth-k spin vector.

Independently mark EVERY vertex with a uniform axis. Condition on the entire resulting marking J; no other marks convey information about spins, since marks and spins are independent. At a particular plateau level, the probability over J that all N_k marks agree is p_k=3^(1-N_k). The events at its distinct levels use disjoint marks and are independent. Thus

  P(no monochromatic level in plateau k)
     =(1-p_k)^(L_k)<=exp(-p_k L_k)=exp(-3k²).

The sum is finite. Borel–Cantelli shows that almost every J has, eventually in every plateau, a level with a_n(J)=b_k. Nonnegativity and b_k→0 imply liminf_n a_n(J)=0.

On the other hand, the annealed comparison above gives E_J a_n(J)>=c>0 uniformly in n: it suffices to use the fixed-channel core's terminal-noise argument, since the plateau merely copies its boundary. Since0<=a_n<=3/4,

  P_J(a_n>=c/2)>=2c/3.

The numerical value of c need not be optimized; any positive constant supplied by the fixed core flow suffices. Distinct levels again use independent marks. The probability that plateau k has no such good level is at most exp(-(2c/3)L_k), a summable sequence since L_k>=k². A second Borel–Cantelli application yields limsup_n a_n(J)>=c/2 almost surely. Thus the quenched advantage need not converge at all, despite positive full-label capacity and uniform positive annealed advantage.
