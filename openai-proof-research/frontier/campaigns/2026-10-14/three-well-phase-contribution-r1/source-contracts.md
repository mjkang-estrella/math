## External primary interfaces

* [Borisov 2006](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0608006v1): localized operators L_i map H²(Omega_i) into L² functions supported in bounded smooth Omega_i, symmetric and relatively form bounded with c0<1; translated distances all tend to infinity, self-adjointness assumed. Thms 1.1–1.7, Lemmas 4.2–4.4, (4.6)–(4.16), and the actual §6 expansion were read. Thm 1.2 permits eigenvalues tending to zero. Compact localization and splitting-order remainders prevent direct application to P3R. The method explicitly permits further expansion after cancellations. No matrix-valued extension is silently claimed.
* [Rodriguez-Lopez, Rahi, Emig 2009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.0448v1): two polarizable objects beside a perfect conductor; scattering determinant (1)–(4), dipole/retarded specialization (5)–(10). This gives multiple-scattering loops and nonmonotonic force mechanisms, not P3R's negative spectral moment or equal-pair construction.
* [Schaden 2011](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.2475v2): scalar positive local potentials in a finite Euclidean box; irreducible spectral function is an inclusion-exclusion sum. Finiteness assumes no common intersection; (17) gives odd/even sign for the irreducible Casimir energy. Read its derivation (13)–(18) and three-body determinant (21)–(23). Attractive noncommuting matrix wells, noncompact common support and a negative-eigenvalue sum do not meet that interface.
* [Mukunda et al. 1999](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9907080v1): normalized vectors with consecutive nonzero overlaps; (2.8) identifies the cyclic overlap product with the trace of the projection product, invariant under individual phases. Read introduction and §II through (2.9), and opening §III. This identifies the established phase invariant, not a continuum spectral trace theorem.
* [Benseny et al. 2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.02398v2): charged particle on a three-trap ring; (2)–(5) rephase the three-level tunnelling matrix to a single total phase. Read §2 and selected §5/conclusion passages. Continuum §5 explicitly numerically integrates time-dependent dynamics; it gives no full negative-trace/count theorem on the line.
* [Benguria–Loss 2000](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2000/0007/0002/MRL-2000-0007-0002-a005.pdf): smooth compactly supported negative Hermitian potentials, sharp gamma=3/2 bound. Read §I, complete §II and start of §III through Lemma2/remark. Scalar (5)–(9) gives factorization and removal of the ground state via partner spectra. It does not supply P3R; no gamma=1 sharpness is imported from its endpoint theorem.

These are bounded primary-body comparisons. Unread leads and reading extents are in primary-reading.md; no secondary search snippet is used as theorem evidence.
