## Pinned bodies: strongest evidence

[P] means Power-law violations of Yau's nodal upper bound; [Q] means Smooth counterexamples to Yau's nodal upper bound in dimensions three and four. Both are authenticated at fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb. Full paths/blob identities are in source-authentication.json.

1. **Strict-to-full operation.** Q s3-profiles 159–199 chooses a local first integral s annihilating both the old gradient and a strict direction and adds CN⁻²χh(Ns). Its gradient change is small; its leading Hessian is Cχh''ds². The surviving line lies in the kernel of that rank-one addition. Its evaluation on the new gradient is O(N⁻²), so a negative h'' does not destroy strictness. Candidate 466–539 uses the same rank-one addition and the same annihilating line, replacing a first integral by a frozen affine covector. 2. **Fullness.** Q 201–219 forms the two-dimensional transverse matrix, controls one positive diagonal and its mixed entry, and adds enough in the other direction to make its Schur complement positive. Candidate 541–571 repeats this operation with explicit normalized bounds. Its genuine task is to carry the bounds uniformly, rather than a new positivity mechanism. repairs.md explicitly supplies the omitted norm factors.
3. **Large favorable fraction.** Q uses a cosine with favorable fraction 1/3 and rough localization losses, ending with p_h/8 mass. Candidate replaces it with H''=1−β_κ, integral zero, equal to one on fraction 1−κ. Periodic double integration, a large negative return on the remaining κ interval, and shrinking the cutoff losses are standard scalar oscillatory preparation. The large negative part only affects constants because κ is fixed before the period. It imposes no additional tensor compatibility condition.
4. **Repeatable mass.** P envelopes 180–252, 435–515 already gives near-unit disk/ball packing, core selection, pattern-dependent gain, and a favorable repeat subset heavier than the entire old parent. Candidate 282–420 is its two-transverse-variable port with full input. Its retained-line argument is the source's small/large leading-coefficient dichotomy, with the full input kernel resolving the dimension count. Q 463–523 already alternates preparation and radial amplification, but does not carry a fixed derivative invariant. Candidate combines these two schemes by retaining C³ bounds and taking μ=ν³; multiplication by ν¹² and ν¹⁰ absorbs old third derivatives at r=ν⁶. This is a useful quantitative closure of existing operations.
5. **Power and global realization.** P envelopes 547–658 already converts a fixed ratio and geometric selected mass into a fixed power using O(log k) generations, including additive all-order derivative estimates. P waves/global supplies every subsequent operation. The transfer to base three changes finite ranks, net dimension, Sobolev inequalities and certificate dimension; source-transfer.md pays these changes but they are technical transfer, not a separate novel construction.

A normalized formulation exposes what is being paid: on a parent rescaled by S and divided by sS, assume gradient bounded above/below, C² and C³ bounds fixed and one transverse J-margin ≥c. A small fixed box has a frozen favorable line by the mean-value theorem, up to the O(S) connection error. Add bε²χH(z₃/ε), where ε is a small fixed ratio and b is a fixed Schur-complement amplitude. This makes the transverse matrix positive on nearly all the box while moving the gradient little. Once these bounded constants are fixed, one common small ε works on the whole class. Candidate's ν,ν² are an explicit implementation. Neither Q's uncontrolled full-profile thresholds nor P's dimensional statement can be invoked directly, but uniformizing Q on this bounded normalized class requires exactly these elementary estimates. That is the basis for TECHNICAL rather than SUBSTANTIAL.

## Bounded external primary-body comparison

Retained versioned PDFs and extracted texts are in inputs/primary; source-reading.md lists exact reading extents. These papers provide comparisons, not T4 proof dependencies.

- **Conti–De Lellis–Székelyhidi, arXiv:0905.0370v1, §§3–5.2.** Their construction adds a primitive rank-one metric by a periodic correction, differentiates its leading oscillatory term and lower-order errors, tracks arbitrary finite derivative costs, and organizes fixed-ratio stages. Proposition 4.1 and Lemma 4.4 supply the step; Proposition 5.1 supplies the stage. This establishes that separating amplitude, period and localization scales and then keeping normalized derivative invariants is standard quantitative corrugation practice. It does not establish the nodal Hessian-test preparation, high-fraction scalar H'' pattern, or T4 itself. [Primary body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.0370v1).
- **Cao–Székelyhidi, arXiv:1906.08608v2, Lemma 2.1, Proposition 3.1 and proof, Proposition 3.2.** The periodic primitive and compact support have explicit amplitude/frequency derivative estimates; the construction splits the leading rank-one tensor from localization errors and fixes frequency thresholds after bounded input parameters. This corroborates the same quantitative methodology. Its metric-addition equation is different from J_h and is not a duplication theorem for the candidate. [Primary body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.08608v2).
- **Dencker–Sjöstrand–Zworski, arXiv:math/0301242v1, §3, proof of Theorem 2', pp.8–10.** A positive complex Lagrangian supplies a phase, followed by transport amplitudes and cutoff localization. P's wave proof explicitly uses this tradition. Candidate's dimension transfer of P's finite Taylor estimates is inherited analytic machinery; no new WKB operation is identified. This comparison does not import their hypotheses as if they were already T4. [Primary body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0301242v1).
- **Enciso–Peralta-Salas, arXiv:1404.1039v1, Theorem 1.1 and §2 selections.** Their nodal prescription uses a product tube, metric contraction outside it, low-spectrum comparison to Neumann modes, smoothing and isotopy. It addresses prescribed nodal geometry for finitely many low modes and adjusts the metric to that data. These read portions do not supply one fixed metric with power-excess nodal measure along an infinite high-energy sequence. [Primary body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.1039v1).
