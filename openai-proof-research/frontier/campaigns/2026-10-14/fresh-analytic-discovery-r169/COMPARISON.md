Zhan's paper is later than the two pinned source manuscripts and was found during primary research before this notebook was authored. It blocks an otherwise tempting nomination of Hausdorff/natural-clock identification. Its proof uses regular-variation conformal covariance, an adapted first-visit clock, a reverse-Loewner invariant expectation measure, hypoelliptic regularity, a homogeneous angular ODE, reflection symmetry, and continuous finite-variation martingale uniqueness. The angular even solution gives the usual Green density; this is an actual proof operation, not an abstract-only title match.

The original i126 sections explicitly reject deriving discrete traversal count from ordinary SLE exactness. Their immutable target concerns fixed quenched bond disorder, same-sample curve/measure convergence, normalization, tightness and boundary mass. This round uses only ordinary continuum SLE and proposes no discrete counting conclusion. It neither restricts nor retries that scope. The completed A168 exceptions are unrelated matrix-process work; their retained finite-n operations and unpaid joint-time passage remain unchanged. All other supplied immutable dispositions are left unchanged, and their prohibited bodies were not read.

No worldwide priority, exhaustive literature clearance, or perfect history classification is claimed. The decision not to nominate these compositions is a discovery decision, not a self-issued TECHNICAL grade or a claim that every related theorem is routine.
