Research date: 2026-10-16. Work ran at the requested normal/default setting.

Ownership was limited to this `strict-bv-continuity-r2/` directory. The
directory did not exist initially and was created for this assignment.
The old r5 directory was read-only. Its detached seal, inventory digests
and each inventoried file were authenticated BEFORE the first execution
of its checker/verifier. It was then copied byte-for-byte into the private
input tree. The recorded prior verification receipt is deterministic and
replayed after authentication by the new verifier. No independent proof
PASS for its technical theorem A is claimed or inferred.

The pinned OpenAI source at fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb is
assumed true under explicit user authorization. Exact scope and proof
interfaces were read, with per-file limits in `source-contracts.json`.
The source's truth was not audited. No newer upstream theorem or
unverified companion was introduced as a premise. The full frozen r5
context includes the upstream catalogue/tree and earlier campaign vetoes.

New primary reading: Gonzalez-Riquelme--Kosz, arXiv:2009.05729v1,
private PDF plus pdftotext extraction. Retrieval URL/hash/size are in
`retrieval-new-literature.json`. The arXiv Lahti--Weigt version page was
checked and listed only v1. Other current and inherited reading extents
are distinguished in the contracts. Search hits from automated
expositions were not mathematical evidence or a novelty certificate.

All new mathematical arguments are in `candidate.md`. Supporting progress
includes U-tail, fixed-positive-radius continuity under the actual signed
strict convergence, vanishing exact source budgets, and an arbitrary
monotone signed flat-layer calculation. Full B and U-sharp remain
unresolved. These observations are not nominated as a new substantial
campaign theorem. No claim of an independent proof review is made.

The checker uses only Python's standard library. Exact rational hat
identities test the telescoping budgets. Disk-profile checks use the
closed-form disk marginal, finite radii/grids and a fixed random seed;
they validate formulas rather than replacing the proofs or the full
target. Their finite nature and their distinction from independent
review are explicit in the receipt. No computed example is asserted to
disprove B. No numerical search can establish absence of counterexamples.

All final inputs and outputs are inventoried by byte size and SHA256.
The root seal binds both inventories; its hash is detached in the
handoff. `seal.sha256` is a convenience copy. `verify.py` authenticates
everything, including receipts and checker bytes, before replay; it never
generates manifests, receipts or seals. Authentication and receipt replay
are not a formalization of the mathematics. Mutation tests are run on
disposable fixtures inside this owned directory and deleted before final
inventory construction; their fixture seal is separately recorded.

No nested agents, shared or sibling writes, staging, commits, count or
acceptance changes, external messages, uploads, manuscript, formalization
or publication occurred. Searches and primary downloads were read-only
network access. There was no attempt to contact source authors or others.
