# Family 081: the lower-density interface remains unpaid

Pre-freeze screen only. No target was frozen, no complete deduction is nominated, and no contribution/acceptance verdict is awarded. Source truth at the stipulated revision is accepted. The contemplated natural conclusion was rectifiability of every finite-Hausdorff-measure set whose n-dimensional Riesz transform is uniformly L2 bounded in higher codimension, without assuming positive lower density. This was feasibility work, not a selected frozen theorem.

The source's main theorem is for n-AD regular measures, with constants depending on both regularity and operator bounds. Its actual flat-seed argument and planar-limit statement do not remove lower regularity. In 02-flat-balls.tex, the capped lower growth condition (eq:capped-lower-growth-flat) pays bilateral support convergence, nonzero normalized tangent limits, preservation of nonflatness, and both successive tangent steps. The second tangent's vanishing normal moment is proved by positive-kernel integrability, but the conclusion still needs a nonzero limit. Upper growth alone does not give that. In 03-planar-limits.tex, Lemma lem:planar-rigidity already assumes support in an n-plane and two-sided growth. Proposition prop:flat-limit assumes two-sided growth and geometric excess tending to zero. The paragraph immediately before it expressly declines a classification of arbitrary higher-codimension reflectionless measures.

This is not merely a missing citation. A sequence of measures can keep support at points whose local mass disappears: let P and Q be distinct parallel n-planes, and set mu_j=H^n|P+j^-1 H^n|Q. These have a common upper growth bound and converge locally weakly to H^n|P. Their supports P union Q do not converge bilaterally to P on a ball meeting Q. Thus the exact support-convergence argument cannot be retained from upper growth and weak convergence alone. This example is a compactness obstruction, not a counterexample to the contemplated rectifiability theorem.

## Actual primary comparison

Nazarov--Tolsa--Volberg, *The Riesz transform, rectifiability, and removability for Lipschitz harmonic functions*, author PDF https://mat.uab.cat/~xtolsa/removfinal.pdf, extracted lines1--621 read (all introduction, Theorem1.1, MainLemma2.1, its theorem reduction, Proposition3.1 and the complete proof of MainLemma2.1). The later removability proof is not used; lines622--650 were incidentally displayed, not treated as a complete read of that proof.

Their MainLemma2.1 is already stated in arbitrary ambient dimension d. It converts positive lower density, upper growth and Riesz boundedness into a countable AD-regular domination with Riesz-bounded constituents. The proof uses Pajot's F_p/F_(p,s) decomposition; adds flat disks to fill holes; bounds their mass by mu(B_x) through the lower-density constant p; transfers Riesz boundedness by equal-mass local/nonlocal comparison and a maximal-operator bound. Therefore the positive-lower-density consequence of family081 is an existing general reduction, not a candidate substantial new mechanism.

For the full finite-Hausdorff-measure theorem, their Section2.2 first invokes Eiderman--Nazarov--Volberg to eliminate the set with zero lower density. Their introduction explicitly identifies the higher-codimension versions of both the AD theorem and this elimination input as then unresolved. Family081 pays the first interface. The actual source passages inspected here do not pay the second.

A suggested 'fill all small-density holes and apply the source' has the same unpaid step: the primary added-disk growth estimate uses r(B_x)^n <= p mu(B_x), and the comparison coefficient c_x=H^n(P_x)/mu(B_x) must remain bounded. On zero-lower-density points there is no finite p covering all sufficiently small radii. Neither source normalized-height rigidity nor operator norm boundedness, as currently used, bounds these coefficients or total inserted mass. No convergence of such a completion with uniform constants has been proved here.

## Status

No feasible independent density-elimination mechanism emerged from these operative reads. Do not freeze the general target merely by deleting AD regularity, and do not nominate its positive-lower-density subclass. The exact obstacle is controlling disappearing mass/support or constructing a Riesz-bounded regular completion across infinitely many arbitrarily low-density scales. This screen is retained while distinct discovery continues.
