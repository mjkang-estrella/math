No target frozen and no candidate nominated. The intended operation was convergence of the entire double-dimer loop ensemble as unparametrized curves on finite-modulus annular square-lattice approximations, retaining winding loops and all nested contractible loops. Boundary/parity conventions and the precise continuum annular law must first be fixed. Consequently this is a research direction, not a fully formulated asserted theorem. It is not enough to report convergence of winding-loop counts.

Its prop:localization couples bounded half-plane matching windows to large corner-rooted rectangles. In the read proof, the primal tree is directed toward the deleted corner and the complementary dual tree toward the exterior face. Its remaining proof was not read.

Attempt1: cut the annulus along a seam and apply the rectangle theorem. Deleting seam edges changes the measure and excludes winding cycles. Even the finite graph C_(2k), k≥2, embedded around a hole has two perfect matchings; two independent uniform matchings yield a winding loop with probability1/2. Delete one cycle edge and the resulting path has a unique perfect matching, hence no loop under its double-dimer law. This is an exact counterexample to seam deletion preserving the law, not a counterexample to annular convergence on two-dimensional lattices. Cutting while retaining seam boundary states is a different construction and requires their actual correlated law.

Attempt2: combine known homotopy observables with source curve tightness. The source tightness bounds were proved under its corner-rooted rectangle caps and half-plane localization. They have not been shown uniformly under the winding-sector boundary states generated by gluing. A finite collection of puncture observables cannot detect an arbitrarily thin contractible excursion in a puncture-free disk. One can insert such an outward-returning simple detour into a loop without changing any puncture winding. Therefore homotopy convergence alone does not bound the modulus of continuity of curves. The source pays this issue for its own law; citing that paid estimate does not establish the new conditioned version.

Unpaid contract: traversal and short-segment cancellation bounds for the actual annular matching law, uniform over the relevant sector mixture, together with a correctly specified continuum identification. No uniform sector estimate or full construction was obtained. No replacement by a slit domain, fixed sector, loop count, or local bulk statement is nominated.
