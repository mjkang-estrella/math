Its input is a phase-isometry on *every vector*, including interior points of segments. The frozen sphere input does not provide that input, so their Theorem 4.2 cannot simply be applied to the radial formula. R198 proves a sphere sign lift instead of assuming radial phase preservation.

The [Tan--Gao C(K) proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.09282v2), read in full, treats arbitrary Banach targets but uses C(K)-specific faces, norming functionals, and Urysohn constructions to synchronize signs. The [Enami--Matsuzaki C0 proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.00583v1), also read in full, constructs local face rearrangements, proves compatibility on all signed triple intersections (Lemma 3.7), and then recovers a weighted composition representation using a two-face perturbation. These are stronger actual operations than their abstracts. They do not give an arbitrary-source proof in those read bodies. R198 does not assume that all signed triple intersections are nonempty; planar strict-distance regions and the double covering replace that compatibility condition.
