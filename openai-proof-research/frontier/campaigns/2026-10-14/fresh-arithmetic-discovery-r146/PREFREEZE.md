The substantial proposed interface would have to distinguish these algebras under arbitrary complex isomorphisms, not merely isomorphisms preserving u or the chosen ruling. The source/r29 operation reduces it to the pair (Q₂×A¹,D_f), with D_f the embedded A² given by the two-generator ideal I_f. The actual Blanc–van Santen proof obtains a characteristic ruling by studying generic fibers of a principal defining polynomial; here the center ideal is nonprincipal and its principalization lives on SL₂×A¹.

A stronger construction is being carried through instead of stopping at that mismatch. On the torsor, v_f=a³b−a²f(u)−d² is a weight-two function. A lift of a pair isomorphism should transport v_f up to a constant and hence its nonzero fibers E_f={v_f=1}. These threefolds admit explicit charts with common coordinates (a,u) and translations

    t_- = t_+ + 2i/a⁴ + i f(u)/a².

The chart formula is d=ε+(ε/2)a²f(u)+(1/2)a³+a⁴t_ε for ε=±i, with b=(d²+a²f(u)+1)/a³ and c=(d³+d+a²df(u)+a³)/a⁴. Both quotients are polynomials; the omitted special branch is d=−ε. This gives an actual affine-line torsor over the doubled a-line times A¹_u. Its implications for *unmarked* isomorphisms, and whether classical Danielewski fiber-product operations erase the parameter, are now being checked. A marked torsor-class calculation alone is not a full algebra invariant.
