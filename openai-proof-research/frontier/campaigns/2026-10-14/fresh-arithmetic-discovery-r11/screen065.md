Status: pre-freeze technical screen. Parent independently assessed the added operation as technical on 2026-10-17. No original-scope freeze or substantiality nomination. Source truth remains stipulated, not audited.

The contemplated full statement was an equivalence, for every smooth connected projective complex B and every arbitrary algebraic vector bundle E of rank r>=2, between full ordinary Virasoro constraints for B and P_B(E), in every genus, actual integral curve class and with arbitrary primitive/odd descendants. It would imply the corresponding equivalence for all type-A partial flag bundles. Neither semisimplicity nor splitting/positivity of E is assumed in this contemplated statement.

## Preliminary algebra and the converse route

Let N=r+1 and let e_i be elements of any characteristic-zero vector space. If sum_{i!=j} e_i=0 for every j, put T=sum_i e_i. Then e_j=T for every j, hence T=N T and rT=0. Thus every e_i=0. Equivalently the complement-incidence matrix J-I is invertible, with inverse -I+J/r. This is elementary linear recovery.

In the source setting the e_i are positive-insertion coefficients of A_W^{-1} op(A_{k,i}) A_W. Starting with constraints on X gives one complement sum near y=0. A loop sending the omitted B branch to branch j transports that sum to the complement of j, since the ancestor coefficients are single-valued polynomials. Continued logarithms can differ independently on the branches. For k=-1 this makes no difference. For k>=0 the source formula gives the original kth mode plus a linear combination of strictly lower modes on the same branch, with coefficients independent of insertions. If those lower coefficients have already been shown zero, all continued complement equations reduce to the same fixed-log kth-mode complement sums. The displayed linear recovery then gives each kth equation. Induction treats all modes. Infinitesimal symplecticity can be continued from the B branch using fixed:equivalence without assuming the B equations, so this does not assume the desired Virasoro conclusion in order to define the quantum operators. Returning to y=0, fixed:equivalence and cont:exact recover ordinary constraints on B.

This is a feasibility deduction using the source's paid operators, not a new localization, quantization, convergence or scalar-normalization argument. No full theorem packet is frozen. The flag extension would only add standard geometry: the full flag bundle of E is a tower of projectivizations over B; over any partial flag bundle, it is a tower of full flags of the tautological graded vector bundles. Iterating the iff along the two towers gives the partial-flag iff. Rank-one stages are identities and introduce no hypothesis gap.

These passages were read in the retained published primary text, not inferred from the abstract.

Conversely, the added complement inversion above supplies no new geometry beyond that source. The logarithm adjustment is a direct lower-mode induction using its explicit binomial identity. Thus the difference from the primary split-bundle theorem is mathematically precise, but the residual operation is still technical under this campaign's standard.
