## The claim under review (frozen-scope.md, SHA256 6e39d59e65aecf9fc8710244c7eccbe7964e38af10287ad173663585539d790a)

Uniform logarithmic stability for BOTH smooth isotropic Lame coefficients on every bounded connected smooth domain of R^3, from the FULL static physical displacement-to-traction map, with constants depending only on (Omega, c, M, fixed Sobolev norms), over the class mu>=c, 3lambda+2mu>=c, ||lambda||_C1000+||mu||_C1000<=M, modulus [log(3+1/delta)]^(-alpha), no boundary agreement.
