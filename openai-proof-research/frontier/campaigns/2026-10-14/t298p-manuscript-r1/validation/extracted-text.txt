          A ﬁnite free-entropy gap for two self-adjoint variables
                                           Myeongjin Kang

                                                Abstract
         Starting from the stipulated ﬁnite-entropy separation in the pinned OpenAI Math preprint,
     we construct exactly two bounded self-adjoint elements with −∞ < χ < χ∗ < ∞. The mi-
     crostates entropy is the original operator-norm-cutoﬀ entropy with an ordinary limsup over all
     matrix sizes. For the source tuple X of length n = 264 + 1, put k = 232 . A normal-slice encoding
     Eε (X) satisﬁes lim inf ε↓0 (χ∗ (Eε (X)) − χ(Eε (X))) ≥ k −2 (χ∗ (X) − χ(X)). The proof couples an
     unconditional Fisher-information estimate with a matched microstates-volume estimate. The
     latter recovers matrix units from arbitrary pair microstates after removing a vanishing frac-
     tion of the dimension, so the upper bound includes every output size. All analytic estimates
     needed for this transfer are proved below; the source separation and explicitly named classical
     free-entropy interfaces are inputs. No assertion about generation of the ambient von Neumann
     algebra is made.

All tracial von Neumann algebras in this paper have a faithful normal tracial state. All tuples are
ﬁnite and consist of bounded self-adjoint elements. The two free entropies have the normalizations
of Section 2. In particular, χ never denotes a relative entropy or an ultraﬁlter entropy unless
explicitly decorated.

8.1    Orbit volumes and supplied matrix-unit compression

the ordinary limsup and the same CD .
    Shlyakhtenko [4, Section 5, Theorem 5.1] already uses the corner/compression/packing pattern.
Its proof takes a corner p ≤ e11 with [e11 , p] = 0 under supplied matrix-unit conditioning models,
