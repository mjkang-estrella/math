# Evidence boundary

MANIFEST.json lists every payload file and directory. Its SHA256 is supplied externally to the parent. verify.py requires that external anchor and checks exact files/directories, bytes and hashes while rejecting symlinks, nonregular files, hardlinks and nested manifests. This small support packet contains no copied third-party bodies. Provenance hashes identify the read-only external inputs; the verifier checks this packet, not the present availability or truth of those external documents.

After this record was saved, the manifest was generated and a positive read-only verification plus a zero/wrong-anchor rejection were run. No copied mutation-control campaign or numerical theorem test was run for this focused support note. The parent receives the external anchor and command results separately.
