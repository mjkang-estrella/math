## Graded boundary operators

On ordered distinct color tuples i=(i_1,...,i_k), use

 Mhat_k(q) b_i = sum_{c notin i} x_c q^{#{r:i_r<c}} b_(i,c),
 Dhat_k b_(i_1,...,i_k) = b_(i_2,...,i_k).

Products of these operators enumerate colorings in a linear clique-window construction, assigning the new-vertex ascent factors when that vertex is appended. For circular closure, each original directed arc must be assigned exactly once to a lifted head; simply weighting every edge of a duplicated boundary graph double-counts boundary-clique arcs. This bookkeeping is separate from positivity.

For i=(a,w), direct subtraction gives

 (Mhat_(k-1)(q) Dhat_k - Dhat_(k+1) Mhat_k(q)) b_(a,w)
 = x_a q^{#{w_r<a}} b_(w,a)
   +(1-q) sum_{c>a,c notin (a,w)} x_c q^{#{w_r<c}} b_(w,c).

At q=1 this is precisely Vailaya's weighted cyclic shift Rhat_k. At variable q the additional terms survive. This does not disprove a deformed intertwiner.

For two colors a=x_1,b=x_2,s=a+b, Vailaya's U_1 at q=1 is
 [[a,ab/(2s)],[b,ab/(2s)]].
The one-port append/delete operator is A_q=[[0,a],[q*b,0]].
Conjugating by the unchanged U_1 yields diagonal entries
 (1-q)ab/(a-b), (q-1)ab/(a-b).
They are not symmetric in a,b, and one is negative for a>b>0 and q>1. Thus the unchanged tableau basis is insufficient. The off-diagonal entries are c(a-qb)/(a-b) and 2s(qa-b)/(a-b), where c=ab/(2s).

But directed cycles admit a stronger, compatible repair. For N colors A_q has off-diagonal entry x_c q^{[a<c]} at row c, column a, zero diagonal. Every principal j-minor is (-1)^{j-1} q[j-1]_q e's corresponding squarefree monomial, giving
 det(I-z A_q)=1-sum_{j=2}^N q[j-1]_q e_j z^j.
Consequently -z(d/dz)log det(I-zA_q) has coefficients tr(A_q^n) and an e-positive q expansion. A nonnegative companion matrix with characteristic coefficients q[j-1]_q e_j has the same traces. This is the already-known directed-cycle result (Ellzey), not a new nominee or a no-go for circular graphs.

At two appended colors, the ordinary transposition identity fails:
 Mhat_1(q) Mhat_0 b_empty = x_a x_b(q b_(a,b)+b_(b,a)) for a<b,
which is not invariant under ordinary slot swap. A Hecke-type operator H with H b_(a,b)=b_(b,a), H b_(b,a)=q b_(a,b)+(1-q)b_(b,a) does fix this vector and obeys (H-I)(H+qI)=0. Its signed coefficient 1-q shows that merely deforming the q=1 permutation identities does not retain entrywise positivity. It does not exclude cancellation, another normalization or a different positive representation.
