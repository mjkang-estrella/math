## Local corners alone do not give a compactly supported function

At a single two-line vertex, q=(1,-1) satisfies the local sum-zero condition. It cannot be the entire corner data of a compactly supported function, because each of the two line totals is nonzero. One jump persists to infinity. The candidate imposes bq=0 on **every line**, integrates jumps with zero initial value, and uses the same condition for the final value.
