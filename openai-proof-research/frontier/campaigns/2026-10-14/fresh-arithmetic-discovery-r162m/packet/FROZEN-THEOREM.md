# Frozen theorem — r162m

Frozen before author-proof development. One Source077 operation: compactness of diagonal extension extremizing sequences.

Let Σ be any nonempty compact C∞ embedded two-dimensional surface in R³, possibly with smooth boundary, whose second fundamental form is definite at every point (choosing the normal on each chart). Let σ be its induced surface measure. For complex f∈Lᵖ(Σ,σ), define

EΣ f(x)=∫Σ exp(2πi x·ξ) f(ξ) dσ(ξ),   x∈R³.

For every real 3<p<∞ set

CΣ,p=sup{‖EΣ f‖Lᵖ(R³): f∈Lᵖ(Σ), ‖f‖Lᵖ(Σ)=1}.

Then 0<CΣ,p<∞, and the following holds. For every sequence (f_n) of complex functions in Lᵖ(Σ) satisfying ‖f_n‖Lᵖ(Σ)=1 for every n and ‖EΣ f_n‖Lᵖ(R³)→CΣ,p, there exist a strictly increasing sequence of integers n_k, vectors a_k∈R³, and f_*∈Lᵖ(Σ) such that

‖exp(2πi a_k·ξ) f_{n_k}(ξ)−f_*(ξ)‖Lᵖ(Σ)→0,
‖f_*‖Lᵖ(Σ)=1, and ‖EΣ f_*‖Lᵖ(R³)=CΣ,p.

Consequently the supremum is attained. If MΣ,p denotes all normalized extremizers, then for every ε>0 there is δ=δ(Σ,p,ε)>0 such that every complex f∈Lᵖ(Σ) with ‖f‖p=1 and ‖EΣ f‖p≥(1−δ)CΣ,p satisfies inf{‖f−g‖p:g∈MΣ,p}<ε.

This is a fixed-surface qualitative statement.
