The standard blow-up cohomology decomposition is p*H*(M) plus the exceptional summands j*(ξ^i π*H*(S)), 0≤i≤codim(S,M)−2. The source's normal-cone extension discussion permits pullback inputs from M and end-supported Gysin inputs from the fixed source D. In particular the source's detection theorem itself cannot simply be applied to arbitrary M: its diagonal coherent subspace must contain all nonmiddle cohomology, a special hypothesis of the complete-intersection application.

## Exact remaining scope limitation

The transport theorem is explicitly at coherent numerical degree tests, and its final paragraph warns that it has not matched individual homology classes across smoothings. One cannot conclude coefficientwise vanishing at every integral class merely from vanishing after summing classes with the same numerical tests. Torsion is invisible to rational divisor pairings. A full general integral-class formulation would require an actual refinement of the gluing/cap inverse by compatible group-valued degrees (or another class-separation argument), not a silent replacement of integral classes by numerical classes. No assertion is made that this refinement is impossible, or that a geometric counterexample exists.

This gap is not a substantial contribution by itself. For cases already satisfying class separation and coherent spanning, the operation is direct recombination of the two stipulated source interfaces and the classical blow-up cohomology formula. Changing the title from bundle towers to blow-ups would not supply a new proof mechanism. No restricted class is substituted as a nominee.
