## Concrete construction and its attribution
The contemplated conclusion was a separable simple unital stably finite A with A tensor_min A properly infinite. The following construction appears to pay it by standard operations, so it is not selected as substantial.

Haagerup2014 Proposition4.2 and Lemma5.6 give N=product M_n(D)/J_tau, a finite AW algebra with faithful normal quasitrace, and unital embeddings of D (constant diagonal matrices) and the matricial tracial ultraproduct M_U (scalar matrices). This is exactly the first half of Lemma5.7, printed p17; exactness is used only in the subsequent p18 step identifying the induced commuting norm with min. Proposition5.5 supplies a unital L(F2), hence R, inside M_U. Thus N contains the finite witness tuple and R.

Let C be the separable unital algebra generated by that tuple and R. It inherits a normalized quasitrace from N. Choose an extreme normalized quasitrace, quotient by its null ideal, then apply Haagerup Proposition4.6 to its AW completion F. This is a finite AW factor. The finite trace-obstruction relations survive every unital quotient, so F has no trace. The unital map on R is injective because R is simple. Inside F, a standard separable simple hull A containing the witness tuple and R remains stably finite and traceless. Then A tensor_min R is properly infinite, and its unital inclusion in A tensor_min A preserves the two isometries. This is self-tensor failure, but its construction is classical once the source supplies the traceless finite factor.

For completeness, the separable simple-hull procedure is the usual countable fullness construction. Starting with a separable unital subalgebra of a simple ambient factor, at each stage take a countable dense positive subset and every rational positive cutoff below its norm. For each resulting nonzero cutdown c choose finitely many ambient elements x_i with sum x_i* c x_i invertible, and add them. The countable union is separable. Every nonzero ideal of the limit contains such a cutdown (approximation and the standard positive-cutdown comparison), hence contains an invertible full sum and is the whole algebra. This is Blackadar's separably inheritable simplicity, not a new lemma.

The norm distinction matters: the commuting copies inside N supply some C*-tensor norm, not an embedding of D tensor_min R. The latter is properly infinite by the source and therefore cannot embed in N. The final minimal tensor embedding A tensor_min R into A tensor_min A is a DIFFERENT map, obtained from R subset A by injectivity of the minimal tensor functor; it does not multiply the two commuting copies in N.
