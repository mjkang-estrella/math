# Arithmetic r143: prefreeze derivations

No theorem is frozen or nominated. These are original working derivations and a strongest-payment comparison, not an author proof submitted for grading. Source pin fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb and all its operative upstream proofs are stipulated true. The question developed here is whether the geometric Oka construction yields a genuinely new, sharply controlled vector-bundle spray operation on projective K3 surfaces. ## 1. Exact source input and the interface it pays

For every complex K3 surface X, every m≥1, nonempty compact convex K⊂C^m, open neighbourhood U of K, holomorphic f:U→X, Hermitian metric and ε>0, the source proves approximation on K by an entire C^m→X. Nonprojective K3 surfaces are explicitly included. The entire source, including every TeX/Bib include, was read before these deductions. Its local property L is a planar continuation property with arbitrary passive holomorphic parameters on compactly contained parameter sets. Two transverse entire strips provide the local criterion. Finite proper genus-one families on projective surfaces give the strips; analytic exceptional sets are handled by continuation on tilted discs.

The global proof does not require a fixed complex-normal thickness along a real face. Its concentration automorphisms thin transverse variables at scale O(1/d). Cauchy–Green splitting, finite derivative control, and quadratic errors with summable domain losses permit face induction. In the symplectic annulus sewing, the exact period integral removes the transverse zero Fourier mode to quadratic order. The other zero mode changes the shear. Fixed buffered charts, period recentering and doubly exponential errors complete the chain. Degeneration supplies finite raw charts, while rational-period loci and orbit closure arguments reach all marked K3 surfaces. These are paid proof operations, not new claims here.

The source already includes prescribed discrete jets, dense entire immersions and strong domination. It also proves the actual minimal classification: every connected minimal compact complex surface of Kodaira dimension zero is Oka; a connected minimal class VII surface is Oka exactly when it is Hopf or Enoki. The required global-spherical-shell companion was fully read. Its weighted cyclic-cover construction, end-framed classes, index calculation, decay/residue currents and cylinder bounds supply the missing class VII classification input. The classification does not itself assert blowup permanence or a relative Oka-map theorem.

The projective global-spray corollary (cor:global-spray) is also paid. The canonical K3 main label is thm:main and the minimal-surface label is cor:surface-classification. [Forstnerič–Lárusson v6, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20028v6) proves projective Oka manifolds elliptic by extending local flows on sufficiently negative line-bundle sums across the exceptional zero section. [Kusakabe–Matsumura v1, 8 October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12175v1), a separately identified newer primary, gives a stronger constructive interface: a quadratic φ-vector field integrates to a local spray, and weighted L² gluing globalizes suitable negative-bundle local sprays while retaining prescribed finite jets at the zero section. In that globalization, the Oka property supplies each new bump map before splitting/gluing. The weight kills unwanted low-degree normal terms; it does not create an arbitrary prescribed-base lift.

Thus there are two genuinely different domains: the Stein source variables used by CAP, and a vector bundle over compact X whose zero section must be fixed. CAP equivalences alone do not transfer between them. The actual primary spray constructions perform that transfer for projective X. They also already disprove all-K3 ellipticity: algebraic-dimension-zero Kummer surfaces have no local dominating spray. None of those consequences is a nominee.

## 2. A closed, paid rank-four construction

Fix a projective K3 X. It is compact Kähler and weakly pseudoconvex (a constant plurisubharmonic exhaustion is proper on a compact space). The stipulated source supplies its Oka property. Choose a positive line bundle B sufficiently large that TX⊗B is generated by global sections. On a K3, H¹(B)=0 for positive B. The primary construction works with E=B⁻¹⊗C^N and an epimorphism φ:E→TX; its quadratic obstruction lies in

    H¹(Sym²E*⊗E) = H¹(B)⊗Sym²(C^N)*⊗C^N = 0.

By KM Theorem 2.6 and Proposition 2.4, a quadratic φ-vector field exists and its flow yields a spray on a neighbourhood of the zero section. For K3, KX is trivial, so the positivity requirement on KX⁻¹⊗B^k in the primary negative-bundle globalization is satisfied for positive k. KM Theorem 1.4 globalizes the local spray and preserves its first fibre derivative φ (order2 in that paper means derivatives of order<2). This uses the proved fixed-target extension, not a proposed relative substitute.

There is also a route requiring only the FL global spray on B⁻¹⊗C^N: compress that already global spray. We give the compression explicitly. For A∈Hom(C⁴,C^N), consider

    φ_A(x): B_x⁻¹⊗C⁴ → TX,x,       φ_A(x)=φ(x)∘A.

For each fixed x, evaluation is a surjection from the A-parameter space to Hom(C⁴,C²), because φ(x) is surjective. The locus of 2×4 matrices of rank≤1 has codimension (2−1)(4−1)=3. Hence the incidence set of pairs (x,A) with rank φ_A(x)≤1 has dimension at most

    dim X + 4N − 3 = 4N − 1.

The vector bundles and the bundle morphism φ on projective X are algebraic by GAGA; the spray itself is only asserted holomorphic. Projection from X times the affine A-space is proper, so its bad image is a proper closed subset. Choose A outside that image. If desired, first pad N to at least four and intersect with the open set of injective A. Restricting the global spray by the bundle map B⁻¹⊗C⁴→B⁻¹⊗C^N yields a global dominating spray of rank four.

This is a routine incidence reduction of an actual primary theorem. It is not a substantial new result submitted by this round. It is nevertheless important payment: an attempted discovery cannot treat finite-rank existence, or an unspecified number of spray parameters, as an unpaid bridge.

The same dimension argument explains the threshold. Three columns give a rank-deficient locus of codimension two. With a two-dimensional base, the incidence estimate no longer removes its image. This is an exact limit of this argument, not a proof that rank three is impossible.

## 3. Rank two: the actual second-jet obstruction

A dominating local spray on a rank-two vector bundle E over a surface has an isomorphic vertical derivative φ:E→TX. Identify E with TX by φ. In a holomorphic coordinate chart its Taylor expansion is

    s_i(x,v)=x+v+(1/2)B_i(x)(v,v)+O(v³).

For a coordinate change y=f(x), comparing the expansions gives

    B_j(f(x))(Df·v,Df·v)
      = Df·B_i(x)(v,v) + D²f(x)(v,v).

Therefore Γ_i=−B_i transforms as the Christoffel symbol of a torsion-free holomorphic connection on TX. This conclusion only uses a neighbourhood of the zero section; global completeness of the spray is irrelevant.

The curvature of a holomorphic connection is of type (2,0). Its degree-four Chern–Weil form is a polynomial in two curvature factors and has type (4,0), hence is identically zero on a complex surface. It represents c₂(TX) in de Rham cohomology. For a K3, ∫X c₂(TX)=24. This contradiction excludes rank two. Ranks zero and one cannot dominate a surface at all.

Combining this with the paid rank-four construction places the least possible rank of a global dominating vector-bundle spray on each projective K3 in {3,4}. This bound is a derived working fact, not a frozen nominee or a claim of literature novelty. It leaves rank three unresolved. In particular, the rank-two calculation must not be promoted into a rank-three obstruction by pretending that a bundle epimorphism has a holomorphic right inverse.

## 4. General rank three: explicit gluing obstruction

Suppose a rank-three bundle E admits a dominating local spray, with first derivative φ. Its kernel is a line bundle K and there is an exact sequence

    0 → K → E --φ→ TX → 0.                            (4.1)

Write the quadratic coefficient in local fibre coordinates as B_i:Sym²E→TX. For target coordinate change f and fibre transition g, comparison yields

    B_j(gw,gw)=Df·B_i(w,w)+D²f(φ_iw,φ_iw).           (4.2)

The normalized target Hessians form the symmetric Atiyah cocycle of TX. Equation (4.2) says that its pullback through Sym²φ is a coboundary. Thus a necessary condition for an arbitrary local spray is

    b_φ := (Sym²φ)*a_sym(TX)
       = 0 in H¹(X,Sym²E*⊗TX).                      (4.3)

This is exactly the obstruction to gluing the two-jet with first derivative φ. It is not asserted sufficient for a convergent spray: higher Taylor equations and analytic convergence remain.

One must distinguish (4.3) from the primary's α_φ(E)=Sym(φ*a(E)) in H¹(Sym²E*⊗E). Vanishing of α_φ(E) is equivalent to existence of its quadratic φ-vector field, whose flow provides a local spray. It is sufficient for our purpose but is not necessary for every spray. The primary explicitly supplies a counterexample to that converse. Using its criterion as a universal obstruction would invalidate the argument.

Dualizing (4.1) gives 0→Ω¹X→E*→K*→0. Let Q=Sym²E*/Sym²Ω¹X. It fits into

    0 → Ω¹X⊗K* → Q → (K*)² → 0,
    0 → Sym²Ω¹X → Sym²E* → Q → 0.                   (4.4)

After tensoring the second row with TX, exact cohomology shows that (4.3) holds precisely when

    a_sym(TX) ∈ image[H⁰(Q⊗TX) --δ→
                          H¹(Sym²Ω¹X⊗TX)].         (4.5)

This is a concrete extension calculation, not a statement that an unspecified Atiyah obstruction vanishes. For example, in local compatible frames of K*, choose lifts t_i to E*. After transporting frames, write t_j=t_i+λ_ij with λ_ij a one-form. A section of Q⊗TX has local expression u_i t_i+v_i t_i². Its transition equations modulo Sym²Ω¹X are

    v_i=v_j,       u_i=u_j+2v_j λ_ij.

The remaining quadratic defect is

    u_j λ_ij + v_j λ_ij²,

an element of Sym²Ω¹X⊗TX. Restoring the line-bundle transition factors makes these equations intrinsic; δ in (4.5) is their cohomology class. Hence a solution requires the extension class λ, the compatible pair (u,v), and the target Hessian cocycle to satisfy this exact equation. The λ² term cannot be discarded.

Two useful consequences follow without solving (4.5). If (4.1) splits holomorphically, restricting a hypothetical spray to a right inverse TX→E produces a rank-two local spray, already excluded. Therefore every possible rank-three spray requires a nonsplit extension. Also, if both H⁰(TX⊗K⁻²) and H⁰(Ω¹X⊗TX⊗K⁻¹) vanish, the first sequence in (4.4) forces H⁰(Q⊗TX)=0. Then (4.5) would force a_sym(TX)=0, contradicting the connection calculation above. These are diagnostic tests on an actual proposed E, not hypotheses of a replacement nominee.

No general classification of extensions satisfying (4.5) has been proved here. Even finding one would only settle the two-jet problem; it would still require an all-order local spray and an applicable global extension theorem for that exact E. An arbitrary rank-three extension is not automatically a negative line-bundle sum to which the read globalization theorem applies.

## 5. Carrying the negative equal-line-bundle route to its geometric endpoint

The strongest paid constructive route suggests E=B⁻¹⊗C³. Put l=c₁(B). Surjectivity φ would give a line kernel of first Chern class −3l, because det TX is trivial. Multiplicativity of total Chern classes on a surface gives

    c₂(E)=3l²=c₂(TX)+c₁(K)c₁(TX)=24.

Thus l²=8 is necessary. This is not a sufficient condition. In the twisted form F=TX⊗B, a surjection O³→F has kernel B⁻², and

    c₁(F)=2l,       c₂(F)=24+l²,
    c₁(F)²−c₂(F)=3l²−24.

The last expression is the degree of the expected zero-dimensional rank-drop scheme for a transverse generic three-section map. It equals zero only at l²=8. Expected-dimension reasoning alone does not ensure global generation or transversality for a particular surface and bundle.

Assume for the reduction that such a surjection actually exists and B is ample. Its classifying map is

    f:X→Gr(2,3)=P²,
    F ≅ f*Q,       f*O(1) ≅ B²,

where Q is the universal rank-two quotient, equivalently TP²(−1). Because B² is ample, no curve is contracted by f. The proper map is finite, its image has dimension two, and it is surjective. Its degree is

    deg f = ∫X c₁(f*O(1))² = 4l² = 32.

The second Chern class gives the same degree: ∫c₂(F)=24+8=32, since c₂(Q)=1. This agreement is essential: there is no Chern-number contradiction here. Using the holomorphic symplectic identification TX≅Ω¹X, the demanded exact sequence becomes

    0 → B⁻² → O³ → Ω¹X⊗B → 0.                     (5.1)

This is much more restrictive than merely choosing a degree-32 finite map to P². The isomorphism f*Q≅Ω¹X⊗B is part of the unpaid algebraic construction. The ramification divisor of any such finite f would lie in |B⁶|, by KX≅O and f*KP²⁻¹≅B⁶. Again that is compatible numerically; it gives no counterexample or existence proof.

If an epimorphism in (5.1) were produced, H¹(B)=0 would kill α_φ(E), yielding the local quadratic-flow spray. The KM negative-bundle extension would then give the global spray with the same first derivative. Thus for this exact equal-line route the analytic bridge is paid; the unpaid step is an actual everywhere-surjective three-section presentation of Ω¹X⊗B at square eight. It is incorrect to retain a generic zero-free assertion at that boundary without proving it.

One can also let E=H⁻a⊕H⁻b⊕H⁻c on a Picard-rank-one K3 with H ample, H²=2d and positive integers a≤b≤c. A necessary condition is

    d(ab+ac+bc)=12.                                  (5.2)

The solutions are finite: since ab+ac+bc≥3, d≤4; and since a,b≥1, 2c+1≤12/d. Direct enumeration gives

    d=1: (a,b,c)=(2,2,2);
    d=4: (a,b,c)=(1,1,1),

and no positive solutions for d=2 or d=3. Consequently the split-negative rank-three route in Picard rank one can only occur for E=H⁻²⊗C³ when H²=2, or E=H⁻¹⊗C³ when H²=8. Both reduce to the same B²=8 condition. This enumeration is only a necessary Chern-class test; no rank-three existence or nonexistence follows from it.

For general nonsplit E, none of these equal-line numerical identities is available. Substituting 3l² for c₂(E) in that setting would silently assume precisely the structure that needs proof. The kernel in (4.1) can be arbitrary, and its extension class enters the second-jet equation.

## 5A. Closing the split-negative route on two explicit polarized models

The degree-32 endpoint can be tested without claiming that every polarized K3 has one of these models. This supplies actual geometry beyond the Chern-number necessary condition.

First let X be any smooth complete intersection of three quadrics in P⁵ and H=O_X(1). Adjunction gives KX=O, and the Koszul resolution gives H¹(O_X)=0, so X is a K3. Its degree is H²=8. The restricted Euler sequence and the twisted conormal sequence are

    0 → Ω¹P⁵(1)|X → O_X⁶ → H → 0,
    0 → H⁻¹⊕H⁻¹⊕H⁻¹ → Ω¹P⁵(1)|X → Ω¹X⊗H → 0.

The six coordinate sections give all sections of H (restriction from P⁵ is an isomorphism, also seen from Koszul). Thus H⁰(Ω¹P⁵(1)|X)=0. Serre duality and Kodaira vanishing give H¹(H⁻¹)=H¹(H)*=0. The second sequence now implies

    H⁰(Ω¹X⊗H)=0.

It follows that H⁻¹⊗C³ cannot even admit a nonzero derivative map to TX, using TX≅Ω¹X. In particular it cannot support a dominating spray. For an arbitrary direct sum H⁻a⊕H⁻b⊕H⁻c with positive exponents, the enumeration at d=4 forces (a,b,c)=(1,1,1). Hence no such rank-three negative-power sum has a surjection to TX. This is a concrete failure of the natural three-column plan, without a claim about arbitrary rank-three E.

Second let π:X→P² be a smooth double cover branched over a smooth sextic and put H=π*O(1). The canonical bundle formula gives KX=O; π_*O_X=O⊕O(−3) gives H¹(O_X)=0, so again X is a K3, with H²=2. The ramification divisor R is smooth and O_X(R)=3H. To determine the relative differential quotient, write the cover locally as w²=b(z). Modulo base differentials its differential relation is 2w dw=0. The fibre differential dw transforms as π*O(−3), since w is a fibre coordinate on the line bundle O(3). Therefore

    Ω¹_(X/P²) ≅ O_R(−3H),
    Ω¹X⊗2H ↠ O_R(−H).

The quotient line bundle has degree −H·R=−6 on R and is not globally generated. Consequently Ω¹X⊗2H is not globally generated. At d=1 the Chern enumeration forces (a,b,c)=(2,2,2). Hence no direct sum of three negative positive powers of this displayed H can surject onto TX either.

The second example also checks a sign that matters: replacing the relative differential quotient by O_R(+3H) would incorrectly erase the obstruction. The local w²=b equation fixes the sign. These calculations require no universal classification of polarized K3 surfaces and make none. They are not offered as a weakened replacement for either the all-K3 ellipticity target or a general minimum-rank theorem. Arbitrary nonsplit rank-three bundles remain outside these obstructions.

## 6. Compatibility with the actual original-history mechanisms

The read i98/i102 scopes prevent a misleading alternative nomination. R98 is the full relative Oka-map statement for arbitrary proper holomorphic K3 submersions, with the specified reduced Stein source, base map, interpolation and continuous parameters. It is unchanged. A local absolute spray or pointwise Oka fibre does not solve that problem. The read fixed-target weighted gluing still needs a new-side map whose projection is the prescribed base map; replacing it by an absolute Oka approximation breaks that constraint.

The original i102 analysis already pays holomorphic-parameter sewing once a common infinite holomorphic chain is supplied, and supplies compatible graph germs with radii that may shrink to zero on growing source compacta. The missing issue is not an unperformed formal jet induction. The source's finite atlases and finite target passages do not provide one infinite holomorphic itinerary over a fixed base neighbourhood. The example c_j(b)=[jb] in an elliptic curve diagnoses failure of one fixed chart across all j; it is not an impossibility theorem. Similarly, polynomial precomposition changes a prescribed g to g∘h. The real rational-period locus is not a holomorphic parameter neighbourhood. None of those paid operations closes R98 by assertion.

E98, the all-K3 global-spray target, is already defeated by actual nonprojective Kummer examples in the stronger primary. The present projective rank calculations do not rename that target or revive it with a projectivity restriction. P98 (punctured K3) and S98 (relative splitting) are unchanged. The exact i74 relative Moser/packing constraints and i80 fixed-action hypersymplectic scope are also unchanged.

The explicit completed r142 reads preserve its primitive normalization, actual relative Veronese algebra, and dβ=(n+1)β=0 Brauer annihilators. Neutrality of the projective bundle and extracting the remaining base line-bundle root are different operations. Its quaternion (4,4) K3 construction detects unconditional algebraic neutrality under its two-system hypotheses only. It is not evidence of analytic nonsplitting. The exact graph projector keeps H²⁰, so no torsion complement may be inferred. None of these algebraic operations supplies (4.5), a local spray, or a prescribed-base section.

## 7. Decision before any freeze

No distinct substantial full-scope operation has closed. The rank-four construction and rank-two obstruction are paid/classical combinations. The rank-three exploration has been carried through the actual two-jet cocycle, the extension boundary, the split-negative Chern constraints and the square-eight degree-32 classifying-map endpoint, and explicit conormal/ramification obstructions closing that split-negative plan on two polarized models. It has not solved the general extension problem or the special three-section presentation. There is therefore no theorem freeze and no author-proof nominee.
