## Exact boundary

The new author-side progress is an explicit restriction-map telescope for the unchanged fixed group, a bounded-vertex nullhomotopy argument, and a rigorous obstruction to normalizing its punctured-region stabilizers even by abstract isomorphisms to standard track groups. These close/clarify particular construction interfaces. They are technical supporting results, not a full proof of T253F and not independent nominee proposals.

For each n, Brown would still require FP_(n−p) for p-cell stabilizers along a suitable tail; vertices require FP_n. This remains unproved. The nonisomorphism argument does not imply failure of any FP_n or F_n property, nor preclude a different Brown complex or a direct finiteness proof on arbitrary reductions.
