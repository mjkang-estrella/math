## Two exact interfaces

For the unit-roof suspension of a map T with simple Lebesgue centered spectrum, split the suspension Hilbert space into functions fiberwise constant in the base and the fiberwise centered part. The first part is L2(R/Z), hence supplies eigenfunctions exp(2 pi i n s) for every integer n. On the second part, diagonalize U_T as multiplication by exp(2 pi i x), x in [0,1). The suspension generator has frequencies x+k, k in Z. The pair (x,k) parametrizes R uniquely modulo null endpoints. Thus the continuous spectral part is simple Lebesgue, but the entire centered space additionally has every nonzero integer eigenfrequency. This is not the desired full-space flow. It is a classical suspension calculation, not a nominee.

Changing the roof by a smooth coboundary cannot remove this defect. For a positive roof r=1+b-b composed with T, the suspension is measurably conjugate to the unit-roof suspension by changing the height coordinate; equivalently the roof cocycle retains integer-frequency eigenfunctions. A noncoboundary roof therefore needs actual new spectral control, not smooth approximation alone.

The source passage is exactly T_N(theta,u,a)=(theta+H_N(u+sigma_N(a))-H_N(u),u+sigma_N(a),a), with H_N'=N(c+phi+Delta_N), sigma_N=1/N+J/N².
