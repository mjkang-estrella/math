That is a bounded finding, not a priority certificate. It did identify strong
antecedents, especially ADN, which must not be omitted from contribution
review.

1. **Geometry and global weights already known.** Fill–Janson gives the
   N^{alpha+1/2} subtree-power scale with finite second offspring moment.
   ADN gives a general global additive-measure framework, including the
   size-biased skeleton measure and the 1/2 threshold. Naming a stable
   CRT skeleton is not a contribution. Candidate Section 4 instead proves
   the location-bearing preorder interval measure with the exact link/
   opposite-child thinning via source bounded path sums and bounded-
   variation summation by parts. A contribution reviewer should test whether
   an existing marked version of ADN already pays this step routinely.
2. **General resistance convergence is conditional on geometric convergence.**
   Croydon's theorem requires convergence of the actual resistance metric
   spaces. Original graph CRT convergence has no such implication for
   infinite-mean resistances. The candidate does not assert compact
   resistance-metric convergence; it constructs a trace-class L² operator,
   using mass-weighted rather than diameter tails.
3. **Heavy conductance FIN and barrier disorder differ.** CHK Section 6.1
   uses conductances bounded below; the resistance law homogenizes and the
   speed measure becomes stable for the conductance-degree clock. In G
   conductances approach zero, resistance has infinite mean, and the speed
   measure remains original degree. Replacing omega by 1/R does not verify
   CHK's hypotheses. Faggionato is the closest directly read barrier
   spectral theorem, but its segment has deterministic linear ordering,
   Dirichlet boundary and no cyclic blocks or conditioned branching.
4. **Decorated-tree theorems have actual hypotheses.** Archer's exponents
   require stated diameter/resistance/volume tails and concern an infinite
   stable-offspring setting. Andriopoulos requires convergence of continuous
   potentials and measured resistance trees. Neither inspected statement
   supplies the missing tails for the present all-edge FK map. No universal
   transfer to every finite-variance decorated map family is asserted.
5. **Electrical decomposition and finite-dimensional operations are credited.**
   Positive inverse-block sums, exact bridge rank-ones, Menger/Thomson,
   two-path Pareto moment gain, conditional Poisson products and the compact
   positive spectral theorem are classical/elementary. They were used in F;
   they are not new separately counted results. The full proposed bridge
   must stand as one package, including its two infinite-dimensional trace
   tails and the cyclic-block second-moment estimate (7.6).
6. **No microscopic Frechet transplant.** The new limit is an interacting
   sum. The exact nested-cut example has norm 1+1/sqrt(3) while both individual
   norms are 1. F's integrable microscopic toll E|T|^alpha diverges here.
   Parent's unit-path counterexample is retained, and no false universal
   largest-cut inequality reappears. No endpoint conclusion is borrowed:
   at alpha=1/2 Fill–Janson has N log N expectation and the small-mass
   remainder bound no longer vanishes.
