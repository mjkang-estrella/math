## Primary literature

New retrieved PDFs and text extractions are under inputs/private/literature/;
literature-retrieval.json gives source URLs, PDF byte counts/SHA256s and local
copy provenance. The final input manifest authenticates PDFs and text.
Text was extracted with local pdftotext -layout; layout is not relied upon
for a diagram, numerical result or theorem. None of this is third-party code.
Line references below are to these plain-text readings, not PDF line numbers.

* Fill–Janson, [2104.02715v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.02715v1): inspected
  lines 80–320, Theorems 1.1–1.12 and Remarks 1.13–1.15. The open regime,
  finite-second-moment assumption, different endpoint expectation and
  general-additive references were checked. PDF/text copied unchanged from
  fk-frechet-contribution-r1/literature, with hashes recorded; no claim that
  all proofs or later moment recursion sections were read. Not a premise
  for unproved spectral convergence; construction is paid in candidate.
* Abraham–Delmas–Nassif, [2009.08185v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.08185v1):
  lines 1–250 read. Theorem 1.1, Remark 1.2, measure definitions (1.6)–(1.8),
  and discussion of noncontinuity of unweighted length pushforwards were
  inspected. General subtree measures/global regime credited. Later
  Proposition 7.1/Theorem 7.3 proofs not read or invoked.
* Croydon, [1609.05666v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.05666v1): lines 1–135
  inspected, Assumption 1.1, Theorem 1.2, Remark 1.3. Requires actual measured
  resistance-space convergence and nonexplosion; does not itself pay G.
* Croydon–Hambly–Kumagai,
  [1609.02120v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.02120v1): introduction/Assumptions
  1.2 and 1.4/Theorem 1.3 inspected; Section 6.1 lines 1470–1665 inspected,
  especially Assumption 6.1, Lemmas 6.2–6.3 and Proposition 6.4. Resistance
  homogenization uses omega^-1<=1 and finite mean. Stable marks are a speed
  measure for large conductances. That is different from infinite-mean
  Pareto barriers at a fixed original-degree speed measure. Other fractal
  proof sections not read.
* Andriopoulos, [1812.10197v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.10197v1): abstract,
  introduction theorem statements located/inspected and lines 535–590 read
  closely (Assumption 1, Theorem 5.2). Continuous Gaussian potentials and
  measured resistance convergence are not G. No full proof-body reading.
* Archer, [2011.07266v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.07266v1): title/abstract,
  lines 100–145 and 290–319 read; located Theorems 1.3,1.7–1.10. Assumptions
  1.1/1.4 require explicit decoration growth/tail bounds, unit conductances
  and infinite stable-offspring geometry. Those statements do not pay G;
  proof bodies not read. No broad decorated-family transfer is inferred.
* Aldous, [CRT III](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Papers/CRT3.pdf):
  lines 350–420, Section 2.3 continuum-tree definition, binary property,
  nonatomic leaf mass and positive upper-subtree mass inspected; Corollary
  22 located (normalization is 2e there). The elementary leaf-coding and
  dense-clade-span argument needed for H0 are written in candidate Section 8.
* Faggionato, EJP 17 (2012), paper 15, preserved in the authenticated F
  private literature: lines 537–597 read, especially Theorem 2.5. Stable
  segment barriers have spectral limit n^{1+1/alpha} and generalized
  differential operator; this is a directly relevant one-dimensional
  antecedent, not a theorem on random branching maps. Other proof sections
  not read this round.
* Janson 1312.1211v1 and Flegel papers are preserved with the F package and
  their roles/full earlier reading limits retained. The full separate
  contribution review's Janson reduction was read. No new full-paper reading
