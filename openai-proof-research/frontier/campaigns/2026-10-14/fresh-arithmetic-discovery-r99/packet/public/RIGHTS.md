# Rights

- `public/` (except third-party metadata fields such as titles and URLs in the
  registers) and `code/`: original r99 author prose and code.
- `private/upstream/`: byte copies of pinned files from the OpenAI math
  repository at `fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb`, under that
  repository's terms. Not for redistribution beyond this project.
- `private/primary/`: arXiv PDFs and abstract pages, under their authors'
  and arXiv's licences. Kept for local verification only.
- `private/history/`: copies of earlier project records, unchanged.
