# One-cell plus construction and the exact source-level gap

Prefreeze derivation; closed consequences below are paid source/classical reductions, not nominees. Write R=ZG and use the source notation in note 01.

## 1. Compress the normal generators, without changing the quotient

Set u=t1 t2=x1² x2³. Certainly normal_P(u)⊂K. In P/normal_P(u), t2=t1^-1. The source identity t1=a2^26 says t1 commutes with a2, so t2 commutes with a2 too. The other exact identity t1=[t2,a2] now gives t1=1, hence t2=1. Therefore

    normal_P(u)=K.                                             (2.1)

This uses the nonabelian group identities, not just the zero module K_ab. Apply the source plus construction with this ONE normal generator. It produces X1⊃B with cell counts (1,5,5,1), π1=G, and all the source coefficient conditions. Mannan's relative plus uniqueness identifies X1≃X under B. No simple-homotopy uniqueness of arbitrary plus attachments is asserted. K's perfectness is the already-paid source proof; (2.1) is not a new substitute for it.

Put J=X1^(2), the actual presentation complex with r1,r2,r3,r4,u. Its chain boundary has

    C3(X1)=R, C2(X1)=R4⊕R, ∂3(e)=(a,1).

The elementary triangular basis change (v,w)↦(v-wa,w) (where wa is left scalar multiplication of the vector a) makes ∂3 the second-summand inclusion. Cancelling that contractible CHAIN summand leaves the completely explicit free algebraic complex

    A: R4 --D--> R5 --(x1-1,a1-1,x2-1,a2-1,s-1)--> R.          (2.2)

Here the four columns of D are the original Fox derivatives evaluated in G. Since K is perfect the first homology of this cover of B is zero; equivalently A is exact in degree one and augments to Z. No unknown projective complement remains in (2.2). Nevertheless it has no finite geometric two-dimensional realization of this homotopy type, by the source theorem.

## 2. Carry the geometric stabilization, including its map

The cochain q:C2(J)→R with q∂3=1 splits π2(J)=π2(X1)⊕Re, where e is the actual spherical attaching class. Add a sphere with class f. One elementary shear on J∨S² fixes the one-skeleton and pinches the 2-cells into f with coefficients q; on π2 it adds q(v)f to v. The inverse uses -q. Another shear fixes J and sends f to f+e, represented by a pinch followed by the chosen attaching sphere. Composing these shears and their inverses realizes the elementary rotation of Re⊕Rf, identity on π2(X1), taking e to ±f. Reorient the target 3-cell if necessary. Extend over the unique 3-cell, now attached to the sphere wedge factor, and cancel that sphere/3-cell pair. The resulting target is J. These are the explicit geometric elementary operations of Hambleton Lemma 2.1; their chain matrices have elementary torsion.

Thus X1∨S² is simple-homotopy equivalent to J. X itself is homotopy equivalent to X1, so its minimum number of wedge spheres needed for a finite 2-model is exactly one: zero is excluded by the source, one is supplied here. This is stabilization bookkeeping with an explicit normal-generator simplification, not the requested distinct substantial operation. It does NOT give a cellular cancellation on X1 itself.

## 3. Exact normal-versus-module generator counts

Let F be free on the five named generators, N=normal_F(r1,...,r4), L=ker(F→G), so L/N=K. Perfectness gives L=N[L,L]. Hence L_ab=L/[L,L] is generated as a left R-module by the four images of r1,...,r4.

These four generators are minimal. The relation-module embedding L_ab→R5 sends a relator class to its Fox vector. After tensoring with the right module Cρ, the composite Cρ4→Cρ⊗R L_ab→Cρ5 has rank four by the source minor. A module generated by at most three elements cannot have such an image. This argument does NOT incorrectly assume tensor preserves the relation-module injection. Therefore

    d_R(L_ab)=4.                                               (2.3)

The balanced presentation J gives d_F(L)≤5. To prove the reverse inequality, take ANY finite presentation complex Y for G, with ρ transported along its marking. Degree-one twisted homology depends only on π1, so H1(Y;Cρ)=H1(X;Cρ)=0. H0 is zero too. Therefore χ(Y)=dim H2(Y;Cρ)≥0. Equality would give H2=0 while ρ detects the order-two element x1, contradicting the actual source tower corollary. Consequently χ(Y)≥1 for every such Y. With the convention def=generators−relators this says def(G)≤0. J attains zero, hence

    def(G)=0, d_F(L)=5, d_F(L)-d_R(L_ab)=1.                     (2.4)

Also def(P)=1: B attains one, while the quotient by a perfect subgroup preserves abelianization and P_ab=Z⊕C6 bounds deficiency above by rational b1=1. The source therefore realizes the strict deficiency drop in Mannan's original Lemma 4.4, with no conditional relation-gap premise left.

For completeness μ2(G)=0 in the usual free partial-resolution convention. Complex (2.2) attains zero. For any finite free algebraic 2-complex, extend it to a free resolution and tensor with Cρ. H0 and H1 are zero, so its Euler characteristic is the dimension of its remaining degree-two kernel and is nonnegative. Thus μ2(G)=0<1-def(G)=1. This is exactly the strongest existing algebraic/deficiency reduction, not a new realization mechanism.

## 4. Why this does not repair an old target

A free rank-four degree-two chain module, an explicitly elementary split third boundary, a balanced presentation of G, and a stable simple equivalence are now all present simultaneously. The obstruction still distinguishes A from the cellular chain type of every finite 2-model for X. Replacing that problem by stable freeness, an equality of Euler characteristics, or existence of SOME presentation of G would discard the issue the source resolves. These computations establish the paid baseline before the more specific integral comparison in notes 03–04. No independent correctness or contribution verdict is assigned.
