The current screen concerns family 080, endpoint Schrödinger convergence.
The weighted scalar calculation in weighted-tree.md is technical reuse of
the stipulated source, not a candidate. A possible genuinely unpaid bridge
would be a finite Schatten estimate for arbitrary common time graphs at
s=n/[2(n+1)], sufficient for orthonormal-density convergence. It is presently
unproved. No positivity of that bridge, natural full exponent range, or
literature novelty is asserted.

Primary comparison so far: Bez–Kinoshita–Shiraki 2023, Theorem 2.2 and §6.2,
proves orthonormal-density maximal estimates for Schrödinger regularity s≥n/4.
Its proof uses absolute oscillatory-kernel decay, Schatten interpolation,
and dyadic spatial/frequency summation. At source critical regularity, s<n/4
for every n≥2; the positive frequency exponent in that proof is not summable.
This is a limitation of that proof, not a counterexample to the desired bridge.
Their §6.3 Condition 2 gives the static necessary bound β≤n/(n−2s) for
Lebesgue measure; at source s this is β≤(n+1)/n. No admissibility below it
has been shown here.
