# Rights and separation

`public/` contains original r123 research prose, integrity code and factual provenance registers. The verifier/control code is adapted from the project's analyticr122 original programs, read before reuse; this attribution preserves their provenance. No new publication or license grant is made by this local archive.

`private/upstream/` contains pinned OpenAI source bodies under their original repository terms. `private/primary/` contains third-party original PDFs, extracted text and one page image under the respective original rights. `private/history/` and `private/context/` are local evidence copies, not newly authored public prose. Private search-location receipts likewise confer no rights to originating works. None of these private bodies is designated a public-release payload.

No upload, external contact or publication was authorized or performed. Parent integration must preserve this separation. Hashing an input does not transfer its rights or certify its mathematics.
