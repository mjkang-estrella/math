## 290: countably many ambient algebras

The actual main theorem of *Expected amenable subalgebras preserving core commutants*, `thm:main`, supplies an expected amenable `P subset N` with

    P' intersect c(M) = N' intersect c(M)

for every expected separable-predual inclusion `N subset M`. Its `cor:masa` already constructs an expected MASA with the corresponding continuous-core commutant formula. Neither the MASA conclusion nor a type-III1-only version is fresh.

The proof's terminal continuous-core argument, not just its main theorem, was read. In the factor case the conjugated copy of N acts in one tensor factor and the flow-fixed algebra in the complementary factor. The intertwining unitary combines the previously constructed operator with `exp(iX tensor Y)`. In the general-center case the proof uses `Z(N)'`, central disintegration and Abelian orbit averaging. Thus general centers and a common exact continuous core are already part of the paid interface. The preceding uniform total-energy and long product-kernel proofs were not fully reread; they are stipulated operations, not newly verified estimates.

The strongest compatible reduction for simultaneous countably many inclusions does not require a new diagonal amenable construction or an amalgamated free product. Given expected inclusions `N subset M_j` with faithful normal expectations E_j, choose positive weights w_j with sum1 and put

    M=product_j M_j,
    E((x_j))=diagonal(sum_j w_j E_j(x_j)).

The series converges ultraweakly and defines a unital normal N-bimodular conditional expectation. Positivity and all w_j>0 give faithfulness. A countable product has separable predual when its factors do, since the predual is the l1 sum. With a faithful normal state phi on N, `phi composed with E` has coordinate states `w_j phi composed with E_j`; their modular actions restrict to the same action on diagonal N. The central coordinate projections are fixed. Decomposing the crossed product by these projections identifies its jth corner with `c(M_j)` compatibly with N.

Apply the source once to this expected diagonal inclusion. Taking the jth central corner of its exact relative-commutant equality yields the desired equality for the **same** P in each `c(M_j)`. Expectations onto P can be composed with each E_j. Countable simultaneity, including arbitrary centers, fits the already supplied source contract. This is a feasibility comparison with the actual strongest input, not a new theorem submitted for review.

The closest primary inspected was Marrakchi, [arXiv:2308.15163v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.15163v3), *Kadison's problem for type III subfactors and the bicentralizer conjecture*, November10,2024. TheoremD and the actual Theorem10.17 construction were read: finite-dimensional subalgebras and a dense list of normal-functionals are handled by successive local approximations, convergent states and a faithful limit expectation. The concluding type cases and countable reductions in Lemmas10.21/10.22 were inspected. This is much stronger primary construction precedent than a qualitative existence headline. The source's general-center core theorem remains the stipulated exact interface; no uninspected primary hypothesis is silently removed.

Demanding that P itself be invariant under a group action is different. Simultaneous equalities for ambient translates do not make the selected algebra invariant. Joining its orbit translates need not preserve amenability. The countable-product reduction does not solve that equivariant selection problem; this round built no replacement. This is a missing step of a proposed method, not an impossibility theorem or a new frozen target.

## Bounded modular recovery: actual r96 already stronger

Three prospective bodies from the bounded-recovery companion were acquired and authenticated before the historical comparison. They were not semantically read in sufficient depth and are removed before sealing, with acquisition metadata retained. Their contents are not used to claim a new proof interface.

The actual structural r96 `proof.md` was read completely. It already characterizes bounded modular spectral recovery by finite dimensionality of the centralizer. Its finite-direction proof uses the smallest nonzero projection mass in the finite-dimensional centralizer to replace scalar domination, then reuses the source randomization/truncation mechanism. The other direction constructs orthogonal small centralizer corners and a commuting spectral selector. The scalar-centralizer or finite-block variants are therefore not fresh directions. This comparison follows the argument, not the prior author's classification. No independent correctness verdict on r96 is issued.

Other operator leads were shorter history exclusions. In particular actual r118's source262 conditioning/infinite-channel discussion was consulted; no full new quantum-channel theorem or source262 body was developed. Catalogue Markov-type/fixed-point and tree-metric leads were navigation and actual-history filtering only, not additional full primary/source audits.
