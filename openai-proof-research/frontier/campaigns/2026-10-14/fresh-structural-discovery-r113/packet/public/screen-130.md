The strongest compatible source is *An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform*, not just the finite-tensor companion's subsequence theorem. Its `thm:main` gives O(n(log n)^θ(log log n)^(4−θ)), θ<1, including preparation and addresses, from one supplied specified root of order D_*<1024n³. `cor:polynomial-multiplication` already gives exact complex convolution. `loc:compiler` preserves width exactly, with arbitrary borrowed data restored. `syn:extraction` supplies a finite rational algebra with inverse witnesses from a complex polynomial certificate; `syn:simulation` uses its regular representation at a constant cost fixed before n. These are paid and were considered before claiming a coefficient-selection gap. I103 already explicitly compares the stronger all-length companion and excludes the all-length complex restatement.

The local Newton factorization itself is algebraic: `loc:newton-reduction` divides by H_j=∏_(s=1)^j(1−u^s), j<r. These factors are nonzero for a primitive root u of order r in any field. This part is not the missing mechanism. Good–Thomas indexing and a separable-root convolution likewise do not, alone, present a new interface.

The actual finite saving uses

    C = ((1+i,1−i),(1−i,1+i))/2,

and binary address frames, separately distinguished from the scalar data field. `net:schedule` also uses halves. In characteristic two, 2 has no inverse and there is no primitive fourth root: x^4−1=(x−1)^4. The Walsh frame collapses because −1=1. Extending the field does not repair either fact. The source's discussion of nonalternating *binary address forms* is not a theorem placing the complex data kernel in characteristic two.

The local compiler additionally invokes power-of-two convolution roots, and the all-length working modulus L includes a power of two and initial odd primes; the chirp starts from a 2n-th root. A literal reduction modulo a fixed prime can therefore encounter lengths divisible by that prime, even when the requested transform length is admissible. Removing finitely many denominator primes from one finite algebra certificate also does not furnish the missing small-characteristic certificate. Its fixed multiplication table can be reduced away from its denominators, but it remains necessary to verify the entire root and compilation interface. No unrestricted result is inferred from that observation.
