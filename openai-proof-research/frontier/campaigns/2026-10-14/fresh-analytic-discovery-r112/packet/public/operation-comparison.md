## 356: bounded upper-semicontinuous Hessian bounds

Keep the exact source hypotheses on the RCD space and F. Suppose G is bounded upper-semicontinuous instead of continuous. For integers j>=1 define

`G_j(x) = sup_y (G(y) - j d(x,y))`.

If |G|<=M, each G_j is finite, j-Lipschitz, lies in [-M,M], and is at least G. They decrease in j. For fixed x, points contributing within epsilon of the supremum can be taken at distance at most (2M+epsilon)/j. Upper semicontinuity therefore gives G_j(x) decreasing to G(x). This works on the entire metric space; no compactness, chosen geodesic, or finite-dimensional truncation is required for the envelope.

For nonnegative test weights the weak Hessian bound by G implies the bound by every G_j. Apply the stipulated `weight:every-geodesic` to each j. On any prescribed geodesic and any nonnegative compactly supported smooth time test phi,

`integral F(sigma_t) phi''(t) dt <= length(sigma)^2 integral G_j(sigma_t) phi(t) dt`.

Bounded convergence gives the same inequality with G. A countable family of deterministic implications creates no probabilistic exceptional-set issue. The full all-geodesic quantifier is retained because the source has it separately for each j.

This verifies that this particular proposed regularity extension has a standard compatible reduction. It does not establish a new substantial candidate. It does not extend the theorem to arbitrary Borel representatives. Indeed an ambient-a.e. bound is insensitive to changes on a null geodesic, whereas the displayed path integral is sensitive to them.
