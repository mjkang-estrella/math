&emsp;[A dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave measures](preprints/A-dimension-free-logarithmic-Sobolev-inequality-for-subgaussian-log-concave-measures-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

We prove that every centered log-concave probability measure with a Lebesgue density on ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup> and linear subgaussian parameter <i>a</i> satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Ent}}\nolimits _\mu(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|Df|^2\,d\mu`$ for compactly supported smooth <i>f</i>, with one universal constant <i>C</i>. This resolves positively the dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev conjecture for subgaussian log-concave measures.
