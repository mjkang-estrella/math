# T153M64 — quartic Salem series with symmetric Markov signs

Full target, unresolved at freeze. Frozen UTC 2026-10-18T19:11:31.059274+00:00.

For EVERY Salem number beta in (1,2) of degree four and EVERY q in (0,1), let (epsilon_n)_{n≥0} be the stationary Markov chain on {−1,+1} with uniform initial distribution and P(epsilon_{n+1}=−epsilon_n | epsilon_n)=q. Prove that the law of Y=sum_{n≥0} epsilon_n beta^(−n) is singular with respect to Lebesgue measure on R: there exists a Borel Lebesgue-null set E with P(Y in E)=1.

No restriction to almost every q, algebraic q, a small neighborhood of1/2, an entropy-deficient subrange, one beta, or a subsequence of digit positions is allowed. Constants in a proof may depend on the fixed beta and q; no uniformity as q approaches0,1 or1/2 is requested. No dimension-drop strengthening is claimed. The q=1/2 case is source-paid; endpoints q=0,1 are not part of this target.
