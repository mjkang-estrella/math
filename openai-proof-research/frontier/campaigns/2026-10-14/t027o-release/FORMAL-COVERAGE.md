# Scoped formal support

The author package proves a triangle-coboundary theorem and the polynomial core of principal-minor rigidity over an arbitrary field. It uses a chosen reference index, nonzero off-diagonal matrix entries, stationary pair and cycle polynomials, and unequal forward and reverse triangle products. The conclusion is an entrywise diagonal commutator. Three support declarations and twelve control declarations include a nonzero rational example and a rational counterexample to deleting genericity.

The independent reviewer adds 39 declarations. They identify the pair and cycle polynomials with first-order coefficients of actual two-by-two and three-by-three principal minors after the lower-order identities are imposed, derive the corresponding rigidity statement, and check characteristic-five, small-index and hypothesis-deletion examples. Genericity cannot be deleted in general; the rational counterexample does not establish that every triangle inequality is necessary for every individual matrix.

Neither package formalizes the Vandermonde reduction, generic Cartan construction, dense arithmetic torus, simple surface curves, tangent certificate, finite-open arithmetic selection, complex Baire argument, character-variety geometry, Goldman flows, or orbit density. Those are obligations of the full mathematical proof and its separate independent reviews. The entire T027O theorem is not formally verified.

The 54 declarations were freshly compiled under Lean 4.34.1, commit 5045d0056413266e57c625dcd7c365b10e377c52. Printed dependencies are limited to propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The compiler and cached objects for mathlib d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612 and eight other pinned packages are trusted; they were not rebuilt from source. The frozen author builder has fixed network/cache receipt flags and a limited declaration scanner. Use GIT_OPTIONAL_LOCKS=0 when invoking it. Its integrity controls check exit codes; the independent review separately checks rejection reasons. Hashes, finite diagnostics and successful compilation do not establish the full theorem or its novelty.
