# T027O local release preparation

This directory prepares the local release of **One integral mapping-class orbit in every rank**, by Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. The theorem retains every g≥2 and r≥2, an actual representation into SL_r(O_L) over a number field, and one orientation-preserving mapping-class orbit dense in the entire complex character variety. Rank two is credited to Coccia–Litt; the higher-rank claim is new to this project, without a worldwide priority claim.

The manuscript, proof, contribution and scoped formal-support reviews have passed and are bound by FINAL-GATE.json. The assembly helper checks all seven adopted packet anchors. Local archive checks and parent acceptance are separate subsequent decisions, recorded outside the archive. Uploads are stopped by user instruction.

The explicit payload selection contains the current manuscript and source, original proof and comparison notes, scoped Lean support, independent review evidence and parent adoption receipts. Third-party bodies and superseded manuscript builds are excluded. RIGHTS.md records licensing, FORMAL-COVERAGE.md states the exact Lean boundary, and REPRODUCTION.md explains portable rebuilds and trusted dependencies.

See preparation.json for code provenance, prepared-entry-checks.json for the historical refusal checks while the review was pending, and ERRATA.txt for the five optional manuscript findings. These helpers authenticate bytes and declared gate bindings; they cannot establish mathematical correctness or independent judgment on their own.
