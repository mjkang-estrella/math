# T027O local release preparation

This directory prepares the local release of **One integral mapping-class orbit in every rank**, by Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. The theorem retains every g≥2 and r≥2, an actual representation into SL_r(O_L) over a number field, and one orientation-preserving mapping-class orbit dense in the entire complex character variety. Rank two is credited to Coccia–Litt; the higher-rank claim is new to this project, without a worldwide priority claim.

The manuscript is sealed, reproduced and undergoing independent review. Proof, contribution and scoped formal-support reviews have passed. Final manuscript-review evidence, archive checks and parent acceptance remain pending.
