There exist fixed source elliptic-contact data over Qbar, a nonempty principal open U in the affine space

    W = kappa0 + gamma H0(P1 x P1, O(6,2)) = A^21_Qbar,

and a finite étale surjective morphism rho:B→U of degree d≥1, with B smooth, affine and geometrically integral over Qbar, for which the following objects and properties exist.

There are an affine flat morphism of finite presentation p:X→B, a section s:B→X, a smooth morphism Y→B of relative dimension two, a projective B-morphism pi:Y→X, and an effective Cartier divisor E⊂Y. For every geometric point b of B (over any algebraically closed extension of Qbar):

1. X_b is an integral normal affine surface, and its singular locus is exactly the one point s(b).
2. The relative derivation sheaf T_(X/B)=Hom_OX(Omega1_(X/B),OX) has a global ordered basis of two sections. Formation of this sheaf and its specified basis commutes with every base change B'→B. In particular Der_k(O(X_b)) is free of rank two over O(X_b), where k is the algebraically closed residue extension at b.
3. pi_b:Y_b→X_b is the minimal resolution. It is an isomorphism away from E_b, its reduced exceptional fiber is E_b, and (E_b,N^*_(E_b/Y_b)) is isomorphic to (C_(rho(b)),L_(rho(b))). Here C_u=V(kappa_u)⊂P1 x P1 is the source (8,4)-curve and L_u=O(1,0)|C_u+Z. Thus E_b is a smooth genus-21 curve with self-intersection −10 and L_u^4≅K_(C_u).
4. X_b is Gorenstein with trivial dualizing sheaf, and the discrepancy of E_b is −5: with compatible canonical divisors, K_(Y_b)=pi_b^*K_(X_b)−5E_b.
5. For every complete local C-algebra R, the set

    { b in B(C) : completed O_(X_b,s(b)) is isomorphic to R as a complete local C-algebra }

has at most 720·8^42·d elements. Consequently the complex fibers have finite repetition under arbitrary affine-surface isomorphism as well. In particular, this is a 21-dimensional algebraic family whose singular germs vary in 21 dimensions, not merely a family of marked presentations of one germ.
