No candidate other than T238B125 was frozen. Several families were screened by actual operations before its freeze, with deeper follow-ups sometimes later. These are exclusions and exact missing interfaces, not additional nominees.

**185, infinite matroids.** The actual source introduction, matroid setup, counterexample and consequences were read, together with r21's complete checkpoint/candidate. The source supplies its infinite-basis construction, and r21 already develops connected self-dual examples by the (M-plus)-minus construction and a finite-error ordinal rank. Bowler–Geschke's author-hosted UniformMatroid7.pdf, Theorems6/8/10 and nearby proofs, supplies the nested-basis/uniform-intersection and infinite-connectivity context. The full filters/local-bases/assembly proofs were acquired but not read and removed. A connected/self-dual repackaging was not selected; no new construction preserving an additional unpaid invariant was built.

**114, common integer polymatroid bases.** The complete source introduction and actual I29 rank/covariance argument show stronger existing binary-capacity and entropy interfaces than a unit-capacity headline. I13 already distinguishes projection from fiber-weighted counting. The source polyhedral-controls proof was read after freeze: exact oracle/control structure is paid. A generic counting-to-sampling or binary-encoding consequence was not selected. Main/counting proof bodies were not freshly read; no stronger weighted-marginal claim is made from their titles.

**120, degree factors and weighted matching.** The source introduction and actual r109 history first screened this family. The source's complete f-factor section, read after freeze, provides a Beneš-port reduction with every path and size bound, rather than an unproved capacity gadget. R35's matching120 note was also fully read after freeze. Its weighted signed binary target still needs dual-aware contraction; the two-triangle 6W-to-2W example is a limitation of a simplistic contraction operation, not a theorem impossibility. No unweighted factor reduction or renamed weighted gap is nominated.

**161, incidence patterns.** The actual complete source complex section and I13 Section3 were read before freeze. I13 already writes the biregular score identity and the sign-averaging obstruction. The later source kernel support/pinning proof was read after freeze. Restricting hosts or renaming regular patterns does not build a new regular-pattern construction. No full new target was developed here, and no missing bridge was conditionally nominated.
