# Rights

Public original prose, registers and code in `packet/public` and `packet/code` were written for this research round; the parent may integrate them.

`packet/private` holds third-party and upstream bodies under their original rights: pinned openai/math source files, primary PDFs/text extractions (arXiv, Springer Open Access/d-nb.info), renders of those PDFs, copies of project context/history files, and search outputs. They are kept only as evidence for reading and provenance; no redistribution or publication is implied.
