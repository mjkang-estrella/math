## 063 generalized Mukai: the log version is a paid composition

Actual source063 introduction (`thm:main`): every smooth complex Fano manifold of positive dimension satisfies ρ(ι−1) ≤ n, with equality exactly for (P^{ι−1})^ρ.

Fujita, arXiv:1206.2475v1 (prior runner's acquisition; v2 and v3 returned 404). I read the introduction through Corollary 1.9. Theorem 1.8 (= 4.4) says that GM^n_ρ together with LGM^n_ρ implies LGM^{n+1}_ρ for all n, ρ ≥ 2. Remark 1.5 cites LGM^2_ρ as true from [Fjt12, Prop. 4.1]. That base paper was not acquired.

Source063 implies Fujita's GM^n_ρ in his formulation. Suppose ρ(X) ≥ ρ and ι ≥ (n+ρ)/ρ > 1. Then n ≥ ρ(X)(ι−1) ≥ ρ(ι−1) ≥ n, so all of these are equalities, and the source's equality case applies. Induction then gives the full log generalized Mukai conjecture for every n and every ρ ≥ 2. This is a direct composition paid by Fujita. It is not new.

The defect-one direction is already the accepted R034 (history copies 07 and 08). Singular or positive-characteristic Fano versions would need Gromov–Witten correspondence machinery that does not exist in those settings. That invariant is wholly unbuilt, so I did not pursue it.
