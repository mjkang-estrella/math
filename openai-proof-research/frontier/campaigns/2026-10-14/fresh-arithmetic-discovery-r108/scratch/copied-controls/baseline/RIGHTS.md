# Local research rights boundary

This is a private/local research handoff. Publication, uploads, submissions and downstream release work are stopped.

- notes/, controls/, READING.md, RIGHTS.md, FINAL.md and REPORT.md contain this round's original prose/code and individually named metadata. They are kept separate from protected bodies. No new publication licence is assigned by this handoff.
- private/sources/ holds pinned upstream source bodies; private/primary/ holds third-party PDFs, extracted text and the inspected page render. Their original rights remain in force.
- private/history/, private/history-search*.json and private/controls/ contain project originals, search excerpts or copied controls under the originals' rights. Private metadata receipts do not turn protected source bodies into public material.
- scratch/copied-controls/ contains only disposable local integrity-test copies with the same respective rights. It is outside the exact payload manifest and is never a publication package.

No protected body is intended for redistribution. Source authentication, file hashes and artifact checks say nothing about mathematical correctness, contribution, acceptance or priority.
