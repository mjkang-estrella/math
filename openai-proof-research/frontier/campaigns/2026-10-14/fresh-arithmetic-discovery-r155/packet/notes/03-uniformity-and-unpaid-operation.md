# Family uniformity, a concrete stress test, and the unpaid operation

PREFREEZE/no nominee. No exact substantial theorem was frozen. The initial-model counting in Note 02 is retained as a complete feasibility reduction from paid source and primary operations, not tendered as a weaker replacement or assigned a contribution grade.

## 1. The attempted stronger operation

The prospective uniformity question was this: in the entire generalized-canonical category of Note 02, can the lengths of ALL supplied flip sequences be bounded by a function of the denominator m, the ambient surface-cycle rank C, and the coefficient-labelled normalized-boundary ranks (b_j,R_j), without supplying the size of an auxiliary resolution or the finite low-place set as an additional unbounded input? The b-divisor remains fixed throughout EACH program, as required by the source. Across inputs in such a class it can vary; the question does not assert termination for a program that changes its b-divisor.

This was a feasibility direction, not a theorem statement freeze. Its full category has not been proved or disproved. Restricting it to bounded chosen resolutions and then calling (10) the desired result would change the problem. We do not do that.

The integrated derivation has eliminated several plausible but false gaps. One does NOT need finite branching of the ray choices. One does NOT need a new finite-place lemma at every later model. Repeated blowups can be enumerated exactly, initial surface centres can be charged to their first contraction, multiple normalization branches are bounded by the actual target multiplicities, and ambient rank increases are paid by target surface incidences. Formula (10) closes these interfaces for a fixed initial low-place budget f.

The remaining f-dependence is genuine dependence of this COUNTING METHOD. Equation (1) bounds it by a chosen resolution's exceptional primes and boundary intersections. Because every positive coefficient is a multiple of 1/m below one, the repeated-chain lengths and branch factors are bounded by m−1 already. The part not removed by this arithmetic is the supply of initial exceptional places associated with singularities or nef-trace defect.

## 2. A full global example: unbounded low-place supply with fixed visible data

For each integer q≥4, take

    X = P^1 × P^3,
    B = 0,
    A = pullback O_{P^1}(1),  H = pullback O_{P^3}(1).

Choose q distinct lines L_1,...,L_q in one ruling of a smooth quadric in P^3. They are pairwise disjoint. Put S_i=P^1×L_i, disjoint smooth codimension-two surfaces in X. Blow them up:

    π: W = Bl_{disjoint union S_i} X → X,
    E_i = the exceptional prime over S_i,
    M' = q π*H − sum_i E_i.

Every space here is smooth projective, so all global Weil Q-factorial and canonical-divisor requirements are satisfied. The carrier morphism π is projective.

The integral line bundle M' is globally generated, hence analytically nef. Indeed each ideal I_{L_i}(1) on P^3 is generated by its two linear equations. Tensor the q evaluation maps and compose with multiplication; the product ideal (product I_{L_i})(q) is globally generated. Since the centres are disjoint, the product ideal is the ideal of their union. On the blowup its pullback ideal becomes O_W(−sum E_i). Pulling the generating sections to the blowup therefore generates qπ*H−sum E_i. The same argument commutes with the P^1 product. This establishes nefness as a line-bundle fact, not merely a test on finitely many curves.

Let M be the b-divisor determined by M'. Its trace on X is qH. The codimension-two blowup canonical formula gives

    K_W = π*K_X + sum_i E_i,
    K_W + M' = π*(K_X + qH).                             (12)

Thus the generalized crepant boundary on W is EXACTLY zero. On W every further exceptional log discrepancy is the ordinary smooth zero-boundary discrepancy, at least two. Each E_i, viewed as exceptional over X, has log discrepancy one. Nonexceptional primes have log discrepancy one. Consequently (X,0+M) is generalized canonical and generalized klt in precisely the source convention; equality one at exceptional places is permitted. Its entire below-two exceptional-place set consists of these q primes. Since B=0, no boundary-surface exception in the low-place lemma can exclude them:

    minimal possible f = q.                              (13)

The visible data remain

    m=1,   C=c_2(P^1×P^3)=2,   no positive boundary ranks.

The two surface classes generating H_4 are represented by P^1×line and point×plane, so the equality C=2 is exact. Nevertheless q is arbitrary. Thus there is NO bound for the total below-two exceptional-place supply in terms of those displayed visible data alone. This is an actual global example in the source category, not an arbitrary abstract transition system or a singularity germ without a compact realization.

It is also non-nef, so simply discarding initially nef adjoints does not remove this stress test:

    D=K_X+M_X=−2A+(q−4)H,
    D·(P^1×point)=−2.

Projection to P^3 is a negative fibre-type contraction. No claim about existence of arbitrary generalized steps is needed.

There is no nontrivial bimeromorphic contraction of X to a normal compact Kähler target. To verify this directly, suppose f:X→Z were one and choose a Kähler class κ on Z. Its pullback has class aA+bH with a,b≥0, by evaluation on the two fibre-curve classes. Bimeromorphicity makes its top self-intersection strictly positive: the pulled-back form is positive on a nonempty open set and semipositive everywhere. Since

    integral_X(aA+bH)^4 = 4ab^3,

we have a,b>0. Such a class has positive degree on every nonconstant compact curve in P^1×P^3 (at least one projection is nonconstant). A contracted curve would have degree zero, a contradiction. A nontrivial projective birational morphism has a curve in a positive-dimensional fibre, so none exists. In particular every supplied small flip sequence starting at these examples has length zero.

This last fact is essential to interpreting (13). The example disproves only the proposed universal bound on ALL initial low places from the small visible data. It is NOT a counterexample to a bound on actual flip lengths. It does not refute a sharper invariant that ignores low places which never participate in negative small diagrams. It does not refute or repair any stipulated upstream theorem.

## 3. The exact unpaid interface

A successful continuation of the family-uniform direction would need a geometric bound, from specified initial visible data, for the part of the low-place supply that can actually be spent by negative small diagrams, or a substitute decreasing quantity that avoids counting the irrelevant supply in (13). It must handle every permitted projective small diagram, every repeated use of a place, and all the first-contraction boundary-centre transfers in Note 02.

Defining F_active as 'the places that occur as witnesses in some future program' is not an initial geometric construction or a proof of boundedness. It quantifies over the programs whose lengths are at issue. The example gives F_active empty even though f=q, demonstrating why replacing F_active by F loses precisely the needed discrimination. Neither source finite-use nor exact normalized-boundary cancellation supplies a bound on F_active uniform in the proposed visible data.

Possible negativity-locus or nef-defect refinements must therefore prove, rather than assume, that this spending is controlled before the choices of diagrams. We have not constructed such a refinement. Adding an upper bound on f, on a chosen nef carrier, or on its resolution's exceptional topology would give the direct bound (10), but would be a restricted/conditional substitute for this stronger direction. No such substitute is nominated.

The comparison used actual Fujino corrected difficulty and AHK echo/normalization operations, not only the source headline. The initial charging and exact telescoping in Note 02 show that a weak 'tail dependence' objection alone is not an unpaid bridge: that dependence can be eliminated when the full initial exceptional supply is retained. This is useful mathematical reduction, but it does not establish the distinct family-uniform operation. Its elementary numerical bound is not offered as evidence of substantial novelty, and no independent review or grade is claimed.

The stronger generalized-lc source and projective-lc source are accepted in full. Their unread proof bodies are not invoked to claim that they do or do not furnish a more refined invariant. No assertion of exhaustive screening, impossibility of the family-uniform question, optimality of (10), new-to-literature priority, abundance, higher-dimensional termination, real generalized boundary, moving b-divisors, or flip existence is made.

All older full targets and dispositions remain immutable. The outcome is PREFREEZE/no nominee, with (1)–(13), the exact source and primary operations, and the above unpaid geometric distinction retained for the parent.
