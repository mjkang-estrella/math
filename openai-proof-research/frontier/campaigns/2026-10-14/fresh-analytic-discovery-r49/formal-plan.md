No Lean proof or formal PASS is claimed. The mathematics was assembled first. A useful formal gate should verify a precisely declared finite/algebraic and asymptotic interface, while exposing the substantial analytic assumptions as imported contracts. Hash verification and finite numeric checks are not formal verification of T216A.
