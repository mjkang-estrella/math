The actual added operation in the author proof is organizing the qth trace as a q-cycle of empirical vertex types and compatible ordered block-pair types. After exact normalization, the exponent is -TC+sum I_vertex-(1/q)sum I_block. Coordinate data processing and the average of the q tree entropy inequalities cancel it. The number of types and every factorial error are polynomial uniformly at zero frequencies. This obtains the qth moment BEFORE applying source-style eigenspace dimensions and compression. The failure of the second moment for monochromatic q-block tables is a diagnostic motivating the order; it is not the claimed contribution or an impossibility theorem.
