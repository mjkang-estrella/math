# O217 bounded operation and eligibility comparison, before freeze

The pre-freeze feasibility note already contains the main cancellation argument. The ensuing variance-square repair was independently considered in this lane and communicated by the parent in `o217-parent/prefreeze-variance-note.md`, which has now been read in full and will be credited as author-side assistance. It is not independent review. No claim that proof ideas first occurred after scope freeze is made.

The proposed operation is a second coordinate replacement, on a deleted edge's remaining vertex star. The cubic outer derivative is odd under that entire star reflection, while its inner third derivative is a mixed sixth derivative. O(n) star coordinates then reduce the otherwise O(n^(-3/2)) cubic derivative expectation to O(n^(-2)). This is deterministic gauge invariance of F combined with a comparison of two product laws, not invariance of an asymmetric disorder law. It requires zero external field. For squared free energy, expanding the square before Taylor avoids a fifth-moment assumption; a finite product-law maximum closes the variance comparison without assuming an intermediate variance estimate.
