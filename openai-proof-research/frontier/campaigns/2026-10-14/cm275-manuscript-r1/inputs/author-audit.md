# Author adversarial audit of CM275

This is author checking, not an independent proof or contribution verdict. The full frozen scope is unchanged. No numerical theorem testing was used.

* Form constants: attraction <=2sqrt(2)M sqrt(NT)<=T/2+4NM². Each pair cap error is assigned to one coordinate, counted at most N-1 times, giving the safe 8epsilon(M+N)T. Neither estimate contains separation or coordinate magnitude.
* Compactification: the IMS gradient sum is <=4N/s² and kinetic normalization gives 2N/s². Conditional inner spinors are antisymmetric in exactly their surviving k labels, which is sufficient for E_k. The exterior wedge is justified by disjoint one-particle support sectors, including an arbitrary correlated inner state. Its pair bound counts at most N²/2 pairs. The sum NM/s+N²/(2s)+17N/s² is <eta for the stated s, since eta<=1 and s>=100(NM+N²+N+1)/eta. k=0 and k=N both remain legal.
* Cap comparison: E_Q<=eta, near-minimizer kinetic <=2(C+eta+o(1)); alpha=eta/[16(C+2)] gives forward error <=eta/8. E_epsilon<=2 and the reverse near-minimizer kinetic <=2(C+2+o(1)) gives the same reverse error. The displayed eta/4 is safe.
* Cutoff: J>=1, P>=64J/eta implies P²>=8J, outside norm²<=4J²/P⁴<1/4, and normalized excess <=(2+4J/P²)J²/[P²(3/4)]<4J²/P²<=eta/8. The operator norm estimate ||T psi||, not its expectation alone, is essential. All P-dependent bounds are polynomial because eta^(-1)<=100L.
* Aliasing: q>4K implies every Fourier difference of two retained modes has magnitude <q in each coordinate. This proves exact sampling norms also for translated differences. Averaging shifted quadratures is an exact integral identity. No estimate involves the number q^D of samples, and no enumeration of them is proposed. Reflection folding is globally 1-Lipschitz for the torus metric; it is essential to the claimed Dirichlet interpretation.
* Total finite-model error: eta+eta/4+eta/8+eta/16=23eta/16<2eta. Potential and kinetic evaluation errors are charged directly to final acceptance, not silently omitted from this bound.
* Projection soundness: cutoff and all odd projections commute; their product commutes with the full sign projector because the same three-coordinate restrictions apply to every particle. A failed test rejects. A successful arbitrary input has unnormalized state Pi_A rho Pi_A, so its acceptance is its trace times a valid-state acceptance. Approximate projections are controlled before conditioning, uniformly in input; there is no division by a small acceptance probability.
* Circuit size: each coordinate register has log q bits. Scalar arithmetic, kernel summation, QFT, sign-controlled block permutation, and uniform factorial digits use polynomially many gates in L and log inverse accuracy. Uniform permutation is implemented by decoding digits, not by looping over N! group elements. The kinetic value may be clamped off the accepted band, ensuring all approximate test effects remain bounded.
* Thresholds: with -C<=E<=0, b>0/a>=0 excludes NO, and a<-C/b<=-C excludes YES. Remaining a,b are polynomially bounded; hence Q_* and gamma^(-1) are polynomial. g/2-2eta>=0.48g, so ideal margin >=0.12g/Q_*>gamma. Measurement errors <=gamma/10 retain the stated margin.
* Amplification soundness: tensor-product acceptance effects commute across registers; diagonalizing each one turns even an entangled witness's maximum acceptance into the maximum classical Bernoulli-majority eigenvalue. This is bounded using the individual operator-norm soundness bound. No independent-copies promise on Merlin is used.

Known boundary: the total nuclear charge must be polynomially bounded for these constants. This is automatic for the frozen explicit unit-nucleus list. Extending to exponentially large binary charges is not proved and is not part of the frozen target. The result supplies no classical state construction and no binding claim.
