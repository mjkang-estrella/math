# Discovery round 17: no nomination after the mechanism screen

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0.

**PRE_FREEZE_SCREEN_UNPAID / counted=false.** No full quantified theorem
was frozen, no substantial result is nominated, and no independent proof
or contribution verdict is claimed. The campaign's ten-result objective
is unchanged. This report does not count its supporting obstructions as
a substitute result.

The most developed distinct screen was family 089, constant-distortion
L1 embeddings for planar and bounded-treewidth graph metrics. The actual
planar companion already combines its theorem, the bounded-treewidth
theorem and Lee--Sidiropoulos to cover each fixed almost-embeddable family.
Fixed genus, apices and bounded vortices therefore do not supply a new
deduction. The remaining clique-sum operation is a classical named open
closure problem, not a target made substantial merely by removing a
source hypothesis.

The concrete operation investigated was construction of consistent cut
laws for trees of large planar pieces with bounded clique interfaces.
The finite-state transport part can use separator assignments. The hard
part is a compatible reservoir that simultaneously has uniform edge
upper bounds, preserves boundary laws, creates within-piece separation,
and retains conditional uncertainty through arbitrary depths. The source
achieves these jointly when entire bags have bounded cardinality. A
bound on interface cardinality alone has not paid them.

The self-contained proofs in `interface-obstructions.md` establish:

1. A fixed finite number of uniformly Lipschitz scalar particles with a
   uniform expected lower bound would imply uniformly bounded Hilbert
   distortion even for planar diamonds, contradicting an explicit
   square-sum lower bound sqrt(h+1).
2. A chain of K4 bags admits perfectly compatible symmetric cut laws,
   each exactly isometric on its bag, whose Markov gluing forces
   four-periodic labels and collapses vertices at graph distance two.
3. Even arbitrarily small subtraction of a cut metric can violate a
   tight triangle inequality, preventing the proposed exact boundary
   calibration by residual lengths.
4. The L1-valued particle repair has no fixed finite weak-order
   pigeonhole principle; coordinate-dependent pair selection, proposed
   to circumvent that problem, admits exact linear norm growth despite
   norm-one incoming perturbations.

These refute particular operations, not the full closure conjecture.
Neither the source theorem nor a full new theorem is refuted. The screen
did not find a tractable replacement reservoir. In accordance with the
brief, no famous closure conjecture is frozen merely because it remains
unpaid, and no bounded subclass or conditional bridge is nominated.
