# Working semantic audit: not a final verdict

The first captured implementation has 262 lines; snapshot/hash provenance and the working-build failures are recorded separately. These statements concern that snapshot only. Final bytes must be reread and compiled before any verdict.

Sampling is a finite sum of actual complex AddChar functions. character_parseval assumes injectivity of the selected character family and derives the normalized discrete inner product identity using mathlib's actual finite-character orthogonality, which was inspected at its definition and proof. It does not assume the advertised Parseval conclusion. The finite group is nonempty by its group structure. Empty frequency index types are allowed but do not make the universally quantified result vacuous; independent controls use singleton and two-frequency examples.

phase_parseval explicitly assumes star(u_i)*u_i=1. discrete_translation is the actual AddChar map-add identity; its shift lies in the finite group. A continuous torus translation's complex exponential phase and its unit modulus are not identified in Lean by this snapshot. Likewise normalized finite-group inner product is not itself the physical torus L2 integral or the h^(D/2) sampling map. Product-grid instantiation, continuous finite-band Fourier expansion, volume normalization, Lipschitz estimates and shifted-cell averaging remain human interfaces unless later declarations explicitly add them.

mode uses the standard additive character of ZMod q and multiplication shift. Its injectivity is proved from evaluation at1 and stdAddChar injectivity. grid_parseval exposes the frequency-map injection. initial_modes_injective proves the actual interval embedding when K<=q; centered_modes_injective translates an interval of length2K+1 and requires that length<=q. The q=0 case of the standalone injectivity statements is harmless (initial K=0 only, or impossible centered premise); grid Parseval separately requires NeZero q. The manuscript's q>4K is stronger than the natural finite-grid no-alias bound, and needs no new arithmetic mechanism. A control constructs real aliases when five centered frequencies are placed modulo3.

The group projector is an algebraic average of a genuine Representation twisted by a genuine multiplicative character into complex units. The inverse character coefficient is correct for projection onto the chi eigenspace. Mathlib averageMap/invariant/id identities were inspected. The implementation derives eigen-range, fixed vectors, idempotence and commutation with an intertwining linear map. No inner-product structure is present; these theorems cannot be advertised as proving self-adjointness, contractivity or an orthogonal quantum measurement. That omission is consistent with the current statements. A later orthogonality claim must introduce and use suitable unitarity hypotheses.

complexSign is an actual homomorphism built from permutation sign and integer-to-complex units. signProjector_eigen specializes the character result to the permutation group, but the abstract Representation has not been instantiated as a permutation of physical particle/spin registers. The still-developing inverse-sign/apply formula has the correct mathematical statement. An independent control uses the trivial S_2 action: trivial-character projection fixes vectors, while sign-character projection annihilates them.

The scalar interfaces are conditional arithmetic support. total_energy_transfer takes four actual error inequalities as premises; it does not prove Hardy, IMS, cap, Ritz or quadrature estimates. yes_margin/no_margin expose positive scale and threshold separation. approximation_yes/no use the correct respective sides of the absolute error. subnormalized_no correctly requires an upper bound1 on surviving weight and nonnegative normalized acceptance. Its lower bound0 on the weight is redundant for this upper inequality but appropriate probability semantics, not a defect. subnormalized_approximation_no bounds the unconditioned accepted probability directly; it never divides by a success weight. No quantum state, instrument construction, all-register circuit approximation or majority amplification is formalized by these scalar facts.

Independent controls are drafted to show actual alias collision changes squared norm, nonunit phase changes energy, the centered endpoint is nonvacuously injective, sign/trivial sectors differ, and missing failure-weight/nonnegative-probability restrictions permit false conclusions. They are not yet reported as compiled. No false variant has yet been counted as a mathematical rejection.
