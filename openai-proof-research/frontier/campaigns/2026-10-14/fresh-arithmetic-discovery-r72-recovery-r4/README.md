# fresh-arithmetic-discovery-r72 — recovery r4

Outcome: **T242Z unresolved.** No full proof, no counterexample, no
nominee, no count claim.

T242Z asks for single-fold Diophantine representations of every r.e.
S ⊆ ℤ^n with all unknowns in ℤ. Source242 proves the ℕ-version and states it
makes no claim over ℤ. Given the source, T242Z is equivalent to (N_ℤ), a
single-fold definition of ℕ in ℤ (Theorem R). Every route tried here stops
at a single-fold positivity certificate over ℤ; the route limits are proved
as Lemmas O1–O5.

Files:

- `scope-freeze.md`: frozen statement, source dependency, honest chronology.
- `screening.md`: families screened and why 242 was chosen.
- `reduction-and-routes.md`: F1, F3, Theorem R, route attempts A1–A10,
  Lemmas O1–O5, status and remaining gap.
- `reading-limits.md`: exactly what was read, and novelty limits.
- `source-receipts.json`: pinned upstream blobs and SHA-256.
- `primary-identities.json`: outside sources (navigation only).
- `status.json`: machine-readable status.
- `tools/fetch_pinned.py`: pinned fetch with blob check (used once).
- `tools/controls.py`: exact finite controls (not proofs).
- `tools/make_manifest.py`, `tools/verify.py`: manifest and read-only
  verifier. Run `python3 -B tools/verify.py <manifest-sha256> [--controls]`
  from this directory, with the hash taken from the parent report, not
  from `MANIFEST.sha256`.
- `private/`: local copies of upstream files and of CCO24; not for
  redistribution.
