The provisional question investigated is: does there exist an absolute C>0 such that for every integer d>=0, every red/blue edge-coloring of the complete graph on ceil(C*3^d) vertices contains a monochromatic ordinary subgraph isomorphic to P_3 Cartesian-power d? P_3 has three vertices; P_3 Cartesian-power 0 is a single vertex. Colors, dimension and host are unrestricted within these quantifiers. This is recorded after exploration as a scope-status record, not a retrospectively claimed prior freeze. No completed full proof was assembled. Neither a conditional discrepancy theorem, a bounded-dimensional restriction, nor an extraction obstruction is nominated as a replacement.
