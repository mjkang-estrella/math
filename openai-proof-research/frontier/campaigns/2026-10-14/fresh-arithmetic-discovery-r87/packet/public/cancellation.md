# Cancellation: the actual old weighted-rigidity interface

Prefreeze screen only. The old r74 fivefold target is neither restarted nor narrowed. No result here is nominated.

Source047's `prop:extracted-lnd` does not merely ask for an additive action. Assuming the source fourfold polynomial, it extracts a nonzero locally nilpotent derivation E of T=C[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1), homogeneous for weights(1,1,-1,-1,0), with shift e≤0 and E²(v)=0, where v=a³b-a²u³-d². Its two branches handle fixed and moving tau; in the moving case, LND order proves local nilpotence after division by the common tau/V factor. `prop:rigidity` uses the commuting auxiliary grading(0,3,-3,0,2), the whole polynomial orbit of N=d²+a²u³, Mason–Stothers, an original-field nonsquare, and the intersection C[a,d,u][a^-1]∩C[a,d,u][d^-1]. It concludes that the residual derivation would have a coefficient of negative fiber weight in a ring with only nonnegative weights. E_+=a∂_b+d∂_c has positive shift2 and E_+²(v)=0, so the sign is an essential operative hypothesis.

The actual r74 research and history-correction already develop f=u³+t⁷ with t an additional variable, not a scalar parameter. The source's construction, stable-coordinate compiler, modified filtration and determinant reduction are already compatible there. The old unresolved problem is to exclude the resulting nonpositive-shift derivation on T=C[a,d,b,c,u,t]/(ac-bd-1) with E²(a³b-a²f-d²)=0. Auxiliary weights(0,21,-21,0,14,6) reduce it to E'²(N)=0 for N=d²+a²(u³+t⁷). The orbit retains gcd(A(q),D(q))=1, but a and d may move. Fixing them permits the old Mason argument; the unrestricted step is unproved.

The strongest actual primary reduction is Freudenburg–Moser-Jauslin, *Locally nilpotent derivations of rings with roots adjoined*, [author PDF](https://moser.perso.math.cnrs.fr/adjoinedroots.pdf), Lemma4.1 and Theorem4.1. For a root r^k=N, E'²(N)=0 gives the lifted locally nilpotent derivation with restriction r^(k-1)E' and Dr=E'N/k. The primary proof handles the kernel/degree calculation, not only a formal derivation on the quotient. Theorems8.1–8.2 prove stable rigidity for the (2,3,7) Brieskorn surface using relative primality, LND orders and Mason's inequality. These actual proofs were read. They do not imply that an arbitrary E' preserves N or a, so they do not justify restricting the derivation to N=0 or localizing at a before proving invariance. In r74's k=5 bookkeeping the lifted shift is8+5e≤8, whereas the manifest positive action has shift10. That is the old precise weighted problem, not solved by stable rigidity of one divisor.

A further feasibility calculation checks a tempting birational substitute. Put B=T[r]/(r^k-N) for odd k≥3, in its fraction field. The original N is prime in the a-chart, so the usual N-adic Eisenstein argument supplies the field extension; equivalently the following inverse rational maps identify its function field directly. Let

    z=d/a,   F=z²+u³+t⁷,   s=r^((k+1)/2)/a.

Then s²=rF, and hence

    r=s²/F,
    a=s^k/F^((k+1)/2),
    d=az,
    c=a^-1+bz.

These identities are inverse on the nonempty open set where the denominators are nonzero. Conversely substitute independent s,z,u,t,b into them: ac-bd=1 and r^k=a²F=d²+a²(u³+t⁷). Thus Frac(B)=C(s,z,u,t,b) is rational. In particular a proposed obstruction based on non-uniruledness of the WHOLE root cover cannot be read off from the rigid Brieskorn divisor.

This rational parametrization gives no regular additive action on B: denominators involve a and F, whose invariance is precisely unavailable. It does not refute the full old cancellation target or the sign-sensitive LND obstruction. It only removes a proposed birational shortcut. No limited fixed-coordinate repair, root-cover counterexample, or renamed fivefold theorem is put forward.
