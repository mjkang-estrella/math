## 050: trace compactness, actual duality, and an effective gap

Source050, *Ample rank-two bundles on the quadric surface without Griffiths-positive metrics*, gives G on S=P¹×P¹, P=O(1,1), power maps f_m, and ample E_m=f_m^*G⊗P for all m≥1, with no Griffiths-positive smooth Hermitian metric for all sufficiently large m. The entire `prop:limit` and `prop:obstruction` proofs were read. The former traces root-twisted metrics over the finite power map, uses global supremum normalization, coefficient bounds and psh submean noncollapse, and reconstructs a finite nonzero nonnegative psh Hermitian quadratic on every fiber by radial convolution and polarization. It does not take a weak limit and then assert unrestricted trace to a divisor. The latter uses the two line subbundles, stationary values along P¹ fibers, the SAME graph restrictions, invariant seminorms under order-three projective transformations, curvature measure, expanding annuli, and the no-common-fixed-line contradiction.

The actual r15 §§2–3 already make the stronger small-curvature and linear-twist comparison. In particular there is δ>0 such that semipositive metrics on f_m^*G⊗P^a are impossible whenever a/m<δ (with the precise nonnegative integer conventions stated there). This is the source compactness argument with exponent a/m tending to0, not an unpaid parameter-uniform bridge. Actual structuralr29 proves the rank-four P² consequence through finite duality/double-cover kernel exact sequence, ampleness, pullback/quotient curvature and the same fixed-twist limit. These exact bodies were read; their author ratings are not used as mathematical verdicts here.

I next explored an explicit or effective positive lower bound for the ratio a/m. Two newer primary interfaces were checked in their proof bodies:

* Du–Xie, [2609.26504v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26504v1), Propositions2.1–2.3. A fixed background metric identifies horizontal Levi with a linear operator L_J on Hermitian endomorphisms. If a smooth separable Q has L_J^*Q≤−ηI, then integration against every positive candidate metric is strictly negative, contradicting its required nonnegative Levi pairing. Their C¹ connection-difference estimate for the adjoint makes this certificate stable. The exact tensor cone, noncommuting trace inequality and integration-by-parts estimate were read; no only-formal adjoint claim is used.
* Du–Xie, [2609.26528v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26528v1), Theorem1.1, Proposition2.1 and the complete Section2 proof. For positive twists and arbitrary Hermitian coefficient matrices B, the first-jet map is Ψ_m=e^(−mφ)(r_m r_m^*−mκ p_m p_m^*). Level success is 0 outside conv Ψ_m(K). Failure at one level has an atomic probability certificate with at most N_m²+1 points. Fubini–Study multiplication makes the spaces nested; C² density and compactness give a nonzero positive matrix-valued measure T with D^*T=0 if EVERY level fails. This is a full theorem of alternatives for strict positivity, not an unproved abstract separation heuristic.

These are stronger reductions than a bare absence-of-invariant-measure argument. They nevertheless do not close the proposed EFFECTIVE quantitative task. The second theorem detects strict positivity at some finite level; its negative direction passes to a weak limit from failures at ALL levels and supplies no finite stopping index. Its measure annihilator is not automatically the smooth strictly negative adjoint certificate in the first paper. The first paper constructs such a certificate for its own split model and deformations, not for arbitrary G. No theorem that all failures of nonzero psh Hermitian quadratics admit a specified computable smooth certificate is inferred.

Likewise, finite separation of the invariant-probability problem on P¹ does not itself control the discrepancy of the traced approximate quadratics restricted to the three graph curves. No finite-jet truncation or successful numerical test can replace it. The actual stronger primary was read after initial feasibility, before sealing; no freeze ever occurred. No effective bound, generic robustness theorem, or conditional certificate is nominated.
