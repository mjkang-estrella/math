# Pre-freeze author exploration: exact-width expander frame

This is a proposed mechanism, not a frozen theorem or independent review. Full comparison and source reading remain in progress.

Input colored Y has maximum degree3 and at most64 vertices in each named sort. Two versions have the same nonempty sort cardinalities. Source133 supplies such Y before its triangle/path uncoloring. Use m=max(2,ceil(sqrt(s))) and label m² stations by Z_m², assigning the s sorts injectively. Four permutations are (x+2y,y), (x+2y+1,y), (x,y+2x), (x,y+2x+1), all modulo m. Their undirected arc multigraph has degree8 and classical edge expansion at least (8-5sqrt2)/2>2/5. Loops/multiple arcs are retained as distinct arc records.

For each directed arc v->T_i v, i=0,1,2,3, replace it by v--a_(v,i)--b_(v,i)--T_i v, using distinct new vertices. Give a type degree30+2i and b type degree31+2i. Each original Y vertex is attached to the station of its sort and has target degree20. Stations have target degree100, except the root (0,0) with degree101. Achieve every target degree by attaching the required number of private degree1 leaves. No target deficit is negative: station base degree8+fiber size<=72, Y vertex base degree<=4, arc internal base degree2. Dummy stations have no fiber. The result is simple even for loop arcs, connected, uncolored, and maximum degree101.

Degree distinguishes main vertices, stations/root, all port types and leaves. Under any winning bijective game strategy with at least3 slots, each station must map to the same station: retain it, mark the uniquely recognized root, and walk a deterministic directed/inverse generator word from the root to the station, retaining at most the endpoint plus current/next walker. Every step is forced by adjacency and degree type. Equality at the endpoint forces its identity. Thus a main vertex's sort is recovered by its unique station neighbor. This uses hypothetical continuations, so is valid with all slots occupied, as in source133's recognition proof. Adjacency between main vertices is exactly original Y adjacency. Four slots trace the source sort-prescribed shared-readout walk, retaining its two endpoints.

For source block-offset maps, assign station/its leaves to its source sort's block; assign dummy stations and arc interiors to any fixed block (or by arc tail's block). All frame maps are the identity on the common frame labels. Original vertex maps are the source colored translations; leaves at a vertex map by local index. Degree preservation needs explicit checking: internal block incidences are preserved; external anchor incidences are fixed and every proper-channel root has exactly one matching neighbor. Thus leaf counts are invariant under each block translation. Compatible-offset union maps preserve all frame edges, fiber attachments and source edges. No joint choice of multiple new offsets is needed for the announced whole bijection. The source realization interface then applies unchanged at r>=8, K=r+1.

Expansion calculation: put in each station cell its station/leaves, source fiber vertices/leaves, and the two internal vertices/leaves of its four outgoing arc paths. Each cell is connected and has at most C=2048 vertices (indeed a crude bound is101+64*20+260). Between cells the arc-end edges realize the base multigraph, with degree bound d=8; extra Y edges can only help. Given 0<|S|<=|V|/2, let I,J,M be full, empty and mixed cells. Each mixed cell contributes a distinct internal cut edge. If |M|>=h|S|/(4Cd), this already gives the desired bound. Otherwise, since h=2/5 and d=8, |I|,|J|>=|S|/C-|M|>=|S|/(2C). Base expansion yields at least h|S|/(2C) edges from I to its complement; at most d|M| hit mixed cells. Hence I-J edges give at least h|S|/(4C), which is stronger. Thus h_out-edge>=h/(4Cd)=1/163840; the safer rational1/200000 suffices.

Possible full consequence: the language of equal-positive-order uncolored simple graph pairs with maximum degree<=101 and edge expansion>=1/200000, together with binary k>=2, satisfying joint k-WL equivalence, is EXPTIME-complete. Reject malformed or out-of-class inputs. Upper bound can enumerate all cuts in exponential time and invoke the source dimension-capping/WL upper bound. Hardness would use the source computation compiler through its colored Y stage and this frame. Output order is O(number of sorts), instead of quadratic path-label overhead; this size improvement is incidental, not a separate result. Source theorem truth is stipulated; actual low-level interfaces must still be fully inventoried.

A more general exact-width compiler for arbitrary bounded-degree bounded-sort-size colored graphs may be provable by a virtual-parent pebble strategy (leaves point to original parents; frame fixed), but that broader statement is not currently claimed and is unnecessary for the source-specific consequence.
