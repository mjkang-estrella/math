Its mixed-character product Delta is integral at odd primes by the local cyclic descent and at every remaining prime because that scalar is a unit. Kronecker then makes Delta torsion, and residue-one comparisons force odd order. The final first-order mod2 contradiction is essential.

This observation was not assembled as a final theorem or independently reviewed and is not nominated.

For an arbitrary larger2-primary factor, division by2 leaves extra powers of2 in the scalar norms. The source's integrality proof at primes over2 no longer applies: those character values need not be units there. Thus the mixed-character products are not proved integral or torsion. No higher-order2-adic coupling argument was found. This is a missing interface, not a counterexample to the larger Hadamard-group question or to every extension of the source.
