Author-side disposition: NO NOMINEE, not an independent review. The potentially unpaid operation would be a thermal, depletion-scale version of the pinned long-wavelength deletion/RMS estimate, together with an occupation-sensitive local thermal comparison for the exact hard-core model. It is not proved here. No claim that the target is false, open in all literature, or eligible for the campaign follows from the failure of these routes.

No Lean work was started. A sensible limited formalization would encode the three-level spectra/occupations, the oscillator discriminant identity, and the scalar asymptotic bounds in the single-mode example. This would not formalize interacting hard-core Gibbs states, the thermal free-energy comparison, or the desired universal limit. Those mathematical interfaces must be completed before a full proof/formal plan is appropriate.
