# AGM063: fixed full-scope candidate

Status: author candidate for independent proof and substantial-contribution review. No acceptance, new campaign count, complete formalization, or publication is claimed.

Let X be any smooth connected complex projective Fano manifold of positive dimension n. Write r=ρ(X) for its actual Picard number and i=ι(X) for its pseudoindex, the minimum anticanonical degree of an irreducible rational curve. Suppose

    r(i−1) ≥ n−1.

Then X is isomorphic to one of the following varieties, with the displayed restrictions:

1. (P^(i−1))^r.
2. Q^i × (P^(i−1))^(r−1), with i≥3.
3. P_(P^i)(O^(⊕(i−1)) ⊕ O(1)) × (P^(i−1))^(r−2), with r≥2 and i≥2.
4. P_(P^i)(T_(P^i)) × (P^(i−1))^(r−2), with r≥2 and i≥2.
5. P^i × (P^(i−1))^(r−1), with r≥2 and i≥2.

Q^i is a smooth complex quadric of dimension i. Projectivization uses one-dimensional quotients. A zero product exponent means a point. No negative exponent is allowed. Every listed variety with the stated restrictions has the stated actual Picard number and pseudoindex, is Fano, and satisfies the hypothesis. Type 1 has n=r(i−1); the other four have n=r(i−1)+1.

This is the full advanced generalized Mukai classification in terms of the actual invariants. It has no dimension, Picard-number, symmetry, extremal-contraction, or pre-existing covering-family restriction. The equality case is already the pinned source's theorem and is credited to it. The proposed new conclusion is the entire defect-one range.

Boundary conventions are substantive:

* The hypothesis itself forces i≥2: if i=1, n≤1, whereas the only positive-dimensional Fano curve is P^1 and has i=2.
* If r=1, the list is P^n for n≥1 and Q^n for n≥3. Q^2 has actual Picard number two and belongs to type 1 with i=2; it is not a defect-one r=1 case.
* In dimension one the only case is P^1. In dimension two the only cases are P^2 and P^1×P^1; neither has defect one.
* For i=2, the quadric type is absent at the stated actual r. The defect-one list consists of the two P^1-bundles over P^2 times (P^1)^(r−2), and P^2×(P^1)^(r−1), all with r≥2. In particular the nontrivial bundle type is retained; the statement does not assert that every minimal family is covering.
* The mixed product requires r≥2 because P^i alone has pseudoindex i+1.
