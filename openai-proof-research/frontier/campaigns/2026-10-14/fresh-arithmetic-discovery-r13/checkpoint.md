## Actual mathematical checkpoint: a barrier to naïve undecidability

The general source realization theorem is for every finitely presented k-algebra R, central idempotent e and unital endomorphism alpha, with H(Y)=alpha^*(eY). The source produces a fixed bounded bimodule complex with tensor action M(H(Y)) + M(H(Y))[3]. A tempting new direction was an undecidability theorem for the finite projective dimension of these encoded modules. Mere arbitrary extinction lengths do not establish it.

Here is a concrete obstruction to that direction. Let k be algebraically closed and let Rep_d(R) denote the affine scheme of d-dimensional representations of finitely presented R. Its connected-component set is finite. For fixed d, H admits a deterministic transition on the connected-component states of all Rep_r(R), 0<=r<=d. Consequently every H-extinction time in dimension<=d is at most

    C_d = sum_{r=1}^d # pi_0(Rep_r(R)).

This is a screen lemma, not a claimed substantial discovery.

Proof: On a connected component C of Rep_r(R), the idempotent matrix representing e has constant rank s, and its image forms a rank-s vector bundle E on C. Because e is central, alpha(R) preserves E, yielding the family H. Its frame bundle is a GL_s torsor over C, hence connected (GL_s is connected and the bundle is Zariski locally trivial). The frame bundle maps to Rep_s(R); its image lies in a unique connected component. This assigns the transition independently of the representation and frame. If s=0, take the zero state. Each application H follows this finite deterministic graph. A trajectory ending at zero cannot repeat a nonzero state; otherwise it would cycle forever. There are at most C_d nonzero states. This proves the bound.

The conclusion uses connected components, not irreducible components, and uses the frame bundle to avoid choosing global bases. It holds for endomorphisms, not just automorphisms. It does not assert that H's entire isomorphism-class dynamics is finite or periodic. Only its connected-component state and extinction are controlled. Over effective algebraic-number input one can in principle compute components and transition maps using polynomial algorithms; that effectivity claim has not yet been developed into an independently sourced theorem and is not relied on here.

## Consequence for this screen
Any naïve attempt to encode unrestricted halting into this particular central-idempotent selection process has to overcome the finite-component transition mechanism. This is a paid barrier, not evidence of a new undecidability theorem. Field changes (replace central involutions by p-torsion and averaging in a different characteristic), ordinary derived-category consequences, and the source's direct source-to-bimodule realization are expected technical transfers unless a genuinely additional operation is proved.
