# Feasible scoped formal support; no formal verdict

No Lean code or formal theorem has been produced in this discovery packet. The full mathematical proof is candidate-008.md. The following is a proposed honest division of labor for a later independently audited formal task, conditional on the parent advancing the candidate.

1. **Weighted words and cut.** Words are lists of natural odd integers≥3; weight is list sum. For 0<m≤T and wt(w)=T, prove a unique decomposition w=a++[d]++v with wt(a)<m≤wt(a)+d. Define r=m−wt(a)>0 and s=T−m−wt(v)≥0, and prove r+s=d. Include equality at the cut and letters of weight greater than m.
2. **Concrete states and edges.** Use the disjoint finite subtypes P(a,r),Q(v,s) in candidate §2. Define the three actual transitions; prove their integer-level increment is exactly d, that only P-to-Q crossing is possible, and that every path has total weight at most T. A formalization of just these scalar inequalities must be labelled support, not formal faithfulness.
3. **Path uniqueness.** Induct on the prefix length, then suffix length, to prove a path from the extracting states has exactly the word a++[r+s]++v and has multiplicity one. This should be proved for the concrete transition relation, not assumed as an abstract separation hypothesis. One generator per allowed weight is a load-bearing hypothesis.
4. **Matrix/path interface.** Relate products of the actual finite 0/1 matrices to sums over labelled paths using the stated row-to-column convention. Derive the exact delta extracting entry. Coefficient extraction then proves top homogeneous injectivity over any field. A proof over Boolean matrices alone would not establish linear faithfulness.
5. **Three caps.** Prove the elementary residue lemma: for every 0≤k≤N, N≥3, there is j≥0 with k+3j in {N−2,N−1,N}. Prove right concatenation injective and connect the exact matrix homomorphism to top detection. Grade-separated entries must be handled explicitly for inhomogeneous polynomials; no assumption that homogeneous faithfulness implies total faithfulness is permissible.
6. **Counts.** Formalize the two finite state-count sums and the recurrence for a_j. The geometric bounds can be supported separately by real-number lemmas for rho>1 satisfying rho³=rho+1. Constants need not be optimized.

A realistic scoped first artifact could cover items1–3, the residue lemma and exact count identities, with matrix linearization, free-Lie/PBW, source scalar extension, Witt inversion and the asymptotic limit explicitly outside formal scope. A stronger artifact could include item4/5; it must not be advertised before it compiles and is audited. The source freeness theorem is a stipulated mathematical input, never a newly formalized theorem of this project. Finite diagnostic outputs are not formal support.

Adversarial formal checks should mutate gap signs, permit a Q-to-P transition, reverse suffix concatenation, or remove the single-generator-per-weight premise. A genuinely concrete extracted-entry theorem must reject those changes. No tests should merely instantiate a theorem whose hypotheses already assume the desired conclusion.
