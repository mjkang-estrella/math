# Pre-freeze screen: lex-plus-powers Betti rigidity

Status: FALSE PROVISIONAL ASSERTION; no target frozen, no nomination. The parent authorized continuing a distinct mechanism within r28 after preserving this obstruction.

Provisional assertion (never frozen): over every characteristic-zero field, for any homogeneous ideal containing a regular sequence of prescribed degrees and its Hilbert-matching lex-plus-powers ideal J, equality of total quotient Betti numbers at one positive index forces equality at all higher indices.

## Exact obstruction

Let S=Q[x1,x2,x3,x4], P=(x1²,x2²,x3²,x4²),
I=P+(x1x2,x1x3,x2x4,x3x4), and
J=P+(x1x2,x1x3,x1x4,x2x3).
The surviving squarefree edges for I are {x1x4,x2x3}; those for J are {x2x4,x3x4}. In either quotient every cubic vanishes, and the Hilbert function is (1,4,2,0,...). The four squarefree quadrics added to J are precisely the lexicographically largest squarefree quadrics, so J is exactly the prescribed lex-plus-powers ideal, with all degrees a_i=2. The four squares form a regular sequence.

Each ideal is minimally generated by its eight displayed quadrics. Hence beta_1(S/I)=beta_1(S/J)=8, even degree by degree. The socle of S/I is exactly the two-dimensional degree-two space: a degree-one linear combination killed by all variables must have zero coefficient at every vertex, since each vertex has a surviving incident edge. For S/J, x1 is also annihilated by every variable, while x2,x3,x4 are not; testing multiplication by x2 and x3 and then x4 rules out any further linear combination. Its socle is therefore spanned by x1,x2x4,x3x4. Top Koszul homology identifies Tor_4^S(B,Q) with Soc(B)(-4). Consequently beta_4(S/I)=2 and beta_4(S/J)=3. This is a characteristic-independent elementary counterexample to the provisional assertion, not a counterexample to any stipulated source theorem.

The executable diagnostic also gives complete quotient Betti totals (1,8,14,9,2) and (1,8,15,11,3), using exact rational ranks for this witness. These extra numbers are computational confirmation; the preceding socle proof alone disproves the assertion.

## Actual operations and earlier work

I226 already proves integral consecutive cancellation from the source division-coefficient comparison and the Mermin–Murai monomial comparison. That result is not new here. If Delta_p,j=c_p,j+c_(p+1),j, equality at p only forces these two adjacent cancellation counts to vanish; it does not kill counts farther in the tail. Here the differences are Delta_(2,4)=Delta_(3,4)=1 and Delta_(3,5)=Delta_(4,5)=1, realized by c_(3,4)=c_(4,5)=1. Neither touches homological index1.

Conca–Herzog–Hibi, Rigid resolutions and big Betti numbers, arXiv:math/0306236, Theorem2.3, Corollaries2.4 and2.7: equality with the generic-annihilator bound propagates via annihilation of partial Koszul homology for every subset of a generic sequence. Ordinary lex propagation additionally uses strongly stable ideals, inequalities of generator counts by maximal variable, and the Eliahou–Kervaire formula. It is not a statement for lex-plus-powers ideals. The source's filtered associated-graded rank comparison supplies adjacent differential rank inequalities, and its bounded-box endpoint proof compares rank deficits of submodules and quotients; neither states the generic-annihilator equality needed by CHH. The explicit example rules out obtaining it uniformly in this setting. Ordinary componentwise linearity also cannot be presumed: I is generated in degree2 yet has beta_(2,4)(S/I)=2.
