# T023P55 — exact full working question

For every fixed nonzero integer m, with O=Z[omega], primary generators congruent to 1 modulo 3, and G normalized exactly as source023, prove unconditionally

    sum_{primary prime pi, N(pi)<=X} G(pi)^(3m) = o_m(X^(5/6)/log X)

as X tends to infinity. Both conjugate prime ideals are included; there is no exceptional set of X, no averaging over m, no GRH, and no replacement of 5/6 by 1. Negative powers are well-defined since |G(pi)|=1.

Status at freeze: a working question, not a claimed theorem. This is explicitly a remaining case of Patterson's complementary conjecture, already stated in Dunn–Radziwill v3 (1.9), with a smoothed conditional result in their Theorem 1.3. No novelty is claimed for the question. Preliminary source reading identifies an exact analytic obstruction and a possible dispersion route to test. There is presently no credible complete proof. Freeze occurs after that exploration and before assembling the two proof attempts; it does not retrospectively describe the exploration as a proof.

The target will remain unchanged if either route fails. A logarithmic-saving or smoothed/conditional substitute will not be nominated.
