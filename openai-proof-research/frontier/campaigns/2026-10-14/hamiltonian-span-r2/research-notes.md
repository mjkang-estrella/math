# Mathematical checkpoint: the C4 rank kernel and exchange obstruction

Myeongjin Kang; no affiliation. Original prose CC BY 4.0. 2026-10-16.

H remains UNRESOLVED. These are technical deductions for the full attempt,
not a smaller-result nomination.

Let G have n vertices, e,f the allowed GSW expansion edges, and
F=G-{e,f}. Write q0,q1,q2,q3 for the new square cyclically, r_i=q_iq_(i+1),
s_i=t_iq_i for its spokes. The old terminals t0,t1,t2,t3 are respectively
u,v,y,x in the GSW convention. The four L_i traces use all ring edges
except r_i and spokes s_i,s_(i+1). P uses r1,r3 and all spokes; its exterior
paths pair (t0,t1),(t2,t3). Q uses r0,r2 and all spokes; its exterior paths
pair (t0,t3),(t1,t2). Planarity excludes the crossing third pairing.

The six gadget vectors gamma_i have affine rank four and unique affine
relation gamma_0+gamma_2-gamma_1-gamma_3-gamma_P+gamma_Q=0.
Let S_i be the complete exterior path-cover family for each trace. Choose
z_i in S_i. Define W=sum_i span(S_i-z_i), and
 d=z_0+z_2-z_1-z_3-z_P+z_Q.
Then
 dim aff HC(G+) = 4 + dim(W+R d).
Indeed projection to the gadget has rank four. Its kernel on the cycle
difference space is generated by differences within each fiber and the
unique affine relation between representatives. All the kernel vectors
vanish on the gadget, so their exterior coordinates identify the kernel.
Changing representatives changes d only by W. Each vector is in ker B_F.
F is connected since G is 3-edge-connected, and rank B_F=n-1. Thus
 dim ker B_F=(3n/2-2)-(n-1)=n/2-1.
Full H on G+ is exactly W+R d=ker B_F. Nonempty fibers alone pay only the
four gadget dimensions, not this remaining identity.

The source P4 statement pays every nonempty fiber. A ring P4 forces each
L_i trace. The path t_i-q_i-q_(i+1)-t_(i+1) forces the matching trace
containing r_i: the two adjacent ring edges are excluded at its endpoints,
so the opposite ring edge is forced. Its exterior is a two-path cover,
whose pairing must be opposite to the internal matching to give one cycle.

Failed integer exchange, with a universal proof of failure:
A Hamiltonian C of F gives a disconnected spanning 2-factor D=C union Q4
in G+. Its gadget coordinates equal gamma_0+gamma_2-gamma_P, also
gamma_1+gamma_3-gamma_Q. It was tempting to seek those same three-term
identities on the exterior. This is impossible in a bipartite Pfaffian
graph, independent of the exterior.
Choose a Pfaffian orientation and let epsilon_e be 1 for edges oriented
from the second bipartition class to the first, 0 otherwise. A conformal
cycle of length 2k has epsilon-sum k-1 mod 2: in an alternating traversal,
the number of aligned edges has parity k+sum epsilon, and is odd. Each
component of a spanning 2-factor is conformal (the remaining even cycles
have perfect matchings). Hence for every spanning 2-factor D,
 epsilon.D = |V|/2 - c(D) (mod 2).
All Hamiltonian vectors have the parity |V|/2-1; D with two components has
the opposite parity. Integer affine combinations preserve that parity.
Multiplication by an odd common denominator gives the same contradiction
for a rational certificate with odd common denominator. This is an
integral obstruction only; real H is not contradicted.

A half-integral six-trace candidate also fails on the expanded cube:
 D=(H_L0+H_L1+H_L2+H_L3-H_P-H_Q)/2.
There are eight total Hamiltonian cycles in the diagnostic (six trace
class sizes 1,1,1,1,1,3). They are affinely independent. In that order,
D's unique affine coefficients are
 (1/4,3/4,1/4,3/4,-1/4,-1/4,-1/4,-1/4).
The frozen exact certificate will include complete edge supports.
The denominator four is forced, so even arbitrary half-integral affine
certificates fail for this D. It does lie in the REAL affine hull.

A useful caution against bounded-denominator repairs follows already from
the paid even-prism basis. For P_m with even m, let L_i be the two-rung
cycles and Z0,Z1 the two all-rung zigzags. The two separate ring cycles D
have the unique affine representation
 D=(sum_i L_i-Z0-Z1)/(m-2).
For every ring edge the numerator has coefficient (m-1)-1=m-2; each rung
has coefficient 2-2=0; the affine coefficients sum to one. Independence
is the prism basis argument in the authenticated r6 proof, checked again
for this use. The exact classification of all prism Hamiltonian cycles
is also needed for uniqueness and is supplied there. Thus no fixed common
denominator works for all such integral degree-two vectors, even within
H-valid graphs. This does not preclude a real rank proof or compositions
of local rational identities.
