## 7. An exact lifting criterion, and where it applies

  m^s subset J_U(f)+m^{s+1},

then it establishes m^s subset J_U(f) in B_h. Proof: for A=B_h/J_U(f), the image m^s A is a finite A-module and the displayed inclusion gives m^s A=m(m^s A). Nakayama makes that module zero. Thus in the m-primary case, simultaneous degree-s monomial containment at precision s+1 is an honest finite certificate. The argument uses ALL degree-s monomials, not just x_1^s.

This gives a terminating precision-search in the case sqrt(J_U(f))=m, already forced when j=r. It gives no formula for the stopping precision s. In the other height cases j<r, m^s can never lie in J_U(f), and this criterion is inapplicable. The desired statement there is (x_1,...,x_j)^e subset J_U(f), and a local lifting criterion would require a compatible containment modulo m times that ideal's image, not merely modulo m^M.

For a fixed e, closedness proves

  x_1^e in J_U(f) iff x_1^e in J_U(f)+m^M for EVERY M.
