# Scoped formal support

RCS128.lean contains sixteen finite or scalar support theorems, not a formal proof of the full algorithm. They cover supplied free-involution parity, sorted finite-tail pairing, removal of pairs, threshold and pigeonhole arithmetic, offset fitting, and scalar composition of the final bound. Actual records, axes, geometrical counts and graph constructions are external hypotheses.

Words, reverse complements, blocking, sorted staircase geometry, spliced hosts, quotient cycles, half-Euler extraction and polynomial bit complexity remain outside the formalization. The unblocked/nonfixed-bracket sum and composed remove/re-index/re-pair operation are not formalized. Both members of a phase-absent removed pair must still belong to the underlying set.

The seven author controls are numerical or finite witnesses. Fifty-nine independent reviewer declarations supply the additional sensitivity and boundary evidence. Lean 4.34.1, pinned mathlib d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612 and other pinned dependency objects are trusted. The review rebuilt the own modules, audited printed types/axioms and performed module-only kernel replay, not a fresh rebuild or audit of every dependency.

The original harness can compile a weakened factor-four statement. Compilation therefore does not establish statement identity; the frozen hashes and independent type audit are necessary. Integrity checks and finite diagnostics are not proofs of the full theorem or its novelty.
