# Reverse-complement superstrings: reviewed local package

By Myeongjin Kang, no affiliation. All four independent review gates have passed. The local archive is bound to the final review gate; parent acceptance is recorded separately after portable release checks. Uploads remain stopped.

The unchanged target is a deterministic polynomial-bit-time factor-two algorithm for every explicitly encoded finite alphabet with an involution and every finite listed word family. Fixed letters, reverse-complement palindromes, empty words and lists, duplicates and containment are included. The stronger count, layer and connection results in the ordinary-superstring source pinned at fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb are stipulated. The claim is source-relative, not an independent proof of that source or a black-box consequence of the existence of an ordinary factor-two algorithm.

Contribution credit is limited to one matched package: the phase-free self-text 2k+1 rule, shared axes for adjacent sorted profiles, and the matched 2k-1 odd core in a 2k-layer budget unit. Splicing, parity, anchored host selection, batch flips, request reclassification and the established traversal operations are credited antecedents. The literature comparison is bounded and does not claim worldwide priority.

Sixteen Lean support theorems cover finite or scalar interfaces only. The word, graph, geometric and algorithmic theorem is not fully formalized.
