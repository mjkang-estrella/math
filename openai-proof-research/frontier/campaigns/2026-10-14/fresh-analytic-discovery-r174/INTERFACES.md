## Category and signs

The test category uses real sheaves, compact support and finite compact-polyhedron proper-image generators; its thick closure admits actual direct summands. Test targets are the specified finite-polyhedral spaces and allowed open/product/homeomorphic variants. They are finite-dimensional separable locally compact metrizable spaces. An arbitrary orbit space is not silently promoted to a test target. Stalks may be infinite-dimensional. The infinite character decomposition is algebraic, not a Hilbert space decomposition.

D_{X,r}=[-r]D_X and W_r=W^{-r}; the shift exactness sign is (-1)^r. The appendix verifies product/cone endpoint signs (-1)^r(α,-α). An oriented d-manifold has a symmetric degree-d orientation form in both odd congruence classes; no extra mod4 exclusion is imposed. Compact support means support contained in a compact subset. An arbitrary open restriction is not automatically an object of the same global T category.

The localization proof is an actual finite-source subdivision/cutoff and cone construction. Quotient morphisms are recovered from derived germs around the complement. The quotient need not split every idempotent. A dense inclusion has a reverse Witt operation represented by q⊥q⊥H(x[1]); the two composites are multiplication by 2. The proof includes neutrality/lagrangian data, not merely a Grothendieck-group identity.

## Fixed lattice and local test

At a point E_r is Z for r≡0 mod4 and zero otherwise. The sphere comparison has a displayed bound e_0=0, e_j=9e_{j-1}+6, hence e_j=3(9^j-1)/4. For odd d≥3 the rational group E_d(S^d)⊗Q is Q u_d and the integral image lies in L_d^{-1}Z u_d with L_d=2^{e_d}. This bound is already source mathematics. It depends on the sphere dimension, not the action, prime, character partition or concentration degree.

A degree-s sphere self-map acts rationally by s. The source also computes degree for an oriented closed triangulable manifold source. Extending that formula to arbitrary ENR homology manifolds is not licensed by the word 'degree'. The contractible-open localization isomorphism is integral: in its exact sequence E_d(U)=0 and the map E_{d-1}(U)→E_{d-1}(X) splits by a point. It works in both odd congruence classes.

The original manifold construction takes an invariant chart O and W=O×R^{d-n} with odd d>n, uses a sphere collapse, and averages a local pinch over a small p-adic subgroup. Its rational orbit injection uses proper zero-dimensional fibers and an averaging retraction of constant sheaves, without an orbit-space dimension bound. Its odd-sphere telescope plus finite nerves kills rationally trivial sphere maps after a positive-degree postcomposition. A closed-subset homotopy is extended by vector extension, normalization and cutoff. Finite quotient sheets exist for each k even when the original action has stabilizers. These are substantive paid operations, not hypotheses to be left unresolved.

## Character operation

The source works with C as a positive real plane, so the untwisted orientation push has factor 2. It constructs self-adjoint projectors on a punctured target, then integral twisted classes z_i=[A,α]+[A,α(2e_i-1)], without assuming quotient splitting. Their sum is 2[A]. Real orthogonality uses both the group operator and the common complex scalar operators. The equality of concentrated parts is local away from the bad value and lifted by the one-contractible-open isomorphism. Covariance controls the compact image of the bad set; homotopy uses C_d and its projections, keeping the form degree d although C_d has dimension 2d.

In the original sphere construction this gives rational coefficients 4/p^k and contradicts the fixed lattice. In CHARACTERS.md, a compact ENR carrier makes the character projectors global actual summands. This changes the bookkeeping to x_i with sum [A], hence 2μ/p^k. It does not itself detect μ.
