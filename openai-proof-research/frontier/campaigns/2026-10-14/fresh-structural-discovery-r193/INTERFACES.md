## Foundations and tensor conventions

Section 02 uses the orientals and the colimit definition of c_n, not an assertion that ν preserves arbitrary colimits. Strong Steiner complexes control the specified tensor on the relevant atoms. The completed monoidal extension is Ara–Maltsiniotis 2020 Theorem A.15; it is not replaced by an incomplete earlier tensor extension. The right cylinder is A⊗D1. At the directed-chain level d(x⊗u)=dx⊗u+(-1)^|x|x⊗du. This sign is material in DERIVATIONS §9.

For finite n, truncation identifies n-cells across (n+1)-cell boundaries. Lemma lem:internal-truncation identifies the right internal hom into an n-category as n-truncated, giving the cylinder adjunction needed to glue maps in nCat even when an untruncated tensor has higher cells. For ω no finite truncation is inserted. Neither argument imposes a finite bound on the number of cells.

Lemma lem:gray-nerve uses the Alexander–Whitney map to obtain N_n(A)×Δ[1]→N_n(A⊗D1), natural in A. A stationary strict cylinder therefore gives a stationary simplicial homotopy. This is a one-way interface for the present construction: it does not assert that every chain or simplicial homotopy comes from a strict cylinder. Lemma lem:filtered supplies the required filtered-colimit behavior; lem:simplicial-gluing supplies weak-equivalence gluing along the indicated monomorphism.

## Positive prisms and labelled attachment pieces

Lemma lem:relative-prism applies to a sieve A⊂E and monotone f≤g agreeing on A, with g constant on E\A. Its mixed basis value on [w0,...,wp]⊗u is [f(w0),...,f(wp),g(wp)], with repeated entries normalized to zero. The endpoint values and lower table entries are retained. The proof checks the differential, positivity and the WHOLE map under faces and degeneracies, including degeneracies that collapse the first outside vertex into the old region. Stationarity is factorization through the interval augmentation on the old subcomplex, not only the vanishing of a selected top component. Lemma lem:pushout-cylinder uses the internal-hom adjunction to glue this stationary cylinder over an arbitrary attaching map U_n(A)→X.

Section 04 labels objects by D1. Lemma lem:fibers provides the adjoints controlling fibers and objects under colimits; lem:oriental-segments describes full initial and final pieces of orientals. Proposition prop:labelled-pushout and cor:attachment-covers identify the actual old and new full sieve/cosieve pieces AFTER attachment, including old object and higher-cell identifications. Intersections are computed by the min/max label maps. Lemma lem:nerve-cover turns a two-piece full-sieve cover, or the specified cosieve cover, into a union of Street nerves. It does not state that the Street nerve preserves every pushout.

These interfaces justify the exact sequence of relative nerve chains used in DERIVATIONS §§3–4, even for a nonfaithful attaching map. The computation there deliberately sends an old arrow to an idempotent and identifies old objects.

## Couniversality and its finite-to-small passage

For a retract sieve i:A⊂E with right adjoint r, thm:couniversal proves that X→X⊔_{U_n(A)}U_n(E) is a Street weak equivalence for every small X and every attaching functor. If E\A has greatest element x, lem:greatest-new defines t=r(x), k equal to t on new elements, and g equal to x there. The relative prisms give id→g←k. The induced retraction p_k need not equal the canonical p_r. The signed construction in DERIVATIONS §5 explicitly handles that difference.

Without a greatest new element, the source deletes two maximal new elements, identifies the two full sieves and their intersection after attachment, and uses nerve gluing. It does not construct compatible global strict cylinders by that gluing argument. The finite induction decreases the number of new elements. For general small E, finite full ir-stable subsets form a filtered system. They need not be sieves of E. Each stage attaches to the same X along its own old finite subset. Transition maps of the attached categories need not be injective. Filtered-colimit preservation gives the required weak-equivalence passage. Proposition prop:poset-unit then proves the unit equivalence on every poset.

The r193 chain recursion uses exactly those finite covers. Its small-poset extension keeps the possible noninjectivity and uses finite equality witnesses, rather than pretending that all stages are literal subsets of the final pushout.

## Subdivision, transfer, properness and the unit

Section 06 treats a Dwyer-style poset inclusion A⊂B using the upward closure Z of A and the right-adjoint retraction Z→A. With C=B\A and T=Z\A, the Z/C cosieve cover and its intersection control the canonical pushout-to-nerve comparison gamma in prop:attachment-comparison. The simplicial gluing uses the actual monomorphism N U_n(T)→N U_n(C). This is stronger and more precise than assuming the entire categorical pushout is sent to a simplicial pushout.

Lemma lem:subdivision-posets represents twice-subdivided finite attachments by chains of nonempty faces. The retraction is intersection of a face-chain with the old face poset, on the appropriate upward region. The proof includes the empty-boundary case. cor:horn-acyclic supplies trivial-cell acyclicity; cor:attachment-invariance supplies boundary-attachment invariance.

Section 07 then builds the model structure. lem:transfinite-horns combines successor horn attachments with filtered limits at limit stages. prop:model-structure checks the boundary/horn injective classes and applies small-object factorizations with presentability. prop:properness proves left properness through relative I-cell maps and the fixed-source retract diagram; it does not assume every cofibration is a Dwyer map. Right properness follows by the right adjoint's preservation of pullbacks and the detected Kan structure. prop:subdivided-unit proves the unit for arbitrary simplicial sets through their skeletal attachments, including noninjective attaching maps. thm:quillen-equivalence uses the actual composite adjunction unit, with the Sd²/Ex² unit, rather than silently replacing it by a different nerve comparison.

## Presentability and the expressly limited context appendix

Appendix 09 constructs strict categories by guarded finite terms. Definedness of partial composition and equality of terms are generated together. A boundary identification can make a previously unavailable composite defined. The proof thus accounts for composites created by quotients rather than quotienting a fixed list of already defined words. Every derivation has finite support, so it appears at a finite stage of a filtered diagram. Finite presentations yield local finite presentability, including countably many globular sorts for ω. This does not give explicit cell-count or homotopy-length bounds. r193's chain norm bounds come from its own signed recursion, not this appendix.

Appendix 08 explains the strict/weak methodological contrast and mentions coherator ideas as context. It supplies no theorem transferring the model structure or the presentability argument to weak, enriched or nonglobular categories. No such transfer is claimed here.
