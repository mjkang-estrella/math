\[

P(z)=\begin{pmatrix}
(3/4)z^2&(\sqrt7/4)z^3\\
(\sqrt7/4)z&-(3/4)z^2

\]

Then N is unitarily irreducible, W(N) is the closed unit disk, and

\[
\|P[N]\|=2\max_{z\in W(N)}\|P(z)\|=2.
\]

For every pair of constant unit u,v in C^2,

\[
\|(v^*P u)(N)\|\le\sqrt{849}/16<2.
\]

Thus the specified complete witness cannot be scalarized in the sense of R016. This disproves the universal same-witness scalarization extension in size six. It does not disprove disk rigidity or claim a full size-six classification.
