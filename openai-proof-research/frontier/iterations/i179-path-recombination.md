## 4. Actual recombination attempt and its obstruction

### 4.1 What two-colour component exchange really pays

Overlay two actual packings, red $a$ and blue $b$, retaining doubled
common edges. At an internal cubic vertex each packing uses two incident
edges. Their symmetric difference has degree zero or two, with one
edge of each colour when nonzero. At a boundary vertex both degree-one
packings have the same property; when their prescribed degrees differ,
the symmetric difference has degree one. Thus the difference consists
of alternating cycles and alternating paths with the appropriate
boundary endpoints.

Exchanging colours on whole components preserves the summed incidence
vector **coordinatewise**. At internal vertices it preserves both
degrees; at ends of an alternating path it exchanges the endpoint
deficits. This is actual edge recoloring, not parity-based state
availability. It preserves use of the original embedded graph and
vertex coverage when the resulting degrees remain one or two.
But endpoint pairing must still be traced, and cycle components must
still be excluded. The next example pays the other checks and fails
precisely this last one.

### 4.3 A complete two-colour lock, not merely one unlucky toggle

Any recoloring that preserves the total edge multiplicities and the
two degree prescriptions must keep doubled edges in both colours.
At every internal degree-two difference vertex, choosing the colour
of one difference edge forces the other. Hence it must swap whole
components of $p\triangle s$.

There are only four choices: neither component, $Q$ only, $T$ only,
or both. The first is the original pair. The $Q$-only choice fails
above. The $T$-only choice gives the same two bad outputs with colours
interchanged, since swapping both gives $(s,p)$. Swapping both
therefore gives only the original unordered pair. This proves:

> For this actual pair, no nontrivial two-packing recombination with
> the same edge-multiplicity vector produces two complete admissible
> packings, even if the two endpoint types may exchange colours.

The frozen finite diagnostic checks the five complete $P$ packings
against the sole state-0 packing. Their difference component counts
are four two-component cases and one one-component case. It exhausts
**18** component-subset recolorings; the only valid ones in every case
are the original ordered pair and its swap. Of the **nine**
single-component switches, eight create isolated cycles and one is
just the whole-pair swap. No new graph is tested.

This refutes only the proposed **two-colour, same-edge-sum local
connectivity mechanism** on these pairs. It does not refute L179:
the I177 kernel is all of $H$, and other helper packings can contribute
additional identities.

## 5. What a substantive missing recombination would have to do

For lost types $P,D$, a useful path-sum bridge would need both:

1. control of variation **within** each complete lost fibre by actual
   admissible survivor exchanges, not merely within a selected subfamily;
2. control linking the two lost types. For example an actual identity
   $d+x_0=p+x_7$, with all four terms complete packings, would give
   equality of their residual classes because $t_D-t_P=x_7-x_0$.
   A sequence of properly checked helper exchanges could replace that
   single identity. Its existence is **not assumed or proved here**.

If every nonempty lost packing could be linked to one common reference
by such exchanges, all residuals would lie on one line. This is a
sufficient strategy, not an equivalent restatement of L179 and not a
proved lemma. The actual configuration in Section 4 shows why an
exchange restricted to just the initial lost packing and state 0
cannot supply it: the only total-sum-preserving two-colour outputs
are trivial.

Brace extension gives a perfect matching through any two disjoint
edges. Its cubic complement is a 2-factor and may have isolated cycles.
The stipulated three-edge-path theorem gives a Hamiltonian cycle
through a specified path, under its full hypotheses. It does not
hold the other edges of either packing fixed, preserve a prescribed
five-port pairing, prescribe which full exterior packing is used, or
give a specified sum of two packing vectors. No such control was
deduced from either theorem. In particular, existence of another cycle
does not repair the displayed two-colour lock.

The missing graph step is therefore not a rank inequality: it is a
way to introduce an additional **actual** helper and reroute around
the closed middle segments while accounting for every changed edge
and ending with complete cycle-free packings of allowed types.
