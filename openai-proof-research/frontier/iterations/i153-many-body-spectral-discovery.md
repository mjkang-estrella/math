### 2.3 Candidate B153 — connected hard-sphere structure factor

`q(F Psi)-E_0||F Psi||² = sum_j integral |grad_j F|² |Psi|²`.

`sum_(a=c,s) <F_a Psi,(H-E_0)F_a Psi> = N|k|²`.                 (FS153)
