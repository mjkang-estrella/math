# I307 follow-up literature note

2026-10-13. This supplements the frozen I307 proposal and is not a proof revision or contribution verdict.

The parent read the complete substantive web rendering of Klaja–Mashreghi–Ransford, *On mapping theorems for numerical range*, arXiv:1510.08132v1, https://arxiv.org/html/1510.08132v1, lines 27–397. The first returned passage covered through line 190 and the second from 112 through the references. No raw HTML hash or page-image inspection is claimed; no full external body is saved.

Theorem 2.1 and its full proof establish Berger–Stampfli numerical-radius contraction for disk-algebra functions with f(0)=0. The proof uses positive partial-fraction coefficients for finite Blaschke products, then finite Blaschke approximation and radial limits. Theorem 3.1 gives the local norm/numerical-radius refinement. Section 4 proves the Drury teardrop mapping theorem for arbitrary f(0), via the quadratic operator inequality. Section 5.1 explicitly connects the partial fractions to Aleksandrov–Clark measures and credits Kato; the remaining sections discuss equivalent norm bounds and general domains.

The paper does not state the full spectral-factor form E307, the maximal-Jordan-block multiplicity bounds, or the nilpotent scalar-supremum criterion in the passages read. This is only a bounded textual comparison, not a novelty conclusion. A contribution reviewer must test whether equality in those older positive-measure arguments supplies E307 by a routine extraction. The fact that our proof instead uses exact unweighted Hardy coefficients and the pinned ordered-test argument is not by itself evidence of substantiality.
