## 2. Mechanism I: retain residual curvature rather than erase it

Here is a paid replacement for one part of the formal construction, derived from actual marked cuts. It deliberately does not assert the missing nonzero tensor theorem.

**Residual formal bridge.** Suppose (6) has `coker Δ¹=0`. For a vertex write

\[
e_v=\dim\ker\left(H^2(V_v)\longrightarrow
                    \bigoplus_{\text{incident ends}}H^2(Y_e)\right).
\]

Then `Σe_v≤dim ker Δ²=dk`. In N's joint formal coefficient ring, with the complete squarefree plus parameters and all prescribed extra-loop holonomies, there is a curvature presentation `I_v=(r_v)` of length `q_v=3b₂(V_v)` and edge parametrization `M_v` for which

\[
S_v(M_v)\in I_v^2,\qquad
(\partial_hS_v)(M_v)=L_vr_v,
\tag{7}
\]

where `rank L_v(0)=q_v−3e_v`. There are `3e_v` scalar residual functions `ρ_v` such that

\[
 I_v=((\partial_hS_v)(M_v),\rho_v).
\tag{8}
\]

All of N's marked law products belong to this ideal. The tangent local graphs remain injective and their signed sum `Λ` remains an isomorphism. Those last facts concern degree one and do not assert that (8) is a gradient ideal.

**Proof.** Surjectivity of `Δ¹`, the kernel identification by extra-loop evaluations, and the coordinate-line argument reproduce the complete independent holonomy slice and tangent-isomorphism calculation of N. The residual recursion in the punctured based form complex gives `q_v` entries and expresses full curvature in their ideal by Bianchi and the slice identity. ## 3. An exact counterexample to the rank-only residual surrogate

To make the failed claim testable, define the surrogate precisely. Retain N's odd cycle, full ports and law list, squarefree coefficient rings, separate edge potentials, injective local tangent graphs and isomorphism `Λ`. Replace the zero-defect vertex conclusions by (7)–(8), permitting at most `6d` scalar residual slots in total, consistent with `k=2`, `coker Δ¹=0`, `dim ker Δ²=2d`. Require all full word memberships before Laurent passage and all source law-product memberships afterward. The assertion that these data alone are impossible is **false**.

For every edge from `v` to `v+1` designate its source plus. At each vertex there are five plus red ports, five plus blue ports, and five minus of each color. Make every cut model a solid torus with its coordinate slope. Set its internal tangent dimension `N_e=0`, its potentials `F_e=b_e=0`, and its undetected peripheral holonomy to identity. Set every vertex parameter space `y_v=0`. Then `M_v` is the unique map between zero-dimensional spaces, each tangent graph is injective, and `Λ:0→0` is an isomorphism. The unmodified graph contraction is the empty determinant, one.

Write the plus parameters as `r₁,…,r₅,b₁,…,b₅` and set

\[
T=K[r_1,\ldots,r_5,b_1,\ldots,b_5]/(r_i^2,b_j^2),
\quad K=\mathbb C((\sigma)),\qquad
J=(r_3-r_1-r_2,r_4,r_5).
\tag{9}
\]

Every product of two distinct red variables belongs to `J`. Indeed in the quotient `r₃=r₁+r₂`, `r₄=r₅=0`, and `0=r₃²=2r₁r₂`; all other red products now vanish. Equivalently,

\[
(r_3+r_1+r_2)(r_3-r_1-r_2)=-2r_1r_2
\]

in the squarefree ring, and products involving `r₃` follow by substitution. Hence all 100 unordered all-plus law products `r_i r_j b_a b_c` belong to `J`. Any law with a minus input has its corresponding `b_e(M_v)=0`. Take six scalar slots per vertex with residual list `(r₃−r₁−r₂,r₄,r₅,0,0,0)` and `L=0`. Equations (7)–(8) hold, using the first three nonzero residuals. Six slots correspond to two ordinary degree-two kernel dimensions, since the source Lie algebra has dimension three. Over `d` vertices the assigned ordinary defect is exactly `2d`, with no degree-one defect. This is bookkeeping for the surrogate, not cohomology of a constructed vertex manifold.

The example has a valid **joint formal** version, so it is not exploiting an illicit early inversion of `σ`. Work over `C[[σ]][r,b]/squares` and take

\[
\rho=(\sigma(r_3-r_1-r_2),\sigma r_4,\sigma r_5,0,0,0).
\tag{10}
\]

These entries have joint order at least two. Give every plus tip holonomy `exp(±tE)=1±tE`, with `E` the upper nilpotent `sl₂` matrix, and every minus tip identity. On eight named extra loops put the eight nonidentity `g_ab(σ)=exp(aσE)exp(bσF)`, `a,b∈{0,1,2}`; put identity on the ninth. The identity conjugating choice is also retained, so there are exactly the stipulated ten choices at each of four slots.

Here is a universal check of the entire list, rather than a selected convenient sublist. Each actual four-tip source word is Brunnian in its four tip inputs. Thus a word involving a minus tip is identity. For four plus tips the entries of the word minus identity are divisible by the four distinct squarefree tip variables: set any one to zero and the word becomes identity. At `σ=0` all the conjugators are identity and all four tip matrices commute, so the coefficient of their product vanishes at `σ=0`. Consequently every such entry is a multiple of `σ r_i r_j b_a b_c`, which belongs to (10) by (9). Independent signs, all independent conjugators, the source internal tip conjugations, and overall conjugation preserve this argument. After Laurent passage it also proves the required scalar law-product memberships. The nine adjoints still span `End(sl₂)` (the source determinant is `512σ¹⁸`); that spanning does not make these elements independent in `J`.

This treats 8,100 ordered port choices: 400 all-plus choices and 7,700 with a minus input at each vertex, each with all `10⁴·16` ordered decorations. Nothing is removed from the original list. The diagnostic checks the finite port count and exact ideal calculation; the universal Brunnian proof, not an enumeration of billions of bodies, covers the decorations.

Within the explicitly narrower class of **linear ideals in the five red variables annihilating every red quadratic**, three generators are minimal. With at most two independent linear relations at least three linear variables remain. Their symmetric square has dimension at least six; the five inherited square relations span at most five dimensions. Some quadratic survives. The three relations in (9) attain zero degree-two quotient. This is not a minimum among arbitrary nonlinear mixed red/blue ideals, nor a lower bound on geometric defects.

The counterexample is admissible for the precisely stated algebraic surrogate. It does **not** assert a vertex Poincaré–Lefschetz complex, a cup product, a curvature recursion realizing (10), an actual cocore system, an oriented TOP filling, or the required relative double cover. Only the cut *edge* models are actual solid tori. In particular it is not a homotopy-cover substitution or an action on another filling. It refutes a rank-only robust tensor claim, not a stronger theorem incorporating further geometric constraints and not R94. Any successful replacement must exclude this algebra by proving additional consequences of actual marked `Z`.

## 4. Mechanism II: geometric localization before covering

The first route allows defects to be placed arbitrarily among cover vertices. There is a genuinely geometric improvement: make the cut choice once downstairs. This is not an endpoint assignment declared to be realizable.

**Periodic localization bridge.** For an actual hypothetical `Z` coming from a solution on `W_k`, there is a compatible choice of the cuts of E in every cyclic cover obtained by lifting one connected base vertex piece `C` and ten connected base cut types `Y₁,…,Y₁₀`. Their actual outside faces, law-cocore disks, orientations and markings are retained. There are nonnegative integers `c₁,c₂` and `e` such that

\[
\dim\operatorname{coker}\Delta^1_d=dc_1,
\quad \dim\ker\Delta^2_d=dc_2,
\quad c_1+c_2=k,
\quad 0\leq e\leq c_2.
\tag{11}
\]

Here `e` is the dimension of the kernel of restriction from `H²(C)` to **both incidence copies** of all ten cuts. At most `c₁` base cut types have anomalous peripheral line. In addition every such actual quotient must satisfy

\[
c_1+e\geq1.
\tag{12}
\]

**Proof.** Attach the actual dual handles in `Z`, giving `U` with `π₁U=F_{10+p}`, prescribed boundary `∂X`, law cocores, `b₂U=k`, and `H³U=0`, by the independently checked E handle sequence. Map to the rose of ten plumbing loops, forgetting the extras. The same equivariant tree-bundle construction and component-tree pruning as in E produce one connected principal piece downstairs and one cut for each loop, with two incident copies of a loop cut in the vertex boundary. Although N states its formal application for `d≥3`, its cut construction takes place in the universal tree, where there are no loop edges. The absence of repeated incident *edge variables* is needed later in its tensor application; it is not used in extending this map, finding connected cuts, or pruning branches. Thus performing these geometric steps over the base rose is legitimate.

Each piece maps into a vertex neighborhood and each cut into an edge midpoint. The cyclic class `w` is therefore zero on its fundamental group. Its degree-`d` preimage consists of `d` identical copies. The original boundary identifications and proper law disks lift with them. After lifting to `d≥3`, the distinct ports are distinct incident edges as required by the source formal proof. This proves the claimed periodic choice on actual manifolds, with the original TOP smoothing convention retained.

Put `A_i=dim H^i(C)`, `B_i=Σ_{j=1}^{10}dim H^i(Y_j)`. Connectedness and the marking identify `ker Δ¹_d` with `H¹(U_d)/H¹(Γ_d)`, of dimension `dp`, by evaluation on the named extra loops. Hence

\[
\dim\operatorname{coker}\Delta^1_d=d(B_1-A_1+p).
\]

Surjectivity of `Δ²_d` gives `dim ker Δ²_d=d(A₂−B₂)`. Define `c₁,c₂` by these two integer expressions. Equation (6) proves nonnegativity and their sum `k`. A local kernel at each vertex extends by zero to a global kernel vector, so `de≤dc₂`. Every anomalous base type lifts to `d` anomalous cuts; E gives its number at most `c₁`.

Finally suppose `c₁=e=0`. Degree-one surjectivity gives every independent peripheral condition, the coordinate-line designations, injective local graphs and isomorphism `Λ`. Every vertex degree-two restriction is injective, since its local kernel is zero. The complete N formal proof consequently has its full-column curvature-gradient minor at every vertex. Inspection through the proof end shows that global injectivity of `Δ²` is used there only to deduce these individual injectivities; all subsequent square-ideal, correction, log-polynomial, conjugator and law operations are local. Thus these stronger local hypotheses suffice even if the global kernel is nonzero. They supply precisely the original zero-residual tensor hypotheses in an odd cover, contradicting N's endpoint-assignment theorem and odd-cycle corollary. This proves (12), without asserting a new unrestricted disk theorem. ∎

At `k=2`, the possible defects are now `c₁=0,e=1 or 2`; `c₁=1,e=0 or 1`; or `c₁=2,e=0`. This is a necessary restriction on actual quotients. It is technical under the full-operation premise and does not improve `κ≥1`.

When `c₁=0`, periodicity forces exactly five plus red and five plus blue ports at every vertex: each base type chooses one of its two incidence ends, and its cyclic lifts contribute exactly one plus incidence per vertex. Thus the local law pattern used in (9) is also the pattern forced by these periodic coordinate cuts. The algebraic counterexample does not depend on choosing an unusually favorable nonperiodic endpoint pattern.

This localization prevents one *particular* kind of harmless concentration. In the elementary edge-deletion relaxation, the original minimum-three witness puts three deleted edges on one closing adjacency. An anomalous **type** in (11) instead repeats on every adjacency. Deleting at most two such types leaves at least three edges of each color on every adjacency, so the relaxed deficient-color alternation contradiction remains on an odd cycle. However deleting an anomalous cut is not a permitted tensor or manifold operation. Nor do the remaining coordinate cuts cure `coker Δ¹`. This observation is explicitly combinatorial; it is not a nonfilling proof.

The full four-tip list suggests a second local test: force distinct laws to consume independent residual directions at each vertex. The algebra (9)–(10) blocks the inference from law count alone. It is already periodic and all its many laws share three residual generators. In its conormal module `J/𝔪J` the quartic law products vanish, rather than providing hundreds of independent vectors. Conjugator spanning extracts membership, not independence of memberships. Thus neither the 1.296 billion bodies nor their signs multiply the defect budget automatically.

To complete this route one needs a geometric statement about the actual residual ideal, its local pairing or its restrictions to law disks that excludes such concentration. It must follow from the oriented marked `Z`, not from assumed arbitrary position of the cocores; those cocores have already been accounted for as real disks. Poincaré duality, TOP spin and the ordinary hyperbolic form alone have not been shown to provide that statement. In particular we do not assert that the degree-one defect subspace is automatically isotropic: the cuts have torus boundary, and producing the required relative representatives has its own boundary corrections.
