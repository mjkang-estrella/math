## Why the full result is technical

The reviewed statement retains every quantifier: for each $\epsilon>0$,
positive constants $c_\epsilon,C_\epsilon$ handle every $d\ge2$, every
normalized $C^2$ potential with supplied minimizer zero and
$I\preceq\nabla^2V\preceq2I$, and arbitrary measurable causal, stateful,
inconsistent value and Euclidean gradient errors bounded by $c_\epsilon$.
The target is TV error at most $1/10$ and at most $C_\epsilon d^\epsilon$
real queries on **every execution**, with unrestricted intervening computation.
There is no common tolerance for all $\epsilon$, finite-bit guarantee,
or arbitrary-accuracy claim.

I161 gives a positive reduction of this whole statement, not an argument
from failed literature searches:

1. The stipulated compiler already has fixed routine height, small
   coefficient feedback and constant final row sums. Tracking finitely many
   height constants in that same induction removes unused polylogarithmic
   slack. Its inhomogeneous version pays inconsistent oracle errors by
   absolute coefficient sums without differentiating erroneous replies.
2. An untouched **standalone** Gaussian reserve yields conditional
   divergence of order $h\eta^2$. Keeping the same conditional next-state
   kernel cancels the state contribution in KL and TV, including causal
   memory. This does not assert independence of transformed internal reserves.
3. Existing shifted composition over $\lceil1/h\rceil$ steps pays the
   fixed-time regularization. A separate ideal-prefix TV budget pays
   compiler approximation; no KL triangle inequality is used.
4. Source warm-up, descent and final unnoising return the unsmoothed target.
   Capped rejection in the remaining finite dimensions and prescribed
   circuits/loop lengths give a positive tolerance and an every-run cost.

These are concrete source-proof and classical operations at the claimed
scope. The stronger conclusion remains useful, but no additional nonroutine
mechanism is established after crediting those operations. The parent
accepts the contribution disposition on that basis, separately from proof
correctness and evidence reproducibility.
