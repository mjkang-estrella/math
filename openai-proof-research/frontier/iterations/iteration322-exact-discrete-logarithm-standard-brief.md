A valid candidate a is obtained from the two decoded labels if the orientation gives j'=a*j modulo q, then checked by modular powering. The exact orientation and window decoder need proof, not assumption. Handle h=1 separately.

For a FIXED accepted label j and FIXED candidate a (now known to the probability computation), the exact joint probability p_j of success may be accessible without summing exponentially many labels: expand the squared two-register amplitude and trace out the group-value register. Equal group values impose a linear congruence x+a*y=x'+a*y' modulo q. Each polygonal phase factor g(kx/Q) is piecewise linear; introduce floor/quarter-bin integer variables and four residue classes, turning its evaluation into a constant-dimensional rational polytope with polynomial weights of fixed degree. Frequency windows contain only constantly many actual k,l values. Because k,l,a are fixed parameters for this calculation, all constraints are linear in the summation variables; their bit lengths are polynomial.
