   contradicting two-extendability. A common outside endpoint instead
   closes an eight-vertex component. Connectedness and the thirteen
   exterior vertices plus six patch vertices exclude it. Distinctness
   therefore follows from the stated hypotheses, not finite examples.

   leave the patch but terminal distinctness is not assumed.
   Degrees are $2-m(v)$ after removing a perfect matching. Adding the
   four labeled stubs gives two intervals and possible stub-free cycles;

   Parallel edges and singleton intervals cannot be discarded. The
   five-port type is read from the entire lifted complement, not just
