## Mathematical verdicts

1. **Assigned I170 audits: PASS ORIGINAL.** H170's short-gap bijections and
   bridge identity, R170's residual decomposition, the three containment
   certificates, and the specified finite populations are reproduced in their
   original scope. The legacy JSON-key crash and dropped null-alpha rows were
   I171 implementation failures, not mathematical failures in I170.
2. **W171: accepted as proved, with its stated conditional scope.** If the
   square vector lies in the parent difference span, every signed affine
   representation of the prescribed factor has N00 coefficient total
   $1+\alpha$. In the cyclic group generated by $[r],[s]$, exchange fails
   precisely at the primitive pair $(m,k)=(2,-3)$. The observed
   $-(h-1)/h$ shape is equivalent to the specific switched residual being the
   positive generator. This is a reformulation, not its exclusion.
3. **Convex-representation shortcut: refuted.** Coordinatewise extremality of
   a binary vector forces every positively weighted binary summand to equal
   it. A disconnected factor therefore cannot be a convex combination of
   Hamiltonian incidence vectors.
4. **C171: accepted as a certified existence result.** In the same normalized
   weighted order-32 brace, a two-component factor T has weight $-\tfrac12$,
   whereas the prescribed square switch F has weight $+\tfrac1{14}$.
   Generic two-component-factor positivity and generic exclusion of
   $-\tfrac12$ are false. The square-specific exclusion remains open.
5. **O171: accepted as a local classification.** On a length-three boundary
   arc, binary toggling is equivalent to absence of the middle edge. It can
   leave a path plus a cycle; toggling both arcs can leave two paths plus two
   cycles. Binary coordinates do not certify compatible spanning paths.
   The component argument proves these alternatives, not the rank inequality.
6. **Index 431: accepted as an exact bridge witness.** All three compatible
   old families are empty and $U\subseteq L_0$; nevertheless the new separator
   certifies $\eta=1$. It is distinct from I170's U-only separator.
