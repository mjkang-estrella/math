`Δ≥(c²/2)[1−Σ_j||Tρf_j||₂²]`.

The constant/nonconstant spectral split and Σ_j||f_j||₂²=1 give

`Σ_j||Tρf_j||₂²≤ρ²+(1−ρ²)Σ_jμ_j²≤ρ²+(1−ρ²)max_j|μ_j|`,

because Σ_j|μ_j|≤1. All selector-coordinate correlations are zero, and payload correlations are aμ_j. Therefore

`Δ≥[ρ²(1−ρ²)/4]d_a(u)`.  (H131)

This proves a full dimension-independent subclass estimate, with arbitrary hierarchy and sign switching. It does not cover selectors that reuse payload coordinates or variable amplitudes correlated with the selected bit.
