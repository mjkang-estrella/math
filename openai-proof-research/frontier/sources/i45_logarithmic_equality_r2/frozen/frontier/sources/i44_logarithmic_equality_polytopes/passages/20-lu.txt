77: equality. The planar case was proved in [4].

83:    For unconditional bodies, the logarithmic inequality follows from the coordinatewise
84: geometric-mean inequality of Bollobás and Leader [2] and Cordero-Erausquin, Fradelizi,
85: and Maurey [7]; the equality analysis of Saroglou [24] was completed in [3]. Böröczky
86: and Kalantzopoulos [3] proved it for bodies invariant under n linear reflections whose
87: fixed hyperplanes intersect only at the origin. Rotem [23] established the complex case.
88: Yang and Zhang [30] considered convex bodies in the relative Bonnesen class in R3 .
89: Tao, Xiong, and Xiong [26] focused on origin-symmetric cylinder cases in R3 . Xi and
90: Leng [29] obtained a nonsymmetric planar extension for bodies put in dilation position
91: after suitable translations.
92:    Kolesnikov and Milman [15] developed a new method to derive local Brunn-Minkowski
93: inequality by studying its infinitesimal form. They obtained local Lp -Brunn–Minkowski
94: inequalities for C+2 origin-symmetric convex bodies when p ∈ [1 − cn−3/2 , 1). Chen,
95: Huang, Li, and Liu [6] and Putterman [22] gave a direct equivalence between the local
