## 3. Strongest reduction: the source proof already supplies the recursive operation

A bare appeal to `ten:assignment` would be invalid: it realizes no four-dimensional geometry. A bare appeal to classical grope compression would also miss external basing, the exact boundary problem, framings and the full chamber module. The reduction below uses the **marked geometric proof in MT and B**, then checks why its existing interfaces are stable under this particular replacement. It does not posit a theorem geometrically realizing every abstract law family.

### 3.1 The local step is an iteration of a proved marked step

In MT's proof, an old terminal annulus is primitive in the marked thickening: its solid torus is an annulus times an interval. Gluing the boundary annulus of a new punctured-torus product to that **old** annulus merely adds a collar to the new attaching annulus. The new boundary commutator need not itself be primitive. Extend the two new meridian disks through that collar; move the finitely many connector feet away from their strips. The old remaining disk duals and the two new ones stay disjoint. Cutting along them gives a ball with disjoint marked rectangles. Standardizing the rectangles and reattaching the handles gives the marked boundary join with one additional terminal collar.

B explicitly performs this operation on the two lower annuli to obtain its four-tip body. Its proof also transports the initial boundary and all attaching markings, treats the connector graph as a tree so no extra generator is introduced, and allows arbitrarily many finitely prescribed disjoint external paths. Thus the operation is available again at either terminal annulus of either upper branch. Applying it at one terminal annulus in each branch gives precisely

\[
 [[X_0,[X_1,X_2]],[Y_0,[Y_1,Y_2]]].
\]

No new kind of attaching region is needed. There are two additional punctured-torus stages and six terminal marked annuli instead of four. The invariant used by the old proof—disjoint meridian disk duals to the terminal marked annuli—is preserved by each step. This supplies a concrete inductive proof of this body, rather than the assertion that a free group with six generators can be embedded as required.

Framing does not introduce a new obstruction. B already twists each separated terminal longitudinal collar by an arbitrary integer, takes the twist to be constant near connector feet, transports the complete body, and then constructs its returning collar. The initial-boundary link is fixed **after** these transports. The same finite independent adjustments work for six terminal collars. There is no demand to preserve an earlier, independently fixed four-tip initial link while changing the body. That distinction is important to why the operation transfers.

### 3.2 Three choices are the leaves of the same compression tree

A contraction keeps one member of a symplectic pair, discards the other branch, and uses two parallel copies of the retained cap/subgrope. Repetition along a root-to-leaf path gives a disk for that chosen terminal leaf. For the right-comb branch [X_0,[X_1,X_2]], the three paths have lengths 1, 2, 2 and use **2, 4, 4** terminal cap parallels. The normal-frame change at each compression is the source's same local quarter-turn. Classical contraction is explicit in Krushkal–Quinn §1.2; the required based/frame/intersection refinement is already in MT/B. [Primary Krushkal–Quinn text](https://msp.org/gt/2000/4-1/gt-v4-n1-p14-s.pdf).

All this is applied to a **chosen** finite family. Put the finite stages at successively separated collar levels, use disjoint product parallels, and retain the opposite-side convention at each symplectic pair. The only intersections of selected caps arise from opposite plumbing ports, as before. One does not need to place all three alternative compression disks disjointly at the same time. The parent clarification makes exactly this source-compatible assertion. Thus there is a genuine new three-choice body in the literal sense, but its construction and simultaneous-family control use a closed recursive operation of the source proof.

For the algebraic duals, select the shallow tip on each branch. This keeps the source's two-inner-sheet calculation. The other basis loop now bounds an upper capped subtree. The ensuing hermitian clearing, full framed μ including its identity coefficient, and direct-finiteness argument proving a basis of the **whole** module are unchanged. ### 3.4 A uniform finite-arity template explains the odd-order change

The reduction is not confined to noticing two similar pictures. For each fixed q≥2 use a right-comb bracket B_q on q leaves, with B_1(X)=X and

\[
 B_q(X_0,\ldots,X_{q-1})=[X_0,B_{q-1}(X_1,\ldots,X_{q-1})],
 \qquad W_q=[B_q(X),B_q(Y)].
\]

Starting from B's four-tip body, perform q−2 primitive-annulus replacements on each branch, always retaining a shallow first leaf. The terminal-disk-dual invariant above proves the marked model by induction. There are 2q terminal annuli and 2q−1 punctured-torus stages. The same shallow choice preserves the chamber cancellation, and choosing any leaf is obtained by repeated contraction. The number of finite pieces grows; the ambient dimension and the kind of geometric step do not.

Put colors in Z/q. Between successive vertices, install five separate plumbing edges for every ordered color pair (a,b) with b≠a+1. This gives m=5q(q−1) edges per step and n=10(q−1) incident ports of each color. Use all laws containing two ports of every color, arranged as two ordered branches with one port of each color per branch, with the same finite independent conjugation/sign list as the source. At q=3 these are exactly the proposed six-tip bodies and successor constraint.

The negative combinatorics is uniform. If no law is assigned at a vertex, some color has at most one incident edge assigned there; call these nonempty deficient-color sets D_v. If a∈D_v and b∈D_{v+1} with b≠a+1, the five edges of that type could supply at most one assignment to each endpoint, impossible since each edge must be assigned somewhere. Consequently

\[
 D_v\times D_{v+1}\subset\{(a,a+1):a\in\mathbb Z/q\}.
\]

Nonempty sets satisfying this inclusion must be singletons, and their values advance by one. Closing a d-cycle therefore requires q|d. If q|d, select the color v mod q at vertex v. No forbidden plumbing has both ends selected, so the chosen-family filling applies. The two directed lifts when d=2 are distinct edges; they must not be collapsed into one undirected edge. The exceptional one-vertex cover need not be fed to the loopless tensor theorem: if it had a manifold model, so would every finite cover, including a degree at least two not divisible by q.

This uniform recipe is offered as a **source-proof reduction for assessing I15**, not an additional independently reviewed all-q result, and no extra finding is claimed for it. It directly addresses odd-prime alternatives: three is not singled out by a new local four-dimensional obstruction once finite marked replacement trees are allowed. ### 3.5 The actual-ideal argument is uniformly multilinear

The right-comb law has a nonzero 2q-linear leading Lie tensor in sl₂. An explicit witness uses [h,e]=2e, [h,f]=−2f, [e,f]=h:

\[
 [B_q(h,\ldots,h,e),B_q(h,\ldots,h,f)]
       =(-4)^{q-1}h\ne0.
\]

Independent conjugation of each slot by the source's finite adjoint-spanning list therefore extracts every product of one coordinate from each slot: rank-one endomorphisms send the slots of a nonzero tensor witness to any desired coordinate inputs. Nothing here depends on four or six beyond the number of factors.

The commutator word becomes trivial when any terminal variable is zero. Its higher terms therefore contain all slots and at least one extra factor. After substitution, these terms lie in mJ for the finite product ideal J of the port entries. The source's linear extraction and Nakayama argument give **J⊂I_v**, not merely membership in a radical or vanishing on points. The same argument remains valid with repeated powers in a slot; individual plus-parameter squares vanish, but distinct mixed parameters remain.

All preceding cut, cohomology, mixed-sign Stokes, unit-minor, algebraicity and common-background arguments in B occur before use of the law degree. At q=3 the ambient graph has b₁=38d+1, exactly the required graph count; replacing m by 5q(q−1) changes that count to (m+8)d+1, not the argument. The flat-quotient comparison in the integration note, the polynomial holonomy replacement and the nilpotent correction are unchanged. Thus the source tensor theorem alone would not suffice, but the complete source geometric/formal proof gives the particular new tensor law without a new algebraic interface. I15's careful six-slot calculation verifies this transfer rather than supplying a new tensor obstruction principle.

## 4. Why the binary-majority objection does not defeat this reduction

I accept the candidate's scope-limited Boolean obstruction. A finite network of the old binary cap constraints, with conjunction and existentially quantified auxiliary choices, has a majority-closed boundary relation. The graph of a three-state successor permutation, under any injective Boolean encoding, cannot have this property: take its three successive pairs. Coordinatewise majority gives (m,m), which is a diagonal pair, while a fixed-point-free three-cycle contains none. Existential witnesses do not repair this because majority also combines the witnesses. This is consistent with the primitive-positive/polymorphism facts in the retained primary constraint-complexity text. [Primary Allender et al. text, Fact 2.2 and the Boolean classification](https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~allender/papers/csp.pdf).

Thus finite replication of **unchanged binary gadgets** does not give the new selection relation. I15 does more than I07's all-binary-direction replication, and it deserves that acknowledgement. I have not reduced the ternary successor relation to a binary majority-closed constraint language.

The relevant reduction enlarges that language using a different permitted source operation: **replace a primitive marked leaf by a punctured-torus stage and repeat the source contraction argument**. The source proof already tells how to construct this enrichment, including its markings and frames. Majority closure says nothing about the cost or novelty of that geometric operation. A barrier for one restricted method cannot establish that every source-level transfer is nonroutine.

Nor is my judgment simply that the new proof is long or uses familiar ingredients. The explicit reduction identifies the preserved local invariant, the precise replacement step, the unchanged shallow cancellation, the arity-free full-module/reflection contract, the nonzero multilinear witness and actual-ideal extraction, and the uniform successor graph. I15's new decisions are the number and arrangement of repeated stages and the finite color table. These are useful design and verification work, but no interface in this chain requires a new four-dimensional construction technique.

I07's suggestion to investigate odd order was a reasonable research direction, not a guarantee that every successful order-three extension would satisfy the substantial gate. I14's precedent likewise requires comparison with source proofs even when no existing theorem states the final extension. On that standard, I15 remains a technically correct, potentially useful extension below the requested threshold.
