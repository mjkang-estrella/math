## Egyptian fractions

[Tenenbaum–Yokota, *Length and Denominators of Egyptian Fractions, III*, JNT 35 (1990), 150–156](https://tenenb.perso.math.cnrs.fr/PPP/Egyptian.pdf). Author-hosted PDF retained as `tenenbaum-yokota-1990.pdf`; all seven pages read through text extraction, with OCR limitations documented in the reading record. The introduction explicitly frames simultaneous length/denominator optimization and attributes a stronger lower obstruction to Yokota's earlier work. This is observed importance evidence for the selected target. The near-linear-cap theorem is not the polynomial-cap/double-logarithmic target. The elementary p-adic inequality proved here is not a substantial advance on that literature. Some displayed OCR is ambiguous, so no exact formula for the earlier lower obstruction is asserted on that basis.

The source's own exact-marker, padding, counting, and rational-divisor results were read in full and excluded as fresh contributions.

## Integral character varieties

[Coccia–Litt, *Density of integral points in the Betti moduli of quasi-projective varieties*, arXiv:2507.00167v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00167v1). Read abstract and introductory Conjecture 1.1.1/Theorem 1.1.2, plus the surface passages around Theorems 5.0.2/5.0.4, Lemma 5.0.3, and Remarks 5.0.5/5.0.6. This is a partial primary reading, not a full-paper proof audit. The rank-two density/dense-orbit constructions already preclude treating rank-two specialization as new. The stated Chevalley-group conjecture gives actual context for arithmetic extensions; no importance ranking is inferred.

The corpus's general-rank diagram density, fixed unit-root field, exact complex boundary strata and determinant-one descent are accepted inputs. A finite-congruence free-group corollary is routine. Preserving a strict boundary stratum over every finite prime or arbitrary admissible local representation data would require a further construction. Neither is proved here. The no-match search for the word “congruence” in Coccia–Litt is not evidence of novelty.
