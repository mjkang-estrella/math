## Lacasse–Grest–Levine

[Deformation of Small Compressed Droplets, arXiv:mtrl-th/9603005v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/mtrl-th/9603005v1), related publication [Physical Review E 54, 5436 (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.54.5436).

Read the rigid-two-plate setup and variational calculation in Section III through equations (9)–(32), and the later displayed relation (33); neighboring numerical discussion was incidental. Equations (24), (27)–(31) and page 5 were visually checked. Their area normalization and displacement normalization give a logarithmically corrected quadratic cost. The derivation uses rotational symmetry and fixed plates. It does not provide the all-finite-perimeter moving-outer-interface theorem in I275. Full paper reading and independent verification of the elliptic asymptotic remainder are not claimed.

## Cunjing Lv

[Effects of geometric confinement on a droplet between two parallel planes, arXiv:1704.05830v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.05830v2).

Read Section 4.2, Section 4.3 through equation (4.16), and the constant-volume Appendix B.2 through (B22). Page 13 was visually checked. Equations (4.9)–(4.15) give explicit small-compression formulas, including logarithmic force-displacement behavior at fixed volume. The external planes are rigid; contact-angle and axial-profile assumptions are part of the model. This confirms the classical nature of the logarithmic scale and is not an exact duplicate of I275. Earlier complete elliptic derivations and the whole paper were not read.
