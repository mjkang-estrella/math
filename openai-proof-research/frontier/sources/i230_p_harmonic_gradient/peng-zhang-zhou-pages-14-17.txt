In this paper, unless other specify, we always assume that Ω is a convex ring, that is, Ω = Ω0 \ Ω1 ,
Ω0 is bounded convex domain and Ω1 is a convex compact subset of Ω0 . We always denote by
u ∈ C 0 (Ω) ∩ W 1,∞ (Ω) the ∞-harmonic potential in Ω. Naturally, one extends u to the whole domain
Rn by setting
                                  u = 1 in Ω1 and u = 0 in Rn \ Ω0 .
For each p ∈ (2, ∞), we always denote by up ∈ C 0 (Ω) ∩ W 1,p (Ω) the p-harmonic potential in Ω, that

                                  up = 1 in Ω1 and up = 0 in Rn \ Ω0 .

not empty set, or equivalently,

                      f (λx + (1 − λ)y) ≥ min{f (x), f (y)}   ∀λ ∈ [0, 1], x, y ∈ Rn .
