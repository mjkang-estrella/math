1007:                                       5. Implications
1008:    As we recalled in Theorem 1.2, the validity of the local logarithmic Brunn-
1009: Minkowski inequality (1.9) for all K P Ksn is equivalent to the validity of the
1010: logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski and the logarithmic Minkowski inequalities. The
1011: proof of these facts is based on several recent deep results on uniqueness in the
1012: Lq -Minkowski problem for q ă 1. While this equivalence does not hold for fixed
1013: K P Ksn , it is explained in [15, §2.4] that the theory behind Theorem 1.2 still yields
1014: nontrivial implications when (1.9) is known to hold in a sufficiently rich sub-class
1015: of Ksn . The aim of the final section of this note is to investigate what conclusions
1016: may be drawn by combining these results with Theorems 1.4–1.5.
