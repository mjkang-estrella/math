119:    A convex body in Rn or a function on S n−1 is called unconditional if it is invariant under
120: reflection in every coordinate hyperplane. For an unconditional C+2 body K, the infimum
121: in (1.1), restricted to unconditional functions, is at least n. This follows from Saroglou’s
122: work [Sar15, Thm. 1.2]; see also Kolesnikov and Milman [KM22, Sec. 8.1] and [HI26a, Thm.
123: 8.12]. Approximation and second variation of the log-Minkowski inequality in [HI26b, Cor.
124: 1.2] establish the stronger result λ1,e ≥ n. The same lower bound n holds for every C+2
125: origin-symmetric zonoid [vH23b, Thm. 1.4].
126:    Böröczky [Bor23] and Huang, Yang, and Zhang [HYZ25] survey the log-Brunn–Minkowski
