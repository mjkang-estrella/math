                                                                                   Abstract
                                                       We study, for 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞, the Hardy space hpe (B), the elastic analogue
                                                  of the classical Hardy spaces of harmonic functions in the unit ball of R3 .
                                                  The space consists of vector-field solutions of the Lamé system satisfying
                                                  the standard integrability condition on concentric spheres centered at the
                                                  origin. Using the elastic Poisson kernel, we establish a Fatou-type theorem
                                                  and show that hpe (B) is isomorphic to the R3 -valued Lebesgue space Lp
                                                  on the unit sphere for 1 < p ≤ ∞, while h1e (B) corresponds to the space
                                                  of R3 -valued Borel measures on the unit sphere.
                                                       For 1 < p < ∞, we prove that hpe (B) decomposes as the direct sum of
                                                  three subspaces. The main contribution of this paper is to describe each of
                                                  these subspaces along with the corresponding spaces of boundary values.
                                                  In particular, two of these spaces consist of solutions of the Lamé equation
                                                  for all eligible choices of the Lamé constants: one of them is the space of
                                                  Riesz fields (solutions of the generalized Cauchy–Riemann equations) in
                                                  hpe (B); the second is the space of fields given by the cross product of x
                                                  with such Riesz fields. The results rely on the classical decomposition of
                                                  L2 vector fields on the sphere into the direct sum of three spaces of vector
                                                  spherical harmonics, which we extend to Lp .

from the Hardy space theory, consisting of the supremum of the Lp norms on
concentric spheres centered at the origin and radius less than one. Since for
λ = −µ the solution of the Lamé equations are identical to the vector-valued
harmonic functions, then these spaces generalize the Hardy spaces of harmonic

in hpe (B) is the R3 -valued Lebesgue Lp space in the sphere for 1 < p ≤ ∞, and
for h1e (B), it corresponds to the space of borel measures in the unit sphere with
values in R3 .
    For the Lamé equation and other applications it is natural to use of certain
vector spherical harmonics (see [7]) that provide a decomposition of the vector
L2 (S) space into three orthogonal subspaces. We will prove that for 1 < p <
∞, a decomposition of the Lp space in the sphere holds as well, leading via
the elastic Poisson transform to a decomposition of hpe (B) as a Banach direct
product of three subspaces. The aim of this paper is the precise description of
each of these spaces. This will provide insight of the local structure of the Lamé
equation.
    Remarkably, one of them will consist of Riesz fields, namely fields u ∈ hpe (B)
that satisfy the generalized Cauchy-Riemann equations div u = 0 and ∇×u = 0.
It is well known the relevance of these fields in harmonic analysis. These vector
fields are common elements of hpe (B) for every admissible values of λ and µ.
Moreover in this case, the vector field x × u ∈ hpe (B) for such λ and µ.
    The paper is organized as follows: In Sections 2 and 3 we will be dedicated
to give definitions and to state all the preliminary results needed in the paper
like Sobolev space and distributions in the sphere. In Section 4 we will study
the basic structure of the elastic Hardy spaces including the elastic Poisson
transform and we will prove Fatou theorems for these Hardy spaces. In Section
5 we will review the mentioned vector-valued spherical harmonics decomposition
and we will prove the decompositions of the vector Lp spaces of the sphere.
In Section 6 we complete the aim of this paper: to characterize the elastic
extensions of vector fields on each of the Lp subspaces defined in Section 5. This
will be achieved after describing in detail the three boundary Lp subspaces.
