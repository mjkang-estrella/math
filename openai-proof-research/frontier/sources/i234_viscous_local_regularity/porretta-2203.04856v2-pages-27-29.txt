6.3    Weak solutions, extensions and further comments
We conclude with a few further remarks.

A further estimate for optimal transport. It is natural to ask what happens if we drop the strict
positivity condition on the marginals m0 , m1 , together with letting the entropy term vanish, or even inde-
pendently. Alternatively, one can wonder whether the positivity estimates for m, or the gradient estimates
for u, can be localized in time. This seems to be an interesting question. In this direction, we only give the
following estimate for the case of pure transport with entropy. It suggests, roughly speaking, that u should
remain smooth at least on the support of m, giving a precise quantitative estimate. Let us stress that this
result only requires boundedness of the marginals m0 , m1 , and the estimate is independent of ε.

Remark 6.6 As we mentioned, the above estimate says that, once we drop the positivity condition on
the marginals, u should remain smooth on the support of m. But unfortunately, we do not have precise
informations on the behavior of the support of the solution in that case (at least in dimension d > 1).
This issue is possibly related to the regularity of solutions in critical sets for degenerate quasilinear elliptic
problems.
