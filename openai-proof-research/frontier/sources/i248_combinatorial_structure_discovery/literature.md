## External primary comparison

**Ferber–Luh–Nguyen, Embedding Large Graphs into a Random Graph**, [arXiv:1606.05923v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05923v3), PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.05923v3. Read the complete relevant argument: §2.4, Lemmas 2.5 and 2.7, their proofs through the end of §2.4, plus the introductory hypotheses, decomposition lemma, and final assembly. Retained a text excerpt of all of §2.4.

This is serious prior art, not merely a citation for the matching theorem. The authors embed small connected blocks by assigning to each block an s-uniform hypergraph of compatible images, check large matching number for every subfamily, and use the Aharoni–Haxell criterion. Their §2.4 root sets W_ij are disjoint across different blocks, after a distance-three decomposition; bounded maximum degree and density restrictions control their Janson estimates. I248 does not claim that rooted-block hypergraphs, hypergraph Hall, or independent sprinkling are new.

The distinction requiring independent contribution review is I248's uniform cross-product comparison of ALL rooted-pattern packing ranks, allowing arbitrary overlaps/repetitions of the root-neighborhood requirements and s growing with n. It obtains that comparison from a compressed maximum-weight packing and exchangeable optimum matchings. No identical theorem was found in this bounded search; that fact is not a priority certificate. I248's density condition is deliberately much stronger than optimal fixed-pattern thresholds, and its nonroot support in the embedding application is a small fixed fraction. A contribution reviewer may judge these restrictions to outweigh the new uniform operation.

**Aharoni–Haxell**, Hall's theorem for hypergraphs, J. Graph Theory 35 (2000), 83–88, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/1097-0118(200010)35:2%3C83::AID-JGT2%3E3.0.CO;2-V. The exact contract used is reproduced as Theorem 2.6 of Ferber–Luh–Nguyen: for d-uniform L_i, the inequalities ν(∪_{i∈I}L_i)>d(|I|−1) for every nonempty I imply disjoint representatives. The original publisher landing page was accessible but did not expose its full proof in this session. Therefore I248 does NOT claim to have read the original topological proof. The primary FLN application proof was fully read. The nominated rank theorem itself does not depend on Aharoni–Haxell; only its application does.

**Ascoli–He–Park–Talagrand**, [A reformulation of the discrete Convexity Conjecture via k-thresholds](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11183v1), §8 read in full. Conjecture 8.1 is the O(1)-piece threshold decomposition, already stipulated solved by 175. The general assembly and their original proofs were not needed as mathematical inputs to I248 and are not claimed fully read.
