Appendix B Derivative formulas for \log\rho(t)
Let \rho(t)
be a one parameter family of density matrices twice differentiable
in some neighborhood {\cal N}(0) of t=0 so that for t_{1}\in{\cal N}(0)
there is a neighborhood {\cal N}(t_{1})\subset{\cal N}(0) in which
\displaystyle\rho(t)=\rho(t_{1})+(t-t_{1})\rho^{\prime}(t_{1})+O(t-t_{1})^{2}.
(B.1)
and \rho(t) has full rank.
To find the derivative of \log\rho(t) we begin with
the integral representation
\displaystyle\log\rho=\lim_{M\rightarrow\infty}\int_{0}^{M}\Big(\frac{1}{1+u}-\frac{1}{\rho+u}\Big)du
(B.2)
which is valid when \rho has full rank.
Then
\displaystyle\log\rho(t_{2})-\log\rho(t_{1})=\int_{0}^{\infty}\Big(\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}-\frac{1}{\rho(t_{2})+uI}\Big)du
(B.3)
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}\big[\rho(t_{2})-\rho(t_{1})\big]\frac{1}{\rho(t_{2})+uI}du
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle(t_{2}-t_{1})\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}\rho^{\prime}(t_{1})\frac{1}{\rho(t_{2})+uI}du+O(t_{2}-t_{1})^{2}
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle(t_{2}-t_{1})\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}\rho^{\prime}(t_{1})\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}du+O(t_{2}-t_{1})^{2}
where we used A^{-1}-B^{-1}=A^{-1}(B-A)B^{-1}. Then
\displaystyle\frac{d~}{dt}\log\rho(t)\Big|_{t=t_{1}}
\displaystyle\equiv
\displaystyle\lim_{t_{2}\rightarrow t_{1}}\frac{\log\rho(t_{2})-\log\rho(t_{1})}{t_{2}-t_{1}}
(B.4)
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}\rho^{\prime}(t_{1})\frac{1}{\rho(t_{1})+uI}du
Next, we rewrite (B.4) with \rho(0)=\rho and \rho^{\prime}(0)=\Gamma
\displaystyle L_{\log\rho}-R_{\log\rho}
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\bigg[\Gamma\frac{1}{\rho+uI}-\frac{1}{\rho+uI}\Gamma\bigg]du
(B.5)
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho+uI}[\rho\Gamma-\Gamma\rho]\frac{1}{\rho+uI}du
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle\big(L_{\rho}-R_{\rho}\big)\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\rho+uI}\,\Gamma\,\frac{1}{\rho+uI}du~.
Then dividing by L_{\rho}-R_{\rho} and using L_{\log\rho}=\log L_{\rho} gives (16) with \Delta_{\rho}=L_{\rho}R_{\rho}^{-1} .
Since \frac{\log x}{x-1} can be defined by continuity at x=1,
\frac{\log\Delta_{\rho}}{L_{\rho}-R_{\rho}}(W)=R_{\rho}^{-1}\frac{\log\Delta_{\rho}}{\Delta_{\rho}-I}(W)=\rho^{-1}W
when L_{\rho}W=R_{\rho}W.
