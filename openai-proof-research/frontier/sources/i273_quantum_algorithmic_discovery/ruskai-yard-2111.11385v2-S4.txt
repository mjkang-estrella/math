4 First derivative essentially additive
We now assume that
S(X^{2})\big|_{X\in{\cal K}^{o}_{B}} and S(X^{2})\big|_{X\in{\cal K}^{o}_{C}} have local minima
at X_{B}>0 and X_{C}>0 so that
D_{1}[X_{B},Y_{B}]=D_{1}[X_{C},Y_{C}]=0 for all Y_{B}\in{\cal K}_{B}^{o}\cap X_{B}^{\perp} and Y_{C}\in{\cal K}_{C}^{o}\cap X_{C}^{\perp}.
Then for all Y_{BC}\in({\cal K}_{B}\otimes{\cal K}_{C})^{o}\cap(X_{B}\otimes X_{c})^{\perp}
\displaystyle D_{1}[X_{B}\otimes X_{C},Y_{BC}]
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle-\hbox{\rm Tr}_{BC}\big[(X_{B}\otimes X_{C})Y_{BC}^{\dagger}+Y_{BC}(X_{B}\otimes X_{C})^{\dagger}\big]\log X_{B}^{2}\otimes X_{C}^{2}
(29)
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle-\hbox{\rm Tr}_{B}\,\big(X_{B}\widetilde{Y}_{B}^{\dagger}+\widetilde{Y}_{B}X_{B}\big)\log X_{B}^{2}-\hbox{\rm Tr}_{C}\,\big(X_{C}\widetilde{Y}_{C}^{\dagger}+\widetilde{Y}_{C}X_{C}\big)\log X_{C}^{2}
\displaystyle=
\displaystyle D_{1}[X_{B},\widetilde{Y}_{B}]+D_{1}[X_{C},\widetilde{Y}_{C}]=0+0~=~0
where \widetilde{Y}_{B}=\hbox{\rm Tr}_{C}\,X_{C}Y_{BC} and \widetilde{Y}_{C}=\hbox{\rm Tr}_{B}\,X_{B}Y_{BC} and, e.g.,
\hbox{\rm Tr}_{B}X_{B}\widetilde{Y}_{B}^{\dagger}=\hbox{\rm Tr}_{BC}\,(X_{B}\otimes X_{C})Y_{BC}^{\dagger}=0.
Note that we did not find D_{1}[X_{B}\otimes X_{C},Y_{BC}]=D_{1}[X_{B},Y_{B}]+D_{1}[X_{C},Y_{C}].
Instead we used the fact that the directional derivatives with respect to all allowed perturbations are zero.
We will use a similar strategy to treat the second derivative.
