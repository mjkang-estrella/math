**074. Kakeya in three and four dimensions.** Resolves the Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions and the Hausdorff-dimension conjecture in four. In three dimensions, the radius-<i>δ</i> tube maximal operator maps $`L^3(\mathbb R^3)`$ to $`L^3(S^2)`$ with norm $`O_\varepsilon(\delta^{-\varepsilon})`$ for every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. In four dimensions, every set containing a unit segment in every direction has Hausdorff dimension four.
