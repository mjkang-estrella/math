**078. The three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture.** Resolves the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict range: the Bochner–Riesz multipliers of order <i>δ</i> are bounded on $`L^p(\mathbb R^3)`$ for every $`1\le p\le\infty`$ whenever $`\delta\gt \max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}`$.

&emsp;[Bochner–Riesz multipliers in three dimensions](preprints/Bochner-Riesz-Multipliers-in-Three-Dimensions-September-24-2026/Bochner-Riesz-Multipliers-in-Three-Dimensions-September-24-2026.pdf)

We prove the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict-order formulation. The main result is boundedness of the Bochner–Riesz multipliers on $`L^3(\mathbb R^3)`$ for every positive order. Interpolation and duality then give the full conjectured strict range.
