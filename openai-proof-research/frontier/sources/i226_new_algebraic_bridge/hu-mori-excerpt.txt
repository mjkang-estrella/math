    We first recall the following definition in algebraic geometry.
Definition 1.1. We say that B is a commutative quadratic complete inter-
section of length r in n variables if B = k[u1 , . . . , un ]/(f1 , . . . , fr ) for some
regular sequence f1 , . . . , fr ∈ k[u1 , . . . , un ]2 . We denote by Bn,r the set
of isomorphism classes of commutative quadratic complete intersections of
length r in n variables.

  We propose the following definition for a noncommutative analogue of the
quadratic complete intersection.
Definition 1.4. We say that A is a (Calabi-Yau) quantum quadratic complete
intersection of length r in n variables if A = S/(f1 , . . . , fr ) for some n-
dimensional (Calabi-Yau) quantum polynomial algebra S and some central
regular sequence f1 , . . . , fr ∈ Z(S)2 . We denote by An,r the set of isomor-
phism classes of Calabi-Yau quantum quadratic complete intersections of
length r in n variables.
  One of the goals of this project is to study and classify Calabi-Yau quan-
tum hypersurfaces, namely, algebras in An,1 . It turns out that Clifford
quadratic complete intersections are special (and very interesting) cases in
An,r when n is odd (Corollary 3.18). In this paper, we focus on studying
Clifford quadratic complete intersections defined as follows.
