## Primary prior art actually read

Only the listed pages are retained, as complete text extracts. The remaining pages were transiently processed for extraction but were not retained or represented as read.

* Jeff Mermin and Satoshi Murai, *The Lex-Plus-Powers Conjecture holds for pure powers*, Adv. Math. 226 (2011), 3511–3539. [Author manuscript](https://math.okstate.edu/people/mermin/papers/the_lex_plus_powers_conjecture_holds_for_pure_powers.pdf); [retained excerpt](mermin-murai-excerpt.txt), PDF pp.1–3, 26–30, all read. Theorem 9.2 is the exact pure-powers consecutive-cancellation input; its proof and Lemma 9.3 are included. Conjecture 10.2 is the monomial-reduction problem already solved by the pinned source. Conjecture 10.5 concerns Betti numbers over the complete-intersection quotient, a different problem from C226. We do not claim it remains open: Murai–Peeva's subsequent paper below addresses it. Theorem 8.1 is cited through the full statement of Theorem 9.2 and the introduction; its separate proof is not claimed read.
* David Eisenbud, Irena Peeva and Frank-Olaf Schreyer, *Quadratic complete intersections*, J. Algebra 571 (2021), 15–31. [Published primary PDF](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10274618); [retained excerpt](eps-excerpt.txt), PDF pp.1–6 (printed pp.15–20), all read. Theorem 2.1, its complete proof and the opening of Corollary 2.2 were read. Their generic Clifford algebra is division when the quadrics cut out the origin, and it yields large matrix-factorization constraints. This is direct overlap with the size-obstruction mechanism. Their coefficients are independent generic parameters; Q226 explicitly handles specialized noncentral coefficients by an internal field and the actual rank. That distinction is stated without declaring novelty.
* Haigang Hu and Izuru Mori, *Clifford quadratic complete intersections*, [arXiv:2302.04446v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.04446v1). [Retained excerpt](hu-mori-excerpt.txt), pp.1–4, all read. Definition 1.5, Remark 1.7 and Theorem 1.9 make the classical Clifford relation and quadratic-dual correspondence explicit. The general correspondence is not a new bridge of I226. Only this identified version and excerpt are claimed read.
* Aldo Conca, *Gröbner bases for spaces of quadrics of codimension 3*, [arXiv:0709.3917v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.3917v2). [Retained excerpt](conca-excerpt.txt), pp.1–2, all read. Theorem 1.1(2) already identifies complete intersections of three quadrics with no linear square as the relevant failure of G-quadraticity. The written example in I226 is a transparent diagnostic within that known class, not a novelty claim.

## Search limits and inference

Focused searches covered LPP consecutive cancellation, pure-power reductions, GHP quotient Betti numbers, scalar Gröbner degenerations, quadratic Clifford algebras, and matrix-size obstructions. Search results pointing to mutable `openai/math/main` were not used as accepted inputs: the actual dependencies are the pinned copies above. Search-engine crawl dates were not treated as publication dates.

No worldwide novelty claim follows from the absence of an exact match for Q226/G226. The no-square obstruction and the general Clifford mechanism have direct prior art. Whether the precise auxiliary-size divisibility is recorded elsewhere remains unresolved; even its absence would not automatically make this application substantial.
