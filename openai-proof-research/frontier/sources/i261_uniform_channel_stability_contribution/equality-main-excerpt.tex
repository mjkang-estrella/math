% Reviewer paraphrase: finite Hermitian PSD potentials with integrable p-th trace power; gamma in (1/2,3/2). The displayed classification is exhaustive up to constant unitary conjugation, with independent positive scales and real centers. Each active channel has sole negative eigenvalue -a_j^2.
\begin{equation}\label{intro:sharp}
 \Tr(H_W)_-^\gamma\le C_\gamma\int_\R\tr(W^p),
 \qquad
 C_\gamma=\left(\frac{\gamma-1/2}{\gamma+1/2}\right)^{\gamma-1/2}
       \frac{\Gamma(\gamma+1)}{\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(\gamma+3/2)}.
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}\label{intro:classification}
 W(x)=U\diag\bigl(w_1(x),\ldots,w_k(x),0,\ldots,0\bigr)U^*,
 \quad
 w_j(x)=(r+1)a_j^2\sech^2\!\bigl(ra_j(x-x_j)\bigr).
\end{equation}
