AN EXPLICIT SIEVE BOUND AND SMALL VALUES OF σ(φ(m))


                                        Kevin Ford

        Abstract. We prove an explicit sieve upper bound based on the large sieve of
        Montgomery and Vaughan [MV], and apply it to show that σ(φ(m)) ⩾ m/39.4 for
        all positive integers m.
