    Abstract. We answer a question of H. Furstenberg on the pointwise convergence of
the averages
                                  N
                               1 X n
                                     U (f · Rn (g)),
                              N n=1
where U and R are positive operators. We also study the pointwise convergence of the
averages
                                   N
                                1 X
                                      f (S n x)g(Rn x)
                               N n=1
when T and S are measure preserving transformations.

    Introduction. Throughout this paper we will denote by (X, B, µ) a
finite Lebesgue measure space. An operator V on Lp (µ) is said to be positive
if f ≥ 0 implies V f ≥ 0. We denote by U and R two positive linear operators
on Lp (µ), 1 < p < ∞, such that supn∈Z kU n kp < ∞ and supn∈Z kRn kp
< ∞. We assume that their inverses U −1 and R−1 are also positive. This
implies (see [Ka]) that there are nonsingular transformations φ and θ and
functions ω and ∆ such that U (f )(x) = ω·f (φ)(x) and R(f )(x) = ∆·f (θ)(x)
for all functions f in Lp (µ). We will also consider two dynamical systems
(X, B, µ, T ) and (X, B, µ, S), where T : X → X and S : X → X are measure
preserving transformations. The present paper is motivated by the following
two questions of H. Furstenberg [F]:
