Claimed by OpenAI. The manuscript claims positive metric entropy with respect to normalized torus area for the exact standard sine map at every sufficiently large positive parameter. Its stated coordinate conjugacy identifies that parameter with the page’s lambda. This addresses the large-positive-parameter subcase of Sinai’s conjecture; small or negative parameters, ergodicity and a uniform entropy lower bound are not claimed.
