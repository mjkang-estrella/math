Frozen working objections, before final report (2026-10-09).
1. Source operations, not titles, delimit novelty. A first-crossing proof may already imply h/log K -> 1. Audit zero-exponent uses before nominating any logarithmic lower bound.
2. Additive entropy lower bound requires all times at fixed parameter. Finite-time lower bounds on subadditive growth are NOT lower bounds on entropy.
3. Projective expansion requires joint position/direction control; area stationarity makes adjacent positions independent, not position and tangent slope.
4. Fast-bridge estimate has an event indicator and constants depending on threshold/Lipschitz data. Removing the event or shrinking thresholds with parameter is unpaid.
5. Weak mixing excludes atoms in spectral measures, not non-Rajchman continuous spectra. Exact eigenfunction rigidity is not a resolvent estimate for approximate eigenfunctions.
6. Nilsequence convergence follows from known pointwise-to-Wiener-Wintner transfer; no new finding from this alone. Fixed observables and one common set over weights must be kept distinct from arbitrary observables.
7. Equivalent-to-volume invariant densities need not be smooth. A rough change of coordinates cannot preserve the source smooth-model hypotheses.
8. Knill math/9908014v2 explicitly deletes an incorrect proof: its lower bound is a conjecture, not a premise. Random perturbations and increasing-parameter maps are different systems.
9. Corpus search outputs compared as line multisets. Mutable front-page drift reported separately; no previous seals or scripts changed.
