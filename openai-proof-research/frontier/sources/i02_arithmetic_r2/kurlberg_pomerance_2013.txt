                          Pär Kurlberg and Carl Pomerance

     For coprime integers g and n, let `g (n) denote the multiplicative order of g
     modulo n. Motivated by a conjecture of Arnold, we study the average of `g (n)
     as n ≤ x ranges over integers coprime to g, and x tending to infinity. Assuming
     the generalized Riemann Hypothesis, we show that this average is essentially as
     large as the average of the Carmichael lambda function. We also determine the
     asymptotics of the average of `g ( p) as p ≤ x ranges over primes.
