                 3. U NIFORM ESTIMATES FOR THE PLANAR INFINITY-L APLACIAN
   In this section, we prove Theorem 1.3. Infinity-harmonic functions are understood in the
viscosity sense. The passage from qualitative regularity to a uniform estimate requires particular
care for the infinity-Laplacian. Subtracting a nonconstant affine function changes the equation,
and unbounded affine shifts can converge to an equation that controls only one direction.
We instead rescale the solution about its value at the center. Compactness gives a uniform
improvement while the normalized central gradient is small; once that gradient reaches a fixed
threshold, a quantitative estimate near affine functions completes the argument.
