Abstract:We present a construction with seven points on a circle that is not Ramsey, disproving a conjecture of Erdős, Graham, Montgomery, Rothschild, Spencer and Straus.
Comments:
This manuscript was written entirely by ChatGPT. I (Dömötör Pálvölgyi) contributed nothing to the proofs or ideas, I only gave suggestions about the presentation. I also wrote some remarks about the proof, which can be found right before the Appendix, which contains further results by ChatGPT that I have not verified
