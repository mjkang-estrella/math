Title:Almost all sets of d+2 points on the (d-1)-sphere are not subtransitive

Abstract:We generalise an argument of Leader, Russell, and Walters to show that almost all sets of d + 2 points on the (d - 1)-sphere S^{d-1} are not contained in a transitive set in some R^n.
