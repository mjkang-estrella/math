Title:One-point extensions of Euclidean Ramsey sets
Authors:Mostafa Mirabi
View a PDF of the paper titled One-point extensions of Euclidean Ramsey sets, by Mostafa Mirabi
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:Let $X$ be a finite Euclidean Ramsey set. We prove that adjoining any point outside the affine hull of $X$ gives another Euclidean Ramsey set, answering a conjecture of Ivan, Leader, and Walters. We first give an elementary product proof under the additional assumptions that the orthogonal projection of the new point lies in $conv(X)$ and that its distance from $aff(X)$ is sufficiently large. We then prove the general case by combining the product theorem for $E$-Ramsey configurations and Kř\'ıž's orbit-gluing theorem with a cyclic construction. No transitivity assumption on $X$ is needed.
