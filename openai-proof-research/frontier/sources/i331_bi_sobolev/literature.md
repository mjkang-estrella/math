3. **Aldo Pratelli, On the bi-Sobolev planar homeomorphisms and their approximation.** [Author PDF](https://cvgmt.sns.it/media/doc/paper/2782/biSobolev.pdf), [arXiv:1509.01045](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01045). Theorem A and Section 4 give simultaneous planar bi-W¹,¹ approximation. Theorem 3.3 and equations (17)–(18) supply the energy identity used to pay the complement. Dimension and exponent restrictions are essential for this comparison.

4. **Daniel Campbell, Luigi D’Onofrio, Tomáš Vítek, Diffeomorphic approximation of piecewise affine homeomorphisms.** [Primary text, arXiv:2507.02854](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02854), [2026 publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12220-026-02398-w). Theorem A permits simultaneous W¹,p/W¹,q smoothing of a locally finite PL homeomorphism in dimensions 3 and 4, for finite p,q≥1. Section 3 fixes local forward/inverse bounds before shrinking modification regions. It is a final-stage interface, not arbitrary-input density.

5. **Marianna Csörnyei, Stanislav Hencl, Jan Malý, Homeomorphisms in the Sobolev space W¹,n−1.** [Author preprint](https://www2.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/kma-preprints/2007-pap/2007-252.pdf), [published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/CRELLE.2010.057), J. Reine Angew. Math. 644 (2010), 221–235. Numbering here follows the inspected author preprint: Theorem 1.3 (N on almost every hyperplane), Theorem 4.4 (coarea), Theorem 4.5 (inverse finite distortion when the inverse is Sobolev), Section 2 (full-measure Lipschitz decomposition). Theorem 4.5 is the decisive primary inverse-map input for the obstruction correction.

6. **Marcos Oliva, Bi-Sobolev homeomorphisms f with Df and Df⁻¹ of low rank using laminates.** [Primary text, arXiv:1607.02972](https://arxiv.org/html/1607.02972), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00526-016-1080-x), Calc. Var. PDE 55 (2016), article 135. Theorems 1–4 and the sharpness proofs were inspected. The construction supplies p,q<2, not the spatial bi-W¹,² endpoint needed here. Its equality discussion does not construct that endpoint.

Acad. Sci. Fenn. Math. 36 (2011), 593–602. Read the introduction and Theorems 1.1–1.3. Its criticality discussion concerns R²; no n=3 conclusion is imported from it.
