The essential foundational materials for the analysis of the Mobius function are
covered in Section 2. The proof of Theorem 1.1 for the summatory Mobius function
over the shifted primes is assembled in Subsection 2.4, and the proof for the Mobius
autocorrelation function over the shifted primes is assembled in Subsection 2.11.
The essential foundational materials for the analysis of the Liouville function are
covered in Section 3. The proof of Theorem 1.2 for the summatory Liouville func-
tion over the shifted primes is assembled in Subsection 3.4. The proof of Theorem
1.4 for the Liouville autocorrelation function over the shifted primes appears in Sub-
section 3.8

For completeness some results on the Mobius autocorrelation function and the Liou-
ville autocorrelation function over the integers, based on the sign patterns technique,
are included in Section 5.1 and Section 7 respectively.
