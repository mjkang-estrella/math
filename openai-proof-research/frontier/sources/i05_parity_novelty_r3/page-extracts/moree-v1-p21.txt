7     Value distribution of Ramanujan sums
We discuss the value distribution of cn (m) as n varies over the integers and m
is fixed. We do this following Aurel Wintner’s paper [25]. Wintner argues that
the prime number theorem is equivalent with the Möbius function having an
asymptotic distribution function and notes that cn (1) = µ(n). A natural question
arising then is whether cn (m) for m fixed has an asymptotic distribution function
and if so, what it looks like. We next recall some facts regarding moments and
distribution functions.
