of [11] (or [14]). Both proofs begin with Fourier-analytic identities, namely [11, Lemma 2.1]
and Proposition 3.2 below. The latter allows one to study mixed 2-point correlations on
average, for some functions f and g which satisfy certain technical hypotheses. Crucially, in
Proposition 3.2 having qualitative cancellation for the exponential sum of f on average over
short intervals [x, x + H] suffices, whereas in [11, Lemma 2.1] one needs to save a factor
