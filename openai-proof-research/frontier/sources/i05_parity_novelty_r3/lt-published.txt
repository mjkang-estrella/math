            Abstract
            There has been recent interest in a hybrid form of the celebrated conjectures of Hardy–Littlewood and of Chowla.
            We prove that for any 𝑘, ℓ ≥ 1 and distinct integers ℎ2 , . . . , ℎ 𝑘 , 𝑎 1 , . . . , 𝑎 ℓ , we have:

            for all except 𝑜(𝐻) values of ℎ1 ≤ 𝐻, so long as 𝐻 ≥ (log 𝑋) ℓ+𝜀 . This improves on the range 𝐻 ≥ (log 𝑋) 𝜓 (𝑋 ) ,
            𝜓(𝑋) → ∞, obtained in previous work of the ﬁrst author. Our results also generalise from the Möbius function 𝜇
            to arbitrary (non-pretentious) multiplicative functions.
