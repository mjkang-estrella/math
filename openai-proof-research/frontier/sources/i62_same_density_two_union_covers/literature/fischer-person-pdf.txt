                                                  FURTHER REMARKS ON FRACTIONAL VS. EXPECTATION THRESHOLDS

                                                                               THOMAS FISCHER AND YURY PERSON


                                                   Abstract. A conjecture of Talagrand (2010) states that the so-called expectation and fractional ex-
                                                   pectation thresholds are always within at most some constant factor from each other. In this note we
                                                   generalize a method of DeMarco and Kahn in [1] and settle a few more special cases.
