                                                  Abstract. In this paper we analyze the behavior of the distance function under Ricci
                                                  flows whose scalar curvature is uniformly bounded. We will show that on small time-
                                                  intervals the distance function is 12 -Hölder continuous in a uniform sense. This implies
                                                  that the distance function can be extended continuously up to the singular time.
