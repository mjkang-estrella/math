## 5. Route two — convex aggregation of equal-subset nef classes

Let q₀=r₀(X,L) be a source equal-weight threshold. For each fixed r≥q₀ choose a tuple simultaneously good for every subset S of size q≥q₀. \[

\]

is the pullback of the nef class on the subset blowup and is nef. This establishes a concrete convex feasibility criterion, not a claim that all volume-admissible vectors lie in that region.

A readily usable sufficient scalar test results by sorting w₁≥⋯≥w_r>0 and putting w_(r+1)=0. Raise the first q₀ entries to w₁, leaving the rest unchanged. Decompose that dominating vector into initial-segment indicators. The coefficient on q₀ entries is w₁−w_(q₀+1); that on q>q₀ entries is w_q−w_(q+1). Consequently D(w) is nef if

\[
 \mathcal C(w)=V^{-1/n}\left[
 q_0^{1/n}(w_1-w_{q_0+1})+
 \sum_{q=q_0+1}^{r}q^{1/n}(w_q-w_{q+1})\right]\le1.       \tag{C1}
\]

All constants are explicit once the qualitative source threshold is supplied. This is a finite construction and works on one common tuple. It needs no stronger claim about all subspaces, Hilbert-scheme boundedness or uniform families.

**A separating inequality for the entire convex method.** Even grant all equal-subset vectors for 1≤q≤r, stronger than the actual source. In dimension three with r=2N, put y=(4,…,4,1,…,1), each repeated N times. The largest dot product with a size-q generator is

\[
 V^{1/3}\begin{cases}4q^{2/3},&q\le N,\\
 (q+3N)q^{-1/3},&N\le q\le2N.
 \end{cases}
\]

The first branch is increasing. The second decreases until 3N/2 and then increases, so its maximum is at an endpoint. Its value at 2N is smaller than at N because 5/2^(1/3)<4, equivalently 125<128. Thus every vector in the downward convex hull satisfies

\[
 y\cdot w\le4V^{1/3}N^{2/3}.                              \tag{C2}
\]

For the actual strictly volume-admissible vector (J3), y·w=9Nb_N=(99/100)9^(2/3)V^(1/3)N^(2/3), which is larger: cubing gives (99/100)³·81>64. Hence even the enlarged convex method fails on this family, although its maximum weight tends to zero. This is a constraint-respecting obstruction to the method, **not a nonnefness example**. The weights might well satisfy W by another construction.

The route pays a common-locus, all-subsets convex positivity theorem and an exact dual obstruction. To prove W one would need genuinely new unequal-weight nef classes or a nonlinear geometric construction beyond aggregation of equal ones. Renaming the weighted jet-transfer lemma would not overcome (C2).
