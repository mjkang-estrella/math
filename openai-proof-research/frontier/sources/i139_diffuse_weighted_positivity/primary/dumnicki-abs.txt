Title:Reduction method for linear systems of plane curves with base fat points
Authors:Marcin Dumnicki
View a PDF of the paper titled Reduction method for linear systems of plane curves with base fat points, by Marcin Dumnicki
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract: In the paper we develop a new method of proving non-speciality of a linear system with base fat points in general position. Using this method we show that the Hirschowitz-Harbourne Conjecture holds for systems with base points of equal multiplicity bounded by 42.
