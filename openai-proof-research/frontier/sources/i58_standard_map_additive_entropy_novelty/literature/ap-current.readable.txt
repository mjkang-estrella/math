Title:The Avalanche Principle and other estimates on Grassmann manifolds
Authors:Silvius Klein, Pedro Duarte
View a PDF of the paper titled The Avalanche Principle and other estimates on Grassmann manifolds, by Silvius Klein and Pedro Duarte
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:The main result of this paper, called the Avalanche Principle (AP), relates the expansion of a long product of matrices with the product of expansions of the individual matrices. This principle was introduced by M. Goldstein and W. Schlag in the context of ${\rm SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$ matrices. Besides extending the AP to matrices of arbitrary dimension, possibly non-invertible, the geometric approach we use here provides a relation between the most expanding (singular) directions of such a long product of matrices and the corresponding singular directions of the first and last matrices in the product. The AP along with other estimates on the action of matrices on Grassmann manifolds will play a fundamental role in [6] to establish the continuity the Lyapunov exponents and of the Oseledets decomposition for linear cocycles.
This is the draft of a chapter in our forthcoming research monograph [6].
