Sinai's positive entropy conjecture for the standard mapAt least 26 years old · documented by 1999
For each parameter λ\lambdaλ, define the standard torus diffeomorphism
fλ(x,y)=(2x−y+λsin⁡2πx,x).f_\lambda(x,y)=(2x-y+\lambda\sin 2\pi x,x).fλ​(x,y)=(2x−y+λsin2πx,x).
Sinai's conjecture. For every parameter λ≠0\lambda\ne 0λ=0, the metric entropy of fλf_\lambdafλ​ is positive. This is presented as another famous positive-entropy conjecture; the source does not report a resolution.References
Primary source
Marie-Claude Arnaud, “Berger And Turaev's Proof of Herman's Conjecture of Positive Entropy”, arXiv:2003.09329 (2020).
Additional references
4 papers in this index state this conjecture (1999–2020). The statement above is taken from the most recent of them; the others are arXiv:2003.00236, arXiv:1704.02473, arXiv:math/9908014.
