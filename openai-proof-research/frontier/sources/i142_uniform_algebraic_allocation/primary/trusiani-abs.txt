Title:Multipoint Okounkov bodies
Authors:Antonio Trusiani
View a PDF of the paper titled Multipoint Okounkov bodies, by Antonio Trusiani
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:Starting from the data of a big line bundle $L$ on a projective manifold $X$ with a choice of $N\geq 1$ different points on $X$ we provide a new construction of $N$ Okounkov bodies which encodes important geometric features of ($L\to X,p_{1},\dots,p_{N}$) such as the volume of $L$, the (moving) multipoint Seshadri constant of $L$ at $p_{1},\dots,p_{N}$, and the possibility to construct Kähler packings centered at $p_{1},\dots,p_{N}$. Toric manifolds and surfaces are examined in detail.

Final version, to appear in "Annales de l'Institut Fourier"
