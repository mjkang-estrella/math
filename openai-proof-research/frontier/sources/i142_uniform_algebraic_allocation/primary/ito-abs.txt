Title:Okounkov bodies and Seshadri constants
Authors:Atsushi Ito
View a PDF of the paper titled Okounkov bodies and Seshadri constants, by Atsushi Ito
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:Okounkov bodies, which are closed convex sets defined for big line bundles, have rich information on the line bundles. On the other hand, Seshadri constants are invariants which measure the positivity of line bundles. In this paper, we prove that Okounkov bodies give lower bounds of Seshadri constants.
