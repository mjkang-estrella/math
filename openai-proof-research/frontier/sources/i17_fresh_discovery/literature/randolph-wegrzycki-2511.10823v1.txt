Technical Overview
In this section we overview the conceptual ingredients used in our results. One (the representation technique) is familiar but needs an introduction to provide context for the others. Two (mixing dichotomies and coefficient shifting) are original, and two (representations admitting pseudosolutions and compatibility certificates) generalize ideas implicit in existing work.
