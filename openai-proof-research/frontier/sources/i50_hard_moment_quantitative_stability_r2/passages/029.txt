     An absolutely-continuous probability measure on Rn is called log-concave if
 it is supported on an open, convex set K ⊂ Rn , and its density takes the form
 exp(−ρ) where the function ρ : K → R is convex. An important example of a log-
 concave measure is the uniform probability measure on a convex body in Rn . Here
 we assume that µ is log-concave and furthermore, we require that the following

(2) The convex set K ⊂ Rn is bounded, the function ρ is C ∞ -smooth, and ρ and its
