4.3. Pinching estimates via Bochner formula. In this subsection, we prove the so-called
pinching estimates. The result of this section is not new, it was obtained before by Ivaki
and Milman [32]. In the proof, they use deep geometric arguments involving the so-called
centroaffine connection on Sn−1 and a variant of the Bochner formula on Sn−1 . Our approach
is simpler, and is based on the use of the Euclidean Bochner formula. We note that the
constant from [32] is slightly better, because it was improved by additional arguments.
