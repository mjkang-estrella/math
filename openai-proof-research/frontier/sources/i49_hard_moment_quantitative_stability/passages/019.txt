  4. Stability for the Brunn-Minkowski inequality on convex sets
   In this section we prove Theorem 1.2 and discuss its sharpness. To this end we
follow the standard derivation of the Brunn-Minkowski inequality for convex sets
from the anisotropic isoperimetric inequality [HO]. Note first that whenever E
and F are open bounded convex sets containing the origin and G is a set of finite
perimeter, then
                            PE (G) + PF (G) = PE+F (G) .                     (4.1)
This is easily verified by starting from the definition of E + F and of k · k∗ , see (1.1).
