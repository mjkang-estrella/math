# I151 spatial continuation: paid estimates and remaining identification

This is a deduction from stipulated lower operations, not a truth audit of them. In particular the necessary-influence EVENT proof, not merely its displayed signed-kernel bound, is used below. No claim of substantial contribution or priority is attached.

## Model, state space, metric and clocks

Fix the bounded Jordan domain D=(-2,2)^2. All quads below are compactly inside D; its outer square corners impose no percolation boundary condition. Use whole-plane Poisson nuclei of intensity e^-2, frozen thereafter, and independent fair initial colors. A crossing uses the intersections of closed black cells with a quad; there is no boundary wiring, no deletion of cells whose nuclei are outside the quad or D, and no moving nuclei. Quads are homeomorphic images of [0,1]^2 with image compactly contained in D, with the marked sides inherited from that parameterization. Their distance is d_Q(Q1,Q2)=inf_phi sup_{z in boundary[0,1]^2}|Q1(z)-Q2(phi(z))|, where phi is a homeomorphism of the square preserving each of its four corners. Only the boundary enters this sup norm, exactly as in GPS Definition 2.1. A state is a closed hereditary set of crossed quads, with heredity for the usual robust crossing order. The topology is generated by complements of {omega:Q in omega} and by {omega:omega intersects U}, U open in quad space. Hausdorff distance on sets of quads is not this topology.

Here is an exact choice of compatible geometric metric, avoiding identification with the coordinate space X. Use GPS Definitions 3.2–3.3: finite dyadic polygon families Q^k, radius-2^(-k-10) neighborhoods Qhat_k, and the outward harder quads Qbar_k on the (k+10)-grid, with their stated marked-side convention. Put

O_k(a) = intersection_{Q in Q^k, Q notin a}{b:Qbar_k notin b} intersect intersection_{Q in Q^k,Q in a}{b:b intersects Qhat_k},
E_k={(a,b): b in O_k(a), a in O_k(b)}, E_0=H(D)^2.

Fix k_0=0 and recursively choose the least integer k_n>k_(n-1), k_n>=n, for which E_(k_n) composed with itself three times is contained in E_(k_(n-1)). Existence follows from the two uniform-structure implications of GPS Proposition 3.9 and compactness. Write V_n=E_(k_n). Define d_H(a,b) as the infimum of sums sum_i 2^(-n_i) over finite chains a=a_0,...,a_l=b with (a_(i-1),a_i) in V_(n_i). Frink's chain metrization gives a genuine compatible bounded metric. This formula, the dyadic conventions, and the least-integer rule fix our metric. In particular V_n implies d_H<=2^-n. Equality of all crossing bits in Q^(k_n) implies V_n: an actual crossed Q belongs to its open neighborhood, and noncrossing Q forbids its harder Qbar. This is the geometric finite-entourage bridge used below, not a claim that arbitrary vectors in X are geometric states.

Let r_e=1/E N_e(Q*) (annealed unit-square pivotal expectation), q_e=1/E N_e(Q0), with the source r_e/q_e->1/B, B>0. Every nucleus has a rate-r_e refresh Poisson process with independent fair postmark colors. Actual flips occur at half this rate. Histories are right continuous; at a mark use the new color, and use the initial color at zero. Almost surely there are no simultaneous marks among the finitely many cells relevant to a fixed bounded set, nor a mark at a specified time. Repeated marks at one nucleus are permitted.

Tile the whole plane by half-open squares of side delta (dyadic delta suffices). Assign each nucleus v its unique square S(v). Declare v initially delta-important if four alternating arms, not using v's own color, run from the boundary of its cell to the boundary of the concentric triple square 3S(v), inside that square. The definition at abnormally large cells is the literal four-arm event; all comparisons involving small cells below explicitly discard a geometry event. Let I_delta be this initial set. The cutoff configuration applies exactly the same clocks and fair marks as the full process at v in I_delta and ignores the other clocks. Importance is not recomputed. Whole-plane cells and clocks are retained even near the boundary of D. This eliminates a hidden domain-boundary convention; every tested quad is compactly inside D.

## B: an actual supremum-in-time cutoff estimate

Fix a finite family F of polygonal crossing tests, T<infinity, and a bounded box B containing their images with a positive margin. Write a=1-g/2>0 for the source forest exponent, choose any p>1 with gp<2, and put kappa=1-1/p>0. There exist constants C_T,F,B and C_K,T,F,B such that, for every integer K>=1, sufficiently small delta and then e sufficiently small relative to delta,

P(exists t<=T: F(omega_e(t)) != F(omega_e^delta(t)))
 <= C_K,T,F,B (delta+e)^kappa
    + sum_{k>K} C_T,F,B^k k^(-a k)
    + C_B delta^-2 exp[-c_B(delta/e)^2] + C_B exp[-c_B/e^2].                 (B151)

The constants in the last term absorb the fixed positive margin. They are not asserted uniform as F grows or K grows. Thus choose K first, then delta, then e. The conclusion is annealed; dividing the right-hand side by u gives a bound for the probability over eta that its conditional cutoff-error probability exceeds u.

Proof. The box-screen event Gamma ensures that all cells meeting the tested quads have nuclei in B, with complement bounded by the last exponential term. An empty-square cover, including an enlargement around the quads, gives an additional geometry event on which every cell meeting that enlargement has diameter less than delta/100. Its failure probability is at most C_B delta^-2 exp[-c_B(delta/e)^2]. Convexity of Voronoi cells and the nearest-nucleus comparison justify this bound also for nuclei outside the enlargement; no deterministic diameter claim is used. Fixed constants can be adjusted so that the region contains all triple squares under discussion.

Let X be the nuclei in B whose refresh clock rings before T. Conditional on eta, its membership indicators are independent Bernoulli variables of parameter p_e=1-exp(-T r_e), independent of the initial colors. For a scalar Boolean crossing f, regard each coordinate as whether it has been toggled relative to its initial bit. Its exact multilinear expansion at the all-zero toggle configuration is

f(omega_0 toggled on a(t)) = sum_{S subset X} Delta_S f(omega_0) product_{v in S} a_v(t).

The cutoff substitutes a_v(t) 1_{v in I_delta}. Hence every term in the difference contains a v notin I_delta, and its magnitude is at most 2^|S|. A nonzero coefficient requires necessary influence at every selected site: each such bit can change f for some assignments of the other selected bits. These assignments need not agree. This is exactly the EVENT constructed before signed cancellation in the source forest proof. In the probes outside the selected sites the colors are the original fair bits.

If all selected nuclei are farther than C delta from each other and from every polygon side, a necessary influence at v produces four arms out to 3S(v). All other selected cells lie outside this region on the small-cell geometry event. These arms are therefore already present initially outside v, so v is initially important. A contributing subset containing an inactive site must consequently lie in the source bad-location set: a close pair or a C delta boundary strip. This implication pays changing importance and arbitrary sequences of recolorings simultaneously. It is not a monotone differential estimate.

Let E_S be the necessary-influence event under the Palm insertion of the distinct nuclei S. The source's disjoint buffered probes, virtual vertex root, diameter charging and outermost-screen summation bound P(E_S) by C^k a_e^k times the merging-forest product of clipped edge lengths to the power -g. The proof of the factorial integral and of its L^p version applies to this nonnegative EVENT bound itself. For fixed k the scaled L^p norm is bounded, and the bad-location volume is at most C_k(delta+e); pair tubes actually cost order (delta+e)^2 and side strips cost order delta+e. For arbitrary k the normalized factorial integral is at most C^k k^(-a k).

Mecke and P(S subset X)=p_e^k now give the result, since p_e/q_e<=T r_e/q_e is bounded. Absorb 2^k and the finite number of tests into C_T,F,B. The expansion is finite for each geometry inside B; Poisson exponential moments justify the initial expectation/sum interchange. Uniform convergence is then supplied by the forest series, not by multiplying a one-site remainder by the raw site count. The geometry failures were discarded before this summation, so their probabilities are not multiplied by that exponentially large expansion. This also explains why no independence of pivotal sets at different times is assumed. The lattice proof uses the source normalized counting measure and the same forest integral.

For z>0 choose n with 2^-n<z, and take F to be Q^(k_n). The finite-entourage bridge gives

P(sup_{t<=T} d_H(omega_e(t),omega_e^delta(t))>z) <= RHS(B151).             (B151-H)

Consequently the ordered limit in G149 is zero, also quenched in probability by Markov's inequality. This is an estimate in the actual geometric metric for the explicit initial-important-cell same-clock process. It is stronger in this respect than the O(h^2) finite-coordinate tightness argument in D149. It does not alone identify the limiting spatial marks or give convergence of the fixed-delta construction.

Comparison with GPS: their sharp triangular bound is C_T,z delta^2/alpha4(delta,1), with the supremum over time. B151 pays the same removal obligation with a weaker tail-plus-power estimate. Even with exponent 5/4, B151 does not assert a delta^(3/4) rate: C_K is uncontrolled in K and the boundary-strip Holder step loses power. The stronger stipulated all-order event construction is the reason this alternative works. Vanneuville's static derivative bounds alone would not prove it.

## A: a paid spatial pivotal-measure and mark construction at fixed-quad scope

For each fixed polygonal quad Q define the actual measure

mu_Q,e = r_e sum_{v pivotal for Q at omega_0} delta_v.

These are measures on nuclei, not just total pivotal counts. A finite collection of these measures jointly with the initial critical state converges to the correspondingly normalized triangular collection, annealed and quenched in probability. Here quenched refers to the joint random initial coloring/measure law conditional on eta; the limit measures are the GPS quad-pivotal measures, measurable from the limiting critical configuration. The normalization makes E mu_Q*,e(R^2)=1 exactly.

The required extraction from the source history operation is explicit. For k distinct inserted sites use observation labels 0,1,...,k. At site i let L_i be the history which starts with a fair bit and differs from that bit only at label i; put w_i=L_i-P0. This centered signed measure has full variation norm at most 2. Let

G = H(omega^0) product_{i=1}^k 1{ Q_i has different crossing at labels i and 0 },

where H is any bounded function of finitely many critical crossings. In any term where w_i supplies -P0, no site changes at label i, so its factor in G vanishes. The surviving term is exactly H(omega_0) times the product of the pivotal indicators at the k selected sites. Thus the history kernel is this nonnegative mixed pivotal moment (or its H-weighted version), not a signed inequality substituted for one. Spatial continuous test functions multiply the insertion integrals. These histories are generally nonmonotone; the source operation permits them.

Apply complete sewing/calibration to these fixed coefficients, first away from diagonals and polygon boundaries, then use event L^p integrability to remove those cutoffs. The result identifies every mixed spatial factorial moment with the triangular one. Repeated sites in ordinary moments cost additional powers of r_e and vanish; their lower factorial moments are bounded by the same forest operation. For a fixed finite quad family the uniform bound has the form E M_e^k/k! <= C^k k^(-a k), up to the harmless repeated-site partition terms, where M_e is its total mass. Equivalently the factorial generating functions are locally uniformly bounded and the ordinary exponential moments follow since r_e->0. This supplies tightness, uniform integrability and moment determinacy, not just convergence of means. Total mass outside a fixed larger box vanishes by the source localization and polynomial cell-count moments. Boundary lines have zero limiting mass by location integrability; near-diagonal two-point integrability gives diffuse limiting measures.

For two independently colored replicas on the same eta use the source two-replica kernels. Distinct labels at the same inserted site carry its extra q_e; the summable bound makes their total vanish. The triangular comparison has independent replicas. This proves the L2 limit of conditional bounded tests. Compact tightness sets and finite Lipschitz nets then give quenched bounded-Lipschitz convergence of the joint measure/state law. To pass from critical cylinder functions to H, use the finite geometric entourages above to uniformly approximate a continuous H test by a function of finitely many crossing bits. This is a justified geometric step, not identification of all X with H.

Conditional on eta and the initial coloring, the clocks at these pivotal nuclei form a Poisson random measure of intensity mu_Q,e(dx) dt (delta_0+delta_1)/2 on a bounded time interval. Its Laplace functional is exp[-int(1-exp(-f)) dmu_Q,e dt dFair]. The established joint weak-measure convergence therefore gives the corresponding Cox marked measure jointly with the state, also for two replicas. For overlapping quad measures one uses the same nucleus clocks; disjoint tests can first be partitioned by their finite pivotal-membership patterns, whose indicators are Boolean polynomials in the extraction above. This retains shared marks rather than assigning independent clocks to two occurrences of one site. The expected number of repeated-clock pairs at one retained site is at most T^2 r_e E M_e/2 and vanishes. Postmark fair bits retain the flip factor 1/2; they are not monotonically ordered threshold marks.

## Remaining spatial identification: exact model-specific scope

The preceding construction does not yet identify mu_delta,e=r_e sum_{v initially delta-important} delta_v jointly with all connectivity annotations needed to update every geometric quad at its marked sites. A cell can be delta-important without being pivotal for any prescribed finite list of macroscopic quads. Its clock must nevertheless be retained, since another retained change can make it influential. B151 controls the removal of initially unimportant cells, not the omission of these initially important but currently invisible cells.

There is a concrete finite-quad reconstruction route, rather than a new name for the target. In each square S use quads with domain 3S and four boundary marks from an increasingly fine dyadic boundary mesh h. Let J_(S,h) be the union of their color-pivotal sites in S. Planarity gives J_(S,h) subset I_delta. The exact unresolved comparison to complete this route is the joint reconstruction, with retained crossing/connectivity annotations, whose mass part would follow from

limsup_(e->0) E[ r_e sum_{v in K} 1{v in I_delta but v notin J_(S(v),h)} ]
 <= C_K alpha4(delta,1)^(-1) (h/delta)^c,                                (A151-rem)

for fixed delta and compact K, and its annotated version under two replicas sharing eta. Limits must be e->0 first and h->0 afterward. A mass bound alone would permit weak-measure/mark approximation; consistency of the finite networks at the resulting marks must also be checked.

Vanneuville Lemma 2.14 and its proof provide a useful actual operation: at intensity one, for outer radius R>=100/u, P((GI_ext_u(R))^c)<=Cu. It excludes a three-arm event near the outer square boundary and controls the distance of every outgoing interface endpoint from every other interface, not just pairwise endpoint distance. Rescale R=O(delta/e), u=O(h/delta). The restriction e sufficiently small relative to h is essential. Its outer-annulus event can be localized disjointly from an inner four-arm probe at a nucleus in S; the missing mass comparison should then cost r_e e^-2 A_e(b_e,delta) times the boundary-separation error, plus the exact outermost-screen failures. This is the concrete prospective cost in A151-rem. But proving the finite boundary-quad characterization with the full marked network annotation, including landing at polygon corners and cells crossing the artificial square boundary, has not been completed here. It is not certified by the unconditional GI probability, by L2 comparability of arm probabilities, or by the already established fixed-quad measures.

The alternative GPS reconstruction requires normalized conditional counts inside a small box given four exterior faces AND the crossing bit U_Theta. The read GPS proof pays the beta versus betahat comparison by color switching and a coupling of these annotated faces. The Vanneuville operations read here give positive-probability Hex cell connections, arm extension/quasi-multiplicativity, two-replica L2 comparison, and concentration of annular arm probabilities. They do not by themselves identify that annotated conditional mass coefficient. This is the exact limitation of importing GPS's reconstruction verbatim. No claim is made that A151-rem or the annotated reconstruction is false, or absent from every possible consequence of the stipulated sources; it looks technical after the complete source screen.
