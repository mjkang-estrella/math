4     Semidiscrete MMP
In this section, we assume that ν is a centered probability measure on Rd that is not supported on a
hyperplane and that has finite support. The measure ν should be thought of as approximating the
measure µ for which we are trying to solve the MMP, i.e., find mm−1 (µ) (1.5). Then, according
to Theorem 1.3, ψν (1.4) is an approximation of ψµ (modulo a translation). The idea is then to
recast the MMP for the finitely-supported ν as the minimization of an energy over RN . The latter,
in turn, is equivalent to considering piecewise affine competitors for a bona fide MMP. Here, one
makes use of Legendre duality to go back and forth between vectors and such competitors. This
approach gives a solution of the MMP for such ν (Proposition 4.2).
    The discrete problem and its associated energy functional Eν is introduced in subsection 4.1,
and its relation to the original (continuous) MMP and its energy Eν is described in Propositions 4.1
and 4.2. In subsection 4.2, we compute the first and second derivatives of the discrete energy
functional Eν . This is motivated by subsection 4.3, in which we describe a damped Newton method
for solving the discrete MMP.
