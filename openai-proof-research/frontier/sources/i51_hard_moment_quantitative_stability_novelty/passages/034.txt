   In a different direction, equation (1) can be adapted to study the equality cases of LLBM
assuming LLBM is true. We show that for smooth bodies with C 2 support functions, only trivial
equality cases can occur. In a paper [6] which appeared at around the same time as the current
one, a similar result was obtained. While both their and our results are based on calculus of
variations of the deficit of LLBM, their proof proceeds with a PDE method while our proof is
geometric. Our approach also only requires a weaker assumption on the regularity of the body K.

Theorem 1.3. If K ∈ Kns is smooth with C 2 support function and L is origin-symmetric with
C 2 support function satisfying ∆(K, hL ) = 0, then L is a dilate of K; that is, L = cK for some
c ≥ 0.
