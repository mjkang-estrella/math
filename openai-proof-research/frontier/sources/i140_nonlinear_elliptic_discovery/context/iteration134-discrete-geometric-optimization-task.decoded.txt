For a one-coordinate kernel m+bX₁, let J=I(m+bX₁)>0, α=1−m². The source capacity operation gives `D≥αF(J/α)`. Since F″≥4/3 and F(0)=0,

`D−F(J)≥(2/3)J²(α^−1−1)≥(2/3)m²J²`.  (N131)

Indeed F(x)−(2/3)x² is convex and vanishes at zero, so its perspective dominates its value at J. This estimate is valid at every positive one-coordinate information, not only B128's subregion. It is **not** an n-dimensional production bound.

It proves S128 for n=1 (also for a kernel depending on just one coordinate in a larger cube). Put w_min=ψ(ρ₀a₀). If Δ≥w_min/2, use d_a≤4. Otherwise along [0,t], y≥w_min/2. P128 and N131 give `Δ≥(m²w_min²/6)(1−e^(−Kt))/K`; on the compact parameter rectangle this controls m². Orient b≥0. For a fixed b, J(m,b)≥J(0,b)=ψ(b): for m≥0, the derivative of [V(m+b)+V(m−b)]/2−V(m) in b is [V′(m+b)−V′(m−b)]/2≥0. Thus b≤a. If |m|≤(1−a₁)/4, Taylor expansion in m on a fixed interior interval gives 0≤ψ(a)−ψ(b)≤C m². Strong convexity gives (a−b)²≤2[ψ(a)−ψ(b)], hence d_a=m²+(a−b)²≤C′m². For larger |m|, the same conclusion follows from d_a≤4 and its fixed positive lower bound on m². No compactness in a growing-dimensional function space occurs here.

### 3.4 Why this route does not close

T131, C131, A131 and R131b retain real losses with no n-dependent constant. They do not pay for all reserve-region entropy allocations, nor round near-boundary canonical contacts, nor force face coordinate choices to agree through arbitrarily many unequal-mean splits. The low-information general mean reserve can be zero even though N131 is positive for a genuine one-coordinate kernel. Substituting N131 at each node would change its hypothesis and lose the unresolved joining problem. Paying δ²(1−k) is not paying (1−k); summing cannot remove the δ² without a new argument. This is the concrete structural obstruction in the current induction, not a counterexample to S128.

## 4. Route two: soft compression, spike-tolerant L2 coercivity and an entropy-paid backward bridge

### 4.1 Quantitative compression transfer for arbitrary soft kernels

Sort the two values on every coordinate fiber increasingly, one coordinate at a time; call the final function g. Sorting preserves the complete multiset of values, hence mean, M=Eu² and **exact** information. It preserves monotonicity in already sorted coordinates and all other singleton coefficients. Convexity of ψ implies I(Tρg)≥I(Tρu). These statements hold for soft kernels.

Choose i maximizing c=ĝ(i)≥0. Immediately before sorting i write the current function as b+X_i d. Then Ed=b_i=û(i), E|d|=c, and Ed²≤M. Later sorts preserve c. On each noisy outside fiber, the even function `q↦[ψ(B+ρq)+ψ(B−ρq)]/2` has second derivative ≥ρ². Since |Tρd|≤Tρ|d|, the information gain in this step is at least

`(ρ²/2)(||Tρ|d|||₂²−||Tρd||₂²)`.

Jensen and the nonconstant spectral bound yield

`||Tρ|d|||₂²−||Tρd||₂² ≥ (1−ρ²)(c²−b_i²)−ρ²(M−c²)`.

Thus for the total gain L_gain=I(Tρg)−I(Tρu),

`(1−ρ²)(c²−b_i²)≤2L_gain/ρ²+ρ²(M−c²)`.  (Q131)

No Boolean identity Ed²=E|d| is used. Since `d_a(g)=M+a²−2ac≥M−c²`, if d_a(g)≤a²/4 then c≥a/2 and

`d_a(u)≤[1+4ρ²/(1−ρ²)]d_a(g)+8L_gain/[ρ²(1−ρ²)]`.

Otherwise d_a(u)≤4≤16d_a(g)/a₀². Hence throughout the full model

`d_a(u)≤C_comp[d_a(g)+L_gain]`,  (Q131b)

where one valid constant is the maximum of `16/a₀²`, `1+4ρ₁²/(1−ρ₁²)` and `8/[ρ₀²(1−ρ₁²)]`. Also Δ(u)=L_gain+Δ(g). Thus a dimension-independent stability theorem for increasing soft kernels would transfer to all soft kernels with a paid constant. Sorting alone still does not select a coordinate in that increasing class. The companion's soft influence lemmas do not supply that missing conclusion.

### 4.2 Uniform local L2 stability allowing spikes and endpoints

There exist ε_*>0 and c_*>0, depending only on the four endpoints, such that

`I(u)=ψ(a), ||u−σaX_i||₂≤ε_*  ⇒  Δρ(u)≥c_*||u−σaX_i||₂²`.  (L131)

No L∞ proximity or restriction excluding ±1 values is imposed. This is a new-to-this-round local estimate; it is not global S128.

Here is a proof with all truncation and smoothing losses paid. Orient v=aX₁ and put h=u−v, ε=||h||₂, m=Eh. Keep k,c*,D₃ from §1 and let k_min=min k>0. With the Bregman remainder `Bψ(x+z,x)=ψ(x+z)−ψ(x)−V(x)z`, first-variation cancellation gives the exact identity

`G(u)=k E Bψ(v+h,v)−E Bψ(Tρv+Tρh,Tρv)+(1−k)ψ(m)`.

Choose η≤(1−a₁)/2 with D₃η/6≤c*/8. Split h=g+b, where g=h1_{|h|≤η}, b=h1_{|h|>η}; their supports are disjoint. Let p=P(|h|>η)≤ε²/η². For A=1/(1−a²), Taylor on the good set and global ψ″≥1 on the bad set give

`k E Bψ(v+h,v)≥(kA/2)||g||₂²−(D₃η/6)||g||₂²+(k_min/2)||b||₂²`.  (L131a)

This truncation is only a proof decomposition: the kernel u itself and its exact information are not replaced.

Let q=min{3,1+ρ₁^−2}>2 and B_max=(1−ρ₁²a₁²)^−1. For |x|≤ρ₁a₁ and x+z∈[−1,1] there is a finite scalar constant C_out such that

`|Bψ(x+z,x)−ψ″(x)z²/2|≤C_out|z|^q`.  (L131b)

For example take C_out as the supremum of the displayed quotient on that scalar compact domain, assigning its continuous limit at z=0 when it exists, or its bounded upper limiting value. Near zero ψ is C³ on a fixed interior interval and q≤3; away from zero continuity of ψ, including at ±1, gives finiteness. This argument never differentiates ψ at an endpoint.

Cube hypercontractivity, `||Tρ f||_q≤||f||_(1+(q−1)ρ²)`, therefore bounds the integrated output remainder by C_out ε^q. For p₀=1+ρ₁² and θ=1/p₀−1/2>0 it also gives

`||Tρ b||₂≤||b||_(1+ρ²)≤||b||_(p₀)≤p^θ||b||₂`.

This is an entropy-tail payment through small support, not an L∞ smoothing claim. Finally |Eb|≤sqrt(p)||b||₂ and ψ(m)≥m²/2. The good quadratic terms, including `(1−k)(Eg)²/2`, are exactly Q(g)/2≥c*||g||₂²/2. The bad output square costs at most `(B_max/2)p^(2θ)||b||₂²`; the output and mean cross terms cost at most

`L(p)||g||₂||b||₂`, where `L(p)=B_max p^θ+sqrt(p)`.

Young's inequality bounds this by `c*||g||₂²/8+2L(p)²||b||₂²/c*`. Choose p_*∈(0,1] so that

`(B_max/2)p_*^(2θ)+2L(p_*)²/c*≤k_min/4`.

All these constants depend only on the endpoint rectangle. Put c₁=min{c*/4,k_min/4}, and choose

`ε_*≤min{η sqrt(p_*),(c₁/(2C_out))^(1/(q−2))}`

(with the second restriction omitted if C_out=0). Combining the estimates gives

`G(u)≥c₁(||g||₂²+||b||₂²)−C_out ε^q≥(c₁/2)ε²`.

On the exact information surface G=Δ, proving L131 with c_*=c₁/2. Small support, high amplitude, arbitrary bias and endpoint values have all been included. Hypercontractivity is used on real functions, with the displayed exponents; no Boolean singleton identities or noisy seed variables enter. The norm inequality is the classical two-point hypercontractive inequality tensorized with Minkowski: for 1<p≤2, `[(|A+B|^p+|A−B|^p)/2]^(2/p)≥A²+(p−1)B²`; it gives Lp→L2 at correlation sqrt(p−1), and duality gives L2→Lq. This explicit norm theorem is separate from the external papers' computer-assisted production claims.

### 4.3 A backward bridge with its actual loss

Suppose λ≥λ₀>0 and some oriented coordinate satisfies `||Tλu−λaX_i||₂²≤δ`. Then `|û(i)−a|≤sqrt(δ)/λ₀` and m²≤δ. For δ small enough to enter E128's correlation neighborhood, it follows that

`E|u−aX_i|² ≤ (2V((1+a₁)/2)/λ₀)sqrt(δ)+2Lδ+δ/λ₀²`.  (E131)

This uses the **original exact information** to pay the unsmoothed entropy residual. It is not inversion of Tλ. Its square-root loss is sufficient for a qualitative uniform L2 entrance statement: once its right side is ≤ε_*², L131 gives the linear deficit bound at the original kernel. A smoothed-coordinate entrance theorem would therefore suffice, with an explicit loss; none is proved here. P128 supplies only average surplus and does not furnish that entrance by itself.

### 4.4 Hierarchical switching with an independent selector is not a counterexample

Let Z be any number of uniform selector bits independent of payload bits X₁,…,X_r, let J(Z)∈[r] and s(Z)∈{−1,1} be arbitrary, including any depth of hierarchical switching, and set `u=a s(Z)X_(J(Z))`. Then Eu=0, |u|=a, and I(u)=ψ(a) **exactly**. Put f_j=s1_{J=j}, μ_j=Ef_j, q_j=Tρf_j, c=ρa. The noisy kernel is `c Σ_j q_j X_j`.

Conditionally on the noisy selector, this is the average of values ±c over the latent selector. Strong convexity of ψ gives, after averaging payload bits,

`Δ≥(c²/2)[1−Σ_j||Tρf_j||₂²]`.

The constant/nonconstant spectral split and Σ_j||f_j||₂²=1 give

`Σ_j||Tρf_j||₂²≤ρ²+(1−ρ²)Σ_jμ_j²≤ρ²+(1−ρ²)max_j|μ_j|`,

because Σ_j|μ_j|≤1. All selector-coordinate correlations are zero, and payload correlations are aμ_j. Therefore

`Δ≥[ρ²(1−ρ²)/4]d_a(u)`.  (H131)

This proves a full dimension-independent subclass estimate, with arbitrary hierarchy and sign switching. It does not cover selectors that reuse payload coordinates or variable amplitudes correlated with the selected bit.
