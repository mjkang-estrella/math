1. **P. W. Jones, M. Maggioni, R. Schul, _Universal Local Parametrizations via Heat Kernels and Eigenfunctions of the Laplacian_.** [arXiv:0709.1975v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1975v4). Retrieved through the unversioned PDF endpoint; the file identifies v4, 9 October 2008, with an internal compilation date of 30 October 2018. This is the full preprint associated with the 2010 Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae paper, not the six-page PNAS announcement. All 45 PDF pages, extracted lines 1–2743, read: introduction, all theorem statements/proofs, estimates, examples, captions, notation and references. No rendered figure inspection. Theorems 2.1.1/2.2.1 provide quantitative local coordinates; the introduction also notes global larger-band coordinates for closed manifolds. Neither is the fixed two-first-mode Neumann theorem. No claim that the published 2010 typesetting/version was independently read. Files: `primary/jones-maggioni-schul-full.{pdf,txt}`.

2. **J. Bates, _The embedding dimension of Laplacian eigenfunction maps_.** [arXiv:1605.01643v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.01643v1), author version of ACHA 37 (2014), 516–530. All 16 pages, extracted lines 1–784, including proofs and references; no independent rendered figure inspection. Theorems 1–3 distinguish local immersion from uniform global embedding by finitely many initial modes for closed manifolds. The number of coordinates depends on geometric bounds. File pair: `primary/bates-embedding.{pdf,txt}`.

3. **F. Braun, J. Llibre, _Parallel vector fields and global injectivity in two dimensions_.** Pacific J. Files: `primary/braun-llibre-univalence.{pdf,txt}`.
