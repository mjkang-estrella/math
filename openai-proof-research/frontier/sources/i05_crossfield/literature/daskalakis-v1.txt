   The proofs of Theorem 1.5, Theorem 1.7 and Theorem 1.10 are very similar in spirit, following the
approach from [4]. Loosely speaking, a sophisticated “change of variables” will be performed, allowing
one to pass from summation over the thin set Nh ∩ [1, N ] := {⌊h(k)⌋ : k ∈ N} ∩ [1, N ] to summation

can be appropriately bounded by U 3 -norms. More precisely, one may employ the strategy we lay out
