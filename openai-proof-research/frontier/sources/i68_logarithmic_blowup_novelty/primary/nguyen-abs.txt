Title:Construction of a stable blow-up solution for a class of strongly perturbed semilinear heat equations
Authors:Van Tien Nguyen, Hatem Zaag
View a PDF of the paper titled Construction of a stable blow-up solution for a class of strongly perturbed semilinear heat equations, by Van Tien Nguyen and Hatem Zaag
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:We construct a solution for a class of strongly perturbed semilinear heat equations which blows up in finite time with a prescribed blow-up profile. The construction relies on the reduction of the problem to a finite dimensional one and the use of index theory to conclude.
