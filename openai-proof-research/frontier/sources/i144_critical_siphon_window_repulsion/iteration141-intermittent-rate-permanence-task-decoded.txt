a.e. on B_h. All other active slopes give nonnegative derivative. Caratheodory solutions exist locally because bounded measurable coefficients multiply polynomials locally Lipschitz in x. ## 5. Route I — chronological paths, explicit permanence and contraction

Consider a strongly connected monomolecular network on n>=2 species, with reactions A_i->A_j on its fixed directed graph and PE rates. Parallel edges can be excluded by the graph convention; otherwise use total outgoing-rate bounds below. Total mass M=sum x_i is conserved, and each positive class is precisely the open simplex of that mass. Put

`L=(n-1)K, H=(n-1)T, beta=exp(-L H) min(1,alpha^(n-1))`.  (3)

Let U(t,s) be the column-stochastic evolution matrix, Q(t) its column generator. Off-diagonal Q_(ji)=k_(i->j), and the diagonal is minus total outgoing rate. Positivity and column sums follow from the integral equation, or the convergent Peano series for G=Q+LI>=0 with U=e^(-L(t-s))V.

For any i,j choose a simple directed path j->...->i of length m<=n-1. In the mth nonnegative Peano integral over [s,s+H], restrict its successive edge times to the first m consecutive windows of length T. These times are automatically ordered, and integration factors into m edge integrals, each at least alpha. Thus `U_(ij)(s+H,s)>=exp(-LH)alpha^m>=beta`. For i=j use the zeroth term. This proves the estimate without simultaneous activity, reversibility of instantaneous graphs, or exchanging time order. Consequently, for every positive initial point and **all t>=H**,

`beta M <= x_i(t) <= M`.  (4)

This is an actual uniform time independent even of the initial point on this particular simplex. It is a bound on all future states of admissible trajectories; the coordinate-floor simplex is not asserted forward invariant from every one of its points under every fresh PE schedule.

There is also a usable quantitative stability estimate for two solutions driven by the same rates and having the same M. Put b=beta/2 and theta=1-nb. Column stochasticity forces n beta<=1, so 1/2<=theta<1. Over each H block,

`U=b 11^T+theta R`,

where R is column stochastic. The first term kills zero-sum differences and ||R||_(1->1)=1. Remaining shorter intervals are nonexpansive. Hence

`||x(t)-x_tilde(t)||_1 <= theta^floor((t-s)/H) ||x(s)-x_tilde(s)||_1`.  (5)

For a weakly reversible monomolecular graph with several linkage components, apply this to each strongly connected component with its conserved positive mass M_l; isolated species are constant. The full class is the product of those mass simplexes. Uniform bounds across classes require each M_l bounded below; conservation itself prevents an absolute lower bound as M_l->0. No equilibrium convergence under arbitrary time-dependent rates is inferred from (5).

**Operation comparison and limit of route.** Chronological path minorization and stochastic contraction are classical. The current [Proskurnikov–Calafiore extended version, 2105.07183v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07183v1), allows locally integrable coefficients and uses integral connectivity; its evolution-matrix Lemma 14 and proof turn integrated edges into positive entries, and its uniform-window discussion gives exponential consensus. Its row-consensus and delay conventions differ from the conservative column flow here; our proof independently pays the orientation and constants instead of asserting that transposition preserves a nonautonomous evolution. The retained two-column PDF text is read through that lemma's ending. No exact printed duplicate of (3) is required for the technical classification: the entire mechanism is positive time-ordered propagation plus the elementary stochastic decomposition.

For nonlinear complexes, the state-dependent effective rate along a species transfer may vanish like another concentration. A lower bound on integral k_e alone is not a lower bound on those effective rates. Strong connectivity of the complex graph is not a species-level Markov chain. Thus (3)–(5) do not solve P138 by renaming complexes as species.

## 6. Route II — exact nonlinear ratio dynamics and sharp scope obstructions

Take the genuinely nonlinear weakly reversible pair

`A+B --a(t)--> 2B`, `2B --b(t)--> A+B`,

with 0<=a,b<=K and each satisfying the same T-window integral bound alpha. Fix M=A+B>0 and positive initial A,B. The stoichiometric class is 0<B<M. Direct mass-action algebra gives

`B'=a(t)(M-B)B-b(t)B²`.

Use the physical ratio r=A/B=M/B-1, not an added species. It obeys exactly

`r'=-M a(t) r+M b(t)`.

Thus, writing A_a(s,t)=integral_s^t a,

`r(t)=e^(-M A_a(0,t)) r(0)+M integral_0^t e^(-M A_a(s,t)) b(s)ds`.  (6)

The formula is valid for measurable rates and gives finite r>0 at finite times, hence a global positive solution with the original conservation law. It does not change the reaction model.

Every interval of length v contains floor(v/T) complete windows, so `A_a(s,t)>=alpha floor((t-s)/T)`. Set

`q=exp(-M alpha), R=MKT/(1-q), ell=M alpha exp(-MKT)`.  (7)

Partitioning the integral in (6) backwards from t into T-length pieces gives its upper bound R. For t>=T, retain its last window and use A_a(s,t)<=KT there; the b integral is at least alpha. Therefore

`ell <= r(t) <= q^floor(t/T) r(0)+R` for t>=T.  (8)

Choose `N=max(1,ceil(log^+(r(0))/(M alpha)))`. For all t>=NT,

`B(t)>=M/(2+R), A(t)>=M ell/(1+ell), A(t)+B(t)=M`.  (9)

These are explicit PE permanence bounds on a nonlinear network with bounded class. Each constant is uniform over the rate path. For a compact initial subset with r(0)<=R_0, replace r(0) by R_0 in N.

For a parameter range 0<M_-<=M<=M_+<infinity, common choices are

`R_*=M_+KT/(1-exp(-M_-alpha)), ell_*=M_-alpha exp(-M_+KT)`.

Replace both numerator M's in (9) by M_-, R by R_*, ell by ell_*, and M in the entrance denominator by M_-. This pays class-parameter uniformity only on that declared range. Two solutions with the same M and rate path satisfy exactly

`|r(t)-r_tilde(t)|<=exp(-M alpha floor((t-s)/T))|r(s)-r_tilde(s)|`.

Since the map r->(Mr/(1+r),M/(1+r)) is 2M-Lipschitz into l1 for r>=0, this yields a concentration stability estimate with the same decay and factor 2M. It asserts synchronization for a common forcing, not convergence to a fixed equilibrium.

This route uses the actual autocatalytic term but is a Bernoulli/logistic linearization, not a new general network mechanism. CNP's positive-box theorem covers this graph under its stronger pointwise positivity hypothesis; (6)–(9) pay the off intervals directly. The exact solvability explains the technical contribution without needing a novelty claim based on missing terminology.

### Genuine obstructions to broader conclusions

**No uniform entrance time over all positive initial states, even at fixed rates.** In this same model, B'<=MK B, so B(t)<=B(0)e^(MKt). To reach any fixed delta>0 takes at least log(delta/B(0))/(MK). As B(0)->0 this diverges while M and positive constant rates stay fixed. Thus compact-initial uniformity in P138 is substantive; its global replacement is false.

**Unbounded-class PE extension is false in the original mass-action model.** Take 2A reversible with 3A. With period 2, use k_+=1,k_-=0 for the first unit interval and k_+=0,k_-=1 for the second. Both rate integrals equal 1 on every length-2 window; PE holds with K=1,T=2,alpha=1. Starting at x(0)=2 gives x'=x² until time 1 and x(t)=2/(1-2t), which blows up at t=1/2. The union network is weakly reversible and has unbounded positive class. This refutes the proposed all-classes/global-existence PE extension, not the stipulated autonomous theorem or positive-box extension. Adding a positive pointwise lower reverse rate removes this particular example's hypothesis.

**A common positive compact set invariant for every fresh PE schedule is too strong.** Already for A reversible with B, mass M, permit the same alternating schedules. A nonempty compact subset K of the positive simplex invariant for every schedule would in particular be invariant under the time-one pure A->B map. Iterating that fixed map on a point of K sends A to zero while remaining in K, contradicting compactness inside the positive simplex. The iteration is an argument about invariance of the same set under one map, not a claim that the concatenated pure-reaction path satisfies PE. Eventual bounds such as (4) and (9) survive; arbitrary-restart common invariance does not.

### Deepest paid partial and missing operation

Equations (3)–(5) and (6)–(9) are complete estimates with rate/class/time quantifiers, and the three obstructions respect the actual reaction models. In the general bounded class, positivity for finite time follows because each consumption monomial contains the consumed species and all coordinates are bounded; a Gronwall lower estimate prevents finite-time boundary hitting. This does not give an eventual lower bound independent of initial distance to every boundary face.

To finish P138 one would need a uniform lower bound on chronological **state-weighted** reaction fluxes near every possible boundary face, or another boundary-repulsion construction stable under independently off edges. The source's instantaneous active-label comparison supplies neither a window-invariant label nor this weighted flux bound. Route I pays it for linear propagation; Route II pays it through a special exact ratio equation. Neither argument closes it for arbitrary complexes and linkage classes. No substantial finding is nominated, and no external open-problem ranking or worldwide-priority assertion is made.
