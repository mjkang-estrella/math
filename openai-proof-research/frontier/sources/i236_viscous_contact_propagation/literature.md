## The viscosity-to-weak mechanism actually used

V. Julin and P. Juutinen, [A new proof for the equivalence of weak and viscosity solutions for the p-Laplace equation, arXiv:1104.2197v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.2197v1), [retained PDF pages 1–6](julin-juutinen-1104.2197v1-pages-1-6.txt). Read completely, including Theorem 3.1, the inf-convolution, inequality (3.5), and Remark 3.3 on almost-everywhere convergence of mollified Hessians. Section 3's non-singular proof supplies the established model for one-sided Fatou; Section 4 only begins on the last retained page and is not used. The introduction explicitly discusses extension to other autonomous divergence equations, including minimal surface. I236 nevertheless proves the passage for its explicit exponential flux, including the epsilon-to-zero gradient convergence, instead of relabeling their p-Laplace theorem. Appendix A and PDF pages 7 onward were not read. The downloaded version bears the arXiv v1 banner and a later internal compilation date; the retained bytes and URL are authenticated without trying to resolve that bibliographic artifact.

R. Crescimbeni, L. Forzani, A. Perini, [A new proof of Harnack's inequality for elliptic partial differential equations in divergence form, EJDE 2007, No. 38](https://ejde.math.txstate.edu/Volumes/2007/38/crescimbeni.pdf), [retained pages 1–5](crescimbeni-forzani-perini-2007-pages-1-5.txt). Read completely. Section 2 assumes measurable symmetric coefficient matrices with fixed positive lower/upper ellipticity bounds and defines weak solutions in W¹,². Its equations (2.3)–(2.5) and Theorems 3.1–3.2 give interior Hölder representatives and Harnack for nonnegative weak **solutions**. I236 uses exactly those solution conclusions via a linear Lax–Milgram replacement and energy comparison; it does not pretend that the paper's vector space of solutions is a class of supersolutions. Proof pages 6–12 were not read or reproduced.

## Why the tempting comparison citations are not used

N. S. Trudinger, [Comparison principles and pointwise estimates for viscosity solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 4 (1988), 453–468](https://doi.org/10.4171/RMI/80). The publisher PDF is scanned. PDF pages 1–12 (printed pp. 453–464) were rendered and read completely, retained as `trudinger-1988-page-1.png` through `trudinger-1988-page-12.png`. Page 2 states F2 with a Hessian-independent gradient-Lipschitz constant. Theorem 1.1 and Remark 3.2 do not license ignoring that hypothesis. Even on bounded p-ranges, tr(A_tau(p)X) fails F2 as X varies; I236 gives actual smooth solution jets demonstrating this. Pages 13–16 were not read. The long linear/nonlinear proof was read to check the interface, not adopted as an alternative proof.

## Existing exponential operator

H. Sánchez Morgado, [Time-periodic Evans approach to weak KAM theory, arXiv:1712.10275v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.10275v1). Reused I231 excerpt pages 1–5 was displayed; the relied-on source comparison is [equation (EL), equation (4), and equation (7) on pages 1–3](../i231_viscous_liouville/sanchez-morgado-1712.10275v1-pages-1-5.txt).
