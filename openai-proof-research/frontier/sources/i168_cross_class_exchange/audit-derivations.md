# Independent I168 audit derivations (before the attack)

All pinned OpenAI theorems are stipulated true. No ancestor executable is read or executed.

CW167: remove the four inserted square vertices from a child cycle. With two incident arms the square must be its unique three-edge path joining adjacent corners. Opposite corners cannot be joined by a spanning square path. The four adjacent arm pairs give N10, N01, Pux, Pvy. With four arms the square is one of its two perfect matchings; the exterior is two disjoint spanning paths, with no cycle component. Terminals occur u,v,y,x on the face. The pairing (u,y),(v,x) crosses in the complementary disk and is impossible. For either remaining pairing exactly one square matching connects the exterior into one cycle. These give N11 and D11. Zero arms cannot yield one spanning cycle. Thus there are exactly six disjoint fibres.

For square weights a=pq,b=rs,c=pr,d=qs, the six added weights, in that order, are tu+tv+b+c+d; tx+ty+a+c+d; tu+tx+a+b+d; tv+ty+a+b+c; tu+tv+tx+ty+a+b; tu+tv+tx+ty+c+d. Using W(uv)=tu+tv-a and W(xy)=tx+ty-b reproduces CW167, including its plus-delta N11 offset. Each identity holds per object, so their conjunction is necessary and sufficient, including vacuous empty fibres.

The I166 counterexample uses alpha_p=1. Every child cycle uses exactly two incident edges at p, hence has weight 2. The aggregated uv weight is 2, the aggregated xy weight 0, and delta=0. Nonempty N10 and N01 therefore contradict the original common-constant assertion. This is a failure of I166, not of any stipulated theorem.

P167: in K, v and y are degree two and internal in every ux spanning path. Differences vanish on all their incident edges. G-{v,y}=K-{v,y} is connected by 3-connectivity. Deleting these vertices removes 4-epsilon edges of K, so its unsigned incidence kernel has dimension (mK-4+epsilon)-(n-2)+1=beta(K)-2+epsilon. Connected bipartite unsigned incidence rank is n-1: multiply one color's rows by -1 to obtain an oriented incidence matrix and use a spanning tree. Since epsilon is 0 or 1 the deficit is strict. Its annihilator strictly exceeds the vertex-potential space, so path constancy alone cannot force potentials. Nonemptiness uses only the stipulated P4 corollary on the child, not a proof of that corollary.

Transport: on surviving parent coordinates E=chi(uv), F=chi(xy), arms=(E,E,F,F), square=(F,E,2-E-F,2-E-F), and K is unchanged. This affine map has a left inverse reading K and one arm of each pair. Thus it preserves affine dimension exactly, including differences across the three surviving classes. Rank accounting requires dG+2-dsurv new directions, not merely two.

A167: for nested rooted tree shores S<T, any connected component R of T-S attaches exactly once to S in the tree T, and exactly once to the complement of T in the tree complement(S). No edges join distinct components of T-S. Hence its edge cut has size two, impossible in a 3-edge-connected graph. Both other shores are nonempty since they avoid a fixed root. For plane cubic 3-connected G, its dual has edge connectivity at least three: a smaller dual bond would correspond to a primal loop or 2-cycle, forbidden by simplicity. A primal Hamiltonian cycle cuts the sphere into two disks; noncycle edges are disjoint noncrossing chords, so faces in each disk induce a dual tree. Conversely a two-induced-tree dual partition has one monochromatic edge per dual triangular face; its primal crossing edges have degree two everywhere. These edges are connected because the complementary dual edges are a forest (equivalently, a disconnected primal spanning subgraph yields a dual cycle separating components). Thus they form one Hamiltonian cycle. These arguments prove the complete correspondence, not just injectivity.

Typed ports: the hypothesis is a cubic graph, an edge uv, two disjoint contraction triples {a,u,b},{c,v,d}, and a simple loop-free retract with distinct retained edges. A cycle avoiding uv contains a-u-b and c-v-d. Contracting these fixed paths preserves a single spanning cycle; at each merged vertex it selects one retained edge from each outer vertex's port pair. Conversely this rule uniquely subdivides each merged vertex into its three-vertex path, yielding a Hamiltonian cycle avoiding uv. Unchanged retained coordinates and fixed path coordinates make the bijection affine in both directions. No brace generation theorem or unrestricted retract-cycle induction is needed for this assertion.
