Theorem 1.3.



Let A\in\mathcal{B}(H) and let f_{1},\ldots,f_{q}\in\mathcal{A}(K_{A}) satisfy




(1.6)

\sum_{j=1}^{q}\left\lvert f_{j}(z)\right\rvert^{2}\leq 1\qquad(z\in K_{A}).



For every 0<\tau<1, the series




(1.7)

\displaystyle P_{\tau}(f,A)
\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\tau^{2k}\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal{W}_{k}}f_{\alpha}(A)^{*}f_{\alpha}(A),



(1.8)

\displaystyle Q_{\tau}(f,A)
\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\tau^{2k}\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal{W}_{k}}f_{\alpha}(A)f_{\alpha}(A)^{*}



converge in operator norm and satisfy




(1.9)

I\preceq P_{\tau}(f,A)\preceq\frac{1}{1-\tau}I,\qquad I\preceq Q_{\tau}(f,A)\preceq\frac{1}{1-\tau}I.



In particular,




(1.10)

\left\lVert(f_{1}(A),\ldots,f_{q}(A))^{\mathsf{T}}\right\rVert\leq 2,\qquad\left\lVert(f_{1}(A),\ldots,f_{q}(A))\right\rVert\leq 2.



The constant 2 is best possible.
