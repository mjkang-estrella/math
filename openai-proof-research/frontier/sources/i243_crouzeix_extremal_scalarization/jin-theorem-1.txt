Theorem 1.

For every N >= 1 and A in M_N(C),

    psi(A)=2  =>  W(A)={z in C: |z-gamma| <= rho}

for some gamma in C and some rho > 0.

Only the primary theorem statement is retained here. The all-dimensional scalar theorem is used as an external dependency; this excerpt is not a claim to have read its complete proof. The surrounding primary abstract and Section 1 explicitly distinguish this scalar statement from complete equality.
