                            Q

               Let K be a number field with Galois group Gal(K/Q). We write ωK (d) for the number of
            prime ideals dividing d in a number field K. Given a rational prime p unramified in K/Q,
            we denote by Frobp ⊂ Gal(K/Q) the Frobenius symbol of p; it is always a conjugacy class in
