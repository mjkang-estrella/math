                                   Withdrawal notice
            The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces
                                                 OpenAI
                                       Withdrawn on October 6, 2026


The proof relies on an adaptation of the flawed stabilization-trace construction in Algebraicity of Weil
classes on split abelian eightfolds. The sign error in that construction prevents the required
signed-double-point cancellation. The resulting gap leaves this paper's claimed rational Hodge theorem for
products of projective K3 surfaces unproved.

This withdrawal concerns the proof; it does not assert that the mathematical statement is false.
