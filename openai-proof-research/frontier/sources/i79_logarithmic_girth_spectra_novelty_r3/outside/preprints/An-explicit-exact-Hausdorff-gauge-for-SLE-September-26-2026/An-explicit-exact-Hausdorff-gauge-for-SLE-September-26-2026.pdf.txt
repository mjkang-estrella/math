                   An explicit exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE
                  A regular formula from quantitative tails and dense visits

                                                  Abstract
         For each fixed 0 < κ < 8, let d = 1 + κ/8. The gauge h(r) = rd (log log(1/r))(2−d)/2 at
     sufficiently small radii almost surely gives positive finite Hausdorff measure to every nontrivial
     positive-time compact segment of chordal SLEκ . This gives an explicit solution to Schramm’s
     Hausdorff-measure problem in this parameter range. The entire trace has finite expected measure
     in every bounded disk. The exponent-one iterated-logarithm gauge suggested by Schramm is not
     sigma-finite on any such segment.


1    Introduction
A random curve whose dimension lies strictly between one and two requires a finer notion of length
than arc length. A dimension theorem specifies the critical power of a small covering diameter, but
that power alone need not give a nonzero measure. For ordinary chordal SLEκ , 0 < κ < 8, Beffara
identified the dimension d = 1 + κ/8 [2, main Theorem]; Rezaei proved that its d-dimensional
Hausdorff measure is zero [14, Theorem 1.1]. The remaining question concerns the correction to the
critical power, not a further change of dimension.
