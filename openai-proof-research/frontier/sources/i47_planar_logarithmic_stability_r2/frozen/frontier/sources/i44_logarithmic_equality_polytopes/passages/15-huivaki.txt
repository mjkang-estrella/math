234:    The Hessian of the volume polynomial connects the spectral problem (1.3) to earlier work
235: on polyhedral geometry. Izmestiev [Izm10] studied the support-parameter space of polytopes
236: with prescribed facet normals and constructed a Colin de Verdière matrix from the negative
237: volume Hessian. Fillastre and Izmestiev [FI17] described irredundancy domains and the
238: decomposition of their interiors into type cones. Together, the two works provide a blueprint
239: for extending smooth centro-affine geometry to the polytopal setting.
240:    The degree-one Hodge–Riemann relation is precisely the second Minkowski inequality. In
241: a simple type chamber, it is equivalent to concavity of h 7→ Vol(P (h))1/n under linear support
242: interpolation. The proof of Theorem 1.1 uses the degree-two Hodge–Riemann relation. Van
243: Handel [vH23a] formulated the higher-degree Hodge–Riemann relations as mixed-volume
244: inequalities and used the degree-two relation to disprove Fedotov’s conjectured extension
245: of Shephard’s inequalities. We also mention that Shenfeld and van Handel [SvH19] proved
246: the Alexandrov–Fenchel inequality by the Bochner method, including a finite-dimensional
247: spectral argument for polytopes. See also their later work, where they treat the equality cases
248: of Minkowski’s quadratic inequality and the Alexandrov–Fenchel inequality [SvH22, SvH23].
