8:          Abstract. We demonstrate that C 2,α estimates for the Monge-Ampère equation depend
9:          in a highly nonlinear way both on the C α norm of the right-hand side and 1/α. First, we
10:          show that if a solution is strictly convex, then the C 2,α norm of the solution depends
11:          polynomially on the C α norm of the right-hand side. Second, we show that the C 2,α
12:          norm of the solution is controlled by exp((C/α) log(1/α)) as α → 0. Finally, we construct
13:          a family of solutions in two dimensions to show the sharpness of our results.
14: 
15:          Keywords: Monge-Ampère; Schauder estimates

21:    The model case in the study of linear elliptic equations is the Poisson equation ∆v = g. It
22: is well known that for such an equation, the solution v gains two derivatives with respect to
23: g in Hölder spaces. More precisely, if
24:                                        ∆v = g        in B1 ⊂ Rn ,
25: then there exists a constant C > 0 depending only on n and α such that

28: In addition, it is known that C ∼ 1/α as α → 0. This estimate extends easily to linear
29: uniformly elliptic equations, and even to fully nonlinear uniformly elliptic equations (see the
30: Appendix). It is therefore natural to ask whether or not one may extend such a well-quantified
31: estimate to other more general elliptic equations.
32:    In this note, we address this issue for the Monge-Ampère equation investigating the
33: following general question:
