where V (K) stands for the n-dimensional Lebesgue measure. The corner-

for some absolute constant c > 0, which was improved to cn−7 by Segal [87],

combining the general estimate of Kolesnikov-Milman [66], Theorem 12.2,
with the logarithmic bound of Klartag [63] on the Cheeger constant of a

the Kannan-Lovász-Simonovits conjecture. Harutyunyan [51] conjectured
that γ ∗ (n) = cn−2 is the optimal order of the constant, and showed that it
can’t be of smaller order. Actually, Segal [87] observed that Dar’s conjecture
in [29] would imply that we may choose γ ∗ (n) = cn−2 for some absolute
constant c > 0.
    We note that recently, various breakthrough stability results about ge-
ometric functional inequalities have been obtained. Fusco, Maggi, Pratelli

equalities are verified by Colesanti [26], Colesanti, Livshyts, Marsiglietti [27],
P. Nayar, T. Tkocz [76, 77], Xi, Leng [90].

    The cone volume measure or L0 -surface area measure VK on S n−1 ,
whose study was initiated independently by Firey [42] and Gromov and
Milman [49], has become an indispensable tool in the last decades (see say
Barthe, Guédon, Mendelson, Naor [8], Naor [75], Paouris, Werner [79]).
If a convex body K contains the origin, then its cone volume measure is
dVK = n1 hK dSK where hK is the support function of K and the total
measure is the volume of K.

measures by the so called subspace concentration condition. As it turns out,
subspace concentration condition also holds for the cone-volume measure VK
if the centroid of a general convex body K is the origin (see Henk, Linke
[53] and Böröczky, Henk [15, 16]).

     We say that A ∈ GL(n) is a linear reflection associated to a linear (n−1)-
space H ⊂ Rn if A fixes the points of H and det A = −1. In this case, there
exists u ∈ Rn \H such that Au = −u where the invariant subspace Ru
is uniquely determined (see Davis [30], Humphreys [57], Vinberg [94]). It
follows that a linear reflection A is a classical ”orthogonal” reflection if and
only if A ∈ O(n). We say that the linear reflections A1 , . . . , An associated to
linear (n−1)-spaces H1 , . . . , Hn ⊂ Rn are independent if H1 ∩. . .∩Hn = {o}.
In this case, the n linear (n − 1)-spaces H1 , . . . , Hn ⊂ Rn are also called
independent. We also observe that if a compact convex set K ⊂ Rn is
invariant under n independent linear reflections, then K is centered.
