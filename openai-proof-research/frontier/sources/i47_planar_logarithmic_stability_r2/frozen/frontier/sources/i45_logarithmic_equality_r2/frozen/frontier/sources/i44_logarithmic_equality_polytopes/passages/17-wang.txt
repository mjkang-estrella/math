220: hand, by a simple application of the Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality, one can verify LLBM for the
221: cube (and hence all parallelotopes since LLBM is covariant under linear transformation). Now,
222: start with a parallelotope and apply the monotonicity principle iteratively to different intervals I,
223: we conclude that LLBM holds for all zonotopes. Since zonoids are limits of zonotopes and mixed
224: volume is a continuous functional on the space of convex bodies [11, Thm.5.1.7], LLBM holds
225: for all zonoids. We carry out this approach in Section 3 to exhibit a self-contained new proof of
226: LLBM for zonoids.
