                                                  A very short unified proof of Dar’s conjecture and
                                                         Log-Brunn–Minkowski in the plane

                                                                                           Dongmeng Xi

                                                  Abstract. This short note gives a very short unified proof of both Dar’s conjecture and the
                                                  log-Brunn–Minkowski inequality in the plane. A planar Lp -Brunn-Minkowski inequality for p ∈
                                                  [−∞, 0] is established and gives the two key inequalities at p = 0 and p = −∞. Motivated by this
                                                  new proof, the author proposes a related conjecture in R3 , to attack the two conjectures in higher
