142: the unit ball and the unit sphere, respectively. We shall denote the Lebesgue measure (the
143: volume) in Rn by | · |.
144:    We say that a set A ⊂ Rn is symmetric if for every x ∈ A one has −x ∈ A. All
145: measures under consideration will be tacitly assumed to be Radon measures, and all sets
146: will be assumed to be measurable. A measure γ on Rn is called symmetric if for every set
147: S ⊂ Rn , γ(S) = γ(−S). If the measure has a density then it is symmetric whenever the
148: density is an even function.
