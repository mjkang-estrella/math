1: \section{Introduction}
2: \label{sec:introduction}
3: 
4: A convex body in $\R^n$ is a compact convex set with nonempty interior.
5: If $K=-K$, then $0$ lies in its interior.
6: Its support function is
7: \[
8:  h_K(u)=\max_{x\in K}\langle x,u\rangle,\qquad u\in S^{n-1}.
9: \]
10: For an origin-symmetric body this function is strictly positive.
11: For a positive continuous function $f$ on the unit sphere, its
12: \emph{Wulff body} is
13: \[
14:  \mathcal W[f]=\bigcap_{u\in S^{n-1}}
15:  \{x\in\R^n:\langle x,u\rangle\le f(u)\}.
16: \]
17: It is a convex body: it contains the ball of radius $\min f>0$ and
18: is contained in the ball of radius $\max f$. Write $|K|$ for
19: $n$-dimensional Lebesgue measure. Our main result is the following.
20: 
21: \begin{theorem}[Even logarithmic Brunn--Minkowski inequality]
22: \label{thm:main}
23: Let $n\ge1$ and let $K,L\subset\R^n$ be convex bodies satisfying
24: $K=-K$ and $L=-L$. For every $0\le\lambda\le1$,
25: \begin{equation}\label{eq:main}
26:  \left|\mathcal W[h_K^{1-\lambda}h_L^\lambda]\right|
27:  \ge |K|^{1-\lambda}|L|^\lambda.
28: \end{equation}
29: \end{theorem}
30: 
31: The interpolation in \eqref{eq:main} takes the geometric mean of the
32: support bounds before intersecting the half-spaces. This gives a
33: strengthening, in the symmetric setting, of the multiplicative form
34: of the classical Brunn--Minkowski inequality: the arithmetic--geometric
35: mean inequality gives
36: \[
37:  \mathcal W[h_K^{1-\lambda}h_L^\lambda]
38:  \subset (1-\lambda)K+\lambda L.
39: \]
40: In general $h_K^{1-\lambda}h_L^\lambda$ need not itself be a support
41: function. The intersection defining $\mathcal W$ is essential throughout
42: the argument.
43: 
44: For $0<p<1$, the $L_p$ interpolation replaces the geometric mean of the
45: support bounds by the weighted power mean
46: $((1-\lambda)h_K^p+\lambda h_L^p)^{1/p}$, still taking the Wulff body.
47: The logarithmic endpoint implies the full symmetric volume inequality in
48: this intermediate range. B\"or\"oczky, Lutwak, Yang and Zhang record
49: this monotone implication
50: \cite[equation~(1.10) and the following paragraph, p.~1977]{BLZY2012}.
51: The additive
52: volume-power form below follows by normalizing the bodies and reweighting
53: the interpolation parameter.
54: 
55: \begin{corollary}[Symmetric $L_p$ Brunn--Minkowski inequality]
56: \label{cor:lp}
57: Let $n\ge1$, let $K,L\subset\R^n$ be full-dimensional origin-symmetric
58: convex bodies, and let $0<p<1$. For every $0\le\lambda\le1$,
59: \begin{equation}\label{eq:lp}
60:  \left|\mathcal W\!\left[
61:   \bigl((1-\lambda)h_K^p+\lambda h_L^p\bigr)^{1/p}
62:  \right]\right|^{p/n}
63:  \ge (1-\lambda)|K|^{p/n}+\lambda|L|^{p/n}.
64: \end{equation}
65: \end{corollary}
66: 
67: The proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:lp} is given in
68: Section~\ref{subsec:lp-proof}.
69: 
70: 
71: The logarithmic volume theorem has a separate measure-theoretic consequence.
72: An even log-concave density has the form $e^{-V}$, where $V$ is an even
73: convex function with values in $\R\cup\{+\infty\}$; no probability
74: normalization is required. Saroglou proved that the Lebesgue inequality in
75: dimension $n$ implies the same Wulff inequality for every such density in
76: that same dimension \cite[Theorem~3.1]{Saroglou2016}.
77: Taking two scalar dilates of a single body then gives the $(B)$-conjecture:
78: for the measure $d\mu=e^{-V(x)}\,dx$ and every origin-symmetric convex body
79: $K$, the function $t\mapsto\mu(e^tK)$ is log-concave on $\R$
80: \cite[Corollary~3.2]{Saroglou2016}.
81: Section~\ref{sec:measure} applies this transfer and also treats even
82: log-concave Radon measures supported on proper subspaces.

84: \subsection*{Context and prior work}
85: Theorem~\ref{thm:main} resolves the origin-symmetric logarithmic
86: Brunn--Minkowski conjecture of B\"or\"oczky, Lutwak, Yang and Zhang
87: \cite[Problem~1.1]{BLZY2012}.
88: Stancu \cite[Theorem~2, p.~3264]{Stancu2018} announced the
89: all-dimensional logarithmic Minkowski inequality and explicitly concluded
90: the equivalent logarithmic Brunn--Minkowski inequality; the report deferred
91: details of the flow asymptotics.
92: The conjecture belongs to the $L_p$ Brunn--Minkowski theory initiated
93: by Firey's $p$-means of convex bodies
94: \cite{Firey1962} and developed by Lutwak through $L_p$ mixed volumes
95: and the $L_p$ Minkowski problem \cite{Lutwak1993}.
96: For $p>0$, the support bounds are interpolated by the weighted power
97: mean appearing in Corollary~\ref{cor:lp}; the geometric mean is
98: its limit as $p\downarrow0$. This endpoint has a particularly close
99: connection with cone-volume measures and the logarithmic Minkowski
100: problem. The cone-volume measure records the volumes of cones from the
101: origin to boundary patches, indexed by their outer normals. B\"or\"oczky, Lutwak, Yang and Zhang established the equivalence
102: between the corresponding logarithmic volume and mixed-volume inequalities
103: \cite[Lemma~3.2]{BLZY2012}; their subsequent work characterized which even
104: measures arise as cone-volume measures \cite[Theorem~1.1]{BLYZ2013}. The latter is an existence result,
105: separate from the volume inequality proved here.
106:

108: B\"or\"oczky, Lutwak, Yang and Zhang proved the planar case
109: \cite[Theorem~1.3]{BLZY2012}.
