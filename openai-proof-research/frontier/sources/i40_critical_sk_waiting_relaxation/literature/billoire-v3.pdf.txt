                                                                  A detailed numerical study is made of relaxation at equilibrium in the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick
                                                               Ising spin glass model, at and above the critical temperature Tg . The data show a long time stretched
                                                               exponential relaxation q(t) ∼ exp[−(t/τ (T ))β(T ) ] with an exponent β(T ) tending to ≈ 1/3 at Tg .
                                                               The results are compared to those which were observed by Ogielski in the 3d ISG model, and are
                                                               discussed in terms of a phase space percolation transition scenario.
