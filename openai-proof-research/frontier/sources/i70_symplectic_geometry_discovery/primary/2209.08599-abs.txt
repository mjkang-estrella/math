Title:Arnold conjecture over integers
Authors:Shaoyun Bai, Guangbo Xu
View a PDF of the paper titled Arnold conjecture over integers, by Shaoyun Bai and 1 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract:For any closed symplectic manifold, we show that the number of 1-periodic orbits of a nondegenerate Hamiltonian thereon is bounded from below by a version of total Betti number over Z of the ambient space taking account of the total Betti number over Q and torsions of all characteristic. The proof is based on constructing a Hamiltonian Floer theory over the Novikov ring with integer coefficients, which generalizes our earlier work for constructing integer-valued Gromov-Witten type invariants. In the course of the construction, we build a Hamiltonian Floer flow category with compatible smooth global Kuranishi charts. This generalizes a recent work of Abouzaid-McLean-Smith, which might be of independent interest.
