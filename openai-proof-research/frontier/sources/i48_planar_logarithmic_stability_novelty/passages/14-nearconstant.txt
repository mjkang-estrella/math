60:    A quick corollary of Theorem 1.1 is the uniqueness of solutions to the
61: regular logarithmic Minkowski problem without a symmetry condition,
62: provided the prescribed data is sufficiently close to 1 in the C α -norm.
63: Corollary 1.2. [CFL22, BS23] Let α ∈ (0, 1), n ≥ 2 and f ∈ C α (Sn ).
64: Then there exists a constant ε0 > 0 depending only on n, α, such that
65: if kf − 1kC α ≤ ε for some ε ∈ (0, ε0 ), then the log-Minkowski problem
66: h/K = f has a unique, positive, strictly convex solution.
67:    This corollary was recently proved by Chen, Feng, and Liu [CFL22]
68: for n = 2, and by Böröczky and Saroglou for the case n ≥ 2 and
69: h1−p /K = f for 0 ≤ p < 1 in [BS23]. We refer the reader to [Bor24,
70: IM23b, Mil23, KM22] and the references therein for the importance of
71: uniqueness results on the log-Minkowski problem.
