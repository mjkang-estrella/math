12:                                                       The logarithmic Brunn–Minkowski and logarithmic Minkowski inequalities are proved for
13:                                                   convex bodies whose centroids are at the origin, with equality precisely for independent positive
14:                                                   dilations of common direct summands. This characterizes when centered convex bodies have
15:                                                   the same cone-volume measure. The Lp Brunn–Minkowski and Minkowski inequalities are also
16:                                                   obtained for every p > 0, with equality only for positive dilates, and the corresponding uniqueness
17:                                                   of centered Lp surface area measures is established.
