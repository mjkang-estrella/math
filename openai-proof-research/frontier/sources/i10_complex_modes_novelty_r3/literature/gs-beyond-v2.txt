   We have exhibited two fundamental obstructions to a Bombieri-
Vinogradov Theorem for multiplicative functions:
   (i) If f correlates closely with a character of small conductor; or
   (ii) If the values of f (p) with x/2 < p ≤ x conspire against equi-
distribution.
Consequently, although we have been able to beat the “trivial bound”
by a factor of (log x)1−ε by taking (i) into account in Theorem 1.2, (ii)
ensures that we cannot do much better in general.
   These two obstructions have arisen before in the multiplicative func-
tions literature, in Montgomery and Vaughan’s seminal work [27] on
bounding exponential sums twisted by multiplicative coeﬃcients. For
this question, the contributions from obstruction (i) are identiﬁed pre-
cisely in [6], but the sharpness of the bounds are inevitably restricted
by obstruction (ii). However, in Proposition 1 of [5], de la Bretèche
showed that one can obtain much better bounds if one restricts at-
tention to f that are supported only on smooth numbers,5 since then
obstruction (ii) is rendered irrelevant. We can do much the same here:
