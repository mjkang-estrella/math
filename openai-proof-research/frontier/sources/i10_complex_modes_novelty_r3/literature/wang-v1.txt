                                              DYNAMICAL GENERALIZATIONS OF CHOWLA’S CONJECTURE ON
                                                                SHORT AVERAGES

                                                                                               BIAO WANG


                                                   Abstract. In 1965, Chowla conjectured that the signs of the Liouville function become asymp-
                                                   totically uncorrelated at any fixed collection of distinct shifts. In 2015, Matomäki, Radziwiłł
                                                   and Tao proved an averaged form of Chowla’s conjecture. In 2022, Lichtman proved a variant of




arXiv:2609.14492v1 [math.NT] 13 Sep 2026
                                                   this conjecture over primes on average. In the same year, Lichtman and Teräväinen proved the
                                                   Hardy–Littlewood–Chowla conjecture on average. In this article, motivated by the recent work
                                                   of Bergelson and Richter on the dynamical generalizations of the prime number theorem, we will
                                                   establish the dynamical generalizations of these three results related to Chowla’s conjecture. In the
                                                   proofs, we will show a uniform pretentious-distance estimate on the prime Omega function. Then
                                                   exponential sum estimates are used to establish the averaged shift invariance of the distributions
                                                   related to the shifted sum of the prime Omega function.
