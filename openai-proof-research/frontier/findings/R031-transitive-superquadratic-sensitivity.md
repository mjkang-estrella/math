# R031 — A superquadratic sensitivity separation for transitive Boolean functions

**Accepted as the ninth substantial result of the 2026-10-14 campaign: 9/10.** This is internal acceptance under the stipulated source contract and bounded contribution comparison, not external journal peer review or worldwide priority certification.

For every real C>0 there is a finite coordinate set and a nonconstant total Boolean function f, invariant under a group transitive on all raw input coordinates, with f(0)=0 and bs(f,0)>C s(f)^2. Here s(f) is ordinary sensitivity maximized over every Boolean input. There is also one fixed alpha>2 and a sequence of such functions F_m with bs(F_m,0)>=s(F_m)^alpha and bs(F_m,0) tending to infinity. Neither promise inputs, spectral sensitivity, row-only symmetry nor minterm subclasses replace the full target.

The new operation is a compatible deterministic labelling of a fixed-depth Paley tournament product: first-difference labels give a constant head-pattern exclusion bound, an alphabet small relative to the square root of the row count, and affine covariance transitive on row-position pairs. The pinned OpenAI source supplies the nested Hamming-predicate architecture, four ordinary/joint sensitivity recurrences, zero blocks, OR balancing and composition. Meiburg's spectral/cyclotomic symmetry and classical transitivity-preserving operations are credited antecedents. The actual primary comparison is bounded; absence of a literal duplicate is not treated as proof of substantiality.
