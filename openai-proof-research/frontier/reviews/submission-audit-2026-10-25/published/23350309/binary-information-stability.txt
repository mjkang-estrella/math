DIMENSION-FREE STABILITY OF BINARY-INFORMATION CONTRACTION

                                             MYEONGJIN KANG


       Abstract. Under the sharp contraction and joining results speciﬁed below, we prove linear sta-
       bility of binary-information contraction on every compact amplitude and correlation rectangle,
       uniformly over ﬁnite cube dimensions. For information ψ(a) tending to zero, the constant grows at
       most as a−4 , and this exponent is necessary. The statements apply to arbitrary [−1, 1]-valued ker-
       nels, without a mean restriction or a pointwise interior margin. We also strengthen the inequality
       D(u) ≥ F (I(u)) by an absolute positive multiple of (Eu)2 I(u)2 . The proof combines strict Gauss-
       ian contraction, a compactness argument for joining laws, and estimates that retain the entropy of
       rare endpoint values.
