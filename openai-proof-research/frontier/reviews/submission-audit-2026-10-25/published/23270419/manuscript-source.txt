\title[Factor-count residues for prime predecessors]{Factor-count residues and Poisson--Dirichlet limits for prime predecessors}
\author{Myeongjin Kang}
\address{Independent researcher}
\date{}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Assuming specified analytic estimates for shifted primes and Dirichlet
$L$-functions, we prove that $\Omega(p-1)$ modulo any fixed positive integer is
asymptotically uniform and independent of the ranked logarithmic prime
factor sizes, for primes in each fixed reduced arithmetic progression.
The factor-size limit is the Poisson--Dirichlet distribution with
parameter one. For parity, we also obtain joint independence from the
squarefree indicator, with its progression-dependent density. This gives
the Moree--Hommersom parity conjecture under the same assumptions.
The proof combines marked correlations with a principal-term model
that is compatible with small-prime multiplication and the extraction
of prime arguments.
\end{abstract}
