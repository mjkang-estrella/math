    FACTOR-COUNT RESIDUES AND POISSON–DIRICHLET LIMITS FOR
                     PRIME PREDECESSORS

                                              MYEONGJIN KANG


       Abstract. Assuming speciﬁed analytic estimates for shifted primes and Dirichlet L-functions, we
       prove that Ω(p − 1) modulo any ﬁxed positive integer is asymptotically uniform and independent of
       the ranked logarithmic prime factor sizes, for primes in each ﬁxed reduced arithmetic progression.
       The factor-size limit is the Poisson–Dirichlet distribution with parameter one. For parity, we also ob-
       tain joint independence from the squarefree indicator, with its progression-dependent density. This
       gives the Moree–Hommersom parity conjecture under the same assumptions. The proof combines
       marked correlations with a principal-term model that is compatible with small-prime multiplication
       and the extraction of prime arguments.

1.1. Related work and proof outline. The ordinary-integer distribution results of Pintz [11]
and Granville and Shao [8] supply progression estimates used below. They do not restrict the
averaging variable to prime predecessors. Lichtman [9] proves cancellation averaged over a growing
range of shifts. Carella [6, Theorems 1.1–1.2] states shifted-prime cancellation subject to a separate
distribution hypothesis. The joint law in (1.1) concerns the ﬁxed shift −1.
   The factor-count residue is recovered by ﬁnite Fourier inversion. The analytic problem is to
prove cancellation for fz (n) = z Ω(n) , for each nontrivial root of unity z. For z = −1, the ordinary
mean has arbitrary logarithmic cancellation. For nonreal z, the Selberg–Delange expansion has a
