          A CONDITIONAL POWER STABILITY ESTIMATE FOR THE
             LOGARITHMIC BRUNN–MINKOWSKI INEQUALITY

                                                  MYEONGJIN KANG


       Abstract. Assuming the smooth variance and moment-coordinate identities speciﬁed in this paper,
       we prove a power stability estimate for the logarithmic Brunn–Minkowski inequality on origin-
       symmetric convex bodies. In each dimension at least three, a small deﬁcit forces the two bodies
       close in Hausdorﬀ distance to independent positive dilations of common complementary factors.
       The exponent depends only on dimension; the constant also depends on ﬁxed inner and outer radii
       and on an interior range of interpolation parameters. No boundary regularity, common reﬂection
       group, facet bound, or lower bound on block angles is required. The proof retains nonzero derivative
       energy in a compact-target moment limit, converts a weak boundary identity to an absolute ﬂux
       estimate, and constructs one product body whose two dilations approximate the original endpoints.
       The exponent is explicit but not sharp.
