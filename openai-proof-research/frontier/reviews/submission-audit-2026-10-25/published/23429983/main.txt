                              Functional hard-sphere ﬂuctuations
                               on a regular Boltzmann lifespan
                                                   Myeongjin Kang∗

                                                         Abstract
              We prove a path-space upgrade of the ﬁnite-dimensional hard-sphere ﬂuctuation theorem on
          an entire supplied regular Boltzmann interval. The setting is the deterministic, nonequilibrium,
          grand-canonical gas in three-dimensional whole space, with exact microscopic centering and no
          time smoothing. Relative to the pinned ﬁnite-history interfaces, one collective time translation
          of a connected history gives a shrinking-window factor at its sharp factorial-cumulant scale.
          This also localizes redundant contacts, whose times are determined rather than independent
          coordinates. A preﬁx-measurable global chronology indicator is retained through the translation
          and the ﬂux-tree estimate. Initial-link cylinders preserve the connected activity scale, and a
          ﬁxed Gaussian reserve keeps summability independent of the later moment order. A decorated
          ﬁnal-slab output kernel controls the number of jumping particles. Third moments at scale µ−3/2 ,
          dyadic chaining, and a uniform exact-centering comparison yield J1 convergence and C-tightness
          for every ﬁnite vector of compact smooth tests, with the Gaussian covariance of the credited
          ﬁnite-dimensional theorem.
