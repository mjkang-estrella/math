                                               Abstract
         For every convex polytope K ⊂ R with nonempty interior and at most six actual facets, we
                                         4

     prove V4 (ΠK) ≤ (160/3)V4 (K)3 , with equality precisely for simplices. Here hΠK (u) = V3 (K |
     u⊥ ) for unit u. An attained simplex-section maximum reduces the six-facet case to a signed sum

8    Minimum facets, aﬃne invariance and equality
A full-dimensional n-polytope has at least n + 1 facets. Indeed, after putting zero in its interior,
write its irredundant inequalities as hqi , xi ≤ 1. Its polar is conv(qi ): the inclusion in the polar
is immediate, and separation gives the reverse using boundedness and the interior point. Irredun-
dancy makes each qi a vertex (a nonvertex inequality would follow from the others). The polar
is full-dimensional and needs at least n + 1 vertices. With exactly n + 1 vertices they are aﬃnely
independent, so it is a simplex. Its polar is also a simplex: intersecting every choice of n of its facet
hyperplanes gives the n + 1 dual vertices, as follows directly from aﬃne barycentric coordinates
and the strictly positive barycentric coordinates of zero. Thus K with n + 1 facets is a simplex.
