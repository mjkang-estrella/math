\title[Weighted capacity for four-state reconstruction]{Weighted capacity for four-state reconstruction with variable edge channels}
\author{Myeongjin Kang}
\address{Independent researcher}
\date{}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Assuming a specified moment-saving inequality for a preserved class of
four-state posterior laws, we characterize reconstruction on deterministic
locally finite trees with variable symmetric ferromagnetic edge channels
by positivity of a weighted nonlinear capacity. The channel parameters
may approach either zero or one, and perfectly transmitting edges are
allowed. We give quantitative comparisons between capacity and limiting
posterior information with constants independent of the tree, its degrees,
and the edge parameters. The proof uses local posterior estimates valid
without channel smoothing and a flow estimate controlled by branch
splitting. The capacity is unchanged by replacing a chain by its composite
channel; on a ray, it gives the exact criterion that the product of the
channel parameters is positive.
\end{abstract}
