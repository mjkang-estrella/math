## Failed routes, corrections, and active obstructions

- **Setting γ=2 before shrinking Ω.** Invalid: the normalized witness on a strictly enclosing domain has γ_j=2/M_j<2 in general. The completed proof keeps γ_j and establishes bounded a_j before taking limits.
- **Weak convergence of positive fields through the trace quadratic.** Insufficient: the quadratic form in H is not a convex function on arbitrary Hermitian matrices. The proof pays strong L² convergence by the logarithmic-derivative Herglotz representation and uniform Fourier tails. No weak-limit shortcut remains.
- **Backward moments alone.** X*B^kY=0 gives an orthogonality/invariant-subspace constraint, not disk geometry. The essential extra equality is H^(1/2)(XX*−YY*)H^(1/2)=0. A rank-(d−1) boundary field then forces rank-one X,Y.
- **Generic simple spectrum by assumption.** Not allowed. The simple maximal support eigenvalue is proved for every irreducible block of size at most four, including repeated eigenvalues of B itself. The no-simple-direction alternative produces an explicit reducible Clifford pair.
- **Replacing W(A) by the active block's disk.** Invalid without a final argument: other reducing blocks might enlarge the convex hull. The scalar polynomial extracted from the original P is bounded on the full W(A); its monomial form excludes any larger hull. The strict domain monotonicity lemma gives a separate check.
- **Operator-valued maximum principle.** Norm saturation does not force a matrix Schur function to be scalar or constant; constant unitary channels are an obstruction. The common positive density is not itself a numerical-range inverse theorem. It becomes useful after the rank reduction, when a genuine scalar witness is available.
- **Scalar output rigidity under amplification.** Still false. For A=[[0,2],[0,0]] and P(z)=[[0,z],[1,0]], P[A] is the irreducible weighted chain (1,2,1), has norm 2, and lacks a reducing 2×2 norm-two nilpotent block. Its top-vector square-function sum is 9/4. This does not contradict base disk rigidity; the extracted scalar witness is p(z)=z.
- **Whole-operator rigidity.** A disk extremal summand can coexist with arbitrary summands whose numerical ranges lie inside the disk. E4 classifies geometry and extracts a scalar witness, not the whole A as a single Jordan block.
- **Claiming Q4(r)'s power modulus from equality.** Not done. Support spectral gaps, positive-curvature arc size and active-block separation can degenerate. A uniform quantitative argument through these cases remains necessary. Neither qualitative compactness nor semialgebraic existence is offered as a substitute.
