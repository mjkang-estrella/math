## Failed routes and decisions

1. Full consecutive-integer joint PD: already paid by J's bin-character factorization and completed I07 §9; demoted.
2. Largest-factor orderings, fixed congruences, parity and cyclic factor-count modes: spent R006/R008/R010 territory; excluded.
3. Recursive prime-predecessor/Pratt-tree law or two prime-neighbor partitions: require new conditional or shifted-size estimates; D introduction explicitly separates recursive independence assumptions, and I18 already records the prime-neighbor gap. Not renamed as I282.
4. Shrinking smoothness thresholds or rare conditioning from fixed PD: no source rate or relative error; TT's quantitative good-endpoint theorem is a direct close predecessor. Not nominated.
5. Small-vector marginal plus macroscopic PD: insufficient. The analytic record gives a complete countermodel preserving exact marginals and all fixed local independence.
6. Arbitrary small weight in D: literal inert-prime invariance fails at the selected cutoff; first-core, low-time and high-time comparison hypotheses also remain unpaid. Reducing the cutoff removes only the literal multiplier failure.
7. BV count swap across the full simplex: exact swap valid, required distribution bound unpaid beyond the classical strict budget. P deliberately stops below total selected mass 1/2.
8. General Kubilius theorem on a biased sample: valid only after its weighted divisibility sum is paid. That sum is paid for P and explicitly unpaid for an arbitrary macroscopic test in (B).
