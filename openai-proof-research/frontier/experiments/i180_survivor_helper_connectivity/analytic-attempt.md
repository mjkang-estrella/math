# Analytic attempt before final diagnostics

No new graph is proposed or tested. This record concerns arbitrary X
under the exact H180 hypotheses, and the already authorized I177 calibration.

## Attempt 1: choose a component of p symmetric-difference q

Because p and q have identical prescribed degrees, their difference is
a disjoint union of alternating cycles. Swapping all components yields
q. Swapping one component preserves degrees but may close a proper
middle segment of p. Existence of the final q does not make intermediate
packings acyclic. Moreover q is lost, not an allowed surviving helper.
Neither observation constructs an edge in H180's graph.

## Attempt 2: use a matching extension to supply a helper

Fix p and a prospective alternating cycle C. An opposite-colour helper
must use exactly C minus p on C, and omit C intersect p. At every
nonendpoint vertex of C, its third incident edge must then be used by
both packings. At a port used as an endpoint by just one of the two
types, the difference has an endpoint and cannot be a cycle through
that port. Thus even placing C in a helper overlay is prescribed
multi-edge data, not an arbitrary two-edge matching extension.

The complementary global perfect matching must omit/include all these
edges simultaneously, as well as respect a surviving exterior choice.
Brace two-extendability gives no stated simultaneous extension theorem
for the whole cycle's prescription. Even if that matching exists, its
complement may have several cycles. This route stops before constructing
u, and certainly before proving that its exchanged v is acyclic.
This is a failure to obtain a theorem from the available statements,
not a proof that no stronger deduction could exist.

## Attempt 3: prescribed three-edge Hamiltonian paths

The stipulated theorem prescribes three consecutive edges. The cycle
halves here are generally several disjoint edges; their continuations
also determine a complete local pairing. No construction reducing these
requirements to one prescribed three-edge path has been supplied.
Replacing the unspecified remainder of a given packing by another
Hamiltonian cycle need not preserve any required edge sum. The known
parent six-cycle works because its actual helper words are supplied,
not because a three-edge existence theorem supplies them.

## Attempt 4: strict Hamming descent toward a chosen target

The complete allowed finite calibration refutes this proposed proof
strategy even though it supports H180 on that graph. With target p4,
the only graph route from p0 begins p0,p1,p2. Their Hamming distances to
p4 are 8,8,8, followed by 6,0 at p3,p4. In particular no legal first
move strictly decreases the distance from p0 to p4. This is an exact
finite obstruction to that particular strict descent measure, not a
counterexample to H180.

The nine previously known nonsingle-component identities also have no
legal first move while their original two input packings alone remain
the two colours. The complete helper graph permits replacing the helper
between moves; it does not assert a decomposition of those fixed-pair
identities into safe switches.

## What remains unpaid

Given a proper reachable component of the complete P fibre, construct a
surviving helper and a whole alternating cycle whose actual complete
outputs cross its boundary. The stipulated eleven nonempty fibres give
some packing in each type, not this packing with this prescription.
A valid argument also needs a well-founded global measure or an argument
excluding a closed proper reachable component. Both are missing.

The deleted-segment test in the report is a verification criterion,
not a helper-existence result and not a newly paid structural bridge.
H180 remains UNRESOLVED, as do universal diff(P) containment and L179.
