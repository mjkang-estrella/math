## 2. Notation and separate I170 audit verdicts

Let B consist of finite simple cubic bipartite planar 3-vertex-connected graphs with a specified plane embedding. An admissible expansion deletes disjoint cofacial edges `e=uv`, `f=xy`, with cyclic order `u,v,...,y,x,...`, and inserts arms `up,vq,xr,ys` and square `pq,qs,sr,rp`, obtaining another member of B. Let `K=G-{e,f}`. All spaces and affine combinations below are over Q. Edge coordinates are in sorted old-edge order, including zero coordinates at deleted edges when appropriate. `diff(A)` means the span of pairwise incidence differences; it is zero for an empty or singleton family.

`Nij` denotes parent Hamiltonian cycles with e-status i and f-status j. Set

    S = N10 union N01 union N11,    L = diff(S),
    L0 = diff(N10)+diff(N01)+diff(N11),
    V = diff(N00),
    U = diff(Pux)+diff(Pvy)+diff(D11),
    ell = d(G)-dim L,    t = dim(L+U)-dim L.

`Pux,Pvy` are spanning paths in K with the indicated endpoints. `D11` consists of spanning two-path covers paired u-v and x-y. With representatives `C0 in S`, `P in Pux`, `Q in Pvy`, `D in D11`, write

    b = P+Q+D+2e+2f-3C0,
    eta = dim(L+U+<b>)-dim(L+U).

Different representatives change b only by L+U. The six-fibre expansion identity is `d(H)=2+dim(L+U+<b>)`; hence exchange is equivalent to `t+eta>=ell`. The three surviving deleted-edge signatures give `rho(L)=Q^2`, `dim L=2+dim L0`, and `L intersect ker rho=L0`. For `A in N10,B in N01,C in N11,N in N00`, the useful K-supported vectors are

    beta=P+Q+D+2e+2f-A-B-C,    c=N-A-B+C,
    d(G)=2+dim(L0+V+<c>).

These are exact affine identities; no deleted-edge separator or typed-fullness premise is used.

### (a) H170 — PASS

Assume `a=ux` is an edge of K. The maps `N -> N-a` from N00 to Pux and `Q -> Q-a` from Pvy to D11 are exact translation bijections. Here is the independent argument.

Every N00 cycle must use a, since each terminal has degree two in K. Conversely a spanning ux path cannot contain ux: the shared endpoint edge would itself constitute an isolated two-vertex path component. Adding a therefore closes a spanning cycle in K. For Q in Pvy, the internal vertices u,x must use a. Deleting a separates them into different path components. The crossing pairing u-y/v-x is excluded by two disjoint arcs in the disk complementary to the face; the remaining pairing is u-v/x-y. Conversely a D11 cover cannot use a, and adding it joins its two paths into one spanning vy path. P4 nonemptiness of Pux ensures N00 is nonempty. The argument with ux and vy interchanged is identical.

Thus, with `Z=diff(Pvy)`, `U=V+Z`. The parent difference space is `L+V+<r>`, where `r=N-C0`, so `t>=ell-1`. On a bipartite admissible facial expansion the two complementary arcs have positive odd lengths. For a hexagon their lengths sum to four, so one has length one. Every facial hexagon therefore has a short gap; quadrilaterals have two.

Choose `P=N-a`, `D=Q-a` and set `z=Q+e+f-a-C0`. Direct vector addition gives `b=r+2z`; arbitrary representatives preserve this equality modulo L+U. In the equality case `t=ell-1`, r is nonzero modulo L+U, and exchange fails exactly when `z=-r/2` in that quotient. This is an equivalence, not a proof of equality-case bridge independence.

Fresh enumeration verifies both full set bijections, space equality, lower bound, and exact bridge quotient on **580** short-gap census steps, including all **282** hexagonal steps.

### (b) R170 — PASS

Assume N00 is nonempty. For the odd boundary arc A from u to x avoiding e,f, define `q_A=h0-h1+...+h(2k)`; define q_B on the v-y arc similarly. For J=Pux, Pvy, D11 use `q_J=q_A,q_B,q_A+q_B`, respectively. Set

    F_J = {N in N00 : N-q_J belongs to J},
    R_J = {P in J : P+q_J does not belong to N00}.

Membership here includes binary entries, spanning connectivity, and the specified endpoint pairing. If F_J is nonempty, choose `N_J in F_J`, put `P_J=N_J-q_J`, and let `E_J={P-P_J:P in R_J}`. If F_J is empty, choose any P_J in J and use all J in that last expression.

The translation `N -> N-q_J` is a bijection from F_J to `J\R_J`. Differences in its image equal `diff(F_J)`. Differences from one image anchor to the residual elements add exactly `span(E_J)`. If the image is empty, differences from any anchor give all of diff(J). Consequently, in both cases,

    diff(J) = diff(F_J)+span(E_J).

Let `V_good=sum_J diff(F_J)` and `R=span(union_J E_J)`. Then `V_good subset V` and `U=V_good+R`, with no blanket containment of V asserted. Define

    delta_V = dim(L0+V)-dim(L0+V_good),
    delta_R = dim(L0+V_good+R)-dim(L0+V_good),
    epsilon = dim(L0+V+<c>)-dim(L0+V).

Subtracting the exact dimension formulas yields

    ell-t = delta_V+epsilon-delta_R,
    t>=ell-1  iff  delta_R>=delta_V+epsilon-1,
    exchange  iff  delta_R+eta>=delta_V+epsilon.

All class corrections and joint-space equalities are checked on the 636 census steps with both exact ranks. If N00 is empty, ell=0 and these anchors are unnecessary; the lower bound and exchange follow immediately. None of the enumerated steps has empty N00.

## 3. Primary attack: exact square-weight reformulation

**Proposition W171 (proved).** For every G in B and every admissible facial quadrilateral expansion in the equality case `t=ell-1`, let the face be `u-v-y-x-u`, let `a=ux,c'=vy`, choose `N in N00,C0 in S`, and set

    W=L+V,    r=N-C0,    s=e+f-a-c',    F=N+s.

Then F is a spanning 2-factor with exactly two components. The parent difference space is `D_G=W+Qr`, with `r notin W`, and `b=3r+2s mod W`.

If s is outside D_G, b is outside W and exchange holds. Otherwise there is a unique rational alpha with `s=alpha*r mod W`. Every linear functional annihilating W and normalized by `w(r)=1` satisfies

    w(C-C0)=0 for C in S,
    w(C-C0)=1 for C in N00,
    w(F-C0)=1+alpha.

Exchange fails if and only if this last value is `-1/2`. Moreover F belongs to the affine span of the Hamiltonian cycles. In **every signed affine representation** `F=sum_C lambda_C C`, with `sum_C lambda_C=1`, one has

    sum_{C in N00} lambda_C = 1+alpha.

**Proof.** The quadrilateral deletion bijections identify all three exterior difference spaces with V. Removing a,c' from N yields the D11 pairing; adding e,f closes each of its two paths separately, proving the two-component assertion. The difference-space and bridge formulas follow from the representative choices `N-a,N-c',N-a-c'`. When s is in D_G, apply w to these identities. Applying w to any affine representation proves the coefficient-total formula and shows it is representation-independent. This uses rational affine combinations; no positivity is assumed.

There is also an exact integral reformulation. In the one-dimensional rational quotient D_G/W, let Gamma be the additive subgroup generated by `[r]` and `[s]`. It is infinite cyclic because alpha is rational. Choose its generator g with `[r]=m g`, m>0, and write `[s]=k g`; then `gcd(m,k)=1` and `alpha=k/m`. Thus

    exchange fails iff (m,k)=(2,-3),
    alpha=-(h-1)/h for a positive integer h
        iff [F-C0]=g and [r]=h g.

Indeed the first statement is just the primitive integral solution of `3m+2k=0`. For the second, `[F-C0]=(m+k)g`; positivity and primitiveness mean `m+k=1`. This identifies the observed integer shape with a **positive primitive generator assertion about this particular square switch**. Neither that assertion nor a weaker obstruction to `(2,-3)` has been proved from graph structure here. Integrality alone does not forbid the pair `(2,-3)`.

### A convex-combination shortcut is refuted

For every disconnected 0/1 spanning 2-factor F, an equality `F=sum lambda_C C` with Hamiltonian incidence vectors, `lambda_C>=0`, and `sum lambda_C=1` is impossible. At an edge coordinate where F is zero all positive-weight summands must be zero; at a coordinate where F is one all must be one. Hence every positive-weight summand would equal F in all coordinates, contradicting connectedness of C. This is stronger than treating that shortcut as merely unproved. It does not refute a scalar bound specifically on `w(F-C0)`.

## 5. Secondary attack: octagons with two length-three arcs

**Proposition O171 (proved local classification).** For every admissible expansion in B of an octagonal face with complementary arcs `u-a1-a2-x` and `v-b1-b2-y`, and every N in N00, the two outer edges of each arc belong to N. For one arc A, `N-q_A` is binary if and only if its middle edge is absent from N. When binary, it consists of a spanning u-x path and either zero or one cycle. For both arcs together, `N-q_A-q_B` is binary if and only if both middle edges are absent; when binary it consists of two paths and zero, one, or two cycles. The endpoint pairing must still be checked to obtain D11.

**Proof.** The four terminal degrees in K force the outer edges. Subtraction of `(+1,-1,+1)` removes the two outer edges and adds one copy of the middle edge. The only possible nonbinary coordinate is therefore the middle edge, which would become two. Removing two edges from a Hamiltonian cycle leaves two path components, and adding one edge either joins them or closes one, giving the claimed alternatives. With four removed outer edges and two added middle edges, the corresponding alternatives give two paths and at most two cycles. Degrees leave precisely the four terminals as path endpoints. Binary entries alone do not enforce connectivity or the desired pairing.

All these assertions are additionally checked during every minimal-octagon diagnostic. They reduce the compatibility test to a small list of topological failures but supply no bound on the dimensions of the missing old directions. The desired universal statement

    delta_R >= delta_V+epsilon-1

for this octagonal case remains **OPEN**.
