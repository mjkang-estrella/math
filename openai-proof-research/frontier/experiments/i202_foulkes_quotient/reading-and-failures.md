## Mathematical attempts and failures

1. **Determinant multiplication succeeds, with a narrow boundary.** The covariant K(F;y) gives D^(p-1) A_1 inside A_p when AT(p) is nonzero. Kotlar's prime theorem applies at p=7. The constant is a signed count; the nonzero proof is not positivity. Reducing the whole constant modulo p would be invalid because it contains p!; only AT(p) is reduced. All-dimensional extension uses the precise Pieri support and the target's p-row bound.
2. **The determinant is not the module.** D^2 z(e_1)^4 and D^4 z(e_1)^2 are distinct nonzero seven-row classes in C_6. Their survival at degree six is not survival at degree eight. The new multiplication identity does not cover them or establish a generating description of C_6.
3. **Trace straightening fails to give the required filtration.** The eight-trace relation reduces trace length but can merge factors into high-degree monomials in a single trace. Converting back to elementary generators increases the relevant length. Per-row degree bounds can hold only after cancellation of such terms. Neither Theorem 2.5 nor the finite total-degree relation bound supplies that cancellation estimate.
4. **Complete jets were retained, not separated termwise.** In the first two-index coefficient identity, the one-slot and two-slot terms have identical perturbation degree and opposite possible values. No ordering separating them was proved. The complete repeated-index system is exact, but merely writing it does not prove its rank.
5. **Stronger singularity constraints still fail.** Differentiating the known gradient identity pays tangent-Hessian vanishing. The binary q(P)^2 a_0(P)^3 example satisfies this and fails at the explicit split product X^8+Y^8. It refutes that proposed replacement of the original condition; it is not a Foulkes counterexample.
6. **Projection positivity is insufficient.** P_H P_K P_H is positive semidefinite. A strictly positive lower bound is exactly the missing (7,8) injectivity. Known (5,5) kernels show there can be no blanket argument that row/column symmetrization makes it positive definite.
7. **No old gap was relabeled as solved.** Generic ambient differential injectivity, regularity 36, chart localization and the ternary determinant-square restriction were not asserted to prove the target. The new filtered chart model is an exact bookkeeping device for the remaining estimate, not a denominator-clearing improvement.
