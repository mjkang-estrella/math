## Serious approaches and exact failures

1. **Retain phase-damping/Epstein slack alone.** The parent's maximally-entangled/identity-tail saturation already defeats root-impurity payment in that single step. It was treated as a warning, not as a counterexample to every King ensemble.
2. **Combine phase and mixture/Jensen losses.** The nonmaximal equatorial Schmidt-label counterexample defeats the combined tradeoff: every balanced measurement loses the label, both old losses vanish, and root impurity is strictly positive. Equality of every phase branch is proved from equality in the Euclidean triangle inequality.
3. **Replace root impurity by exact two-copy drift for arbitrary Omega.** The same two-qubit example has F_2=p(1-p)>0 and every F_1=0, but zero entropy residual. Parent proposed this investigation and later explicitly corrected its arbitrary-partner version; both input and correction are credited. No such estimate is assumed.
4. **Use faithful higher-copy local payment with arbitrary Omega.** A test seeing entanglement at two sites and accepting every pure one-site state has the same zero-residual obstruction. Copy number alone cannot fix the arbitrary-partner quantifier.
5. **Restrict root-impurity payment to actual GAD tensor tails.** Still false: equatorial cats have fixed impurity but residual tending to zero, uniformly over balanced ensembles. I279's collective-label mechanism is credited. No claim against the true global target follows.
6. **Use those cats against tensor-tail exact-drift payment.** This counterexample attempt FAILS. The actual drift is 2p(1-p)2^(1-n). Complementary-pair blocks show a coherence entropy loss at rate s², and Bayesian label ambiguity contributes rate sqrt(1-s²). Their maximum is always greater than 1/2, so the cat residual/drift ratio grows, not shrinks. Exact phase-uniform asymptotics and parity constants are proved analytically.
7. **Amosov/complementary dephasing.** Direct balanced-qubit reconstruction identifies a relative-entropy data-processing loss, but the label-recording example saturates it. Sequentially applying the pure-input result to a mixed intermediate state is unjustified. No recovery/tensorization estimate was obtained.
8. **Unbalanced or block/amortized mechanisms.** Unbalanced X measurement restores the label in the counterexample, but no universal same-ensemble entropy bound follows. Actual GAD tensor-partner exact-drift payment, heterogeneous partial-test payment, and genuine block/amortized estimates remain unpaid. The alternative mixture and average partial-projector inequality alone do not establish any of them.
9. **Known invalid shortcuts.** I284's forced diag(1,1,s) same-s postmap is not CP; isolating an all-depolarizing tensor branch loses exponentially. I279's sum of original marginal impurities is false. Fixed-n compactness, local coefficients and an n-dependent inverse do not give the requested result.
