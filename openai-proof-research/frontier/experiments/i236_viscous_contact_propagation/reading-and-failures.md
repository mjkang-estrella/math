## Chronology of the mathematical work

A partial mathematical file was written after the initial source-contract read, before the comparison route was closed. It first recorded the spatial cone-infimum reduction and a genuinely unpaid contact theorem. An append then recorded the spatial-quadratic maximization lemma for time control, followed by the divergence/Fatou route. The final file integrates those steps with full proofs.

1. The parent's least-concave-majorant observation was checked against source 05. The spatial semiconcavity constant is 1/t, locally uniform away from t=0. No space-time semiconcavity was inferred.
2. A direct viscosity/Pucci route was investigated. Bounded horizontal gradients give local uniform ellipticity, but do not bound the change in the operator from perturbing a gradient when Hessians grow. The smooth one-dimensional exact solution family in analytic Section 7 demonstrates that obstruction with actual solution jets. The direct difference-inequality shortcut was rejected.
3. A spatial cone infimum of the supersolution was proved to preserve contact, order and the supersolution PDE, and to bound horizontal slopes. It initially left the time issue open.
4. Spatial quadratic maximization of W gives a one-dimensional semiconvex function of time by the actual PDE. This proves local full Lipschitz continuity of W. A full-space cone infimum then yields a locally Lipschitz supersolution near each contact; its strict slope gap gives recovery of contact with the original V.
5. Trudinger's actual comparison hypotheses were checked, not inferred from later citations. F2 fails for this quasilinear principal part. This remains true despite bounded gradients. No theorem from that source is used to close SC_tau.
6. The operator's exponential flux was used only after the full gradients were bounded. Autonomous sup/inf convolutions preserve the viscosity inequalities; ordinary mollification enters only the one-sided Fatou argument. Gradient convergence at differentiability points pays the nonlinear flux limit. The resulting linear divergence inequality is uniformly elliptic on each local gradient range.
7. The final contact propagation uses linear weak-solution Harnack through an explicit harmonic replacement, so the cited solution theorem is not silently applied to supersolutions. The contact set of the original pair is open and closed.

## Counterexamples/checks

Analytic Section 7 gives (a) smooth exact G_tau-solution jets disproving a Hessian-independent gradient-Lipschitz operator bound, (b) a smooth exact solution whose ordinary spatial mollification is not a subsolution, and (c) the all-center/all-radius fitted time-only envelope t log|t| showing that slice semiconcavity without the PDE does not imply time Lipschitz continuity. The first two are local operator counterexamples and are explicitly not claimed to satisfy global FIT. There is no counterexample to SC_tau or finite-tau affinity here.

The I231 infinite-tau obstruction sin t remains untouched. Affinity at each finite tau implies that any locally uniform limit of exact entire finite-tau fitted solutions is affine, but the divided limiting PDE plus FIT alone still admits sin t. No sequence realizing that nonaffine limit is claimed.
