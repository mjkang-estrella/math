## Primary-source change that motivates this branch

He--Sahai, [arXiv:2608.06681v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06681v1),
Theorem 1.1 and Remark 4.2, rule out a positive one-dimensional divisor-cover
progression of length n^(2/3+o(1)) and logarithmic height n^(1/3+o(1)).
Their obstruction does not cover higher-rank progressions. Parent read the
statement and full incidence proof, including the positivity and zero guard.

Umans--Wang, [arXiv:2511.10851v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.10851v1),
Sections 3, 5 and Appendix A, supply the conditional structured-difference
consumer and a prefactored-leaf condition. Parent inspected those specific
definitions and algorithms, not a complete independent audit of the paper.
Construction cost and coverage across all input sizes remain obligations here;
existence on infinitely many sizes is not supplied as uniform advice.

## A. A concrete rank-two CRT grid, and its height cost

Take a public band of primes P in [a*sqrt(n),sqrt(n)] for fixed 0<a<1.
For this construction their enumeration is public paid preprocessing, not
hidden factors of the eventual N. Split the band into nonempty groups A,B.
Choose desired indices i_p,j_q in [0,H) for p in A,q in B. Put

    C=product_(q in B) q,  D=product_(p in A) p.

By CRT choose b modulo CD satisfying

    b=-C*i_p mod p (p in A),
    b=-D*j_q mod q (q in B).

Then the rank-two grid F(i,j)=b+C*i+D*j satisfies

    p*q divides F(i_p,j_q) for every p in A,q in B.

Indeed p kills D and q kills C; the remaining congruences are the chosen
ones. Add CD to b if necessary to make all grid entries positive, excluding
zero as a vacuous divisibility witness. Using O(log |P|) binary partitions
of distinct prime labels places every distinct pair on opposite sides of at
least one partition, so a union of these grids covers every band semiprime.
This is an actual cover construction for that scoped subset, not all d<=n.

It saves explicit pair assignment: one congruence per prime supplies all
cross-pair witnesses. It does NOT meet the desired height or construction
cost. The product CD contains every prime in the band, hence

    max(log C,log D) >= (1/2)*sum_(p in P)log p = Theta(sqrt(n)).

The last asymptotic is the standard prime-number-theorem band estimate
also used in the cited incidence proof. With positive coefficients and
H>=2, grid height is at least max(C,D), before even accounting for b.
Explicit prime enumeration also has sqrt(n)/log n output entries. This
axis-aligned CRT template cannot obtain logarithmic height n^(1/3).
This is a restriction of the template, not a theorem against rank-two covers.

The exact failed assumption is that independent row/column prime constraints
can be encoded for free in short coefficients. A possible escape must use
oblique modular lines or shared algebraic constraints, without forcing a
whole prime group to divide one coefficient. Merely replacing the word
"progression" by "grid" does not supply that computation.

## B. Remove prefactoring by demanding separating collision signatures

Integer splitting needs a prime divisor, not a multiple of every composite
up to n in an individual leaf. Consider an N-only generated pair of lists
S,T with m entries each. The sufficient guarantee for squarefree N=pq is:

    some s in S,t in T has exactly one of p,q dividing s-t.       (1)

This is a *separating prime cover*. Ordinary prime coverage alone does not
give (1): both primes may divide exactly the same differences. Arbitrary
generation, input-dependent choices and complete construction time remain
explicitly unpaid until a construction is supplied. The following consumer,
however, is concrete and needs no factorization of an enormous integer leaf.

Reduce the acquired entries modulo N and deduplicate each list exactly.
Let F(X)=product_(t in T)(X-t), a monic polynomial over Z/NZ. Compute F and
its formal derivative and evaluate both at all s in S by monic product and
remainder trees. For each s use

    H_s=F(s),       if s is not an exact residue in T;
    H_s=F'(s),      if s is an exact residue in T.

Because T has no repeated exact residues, H_s is precisely the product of
all s-t with t unequal to s modulo N. Differentiation removes ONLY the full
modulus zero difference at that row; it does not remove other edges that
can carry a proper factor. The derivative identity holds over any commutative
ring, and introduces no nonunit division or factorial denominator.

If a row gcd is proper, return it. If gcd(H_s,N)=N, descend the T product
tree for that one row, evaluating the same exclusion product at each child:
ordinary value if the exact residue s is absent, derivative if it is present.
Choose a child with gcd>1. At a leaf the excluded exact match has product 1;
every other difference is nonzero modulo N, so its nonunit gcd is proper.
Thus this descent cannot terminate at an unhelpful full-zero leaf. A single
row descent suffices to find one factor, and costs O(m) scalar work plus
gcds if its subtree polynomials are retained. Monic batch construction and
evaluation cost soft O(m*poly(log N)); deduplication and residue generation
are charged separately. There is no m^2 list of differences.

Under (1), a separating edge survives exact-residue exclusion, so a row has
a nonunit H_s. The full-modulus case is handled by this paid descent. This
also works when an actual difference divisible by N is huge: it is discarded
as an exact modular equality and never needs its own integer factorization.
If ALL covered edges are full equalities, the construction fails its required
separation premise. No claim that ordinary divisor coverage implies (1) is made.

## Selection and decisive obligations

The axis-aligned construction is rejected at its height/preprocessing gate.
The separating consumer is the strongest usable primitive in this wave, but
is not a factor-cover generator. Its exact deletion/localization proof and
prior art should be audited; this may be established collision machinery in
a different presentation, and no novelty claim is made.

Next constructive target: acquire structured prime-separating signatures
below the ordinary square-root search scale, without an explicit prime table,
large coefficient products, or a prefactored-leaf oracle. Oblique rank-two
lines are a concrete representation to investigate; their generation and
coverage remain missing. A proposed circuit must specify actual coefficients
and a paid algorithm before any experiment or complexity promotion.

An exact failure test for each proposed generator is whether two primes have
the same cross-collision incidence after exact-residue exclusion. This is a
symbolic or preregistered finite falsifier, not permission for unrestricted
post-hoc parameter search. No new numerical run is selected by this note.
