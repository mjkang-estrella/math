# W034 — input-dependent order annihilators

Parent constructive proposal, 2026-10-08. **Unreviewed; no numerical run.**
Verified complete factoring improvement: **NONE**. This is a separate order
route; it does not use the W032 carry family or relation collection.

## Executable mechanism

Choose a public positive integer k and form the positive even exponent

    E_k(N) = product_(j=1..k) (N^j-1),   N odd and N>1.

Construct powers N^j successively, and form their product with a product
tree. Its bit length is O(k^2 n), n=bitlength(N). For a uniformly sampled
unit a modulo N (each rejected raw sample's gcd is checked and charged),
write E=2^v u, u odd, compute x=a^u modN and inspect gcd(x-1,N) followed
by all v successive squarings and gcds, including the final a^E value.
Validate every proper divisor. One attempt takes O(k^2 n M(n)) modular
bit work plus exponent construction, gcds, sampling and storage. A loose
polynomial bound for constructing E suffices when k=poly(n); no giant
integer or exponent circuit is treated as one operation.

The work this replaces is enumeration of small primes to build a smooth
annihilator. Since N=pq implies N=q modulo p-1, this exponent kills the
whole p-component precisely when

    p-1 divides product_(j=1..k)(q^j-1).

This condition can hold with large prime divisors of p-1. Neither p nor q
is used by the algorithm. Prime-power valuations in the displayed product,
not just its radical, are required. The analogous q-component condition
exchanges p and q. No assertion of novelty or frequent occurrence is made.

## Exact proposed success law for distinct odd prime factors

For N=pq define diagnostic quantities only:

    d_p=gcd(E,p-1),  h_p=d_p/(p-1),  s_p=v2(d_p),
    d_q=gcd(E,q-1),  h_q=d_q/(q-1),  s_q=v2(d_q).

Uniform units give independent uniform cyclic components. An E-th root of
one occupies fraction h_p of the p-component. If exactly one component is
an E-th root, the final gcd is proper. Conditional on both being roots,
the full two-adic chain fails exactly when the two element orders have the
same power of two. In a cyclic 2^s group that valuation is zero with mass
2^-s and j>=1 with mass 2^(j-1-s). Thus the proposed exact probability is

    P = h_p+h_q-h_p*h_q*(1+C(s_p,s_q)),
    C(s,t)=(4^min(s,t)+2)/(3*2^(s+t)).

E is even, so s_p,s_q>=1 and C<=1/2. If either component is wholly
annihilated, P>=1/2, including the case of a full final gcd. This is a
conditional factoring guarantee, not a theorem that the input-dependent
annihilator works on general inputs. It uses the original uniform base;
conditioning a base on a selected successful chart would change the law.
General composites, perfect powers and repeated factors need their own
reduction before any general algorithm claim.

## Concrete obstruction and constructive decision

For an odd prime ell dividing p-1, assume ell does not divide q. The
radical condition is ord_ell(q)<=k for at least one j in [1,k], that is,
ord_ell(q) is itself at most k. The union of roots of the polynomials
X^j-1 has at most k(k+1)/2 residues in F_ell. If ell divides q, no factor
q^j-1 vanishes at ell. A large rough component with order exceeding k
therefore prevents full annihilation; valuation requirements can only
make coverage narrower. This is a local root-count bound, NOT a bound
on the distribution of prime q or on every input-dependent exponent.

The decisive cheap test is symbolic: identify whether this is already a
known variant of Pollard p-1, verify the exact two-adic success law, and
derive a paid family of inputs where the cross-order condition holds or
fails. Do not start a numerical screen yet. A general complexity gain
would require a proved useful-input law or a materially different exponent
family with better coverage per bit of exponent. Naming that family is
not enough: its integer construction, powering, and two-adic consumption
must remain explicit.

Primary literature to check includes Pollard's original p-1 method and
the Bach–Shallit cyclotomic methods; this candidate uses polynomials in
the known N for an exponent, so resemblance is not a novelty conclusion.
The unrelated W032 sweep remains a finite negative on two completed cases;
its third prescribed case was budget-stopped, not counted as a failure.
