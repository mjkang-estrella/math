# W005 — Symbolic orientation law for a precisely fixed four-squares sampler

2026-10-08. Supplement to [the independent review](W005-quaternion-validation.md).
This is an additional deduction proved here, not a distribution claim attributed
to the authors. No mathematical program or sampling was executed. It should receive the
parent's mathematical review before acceptance. Verified factoring improvement:
**NONE**.

## Fixed sampler and scope

Use [Pollack–Treviño, §4](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/sjs15/sjs15.pdf),
specifically amended trial (T), p. 11. Handle even N and N<=20 separately;
stop with a factor if its preliminary small-prime divisions split N.
For the remaining odd N put

    P = product of primes l<=log(N) with l=3 mod 4,
    M = N P / gcd(P,N).

Each trial draws x,y independently uniformly modulo M, sets
`r=-(x^2+y^2) mod M`, and uses fresh independent randomness in (T).
Success has `gcd(r,M)=1`, and produces z,w with `z^2+w^2=r`.
All acceptance decisions and z,w depend on r and that fresh randomness,
not otherwise on x,y. Output

    A = gcrd(N, q),       q=x+yi+zj+wk,

normalizing to integer coordinates only by a **left** Hurwitz unit.
These conventions are essential to the claim. Changing acceptance using
x,y, correlating the fresh randomness with x,y beyond r, or postprocessing
by an adaptive right unit requires a new proof. The original sampler's
cost is charged in the main report.

## A local bijection

Let `p^e || N`, `R=Z/p^e Z`, `S=R[i]=R[T]/(T^2+1)`, and let bar send
i to -i. Put `chi=(-1/p)`. The algebra S is split if chi=1 and unramified
quadratic if chi=-1. Both have a unit c with `c*bar(c)=-1`: this follows
over F_p from the split product or finite-field norm and lifts since p
is odd. Represent Hamilton quaternions on the free rank-two R-module S by

    i(s)=i*s,                 j(s)=c*bar(s).

These operators square to -1 and anticommute, giving the same split matrix
model used in the main review. Fix any r unit and `v=z+wi` with norm r.
For each `u=x+yi` of norm -r, the quaternion `q=u+v j` acts as

    s |-> u*s + v*c*bar(s).                                  (S1)

Its matrix is primitive with determinant zero in R. A unit entry permits
row/column elimination to `diag(unit,0)`, so its kernel is a free
rank-one summand.

Every primitive vector in that kernel belongs to `S^*`. In the unramified
case a nonunit is precisely an element of pS and cannot be primitive. In
the split case `S=R x R`, equation (S1) couples the two coordinates with
unit coefficients; if one coordinate of a kernel vector is divisible
by p, so is the other. Thus a primitive kernel vector has both coordinates
units.

Consequently the possible kernel lines lie in the fixed set

    Omega_(p,e) = S^* / R^*  subset P^1(R).

They are all attained exactly once as u varies on its norm fiber. Indeed,
for a representative `s in S^*`, the unique choice is

    u = -v*c*bar(s)/s.                                       (S2)

It has norm -r. Scaling s by an R-unit changes nothing. Conversely, equality
of (S2) for s and s' says that s/s' is fixed by bar, hence is an R-unit
(2 is invertible). This proves injectivity; the kernel description proves
surjectivity. Thus uniform u on its norm fiber gives uniform kernel line
on Omega, **independently of r and v**.

In the unramified case Omega is all of `P^1(R)`. In the split case it is
the lines with both coordinates units in an eigenbasis of i; equivalently,
their reductions avoid the two eigenlines of i modulo p. Counting units,

    |Omega_(p,e)| = |S^*|/|R^*| = p^(e-1)(p-chi).             (S3)

Reduction to `Omega_(p,a)` has fibers of size `p^(e-a)` for `1<=a<=e`.

## Conditioning on successful trials and on the quaternion gcd

Condition on a successful residue r and all the trial's auxiliary
randomness, including the choice of z,w. The pair (x,y) remains uniform
among the solutions of `x^2+y^2=-r mod M`. This fiber is a Cartesian
product of local fibers by CRT; its components at the prime powers of N
are independent uniform fibers. The local bijection proves that the
kernel lines of q are independently uniform on their respective Omega's.
The distribution does not depend on the conditioned values r,z,w.
Unconditioning, including over the number of rejected trials, therefore
preserves this product law. This is the step that cannot be inferred just
from “independent random seeds.”

The gcd does not change these right-kernel lines. Its defining ideal is
`O N + O q = O A`. Modulo `p^e`, the scalar N vanishes, so the row modules
generated by q and A coincide. Their kernels coincide. Left unit
normalization of A preserves its right kernel as well. Hence the output
A has exactly the product distribution just proved. No local splitting
or unknown prime is required to execute the sampler; they occur only in
this analysis.

This also verifies the output norm locally: q has Smith exponents
`0,f` with `f>=e`, and adding `p^e R^2` to its row lattice gives exponents
`0,e`. Thus the gcd is primitive and has norm valuation exactly e at
each p dividing N; its norm has no other prime divisors because it
divides the norm N^2 of the scalar input N. Its norm is therefore N.

For two independent outputs of this fixed sampler, if
`k=v_p(norm(gcrd(A_1,A_2)))`, the main report's lattice formula now gives

    Pr(k>=a) = 1/(p^(a-1)(p-chi)),       1<=a<=e.            (S4)

The k's are independent across primes. For distinct primes N=pq, set
`H_p=p-(-1/p)` and `H_q=q-(-1/q)`. The exact one-pair split probability is

    rho = 1/H_p + 1/H_q - 2/(H_p H_q).                     (S5)

This is a separately proved sampler law, not a transfer of the idealized
`1/(p+1)` law. It covers only right gcds and the fixed variant above;
no output-image distribution or arbitrary sampler guarantee is asserted.
After the preliminary factor checks, balanced semiprimes still have
`rho=Theta(N^(-1/2))`. Repeated disjoint pairs therefore require
`Theta(sqrt(N))` attempts in expectation. With the main report's cost
bound, expected work is `O((S(n)+n^3)/rho)`: restart accounting follows
from `E[total]=E[one attempt]+(1-rho)E[total]`, and does not require an
attempt's duration to be independent of its success.

The birthday sample scale is still `Theta(N^(1/4))`, with quadratic
all-pairs extraction unless a new detection algorithm is supplied.
There is no new factoring exponent here.

Finally, the cheap changed generator `B=A i` **always fails to split**
when A comes from this remaining-input core. At primes `3 mod 4`, i has
no eigenline; at primes `1 mod 4`, Omega excludes its two eigenlines.
Thus its right-gcd norm is 1, while its left-gcd norm is N. The main
report's norm-15 counterexample uses an admissible A outside this sampler's
right-orientation support. It remains a valid refutation of the general
norm-of-difference domination claim.

Read scope is exactly that recorded in the main report; no additional
source or another child's result was consumed for this deduction. The
local bijection, conditioning argument, and consequences (S1)–(S5) are
symbolic derivations, not measured or formally verified results.
