point of that cover. Therefore no nonconstant rational one-parameter
parametrization of its full splitting data exists. No conclusion that a
nonzero further split fiber is impossible follows. Faltings finiteness is
external qualitative background, not an effective solver or complexity bound.

branch configuration need not have good reduction there: b0-b1 vanishes at
p=17. Do not transfer the characteristic-zero group/genus assertion to every
such prime. The anti-commuting determinant has at most two distinct local
roots; prime-power valuation amplification can lose a factor by producing
full gcd N, not create a factor absent from the unpowered base.
