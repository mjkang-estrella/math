# H028 — Sharing work across number-field relation searches

2026-10-08. **Exploratory analytic round; no accepted algorithm claim.**
Verified full factoring complexity improvement: **NONE**.

## Question and prior-art gate

Can a concrete polynomial-family construction share enough work or relation
columns to improve end-to-end classical factoring complexity beyond known
multiple-polynomial NFS? Sharing rational smoothness across fields is already
part of Coppersmith's method. First reconstruct the relevant primary model,
including polynomial generation, multiplicity and enlarged algebraic bases.
The reference heuristic constant has cube (92+26*sqrt(13))/27, approximately
1.90188 after taking the cube root. Its separate amortized-precomputation
result is not the cold cost of factoring an arbitrary single N.

H022's accepted conditional bound concerns fixed-field strata with uniform
geometry and yield assumptions. H023 gives structured exceptional fields but
no new full factoring bound. Neither result supplies a many-field lower bound
or a free supply of low-height polynomials for arbitrary N.

## Constructive alternatives

Assess at least two materially different possible savings, then select at most
one concrete survivor: shared smoothness/evaluation work over an explicit
input-only polynomial family, or genuine reuse of algebraic relation columns
with a compatible square-reconstruction map. Different representations of the
same field may merely relabel old relations; different fields normally require
separate ideal coordinates. State exact compatibility and independence claims.

Every survivor must specify how to construct its family from N, common modular
root and degree/height bounds; enumerate candidates; process all norm values;
identify distinct useful relations; and lift a matrix dependency to an actual
square congruence modulo N with a proper-split probability. Norm evaluation or
factor testing for K fields times M pairs is not free because the inputs have
a compact description. Shared rational testing alone is not a new mechanism.

## Cost model and falsifier

Charge selection failures, field multiplicity, factor bases per field, prime
ideals/bad primes, integer/ideal valuation extraction, table access, duplicate
relations, matrix dimensions/nonzeros, restarts and all square-root work.
Use actual coefficient height and explicitly state any geometry, smoothness,
rank or adaptive-yield assumptions. Baseline and candidate must use the same
cold versus amortized and heuristic versus rigorous accounting.

Derive a symbolic cost/supply inequality capable of deciding whether the
proposed saving survives these charges. A new primitive that only moves the
same work to preprocessing fails the gate. If the surviving question requires
computation, propose a minimal exact protocol with seeds, controls, budgets and
fixed rejection criteria for separate preregistration. No finite experiment is
authorized within this analytic round; no lack of a counterexample proves yield.
