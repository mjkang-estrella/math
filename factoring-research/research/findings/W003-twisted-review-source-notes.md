## Primary inspection

1. **Dixon (1981), Asymptotically Fast Factorization of Integers.**
   Inspected the archived text of section 2's AL algorithm and operation
   ledger, its theorem and expected-time remark on printed pages 256–257,
   and section 3's Lemma 3 and theorem completion on pages 259–260. The
   squared-list conditioning and expected subexponential class support the
   review's comparison. Exact theorem constants are unnecessary here and
   are not reconstructed from damaged OCR. The claim is a time comparison;
   no polynomial-space assertion for Dixon is made.
   The inherited PDF, text and retrieval ledger are preserved among the
   original 29 snapshots and their review copies.
   Primary URL: https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~cs812-1/dixon.pdf .

2. **Coppersmith (1993), Modifications to the Number Field Sieve.**
   Inspected the archived text's explicit heuristic qualification on page 170,
   section 3's field-specific ideal/character blocks on page 175, fieldwise
   roots and common-image reconstruction on page 176, and the cold many-field
   cost statement on page 177. The factory is a separate amortized comparison
   and is not used. These are homogeneous reconstruction methods, not a
   construction of the reviewed mixed target.
   Primary URL: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF00198464.pdf .

3. **Stevenhagen (2008), The Number Field Sieve.**
   Inspected the archived text of sections 6–7, including obstructions
   (6.3)–(6.6) and the derivative order multiplier; section 10 on root
   extraction; and the opening norm/parameter discussion in section 11.
   The review additionally proves the needed quadratic multiplier directly
   using integer traces. Neither a maximal order nor generic field-root
   algorithm is granted to the reviewed program.
   Primary URL: https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~stevenhagenp/ANTproc/04psh.pdf .

4. **Lee–Venkatesan, arXiv:1805.08873v1.**
   Inspected the archived theorem statements 2.1/2.3, Conjecture 7.82 and its
   use, and Remark 7.85. The primary web HTML also opened successfully in
   this round. The unconditional square-congruence claim and conditional
   useful-congruence claim retain their different scopes; no law is
   transplanted to A. The previously archived exact HTML/text identities
   are frozen here; the fresh web rendering is not claimed to be a newly
   archived byte-identical download.
   Primary URL: https://arxiv.org/html/1805.08873v1 .

5. **Rabin (1979), MIT-LCS-TR-212.**
   The MIT primary repository record opened successfully in this round.
   Independently inspected the already archived primary text at printed
   pages 12–14, Theorems 1–2 and their proofs, including conditioning on the
   four roots, aborting an unsuccessful bounded inversion attempt, and the

   existing Lens B source files, not obtained from a review conclusion.
   Primary record:
   https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/15016449-ca26-4d45-82ea-7c200d82d777 .
