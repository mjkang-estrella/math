# W024: public prime residues remove zero-exponent guards

Parent construction, 2026-10-08 local. Pending independent validation.
Verified new complete factoring improvement: **NONE**. This supplies a concrete
repair to W022's unbalanced-range correctness guard; it does not supply the
missing fast product evaluator or reduce its carry lengths.

## 1. The computation being replaced

W022 represents each covered exponent on a signed prime-power root chart as

    E(x,y,tau)=u+Nv-sigma*R(x,y)-Q*tau,
    u=u0+H*x, v=v0+H*y, Q=ell^(2h), ell | H,
    R(x,y) = r0 modulo ell.

Both u0 and v0 are units modulo the odd prime ell, and
r0^2=4Nu0v0 modulo Q. The root representation and common carry interval
cover S=up+vq, but the enlarged leaf set can have E=0. W020 recovery cannot
treat a zero exponent as a useful annihilator. W022 prevented it with the
guard U+W+2Rmax+Q<N. That guard fails on growing unbalanced ranges.

Replace that magnitude test by the following O(1)-per-sign residue test:

    epsilon_sigma = u0+Nv0-sigma*r0 modulo ell.
    KEEP the chart/sign branch iff epsilon_sigma != 0.

Nothing depends on x, y or tau. Entire ordinary boxes are kept or discarded;
there are no punctured domains, variable carry selectors, or per-pair tests.
Every exponent on a retained branch is nonzero modulo ell and hence is a
nonzero integer. Its magnitude can be large; its binary width must still be
paid. This rejects more than literal zero leaves, so coverage must be proved.

At least one sign of every rooted chart survives. If both epsilon values
were zero, subtracting them would give 2r0=0 modulo ell, impossible for
odd ell and a unit root. When u0-Nv0 is nonzero modulo ell both signs survive,
since (u0+Nv0)^2-r0^2=(u0-Nv0)^2 there. Retaining the sign separately is
stronger than discarding every chart on the public line u0=Nv0.

## 2. Exact coverage condition

Let N=pq, with p prime, gcd(N,ell)=1, and take a primitive covered pair from
ANY valuation stratum in W022. Thus ell does not divide u or v, and

    up-vq = 0 modulo ell^h,
    S=up+vq=sigma*R(x,y)+Q*tau

for a represented root sign and an in-range carry. At this leaf,

    E=u+Nv-S=(p-1)(vq-u)
     =u*(p-1)^2-(p-1)*(up-vq).

Therefore

    E = u*(p-1)^2 modulo ell.

If p != 1 modulo ell, this is nonzero. The actual covered sign survives
the public test, while every retained leaf has nonzero exponent. In
particular p=-1 modulo ell is FINE: the sign test retains the correct root.
Discarding a whole chart instead would unnecessarily lose that case.

This argument survives division by the common weight valuation: the
primitive u is a unit and h>=1 in every nonempty stratum. There is no need
to weaken the modulus to a different exception class in lower strata.
Signed negative v and arbitrarily large signed tau cause no change.

The algorithm never tests the hidden condition p!=1 modulo ell. It executes
the public branch filter for a list of small primes, as follows.

## 3. Public small-prime schedule, no hidden-prime choice

Preprocess even inputs and the prime-power cases exactly as required by the
previous complete splitter interface; that work is not silently removed.
For the odd input under the W020 promise, generate odd primes ell until
their product exceeds N. For each prime, gcd(N,ell) either already yields
a proper factor or makes ell a legal chart base.

For any chosen prime divisor p of N, not every remaining ell can divide
p-1: otherwise their product would divide p-1<N. Thus at least one legal
base has p!=1 modulo ell and preserves a covered witness. This existential
argument uses p only in the proof. The candidate processes all listed
bases on public N-only data, stopping whenever an actual factor is found.

The schedule needs at most ceil(log_2 N)+1 primes, since every listed prime
is at least 3. To claim polynomial-logarithmic generation cost, also bound
their SIZE: standard elementary prime-counting bounds give the K-th prime
O(K log K), so sieving to a polynomial in log N suffices. Independent review
must provide the precise constructive citation or elementary bound and
include prime generation, gcds, root acquisition and every scheduled base.
Do not infer a size bound merely from the bound on the number of primes.

For a target M, choose a positive power ell^k within a factor ell of the
desired size, treating finite small ranges separately. With ell at most
poly(log N), this rounding and a polynomial number of bases contribute
only polynomial-logarithmic factors to the fixed-degree exponent models.
Brute-force root finding modulo ell then costs polynomial-logarithmic
work per rooted residue pair; Hensel lifting and inversions are still paid.
The fixed-ell constants of W022 must be checked uniformly rather than
silently reused. Powers, strata, residue endpoints and all empty boxes
remain explicit public data.

## 4. What this repairs, and what it does not

The filter removes W022's MAGNITUDE GUARD entirely. It allows the original
ordinary chart/carry boxes even when U+W+2Rmax+Q>=N, without contaminating
the product by a literal zero exponent. It also preserves ordinary
subdivision/localization: descendants inherit a nonzero epsilon.

For fixed degree and M=N^O(1), even the unbalanced Rmax=M^(22/5) has only
O(log N) exponent bits. Its enlarged tau interval is nevertheless large.
This construction does NOT remove that occurrence count, the chart cost,
small-factor preprocessing or the unbalanced parameter schedule. Dropping
some signs never increases the per-base leaf count, but processing several
bases costs the stated polynomial-logarithmic overhead. There is still no
sub-one-fifth complete factoring claim, and no GNFS comparison is implied.

Once an evaluator supplies the covered product and paid localization for
these ordinary filtered boxes, W020 can use the ORIGINAL uniform alpha,
its nonunit-leaf recovery and full two-adic chain. The filter is independent
of alpha and does not add a high-order-base assumption. Acceptance of this
composition is part of the requested audit; no new random-success estimate
is inferred from prime residues alone.

The next independent construction is a paid shared evaluator on these
ordinary boxes. Removing the zero guard permits broader integer lifts and
lower-height approximations to be compared honestly; it does not make their
