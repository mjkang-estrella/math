# H019 — Exact root-certificate screen at growing input widths

Status: preregistered mathematical diagnostic protocol, 2026-10-08.
Implementation/source and expanded manifest must also be frozen before the
single run. H017's theorem and certificate passed independent review.

The experiment tests whether a bounded stdlib implementation can certify
H017's finite primitive-supply bound on the fixed inputs below. It does not
factor N, estimate an asymptotic density, prove a field-selection probability,
or test a complete NFS implementation. Input N is the only instance data
given to each worker. These are public odd integers, without a primality,
semiprime or difficult-factorization promise.

## Fixed cases and parameters

Use Python random.Random(1919), in this order, once for each pair:

| requested bit length n | public degree d |
|---:|---:|
| 4096 | 15 |
| 16384 | 22 |
| 65536 | 34 |
| 262144 | 52 |

N=(1<<(n-1)) | rng.getrandbits(n-1) | 1. Archive N in hexadecimal and
verify its bit length. Construct m=floor(N^(1/d)) by exact integer arithmetic,
and f by ordinary base-m digits. Reject an invalid degree/monicity as unknown;
do not alter d or N. Define hbits=ceil(67473*n/(20000*d^2)), H=2^hbits,
and h_*=ceil(H^(4/5)), all by exact integer operations. This is a fixed
rounding of H014's public a*delta^2 scaling, not a fit to the results.

Require square-freeness. One sufficient inexpensive certificate is
gcd(f mod 65537,f' mod 65537)=1, with an independently checked Bezout identity.
Verify the fixed modulus is prime by deterministic trial division once.
If the sufficient modular check fails, return unknown; do not add moduli.
Monicity makes a unit gcd modulo a prime sufficient for square-freeness over Q.

## Candidate certificate

Use H017's exact monomial dominance test with coefficient index d-2 and
R=2H+2. If it fails, return unknown. Its success proves exactly d-2 roots
inside R and two outside. For the inside roots, produce d-2 disjoint rational
disks, each certified to contain exactly one root of the full f and to lie
inside R. The dominance count plus these disks accounts for all d roots;
individual locations of the two exterior roots are unnecessary. This combines
the two reviewed Rouche predicates, without assuming unobserved roots away.

Generate approximate centers from the degree-(d-2) truncation of f using a
single deterministic simultaneous Aberth iteration. Normalize coefficients
by their maximum before floating conversion. Initial centers lie at angles
2*pi*(j+1/2)/(d-2) on its Cauchy-bound circle. Maximum 500 simultaneous sweeps;
stop if every correction is <=2^-42. A collision, nonfinite value or failed
convergence returns unknown. These approximations are proposals only.
Round centers to denominator 2^40 and use radius exactly 2^-24. No alternative
precision, radius, start or root-finder sweep may be tried after outcomes.

The exact verifier checks the Taylor/Rouche inequality from H017 at every
disk, using rational coordinate-sum upper bounds and coordinate-maximum lower
bounds. It checks disjointness and containment, then computes kappa_cert from
the reviewed rational triple formula with k=d-2, r=2. Require positive kappa
and H017's cleared integer inequality (11) at h_*. Archive the resulting exact
primitive upper count (h_*-1)(2h_*-1)^2+k. Do not present an unspecified big-O
constant as a finite all-output bound. The accepted reducible asymptotic
qualification stays separate.

The candidate may form exact Taylor coefficients by Horner shifting. The
independent arithmetic reference must reconstruct them from the direct binomial
formula, check all certificate inequalities and the input/base identities,
and reject a mismatched certificate. Both computations count against the cap.
The reference must not call the candidate's Taylor or predicate functions as
its oracle. An independently verified certificate is a finite theorem witness,
not evidence that approximate roots alone are reliable.

Before the four research inputs, run five fixed verifier controls: correct
disks at both roots of X^2-2; correct disks at both roots of X^2+1; an incorrect
disk centered at zero for X^2-2; a duplicated otherwise valid disk list; and
the nonsquare-free polynomial (X-1)^2. Controls use the same center denominator
and radius. The first two must be accepted and the last three rejected by
their relevant verifier gates. They are not counted as research-input passes.

## Budget, outcomes and immutable evidence

One run only: 55 seconds internal for input setup, controls, four attempts,
independent verification and incremental evidence; external cap 60 seconds.
Each research input has a killable worker cap of 12 seconds, further limited
by the remaining wave budget. Controls together have at most 3 seconds.
Unstarted inputs stay in the fixed denominator. Source writing/reading primary
methods is not an unrecorded candidate test. Syntax checks are allowed before
freezing; no candidate execution or tuning may precede the registered run.

Freeze protocol, both sources, exact input-generation rule, all constants and
the expanded case list with hashes before execution. Use an outer runner that
preserves partial statuses even if a worker dies; refuse overwriting evidence.
Retain pass, unknown, verification failure, error, timeout and unstarted statuses,
stage costs, disk data, normalized geometry bounds, exact comparison factors
and hashes/bit widths of large products. Hexadecimal/binary encodings avoid
decimal conversion limits for large N. No budget enlargement or second run.

Implementation rejection: any falsely accepted or inconsistent certificate.
Finite continuation pass: all verifier controls behave correctly and both
largest registered research inputs produce independently verified H017 count
certificates. Otherwise the screen is inconclusive at this implementation/budget;
each individually valid certificate retains its own finite claim. Unknown or
timeout is not positive relation supply. No population frequency or asymptotic
factoring conclusion follows even if all four pass.

Owned result: research/findings/H019-root-certificate-screen.md and adjacent
H019-certificate-* files. No shared solver/Lean edits or full sieve. Run project
checks after the completed owned implementation is handed back. Independent
review of significant finite claims follows the single run. Verified factoring
complexity improvement: NONE.
