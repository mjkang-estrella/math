# W009 three-anchor sampler — parent disposition

2026-10-08. Exact acquisition and moment laws **accepted with execution and
conditioning qualifications**. Verified factoring complexity improvement: NONE.

The [independent audit](W009-pinned-review.md) validates the homogeneous map,
all projective fibers, legal chart, pinned multiplicative coordinates and
both marked-atom subtractions. The parent verified all 14 delivery hashes.
The 20 symbolic identities and six triples over F_7 are exact certificates,
not candidate runs or evidence of asymptotic factoring performance.

Three paid samples acquire (a,t) with unnormalized probability
nu(a)*Pi(a,t). This is an actual bias toward large merger mass, without
computing Pi. The squared-energy term is therefore meaningful, but not a
proved net gain. Flat residual support has pointwise nonnegative residual
drift; flatness is not preserved. The weighted F_7 law has drift -5/147,
without any claim that this law is reachable. Its simultaneous root
absorption also prevents interpreting negative residual drift as failure
of the entire factoring attempt.

The authoritative qualifications are:

- At ell=0, conditional expectations given legal are undefined. Use the
  legal-truncated sums, which are zero, rather than multiplying an undefined
  expectation by zero.
- The O(c^2) forced-pair bound is an unnormalized expected contribution.
  It is neither a pointwise bound nor a bound after arbitrary conditioning.
- Three full evaluations per attempt refers to the protocol completing
  them before checking. An eager implementation charges its actual prefix.
- At a fixed map, let C,R,J be the probabilities of clean acquisition,
  early proper factor and full-gcd failure. Retrying only failures stops
  after mean 1/(C+R) attempts, with expected paid cost e/(C+R).
  The often-quoted 1/C applies to a different process that ignores factors.
  If C=0 there is no acquired clean law, even when early factoring can occur.
- Current-map CRT independence does not imply independence after conditioning
  on survival. Full-gcd masks, optional separate checks, and their order
  remain part of the executable algorithm's success law.

The audit also specializes a flat coset to a concrete shifted-subgroup
energy of (1-H) minus zero. No prior-art energy theorem was imported and
no arbitrary weighted-law conclusion follows. The next mathematical
