# H023 — Clustered ordinary-base polynomials and anisotropic relations

2026-10-08. Preregistered analytic proposal, **unreviewed**.
Verified new full factoring complexity improvement: **NONE**.

## Precise question

Can a publicly recognizable family with strongly clustered roots evade H020/H022's
uniform separation hypothesis and support a cheaper, fully costed relation
architecture? Separate a counterexample to uniform geometry, a special-family
heuristic model, and an end-to-end factoring algorithm. No all-input claim.

## Proposed family and falsifiers

For even d>=4, even m large, and independently chosen odd M in
[m/2^(d+3), m/2^(d+2)], consider

    f(X)=(X+1)^(d-2)(X²+M+1)+1,    N=f(m),
    G(Y)=f(Y-1)=Y^d-2Y^(d-1)+(M+2)Y^(d-2)+1.

These are proposed checks, not accepted results:
1. All coefficients of f are nonnegative and below m, with leading one,
   and m^d<N<(m+1)^d, so f is exactly the ordinary-base polynomial of N.
   Recover M from N,m,d and verify in polynomial bit work without factors.
2. As d grows at the usual L[1/3] degree scale and log M~log m, actual
   coefficient height has gamma=1. Nevertheless d-2 roots may cluster near
   X=-1 at radius roughly M^(-1/(d-2)). Prove a strict Rouche disk count,
   square-freeness or costed avoidance of exceptional M, and an upper
   bound on actual normalized separation. Do not confuse a poor certificate
   with genuinely small separation. Number-field use additionally needs
   irreducibility or a costed decomposition; geometry alone does not.
3. For g=xX+y=xY+z, z=y-x, and positive x,z, even d gives the proposed identity

       Res(G,xY+z)
        =z^d+2xz^(d-1)+(M+2)x²z^(d-2)+x^d.

   Retain x^d. Omitting it produces a false free-width advantage.
4. In an anisotropic primitive box x<=exp(aD), z<=exp(cD), a>=c>0,
   proposed population exponent is a+c; rational norm coefficient is 1/delta;
   algebraic coefficient is max(a delta,1/delta+c delta), with
   D=t^(1/3)(log t)^(2/3), t=log N. Check sizes uniformly and exact endpoint
   scope. gcd(x,z)=1 is equivalent to gcd(x,y)=1 but proves no useful rank.

A preliminary parent relaxation balancing the two algebraic terms suggests
u=a+c, a=c+delta^(-2), W=u delta/2+3/(2delta). Optimizing delta²=3/u and
b=u/2 would give u³=16/3. This is a question to derive or refute, **not a
verified complexity bound**. The joint smoothness law, enough independent
relations, algebraic square roots and proper splitting are all unpaid unless
explicitly addressed. General GNFS and existing special-form methods are
different baselines.

## Prior-art and construction traps

Search primary sources for generalized/special NFS, polynomial-shape and
multivariate representations only as needed. Arbitrary m,M must be allowed;
choosing M=floor(m/2^d) imposes a small remainder relation and can yield a
smaller-coefficient SNFS representation after substitution, already better
than the proposed model. Check whether even independent parameters admit a
known stronger representation. The exact known factorization
N-1=(m+1)^(d-2)(m²+M+1) may also affect the appropriate baseline.

No claim of infinitely many balanced semiprimes or hard inputs follows from
the identity. A generator for N is not an all-input polynomial selector.
Include recognition, exclusions, enumeration, processing and useful-supply
costs; repeated roots or reducibility cannot be silently ignored.
