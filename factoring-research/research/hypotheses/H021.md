# H021 — Exact low-energy generation for the structured logarithm lattice

Status: proposed constructive analysis, 2026-10-08. No experiment registered.
Verified full factoring complexity improvement: NONE.

The deferred tensor-network lead motivates a distinct classical primitive.
The primary paper's original exponent-coordinate objective has the form

    F(e) = sum_j f_j² e_j² + (sum_j a_j e_j - T)²,
    e in Z^k, f_j positive integers.

Source: [Tesoro et al., equations 20–21 and 25–31](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.16355v3).
Its nearby binary domain after basis reduction differs from the unrestricted
original exponent domain. The perturbed tensor-network sampling state is
also distinct from a list of unperturbed minimum-energy vectors.

Parent proposal, requiring derivation and review: a pseudo-polynomial dynamic
program for the single weighted sum becomes polynomial in ell=bit_length(N)
when the rounding scale C, k, and energy cutoff E have polynomial magnitude.
The zero vector supplies E=T² for global minimization. For E>=0 set
R_j=floor(sqrt(E)/f_j), S=sum_j |a_j|R_j. A prefix table storing the minimum
diagonal cost at each sum s in [-S,S] appears to solve the exact minimum.
Suffix tables may also report all F(e)<=E with paid output/delay or support
exact counting and uniform sampling using an additional energy coordinate.
No useful-relation density follows from exact low-energy generation.

The precision parameter matters in magnitude: C=10^c with c=O(log ell)
is polynomial, whereas merely c=poly(ell) is insufficient. Charge prime
generation, log approximation, signed sums, exponent-vector output and the
bit lengths of constructed u and v. Computing an exact nearest rounded log
must not hide an unbounded tie-separation search. A changed, explicitly
bounded-error integer approximation is acceptable if its error is propagated.

Determine the exact precision-to-cancellation statement. If a_j approximate
C log p_j and T approximates C log N with bounded errors, a small rounded
residual controls log(u/(Nv)) only up to a term proportional to
(1+sum |e_j|)/C. Quantify when this supplies a small integer u-Nv and when
it does not. A polynomial rounding scale may make exact optimization cheap
while leaving the required smoothness/rank probability unproved.

Success of this analytic round means a fully costed exact generator with
clearly delimited domain and a precise remaining useful-supply question, or
a concrete correctness/cost obstruction. It does not mean polynomial-time
factoring. Do not infer actual yield from generic random integers, generic
CVP hardness, energy rank, or a fitted tensor resource law.

No finite experiment, adaptive sweep, tensor package or full siever is
authorized by this hypothesis. A later minimal correctness/yield test needs
its own frozen protocol. New substantive claims need independent review.
