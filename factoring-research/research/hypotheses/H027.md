# H027 — Constructive relation generation beyond the low-energy obstruction

2026-10-08. **Exploratory analytic round; no accepted algorithm claim.**
Verified full factoring complexity improvement: **NONE**.

## Question and target

Can a concrete N-only change to the logarithm-lattice relation generator
produce useful smooth residuals at a lower complete classical factoring cost?
H026 closes the fixed first-ell-prime, aligned-weight, E_star<40ell² supply
statement. Escaping that theorem by changing parameters is only a prerequisite.
The goal is a constructive supply mechanism and its full cost, not another
optimizer theorem for a domain that contains no usable relations.

Consider two genuinely distinct mechanisms before selecting at most one:

1. A different factor-base/weight/energy scaling, with an explicit input-only
   selector and a quantitative residual-supply argument that follows from its
   actual output distribution.
2. Arithmetic conditioning or structured joins that directly favor smooth
   residuals, with congruence construction, determinant loss, search, precision,
   duplicate handling and processing charged.

These are lenses, not proposed algorithms. A simple permutation, polynomially
larger base, or larger cutoff has no presumed benefit. Do not infer a randomness
law merely because H026's proof no longer applies. An all-input result, a
random-input model and a special-family result must be labeled separately.

## Acceptance gate and cheapest falsifier

For each mechanism specify executable primitives from N alone, bit widths,
preprocessing, time/space, generation cost per distinct accepted vector,
smoothness/valuation costs, and the exact unsupported yield statement. Include
the number and width of relation columns, dependence/rank, square reconstruction
and probability of a proper split. Rejection remains useful if one named
primitive or scaling inequality provably loses the proposed gain.

Compare complete claims with the appropriate primary-literature baseline,
including Coppersmith's multiple-polynomial constant for heuristic NFS claims.
Do not claim a full gain from faster closest-vector optimization, a model
minimum, or a factored residual with no useful dependency. Ordinary true-log
rounding and H021's rational bounded-error rule are different constructions.

Prefer a decisive symbolic population/precision/work test first. If a finite
test is needed, deliver its exact protocol for coordinator preregistration:
input families and construction, frozen parameters, seeds, baselines, budgets,
all-failure denominator, pass/reject thresholds and which narrow claim changes.
Do not execute that experiment during this round. Report no survivor candidly
instead of silently broadening the question or increasing budgets.
