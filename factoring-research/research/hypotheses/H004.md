# H004 — Constructible prime-only difference covers

Status: proposed; mathematical reduction and concrete construction searches pending.

## Motivation and distinction from prior art

The W001 baseline pass identified the conditional difference-cover route in
[Umans–Wang](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.10851), especially Theorem 5.2 and Section 6.
The parent verified the source. Their stated strong prefactored divisor condition
is sufficient for improved deterministic integer factoring. Its existence and
efficient uniform construction are not established here. This is existing prior
art, not our discovery or a claimed faster factorer.

Our question is whether a useful *restricted construction* becomes attainable
when only prime coverage and factor extraction from a gcd are required. Dropping
conditions is not automatically an algorithmic improvement; every retained
primitive must have a complete executable cost bound.

## Candidate reduction to review

Let R=floor(sqrt(N)). Choose nonzero integers A_i independent of N's hidden
factors such that every prime <=R divides at least one A_i. Suppose interval
products modulo d<=N and a leaf routine that returns the prime factors of
gcd(d,A_i) are both cheap, uniformly in N and the interval/leaf. Compute
gcd(N, product A_i), recursively identify relevant leaves, collect their prime
divisors, and trial-divide those primes repeatedly into the original N. At most
one prime factor larger than R can remain. The leaf routine is a required
algorithm, not a free factoring oracle. A theorem must state all cost parameters,
including generation of A_i, recursion, repeated primes, output and prime proofs.

This appears weaker than covering all integers individually by S−T and fully
factoring each possibly enormous difference. Verify the exact logical reduction,
whether it is already explicit in prior art, and whether it helps any actual
construction. Prime coverage alone does not satisfy the polynomial-factoring
application, and no transfer to that task is intended.

## Construction lens

Seek S,T with short addition descriptions or another representation enabling
interval products, and A_i the nonzero cross differences. Try one of:
short shifted arithmetic progressions; structured allocation of primes to
differences; a product of known factors plus a controllable leaf cofactor.
For a finite search, report full construction/search work and the largest missed
prime. Random/greedy examples are diagnostic, not an all-input family.

The alpha=beta=1/3 aspiration is a reference target from the paper. It is not a
measured parameter or assumed existence result. To change the known deterministic
exponent, all relevant costs must beat N^(1/5) in the accepted model. Explicitly
listing R^beta integers with R^alpha bits costs R^(alpha+beta), and exhaustive
search for a finite cover cannot be hidden as preprocessing or advice.

## Required next evidence

1. Independent reduction review with either a proof or a precise counterexample.
2. A concrete uniform construction or a bounded falsifier for one specific family.
3. Reconciliation with the initial deterministic/algebraic W001 proposals.

No benchmark is preregistered yet: first obtain an executable primitive and
define its rejection threshold. If the missing primitive simply restates
factoring, reject that version and preserve the obstruction.
