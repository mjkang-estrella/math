## Additional structural identities to investigate

Let beta_i^2=alpha_i on that remaining branch. The reciprocal quartic is

    beta_i^4-s_i beta_i^2+t^2=0.

The expressions beta_i+t/beta_i and beta_i-t/beta_i square to s_i+2t and
s_i-2t. Conversely beta_i is half their sum. Thus the proposed extension
is generated by the two quadratic radicals. Check its actual degree and
automorphisms; do not assume an unspecified norm equation or descent solver.

For a row packet associated to j, define

    P_j(T)=(u_j^2-T)(v_j^2-T).

Direct reduction proposes

    P_j(alpha_i)=(s_i-s_j)alpha_i + c_j,
    c_j=u_j^2 v_j^2-t^2=(u_j v_j-t)(u_j v_j+t).

In its own field this packet is rational. In the other field the coefficient
of alpha_i is a multiple of N. Both packet images agree modulo N. This is
a concrete low-degree relation pattern, not proof that c_j is smooth, square
or cheaply factored. Check zero and nonunit images before using it.

More generally for Q(alpha_i)=U_i+V_i alpha_i,

    Norm(Q)=U_i^2+s_i U_i V_i+t^2 V_i^2.

If this is m^2 and Tr(Q)+2m is nonzero, then

    (Q+m)^2=(Tr(Q)+2m) Q.

Likewise (alpha_i+t)^2=(s_i+2t)alpha_i. These elementary identities may
turn the prescribed square-class target into explicit rational conditions.
Handle the zero-trace exceptional case. Necessary rational norm squareness
alone is not sufficient actual field squareness.
