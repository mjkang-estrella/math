# H029 — Power-difference covers with a paid leaf routine

2026-10-08. **Exploratory analytic round; no accepted new theorem or algorithm.**
Verified full factoring complexity improvement: **NONE**.

## Target and distinction

Return to H004's accepted deterministic reduction with a materially changed
cover construction. Its shifted factorial/power progressions failed coverage.
The remaining interfaces are construction from N, interval products for every
modulus d<=N, and actual prime-factor recovery from each visited leaf gcd.
The target is a deterministic total exponent below N^(1/5); N^(1/6) is only
an aspiration and remains exponential in bit length. This is separate from
the live H027 lattice-supply and H028 multi-field NFS tasks.

The concrete starting family consists of nonzero differences a^x-a^y with
integer bases a>=2 and bounded logarithmic coordinate height. Algebraic
factorizations of power differences might give recursive leaf structure, but
cyclotomic factors are not automatically prime or cheaply factorable.
The parent has derived an UNREVIEWED prime-capacity bound for differences
whose two terms use the same base; see H029-intake.md. Check that proof first.

Then examine two distinct escapes before selecting at most one:

1. Cross-base differences with a precise, exploitable algebraic decomposition
   and an input-only construction. State actual coverage and all intermediate
   widths; a compact expression does not give a cheap modular interval product.
2. An N-dependent cover that only needs to encounter a prime divisor of N,
   paired with a genuine mechanism for separating factors at a leaf. Such a
   cover is outside the universal-prime capacity count, but A_1=N itself shows
   why coverage alone is vacuous. Give a new algorithm, not a leaf oracle.

These are exploratory directions, not granted mechanisms. Reject simple
repackagings of p-1, power-image rho, factorial products or the failed H004
shift grid after a matched cost comparison. Do not assume infinitely many
safe primes, uniform small orders, an unproved exponent-menu coverage law,
or a construction of the Umans–Wang conjectural covers.

## Required evidence and falsifier

For each candidate identify Gen, Prod and Leaf as finite algorithms with
complete deterministic bit costs and space. An exponent's magnitude, bit
length, and the bit length of its power are different costs. Modular repeated
squaring can avoid writing a huge power; it does not pre-factor a difference.
Any coverage proof must hold at usable scales for every input claimed. Explicit
enumeration of all primes through R=floor(sqrt(N)) already loses the target.

Use H004's recurrence, including multiplicity stripping from original N and
the possibility gcd(d,A_i)=d. A known-factors-plus-small-residual leaf promise
must be proved for every visited leaf, and the known factors must be generated
within budget. A repeated gcd equal to N is not successful extraction.

Prefer a symbolic missed-prime family, capacity bound, or charged cost
inequality as the cheapest falsifier. If a concrete candidate survives, supply
one minimal exact experiment protocol with fixed parameters, seeds if used,
controls, budgets, all failures and rejection threshold for separate coordinator
preregistration. No experiment is authorized by this analytic brief.
