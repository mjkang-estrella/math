# H015 — geometric batching by certificate family

Date: 2026-10-08. Parent-owned mathematical screen, no finite run planned.

W001 deterministic Card B loses the hoped-for logarithm at its arbitrary-point
tree. H012 certificates suggest a specific extra structure: if u,t and
v modulo m² are fixed, changing v by m²*k changes the giant step by gamma^k,
where gamma=alpha^(m²). Can grouping by this exact certificate family reduce
the giant points to only polynomially many geometric blocks?

Fix N,m positive, gcd(N,m)=1, H<=C*sqrt(N*m) for a fixed C>0. Certificates
are integer u,v,t with u or t nonzero and

    v+2uj=0 mod m,    Nt+uj²+vj=0 mod m².

Assume every unit residue j modulo m has a selected certificate that covers
one fixed whole dyadic cell [P,2P], with sqrt(N)/4<=P<=sqrt(N)/2, meaning
|u*z+v+Nt/z|<=H for every real z in the cell. This is a coverage requirement
of the stated certificate method, not advice about the actual factor. The
cell requirement is explicit; arbitrary subdivisions require another analysis.

Use family key (u,t,v mod m²). This is a sufficient geometric grouping rule,
not a claim to recognize every progression or equality among modular values.
Count every distinct such family; disconnected or sparsely occupied center
ranges may require additional blocks and cannot reduce this lower count.

Decisive falsifier: a uniform family-capacity bound forcing a positive power
of m families would reject the polynomial-family premise, even with free
within-family arithmetic. It would not rule out merging distinct families
via their giant exponents, order-specific coincidences, a different point
representation, or a better general product algorithm. No factoring, generic
arithmetic-circuit, or multipoint-evaluation lower bound is sought.

This screen is an analytic implication, not a parameter sweep. No finite
inputs, timings, or asymptotic fit will be used. A future experimental or
independent-review round must retain this exact scope.
