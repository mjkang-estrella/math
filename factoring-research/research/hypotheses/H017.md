# H017 — Useful supply in the stronger resultant tail

Status: proposed analytic screen, 2026-10-08.

Claim class: supply or obstruction lemma for the changed H014 mechanism.
The exact enumerator is under independent review. For ordinary nonnegative
monic base-m f, define C0=(3H)^d*(d+1)*A^2. The stronger eta=1/10 set is
1<=a<=H, |b|,|c|<=H, |Res(f,aX^2+bX+c)|^(5d)*N^9<=C0^(5d).
Primitive irreducibles are the main target; zero resultants and reducibles
must be separately accounted for. N and the public parameters are the only
inputs. A special polynomial or factor-informed construction is not a general
input sampler.

H013/H014 give only an upper population exponent
s=max(3a0-9/(5*delta^2),2a0). At a0=153/200, b0=189/200, delta=21/10,
s=18491/9800, while the modeled attempt requirement is
b0+(11/(5*delta)+a0*delta)/(3*b0)<s. These strict inequalities leave a
possible envelope 1.89; they do not establish any survivor supply or smoothness.

The question is whether a materially sharper count, valid uniform lower
construction, or certified geometric diagnostic can resolve that gap. Account
for growing d, coefficient/root separation, actual f construction, and the
distribution of useful independent relations. Fixed-degree constants and a
single resultant-1 example cannot be extrapolated. A sharp upper bound below
the modeled attempt requirement closes this route within that model; an upper
bound above it does not support it.

No numerical experiment is registered here. Tiny-H acceptance fractions are
not the proposed falsifier because C0's slack can dominate. A necessary finite
diagnostic must be preregistered with exact criteria, widths, samples, hashes,
reference, and a single <=60-second run retaining all statuses. Prefer an
analytic statement over a parameter sweep. No full sieve, Lean infrastructure,
packages, or hidden factor advice. New mathematical claims require review.

Prior-art lead: https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.05767 concerns fixed degree and
varying polynomial pairs; inspect its actual hypotheses before applying it.
