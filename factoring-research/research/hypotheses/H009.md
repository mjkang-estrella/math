# H009 — Reduced-ring ECM: intermediate-density audit

Status: preregistered diagnostic, parent-owned.

## Exact question

W001-dynamics Card 4 proposes evaluating the division polynomial
F_k(T)=psi_k(0,1; T,1) for many curves E_a: y²=x³+a*x+1. A concrete
implementation computes the ordinary recurrence in (Z/NZ)[T]/A(T), with
A(T)=product(T-a_i), reducing after each multiplication. It avoids degree
Theta(k²) expansion but apparently retains multiplicative batch-size times
scalar-chain-length work. Test the specific hope that these reduced states
become sparse or small-degree and can therefore be charged cheaply.

No general lower bound on batch evaluation is proposed. A dense intermediate
representation can be replaced only by a specified algorithm with new cost
analysis; this diagnostic does not rule such algorithms out.

## Frozen experiment

- Seed 9009. Generate two distinct balanced semiprimes at each bit length
  32 and 48, using distinct half-size primes and rejecting products of the
  wrong bit length. Trial primality is diagnostic setup, charged to the cap.
  Neither factors nor group orders enter the ring evaluator.
- For each N, batch sizes r=8,16,32. Draw r distinct residues uniformly by
  rejection sampling modulo N. Record all parameters. Check discriminant
  gcds; report singular or factor-revealing choices without redrawing a
  favorable batch. The algebraic recurrence is defined even for those controls.
- Compute F_(2^j) for every j=1,...,64 using the usual memoized recurrence,
  with all polynomial products reduced modulo A and N. Division is only by
  the unit 2, since N is odd. A is monic; its reduction needs no other inverse.
- Record every stage's degree, nonzero coefficient count, degree-slot count,
  polynomial multiplication count, and total input/output coefficient volume.
  Volume is a diagnostic, not certified bit complexity or wall-time speedup.
- Validate every stage by evaluation at every sampled a_i against a scalar
  recurrence. In addition compare j<=4 with unreduced integer-polynomial
  computation followed by reduction; this is an independent arithmetic route.
- Rejection criterion for sparse-collapse hope: at least 90% of all completed
  stages with j>=8 have at least 90% nonzero coefficients. This is a finite
  rejection only. Any validation mismatch is a soundness failure.
- One run, 55-second internal deadline and 60-second external kill. Preserve
  completed, partial, unstarted configurations and sample shortfalls. No larger
  rerun after looking at results. Save exact parameters, source/protocol hashes,
  all stage records and counters in experiments/H009/result.json; never overwrite.

## Mathematical audit to accompany the data

For any polynomial F over characteristic zero with degree D>=r, analyze
F modulo a generic monic degree-r polynomial. Specializing A=(T-u)^r
expresses the remainder through Taylor coefficients and can show each
coefficient is generically nonzero. Applied to successive division polynomials,
this prices the stated materializing recurrence over that coefficient field.
It is not automatically a theorem for every finite/composite modulus or every
curve batch; finite-field specializations and early factor discovery matter.

The comparator is independent scalar evaluation at cost proportional to r*h
for k=2^h. This power-of-two audit is not a full ECM smoothness experiment.
The target additive batch primitive needs roughly r+h work, with all costs and
success assumptions included. No factorization complexity improvement can be
claimed from dense or sparse toy data alone.
