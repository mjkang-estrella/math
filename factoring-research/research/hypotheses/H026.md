# H026 — Exact absence of base-smooth residuals on most low-energy inputs

2026-10-08. Parent analytic proposal, **unreviewed**.
Verified full factoring complexity improvement: **NONE**.

## Precise question

H024's independent reviewer named this missing lemma: for every sufficiently
large composite N coprime to the first ell primes, is a polynomial fraction
of the exact low-energy set S_N={e:F_N(e)<=E_star(N)} supported by nonzero
p_ell-smooth residuals w=u-Nv? Test this exact statement, not a random-integer
smoothness surrogate.

Proposed falsifier: H024's diagonal universe has exp(O(ell^(2/3))) vectors,
each with u,v and residual widths O(ell). An elementary Euler-product upper
bound may show that the TOTAL number of positive p_ell-smooth integers of
O(ell)-bit size is exp(o(ell)). Every vector and signed residual specifies
at most one N. Thus only exp(o(ell)) ell-bit N may have even one such vector.
A prime-count lower bound can compare this with an explicit balanced-semiprime
set of size Omega(2^ell/ell²), ruling out the named all-input supply statement.

## Proof obligations and limits

- Use one N-independent first-prime/weight/energy universe q(e)<=40ell².
- Derive an explicit residual bound uniformly over that universe and ell-bit N.
- Prove the finite Euler-product counting inequality with exact sign/zero scope;
  no Dickman law or heuristic transfer is allowed.
- Bound its logarithm by o(ell) with a specified vanishing exponent.
- Count vector/residual pairs mapping to N without assuming injectivity.
- Check primary Rosser–Schoenfeld prime-count bounds and use a correct
  squarefree balanced-semiprime interval with no endpoint or multiplicity issue.
- Separate uniform input density, a particular balanced-semiprime ensemble,
  and worst-case factoring. Show what fails about the named density lemma,
  while preserving exceptional inputs and alternative generators/bases.
- A larger factor base is a material change; do not extend this conclusion to
  arbitrary polynomial-size smoothness bases, all cutoffs, LLL/TN domains,
  or all factoring algorithms.

## Method

Symbolic proof and independent review only. No executable, finite sample,
benchmark, sweep, package or Lean change. No empirical threshold or seed is
needed. The bounds may be vacuous at practical sizes; no finite performance
claim follows. Preserve the original draft and all objections before repair.
Accepting the conclusion would close this exact low-energy/base-smooth route
on most inputs, not provide a faster factoring method or prove factoring hard.
