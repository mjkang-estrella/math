# H002 — Price the missing square-relation step

Status: queued idea; no candidate implemented.

Question: can a concrete representation of relations x² ≡ y² (mod N) reduce the
total work needed to obtain a nontrivial gcd(x − y, N) on general composites?
The identity alone does not supply a fast way to find useful x,y.

Before running: specify a relation-generation mechanism, compare it with known
quadratic-sieve/NFS methods, and identify one unproved density/independence or
construction-cost assumption. Charge generation, storage, linear algebra,
dependencies, failed gcd extractions, and repetitions. If the idea needs order
finding or modular square roots as a free primitive, reject or price that step.

Cheap falsifier: a declared small-size experiment where all relation work is
counted and matched against a baseline, across separated and unbalanced factors.
Do not run the placeholder as though it were a completed hypothesis; replace
this card with a concrete preregistered proposal first.
